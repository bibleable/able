# Acts 9 — Jesus calls Saul, and Peter visits believers

**About this chapter**

Saul sets out to arrest people who follow Jesus.

On the road to Damascus, Jesus speaks to Saul.

Later, Peter visits believers in two towns.

## Saul hears Jesus on the road

## Acts 9:1

Saul still threatened to kill the followers of Jesus.

He went to the high priest.

## Acts 9:2

Saul asked for letters to the synagogues in Damascus.

He wanted to find people who followed the Way.

He wanted to take them back to Jerusalem as prisoners.

He wanted to arrest both men and women.

**Help with these words**

The high priest was the chief Jewish priest. A synagogue is a place where Jewish people gather to worship. The Way was a name people used for those who followed Jesus.

## Acts 9:3

Saul travelled towards Damascus.

As he came near, light suddenly flashed around him.

The light came from heaven.

## Acts 9:4

Saul fell to the ground.

He heard a voice ask, “Saul, Saul, why are you hurting me?”

## Acts 9:5

Saul asked, “Who are you, Lord?”

The voice answered, “I am Jesus.

I am the one you are hurting.”

## Acts 9:6

Jesus said, “Get up and go into the city.

Someone there will tell you what to do.”

## Acts 9:7

The men travelling with Saul stood still.

They heard the voice, but saw no one.

They could not say a word.

## Acts 9:8

Saul got up and opened his eyes.

He could see nothing.

So the men led Saul by the hand into Damascus.

## Acts 9:9

For three days Saul could not see.

He ate no food and drank nothing.

**Pause and answer**

How did Saul find his way into Damascus?

## Jesus sends Ananias to Saul

## Acts 9:10

Ananias lived in Damascus and followed Jesus.

The Lord Jesus called to him in a vision, “Ananias!”

Ananias answered, “Here I am, Lord.”

**Help with these words**

In a vision, God shows someone something or speaks to them.

## Acts 9:11

Jesus said, “Get up.

Go to the house of Judas on Straight Street.

Ask for Saul from Tarsus.

Saul is praying.”

## Acts 9:12

Jesus said, “Saul has seen you in a vision.

He saw a man named Ananias come to him.

He saw you put your hands on him so he could see again.”

## Acts 9:13

Ananias answered, “Lord, many people have told me about Saul.

He has hurt many of your people in Jerusalem.

## Acts 9:14

Now the chief priests have given him power to arrest people here.

He can arrest everyone who calls on you.”

## Acts 9:15

Jesus said, “Go.

I have chosen Saul to tell people about me.

He will tell the Gentiles and their kings.

He will also tell the people of Israel.

## Acts 9:16

I will show Saul how much he must suffer because of me.”

**Help with these words**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Jesus send Ananias to find Saul?

## Saul can see again

## Acts 9:17

Ananias went to the house.

He put his hands on Saul.

He said, “Brother Saul, Jesus appeared to you on the road.

The Lord Jesus sent me so you can see again.

He sent me so the Holy Spirit can fill you.”

## Acts 9:18

At once, something like scales fell from Saul’s eyes.

Saul could see again.

He stood up, and someone baptised him.

**Help with these words**

Scales are the small plates that cover a fish. Baptism uses water to show that someone belongs to Jesus.

## Acts 9:19

Saul ate some food and felt strong again.

He spent several days with the followers of Jesus in Damascus.

**Pause and answer**

What happened to Saul’s eyes after Ananias came?

## Saul tells people about Jesus

## Acts 9:20

Right away Saul began speaking in the synagogues.

He said, “Jesus is the Son of God.”

## Acts 9:21

Everyone who heard Saul was amazed.

They asked, “Is this the man who hurt people in Jerusalem for calling on Jesus?

Did he come here to take believers to the chief priests as prisoners?”

## Acts 9:22

Saul grew stronger as he spoke.

He showed the Jewish people in Damascus that Jesus is the Christ.

They could find no answer to what Saul said.

**Help with these words**

The Christ is the King God chose.

**Pause and answer**

What did Saul tell people about Jesus?

## Saul escapes from Damascus

## Acts 9:23

Many days later, some Jewish men planned to kill Saul.

## Acts 9:24

Saul found out about their plan.

The men watched the city gates day and night.

They wanted to catch Saul and kill him.

## Acts 9:25

One night Saul’s followers helped him escape.

They put him in a basket.

They lowered him through a window in the city wall.

**Pause and answer**

How did Saul’s followers get him outside the wall?

## Barnabas helps Saul in Jerusalem

## Acts 9:26

Saul went to Jerusalem.

He tried to join the followers of Jesus there.

They were all afraid of Saul.

They did not believe he followed Jesus.

## Acts 9:27

Barnabas took Saul to the apostles.

Barnabas told them what happened on the road to Damascus.

Saul had seen the Lord Jesus.

Jesus had spoken to Saul.

Then Saul had spoken boldly for Jesus in Damascus.

**Help with these words**

Jesus chose the apostles and sent them to speak for him.

## Acts 9:28

Saul stayed with the believers.

He went freely about Jerusalem and spoke boldly for Jesus.

## Acts 9:29

Saul spoke and argued with Greek-speaking Jewish people.

Some of them tried to kill Saul.

## Acts 9:30

The other believers heard about this.

They took Saul to Caesarea.

From there they sent him to Tarsus.

## Acts 9:31

The church had peace throughout Judea, Galilee and Samaria.

The believers showed deep respect for the Lord.

The Holy Spirit gave them courage.

The church grew stronger, and more people joined.

**Pause and answer**

Who took Saul to meet the apostles?

## Aeneas gets up

## Acts 9:32

Peter travelled through the area.

He visited God’s people in Lydda.

## Acts 9:33

There Peter met a man named Aeneas.

For eight years Aeneas had been unable to leave his bed.

He could not move his body freely.

## Acts 9:34

Peter said, “Aeneas, Jesus Christ heals you.

Get up and put your mat away.”

Aeneas got up at once.

## Acts 9:35

All the people in Lydda and Sharon saw Aeneas.

They turned to the Lord Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

What did Peter ask Aeneas to do with his mat?

## The believers in Joppa send for Peter

## Acts 9:36

Tabitha lived in Joppa and followed Jesus.

Her name in Greek was Dorcas.

She often did kind things and helped people in need.

## Acts 9:37

Tabitha became sick and died.

People washed her body.

They laid her in a room upstairs.

## Acts 9:38

Lydda was near Joppa.

The believers heard that Peter was in Lydda.

They sent two men to ask Peter for help.

The men said, “Please come to us at once.”

## Acts 9:39

Peter went with them.

They took Peter upstairs.

Women whose husbands had died stood around him, crying.

They showed Peter the shirts and other clothes Tabitha had made.

**Pause and answer**

What did the women show Peter?

## Tabitha opens her eyes

## Acts 9:40

Peter sent everyone out of the room.

He knelt down and prayed.

Then he turned towards her body.

He said, “Tabitha, get up!”

Tabitha opened her eyes.

She saw Peter and sat up.

## Acts 9:41

Peter took her hand and helped her stand.

He called the believers and the women back.

They saw that Tabitha was alive.

## Acts 9:42

People all over Joppa heard what happened.

Many of them believed in the Lord Jesus.

## Acts 9:43

Peter stayed in Joppa for several days.

He stayed with Simon, a man who made leather from animal skins.

**Pause and answer**

What did Tabitha do when she saw Peter?
