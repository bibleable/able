# Acts 20 — Paul says goodbye to the leaders from Ephesus

**About this chapter**

Paul visits believers on his journey.

Before he leaves for Jerusalem, he prays with the leaders from Ephesus.

## Paul and his friends travel to Troas

## Acts 20:1

The trouble in Ephesus ended.

Paul called the believers together and encouraged them.

Then he said goodbye and left for Macedonia.

## Acts 20:2

Paul travelled through Macedonia and encouraged the people there.

Then he arrived in Greece.

## Acts 20:3

Paul stayed in Greece for three months.

He was about to sail to Syria.

Some Jewish people made a plan to harm him.

So Paul decided to go back through Macedonia.

## Acts 20:4

Several men travelled with Paul.

Sopater, the son of Pyrrhus, came from Berea.

Aristarchus and Secundus came from Thessalonica.

Gaius came from Derbe.

Timothy came too.

Tychicus and Trophimus came from the province of Asia.

## Acts 20:5

These men went ahead and waited for us in Troas.

**Help with these words**

The writer says “us” because he was travelling with Paul.

## Acts 20:6

After the Feast of Unleavened Bread, we sailed from Philippi.

Five days later we met the others in Troas.

We stayed there for seven days.

**Help with these words**

During this feast Jewish families ate bread made without yeast. They remembered how God brought their people out of Egypt.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the men wait for Paul and his companions?

## Eutychus falls from a window

## Acts 20:7

On the first day of the week, we gathered to break bread together.

Paul planned to leave the next day.

So he spoke to the people until midnight.

## Acts 20:8

Many lamps lit the upstairs room where we had gathered.

## Acts 20:9

A young man named Eutychus sat in the window.

As Paul kept talking, Eutychus fell deeply asleep.

He fell from the third floor.

When people picked him up, he was dead.

## Acts 20:10

Paul went downstairs and bent over Eutychus.

He put his arms around him.

Paul said, “Do not be afraid.

He is alive!”

## Acts 20:11

Paul went back upstairs, broke bread and ate.

He talked with the people until dawn.

Then he left.

## Acts 20:12

The people took Eutychus home alive.

What a relief that was for them!

**Pause and answer**

Why did the people feel relieved when they took Eutychus home?

## Paul joins the ship at Assos

## Acts 20:13

We went ahead by ship to Assos.

Paul had asked us to meet him there.

He planned to walk to Assos.

## Acts 20:14

Paul met us in Assos and came aboard.

Together we sailed to Mitylene.

## Acts 20:15

The next day we reached a place across from Chios.

A day later we reached Samos.

The following day we reached Miletus.

## Acts 20:16

Paul chose to sail past Ephesus.

He wanted to avoid spending more time in the province of Asia.

He hoped to reach Jerusalem by the feast of Pentecost.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Paul choose to sail past Ephesus?

## Paul reminds the leaders how he taught

## Acts 20:17

In Miletus Paul sent for the elders of the church in Ephesus.

**Help with these words**

Elders were leaders who cared for the believers.

## Acts 20:18

The elders came to Paul.

He said, “You know how I lived among you.

You saw me from my first day in the province of Asia.

## Acts 20:19

I served the Lord humbly and often in tears.

It was hard when some Jewish people made plans to harm me.

## Acts 20:20

Whenever something would help you, I told you about it.

I taught you in public and in your homes.

## Acts 20:21

I gave Jews and Greeks the same message.

Turn away from sin and turn to God.

Trust in our Lord Jesus Christ.”

**Pause and answer**

Where did Paul teach the people?

## Paul explains why he is going to Jerusalem

## Acts 20:22

Paul continued, “The Holy Spirit urges me to go to Jerusalem.

I do not know what will happen to me there.

## Acts 20:23

In town after town the Holy Spirit warns me.

People will put me in chains, and I will suffer.

## Acts 20:24

Finishing the work the Lord Jesus gave me matters more to me than my own life.

My work is to tell the good news of God’s kindness.

## Acts 20:25

I have told you about God’s kingdom.

Now I know none of you will see me again.

## Acts 20:26

So today I tell you this.

If anyone is lost, I am not to blame.

## Acts 20:27

I held nothing back.

I told you everything God wanted you to know.”

**Pause and answer**

What work did the Lord Jesus give Paul?

## Paul asks the leaders to care for the church

## Acts 20:28

Paul said, “Watch over yourselves and the whole flock.

The Holy Spirit put these people in your care.

Be shepherds to God’s church.

God bought the church with his own blood.

**Help with these words**

A shepherd looks after a flock of sheep. Paul asks the leaders to care for the believers like that. The words about blood point to the price Jesus paid by dying.

![A sheep pen with a gate, where a shepherd can gather the flock.](../pictures/sheep-pen.svg)

## Acts 20:29

I know that after I leave, fierce wolves will come among you.

They will hurt the flock.

## Acts 20:30

Even some of you will twist the truth.

They will try to make believers follow them instead.

**Help with these words**

The wolves in Paul’s warning stand for people who will harm the believers.

## Acts 20:31

So stay alert.

Remember how I warned each of you for three years.

I warned you night and day, often in tears.”

**Pause and answer**

Who is the flock in Paul’s warning?

## Paul asks the leaders to help the weak

## Acts 20:32

Paul continued, “Now I leave you in God’s care.

I trust the message about his kindness to make you strong.

Through it you can receive what God gives to all his holy people.

## Acts 20:33

I never wanted anyone else’s silver, gold or clothes.

## Acts 20:34

You know I worked with my own hands.

I earned what my companions and I needed.

## Acts 20:35

I showed you how to work hard and help the weak.

Remember what the Lord Jesus said.

‘There is more blessing in giving than in receiving.’”

**Pause and answer**

How did Paul meet the needs of his companions?

## The leaders say goodbye

## Acts 20:36

Then Paul knelt down with all the leaders and prayed.

## Acts 20:37

They all cried as they hugged and kissed Paul.

## Acts 20:38

What saddened them most was hearing Paul say they would never see him again.

Then they walked with him to the ship.

**Pause and answer**

What did Paul and the leaders do when they knelt down?
