# Acts 22 — Paul tells the crowd how he met Jesus

**About this chapter**

Paul speaks to the crowd in Jerusalem.

He tells them why he began following Jesus.

## Paul tells the people about his past

## Acts 22:1

Paul said, “My brothers and fathers, please listen.

Let me answer the charges against me.”

## Acts 22:2

The people heard Paul speaking Hebrew.

They became even quieter.

Then Paul spoke again.

## Acts 22:3

“I am Jewish.

I was born in Tarsus in Cilicia.

I grew up here in Jerusalem.

My teacher, Gamaliel, taught me to follow our ancestors’ law closely.

I was as eager to serve God as you are today.

## Acts 22:4

I attacked people who followed the Way.

I even had people put to death.

I arrested both men and women and put them in prison.

**Help with these words**

The Way was a name for following Jesus.

## Acts 22:5

The high priest and the whole council can tell you this is true.

They gave me letters for the Jewish leaders in Damascus.

I set out to arrest people who followed Jesus there.

I planned to bring them to Jerusalem for punishment.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did Paul first set out for Damascus?

## Jesus speaks to Paul on the road

## Acts 22:6

Paul continued, “About noon I came near Damascus.

Suddenly a bright light from heaven flashed around me.

## Acts 22:7

I fell to the ground.

A voice said, ‘Saul, Saul, why are you hurting me?’

**Help with these words**

Saul was another name for Paul.

## Acts 22:8

I asked, ‘Who are you, Lord?’

He answered, ‘I am Jesus of Nazareth.

I am the one you are hurting.’

## Acts 22:9

The people travelling with me saw the light.

But they could not understand the voice speaking to me.

## Acts 22:10

I asked, ‘What should I do, Lord?’

Jesus said, ‘Get up and go into Damascus.

There someone will tell you everything God has chosen you to do.’

## Acts 22:11

The light was so bright that I could no longer see.

My companions led me by the hand into Damascus.”

**Pause and answer**

How did Paul get into Damascus when he could no longer see?

## Ananias comes to Paul

## Acts 22:12

Paul said, “A man named Ananias lived in Damascus.

He followed God’s law with care.

All the Jewish people there respected him.

## Acts 22:13

Ananias came and stood beside me.

He said, ‘Brother Saul, see again.’

At that moment I could see him.

## Acts 22:14

Ananias said, ‘The God of our ancestors has chosen you.

He wants you to know what he wants you to do.

He has chosen you to see the Righteous One and hear his voice.

**Help with these words**

The Righteous One is Jesus. He does what is right in God’s sight.

## Acts 22:15

You will be his witness.

You will tell everyone what you have seen and heard.

## Acts 22:16

What are you waiting for?

Get up and receive baptism.

Call on Jesus and wash away your sins.’”

**Help with these words**

Baptism uses water. Washing away sins is a picture of forgiveness for the wrongs a person has done.

**Pause and answer**

What did Ananias say Paul would tell people?

## Paul sees Jesus in Jerusalem

## Acts 22:17

Paul continued, “Later I returned to Jerusalem.

While I was praying in the temple, I had a vision.

## Acts 22:18

I saw the Lord Jesus.

He said, ‘Hurry!

Leave Jerusalem quickly.

People here will refuse to believe what you say about me.’

## Acts 22:19

I answered, ‘Lord, they know what I did.

I went from one synagogue to another.

I beat people who believed in you and put them in prison.

**Help with these words**

A synagogue is a place where Jewish people meet to worship.

## Acts 22:20

Stephen was your witness.

When people killed him, I stood there and approved.

I guarded the clothes of the people who killed him.’

## Acts 22:21

Jesus told me, ‘Go!

I will send you far away to the Gentiles.’”

**Help with these words**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish.

**Pause and answer**

Where was Paul praying when he saw Jesus in a vision?

## The Roman commander hears the shouting

## Acts 22:22

Until Paul said this, the crowd listened.

Then they shouted, “Get rid of him!

He does not deserve to live!”

## Acts 22:23

The people shouted and threw off their cloaks.

They threw dust into the air.

![A cloak, an outer garment like the ones people threw off.](../pictures/cloak.svg)

## Acts 22:24

The Roman commander ordered the soldiers to take Paul into their barracks.

He told them to whip Paul and question him.

He wanted to know why people were shouting against Paul.

**Help with these words**

The barracks were the buildings where soldiers stayed.

## Acts 22:25

The soldiers stretched Paul out to tie him down.

Paul asked an officer, “Does the law allow you to whip a Roman citizen without a trial?”

## Acts 22:26

The officer went to the commander.

He said, “What are you going to do?

This man is a Roman citizen.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the officer tell the commander about Paul?

## The commander brings Paul before the council

## Acts 22:27

The commander came and asked Paul, “Are you a Roman citizen?”

Paul answered, “Yes.”

## Acts 22:28

The commander said, “I paid a lot to become a citizen.”

Paul answered, “I was born a citizen.”

## Acts 22:29

The men who were about to question Paul stepped back at once.

The commander was afraid.

He realised he had put a Roman citizen in chains.

## Acts 22:30

The next day the commander released Paul.

He wanted to know exactly why the Jewish leaders accused him.

He called the chief priests and the whole Jewish council together.

Then he brought Paul to stand before them.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the commander call the council together?
