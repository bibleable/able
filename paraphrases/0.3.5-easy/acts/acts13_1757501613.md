# Acts 13 — Barnabas and Saul go out to share God’s message

**About this chapter**

Barnabas and Saul sail to Cyprus.

Later, Saul speaks about Jesus in a synagogue.

## The church in Antioch sends Barnabas and Saul

## Acts 13:1

The church in Antioch had prophets and teachers.

They included Barnabas, Simeon (also called Niger), and Lucius from Cyrene.

Manaen and Saul were there too.

Manaen had grown up with Herod, a local ruler.

**Help with these words**

Prophets speak God’s messages to people.

## Acts 13:2

They worshipped the Lord and went without food.

The Holy Spirit said, “Choose Barnabas and Saul for me.

I have work for them to do.”

## Acts 13:3

The others went without food and prayed.

Then they placed their hands on Barnabas and Saul.

They sent the two men on their way.

**Pause and answer**

Who chose Barnabas and Saul for this work?

## The governor of Cyprus wants to listen

## Acts 13:4

The Holy Spirit sent Barnabas and Saul to Seleucia.

From there they sailed to the island of Cyprus.

## Acts 13:5

They reached Salamis and spoke in the Jewish meeting places.

These places were called synagogues.

They told people God’s message.

John went with them to help.

## Acts 13:6

They crossed the island and reached Paphos.

There they met a Jewish man named Bar-Jesus.

He practised magic and falsely claimed to speak for God.

## Acts 13:7

Bar-Jesus worked for the governor, Sergius Paulus.

The governor was a man of good understanding.

He called for Barnabas and Saul.

He wanted to hear God’s message.

## Acts 13:8

Bar-Jesus also went by the name Elymas, meaning someone who practises magic.

Elymas spoke against Barnabas and Saul.

He tried to keep the governor from believing.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the governor call for Barnabas and Saul?

## Paul speaks to Elymas

## Acts 13:9

Saul also went by the name Paul.

The Holy Spirit filled Paul.

Paul looked straight at Elymas.

## Acts 13:10

Paul said, “You act like a child of the devil.

You fight against everything good.

You tell lies and trick people in all kinds of ways.

Why do you keep twisting what the Lord says is right?

## Acts 13:11

Now the Lord will act against you.

For a time you will be blind.

You will not even see the sun.”

At once everything looked misty and dark to Elymas.

He reached around for someone to lead him by the hand.

## Acts 13:12

The governor saw what happened and believed.

What he learned about the Lord amazed him.

**Pause and answer**

Who needed someone to lead him by the hand?

## Paul stands up to speak

## Acts 13:13

Paul and the others sailed from Paphos to Perga in Pamphylia.

There John left them and went back to Jerusalem.

## Acts 13:14

From Perga they travelled inland to another city called Antioch.

This was Antioch in Pisidia.

On the Sabbath they went into the synagogue and sat down.

**Help with these words**

The Sabbath is the Jewish day of rest.

## Acts 13:15

Someone read aloud from the Law and the Prophets.

Then the synagogue leaders sent them a message.

“Brothers, please speak if you have words to encourage the people.”

**Help with these words**

The Law and the Prophets are parts of the Jewish Scriptures. People read these writings from scrolls.

![A scroll opened to show lines of writing.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## Acts 13:16

Paul stood up and raised his hand.

He said, “People of Israel, listen to me.

You Gentiles who honour God, listen too.”

**Help with these words**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish.

**Pause and answer**

What did the leaders invite Paul and his companions to do?

## Paul tells how God helped Israel

## Acts 13:17

Paul said, “The God of Israel chose our ancestors.

In Egypt, God made them a great people.

Then God used his power to lead them out.

## Acts 13:18

For about forty years, God put up with their behaviour in the wilderness.

## Acts 13:19

God defeated seven nations in Canaan.

He gave their land to his people to keep.

## Acts 13:20

All this took about 450 years.

Then God gave them leaders called judges.

This continued until the time of Samuel the prophet.

## Acts 13:21

Then the people asked for a king.

God gave them Saul, son of Kish, from the tribe of Benjamin.

King Saul ruled for forty years.

## Acts 13:22

God removed Saul and made David king.

God said, ‘I have found David, the son of Jesse.

He cares about what matters to me.

He will do everything I want him to do.’”

**Pause and answer**

Who became king after King Saul?

## God keeps his promise to send a Saviour

## Acts 13:23

Paul continued, “God promised Israel a Saviour from David’s family.

God kept that promise by sending Jesus.

## Acts 13:24

Before Jesus began his work, John spoke to all Israel.

He urged people to turn away from sin and receive baptism.

**Help with these words**

This was John the Baptist. In baptism, a person takes part in a ceremony with water.

## Acts 13:25

Near the end of his work, John asked, ‘Who do you think I am?

I am not the one you are waiting for.

Someone is coming after me.

I am not even worthy to untie his sandals.’

## Acts 13:26

My brothers, you who belong to Abraham’s family, listen.

You Gentiles who honour God, listen too.

God sent us this message about how he saves us.”

**Pause and answer**

Who came from David’s family to save people?

## God raises Jesus from the dead

## Acts 13:27

Paul said, “The people of Jerusalem and their leaders failed to recognise Jesus.

Every Sabbath they heard the prophets’ words.

When they judged Jesus guilty, those words came true.

## Acts 13:28

They found no reason to put Jesus to death.

Even so, they asked Pilate to have him killed.

## Acts 13:29

Everything the Scriptures said about Jesus happened.

Then people took Jesus down from the wooden cross.

They laid his body in a tomb.

## Acts 13:30

But God raised Jesus from the dead.

## Acts 13:31

For many days his companions saw him alive.

They had travelled with him from Galilee to Jerusalem.

Now they tell our people what they saw.”

**Pause and answer**

Who saw Jesus alive after God raised him?

## Paul explains the promises in Scripture

## Acts 13:32

Paul continued, “We bring you good news.

God made a promise to our ancestors.

## Acts 13:33

Now God has kept that promise to us, their children.

He has raised up Jesus.

In the second Psalm, God says, ‘You are my Son.

Today I have become your Father.’

**Help with these words**

A psalm is a song or prayer in the Bible.

## Acts 13:34

God raised Jesus from the dead.

His body will never rot.

God said, ‘I will give you the holy gifts I promised David.

You can count on my promise.’

## Acts 13:35

Another psalm says, ‘You will never let the body of your Holy One rot.’

## Acts 13:36

David did what God wanted during his lifetime.

Then David died.

People buried him with his ancestors, and his body rotted.

## Acts 13:37

God raised Jesus from the dead.

Jesus’ body did not rot.”

**Pause and answer**

What happened to Jesus’ body that was different from David’s?

## Paul speaks about forgiveness

## Acts 13:38

Paul said, “My brothers, hear this news.

Through Jesus, God offers to forgive your sins.

## Acts 13:39

The law of Moses could not clear you of every wrong.

Through Jesus, God clears everyone who believes of all those wrongs.

## Acts 13:40

Take care that the prophets’ warning does not come true for you.

## Acts 13:41

God said, ‘Look, you who laugh at my message!

Be amazed and die!

I am doing something in your own time.

Even if someone told you, you would never believe it.’”

**Pause and answer**

Through whom does God offer forgiveness?

## People ask to hear more

## Acts 13:42

As Paul and Barnabas left the synagogue, people asked them to return.

They wanted to hear more on the next Sabbath.

## Acts 13:43

After the meeting, many people followed Paul and Barnabas.

Some were Jewish by birth.

Others had chosen to worship God by following the Jewish faith.

Paul and Barnabas urged them all to keep trusting in God’s kindness.

## Acts 13:44

The next Sabbath almost everyone in the city gathered.

They wanted to hear the Lord’s message.

## Acts 13:45

Some Jewish people saw the crowds and grew jealous.

They argued against Paul’s words and spoke insults against what was holy.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the crowd gather on the next Sabbath?

## Gentiles welcome the good news

## Acts 13:46

Paul and Barnabas answered boldly, “We had to tell you God’s message first.

You have pushed it away.

You judge yourselves unworthy of life that lasts for ever.

So now we will speak to the Gentiles.

## Acts 13:47

The Lord gave us this command.

He said, ‘I have made you a light for the Gentiles.

Bring the news that I save to the ends of the earth.’”

**Help with these words**

A light helps people see where to go. Here it means showing people everywhere that God can save them.

## Acts 13:48

The Gentiles were glad to hear this.

They praised the Lord’s message.

All those God had chosen for life that lasts for ever believed.

## Acts 13:49

The Lord’s message spread through the whole region.

**Pause and answer**

What made the Gentiles glad?

## Paul and Barnabas leave the city

## Acts 13:50

The Jewish people who opposed Paul stirred up others against him and Barnabas.

They turned influential women who worshipped God against the two men.

They also turned the city’s leading men against them.

Together they attacked Paul and Barnabas and drove them away.

## Acts 13:51

Paul and Barnabas shook the dust off their feet.

This showed their protest against the people who drove them away.

Then they went to Iconium.

## Acts 13:52

The followers of Jesus were full of joy.

The Holy Spirit filled them.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Paul and Barnabas go next?
