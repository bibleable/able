# Acts 19 — Paul teaches in Ephesus, and a crowd gathers

**About this chapter**

Paul teaches people about Jesus in Ephesus.

A silver worker worries about what this means for his work.

## Paul meets believers in Ephesus

## Acts 19:1

Apollos was in Corinth.

Paul travelled inland and reached Ephesus.

There he met some believers.

## Acts 19:2

Paul asked, “Did you receive the Holy Spirit when you believed?”

They answered, “No.

We have never even heard of the Holy Spirit.”

## Acts 19:3

Paul asked, “Then what baptism did you receive?”

They answered, “The baptism John taught.”

## Acts 19:4

Paul said, “John baptised people who turned away from sin.

He told them to believe in the one coming after him.

He meant Jesus.”

**Help with these words**

In baptism, people use water as a sign of their faith.

## Acts 19:5

When the men heard this, they received baptism in the name of the Lord Jesus.

## Acts 19:6

Paul placed his hands on them.

The Holy Spirit came upon them.

They spoke in other languages and gave messages from God.

## Acts 19:7

There were about twelve men in the group.

**Pause and answer**

What happened when Paul placed his hands on the men?

## People hear the Lord’s message across the province

## Acts 19:8

Paul went to the synagogue, the Jewish place of worship.

For three months he spoke boldly there.

He tried to persuade people about God’s kingdom.

**Help with these words**

God’s kingdom means God’s rule over his people.

## Acts 19:9

Some people firmly refused to believe.

They spoke against the Way in front of everyone.

So Paul left the synagogue and took the believers with him.

Every day he held discussions in the teaching hall of Tyrannus.

**Help with these words**

The Way was a name for following Jesus.

## Acts 19:10

Paul taught there for two years.

Everyone in the province of Asia heard the Lord’s message.

Both Jews and Greeks heard it.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Paul teach after he left the synagogue?

## Seven men try to use Jesus’ name

## Acts 19:11

God did unusual miracles through Paul.

## Acts 19:12

People took cloths and aprons that had touched Paul to those who were sick.

The sick people got well.

Evil spirits left people too.

## Acts 19:13

Some Jewish men travelled around driving out evil spirits.

They tried using the name of the Lord Jesus.

They told the spirits, “I command you by Jesus, the one Paul speaks about.”

## Acts 19:14

Seven sons of Sceva did this.

Sceva was a Jewish chief priest.

## Acts 19:15

One day an evil spirit answered them.

“I know Jesus.

I know about Paul.

But who are you?”

## Acts 19:16

The man with the evil spirit jumped on them.

He overpowered all seven men.

They ran from the house, naked and wounded.

**Pause and answer**

What did the evil spirit ask the seven men?

## Believers speak openly about what they have done

## Acts 19:17

All the Jews and Greeks in Ephesus heard what happened.

They were afraid and showed great respect for the Lord Jesus.

## Acts 19:18

Many believers came forward.

They told people openly what they had done wrong.

## Acts 19:19

Many people who had practised magic brought their books.

They burned the books in front of everyone.

People added up what the books were worth.

The total came to fifty thousand silver coins called drachmas.

![Two coins. The books together were worth fifty thousand silver coins.](../pictures/two-coins.svg)

The picture shows two coins. The books were worth many more.

## Acts 19:20

The Lord’s message kept spreading.

It had a powerful effect on people.

**Pause and answer**

What did the people who had practised magic do with their books?

## Paul makes plans for his journey

## Acts 19:21

After this, the Spirit guided Paul in making a decision.

Paul would go through Macedonia and Achaia to Jerusalem.

He said, “After Jerusalem, I must visit Rome too.”

## Acts 19:22

Paul sent two helpers, Timothy and Erastus, to Macedonia.

Paul stayed a while longer in the province of Asia.

**Pause and answer**

Who went ahead of Paul to Macedonia?

## Demetrius calls the workers together

## Acts 19:23

About this time a great uproar began over the Way.

## Acts 19:24

It began with Demetrius, a man who worked with silver.

He made small silver models of the shrine of Artemis.

This work brought in plenty of money for the workers.

**Help with these words**

People in Ephesus worshipped Artemis as a goddess. A shrine is a place set apart for worship.

## Acts 19:25

Demetrius gathered the workers, including others who did similar work.

He said, “Friends, you know how we earn our money.

## Acts 19:26

You see and hear what Paul is doing.

He says the gods people make are no gods at all.

Many people have listened to him and turned away from them.

This has happened here and across almost all of Asia.

## Acts 19:27

People may stop respecting our work.

They may stop honouring the temple of our great goddess Artemis.

Artemis herself could lose her greatness!

People across Asia and around the world worship her.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Demetrius make for a living?

## The crowd rushes into the theatre

## Acts 19:28

The workers grew angry and shouted, “Great is Artemis of Ephesus!”

## Acts 19:29

Soon the whole city was in confusion.

The crowd rushed into the theatre.

They dragged Gaius and Aristarchus with them.

These men came from Macedonia and travelled with Paul.

**Help with these words**

The theatre was a large place where people gathered to watch or listen.

## Acts 19:30

Paul wanted to speak to the crowd.

The believers would not let him go.

## Acts 19:31

Some officials in the province were Paul’s friends.

They sent him a message begging him to stay away from the theatre.

## Acts 19:32

Inside, people shouted different things.

Most did not even know why they had come.

## Acts 19:33

Some Jewish people in the crowd pushed Alexander forward.

Alexander raised his hand to ask for quiet.

He wanted the people to hear his defence.

## Acts 19:34

The crowd realised Alexander was Jewish.

For about two hours they shouted the same words together.

“Great is Artemis of Ephesus!”

**Pause and answer**

Why did Alexander raise his hand?

## A city official quiets the crowd

## Acts 19:35

At last a city official quieted the crowd.

He said, “People of Ephesus, everyone knows what our city guards.

We guard the temple of the great Artemis.

We also guard her image that fell from heaven.

## Acts 19:36

No one disputes these things.

So stay calm.

Think before you act.

## Acts 19:37

You dragged these men here.

They have not robbed our temple or insulted our goddess.

## Acts 19:38

If Demetrius and the workers have a complaint, the courts are open.

The governors can hear their case.

Let people bring their charges there.

## Acts 19:39

If you want anything else, bring it to a lawful town meeting.

## Acts 19:40

After today, someone could accuse us of starting a riot.

We have no good reason to give for all this trouble.”

## Acts 19:41

Then the official sent everyone away.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the official tell Demetrius to take his complaint?
