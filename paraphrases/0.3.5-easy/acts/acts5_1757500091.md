# Acts 5 — Peter speaks about a lie and the apostles keep teaching

**About this chapter**

Ananias and Sapphira lie about the price of their land.

Later, the council orders the apostles to stop teaching about Jesus.

## Peter speaks to Ananias about the money

## Acts 5:1

Ananias and his wife Sapphira sold some land.

## Acts 5:2

Ananias kept some money for himself.

Sapphira knew what he had done.

He brought the rest and put it at the apostles’ feet.

## Acts 5:3

Peter said, “Ananias, why have you let Satan fill your heart?

You kept some money from the sale and lied to the Holy Spirit.

## Acts 5:4

“The land was yours before you sold it.

After the sale, you could decide what to do with the money.

How could you plan to do this?

Your lie was a lie to God himself.”

**Help with these words**

Peter says Ananias was free to decide how much to give.

Ananias pretended that his gift was the whole price.

## Acts 5:5

When Ananias heard this, he fell down and died.

Everyone who heard what happened was very afraid.

## Acts 5:6

Some young men wrapped his body.

They carried him outside and buried him.

**Pause and answer**

Who could decide what to do with the money after the sale?

## Peter asks Sapphira about the sale

## Acts 5:7

About three hours later Sapphira came in.

She did not know what had happened to her husband.

## Acts 5:8

Peter asked, “Is this all the money you got for the land?”

Sapphira answered, “Yes, that is how much we got.”

## Acts 5:9

Peter said, “Why did you two agree to test the Lord’s Spirit?

The men who buried your husband are at the door.

They will carry you out too.”

## Acts 5:10

At once Sapphira fell at Peter’s feet and died.

The young men came in and found her dead.

They carried her out and buried her beside her husband.

## Acts 5:11

The whole church was afraid.

So was everyone else who heard what happened.

**Help with these words**

Here the church means the people who trust Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

What did Sapphira tell Peter about the price of the land?

## People come for healing

## Acts 5:12

The apostles did many signs and wonders among the people.

The believers gathered in the covered walkway called Solomon’s Colonnade.

## Acts 5:13

The people respected the believers.

Yet no one else dared to join their gathering.

## Acts 5:14

Still, many more men and women came to trust the Lord.

## Acts 5:15

People carried sick people into the streets on beds and mats.

They hoped that even Peter’s shadow might fall on some of them as he passed.

**About the picture**

## Acts 5:16

Crowds came from towns around Jerusalem too.

They brought sick people and people troubled by evil spirits.

All of them were healed.

**Pause and answer**

Why did people bring beds and mats into the streets?

## An angel opens the jail

## Acts 5:17

The high priest and those with him were jealous.

They belonged to a group called the Sadducees.

## Acts 5:18

They arrested the apostles and put them in the public jail.

## Acts 5:19

That night an angel of the Lord opened the jail doors.

The angel led the apostles out.

## Acts 5:20

The angel said, “Go and stand in the temple courts.

Tell the people the whole message about this new life.”

**Pause and answer**

Where did the angel tell the apostles to go?

## The officers find the jail empty

## Acts 5:21

At dawn the apostles went there and began to teach.

Meanwhile the high priest and his companions called together Israel’s council of elders.

They sent officers to the jail to fetch the apostles.

## Acts 5:22

The officers went to the jail.

The apostles were gone.

The officers went back and reported what they found.

## Acts 5:23

They said, “The doors were firmly locked.

The guards were at the doors.

But when we opened the doors, no one was inside.”

## Acts 5:24

The captain of the temple guard and the chief priests heard the report.

They could not understand what was happening.

## Acts 5:25

Then someone brought news.

“The men you put in jail are teaching in the temple courts!”

**Pause and answer**

Where did someone find the apostles teaching?

## The apostles answer the council

## Acts 5:26

The captain and his officers brought the apostles back without using force.

They were afraid the people might throw stones at them.

## Acts 5:27

The officers brought the apostles before the council.

The high priest questioned them.

## Acts 5:28

He said, “We ordered you to stop teaching in Jesus’ name.

Now people all over Jerusalem have heard your teaching.

You want to blame us for his death.”

**Help with these words**

Speaking in Jesus’ name means speaking with his authority.

## Acts 5:29

Peter and the other apostles answered, “We must obey God, even when people tell us otherwise.

## Acts 5:30

“You had Jesus killed on a wooden cross.

The God of our ancestors raised him from the dead.

## Acts 5:31

“God gave Jesus the place of honour at his right hand as Leader and Saviour.

Through Jesus, God gives the people of Israel a way to turn back to him and receive forgiveness for their sins.

**Help with these words**

A saviour is someone who rescues people.

## Acts 5:32

“We are witnesses to these things.

The Holy Spirit is a witness too.

God gives his Spirit to those who obey him.”

**Pause and answer**

Whom did the apostles say they must obey?

## Gamaliel asks the council to think

## Acts 5:33

The council members were furious.

They decided to kill the apostles.

## Acts 5:34

A Pharisee named Gamaliel stood up in the council.

He taught God’s law, and everyone respected him.

He ordered that the apostles wait outside for a while.

## Acts 5:35

Gamaliel said, “Men of Israel, think carefully about what you are about to do to these men.

## Acts 5:36

“Some time ago Theudas claimed to be an important man.

About four hundred men joined him.

Someone killed Theudas, and all his followers scattered.

Their plan came to nothing.

## Acts 5:37

“Later, when the rulers were counting the people, Judas of Galilee gained followers.

He also died, and all his followers scattered.

**Pause and answer**

What happened to the followers of Theudas after he died?

## The council hears Gamaliel’s advice

## Acts 5:38

“Here is my advice about these apostles.

Leave them alone. Let them go.

If this work is only a human plan, it will fail.

## Acts 5:39

“But if it comes from God, you cannot stop them.

You might even find yourselves fighting against God.”

## Acts 5:40

The council accepted Gamaliel’s advice.

They called the apostles back and had them whipped.

They ordered them to stop speaking in Jesus’ name.

Then they let them go.

**Pause and answer**

What did Gamaliel say would happen if the work came from God?

## The apostles keep teaching

## Acts 5:41

The apostles left the council full of joy.

They were glad God counted them worthy to suffer shame for Jesus’ name.

## Acts 5:42

Every day they taught in the temple courts and in people’s homes.

They kept telling people the good news that Jesus is the Christ.

**Help with these words**

The Christ is the King God chose.

**Pause and answer**

What good news did the apostles keep telling people?
