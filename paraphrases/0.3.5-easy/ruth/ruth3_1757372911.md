# Ruth 3 — Ruth asks Boaz to marry her

**About this chapter**

Naomi wants Ruth to have a home of her own. Ruth goes to speak to Boaz.

## Naomi tells Ruth where to find Boaz

## Ruth 3:1

One day Naomi spoke to Ruth, her son’s widow.

“My daughter, I want to find you a home where you can live well.

## Ruth 3:2

You have worked with the women in Boaz’s fields.

Boaz is our relative.

Tonight he will winnow barley on the threshing floor.

**Reading help**

A threshing floor was a firm, flat place for working with grain. To winnow barley, people tossed it into the air. The wind carried away the light, dry husks. The heavier grain fell back down.

![Wind carries the light husks sideways. The heavier barley grains fall down.](../pictures/winnowing-barley.svg)

## Ruth 3:3

Wash yourself and put on perfume.

Wear your best clothes.

Go down to the threshing floor.

Wait until Boaz finishes eating and drinking before he sees you.

## Ruth 3:4

Watch where Boaz lies down.

Then uncover his feet and lie down there.

He will tell you what to do.”

## Ruth 3:5

Ruth answered, “I will do everything you say.”

**Pause and answer**

Where would Ruth find Boaz that night?

## Ruth speaks to Boaz at night

## Ruth 3:6

Ruth went down to the threshing floor.

She did what Naomi had told her.

## Ruth 3:7

Boaz ate and drank.

He felt happy.

He lay down beside the pile of grain.

Ruth came quietly and uncovered his feet.

Then she lay down.

## Ruth 3:8

At midnight something startled Boaz.

He turned over.

A woman was lying at his feet!

## Ruth 3:9

Boaz asked, “Who are you?”

She answered, “I am Ruth, your servant.

Spread the corner of your clothing over me.

You are one of our family’s redeemers.”

**Reading help**

Ruth asks Boaz to cover her with his clothing. This is her way of asking him to marry her. A family redeemer is a close relative who can act to help the family.

## Boaz makes a promise

## Ruth 3:10

Boaz said, “May the LORD bless you, my daughter.

Your kindness now is even greater than before.

You could have looked for a younger husband, rich or poor.

Instead you came to me.

## Ruth 3:11

My daughter, you can put your fear aside.

I will do what you ask.

Everyone in town knows you are a good woman.

## Ruth 3:12

Yes, I am one of your family’s redeemers.

But one man is a closer relative than I am.

## Ruth 3:13

Stay here tonight.

In the morning he may agree to be your redeemer.

If so, let him do it.

If he refuses, I promise to do it myself.

I swear this as surely as the LORD lives.

Lie here until morning.”

**Pause and answer**

Who has the first right to help Ruth?

## Ruth brings barley to Naomi

## Ruth 3:14

Ruth lay at his feet until morning.

She got up while it was still too dark for anyone to recognize her.

Boaz said, “Keep her visit to the threshing floor private.”

## Ruth 3:15

Boaz told Ruth, “Hold out your shawl.”

Ruth held it out.

Boaz poured six measures of barley into it.

He lifted the load onto Ruth.

Then Boaz went into town.

**Reading help**

A shawl is a large cloth worn around the shoulders.

## Ruth 3:16

Ruth went back to Naomi.

Naomi asked, “How did things go, my daughter?”

Ruth told Naomi everything Boaz had done for her.

## Ruth 3:17

Ruth said, “Boaz gave me these six measures of barley.

He wanted me to have something to bring you.”

## Ruth 3:18

Naomi said, “Wait here, my daughter.

Soon you will know what happens.

Boaz will keep working until he settles this today.”
