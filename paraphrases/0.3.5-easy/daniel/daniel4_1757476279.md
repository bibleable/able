# Daniel 4 — The king dreams about a great tree

**About this chapter**

King Nebuchadnezzar tells how a dream frightened him. Daniel explains why the great tree matters to the king.

## The king sends a message

## Daniel 4:1

King Nebuchadnezzar sent this message to people everywhere, whatever language they spoke.

“May things go well for you!

## Daniel 4:2

I am glad to tell you the wonderful signs God Most High has shown me.

## Daniel 4:3

His signs are great.

His wonders show his power.

His kingdom lasts forever.

He rules from one generation to the next.”

## A frightening dream

## Daniel 4:4

I, Nebuchadnezzar, felt safe at home.

All was going well in my palace.

## Daniel 4:5

Then I had a dream that frightened me.

As I lay in bed, I kept seeing pictures that troubled me.

## Daniel 4:6

I ordered all the wise men of Babylon to come.

I wanted them to explain the dream.

## Daniel 4:7

Some used magic.

Others studied the stars or looked for signs.

I told them my dream.

None could explain it.

## Daniel 4:8

At last Daniel came in.

I called him Belteshazzar, a name taken from my god.

I believed the spirit of the holy gods was in him.

I told him my dream.

## Daniel 4:9

“Belteshazzar, you lead my magicians.

I know the spirit of the holy gods is in you.

No mystery is too hard for you.

Tell me what the pictures in my dream mean.

## A tree gives food and shelter

## Daniel 4:10

As I lay in bed, I saw a very tall tree in the middle of the land.

## Daniel 4:11

It grew so large and strong that its top reached the sky.

People could see it from the ends of the earth.

## Daniel 4:12

Its leaves were beautiful.

Its fruit fed every creature.

Wild animals sheltered beneath it.

Birds nested in its branches.

## Daniel 4:13

Then I saw a holy watcher, a messenger from heaven, coming down.

## Daniel 4:14

He cried out, ‘Cut down the tree!

Chop off its branches.

Strip its leaves and scatter its fruit.

Let the animals run away and the birds fly off.

## Daniel 4:15

Leave the stump and its roots in the grass.

Put a band of iron and bronze around it.’ Then the watcher spoke of a person. ‘Let the dew soak him.

Let him eat grass with the wild animals.

## Daniel 4:16

Let him think like an animal instead of a man.

Let seven periods of time pass over him.

## Daniel 4:17

This is the decision the holy watchers announce.

Everyone alive must know that God Most High rules human kingdoms.

He gives power to whomever he chooses, even to the lowliest person.’

## Daniel 4:18

That was my dream, Belteshazzar.

None of my wise men can explain it.

You can, because the spirit of the holy gods is in you.”

## Daniel explains the tree and the stump

## Daniel 4:19

For a while Daniel was silent and troubled.

The king said, “Belteshazzar, do not be afraid of the dream or its meaning.” Daniel answered, “My king, I wish this dream were about your enemies!

## Daniel 4:20

You saw the great tree.

It grew strong and reached the sky.

Everyone on earth could see it.

## Daniel 4:21

Its leaves were beautiful.

It grew enough fruit to feed everyone.

Animals lived beneath it, and birds nested in it.

## Daniel 4:22

King, you are that tree!

You have become great and strong.

Your greatness reaches the sky.

You rule to the ends of the earth.

## Daniel 4:23

The holy watcher came down from heaven and ordered the tree cut down.

The stump and roots were to stay in the grass with a metal band around them.

Then he spoke about someone wet with dew, eating grass with the animals for seven periods of time.

## Daniel 4:24

Here is what the dream means.

God Most High has made this decision about you, my king.

## Daniel 4:25

You will have to live away from other people, among wild animals.

You will eat grass like an ox.

Dew will soak you.

Seven periods of time will pass until you admit that God Most High rules human kingdoms.

He gives them to whomever he chooses.

## Daniel 4:26

Remember the stump with its roots still in the ground.

You will receive your kingdom back when you admit that heaven rules.”

## Daniel 4:27

Daniel said, “King, please take my advice.

Turn from sin and do what is right.

Show mercy to people who are treated cruelly.

Then perhaps things will continue to go well for you.”

## The king loses his power

## Daniel 4:28

All this happened to King Nebuchadnezzar.

## Daniel 4:29

Twelve months later, he walked on the roof of his palace in Babylon.

## Daniel 4:30

He said, “Look at great Babylon!

I built this royal home by my own power.

It shows how great I am!”

## Daniel 4:31

Before the king had finished speaking, a voice came from heaven.

“King Nebuchadnezzar, this is the decision.

You have lost your kingdom.

## Daniel 4:32

You will live away from people and eat grass with the wild animals like an ox.

Seven periods of time will pass until you admit that God Most High rules human kingdoms.

He gives them to whomever he chooses.”

## Daniel 4:33

At once the king had to leave human company.

He ate grass like an ox.

Dew soaked his body.

His hair grew like eagle feathers and his nails like a bird’s claws.

## The king praises God

## Daniel 4:34

At the end of that time I, Nebuchadnezzar, looked up to heaven.

I could think clearly again.

I praised and honored God Most High, who lives forever.

His rule never ends.

His kingdom lasts through every generation.

## Daniel 4:35

Compared with him, all the people on earth count as nothing.

He does what he chooses with heaven’s army and with people on earth.

No one can stop him or demand that he explain himself.

## Daniel 4:36

When I could think clearly again, my royal honor returned.

My advisers and nobles came looking for me.

I became king again, with even greater power than before.

## Daniel 4:37

Now I, Nebuchadnezzar, praise and honor the King of heaven.

Everything he does is true and fair.

He can humble those who are proud.
