# Daniel 3 — Three men refuse to worship the gold statue

**About this chapter**

King Nebuchadnezzar orders people to bow to a statue. Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego must decide whom they will worship, even if it costs them their lives.

## The king gives an order

## Daniel 3:1

King Nebuchadnezzar made a gold statue.

It was about 27 meters tall and almost 3 meters wide.

He set it up on the flat land called Dura in the province of Babylon.

## Daniel 3:2

The king sent for the leaders of all his provinces.

He called the governors, advisers, treasurers, judges, and other officials.

He wanted them at the ceremony for the new statue.

## Daniel 3:3

The officials gathered for the ceremony.

They stood in front of the king’s statue.

## Daniel 3:4

A messenger called out loudly, “Listen, people of every nation and language!

Here is the king’s order.

## Daniel 3:5

You will hear a horn, a flute, pipes, and stringed instruments, including harps.

When the music starts, bow to the ground.

Worship the gold statue King Nebuchadnezzar has set up.

## Daniel 3:6

Anyone who refuses will go straight into a blazing furnace.” A furnace is a large, very hot oven.

## Daniel 3:7

The music began.

People from every nation and language bowed down and worshiped the gold statue.

## Three men are accused

## Daniel 3:8

Some men who studied the stars wanted to cause trouble for the Jews.

They came to accuse them.

## Daniel 3:9

They said to Nebuchadnezzar, “May you live forever, O king!

## Daniel 3:10

You ordered everyone to bow down and worship the statue when the instruments play.

## Daniel 3:11

You said that anyone who refuses must go into the blazing furnace.

## Daniel 3:12

You put three Jewish men in charge of work in Babylon.

Their names are Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego.

They pay no attention to your order.

They refuse to serve your gods or worship your gold statue.”

## Daniel 3:13

Nebuchadnezzar was furious.

He sent for the three men.

They came before him.

## Daniel 3:14

He asked, “Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, is this true?

Do you refuse to serve my gods and bow to my statue?

## Daniel 3:15

When you hear the music again, you can still bow and worship.

If you refuse, you will go straight into the fire.

What god could save you from my power then?”

## The three men answer

## Daniel 3:16

Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego answered, “Nebuchadnezzar, we have no need to defend ourselves before you.

## Daniel 3:17

If the God we serve exists, he can save us from the fire.

He can rescue us from your power, O king.

## Daniel 3:18

Even if he does not rescue us, our answer is the same.

We will never serve your gods or bow to your statue.”

## The king sees someone else in the fire

## Daniel 3:19

The king’s face changed as he looked at them in anger.

He ordered the furnace heated seven times hotter than usual.

## Daniel 3:20

He ordered strong soldiers to tie up the three men and throw them into the fire.

## Daniel 3:21

The men were still wearing their robes, trousers, head coverings, and other clothes.

The soldiers tied them up and threw them into the furnace.

## Daniel 3:22

The king had demanded haste.

The flames were so hot that they killed the soldiers who carried the men up.

## Daniel 3:23

Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego fell into the fire, still tightly bound.

## Daniel 3:24

Suddenly the king jumped up in amazement.

“Didn’t we throw three men into the fire, tied up?” His advisers answered, “Yes, O king.”

## Daniel 3:25

“Look!” said the king.

“I see four men walking around in the fire.

They are free and unharmed.

The fourth looks like a son of the gods!”

## The three men come out unharmed

## Daniel 3:26

Nebuchadnezzar went near the furnace door.

He called, “Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, servants of God Most High, come out!” All three came out of the fire.

## Daniel 3:27

The governors, officials, and royal advisers gathered around them.

The fire had not hurt their bodies or burned a single hair.

Their robes were whole.

They did not even smell of smoke.

## Daniel 3:28

Nebuchadnezzar said, “Praise the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego!

They trusted him, and he sent his angel to rescue them.

They disobeyed my order and risked their lives to worship their own God alone.

## Daniel 3:29

Here is my order for every nation and language.

If anyone insults their God, that person will be cut to pieces.

Their house will be torn down.

No other god can rescue people like this.”

## Daniel 3:30

Then the king gave Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego higher positions in the province of Babylon.
