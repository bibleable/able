# Daniel 9 — Daniel prays for Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

Daniel reads what the LORD told Jeremiah about Jerusalem. He asks God to forgive his people. While Daniel is still praying, Gabriel comes with an answer.

## Daniel reads the words of Jeremiah

## Daniel 9:1

Darius, son of Xerxes, came from the people called the Medes.

He became ruler over the kingdom of the Chaldeans.

## Daniel 9:2

In his first year as ruler, I, Daniel, read the holy writings.

I understood the message the LORD had given the prophet Jeremiah.

Jerusalem would lie in ruins for seventy years.

![An open scroll like the writings Daniel read.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## Daniel 9:3

I turned to the Lord God to pray and ask for help.

I went without food for a time.

I wore rough cloth and used ashes to show my grief.

## Daniel admits what Israel has done

## Daniel 9:4

I prayed to the LORD my God and admitted our wrongs.

“Lord, you are great and fill us with awe.

You keep your covenant, your solemn promise of love, with those who love and obey you.

## Daniel 9:5

We have sinned.

We have done evil and turned against you.

We have turned away from your commands and laws.

## Daniel 9:6

Your servants the prophets spoke for you.

They spoke to our kings, leaders, ancestors, and everyone in our land.

We refused to listen.

## Daniel 9:7

Lord, you do what is right.

Today we are ashamed.

This is true of Judah, Jerusalem, and all Israel, near and far.

You drove us to other countries because we broke faith with you.

## Daniel 9:8

LORD, we are ashamed.

So are our kings, our leaders, and our ancestors.

We have sinned against you.

## Daniel 9:9

Lord our God, you show mercy and forgive, even though we turned against you.

## Daniel 9:10

We did not listen to you or follow your laws.

Your servants the prophets told us how to live.

## Daniel 9:11

All Israel broke your law and refused to listen.

Moses, your servant, wrote down the punishment you swore would come.

It has come upon us because we sinned against you.

## Daniel asks the LORD to show mercy

## Daniel 9:12

You did what you told us and our rulers you would do.

You brought terrible suffering on us.

Nowhere under heaven has anything happened like this disaster in Jerusalem.

## Daniel 9:13

The law of Moses warned us about this disaster.

Yet we still have not turned from our sins to seek your favor.

We have paid no attention to your truth.

## Daniel 9:14

LORD, you kept the punishment ready and brought it upon us.

Everything you do is right.

Still we have refused to obey you.

## Daniel 9:15

Lord our God, you used your great power to bring your people out of Egypt.

People still know your name because of what you did.

We have sinned and done evil.

## Daniel 9:16

Lord, you have always done what is right.

Please turn your anger away from Jerusalem, your city on your holy mountain.

Our neighbors mock your city and your people because we and our ancestors have sinned.

## Daniel 9:17

Our God, hear me, your servant, as I pray.

Lord, for your own sake look with kindness on your ruined temple.

## Daniel 9:18

My God, listen!

Look at the ruins of the city that bears your name.

We cannot claim that we have earned your help.

We ask because your mercy is so great.

## Daniel 9:19

Lord, listen!

Lord, forgive!

Lord, hear us and act!

For your own sake, my God, come quickly.

Your city and your people bear your name.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did Daniel believe he could ask God for help?

## Gabriel comes while Daniel is praying

## Daniel 9:20

I was still praying.

I admitted my own sin and the sin of my people Israel.

I asked the LORD my God to help his holy mountain.

## Daniel 9:21

Just then Gabriel flew swiftly to me.

He was the man I had seen in an earlier vision.

It was about the time of the evening sacrifice.

## Daniel 9:22

Gabriel taught me.

He said, “Daniel, I have come to help you understand.

## Daniel 9:23

An answer went out as soon as you began to pray.

You are very dear to God, so I have come to tell you.

Think carefully about this message so you can understand the vision.

## Gabriel speaks about seventy weeks

## Daniel 9:24

God has set seventy weeks for your people and your holy city.

This time is set for rebellion and sin to end, and for guilt to be taken away.

What is right will last forever.

Visions and the prophets’ words will be sealed up.

The Most Holy Place will be anointed, set apart for God.

## Daniel 9:25

Pay attention.

First an order will go out to restore and rebuild Jerusalem.

Then seven weeks and sixty-two weeks will pass before the Messiah, the Prince, comes.

Messiah means the Anointed One.

People will rebuild the streets and the trench around the city during troubled times.

## Daniel 9:26

After the sixty-two weeks, the Messiah will be cut off and have nothing left.

The people of a ruler yet to come will destroy the city and its holy place.

The end will sweep in like a flood.

War will last until the end.

God has decreed that places will be left in ruins.

## Daniel 9:27

He will confirm a covenant, a solemn agreement, with many people for one week.

Halfway through the week he will stop the sacrifices and offerings.

On a wing of the temple will come something hateful to God that leaves the place ruined.

This will last until the one responsible meets the end God has decreed.”
