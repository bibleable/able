# Daniel 12 — A promise for Daniel at the end

**About this chapter**

Daniel hears about a time of great trouble. He asks how it will end. The messenger gives Daniel a promise about his own future.

## A time of trouble and a promise of life

## Daniel 12:1

The messenger told Daniel, “Michael is the great prince who watches over your people.

At that time he will rise to help them.

No nation has ever seen trouble like the trouble that will come.

Yet everyone among your people whose name is in the book will be rescued.

## Daniel 12:2

“Many who lie asleep in the dust will wake up.”

The messenger was speaking about people who have died.

“Some will rise to live forever.

Others will rise to shame and lasting disgrace.

## Daniel 12:3

“Wise people will shine like the bright sky.

Those who help many others do what is right will shine like stars forever.

## Daniel 12:4

“Daniel, close the book and seal these words inside.

Keep it sealed until the time of the end.

Many people will travel here and there.

People will know more.”

## A question at the river

## Daniel 12:5

Then I, Daniel, looked and saw two others.

One stood on my side of the river.

The other stood on the opposite bank.

## Daniel 12:6

Above the river was a man dressed in linen.

Linen is a type of cloth.

One of the two asked him, “How long until all these wonders come true?”

## Daniel 12:7

The man above the water raised both hands toward heaven.

He made a solemn promise by the One who lives forever.

He said, “It will be a time, times, and half a time.

When the holy people have lost all their power, these things will be complete.”

## Daniel asks what will happen

## Daniel 12:8

I heard his words, but I did not understand.

I asked, “Sir, how will all this end?”

## Daniel 12:9

He answered, “Go on your way, Daniel.

The words will stay sealed until the time of the end.

## Daniel 12:10

“Many people will become pure, like something washed completely clean.

Those who do evil will keep doing evil.

They will not understand.

But the wise will understand.

## Daniel 12:11

“The daily offering to God will stop.

Something hateful that leaves the holy place ruined will be set up there.

From that time there will be 1,290 days.

## Daniel 12:12

“God blesses the person who waits and reaches the end of the 1,335 days.

## Daniel 12:13

“Daniel, go on your way until the end.

You will rest.

Then, at the end of the days, you will rise.

You will receive the share set aside for you.”
