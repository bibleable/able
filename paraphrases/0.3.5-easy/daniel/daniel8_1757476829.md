# Daniel 8 — Gabriel explains the ram and the goat

**About this chapter**

Daniel sees two animals in a vision, a picture God shows him. One is a ram, a male sheep. The other is a goat. Gabriel tells Daniel what the animals and their horns mean.

## A ram stands beside a canal

## Daniel 8:1

I, Daniel, saw another vision after the one I saw before.

It came in King Belshazzar’s third year as king.

## Daniel 8:2

In the vision I was in the fortress of Susa, in the region of Elam.

I stood beside a waterway called the Ulai Canal.

## Daniel 8:3

I looked up.

A ram with two long horns stood beside the canal.

The horn that grew later was longer.

## Daniel 8:4

The ram charged west, north, and south.

No animal could stop him.

No one could rescue another animal from him.

The ram did as he pleased and became powerful.

## A goat comes from the west

## Daniel 8:5

As I watched, a goat came from the west.

He crossed the whole earth without touching the ground.

One large horn stood out between his eyes.

## Daniel 8:6

The goat rushed furiously at the ram beside the canal.

## Daniel 8:7

He struck the ram and broke both its horns.

The ram had no strength to resist.

The goat knocked him down and trampled him.

No one could save the ram.

## Daniel 8:8

The goat became very powerful.

Just when he was strongest, his large horn broke off.

Four large horns grew in its place.

Each pointed in a different direction.

## A little horn grows powerful

## Daniel 8:9

A little horn grew out of one of the four horns.

It grew far to the south and east and toward the land called the Beautiful Land.

## Daniel 8:10

It reached as high as the army of heaven.

It threw some of that army and some stars to earth.

It trampled them.

## Daniel 8:11

The horn made itself out to be as great as the Prince who led that army.

It stopped the daily sacrifice to the Prince.

It brought down his place of worship.

## Daniel 8:12

During the rebellion, the horn gained control of the army and the daily sacrifice.

It threw truth to the ground, treating it as worthless.

Everything it did succeeded.

## Daniel 8:13

I heard two holy ones speaking.

One asked the other, “How long will these things last?

The daily sacrifice has stopped.

Rebellion brings ruin.

The holy place and the army are being trampled.”

## Daniel 8:14

He told me, “It will take 2,300 evenings and mornings.

Then the holy place will be put right again.”

## Gabriel helps Daniel understand

## Daniel 8:15

I was watching and trying to understand the vision.

Someone who looked like a man stood before me.

## Daniel 8:16

I heard a man’s voice from between the banks of the Ulai.

He called, “Gabriel, explain the vision to this man.”

## Daniel 8:17

When Gabriel came close, I was terrified.

I fell with my face to the ground.

He said, “Son of man, understand this.

The vision is about the time of the end.”

## Daniel 8:18

As Gabriel spoke, I fell deeply asleep, face down.

He touched me and helped me stand.

## Daniel 8:19

Gabriel said, “I will tell you what happens near the end of the time of anger.

The vision concerns the time set for the end.

## Daniel 8:20

The ram with two horns stands for the kings of Media and Persia.

## Daniel 8:21

The shaggy goat stands for the king of Greece.

The large horn between its eyes stands for the first king.

## Daniel 8:22

Four horns grew where that horn broke off.

Four kingdoms will rise from the same nation.

They will have less power than the first king.

## Gabriel describes a king who deceives people

## Daniel 8:23

Late in their rule, rebellion will reach its height.

A bold, disrespectful king will take power.

He will know how to carry out secret plans.

## Daniel 8:24

He will be very powerful, though the power will come from outside himself.

He will cause terrible destruction and succeed in what he does.

He will destroy powerful people and God’s holy people.

## Daniel 8:25

He will use clever lies to get his way.

He will think himself great.

When people feel safe, he will destroy many of them.

He will even oppose the Prince who rules over princes.

Yet his power will break, and no human hand will break it.

## Daniel 8:26

What you heard about the evenings and mornings is true.

Seal up the vision now.

It concerns a time far in the future.”

## Daniel 8:27

I, Daniel, was worn out and lay sick for days.

Then I got up and went back to work for the king.

The vision still troubled me.

I could not understand it all.
