# Daniel 10 — A messenger helps Daniel stand

**About this chapter**

Daniel grieves for three weeks. Then, beside the Tigris River, he sees a vision. A vision is something God shows a person. Daniel tells what happened in his own words.

## Three weeks of sorrow

## Daniel 10:1

Cyrus was in his third year as king of Persia.

Daniel, also called Belteshazzar, received a message.

It was true. It told of a great struggle.

A vision helped Daniel understand the message.

## Daniel 10:2

I, Daniel, grieved for three whole weeks.

## Daniel 10:3

During those weeks I ate no special foods.

I ate no meat and drank no wine.

I also went without the oil I usually put on my skin.

## Daniel sees a man by the river

## Daniel 10:4

On day 24 of the first month, I stood beside the Tigris.

The Tigris is a great river.

## Daniel 10:5

I looked up and saw a man wearing linen clothing.

Around his waist was a belt of fine gold from Uphaz.

## Daniel 10:6

His body shone like a jewel called beryl.

His face flashed like lightning.

His eyes looked like burning torches.

His arms and legs gleamed like polished bronze, a metal.

His voice sounded like a whole crowd speaking.

## Daniel 10:7

I was the only one who saw the vision.

The men with me saw nothing.

Yet fear swept over them. They ran and hid.

## Daniel 10:8

I stood alone, looking at this amazing sight.

My face went pale. All my strength left me.

## Daniel 10:9

I heard the man speak.

As I listened, I fell into a deep sleep.

I lay face down on the ground.

## The messenger explains the delay

## Daniel 10:10

Suddenly a hand touched me.

It lifted me onto my hands and knees.

I was shaking.

## Daniel 10:11

The messenger said, “Daniel, you are very dear to God.

Listen carefully to what I say.

Stand up. God has sent me to you.”

I stood up. I was still shaking.

## Daniel 10:12

He said, “Daniel, you can put your fear aside.

God heard you on the very first day.

You wanted to understand. You came humbly before your God.

I came because of what you said.

## Daniel 10:13

But the prince of Persia held me back for 21 days.

I was there with the kings of Persia.

Then Michael, one of the chief princes, came to help me.

## Daniel 10:14

Now I have come to explain the vision.

It shows what will happen to your people in days to come.”

## Daniel finds strength to speak

## Daniel 10:15

As the messenger spoke, I looked down at the ground.

I could say nothing.

## Daniel 10:16

Then someone who looked like a man touched my lips.

I could speak again. I addressed the one before me.

“Sir, what I saw has upset me deeply.

All my strength has gone.

## Daniel 10:17

I am your servant, sir. How can I speak to you?

I have no strength left. I can hardly breathe.”

## Daniel 10:18

The one who looked like a man touched me again.

He gave me strength.

## Daniel 10:19

He said, “You are very dear to God.

You can put your fear aside. May you have peace.

Be strong now. Be very strong!”

As he spoke, my strength came back.

I said, “Speak, sir. You have given me strength.”

## Daniel 10:20

The messenger asked, “Do you know why I came?

I must go back now and fight the prince of Persia.

When I leave, the prince of Greece will come.

## Daniel 10:21

First I will tell you what the Book of Truth says.

Only Michael, your prince, has the courage to stand with me against them.”
