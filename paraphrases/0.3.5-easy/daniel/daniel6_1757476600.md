# Daniel 6 — Daniel keeps praying

**About this chapter**

King Darius trusts Daniel. Other officials use Daniel’s habit of prayer to bring a charge against him. The king must decide what to do about the law he signed.

## The officials look for a charge against Daniel

## Daniel 6:1

King Darius chose 120 governors to rule different parts of his kingdom.

## Daniel 6:2

The governors reported to three officials.

Daniel was one of the three.

They made sure the king did not suffer any loss.

## Daniel 6:3

Daniel showed an unusual spirit in his work.

He stood out among all the officials and governors.

The king planned to put him in charge of the whole kingdom.

## Daniel 6:4

The other officials and governors looked for something Daniel had done wrong at work.

They found nothing.

Daniel was honest and took care to do his work well.

## Daniel 6:5

They said, “We will only find a charge against Daniel if it has to do with the law of his God.”

## The king signs a law

## Daniel 6:6

The officials and governors went to the king together.

They said, “King Darius, may you live forever!

## Daniel 6:7

All your officials, governors, and advisers agree on this law.

For thirty days, anyone who asks a god or a person for something must ask you alone, O king.

Anyone who breaks this law must go into the lions’ den.” A den is a place where lions live.

## Daniel 6:8

They said, “King, sign this law.

Under the law of the Medes and Persians, no one can change it once you sign.”

## Daniel 6:9

King Darius signed the law.

## Daniel prays as usual

## Daniel 6:10

Daniel heard that the king had signed.

He went home to his upstairs room.

Its windows opened toward Jerusalem.

Three times a day he knelt, prayed, and thanked God, just as he had before.

## Daniel 6:11

The officials came together and found Daniel asking his God for help.

## Daniel 6:12

They went to Darius.

“King, didn’t you sign a law for thirty days?

Anyone who asks anything of a god or person other than you must go into the lions’ den.” Darius answered, “Yes.

Under the law of the Medes and Persians, that order must stand.”

## Daniel 6:13

They said, “Daniel is one of the people taken away from Judah.

He pays no attention to you or the law you signed.

He still prays three times a day.”

## Darius tries to rescue Daniel

## Daniel 6:14

Darius was deeply upset.

He worked until sunset to find a way to save Daniel.

## Daniel 6:15

The men came back together.

“Remember, King, the law of the Medes and Persians stands.

No one can change an order you have made.”

## Daniel 6:16

So Darius gave the order.

His men brought Daniel and threw him into the lions’ den.

Darius told Daniel, “May the God you always serve rescue you!”

## Daniel 6:17

They covered the opening with a stone.

Darius and his nobles used their rings to leave marks on the seal.

These marks showed that no one had permission to change what had happened to Daniel.

## Daniel 6:18

Darius went back to his palace.

He went without food that night.

No one came to entertain him.

He could not sleep.

## A voice answers from the den

## Daniel 6:19

At first light the king got up.

He hurried to the lions’ den.

## Daniel 6:20

In great distress Darius called, “Daniel, servant of the living God!

Has the God you always serve been able to save you from the lions?”

## Daniel 6:21

Daniel answered, “King, may you live forever!

## Daniel 6:22

My God sent his angel and shut the lions’ mouths.

They have not hurt me.

God saw that I was innocent.

I have also done you no wrong, O king.”

## Daniel 6:23

The king was full of joy.

He ordered his men to lift Daniel out.

They found no wound on him because he had trusted his God.

## Daniel 6:24

The king gave another order.

His men threw Daniel’s false accusers, their wives, and their children into the den.

Before they reached the bottom, the lions seized them and crushed their bones.

## Darius writes about Daniel’s God

## Daniel 6:25

King Darius wrote to people of every nation and language across the land.

“May all go well with you!

## Daniel 6:26

I order everyone throughout my kingdom to show deep respect and fear before Daniel’s God.

He is the living God.

He lives forever.

No one can destroy his kingdom.

His rule will never end.

## Daniel 6:27

He saves people from danger.

He does wonderful things in the heavens and on earth that show his power.

He rescued Daniel from the lions.”

## Daniel 6:28

Daniel did well while Darius ruled and while Cyrus of Persia ruled.
