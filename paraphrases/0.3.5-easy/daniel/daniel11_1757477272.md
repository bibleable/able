# Daniel 11 — Kings of the North and South struggle for power

**About this chapter**

The messenger continues speaking to Daniel. He describes two kingdoms, one north of Daniel’s homeland and one south of it. Different men become king, but the titles “king of the North” and “king of the South” stay the same.

## A great kingdom is divided

## Daniel 11:1

The messenger said, “In the first year of Darius the Mede, I stood up to give him strength and protection.

## Daniel 11:2

Now I will tell you the truth.

Three more kings will rule Persia.

A fourth will be far richer.

He will use his wealth to turn everyone against Greece.

## Daniel 11:3

Then a powerful king will rise.

He will rule as he pleases.

## Daniel 11:4

Soon after he takes power, his kingdom will break apart in four directions.

Others will receive the pieces.

His own family will lose the kingdom, and those who follow will have less power.

## Two royal families make an agreement

## Daniel 11:5

The king of the South will grow strong.

One of his commanders will grow stronger still and rule a powerful kingdom of his own.

## Daniel 11:6

Years later the two kingdoms will join together.

The southern king will send his daughter to the northern king to make the agreement firm.

She will lose her power, and the king’s strength will fail.

She, her companions, her father, and her supporter will all be given up.

## Daniel 11:7

A man from her family will become king in the South.

He will fight the northern army, enter its stronghold, and win.

## Daniel 11:8

He will take their metal gods and their precious gold and silver vessels back to Egypt.

For some years he will leave the northern king alone.

## Daniel 11:9

The northern king will invade the South and then return home.

![The northern kingdom lies north of Daniel’s homeland. Egypt lies to the south.](../pictures/daniel-north-and-south.svg)

## The fighting begins again

## Daniel 11:10

The northern king’s sons will gather a great army.

It will sweep through like floodwater and fight all the way to the fortress.

## Daniel 11:11

In anger the southern king will march out to fight.

The northern king will gather many soldiers, but the southern king will defeat them.

## Daniel 11:12

The victory will make the southern king proud.

He will strike down tens of thousands, yet his victory will not last.

## Daniel 11:13

Years later the northern king will return.

His army will be larger than before and will bring many supplies.

## Daniel 11:14

Many people will turn against the southern king.

Some violent men among your own people will rise up to fulfill the vision.

They will fail.

## Daniel 11:15

The northern king will surround a walled city and capture it.

Even the best soldiers from the South will be unable to stop him.

## Daniel 11:16

No one will stand in his way.

He will take control of the Beautiful Land, the land of your people.

He will have power to destroy.

## Daniel 11:17

He will bring the strength of his whole kingdom to bear.

He will make an agreement with the southern king and give him his daughter in marriage.

He hopes to bring down the southern kingdom this way.

The plan will do him no good.

## Daniel 11:18

He will turn to the coastlands and capture many places there.

Then a commander will put a stop to his insults and bring the shame back on him.

## Daniel 11:19

The northern king will head for the strongholds of his own land.

He will fall, and that will be his end.

## Daniel 11:20

The next ruler will send someone to collect taxes for the splendor of his kingdom.

Within days the ruler will die, away from battle and without an angry attack.

## A new king gains power by deceit

## Daniel 11:21

A man people despise will take his place.

No one will give him the honor of becoming king.

In a time of peace he will take power through secret schemes.

## Daniel 11:22

Armies will come like a flood, but he will sweep them away.

A prince linked to the covenant, a solemn agreement, will also fall.

## Daniel 11:23

He will make an agreement and then deceive his partners.

With only a few followers he will grow powerful.

## Daniel 11:24

In peacetime he will enter the richest regions.

He will do something his ancestors never did.

He will shower his followers with the wealth he takes.

For a limited time he will make plans against fortified places.

## Daniel 11:25

With a large army he will gather his strength and courage against the South.

The southern king will gather an even greater force.

But people will plot against the southern king, and he will fall.

## Daniel 11:26

Men who share the southern king’s food will work to ruin him.

His army will scatter, and many will die.

## Daniel 11:27

The two kings will sit at one table.

Both will tell lies because both intend harm.

Their lies will achieve nothing.

The end will come at the appointed time.

## Daniel 11:28

The northern king will head home with great riches.

On his way he will act against the holy covenant, God’s solemn agreement with his people.

After doing harm, he will return to his land.

## People who know God stand firm

## Daniel 11:29

At the appointed time the northern king will invade the South again.

This time things will turn out differently.

## Daniel 11:30

Ships from Kittim will come against him.

He will lose courage and turn back.

In anger he will attack the holy covenant.

He will favor people who abandon it.

## Daniel 11:31

His forces will treat the temple fortress as unholy.

They will stop the daily sacrifice.

They will set up something hateful to God that leaves the holy place ruined.

## Daniel 11:32

He will use sweet words to win over those who break the covenant.

But the people who know their God will stand firm against him.

## Daniel 11:33

Wise people will teach many others.

For a time some will die by sword or fire.

Others will lose their freedom or their possessions.

## Daniel 11:34

When they fall, they will receive a little help.

Many who join them will only pretend to be friends.

## Daniel 11:35

Some of the wise will fall.

Through this testing they will be made pure and clean.

The time of the end will still come when it is due.

## A proud king comes to his end

## Daniel 11:36

The king will do whatever he pleases.

He will claim to be greater than every god and say terrible things against the God of gods.

He will succeed until the time of anger ends.

What God has decided must happen.

## Daniel 11:37

He will ignore his ancestors’ gods and the one women desire.

He will disregard every other god.

He will think himself greater than them all.

## Daniel 11:38

Instead he will honor a god of fortresses his ancestors never knew.

He will give this god gold, silver, costly stones, and other treasures.

## Daniel 11:39

With a foreign god’s help he will attack the strongest forts.

He will give great honor to people who recognize him.

He will make them rulers over many and hand out land for a price.

## Daniel 11:40

At the time of the end the southern king will go to battle.

The northern king will rush out with chariots, mounted soldiers, and many ships.

He will sweep through many lands like floodwater.

## Daniel 11:41

He will enter the Beautiful Land too.

Many countries will fall.

But Edom, Moab, and the leaders of Ammon will escape him.

## Daniel 11:42

His power will spread over country after country.

Even Egypt will fall to him.

## Daniel 11:43

He will control Egypt’s gold, silver, and other riches.

The people of Libya and Cush will also submit to him.

## Daniel 11:44

News from the east and north will frighten him.

In a rage he will set out to wipe out many people.

## Daniel 11:45

He will set up his royal tents between the sea and the beautiful holy mountain.

There he will meet his end.

No one will help him.”
