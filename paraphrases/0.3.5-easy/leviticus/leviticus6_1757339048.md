# Leviticus 6 — Repaying a neighbor and caring for the offerings

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Moses how to put things right after someone cheats a neighbor. Then he gives the priests rules for the offerings and the altar fire.

## Give back what belongs to a neighbor

## Leviticus 6:1

The LORD spoke to Moses.

## Leviticus 6:2

Cheating a neighbor is also a sin against the LORD.

Someone might lie about property a neighbor left for safekeeping or as a pledge.

Someone might steal from a neighbor or force a neighbor to hand something over.

## Leviticus 6:3

Someone might find a lost item and deny finding it.

They might even swear that their lie is true.

These are sins.

## Leviticus 6:4

The guilty person must give the property back.

This includes anything stolen or taken by force.

It includes anything left for safekeeping or found after someone lost it.

## Leviticus 6:5

This also applies to anything the person lied about under oath.

The person must repay its full value plus one fifth.

They must pay the owner the day they admit their guilt.

**Reading help**

Suppose the lost item was worth five silver coins. The extra fifth is one coin. The owner receives six coins in all.

## Bring a guilt offering

## Leviticus 6:6

Then the person brings a ram from the flock to the priest.

It must have no defect and be worth the proper amount.

This is a guilt offering to the LORD.

## Leviticus 6:7

The priest makes atonement for that person before the LORD.

The LORD forgives the wrong that made the person guilty.

**Reading help**

To make atonement means to deal with the sin that has come between people and God.

## Keep the altar fire burning

## Leviticus 6:8

Then the LORD spoke to Moses again.

## Leviticus 6:9

The LORD told Moses to give Aaron and his sons this rule.

The burnt offering stays on the altar fire all night until morning.

The priests must keep the fire burning.

## Leviticus 6:10

The priest puts on his linen robe and linen underclothes.

He gathers the ashes left after the offering burns.

He puts them beside the altar.

## Leviticus 6:11

Then he takes off those clothes and puts on other clothes.

He carries the ashes outside the camp to a place kept clean for worship.

## Leviticus 6:12

The altar fire must keep burning.

Each morning the priest adds wood.

He lays the burnt offering on it.

He also burns the fat from the peace offerings there.

## Leviticus 6:13

The priests must always keep the altar fire burning.

They must never let it go out.

## The priests’ share of a grain offering

## Leviticus 6:14

This is the rule for the grain offering.

Aaron’s sons bring it before the LORD at the front of the altar.

## Leviticus 6:15

The priest takes a handful of fine flour and olive oil.

He adds all the frankincense and burns this portion on the altar as a reminder.

Its smell pleases the LORD.

**Reading help**

Frankincense gives off a scent when it burns.

## Leviticus 6:16

Aaron and his sons eat the rest without yeast.

They eat it in a holy place, the courtyard of the Tent of Meeting.

## Leviticus 6:17

They must bake it without yeast.

The LORD has given them this share of his food offerings.

It is most holy, like the sin offering and the guilt offering.

## Leviticus 6:18

Any male descendant of Aaron may eat this share.

It is their share of the LORD’s food offerings for every generation.

Anything that touches these offerings becomes holy.

## The grain offering for a priest

## Leviticus 6:19

Then the LORD spoke to Moses again.

## Leviticus 6:20

On the day of anointing, Aaron and his sons must bring the LORD a regular grain offering.

They bring one tenth of an ephah of fine flour.

They offer half in the morning and half in the evening.

**Reading help**

To anoint a priest means to put oil on him to set him apart for God. An ephah was a measure for dry food.

## Leviticus 6:21

Knead the dough well.

Cook it with oil on a flat cooking surface called a griddle.

Break it into pieces and bring it as a grain offering.

Its smell pleases the LORD.

## Leviticus 6:22

The son anointed as priest in Aaron’s place must prepare this offering.

The whole offering belongs to the LORD and must burn completely.

This rule will always apply.

## Leviticus 6:23

Every grain offering for a priest must burn completely.

No one may eat it.

| Grain offering | What happens to it? |
| --- | --- |
| The offering in verses 14–18 | The priest burns a handful with all the frankincense. Aaron and his sons eat the rest. |
| The offering for a priest | The whole offering burns. |

## The sin offering

## Leviticus 6:24

Then the LORD spoke to Moses again.

## Leviticus 6:25

He told Moses to give Aaron and his sons the rule for the sin offering.

The animal is killed before the LORD in the same place as the burnt offering.

It is most holy.

## Leviticus 6:26

The priest who offers it eats the meat.

He eats it in the courtyard of the Tent of Meeting.

That is a holy place.

## Leviticus 6:27

Anything that touches the meat becomes holy.

If blood splashes on clothing, wash the clothing in a holy place.

## Leviticus 6:28

If the meat boils in a clay pot, break the pot afterward.

If it boils in a bronze pot, scrub the pot and rinse it with water.

## Leviticus 6:29

Any male among the priests may eat the meat.

It is most holy.

## Leviticus 6:30

Sometimes the priest brings blood from a sin offering into the Tent of Meeting to make atonement in the Holy Place.

In that case, no one may eat the offering.

It must be burned.
