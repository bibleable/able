# Leviticus 26 — The LORD remembers his covenant

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Israel what life in the land could be like. He warns of hunger and exile if they break his covenant. He also tells them what he will remember.

## Worship and rest

## Leviticus 26:1

The LORD told Israel to make no idols.

They must not set up carved images or sacred pillars.

They must not bow down to a carved stone.

The LORD was their God.

## Leviticus 26:2

They must keep his Sabbath days of rest.

They must show respect for his holy place.

He is the LORD.

**Pause and answer**

How were the people to treat the LORD’s holy place?

## Rain, food and peace

## Leviticus 26:3

The LORD made this promise to Israel.

It depended on the people following his laws and carefully keeping his commands.

## Leviticus 26:4

Then he would send rain at the right time.

Their fields would grow crops.

Their trees would bear fruit.

## Leviticus 26:5

They would still be threshing grain when the grapes ripened.

They would still be picking grapes when planting time came.

They would have plenty to eat and live safely.

**Reading help**

Threshing means loosening the grains from the stalks after cutting the crop.

## Leviticus 26:6

The LORD would give their land peace.

They could lie down without fear.

He would remove dangerous animals and keep war out of their land.

## Leviticus 26:7

They would chase their enemies.

Their enemies would fall before them in battle.

## Leviticus 26:8

Five Israelites would chase a hundred enemies.

A hundred Israelites would chase ten thousand.

Their enemies would fall by the sword.

**Pause and answer**

When would the people finish threshing their grain?

## The LORD lives among his people

## Leviticus 26:9

The LORD would turn toward his people with kindness.

They would have children and grow in number.

He would keep his covenant with them.

**Reading help**

A covenant is a lasting agreement. Here the LORD makes promises and gives Israel commands.

## Leviticus 26:10

They would still be eating grain from the last harvest.

They would need to move it to make room for new grain.

## Leviticus 26:11

The LORD would make his home among them.

He would not turn away from them in disgust.

## Leviticus 26:12

He would walk among them and be their God.

They would be his people.

## Leviticus 26:13

The LORD had brought them out of Egypt.

They no longer had to serve the Egyptians as slaves.

He said he had broken their yoke.

Now they could walk with their heads up.

**Reading help**

A yoke is a wooden bar that harnesses animals for work. A broken yoke pictures Israel’s freedom from slavery.

**Pause and answer**

What did the broken yoke mean for Israel?

## If Israel breaks the covenant

## Leviticus 26:14

The LORD also gave a warning.

What if Israel would not listen or keep his commands?

## Leviticus 26:15

If they hated his rules and refused to follow them, they would break his covenant.

## Leviticus 26:16

Then the LORD would bring sudden fear, sickness and fever.

Their sight would fail and their strength would drain away.

They would sow seeds, but enemies would eat the crops.

## Leviticus 26:17

The LORD would turn against Israel.

Their enemies would defeat them.

People who hated them would rule them.

They would run even when no one chased them.

**Pause and answer**

Who would eat the crops if Israel broke the covenant?

## Fields and roads grow empty

## Leviticus 26:18

If they still refused to listen, the LORD would punish them seven times over for their sins.

## Leviticus 26:19

He would break their stubborn pride.

He would make their sky like iron and their ground like bronze.

## Leviticus 26:20

They would work until their strength was gone.

Their fields would yield no crops.

Their trees would grow no fruit.

**Reading help**

| Picture in verse 19 | What happens in the field |
| --- | --- |
| A sky like iron | No rain falls. |
| Ground like bronze | The soil is hard and dry. Crops fail. |

## Leviticus 26:21

If they kept fighting him and refused to listen, the LORD would send seven times as many disasters for their sins.

## Leviticus 26:22

He would send wild animals against them.

The animals would take their children and kill their livestock.

So few people would remain that the roads would be empty.

**Pause and answer**

What would happen to the roads?

## War and hunger

## Leviticus 26:23

What if they still refused to learn from the LORD?

What if they kept fighting him?

## Leviticus 26:24

Then he would turn against them.

He would punish them seven times over for their sins.

## Leviticus 26:25

He would send war because they had broken his covenant.

The people would take shelter in their cities.

The LORD would send disease there.

Their enemies would take them captive.

## Leviticus 26:26

The LORD would cut off their supply of bread.

One oven would hold the bread ten women baked.

Each person would get a share weighed out for them.

They would eat and still be hungry.

**Pause and answer**

How much bread would the people have?

## Cities fall

## Leviticus 26:27

What if they still refused to listen?

What if they kept fighting the LORD?

## Leviticus 26:28

Then the LORD himself would turn against them in anger.

He would punish them seven times over for their sins.

## Leviticus 26:29

They would be so hungry they would eat the flesh of their own sons and daughters.

## Leviticus 26:30

The LORD would tear down their hilltop places of worship.

He would break the altars where they burned incense.

He would pile their dead bodies on their broken idols.

He would look on them with disgust.

## Leviticus 26:31

He would leave their cities in ruins.

He would destroy their places of worship.

He would refuse the sweet smell of their offerings.

## Leviticus 26:32

The LORD would lay waste to the land.

Even the enemies who settled there would be horrified.

## Leviticus 26:33

He would scatter the Israelites among other nations.

War would follow them.

Their land would lie empty and their cities in ruins.

**Pause and answer**

Where would the LORD scatter the Israelites?

## The land rests while Israel is away

## Leviticus 26:34

While the people lived in enemy lands, their own land would rest.

It would have its Sabbath years.

## Leviticus 26:35

The land would rest as long as it lay empty.

It would have the rest the people had denied it while they lived there.

## Leviticus 26:36

The LORD would fill the survivors with fear in enemy lands.

Even a leaf rustling in the wind would frighten them.

They would run as if fleeing a sword.

They would fall with no one chasing them.

## Leviticus 26:37

They would trip over one another trying to escape.

No one would be behind them.

They would be unable to stand against their enemies.

## Leviticus 26:38

They would die among other nations.

The land of their enemies would swallow them up.

**Reading help**

A land that swallows people up is a picture. The people would lose their lives far from home.

## Leviticus 26:39

Those still alive in enemy lands would waste away.

They would suffer for their sins and their ancestors’ sins.

**Pause and answer**

What small sound would frighten the survivors?

## The LORD remembers his people

## Leviticus 26:40

But the survivors could admit what they had done wrong.

They could admit what their ancestors had done too.

They had been unfaithful to the LORD and fought against him.

## Leviticus 26:41

So the LORD had opposed them and taken them into enemy lands.

Now they could let go of their stubborn pride.

They could accept the penalty for their wrongs.

## Leviticus 26:42

Then the LORD would remember his covenant with Jacob.

He would remember his covenant with Isaac and with Abraham.

He would remember the land too.

## Leviticus 26:43

The land they left would lie empty and have its Sabbath rest.

The people would pay the penalty for their sins.

They had rejected the LORD’s rules and hated his laws.

## Leviticus 26:44

Even in enemy lands, the LORD would still be their God.

He would not reject them or destroy them completely.

He would keep his covenant with them.

## Leviticus 26:45

For their sake, he would remember his covenant with their ancestors.

The nations had seen him bring those ancestors out of Egypt.

He brought them out to be their God.

He is the LORD.

## Leviticus 26:46

On Mount Sinai the LORD gave these laws and rules to Moses.

Through Moses he established them between himself and the people of Israel.

**Pause and answer**

Whose covenant would the LORD remember when the people admitted their wrongs?
