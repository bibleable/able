# Leviticus 8 — Moses prepares Aaron and his sons to serve

**About this chapter**

Moses gathers the people to see Aaron and his sons become priests. He dresses them, brings the offerings and tells them where to stay.

## The people gather

## Leviticus 8:1

The LORD spoke to Moses.

## Leviticus 8:2

“Bring Aaron and his sons.

Bring their special clothes and the anointing oil.

Bring a bull for the sin offering and two rams.

Bring the basket of bread made without yeast.

## Leviticus 8:3

Gather all the people at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.”

## Leviticus 8:4

Moses obeyed the LORD.

The people gathered at the tent entrance.

## Leviticus 8:5

Moses told them, “This is what the LORD has told us to do.”

**Pause and answer**

Where did the people gather?

## Moses dresses Aaron

## Leviticus 8:6

Moses brought Aaron and his sons forward.

He washed them with water.

## Leviticus 8:7

Moses dressed Aaron in a tunic and tied on a sash.

He put the robe and the ephod on Aaron.

He fastened the ephod with its woven band.

**Reading help**

A tunic was a long shirt. A sash tied around the waist. The ephod was a special garment over the robe.

## Leviticus 8:8

Moses put the breastpiece on Aaron.

He placed the Urim and Thummim inside it.

**Reading help**

The Urim and Thummim were objects the priest used to seek God’s decisions.

## Leviticus 8:9

Moses put the turban on Aaron’s head.

He fastened the holy gold plate to its front.

He did just as the LORD had commanded him.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Moses put the gold plate?

## Moses sets people and things apart

## Leviticus 8:10

Moses put anointing oil on the holy tent and everything inside.

This set the tent and its contents apart for God.

## Leviticus 8:11

Moses sprinkled oil on the altar seven times.

He put oil on the altar and all its tools.

He also put oil on the washing bowl and its stand.

This set them apart for God.

## Leviticus 8:12

Moses poured some oil on Aaron’s head.

In this way he set Aaron apart for God.

## Leviticus 8:13

Moses brought Aaron’s sons forward.

He dressed them in tunics and tied their sashes and headbands.

He did just as the LORD had commanded him.

**Pause and answer**

What did Moses pour on Aaron’s head?

## The bull for the sin offering

## Leviticus 8:14

Moses brought the bull for the sin offering.

Aaron and his sons put their hands on its head.

## Leviticus 8:15

Moses killed the bull.

With his finger he put blood on all four horns of the altar.

This made the altar clean for worship.

He poured the rest at the altar’s base.

He set the altar apart for offerings that would make atonement.

**Reading help**

The horns were the raised corners of the altar. Making atonement means dealing with the sin that comes between people and God.

## Leviticus 8:16

Moses took all the fat on the inner parts.

He took part of the liver and both kidneys with their fat.

He burned these parts on the altar.

## Leviticus 8:17

Moses burned the rest of the bull outside the camp.

This included its skin, meat and dung.

He did just as the LORD had commanded him.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Moses burn the bull’s skin and meat?

## The first ram

## Leviticus 8:18

Moses brought the ram for the burnt offering.

Aaron and his sons put their hands on its head.

## Leviticus 8:19

Moses killed the ram.

He splashed its blood on every side of the altar.

## Leviticus 8:20

He cut the ram into pieces.

He burned the head, the pieces and the fat.

## Leviticus 8:21

Moses washed the inner parts and legs with water.

He burned the whole ram on the altar.

It was a food offering whose smell pleased the LORD.

Moses did just as the LORD had commanded him.

**Pause and answer**

What did Aaron and his sons do when Moses brought the first ram?

## The ram for the ordination

## Leviticus 8:22

Moses brought the second ram for the ordination.

Aaron and his sons put their hands on its head.

**Reading help**

Ordination is the ceremony that sets people apart for their work. Here Aaron and his sons become priests.

## Leviticus 8:23

Moses killed the ram.

He put some blood on Aaron’s right earlobe.

He also put blood on Aaron’s right thumb and right big toe.

## Leviticus 8:24

Moses brought Aaron’s sons forward.

He put blood on each son’s right earlobe, right thumb and right big toe.

Then he splashed blood on every side of the altar.

## Leviticus 8:25

Moses took the fat tail and all the fat on the inner parts.

He took part of the liver and both kidneys with their fat.

He also took the right thigh.

**Pause and answer**

Whose right thumbs did Moses mark with blood?

## The gifts in their hands

## Leviticus 8:26

The basket of bread without yeast stood before the LORD.

Moses took one plain bread cake, one cake made with oil and one thin wafer.

He laid them on the fat and the right thigh.

Moses took three kinds of bread from a basket.

## Leviticus 8:27

Moses put these gifts in the hands of Aaron and his sons.

He waved the gifts before the LORD as a wave offering.

## Leviticus 8:28

Moses took the gifts from their hands.

He burned them on the altar with the burnt offering.

This ordination offering was a food offering to the LORD.

Its smell pleased him.

## Leviticus 8:29

The breast of the second ram was Moses’ share.

He waved it before the LORD as a wave offering.

He did just as the LORD had commanded him.

## Seven days at the tent entrance

## Leviticus 8:30

Moses took some anointing oil and some blood from the altar.

He sprinkled them on Aaron, his sons and their clothes.

This set the men and their clothes apart for God.

## Leviticus 8:31

Moses told Aaron and his sons, “Boil the meat at the tent entrance.

Eat it there with the bread from the ordination basket.

This is the meal I told you to eat.

## Leviticus 8:32

Burn any meat and bread left over.

## Leviticus 8:33

Stay at the tent entrance for seven days.

Your ordination takes seven days to complete.

## Leviticus 8:34

The LORD commanded us to do these things today to make atonement for you.

## Leviticus 8:35

Stay at the tent entrance day and night for seven days.

Do what the LORD requires so that you will not die.

This is the command I received.”

## Leviticus 8:36

Aaron and his sons did everything the LORD told them through Moses.
