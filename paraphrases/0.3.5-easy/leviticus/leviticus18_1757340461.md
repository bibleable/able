# Leviticus 18 — The LORD gives Israel rules about sex and family

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks to Israel through Moses. He names close relatives and gives rules about sex. He tells Israel what happened to people who broke these rules.

## Which customs should Israel follow?

## Leviticus 18:1

The LORD spoke to Moses.

## Leviticus 18:2

The LORD told Moses to speak to Israel.

This was his message.

“I am the LORD your God.”

## Leviticus 18:3

You used to live in Egypt.

Do not follow the customs you saw there.

I am bringing you to Canaan.

Do not follow the customs there either.

## Leviticus 18:4

Follow my rules.

Do what I tell you each day.

I am the LORD your God.

**Reading help**

Customs are ways people usually do things.

## Leviticus 18:5

Keep my laws and follow my rules.

The person who does these things will live by them.

I am the LORD.

**Pause and answer**

Whose rules does the LORD tell Israel to follow?

## Parents, sisters and granddaughters

## Leviticus 18:6

None of you may have sex with a close relative.

I am the LORD.

## Leviticus 18:7

Do not have sex with your mother.

That would bring shame on your father.

She is your mother.

## Leviticus 18:8

Do not have sex with your father’s wife.

That would bring shame on your father.

## Leviticus 18:9

Do not have sex with your sister.

She may be your father’s daughter or your mother’s daughter.

This rule holds wherever she was born.

## Leviticus 18:10

Do not have sex with your granddaughter.

This means your son’s daughter or your daughter’s daughter.

Doing this would bring shame on your family.

## Leviticus 18:11

Your father and his wife may have a daughter together.

She is your sister.

Do not have sex with her.

**Pause and answer**

What family word means your son’s daughter or your daughter’s daughter?

## Aunts and relatives by marriage

## Leviticus 18:12

Do not have sex with your father’s sister.

She is your father’s close relative.

## Leviticus 18:13

Do not have sex with your mother’s sister.

She is your mother’s close relative.

## Leviticus 18:14

Do not have sex with the wife of your father’s brother.

She is your aunt.

Doing this would bring shame on your uncle.

## Leviticus 18:15

Do not have sex with your daughter-in-law.

She is your son’s wife.

**Family words**

| Family word | How the person is related |
| --- | --- |
| Granddaughter | Your child’s daughter |
| Aunt in verse 14 | The wife of your father’s brother |
| Daughter-in-law | Your son’s wife |

## Leviticus 18:16

Do not have sex with your brother’s wife.

That would bring shame on your brother.

**Pause and answer**

Who is the daughter-in-law in verse 15?

## A woman and her close relatives

## Leviticus 18:17

Do not have sex with both a woman and her daughter.

Do not marry the woman’s granddaughter and have sex with her.

This includes her son’s daughter and her daughter’s daughter.

They are close relatives.

Doing this is deeply wrong.

## Leviticus 18:18

While your wife lives, do not marry her sister and have sex with her.

That would make the sisters rival wives.

**Reading help**

Rival wives are wives who compete with each other for the same husband’s love or attention.

**Pause and answer**

When does the rule about a wife’s sister apply?

## Other acts the LORD forbids

## Leviticus 18:19

Do not have sex with a woman during her period.

## Leviticus 18:20

Do not have sex with your neighbor’s wife.

Doing this would make you unclean.

## Leviticus 18:21

Do not give any of your children as a sacrifice to Molech.

That would bring shame on the name of your God.

I am the LORD.

**Reading help**

Molech was the name of a god some people worshiped.

## Leviticus 18:22

A man must not have sex with a man as with a woman.

The LORD calls this act deeply wrong.

## Leviticus 18:23

Do not have sex with an animal.

Doing this would make you unclean.

A woman must not mate with an animal either.

This act is deeply wrong.

**Pause and answer**

What does the LORD tell Israel about giving children to Molech?

## The land and its people

## Leviticus 18:24

Do not make yourselves unclean by doing these things.

The nations I am driving out ahead of you did them.

That is how those nations became unclean.

## Leviticus 18:25

Even the land became unclean.

So I am punishing the land for the wrong done there.

The land will spit its people out.

**Reading help**

“Spit its people out” is a picture. It means the people will have to leave their land.

## Leviticus 18:26

Keep my laws and rules.

Do none of these things that I call deeply wrong.

This applies to people born among you.

It also applies to foreigners who live among you.

## Leviticus 18:27

The people who lived there before you did all these things.

That is how the land became unclean.

## Leviticus 18:28

If you make the land unclean, it will spit you out.

That is what happened to the nations before you.

## Leviticus 18:29

Anyone who does any of these things must be cut off from their people.

**Reading help**

“Cut off” means the person can no longer belong to the community of Israel.

## Leviticus 18:30

Do what I have told you.

Do not copy the deeply wrong customs of those who lived there before you.

Those customs would make you unclean.

I am the LORD your God.

**Pause and answer**

Who else must keep these rules alongside people born in Israel?
