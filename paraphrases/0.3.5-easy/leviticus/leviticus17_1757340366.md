# Leviticus 17 — Offerings and the blood that gives life

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Israel where to bring offerings. He explains why the people must never eat blood.

## Bring the animal to the tent

## Leviticus 17:1

The LORD spoke to Moses.

## Leviticus 17:2

The LORD said, “Speak to Aaron and his sons.

Speak to all the people of Israel.

Tell them what I have commanded.”

## Leviticus 17:3

An Israelite might kill an ox, a lamb or a goat.

This rule applies both inside and outside the camp.

## Leviticus 17:4

The Israelite must first bring the animal to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

There the person gives it to the LORD as an offering.

Anyone who skips this is guilty of shedding blood.

That person must be cut off from the people.

**Reading help**

Here, “cut off” means the person can no longer be part of the community of Israel.

**Pause and answer**

Where must the Israelite bring the animal?

## Give the offerings to the LORD

## Leviticus 17:5

The Israelites used to give sacrifices in open fields.

Now they must bring those animals to the LORD.

They bring them to the priest at the tent entrance.

There they give them to the LORD as peace offerings.

## Leviticus 17:6

The priest splashes the blood on the LORD’s altar at the tent entrance.

Then the priest burns the fat.

The smell pleases the LORD.

## Leviticus 17:7

The people have been unfaithful to the LORD.

They must stop giving sacrifices to goat demons.

Their children and all who come after them must keep this rule.

**Reading help**

A peace offering is a gift made to the LORD that includes a shared meal. Goat demons were spirits to which people gave offerings. The LORD calls that worship unfaithful because the people had promised to worship him.

## Leviticus 17:8

The LORD told Moses to give another rule.

It covers Israelites and foreigners who live among them.

It applies to anyone bringing a burnt offering or another sacrifice.

## Leviticus 17:9

They must bring it to the tent entrance.

There they offer it to the LORD.

Anyone who fails to do this must be cut off from the people.

**Pause and answer**

Who splashes the blood on the altar?

## Why Israel must not eat blood

## Leviticus 17:10

The LORD said, “An Israelite or a foreigner living among you might eat blood.

If anyone eats any blood, I will turn against that person.

I will cut that person off from the people.”

## Leviticus 17:11

The life of a body is in its blood.

The LORD gave Israel blood to use on the altar.

There the blood makes atonement for the people’s lives.

**Reading help**

Atonement is the way God gives people to deal with their sins and come before him.

## Leviticus 17:12

That is why the LORD tells Israel, “None of you may eat blood.

Foreigners living among you must follow this rule too.”

**Pause and answer**

What does the LORD say is in the blood?

## When someone hunts or finds an animal

## Leviticus 17:13

An Israelite or a foreigner living among them might hunt a wild animal or bird.

If the law allows people to eat it, the hunter must drain its blood.

Then the hunter must cover the blood with dirt.

## Leviticus 17:14

Every creature’s life is in its blood.

That is why the LORD told Israel never to eat any creature’s blood.

Anyone who eats blood must be cut off from the people.

## Leviticus 17:15

Someone might eat an animal they found dead.

Or they might eat one that wild animals tore apart.

Whether born in Israel or elsewhere, that person must wash their clothes.

They must also bathe in water.

They stay unclean until evening.

Then they are clean.

**Reading help**

Here, “unclean” describes the person’s standing under Israel’s rules for worship.

## Leviticus 17:16

Anyone who fails to wash their clothes and bathe must answer for that wrong.
