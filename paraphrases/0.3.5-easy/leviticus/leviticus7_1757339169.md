# Leviticus 7 — Sharing the offerings

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Israel how to share the offerings. The rules explain who receives each part and when people can eat it.

## The guilt offering

## Leviticus 7:1

Here is the rule for the guilt offering.

This offering is most holy.

## Leviticus 7:2

The animal is killed in the same place as the burnt offering.

The priest splashes its blood on every side of the altar.

## Leviticus 7:3

All its fat is offered.

This includes the fat tail and the fat covering the inner parts.

## Leviticus 7:4

Both kidneys go with the fat near the lower back.

A section of the liver comes out with the kidneys.

## Leviticus 7:5

The priest burns these parts on the altar.

They are a food offering to the LORD.

This is a guilt offering.

## Leviticus 7:6

Any male among the priests may eat the meat.

The priests must eat it in a holy place.

This offering is most holy.

## What the priests receive

## Leviticus 7:7

One rule applies to both guilt offerings and sin offerings.

The offering belongs to the priest who makes atonement with it.

**Reading help**

To make atonement means to deal with sin before God.

## Leviticus 7:8

When a priest brings a burnt offering for someone, the priest keeps the animal’s skin.

## Leviticus 7:9

A grain offering may be baked in an oven or cooked in a pan or on a griddle.

It belongs to the priest who presents it.

## Leviticus 7:10

Aaron’s sons share equally in the grain offerings, whether dry or mixed with oil.

## Bread to give thanks

## Leviticus 7:11

Here are the rules for bringing a peace offering to the LORD.

## Leviticus 7:12

A person bringing a peace offering to give thanks brings bread too.

They bring cakes without yeast, mixed with olive oil.

They bring thin wafers without yeast, brushed with oil.

They also bring cakes of fine flour kneaded well with oil.

## Leviticus 7:13

With this peace offering of thanks, the person also brings bread made with yeast.

## Leviticus 7:14

The person gives the LORD one portion of each kind of bread.

These portions belong to the priest who sprinkles the blood of the peace offering.

## When to eat the meat

## Leviticus 7:15

People must eat the meat of this thanksgiving offering the day it is offered.

They must leave none until morning.

## Leviticus 7:16

A person may bring a peace offering to keep a promise or simply because they want to give.

People eat the meat that day.

They may eat what is left the next day.

## Leviticus 7:17

On the third day they must burn any meat still left.

## Leviticus 7:18

If anyone eats the meat on the third day, the LORD rejects the offering.

The person who brought it gets no credit for it.

The offering has become offensive to the LORD.

The person who eats the meat bears the guilt.

| Reason for the peace offering | When people may eat the meat |
| --- | --- |
| To give thanks | Only on the day of the offering |
| To keep a promise or give freely | On the day of the offering and the next day |

## Who may eat the peace offering

## Leviticus 7:19

If the meat touches anything unclean, burn the meat.

No one may eat it.

Anyone who is clean for worship may eat the other meat.

**Reading help**

Here “unclean” means unfit for worship under Israel’s law.

## Leviticus 7:20

The peace offering belongs to the LORD.

If someone eats its meat while unclean, that person must be cut off from the people.

## Leviticus 7:21

Someone might touch something unclean from a person or an animal, or another thing the law calls unclean and hateful.

If they then eat the LORD’s peace offering, they must be cut off from the people.

## The rule about fat and blood

## Leviticus 7:22

Then the LORD spoke to Moses.

## Leviticus 7:23

He told Moses to give Israel this rule.

They must not eat the fat of cattle, sheep or goats.

## Leviticus 7:24

They might find an animal dead or torn by wild animals.

They may use its fat for other things.

They must not eat it.

## Leviticus 7:25

Some animals can be food offerings to the LORD.

Anyone who eats their fat must be cut off from the people.

## Leviticus 7:26

Wherever the Israelites live, they must not eat the blood of any bird or animal.

## Leviticus 7:27

Anyone who eats blood must be cut off from the people.

## The breast and the right thigh

## Leviticus 7:28

Then the LORD spoke to Moses again.

## Leviticus 7:29

He told Moses to give Israel another rule.

Anyone giving a peace offering must bring their sacrifice to the LORD.

## Leviticus 7:30

The person brings the food offering with their own hands.

They bring the fat and the breast.

They wave the breast before the LORD as a wave offering.

## Leviticus 7:31

The priest burns the fat on the altar.

The breast belongs to Aaron and his sons.

## Leviticus 7:32

The person gives the priest the right thigh from the peace offering.

## Leviticus 7:33

The priest who offers the blood and fat receives that right thigh as his share.

## Leviticus 7:34

The LORD takes these portions from Israel’s peace offerings.

He gives the breast and the thigh to Aaron and his sons.

The Israelites must always give them this share.

## A lasting share for the priests

## Leviticus 7:35

This share of the LORD’s food offerings belongs to Aaron and his sons.

It has been theirs since they came forward to serve as priests.

## Leviticus 7:36

On the day they were anointed, the LORD told Israel to give them this share.

This rule continues for generations to come.

**Reading help**

To anoint a priest means to put oil on him to set him apart for God.

## Leviticus 7:37

These are the rules for burnt offerings, grain offerings, sin offerings and guilt offerings.

They also cover offerings for appointing priests and peace offerings.

## Leviticus 7:38

The LORD gave Moses these rules on Mount Sinai.

That day he told the Israelites to bring him offerings in the wilderness of Sinai.
