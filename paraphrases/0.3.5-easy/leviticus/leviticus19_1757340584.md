# Leviticus 19 — Be holy and love your neighbor

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Israel how to live as his holy people. He speaks about worship, work and neighbors. Even picking grapes gives someone a chance to show love.

## Be holy

## Leviticus 19:1

The LORD spoke to Moses.

## Leviticus 19:2

He told Moses to speak to everyone in Israel.

His message was this.

Be holy, because I, the LORD your God, am holy.

**Reading help**

Holy means set apart as sacred. Israel belongs to the LORD. He tells the people how to live.

## Leviticus 19:3

Each of you must respect your mother and father.

Keep my Sabbath days of rest.

I am the LORD your God.

## Leviticus 19:4

Do not turn to idols for help.

Do not make gods for yourselves from melted metal.

I am the LORD your God.

**Pause and answer**

Whose days of rest must Israel keep?

## When to eat a peace offering

## Leviticus 19:5

When you give the LORD a peace offering, give it in a way he will accept.

## Leviticus 19:6

Eat the meat that day or the next day.

On the third day, burn whatever meat remains.

## Leviticus 19:7

If anyone eats it on the third day, the meat is unfit.

The LORD will refuse the offering.

## Leviticus 19:8

That person must answer for the wrong.

They treated something holy to the LORD with disrespect.

That person must be cut off from their people.

**Reading help**

A peace offering includes a meal shared after the sacrifice. Here, “cut off” means the person can no longer belong to the community of Israel.

**Pause and answer**

What happens to meat left on the third day?

## Leave food for others

## Leviticus 19:9

When you harvest grain, leave the edges of your field uncut.

Leave behind the grain the workers miss.

![A stalk has ripe grain at its tip.](../pictures/grain.svg)

Leaving stalks at the edge of the field gives others grain to gather.

## Leviticus 19:10

When you pick grapes, leave some on the vines.

Leave the fallen grapes on the ground.

Leave this food for poor people and foreigners.

I am the LORD your God.

**Pause and answer**

Who may gather the food left behind?

## Tell the truth and pay workers

## Leviticus 19:11

Do not steal.

Do not lie to one another.

Do not trick each other.

## Leviticus 19:12

Do not use my name to swear that a lie is true.

That would show disrespect for your God’s name.

I am the LORD.

## Leviticus 19:13

Do not cheat or rob your neighbor.

Pay hired workers what you owe them.

Do not keep their pay until morning.

## Leviticus 19:14

Do not curse a person who is deaf.

Do not put anything in a blind person’s path to trip them.

Show deep respect for your God.

I am the LORD.

When is it too late to pay the worker in this verse?

## Love your neighbor

## Leviticus 19:15

When you judge a case, be fair to your neighbor.

Do not take someone’s side because they are poor.

Do not favor someone because they are rich.

## Leviticus 19:16

Do not spread lies that hurt someone’s good name.

Do not put your neighbor’s life in danger.

I am the LORD.

## Leviticus 19:17

Do not hold hatred for your brother in your heart.

Tell your neighbor directly when they have done wrong.

Then you will not share the blame.

## Leviticus 19:18

Do not try to get even with your people.

Do not hold on to anger against them.

Love your neighbor as you love yourself.

I am the LORD.

**Pause and answer**

How does the LORD say to love your neighbor?

## Rules about mixtures and a guilt offering

## Leviticus 19:19

Keep my rules.

Do not breed two different kinds of farm animals together.

Do not plant two kinds of seed in one field.

Do not wear clothes made from two different materials.

## Leviticus 19:20

Suppose a man has sex with an enslaved woman promised to another man.

No one has bought her freedom or set her free.

The law calls for punishment.

Neither person is to die, because the woman was still enslaved.

## Leviticus 19:21

The man must bring a ram to the Tent of Meeting.

At the entrance, he gives it to the LORD as a guilt offering.

## Leviticus 19:22

The priest offers the ram before the LORD.

In this way the priest makes atonement for the man.

The LORD will forgive the man for that sin.

**Reading help**

A ram is a male sheep. To make atonement here, the priest gives the offering God asks for when someone has sinned.

**Pause and answer**

Who brings the ram to the tent?

## Fruit from a new tree

## Leviticus 19:23

When you enter the land, you may plant fruit trees.

For the first three years, leave their fruit alone.

You must not eat it.

## Leviticus 19:24

In year four, set apart all the fruit for the LORD.

Give it to him as an offering of praise.

## Leviticus 19:25

In year five, you may eat the fruit.

In this way your harvest will grow.

I am the LORD your God.

**Follow the years**

| Years after planting | What happens to the fruit? |
| --- | --- |
| Years one to three | No one eats it. |
| Year four | The people offer it to the LORD. |
| Year five | The people may eat it. |

## How Israel honors the LORD

## Leviticus 19:26

Do not eat anything that still has blood in it.

Do not try to learn secrets through magic.

Do not cast spells.

## Leviticus 19:27

Do not cut off the hair at the sides of your head.

Do not trim off the edges of your beard.

## Leviticus 19:28

Do not cut your body to mourn the dead.

Do not put tattoo marks on yourselves.

I am the LORD.

## Leviticus 19:29

Do not make your daughter have sex for money.

That would dishonor her.

This practice could spread and fill the land with wrongdoing.

## Leviticus 19:30

Keep my Sabbath days of rest.

Show respect for my holy place of worship.

I am the LORD.

## Leviticus 19:31

Do not seek out people who claim to speak with spirits.

Turning to them would make you unclean.

I am the LORD your God.

**Pause and answer**

How must the people treat the LORD’s holy place?

## Respect older people and welcome foreigners

## Leviticus 19:32

Stand up when an older person comes near.

Show respect for older people.

Show deep respect for your God.

I am the LORD.

## Leviticus 19:33

When someone from another country lives among you, do not mistreat them.

## Leviticus 19:34

Treat that person like someone born in your land.

Love that person as you love yourself.

Remember, you were foreigners in Egypt.

I am the LORD your God.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the Israelites once live as foreigners?

## Measure honestly

## Leviticus 19:35

Be honest when you measure how long something is.

Be honest when you weigh it or measure how much it holds.

## Leviticus 19:36

Use honest scales and weights.

Use a full ephah for dry goods and a full hin for liquids.

I am the LORD your God.

I brought you out of Egypt.

**Reading help**

An ephah and a hin are measures. A seller must give the buyer the full amount.

## Leviticus 19:37

Keep all my laws and rules.

Do what they say.

I am the LORD.
