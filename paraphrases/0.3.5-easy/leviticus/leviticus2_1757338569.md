# Leviticus 2 — Bringing a grain offering

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Israel how to bring a gift of grain. The priest burns part on the altar. Aaron and his sons receive the rest.

## A gift of flour and oil

## Leviticus 2:1

When someone brings a grain offering to the LORD, they bring fine flour.

They pour olive oil on the flour.

They add frankincense.

**Reading help**

Frankincense comes from the sap of a tree. It gives off a scent when it burns.

## Leviticus 2:2

They bring the offering to Aaron’s sons, the priests.

A priest takes a handful of the flour and oil.

He adds all the frankincense to that handful.

He burns this part on the altar as a reminder before the LORD.

It is a food offering.

Its smell pleases the LORD.

## Leviticus 2:3

The rest of the grain offering belongs to Aaron and his sons.

Their share is a most holy part of the food offered to the LORD.

## Three ways to cook the grain

## Leviticus 2:4

You may bake a grain offering in an oven.

Use fine flour without yeast.

Make cakes with oil mixed into the flour, or make thin wafers and coat them with oil.

## Leviticus 2:5

You may cook the offering on a flat cooking surface called a griddle.

Make the bread with fine flour mixed with oil.

Leave out the yeast.

## Leviticus 2:6

Break the bread into small pieces.

Pour oil over the pieces.

This is a grain offering.

## Leviticus 2:7

You may also cook a grain offering in a pan.

Use fine flour and oil.

## The priest takes the gift to the altar

## Leviticus 2:8

Whichever way you prepare the offering, bring it to the LORD.

Give it to the priest.

The priest takes it to the altar.

## Leviticus 2:9

The priest takes the part that serves as a reminder.

He burns that part on the altar.

It is a food offering.

Its smell pleases the LORD.

## Leviticus 2:10

The rest belongs to Aaron and his sons.

Their share is a most holy part of the food offered to the LORD.

**Pause and answer**

## Leaven, honey and salt

## Leviticus 2:11

Keep leaven out of grain offerings for the LORD.

Do not burn leaven or honey as food offerings to him.

**Reading help**

Leaven makes dough rise. Yeast is one kind of leaven.

## Leviticus 2:12

You may bring leaven and honey as firstfruits for the LORD.

But do not burn them on the altar for their smell.

**Reading help**

Firstfruits are gifts from the first part of a harvest.

## Leviticus 2:13

Put salt on every grain offering.

Remember the salt of your covenant with God.

Put salt on every offering you bring.

**Reading help**

A covenant is a solemn agreement. The salt went with the offerings Israel brought under its agreement with God.

## Grain from the first harvest

## Leviticus 2:14

You may bring the LORD grain from your first harvest.

Roast the heads of new grain over a fire.

Bring the crushed grain as your offering.

## Leviticus 2:15

Put oil and frankincense on the grain.

This is a grain offering.

## Leviticus 2:16

The priest takes part of the crushed grain and oil.

He adds all the frankincense.

He burns this part as a reminder before the LORD.

It is a food offering to the LORD.
