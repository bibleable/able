# Leviticus 1 — Bringing a burnt offering

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Moses about burnt offerings. A person brings a gift, and a priest burns it on the altar.

## The LORD speaks from the tent

## Leviticus 1:1

The LORD called to Moses.

He spoke from the Tent of Meeting.

## Leviticus 1:2

He said, “Tell the Israelites how to bring me an offering.

They may bring an animal from their herd or flock.”

**Reading help**

An offering is a gift brought to God. The Tent of Meeting was the holy tent where the LORD spoke to Moses.

**Pause and answer**

Where was the LORD when he called to Moses?

## A young bull from the herd

## Leviticus 1:3

For a burnt offering from the herd, the person brings a male without any defect.

He brings it to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

There the LORD can accept his offering.

## Leviticus 1:4

The person rests his hand on the animal’s head.

The LORD accepts the offering on that person’s behalf.

The offering makes atonement for him.

**Reading help**

To make atonement means to deal with the sin that comes between a person and God.

## Leviticus 1:5

The person kills the young bull before the LORD.

Aaron’s sons, the priests, bring the blood to the altar at the tent entrance.

They splash the blood on every side of the altar.

## Leviticus 1:6

The person removes the skin from the bull.

Then he cuts the bull into pieces.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the person rest his hand?

## The priests prepare the altar

## Leviticus 1:7

Aaron’s sons put fire on the altar.

They arrange wood on the fire.

## Leviticus 1:8

The priests lay the pieces on the burning wood.

They include the head and the fat.

## Leviticus 1:9

The inner parts and legs must be washed with water.

The priest burns the whole offering on the altar.

This burnt offering is a food offering.

Its smell pleases the LORD.

**Pause and answer**

Who arranged the pieces on the burning wood?

## A sheep or goat from the flock

## Leviticus 1:10

A person may bring a burnt offering from the flock.

He brings a male sheep or goat without any defect.

## Leviticus 1:11

The person kills the animal before the LORD on the north side of the altar.

Aaron’s sons splash the blood on every side of the altar.

## Leviticus 1:12

The person cuts the animal into pieces.

The priest lays the pieces on the burning wood.

He includes the head and the fat.

## Leviticus 1:13

The inner parts and legs must be washed with water.

The priest presents the whole offering and burns it on the altar.

This burnt offering is a food offering.

Its smell pleases the LORD.

**Pause and answer**

On which side of the altar did the person bring the sheep or goat to kill it?

## A bird as an offering

## Leviticus 1:14

A person may bring a bird as a burnt offering to the LORD.

He brings a turtledove or a young pigeon.

## Leviticus 1:15

The priest brings the bird to the altar.

He twists off its head and burns the head on the altar.

He drains the blood against the side of the altar.

## Leviticus 1:16

The priest removes the bird’s food pouch and the food inside.

He throws them onto the ash heap east of the altar.

**Reading help**

The food pouch is called the crop. It holds food the bird has swallowed.

## Leviticus 1:17

The priest opens the bird by its wings.

He leaves the two sides joined.

Then he burns the bird on the wood on the altar.

This burnt offering is a food offering.

Its smell pleases the LORD.

| Where the gift came from | What the person brought |
| --- | --- |
| The herd | A young bull |
| The flock | A male sheep or goat |
| The birds | A turtledove or a young pigeon |

Using the table, name a gift a person could bring from the flock.
