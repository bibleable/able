# Leviticus 15 — When fluid or blood leaves the body

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives Israel rules about fluid and blood. Some rules say to wash and wait until evening. Others say to count seven days.

## A man has a discharge

## Leviticus 15:1

The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron.

## Leviticus 15:2

The LORD told them to give Israel this rule.

A man may have a discharge from his body.

That fluid is unclean under this law.

**Reading help**

A discharge is fluid coming from the body. Here, “unclean” describes a person or thing under Israel’s rules for worship.

## Leviticus 15:3

The fluid may flow out.

Or it may build up because it cannot flow.

In either case, the discharge makes the man unclean.

## Leviticus 15:4

Any bed the man lies on becomes unclean.

Any furniture he sits on becomes unclean.

What happens to the bed the man lies on?

## Touching the man or his things

## Leviticus 15:5

Anyone who touches his bed must wash their clothes.

They must also bathe in water.

They stay unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 15:6

Someone else may sit where the man sat.

That person must wash their clothes and bathe in water.

They stay unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 15:7

Anyone who touches the man must wash their clothes.

They must also bathe in water.

They stay unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 15:8

The man may spit on someone who is clean.

That person must wash their clothes and bathe in water.

They stay unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 15:9

Any saddle the man rides on becomes unclean.

## Leviticus 15:10

Anyone who touches something that was under the man stays unclean until evening.

Someone who carries it must also wash their clothes and bathe in water.

That person stays unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 15:11

Suppose the man touches someone before rinsing his hands with water.

The person he touches must wash their clothes and bathe in water.

That person stays unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 15:12

If the man touches a clay pot, someone must break it.

If he touches a wooden tool, someone must rinse it with water.

## When the discharge stops

## Leviticus 15:13

When the discharge stops, the man counts seven days.

He washes his clothes.

He bathes in fresh water.

Then he is clean.

## Leviticus 15:14

On day eight, he takes two turtledoves or two young pigeons.

He comes before the LORD at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.

There he gives the birds to the priest.

## Leviticus 15:15

The priest gives one bird as a sin offering.

He gives the other as a burnt offering.

Because of the discharge, the priest makes atonement for the man before the LORD.

**Reading help**

To make atonement here, the priest gives the offerings God asks for so the person can worship God again.

## When semen leaves the body

## Leviticus 15:16

When semen leaves a man's body, he must bathe his whole body in water.

He stays unclean until evening.

**Reading help**

Semen is fluid from a man’s body that can carry sperm.

## Leviticus 15:17

Someone must wash any clothes or leather that the semen touched.

Those things stay unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 15:18

Suppose a man and woman have sex and semen comes out.

Both must bathe in water.

Both stay unclean until evening.

**Pause and answer**

How long do both people stay unclean?

## During a monthly period

## Leviticus 15:19

When a woman bleeds during her period, she is unclean for seven days.

Anyone who touches her stays unclean until evening.

**Reading help**

A period is the bleeding a woman usually has each month.

## Leviticus 15:20

Anything she lies on during her period becomes unclean.

Anything she sits on also becomes unclean.

## Leviticus 15:21

Anyone who touches her bed must wash their clothes.

They must also bathe in water.

They stay unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 15:22

Anyone who touches furniture she sat on must wash their clothes.

They must also bathe in water.

They stay unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 15:23

The same time applies to the bed and the seat.

Anyone who touches either stays unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 15:24

Suppose a man has sex with her and her period blood touches him.

The man stays unclean for seven days.

Any bed he lies on becomes unclean.

**Pause and answer**

How long does the woman stay unclean during her period?

## When bleeding lasts beyond a period

## Leviticus 15:25

A woman may bleed for many days outside her period.

Or she may keep bleeding after her usual period.

She is unclean as long as this bleeding lasts, just as during her period.

## Leviticus 15:26

Any bed she lies on becomes unclean during this time.

Any furniture she sits on becomes unclean too.

The same rule applies during her period.

## Leviticus 15:27

Anyone who touches those things becomes unclean.

That person must wash their clothes and bathe in water.

They stay unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 15:28

When this bleeding stops, the woman counts seven days.

After that, she is clean under the law.

## Leviticus 15:29

On day eight, she takes two turtledoves or two young pigeons.

She brings them to the priest at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.

## Leviticus 15:30

The priest gives one bird as a sin offering.

He gives the other as a burnt offering.

Because of the bleeding, the priest makes atonement for the woman before the LORD.

**Follow the days**

| When | What the woman does |
| --- | --- |
| When the bleeding outside her usual period stops | She begins counting seven days. |
| After seven days | She is clean under the law. |
| On day eight | She brings two birds to the priest. |

## The holy tent among the people

## Leviticus 15:31

The LORD told Moses and Aaron to keep Israel apart from what makes people unclean.

The LORD's holy tent stood among the people.

If they made his tent unclean, they would die.

## Leviticus 15:32

These rules cover a man who has a discharge.

They also cover a man who becomes unclean when semen leaves his body.

## Leviticus 15:33

The rules cover a woman during her period.

They cover anyone who has a discharge.

They also cover a man who has sex with a woman who is unclean.
