# Leviticus 20 — The LORD sets Israel apart

**About this chapter**

The LORD gave Moses laws for ancient Israel. These laws named wrong actions and their penalties. The LORD reminded Israel why he had set them apart.

## When a parent gives a child to Molech

## Leviticus 20:1

The LORD spoke to Moses.

## Leviticus 20:2

The LORD told Moses to speak to Israel.

A parent might give a child as a sacrifice to Molech.

Under this law, the people of the land were to stone that parent to death.

This applied to Israelites and to foreigners living among them.

## Leviticus 20:3

The LORD would turn against that parent.

He would cut the parent off from the people.

Giving a child to Molech made the LORD’s holy place unclean.

It also dishonored his holy name.

**Reading help**

Molech was the name of a god some people worshiped. Here, “cut off” means someone could no longer belong to Israel’s community.

## Leviticus 20:4

Suppose the people knew what the parent had done.

They might look away and let the parent live.

## Leviticus 20:5

Then the LORD would turn against that parent and family.

He would cut the parent off from the people.

He would also cut off everyone who followed the parent in worshiping Molech.

That worship meant being unfaithful to the LORD.

## The LORD calls Israel to be holy

## Leviticus 20:6

Some people went for help to those who claimed to speak with spirits.

In doing this they were unfaithful to the LORD.

He would turn against them and cut them off from their people.

## Leviticus 20:7

The LORD told Israel to set themselves apart for him.

They were to be holy because he was their God.

## Leviticus 20:8

They were to keep his laws and do what he said.

The LORD himself made them holy.

**Reading help**

Holy means set apart for God. Verse 7 tells what the people were to do. Verse 8 tells who made them holy.

## Laws about parents and sexual relationships

## Leviticus 20:9

If someone cursed their father or mother, Israel’s law required death.

The person who spoke the curse bore responsibility for that death.

## Leviticus 20:10

If a man had sex with his neighbor’s wife, both had committed adultery.

Under this law, both faced death.

**Reading help**

Adultery means having sex with someone who is married to another person, or being married and having sex with someone other than your husband or wife.

## Leviticus 20:11

If a man had sex with his father’s wife, he dishonored his father.

The law required death for both the man and the woman.

Each bore responsibility for that death.

## Leviticus 20:12

If a man had sex with his son’s wife, both had done wrong.

The law required death for both.

Each bore responsibility for that death.

## Leviticus 20:13

If a man had sex with another man as with a woman, the law called their act deeply wrong.

Under that law both men faced death.

Each bore responsibility for his death.

Whom did the man in verse 11 dishonor?

## Other acts the law forbade

## Leviticus 20:14

The law called it deeply wrong for a man to marry both a woman and her mother.

It required death by fire for all three.

Israel was to keep this practice out of the community.

## Leviticus 20:15

If a man had sex with an animal, the law required his death.

It also required the animal’s death.

## Leviticus 20:16

If a woman went to an animal to mate with it, the same law applied.

Both the woman and the animal faced death.

The law held them responsible.

## Laws about close relatives

## Leviticus 20:17

A man might marry his sister and have sex with her.

She might be his father’s daughter or his mother’s daughter.

The law called this shameful.

Both would be cut off in front of their people.

The man had to answer for having sex with his sister.

## Leviticus 20:18

Suppose a man and a woman had sex during her period.

In doing so, both exposed the source of her bleeding.

Both would be cut off from their people.

## Leviticus 20:19

The LORD forbade sex with a mother’s sister or a father’s sister.

An aunt was a close relative.

Both had to answer for that wrong.

## Leviticus 20:20

A man who had sex with his uncle’s wife dishonored his uncle.

Both the man and the woman would answer for their sin.

They would die without children.

## Leviticus 20:21

A man who married his brother’s wife dishonored his brother.

The law called this act unclean.

The couple would have no children.

## The land the LORD promised

## Leviticus 20:22

The LORD told Israel to follow all his laws and rules.

He was bringing them to a land to live in.

If they obeyed, the land would not spit them out.

## Leviticus 20:23

They must not copy the nations the LORD was driving out.

Those nations had done all these things.

Because of that, the LORD could no longer bear them.

## Leviticus 20:24

The LORD promised to give Israel that land as their own.

He called it a land flowing with milk and honey.

The LORD was their God.

He had set them apart from other peoples.

**Reading help**

| Picture in the passage | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| The land spits people out. | People lose their place in the land. |
| The land flows with milk and honey. | The land has plenty of good food. |

## A people who belong to the LORD

## Leviticus 20:25

Israel had to tell clean animals and birds from unclean ones.

The LORD had named certain animals, birds and crawling creatures as unclean.

The people must not make themselves unclean through those creatures.

**Reading help**

Here, “clean” and “unclean” describe what Israel’s law allowed the people to use.

## Leviticus 20:26

The LORD told the people to be holy because he is holy.

He had set them apart from other nations.

They belonged to him.

## Leviticus 20:27

Israel’s law also spoke about people who claimed to speak with spirits.

Whether the person was a man or a woman, the penalty was death by stoning.

The law held that person responsible for their death.
