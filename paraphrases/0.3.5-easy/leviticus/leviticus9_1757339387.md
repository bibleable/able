# Leviticus 9 — Aaron brings the offerings and blesses the people

**About this chapter**

After seven days of preparation, Aaron begins his work as a priest. His sons help him. The people gather to see what the LORD will do.

## Moses calls Aaron and the leaders

## Leviticus 9:1

On the eighth day, Moses called Aaron and his sons.

He also called the leaders of Israel.

## Leviticus 9:2

Moses told Aaron, “Bring a young bull for your sin offering.

Bring a ram for your burnt offering.

Both animals must have no defect.

Bring them before the LORD.

## Leviticus 9:3

Tell the Israelites to bring a male goat for their sin offering.

For their burnt offering, bring a calf and a lamb.

Both must be a year old and have no defect.

## Leviticus 9:4

Tell them to bring an ox and a ram as a peace offering before the LORD.

Bring a grain offering mixed with oil too.

Today the LORD will appear to you.”

| Offering | What Aaron brings for himself | What the people bring |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sin offering | A young bull | A male goat |
| Burnt offering | A ram | A calf and a lamb |

## The people gather at the tent

## Leviticus 9:5

The people brought everything Moses had asked for to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

The whole community came close and stood before the LORD.

## Leviticus 9:6

Moses said, “The LORD has told you what to do.

Do it so that you may see his glory.”

## Leviticus 9:7

Moses told Aaron, “Go to the altar.

Bring your sin offering and your burnt offering.

Make atonement for yourself and for the people.

Then bring the people’s offering to make atonement for them.

This is what the LORD has commanded.”

**Reading help**

To make atonement means to deal with sin before God.

**Pause and answer**

Who tells Aaron to go to the altar?

## Aaron brings his own offerings

## Leviticus 9:8

Aaron went to the altar.

He killed the young bull as his own sin offering.

## Leviticus 9:9

Aaron’s sons brought him the blood.

Aaron dipped his finger in it.

He put blood on the horns of the altar.

He poured the rest at the base of the altar.

**Reading help**

The horns were the raised corners of the altar.

## Leviticus 9:10

Aaron burned the fat, the kidneys and part of the liver on the altar.

He did what the LORD had commanded Moses.

## Leviticus 9:11

Aaron burned the meat and skin outside the camp.

## Leviticus 9:12

Then Aaron killed the animal for his burnt offering.

His sons brought him the blood.

He splashed it on every side of the altar.

## Leviticus 9:13

His sons brought the animal to him piece by piece, including the head.

Aaron burned the pieces on the altar.

## Leviticus 9:14

Aaron washed the inner parts and legs.

He burned them on top of the burnt offering.

**Pause and answer**

## Aaron brings the people’s offerings

## Leviticus 9:15

Then Aaron brought the people’s offering.

He killed the male goat for their sin offering.

He offered it as he had offered the young bull.

## Leviticus 9:16

Aaron brought the people’s burnt offering.

He followed the rule for that offering.

## Leviticus 9:17

Next Aaron brought the grain offering.

He burned a handful on the altar.

This was in addition to the morning’s burnt offering.

**Pause and answer**

How much of the grain offering does Aaron burn?

## The peace offering

## Leviticus 9:18

Aaron killed the ox and the ram for the people’s peace offering.

His sons brought him the blood.

Aaron splashed it on every side of the altar.

## Leviticus 9:19

His sons also brought parts from the ox and the ram.

They brought the fat tail and the fat covering the inner parts.

They brought the kidneys and part of the liver.

## Leviticus 9:20

His sons laid these parts on the breasts of the animals.

Aaron burned the fat portions on the altar.

## Leviticus 9:21

Aaron waved the breasts and the right thigh before the LORD.

This was a wave offering, just as Moses had commanded.

**Pause and answer**

What does Aaron wave before the LORD?

## The LORD’s glory appears

## Leviticus 9:22

Aaron lifted his hands toward the people and blessed them.

He had finished the sin offering, burnt offering and peace offering.

Then he stepped down.

## Leviticus 9:23

Moses and Aaron entered the Tent of Meeting.

When they came out, they blessed the people.

All the people saw the LORD’s glory.

**Reading help**

The LORD’s glory is his greatness. Here the people see a sign of his presence.

## Leviticus 9:24

Fire came from the LORD.

It burned up the burnt offering and the fat on the altar.

Everyone saw it and shouted for joy.

Then they bowed with their faces to the ground.
