# Leviticus 22 — Holy food and gifts for the LORD

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells the priests who may eat holy food. He also tells Israel which animals to bring as gifts.

## Respect the holy gifts

## Leviticus 22:1

The LORD spoke to Moses.

## Leviticus 22:2

Moses was to tell Aaron and his sons to respect the holy offerings.

The Israelites had set these gifts apart for the LORD.

Careless use would dishonor his holy name.

He is the LORD.

## Leviticus 22:3

This rule also applied to Aaron’s family in years to come.

A priest might come near Israel’s holy gifts while unclean.

If so, the LORD would cut him off from his presence.

He is the LORD.

**Reading help**

Here, “unclean” means a priest must wait before taking part in worship.

**Pause and answer**

Who set the gifts apart for the LORD?

## When a priest can eat again

## Leviticus 22:4

A man in Aaron’s family might have a skin disease or a flow of fluid from his body.

He must wait until he is clean to eat holy food.

A man might also touch something made unclean by a dead body or by a man who had released semen.

**Reading help**

Semen is fluid from a man’s body that can carry sperm.

## Leviticus 22:5

He might touch a crawling creature that makes him unclean.

Or he might touch someone who makes him unclean.

The rule applies whatever caused the uncleanness.

## Leviticus 22:6

Anyone who touches these things stays unclean until evening.

He must bathe in water before eating holy food.

## Leviticus 22:7

After he bathes and the sun sets, he is clean.

Then he may eat the holy offerings.

They are his food.

## Leviticus 22:8

He must not eat an animal he finds dead.

He must not eat one torn by wild animals.

Eating it would make him unclean.

This is the LORD’s command.

## Leviticus 22:9

The priests must carry out the work the LORD gave them.

If they dishonor it, they will bear guilt and die.

The LORD sets the priests apart for himself.

## Who shares the priest’s food?

## Leviticus 22:10

Someone outside the priest’s family must not eat holy food.

A guest may not eat it.

A hired worker may not eat it either.

## Leviticus 22:11

A priest might buy an enslaved person with his own money.

That person may eat the priest’s food.

An enslaved person born in his household may eat it too.

## Leviticus 22:12

A priest’s daughter might marry someone who is not a priest.

In that case she may not eat the holy gifts.

## Leviticus 22:13

Her husband might die, or they might divorce.

If she has no children and returns to her father’s house, she may share his food again.

She may eat as she did when young.

Anyone outside the household still may not eat it.

## When someone eats holy food by mistake

## Leviticus 22:14

Someone might eat a holy offering by mistake.

That person must give the priest a replacement.

They must also give one fifth of its value.

**Reading help**

Imagine the offering was worth five coins. One fifth of five coins is one coin. The person gives the replacement and one extra coin.

## Leviticus 22:15

The Israelites bring holy offerings to the LORD.

The priests must treat those gifts as holy.

## Leviticus 22:16

The priests must keep people from eating gifts they may not eat.

Otherwise those people would be guilty and face punishment.

The LORD is the one who makes them holy.

**Pause and answer**

Who receives the replacement for an offering eaten by mistake?

## Promised gifts and freely chosen gifts

## Leviticus 22:17

The LORD spoke to Moses again.

## Leviticus 22:18

Moses was to speak to Aaron, his sons and all Israel.

An Israelite or a foreigner living among them might bring a burnt offering.

The person might have promised this gift to the LORD.

Or the person might freely choose to give it.

**Reading help**

The Bible calls a promise to God a vow. A freewill offering is a gift someone chooses to give.

## Leviticus 22:19

For the LORD to accept this offering, the animal must be male and free of defects.

It may come from the cattle, sheep or goats.

## Leviticus 22:20

Do not bring an animal with a defect.

The LORD will not accept it for you.

## Leviticus 22:21

Someone might bring a peace offering from a herd or flock.

It might be a promised gift or a freely chosen gift.

To be acceptable, the animal must be free of defects.

What is a vow?

## Which animals may people bring?

## Leviticus 22:22

Do not bring a blind, injured or badly wounded animal.

Do not bring one with a leaking sore, an infected rash or a scab.

Do not put such animals on the altar as food offerings to the LORD.

## Leviticus 22:23

An ox or sheep might have a leg that grew out of shape or stayed too short.

You may give that animal as a freely chosen gift.

You may not use it to keep a promise.

## Leviticus 22:24

Do not offer an animal with bruised, crushed, torn or cut testicles.

Do not sacrifice such animals anywhere in your land.

## Leviticus 22:25

Neither an Israelite nor a foreigner may bring such animals as food offerings to God.

Because of these injuries or defects, God will refuse those offerings.

**Pause and answer**

## Seven days with the mother

## Leviticus 22:26

The LORD spoke to Moses again.

## Leviticus 22:27

A newborn calf, lamb or young goat must stay with its mother for seven days.

From day eight onward, you may give it to the LORD as a food offering.

**Reading help**

| Age of the young animal | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Days one through seven | The young animal stays with its mother. |
| Day eight and later | The people may bring the young animal as an offering. |

## Leviticus 22:28

Do not kill a cow and her calf on the same day.

The same rule applies to a sheep and her lamb.

**Pause and answer**

How long must a newborn lamb stay with its mother?

## An offering to give thanks

## Leviticus 22:29

You may bring an offering to thank the LORD.

Give it in the way he will accept for you.

## Leviticus 22:30

Eat the offering that same day.

Leave none of it until morning.

This is the LORD’s command.

## Leviticus 22:31

The LORD told Israel to keep his commands.

They must do what he says.

He is the LORD.

## Leviticus 22:32

They must honor his holy name.

The people of Israel must recognize him as holy.

The LORD sets them apart for himself.

## Leviticus 22:33

He brought them out of Egypt to be their God.

He is the LORD.
