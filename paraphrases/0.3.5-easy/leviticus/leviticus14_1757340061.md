# Leviticus 14 — Returning to camp and checking a house

**About this chapter**

The priest visits a person whose skin has healed. The person returns to camp in stages. Later, the LORD tells the priest how to check a house for mildew.

## The priest goes outside the camp

## Leviticus 14:1

The LORD spoke to Moses.

## Leviticus 14:2

He gave this rule for a person who had a skin disease.

On the day of cleansing, someone brings the person to meet the priest.

## Leviticus 14:3

The priest goes outside the camp.

He looks at the person's skin to see if the disease has healed.

## Leviticus 14:4

If it has healed, the priest asks for two live birds of a kind Israel calls clean.

He also asks for cedar wood, bright red yarn and a plant called hyssop.

## Leviticus 14:5

The priest tells someone to kill one bird over a clay pot that holds fresh water.

## Leviticus 14:6

The priest takes the living bird, the wood, the yarn and the hyssop.

He dips them in the blood of the bird killed over the water.

## Leviticus 14:7

He sprinkles the person seven times.

He declares the person clean.

Then he lets the living bird fly away into a field.

Where does the priest go to see the person?

## Coming back into camp

## Leviticus 14:8

The person washes their clothes, shaves off all their hair and bathes in water.

Now they are clean under the law for worship.

They may come back into camp.

For seven days they must stay outside their own tent.

## Leviticus 14:9

On the seventh day the person shaves again.

This includes the hair on their head, their beard, their eyebrows and all their other hair.

They wash their clothes and bathe.

Then they are clean.

## Leviticus 14:10

On the eighth day the person brings two male lambs and a female lamb one year old.

Each lamb must be free of defects.

For the grain offering they bring fine flour mixed with olive oil.

The flour measures three tenths of an ephah.

They also bring one log of olive oil.

**Follow the days**

- After the first washing, the person may enter the camp and stays outside their tent.
- On day seven, the person shaves and washes again.
- On day eight, the person brings gifts.

**Reading help**

An ephah measures dry things like flour. A log measures liquids like oil.

## The priest gives the offerings

## Leviticus 14:11

The priest brings the person and the gifts to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

There he presents them to the LORD.

## Leviticus 14:12

The priest takes one male lamb as a guilt offering.

He waves the lamb and the measure of oil before the LORD.

## Leviticus 14:13

He kills the lamb in the holy place where priests kill animals for the sin offering and the burnt offering.

The guilt offering belongs to the priest, just as the sin offering does.

It is most holy.

## Leviticus 14:14

The priest puts some of the lamb's blood on the lower part of the person's right ear.

He also puts blood on their right thumb and their right big toe.

## Leviticus 14:15

The priest pours some olive oil into his left palm.

## Leviticus 14:16

He dips his right index finger in the oil.

With that finger he sprinkles oil seven times before the LORD.

## Leviticus 14:17

He puts oil over the blood on the person's right ear, right thumb and right big toe.

## Leviticus 14:18

He puts the oil left in his palm on the person's head.

He does this to make atonement for them before the LORD.

**Reading help**

Here, making atonement means the priest carries out the steps the LORD gave so the person can be clean before him.

## Leviticus 14:19

Next the priest gives the sin offering.

He makes atonement for the person who is being made clean.

Then he kills the animal for the burnt offering.

## Leviticus 14:20

He gives the burnt offering and the grain offering on the altar.

The priest makes atonement for the person.

Now the person is clean.

Where does the priest put the oil left in his palm?

## When someone can afford less

## Leviticus 14:21

A poor person may be unable to afford all those gifts.

They bring one male lamb for the priest to wave as a guilt offering to make atonement for them.

They bring one tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with olive oil for the grain offering.

They also bring one log of olive oil.

## Leviticus 14:22

They bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons, whichever they can afford.

One bird is for a sin offering.

The other is for a burnt offering.

## Leviticus 14:23

On the eighth day the person brings the gifts to the priest for cleansing.

They meet before the LORD at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

## Leviticus 14:24

The priest waves the lamb for the guilt offering and the measure of olive oil before the LORD.

## Leviticus 14:25

He kills the lamb.

He puts some of its blood on the lower part of the person's right ear, on their right thumb and on their right big toe.

## Leviticus 14:26

The priest pours some oil into his left palm.

## Leviticus 14:27

With his right index finger he sprinkles oil from his palm seven times before the LORD.

## Leviticus 14:28

He puts oil on the person's right ear, right thumb and right big toe.

He puts it where he put the blood.

## Leviticus 14:29

He puts the oil left in his palm on the person's head to make atonement for them before the LORD.

## Leviticus 14:30

Then the priest offers the birds the person could afford.

These are the turtledoves or young pigeons.

## Leviticus 14:31

He gives one bird as a sin offering and one as a burnt offering.

He gives the grain offering too.

In this way he makes atonement before the LORD for the person being made clean.

## Leviticus 14:32

This is the rule for someone who had a skin disease and cannot afford all the usual gifts for cleansing.

## The owner sees a mark on the wall

## Leviticus 14:33

Then the LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron.

## Leviticus 14:34

He said, “I am giving you the land of Canaan as your own.

When you live there, I may cause mildew to appear in a house.”

## Leviticus 14:35

The owner must tell the priest, “I see something in my house that looks like mildew.”

## Leviticus 14:36

The priest tells people to empty the house before he examines it.

That way the things inside can stay clean under the law.

Then the priest goes in to look.

## Leviticus 14:37

He looks for green or red marks on the walls.

Do the marks look as if they sink below the surface?

**Reading help**

Mildew is a growth that can leave marks. Plaster is the layer that covers a wall.

## Leviticus 14:38

If they do, the priest steps outside.

He closes the house for seven days.

**Pause and answer**

What must happen before the priest goes inside?

## The priest checks the house again

## Leviticus 14:39

On the seventh day the priest comes back.

He checks whether the mildew has spread along the walls.

## Leviticus 14:40

If it has spread, he tells people to pull out the stones with mildew on them.

They throw these stones in a place outside the city set aside for unclean things.

## Leviticus 14:41

The priest has people scrape the plaster off all the inside walls.

They take the old plaster to the unclean place outside the city.

## Leviticus 14:42

They put new stones where the old ones were.

They use fresh material to cover the walls again.

## Leviticus 14:43

Suppose the mildew comes back after people have replaced the stones, scraped the walls and covered them again.

## Leviticus 14:44

The priest comes to look.

If the mildew has spread, it is the kind that destroys a house.

The house is unclean.

## Leviticus 14:45

People must pull the house down.

They take all the stones, wood and plaster to the unclean place outside the city.

## Leviticus 14:46

Anyone who goes into the house while it is closed will be unclean until evening.

## Leviticus 14:47

Anyone who sleeps or eats in the house must wash their clothes.

## When the mildew is gone

## Leviticus 14:48

Suppose the priest sees that the mildew has not spread since the walls were covered again.

He declares the house clean because the mildew is gone.

## Leviticus 14:49

To cleanse the house, the priest takes two birds, cedar wood, bright red yarn and hyssop.

## Leviticus 14:50

He kills one bird over a clay pot that holds fresh water.

## Leviticus 14:51

He takes the wood, the hyssop, the yarn and the living bird.

He dips them in the dead bird's blood and the fresh water.

He sprinkles the house seven times.

## Leviticus 14:52

The priest cleanses the house with the bird's blood, the fresh water, the living bird, the wood, the hyssop and the red yarn.

## Leviticus 14:53

He lets the living bird fly away into the fields outside the city.

This is how he makes atonement for the house.

Now it is clean.

**Pause and answer**

Where does the priest release the living bird?

## What the rules cover

## Leviticus 14:54

These rules cover skin diseases that can spread and patches of scaly skin.

## Leviticus 14:55

They also cover mildew on clothing or in a house.

## Leviticus 14:56

They cover a swelling, a rash or a spot on the skin.

## Leviticus 14:57

The rules help the priest decide when someone or something is clean or unclean.

These are the rules for skin diseases and mildew.
