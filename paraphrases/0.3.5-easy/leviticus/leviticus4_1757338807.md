# Leviticus 4 — An offering for sin done by mistake

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Moses what to do when someone sins by mistake. The offering depends on who has sinned.

## When the priest sins

## Leviticus 4:1

The LORD spoke to Moses.

## Leviticus 4:2

He told Moses to give the Israelites these instructions.

Sometimes a person does something the LORD has forbidden without meaning to.

Here is what to do about that sin.

## Leviticus 4:3

Suppose the anointed priest sins and brings guilt on the people.

He must bring the LORD a young bull without any defect.

The bull is a sin offering for his own sin.

**Reading help**

The anointed priest had received special oil as a sign that God had set him apart to serve. A sin offering was a gift brought to God to deal with sin.

## Leviticus 4:4

The priest brings the bull to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

He puts his hand on its head.

Then he kills the bull before the LORD.

**Pause and answer**

What animal does the priest bring for his own sin?

## The blood at the two altars

## Leviticus 4:5

The anointed priest takes some of the bull’s blood into the tent.

## Leviticus 4:6

He dips his finger in the blood.

He sprinkles some blood seven times before the LORD, in front of the inner curtain.

## Leviticus 4:7

Inside the tent, the priest puts blood on the horns of the incense altar before the LORD.

Then he goes to the altar for burnt offerings at the tent entrance.

He pours the rest of the blood at its base.

**Reading help**

The horns were raised parts at the corners of an altar.

**Pause and answer**

Where does the priest pour the blood that is left?

## What happens to the bull

## Leviticus 4:8

The priest removes all the fat covering the bull’s inner parts.

He also takes all the fat on those parts.

## Leviticus 4:9

He takes both kidneys with the fat near the lower back.

With the kidneys he removes a section of the liver.

## Leviticus 4:10

He removes these parts just as for a peace offering from the herd.

He burns them on the altar for burnt offerings.

## Leviticus 4:11

What remains includes the skin, meat, head and legs.

The inner parts and dung remain too.

## Leviticus 4:12

The priest carries all these remains outside the camp.

He takes them to the ash heap, a place kept clean for worship.

There he burns them on a wood fire.

Where does the priest burn the rest of the bull?

## When the whole community sins

## Leviticus 4:13

Suppose the whole community breaks one of the LORD’s commands by mistake.

The people are guilty even before they notice what they have done.

## Leviticus 4:14

When they learn of their sin, they must bring a young bull.

They present it as a sin offering in front of the Tent of Meeting.

## Leviticus 4:15

The community leaders place their hands on the bull’s head before the LORD.

Then the bull is killed there before the LORD.

**Pause and answer**

Who puts their hands on the bull for the whole community?

## An offering for the community

## Leviticus 4:16

The anointed priest takes some of its blood into the Tent of Meeting.

## Leviticus 4:17

He dips his finger in the blood.

He sprinkles blood seven times before the LORD, in front of the inner curtain.

## Leviticus 4:18

The priest puts some blood on the horns of the altar inside the tent.

He pours the rest at the base of the altar for burnt offerings at the entrance.

## Leviticus 4:19

He removes all the fat and burns it on the altar.

## Leviticus 4:20

The priest offers this bull just as he offered the first bull for sin.

In this way he makes atonement for the people.

The LORD forgives them.

**Reading help**

To make atonement means to deal with the sin that has come between people and God.

## Leviticus 4:21

The priest takes the rest of the bull outside the camp.

He burns it as he burned the first bull.

This is the sin offering for the whole community.

**Pause and answer**

What does the LORD do after the priest makes atonement for the people?

## When a leader sins

## Leviticus 4:22

Suppose a leader breaks a command of the LORD his God by mistake.

The leader is guilty of doing what the LORD forbids.

## Leviticus 4:23

When the leader learns of his sin, he brings a male goat without any defect.

## Leviticus 4:24

He places his hand on the goat’s head.

He kills it before the LORD where people kill the burnt offering.

This goat is a sin offering.

## Leviticus 4:25

The priest takes some blood on his finger.

He puts it on the horns of the altar for burnt offerings.

He pours the rest at the base of that altar.

## Leviticus 4:26

The priest burns all the fat on the altar, as for a peace offering.

In this way he makes atonement for the leader’s sin.

The LORD forgives the leader.

**Pause and answer**

Which goat does a leader bring?

## When someone brings a goat

## Leviticus 4:27

Suppose an ordinary member of the community breaks one of the LORD’s commands by mistake.

That person is guilty.

## Leviticus 4:28

When the person learns of the sin, they bring a female goat without any defect as an offering for it.

## Leviticus 4:29

The person puts a hand on the goat’s head.

They kill it at the place for the burnt offering.

## Leviticus 4:30

The priest takes some blood on his finger.

He puts it on the horns of the altar for burnt offerings.

He pours the rest at the base of that altar.

## Leviticus 4:31

All the fat is removed, as for a peace offering.

The priest burns it on the altar.

Its smell pleases the LORD.

The priest makes atonement for the person, and the LORD forgives them.

**Pause and answer**

What does the priest burn on the altar?

## When someone brings a lamb

## Leviticus 4:32

The person may bring a lamb as a sin offering instead.

The lamb must be female and have no defect.

## Leviticus 4:33

The person puts a hand on the lamb’s head.

They kill it as a sin offering where people kill the burnt offering.

## Leviticus 4:34

The priest takes some blood on his finger.

He puts it on the horns of the altar for burnt offerings.

He pours the rest at the base of that altar.

## Leviticus 4:35

The fat is removed as for a lamb brought as a peace offering.

The priest burns it with the food offerings to the LORD.

In this way the priest makes atonement for the person’s sin.

The LORD forgives them.

| Who has sinned? | What offering do they bring? |
| --- | --- |
| The anointed priest | A young bull |
| The whole community | A young bull |
| A leader | A male goat |
| An ordinary member of the community | A female goat or a female lamb |
