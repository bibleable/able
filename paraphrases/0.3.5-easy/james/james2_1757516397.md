# James 2 — Show your faith by what you do

**About this chapter**

James asks us to picture two visitors at a meeting.

Later he asks what we would do for a hungry person.

## Two visitors at the same meeting

## James 2:1

Brothers and sisters, you trust our glorious Lord Jesus Christ.

So do not give anyone special treatment because of their wealth.

## James 2:2

Imagine two men coming to your meeting.

One wears fine clothes and a gold ring.

The other is poor and wears worn-out clothes.

## James 2:3

You give the first man lots of attention.

You offer him the best seat.

Then you tell the poor man to stand.

Or you tell him to sit down by your feet.

## James 2:4

You have judged the two men by their clothes.

You have treated them unfairly.

Wrong thoughts have guided your decision.

**Pause and answer**

Why did one visitor get the best seat?

## God honors people who are poor

## James 2:5

Listen, dear brothers and sisters.

God chose people the world sees as poor.

They can be rich in faith.

They will receive the kingdom God promised those who love him.

## James 2:6

Yet you have shamed the poor.

Think of the rich people who mistreat you.

They even drag you into court.

## James 2:7

Those same people insult the honored name of Jesus.

You belong to him and bear his name.

**Pause and answer**

What has God promised to those who love him?

## Love your neighbor

## James 2:8

Scripture gives this royal law. “Love your neighbor as you love yourself.” You do well when you obey it.

## James 2:9

When you give some people special treatment, you sin.

God’s law shows you have broken it.

## James 2:10

Suppose you keep every command except one.

You are still guilty of breaking the law as a whole.

## James 2:11

God told married people to be faithful to each other.

God also said, “Do not murder.” You may be faithful in marriage.

If you murder someone, you still break God’s law.

## James 2:12

God will judge you by the law that gives freedom.

Remember that when you speak and act.

## James 2:13

God will show no mercy to those who show none.

Mercy wins out over judgment.

Mercy means showing kindness to someone you could condemn.

**Pause and answer**

How does James say to treat your neighbor?

## When someone needs food

## James 2:14

Brothers and sisters, suppose someone says, “I trust God.” Yet they do nothing to act on that trust.

Can faith like that save them?

## James 2:15

Imagine a brother or sister without enough clothes.

They also need food for today.

## James 2:16

You say, “Go in peace.

Stay warm and eat well.” Yet you give them nothing for their needs.

What good have your words done?

🍞 **Bread for a meal**

Picture handing bread to the person who needs food.

## James 2:17

Faith is like that too.

Faith that never leads to action is dead.

**Pause and answer**

What could you give the hungry person?

## How can people see faith?

## James 2:18

Someone might say, “You have faith.

I have actions.” How could you show me your faith without doing anything?

I can show you my faith by what I do.

## James 2:19

You believe there is one God.

That is good.

Even demons believe that, and they shake with fear.

Demons are evil spirits.

## James 2:20

How foolish to miss this!

Do you need proof that faith without action is useless?

**Pause and answer**

How can someone see that you trust God?

## Abraham and Rahab act on their faith

## James 2:21

Think of our ancestor Abraham.

He offered his son Isaac to God on an altar.

An altar is a place for offering gifts to God.

God counted Abraham as right with him for what Abraham did.

## James 2:22

Abraham trusted God and acted on that trust.

What Abraham did brought his faith to completion.

## James 2:23

This fulfilled the words of Scripture.

Abraham trusted God.

Because of that trust, God called Abraham righteous.

Righteous means right with God.

People called Abraham God’s friend.

## James 2:24

So you see how God counts someone as right with him.

What they do matters.

Faith alone is not enough.

## James 2:25

Think of Rahab, a woman who sold sex.

She welcomed the spies and sent them away by another road.

God counted Rahab as right with him for what she did.

## James 2:26

When the spirit leaves the body, the body is dead.

In the same way, faith without action is dead.

**Pause and answer**

What did Rahab do for the spies?
