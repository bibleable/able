# James 5 — Be patient and pray for each other

**About this chapter**

James speaks to people who have kept their workers’ pay.

Then he encourages people who are waiting or suffering.

## God hears the workers

## James 5:1

Listen, you rich people.

Weep and cry out.

Terrible trouble is coming upon you.

## James 5:2

Your riches have rotted.

Moths have eaten holes in your clothes.

## James 5:3

Even your gold and silver have corroded.

James pictures the damaged metal as a witness against you.

It will eat into your flesh like fire.

You have piled up treasure in these last days.

## James 5:4

Workers cut the grain in your fields.

You kept the pay you owed them.

That unpaid money cries out against you.

The Lord who commands heaven’s armies has heard the workers cry.

## James 5:5

On earth you have lived in luxury.

You have given yourselves every pleasure.

You are like animals growing fat on the day of slaughter.

You feast while judgment draws near.

## James 5:6

You have condemned and killed innocent people.

They did not fight back.

**Pause and answer**

Whose cries did the Lord hear?

## Wait like a farmer

## James 5:7

Brothers and sisters, wait patiently until the Lord comes.

Think of a farmer waiting for the autumn and spring rains.

The farmer waits patiently for the precious crop.

![A stalk of ripe grain.](../pictures/grain.svg)

Look at the grain the farmer waited for.

## James 5:8

You too can be patient.

Take courage, because the Lord will come soon.

## James 5:9

Brothers and sisters, stop complaining about each other.

Otherwise you too will face judgment.

Picture the judge already standing at the door.

The Lord is close.

**Pause and answer**

What does the farmer wait for?

## Keep going and speak honestly

## James 5:10

Remember the prophets who spoke for the Lord.

Look how patiently they endured suffering.

Follow their example.

## James 5:11

We call those who kept going blessed.

You have heard how Job kept going.

You know what the Lord finally did for Job.

The Lord is full of kindness and mercy.

## James 5:12

Above all, brothers and sisters, do not swear an oath.

An oath calls on something to back up your word.

Do not swear by heaven, earth, or anything else.

Say yes when you mean yes.

Say no when you mean no.

Then you will avoid judgment.

**Pause and answer**

What should you say when you mean yes?

## Pray for each other

## James 5:13

Are you suffering?

Pray to God.

Are you glad?

Sing praises to God.

## James 5:14

If you are sick, call the elders of the church.

Elders are leaders who care for the church.

Ask them to pray over you.

They should put oil on you in the Lord’s name.

## James 5:15

Prayer offered with trust in God will restore the sick person.

The Lord will raise that person up.

If the person has sinned, God will forgive them.

## James 5:16

So tell each other what you have done wrong.

Pray for each other so you may be healed.

The prayer of someone who lives rightly has great power.

**Pause and answer**

Who can a sick person call to pray with them?

## Elijah prays

## James 5:17

Elijah was a person just like us.

He prayed with all his heart for the rain to stop.

No rain fell for three and a half years.

## James 5:18

Then Elijah prayed again.

Rain fell from the sky.

Crops grew from the ground.

**Pause and answer**

What happened when Elijah prayed again?

## Help someone come back

## James 5:19

Brothers and sisters, someone among you may wander from the truth.

Another person may help them come back.

## James 5:20

Remember what it means to help someone turn from sin.

You save that person from death.

And many sins are covered over.

That means those sins are forgiven.

**Pause and answer**

How can you help someone who wanders from the truth?
