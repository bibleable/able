# James 3 — Take care with your words

**About this chapter**

James shows how a few words can cause great harm.

He describes how wise people make peace.

## A small part can guide the whole body

## James 3:1

Brothers and sisters, only a few should become teachers.

God will judge those of us who teach more strictly.

## James 3:2

All of us make many mistakes.

Anyone who never speaks wrongly would be perfect.

Such a person could control their whole body too.

## James 3:3

Think of a horse with a bit in its mouth.

A bit is a small piece attached to the reins.

With it we can guide the whole horse.

## James 3:4

Now picture a large ship in a strong wind.

The person steering turns a small part called a rudder.

That small rudder guides the whole ship wherever the person wants.

**Picture the turn**

Rudder turns → Ship changes direction

A small movement changes where the whole ship goes.

**Pause and answer**

What small part guides the ship?

## A spark can start a fire

## James 3:5

Our tongue is small too.

Yet we use it to make great boasts.

Think how one spark can set a whole forest burning.

## James 3:6

James calls the tongue a fire.

So much evil can come through what we say.

Evil words stain the whole person.

Like a spreading fire, they can ruin a whole life.

James says this fire comes from hell.

## James 3:7

People have tamed all kinds of creatures.

They still tame animals, birds, reptiles and sea creatures.

## James 3:8

Yet no one can tame the tongue.

Its evil is hard to keep still.

Hurtful words can do harm like deadly poison.

**Pause and answer**

What does James compare harmful words to?

## One mouth can speak two ways

## James 3:9

With our tongue we praise our Lord and Father.

With that same tongue we curse people.

We call down harm on people God made like himself.

## James 3:10

One mouth speaks both praise and curses.

Brothers and sisters, this should never happen.

## James 3:11

Think about a spring where water comes from the ground.

Can fresh water and salt water flow from that same spring?

## James 3:12

Brothers and sisters, can olives grow on a fig tree?

Can figs grow on a grapevine?

A salt spring cannot give fresh water either.

**Pause and answer**

Whose likeness do the people we speak about bear?

## What wisdom looks like

## James 3:13

Who among you understands how to live wisely?

Show your wisdom by living well.

Wisdom helps you do good without boasting.

## James 3:14

Perhaps you are bitter because someone else has what you want.

Perhaps you want to put yourself first.

Be honest about it.

Do not boast or cover up the truth.

## James 3:15

That kind of wisdom comes from this world.

It has nothing of God’s Spirit in it.

It belongs to evil spirits.

## James 3:16

Envy and the desire to get ahead bring confusion.

All kinds of wrongdoing follow.

## James 3:17

Wisdom from God is pure first of all.

A wise person loves peace and treats others gently.

They are willing to listen and yield.

They show mercy and do good.

They treat people fairly and mean what they say.

## James 3:18

People who make peace are like farmers planting seeds.

They work in peace.

What grows from their work is a harvest of right living.

**Pause and answer**

How would a wise person treat someone in a disagreement?
