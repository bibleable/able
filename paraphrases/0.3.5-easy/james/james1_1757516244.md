# James 1 — Listen to God and do what he says

**About this chapter**

James writes to people far from home.

He explains how trusting God changes what we do.

## James greets his readers

## James 1:1

I am James.

I serve God and the Lord Jesus Christ.

I greet the twelve tribes of Israel who live scattered far from home.

**Pause and answer**

Who is sending these greetings?

## Keep going and ask for wisdom

## James 1:2

My brothers and sisters, you face many kinds of trouble.

Even then, you have reason for great joy.

## James 1:3

Trouble puts your trust in God to the test.

You learn to keep going.

## James 1:4

Let this work continue until it is complete.

Then you will be fully grown in faith.

You will have all you need.

## James 1:5

Do you need wisdom to know what to do?

Ask God.

God gives generously to everyone who asks.

He will give you wisdom without scolding you for asking.

## James 1:6

When you ask, trust God.

Someone who doubts is like a wave at sea.

The wind pushes the water back and forth.

## James 1:7

Someone like that should expect nothing from the Lord.

## James 1:8

That person keeps choosing two different ways.

This makes every part of life unsteady.

**Pause and answer**

Who can you ask for wisdom?

## The flower and the crown

## James 1:9

A believer who has little can rejoice.

God has given that person a place of honor.

## James 1:10

A rich person can rejoice in having a lowly place.

Like a wildflower, the rich person will pass away.

## James 1:11

The sun rises, and its heat dries the plant.

The flower falls and loses its beauty.

Rich people can fade away just as quickly, even while busy with their plans.

## James 1:12

God blesses the person who keeps going through trouble.

After the test comes the crown of life.

Like a winner receiving a crown, that person receives life.

God promises this life to those who love him.

**Pause and answer**

What does God promise those who love him?

## What draws us toward sin?

## James 1:13

When you feel drawn to do wrong, do not blame God.

Evil has no power to tempt God.

God does not tempt anyone to do wrong.

## James 1:14

Our own wrong desires tempt us.

What we want can draw us away from what is right.

## James 1:15

James pictures what happens next as a birth.

Wrong desire gives birth to sin.

When sin has grown, it brings death.

**Pause and answer**

What can draw a person toward sin?

## God gives good gifts

## James 1:16

My dear brothers and sisters, do not let anyone fool you.

## James 1:17

Every good and perfect gift comes from God above.

Our Father made the lights in the sky.

Shadows move as the light changes.

Our Father always stays the same.

## James 1:18

God chose to give us new life through his true message.

James compares us to the first fruit of a crop.

We are like that first fruit among all God has made.

**Pause and answer**

How did God choose to give us new life?

## Be ready to listen

## James 1:19

My dear brothers and sisters, remember this.

Be ready to listen.

Take time before you speak.

Be slow to get angry.

![An eye and an ear.](../pictures/eye-and-ear.svg)

Point to the ear. Remember James’s words, “Be ready to listen.”

## James 1:20

Our anger does not produce the right life God wants.

## James 1:21

So put away every kind of evil.

Humbly welcome the message God has planted in you.

Like a seed taking root, his message grows in you.

That message can save you.

**Pause and answer**

What does James ask us to do before speaking?

## Put God’s message into practice

## James 1:22

Listen to God’s message and do what it says.

If you only listen, you fool yourself.

## James 1:23

Imagine hearing the message and doing nothing about it.

You are like someone looking at their face in a mirror.

## James 1:24

That person walks away.

Straight away they forget what they saw.

## James 1:25

Look carefully at God’s perfect law that brings freedom.

Keep looking and remember what you heard.

God blesses the person who does what it says.

## James 1:26

Someone may think they serve God well.

But if they let their words run wild, they fool themselves.

Their worship is worthless.

## James 1:27

Here is the worship God our Father sees as pure.

Care for children who have lost their parents.

Care for women whose husbands have died.

Help them in their trouble.

Keep the evil around you from shaping how you live.

**Pause and answer**

Who does James ask us to care for in their trouble?
