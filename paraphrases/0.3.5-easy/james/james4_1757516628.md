# James 4 — Come close to God

**About this chapter**

James asks why people fight.

He also asks how we make plans.

## What makes you fight?

## James 4:1

Why do you argue and fight?

The trouble starts with what you want.

Different desires fight for control inside you.

## James 4:2

You want things you do not have.

You kill and still long for what others have.

Yet you cannot get what you want.

You quarrel and fight.

You go without because you do not ask God.

## James 4:3

Sometimes you ask and still receive nothing.

You ask for the wrong reason.

You want to spend it all on your own pleasure.

**Pause and answer**

Why do some people receive nothing when they ask?

## Give God your whole heart

## James 4:4

You have been unfaithful to God, like someone unfaithful in marriage.

You choose to side with a world that opposes God.

That choice makes you God’s enemy.

## James 4:5

Take seriously what Scripture tells us.

God has given us his Spirit to live in us.

His Spirit longs for us to be faithful to God.

## James 4:6

Yet God gives us even more grace.

Grace is God’s freely given kindness.

Scripture says God stands against proud people.

He shows kindness to humble people.

**Pause and answer**

Who receives God’s kindness?

## Come close to God

## James 4:7

So put yourself under God’s care and obey him.

Stand against the devil.

The devil will run away from you.

## James 4:8

Come close to God.

God will come close to you.

James tells sinners to wash their hands.

This means they must stop doing wrong.

If your loyalties are divided, make your hearts pure.

Be faithful to God with your whole heart.

## James 4:9

Let yourselves feel sorrow over your sin.

Mourn and weep.

Let your laughter give way to tears.

Let your joy turn to sorrow.

## James 4:10

Be humble before the Lord.

The Lord will lift you up.

**Pause and answer**

What does God promise when you come close to him?

## God judges your neighbor

## James 4:11

Brothers and sisters, do not speak against each other.

When you condemn another believer, you condemn God’s law too.

You take the judge’s seat instead of obeying that law.

## James 4:12

God alone gives the law and judges people.

God has power to save and to destroy.

Who are you to judge your neighbor?

![A scroll representing the written law.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

Picture God’s law written on this scroll. Think about who gave that law.

**Pause and answer**

Who gives the law and judges people?

## Making plans for tomorrow

## James 4:13

Listen to the plans some of you make.

“Today or tomorrow we will travel to another city.

We will trade there for a year and make money.”

## James 4:14

You do not even know what tomorrow will bring.

Think of mist hanging in the air.

It appears for a little while, then disappears.

Your life is brief like that mist.

## James 4:15

Say instead, “If the Lord wishes, we will live.

Then we can carry out our plans.”

## James 4:16

Yet you boast about what you plan to do.

Such proud talk is wrong.

## James 4:17

Suppose you know what you ought to do.

If you leave it undone, you sin.

**Pause and answer**

Why does James compare life to mist?
