# Philemon 1 — Welcome Onesimus as you would welcome me

**About this chapter**

Paul writes from prison to his friend Philemon.

Paul asks Philemon to welcome a man named Onesimus.

## A church meets in Philemon’s home

## Philemon 1:1

I am Paul, a prisoner because I serve Christ Jesus.

Our brother Timothy joins me in writing.

Philemon, you are our dear friend and fellow worker.

## Philemon 1:2

We also greet our sister Apphia and our fellow soldier Archippus.

We greet the church that meets in your house.

**Help with these words**

Paul calls Archippus a fellow soldier because they serve Christ together.

## Philemon 1:3

May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you grace and peace.

**Pause and answer**

Where does the church meet?

## Love gives people fresh strength

## Philemon 1:4

Philemon, whenever I remember you in my prayers, I thank my God.

## Philemon 1:5

I hear how you trust the Lord Jesus.

I also hear how you love all God’s people.

## Philemon 1:6

You and I are partners because we trust Christ.

I pray that you will see all the good things we have in Christ.

May this help us work well together.

## Philemon 1:7

Brother, your love gives me joy and courage.

You have helped God’s people find fresh strength.

**Pause and answer**

What gives Paul joy when he hears about Philemon?

## Paul asks on behalf of Onesimus

## Philemon 1:8

Because I belong to Christ, I could tell you firmly what you ought to do.

## Philemon 1:9

Instead, I ask you out of love.

I, Paul, am an old man now.

I am also in prison for Christ Jesus.

## Philemon 1:10

I am asking you to help Onesimus.

While I was in chains, I became like a father to him.

**Help with these words**

Paul calls Onesimus his child because he helped him come to faith in Christ.

## Philemon 1:11

Once Onesimus was of no help to you.

Now he is a help to both you and me.

## Philemon 1:12

I am sending Onesimus back to you.

He is as dear to me as my own heart.

## Philemon 1:13

I wanted to keep him here with me.

I am in chains for telling the good news.

He could have helped me in your place.

## Philemon 1:14

But I wanted to ask you first.

I want you to choose freely to do good.

I do not want to force you.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Paul ask Philemon before keeping Onesimus with him?

## Welcome him as a brother

## Philemon 1:15

Perhaps you and Onesimus were apart for a little while so you could have him back forever.

## Philemon 1:16

Receive him now as more than a slave.

Receive him as a brother you love.

He is very dear to me.

He can be even dearer to you, both in daily life and in the Lord.

## Philemon 1:17

Philemon, if you see me as your partner, do this.

Welcome Onesimus as you would welcome me.

## Philemon 1:18

If Onesimus has wronged you or owes you anything, put it on my account.

**Help with these words**

Putting a debt on Paul’s account means that Paul agrees to pay it himself.

## Philemon 1:19

I, Paul, am writing this in my own handwriting.

I will pay you back.

Remember that you owe me your very self.

| Onesimus | Philemon | Paul |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Returns to Philemon | Receives Paul’s request to welcome Onesimus | Offers to pay whatever Onesimus owes |

## Philemon 1:20

Yes, brother, do this kindness for me in the Lord.

Give me fresh courage in Christ.

## Philemon 1:21

I write because I trust you to do what I ask.

I know you will do even more.

**Pause and answer**

Who offers to pay if Onesimus owes Philemon anything?

## A room for Paul

## Philemon 1:22

While you wait, get a guest room ready for me.

I hope God will answer your prayers and bring me back to you.

## Philemon 1:23

Epaphras sends greetings.

He shares my imprisonment because he too serves Christ Jesus.

## Philemon 1:24

Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke also send greetings.

They work alongside me.

## Philemon 1:25

May the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with you all in your hearts.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul ask Philemon to prepare?
