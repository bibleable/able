# Numbers 23 — Balaam blesses Israel

**About this chapter**

King Balak wants Balaam to curse Israel. Twice Balaam returns with a message from the LORD. Each time Balak hears words he did not want to hear.

## Seven altars on the hill

## Numbers 23:1

Balaam asked Balak to build seven altars.

He asked for seven bulls and seven rams.

## Numbers 23:2

Balak did what Balaam asked.

Together they offered one bull and one ram on each altar.

**Reading help**

A ram is a male sheep. An altar is a place where people give offerings to God.

## Numbers 23:3

Balaam told Balak, “Wait here beside your burnt offering.

Perhaps the LORD will meet me.

I will tell you whatever he shows me.” Balaam went up to a bare hilltop.

## Numbers 23:4

God met him there.

Balaam said, “I have set up seven altars.

On each one I offered a bull and a ram.”

## Numbers 23:5

The LORD gave Balaam words to say.

He told Balaam to take the message back to Balak.

## Numbers 23:6

Balaam returned.

Balak stood beside his burnt offering.

All the leaders of Moab stood with him.

**Pause and answer**

Who gave Balaam the message for Balak?

## Balaam’s first message

## Numbers 23:7

Balaam spoke the message God had given him.

“Balak, king of Moab, brought me from Aram, from the eastern mountains.

He asked me to curse Jacob and speak against Israel.

## Numbers 23:8

How can I curse people God has not cursed?

How can I speak against people the LORD has not spoken against?

## Numbers 23:9

From these high rocks I see them.

From these hills I watch them.

They live apart.

They see themselves as separate from the other nations.

## Numbers 23:10

Who can count Jacob’s people?

They are like specks of dust.

Who could count even a quarter of Israel?

May I die like those who do right.

May my end be like theirs.”

**Reading help**

Jacob and Israel are two names for the same ancestor. Here both names mean his people. The dust pictures how many people there are.

## Numbers 23:11

Balak asked Balaam, “What have you done to me?

I brought you to curse my enemies.

All you have done is bless them!”

## Numbers 23:12

Balaam answered, “I must say exactly what the LORD tells me.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did Balaam bless Israel?

## Balak tries another place

## Numbers 23:13

Balak asked Balaam to come to another place.

“You will see only the edge of their camp,” he said.

“Curse them for me from there.”

## Numbers 23:14

Balak took Balaam to the field of Zophim on top of Pisgah.

Balak built seven altars there.

On each altar he offered one bull and one ram.

## Numbers 23:15

Balaam told Balak, “Wait beside your burnt offering.

I will go over there to meet the LORD.”

## Numbers 23:16

The LORD met Balaam and gave him a message.

He told Balaam to go back and speak those words to Balak.

## Numbers 23:17

Balaam returned to the burnt offering.

Balak stood there with the leaders of Moab.

Balak asked, “What did the LORD say?”

**Pause and answer**

Where did the leaders of Moab wait?

## God keeps his promises

## Numbers 23:18

Balaam spoke the message.

“Stand up and listen, Balak, son of Zippor.

Hear what I say.

## Numbers 23:19

People may lie or change their minds.

God keeps his word.

He does what he says.

He keeps his promises.

## Numbers 23:20

God told me to bless Israel.

He has given his blessing.

I cannot change it.

**Pause and answer**

Why could Balaam never take back God’s blessing?

## The strength of a lion

## Numbers 23:21

God sees no disaster ahead for Jacob.

He sees no trouble for Israel.

The LORD their God is with them.

The shout of their King rings out among them.

## Numbers 23:22

God brought them out of Egypt with the strength of a wild ox.

## Numbers 23:23

No magic spell can work against Jacob.

No one can use divination against Israel.

People will say of Israel, ‘Look what God has done!’

## Numbers 23:24

See how the people rise like a lioness.

Like a lion, they get to their feet.

The lion eats the animal it has killed and drinks its blood before it rests.”

**Reading help**

| Picture in Balaam’s words | What it helps us understand |
| --- | --- |
| A strong wild ox | God had the strength to bring Israel out of Egypt. |
| A lion finishing its hunt | Israel would keep fighting until it won. |

## Numbers 23:25

Balak told Balaam, “Then say no curse and give no blessing!”

## Numbers 23:26

Balaam answered, “I told you I must do whatever the LORD says.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Balak ask Balaam to stop doing?

## Balak takes Balaam to Peor

## Numbers 23:27

Balak asked Balaam to come to another place.

He hoped God would let Balaam curse Israel from there.

## Numbers 23:28

Balak took Balaam to the top of Peor.

From there they could see the wasteland.

## Numbers 23:29

Balaam asked Balak to build seven altars there.

He asked for seven bulls and seven rams.

## Numbers 23:30

Balak did what Balaam asked.

He offered one bull and one ram on each altar.

**Pause and answer**

What did Balak offer on each altar at Peor?
