# Numbers 9 — Passover and the cloud over the tent

**About this chapter**

Some men want to celebrate Passover with the rest of Israel. Moses listens to their question. Later the people watch the cloud to know when to travel.

## Passover in the first month

## Numbers 9:1

The Israelites had left Egypt.

It was now the first month of the second year.

The LORD spoke to Moses in the Sinai wilderness.

## Numbers 9:2

The LORD told Israel to celebrate Passover at the set time.

**Reading help**

At Passover the Israelites remembered how the LORD had brought them out of Egypt.

## Numbers 9:3

They must celebrate on day fourteen of that month, as daylight faded.

They must follow all the rules for Passover.

## Numbers 9:4

Moses told the Israelites to celebrate Passover.

## Numbers 9:5

They celebrated in the Sinai wilderness.

They began as daylight faded on day fourteen of the first month.

They did everything the LORD had told Moses.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the people celebrate Passover?

## Some men ask Moses for help

## Numbers 9:6

Some men were unclean because of contact with a dead body.

They could not celebrate Passover that day.

That same day they came to Moses and Aaron.

**Reading help**

Here, “unclean” means the men had to wait before taking part in worship.

## Numbers 9:7

The men told Moses, “We are unclean because of a dead body.

Why must we miss bringing the LORD’s offering with everyone else at the set time?”

## Numbers 9:8

Moses answered, “Wait here.

I will find out what the LORD commands for you.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did Moses ask the men to wait?

## Passover in the second month

## Numbers 9:9

Then the LORD spoke to Moses.

## Numbers 9:10

He gave a rule for the Israelites and their descendants.

Someone might be unclean because of a dead body.

Someone else might be away on a journey.

Both could still celebrate Passover to honor the LORD.

## Numbers 9:11

They must celebrate as daylight faded on day fourteen of the second month.

They must eat the lamb with bread made without yeast.

They must also eat bitter herbs.

**Reading help**

| When the daylight faded | Who celebrated? |
| --- | --- |
| First month, day fourteen | The people able to celebrate at the usual time |
| Second month, day fourteen | Those who had been away or unclean because of a dead body |

## Numbers 9:12

They must leave none of the lamb until morning.

They must keep all its bones whole.

They must follow every Passover rule.

**Pause and answer**

When could a traveler who missed Passover celebrate it?

## One rule for everyone

## Numbers 9:13

Suppose someone was clean and had not gone on a journey.

If that person missed Passover, they must be cut off from their people.

They failed to bring the LORD’s offering at the set time.

They must answer for that sin.

**Reading help**

Here, “cut off” means the person could no longer belong to the community of Israel.

## Numbers 9:14

A foreigner living among the Israelites might want to celebrate Passover.

That person must follow the Passover rules to honor the LORD.

The same rules applied to foreigners and people born in Israel.

**Pause and answer**

Which rules did a foreigner follow at Passover?

## The cloud covers the holy tent

## Numbers 9:15

The people set up the holy tent called the Tent of the Testimony.

That day the cloud covered it.

From evening until morning the cloud above the tent looked like fire.

## Numbers 9:16

This kept happening.

By day the cloud covered the holy tent.

At night it looked like fire.

## Numbers 9:17

When the cloud lifted from the tent, the Israelites set out.

Wherever the cloud settled, they camped.

**Reading help**

| What the cloud did | What the people did |
| --- | --- |
| Lifted from the tent | Set out |
| Settled over a place | Made camp there |

## Numbers 9:18

The Israelites set out when the LORD commanded.

They camped when the LORD commanded.

They stayed in camp while the cloud stayed over the tent.

**Pause and answer**

What did the cloud look like at night?

## Waiting for the cloud to lift

## Numbers 9:19

Sometimes the cloud stayed over the tent for many days.

The people obeyed the LORD and stayed there.

## Numbers 9:20

Sometimes the cloud stayed only a few days.

The people camped at the LORD’s command.

They set out at his command too.

## Numbers 9:21

Sometimes the cloud stayed only from evening until morning.

When it lifted in the morning, they set out.

Day or night, they set out when the cloud lifted.

## Numbers 9:22

The cloud might stay two days, a month or longer.

The Israelites stayed as long as it covered the tent.

When it lifted, they set out.

## Numbers 9:23

The LORD told them when to camp and when to go.

The people obeyed the instructions the LORD gave through Moses.

**Pause and answer**

What did the people do if the cloud stayed a whole month?
