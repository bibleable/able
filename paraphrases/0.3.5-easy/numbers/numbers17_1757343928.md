# Numbers 17 — Aaron’s staff grows almonds

**About this chapter**

Twelve leaders give Moses their staffs. When Moses returns to the tent the next day, he finds that one staff has changed.

## One staff for each tribe

## Numbers 17:1

The LORD spoke to Moses.

## Numbers 17:2

He told Moses to collect twelve wooden staffs from Israel.

Each tribe’s leader would give one staff.

Moses must write each leader’s name on his staff.

**Reading help**

A staff is a long stick a person can hold. The name on each staff showed whose it was.

## Numbers 17:3

Moses must write Aaron’s name on Levi’s staff.

Each tribe had one staff for its leader.

## Numbers 17:4

Moses must put the staffs inside the Tent of Meeting.

He must lay them before the record of God’s agreement with Israel.

That was where the LORD met with Moses.

## Numbers 17:5

The LORD said, “The staff of the man I choose will sprout.

This will put an end to Israel’s constant complaints against you.”

**Pause and answer**

How would Moses know which leader the LORD had chosen?

## Moses brings the staffs out

## Numbers 17:6

Moses spoke to the people.

Each leader gave him one staff.

There were twelve staffs in all, including Aaron’s.

## Numbers 17:7

Moses placed the staffs before the LORD inside the holy tent.

## Numbers 17:8

The next day Moses went into the tent.

Aaron’s staff, the one for Levi’s tribe, had sprouted.

It had grown buds and flowers.

It had even produced almonds.

**Follow the growth**

Sprout → buds → flowers → almonds

A sprout is new growth. A flower opens from a bud. An almond is a nut.

## Numbers 17:9

Moses brought all the staffs out from before the LORD.

All the Israelites saw them.

Each leader took back his own staff.

**Pause and answer**

What did Moses find on Aaron’s staff the next day?

## A warning for Israel

## Numbers 17:10

The LORD told Moses to return Aaron’s staff to its place before the record of the agreement.

It would stay there as a warning to anyone who rebelled.

The LORD wanted the people to stop complaining against him so they would live.

## Numbers 17:11

Moses did what the LORD told him.

## Numbers 17:12

The Israelites cried out to Moses, “We are dying!

We are lost!

All of us are lost!”

## Numbers 17:13

They said, “Anyone who comes near the LORD’s holy tent will die.

Are we all going to die?”

**Pause and answer**

Why did the LORD want Moses to keep Aaron’s staff?
