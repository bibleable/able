# Numbers 24 — Balaam sees Israel’s tents and a future king

**About this chapter**

Balak listens as Balaam blesses Israel a third time. Before Balaam goes home, he speaks about a king who will come from Israel.

## Balaam looks at the camp

## Numbers 24:1

Balaam saw that the LORD wanted to bless Israel.

Before, Balaam had looked for signs called omens.

This time he turned toward the wilderness without looking for omens.

## Numbers 24:2

Balaam looked up.

He saw the Israelites camping in their tribes.

Then the Spirit of God came upon him.

![The groups of tribes camp on four sides of the holy tent.](../pictures/four-camps-around-tent.svg)

Balaam could see how Israel’s people had camped in their tribes.

## Numbers 24:3

Balaam began to speak a message from God.

He said, “I am Balaam, son of Beor.

My eyes are open.

## Numbers 24:4

I hear God’s words.

I see what God Almighty shows me.

I bow down with my eyes wide open.

**Reading help**

A vision is something God shows someone. Here, open eyes picture Balaam receiving what God shows him.

**Pause and answer**

What did Balaam see when he looked toward the wilderness?

## A blessing for Israel

## Numbers 24:5

People of Jacob, how lovely your tents are!

People of Israel, how lovely your homes are!

## Numbers 24:6

Your tents spread out like groups of palm trees.

They are like gardens beside a stream.

They are like aloe trees the LORD has planted.

They are like cedar trees growing beside water.

## Numbers 24:7

Water will pour from Israel’s buckets.

There will be plenty of water for Israel’s seed.

Israel’s king will be greater than Agag.

His kingdom will rise high.

## Numbers 24:8

God brought Israel out of Egypt with the strength of a wild ox.

Israel will devour enemy nations, crush their bones and strike them with arrows.

## Numbers 24:9

Israel lies down like a lion or a lioness.

Who would dare wake a lion?

May those who bless you receive a blessing.

May those who curse you receive a curse.”

**Reading help**

| What Balaam pictures | What the picture shows |
| --- | --- |
| Gardens beside a stream | Israel’s camp is lovely and has what it needs to grow. |
| A resting lion | Israel is strong. Its enemies fear disturbing it. |

**Pause and answer**

What animal did Balaam picture resting without fear?

## Balak sends Balaam home

## Numbers 24:10

Balak struck his hands together in anger.

He told Balaam, “I called you to curse my enemies.

Three times you have blessed them!”

## Numbers 24:11

Balak said, “Go home at once!

I promised you a great reward.

The LORD has kept you from receiving it.”

## Numbers 24:12

Balaam reminded Balak what he had told the king’s messengers.

## Numbers 24:13

Balak could give him a house full of silver and gold.

Even then Balaam could do nothing on his own, good or bad, beyond the LORD’s command.

He had to speak the words the LORD gave him.

## Numbers 24:14

Balaam told Balak, “I am going home to my people.

First, let me warn you what Israel will do to your people in days to come.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did Balak become angry with Balaam?

## A ruler will come from Israel

## Numbers 24:15

Balaam spoke another message.

“I am Balaam, son of Beor.

My eyes are open.

## Numbers 24:16

I hear God’s words.

The Most High gives me knowledge.

God Almighty shows me a vision.

I bow down with my eyes wide open.

## Numbers 24:17

I see someone who is still far off in time.

A star will rise from Jacob.

A scepter will rise from Israel.

This ruler will crush the heads of Moab’s people.

He will strike down all the sons of Sheth.

**Reading help**

Jacob and Israel both name the people descended from Jacob. Balaam pictures a coming king as a rising star. A scepter is a staff a king holds as a sign of his right to rule.

## Numbers 24:18

He will take Edom and the land of his enemy Seir.

Israel will act bravely.

## Numbers 24:19

A ruler will come from Jacob’s people.

He will destroy those who survive in the city.”

**Pause and answer**

What does the scepter tell us about the person Balaam sees?

## Messages about other nations

## Numbers 24:20

Balaam saw Amalek and spoke about its people.

“Amalek was first among the nations.

In the end, Amalek will perish.”

## Numbers 24:21

Then Balaam saw the Kenites.

He told them, “Your home seems safe, like a nest high on a cliff.

## Numbers 24:22

Yet Kain will fall when Asshur takes you away as captives.”

**Reading help**

A nest on a cliff pictures a home that is hard for enemies to reach.

## Numbers 24:23

Balaam spoke again.

“Who can live unless God has decided they will live?

## Numbers 24:24

Ships will come from the shores of Cyprus.

The people on those ships will overpower Asshur and Eber.

In the end, the people from the ships will also perish forever.”

## Numbers 24:25

Balaam got up and went back to his homeland.

Balak also went on his way.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Balaam go after speaking these messages?
