# Numbers 26 — Israel counts a new generation

**About this chapter**

Moses and Eleazar count Israel’s men by family. The count will help determine each tribe’s share of land. The Levites have a separate count.

## A count beside the Jordan

## Numbers 26:1

The plague ended.

Then the LORD spoke to Moses and to Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest.

## Numbers 26:2

They must count the people by family.

They must count every man aged twenty or older who could serve in Israel’s army.

## Numbers 26:3

Moses and Eleazar gave the order on the plains of Moab.

They were beside the Jordan River, across from Jericho.

## Numbers 26:4

They ordered a count of men aged twenty or older.

The LORD had told Moses to do this.

Here are the families of the Israelites who came out of Egypt.

**Reading help**

A census is a count of people. A clan is a group of related families within a tribe.

## How Israel will share the land

## Numbers 26:52

Then the LORD spoke to Moses.

## Numbers 26:53

The tribes would receive land to keep as their own.

The number of names on each list would determine its share.

## Numbers 26:54

A larger tribe would receive more land.

A smaller tribe would receive less.

Each share depended on how many men the leaders counted.

## Numbers 26:55

The people must draw lots to divide the land.

Each tribe would receive land under the name of its ancestor.

**Reading help**

Drawing lots meant using marked pieces to assign each tribe its place.

## Numbers 26:56

Both large and small tribes must receive their land by lot.

**Pause and answer**

## A separate count for Levi

## Numbers 26:57

The leaders also counted Levi’s clans.

The Gershonite clan came from Gershon.

The Kohathite clan came from Kohath.

The Merarite clan came from Merari.

## Numbers 26:58

The Levites included the Libnite, Hebronite and Mahlite clans.

They also included the Mushite and Korahite clans.

Kohath was Amram’s father.

## Numbers 26:59

Amram married Jochebed.

She was Levi’s daughter, born to him in Egypt.

Amram and Jochebed had three children.

They were Aaron, Moses and their sister Miriam.

## Numbers 26:60

Aaron’s sons were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

## Numbers 26:61

Nadab and Abihu offered fire the LORD had not commanded.

They died before the LORD.

## Numbers 26:62

The leaders counted 23,000 Levite boys and men aged one month or older.

They counted them separately.

The Levites would receive no share when Israel divided the land among the tribes.
