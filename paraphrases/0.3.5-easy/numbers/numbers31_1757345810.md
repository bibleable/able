# Numbers 31 — Israel goes to war against Midian

**About this chapter**

Israel fights Midian. Moses counts the people and animals the soldiers took.

## The LORD speaks to Moses

## Numbers 31:1

The LORD spoke to Moses.

## Numbers 31:2

The LORD told Moses to punish Midian for what Midian had done to Israel.

After that, Moses would die and join his people who had died.

## Numbers 31:3

Moses told the Israelites to get some men ready for war.

These men would carry out the LORD’s punishment against Midian.

## Numbers 31:4

Moses asked each tribe of Israel to send 1,000 men.

## Numbers 31:5

Each tribe sent 1,000 men.

In all, 12,000 men carried weapons for the war.

## Numbers 31:6

Moses sent these men with Phinehas.

Phinehas was the son of Eleazar the priest.

Phinehas took sacred objects from the place of worship.

He also took trumpets to give signals.

## The soldiers return with prisoners

## Numbers 31:7

The Israelites fought Midian as the LORD had told Moses.

The Israelites killed all the Midianite men they fought.

## Numbers 31:8

They killed five kings of Midian.

Their names were Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba.

The Israelites also killed Balaam with a sword.

Balaam was Beor’s son.

## Numbers 31:9

The Israelites took the Midianite women and children prisoner.

They took all the herds, flocks, and belongings.

## Numbers 31:10

They burned all the towns and camps where the Midianites lived.

## Numbers 31:11

They took away the prisoners, the animals, and the belongings.

## Numbers 31:12

The soldiers brought them to Moses, Eleazar, and the Israelites.

Israel’s camp stood on the plains of Moab.

It was beside the Jordan River, across from Jericho.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the soldiers bring the prisoners?

## Moses meets the officers

## Numbers 31:13

Moses and Eleazar the priest went outside the camp.

All the leaders of Israel went with them to meet the soldiers.

## Numbers 31:14

Moses was angry with the officers returning from battle.

Some officers led 1,000 soldiers.

Others led 100.

## Numbers 31:15

Moses asked the officers, “Did you let all the women live?”

## Numbers 31:16

Moses said the women had followed Balaam’s advice at Peor.

There they had led Israel’s men away from the LORD.

Because of this, a deadly sickness had struck the LORD’s people.

## Numbers 31:17

Moses ordered the officers to kill all the boys.

He also ordered them to kill every woman who had had sex with a man.

## Numbers 31:18

Moses told the soldiers to keep the girls alive for themselves if the girls had never had sex with a man.

## Seven days outside the camp

## Numbers 31:19

Moses spoke to everyone who had killed someone or touched a dead person.

They had to stay outside the camp for seven days.

On days three and seven, they had to make themselves and their prisoners clean for worship.

## Numbers 31:20

They also had to make their clothes clean for worship.

This included everything made from leather, goats’ hair, or wood.

## Numbers 31:21

Then Eleazar the priest spoke to the soldiers.

He told them the rule the LORD had given Moses.

## Numbers 31:22

Eleazar named six metals.

They were gold, silver, bronze, iron, tin, and lead.

## Numbers 31:23

The soldiers had to pass things that could survive fire through fire.

Then they had to use special water to make them clean for worship.

Things that would burn had to go through water.

## Numbers 31:24

On day seven, the soldiers had to wash their clothes.

Then they would be clean for worship.

Then they could enter the camp.

## Two equal shares

## Numbers 31:25

The LORD spoke to Moses again.

## Numbers 31:26

Moses, Eleazar, and the family leaders had to count all the prisoners and animals.

## Numbers 31:27

They had to give half to the soldiers who fought.

The other half would go to the rest of Israel.

## Numbers 31:28

The soldiers had to set aside a share for the LORD.

From every 500 people, they had to set aside one person.

The same rule applied to cattle, donkeys, and sheep.

## Numbers 31:29

The soldiers’ share for the LORD went to Eleazar the priest.

It was an offering to the LORD.

## Numbers 31:30

From the other Israelites’ half, one out of every 50 went to the Levites.

This applied to people, cattle, donkeys, sheep, and other animals.

The Levites cared for the LORD’s tent of worship.

## Numbers 31:31

Moses and Eleazar did what the LORD had told Moses.

**Pause and answer**

Who cared for the LORD’s tent of worship?

## Moses and Eleazar count the totals

## Numbers 31:32

They counted what remained of everything the soldiers had taken.

There were 675,000 sheep.

## Numbers 31:33

There were 72,000 cattle.

## Numbers 31:34

There were 61,000 donkeys.

## Numbers 31:35

They counted 32,000 women who had never had sex with a man.

## The soldiers’ half

## Numbers 31:36

The soldiers’ half included 337,500 sheep.

## Numbers 31:37

Of those sheep, 675 were for the LORD.

## Numbers 31:38

The soldiers’ half included 36,000 cattle.

Of those cattle, 72 were for the LORD.

## Numbers 31:39

The soldiers’ half included 30,500 donkeys.

Of those donkeys, 61 were for the LORD.

## Numbers 31:40

The soldiers’ half included 16,000 people.

Of those people, 32 were for the LORD.

## Numbers 31:41

Moses gave the LORD’s share to Eleazar the priest.

This was the offering the LORD had told Moses to give.

## The other Israelites’ half

## Numbers 31:42

Moses had set aside the other half for the rest of Israel.

## Numbers 31:43

Their half included 337,500 sheep.

## Numbers 31:44

Their half included 36,000 cattle.

## Numbers 31:45

Their half included 30,500 donkeys.

## Numbers 31:46

Their half included 16,000 people.

## Numbers 31:47

From this half, Moses took one out of every 50 people and animals.

Moses gave them to the Levites as the LORD had commanded.

The Levites cared for the LORD’s tent of worship.

## The officers bring gold

## Numbers 31:48

The officers came to Moses.

These were the men who led groups of 1,000 or 100 soldiers.

## Numbers 31:49

The officers told Moses they had counted their soldiers.

Every soldier was there.

**Pause and answer**

How many soldiers were missing?

## Numbers 31:50

So the officers brought the LORD gold things they had taken.

They brought bands for their arms, bracelets, rings, earrings, and necklaces.

They offered the gold to make atonement for themselves before the LORD.

**Reading help**

To make atonement means to deal with guilt so people can have peace with God.

## Numbers 31:51

Moses and Eleazar accepted all these gold objects from the officers.

## Numbers 31:52

Together, the officers’ gold offering weighed 16,750 shekels.

A shekel was a measure of weight.

## Numbers 31:53

Each soldier had taken things from the battle for himself.

## Numbers 31:54

Moses and Eleazar took the officers’ gold into the Tent of Meeting.

The gold would serve as a reminder of Israel before the LORD.
