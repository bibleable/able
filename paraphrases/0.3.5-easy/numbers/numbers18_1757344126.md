# Numbers 18 — Shares for the priests and the Levites

**About this chapter**

Aaron’s family and the other Levites have work at the holy tent. The LORD tells them which gifts they may keep and which share they must give.

## Who cares for the holy place?

## Numbers 18:1

The LORD spoke to Aaron.

Aaron, his sons and his father’s family must answer for wrongs connected with the holy place.

Aaron and his sons alone must answer for wrongs connected with their work as priests.

## Numbers 18:2

Aaron was to bring his relatives from Levi’s tribe.

They would help Aaron and his sons at the tent that held the record of God’s agreement.

## Numbers 18:3

The other Levites would help with the work of the tent.

They must stay away from the holy furnishings and the altar.

If they came near, both they and the priests would die.

## Numbers 18:4

The Levites would join Aaron in caring for the Tent of Meeting.

No outsider could come near the priests there.

**Pause and answer**

Who helped Aaron and his sons at the tent?

## The priests’ work

## Numbers 18:5

Aaron and his sons must care for the holy place and the altar.

Then the LORD’s anger would not fall on Israel again.

## Numbers 18:6

The LORD himself chose the other Levites from among Israel.

He gave them to Aaron as helpers.

They belonged to the LORD and served at the tent.

## Numbers 18:7

Only Aaron and his sons could serve at the altar and behind the curtain.

The LORD gave them this work as priests.

Under this law, an outsider who approached the holy place faced death.

**Pause and answer**

Who could serve behind the curtain?

## Food from the offerings

## Numbers 18:8

The LORD put Aaron in charge of the offerings.

He gave Aaron and his sons a share of Israel’s holy gifts.

This was a lasting rule.

## Numbers 18:9

The priests received the part of the most holy offerings kept back from the fire.

This included grain offerings, sin offerings and guilt offerings.

That share belonged to Aaron and his sons.

## Numbers 18:10

The men in the priestly family could eat this share.

They must treat it as most holy.

## Numbers 18:11

Israel also brought gifts that the priests waved before the LORD.

These gifts belonged to Aaron, his sons and his daughters.

Anyone in Aaron’s household who was clean for worship could eat them.

This rule would last.

**Pause and answer**

Who in Aaron’s household could eat the wave offerings?

## The first of the harvest and the firstborn

## Numbers 18:12

The Israelites gave the LORD the first of their harvest.

The LORD gave Aaron their freshest olive oil and their finest new wine and grain.

## Numbers 18:13

The first of every crop they brought to the LORD belonged to Aaron.

Anyone in his household who was clean for worship could eat it.

## Numbers 18:14

Everything in Israel given to the LORD for good belonged to Aaron.

## Numbers 18:15

The firstborn offered to the LORD belonged to Aaron, whether human or animal.

But each firstborn son must be bought back.

So must each firstborn male animal that Israel’s law called unclean.

**Reading help**

To redeem means to buy back. The next verse gives the weight of silver for the payment.

## Numbers 18:16

At one month old, the price was five shekels of silver.

The price used the weight called the sanctuary shekel.

Twenty smaller weights called gerahs made one shekel.

**Pause and answer**

What was the price for buying back a firstborn son?

## A lasting promise to Aaron’s family

## Numbers 18:17

A firstborn calf, lamb or young goat was holy.

No one could buy it back.

The priests splashed its blood on the altar.

They burned the fat as a food offering.

The smell pleased the LORD.

## Numbers 18:18

The meat belonged to Aaron.

So did the breast and right thigh from the offering waved before the LORD.

## Numbers 18:19

The LORD gave these holy offerings to Aaron and his sons and daughters.

He called this a lasting covenant of salt.

This promise before the LORD would hold for Aaron and his descendants.

**Reading help**

A covenant is a lasting agreement. The words “covenant of salt” here remind the family that the promise will last.

## Numbers 18:20

The LORD told Aaron, “You will receive no share when Israel divides the land.

I am your share.

I am what you inherit among the Israelites.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the LORD call his promise to Aaron’s family?

## The Levites receive Israel’s tithes

## Numbers 18:21

The LORD gave Israel’s tithes to the Levites as their share.

These gifts paid them for their work at the Tent of Meeting.

**Reading help**

A tithe is one tenth. That means one part out of ten equal parts.

## Numbers 18:22

The other Israelites must stay away from the Tent of Meeting.

Coming near would bring guilt and death.

## Numbers 18:23

The Levites must do the work at the tent.

They must answer for wrongs in that work.

This rule was for every generation.

The Levites would receive no share when Israel divided the land.

## Numbers 18:24

The Israelites presented their tithes to the LORD.

The LORD gave those tithes to the Levites as their share.

That was why they received no share of land.

**Pause and answer**

How did the Levites receive pay for their work?

## The Levites give a tenth

## Numbers 18:25

Then the LORD spoke to Moses.

## Numbers 18:26

Moses was to tell the Levites what to do with the tithes they received.

The LORD had given them those tithes as their share.

They must give one tenth of that share to the LORD.

**Picture the Levites’ share**

The dark circle is the one basket they give to the LORD. The other nine are for their households.

## Numbers 18:27

The LORD would count their gift as grain from the threshing floor or juice from the winepress.

**Reading help**

People separated grain from its stalks on a threshing floor. They pressed grapes in a winepress to get the juice.

## Numbers 18:28

The Levites must give the LORD a share of all the tithes they received.

They must bring that share to Aaron the priest.

## Numbers 18:29

From every gift they must choose the best part.

That part was holy and belonged to the LORD.

**Pause and answer**

To whom did the Levites bring the LORD’s share?

## Food for the workers and their families

## Numbers 18:30

Moses must tell the Levites what happened when they gave the best part.

The LORD would count what remained as their own harvest of grain or grapes.

## Numbers 18:31

The Levites and their households could eat the rest anywhere.

It was their pay for working at the Tent of Meeting.

## Numbers 18:32

Once they gave the best part, they could use the rest without guilt.

They must treat Israel’s holy gifts with respect.

If they made those gifts unclean, they would die.

**Pause and answer**

Where could the Levites’ families eat their share?
