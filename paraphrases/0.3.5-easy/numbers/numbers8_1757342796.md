# Numbers 8 — Seven lamps and the Levites’ work

**About this chapter**

Aaron sets up the lamps. Then Israel gathers at the holy tent. The people present the Levites to the LORD for service.

## Aaron turns the lamps

## Numbers 8:1

The LORD spoke to Moses.

## Numbers 8:2

He told Moses to give Aaron these instructions.

Aaron was to set up seven lamps.

Their light must fall in front of the lampstand.

![Three branches on each side of a central stem give seven places for lamps.](../pictures/six-branches-of-lampstand.svg)

Count the six branches and the center. Aaron placed the lamps so they lit the area in front.

## Numbers 8:3

Aaron turned the lamps to face the front.

He did what the LORD had told Moses.

## Numbers 8:4

The maker hammered gold into the shape of the lampstand.

The base and the flowers were all hammered gold.

The maker followed the pattern the LORD had shown Moses.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the seven lamps shine?

## The Levites prepare for worship

## Numbers 8:5

The LORD spoke to Moses again.

## Numbers 8:6

Moses was to bring the Levites out from among the Israelites.

He was to make them clean for worship.

## Numbers 8:7

Moses was to sprinkle the Levites with water set apart for cleansing.

The Levites were to shave their whole bodies.

They were to wash their clothes.

This was how they would make themselves clean.

## Numbers 8:8

The Levites were to bring a young bull.

They were to bring fine flour mixed with oil as its grain offering.

Moses was to bring a second young bull for a sin offering.

**Pause and answer**

What did the Levites mix with the fine flour?

## Israel presents the Levites

## Numbers 8:9

Moses was to bring the Levites to the front of the Tent of Meeting.

He was to call all Israel together.

## Numbers 8:10

Moses was to present the Levites before the LORD.

The people of Israel would lay their hands on the Levites.

## Numbers 8:11

Aaron would present the Levites to the LORD as a wave offering from Israel.

Then the Levites could serve the LORD.

**Reading help**

Here the wave offering is Israel’s gift of people for service. The Levites stand before the LORD ready to work.

## Numbers 8:12

The Levites would lay their hands on the bulls’ heads.

They would offer one bull to the LORD as a sin offering.

The other would be a burnt offering.

These offerings would make atonement for the Levites.

**Reading help**

To make atonement here means to deal with sin so the Levites can serve before God.

## Numbers 8:13

Moses was to have the Levites stand before Aaron and his sons.

Then Moses was to present the Levites to the LORD as a wave offering.

## Numbers 8:14

This would set the Levites apart from the rest of Israel.

The Levites would belong to the LORD.

## Numbers 8:15

Once clean and presented to the LORD as a wave offering, the Levites could begin their work at the Tent of Meeting.

**Pause and answer**

Who laid their hands on the heads of the bulls?

## The Levites take the place of the firstborn

## Numbers 8:16

The LORD said the Levites belonged entirely to him.

He had taken them in place of Israel’s firstborn sons.

## Numbers 8:17

Every firstborn male in Israel belonged to the LORD.

This included both people and animals.

The LORD had set them apart for himself when he struck down Egypt’s firstborn.

## Numbers 8:18

Now the LORD had taken the Levites in place of Israel’s firstborn sons.

## Numbers 8:19

The LORD gave the Levites to Aaron and his sons.

The Levites would serve at the tent on Israel’s behalf.

They would make atonement for the people.

Then no plague would strike when the Israelites came near the holy place.

**Reading help**

A plague is a sickness or disaster that strikes many people.

**Pause and answer**

Whose place did the Levites take?

## The Levites begin their work

## Numbers 8:20

Moses, Aaron and everyone gathered there did what the LORD had told Moses to do for the Levites.

## Numbers 8:21

The Levites made themselves clean and washed their clothes.

Aaron presented them to the LORD as a wave offering.

Aaron made atonement for them so they could be clean.

## Numbers 8:22

Then the Levites began their work at the Tent of Meeting.

Aaron and his sons were there.

The people had followed the LORD’s instructions to Moses about the Levites.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the Levites begin their work?

## When the workers grow older

## Numbers 8:23

The LORD spoke to Moses again.

## Numbers 8:24

He told Moses when Levite men could begin their duties.

They could start work at the Tent of Meeting at age twenty-five.

## Numbers 8:25

At age fifty they had to retire.

Their regular work at the tent ended.

## Numbers 8:26

They could still help the other Levites with their duties at the tent.

The other Levites now had responsibility for the work.

Moses was to assign the Levites their work in this way.

**Reading help**

| Age | Work at the tent |
| --- | --- |
| From twenty-five until fifty | Serve as a worker. |
| Fifty and older | Retire from regular work. Help the other Levites. |

How could a Levite still help after turning fifty?
