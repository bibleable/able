# Numbers 32 — A promise at the Jordan River

**About this chapter**

Two tribes want land east of the Jordan River. Moses asks how they will help the rest of Israel.

## Two tribes ask for land

## Numbers 32:1

The tribes of Reuben and Gad had many animals.

They looked over the lands of Jazer and Gilead.

Their herds and flocks could feed well there.

## Numbers 32:2

So people from Gad and Reuben came to Moses.

Eleazar the priest and Israel’s leaders were there too.

## Numbers 32:3

The people of Gad and Reuben named these places.

They named Ataroth, Dibon, Jazer, Nimrah, and Heshbon.

They also named Elealeh, Sebam, Nebo, and Beon.

## Numbers 32:4

They said the LORD had won this land for Israel.

It was good land for animals.

Both tribes had animals to feed.

## Numbers 32:5

They asked Moses for a favor.

They wanted this land for their own.

They asked to stay on this side of the Jordan River.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Gad and Reuben want this land?

## Moses remembers the valley

## Numbers 32:6

Moses asked them a question.

Would the other tribes go to war while Gad and Reuben stayed behind?

## Numbers 32:7

Moses asked why they were making the Israelites lose courage.

The LORD had given Israel land across the river.

## Numbers 32:8

Moses reminded them what their fathers had done.

Moses had sent their fathers from Kadesh-barnea to explore the land.

## Numbers 32:9

Their fathers went to the Valley of Eshcol.

They saw the land the LORD had given Israel.

But their words made Israel lose courage to enter it.

## Only two men would see the land

## Numbers 32:10

The LORD was angry that day.

The LORD made a solemn promise.

## Numbers 32:11

The LORD had promised land to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

The men who left Egypt at age 20 or older would never see it.

They had failed to follow the LORD with all their hearts.

## Numbers 32:12

The LORD named two men who would see it.

One was Caleb, son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite.

The other was Joshua, son of Nun.

Both men had followed the LORD with all their hearts.

**Reading help**

To follow the LORD with all your heart means to trust and obey him fully.

## Numbers 32:13

The LORD was angry with Israel.

He made the people wander in the wilderness for 40 years.

By then, all those who had done wrong had died.

## Moses warns the two tribes

## Numbers 32:14

Moses told Gad and Reuben they were sinning like their fathers.

They were making the LORD even angrier with Israel.

## Numbers 32:15

Moses warned them about turning away from the LORD.

The LORD would leave Israel in the wilderness again.

Gad and Reuben would bring ruin on the whole people.

## The two tribes make a promise

## Numbers 32:16

The people of Gad and Reuben came closer to Moses.

They wanted to build pens here for their flocks.

They also wanted to build towns for their children.

![A sheep pen with an open gate](../pictures/sheep-pen.svg)

A pen is a fenced place for animals.

## Numbers 32:17

Their men would go ahead of Israel, ready to fight.

They would help the Israelites reach their land.

Their children would stay in towns with strong walls.

The walls would protect them from people living nearby.

## Numbers 32:18

The men promised to wait before coming home.

First, each Israelite must receive a share of land.

**Pause and answer**

When did the men promise to come home?

## Numbers 32:19

Gad and Reuben would take no land across the river.

Their own share lay east of the Jordan.

## Moses tells them when they can return

## Numbers 32:20

Moses answered the two tribes.

They must keep their promise and get ready for battle before the LORD.

## Numbers 32:21

Every one of their fighting men must cross the Jordan before the LORD.

They must stay until the LORD drove out his enemies.

## Numbers 32:22

Once the LORD had brought the land under Israel’s control, the men could go home.

They would have kept their promise to the LORD and Israel.

The land east of the river would be theirs before the LORD.

## Numbers 32:23

If they broke their promise, they would sin against the LORD.

Moses warned them that their sin would find them out.

**Reading help**

“Your sin will find you out” means you cannot escape the results of doing wrong.

## Numbers 32:24

Moses told them to build towns for their children.

They could build pens for their flocks.

But they must do what they had promised.

## The families will stay in Gilead

## Numbers 32:25

Gad and Reuben answered Moses.

They would do just what Moses had told them.

## Numbers 32:26

Their children and wives would stay in the towns of Gilead.

All their animals would stay there too.

## Numbers 32:27

But every man ready for battle would cross the river.

They would fight before the LORD, as Moses had said.

## Moses tells the leaders what to do

## Numbers 32:28

Moses gave instructions to Eleazar the priest and Joshua, son of Nun.

He also spoke to the family leaders of Israel’s tribes.

## Numbers 32:29

Moses said all the fighting men of Gad and Reuben must cross with Israel.

They must go ready for battle before the LORD.

When Israel had control of the land, the leaders must give those tribes Gilead.

## Numbers 32:30

What if the men did not cross to fight?

Then Gad and Reuben must take land in Canaan with the other tribes.

**Pause and answer**

Where would the two tribes receive land if their men stayed behind?

## Numbers 32:31

Gad and Reuben answered that they would obey the LORD.

## Numbers 32:32

Their men would cross into Canaan ready for battle before the LORD.

That way, they could keep their land east of the Jordan.

## The tribes receive towns and land

## Numbers 32:33

Moses gave land to Gad, Reuben, and half the tribe of Manasseh.

Manasseh was Joseph’s son.

One part had belonged to Sihon, king of the Amorites.

Another had belonged to Og, king of Bashan.

The gift included the towns and the land around them.

## Numbers 32:34

The people of Gad built up Dibon, Ataroth, and Aroer.

## Numbers 32:35

They also built up Atroth-shophan, Jazer, and Jogbehah.

## Numbers 32:36

They built up Beth-nimrah and Beth-haran too.

Gad made all these towns strong for protection.

The people also built pens for their flocks.

## Numbers 32:37

The people of Reuben built up Heshbon, Elealeh, and Kiriathaim.

## Numbers 32:38

They also rebuilt Nebo, Baal-meon, and Sibmah.

Reuben gave new names to the towns they rebuilt, including Nebo and Baal-meon.

## Families from Manasseh settle in Gilead

## Numbers 32:39

Machir was Manasseh’s son.

Machir’s descendants went to Gilead and took the land.

They drove out the Amorites who lived there.

## Numbers 32:40

So Moses gave Gilead to Machir’s family.

The family settled there.

## Numbers 32:41

Jair also came from Manasseh’s family.

Jair took villages there and named them Havvoth-jair.

## Numbers 32:42

Nobah took the town of Kenath and its villages.

He named the place Nobah after himself.
