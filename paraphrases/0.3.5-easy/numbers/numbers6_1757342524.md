# Numbers 6 — The Nazirite promise and a blessing for Israel

**About this chapter**

A Nazirite makes a promise for a time set apart for God. Later, the LORD gives the priests words to bless Israel.

## A promise to the LORD

## Numbers 6:1

The LORD spoke to Moses.

## Numbers 6:2

Moses was to tell Israel about a special promise called a vow.

A man or woman could make this promise to the LORD.

For that time, the person was called a Nazirite.

## Numbers 6:3

A Nazirite gave up wine and strong drink.

This included vinegar made from either drink.

A Nazirite also gave up grape juice, fresh grapes and raisins.

## Numbers 6:4

While the promise lasted, the Nazirite ate nothing from a grapevine.

Even the seeds and skins were off limits.

## Numbers 6:5

No razor was to touch the Nazirite’s head.

The Nazirite let the hair grow long.

The person stayed holy until the promised time ended.

## What the long hair showed

## Numbers 6:6

Throughout that time, the Nazirite had to stay away from dead bodies.

## Numbers 6:7

This held even if a parent, brother or sister died.

Going near the body would make the Nazirite unclean.

The long hair showed that this person belonged to God in a special way.

## Numbers 6:8

Throughout the promised time, the Nazirite was holy to the LORD.

**Reading help**

Holy means set apart for God.

## When someone died nearby

## Numbers 6:9

Someone might die suddenly beside the Nazirite.

This would make the hair set apart for God unclean.

The Nazirite shaved it off on day seven.

That was the day of cleansing.

## Numbers 6:10

On day eight, the Nazirite brought two turtledoves or two young pigeons.

The priest received them at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.

## Numbers 6:11

The priest gave one bird as a sin offering.

He gave the other as a burnt offering.

In this way he made atonement for the Nazirite’s sin of being near the dead body.

That day the Nazirite set apart their head for God again.

## Numbers 6:12

The Nazirite began the promised time again for the LORD.

The Nazirite brought a year-old male lamb as a guilt offering.

The earlier days no longer counted because the time set apart had been interrupted by uncleanness.

## Gifts at the end of the promise

## Numbers 6:13

When the promised time ended, someone brought the Nazirite to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

## Numbers 6:14

The Nazirite brought three animals to the LORD.

A year-old male lamb was for a burnt offering.

A year-old female lamb was for a sin offering.

A ram was for a peace offering.

All three animals had to be free of defects.

**Reading help**

A ram is an adult male sheep.

## Numbers 6:15

The Nazirite brought grain offerings and drink offerings too.

A basket held cakes and thin wafers, all made without yeast.

The cakes contained fine flour mixed with oil.

The wafers had oil spread on them.

## Numbers 6:16

The priest presented all these gifts before the LORD.

He gave the sin offering and the burnt offering.

## Numbers 6:17

The priest offered the ram to the LORD as a peace offering.

With it he presented the basket of bread made without yeast.

He also gave the grain and drink offerings.

## The Nazirite shaves at the tent entrance

## Numbers 6:18

At the tent entrance, the Nazirite shaved off the hair set apart for God.

The Nazirite put the hair on the fire beneath the peace offering.

## Numbers 6:19

The priest took the boiled shoulder of the ram.

From the basket he took one cake and one wafer made without yeast.

He placed all three in the hands of the Nazirite who had just shaved.

## Numbers 6:20

The priest waved these gifts before the LORD.

They were a holy share for the priest.

He also received the breast he waved and the thigh offered to God.

After this the Nazirite could drink wine.

## Numbers 6:21

These were the offerings required by the Nazirite vow.

A Nazirite could promise more if they could afford it.

The Nazirite had to keep the whole promise to the LORD.

## Words of blessing

## Numbers 6:22

Then the LORD spoke to Moses.

## Numbers 6:23

Moses was to teach Aaron and his sons these words.

They were to bless the Israelites by saying them.

## Numbers 6:24

May the LORD bless you and keep you safe.

## Numbers 6:25

May the LORD’s face shine on you and may he show you kindness.

## Numbers 6:26

May the LORD turn his face toward you and give you peace.

## Numbers 6:27

The LORD said that in this way the priests would put his name on Israel.

The LORD himself would bless the people.

**Reading help**

A shining face pictures the LORD looking kindly on someone. Turning his face toward someone pictures him giving that person his attention. Putting his name on Israel showed that the people belonged to him.
