# Numbers 30 — Keeping a promise

**About this chapter**

Moses teaches Israel the LORD’s rules about promises.

![An open scroll with lines of writing](../pictures/scroll.svg)

This scroll is a picture to remember the rules Moses taught.

## A man makes a promise

## Numbers 30:1

Moses spoke to the leaders of Israel’s tribes.

Moses told them what the LORD had commanded.

## Numbers 30:2

A man might make a vow to the LORD.

A vow is a serious promise to God.

A man might also swear an oath.

That means he gives his word to do something.

The man must do everything he promised.

**Pause and answer**

What must the man do after making a vow?

## A young woman makes a promise at home

## Numbers 30:3

A young woman still lives in her father’s house.

She makes a vow to the LORD.

Or she gives her word to do something.

## Numbers 30:4

Her father hears about the promise.

If he says nothing to her, the promise stands.

That means she must keep her promise.

## Numbers 30:5

Her father may say no to the promise.

He must speak on the day he hears about it.

If he does, she need not keep the promise.

The LORD forgives her because her father said no.

**Pause and answer**

When can her father say no to the promise?

## A woman marries after making a promise

## Numbers 30:6

A woman may still have a promise to keep when she marries.

It might be a vow.

It might be something she promised without thinking.

## Numbers 30:7

Her husband hears about the promise.

If he says nothing that day, the promise stands.

The woman must keep her promise.

## Numbers 30:8

Her husband may say no that day.

Then he cancels the promise, even one made without thinking.

The woman no longer has to keep it.

The LORD forgives her.

## A widow or a divorced woman makes a promise

## Numbers 30:9

A widow is a woman whose husband has died.

A divorce legally ends a marriage.

A widow or a divorced woman must keep every vow she makes.

## A woman makes a promise during marriage

## Numbers 30:10

A woman lives in her husband’s house.

She makes a vow or gives her word under oath.

## Numbers 30:11

Her husband hears about her promises.

If he says nothing to stop her, every promise stands.

She must do everything she promised.

## Numbers 30:12

Her husband may cancel her promises the day he hears them.

Then she no longer has to keep those promises.

The LORD forgives her because her husband canceled them.

## What happens if her husband waits?

## Numbers 30:13

Her husband can let a vow stand or cancel it.

This includes an oath she takes to go without something.

## Numbers 30:14

Suppose her husband says nothing day after day.

He heard her promises and said nothing that first day.

By keeping silent, he let all her promises stand.

## Numbers 30:15

Suppose her husband waits until later to cancel her promises.

Then her husband bears the guilt for breaking them.

**Pause and answer**

Who bears the guilt if the husband cancels a promise later?

## Numbers 30:16

The LORD gave Moses these rules for Israel.

They tell husbands and wives what to do about vows.

They also cover a young daughter’s promises while she lives with her father.
