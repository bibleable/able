# Numbers 13 — Twelve scouts explore Canaan

**About this chapter**

Moses sends twelve men to look at Canaan. They bring back fruit and tell what they saw. The scouts disagree about what Israel should do.

## Moses sends leaders

## Numbers 13:1

The LORD spoke to Moses.

## Numbers 13:2

The LORD was giving Canaan to the Israelites.

He told Moses to send men to explore it.

One leader must go from each tribe.

## Numbers 13:3

Moses obeyed the LORD.

He sent the men from the wilderness of Paran.

Each man was a leader in Israel.

**Reading help**

A scout goes ahead to look at a place and report back.

**Pause and answer**

Who chose the scouts and sent them out?

## What Moses asks them to look for

## Numbers 13:17

Moses told the scouts where to go in Canaan.

They were to travel through the Negev into the hills.

## Numbers 13:18

Moses asked them to look closely at the land.

Were the people strong or weak?

Were there many people or only a few?

## Numbers 13:19

Was the land a good place to live?

Did people live in open camps or in cities with strong defenses?

## Numbers 13:20

Could the soil grow good crops?

Were there trees?

Moses told the scouts to be brave and bring back fruit.

The first grapes were just getting ripe.

**Pause and answer**

What did Moses ask the scouts to bring back?

## The scouts bring back fruit

## Numbers 13:21

The scouts explored from the wilderness of Zin to Rehob, toward Lebo-hamath.

## Numbers 13:22

They went through the Negev to Hebron.

Ahiman, Sheshai and Talmai lived there.

These men came from Anak’s family.

People had built Hebron seven years before they built Zoan in Egypt.

## Numbers 13:23

In the Valley of Eshcol, the scouts cut a branch.

It held one bunch of grapes.

Two men carried it on a pole between them.

The scouts also took pomegranates and figs.

## The scouts report to Israel

## Numbers 13:25

After forty days the scouts returned.

## Numbers 13:26

They came to Kadesh in the wilderness of Paran.

Moses, Aaron and the people of Israel were there.

The scouts told everyone what they had seen.

They showed them the fruit.

## Numbers 13:27

The scouts told Moses, “We went where you sent us.

The land really does flow with milk and honey.

Here is some of its fruit!”

**Reading help**

Milk and honey picture a land with plenty of food.

## Numbers 13:28

The scouts also said, “The people there are strong.

Their cities are large and well protected.

We even saw people from Anak’s family there.”

## Numbers 13:29

The scouts said the Amalekites lived in the Negev.

The Hittites, Jebusites and Amorites lived in the hills.

The Canaanites lived beside the sea and along the Jordan River.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the scouts show the people the fruit?

## Caleb and the other scouts disagree

## Numbers 13:30

Caleb quieted the people gathered before Moses.

Caleb said, “We should go up and take the land.

We can win it!”

## Numbers 13:31

Other scouts answered, “We cannot go up against them.

They are stronger than we are!”

## Numbers 13:32

Those scouts gave Israel a bad report.

They said, “The land we explored eats up the people who live there.

Everyone we saw there was very tall.”

## Numbers 13:33

The scouts said, “We even saw the Nephilim there! The people from Anak’s family come from the Nephilim.

Beside them, we felt as small as grasshoppers.

We must have looked that small to them too!”

**Reading help**

The scouts spoke of a land that eats people to describe a place they feared people could not survive. They compared themselves to grasshoppers because they felt small beside the people they saw.
