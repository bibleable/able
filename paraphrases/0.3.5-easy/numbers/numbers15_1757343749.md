# Numbers 15 — Offerings and reminders for Israel

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Israel what gifts to bring in their new home. He also gives them a reminder they can wear on their clothes.

## Gifts to bring with a lamb

## Numbers 15:1

The LORD spoke to Moses.

## Numbers 15:2

Moses was to give Israel these instructions.

The people would follow them in the land the LORD was giving them as their home.

## Numbers 15:3

They could bring an animal from their herd or flock.

The smell of this food offering would please the LORD.

It could be a burnt offering or another sacrifice.

They might bring it to keep a promise, give freely or celebrate a feast.

## Numbers 15:4

The person bringing the animal also brought a grain offering.

For a lamb, they mixed one tenth of an ephah of fine flour with one quarter of a hin of olive oil.

**Reading help**

An ephah measures dry grain or flour. A hin measures liquids such as oil and wine.

## Numbers 15:5

With each lamb they also brought one quarter of a hin of wine.

This was a drink offering.

**Pause and answer**

What did the person mix with the flour?

## Gifts to bring with a ram or bull

## Numbers 15:6

For a ram they brought two tenths of an ephah of fine flour.

They mixed it with one third of a hin of olive oil.

## Numbers 15:7

They also brought one third of a hin of wine.

This drink offering was part of a gift whose smell pleased the LORD.

## Numbers 15:8

Someone might bring a young bull as a burnt offering.

Or they might sacrifice it to keep a promise or give it as a peace offering to the LORD.

## Numbers 15:9

With the bull they brought three tenths of an ephah of fine flour.

They mixed it with half a hin of olive oil.

## Numbers 15:10

They also brought half a hin of wine as a drink offering.

The smell of this food offering pleased the LORD.

## Numbers 15:11

The people must follow these instructions for each bull, ram, lamb or goat.

## Numbers 15:12

However many animals they brought, they must prepare the gifts for each one.

**Reading help**

| Animal | Flour in ephahs | Oil in hins | Wine in hins |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lamb | 1/10 | 1/4 | 1/4 |
| Ram | 2/10 | 1/3 | 1/3 |
| Young bull | 3/10 | 1/2 | 1/2 |

**Pause and answer**

Which animal came with the most flour?

## One rule for everyone

## Numbers 15:13

Everyone born in Israel must follow these instructions when bringing a food offering whose smell pleased the LORD.

## Numbers 15:14

A foreigner living among them, or anyone else there, might want to bring such a gift.

They must prepare it in the same way.

This rule was for future generations too.

## Numbers 15:15

The whole community had one lasting rule.

Before the LORD, it applied equally to Israelites and foreigners living among them.

## Numbers 15:16

Israelites and foreigners living there must follow the same law and instructions.

**Pause and answer**

What rule did a foreigner follow when bringing an offering?

## A gift from the first dough

## Numbers 15:17

The LORD spoke to Moses again.

## Numbers 15:18

Moses was to tell the Israelites what to do when they reached the land the LORD was giving them.

## Numbers 15:19

When they ate food from that land, they must lift up a gift to the LORD.

## Numbers 15:20

They must make a cake from their first batch of dough.

They must lift it up as a gift, just as they offered grain from the threshing floor.

**Reading help**

A threshing floor was a place where people separated grain from the stalks.

## Numbers 15:21

Every generation must give the LORD a gift from the first of its dough.

**Pause and answer**

Which batch of dough supplied the gift?

## When the whole community makes a mistake

## Numbers 15:22

The people might break one of the LORD’s commands by mistake.

These were the commands the LORD had given Moses.

## Numbers 15:23

The commands applied from the day the LORD gave them through Moses.

Each new generation must keep them too.

## Numbers 15:24

Suppose the whole community did wrong without knowing it.

Together they must bring one young bull as a burnt offering.

Its smell would please the LORD.

They must bring the grain and wine the law required.

They must also bring one male goat as a sin offering.

## Numbers 15:25

The priest would make atonement for the whole community.

The people had done wrong by mistake.

They brought the LORD a food offering and a sin offering for that wrong.

The LORD would forgive them.

**Reading help**

Here, making atonement means bringing the offerings God asked for so the people could receive forgiveness.

## Numbers 15:26

The LORD would forgive the Israelites and the foreigners living among them.

The whole community had made the mistake.

**Pause and answer**

Who made atonement for the community?

## When one person does wrong

## Numbers 15:27

If one person did wrong by mistake, that person brought a female goat a year old as a sin offering.

## Numbers 15:28

The priest would make atonement before the LORD for that person.

Then the LORD would forgive the person who had done wrong by mistake.

## Numbers 15:29

This rule applied to anyone who made a mistake.

It covered people born in Israel and foreigners living there.

## Numbers 15:30

Someone might know the LORD’s command and openly defy it.

That person insulted the LORD.

Whether born in Israel or elsewhere, that person must be cut off from the people.

**Reading help**

Being cut off meant the person could no longer belong to the community of Israel.

## Numbers 15:31

The person had treated the LORD’s word with scorn and broken his command.

That person must be cut off and would still bear the guilt.

**Pause and answer**

What animal did one person bring after doing wrong by mistake?

## A man gathers wood on the Sabbath

## Numbers 15:32

While Israel was in the wilderness, some people found a man gathering wood on the Sabbath.

## Numbers 15:33

They brought the man to Moses, Aaron and the whole community.

## Numbers 15:34

They kept the man under guard.

They did not yet know what to do with him.

## Numbers 15:35

The LORD told Moses that the man must die.

The whole community was to stone him outside the camp.

## Numbers 15:36

The community took the man outside the camp and stoned him to death.

They did what the LORD had told Moses.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the people wait with the man under guard?

## Blue cords to help people remember

## Numbers 15:37

The LORD spoke to Moses again.

## Numbers 15:38

Moses was to tell Israel to make tassels for the corners of their clothes.

Each tassel must have a blue cord.

Every generation must do this.

**Reading help**

A tassel is a small bunch of threads tied together at one end.

## Numbers 15:39

When they saw the tassels, they would remember the LORD’s commands and obey them.

Chasing whatever they saw and wanted would lead them away from him.

## Numbers 15:40

They would remember all his commands and follow them.

They would be holy, set apart for their God.

## Numbers 15:41

The LORD said, “I brought you out of Egypt to be your God.

I am the LORD your God.”

**Pause and answer**

What were the people to remember when they saw the tassels?
