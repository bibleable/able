# Numbers 10 — The trumpets sound and Israel leaves Sinai

**About this chapter**

The priests use two silver trumpets to call the people. When the cloud lifts, Israel leaves Sinai. Each camp has its place on the journey.

## The calls to gather or move

## Numbers 10:1

The LORD spoke to Moses.

## Numbers 10:2

Moses was to make two trumpets of hammered silver.

The trumpets would call the people together.

They would also tell the camps when to move.

## Numbers 10:3

When both trumpets sounded together, everyone must gather before Moses.

They must meet at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.

## Numbers 10:4

When one trumpet sounded, the clan leaders must gather before Moses.

## Numbers 10:5

Short blasts told the camps on the east side to leave.

## Numbers 10:6

A second series of short blasts told the southern camps to leave.

The short blasts were the signal to move.

## Numbers 10:7

Long blasts called the people together for a meeting.

**Reading help**

| What the people heard | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| Long blasts on both trumpets | Everyone gathered. |
| Long blasts on one trumpet | The clan leaders gathered. |
| First series of short blasts | The eastern camps set out. |
| Second series of short blasts | The southern camps set out. |

**Pause and answer**

Who gathered when just one trumpet called a meeting?

## When the priests sounded the trumpets

## Numbers 10:8

Aaron’s sons, the priests, were to blow the trumpets.

This rule was for every generation.

## Numbers 10:9

An enemy might attack Israel in its own land.

When Israel went to battle, the priests would sound short blasts.

The LORD their God would remember them and save them from their enemies.

## Numbers 10:10

The priests also sounded the trumpets at joyful celebrations.

They played at the feasts and when each new month began.

They played over the burnt offerings and peace offerings.

This brought Israel to mind before the LORD their God.

**Pause and answer**

Who blew the trumpets?

## The cloud lifts

## Numbers 10:11

It was day twenty of month two in the second year.

The cloud lifted from above the holy tent that held the record of God’s covenant.

## Numbers 10:12

The Israelites left the Sinai wilderness.

They traveled from place to place.

Then the cloud settled in the wilderness of Paran.

## Numbers 10:13

On this first departure, they followed the command the LORD had given Moses.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the cloud settle?

## Judah and the tent carriers

## Numbers 10:14

Judah’s camp set out first under its banner.

Nahshon son of Amminadab led the camp.

**Reading help**

A banner was a sign that marked a camp. People could follow their banner as they traveled.

## Numbers 10:15

Nethanel son of Zuar led the group from Issachar.

## Numbers 10:16

Eliab son of Helon led the group from Zebulun.

## Numbers 10:17

The Levites took down the holy tent.

Gershon’s families and Merari’s families set out carrying it.

**Pause and answer**

## Numbers 10:18

Then Reuben’s camp set out under its banner.

Elizur son of Shedeur led the camp.

## Numbers 10:19

Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai led the group from Simeon.

## Numbers 10:20

Eliasaph son of Deuel led the group from Gad.

## Numbers 10:21

Then Kohath’s families set out carrying the holy things.

The other carriers were to set up the tent before Kohath’s families arrived.

## Ephraim and Dan

## Numbers 10:22

Ephraim’s camp came next under its banner.

Elishama son of Ammihud led the camp.

## Numbers 10:23

Gamaliel son of Pedahzur led the group from Manasseh.

## Numbers 10:24

Abidan son of Gideoni led the group from Benjamin.

## Numbers 10:25

Dan’s camp set out last under its banner.

This camp guarded the back of the whole group.

Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai led the camp.

## Numbers 10:26

Pagiel son of Ocran led the group from Asher.

## Numbers 10:27

Ahira son of Enan led the group from Naphtali.

## Numbers 10:28

This was the order the Israelites followed when they traveled.

## Moses invites Hobab

## Numbers 10:29

Moses spoke to Hobab, son of Reuel.

Reuel was from Midian and was Moses’ father-in-law.

Moses said, “We are going to the land the LORD promised us.

Come with us.

We will treat you well.

The LORD has promised good things to Israel.”

## Numbers 10:30

Hobab answered, “I will not go with you.

I am going back to my land and my people.”

## Numbers 10:31

Moses said, “Please stay with us.

You know where we should camp in the wilderness.

You can be our eyes.”

**Reading help**

When Moses said “be our eyes,” he was asking Hobab to guide them. Hobab knew where to find places to camp.

## Numbers 10:32

Moses offered to share whatever good things the LORD gave Israel if Hobab came along.

**Pause and answer**

What help did Moses want from Hobab?

## The ark goes ahead

## Numbers 10:33

The Israelites left the mountain of the LORD.

They traveled for three days.

The ark of the LORD’s covenant went ahead of them for those three days to find a place to rest.

**Reading help**

The ark was a sacred chest. It held the record of the covenant, God’s agreement with Israel.

## Numbers 10:34

When they left camp, the LORD’s cloud was over them during the day.

## Numbers 10:35

Whenever the ark set out, Moses prayed, “Rise up, LORD! Scatter your enemies.

May those who hate you run away from you.”

## Numbers 10:36

When the ark came to rest, Moses prayed, “Return, LORD, to the countless thousands of Israel.”
