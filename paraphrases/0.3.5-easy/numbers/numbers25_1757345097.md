# Numbers 25 — Israel worships Baal at Peor

**About this chapter**

At Peor the Israelites turn to another god. A plague strikes the people. After it stops, the LORD makes a promise to Phinehas.

## Israel bows down to another god

## Numbers 25:1

The Israelites were staying at Shittim.

Some Israelite men began having sex with Moabite women.

This went against God’s law.

## Numbers 25:2

The women invited the men to sacrifices for their gods.

The Israelites ate there and bowed down to those gods.

## Numbers 25:3

In this way Israel joined in worshiping Baal of Peor.

The LORD became very angry with Israel.

**Reading help**

Baal was the god the people worshiped at Peor.

## Numbers 25:4

The LORD told Moses to take all the people’s leaders.

They were to die in public, in daylight before the LORD.

Then his fierce anger would turn away from Israel.

## Numbers 25:5

Moses spoke to Israel’s judges.

Each judge was to put to death the men under his care who had joined in worshiping Baal of Peor.

**Pause and answer**

Which god did the Israelites bow down to at Peor?

## Phinehas gets up from the gathering

## Numbers 25:6

The people were crying at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.

An Israelite man brought a Midianite woman to his family.

Moses and all the people gathered there saw him.

## Numbers 25:7

Phinehas saw them.

His father was Eleazar.

His grandfather was Aaron the priest.

Phinehas stood up among the people and took a spear.

**Phinehas and his family**

Aaron → his son Eleazar → his grandson Phinehas

## Numbers 25:8

Phinehas followed the man into his tent.

There he killed both the man and the woman with the spear.

Then the plague in Israel stopped.

## Numbers 25:9

The plague had killed 24,000 people.

**Reading help**

A plague is a disaster that strikes many people.

**Pause and answer**

Where were the people crying?

## The LORD makes a promise

## Numbers 25:10

The LORD spoke to Moses.

## Numbers 25:11

He said that Phinehas, son of Eleazar and grandson of Aaron the priest, had turned his anger away from Israel.

Phinehas had cared deeply that Israel worship the LORD alone.

So the LORD had spared the people from destruction.

## Numbers 25:12

The LORD told Moses to announce his promise.

“I give Phinehas my covenant of peace.”

## Numbers 25:13

This covenant meant that Phinehas and his descendants would always serve as priests.

Phinehas had stood up for his God.

He had made atonement for Israel.

**Reading help**

A covenant is a lasting agreement. Here, making atonement means dealing with Israel’s sin before God so his anger turns away.

**Pause and answer**

What work would Phinehas and his descendants do?

## Zimri and Cozbi

## Numbers 25:14

The Israelite man who died with the Midianite woman was Zimri.

His father was Salu.

Zimri led a family in Simeon’s tribe.

## Numbers 25:15

The Midianite woman who died was Cozbi.

Her father, Zur, was a family leader in Midian.

## Numbers 25:16

Then the LORD spoke to Moses again.

## Numbers 25:17

He told Moses that Israel was to attack the Midianites and kill them.

## Numbers 25:18

The LORD said the Midianites had deceived Israel at Peor.

They had led Israel into sin in the matter of Cozbi, a woman of their own people.

She was the daughter of a Midianite leader.

She died on the day the plague came because of what happened at Peor.
