# Numbers 28 — Offerings through the year

**About this chapter**

Israel brings offerings every morning and evening. On Sabbaths, at new moons and at feasts, the people bring extra gifts.

## One lamb in the morning and one at twilight

## Numbers 28:1

The LORD spoke to Moses.

## Numbers 28:2

Moses must remind Israel to bring the LORD his food offerings.

Each gift had its set time.

The smell of the offering pleased the LORD.

## Numbers 28:3

Each day the people must bring two male lambs for a burnt offering.

Each lamb must be a year old and free of defects.

## Numbers 28:4

They must offer one lamb in the morning.

They must offer the other as daylight faded.

## Numbers 28:5

With each lamb they brought a grain offering.

They mixed one tenth of an ephah of fine flour with one quarter of a hin of oil from pressed olives.

**Reading help**

An ephah measures dry food like flour. A hin measures liquids like oil and wine.

**Pause and answer**

When did the people offer the second lamb?

## The drink offering

## Numbers 28:6

The LORD had established this daily burnt offering at Mount Sinai.

The smell of this food offering pleased him.

## Numbers 28:7

With each lamb they gave one quarter of a hin of fermented drink.

They poured it out for the LORD in the holy place.

**Reading help**

A fermented drink contains alcohol.

## Numbers 28:8

As daylight faded, they offered the second lamb.

They brought the same grain and drink offerings as in the morning.

The smell of this food offering pleased the LORD.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the people pour the drink offering?

## Extra lambs on the Sabbath

## Numbers 28:9

On the Sabbath they brought two year-old male lambs without defects.

For the grain offering they mixed two tenths of an ephah of fine flour with oil.

They brought a drink offering too.

## Numbers 28:10

Every Sabbath they gave this extra burnt offering.

They still brought the daily burnt offering and its drink offering.

**Pause and answer**

What offering did the people continue even on the Sabbath?

## At the start of each month

## Numbers 28:11

At the start of each month they brought two young bulls and one ram.

They also brought seven year-old male lambs.

All these animals for the LORD’s burnt offering must be free of defects.

## Numbers 28:12

For each bull they mixed three tenths of an ephah of fine flour with oil.

For the ram they mixed two tenths of an ephah of fine flour with oil.

## Numbers 28:13

For each lamb they mixed one tenth of an ephah of fine flour with oil.

The smell of this burnt offering pleased the LORD.

## Numbers 28:14

Each bull’s drink offering was half a hin of wine.

The ram’s was one third of a hin.

Each lamb’s was one quarter of a hin.

They brought this burnt offering at each new moon throughout the year.

**Gifts at each new moon**

| For one animal | Fine flour to mix with oil | Wine |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One bull | 3/10 ephah | 1/2 hin |
| One ram | 2/10 ephah | 1/3 hin |
| One lamb | 1/10 ephah | 1/4 hin |

## Numbers 28:15

They also gave the LORD one male goat as a sin offering.

They still brought the daily burnt offering with its drink offering.

**Pause and answer**

How much wine went with one bull?

## Passover and seven days of bread without yeast

## Numbers 28:16

The LORD’s Passover fell on day fourteen of the first month.

## Numbers 28:17

The feast began on day fifteen of that month.

For seven days the people ate bread made without yeast.

## Numbers 28:18

On the first day they gathered for worship.

They stopped their regular work.

## Numbers 28:19

For the LORD’s burnt offering they brought two young bulls and one ram.

They also brought seven year-old male lambs.

All the animals must be free of defects.

## Numbers 28:20

For the grain offering they mixed fine flour with oil.

They used three tenths of an ephah for each bull.

They used two tenths of an ephah for the ram.

## Numbers 28:21

They used one tenth of an ephah for each of the seven lambs.

**Pause and answer**

On which day of the month did the feast begin?

## Each day of the feast

## Numbers 28:22

They included one male goat as a sin offering to make atonement for the people.

**Reading help**

Here, making atonement means bringing the offering God asked for to deal with the people’s sin.

## Numbers 28:23

These were extra gifts.

The people still gave the regular morning burnt offering.

## Numbers 28:24

They gave the same food offerings each day for seven days.

The smell pleased the LORD.

They brought the drink offering too and continued the daily burnt offering.

## Numbers 28:25

On the seventh day they gathered for worship.

They stopped their regular work.

**Pause and answer**

How many days did the people bring the feast offerings?

## The first grain of the harvest

## Numbers 28:26

During the Feast of Weeks came the day of firstfruits.

The people brought the LORD an offering from their new grain.

They gathered for worship and stopped their regular work.

**Reading help**

Firstfruits means the first part of a harvest. Here the gift was grain.

## Numbers 28:27

They brought two young bulls, one ram and seven year-old male lambs.

The smell of this burnt offering pleased the LORD.

## Numbers 28:28

For the grain offerings they mixed fine flour with oil.

They used three tenths of an ephah for each bull.

They used two tenths of an ephah for the ram.

## Numbers 28:29

They used one tenth of an ephah for each of the seven lambs.

## Numbers 28:30

They included one male goat to make atonement for the people.

## Numbers 28:31

They brought these animals and their drink offerings.

They still gave the daily burnt offering and its grain offering.

All the animals must be free of defects.
