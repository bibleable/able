# Numbers 11 — Moses asks for help and the people ask for meat

**About this chapter**

The people are tired of manna. Moses is tired of leading alone. The LORD hears both Moses and the people.

## Fire at the edge of the camp

## Numbers 11:1

The people complained about how hard life was.

The LORD heard them and became angry.

Fire from the LORD burned the edge of the camp.

## Numbers 11:2

The people cried out to Moses.

Moses prayed to the LORD.

Then the fire died down.

## Numbers 11:3

They called the place Taberah because the LORD’s fire had burned there.

**Pause and answer**

What did Moses do when the people cried for help?

## The people remember Egypt

## Numbers 11:4

Some among them badly wanted other food.

The Israelites began to cry again.

They asked, “Who will give us meat?”

## Numbers 11:5

They said, “In Egypt we ate fish for free.

We remember the cucumbers, melons, leeks, onions and garlic.

## Numbers 11:6

Now we have lost our appetite.

All we see is this manna!”

## Numbers 11:7

Manna looked like coriander seeds.

It also looked like resin, the sticky sap from a tree.

## Numbers 11:8

The people went out and gathered the manna.

They ground it between stones or crushed it in a bowl.

They boiled it in a pot or made it into cakes.

It tasted like pastry made with fine oil.

## Numbers 11:9

At night, dew settled on the camp.

The manna fell with it.

**Reading help**

Dew is water that collects in small drops overnight.

**Pause and answer**

When did manna fall on the camp?

## Moses speaks to the LORD

## Numbers 11:10

Moses heard family after family crying at their tent entrances.

The LORD was very angry.

Moses was upset too.

## Numbers 11:11

Moses asked the LORD, “Why bring this trouble on me?

I am your servant.

Why have you put all these people in my care?

Have I lost your favor?”

## Numbers 11:12

Moses asked, “Did I give birth to all these people?

Why must I carry them like a nurse holding a baby?

Must I carry them to the land you promised their ancestors?”

## Numbers 11:13

Moses said, “Where can I get meat for everyone?

They keep crying to me for meat.

## Numbers 11:14

I cannot carry all these people alone.

The load is too heavy for me.

**Reading help**

Moses speaks of carrying a heavy load. The load is the work of caring for the people.

## Numbers 11:15

If this is how you will treat me, please let me die now.

If you care for me, kill me so I will not have to face this misery.”

**Pause and answer**

What work did Moses find too heavy?

## Seventy elders will help

## Numbers 11:16

The LORD told Moses to choose seventy elders of Israel.

Moses knew these men as leaders and officers.

They were to stand with Moses at the Tent of Meeting.

## Numbers 11:17

The LORD would come down and speak with Moses there.

He would take some of the Spirit resting on Moses and give that Spirit to the elders.

They would share the work of caring for the people.

**Reading help**

| Who asked for help? | What did the LORD promise? |
| --- | --- |
| Moses, who led the people | Seventy elders would share his work. |
| The people, who wanted meat | They would have meat for a month. |

**Pause and answer**

Who would help Moses care for the people?

## The LORD promises meat

## Numbers 11:18

Moses was to tell the people to prepare themselves for the LORD.

Tomorrow they would eat meat.

The LORD had heard them crying for meat.

He had heard them say life was better in Egypt.

Now he would give them meat to eat.

## Numbers 11:19

They would eat it for more than a day or two.

They would keep eating it beyond five, ten or even twenty days.

## Numbers 11:20

They would eat meat for a whole month, until it came out of their noses and made them sick.

They had rejected the LORD who was there among them.

They had cried, “Why did we ever leave Egypt?”

## Numbers 11:21

Moses answered, “There are 600,000 men on foot here with me.

You say you will give them meat for a month.

## Numbers 11:22

Would all our sheep, goats and cattle be enough?

Would all the fish in the sea be enough?”

## Numbers 11:23

The LORD asked Moses, “Is my arm too short?

Now you will see whether my words come true.”

**Reading help**

The LORD’s arm is a picture of his power. He is asking whether Moses believes he can do what he promised.

**Pause and answer**

How long did the LORD say the people would eat meat?

## The Spirit rests on the elders

## Numbers 11:24

Moses went out and told the people what the LORD had said.

He gathered seventy elders and stationed the elders around the tent.

## Numbers 11:25

The LORD came down in the cloud and spoke to Moses.

He took some of the Spirit resting on Moses and gave that Spirit to the seventy elders.

When the Spirit rested on them, they prophesied.

They did this only that once.

**Reading help**

To prophesy means to speak a message from God.

## Numbers 11:26

Two men on the list had stayed in camp.

Their names were Eldad and Medad.

Although they had not gone to the tent, the Spirit rested on them too.

They prophesied there in the camp.

## Numbers 11:27

A young man ran to Moses.

He said, “Eldad and Medad are prophesying in the camp!”

## Numbers 11:28

Joshua son of Nun had helped Moses since he was young.

Joshua said, “Moses, my master, stop them!”

## Numbers 11:29

Moses answered, “Are you jealous for my sake?

I wish all the LORD’s people were prophets.

I wish the LORD would give them all his Spirit!”

## Numbers 11:30

Moses and the elders of Israel went back to camp.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Eldad and Medad speak?

## Quail come to the camp

## Numbers 11:31

The LORD sent a wind that brought quail from the sea.

The birds were about two cubits above the ground.

They were all around the camp, as far as a person could walk in a day.

## Numbers 11:32

The people gathered quail all day, all night and all the next day.

Each person gathered at least ten homers of birds.

They spread the birds out around the camp.

**Reading help**

Quail are small birds. A cubit was about the length from an elbow to the fingertips. A homer was a large measure for dry goods.

## Numbers 11:33

The meat was still between their teeth.

Before they could chew it, the LORD grew angry with them.

He struck the people with a terrible plague.

## Numbers 11:34

They buried the people who had craved other food there.

That is why they named the place Kibroth-hattaavah.

## Numbers 11:35

From there the people traveled to Hazeroth.

They stayed there for a time.

**Pause and answer**

What brought the quail in from the sea?
