# Numbers 7 — Twelve leaders bring their gifts

**About this chapter**

Israel’s leaders bring carts for moving the holy tent. Then each tribe has its own day to bring gifts to the altar.

## Carts and oxen for the holy tent

## Numbers 7:1

Moses finished setting up the holy tent.

That day he put special oil on the tent and its furnishings.

He did the same for the altar and its tools.

He set them all apart for God.

## Numbers 7:2

Israel’s leaders brought a gift.

These men led the tribes and their families.

They had helped oversee the count of the people.

## Numbers 7:3

The leaders brought six covered carts and twelve oxen.

Each leader gave one ox.

Each pair of leaders gave one cart.

They brought them before the LORD at the holy tent.

## Numbers 7:4

The LORD spoke to Moses.

## Numbers 7:5

He told Moses to accept the gifts for the work at the Tent of Meeting.

Moses must give the Levites what they needed for their work.

**Pause and answer**

How many leaders shared the gift of one cart?

## Moses shares out the carts

## Numbers 7:6

Moses accepted the carts and oxen.

He gave them to the Levites.

## Numbers 7:7

Gershon’s families needed two carts and four oxen.

Moses gave them that share.

## Numbers 7:8

Merari’s families needed four carts and eight oxen.

Moses gave them that share.

Ithamar, son of Aaron the priest, directed the work.

## Numbers 7:9

Kohath’s families received no carts or oxen.

Their job was to carry the holy things on their shoulders.

**Reading help**

| Family | Carts | Oxen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gershon | 2 | 4 |
| Merari | 4 | 8 |
| Kohath | 0 | 0 |

## Numbers 7:10

After Moses put oil on the altar, the leaders came forward.

They brought their gifts to the altar to set it apart for God.

## Numbers 7:11

The LORD told Moses how to receive these gifts.

One leader would bring his gift each day.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Kohath’s families receive no carts?

## Day 1 • Judah

## Numbers 7:12

On day 1, Nahshon came forward for the tribe of Judah.

He was the son of Amminadab.

## Numbers 7:13

Nahshon brought a silver platter weighing 130 shekels.

He brought a silver bowl weighing 70 shekels.

Both weights followed the standard at the holy tent.

Both gifts held fine flour mixed with oil for a grain offering.

**Reading help**

A platter is a large serving plate. A shekel is a unit of weight.

## Numbers 7:14

He brought a gold dish filled with incense.

The dish weighed 10 shekels.

**Reading help**

Incense gives off a sweet smell when burned.

## Numbers 7:15

For a burnt offering, he brought one young bull and one ram.

He also brought one male lamb, a year old.

**Reading help**

A ram is an adult male sheep.

## Numbers 7:16

He brought one male goat for a sin offering.

## Numbers 7:17

For a peace offering, he brought two oxen.

He also brought five rams and five male goats.

He added five male lambs, each a year old.

These were the gifts of Nahshon son of Amminadab.

**Pause and answer**

What did the silver bowl hold?

## The gifts from all twelve tribes

## Numbers 7:84

The leaders gave these gifts when Moses set the altar apart with oil.

Together they gave twelve silver platters, twelve silver bowls and twelve gold dishes.

## Numbers 7:85

Each silver platter weighed 130 shekels.

Each silver bowl weighed 70 shekels.

Together the silver weighed 2,400 shekels by the holy tent’s standard.

## Numbers 7:86

Each of the twelve gold dishes held incense.

Each weighed 10 shekels by the holy tent’s standard.

The gold dishes weighed 120 shekels in all.

## Numbers 7:87

For burnt offerings the leaders gave twelve bulls and twelve rams.

They also gave twelve year-old male lambs and the grain offerings that went with them.

For sin offerings they gave twelve male goats.

## Numbers 7:88

For peace offerings they gave twenty-four bulls and sixty rams.

They also gave sixty male goats and sixty year-old male lambs.

These were their gifts for the altar after Moses set it apart with oil.

**Pause and answer**

How many gold dishes did the twelve leaders give?

## Moses hears the LORD

## Numbers 7:89

Moses went into the Tent of Meeting to speak with the LORD.

There he heard the LORD’s voice.

It came from between the two cherubim above the mercy seat on the sacred chest.

That was how the LORD spoke to Moses.

**Reading help**

The mercy seat was the cover of the sacred chest. The cherubim were two figures with wings above the cover.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Moses hear the voice?
