# Numbers 33 — The places Israel camped

**About this chapter**

Moses writes down the places Israel camped between Egypt and the Jordan River.

## Moses records the journey

## Numbers 33:1

The Israelites left Egypt in groups.

Moses and Aaron led them on their journey.

## Numbers 33:2

The LORD told Moses to write down the journey.

Moses listed where each part of the journey began.

**Reading help**

Each time Israel camped, the people stopped and set up their tents.

**Pause and answer**

Who wrote down the places Israel visited?

## Numbers 33:3

Israel left Rameses on day 15 of the first month.

It was the day after the Passover feast.

They marched out boldly where all the Egyptians could see.

## Numbers 33:4

The Egyptians were burying their firstborn children.

The LORD had killed the firstborn throughout Egypt.

The LORD had judged the gods of Egypt.

## Israel crosses the sea

## Numbers 33:5

Israel left Rameses.

They camped at Succoth.

## Numbers 33:6

Israel left Succoth.

They camped at Etham, where the wilderness began.

## Numbers 33:7

Israel left Etham and turned back toward Pi-hahiroth.

Pi-hahiroth was across from Baal-zephon.

They camped near Migdol.

## Numbers 33:8

Israel left Pi-hahiroth and crossed through the sea.

They traveled three days through the Wilderness of Etham.

Then they camped at Marah.

## Numbers 33:9

Israel left Marah and camped at Elim.

Elim had 12 springs of water and 70 palm trees.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Israel find springs and palm trees?

## From Elim to Sinai

## Numbers 33:10

Israel left Elim.

They camped by the Red Sea.

## Numbers 33:11

Israel left the Red Sea.

They camped in the Desert of Sin.

**Reading help**

Here “Sin” is the name of a desert.

## Numbers 33:12

Israel left the Desert of Sin.

They camped at Dophkah.

## Numbers 33:13

Israel left Dophkah.

They camped at Alush.

## Numbers 33:14

Israel left Alush and camped at Rephidim.

There the people had no water to drink.

## Numbers 33:15

Israel left Rephidim.

They camped in the Wilderness of Sinai.

## From Sinai to Rissah

## Numbers 33:16

Israel left the Wilderness of Sinai.

They camped at Kibroth-hattaavah.

## Numbers 33:17

Israel left Kibroth-hattaavah.

They camped at Hazeroth.

## Numbers 33:18

Israel left Hazeroth.

They camped at Rithmah.

## Numbers 33:19

Israel left Rithmah.

They camped at Rimmon-perez.

## Numbers 33:20

Israel left Rimmon-perez.

They camped at Libnah.

## Numbers 33:21

Israel left Libnah.

They camped at Rissah.

## From Rissah to Mithkah

## Numbers 33:22

Israel left Rissah.

They camped at Kehelathah.

## Numbers 33:23

Israel left Kehelathah.

They camped at Mount Shepher.

## Numbers 33:24

Israel left Mount Shepher.

They camped at Haradah.

## Numbers 33:25

Israel left Haradah.

They camped at Makheloth.

## Numbers 33:26

Israel left Makheloth.

They camped at Tahath.

## Numbers 33:27

Israel left Tahath.

They camped at Terah.

## Numbers 33:28

Israel left Terah.

They camped at Mithkah.

## From Mithkah to Ezion-geber

## Numbers 33:29

Israel left Mithkah.

They camped at Hashmonah.

## Numbers 33:30

Israel left Hashmonah.

They camped at Moseroth.

## Numbers 33:31

Israel left Moseroth.

They camped at Bene-jaakan.

## Numbers 33:32

Israel left Bene-jaakan.

They camped at Hor-haggidgad.

## Numbers 33:33

Israel left Hor-haggidgad.

They camped at Jotbathah.

## Numbers 33:34

Israel left Jotbathah.

They camped at Abronah.

## Numbers 33:35

Israel left Abronah.

They camped at Ezion-geber.

## Aaron dies on Mount Hor

## Numbers 33:36

Israel left Ezion-geber.

They camped at Kadesh in the Wilderness of Zin.

## Numbers 33:37

Israel left Kadesh and camped at Mount Hor.

The mountain stood at the edge of Edom.

![A mountain](../pictures/mountain.svg)

Mount Hor was one stop on the journey.

## Numbers 33:38

The LORD told Aaron the priest to climb Mount Hor.

Aaron climbed the mountain and died there.

It was day one of the fifth month.

It was the 40th year since Israel had left Egypt.

## Numbers 33:39

Aaron was 123 years old when he died on Mount Hor.

**Pause and answer**

How old was Aaron when he died?

## Numbers 33:40

The king of Arad heard that Israel was coming.

This Canaanite king lived in the Negev, in southern Canaan.

## Israel reaches the Jordan River

## Numbers 33:41

Israel left Mount Hor.

They camped at Zalmonah.

## Numbers 33:42

Israel left Zalmonah.

They camped at Punon.

## Numbers 33:43

Israel left Punon.

They camped at Oboth.

## Numbers 33:44

Israel left Oboth.

They camped at Iye-abarim on the border of Moab.

## Numbers 33:45

Israel left Iyim.

They camped at Dibon-gad.

## Numbers 33:46

Israel left Dibon-gad.

They camped at Almon-diblathaim.

## Numbers 33:47

Israel left Almon-diblathaim.

They camped in the mountains of Abarim, across from Nebo.

## Numbers 33:48

Israel left the mountains of Abarim.

They camped on the plains of Moab.

The camp stood beside the Jordan River, across from Jericho.

## Numbers 33:49

Israel camped along the Jordan on the plains of Moab.

Their camp stretched from Beth-jeshimoth to Abel-shittim.

**Pause and answer**

Beside which river did Israel camp in Moab?

## The LORD tells Israel about Canaan

## Numbers 33:50

The LORD spoke to Moses on the plains of Moab.

They were beside the Jordan, across from Jericho.

## Numbers 33:51

The LORD gave Moses a message for Israel.

The message told Israel what to do after crossing the Jordan into Canaan.

## Numbers 33:52

The LORD told Israel to drive out everyone living there.

Israel must destroy all the carved images and metal idols.

They must tear down all the high places of worship.

**Reading help**

An idol is an object that people worship as a god.

## Numbers 33:53

The LORD told Israel to take the land and settle there.

The LORD had given them the land for their own.

## Numbers 33:54

The tribes would receive land according to their family lines.

Each clan would draw lots to find its land.

A large clan would receive a larger share.

A small clan would receive a smaller share.

Each clan would keep the land its lot showed.

**Reading help**

A clan is a group of related families.

Drawing lots means using marked objects to make a choice.

**Pause and answer**

Which clans would receive larger shares of land?

## Numbers 33:55

The LORD warned Israel about letting the people stay.

Those who stayed would be like sharp points in Israel’s eyes and thorns in their sides.

This meant those people would keep troubling Israel in its new home.

## Numbers 33:56

Then the LORD would do to Israel what he had planned to do to the people of Canaan.
