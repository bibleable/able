# 1 Timothy 3 — People who lead and serve the church

**About this chapter**

Paul tells Timothy what to look for in church leaders.

He asks Timothy to notice how people live at home as well as in church.

## A person who leads the church

## 1 Timothy 3:1

Here is a saying you can trust.

Someone who wants to be an overseer wants to do good work.

**Help with these words**

An overseer is a leader who cares for the church.

## 1 Timothy 3:2

An overseer must live in a way people can respect.

He must be the husband of just one wife.

He must think clearly and control his actions.

He must welcome guests and know how to teach.

## 1 Timothy 3:3

He must be free from dependence on wine.

He must be gentle and live peaceably with others.

He must avoid violence, quarrels, and the love of money.

## 1 Timothy 3:4

He must manage his family well.

He must guide his children with dignity so they obey him.

## 1 Timothy 3:5

If he cannot care for his own household, how will he care for God’s church?

| At home | In the church |
| --- | --- |
| A leader cares for his household. | A leader cares for God’s people. |

![A leader’s care at home helps show how he will care for the church.](../pictures/care-at-home-and-church.svg)

Paul asks the church to look at how a leader already treats people.

## 1 Timothy 3:6

Give a new believer time before making him an overseer.

Otherwise he may grow proud.

Then God may judge him as he judged the devil.

## 1 Timothy 3:7

People outside the church must also speak well of him.

Otherwise he could fall into disgrace and into the devil’s trap.

**Pause and answer**

How should an overseer treat guests?

## People trusted to serve

## 1 Timothy 3:8

Deacons also must be worthy of respect.

They must speak honestly to everyone.

They must avoid heavy drinking and greed.

**Help with these words**

A deacon is someone the church trusts with work to serve its people.

## 1 Timothy 3:9

They must hold firmly to the truth God has revealed about our faith.

They must do this with a clear conscience.

## 1 Timothy 3:10

First find out whether they live this way.

If there is nothing to hold against them, let them serve as deacons.

## 1 Timothy 3:11

The women too must live in a way people can respect.

They must never spread lies about other people.

They must show good judgment and be trustworthy in everything.

## 1 Timothy 3:12

A deacon must be the husband of just one wife.

He must guide his children and manage his household well.

## 1 Timothy 3:13

Deacons who serve well earn respect.

They also grow very confident in their faith in Christ Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

What should the church do before someone begins serving as a deacon?

## Why Paul writes

## 1 Timothy 3:14

Timothy, I hope to visit you soon.

Still, I am writing these things down.

## 1 Timothy 3:15

If I am delayed, you will know how people should live in God’s household.

That household is the church of the living God.

The church supports the truth like a pillar and a foundation.

**Help with these words**

A pillar holds up a building.

Paul uses this picture to show how the church upholds the truth.

## 1 Timothy 3:16

We agree that the truth God has shown us about serving him is great.

Christ appeared as a human being.

The Spirit showed that Christ was in the right.

Angels saw him.

People announced the news about him among the nations.

People around the world believed in him.

God took him up in glory.

**Pause and answer**

Who belongs to the household Paul describes?
