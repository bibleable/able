# 1 Timothy 2 — Pray for everyone

**About this chapter**

Paul tells Timothy whom the believers should pray for.

Then he gives instructions about worship and learning.

## Pray for everyone

## 1 Timothy 2:1

First of all, pray for everyone.

Bring their needs to God.

Ask him to help them, and give him thanks.

## 1 Timothy 2:2

Pray for kings and everyone who has authority.

Then we can live quiet, peaceful lives.

We can honor God and treat people with respect.

| People to pray for | What to bring to God |
| --- | --- |
| Everyone, including rulers | Their needs and your thanks |

## 1 Timothy 2:3

Praying this way is good.

It pleases God our Savior.

## 1 Timothy 2:4

God wants everyone to be saved and to know the truth.

**Pause and answer**

Whom does Paul ask people to include in their prayers?

## One mediator

## 1 Timothy 2:5

There is one God.

There is one mediator between God and people.

That mediator is Christ Jesus, who is himself human.

**Help with these words**

A mediator helps bring two sides together.

## 1 Timothy 2:6

Jesus gave himself as the price to set everyone free.

At the right time this showed what God had done for all.

## 1 Timothy 2:7

God sent me as an apostle to announce this message.

He gave me the work of faithfully teaching the truth to the Gentiles.

I am telling you the truth about this.

**Help with these words**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish.

**Pause and answer**

Who brings God and people together?

## Prayer and good deeds

## 1 Timothy 2:8

I want men everywhere to pray.

Let them lift holy hands to God, free from anger and quarrels.

## 1 Timothy 2:9

I also want women to dress with modesty and good judgment.

They should avoid showing off with braided hair, gold, pearls, or costly clothes.

## 1 Timothy 2:10

Let their good deeds be what people notice.

That is fitting for women who say they worship God.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul want people to notice about women who worship God?

## Paul’s instructions about learning

## 1 Timothy 2:11

A woman should learn quietly and fully accept instruction.

## 1 Timothy 2:12

I do not permit a woman to teach or exercise authority over a man.

She should remain quiet.

## 1 Timothy 2:13

God made Adam first.

Then he made Eve.

## 1 Timothy 2:14

Adam was not the one the serpent deceived.

The serpent deceived Eve, and she broke God’s command.

## 1 Timothy 2:15

Yet women will be saved through childbearing if they keep trusting God, loving others, and living holy lives with self-control.

**Pause and answer**

In what order did God make Adam and Eve?
