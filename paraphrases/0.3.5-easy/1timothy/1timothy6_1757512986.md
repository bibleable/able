# 1 Timothy 6 — Put your hope in God

**About this chapter**

Paul warns Timothy about wanting to get rich.

He tells him how people with money can help others.

## Instructions for enslaved believers

## 1 Timothy 6:1

Those who live as slaves should show their masters full respect.

Then people will have no reason to speak against God’s name or our teaching.

## 1 Timothy 6:2

Some masters are believers too.

Those who serve them must still show them respect.

Serve them all the more because the people receiving that care are fellow believers whom God loves.

Teach these things and encourage people to do them.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul tell believers whose masters also believe?

## When teachers want to get rich

## 1 Timothy 6:3

Someone may teach things that go against the sound words of our Lord Jesus Christ.

That person rejects the teaching that helps people live for God.

## 1 Timothy 6:4

Such a teacher is proud and understands nothing.

They are too eager to argue and fight about words.

This leads to envy, quarrels, insults, and suspicion.

## 1 Timothy 6:5

People who think this way keep fighting.

Their thinking has gone wrong, and they have lost the truth.

They see serving God as a way to make money.

## 1 Timothy 6:6

Serving God and being content with what we have is a great gain.

## 1 Timothy 6:7

We brought nothing with us at birth.

We can take nothing with us when we die.

## 1 Timothy 6:8

If we have food and clothing, we can be content.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul say we can take with us when we die?

## The danger of loving money

## 1 Timothy 6:9

People who long to get rich can fall into a trap.

Foolish and harmful desires tempt them.

Those desires can lead them to ruin.

## 1 Timothy 6:10

The love of money is a root from which all kinds of evil grow.

Some wanted money so badly that they left the faith.

They brought deep sorrow on themselves.

**Help with these words**

A root feeds a growing plant.

Paul pictures harmful actions growing from the love of money.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul compare to a root?

## Hold on to the life God gives

## 1 Timothy 6:11

Timothy, you belong to God.

Stay far from these things.

Seek to do right and to honor God.

Keep trusting, loving, and showing gentleness even when life is hard.

## 1 Timothy 6:12

Keep up the good struggle to stay faithful.

Hold on to the eternal life God called you to.

Many people heard you openly declare your faith.

## 1 Timothy 6:13

God gives life to everything.

Christ Jesus openly told the truth before Pontius Pilate.

In their presence I give you this charge.

## 1 Timothy 6:14

Keep this command faithfully, so no one can fault you.

Do this until our Lord Jesus Christ appears.

## 1 Timothy 6:15

God will bring this about at the time he chooses.

He alone is the blessed Ruler.

He is King over all kings and Lord over all lords.

## 1 Timothy 6:16

God alone can never die.

He lives in light no one can approach.

No one has seen him or can see him.

May he receive honor and rule forever.

Amen.

**Pause and answer**

Who decides when the Lord Jesus Christ will appear?

## Be ready to share

## 1 Timothy 6:17

Tell those who are rich in this life to guard against pride.

Money can disappear, so it makes a poor place to put your hope.

Trust God, who gives us so much to enjoy.

## 1 Timothy 6:18

Tell them to do good until they are rich in good deeds.

Tell them to give generously and be ready to share.

## 1 Timothy 6:19

In this way they store up treasure for the future.

They lay a firm foundation and take hold of real life.

**Help with these words**

A foundation is the base that holds up a building.

Paul uses it to picture something people can rely on in the future.

**Pause and answer**

How can someone become rich in good deeds?

## Guard what God has given you

## 1 Timothy 6:20

Timothy, guard what has been put in your care.

Keep away from empty talk that dishonors God.

Avoid arguments from people who falsely claim to have special knowledge.

## 1 Timothy 6:21

Some have claimed to know these things and have wandered away from the faith.

May God show grace to you all.
