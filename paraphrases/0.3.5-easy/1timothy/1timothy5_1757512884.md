# 1 Timothy 5 — Care for widows and treat leaders fairly

**About this chapter**

Paul explains how families and the church can care for widows.

He also tells Timothy to treat church leaders fairly.

## Treat people as family

## 1 Timothy 5:1

When an older man needs correction, speak to him as you would to your father.

Treat younger men as brothers.

## 1 Timothy 5:2

Treat older women as mothers.

Treat younger women as sisters, with completely pure intentions.

**Pause and answer**

How should Timothy speak to an older man?

## Help widows who are alone

## 1 Timothy 5:3

Give honor and support to widows who are truly alone.

**Help with these words**

A widow is a woman whose husband has died.

## 1 Timothy 5:4

A widow may have children or grandchildren.

They should first learn to honor God by caring for their family.

They can give back some of the care their parents gave them.

This pleases God.

## 1 Timothy 5:5

A widow who has no one to support her puts her hope in God.

Day and night she prays and asks him for help.

## 1 Timothy 5:6

A widow who lives only for pleasure is already dead inside, though her body lives.

## 1 Timothy 5:7

Teach these things so the believers can live free from blame.

## 1 Timothy 5:8

Anyone who refuses to provide for their relatives has denied the faith.

This is especially true when their own household needs help.

They act worse than someone who does not believe.

**Pause and answer**

Who should help a widow if she has children or grandchildren?

## Who goes on the list

## 1 Timothy 5:9

A widow on the list should be at least sixty years old.

She should have been faithful to her husband.

## 1 Timothy 5:10

People should know her for the good she has done.

Has she raised children and welcomed strangers?

Has she washed believers’ feet and helped people in trouble?

Has she given herself to doing good?

![A basin and towel for washing feet](../pictures/basin-and-towel.svg)

**Help with these words**

Washing dusty feet was a way to serve a guest.

## 1 Timothy 5:11

Leave younger widows off that list.

Their desire to marry may draw them away from their commitment to Christ.

## 1 Timothy 5:12

Then they will face judgment for breaking their first commitment.

## 1 Timothy 5:13

They may also get used to going from house to house without working.

They may spread gossip, meddle in other people’s affairs, and say things they should keep private.

## 1 Timothy 5:14

I advise younger widows to marry, have children, and manage their homes.

Then an opponent will have no grounds to speak evil of us.

## 1 Timothy 5:15

Some have already turned away to follow Satan.

## 1 Timothy 5:16

A believing woman should help widows who depend on her.

Then the church can support widows who have no one else.

**Pause and answer**

Why does Paul ask families to help their own widows?

## Be fair to church leaders

## 1 Timothy 5:17

Give double honor to elders who lead well.

Especially honor those who work hard at preaching and teaching.

**Help with these words**

Elders are people who lead and care for the church.

Here honor includes helping to support them.

## 1 Timothy 5:18

Scripture says to let an ox eat while it walks on the grain.

It also says workers deserve their pay.

## 1 Timothy 5:19

Only hear a charge against an elder if two or three witnesses support it.

## 1 Timothy 5:20

Correct those who keep sinning, and do it before everyone.

Then the others will take the danger of sin seriously.

## 1 Timothy 5:21

God, Christ Jesus, and the chosen angels are witnesses as I charge you.

Follow these instructions fairly.

Do not play favorites.

**Pause and answer**

What must support a charge against an elder?

## Take time before choosing a leader

## 1 Timothy 5:22

Do not hurry to lay your hands on someone.

Do not share responsibility for another person’s sins.

Keep your own life pure.

**Help with these words**

Church leaders laid hands on a person to set that person apart for work.

## 1 Timothy 5:23

Timothy, you often feel ill and have stomach trouble.

Have a little wine instead of drinking only water.

## 1 Timothy 5:24

Some people’s sins are easy to see even before judgment.

Other people’s sins come to light later.

## 1 Timothy 5:25

Good deeds can be easy to see too.

Even good deeds that few people notice will come to light.
