# 1 Timothy 1 — Paul asks Timothy to teach what is true

**About this chapter**

Paul writes to Timothy in Ephesus.

He remembers how Christ showed him patience when he had done wrong.

## Paul writes to Timothy

## 1 Timothy 1:1

I am Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus.

God our Savior and Christ Jesus commanded me to serve.

Christ Jesus is our hope.

**Help with these words**

An apostle is someone Jesus sends to speak for him.

## 1 Timothy 1:2

Timothy, you are like my own child in the faith.

May God our Father and Christ Jesus our Lord give you grace, mercy, and peace.

**Pause and answer**

To whom does Paul write?

## What good teaching is for

## 1 Timothy 1:3

When I left for Macedonia, I asked you to stay in Ephesus.

Tell certain people there to stop teaching what is false.

## 1 Timothy 1:4

Tell them to stop spending their time on made-up stories and endless lists of ancestors.

These lead people to guess and argue.

God has given us work that we carry out by trusting him.

## 1 Timothy 1:5

We teach so that people will love others.

That love comes from a pure heart, a clear conscience, and real faith.

**Help with these words**

Your conscience is your sense of right and wrong.

## 1 Timothy 1:6

Some people have turned away from these things.

They spend their time on empty talk.

## 1 Timothy 1:7

They want to teach God’s law.

They speak as if they are sure of every word.

Yet they do not understand the claims they make.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul want his teaching to produce?

## Use the law rightly

## 1 Timothy 1:8

We know the law is good when people use it rightly.

## 1 Timothy 1:9

The law addresses people who do wrong and rebel.

It addresses those who sin and treat God and holy things with contempt.

It speaks against killing a father, a mother, or anyone else.

## 1 Timothy 1:10

It speaks against sexual wrongdoing, including men having sex with men.

It speaks against trading people as slaves, lying, and lying under oath.

It addresses everything else that goes against sound teaching.

## 1 Timothy 1:11

Sound teaching agrees with the glorious good news from God.

God deserves praise.

He trusted me with this good news.

**Pause and answer**

When does Paul say the law is good?

## Christ showed Paul mercy

## 1 Timothy 1:12

I thank Christ Jesus our Lord for giving me strength.

He counted me trustworthy and gave me work to do.

## 1 Timothy 1:13

I used to speak against God and attack believers.

I was violent.

I acted without understanding and without faith.

Yet the Lord showed me mercy.

**Help with these words**

To show mercy means to treat someone with compassion even when they have done wrong.

## 1 Timothy 1:14

Our Lord gave me more kindness than I could measure.

With it came the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus.

## 1 Timothy 1:15

Here is a saying everyone can trust and accept completely.

Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners.

I am the worst of them.

## 1 Timothy 1:16

That is why Christ showed me mercy.

If Christ Jesus could be so patient with me, others could see his perfect patience.

They too could trust him and receive life forever.

## 1 Timothy 1:17

May the King who rules forever receive honor and glory forever!

He alone is God.

He cannot die, and no one can see him.

Amen.

**Pause and answer**

Whose patience did people see in Paul’s life?

## Hold on to your faith

## 1 Timothy 1:18

Timothy, my child, I give you this instruction.

Remember the messages people spoke from God about you earlier.

Let those words help you fight the good fight.

**Help with these words**

Here fighting the good fight means keeping on with the work God gave Timothy.

## 1 Timothy 1:19

Keep trusting God and keep a clear conscience.

Some have pushed their conscience aside.

They have wrecked their faith like a ship that breaks apart.

Hymenaeus and Alexander are among them.

I have handed them over to Satan so they may learn to stop speaking against God.
