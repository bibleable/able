# 1 Timothy 4 — Timothy can set an example

**About this chapter**

Paul warns Timothy about teachers who give people false rules.

He reminds Timothy how to help the church learn.

## Receive God’s gifts with thanks

## 1 Timothy 4:1

The Spirit says clearly that some people will leave the faith in times to come.

They will follow spirits that deceive them.

They will listen to what demons teach.

## 1 Timothy 4:2

People who pretend to be honest will tell them lies.

Their consciences are like skin burned with a hot iron.

**Help with these words**

Your conscience helps you recognize wrong.

Paul pictures people who have lost their feeling for right and wrong.

## 1 Timothy 4:3

These teachers will forbid people to marry.

They will tell people to avoid certain foods.

Yet God made these foods for people who believe and know the truth to enjoy with thanks.

## 1 Timothy 4:4

Everything God made is good.

We can accept what God gives us and thank him for it.

## 1 Timothy 4:5

God’s word and prayer make it holy.

**Pause and answer**

How does Paul say believers should receive the food God made?

## Practice living in a way that honors God

## 1 Timothy 4:6

Teach these things to the other believers, Timothy.

Then you will serve Christ Jesus well.

The message of faith and the good teaching you follow give you strength.

## 1 Timothy 4:7

Have nothing to do with foolish stories that dishonor God.

Train yourself to live in a way that honors him.

## 1 Timothy 4:8

Training your body does some good.

Training to live for God helps you in every way.

It holds promise for this life and the life to come.

**Help with these words**

An athlete practices often.

Paul wants Timothy to practice living for God with the same care.

## 1 Timothy 4:9

You can trust this saying and accept it fully.

## 1 Timothy 4:10

This is why we work so hard.

We have put our hope in the living God.

He is the Savior of everyone, especially those who believe.

**Pause and answer**

For how long does living for God have value?

## Timothy can set an example

## 1 Timothy 4:11

Teach these things and tell the believers to follow them.

## 1 Timothy 4:12

Do not let anyone look down on you because you are young.

Show believers how to live by what you say and do.

Set an example in love, faith, and purity.

## 1 Timothy 4:13

Until I come, give time to reading Scripture aloud to the church.

Urge the believers to follow it and teach them what it means.

![A scroll open for reading](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## 1 Timothy 4:14

Use the gift God gave you.

You received it through a message from God when the church elders laid their hands on you.

**Help with these words**

Elders are leaders in the church.

## 1 Timothy 4:15

Practice these things and give them your full attention.

Then everyone will see how you are growing.

## 1 Timothy 4:16

Pay close attention to how you live and what you teach.

Keep doing this.

In this way you will save both yourself and those who hear you.
