# 2 Samuel 14 — Absalom returns to Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

David misses his son Absalom. Joab asks a wise woman to tell David a story. She wants David to think about his own son.

## Joab sends a wise woman to David

## 2 Samuel 14:1

Joab was Zeruiah’s son.

Joab could tell that King David missed Absalom.

## 2 Samuel 14:2

Joab sent for a wise woman from Tekoa.

He said, “Pretend someone you loved has died.

Wear clothes that show your grief.

Do not put oil on your skin.

Act as if you have been grieving a long time.

## 2 Samuel 14:3

Then go and speak to the king.”

Joab told her what to say.

## The woman tells a story about two sons

## 2 Samuel 14:4

The woman went to King David.

She bowed with her face to the ground.

She said, “Help me, my king!”

## 2 Samuel 14:5

David asked, “What is troubling you?”

She said, “My husband died. I am a widow.

## 2 Samuel 14:6

My two sons were fighting in a field.

No one was there to pull them apart.

One son hit the other and killed him.

## 2 Samuel 14:7

Now my whole family has turned against me.

They say, ‘Hand over your son.

We will kill him because he killed his brother.

Then the son who would inherit everything will be gone too.’

They would put out my last glowing coal.

No one would remain to carry on my husband’s name.”

**Reading help**

A glowing coal can keep a fire alive. In her story, the last coal stands for her only living son.

## David promises to protect the son

## 2 Samuel 14:8

David told her, “Go home.

I will give orders to help you.”

## 2 Samuel 14:9

The woman said, “My king, if anyone finds fault, let them blame me and my father’s family.

May you and your kingdom be free of blame.”

## 2 Samuel 14:10

David said, “If anyone threatens you, bring him to me.

He will never trouble you again.”

## 2 Samuel 14:11

She said, “Please make a promise by the LORD your God.

Stop the relative who wants to avenge my son’s death.

Do not let him kill my other son too.”

David promised, “As surely as the LORD lives, no one will harm a hair on your son’s head.”

## The woman asks about David’s own son

## 2 Samuel 14:12

The woman asked, “My king, may I say something more?”

David answered, “Speak.”

## 2 Samuel 14:13

She asked, “Why have you done this to God’s people?

Your own words show you are at fault.

You have kept your own son away from home.

## 2 Samuel 14:14

We will all die.

We are like water spilled on the ground.

No one can gather it up again.

Yet God does not take a life away.

God finds ways to bring back those who had to leave him.

![Water spills from a cup and soaks into the ground.](../pictures/water-spilled-on-the-ground.svg)

**Reading help**

The woman compares a life to spilled water. Once a person has died, no one can bring that life back.

## 2 Samuel 14:15

I came to you because the people frightened me.

I thought, ‘I will speak to the king.

Perhaps he will do what I ask.

## 2 Samuel 14:16

The king will listen and save me from this man.

This man wants to take away the place God gave me and my son among his people.’

## 2 Samuel 14:17

My king, may your answer bring me peace.

You can tell good from evil like an angel of God.

May the LORD your God be with you.”

## David asks who sent the woman

## 2 Samuel 14:18

David said, “I have a question for you.

Please tell me the whole truth.”

The woman answered, “Please ask, my king.”

## 2 Samuel 14:19

David asked, “Was this Joab’s plan?”

She answered, “As surely as you live, my king, you are exactly right.

Joab gave me the orders.

He told me every word to say.

## 2 Samuel 14:20

Joab did this because he wanted things to change.

You are as wise as an angel of God.

You know everything that happens in the land.”

**Pause and answer**

Who told the woman what to say?

## Joab brings Absalom home

## 2 Samuel 14:21

David told Joab, “I will do what you ask.

Go and bring young Absalom back.”

## 2 Samuel 14:22

Joab bowed with his face to the ground.

He blessed the king.

Joab said, “Now I know you are pleased with me.

You have given me what I asked for.”

## 2 Samuel 14:23

Joab got up and went to Geshur.

He brought Absalom back to Jerusalem.

## 2 Samuel 14:24

David said, “Absalom may go to his own house.

He must not come to see me.”

So Absalom went home without seeing his father.

## Absalom and his family

## 2 Samuel 14:25

Throughout Israel, people praised Absalom for his good looks.

No other man received such praise.

From head to foot, people could find no fault with his looks.

## 2 Samuel 14:26

Every year Absalom cut his hair because it grew heavy.

He weighed the hair he cut off.

It weighed 200 shekels by the king’s weights.

**Reading help**

A shekel was a unit of weight. The hair weighed a little over two kilograms.

## 2 Samuel 14:27

Absalom had three sons and a daughter.

His daughter’s name was Tamar.

She was a beautiful woman.

## Absalom sends for Joab

## 2 Samuel 14:28

Absalom lived in Jerusalem for two years.

In all that time, he did not see the king.

**Pause and answer**

How long did Absalom live in Jerusalem before seeing his father?

## 2 Samuel 14:29

Absalom wanted Joab to speak to the king for him.

He sent for Joab twice.

Both times Joab refused to come.

## 2 Samuel 14:30

Absalom told his servants, “Joab grows barley beside my field.

Go and burn his field.”

His servants set Joab’s field on fire.

## David sees Absalom again

## 2 Samuel 14:31

Joab came to Absalom’s house.

Joab asked, “Why did your servants burn my field?”

## 2 Samuel 14:32

Absalom answered, “I sent for you.

I wanted you to ask the king why I came back from Geshur.

I would have been better off there.

Now let me see the king.

If I am guilty, let him put me to death.”

## 2 Samuel 14:33

Joab went and spoke to David.

David sent for Absalom.

Absalom came and bowed with his face to the ground.

Then David kissed his son.

**Pause and answer**

What did David do when Absalom bowed before him?
