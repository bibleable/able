# 2 Samuel 13 — Tamar, Amnon, and Absalom

**About this chapter**

Amnon rapes his sister Tamar. Tamar speaks about the harm he does to her. Later, Absalom orders his men to kill Amnon.

## Amnon talks to Jonadab

## 2 Samuel 13:1

David had two sons named Amnon and Absalom.

After some time, Amnon fell in love with Absalom’s beautiful sister Tamar.

## 2 Samuel 13:2

Tamar was also Amnon’s sister.

She was a virgin. She had never had sex.

Amnon thought he could never have her.

He wanted her so much that he felt sick.

## 2 Samuel 13:3

Amnon had a clever friend named Jonadab.

Jonadab’s father was Shimeah, David’s brother.

## 2 Samuel 13:4

Jonadab asked Amnon, “You are the king’s son.

Why are you so sad every morning?

Please tell me.”

Amnon said, “I love Tamar, my brother Absalom’s sister.”

## 2 Samuel 13:5

Jonadab suggested that Amnon pretend to be sick in bed.

When David visited, Amnon could ask for Tamar.

He could ask to watch her cook and eat from her hand.

## David sends Tamar to make food

## 2 Samuel 13:6

Amnon lay in bed and pretended to be sick.

King David came to see him.

Amnon said, “Please let my sister Tamar make two cakes here.

I want to watch and eat from her hand.”

## 2 Samuel 13:7

David sent Tamar a message at the palace.

“Please go to your brother Amnon’s house.

Prepare a meal for him.”

## 2 Samuel 13:8

Tamar went to Amnon’s house.

Amnon was lying down.

While he watched, Tamar worked the dough with her hands.

She shaped it into cakes and baked them.

## 2 Samuel 13:9

Tamar put the pan in front of Amnon.

Amnon refused to eat.

He told everyone else to leave, and they left.

## 2 Samuel 13:10

Amnon asked Tamar to bring the food into his bedroom.

He said he wanted to eat from her hand.

Tamar brought him the cakes she had made.

## Tamar tells Amnon to stop

## 2 Samuel 13:11

As Tamar offered the food, Amnon grabbed her.

He said, “Come have sex with me, my sister.”

## 2 Samuel 13:12

Tamar cried, “No, my brother!

Do not force me!

No one in Israel should do such a thing.

Do not do this shameful thing!

## 2 Samuel 13:13

How could I ever escape the shame?

Everyone in Israel would think you were a fool.

Please speak to the king.

He would let you marry me.”

## 2 Samuel 13:14

Amnon refused to listen to Tamar.

He was stronger than she was.

He raped her. He forced her to have sex.

## Amnon sends Tamar away

## 2 Samuel 13:15

Then Amnon hated Tamar.

His hatred was stronger than the love he once felt.

He told her, “Get up! Go away!”

## 2 Samuel 13:16

Tamar said, “No! You have already done me great harm.

Sending me away is even worse!”

Again, Amnon refused to listen.

## 2 Samuel 13:17

Amnon called his servant.

He said, “Throw this woman out.

Lock the door behind her.”

## 2 Samuel 13:18

The servant threw Tamar out and locked the door.

Tamar wore a robe of many colors.

The king’s daughters who were virgins wore robes like hers.

## Tamar goes to Absalom’s house

## 2 Samuel 13:19

Tamar put ashes on her head and tore her robe.

She put her hand on her head.

She walked away crying out loud.

## 2 Samuel 13:20

Absalom asked Tamar, “Has your brother Amnon had sex with you?”

He said, “Keep quiet for now, my sister.

He is your brother. Try not to dwell on this.”

Tamar lived in her brother Absalom’s house, lonely and deeply unhappy.

## 2 Samuel 13:21

King David heard what had happened.

He was very angry.

## 2 Samuel 13:22

Absalom said nothing at all to Amnon.

He hated Amnon for raping his sister Tamar.

## Two years later, Absalom invites David’s sons

## 2 Samuel 13:23

Two years passed.

Absalom had workers cutting wool from his sheep.

They worked at Baal-hazor, near Ephraim.

Absalom invited all the king’s sons to come.

## 2 Samuel 13:24

Absalom went to King David.

He said, “I have hired men to cut the wool.

Please come with your servants.”

## 2 Samuel 13:25

David said, “No, my son.

Hosting all of us would be too much for you.”

Absalom urged David to come, but David refused.

David gave Absalom his blessing.

## 2 Samuel 13:26

Absalom asked, “Then please let my brother Amnon come.”

David asked, “Why should Amnon go with you?”

## 2 Samuel 13:27

Absalom kept asking.

So David sent Amnon and his other sons.

## Absalom orders Amnon’s death

## 2 Samuel 13:28

Absalom had ordered his men to kill Amnon at his word.

He wanted it done when Amnon was cheerful from drinking wine.

Absalom told his men to be brave.

He reminded them that he had given the order.

## 2 Samuel 13:29

Absalom’s men killed Amnon as Absalom had ordered.

The king’s other sons got up.

Each rode away on his own mule.

## David hears a false report

## 2 Samuel 13:30

While the sons were traveling, David heard a report.

Someone said, “Absalom has killed all the king’s sons!

None are left alive!”

## 2 Samuel 13:31

David stood up and tore his clothes.

Then he lay on the ground.

His servants stood beside him with their clothes torn.

## 2 Samuel 13:32

Jonadab, the son of David’s brother Shimeah, spoke.

He said, “My king, only Amnon has died.

Absalom planned this from the day Amnon raped Tamar.

## 2 Samuel 13:33

My king, put that report out of your mind.

Only Amnon has died.”

**Pause and answer**

## David’s other sons return

## 2 Samuel 13:34

Meanwhile, Absalom had run away.

A young man on guard saw many people coming.

They were on the western road beside the hill.

He told David, “People are coming from the direction of Horonaim.

They are walking along the hillside.”

## 2 Samuel 13:35

Jonadab told David, “Look! Your sons are here.

It is just as I told you.”

## 2 Samuel 13:36

Just then David’s sons came in, crying out loud.

David and all his servants wept bitterly too.

## Absalom stays in Geshur

## 2 Samuel 13:37

Absalom fled to Talmai, the king of Geshur.

Talmai was Ammihud’s son.

David grieved for his son Amnon every day.

## 2 Samuel 13:38

Absalom stayed in Geshur for three years.

## 2 Samuel 13:39

In time David found comfort after Amnon’s death.

David longed to go to Absalom.
