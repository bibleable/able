# 2 Samuel 7 — The LORD makes a promise to David

**About this chapter**

David wants to build a house for God. God sends Nathan back to David with a promise about David’s family.

## David talks to Nathan

## 2 Samuel 7:1

King David settled into his palace.

The LORD had given him peace from every enemy around him.

## 2 Samuel 7:2

David spoke to Nathan the prophet.

David said, “I live in a house made of cedar wood.

But the ark of God stays in a tent.”

**Reading help**

A prophet brings people a message from God. The ark was a sacred chest.

![A tent on the left and a house on the right.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

**Pause and answer**

Where did the ark stay?

## 2 Samuel 7:3

Nathan told David, “Go ahead with your plan.

The LORD is with you.”

## The LORD answers David

## 2 Samuel 7:4

That night the LORD spoke to Nathan.

## 2 Samuel 7:5

The LORD said, “Go to my servant David.

Give him this message from me.

David, are you the one to build me a house to live in?

## 2 Samuel 7:6

I brought the people of Israel out of Egypt.

Since then, I have never lived in a house.

My tent has moved from place to place.

## 2 Samuel 7:7

I traveled with my people Israel.

I chose leaders to care for them.

Did I ever ask those leaders to build me a cedar house?”

## The LORD remembers David in the fields

## 2 Samuel 7:8

The LORD told Nathan to give David this message too.

The LORD who commands armies said, “David, you used to look after sheep.

I took you from the fields.

I made you ruler over my people Israel.

## 2 Samuel 7:9

I went with you wherever you went.

I got rid of the enemies you faced.

I will make you as well known as the greatest people in the land.

## 2 Samuel 7:10

I will give my people Israel a place of their own.

I will plant them there like a tree.

They will live there in peace.

Wicked people used to hurt them.

**Reading help**

A tree puts down roots in one place. God promises his people a place where they can stay.

## 2 Samuel 7:11

They kept hurting Israel while my chosen judges led the people.

I will give you peace from all your enemies.

I, the LORD, will build a house for you.

**Reading help**

David wanted to build a house for God. Now God promises David a “house.” Here “house” means a family whose members will rule as kings.

## The LORD speaks about David’s son

## 2 Samuel 7:12

One day you will die, as your ancestors did.

Then I will make your own son king after you.

I will make his kingdom strong.

## 2 Samuel 7:13

Your son will build a house where people will honor me.

I will make his royal throne last forever.

**Reading help**

A throne is a king’s seat. Here the throne stands for the power to rule.

## 2 Samuel 7:14

I will be his father.

He will be my son.

When he does wrong, I will correct him.

I will let people strike him with rods.

## 2 Samuel 7:15

But I will always love him.

I took my faithful love away from Saul.

I removed Saul so you could rule.

## 2 Samuel 7:16

Your family and your kingdom will last forever before me.

Your family will always have the right to rule.”

## 2 Samuel 7:17

Nathan told David everything the LORD had shown him.

**Pause and answer**

Who will build the house where people will honor God?

## David sits down to pray

## 2 Samuel 7:18

King David went in and sat before the LORD.

David prayed, “Lord GOD, who am I?

Who is my family, that you have done so much for us?

## 2 Samuel 7:19

Lord GOD, that seemed a small thing to you.

You even told me about my family’s future.

Lord GOD, is this how you usually treat people?

## 2 Samuel 7:20

What more can I say to you?

Lord GOD, you know me, your servant.

## 2 Samuel 7:21

You did this great thing because you gave your word.

You did what you wanted to do.

You told me about it.

## David remembers what God did for Israel

## 2 Samuel 7:22

Lord GOD, how great you are!

No one is like you.

From all we have heard, you alone are God.

## 2 Samuel 7:23

What other nation is like your people Israel?

You went to Egypt and set your people free.

You made them your own.

You did great things that filled people with wonder.

You drove out nations and their gods ahead of Israel.

These things made your name known.

## 2 Samuel 7:24

You made Israel your own people forever.

LORD, you became their God.

## David asks God to keep his promise

## 2 Samuel 7:25

LORD God, make your promise to me and my family stand forever.

Please do what you promised.

## 2 Samuel 7:26

Then people will honor your name forever.

They will say, ‘The LORD who commands armies is God over Israel.’

My family will continue to rule before you.

## 2 Samuel 7:27

LORD who commands armies, you are Israel’s God.

You told me, ‘I will build a house for you.’

Your promise gave me courage to pray to you.

## 2 Samuel 7:28

Lord GOD, you are God.

Your words are true.

You promised to do this good thing for me.

## 2 Samuel 7:29

Please bless my family.

Let my family continue forever before you.

Lord GOD, you have given your word.

With your blessing, my family will be blessed forever.”

**Pause and answer**

What did David ask God to do for his family?
