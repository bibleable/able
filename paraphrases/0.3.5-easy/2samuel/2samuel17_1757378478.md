# 2 Samuel 17 — David receives a warning

**About this chapter**

Two men give Absalom different advice about David. A warning reaches David while his people are near the Jordan River.

## Ahithophel offers to go after David

## 2 Samuel 17:1

Ahithophel spoke to Absalom.

He offered to take 12,000 men after David that night.

## 2 Samuel 17:2

Ahithophel said David was tired and weak.

He thought David’s people would run away in fear.

He wanted to kill only David.

## 2 Samuel 17:3

Ahithophel said, “I will bring the people back to you.

They will return like a bride coming back to her husband.

You want only one man dead.

Then everyone else will have peace.”

## 2 Samuel 17:4

Absalom liked this advice.

So did all the elders who led Israel.

## Absalom asks Hushai for advice

## 2 Samuel 17:5

Absalom said, “Call Hushai the Archite too.

Let us hear what he thinks.”

## 2 Samuel 17:6

Hushai came, and Absalom explained Ahithophel’s plan.

Absalom asked, “Should we do this?

What would you suggest?”

## 2 Samuel 17:7

Hushai answered, “This time Ahithophel has given you bad advice.

## 2 Samuel 17:8

You know your father David and his men.

They are strong fighters.

They are as fierce as a bear whose cubs someone has taken.

Your father has fought many battles.

He will spend the night away from his army.

## 2 Samuel 17:9

By now he is probably hiding somewhere, perhaps in a cave.

Suppose some of your men die first.

People will say, ‘Many of Absalom’s men have died!’

## 2 Samuel 17:10

Then even men as brave as lions will lose courage.

Everyone in Israel knows your father is a strong fighter.

They know he has brave men with him.

**Reading help**

Hushai compares David’s men to a mother bear who has lost her cubs. He wants Absalom to understand how fiercely they will fight.

## Hushai urges Absalom to wait for all Israel

## 2 Samuel 17:11

I advise you to gather people from all over Israel.

Bring them from Dan in the north to Beersheba in the south.

They will be as many as the grains of sand on a beach.

Lead them yourself.

## 2 Samuel 17:12

Then we will find David and fall on him like dew on the ground.

None of the men with him will survive.

**Reading help**

Dew is water that forms in tiny drops on the ground. Hushai pictures a crowd covering the ground all around David.

## 2 Samuel 17:13

Suppose David goes into a city.

All Israel will bring ropes and drag the city into the valley.

We will leave behind less than a single stone.”

## 2 Samuel 17:14

Absalom and the men of Israel agreed.

They said, “Hushai’s advice is better than Ahithophel’s.”

The LORD had decided to make Ahithophel’s good advice fail.

The LORD meant to bring disaster on Absalom.

**Pause and answer**

Whose advice did Absalom choose?

## Hushai sends a warning

## 2 Samuel 17:15

Hushai went to the priests Zadok and Abiathar.

He told them what Ahithophel had advised Absalom and the elders.

Then he told them about his own advice.

## 2 Samuel 17:16

Hushai said, “Send word to David quickly.

He must cross the river before spending the night.

If he stays at the crossing in the wilderness, he and his people may all die.”

## 2 Samuel 17:17

Jonathan and Ahimaaz were waiting at En-rogel.

A servant girl would bring them news.

They would take the news to King David.

They feared someone would see them if they entered Jerusalem.

## Two messengers find shelter

## 2 Samuel 17:18

A young man saw them and told Absalom.

Jonathan and Ahimaaz hurried to a house in Bahurim.

They climbed down into a well in the courtyard.

## 2 Samuel 17:19

The man’s wife covered the opening of the well.

She spread grain over the covering.

No one could see that the two men were there.

## 2 Samuel 17:20

Absalom’s servants asked her, “Where are Ahimaaz and Jonathan?”

She answered, “They crossed the stream.”

The servants searched but could not find the men.

So they returned to Jerusalem.

## David and his people cross the Jordan

## 2 Samuel 17:21

When the servants left, Ahimaaz and Jonathan climbed out.

They went to David and explained what Ahithophel had advised.

They told David, “Get up! Cross the river now!”

## 2 Samuel 17:22

David and everyone with him crossed the Jordan River.

By sunrise, everyone had reached the other side.

**Pause and answer**

Where were David and his people by sunrise?

## 2 Samuel 17:23

Ahithophel saw that Absalom had set his advice aside.

He saddled his donkey and rode to his hometown.

At home he put his affairs in order.

Then he took his own life.

People buried him in his father’s tomb.

## Absalom brings his army across

## 2 Samuel 17:24

David went to Mahanaim.

Absalom crossed the Jordan with the men of Israel.

## 2 Samuel 17:25

Absalom chose Amasa to lead the army in Joab’s place.

Amasa’s father was Ithra the Ishmaelite.

Ithra had married Abigail, the daughter of Nahash.

Abigail’s sister Zeruiah was Joab’s mother.

## 2 Samuel 17:26

Absalom and the Israelites with him camped in Gilead.

## Three men bring food to David

## 2 Samuel 17:27

Three men met David at Mahanaim.

Shobi, the son of Nahash, came from Rabbah in Ammon.

Machir, the son of Ammiel, came from Lo-debar.

Barzillai came from Rogelim in Gilead.

## 2 Samuel 17:28

They brought beds, bowls, and clay pots.

They brought wheat, barley, flour, and roasted grain.

They also brought beans and lentils.

![A stalk with grains growing at the top.](../pictures/grain.svg)

**Reading help**

Wheat and barley are plants. People eat their grains.

## 2 Samuel 17:29

They brought honey, thickened milk, sheep, and cheese from their cattle.

They wanted David and his people to have food.

They said, “The people are hungry, tired, and thirsty after traveling through the wilderness.”

Why did the three men bring food?
