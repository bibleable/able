# 2 Samuel 22 — David sings to the LORD who rescued him

**About this chapter**

David sings about danger and rescue. In his song he calls God a rock, a shield, and a lamp.

## David calls the LORD his rock

## 2 Samuel 22:1

The LORD saved David from Saul and all his enemies.

That day David sang this song to the LORD.

## 2 Samuel 22:2

David sang, “The LORD is my rock.

He is my fortress. He rescues me.

## 2 Samuel 22:3

God is my rock. I run to him for safety.

He is my shield and the strong horn that saves me.

My God, you are my safe place and my Savior.

You save me from people who hurt me.

**Reading help**

A fortress is a strong place where people can shelter. A shield protects someone from harm. A strong animal uses its horn to defend itself. David uses these pictures to sing about God’s protection.

## 2 Samuel 22:4

I will call to the LORD.

He deserves praise.

He will save me from my enemies.

## David cries out for help

## 2 Samuel 22:5

Death swept over me like waves.

Trouble rushed over me like a flood.

## 2 Samuel 22:6

The ropes of the grave tangled around me.

Death was like a trap in my path.

## 2 Samuel 22:7

In trouble I called to the LORD.

I cried out to my God.

He heard my voice from his temple.

My cry for help reached his ears.

**Pause and answer**

Who heard David when he called for help?

## The earth shakes

## 2 Samuel 22:8

Then the earth shook.

Even the supports of the sky trembled.

God’s anger burned.

## 2 Samuel 22:9

Smoke rose from his nose.

Fire that burns everything came from his mouth.

Hot coals blazed.

## 2 Samuel 22:10

He opened the sky and came down.

Dark clouds lay beneath his feet.

## 2 Samuel 22:11

He rode a cherub and flew.

He soared on the wings of the wind.

**Reading help**

A cherub is a heavenly being. David pictures God coming swiftly to help, riding on the wind.

## 2 Samuel 22:12

He wrapped darkness around himself like a shelter.

Thick clouds full of water surrounded him.

## 2 Samuel 22:13

Light shone from him.

From that light, burning coals flared up.

## The LORD thunders from heaven

## 2 Samuel 22:14

The LORD thundered from heaven.

The voice of God Most High rang out.

## 2 Samuel 22:15

He scattered my enemies with his arrows.

He sent lightning, and they fled.

## 2 Samuel 22:16

The LORD spoke sharply. His breath blew like a blast of wind.

The deep channels of the sea came into view.

Even the foundations of the earth lay bare.

## God pulls David to safety

## 2 Samuel 22:17

God reached down from high above and took hold of me.

He pulled me out of deep water.

## 2 Samuel 22:18

He saved me from my powerful enemy.

Those who hated me were too strong for me.

**Reading help**

Earlier, danger swept over David like waves. Now he pictures God pulling him out of that water. God has saved him from his enemies.

## 2 Samuel 22:19

They came against me on the day disaster struck.

But the LORD held me up.

## 2 Samuel 22:20

He brought me out into an open place.

He rescued me because he was pleased with me.

## David sings about doing right

## 2 Samuel 22:21

The LORD rewarded me for doing right.

He saw that my hands were clean.

## 2 Samuel 22:22

I followed the LORD’s ways.

I did not do evil by turning from my God.

## 2 Samuel 22:23

I kept all his laws in mind.

I paid attention to his commands.

## 2 Samuel 22:24

I did what was right in his sight.

I kept myself from doing wrong.

## 2 Samuel 22:25

So the LORD rewarded me for doing right.

He saw that I was clean.

**Reading help**

David speaks of clean hands. He means that God saw how he acted.

## David sings about how God treats people

## 2 Samuel 22:26

You are faithful to those who are faithful.

You show those who do right that you do right.

## 2 Samuel 22:27

You show those with pure hearts that you are pure.

When people act dishonestly, you are wiser than they are.

## 2 Samuel 22:28

You save people who suffer.

You see proud people and bring them low.

## The LORD lights the darkness

## 2 Samuel 22:29

LORD, you are my lamp.

You light up my darkness.

![An oil lamp gives light in the dark.](../pictures/davids-lamp.svg)

**Reading help**

A lamp helps a person see the path at night. David calls the LORD his lamp because God helps him find his way.

## 2 Samuel 22:30

With your help I can charge against an army.

With my God I can climb over a wall.

## 2 Samuel 22:31

God’s way is perfect.

Every word of the LORD is trustworthy.

He is a shield for everyone who seeks his protection.

## 2 Samuel 22:32

Who is God except the LORD?

Who is our Rock except our God?

## God gives David steady feet

## 2 Samuel 22:33

God is my strong fortress.

He clears my path.

## 2 Samuel 22:34

He makes my feet steady like a deer’s feet.

He sets me on high ground.

## 2 Samuel 22:35

He teaches my hands to fight.

He gives my arms strength to bend a bronze bow.

## 2 Samuel 22:36

You give me your shield to save me.

Your gentle care makes me great.

## 2 Samuel 22:37

You make the path wide beneath my feet.

My ankles hold firm.

**Pause and answer**

What animal does David mention when he sings about steady feet?

## David remembers the battle

## 2 Samuel 22:38

I chased my enemies and defeated them.

I kept going until I had destroyed them.

## 2 Samuel 22:39

I crushed them, and they could not get up.

They fell beneath my feet.

## 2 Samuel 22:40

You gave me strength for battle.

You brought my enemies down beneath me.

## 2 Samuel 22:41

You made my enemies turn and run.

I destroyed those who hated me.

## 2 Samuel 22:42

They looked for help, but no one saved them.

They called to the LORD, but he did not answer.

## 2 Samuel 22:43

I crushed them like dust on the ground.

I trampled them like mud in the streets.

## David rules over nations

## 2 Samuel 22:44

You saved me when my own people fought against me.

You kept me as ruler over nations.

People I did not know will serve me.

## 2 Samuel 22:45

People from other nations bow low in fear before me.

As soon as they hear me, they obey.

## 2 Samuel 22:46

They lose courage.

They come trembling out of their forts.

## David praises the living God

## 2 Samuel 22:47

The LORD lives! Praise my Rock!

Honor God, the Rock who saves me!

## 2 Samuel 22:48

God punishes those who wrong me.

He brings nations under my rule.

## 2 Samuel 22:49

God frees me from my enemies.

You lift me above those who fight me.

You rescue me from violent men.

## 2 Samuel 22:50

So I will praise you among the nations, LORD.

I will sing praise to your name.

## 2 Samuel 22:51

The LORD gives his king great victories.

He shows faithful love to the king he chose.

He loves David and his descendants forever.”

**Pause and answer**

Who else will receive the faithful love God shows David?
