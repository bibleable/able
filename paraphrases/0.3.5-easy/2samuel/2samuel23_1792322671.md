# 2 Samuel 23 — David’s last words and the names of his warriors

**About this chapter**

David speaks about the kind of king God wants. Then the account remembers the warriors who served David.

## David speaks about a just ruler

## 2 Samuel 23:1

These are the last words of David, the son of Jesse.

The God of Jacob had raised him up and chosen him as king.

David wrote songs that Israel loved to sing.

David gave this message from God.

## 2 Samuel 23:2

“The Spirit of the LORD spoke through me.

His words were on my tongue.

## 2 Samuel 23:3

The God of Israel spoke to me.

He is Israel’s Rock.

He spoke about a ruler who treats people fairly and honors God.

## 2 Samuel 23:4

Such a ruler is like sunlight on a cloudless morning.

He is like light on new grass after rain.

**Reading help**

David compares a fair ruler to morning light. Picture the sun shining on grass after rain. The song uses that welcome sight to describe what a good ruler is like.

## God’s promise to David

## 2 Samuel 23:5

My family is right with God.

God made a lasting covenant with me.

Every part of his promise is sure.

God will save me completely and give me all I long for.

**Reading help**

A covenant is a solemn agreement. David trusts God to keep the promise he made to David’s family.

## 2 Samuel 23:6

But people who do evil are like thorns people rake aside.

No one gathers those thorns with bare hands.

## 2 Samuel 23:7

People use iron or the pole of a spear to handle them.

Fire burns the thorns to ashes where they lie.”

## The warriors called the Three

## 2 Samuel 23:8

These are the names of David’s great warriors.

Josheb-basshebeth the Tahchemonite led the group called the Three.

Once he killed 800 men with his spear.

## 2 Samuel 23:9

Next came Eleazar, son of Dodo the Ahohite.

Eleazar was one of the Three.

He went with David to challenge the Philistines at Pas-dammim.

The Philistines had gathered there for battle.

Israel’s men pulled back.

## 2 Samuel 23:10

But Eleazar stayed and fought the Philistines.

His hand grew tired and clung to his sword.

The LORD gave Israel a great victory that day.

The soldiers returned only to take things from the dead.

## Shammah stays in the field

## 2 Samuel 23:11

Next came Shammah, son of Agee the Hararite.

The Philistines gathered near a field full of lentils.

Israel’s soldiers ran away.

## 2 Samuel 23:12

Shammah stood in the middle of the field.

He defended it and struck down the Philistines.

The LORD gave Israel a great victory.

## Three warriors bring water to David

## 2 Samuel 23:13

At harvest time, David was at the cave of Adullam.

Three of his thirty leading men went there to see him.

Philistine soldiers were camping in the Valley of Rephaim.

## 2 Samuel 23:14

David was staying in a place he could defend.

Philistine soldiers held Bethlehem.

## 2 Samuel 23:15

David longed for a drink of water.

He said, “If only someone would bring me water from the well by Bethlehem’s gate!”

## 2 Samuel 23:16

Three warriors broke through the Philistine camp.

They drew water from the well by Bethlehem’s gate.

They brought the water back to David.

David would not drink it.

He poured it out as an offering to the LORD.

![Water pours from a tipped cup onto the ground.](../pictures/water-spilled-on-the-ground.svg)

## 2 Samuel 23:17

David said, “LORD, I could never drink this!

It would be like drinking the blood of those who risked their lives.”

So David refused the water.

This was one of the brave deeds of those three men.

**Reading help**

The water reminds David that the men could have died bringing it to him. That is why he speaks about their blood.

**Pause and answer**

What did David do with the water?

## Abishai leads the Three

## 2 Samuel 23:18

Abishai was Joab’s brother and Zeruiah’s son.

Abishai led the Three.

He killed 300 men with his spear.

He became famous along with the Three.

## 2 Samuel 23:19

People honored Abishai even more than the Three.

He commanded them, though he was never one of the Three.

## Benaiah guards the king

## 2 Samuel 23:20

Benaiah, son of Jehoiada, was a brave man from Kabzeel.

He did many brave things.

He killed two of Moab’s best fighters.

One snowy day he went into a pit and killed a lion.

## 2 Samuel 23:21

Benaiah also fought a very large Egyptian man.

The Egyptian carried a spear. Benaiah carried a club.

Benaiah killed the Egyptian with the Egyptian’s own spear.

## 2 Samuel 23:22

These deeds made Benaiah, son of Jehoiada, famous along with the Three.

## 2 Samuel 23:23

Among the warriors called the Thirty, Benaiah received the most honor.

He never became one of the Three.

David put Benaiah in charge of his guards.

**Pause and answer**

What job did David give Benaiah?

## The warriors called the Thirty

## 2 Samuel 23:24

These men belonged to the Thirty.

Asahel was Joab’s brother.

Elhanan was the son of Dodo of Bethlehem.

## 2 Samuel 23:25

Shammah the Harodite and Elika the Harodite also served.

## 2 Samuel 23:26

So did Helez the Paltite and Ira, son of Ikkesh the Tekoite.

## 2 Samuel 23:27

The Thirty included Abiezer the Anathothite and Mebunnai the Hushathite.

## 2 Samuel 23:28

Zalmon the Ahohite and Maharai the Netophathite also belonged.

## More men who served David

## 2 Samuel 23:29

Heled was the son of Baanah the Netophathite.

Ittai was Ribai’s son, from Gibeah in Benjamin.

## 2 Samuel 23:30

Benaiah the Pirathonite also served.

Hiddai came from the streams of Gaash.

## 2 Samuel 23:31

Abi-albon the Arbathite and Azmaveth the Barhumite also served.

## 2 Samuel 23:32

The list includes Eliahba the Shaalbonite and the sons of Jashen.

Jonathan also belonged to the group.

## 2 Samuel 23:33

Jonathan’s father was Shammah the Hararite.

Ahiam was the son of Sharar the Hararite.

## The last names on the list

## 2 Samuel 23:34

Eliphelet was the son of Ahasbai the Maacathite.

Eliam was the son of Ahithophel the Gilonite.

## 2 Samuel 23:35

Hezro the Carmelite and Paarai the Arbite also served.

## 2 Samuel 23:36

Igal was the son of Nathan of Zobah.

Bani the Gadite also served.

## 2 Samuel 23:37

Zelek the Ammonite and Naharai the Beerothite also belonged.

Naharai carried armor for Joab, the son of Zeruiah.

## 2 Samuel 23:38

Ira the Ithrite and Gareb the Ithrite also served.

## 2 Samuel 23:39

Uriah the Hittite was among them too.

There were 37 men in all.
