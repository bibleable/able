# 2 Samuel 24 — David counts the soldiers and asks for mercy

**About this chapter**

David orders a count of his soldiers. Later he asks the LORD to forgive him and spare his people.

## David asks Joab to count the soldiers

## 2 Samuel 24:1

Once again the LORD was angry with Israel.

He moved David to act against the people.

He told David, “Go and count the people of Israel and Judah.”

## 2 Samuel 24:2

David spoke to Joab, the man who led his army.

“Go through all the tribes, from Dan to Beersheba.

Count the men who can fight.

I want to know how many there are.”

## 2 Samuel 24:3

Joab answered, “May the LORD your God give you a hundred times as many soldiers!

May you live to see it, my king.

But why do you want to count them?”

## 2 Samuel 24:4

David insisted. Joab and the other commanders had to obey.

They went out to count Israel’s soldiers.

## Joab and the commanders travel through the land

## 2 Samuel 24:5

They crossed the Jordan River and camped near Aroer.

Their camp was south of the town in the valley.

Then they went toward Gad and Jazer.

## 2 Samuel 24:6

They traveled through Gilead and the land of Tahtim-hodshi.

They went on to Dan-jaan and around to Sidon.

## 2 Samuel 24:7

They went toward the fort at Tyre.

They visited all the cities of the Hivites and Canaanites.

Finally they reached Beersheba in the dry south of Judah.

## 2 Samuel 24:8

They had traveled through the whole land.

After nine months and twenty days, they returned to Jerusalem.

## 2 Samuel 24:9

Joab gave David the numbers.

Israel had 800,000 fighting men who could use a sword.

Judah had 500,000.

## David asks the LORD to forgive him

## 2 Samuel 24:10

After the count, David knew he had done wrong.

He told the LORD, “I have sinned greatly.

LORD, please forgive me.

I have acted very foolishly.”

## 2 Samuel 24:11

David got up the next morning.

The LORD had given a message to Gad, David’s prophet.

## 2 Samuel 24:12

The LORD told Gad, “Give David this message from me.

I offer you three choices.

Choose one, and I will make it happen to you.”

## Gad gives David three choices

## 2 Samuel 24:13

Gad went to David and explained the choices.

“Do you choose three years with too little food in your land?

Or three months running from enemies who chase you?

Or three days of deadly sickness in your land?

Think it over. What answer shall I take to the LORD?”

## 2 Samuel 24:14

David told Gad, “I am deeply troubled.

Let us fall into the LORD’s hands because he shows great mercy.

Do not let me fall into human hands.”

**Reading help**

To fall into someone’s hands means to be in their power. David trusts the LORD to show mercy when he decides what to do.

## 2 Samuel 24:15

That morning the LORD sent a deadly sickness through Israel.

It lasted until the time he had set.

From Dan to Beersheba, 70,000 people died.

## The LORD stops the angel

## 2 Samuel 24:16

The angel reached out to destroy Jerusalem.

Then the LORD decided to stop the disaster.

He told the angel, “Enough! Take your hand away!”

The angel stood by the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite.

## 2 Samuel 24:17

David saw the angel striking the people.

He said to the LORD, “I am the shepherd who sinned.

I did wrong. What have these sheep done?

Please let the punishment fall on me and my father’s family.”

**Reading help**

A shepherd cares for sheep. David calls himself a shepherd because he leads Israel. The sheep in his prayer are the people he leads.

## David goes to Araunah

## 2 Samuel 24:18

That day Gad came to David.

“Go up to the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite.

Build an altar to the LORD there.”

**Reading help**

A threshing floor was a flat place where workers loosened grain from the stalks. They could then toss it into the air so the wind carried the dry husks away.

![The wind carries away the light husks. The heavier grains fall down.](../pictures/winnowing-barley.svg)

An altar is a place to give offerings to God.

## 2 Samuel 24:19

David went up, just as Gad had told him.

He did what the LORD commanded.

## 2 Samuel 24:20

Araunah saw David and his servants coming.

He went out and bowed with his face to the ground.

## 2 Samuel 24:21

Araunah asked, “My king, why have you come to me?”

David answered, “I want to buy your threshing floor.

I will build an altar to the LORD here so the sickness will stop.”

## Araunah offers a gift

## 2 Samuel 24:22

Araunah answered, “My king, take whatever you need for your offering.

Here are my oxen for the burnt offering.

Use the wooden tools the oxen pull to loosen grain.

Use their wooden yokes for firewood too.”

## 2 Samuel 24:23

Araunah said, “My king, I give you all this.

May the LORD your God accept you.”

**Reading help**

Oxen are cattle that pull heavy loads. A yoke is the wooden bar they wear to pull together.

## David pays for the offering

## 2 Samuel 24:24

David answered, “No, I must pay you.

I will not give the LORD my God a burnt offering that costs me nothing.”

David paid 50 shekels of silver for the floor and the oxen.

## 2 Samuel 24:25

David built an altar to the LORD there.

He gave burnt offerings and peace offerings.

The LORD answered the prayers for the land.

The deadly sickness in Israel stopped.

**Reading help**

People burned a whole animal as a burnt offering. A peace offering included a meal people could share.
