# 2 Samuel 20 — Sheba rebels and a wise woman speaks

**About this chapter**

Sheba leads people away from King David. When the army reaches a walled city, a woman asks Joab to listen.

## Sheba calls Israel to leave David

## 2 Samuel 20:1

A troublemaker named Sheba was there.

Sheba was Bichri’s son, from the tribe of Benjamin.

He blew a ram’s horn and shouted, “We have no share in David!

Jesse’s son has nothing for us.

People of Israel, go back to your tents!”

**Reading help**

Jesse was David’s father. When Sheba says “Jesse’s son,” he means King David.

## 2 Samuel 20:2

The men of Israel left David to follow Sheba.

The men of Judah stayed with their king.

They went with David from the Jordan River to Jerusalem.

## 2 Samuel 20:3

David returned to his palace in Jerusalem.

He had left ten concubines there to care for it.

Now David put the women in a house under guard.

He provided for them but no longer had sex with them.

They lived like widows there for the rest of their lives.

**Reading help**

A concubine lived with a man as his partner. She had less standing in the household than a wife.

## David sends for the men of Judah

## 2 Samuel 20:4

David told Amasa, “Call the men of Judah together.

Bring them to me within three days.

Come with them yourself.”

## 2 Samuel 20:5

Amasa went to call the men of Judah.

He took longer than David had allowed.

## 2 Samuel 20:6

David told Abishai, “Sheba will hurt us more than Absalom did.

Take my men and go after him.

Otherwise he will escape to a city with strong walls.”

## 2 Samuel 20:7

Joab’s men, the Cherethites, and the Pelethites left Jerusalem.

David’s strongest fighters went too.

They all went after Sheba.

## Joab meets Amasa

## 2 Samuel 20:8

Amasa joined them at the great stone in Gibeon.

Joab wore his army clothes.

As he stepped forward, he drew the knife at his belt.

## 2 Samuel 20:9

Joab asked Amasa, “Are you well, my brother?”

Joab took hold of Amasa’s beard as if to kiss him.

## 2 Samuel 20:10

Amasa did not expect Joab to hurt him.

Joab stabbed Amasa, and Amasa died from that single wound.

Then Joab and his brother Abishai went after Sheba.

## The soldiers follow Joab

## 2 Samuel 20:11

One of Joab’s young men stood beside Amasa.

He called, “Whoever supports Joab and David, follow Joab!”

## 2 Samuel 20:12

Amasa lay in his blood in the road.

The young man saw the soldiers stopping there.

He moved Amasa’s body into a field and covered it with clothing.

## 2 Samuel 20:13

Once the road was clear, all the men followed Joab.

They went after Sheba, Bichri’s son.

## Sheba reaches a walled city

## 2 Samuel 20:14

Sheba traveled through the tribes of Israel to Abel-beth-maacah.

He also went through the land of the Berites.

The Berites gathered and followed him.

## 2 Samuel 20:15

Joab’s soldiers surrounded Sheba inside Abel-beth-maacah.

They built a ramp at the city’s outer defenses.

They began pounding the wall to break it down.

## 2 Samuel 20:16

A wise woman called out from the city, “Listen! Listen!

Please ask Joab to come here.

I want to speak to him.”

## Joab listens to the woman

## 2 Samuel 20:17

Joab came near.

She asked, “Are you Joab?”

He answered, “I am.”

She said, “Please listen to me.”

Joab answered, “I am listening.”

## 2 Samuel 20:18

The woman said, “Long ago people used to say, ‘Ask for advice at Abel.’

That was how people settled their arguments.

## 2 Samuel 20:19

I am one of the peaceful, faithful people of Israel.

This city is like a mother in Israel.

Why are you trying to destroy it?

Why destroy what belongs to the LORD?”

**Reading help**

Abel is the short name for Abel-beth-maacah. The woman calls the city a “mother” because other people in Israel have looked to it for guidance.

## Joab agrees to leave the city

## 2 Samuel 20:20

Joab answered, “Never! I have no wish to destroy your city!

## 2 Samuel 20:21

Sheba, Bichri’s son from the hills of Ephraim, has rebelled against King David.

Give me Sheba alone, and I will leave.”

The woman said, “We will throw his head to you over the wall.”

## 2 Samuel 20:22

The woman gave the people her advice.

They cut off Sheba’s head and threw it to Joab.

Joab blew the ram’s horn.

His soldiers left the city and went home.

Joab returned to King David in Jerusalem.

## People who serve the king

## 2 Samuel 20:23

Joab led Israel’s whole army.

Benaiah, Jehoiada’s son, led the Cherethites and the Pelethites.

## 2 Samuel 20:24

Adoram was in charge of people who had to work by force.

Jehoshaphat, Ahilud’s son, kept the king’s records.

## 2 Samuel 20:25

Sheva wrote documents for the king.

Zadok and Abiathar were priests.

## 2 Samuel 20:26

Ira the Jairite served as David’s priest.
