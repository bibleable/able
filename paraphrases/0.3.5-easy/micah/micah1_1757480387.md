# Micah 1 — Micah weeps for Samaria and Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

The LORD shows Micah what will happen to two cities.

Samaria will fall.

Trouble will reach the gates of Jerusalem too.

## Micah calls everyone to listen

## Micah 1:1

Micah came from the town of Moresheth.

The LORD gave Micah a message about Samaria and Jerusalem.

The LORD showed him what would happen there.

Three kings ruled Judah in turn during that time.

Their names were Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah.

## Micah 1:2

Micah says, “Listen, all you peoples!

Everyone on earth, pay attention!

From his holy temple, the Lord GOD will speak against you.

He will tell what he has seen you do.”

## Micah 1:3

Look! The LORD is coming out of his home.

He will come down and walk on the heights of the earth.

## Micah 1:4

Under his feet the mountains will melt.

The valleys will split open.

Think of wax melting beside a fire.

Think of water rushing down a hill.

Micah uses these pictures to show the LORD’s power.

## The LORD speaks against Samaria

## Micah 1:5

All this is happening because Jacob’s family has sinned.

Jacob’s family is the people of Israel.

Where can you see Israel’s sin? In Samaria.

Where do Judah’s people go to worship on the heights?

Even Jerusalem has become such a place.

## Micah 1:6

The LORD says, “I will leave Samaria in ruins.

Only a pile of broken stones will stand in the field.

People will plant grapevines there.

I will roll the city’s stones into the valley.

You will see the foundations where the buildings once stood.”

![Grapes growing on a vine, like the vines people will plant where Samaria stood.](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

## Micah 1:7

The LORD says, “I will smash Samaria’s carved statues.

I will destroy every idol the people worship.

Fire will burn up the pay they collected.

They gathered that pay as a prostitute earns money.

It will again become pay for a prostitute.”

A prostitute is someone people pay for sex.

## Micah cries for his people

## Micah 1:8

Micah says, “Because of this, I will weep out loud.

I will walk barefoot and naked in sorrow.

I will howl like a jackal, a wild animal.

I will cry like an ostrich, a large bird.

## Micah 1:9

“Samaria is like someone with a wound that cannot heal.

The trouble has spread to Judah.

It has reached my people’s city, Jerusalem.

It is right at the gate.”

## Trouble comes to town after town

## Micah 1:10

Keep the news from the people of Gath.

Do not weep there.

People of Beth-leaphrah, roll in the dust.

This shows how deeply you grieve.

## Micah 1:11

People of Shaphir, you must leave naked and ashamed.

The people of Zaanan will stay inside.

People in Beth-ezel are grieving.

You can no longer count on their help.

## Micah 1:12

The people of Maroth longed for something good.

But the LORD sent disaster.

It reached the gate of Jerusalem.

## Micah 1:13

People of Lachish, hitch your horses to your war carts.

You did the same wrong things Israel did.

The people of Zion first learned these sins from you.

Zion is another name for Jerusalem.

## Micah 1:14

So give the people of Moresheth-gath gifts to say goodbye.

Israel’s kings will look to the households of Achzib for help.

Those households will let the kings down.

## Micah 1:15

The LORD says, “People of Mareshah, I will again bring an enemy.

This enemy will take your town.”

Those who bring Israel honor will go to Adullam.

## Micah 1:16

Cut off your hair to mourn for your dear children.

Shave your heads as bare as an eagle’s head.

Your children will have to leave you.

Enemies will take them to live in another land.
