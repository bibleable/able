# Micah 5 — A ruler will come from Bethlehem

**About this chapter**

An army surrounds the city.

God promises Israel a ruler from Bethlehem.

Micah describes how this ruler will care for the people.

## God promises a ruler

## Micah 5:1

Gather your soldiers!

An enemy army has surrounded us.

The attackers will hit Israel’s leader on the cheek with a stick.

## Micah 5:2

God says, “Bethlehem Ephrathah, you are a small town.

You are home to one of Judah’s small family groups.

Yet from you will come a ruler for Me.

He will rule Israel.

His beginnings reach back to days beyond counting.”

## Micah 5:3

For a time, God will leave Israel without help.

That time will end when the woman gives birth.

Then the ruler’s remaining brothers will rejoin the people of Israel.

## Micah 5:4

The ruler will stand and care for his people.

A shepherd cares for sheep.

That is how the ruler will care for Israel.

The LORD his God will give him strength.

The ruler will act with the LORD’s great authority.

His greatness will reach the ends of the earth.

So his people will live in safety.

![Sheep stand inside a pen where a shepherd can keep them safe.](../pictures/sheep-pen.svg)

## The ruler will save his people

## Micah 5:5

This ruler will be our peace.

Assyria’s army will enter our land.

Its soldiers will march through our forts.

Then we will choose seven leaders, even eight, to face them.

Like shepherds, these leaders will look after our people.

## Micah 5:6

These leaders will rule Assyria with their swords.

They will also rule the land of Nimrod by force.

When Assyria’s army crosses our border, the ruler will save us.

## Micah describes the people who survive

## Micah 5:7

Those who survive from Jacob’s family will live among many peoples.

They will be like dew from the LORD.

Dew is water that settles on the ground in drops.

They will also be like rain on the grass.

Dew and rain come without waiting for people to act.

## Micah 5:8

Jacob’s people will also be like a lion among forest animals.

Think of a young lion among sheep.

It knocks the sheep down and tears them apart.

No one can rescue the sheep.

That is how strong Jacob’s people will be among the nations.

## Micah 5:9

People of Israel, you will defeat your enemies.

None of your enemies will be left.

## The LORD speaks to Israel

## Micah 5:10

The LORD says, “On that day, I will take away your horses.

I will wreck your chariots.

Chariots are carts that horses pull into battle.

## Micah 5:11

“I will destroy the cities in your land.

I will tear down all your forts.

## Micah 5:12

“I will put an end to your magic spells.

No one among you will tell fortunes.

Fortune-tellers claim to know what will happen to you.

## Micah 5:13

“I will remove the statues you carved for worship.

I will take away the stone pillars you worship.

You will stop bowing to things you made.

## Micah 5:14

“I will pull up your Asherah poles.

You used those poles to worship the goddess Asherah.

I will tear down your cities.

## Micah 5:15

“Some nations have refused to obey Me.

In My great anger, I will punish those nations.”
