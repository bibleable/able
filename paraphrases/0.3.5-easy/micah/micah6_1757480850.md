# Micah 6 — What the LORD asks of his people

**About this chapter**

The LORD reminds Israel how he helped them.

Micah tells the people what the LORD asks of them.

The LORD speaks to people who cheat their neighbors.

## The LORD asks Israel to answer

## Micah 6:1

Listen to what the LORD says.

“Stand up and tell your side of the case.

Let the mountains and hills hear you.”

## Micah 6:2

Listen, mountains!

Listen, you ancient foundations that hold up the earth!

The LORD says his people have done wrong.

He will tell Israel why.

Micah speaks as if the mountains could hear both sides.

## Micah 6:3

The LORD asks, “My people, what have I done to you?

How have I made you tired of me?

Answer me.

## Micah 6:4

“I brought you out of Egypt.

I set you free from slavery there.

I sent Moses, Aaron, and Miriam to lead you.

## Micah 6:5

“My people, remember Balak, the king of Moab.

Remember what Balak planned against you.

Remember how Balaam, son of Beor, answered him.

Remember your journey from Shittim to Gilgal.

Remember these things so you can see how I did right by you.”

## What does the LORD want?

## Micah 6:6

What should I bring when I bow before God on high?

Should I bring young cattle that are a year old?

Should I burn them as gifts to the LORD?

## Micah 6:7

Would thousands of male sheep please the LORD?

What if I brought enough oil to fill ten thousand rivers?

Should I give up my first child because I sinned?

## Micah 6:8

The LORD has shown you what is good.

He asks you to treat people fairly.

He asks you to love mercy.

Mercy means showing kindness when you could be harsh.

He asks you to walk humbly with him.

That means letting God guide how you live.

## The LORD speaks about cheating

## Micah 6:9

The LORD calls out to the city.

A wise person takes the LORD seriously.

The LORD says, “Pay attention to the punishment that is coming.

Listen to me, the one who ordered it.

## Micah 6:10

“Can I forget the wealth you gained by cheating?

You use a measure that holds too little.

So a buyer gets less than you promise.

I condemn that measure.

## Micah 6:11

“Can I excuse people who cheat with their scales?

They keep false weights in their bags.

Those weights make the scales show the wrong amount.

## Micah 6:12

“The rich people in your city hurt others.

The people who live there tell lies.

They trick people with their words.

## The people will lose what they worked for

## Micah 6:13

“Because of your sins, I have begun to strike you.

The blow will leave you in ruins.

## Micah 6:14

“You will eat and still feel hungry.

You will try to store things away.

You will lose what you store.

Whatever you save, I will give to enemies with swords.

## Micah 6:15

“You will plant seed.

But you will gather no grain.

You will press olives to squeeze out the oil.

But you will have no oil to rub on your skin.

You will crush grapes under your feet.

But you will drink none of the wine.

## Micah 6:16

“You obeyed the rules of King Omri.

You copied what King Ahab’s family did.

You followed their advice.

So I will leave your city in ruins.

People from other nations will laugh at you in scorn.”

**Pause and answer**

How does the LORD ask you to treat people?
