# Micah 4 — The nations will learn to live in peace

**About this chapter**

Micah looks ahead to a time of peace.

People from many nations will come to learn from the LORD.

Micah also speaks to Jerusalem about the trouble facing its people.

## People come to learn from God

## Micah 4:1

In the last days, one mountain will stand above the hills.

The LORD’s house stands on that mountain.

It will be the most important of all the mountains.

People from many lands will come there in crowds.

## Micah 4:2

People from many nations will say to one another,

“Come up to the LORD’s mountain with us.

Let us go to the house of Jacob’s God.

He will teach us how to live.

Then we can do what he teaches.”

The LORD’s teaching will go out from Zion.

Zion is in Jerusalem.

People will hear the LORD’s message from there.

## Micah 4:3

The LORD will settle disputes between many peoples.

Even powerful nations far away will come to him for decisions.

People will hammer their swords into blades for plowing fields.

They will turn their spears into tools for trimming branches.

Nations will stop fighting each other.

People will stop training for war.

## Micah 4:4

Each person will sit under their own grapevine and fig tree.

No one will make them afraid.

The LORD who commands heaven’s armies has promised this.

## Micah 4:5

Other nations may follow their own gods.

We will follow the LORD our God forever.

## The LORD gathers people who were driven away

## Micah 4:6

The LORD says, “On that day I will gather those who limp.

I will bring back those who were driven away.

I will bring back even those I caused to suffer.

## Micah 4:7

“Those who limp will be the people who survive.

I will make those who were driven away a strong nation.”

The LORD will rule over them on Mount Zion.

His rule will last forever.

## Micah 4:8

Jerusalem, you are like a tower guarding a flock.

You are a place of safety for Zion’s people.

You will again have the power to rule as you once did.

## The LORD promises rescue in Babylon

## Micah 4:9

People of Jerusalem, why are you crying out now?

Do you have no king?

Has the one who advises you died?

Your pain grips you like the pain of giving birth.

## Micah 4:10

People of Zion, cry out like a woman giving birth.

You must leave your city and sleep in open fields.

Then you will go to Babylon.

There the LORD will rescue you.

He will set you free from your enemies.

## The nations gather against Zion

## Micah 4:11

Many nations have now gathered against you.

They say, “Let Zion suffer disgrace!

We want to see it for ourselves.”

## Micah 4:12

Those nations do not know what the LORD is thinking.

They do not understand his plan.

Farmers gather bundles of grain on a threshing floor.

There they beat the grain loose from the stalks.

That is how the LORD has gathered those nations in one place.

## Micah 4:13

The LORD says, “People of Zion, get up and thresh!

I will give you horns of iron and hooves of bronze.”

Micah pictures a strong animal trampling the grain.

The LORD will give Zion strength to defeat many nations.

Their wealth and all they gained will be a gift to the LORD.

He is the Lord of the whole earth.
