# Micah 7 — I will wait for the God who saves me

**About this chapter**

Micah looks for people he can trust.

He sees leaders taking bribes and families turning against one another.

Yet he trusts the LORD to hear him.

## Micah looks for an honest person

## Micah 7:1

How sad I am!

I feel like someone looking for fruit after the summer harvest.

I cannot find even one bunch of grapes to eat.

I long for an early fig, but there are none.

![A bunch of grapes on a vine, the fruit Micah looks for after the harvest.](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

## Micah 7:2

Looking for an honest person feels just as hopeless.

The people who loved God have disappeared from the land.

No one does what is right.

People hide and wait for a chance to kill.

They hunt each other like hunters using a net.

## Micah 7:3

They are good at doing evil with both hands.

Rulers and judges demand bribes.

A bribe is a payment to get someone to act unfairly.

A powerful person tells them what evil thing he wants.

Together they make a plan to do it.

## Micah 7:4

Even the best of them hurt others like thorn bushes.

Your watchmen warned you this day would come.

Now the day of punishment is here.

The people do not know what to do.

## Micah 7:5

You cannot rely on a friend or trust a companion.

Be careful what you say even to the woman you hold in your arms.

## Micah 7:6

A son treats his father with disrespect.

A daughter turns against her mother.

A wife turns against her husband’s mother.

Even people in the same home become enemies.

## Micah waits for the LORD

## Micah 7:7

As for me, I will look to the LORD.

I will wait for the God who saves me.

My God will hear me.

## Micah 7:8

My enemy, do not laugh because I have fallen.

I will get up again.

Though I sit in darkness, the LORD will be my light.

## Micah 7:9

I have sinned against the LORD.

I must bear his anger until he speaks in my defense.

He will see that I receive justice.

He will lead me into the light.

I will see him do what is right.

## Micah 7:10

My enemy asked, “Where is the LORD your God?”

She will see what he does and feel ashamed.

I will see people trample her like mud in the street.

## A day for rebuilding

## Micah 7:11

The day will come to rebuild your walls.

On that day your borders will reach farther.

## Micah 7:12

People will come to you from Assyria and the cities of Egypt.

They will come from Egypt all the way to the Euphrates River.

They will come from lands between seas and between mountains.

## Micah 7:13

The earth will lie in ruins because of what its people have done.

## A prayer for God to lead his people

## Micah 7:14

LORD, guide your people with your staff as a shepherd guides sheep.

We are the flock that belongs to you.

We live alone in the woods with pasture all around.

Let us graze in Bashan and Gilead as we did long ago.

## Micah 7:15

The LORD says, “I will show you wonders again.

Remember the wonders you saw when you came out of Egypt.”

## Micah 7:16

The nations will see those wonders and feel ashamed.

All their strength will fail them.

They will cover their mouths with their hands.

Their ears will no longer hear.

## Micah 7:17

They will lick dust like snakes crawling on the ground.

They will come shaking out of their forts.

LORD our God, they will tremble before you.

## God forgives his people

## Micah 7:18

Who is a God like you?

You forgive the people who are left and still belong to you.

You let their wrongs go.

Your anger does not last forever.

You take joy in showing faithful love.

## Micah 7:19

You will show us mercy again.

You will crush our sins underfoot.

You will throw them all into the depths of the sea.

That is how completely you will take our sins away.

## Micah 7:20

You will keep faith with Jacob and show lasting love to Abraham.

Long ago you gave your word to our ancestors.

You will keep that promise.
