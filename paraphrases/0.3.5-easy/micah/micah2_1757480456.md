# Micah 2 — The LORD sees families losing their homes

**About this chapter**

Micah warns people who take homes and fields from other families. The LORD also promises to gather the people of Israel who remain.

## People use their power to take land

## Micah 2:1

Trouble is coming to people who plan evil in bed at night.

At daybreak they carry out their plans.

They do it because they have the power.

## Micah 2:2

They see fields they want and seize them.

They take houses too.

A family loses its home.

Someone loses the land their family handed down.

## Micah 2:3

The LORD says, “Now I am planning disaster for this nation.

You will not be able to pull yourselves free.

When trouble comes, you will no longer walk with your heads held high.

## Micah 2:4

“That day people will make fun of you.

They will sing your sad song back to you.

‘We have lost everything!

The LORD has taken away our share of the land.

He has given our fields to people who betrayed us.’”

## Micah 2:5

When the LORD’s people meet to divide the land, they will cast lots.

Casting lots is like drawing names to decide who gets each share.

No one there will claim a share for you.

## The people refuse to hear the warning

## Micah 2:6

Some people tell the prophets, “Stop preaching these things!

We will never suffer such shame.”

## Micah 2:7

People of Jacob’s family, should you say, “Has the LORD’s Spirit lost patience?

Is this how He acts?”

The LORD asks, “Do My words not help people who do what is right?

## Micah 2:8

“Lately My people have acted like an enemy.

Travelers pass by without expecting harm, like soldiers coming home from war.

You pull their fine robes off them.

## Micah 2:9

“You force women out of the homes they love.

You take My blessing away from their children for good.

## Micah 2:10

“Get up and leave!

This land will no longer be a place of rest for you.

You have made it unclean.

Terrible destruction will follow.”

## Micah 2:11

Imagine a man who tells empty lies.

He says, “I will preach to you about wine and strong drink.”

That is the kind of preacher these people want!

## The LORD gathers His people

## Micah 2:12

The LORD says, “People of Jacob, I will gather you together.

I will bring back those who remain of Israel.

Think of sheep gathered in a pen or a flock together in a field.

That is how close you will be.

The place will be noisy with people.

## Micah 2:13

“Someone will go ahead and break open a way for them.

The people will pass through the gate and go out.

Their King will go first.”

The LORD Himself will lead them.
