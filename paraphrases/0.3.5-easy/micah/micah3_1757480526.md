# Micah 3 — Leaders take money to judge unfairly

**About this chapter**

Micah speaks to the people who lead Israel. They hurt the people in their care. Micah tells them what will happen to Jerusalem because of what they do.

## The leaders hurt their own people

## Micah 3:1

I said, “Listen, leaders of Jacob’s people!

You rule Israel.

You should know how to treat people fairly.

## Micah 3:2

“You hate what is good and love what is evil.

You treat my people like animals being butchered.

It is as if you tear off their skin.

It is as if you strip their bones bare.

## Micah 3:3

“You treat them like meat for a cooking pot.

It is as if you break their bones and eat their flesh.”

Micah uses this picture to show how cruel the rulers are.

## Micah 3:4

One day these rulers will cry to the LORD for help.

He will turn away from them.

He will give no answer because they have done evil.

## Prophets promise peace in exchange for food

## Micah 3:5

The LORD says, “Some prophets lead My people the wrong way.

They promise peace to anyone who gives them food.

They declare war on anyone who gives them nothing.

## Micah 3:6

“Night will come over those prophets.

They will see no more visions.

They will have no way to tell the future.

For them it will be as if daylight has turned black.

## Micah 3:7

“Those who claim to see the future will feel ashamed.

They will cover their mouths.

God will give them no answer.”

## Micah speaks with courage

## Micah 3:8

The Spirit of the LORD fills me with power.

He gives me courage and a sense of what is fair.

I can tell the people of Israel how they have sinned.

## Micah 3:9

Listen, leaders of Jacob’s people, you rulers of Israel!

You hate justice.

You twist everything that is right.

## Micah 3:10

You build Zion by spilling people’s blood.

You build Jerusalem by doing wrong.

Zion is another name for Jerusalem.

## Micah 3:11

Jerusalem’s leaders take bribes.

A bribe is a gift that buys an unfair decision.

The priests charge people for their teaching.

The prophets tell the future for money.

Yet they claim, “The LORD is here with us.

No disaster can happen to us!”

## Micah 3:12

Because of you leaders, people will plow Zion like a field.

Jerusalem’s buildings will become piles of broken stones.

Trees will grow on the hill where the temple stands.
