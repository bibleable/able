# Revelation 6 — The Lamb opens six seals

**About this chapter**

John watches the Lamb open the seals on the scroll.

Listen for the word each living creature calls out.

## The first two seals — A white horse and a red horse

## Revelation 6:1

I watched the Lamb open the first of the seven seals.

One of the four living creatures called, “Come!” Its voice sounded like thunder.

## Revelation 6:2

I saw a white horse.

Its rider held a bow and received a crown.

He rode out to win victories and conquer.

## Revelation 6:3

The Lamb opened the second seal.

The second living creature called, “Come!”

## Revelation 6:4

A bright red horse came out.

Its rider received a great sword.

He had permission to take peace away from the earth.

People would kill each other.

**Pause and answer**

What did the rider on the red horse take away?

## The next two seals — A black horse and a pale horse

## Revelation 6:5

The Lamb opened the third seal.

The third living creature called, “Come!” I saw a black horse.

Its rider held scales for weighing things.

## Revelation 6:6

I heard something like a voice among the four living creatures.

It said, “A quart of wheat costs a denarius.

Three quarts of barley cost a denarius.

Leave the oil and wine unharmed.”

**Reading help**

A denarius was about a day’s pay. Wheat and barley are grains people used for food. Even a small amount cost a worker a day’s pay.

## Revelation 6:7

The Lamb opened the fourth seal.

The fourth living creature called, “Come!”

## Revelation 6:8

I saw a pale green horse.

Its rider’s name was Death.

Hades, the place of the dead, followed close behind.

Death and Hades received power over a quarter of the earth.

People would die by the sword, hunger, disease and wild animals.

**Pause and answer**

What name did the rider on the pale green horse have?

## The fifth seal — A cry for justice

## Revelation 6:9

The Lamb opened the fifth seal.

Under the altar I saw the souls of people who had been killed.

They had held firmly to God’s word and spoken about it faithfully.

## Revelation 6:10

They called out loudly, “Lord, you are holy and true.

How long before you judge the people on earth?

When will you punish those who shed our blood?”

## Revelation 6:11

Each received a white robe and this message.

They must rest a little longer.

Others who served God with them would also be killed.

They were to wait until that number was complete.

**Pause and answer**

What did the people under the altar ask the Lord to do?

## The sixth seal — The earth shakes

## Revelation 6:12

I watched the Lamb open the sixth seal.

A great earthquake shook the ground.

The sun went black like rough cloth made of goat hair.

The whole moon turned red like blood.

## Revelation 6:13

Stars fell from the sky to the earth.

They fell like unripe figs when a strong wind shakes the tree.

## Revelation 6:14

The sky drew back like a scroll rolling up.

Every mountain and island moved from its place.

**Reading help**

![A rolled scroll.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

Picture the sheet rolling up. John uses that movement to describe the sky.

## Revelation 6:15

People hid in caves and among the mountain rocks.

Kings, nobles and army commanders hid there.

So did the rich and powerful, slaves and free people alike.

## Revelation 6:16

They called to the mountains and rocks, “Fall on us!

Hide us from the face of the one on the throne.

Hide us from the Lamb’s anger!”

## Revelation 6:17

They said, “The great day of their anger has come.

Who can stand through it?”

**Pause and answer**

Where did the people try to hide?
