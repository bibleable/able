# Revelation 5 — The Lamb takes the scroll

**About this chapter**

John sees a scroll that no one can open.

Then an elder tells John why he can stop weeping.

## Who can open the scroll?

## Revelation 5:1

The one sitting on the throne held a scroll in his right hand.

A scroll is a long sheet rolled up.

Writing covered both sides.

Seven seals held it shut.

![A rolled scroll.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

Picture this scroll held shut by seven seals.

## Revelation 5:2

I saw a powerful angel.

The angel called out, “Who is worthy to break the seals and open the scroll?”

## Revelation 5:3

No one in heaven could open it or look inside.

No one on earth or under the earth could do it either.

## Revelation 5:4

I began to weep bitterly.

They could find no one worthy to open the scroll and look inside.

**Pause and answer**

Why did John weep?

## John sees the Lamb

## Revelation 5:5

One elder told me, “Do not cry.

Look!

The Lion from the tribe of Judah has won.

He is the Root of David.

He can break the seven seals and open the scroll.”

## Revelation 5:6

Then I saw a Lamb standing at the center of the throne.

The Lamb looked as though someone had killed him.

The four living creatures and the elders surrounded him.

The Lamb had seven horns and seven eyes.

The eyes stand for the seven spirits of God.

God sent them throughout the earth.

**Reading help**

The Lion and the Lamb are names for Jesus in this vision.

The elder calls him a lion who has won.

John sees him as a lamb who gave his life.

## Revelation 5:7

The Lamb came forward.

He took the scroll from the right hand of the one on the throne.

**Pause and answer**

Who took the scroll?

## A new song for the Lamb

## Revelation 5:8

The four living creatures and the twenty-four elders bowed down before the Lamb.

Each held a harp, a musical instrument with strings.

They held gold bowls full of incense.

Incense gives off a sweet smell when it burns.

Here the incense stands for the prayers of God’s people.

## Revelation 5:9

They sang a new song to the Lamb. “You deserve to take the scroll and open its seals.

You were killed.

With your blood you bought people for God.

They come from every tribe, language, people and nation.

## Revelation 5:10

“You made them a kingdom.

You made them priests to serve our God.

They will rule on the earth.”

**Pause and answer**

What do the bowls of incense stand for?

## Every creature joins in

## Revelation 5:11

Then I looked and heard many angels.

They surrounded the throne, the living creatures and the elders.

There were thousands upon thousands, far too many to count.

## Revelation 5:12

The angels called out together in a loud voice. “The Lamb who was killed deserves power, riches, wisdom and strength.

He deserves honor, glory and praise!”

## Revelation 5:13

Then I heard every creature praising God and the Lamb.

Their voices came from heaven, earth, under the earth and the sea.

They said, “Praise and honor to the one on the throne and to the Lamb!

Glory and power belong to them forever!”

## Revelation 5:14

The four living creatures said, “Amen.” The elders bowed down and worshiped.

**Pause and answer**

Who receives praise from every creature?
