# Revelation 11 — Two witnesses speak and the seventh trumpet sounds

**About this chapter**

John tells what happens to two people who speak for God.

Then he hears voices in heaven.

## John measures the temple

## Revelation 11:1

I received a measuring rod like a walking stick.

A voice told me to measure God’s temple and the altar.

I was to count the people worshiping there.

**Reading help**

![A reed, a plant with a long stem.](../pictures/bending-reed.svg)

Picture a long straight stalk used as a measuring stick.

## Revelation 11:2

The voice said, “Leave out the courtyard outside the temple.

The nations have received that space.

They will trample the holy city for forty-two months.

## Revelation 11:3

“I will give my two witnesses power.

They will speak my message for 1,260 days.

They will wear rough cloth called sackcloth.”

**Reading help**

A witness tells what they know to be true. People wore sackcloth to show sorrow.

**Pause and answer**

Which part of the temple must John leave out?

## The two witnesses speak

## Revelation 11:4

The two witnesses are the two olive trees and the two lampstands in the vision.

They stand before the Lord of the earth.

## Revelation 11:5

If anyone tries to hurt the witnesses, fire comes from their mouths.

The fire burns up their enemies.

This is how anyone who tries to hurt them must die.

## Revelation 11:6

While they speak God’s message, they can stop the rain.

They can turn water into blood.

They can strike the earth with every kind of plague whenever they choose.

**Pause and answer**

What can the two witnesses stop from falling?

## The beast attacks the witnesses

## Revelation 11:7

The two witnesses will finish giving their message.

Then the beast will rise from the deep pit called the Abyss.

It will fight the two witnesses, defeat them and kill them.

## Revelation 11:8

Their bodies will lie in the street of the great city.

This is where people put their Lord to death on a cross.

The vision gives the city two picture names, Sodom and Egypt.

## Revelation 11:9

For three and a half days, people will look at their bodies.

The people will come from every tribe, language, nation and people.

They will refuse to let anyone bury the two witnesses.

## Revelation 11:10

People on earth will be glad the witnesses are dead.

They will celebrate and send each other gifts.

Those two prophets had caused them great distress.

**Pause and answer**

Why do people celebrate when the witnesses die?

## The witnesses stand up

## Revelation 11:11

After three and a half days, God breathed life into the two witnesses.

They stood up.

Everyone who saw them was terrified.

## Revelation 11:12

The witnesses heard a loud voice from heaven say, “Come up here.” They went up to heaven in a cloud.

Their enemies watched them go.

## Revelation 11:13

That same hour, a powerful earthquake shook the city.

One tenth of the city fell.

Seven thousand people died in the earthquake.

The survivors were terrified and gave glory to the God of heaven.

## Revelation 11:14

The second woe has passed.

The third time of terrible trouble is coming soon.

**Pause and answer**

Who gave life to the witnesses?

## The seventh angel blows his trumpet

## Revelation 11:15

The seventh angel blew his trumpet.

Loud voices in heaven said, “The kingdom of the world now belongs to our Lord and his Christ.

He will rule forever.”

## Revelation 11:16

The twenty-four elders sat on thrones before God.

They bowed with their faces to the ground and worshiped God.

## Revelation 11:17

They said, “We thank you, Lord God Almighty.

You are here now, and you were here before.

You have taken up your great power and begun to rule.

## Revelation 11:18

“The nations were angry, and now your anger has come.

The time has come for you to judge the dead.

You will reward your servants the prophets and all your holy people.

You will reward everyone who honors you, both small and great.

You will destroy those who destroy the earth.”

## Revelation 11:19

Then God’s temple in heaven opened.

Inside I saw the ark of his covenant.

Lightning flashed and thunder rumbled and crashed.

The earth shook, and a great hailstorm came.

**Reading help**

An ark here means a chest. The covenant is God’s binding agreement with his people.

**Pause and answer**

What did John see inside God’s temple?
