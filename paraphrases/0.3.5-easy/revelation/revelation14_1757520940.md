# Revelation 14 — The Lamb on Mount Zion and the harvest

**About this chapter**

John hears singing in heaven.

Then he hears three angels speak to the earth.

## A new song before the throne

## Revelation 14:1

I looked and saw the Lamb standing on Mount Zion.

With him stood 144,000 people.

They had two names written on their foreheads.

One was the Lamb’s name and one was his Father’s name.

## Revelation 14:2

I heard a sound from heaven like roaring water and loud thunder.

It also sounded like musicians playing harps.

## Revelation 14:3

They sang a new song before the throne.

The four living creatures and the elders heard it.

Only the 144,000 could learn that song.

They were the ones bought back from the earth.

## Revelation 14:4

They are virgins who have never had sex with women.

They follow the Lamb wherever he goes.

They were bought back from among all people.

They are like the first part of a harvest offered to God and the Lamb.

## Revelation 14:5

They tell no lies.

No one can find fault with them.

**Pause and answer**

Who could learn the new song?

## Two angels speak

## Revelation 14:6

I saw another angel flying overhead.

He carried good news that lasts forever.

He had come to tell everyone on earth.

His message was for every nation, tribe, language and people.

## Revelation 14:7

He called out, “Fear God and give him glory!

The time has come for God to judge.

Worship the one who made heaven and earth.

He made the sea and the springs of water.”

## Revelation 14:8

A second angel followed.

“Babylon the great has fallen!

It has fallen!” he said.

“It made every nation drink the wine of its wild sexual wrongdoing.”

**Reading help**

The wine pictures Babylon drawing other nations into its wrongdoing.

**Pause and answer**

Who does the first angel tell people to worship?

## A warning and a promise of rest

## Revelation 14:9

A third angel followed and called out a warning.

“Some people worship the beast and its image.

They accept its mark on their forehead or hand.

## Revelation 14:10

“They too will drink from the cup of God’s anger.

Its wine is at full strength.

Those people will suffer in fire and sulfur before the holy angels and the Lamb.

**Reading help**

Drinking the cup means facing the full force of God’s judgment.

## Revelation 14:11

“The smoke of their suffering goes up forever.

Those who worship the beast and its image have no rest, day or night.

Neither does anyone who accepts the mark of its name.”

## Revelation 14:12

So God’s holy people must keep going.

They obey God’s commands and stay faithful to Jesus.

## Revelation 14:13

A voice from heaven told me to write these words.

“From now on, God blesses those who die belonging to the Lord.” The Spirit said, “Yes.

They will rest from their hard work.

The things they have done will follow them.”

**Pause and answer**

What does the Spirit promise those who die belonging to the Lord?

## The crop is ripe

## Revelation 14:14

I saw a white cloud.

Someone like the Son of Man sat on it.

He wore a gold crown and held a sharp sickle.

**Reading help**

A sickle is a curved blade for cutting ripe crops.

![A stalk of ripe grain.](../pictures/grain.svg)

Look at the grain. Picture a worker gathering the ripe stalks.

## Revelation 14:15

Another angel came from the temple.

He called to the one on the cloud.

“Swing your sickle and gather the crop.

The crop on earth is ripe.

It is time for harvest.”

## Revelation 14:16

The one on the cloud swung his sickle over the earth.

He gathered the earth’s harvest.

**Pause and answer**

Who gathers the first harvest?

## The grapes go into the winepress

## Revelation 14:17

Another angel came from the temple in heaven.

He too held a sharp sickle.

## Revelation 14:18

An angel who had authority over fire came from the altar.

He called to the angel holding the sickle.

“Swing your sickle.

Gather the bunches of grapes from the earth’s vine.

The grapes are ripe.”

## Revelation 14:19

The angel swung his sickle and gathered the grapes.

He threw them into the great winepress of God’s anger.

**Reading help**

A winepress is a place to crush grapes for their juice. Here the winepress pictures God’s judgment.

## Revelation 14:20

Outside the city, feet crushed the grapes in the winepress.

Blood flowed out as high as the straps on horses’ heads.

It stretched for 1,600 stadia, about 300 kilometers.

**Pause and answer**

What does the angel gather from the vine?
