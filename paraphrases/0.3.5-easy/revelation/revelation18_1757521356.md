# Revelation 18 — People mourn the fall of Babylon

**About this chapter**

John hears a warning to leave Babylon.

Then he hears the people who grew rich there mourn the city.

## An angel announces Babylon’s fall

## Revelation 18:1

I saw another angel come down from heaven.

The angel had great authority.

His glory lit up the earth.

## Revelation 18:2

The angel shouted, “Babylon the great has fallen!

It has fallen!” Demons now made their home there.

Every kind of unclean spirit, unclean bird and hateful beast gathered there.

## Revelation 18:3

The nations drank the wine of Babylon’s sexual wrongdoing.

The kings of the earth joined her in that wrongdoing.

Traders grew rich because Babylon spent so much on luxuries.

**Reading help**

John pictures Babylon as a woman. Her wine pictures the way she drew nations into her wrongdoing.

**Pause and answer**

How had Babylon made traders rich?

## A voice calls God’s people out

## Revelation 18:4

Then another voice from heaven called, “My people, leave Babylon!

Otherwise you will share in her sins and suffer her plagues.”

## Revelation 18:5

Her sins have piled up as high as heaven.

God remembers the wrong she has done.

## Revelation 18:6

The voice called for Babylon to receive what she gave others.

Pay her back double for her deeds.

Fill her own cup with a double share.

## Revelation 18:7

Give her as much pain and grief as she gave herself honor and luxury.

She tells herself, “I sit here as queen.

I am no widow.

I will never have to mourn.”

## Revelation 18:8

Yet her troubles will come in one day.

Death, grief and hunger will strike.

Fire will burn her up.

The Lord God who judges her is mighty.

**Pause and answer**

What did the voice tell God’s people to do?

## The kings and traders weep

## Revelation 18:9

Earth’s kings shared her luxury and her sexual wrongdoing.

When they see the smoke from her fire, they will weep and cry aloud.

## Revelation 18:10

Frightened by her pain, the kings will stand far away.

They will cry, “How terrible for Babylon, that great and mighty city!

In just one hour judgment has come upon you.”

## Revelation 18:11

The traders on earth will weep for her too.

No one will buy the goods they bring.

## Revelation 18:12

They brought gold, silver, jewels and pearls.

They brought fine linen, silk, and purple and bright red cloth.

They brought fragrant wood and goods made of ivory and costly wood.

Other goods were made of bronze, iron and marble.

## Revelation 18:13

They sold cinnamon, spices, incense, myrrh and frankincense.

They sold wine, olive oil, fine flour and wheat.

They sold cattle, sheep, horses and carriages.

They even bought and sold human beings as slaves.

## Revelation 18:14

The traders will say, “Everything you longed for is gone.

Your fine things and all your splendor have vanished forever.”

**Pause and answer**

Why will the traders weep?

## Those who grew rich stand far away

## Revelation 18:15

The traders grew rich selling these goods to Babylon.

Afraid of her suffering, they will stand far away.

They will weep and grieve.

## Revelation 18:16

They will cry, “How terrible for the great city!

She wore fine linen, purple and bright red cloth.

She wore gold, jewels and pearls.

## Revelation 18:17

“In just one hour all that wealth is gone!” Ship captains, passengers and sailors will stand far away.

So will everyone who earns a living at sea.

## Revelation 18:18

They will see the smoke from Babylon’s fire.

They will cry, “Was there ever another city like this?”

## Revelation 18:19

They will throw dust on their heads and weep.

“How terrible for the great city!” they will cry.

“Her wealth made shipowners rich.

In just one hour she has come to ruin.”

## The city falls like a stone

## Revelation 18:20

Rejoice, heaven!

Rejoice, God’s holy people, apostles and prophets!

God has judged Babylon for what she did to you.

## Revelation 18:21

Then a powerful angel lifted a stone as big as a millstone.

He threw it into the sea.

He said Babylon would fall with just such force.

No one would ever see the city again.

**Reading help**

A millstone is a heavy stone used to grind grain into flour.

## Revelation 18:22

The angel said musicians would never play there again.

No one would hear harps, flutes or trumpets.

No one would find workers making things there.

No one would hear a millstone grinding grain.

## Revelation 18:23

No lamp would shine there again.

No one would hear a bride or groom there again.

Babylon’s traders had become powerful across the earth.

Her magic had fooled all the nations.

## Revelation 18:24

In Babylon lay the blood of prophets and God’s holy people.

There too lay the blood of all those killed on earth.
