# Revelation 15 — A song beside the sea of glass

**About this chapter**

John sees people singing beside a shining sea.

Then seven angels come out of the temple.

## The people beside the sea

## Revelation 15:1

I saw another great and wonderful sign in heaven.

Seven angels had the seven last plagues.

These terrible troubles would bring God’s anger to its end.

## Revelation 15:2

I saw what looked like a sea of glass mixed with fire.

Beside it stood those who had overcome the beast, its image and the number of its name.

They held harps that God had given them.

**Pause and answer**

What did the people beside the sea hold?

## A song for the King of the nations

## Revelation 15:3

They sang the song of Moses, God’s servant, and of the Lamb.

“Lord God Almighty, what you do is great and wonderful!

King of the nations, your ways are fair and true.

## Revelation 15:4

“Who would not fear you, Lord, and honor your name?

You alone are holy.

Every nation will come and worship you.

Everyone can see that what you do is right.”

**Pause and answer**

Why will all nations come and worship God?

## Seven angels receive seven bowls

## Revelation 15:5

Then I saw the temple open in heaven.

This was the holy tent called the Tent of the Testimony.

**Reading help**

![A tent beside a house.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

Point to the tent. The holy tent was a place to worship God.

## Revelation 15:6

The seven angels carrying the seven plagues came out.

They wore clean, bright linen clothes.

Each had a band of gold cloth around his chest.

## Revelation 15:7

One of the four living creatures gave them seven gold bowls.

The bowls were full of the anger of God, who lives forever.

## Revelation 15:8

Smoke from God’s glory and power filled the temple.

No one could enter until the seven angels had finished bringing the seven plagues.

**Pause and answer**

What filled the temple while the angels carried out their work?
