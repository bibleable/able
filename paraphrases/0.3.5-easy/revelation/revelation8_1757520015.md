# Revelation 8 — Silence in heaven and four trumpet calls

**About this chapter**

John hears silence after the Lamb opens the last seal.

Then the angels prepare to blow their trumpets.

## Silence and the prayers of God’s people

## Revelation 8:1

The Lamb opened the seventh seal.

All heaven was quiet for about half an hour.

## Revelation 8:2

I saw the seven angels who stand before God.

Each angel received a trumpet.

## Revelation 8:3

Another angel came and stood at the altar.

He carried a gold bowl for burning incense.

He received plenty of incense to offer on the gold altar before the throne.

He offered it with the prayers of all God’s people.

**Reading help**

Incense gives off a sweet smell as it burns.

## Revelation 8:4

Smoke from the incense rose from the angel’s hand.

The prayers of God’s people rose with it before God.

## Revelation 8:5

The angel filled the bowl with fire from the altar.

He threw it down to earth.

Thunder crashed and rumbled.

Lightning flashed, and an earthquake shook the ground.

**Pause and answer**

What rose with the smoke before God?

## The first two trumpets — Land and sea

## Revelation 8:6

The seven angels got ready to blow their trumpets.

## Revelation 8:7

The first angel blew his trumpet.

Hail and fire mixed with blood fell on the earth.

A third of the earth burned up.

A third of the trees burned, and so did all the green grass.

**Reading help**

Picture three equal parts. One of those parts is a third.

## Revelation 8:8

The second angel blew his trumpet.

Something like a huge burning mountain plunged into the sea.

A third of the sea turned to blood.

## Revelation 8:9

A third of the living creatures in the sea died.

A third of the ships broke apart.

**Reading help**

![A boat.](../pictures/boat.svg)

Picture the ships at sea when the second trumpet sounds.

**Pause and answer**

What happened to the sea when the second trumpet sounded?

## The next two trumpets — Water and light

## Revelation 8:10

The third angel blew his trumpet.

A great star fell from heaven, burning like a torch.

It fell on a third of the rivers and on the springs.

## Revelation 8:11

The star was called Wormwood.

A third of the water turned bitter like wormwood oil.

Many people drank the bitter water and died.

## Revelation 8:12

The fourth angel blew his trumpet.

A third of the sun, moon and stars lost their light.

A third of the stars went dark.

Darkness covered a third of the day and a third of the night.

## Revelation 8:13

As I watched, I heard an eagle flying overhead.

It called loudly, “Woe! Woe! Woe to the people on earth! Three more angels are about to blow their trumpets.”

**Reading help**

Woe is a cry that warns of terrible trouble.

**Pause and answer**

How many angels still had trumpets to blow?
