# Revelation 12 — The woman, her child and the dragon

**About this chapter**

John sees two signs in heaven.

As you read, notice who helps the woman.

## Two signs in heaven

## Revelation 12:1

A great sign appeared in heaven.

I saw a woman who wore the sun like clothing.

The moon was under her feet.

Twelve stars made a crown on her head.

**Picture the sign**

| Where to look | What John saw |
| --- | --- |
| On her head | A crown of twelve stars |
| Around her | The sun like clothing |
| Under her feet | The moon |

## Revelation 12:2

She was about to have a baby.

She cried out in pain as she gave birth.

## Revelation 12:3

Another sign appeared in heaven.

I saw a huge red dragon with seven heads and ten horns.

The dragon wore seven royal crowns on his heads.

## Revelation 12:4

His tail swept a third of the stars from the sky.

It flung them down to earth.

The dragon stood in front of the woman.

He waited to swallow her baby as soon as she gave birth.

**Pause and answer**

What did the woman wear on her head?

## The child and his mother reach safety

## Revelation 12:5

She gave birth to a son.

He will rule all nations with an iron rod like a king’s staff.

Her child was taken up to God and his throne.

## Revelation 12:6

The woman escaped into the wilderness.

God had prepared a place for her there.

There she would receive food for 1,260 days.

**Pause and answer**

Who prepared a place for the woman?

## The dragon loses his place in heaven

## Revelation 12:7

War broke out in heaven.

Michael and his angels fought the dragon.

The dragon and his angels fought back.

## Revelation 12:8

The dragon lacked the strength to win.

He and his angels lost their place in heaven.

## Revelation 12:9

The great dragon was thrown down to earth with his angels.

This dragon is the ancient snake called the devil, or Satan.

He tricks the whole world.

**Pause and answer**

What other name does John give the dragon?

## A loud voice announces victory

## Revelation 12:10

I heard a loud voice in heaven.

It said, “Now our God has shown his power to save.

His kingdom has come, and his Christ has authority.

Day and night the devil accused our brothers and sisters before God.

Now their accuser has been thrown down.

## Revelation 12:11

“They overcame him through the blood of the Lamb.

They also spoke the truth about what they had witnessed.

They stayed faithful even when it cost them their lives.

**Reading help**

The Lamb is Jesus. His blood points to the life he gave.

## Revelation 12:12

“So rejoice, heavens and everyone who lives there! But trouble is coming to the earth and sea.

The devil has come down to you in a rage.

He knows his time is short.”

**Pause and answer**

Why is the devil so angry?

## The woman escapes the dragon

## Revelation 12:13

The dragon saw that he had been thrown down to earth.

He chased the woman who had given birth to the boy.

## Revelation 12:14

The woman received two wings like those of a great eagle.

She flew away from the snake to her place in the wilderness.

There she received food for a time, times and half a time.

**Reading help**

John describes a period with a set end. Earlier he counted the woman’s time in the wilderness in days.

## Revelation 12:15

The snake poured water from his mouth like a river.

He wanted the rushing water to sweep the woman away.

## Revelation 12:16

But the earth helped her.

The ground opened like a mouth.

It swallowed the river that had poured from the dragon’s mouth.

## Revelation 12:17

The dragon was furious with the woman.

He went to fight the rest of her children.

They obey God’s commands and hold to the testimony of Jesus.

The dragon stood on the seashore.

**Pause and answer**

How did the earth help the woman?
