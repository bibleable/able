# Revelation 9 — John hears the fifth and sixth trumpets

**About this chapter**

John describes what came from a deep pit.

Then he tells what he saw at the sixth trumpet call.

## The fifth trumpet — The pit opens

## Revelation 9:1

The fifth angel blew his trumpet.

I saw a star that had fallen from heaven to earth.

The star received a key to the deep pit called the Abyss.

## Revelation 9:2

The star opened the pit.

Smoke rose as if from a huge furnace.

The smoke darkened the sun and the air.

## Revelation 9:3

Locusts came out of the smoke and landed on the earth.

They received power to sting like scorpions.

**Reading help**

Locusts are insects that can swarm and eat plants. A scorpion is a small animal with a sting in its tail.

**Pause and answer**

What came out of the smoke?

## Limits on the locusts

## Revelation 9:4

The locusts had orders to leave the grass, plants and trees alone.

They could harm only people without God’s seal on their foreheads.

## Revelation 9:5

The locusts could cause pain for five months.

Their sting felt like a scorpion’s sting.

They had no power to kill those people.

## Revelation 9:6

In those days people will look for death.

They will long to die, but death will escape them.

**Pause and answer**

How long could the locusts hurt people?

## The locusts in John’s vision

## Revelation 9:7

The locusts looked like horses ready for battle.

On their heads they wore what looked like gold crowns.

Their faces looked human.

## Revelation 9:8

Their hair looked like women’s hair.

Their teeth looked like lions’ teeth.

## Revelation 9:9

Their chests had coverings like iron armor.

Their wings roared like horses pulling chariots into battle.

## Revelation 9:10

Their tails had stingers like scorpions’ tails.

With their tails they could hurt people for five months.

## Revelation 9:11

Their king was the angel of the Abyss.

His Hebrew name is Abaddon.

His Greek name is Apollyon.

## Revelation 9:12

The first woe has passed.

Two more times of terrible trouble are still to come.

**Pause and answer**

Who was king over the locusts?

## The sixth trumpet — Four angels go free

## Revelation 9:13

The sixth angel blew his trumpet.

I heard a voice from the gold altar before God.

It came from the four horns on the altar.

**Reading help**

The horns are points that rise from the corners of the altar.

## Revelation 9:14

The voice spoke to the angel who blew the sixth trumpet. “Set the four angels free.

They are bound at the great river Euphrates.”

## Revelation 9:15

The four angels went free to kill a third of all people.

They had been ready for this very hour.

The day, month and year had all been set.

## Revelation 9:16

I heard the number of soldiers on horseback.

There were two hundred million.

**Pause and answer**

Where had the four angels been held?

## The horses and their riders

## Revelation 9:17

In my vision I saw armor covering the riders’ chests.

Its colors were fiery red, sapphire blue and sulfur yellow.

The horses had heads like lions’ heads.

Fire, smoke and sulfur came from their mouths.

## Revelation 9:18

Those three plagues killed a third of all people.

The fire, smoke and sulfur came from the horses’ mouths.

## Revelation 9:19

The horses also had power in their tails.

Their tails looked like snakes with heads.

They used those heads to hurt people.

**Pause and answer**

What three things came from the horses’ mouths?

## The survivors refuse to change

## Revelation 9:20

Even after the plagues, the survivors kept worshiping what they had made.

They still worshiped demons and idols.

Their idols were gold, silver, bronze, stone and wood.

Those idols could never see or hear or walk.

**Reading help**

![An eye and an ear.](../pictures/eye-and-ear.svg)

Find the eye and the ear. John says the idols can neither see nor hear.

## Revelation 9:21

The survivors also refused to turn away from murder and theft.

They kept practicing magic and doing wrong sexually.

**Pause and answer**

What did the survivors keep worshiping?
