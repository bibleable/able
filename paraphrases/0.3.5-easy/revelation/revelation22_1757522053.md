# Revelation 22 — The river of life and the promise of Jesus

**About this chapter**

The angel shows John a river and a tree.

At the end John prays for Jesus to come.

## The river and the tree of life

## Revelation 22:1

The angel showed me the river of the water of life.

It was as clear as crystal.

It flowed from the throne of God and the Lamb.

## Revelation 22:2

The river flowed down the middle of the city’s main street.

The tree of life stood on both sides.

It bore twelve kinds of fruit, with a fresh crop each month.

Its leaves bring healing to the nations.

![A river flows between two banks with trees on both sides.](../pictures/river-and-tree-of-life.svg)

John sees the tree of life bearing fruit each month.

**Reading help**

**Follow the river**

Throne of God and the Lamb → River in the main street → Tree of life on its banks

## Revelation 22:3

There will be no curse anymore.

The throne of God and the Lamb will be in the city.

His servants will worship him.

## Revelation 22:4

They will see his face.

His name will be on their foreheads.

## Revelation 22:5

Night will be gone.

No one will need a lamp or sunlight.

The Lord God will shine on them.

They will rule forever.

## The angel speaks to John

## Revelation 22:6

The angel told me, “You can trust these words.

They are true.

The Lord is God over the spirits of the prophets.

He sent his angel to show his servants what must happen soon.”

## Revelation 22:7

“Look!

I am coming soon.

God blesses anyone who obeys the message of prophecy in this book.”

## Revelation 22:8

I, John, heard and saw these things myself.

I fell at the angel’s feet to worship him.

This was the angel who had shown me these things.

## Revelation 22:9

The angel stopped me.

“Like you, I serve God.

So do your brothers the prophets and everyone who obeys this book.

Worship God!”

## Leave the message open

## Revelation 22:10

The angel told me to leave the prophecy in this book open for people to hear.

The time was near.

## Revelation 22:11

He said, “Let those who do wrong go on doing wrong.

Let those who live filthy lives go on living that way.

Let those who do right keep doing right.

Let those who are holy remain holy.”

## Revelation 22:12

“Look!

I am coming soon.

I bring my reward with me.

I will give each person what their deeds deserve.

## Revelation 22:13

“I am the Alpha and the Omega.

I am the First and the Last, the Beginning and the End.”

**Pause and answer**

What will Jesus bring with him?

## An invitation to come

## Revelation 22:14

God blesses those who wash their robes.

They may eat from the tree of life.

They may enter the city through its gates.

## Revelation 22:15

Outside are those called dogs, those who practice sorcery, those who do wrong sexually, and murderers.

Outside too are those who worship idols and everyone who loves and practices lies.

**Reading help**

John uses “dogs” as a picture for people outside the holy city.

## Revelation 22:16

“I, Jesus, sent my angel to bring this message to the churches.

I am the Root of David and his descendant.

I am the bright Morning Star.”

**Reading help**

A root gives life to a tree. Jesus also comes from King David’s family.

## Revelation 22:17

The Spirit and the bride say, “Come!” Let everyone who hears say, “Come!” Let anyone who is thirsty come.

Whoever wants the water of life may drink freely.

**Pause and answer**

Who may drink the water of life?

## A warning and a final prayer

## Revelation 22:18

I warn everyone who hears the prophecy in this book.

If anyone adds to these words, God will bring on that person the plagues described here.

## Revelation 22:19

This book tells about the tree of life and the holy city.

If anyone takes words away from this prophecy, God will take away that person’s share in both.

## Revelation 22:20

Jesus, who gives this message, says, “Yes, I am coming soon.” Amen.

Come, Lord Jesus!

## Revelation 22:21

May the grace of the Lord Jesus be with all God’s holy people.

Amen.
