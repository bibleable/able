# Revelation 7 — God shelters his people

**About this chapter**

John hears how many people receive God’s mark.

Then he sees a crowd too large to count.

## Four angels hold back the wind

## Revelation 7:1

Next I saw four angels at the four corners of the earth.

They held back the winds from all four directions.

No wind blew on the land, sea or trees.

## Revelation 7:2

I saw another angel rising from the east.

This angel carried the seal of the living God.

The four angels had power to harm the land and sea.

The angel from the east called out loudly to them.

## Revelation 7:3

“Wait before you harm the land, sea or trees.

First we must mark the foreheads of our God’s servants with his seal.”

**Reading help**

A seal leaves a mark. Here the mark shows who belongs to God.

**Pause and answer**

Who must receive the mark before the angels act?

## The number John hears

## Revelation 7:4

I heard how many received the mark.

There were 144,000 people from the tribes of Israel.

## Revelation 7:5

From Judah, 12,000 received the mark.

From Reuben, 12,000 received it.

From Gad, 12,000 received it.

## Revelation 7:6

From Asher, 12,000 received it.

From Naphtali, 12,000 received it.

From Manasseh, 12,000 received it.

## Revelation 7:7

From Simeon, 12,000 received it.

From Levi, 12,000 received it.

From Issachar, 12,000 received it.

## Revelation 7:8

From Zebulun, 12,000 received it.

From Joseph, 12,000 received it.

From Benjamin, 12,000 received it.

**Pause and answer**

How many people from each tribe received the mark?

## A crowd no one can count

## Revelation 7:9

Then I saw a crowd no one could count.

They came from every nation, tribe, people and language.

They stood before the throne and the Lamb.

They wore white robes and held palm branches.

## Revelation 7:10

They called out loudly, “Our God sits on the throne!

Salvation belongs to our God and to the Lamb!”

## Revelation 7:11

All the angels stood around the throne, the elders and the four living creatures.

The angels bowed with their faces to the ground.

They worshiped God.

## Revelation 7:12

They said, “Amen!

Praise and glory belong to our God.

Wisdom, thanks and honor belong to him.

Power and strength are his forever.

Amen!”

**Pause and answer**

What did the people in white robes hold?

## The elder explains the white robes

## Revelation 7:13

One of the elders asked me about the people in white robes. “Who are they?

Where did they come from?”

## Revelation 7:14

“Sir, you know,” I answered.

The elder said they had come through a time of great suffering.

They had washed their robes in the blood of the Lamb.

That was how their robes became white.

**Reading help**

The Lamb is Jesus. The robes made white picture how his death cleanses people from sin.

## Revelation 7:15

That is why they stand before God’s throne.

Day and night they serve him in his temple.

God on his throne will shelter them like a tent spread over them.

**Reading help**

![A tent beside a house.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

Point to the tent. Picture coming under its cover for shelter.

## Revelation 7:16

They will never go hungry or thirsty again.

Neither the sun nor the burning heat will hurt them.

## Revelation 7:17

The Lamb at the center of the throne will be their shepherd.

He will lead them to springs of living water.

God will wipe every tear from their eyes.

**Pause and answer**

Who will lead the people to water?
