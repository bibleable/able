# Revelation 3 — Listen to what Jesus says to three churches

**About this chapter**

Jesus gives John three more messages to write.

In each message, “I” means Jesus.

## Sardis — Wake up

## Revelation 3:1

John, write to the angel of the church in Sardis.

I hold the seven spirits of God and the seven stars.

I know what you do.

People say you are alive.

Yet you are dead.

## Revelation 3:2

Wake up!

Strengthen what little life remains before it dies.

My God sees your deeds.

I find you have left your work unfinished.

**Reading help**

Jesus is speaking to people who can hear him.

Waking up here means paying attention and acting on his warning.

## Revelation 3:3

Remember the message you heard and received.

Obey it and turn back to me.

Otherwise I will come when you least expect me, like a thief.

You will not know the hour I will come.

## Revelation 3:4

A few people in Sardis have kept their clothes clean.

They are worthy to walk with me.

They will walk with me dressed in white.

**Reading help**

The clean clothes picture people staying faithful to Jesus.

## Revelation 3:5

The one who overcomes will also wear white.

I will never erase that person’s name from the Book of Life.

Before my Father and his angels, I will say that person’s name.

## Revelation 3:6

Listen to what the Spirit says to the churches.

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus ask the people in Sardis to remember?

## Philadelphia — An open door

## Revelation 3:7

John, write to the angel of the church in Philadelphia.

I am holy and true.

I hold David’s key.

No one can shut what I open.

No one can open what I shut.

**Reading help**

A key opens a locked door.

David was a king.

His key pictures Jesus’ royal authority.

## Revelation 3:8

I know what you do.

Look at the door I have opened before you.

No one can shut it.

You have little strength.

Yet you have obeyed my word and stayed true to my name.

## Revelation 3:9

Some people there say they are Jews, but they lie.

Their gathering serves Satan.

I will make them come and bow at your feet.

Then they will know I love you.

## Revelation 3:10

You obeyed my command to keep going.

So I will keep you from the coming time of testing.

That test will come upon people everywhere on earth.

**Pause and answer**

Who can shut the door Jesus has opened?

## Philadelphia — Hold on

## Revelation 3:11

I am coming soon.

Hold on to what you have.

Let no one take your crown.

## Revelation 3:12

I will make the one who overcomes a pillar in my God’s temple.

That person will never have to leave.

I will write my God’s name on that person.

I will also write the name of my God’s city.

That city is the new Jerusalem.

It comes down from my God in heaven.

I will write my own new name on that person too.

**Reading help**

A pillar is a strong post that stays in place.

Jesus promises a lasting place with God.

## Revelation 3:13

Listen to what the Spirit says to the churches.

**Pause and answer**

How long can the faithful person stay in God’s temple?

## Laodicea — See what you need

## Revelation 3:14

John, write to the angel of the church in Laodicea.

I am the Amen.

I am the faithful witness who tells the truth.

Everything God created began through me.

## Revelation 3:15

I know what you do.

You are neither cold nor hot.

I wish you were one or the other!

## Revelation 3:16

You are lukewarm, neither hot nor cold.

I am about to spit you out of my mouth.

**Reading help**

Lukewarm means only slightly warm.

Jesus uses the taste of a drink to show how strongly he objects to their way of life.

## Revelation 3:17

You say, “I am rich.

I have made plenty of money.

I need nothing.” You cannot see how miserable you are.

You need pity.

You are poor, blind and naked.

## Revelation 3:18

I advise you to buy from me gold made pure by fire.

Then you will be rich.

Buy white clothes from me to cover your shameful nakedness.

Buy medicine to put on your eyes so you can see.

**Reading help**

The people have money.

Jesus uses gold, clothes and medicine to show how much they still need from him.

![Two coins.](../pictures/two-coins.svg)

Look at the coins. Remember why the people thought they needed nothing.

**Pause and answer**

Why does Jesus offer medicine for their eyes?

## Laodicea — Open the door

## Revelation 3:19

I correct and train those I love.

Take this seriously and turn back to me.

## Revelation 3:20

Look!

I stand at the door and knock.

If anyone hears my voice and opens the door, I will come in.

We will share a meal together.

## Revelation 3:21

I overcame and sat with my Father on his throne.

I will let the one who overcomes sit with me on my throne.

## Revelation 3:22

Listen to what the Spirit says to the churches.

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus promise to do when someone opens the door?
