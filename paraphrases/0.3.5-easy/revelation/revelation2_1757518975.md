# Revelation 2 — Jesus speaks to four churches

**About this chapter**

Jesus tells John what to write to four churches.

Read one church’s message at a time.

In these messages, “I” means Jesus.

## Ephesus — Remember your first love

## Revelation 2:1

John, write to the angel of the church in Ephesus.

I hold the seven stars in my right hand.

I walk among the seven gold lampstands.

## Revelation 2:2

I know what you do.

You work hard and keep going.

You refuse to put up with evil people.

Some people claimed I had sent them as apostles.

You tested their claim and found they were lying.

## Revelation 2:3

You have endured hardship because you belong to me.

You have kept going without giving up.

## Revelation 2:4

But I have this against you.

You have left behind the love you had at first.

## Revelation 2:5

Remember how far you have fallen.

Turn back and do what you did at first.

If you refuse, I will come to you.

I will remove your lampstand from its place.

**Reading help**

In John’s vision, a lampstand stands for a church.

## Revelation 2:6

You do have this in your favor.

You hate the things the Nicolaitans do.

I hate those deeds too.

## Revelation 2:7

Listen to what the Spirit says to the churches.

I will let the one who overcomes eat from the tree of life.

That tree grows in God’s paradise, his garden.

**Reading help**

To overcome means to stay faithful to Jesus through trouble.

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus ask the people in Ephesus to do again?

## Smyrna — Be faithful through suffering

## Revelation 2:8

John, write to the angel of the church in Smyrna.

I am the First and the Last.

I died and came back to life.

## Revelation 2:9

I know how much you suffer.

I know you are poor.

Yet you are rich!

I know who is telling lies about you.

Those people claim to be Jews, but their claim is false.

Their gathering serves Satan.

## Revelation 2:10

Do not fear the suffering ahead.

The devil will put some of you in prison to test you.

You will suffer for ten days.

Stay faithful to me even if you die.

I will give you the crown of life.

**Reading help**

A winner received a crown. Here the crown pictures the life Jesus gives.

## Revelation 2:11

Listen to what the Spirit says to the churches.

The second death cannot hurt the one who overcomes.

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus promise those who stay faithful even to death?

## Pergamum — Turn away from false teaching

## Revelation 2:12

John, write to the angel of the church in Pergamum.

I hold the sharp sword with two cutting edges.

## Revelation 2:13

I know where you live.

Satan has his throne there.

Yet you hold firmly to my name.

Even when people killed Antipas there, you kept trusting me.

Antipas spoke faithfully about me in that place where Satan lives.

## Revelation 2:14

But I have a few things against you.

Some of you follow what Balaam taught.

Balaam showed Balak how to lure Israel’s people into sin.

They ate food offered to idols and did wrong sexually.

## Revelation 2:15

Some of you also follow what the Nicolaitans teach.

## Revelation 2:16

So turn back.

Otherwise I will come to you soon.

I will fight those people with the sword from my mouth.

## Revelation 2:17

Listen to what the Spirit says to the churches.

I will give hidden manna to the one who overcomes.

I will also give that person a white stone.

On it there will be a new name.

Only the person receiving the stone will know that name.

**Reading help**

Manna was the food God gave Israel in the desert.

**Pause and answer**

Who will know the new name on the white stone?

## Thyatira — Jesus sees what people do

## Revelation 2:18

John, write to the angel of the church in Thyatira.

I am the Son of God.

My eyes are like flames.

My feet shine like polished bronze.

## Revelation 2:19

I know what you do.

You love, trust, serve and keep going.

You are doing even more now than at first.

## Revelation 2:20

But I have this against you.

You let a woman called Jezebel teach my servants.

She claims to speak for God.

She leads my servants into sexual wrongdoing.

She teaches them to eat food offered to idols.

## Revelation 2:21

I gave her time to turn from her sexual wrongdoing.

She refuses to turn back.

## Revelation 2:22

I will make her so sick she must stay in bed.

Those who commit adultery with her will suffer greatly unless they turn from what she does.

## Revelation 2:23

I will strike her children dead.

Then all the churches will know that I search people’s minds and hearts.

I know what each of you thinks and wants.

I will repay each of you for what you do.

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus know about each person?

## Thyatira — Hold on until Jesus comes

## Revelation 2:24

Now I speak to the rest of you in Thyatira.

You have refused her teaching.

You have never learned what some call Satan’s deep secrets.

I ask nothing more of you than this.

## Revelation 2:25

Hold firmly to what you have until I come.

## Revelation 2:26

Some will overcome and keep doing my work until the end.

I will give them authority to rule over nations.

## Revelation 2:27

They will rule with an iron rod, like a king holding his staff.

They will shatter the nations like clay pots.

My Father gave me this same authority.

**Reading help**

![A clay jar.](../pictures/clay-jar.svg)

Picture how easily a clay jar can break. This helps explain the picture of a king’s power.

## Revelation 2:28

I will also give the morning star to the one who overcomes.

## Revelation 2:29

Listen to what the Spirit says to the churches.

**Pause and answer**

How long does Jesus ask his people to hold on?
