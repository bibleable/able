# Revelation 10 — John takes the little scroll

**About this chapter**

An angel holds a scroll open.

John hears what to keep secret and what to tell.

## A voice stops John from writing

## Revelation 10:1

I saw another powerful angel coming down from heaven.

A cloud wrapped around him.

A rainbow was above his head.

His face shone like the sun.

His legs looked like tall pillars of fire.

## Revelation 10:2

The angel held a little scroll in his hand.

It lay open.

He put his right foot on the sea.

He put his left foot on the land.

**Reading help**

![A scroll.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

Picture the angel holding the scroll open for John.

## Revelation 10:3

His loud cry sounded like a lion roaring.

Then seven thunders answered with voices of their own.

## Revelation 10:4

I was about to write down what the thunders said.

But a voice from heaven stopped me.

“Keep the words of the seven thunders secret.

Do not write them down.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the voice tell John to keep secret?

## The angel raises his hand

## Revelation 10:5

The angel still stood on the sea and land.

He raised his right hand toward heaven.

## Revelation 10:6

The angel made a solemn promise in God’s name.

God lives forever.

He made heaven, earth and sea and everything in them.

The angel said, “There will be no more waiting!

## Revelation 10:7

“When the seventh angel begins to blow his trumpet, God will complete his hidden plan.

He announced it to his servants the prophets.”

**Reading help**

Prophets are people who speak God’s message.

**Pause and answer**

When will God complete his hidden plan?

## Sweet in the mouth, bitter in the stomach

## Revelation 10:8

The same voice from heaven spoke to me again.

“Go to the angel standing on the sea and land.

Take the little scroll that lies open in his hand.”

## Revelation 10:9

I went to the angel and asked for the scroll.

He said, “Take it and eat it.

It will turn your stomach bitter.

But in your mouth it will taste sweet as honey.”

## Revelation 10:10

I took the scroll from the angel’s hand and ate it.

In my mouth it tasted sweet as honey.

After I ate it, my stomach turned bitter.

## Revelation 10:11

Then I was told to prophesy again.

I must speak God’s message about many peoples, nations, languages and kings.

**Pause and answer**

How did the scroll taste in John’s mouth?
