# Revelation 20 — The thousand years and the great white throne

**About this chapter**

John sees an angel lock the dragon away.

Later he sees the dead stand before a throne.

## The angel binds the dragon

## Revelation 20:1

I saw an angel coming down from heaven.

The angel held a heavy chain and the key to the deep pit called the Abyss.

## Revelation 20:2

The angel seized the dragon and bound him for a thousand years.

The dragon is the ancient snake called the devil, or Satan.

## Revelation 20:3

The angel threw Satan into the pit.

He shut it and sealed it over Satan.

Satan could no longer deceive the nations during those thousand years.

After that, Satan must go free for a short time.

**Pause and answer**

How long must Satan stay in the pit?

## People come to life and rule with Christ

## Revelation 20:4

I saw people sitting on thrones with authority to judge.

I saw the souls of people who had spoken about Jesus and God’s word.

Their enemies had cut off their heads for speaking that message.

I also saw those who refused to worship the beast or its image.

They had refused its mark on their foreheads or hands.

They came to life and ruled with Christ for a thousand years.

## Revelation 20:5

The rest of the dead stayed dead until the thousand years ended.

Those who came to life first share in the first resurrection.

**Reading help**

Resurrection means coming back to life from death.

## Revelation 20:6

God blesses those who share in the first resurrection.

They are holy.

The second death has no power over them.

They will serve as priests of God and of Christ.

They will rule with Christ for a thousand years.

**Pause and answer**

Who rules with Christ for a thousand years?

## Satan leaves his prison

## Revelation 20:7

When the thousand years end, Satan will leave his prison.

## Revelation 20:8

He will go out to deceive nations everywhere on earth.

These nations are called Gog and Magog.

He will gather them for battle.

They are as many as the grains of sand on the shore.

## Revelation 20:9

They marched across the earth and surrounded the camp of God’s holy people and the city he loves.

Fire came down from heaven and burned them up.

## Revelation 20:10

The devil who fooled them was thrown into the lake of burning sulfur.

The beast and the false prophet were already there.

There they will suffer day and night forever.

**Pause and answer**

What happened when the nations surrounded God’s people?

## Books open before the throne

## Revelation 20:11

Then I saw a great white throne and the one sitting on it.

Earth and heaven fled from his presence.

There was no place left for them.

## Revelation 20:12

I saw the dead standing before the throne, great and small alike.

Books lay open, including the Book of Life.

The dead faced judgment for their deeds recorded in the books.

## Revelation 20:13

The sea gave up its dead.

Death and Hades, the place of the dead, gave up theirs.

Each person faced judgment for what they had done.

## Revelation 20:14

Then Death and Hades were thrown into the lake of fire.

The lake of fire is the second death.

## Revelation 20:15

Anyone whose name was missing from the Book of Life was thrown into the lake of fire.
