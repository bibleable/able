# Revelation 1 — John hears a voice and turns

**About this chapter**

John hears a voice while he is on an island.

He writes what he sees for seven churches.

## A message to read aloud

## Revelation 1:1

God gave Jesus Christ a message.

It showed what must happen soon.

Jesus wanted his servants to know these things.

So Jesus sent his angel to show his servant John.

## Revelation 1:2

John tells us everything he saw.

This is God’s word and the message Jesus Christ gave.

## Revelation 1:3

God blesses the person who reads this prophecy aloud.

A prophecy is a message from God.

God also blesses those who listen and do what it says.

The time is near.

**Pause and answer**

What does God bless the listeners for doing?

## John greets seven churches

## Revelation 1:4

I am John.

I write to seven churches in the province of Asia.

May you receive grace and peace from God.

Grace is kindness God freely gives.

God is here now, was here before, and is coming.

Grace and peace also come from the seven spirits before his throne.

## Revelation 1:5

Grace and peace come from Jesus Christ too.

Jesus is a witness who tells the truth.

He is the firstborn from the dead.

He rules over all the kings on earth.

Jesus loves us.

He shed his blood to set us free from our sins.

## Revelation 1:6

Jesus made us a kingdom.

He made us priests who serve his God and Father.

Glory and power belong to Jesus forever.

Amen.

**Pause and answer**

Who set us free from our sins?

## Everyone will see Jesus

## Revelation 1:7

Look!

Jesus is coming with the clouds.

Everyone will see him, even those who pierced him.

All the peoples on earth will weep because of him.

Yes, it will happen.

Amen.

## Revelation 1:8

The Lord God says, “I am the Alpha and the Omega.” These are the first and last letters of the Greek alphabet.

The Lord God is here now.

He was here before, and he is coming.

He has power over all things.

**Pause and answer**

Who will see Jesus when he comes?

## A voice behind John

## Revelation 1:9

I am John, your brother.

We belong to Jesus and share in his kingdom.

We face trouble together and keep going together.

I was on an island called Patmos because I had spoken God’s word and told people about Jesus.

## Revelation 1:10

On the Lord’s day, God’s Spirit came over me.

Behind me I heard a loud voice.

It sounded like a trumpet.

## Revelation 1:11

The voice told me to write what I saw on a scroll.

A scroll is a long sheet rolled up for storage.

I was to send it to seven churches.

These were in Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia and Laodicea.

## What John sees

## Revelation 1:12

I turned to see who was speaking.

I saw seven gold lampstands.

A lampstand holds up a lamp.

## Revelation 1:13

Someone like the Son of Man stood among the lampstands.

He wore a long robe.

A wide gold band went around his chest.

## Revelation 1:14

His hair was white like wool, white as snow.

His eyes looked like flames.

## Revelation 1:15

His feet shone like bronze heated in a furnace and polished.

Bronze is a metal.

His voice sounded like the roar of rushing water.

## Revelation 1:16

He held seven stars in his right hand.

A sharp sword with two cutting edges came from his mouth.

His face shone like the sun at its brightest.

**Pause and answer**

What did John see in the right hand?

## Jesus comforts John

## Revelation 1:17

When I saw him, I fell at his feet as if I were dead.

He put his right hand on me.

He told me to have no fear.

He said he was the First and the Last.

## Revelation 1:18

He said, “I am the Living One.

I was dead.

Now I am alive forever.

I hold the keys of Death and Hades.” Hades means the place of the dead.

The keys show that Jesus has authority there.

## Revelation 1:19

Jesus told me to write down what I had seen.

I must write about what is happening now.

I must also write about what will happen afterward.

## Revelation 1:20

Then Jesus explained the stars and lampstands.

The seven stars in his right hand stand for the angels of the seven churches.

The seven gold lampstands stand for the churches themselves.
