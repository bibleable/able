# Lamentations 5 — LORD, bring us back to You

**About this chapter**

The people of Judah pray together. They tell the LORD what they have lost. They ask Him to bring them close again.

## Look at what has happened to us

## Lamentations 5:1

LORD, remember what has happened to us.

Look at the shame we bear!

## Lamentations 5:2

Strangers now own the land our families left us.

People from other lands have taken our homes.

## Lamentations 5:3

We are children whose fathers have died.

Our mothers have lost their husbands.

## Lamentations 5:4

We have to buy the water we drink.

We even have to pay for our wood.

![Cut wood beside a jar of water.](../pictures/wood-and-water.svg)

## Lamentations 5:5

Our enemies stay close behind us.

We are worn out.

We cannot stop to rest.

## Even finding bread is dangerous

## Lamentations 5:6

We put ourselves under the power of Egypt and Assyria.

We did this to get enough bread.

## Lamentations 5:7

Our fathers sinned.

They have died.

Now we suffer the punishment for their sins.

## Lamentations 5:8

Slaves rule over us.

No one comes to free us from their power.

## Lamentations 5:9

We risk our lives to get bread.

People with swords threaten us in the wilderness.

## Lamentations 5:10

Hunger gives us a fever.

Our skin feels as hot as an oven.

## Our music has stopped

## Lamentations 5:11

Men raped women in Zion, another name for Jerusalem.

They also raped young women in the cities of Judah.

## Lamentations 5:12

Our enemies hung our princes up by their hands.

No one shows respect for our elders.

## Lamentations 5:13

Young men work hard grinding grain between heavy stones.

Boys stumble under heavy loads of wood.

## Lamentations 5:14

Our elders no longer gather at the city gate.

Our young men have stopped making music.

## Lamentations 5:15

Joy has gone from our hearts.

We used to dance.

Now we weep.

## Lamentations 5:16

It is as if our crown has fallen off.

We have lost our honor.

What sorrow we feel!

We have sinned.

## Bring us back to You

## Lamentations 5:17

All this leaves us weak with grief.

Our eyes grow dim.

## Lamentations 5:18

Look at Mount Zion.

It lies empty.

Foxes walk about there.

## Lamentations 5:19

But You, LORD, are King forever.

You rule over each new generation.

## Lamentations 5:20

Why do You keep forgetting us?

Why have You left us alone so long?

## Lamentations 5:21

LORD, bring us back to You so we can return.

Give us a fresh start, like the days we once knew.

## Lamentations 5:22

Or have You rejected us completely?

Are You still so very angry with us?
