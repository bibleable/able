# Lamentations 3 — I remember the LORD’s love

**About this chapter**

A man tells God how deeply he hurts. At first he feels that God has shut out his prayers. He remembers God’s love, weeps for his people, and asks God to hear his case.

## I cannot find a way out

## Lamentations 3:1

I am a man who has suffered.

God’s anger struck me like a rod.

## Lamentations 3:2

He drove me away.

He made me walk in the dark, far from light.

## Lamentations 3:3

All day long he turns against me, again and again.

## Lamentations 3:4

My skin and flesh waste away.

It feels as if he has broken my bones.

## Lamentations 3:5

Trouble surrounds me like an army around a city.

Everywhere I turn there is bitterness and pain.

## Lamentations 3:6

He makes me live in darkness, like someone long dead.

## Lamentations 3:7

I feel walled in.

I cannot get out.

Heavy chains weigh me down.

## Lamentations 3:8

Even when I beg for help, he shuts out my prayer.

## Lamentations 3:9

It is as if he has blocked my road with heavy stones.

He makes my paths twist and turn.

## My people laugh at me

## Lamentations 3:10

God seems like a bear waiting for me, or a lion hiding nearby.

## Lamentations 3:11

He dragged me off the path.

He tore me apart and left me helpless.

## Lamentations 3:12

He bent his bow.

I was the target.

## Lamentations 3:13

His arrows went deep into my body.

## Lamentations 3:14

All my people laugh at me.

All day they sing songs to make fun of me.

## Lamentations 3:15

He has filled me with bitterness.

I stagger as if I had drunk my fill of wormwood, a bitter plant.

## Lamentations 3:16

It feels like he has broken my teeth on gravel and pushed me into the dust.

## Lamentations 3:17

I have no peace.

I forget what it feels like when life goes well.

## Lamentations 3:18

I said, “My strength is gone.

So is my hope that the LORD will help me.”

## What gives me hope

## Lamentations 3:19

Remember how I suffer and wander.

My life tastes as bitter as wormwood and gall.

## Lamentations 3:20

I remember it all.

The memory weighs me down.

## Lamentations 3:21

Yet I remember something else.

It gives me hope.

## Lamentations 3:22

The LORD’s faithful love keeps us from being wiped out.

He never runs out of mercy.

## Lamentations 3:23

Each morning you show us mercy again.

LORD, you always keep faith with us!

## Lamentations 3:24

I tell myself, “The LORD himself is my share.

So I will put my hope in him.”

## Lamentations 3:25

The LORD is good to people who wait for him and seek him.

## Lamentations 3:26

It is good to wait quietly for the LORD to save us.

**Pause and answer**

What does the poet remember each morning?

## The Lord still cares

## Lamentations 3:27

It is good to learn to carry a yoke while young.

A yoke is a wooden bar animals carry.

Here it pictures a hard burden.

![A wooden yoke rests across the necks of two oxen.](../pictures/job-yoke.svg)

## Lamentations 3:28

Let the one who carries it sit alone in silence.

The LORD has given him this burden.

## Lamentations 3:29

Let him bow with his face to the dust.

There may still be hope.

## Lamentations 3:30

Let him turn his cheek toward the one who strikes him.

Let him bear the insults.

## Lamentations 3:31

The Lord will not turn us away forever.

## Lamentations 3:32

Even when he brings grief, he will show us kindness.

His faithful love is great.

## Lamentations 3:33

He takes no pleasure in making people hurt or grieve.

## The Lord sees injustice

## Lamentations 3:34

People trample prisoners underfoot.

## Lamentations 3:35

They deny someone a fair hearing in the sight of God Most High.

## Lamentations 3:36

They cheat a person in court.

The Lord disapproves of all these things.

## Lamentations 3:37

Who can give an order and make it happen unless the Lord has ordered it?

## Lamentations 3:38

Both trouble and good things come at the word of God Most High.

## Lamentations 3:39

We are human and we have sinned.

What reason do we have to complain?

## We turn back to God

## Lamentations 3:40

Let us look closely at what we have done.

Let us turn back to the LORD.

## Lamentations 3:41

Let us raise our hands to God in heaven.

Let us turn our hearts to him and say,

## Lamentations 3:42

“We have sinned.

We have turned against you.

You have not forgiven us.”

## Lamentations 3:43

You wrapped yourself in anger and chased us.

You killed without pity.

## Lamentations 3:44

It is as if you wrapped yourself in a thick cloud.

Our prayers cannot get through.

## Lamentations 3:45

Among the nations, you have made us like rubbish people throw away.

## Lamentations 3:46

Our enemies all speak against us.

## Lamentations 3:47

Terror grips us.

We fall into traps.

Everything around us lies in ruins.

## I weep for my city

## Lamentations 3:48

My tears run like streams because my people have been struck down.

## Lamentations 3:49

I keep weeping.

My eyes get no rest.

## Lamentations 3:50

I will weep until the LORD looks down from heaven and sees.

## Lamentations 3:51

What has happened to the women of my city breaks my heart.

## You heard me in the pit

## Lamentations 3:52

My enemies hunted me like a bird.

I had given them no reason.

## Lamentations 3:53

They threw me into a deep pit while I was still alive.

They threw stones down on me.

## Lamentations 3:54

Water rose over my head.

I thought, “I am going to die.”

## Lamentations 3:55

From the bottom of the pit, LORD, I called to you.

## Lamentations 3:56

You heard me beg, “Please listen to my cry for help.”

## Lamentations 3:57

When I called, you came near.

You told me, “Do not be afraid.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the LORD say when the man called from the pit?

## LORD, hear my case

## Lamentations 3:58

Lord, you speak up for me.

You have rescued my life.

## Lamentations 3:59

LORD, you saw how they wronged me.

Give me justice!

## Lamentations 3:60

You saw how they hated me.

You know every plan they made against me.

## Lamentations 3:61

LORD, you heard their insults.

You heard them plotting against me.

## Lamentations 3:62

All day my attackers whisper lies about me.

## Lamentations 3:63

Watch them when they sit down and when they get up.

They sing songs to make fun of me.

## Lamentations 3:64

LORD, you will repay them for what they have done.

## Lamentations 3:65

Let sorrow cover their hearts.

Let your curse fall on them.

## Lamentations 3:66

LORD, in your anger you will chase them.

You will wipe them out from under your sky.
