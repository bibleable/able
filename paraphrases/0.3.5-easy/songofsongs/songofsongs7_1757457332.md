# Song of Solomon 7 — Come with me to the countryside

**About this chapter**

The man describes the woman he loves in pictures drawn from the land. The woman asks him to come outside with her to see what is flowering.

## The man praises the woman

## Song of Solomon 7:1

Daughter of a prince, your feet look beautiful in sandals!

The curves of your thighs remind me of jewels shaped by a skilled artist.

## Song of Solomon 7:2

Your navel is like a round cup always full of mixed wine.

Your waist reminds me of a heap of wheat with lilies all around it.

## Song of Solomon 7:3

Your breasts are like twin fawns, the young of a gazelle.

## Pools, towers, and a mountain

## Song of Solomon 7:4

Your neck rises like a tower of smooth ivory.

Your eyes remind me of the pools in Heshbon beside the gate of Bath-rabbim.

Your nose is like the tower of Lebanon looking toward Damascus.

## Song of Solomon 7:5

You hold your head high like Mount Carmel.

Your hair reminds me of purple thread.

Even a king feels caught in those flowing locks.

![A folded length of purple cloth.](../pictures/purple-cloth.svg)

**Look at the cloth**

Imagine a single purple thread from this cloth. The man pictures many such threads flowing together when he looks at the woman’s hair.

## Song of Solomon 7:6

How beautiful you are, my love!

What joy you bring me!

## A palm tree and its fruit

## Song of Solomon 7:7

You stand tall like a palm tree.

Your breasts remind me of its clusters of fruit.

## Song of Solomon 7:8

I said, “I will climb that palm tree and hold its fruit.” May your breasts be like bunches of grapes.

May your breath smell sweet like apples.

## Song of Solomon 7:9

May your mouth be like the finest wine.

Let the wine flow smoothly to the one I love, passing gently over lips and teeth.

## The woman invites him to come with her

## Song of Solomon 7:10

I belong to the one I love.

He longs for me.

## Song of Solomon 7:11

Come, my love.

Let us go out to the countryside.

Let us spend the night among the wildflowers.

## Song of Solomon 7:12

Let us get up early and walk among the grapevines.

We can see whether the buds and flowers have opened.

We can see whether the pomegranate trees are in bloom.

There I will give you my love.

## Song of Solomon 7:13

The mandrake plants give off their scent.

At our door are good things to eat, both new and old.

I have saved them for you, my love.
