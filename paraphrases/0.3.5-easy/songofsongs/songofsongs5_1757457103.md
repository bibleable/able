# Song of Solomon 5 — This is my love and my friend

**About this chapter**

The man speaks of coming into his garden. Then the woman tells of a knock at night. When the women of Jerusalem ask about the man she loves, she tells them what she sees in him.

## The garden and the feast

## Song of Solomon 5:1

I have come into my garden, my sister, my bride.

I gathered myrrh and spices.

I ate the honeycomb and its honey.

I drank my wine and my milk.

Eat and drink, friends!

Drink your fill, dear ones.

## The woman hears a knock

## Song of Solomon 5:2

I sleep, but my heart is awake.

Listen!

My love is knocking.

“Open the door, my sister, my darling, my dove.

You are perfect to me.

The night dew has soaked my head and my hair.”

## Song of Solomon 5:3

I have taken off my robe.

Must I put it on again?

I have washed my feet.

Must I get them dirty again?

## Song of Solomon 5:4

My love reached for the latch on the door.

My heart beat fast for him.

## She opens the door

## Song of Solomon 5:5

I got up to let him in.

Sweet-smelling myrrh dripped from my fingers onto the bolt as I took hold of it.

**Imagine the scent**

Picture tipping a little scented oil from this jar onto your fingers. The woman describes the smell of myrrh on her hands as she opens the door.

## Song of Solomon 5:6

I opened the door for my love.

He had turned away and gone.

My heart sank.

I searched for him and could not find him.

I called, but he gave no answer.

## Song of Solomon 5:7

The guards found me as they went around the city.

They beat me and left me bruised.

Those men who guarded the walls took my cloak away.

## A message for the women of Jerusalem

## Song of Solomon 5:8

Women of Jerusalem, promise me this.

If you find the one I love, tell him how I long for him.

I feel sick with love.

## Song of Solomon 5:9

Most beautiful of women, what makes your love better than another?

What makes him so special that you ask this of us?

## The woman describes her love

## Song of Solomon 5:10

My love shines, and his face glows red.

I would notice him among ten thousand men.

## Song of Solomon 5:11

His head is like the purest gold.

His hair falls in waves, black as a raven.

## Song of Solomon 5:12

His eyes remind me of doves beside a stream.

I picture them bathed in milk.

They sit as beautifully as jewels in their settings.

## Song of Solomon 5:13

His cheeks remind me of gardens full of spices.

Their scent rises like towers of perfume.

His lips are like lilies dripping with sweet myrrh.

## Song of Solomon 5:14

His arms are like bars of gold set with jewels called beryl.

His body reminds me of smooth ivory decorated with blue sapphires.

## Song of Solomon 5:15

His legs are like marble pillars on bases of pure gold.

He stands tall and grand like the cedar trees of Lebanon.

## Song of Solomon 5:16

His mouth is so sweet to me.

Everything about him is lovely.

Women of Jerusalem, this is the one I love.

This is my friend.
