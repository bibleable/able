# Song of Solomon 6 — Among the lilies

**About this chapter**

The woman tells where her love has gone. The man describes what he sees when he looks at her. Later, other voices enter the song.

## An offer to help

## Song of Solomon 6:1

Most beautiful of women, where has the man you love gone?

Which way did he go?

We will help you look for him.

## The woman answers

## Song of Solomon 6:2

My love has gone down to his garden.

Sweet spices grow there.

He takes his flock to feed in the gardens.

He gathers lilies there too.

## Song of Solomon 6:3

I belong to the one I love.

He belongs to me.

He leads his flock to feed among the lilies.

## The man praises his love

## Song of Solomon 6:4

My darling, you are as beautiful as the city of Tirzah.

You are as lovely as Jerusalem.

You fill me with wonder, like troops carrying their flags.

## Song of Solomon 6:5

Look away for a moment.

Your eyes overwhelm me.

Your hair flows like goats coming down the hills of Gilead.

## Song of Solomon 6:6

Your teeth remind me of sheep just washed.

Each has a matching partner.

Not one is missing.

## Song of Solomon 6:7

Behind your veil, your brow reminds me of a slice of red pomegranate.

## One of a kind

## Song of Solomon 6:8

There are sixty queens and eighty concubines.

Concubines were women who lived as partners of the king.

There are too many young women to count.

## Song of Solomon 6:9

My dove, my perfect one, is one of a kind.

Her mother treasures her above all others.

The young women see her and call her blessed.

Queens and concubines sing her praises.

## Morning light and a flowering valley

## Song of Solomon 6:10

Who is this, shining like the first morning light?

She is lovely as the moon and bright as the sun.

She is as grand as the stars moving together across the sky.

## Song of Solomon 6:11

I went down among the walnut trees.

I wanted to see the flowers in the valley.

Were there buds on the grapevines?

Were the pomegranate trees in bloom?

![A cluster of grapes hangs from a branch of a vine.](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

**Look along the branch**

Find the grapes. In the song, someone looks at the vine earlier in the season, searching for the first small buds.

## Song of Solomon 6:12

Before I knew it, my longing had carried me among the royal chariots of my people.

## A call to return

## Song of Solomon 6:13

Come back, come back, Shulammite woman!

Come back, come back, so we can see you!

Why are you watching the Shulammite woman as though you were watching the dance of Mahanaim?
