# Song of Solomon 1 — Follow the tracks of the flock

**About this chapter**

A woman looks for the one she loves. Listen for the change from “I” to “we” as voices join the song. The song leaves some speakers unnamed.

## The song begins

## Song of Solomon 1:1

This is Solomon’s Song of Songs.

That title means the finest of songs.

## The woman longs for her love

## Song of Solomon 1:2

I long for his kisses!

Your love brings me more joy than wine.

## Song of Solomon 1:3

Your perfume smells sweet.

Just hearing your name is like smelling perfume poured from a jar.

No wonder the young women love you.

![A small perfume jar with a narrow neck.](../pictures/perfume-jar.svg)

**Imagine opening the jar**

Think of a scent you are happy to smell. The woman feels that kind of delight when she hears the name of the one she loves.

## Song of Solomon 1:4

Take me with you!

Let us hurry.

I long for the king to take me into his rooms.

We will be glad and celebrate you.

We will praise your love more than wine.

They are right to love you.

## The woman speaks to the women of Jerusalem

## Song of Solomon 1:5

Women of Jerusalem, my skin is dark, and I am lovely.

Think of the dark tents of Kedar.

Think of Solomon’s beautiful curtains.

## Song of Solomon 1:6

Please do not stare at my dark skin.

The sun has shone on me.

My brothers were angry with me.

They made me look after the fields where grapes grow.

I had no time to care for my own vines.

## Song of Solomon 1:7

Tell me, my love, where do your sheep feed?

Where do they rest at noon?

Why should I cover my face with a veil and wander among your friends’ flocks?

## A reply about the flock

## Song of Solomon 1:8

Most beautiful of women, follow the tracks the flock has left.

Let your young goats feed near the shepherds’ tents.

## Words of admiration

## Song of Solomon 1:9

My darling, you remind me of a beautiful mare among Pharaoh’s chariots.

A mare is a female horse.

Pharaoh is the king of Egypt.

## Song of Solomon 1:10

Jewels hang beside your lovely cheeks.

Strings of jewels adorn your neck.

## Song of Solomon 1:11

We will make gold jewelry for you.

We will set little silver beads in it.

## The woman remembers sweet scents

## Song of Solomon 1:12

The king sat at his table.

The scent of my perfume filled the air.

## Song of Solomon 1:13

My love is like a little bag of myrrh resting between my breasts.

Myrrh has a rich scent.

I keep it close to me.

## Song of Solomon 1:14

My love is like a bunch of sweet henna flowers.

They bloom among the grapevines of En-gedi.

## The lovers answer each other

## Song of Solomon 1:15

How beautiful you are, my darling!

How beautiful!

Your eyes remind me of doves.

## Song of Solomon 1:16

How handsome you are, my love!

What joy you give me!

The soft green grass is our bed.

## Song of Solomon 1:17

We have a house among the trees.

The cedars are its beams.

Sweet-smelling fir trees hold up our roof.

**Pause and answer**

What tracks can the woman follow to find the shepherds’ tents?
