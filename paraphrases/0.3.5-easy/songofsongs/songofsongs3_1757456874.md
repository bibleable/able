# Song of Solomon 3 — Have you seen the one I love?

**About this chapter**

A woman tells of searching the city at night. Then the song turns to a wedding, and a voice invites the women of Zion to see the king. Zion is a name for Jerusalem.

## A search through the city

## Song of Solomon 3:1

At night I lay on my bed.

I looked for the one I love.

I could not find him.

## Song of Solomon 3:2

I decided to get up and search the city.

I went through the streets and the open squares.

I looked for the one I love.

I could not find him.

## Song of Solomon 3:3

The night guards were walking through the city.

I met them and asked, “Have you seen the one I love?”

## Finding the one I love

## Song of Solomon 3:4

Just after I passed the guards, I found him.

I held him close and would not let him go.

I brought him to my mother’s house, into the room of the woman who gave me life.

## Song of Solomon 3:5

Women of Jerusalem, promise me this by the gazelles and the deer in the fields.

Let love sleep until it is ready to wake.

Give love its own time.

## A carriage comes into view

## Song of Solomon 3:6

Who is coming up from the wild country?

From here it looks like a tall cloud of smoke.

The air smells of myrrh, frankincense, and all the trader’s spices.

Myrrh and frankincense come from trees and have a rich scent.

## Song of Solomon 3:7

Look, it is Solomon’s carriage!

Sixty of the strongest men in Israel go with it.

## Song of Solomon 3:8

These men have fought in battles.

Each man knows how to use a sword.

Each carries one at his side to guard against danger at night.

## The king on his wedding day

## Song of Solomon 3:9

King Solomon made his carriage from wood that came from Lebanon.

## Song of Solomon 3:10

He made the posts of silver and the base of gold.

Purple cloth covers the seat.

The women of Jerusalem decorated the inside with love.

## Song of Solomon 3:11

Women of Zion, come out and see King Solomon!

Look at the crown his mother placed on his head.

She crowned him on his wedding day.

How glad he was that day!
