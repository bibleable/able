# Song of Solomon 4 — A garden full of fragrance

**About this chapter**

A man tells his bride what he loves about her. He calls her both “my sister” and “my bride” as words of affection. He pictures her as a garden. At the end she invites him in.

## The man praises his bride

## Song of Solomon 4:1

How beautiful you are, my darling!

How beautiful!

Behind your veil, your eyes remind me of doves.

Your hair flows like a flock of goats moving down Mount Gilead.

## Song of Solomon 4:2

Your teeth remind me of sheep, freshly clipped and washed.

Each tooth has its partner.

Not one is missing.

## Song of Solomon 4:3

Your lips are like a bright red ribbon.

Your mouth is lovely.

Behind your veil, your brow reminds me of a slice of red pomegranate.

## Song of Solomon 4:4

Your neck rises like David’s tower with its rows of stones.

A thousand shields hang there.

They belong to warriors.

## Song of Solomon 4:5

Your breasts are like twin fawns feeding among the lilies.

They are the young of a gazelle.

## Come with me

## Song of Solomon 4:6

Before dawn comes and the shadows disappear, I will go to the mountain of myrrh and the hill of frankincense.

Both names bring sweet scents to mind.

## Song of Solomon 4:7

My darling, every part of you is beautiful.

I see no flaw in you.

## Song of Solomon 4:8

Come with me from Lebanon, my bride.

Come down from the peaks of Amana, Senir, and Hermon.

Leave the places where lions sleep and leopards roam.

## One look from you

## Song of Solomon 4:9

My sister, my bride, you have won my heart.

One look from you did it.

Even one jewel at your neck catches my heart.

## Song of Solomon 4:10

How I delight in your love, my sister, my bride!

Your love brings more joy than wine.

Your perfume smells sweeter than every spice.

## Song of Solomon 4:11

My bride, your lips are sweet like dripping honey.

I think of honey and milk on your tongue.

Your clothes carry the scent of Lebanon.

## The garden and its spring

## Song of Solomon 4:12

My sister, my bride, you are like a garden with a locked gate.

You are like a spring behind a wall, a fountain kept closed.

## Song of Solomon 4:13

I picture a garden of pomegranate trees full of fine fruit.

Sweet henna flowers and a fragrant plant called nard grow there.

## Song of Solomon 4:14

There is nard and saffron, sweet calamus and cinnamon.

Every kind of frankincense tree grows there.

There is myrrh and aloe wood.

The garden holds all the finest spices.

## Song of Solomon 4:15

You are like a spring in a garden.

You are like a well of fresh water flowing down from Lebanon.

![Two springs flow from different places on a hillside.](../pictures/acsah-springs.svg)

**Follow the water**

Find a spring and follow its water down the hill. Picture that fresh water reaching the fruit trees. This is one of the pictures the man uses for his bride.

## The bride answers

## Song of Solomon 4:16

Wake up, north wind!

Come, south wind!

Blow through my garden.

Carry the smell of its spices through the air.

Let the one I love come into his garden.

Let him taste its finest fruit.
