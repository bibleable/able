# Song of Solomon 2 — Come away with me

**About this chapter**

The lovers speak of each other in pictures. When the woman hears her love coming, she remembers his invitation to go outside together.

## A lily and an apple tree

## Song of Solomon 2:1

I am a rose growing on the plain of Sharon.

I am a lily growing in a valley.

## Song of Solomon 2:2

My darling is like a lily among thorns.

That is how she stands out among the young women.

## Song of Solomon 2:3

Among young men, my love is like an apple tree in a forest.

I love to sit in his shade.

His fruit tastes sweet to me.

## Held close

## Song of Solomon 2:4

He brought me to the house where people drink wine.

I picture a banner flying over me.

It tells everyone he loves me.

## Song of Solomon 2:5

Give me raisins to bring back my strength.

Bring me apples to refresh me.

I feel faint with love.

## Song of Solomon 2:6

His left hand rests under my head.

His right arm holds me close.

## Song of Solomon 2:7

Women of Jerusalem, promise me this by the swift gazelles and the deer in the fields.

Let love sleep until it is ready to wake.

Give love its own time.

## Over the hills

## Song of Solomon 2:8

Listen!

My love is coming.

There he is, leaping across the mountains.

He bounds over the hills.

## Song of Solomon 2:9

My love moves like a gazelle or a young deer.

Now he stands just beyond our wall.

He looks through the windows, between the crossing strips of wood.

## An invitation in spring

## Song of Solomon 2:10

My love calls to me.

“Get up, my darling.

Come away with me, my beautiful one.

## Song of Solomon 2:11

“Winter has passed.

The rains are over.

## Song of Solomon 2:12

“Flowers have opened across the land.

The time for singing has come.

Listen to the soft calls of the doves.

## Song of Solomon 2:13

“Figs are ripening on the tree.

Flowers on the grapevines fill the air with their scent.

Get up, my darling.

Come away with me, my beautiful one.”

## A voice among the rocks

## Song of Solomon 2:14

My dove, you are like a bird hidden in a crack in the cliff.

Let me see your face.

Let me hear your voice.

Your voice is sweet, and your face is lovely.

## Song of Solomon 2:15

Catch the little foxes for us!

Our grapevines are in bloom.

The foxes spoil the vines.

![A cluster of grapes hangs from a vine.](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

**Find the grapes**

Trace the branch to the fruit. The song pictures these vines earlier in the season, when they still have flowers. The little foxes could spoil them before grapes grow.

## Among the lilies

## Song of Solomon 2:16

My love is mine, and I am his.

He leads his flock to feed among the lilies.

## Song of Solomon 2:17

Before dawn comes and the shadows disappear, turn, my love.

Leap like a gazelle or a young deer on the mountains of Bether.
