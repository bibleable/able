# Philippians 2 — Care for others as Christ did

**About this chapter**

Paul asks the believers to remember how Jesus served others.

He tells them about two friends who put this into practice.

## Look after each other

## Philippians 2:1

Does belonging to Christ give you courage?

Does his love comfort you?

Do you share life in the Spirit?

Do you feel tenderness and care for each other?

## Philippians 2:2

Then make my joy complete.

Love each other and seek the same purpose.

Be united in what you want to do.

## Philippians 2:3

Give up trying to get ahead or impress people.

Be humble.

Treat other people as more important than yourself.

## Philippians 2:4

Pay attention to what others need as well as what you need.

![Two branches show whose needs deserve attention: your own and other people’s.](../pictures/notice-others-needs.svg)

Paul asks each person to notice what others need.

**Pause and answer**

Whose needs does Paul ask you to notice?

## Jesus chose to serve

## Philippians 2:5

Think and act toward each other as Christ Jesus did.

## Philippians 2:6

Christ had the very nature of God.

Yet he did not treat equality with God as something to grasp for himself.

## Philippians 2:7

He emptied himself by taking the place of a servant.

He became a human being.

**Help with these words**

Paul explains “emptied himself” by describing the lowly place Jesus chose.

## Philippians 2:8

As a man, Jesus humbled himself.

He obeyed God even when it meant dying on a cross.

## Philippians 2:9

So God raised Jesus to the highest place.

God gave him the name above every other name.

## Philippians 2:10

At the name of Jesus, everyone will bow down.

This includes those in heaven, on earth, and under the earth.

## Philippians 2:11

Everyone will say openly that Jesus Christ is Lord.

This will bring glory to God the Father.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus choose to become?

## God is at work in you

## Philippians 2:12

Dear friends, you obeyed when I was with you.

Obey all the more now that I am away.

With deep respect for God, keep living out the salvation he gives you.

## Philippians 2:13

God is at work in you.

He helps you want what pleases him.

He helps you do it too.

## Philippians 2:14

Do everything without complaining or arguing.

## Philippians 2:15

Then you can live as God’s children, pure and free from blame.

People around you have twisted what is right.

Among them you shine like lights in the world.

**Help with these words**

A light shines where it is dark.

Living as God’s children helps others see what is good.

## Philippians 2:16

Hold out the message that gives life.

Then, on the day Christ returns, I can rejoice.

I will know all my running and hard work had a purpose.

**Pause and answer**

How does God help people do what pleases him?

## Paul shares their joy

## Philippians 2:17

Your faith and service are like a gift offered to God.

Even if my life is poured out like a drink over your offering, I am glad.

I share my joy with all of you.

**Help with these words**

People sometimes poured out wine as a gift to God.

Paul uses that picture for giving his own life in God’s service.

## Philippians 2:18

Be glad too, and share your joy with me.

**Pause and answer**

Who does Paul invite to share his joy?

## Timothy cares for the believers

## Philippians 2:19

I hope the Lord Jesus will let me send Timothy to you soon.

His news about you can cheer me too.

## Philippians 2:20

I have no one else like Timothy.

He truly cares about what you need.

## Philippians 2:21

The others look after their own interests.

They give little thought to what matters to Jesus Christ.

## Philippians 2:22

You already know you can trust Timothy.

He worked beside me like a son beside his father.

Together we helped the good news spread.

## Philippians 2:23

I hope to send Timothy as soon as I know what will happen to me.

## Philippians 2:24

I trust the Lord that I too will come soon.

**Pause and answer**

Why does Paul want to send Timothy?

## Welcome Epaphroditus home

## Philippians 2:25

I thought I should send Epaphroditus back to you.

He is my brother and works beside me.

We have faced struggles like soldiers serving together.

You sent him to bring me the help I needed.

## Philippians 2:26

Epaphroditus misses you all.

He is troubled because you heard he was ill.

## Philippians 2:27

He was so ill that he nearly died.

God showed him mercy.

God also showed me mercy and spared me even more sorrow.

## Philippians 2:28

So I am especially eager to send him home.

Seeing him again will give you joy.

Then I will worry less.

| Who went to Paul? | Who is coming home? |
| --- | --- |
| Epaphroditus took help from Philippi to Paul. | Paul is sending Epaphroditus back to Philippi. |

## Philippians 2:29

Welcome Epaphroditus gladly as someone who belongs to the Lord.

Honor people like him.

## Philippians 2:30

He nearly died doing Christ’s work.

He risked his life to help me in ways you could not help in person.

**Pause and answer**

Why does Paul ask the church to honor Epaphroditus?
