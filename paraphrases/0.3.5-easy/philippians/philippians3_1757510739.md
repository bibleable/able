# Philippians 3 — Paul keeps going toward the goal

**About this chapter**

Paul remembers the things he used to take pride in.

He explains what he is reaching for now.

## What Paul once counted on

## Philippians 3:1

Brothers and sisters, rejoice in the Lord.

I am glad to write these things again.

Hearing them again helps keep you safe.

## Philippians 3:2

Watch out for those people who do evil!

I call them dogs.

They want to cut your bodies.

**Help with these words**

Paul is warning about teachers who insist on circumcision.

Circumcision is the removal of the foreskin from the penis.

## Philippians 3:3

We are the people who bear the true sign of circumcision.

We worship by God’s Spirit and take pride in Christ Jesus.

We do not rely on what people do to the body.

## Philippians 3:4

I myself have reasons to take pride in such things.

If anyone thinks they have reasons, I have more.

## Philippians 3:5

I received circumcision when I was eight days old.

I belong to Israel, to the tribe of Benjamin.

I am a Hebrew born to Hebrew parents.

I followed the law as a Pharisee.

**Help with these words**

The Pharisees were a Jewish group that carefully followed religious law.

## Philippians 3:6

I cared so fiercely about my religion that I attacked the church.

No one could fault me for how I kept the law.

**Pause and answer**

Which tribe did Paul belong to?

## Knowing Christ matters most

## Philippians 3:7

I once saw all those things as gains.

For Christ’s sake, I now count them as losses.

## Philippians 3:8

Knowing Christ Jesus my Lord matters more to me than everything else.

I have lost everything for him.

I count it all as rubbish so I can gain Christ.

| What Paul once valued most | What Paul values most now |
| --- | --- |
| His family background and his record of keeping the law | Knowing Christ Jesus his Lord |

## Philippians 3:9

I want to belong to Christ.

My own efforts to keep the law cannot put me right with God.

God puts me right with him through faith in Christ.

## Philippians 3:10

I want to know Christ.

I want to know the power that raised him from death.

I want to share in his sufferings and become like him in his death.

## Philippians 3:11

My hope is that somehow I too will rise from the dead.

**Pause and answer**

What matters most to Paul now?

## A runner reaches forward

## Philippians 3:12

I have yet to reach that goal or become perfect.

Christ Jesus took hold of me for a purpose.

I press on to take hold of what he wants for me.

## Philippians 3:13

Brothers and sisters, I know I have yet to reach it.

Like a runner, I leave behind what is past.

I stretch forward to what lies ahead.

## Philippians 3:14

I keep going toward the goal to win the prize.

God calls me to heaven in Christ Jesus.

**Help with these words**

Paul pictures his life with Christ as a race.

He wants to keep going until he reaches the goal God set for him.

## Philippians 3:15

Those of us who have grown in faith should think this way.

If you see something differently, God will show you that too.

## Philippians 3:16

Meanwhile, let us keep living by what we have already learned.

**Pause and answer**

Where does Paul focus his attention as he runs?

## Our home is in heaven

## Philippians 3:17

Brothers and sisters, follow my example together.

Watch the people who live the way we showed you.

## Philippians 3:18

I have often warned you about something.

It brings me to tears to say it again.

Many people live as enemies of the cross of Christ.

## Philippians 3:19

They are heading for destruction.

Their appetites have become their god.

They boast about things that should shame them.

They think only about things on earth.

## Philippians 3:20

We are citizens of heaven.

We eagerly wait for our Savior to come from there.

Our Savior is the Lord Jesus Christ.

## Philippians 3:21

Christ has power to bring everything under his rule.

With that power he will change our weak bodies.

They will become like his glorious body.

**Pause and answer**

Whom are the believers waiting for?
