# Philippians 1 — Paul rejoices as the good news spreads

**About this chapter**

Paul writes to friends in Philippi from prison.

He tells them why he can still pray with joy.

## Paul remembers his friends

## Philippians 1:1

This letter comes from Paul and Timothy.

We serve Christ Jesus.

We greet all God’s people who belong to Christ Jesus in Philippi.

We greet the overseers and deacons too.

**Help with these words**

Overseers look after the church.

Deacons have a special responsibility to serve the church.

## Philippians 1:2

May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you grace and peace.

## Philippians 1:3

Whenever I remember you, I thank my God.

## Philippians 1:4

Whenever I pray for all of you, I pray with joy.

## Philippians 1:5

From the first day until now, you have helped me share the good news.

## Philippians 1:6

God began a good work in you.

I trust God to keep working in you.

He will finish that work by the day Christ Jesus returns.

Why does Paul pray with joy?

## Paul prays for their love to grow

## Philippians 1:7

I care deeply about all of you.

So it is right for me to feel this way.

You share God’s grace with me while I am in chains.

You share it as I defend the good news and show that it is true.

## Philippians 1:8

God knows how much I miss you all.

I love you with the love Christ Jesus gives me.

## Philippians 1:9

I pray that your love will keep growing.

May you gain knowledge and understand things more deeply.

## Philippians 1:10

Then you can recognize what matters most.

You can live pure lives, free from blame when Christ comes.

## Philippians 1:11

May Jesus Christ fill your lives with the fruit of doing right.

This will bring God honor and praise.

![Grapes growing on a vine](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

**Help with these words**

Fruit grows on a plant where people can see it.

Here, fruit means the good things people do because of Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul ask God to help the believers recognize?

## People hear about Christ while Paul is in prison

## Philippians 1:12

Brothers and sisters, I want you to know what has happened.

My troubles have helped the good news spread.

## Philippians 1:13

The whole palace guard knows why I am in chains.

Everyone else here knows too.

I am a prisoner because I serve Christ.

## Philippians 1:14

Most of the believers now trust the Lord more because of my chains.

They dare to speak God’s message without fear.

## Philippians 1:15

Some tell people about Christ because they envy me.

They want to compete with me.

Others speak because they want to help.

## Philippians 1:16

Those who speak out of love know my task.

I am here to defend the good news.

## Philippians 1:17

Those who compete with me want to get ahead.

They speak about Christ for selfish reasons.

They hope to make prison harder for me.

## Philippians 1:18

What matters to me is that people hear about Christ.

Some speak for selfish reasons and others speak sincerely.

Either way, I rejoice, and I will keep rejoicing.

**Pause and answer**

What did the palace guards learn about Paul?

## What living and dying mean to Paul

## Philippians 1:19

You are praying for me.

The Spirit of Jesus Christ gives me help.

So I know these troubles will end in my deliverance.

**Help with these words**

Deliverance means being saved or set free.

## Philippians 1:20

I hope with all my heart to have no cause for shame.

I want the courage to honor Christ with my body as always.

I want to honor him whether I live or die.

## Philippians 1:21

For me, living means Christ.

Even death would bring me something good.

## Philippians 1:22

If I go on living, I can keep doing work that helps people.

Which would I choose?

I do not know.

## Philippians 1:23

I feel pulled in both directions.

I long to leave this life and be with Christ.

That would be far better for me.

## Philippians 1:24

But you need me to stay alive.

## Philippians 1:25

I am sure of that.

So I know I will stay and keep helping you all.

I want you to grow in faith and find joy in trusting Christ.

## Philippians 1:26

When I visit again, you will have even more reason to rejoice in Christ Jesus because of me.

**Pause and answer**

Why does Paul want to stay and help the believers?

## Stand together

## Philippians 1:27

Live in a way that honors the good news about Christ.

I may visit you or hear news from far away.

Either way, I want to hear that you stand firm together.

Work side by side for the faith you share.

## Philippians 1:28

Do not let those who oppose you frighten you.

Your courage is a sign from God.

It points to their destruction and to God saving you.

## Philippians 1:29

God has given you the gift of trusting Christ.

He has also given you the privilege of suffering for Christ.

## Philippians 1:30

You face the same struggle you once saw me face.

Now you hear that my struggle continues.

How does Paul want the believers to face opposition?
