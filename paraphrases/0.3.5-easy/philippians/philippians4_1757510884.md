# Philippians 4 — Paul thanks the church for sharing his troubles

**About this chapter**

Paul asks two women who worked beside him to agree.

Later he thanks his friends for sending help when he needed it.

## Help two friends agree

## Philippians 4:1

Dear brothers and sisters, I love you and miss you.

You are my joy and my crown.

Stand firm in the Lord, dear friends.

**Help with these words**

A winner in a race could receive a crown.

Paul calls his friends his crown because they give him joy and make him proud.

## Philippians 4:2

Euodia and Syntyche, I ask each of you to seek agreement.

You both belong to the Lord.

## Philippians 4:3

My trusted companion, please help these women.

They worked hard beside me to spread the good news.

Clement and my other fellow workers helped too.

Their names are in the Book of Life.

**Help with these words**

The Book of Life is a picture of God remembering those who belong to him.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul ask his companion to do?

## Bring your worries to God

## Philippians 4:4

Always rejoice in the Lord.

I will say it again.

Rejoice!

## Philippians 4:5

Let everyone see how gently you treat people.

The Lord is near.

## Philippians 4:6

Do not worry about anything.

In every situation, tell God what you need.

Thank him as you pray.

## Philippians 4:7

God’s peace goes beyond what anyone can understand.

His peace will guard your hearts and minds in Christ Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

What can you tell God when you pray?

## Think about what is good

## Philippians 4:8

Brothers and sisters, think about things that are true and worthy of respect.

Think about what is right, pure, lovely, and admirable.

Give your attention to what is excellent and deserves praise.

## Philippians 4:9

Remember what I taught and passed on to you.

Remember what you heard me say and saw me do.

Put these things into practice.

The God of peace will be with you.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul ask the believers to put into practice?

## Christ gives Paul strength

## Philippians 4:10

I rejoice in the Lord that you could show your care for me again.

You cared before too.

You simply had no chance to show it.

## Philippians 4:11

I am not saying this because I lack something.

I have learned to be content whatever happens.

## Philippians 4:12

I know what it means to have little or plenty.

I have learned to be content with a full stomach or an empty one.

This holds true whether I have plenty or go without.

## Philippians 4:13

I can face all these things through Christ, who gives me strength.

## Philippians 4:14

Even so, you did well to share my troubles.

**Pause and answer**

Who gives Paul strength when he goes hungry?

## The gift from Philippi

## Philippians 4:15

Remember the early days when I first told you the good news.

When I left Macedonia, you were the only church to share with me in giving and receiving.

## Philippians 4:16

Even when I was in Thessalonica, you sent help more than once.

## Philippians 4:17

I am not asking for a gift.

I want you to receive the good that comes from giving.

It is like a gain added to your account.

## Philippians 4:18

Epaphroditus brought me your gifts.

Now I have all I need and more.

Your gifts are like a sweet-smelling offering that pleases God.

![A jar for something sweet-smelling](../pictures/perfume-jar.svg)

**Help with these words**

Paul compares their gift to an offering made to God.

Their care for Paul pleased God.

## Philippians 4:19

My God will meet all your needs.

He will give from his glorious riches in Christ Jesus.

## Philippians 4:20

May our God and Father receive glory forever and ever.

Amen.

**Pause and answer**

Who brought the gifts from Philippi to Paul?

## Greetings from other believers

## Philippians 4:21

Greet each person there who belongs to Christ Jesus.

The believers here with me send their greetings.

## Philippians 4:22

All God’s people here greet you.

Those in Caesar’s household send special greetings.

**Help with these words**

Caesar was the Roman emperor.

His household included people who served him.

## Philippians 4:23

May the Lord Jesus Christ show grace to you in your inmost being.

**Pause and answer**

Which household sends special greetings?
