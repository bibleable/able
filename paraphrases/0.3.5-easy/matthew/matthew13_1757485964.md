# Matthew 13 — Jesus tells stories about God’s kingdom

**About this chapter**

Jesus tells stories beside the lake.

Later his followers ask him what the stories mean.

## A farmer scatters seed

## Matthew 13:1

That same day Jesus left the house.

He sat beside the lake.

## Matthew 13:2

Large crowds gathered around Jesus.

So he got into a boat and sat down.

The people stood on the shore.

## Matthew 13:3

Jesus taught them many things through stories called parables.

He said, “A farmer went out to scatter seed.

**Help with a word**

A parable is a story or comparison that teaches something.

## Matthew 13:4

“Some seed fell on the path.

Birds came and ate it.

**Pause and answer**

Who ate the seed on the path?

## What grows in good soil

## Matthew 13:5

“Some seed fell where rocks lay under a little soil.

The plants came up quickly because the soil was shallow.

## Matthew 13:6

“Then the sun came up and burned the young plants.

They dried up because they had no roots.

## Matthew 13:7

“Some seed fell among thorn plants.

The thorns grew up and crowded out the young plants.

## Matthew 13:8

“Other seed fell on good soil and grew into a crop.

Some gave back a hundred times what the farmer planted.

Some gave back sixty times, and some thirty times as much.

## Matthew 13:9

“If you have ears, listen!”

**Pause and answer**

Where did the seed produce a crop?

## Why Jesus tells stories

## Matthew 13:10

Jesus’ followers came to him.

They asked, “Why do you tell the people parables?”

## Matthew 13:11

Jesus answered, “God has let you know the secrets of the kingdom of heaven.

He has not given these people that same understanding.

**Help with these words**

The kingdom of heaven means God ruling as King.

## Matthew 13:12

“Those who have will receive more.

They will have plenty.

Those who have little will lose even what they have.

## Matthew 13:13

“That is why I tell them parables.

They look but do not really see.

They listen but do not hear or understand.

**Pause and answer**

What did the followers ask Jesus to explain?

## People who hear and understand

## Matthew 13:14

“What the prophet Isaiah said is coming true among them.

‘You will keep listening without understanding.

You will keep looking without seeing.

## Matthew 13:15

“‘These people have closed their hearts.

They hardly hear, and they have shut their eyes.

Otherwise they might see, hear, and understand deep inside.

They might turn back to me, and I would heal them.’

**Help with this picture**

Isaiah describes people who close themselves off to God’s message.

Here seeing and hearing mean taking in what God says.

## Matthew 13:16

“But God has blessed your eyes because they see.

He has blessed your ears because they hear.

## Matthew 13:17

“I tell you truly, many prophets and people who did right longed to see what you see.

They never saw it.

They longed to hear what you hear.

They never heard it.

**Pause and answer**

What did the prophets long to hear?

## The path and the rocky ground

## Matthew 13:18

“Now listen to what the story about the farmer means.

## Matthew 13:19

“Think of the seed on the path.

Someone hears the message about God’s kingdom without understanding it.

The evil one snatches the message from that person’s heart.

**Help with a name**

The evil one means the devil.

## Matthew 13:20

“Think of the seed on rocky ground.

Someone hears the message and gladly accepts it at once.

## Matthew 13:21

“But the message grows no deep roots in that person.

It lasts only a short time.

Trouble comes, or people mistreat them for believing the message.

Then the person quickly turns away.

**Pause and answer**

What happens when trouble comes to the person pictured by rocky ground?

## The thorns and the good soil

## Matthew 13:22

“Think of the seed among thorns.

Someone hears the message, but worries crowd in.

Riches fool the person and crowd out the message too.

So the message produces no crop.

## Matthew 13:23

“Now think of the seed in good soil.

Someone hears the message and understands it.

That person produces a crop, a hundred, sixty, or thirty times what was planted.”

**Help with this picture**

A crop shows that a seed has grown.

Jesus compares this to what happens when people understand his message and live by it.

**Pause and answer**

What does the person pictured by good soil do with the message?

## Weeds appear among the wheat

## Matthew 13:24

Jesus told them another parable.

“The kingdom of heaven is like this.

A farmer planted good seed in his field.

## Matthew 13:25

“While everyone slept, his enemy came.

The enemy scattered weed seeds among the wheat and slipped away.

## Matthew 13:26

“The wheat grew and began to form grain.

Then the weeds showed up too.

**About the picture**

![A stalk of grain with seeds at the top.](../pictures/grain.svg)

Find the seeds at the top.

The farmer wants to keep the wheat that grows grain.

## Matthew 13:27

“The servants came to the farmer.

They asked, ‘Sir, didn’t you plant good seed?

Where did these weeds come from?’

**Pause and answer**

Who scattered weeds in the field?

## The farmer tells the workers to wait

## Matthew 13:28

“The farmer answered, ‘An enemy did this.’

The servants asked, ‘Should we pull up the weeds?’

## Matthew 13:29

“‘No,’ said the farmer.

‘You might pull up the wheat with the weeds.

## Matthew 13:30

“‘Let them grow together until harvest time.

Then I will tell the workers to gather the weeds first.

They will tie them in bundles for burning.

Then they will bring the wheat into my barn.’”

**Pause and answer**

Why did the farmer let the weeds grow for a while?

## A seed and some yeast

## Matthew 13:31

Jesus told another parable.

“The kingdom of heaven is like a mustard seed.

A man planted it in his field.

## Matthew 13:32

“It is the smallest of all the seeds he plants.

Yet it grows bigger than the other garden plants.

It becomes a tree where birds nest in the branches.”

## Matthew 13:33

Jesus told another parable.

“The kingdom of heaven is like yeast.

A woman mixed it into three measures of flour.

The yeast worked through the whole batch of dough.”

**Help with these pictures**

A tiny seed can grow into a large plant.

Yeast makes dough rise as it spreads through it.

Jesus uses both pictures to show how God’s kingdom grows.

**Pause and answer**

How much of the dough did the yeast reach?

## The followers ask about the weeds

## Matthew 13:34

Jesus told all these things to the crowds in parables.

Each time he spoke to them, he used a parable.

## Matthew 13:35

So the prophet’s words came true.

“I will speak in parables.

I will tell things hidden since the world began.”

## Matthew 13:36

Jesus sent the crowds away and went inside the house.

His followers came to him.

They said, “Please explain the story about the weeds.”

**Pause and answer**

Which story did the followers ask Jesus to explain?

## What the field and the harvest mean

## Matthew 13:37

Jesus answered, “The Son of Man plants the good seed.

**Help with a name**

Jesus calls himself the Son of Man.

## Matthew 13:38

“The field stands for the world.

The good seed stands for those who belong to God’s kingdom.

The weeds stand for those who belong to the evil one.

## Matthew 13:39

“The devil is the enemy who plants the weeds.

The harvest stands for the end of this age.

The workers who gather the crop are angels.

**Pause and answer**

Who are the workers in Jesus’ explanation?

## The end of the age

## Matthew 13:40

“Workers gather weeds to burn them.

Something like that will happen at the end of this age.

## Matthew 13:41

“The Son of Man will send out his angels.

They will remove from his kingdom everything that leads people into sin.

They will also remove everyone who keeps doing evil.

## Matthew 13:42

“The angels will throw them into a blazing furnace.

There people will cry and grind their teeth.

**Help with these words**

A furnace is a very hot oven.

Grinding teeth here shows deep distress.

## Matthew 13:43

“Then those who do right will shine like the sun in their Father’s kingdom.

If you have ears, listen!

**Pause and answer**

What will those who do right shine like?

## A treasure and a pearl

## Matthew 13:44

“The kingdom of heaven is like treasure hidden in a field.

A man found the treasure and hid it again.

He was so glad that he sold everything he owned.

Then he bought the field.

## Matthew 13:45

“The kingdom of heaven is also like a trader looking for fine pearls.

## Matthew 13:46

“The trader found one pearl worth a great deal.

He went and sold everything he owned.

Then he bought that pearl.

**Help with these pictures**

A pearl is a small treasure that forms inside a shell.

Each man gives up everything for what he finds.

Jesus shows how precious God’s kingdom is.

**Pause and answer**

What did the trader sell to buy the pearl?

## A net full of fish

## Matthew 13:47

“The kingdom of heaven is also like a fishing net.

People threw the net into the lake.

It caught all kinds of fish.

**About the picture**

![Ropes cross to make a fishing net.](../pictures/net.svg)

Trace a rope across the net.

Fishers pull the net in to gather the fish.

## Matthew 13:48

“When the net was full, they pulled it ashore.

They sat down and sorted the fish.

They kept the good fish in containers.

They threw the bad fish away.

## Matthew 13:49

“That is how it will be at the end of this age.

The angels will separate those who do evil from those who do right.

## Matthew 13:50

“They will throw those who do evil into a blazing furnace.

There people will cry and grind their teeth.

**Pause and answer**

What did the workers do with the good fish?

## Old and new treasures

## Matthew 13:51

“Have you understood all these things?” Jesus asked.

His followers answered, “Yes.”

## Matthew 13:52

Jesus said, “Think of a teacher of God’s law who learns about the kingdom of heaven.

That teacher is like the owner of a house.

From the storeroom the owner brings treasures both new and old.”

**Help with this picture**

A teacher can share what God taught long ago.

The teacher can also share what Jesus is teaching now.

**Pause and answer**

What two kinds of treasure did the owner bring out?

## Jesus teaches in his hometown

## Matthew 13:53

When Jesus finished telling these parables, he left that place.

## Matthew 13:54

Jesus went to his hometown.

He taught in the synagogue, and the people were amazed.

They asked, “Where did he get such wisdom?

Where did he get the power to do miracles?

**Help with these words**

A synagogue is a place where Jewish people meet for worship.

A miracle is a wonderful act of power.

## Matthew 13:55

“Isn’t he the carpenter’s son?

Isn’t Mary his mother?

Aren’t James, Joseph, Simon, and Judas his brothers?

## Matthew 13:56

“Don’t all his sisters live here with us?

Where did he get all this?”

## Matthew 13:57

The people took offence at Jesus.

Jesus told them, “People honour a prophet everywhere except in his hometown and his own home.”

## Matthew 13:58

The people there did not trust Jesus.

So Jesus did few miracles there.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Jesus do few miracles in his hometown?
