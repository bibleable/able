# Matthew 17 — A voice on the mountain

**About this chapter**

Three followers see Jesus shining on a mountain.

Later a father brings his son to Jesus.

## Jesus shines on the mountain

## Matthew 17:1

Six days later, Jesus took Peter, James, and John with him.

John was James’s brother.

Jesus led them up a high mountain by themselves.

## Matthew 17:2

Before their eyes, Jesus began to look different.

His face shone like the sun.

His clothes became as white as light.

**About the picture**

![A mountain with a high peak.](../pictures/mountain.svg)

Find the top of the mountain.

Jesus took three followers up a high mountain.

## Matthew 17:3

Suddenly Moses and Elijah appeared.

The three followers saw them speaking with Jesus.

## Matthew 17:4

Peter said, “Lord, it is good for us to be here.

If you wish, I will put up three shelters.

One for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah.”

**Pause and answer**

Who spoke with Jesus on the mountain?

## A voice from the cloud

## Matthew 17:5

While Peter spoke, a bright cloud covered them.

A voice came from the cloud.

“This is my Son, whom I love.

I am very pleased with him.

Listen to him!”

## Matthew 17:6

The followers heard the voice.

They were so afraid that they fell facedown.

## Matthew 17:7

Jesus came over and touched them.

“Get up,” he said.

“Do not be afraid.”

## Matthew 17:8

They looked up.

Jesus was the only one they saw.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did the voice tell the followers to listen to?

## A question about Elijah

## Matthew 17:9

As they came down the mountain, Jesus gave them an order.

“Keep what you saw to yourselves until the Son of Man rises from the dead.”

**Help with a name**

Jesus calls himself the Son of Man.

## Matthew 17:10

His followers asked, “Why do the law teachers say Elijah must come first?”

## Matthew 17:11

Jesus replied, “Elijah does come to put everything right.

## Matthew 17:12

“But I tell you, Elijah has already come.

People failed to recognise him.

They treated him however they wanted.

They will make the Son of Man suffer too.”

## Matthew 17:13

Then the followers understood.

Jesus was talking about John the Baptist.

**Pause and answer**

Who did the followers realise Jesus was talking about?

## A father asks Jesus for help

## Matthew 17:14

They reached the crowd.

A man came to Jesus and knelt before him.

## Matthew 17:15

“Lord, have mercy on my son,” the father said.

“He has seizures and suffers terribly.

He often falls into the fire or the water.

**Help with a word**

During a seizure a person may fall or lose awareness.

## Matthew 17:16

“I brought him to your followers.

They could not heal him.”

## Matthew 17:17

Jesus answered, “You people have turned away and do not trust!

How long must I stay with you?

How long must I put up with you?

Bring the boy here to me.”

## Matthew 17:18

Jesus spoke sharply to the evil spirit.

The spirit left the boy.

From that moment the boy was well.

**Pause and answer**

Who brought the boy to Jesus?

## Faith like a mustard seed

## Matthew 17:19

Later the followers spoke to Jesus alone.

They asked, “Why couldn’t we drive the spirit out?”

## Matthew 17:20

Jesus answered, “Because you have so little faith.

I tell you truly, even faith as small as a mustard seed can do this.

You can tell this mountain, ‘Move from here to there,’ and it will move.

Nothing will be impossible for you.”

**Help with this picture**

A mustard seed is tiny.

A mountain is huge.

Jesus uses both to show what trust in God can do.

## Sad news in Galilee

## Matthew 17:22

The followers gathered together in Galilee.

Jesus told them, “Someone will soon hand the Son of Man over to men.

## Matthew 17:23

“They will kill him.

On the third day he will come back to life.”

His followers were deeply sad.

## A coin for two people

## Matthew 17:24

They arrived in Capernaum.

The people who collected the two-drachma tax came to Peter.

They asked, “Does your teacher pay the two drachmas?”

**Help with these words**

A drachma was a unit of money.

This tax helped pay for the temple.

## Matthew 17:25

“Yes,” Peter answered.

Peter went inside the house.

Before Peter could speak, Jesus asked him a question.

“Simon, think about kings who collect taxes and fees.

Do they collect from their own sons or from other people?”

## Matthew 17:26

“From other people,” Peter answered.

Jesus said, “Then the sons do not have to pay.

## Matthew 17:27

“But we do not want to offend the tax collectors.

Go to the lake and throw in a fishing hook.

Take the first fish you catch.

Open its mouth and you will find a four-drachma coin.

Give them that coin to pay for you and me.”

**Pause and answer**

Whose taxes would the coin pay?
