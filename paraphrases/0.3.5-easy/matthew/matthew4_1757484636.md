# Matthew 4 — Jesus begins his work in Galilee

**About this chapter**

The devil tests Jesus.

Later, Jesus calls people to follow him.

## Jesus goes without food

## Matthew 4:1

God’s Spirit led Jesus into the desert.

There the devil tried to make Jesus disobey God.

## Matthew 4:2

Jesus ate no food for forty days and forty nights.

After that, Jesus was hungry.

## Matthew 4:3

The devil came to Jesus.

He said, “If you are God’s Son, give the order.

Tell these stones to turn into bread.”

## Matthew 4:4

Jesus answered, “Scripture says, ‘Bread alone cannot keep people alive.

People need every word God speaks.’”

**Help with a word**

Scripture means the sacred writings that tell us God’s words.

**Pause and answer**

What did the devil tell Jesus to make from stones?

## The devil tells Jesus to jump

## Matthew 4:5

Then the devil took Jesus to Jerusalem, the holy city.

He set Jesus on the highest point of the temple.

**Help with a word**

The temple was a place for worshiping God.

## Matthew 4:6

The devil said, “If you are God’s Son, jump down.

Scripture says, ‘God will tell his angels to protect you.

They will lift you up in their hands.

Your foot will never hit a stone.’”

## Matthew 4:7

Jesus answered, “Scripture also says, ‘Do not test the Lord your God.’”

**Help with these words**

Jumping would mean demanding that God prove his care.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the devil tell Jesus to jump from?

## Jesus tells the devil to leave

## Matthew 4:8

Then the devil took Jesus to a very high mountain.

He showed Jesus all the kingdoms of the world.

Jesus saw them in all their greatness.

## Matthew 4:9

The devil said, “Bow down and worship me.

Then I will give you all these kingdoms.”

## Matthew 4:10

Jesus said, “Go away, Satan!

Scripture says, ‘Worship the Lord your God.

Serve him alone.’”

**Help with a name**

Satan is another name for the devil.

## Matthew 4:11

Then the devil left Jesus.

Angels came and cared for Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

Who came to care for Jesus?

## Jesus brings good news to Galilee

## Matthew 4:12

Jesus heard that John was in prison.

So Jesus went back to Galilee.

## Matthew 4:13

Jesus left Nazareth and made his home in Capernaum.

This town was beside the Sea of Galilee.

It was in the lands of Zebulun and Naphtali.

## Matthew 4:14

So the words of the prophet Isaiah came true.

## Matthew 4:15

Isaiah spoke of Zebulun and Naphtali, along the Sea Road.

He spoke of the land beyond the Jordan River.

He spoke of Galilee, where many Gentiles lived.

**Help with a word**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish.

## Matthew 4:16

Isaiah said, “People living in darkness saw a great light.

Death lay like a shadow across their land.

Now light has begun to shine on them.”

**Help with this picture**

People can see their way when light comes.

Isaiah uses light to describe God bringing hope.

## Matthew 4:17

From then on Jesus began to give this message.

“Turn away from sin.

The kingdom of heaven is near.”

**Help with these words**

Sin is wrongdoing against God.

The kingdom of heaven means God ruling as King.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Jesus make his home?

## Jesus calls four fishermen

## Matthew 4:18

Jesus walked beside the Sea of Galilee.

He saw two brothers, Simon and Andrew.

People also called Simon “Peter.”

The brothers fished for a living.

They were throwing a net into the water.

**About the picture**

![A fishing net made of ropes.](../pictures/net.svg)

Find the spaces between the ropes.

The brothers used a net to catch fish.

## Matthew 4:19

Jesus said, “Come and follow me.

I will teach you to fish for people.”

**Help with this picture**

Jesus will teach the brothers to bring people to him.

## Matthew 4:20

At once the brothers left their nets and followed Jesus.

## Matthew 4:21

Jesus walked on and saw two more brothers.

Their names were James and John.

They sat in a boat with their father, Zebedee.

They were fixing their nets.

Jesus called the brothers.

## Matthew 4:22

At once James and John left their boat and their father.

They followed Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

What were James and John fixing?

## People bring the sick to Jesus

## Matthew 4:23

Jesus went all through Galilee.

He taught in the places where Jewish people gathered for worship.

He shared the good news about God ruling as King.

He healed every kind of sickness among the people.

## Matthew 4:24

News about Jesus spread through all Syria.

People brought Jesus everyone who was ill or in great pain.

Some had evil spirits, and some had seizures.

Others could not move parts of their bodies.

Jesus healed them.

**Help with a word**

During a seizure, someone may shake or lose awareness.

## Matthew 4:25

Large crowds followed Jesus.

They came from Galilee, the Decapolis, Jerusalem, and Judea.

Others came from across the Jordan River.

**Help with a name**

Decapolis means “Ten Cities.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus do for the sick people?
