# Matthew 28 — Jesus is alive and sends his followers

**About this chapter**

Two women visit the tomb at dawn.

Later, Jesus meets his followers in Galilee.

## Two women visit the tomb

## Matthew 28:1

The Sabbath was over.

At dawn on the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary went to see the tomb.

**About the picture**

![A tomb cut into rock with a stone by the opening.](../pictures/tomb.svg)

Find the entrance to the tomb where Jesus had been buried.

## Matthew 28:2

Suddenly the ground shook hard.

An angel of the Lord came down from heaven.

He rolled the stone away and sat on it.

## Matthew 28:3

The angel shone like lightning.

His clothes were white as snow.

## Matthew 28:4

The guards trembled with fear at the sight of him.

They became as still as dead men.

**Pause and answer**

Who rolled the stone away?

## The angel gives the women news

## Matthew 28:5

The angel said to the women, “Do not be afraid.

I know you are looking for Jesus, who died on the cross.

## Matthew 28:6

“He is not here.

He has risen, just as he said!

Come and see where his body lay.

**Help with these words**

“He has risen” means Jesus has come back to life.

## Matthew 28:7

“Go quickly and tell his followers this news.

‘Jesus has risen from the dead.

He is going ahead of you to Galilee.

You will see him there.’

Now I have told you.”

**Pause and answer**

Where did the angel say the followers would see Jesus?

## Jesus meets the women

## Matthew 28:8

The women hurried away, afraid and full of joy.

They ran to tell his followers.

## Matthew 28:9

Suddenly Jesus met the women and greeted them.

They came to him, held his feet, and worshiped him.

## Matthew 28:10

Jesus said, “Do not be afraid.

Tell my brothers to go to Galilee.

They will see me there.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the women do when Jesus greeted them?

## The guards report what happened

## Matthew 28:11

While the women were on their way, some guards went into the city.

They told the chief priests everything that had happened.

## Matthew 28:12

The chief priests met with the elders and made a plan.

They gave the soldiers a large sum of money.

## Matthew 28:13

They told the soldiers, “Say, ‘His followers came at night.

They stole his body while we were asleep.’

## Matthew 28:14

“If the governor hears about this, we will speak to him.

We will keep you out of trouble.”

## Matthew 28:15

The guards took the money and told the story as instructed.

That story still spread among Jewish people when Matthew wrote.

**Pause and answer**

What story were the guards paid to tell?

## Jesus sends his followers

## Matthew 28:16

The eleven followers went to Galilee.

They went to the mountain Jesus had chosen.

## Matthew 28:17

They saw Jesus and worshiped him.

But some doubted.

## Matthew 28:18

Jesus came to them and said, “I have received all authority in heaven and on earth.

**Help with a word**

Authority means having the right to rule.

## Matthew 28:19

“So go to people of every nation.

Teach them to follow me.

Baptize them in the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit.

**Help with a word**

Baptism uses water to mark someone as a follower of Jesus.

## Matthew 28:20

“Teach them to obey everything I have told you.

Remember, I am always with you, to the very end of the age.”

**Pause and answer**

What promise did Jesus make to his followers?
