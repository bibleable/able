# Matthew 8 — Jesus heals people and calms a storm

**About this chapter**

People ask Jesus for help.

His followers see what happens when Jesus speaks to a storm.

## A man asks Jesus to make him clean

## Matthew 8:1

Jesus came down from the mountain.

Large crowds followed him.

## Matthew 8:2

A man with leprosy came and knelt before Jesus.

He said, “Lord, you can make me clean if you want to.”

**Help with these words**

Leprosy was a name for serious skin diseases.

The man wanted healing so he could join others in worship again.

## Matthew 8:3

Jesus reached out and touched the man.

Jesus said, “I want to.

Be clean!”

At once the man’s skin became well.

## Matthew 8:4

Jesus told him, “Tell no one about this.

Go and show yourself to the priest.

Bring the gift that Moses commanded.

This will show them what happened.”

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Jesus tell the man to visit?

## An officer asks for help

## Matthew 8:5

Jesus entered Capernaum.

A Roman army officer came and begged Jesus for help.

**Help with a word**

Matthew calls the officer a centurion.

A centurion led a group of Roman soldiers.

## Matthew 8:6

The officer said, “Lord, my servant is at home.

He lies there unable to move.

He is in terrible pain.”

## Matthew 8:7

Jesus answered, “I will go and heal him.”

## Matthew 8:8

The officer replied, “Lord, I do not deserve to have you visit my home.

Just say the word, and my servant will get well.

## Matthew 8:9

“I take orders, and soldiers take orders from me.

I tell one soldier to go, and he goes.

I tell another to come, and he comes.

My servant does what I tell him.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the officer believe Jesus could do from a distance?

## Jesus speaks about the officer’s trust

## Matthew 8:10

The officer’s words amazed Jesus.

Jesus told his followers, “I tell you truly.

Nowhere in Israel have I found trust like this.

## Matthew 8:11

“Many will come from the east and the west.

They will share a great meal in the kingdom of heaven.

They will sit with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

## Matthew 8:12

“But those who expected the kingdom as their own will be thrown outside into the dark.

There they will cry and grind their teeth.”

**Help with this picture**

A place at the meal means a welcome into God’s kingdom.

Grinding your teeth shows deep pain.

## Matthew 8:13

Jesus told the officer, “Go home.

What you trusted me to do will happen.”

At that very hour the servant got well.

**Pause and answer**

What happened to the servant?

## Jesus visits Peter’s house

## Matthew 8:14

Jesus went into Peter’s house.

The mother of Peter’s wife lay sick in bed.

She had a fever.

## Matthew 8:15

Jesus touched her hand, and the fever left her.

She got up and began to serve Jesus.

## Matthew 8:16

That evening people brought many others to Jesus.

Evil spirits were troubling them.

Jesus spoke, and the spirits left.

Jesus also healed everyone who was sick.

## Matthew 8:17

Long ago the prophet Isaiah had spoken about this.

Isaiah said, “He took our weakness upon himself.

He carried our sicknesses.”

Now these words came true.

**Pause and answer**

What happened when Jesus touched the woman’s hand?

## Two people speak about following Jesus

## Matthew 8:18

Jesus saw a large crowd around him.

He told his followers to cross the lake.

## Matthew 8:19

A teacher of God’s law came to Jesus.

He said, “Teacher, I will follow you wherever you go.”

## Matthew 8:20

Jesus answered, “Foxes have holes to sleep in.

Birds have nests.

But the Son of Man has nowhere to rest his head.”

**Help with a name**

Jesus often called himself the Son of Man.

## Matthew 8:21

Another follower said, “Lord, first let me bury my father.”

## Matthew 8:22

Jesus told him, “Follow me.

Let the dead bury their own dead.”

**Help with these words**

Jesus tells this man to follow him without delay.

He tells him to leave the burial to others.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus ask the second man to do?

## A storm on the lake

## Matthew 8:23

Jesus stepped into a boat.

His followers went with him.

**About the picture**

![A boat with a curved hull.](../pictures/boat.svg)

Trace the curved bottom of the boat.

## Matthew 8:24

Suddenly a fierce storm swept across the lake.

Waves washed over the boat.

Jesus was asleep.

## Matthew 8:25

His followers woke him.

“Lord, save us!” they said.

“We are going to die!”

## Matthew 8:26

Jesus answered, “You trust me so little.

Why are you so afraid?”

Jesus stood up and ordered the wind and waves to stop.

The lake became completely calm.

## Matthew 8:27

The men were amazed.

They asked, “What kind of man is this?

Even the wind and the water obey him!”

**Pause and answer**

What happened to the lake when Jesus spoke?

## Two men meet Jesus on the other shore

## Matthew 8:28

Jesus reached the other shore, in the land of the Gadarenes.

Two men with evil spirits came out from the tombs to meet him.

The men were so violent that nobody could pass that way.

**Help with a word**

A tomb is a place where people bury the dead.

## Matthew 8:29

They shouted, “Son of God, what do you want with us?

Have you come to cause us pain before the time set for it?”

## Matthew 8:30

A large herd of pigs was feeding some distance away.

## Matthew 8:31

The evil spirits begged Jesus, “If you send us out, send us into those pigs.”

**Pause and answer**

Where did the evil spirits ask to go?

## The people come out from town

## Matthew 8:32

Jesus said, “Go!”

The spirits left the men and entered the pigs.

The whole herd rushed down the steep bank into the lake.

The pigs drowned.

## Matthew 8:33

The people who cared for the pigs ran into town.

They told everyone what had happened to the pigs and the two men.

## Matthew 8:34

The whole town came out to meet Jesus.

When they saw him, they begged him to leave their land.

**Pause and answer**

What did the townspeople ask Jesus to do?
