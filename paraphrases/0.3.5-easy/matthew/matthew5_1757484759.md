# Matthew 5 — Jesus teaches on the mountain

**About this chapter**

Jesus tells his followers whom God blesses.

He teaches them how to treat other people.

## People who need God

## Matthew 5:1

Jesus saw the crowds and went up a mountain.

Jesus sat down.

His followers came to him.

**About the picture**

![A mountain rising above the ground.](../pictures/mountain.svg)

Find the mountain where you could climb up to sit.

Jesus went up a mountain to teach.

## Matthew 5:2

Jesus began to teach them.

## Matthew 5:3

“God blesses the poor in spirit.

The kingdom of heaven belongs to them.”

**Help with words**

Poor in spirit describes people who know they need God.

## Matthew 5:4

“God blesses people who mourn.

God will comfort them.”

**Help with a word**

People mourn when they feel deep sadness over a loss.

## Matthew 5:5

“God blesses gentle, humble people.

They will receive the earth.”

## Matthew 5:6

“God blesses people who hunger and thirst for what is right.

God will satisfy them.”

**Help with this picture**

Think how much a thirsty person wants water.

These people want what is right just as much.

**Pause and answer**

What does God promise people who mourn?

## Mercy and peace

## Matthew 5:7

“God blesses people who show mercy.

God will show them mercy.”

**Help with a word**

Showing mercy can mean forgiving someone who hurt you.

## Matthew 5:8

“God blesses people whose hearts are pure.

They will see God.”

**Help with these words**

Your heart here means who you are inside.

A pure heart wants what is good and true.

## Matthew 5:9

“God blesses people who make peace.

God will call them his children.”

**Pause and answer**

What does God call people who make peace?

## When people hurt you for following Jesus

## Matthew 5:10

“God blesses those who suffer for doing right.

The kingdom of heaven belongs to them.”

## Matthew 5:11

“God blesses you when people insult you because of me.

They may treat you cruelly because you follow me.

They may tell terrible lies about you.”

## Matthew 5:12

“Be glad!

Your reward in heaven is great.

People persecuted God's prophets in the same way before you.”

**Help with a word**

Prophets speak God's messages.

**Pause and answer**

Who else suffered for speaking God’s message?

## Salt and light

## Matthew 5:13

“You are the salt of the earth.

If salt loses its taste, how can it become salty again?

It is useless.

People throw it out and walk on it.”

**Help with this picture**

Salt pictures the valuable effect Jesus' followers should have.

## Matthew 5:14

“You are the light of the world.

A city on a hill cannot be hidden.”

## Matthew 5:15

“People put a lamp on a stand, not under a basket.

Then it gives light to everyone in the house.”

**Help with this picture**

A lamp helps people see.

Jesus wants people to see his followers doing good.

## Matthew 5:16

“Let your light shine so people can see it.

Let them see the good things you do.

Then they can praise your Father in heaven.”

**Pause and answer**

Where do people put a lamp?

## Jesus and God’s law

## Matthew 5:17

“Do you think I came to end the Law and the Prophets?

I came to fulfil them.”

**Help with these words**

These are writings that tell people God’s words.

Jesus came to bring about what those writings say.

## Matthew 5:18

“I tell you the truth.

Not even the smallest written mark will disappear from the Law until everything is done.

This remains true while heaven and earth remain.”

## Matthew 5:19

“Suppose someone breaks even the smallest command.

Suppose they teach others to break it too.

People will call them least in the kingdom of heaven.

Who will be called great there?

Those who obey these commands and teach them to others.”

## Matthew 5:20

“You must do right more fully than the law teachers and Pharisees.

Otherwise, you will never enter the kingdom of heaven.”

**Help with a word**

Pharisees were a Jewish religious group.

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus say to do with God’s commands?

## Make peace

## Matthew 5:21

“Long ago, people were told not to murder.

Anyone who murders must face judgment.”

## Matthew 5:22

“But anyone angry with a brother must also face judgment.

Anyone calling a brother ‘Raca’ must face the council.

Anyone saying ‘You fool’ will face the fire of hell.”

**Help with a word**

Raca is an insult meaning someone is worthless.

## Matthew 5:23

“Suppose you bring a gift to God's altar.

There you remember that your brother has something against you.”

**Help with a word**

An altar is a place for offerings to God.

## Matthew 5:24

“Leave your gift there.

First go and make peace with your brother.

Then come back and offer your gift.”

**Pause and answer**

What should you do before bringing your gift back?

## On the way to court

## Matthew 5:25

“Make peace quickly with someone taking you to court.

Do this while you are on the way.

Otherwise that person may hand you to the judge.

The judge may hand you to an officer.

The officer may put you in prison.”

## Matthew 5:26

“I tell you the truth.

You will stay there until you pay the very last coin.”

**Pause and answer**

When does Jesus say to settle the disagreement?

## Being faithful in marriage

## Matthew 5:27

“You have heard the command, ‘Do not commit adultery.’”

**Help with a word**

Adultery means having sex with someone else’s husband or wife, or being married and having sex with someone else.

## Matthew 5:28

“But I tell you this.

A man can look at a woman to feed his desire for sex with her.

He has already committed adultery with her in his heart.”

## Matthew 5:29

Jesus used a strong picture about the right eye.

He spoke of throwing away even that eye if it leads you into sin.

He said losing one part is better than going into hell with your whole body.

**Help with this picture**

Jesus shows how seriously to take wrongdoing.

Keep your body safe as you turn away from wrongdoing.

## Matthew 5:30

Jesus gave the same warning about the right hand.

He spoke of losing even that hand if it leads you into sin.

Losing one part would be better than going into hell with your whole body.

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus say about the desires we hold inside?

## When a marriage ends

## Matthew 5:31

“You have also heard this command.

A man who divorces his wife must give her a written divorce paper.”

**Help with a word**

Divorce legally ends a marriage.

## Matthew 5:32

“But I tell you this about a man who divorces his wife.

He brings adultery upon her, unless the divorce is for sexual wrongdoing.

A man who marries a divorced woman also commits adultery.”

**Pause and answer**

What document did the old command require?

## Keep your word

## Matthew 5:33

“Long ago, people were told not to break sworn promises.

They were told to keep their promises to the Lord.”

## Matthew 5:34

“But I tell you not to swear such promises at all.

Do not swear by heaven, because it is God's throne.”

## Matthew 5:35

“Do not swear by earth, because it is God's footstool.

Do not swear by Jerusalem, because it is the great King's city.”

**Help with this picture**

Throne and footstool picture God's rule over everything.

## Matthew 5:36

“Do not swear by your own head.

You cannot make even one hair white or black.”

## Matthew 5:37

“Simply say ‘Yes’ or ‘No’ and mean it.

Anything more comes from the evil one.”

**Pause and answer**

What should you mean when you say yes?

## When someone wrongs you

## Matthew 5:38

“You have heard the saying, ‘An eye for an eye.

A tooth for a tooth.’”

**Help with this saying**

The punishment was to match the harm someone had done.

## Matthew 5:39

“But do not resist an evil person.

If someone slaps your right cheek, turn the other cheek too.”

## Matthew 5:40

“If someone takes you to court for your shirt, give your coat too.”

## Matthew 5:41

“If someone forces you to go 1 mile, go 2 miles.”

## Matthew 5:42

“Give to someone who asks.

Do not turn away someone who wants to borrow.”

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus say about someone who wants to borrow?

## Love your enemies

## Matthew 5:43

“You have heard people say to love your neighbour and hate your enemy.”

## Matthew 5:44

“But I tell you to love your enemies.

Pray for those who persecute you.”

## Matthew 5:45

“Then you can be children of your Father in heaven.

Your Father makes the sun rise on good and evil people alike.

He sends rain to people who do right.

He sends rain to people who do wrong.”

## Matthew 5:46

“What reward is there in loving only those who love you?

Even tax collectors do that.”

## Matthew 5:47

“What is special about greeting only your own people?

Even people outside the Jewish faith do that.”

## Matthew 5:48

“Be perfect, as your Father in heaven is perfect.”

**Help with a word**

Perfect here describes being complete in goodness.

**Pause and answer**

Whom does Jesus ask you to pray for?
