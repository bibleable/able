# Matthew 9 — Jesus calls Matthew and cares for the crowds

**About this chapter**

Jesus forgives a man and calls Matthew to follow him.

People bring their troubles to Jesus.

## Jesus forgives a man

## Matthew 9:1

Jesus got into a boat and crossed the lake.

He came back to his own town.

## Matthew 9:2

Some men brought Jesus a man lying on a mat.

The man could not move his body freely.

Jesus saw how they trusted him.

He told the man, “Take courage, son.

Your sins are forgiven.”

**About the picture**

![A mat spread flat.](../pictures/mat.svg)

Find the mat.

The man lay on a mat like this.

**Help with a word**

Sins are wrong things people do against God.

## Matthew 9:3

Some teachers of God’s law thought, “This man insults God!”

**Pause and answer**

What did the teachers think about Jesus’ words?

## The man gets up

## Matthew 9:4

Jesus knew what the teachers were thinking.

He asked, “Why hold on to these evil thoughts?

## Matthew 9:5

“Which is easier to say?

‘Your sins are forgiven’?

Or ‘Get up and walk’?

## Matthew 9:6

“I want you to know what the Son of Man can do.

Here on earth I have the right to forgive sins.”

Then Jesus told the man, “Get up.

Pick up your mat and go home.”

**Help with a name**

Jesus called himself the Son of Man.

## Matthew 9:7

The man got up and went home.

## Matthew 9:8

The crowds saw this and were filled with wonder and fear.

They praised God for giving such power to human beings.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus tell the man to carry home?

## Jesus calls Matthew

## Matthew 9:9

Jesus went on and saw a man named Matthew.

Matthew sat where people came to pay taxes.

Jesus told Matthew, “Follow me.”

Matthew got up and followed Jesus.

## Matthew 9:10

Later, Jesus ate at Matthew’s house.

Many tax collectors and sinners came to the meal.

They ate with Jesus and his followers.

## Matthew 9:11

Some Pharisees saw them eating together.

They asked his followers, “Why does your teacher eat with tax collectors and sinners?”

**Help with these words**

The Pharisees were a Jewish religious group.

Tax collectors gathered money that people owed the government.

## Matthew 9:12

Jesus heard their question.

He said, “Healthy people do not need a doctor.

Sick people do.

## Matthew 9:13

“Go and learn what God means by these words.

‘I want mercy more than gifts offered in worship.’

I came to call sinners.

I did not come to call those who do right.”

**Help with this picture**

A doctor helps sick people.

Jesus came to help people who had done wrong.

Showing mercy means treating someone with kindness.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Matthew and Jesus share a meal?

## A question about fasting

## Matthew 9:14

John’s followers came to Jesus.

They asked, “We often fast, and the Pharisees fast too.

Why don’t your followers fast?”

**Help with a word**

To fast means choosing to go without food for a time.

## Matthew 9:15

Jesus replied, “Do wedding guests mourn while the groom is there?

One day someone will take the groom away.

Then they will fast.

**Help with this picture**

The groom is the man getting married.

Jesus compares his followers to guests celebrating with him.

## Matthew 9:16

“Nobody patches old clothes with new cloth that can still shrink.

When the patch shrinks, it pulls away.

That makes the tear even bigger.

## Matthew 9:17

“Nobody puts new wine into old leather bags.

The bags would burst.

The wine would spill, and the bags would be ruined.

People put new wine into new bags.

That keeps both the wine and the bags safe.”

**Help with this picture**

Old leather bags could no longer stretch as new wine changed.

Jesus uses cloth and wine to show how something new needs a fitting place.

**Pause and answer**

When does Jesus say the wedding guests will fast?

## A father and a woman seek help

## Matthew 9:18

While Jesus spoke, a leader from a synagogue came.

He knelt before Jesus.

“My daughter has just died,” he said.

“Come and put your hand on her.

Then she will live.”

**Help with a word**

A synagogue is a place where Jewish people meet for worship.

## Matthew 9:19

Jesus got up and went with him.

The followers of Jesus went too.

## Matthew 9:20

A woman came up behind Jesus.

She had been bleeding for twelve years.

She touched the edge of his cloak.

**About the picture**

![A cloak worn over other clothes.](../pictures/cloak.svg)

Find the lower edge of the cloak.

## Matthew 9:21

The woman thought, “I only need to touch his cloak.

Then I will get well.”

## Matthew 9:22

Jesus turned and saw her.

He said, “Take courage, daughter.

Your faith has healed you.”

The woman was well from that moment.

**Pause and answer**

What did the woman touch?

## Jesus takes the girl’s hand

## Matthew 9:23

Jesus came to the leader’s house.

People were playing flutes.

The crowd was making a lot of noise.

## Matthew 9:24

Jesus told them, “Go away.

The girl is asleep, not dead.”

They laughed at Jesus.

**Help with these words**

The father had told Jesus that the girl had died.

Jesus spoke of her as someone who would wake up.

## Matthew 9:25

Once the crowd was outside, Jesus went in.

He took the girl’s hand, and she got up.

## Matthew 9:26

News of this spread throughout that whole area.

**Pause and answer**

What happened when Jesus took her hand?

## Two blind men call out

## Matthew 9:27

Jesus went on from there.

Two blind men followed him.

They called out, “Son of David, show us mercy!”

**Help with a name**

Son of David was a name for the promised King.

King David belonged to Jesus’ family long ago.

## Matthew 9:28

Jesus went into a house.

The men came to him.

Jesus asked, “Do you believe I can do this?”

They answered, “Yes, Lord.”

## Matthew 9:29

Jesus touched their eyes.

He said, “Let it happen just as you have trusted me.”

## Matthew 9:30

Then the men could see.

Jesus warned them firmly, “Make sure no one hears about this!”

## Matthew 9:31

But the men went out and told people about Jesus.

The news spread all through the land.

**Pause and answer**

What did the two men believe Jesus could do?

## A man begins to speak

## Matthew 9:32

As the men left, people brought another man to Jesus.

An evil spirit troubled him, and he could not speak.

## Matthew 9:33

Jesus drove the spirit out, and the man spoke.

The crowds were amazed.

They said, “We have never seen anything like this in Israel!”

## Matthew 9:34

But the Pharisees said, “The ruler of evil spirits gives him power to drive them out.”

**Pause and answer**

What happened after the spirit left?

## Jesus sees people who need care

## Matthew 9:35

Jesus went through all the towns and villages.

He taught in the synagogues.

He shared the good news about God ruling as King.

He healed every kind of sickness.

## Matthew 9:36

Jesus looked at the crowds and felt deep care for them.

People were troubled and helpless, like sheep without a shepherd.

## Matthew 9:37

Jesus told his followers, “There is a great harvest to gather.

Yet there are so few workers.

## Matthew 9:38

“Ask the Lord of the harvest to send workers.

Ask him to send them into his harvest.”

**Help with these pictures**

A shepherd guides and cares for sheep.

A harvest is a crop ready to gather.

Jesus sees many people who need care.

He asks God’s people to pray for helpers.

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus ask his followers to pray for?
