# Matthew 7 — Hear Jesus’ words and act on them

**About this chapter**

Jesus teaches his followers about their choices.

He tells a story about two houses.

## Look at your own actions

## Matthew 7:1

“Do not judge other people, or you too will face judgment.”

## Matthew 7:2

“The same rule you use to judge others will apply to you.

The measure you use for others will be used for you.”

## Matthew 7:3

“Why look at a speck in your brother’s eye?

You miss the great piece of wood in your own eye!”

## Matthew 7:4

“How can you tell him, ‘Let me remove that speck’?

The great piece of wood is still in your eye!”

## Matthew 7:5

“You pretend you see clearly!

First remove the great piece of wood from your eye.

Then you can see to remove your brother’s speck.”

**Help with this picture**

A speck is tiny, like a bit of dust.

A large piece of wood would block your sight.

Jesus says to face your own wrongdoing first.

**Pause and answer**

Whose eye needs attention first?

## Take care with precious things

## Matthew 7:6

“Do not give dogs what is holy.

Do not throw pearls in front of pigs.

The animals may crush them under their feet.

Then they may turn and tear you apart.”

**Help with this picture**

Pearls are precious.

Pigs could step on pearls without seeing their worth.

Jesus urges care with things that belong to God.

**Pause and answer**

What could happen to the pearls?

## Ask your Father

## Matthew 7:7

“Ask, and you will receive.

Look, and you will find.

Knock, and the door will open for you.”

## Matthew 7:8

“Everyone who asks receives.

Everyone who looks finds.

The door opens for everyone who knocks.”

## Matthew 7:9

“Suppose your son asks for bread.

Would you give him a stone?”

## Matthew 7:10

“Suppose he asks for a fish.

Would you give him a snake?”

**About the picture**

![Loaves of bread and fish.](../pictures/loaves-and-fish.svg)

Find the bread and the fish.

Jesus names both as things a child might ask for.

## Matthew 7:11

“Even though you do wrong, you give your children good gifts.

How much more will your Father in heaven give good things to those who ask him!”

**Pause and answer**

What does a good father give his children?

## Choose the road to life

## Matthew 7:12

“Always treat others as you want them to treat you.

This sums up the teaching of the Law and the Prophets.”

**Help with these words**

The Law and the Prophets are writings about God’s commands and messages.

## Matthew 7:13

“Enter through the narrow gate.

There is a wide gate and a broad road.

Many people go that way.

That road leads to ruin.”

## Matthew 7:14

“The gate to life is small.

The road to life is narrow.

Few people find it.”

**Help with this picture**

Jesus uses roads to describe ways people choose to live.

**Pause and answer**

Which road leads to life?

## Look for good fruit

## Matthew 7:15

“Watch out for false prophets.

They come to you dressed like sheep.

Inside they are hungry wolves.”

**Help with this picture**

False prophets claim to speak for God.

They seem gentle but can hurt people.

## Matthew 7:16

“You will know them by their fruit.

Can you pick grapes from thorn bushes?

Can you pick figs from prickly weeds?”

## Matthew 7:17

“Every good tree grows good fruit.

A bad tree grows bad fruit.”

## Matthew 7:18

“A good tree cannot grow bad fruit.

A bad tree cannot grow good fruit.”

## Matthew 7:19

“People cut down every tree that grows no good fruit.

They throw it into the fire.”

## Matthew 7:20

“So you will know these people by their fruit.”

**Help with this picture**

Fruit shows what kind of tree you have.

A teacher’s actions show what kind of person they are.

**Pause and answer**

What shows what a teacher is really like?

## Do what the Father wants

## Matthew 7:21

“Some people call me ‘Lord, Lord.’

Those words alone will never bring them into the kingdom of heaven.

Only those who do what my Father in heaven wants will enter.”

## Matthew 7:22

“On that day many people will say, ‘Lord, Lord!

Didn’t we speak God’s messages in your name?

Didn’t we drive out evil spirits in your name?

Didn’t we do many miracles in your name?’”

**Help with these words**

A miracle is a wonderful act of power.

“In your name” means claiming to act for Jesus.

## Matthew 7:23

“Then I will tell them plainly, ‘I never knew you.

Go away from me, you who keep doing wrong!’”

**Pause and answer**

Who enters the kingdom of heaven?

## A house on rock and a house on sand

## Matthew 7:24

“Think of someone who hears my words and follows them.

That person is like a wise builder.

The builder built his house on rock.”

## Matthew 7:25

“Rain fell, and floodwater rushed against the house.

Strong winds beat against it.

The house stood firm because it rested on rock.”

## Matthew 7:26

“Think of someone who hears my words and ignores them.

That person is like a foolish builder.

The builder built his house on sand.”

## Matthew 7:27

“Rain fell, and floodwater rushed against the house.

Strong winds beat against it.

The house fell with a terrible crash.”

**Pause and answer**

Which house stood firm in the storm?

## The crowd listens

## Matthew 7:28

Jesus finished teaching.

The crowds were amazed at what Jesus taught.

## Matthew 7:29

They were used to hearing their teachers of the law.

Jesus spoke as someone with the right to tell them what to do.

**Pause and answer**

How did the crowd feel about Jesus’ teaching?
