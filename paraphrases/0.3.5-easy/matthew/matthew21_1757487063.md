# Matthew 21 — Jesus enters Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

Crowds welcome Jesus as he enters the city.

In the temple, leaders ask who gave him the right to act.

## Two followers bring the donkeys

## Matthew 21:1

Jesus and his followers were near Jerusalem.

They reached Bethphage on the Mount of Olives.

Jesus sent two followers ahead.

## Matthew 21:2

Jesus said, “Go into the village ahead of you.

Right away you will find a donkey tied there.

Her young donkey will be beside her.

Untie both animals and bring them to me.

## Matthew 21:3

“If anyone asks you why, say, ‘The Lord needs them.’

That person will send them right away.”

## Matthew 21:4

This happened just as the prophet had said.

## Matthew 21:5

“Tell the people of Zion, ‘Look! Your King is coming to you.

He is gentle.

He rides on a donkey, a young donkey.’”

**Help with a name**

Zion is a name for Jerusalem.

**Pause and answer**

What were the two followers to say if someone asked about the donkeys?

## The crowd welcomes Jesus

## Matthew 21:6

The two followers went and did what Jesus told them.

## Matthew 21:7

They brought the donkey and her young one.

They laid their cloaks on the animals.

Jesus sat on the cloaks.

## Matthew 21:8

Many people in the huge crowd spread their cloaks on the road.

Others cut branches from trees and spread them on the road.

**About the picture**

![A branch with leaves.](../pictures/branch.svg)

Trace the branch to a leaf.

People laid branches along the road to welcome Jesus.

## Matthew 21:9

People walked ahead of Jesus and behind him.

They shouted, “Hosanna to the Son of David!

God bless the one who comes in the Lord’s name!

Hosanna in the highest heaven!”

**Help with these words**

Hosanna means “save us, please.”

The crowds used it to praise Jesus.

Son of David is a name for the promised King.

## Matthew 21:10

Jesus entered Jerusalem.

People all over the city stirred with excitement.

They asked, “Who is this?”

## Matthew 21:11

The crowds answered, “This is Jesus, the prophet from Nazareth in Galilee.”

**Pause and answer**

What did people lay on the road?

## Jesus in the temple courtyards

## Matthew 21:12

Jesus went into the courtyards of the temple.

He sent out everyone who was buying or selling there.

He overturned the tables where people exchanged money.

He overturned the seats of the people selling doves.

## Matthew 21:13

Jesus said, “Scripture says, ‘People will call my house a house of prayer.’

But you are making it a hiding place for robbers.”

**Help with this picture**

Robbers hide in a den after stealing.

Jesus uses that picture to warn the people in the temple.

## Matthew 21:14

People who could not see or had trouble walking came to Jesus in the temple.

Jesus healed them.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus say God’s house was for?

## Children praise Jesus

## Matthew 21:15

The chief priests and teachers of the law saw these wonderful things.

They heard children shouting in the temple courtyards.

The children called, “Hosanna to the Son of David!”

The leaders became angry.

## Matthew 21:16

They asked Jesus, “Do you hear what these children are saying?”

“Yes,” Jesus answered.

“Have you read what Scripture says to God?

‘You have made even children and babies praise you.’”

## Matthew 21:17

Jesus left the city and went to Bethany.

He spent the night there.

**Pause and answer**

Whose voices made the leaders angry?

## A fig tree beside the road

## Matthew 21:18

In the morning Jesus went back toward the city.

He was hungry.

## Matthew 21:19

Jesus saw a fig tree beside the road.

He went over but found only leaves.

Jesus said, “May you never grow fruit again!”

At once the tree dried up.

## Matthew 21:20

His followers saw it and were amazed.

They asked, “How did the fig tree dry up so quickly?”

## Matthew 21:21

Jesus answered, “I tell you the truth.

If you trust and do not doubt, you can do what I did to the fig tree.

You can even tell this mountain to rise and fall into the sea.

It will happen.

**Help with this picture**

Moving a mountain seems impossible.

Jesus uses this picture to teach his followers to trust God.

## Matthew 21:22

“If you believe, you will receive whatever you ask for in prayer.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus find on the tree?

## The leaders ask who sent Jesus

## Matthew 21:23

Jesus returned to the temple courtyards and began teaching.

The chief priests and elders came to him.

They asked, “What right do you have to do these things?

Who gave you that right?”

## Matthew 21:24

Jesus replied, “I have one question for you too.

Answer me, and I will tell you who gave me this right.

## Matthew 21:25

“Who sent John to baptize people, God or human beings?”

The leaders talked among themselves.

“If we say ‘God,’ Jesus will ask why we didn’t believe John.

**Help with a word**

John used water to baptize people who were turning to God.

## Matthew 21:26

“But we are afraid to say ‘human beings.’

The people all believe John was a prophet.”

## Matthew 21:27

So the leaders answered Jesus, “We do not know.”

Jesus said, “Then I will not tell you who gave me the right to do these things.

**Pause and answer**

Who did Jesus ask the leaders about?

## A father asks two sons to work

## Matthew 21:28

“What do you think about this?

A father had two sons.

He told the first, ‘Son, work in the vineyard today.’

**Help with a word**

A vineyard is land where people grow grapes.

## Matthew 21:29

“The first son said, ‘I won’t go.’

Later he changed his mind and went.

## Matthew 21:30

“The father asked the second son to do the same.

The second son said, ‘I will, sir.’

But he did not go.

## Matthew 21:31

“Which son did what his father wanted?”

“The first,” the leaders answered.

Jesus told them, “I tell you the truth.

Tax collectors and prostitutes are entering God’s kingdom ahead of you.

**Help with these words**

Tax collectors gathered money for the government.

Prostitutes are people who have sex for payment.

## Matthew 21:32

“John came and showed you the right way to live.

You did not believe him.

The tax collectors and prostitutes did believe him.

Even when you saw this, you did not change your minds and believe John.

**Pause and answer**

Which son did what his father wanted?

## An owner sends servants to his vineyard

## Matthew 21:33

“Listen to another story.

A landowner planted a vineyard and built a wall around it.

He dug a place to press grapes and built a tower.

He rented the vineyard to farmers and went away.

## Matthew 21:34

“Harvest time was near.

The owner sent servants to the farmers to collect his share of the fruit.

## Matthew 21:35

“The farmers grabbed the servants.

They beat one, killed another, and threw stones at a third.

## Matthew 21:36

“The owner sent more servants than before.

The farmers treated them the same way.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the owner send his servants?

## The owner sends his son

## Matthew 21:37

“Finally the owner sent his son.

He said, ‘They will respect my son.’

## Matthew 21:38

“The farmers saw the son coming.

They said, ‘He will inherit the land.

Let’s kill him and take what would be his.’

## Matthew 21:39

“They grabbed the son and threw him outside the vineyard.

Then they killed him.

## Matthew 21:40

“When the owner comes back, what will he do to those farmers?”

## Matthew 21:41

The leaders answered, “He will put those cruel men to a terrible death.

He will rent the vineyard to other farmers.

They will give him his share at harvest time.”

**Pause and answer**

Whom did the owner send last?

## A stone the builders threw away

## Matthew 21:42

Jesus asked, “Have you read these words in Scripture?

‘The builders threw away a stone.

It became the cornerstone.

The Lord did this, and it fills us with wonder.’

**Help with this picture**

A cornerstone is an important stone at the corner of a building.

The builders rejected the very stone the Lord gave that place.

## Matthew 21:43

“So I tell you, God will take his kingdom away from you.

He will give it to people who produce its fruit.

**Help with this picture**

Fruit here means the good things people do when God rules their lives.

## Matthew 21:44

“Anyone who falls on this stone will break into pieces.

Anyone the stone falls on will be crushed.”

## Matthew 21:45

The chief priests and Pharisees heard Jesus’ stories.

They knew he was speaking about them.

## Matthew 21:46

They wanted to arrest Jesus, but they feared the crowds.

The people believed Jesus was a prophet.

**Pause and answer**

Who realised Jesus was speaking about them?
