# Matthew 15 — Jesus hears a mother and feeds a crowd

**About this chapter**

Jesus teaches about words that hurt people.

He hears a mother’s request and cares for a hungry crowd.

## A question about washing hands

## Matthew 15:1

Some Pharisees and teachers of God’s law came from Jerusalem.

They asked Jesus a question.

## Matthew 15:2

“Why do your followers disobey the custom our elders taught us?

They eat without washing their hands first.”

**Help with this custom**

The elders taught a special washing before meals.

The question was about a religious custom.

## Matthew 15:3

Jesus answered, “Why do you disobey God’s command to keep your custom?

## Matthew 15:4

“God said, ‘Honour your father and mother.’

God also said, ‘Anyone who curses a parent must die.’

**Help with a word**

To honour parents includes treating them with respect and helping them.

## Matthew 15:5

“But you allow someone to tell a parent this.

‘What I could have given you is a gift for God.’

## Matthew 15:6

“Then you say that person need not use the gift to help their parents.

Your custom sets aside what God said.

**Pause and answer**

Whose command did Jesus say the custom set aside?

## Words and worship

## Matthew 15:7

“You only pretend to honour God!

Isaiah spoke truly about you when he gave God’s message.

## Matthew 15:8

“‘These people honour me with their words.

But their hearts are far from me.

## Matthew 15:9

“‘Their worship is empty.

They teach human rules as if they came from me.’”

**Pause and answer**

Whose rules were the people teaching?

## Jesus speaks to the crowd

## Matthew 15:10

Jesus called the crowd over.

He said, “Listen and understand.

## Matthew 15:11

“What goes into your mouth does not make you unclean.

What comes out of your mouth can make you unclean.”

**Help with a word**

Here “unclean” means unclean before God.

## Matthew 15:12

His followers came to Jesus.

They asked, “Did you know your words upset the Pharisees?”

## Matthew 15:13

Jesus answered, “Every plant my Father in heaven did not plant will be pulled up, roots and all.

## Matthew 15:14

“Leave those guides alone.

They are like people who cannot see the way.

If one leads another who cannot see, both fall into a hole.”

**Help with this picture**

Jesus is talking about teachers and the people following them.

A teacher who misunderstands God’s command can lead others astray.

**Pause and answer**

How did the Pharisees feel about Jesus’ words?

## Peter asks what Jesus means

## Matthew 15:15

Peter said, “Please explain this picture to us.”

## Matthew 15:16

Jesus asked, “Do you still have trouble understanding?

## Matthew 15:17

“Food goes into your mouth and down to your stomach.

Later it leaves your body.

## Matthew 15:18

“But what you say comes from your heart.

That is what makes you unclean.

## Matthew 15:19

“Evil thoughts come from the heart.

So do murder, adultery, other sexual wrongdoing, and stealing.

People also tell lies about others and speak to ruin their good name.

**Help with these words**

The heart here means who you are inside.

Adultery means being unfaithful in marriage by having sex with someone else.

## Matthew 15:20

“These things make a person unclean.

Eating with unwashed hands does not.”

**Pause and answer**

Where does Jesus say harmful words begin?

## A mother calls to Jesus

## Matthew 15:21

Jesus left and went to the area around Tyre and Sidon.

## Matthew 15:22

A Canaanite woman from that area came to Jesus.

She called, “Lord, Son of David, have mercy on me!

An evil spirit is making my daughter suffer terribly.”

**Help with a name**

Son of David is a name for the promised King.

## Matthew 15:23

Jesus did not say a word.

His followers urged him, “Send her away.

She keeps calling out after us.”

## Matthew 15:24

Jesus answered, “God sent me only to the lost sheep of Israel.”

**Help with this picture**

A shepherd looks for sheep that have lost their way.

Jesus is speaking about the people of Israel.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did the woman want Jesus to help?

## The woman answers Jesus

## Matthew 15:25

The woman came and knelt before Jesus.

She said, “Lord, help me!”

## Matthew 15:26

Jesus answered, “It is wrong to take the children’s bread and throw it to the dogs.”

## Matthew 15:27

“Yes, Lord,” the woman said.

“Even the dogs eat crumbs that fall from their master’s table.”

**Help with this picture**

The woman says the children can still have their bread.

Even the crumbs are enough for the dogs.

She trusts that Jesus can help her daughter too.

## Matthew 15:28

Jesus answered, “Woman, you have great faith!

Let it happen as you asked.”

Her daughter was well from that very hour.

**Pause and answer**

What happened to the woman’s daughter?

## People come to the mountain

## Matthew 15:29

Jesus left there and walked beside the Sea of Galilee.

He went up a mountain and sat down.

## Matthew 15:30

Large crowds came to Jesus.

They brought people who could not walk, see, or speak.

They also brought people with injured limbs and many others.

They laid them at Jesus’ feet, and he healed them.

## Matthew 15:31

The crowd watched in wonder.

People who could not speak were speaking.

People with injured limbs were well again.

People who could not walk were walking.

People who could not see were seeing.

The crowd praised the God of Israel.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did the crowd praise?

## Seven loaves and a few fish

## Matthew 15:32

Jesus called his followers over.

He said, “I feel for these people.

They have stayed with me three days.

They have nothing to eat.

If I send them away hungry, they may faint on the way.

I don’t want that to happen.”

## Matthew 15:33

His followers asked, “Where can we find enough bread for everyone?

We are so far from any town.”

## Matthew 15:34

Jesus asked, “How many loaves do you have?”

They answered, “Seven, and a few small fish.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did Jesus want to feed the crowd?

## Everyone has enough

## Matthew 15:35

Jesus told the crowd to sit on the ground.

## Matthew 15:36

He took the seven loaves and the fish.

He thanked God and broke the food into pieces.

Jesus handed the food to his followers.

His followers handed it to the people.

## Matthew 15:37

Everyone ate until they had enough.

The followers gathered the pieces left over.

The pieces filled seven baskets.

**About the picture**

![A woven basket with a handle.](../pictures/basket.svg)

Find the basket’s handle.

The followers carried away seven baskets of food.

## Matthew 15:38

Four thousand men ate.

Women and children ate too.

## Matthew 15:39

Jesus sent the crowds home.

Then he got into the boat and went to the area of Magadan.

**Pause and answer**

How many baskets did the leftovers fill?
