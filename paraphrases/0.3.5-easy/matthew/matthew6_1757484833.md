# Matthew 6 — Pray and trust your Father

**About this chapter**

Jesus teaches his followers to give, pray, and trust God.

## Give quietly

## Matthew 6:1

“Do not do good just so people will notice you.

If you do, your Father in heaven will not reward you.”

## Matthew 6:2

“When you give to people in need, give quietly.

Do not blow a trumpet to announce your gift.

Some people pretend they only want to please God.

They show off in worship meetings and in the streets.

They want people to praise them.

I tell you, they already have their full reward.”

## Matthew 6:3

“When you give, picture your two hands.

Keep your left hand from knowing what your right hand gives.”

**Help with this picture**

Jesus means to give quietly, without showing off.

## Matthew 6:4

“Keep your giving secret.

Your Father sees what is done in secret and will reward you.”

**Pause and answer**

Who sees what you give in secret?

## Speak to your Father

## Matthew 6:5

“When you pray, do not pray to show off.

Some people love to stand and pray where others can see.

They choose places of worship and busy street corners.

I tell you, they already have their full reward.”

## Matthew 6:6

“Go into your room and shut the door.

Pray to your unseen Father.

He sees what is done in secret and will reward you.”

## Matthew 6:7

“When you pray, do not go on repeating empty words.

Some people who worship other gods pray that way.

They think more words will make their gods listen.”

## Matthew 6:8

“Do not be like them.

Your Father knows what you need before you ask.”

**Pause and answer**

What does your Father know before you ask?

## A prayer Jesus teaches

## Matthew 6:9

“Pray like this.

Our Father in heaven, may everyone honour your holy name.”

**Help with a word**

To pray means to talk to God.

## Matthew 6:10

“May your kingdom come.

May people on earth do what you want, as in heaven.”

**Help with these words**

God’s kingdom means God ruling as King.

## Matthew 6:11

“Give us today the bread we need.”

## Matthew 6:12

“Forgive our debts, as we have forgiven those who owe us.”

**Help with this picture**

Debts picture wrongs that need forgiveness.

## Matthew 6:13

“Do not lead us into temptation.

Rescue us from the evil one.”

**Help with words**

Temptation is pressure to do wrong.

The evil one means the devil.

**Pause and answer**

What food does Jesus teach us to ask for?

## Forgive others

## Matthew 6:14

“If you forgive others for their wrongs, your Father in heaven will forgive you too.”

## Matthew 6:15

“If you refuse to forgive others, your Father will not forgive your wrongs.”

**Pause and answer**

Whom does Jesus tell us to forgive?

## When you go without food

## Matthew 6:16

“When you fast, do not put on a sad face.

Some people change how their faces look.

They want others to see they are fasting.

I tell you, they already have their full reward.”

**Help with a word**

To fast means choosing to go without food for a time.

## Matthew 6:17

“When you fast, put oil on your head and wash your face.”

## Matthew 6:18

“Then people will not notice your fasting.

Only your unseen Father will know.

He sees what is done in secret and will reward you.”

**Pause and answer**

Who sees you when you fast in secret?

## Two places to keep treasure

## Matthew 6:19

“Do not store up treasures on earth.

Moths and rust destroy them.

Thieves break in and steal them.”

**About the picture**

![Two coins.](../pictures/two-coins.svg)

Look at the coins.

Money is one treasure people can store on earth.

## Matthew 6:20

“Store up treasures in heaven.

Moths and rust cannot destroy them.

Thieves cannot steal them.”

## Matthew 6:21

“Your heart will be where your treasure is.”

**Help with this picture**

What you treasure most shapes your thoughts and desires.

**Pause and answer**

Where does Jesus say to keep treasure?

## What gets your attention

## Matthew 6:22

“The eye is like a lamp for the body.

If your eyes are good, your whole body is full of light.”

## Matthew 6:23

“If your eyes are bad, your whole body is full of darkness.

If your inner light is darkness, that darkness is very great!”

**Help with this picture**

Jesus uses sight to picture a person's inner life.

## Matthew 6:24

“No one can serve two masters.

You will hate one and love the other.

You will stay close to one and look down on the other.

You cannot serve both God and money.”

**Pause and answer**

What two masters does Jesus name?

## Look at the birds and flowers

## Matthew 6:25

“Do not worry about food, drink, or clothes.

Life is more than food.

Your body is more than clothes.”

## Matthew 6:26

“Look at the birds flying in the sky.

They do not plant seeds or gather crops.

They do not store food in barns.

Yet your Father in heaven feeds them.

You matter to him much more than birds.”

## Matthew 6:27

“Can worrying add even 1 hour to your life?”

## Matthew 6:28

“Why worry about clothes?

Look at the field's lilies.

They grow without working or making cloth.”

## Matthew 6:29

“Even King Solomon wore beautiful clothes.

Yet none matched the beauty of one of these flowers.”

## Matthew 6:30

“God dresses the grass in the field like that.

Today it grows.

Tomorrow people burn it in an oven.

How much more will God give you clothes!

You trust him so little.”

**Pause and answer**

Who feeds the birds?

## What to seek first

## Matthew 6:31

“Do not worry about what to eat, drink, or wear.”

## Matthew 6:32

“Gentiles chase after all these things.

Your Father in heaven knows you need them.”

**Help with a word**

Gentiles means people who are not Jewish.

## Matthew 6:33

“Seek God’s kingdom first.

Seek what God says is right.

God will give you these other things too.”

## Matthew 6:34

“Do not worry about tomorrow.

Tomorrow will have its own worries.

Today has enough trouble of its own.”

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus tell us about tomorrow?
