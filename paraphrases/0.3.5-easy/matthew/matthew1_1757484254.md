# Matthew 1 — Jesus’ family and his birth

**About this chapter**

Matthew names people in Jesus’ family.

Then Matthew tells how Jesus got his name.

## From Abraham to King David

## Matthew 1:1

Matthew lists the family of Jesus Christ.

Jesus came from the family of King David.

Long before David, Abraham belonged to this same family.

**Help with a word**

Christ means the King God promised to send.

## Matthew 1:2

Abraham was Isaac's father.

Isaac was Jacob's father.

Jacob was the father of Judah and his brothers.

Start at Abraham. Follow the arrows to Jacob.

## Matthew 1:3

Judah and Tamar were the parents of Perez and Zerah.

Perez was Hezron's father.

Hezron was Ram's father.

## Matthew 1:4

Ram was Amminadab's father.

Amminadab was Nahshon's father.

Nahshon was Salmon's father.

## Matthew 1:5

Salmon and Rahab were Boaz's parents.

Boaz and Ruth were Obed's parents.

Obed was Jesse's father.

## Matthew 1:6

Jesse was King David's father.

David and Uriah's wife were Solomon's parents.

**Pause and answer**

Who was King David’s father?

## From David’s family to Babylon

## Matthew 1:7

Solomon was Rehoboam's father.

Rehoboam was Abijah's father.

Abijah was Asa's father.

## Matthew 1:8

Asa was Jehoshaphat's father.

Jehoshaphat was Joram's father.

Joram was Uzziah's father.

## Matthew 1:9

Uzziah was Jotham's father.

Jotham was Ahaz's father.

Ahaz was Hezekiah's father.

## Matthew 1:10

Hezekiah was Manasseh's father.

Manasseh was Amon's father.

Amon was Josiah's father.

## Matthew 1:11

Josiah was the father of Jeconiah and his brothers.

At that time, enemies took the people to Babylon.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the enemies take the people?

## From Babylon to Jesus

## Matthew 1:12

After the people went to Babylon, Jeconiah became Shealtiel’s father.

Shealtiel was Zerubbabel’s father.

## Matthew 1:13

Zerubbabel was Abiud's father.

Abiud was Eliakim's father.

Eliakim was Azor's father.

## Matthew 1:14

Azor was Zadok's father.

Zadok was Achim's father.

Achim was Eliud's father.

## Matthew 1:15

Eliud was Eleazar's father.

Eleazar was Matthan's father.

Matthan was Jacob's father.

## Matthew 1:16

Jacob was Joseph’s father.

Joseph was Mary’s husband.

Mary gave birth to Jesus.

People call Jesus the Christ.

## Matthew 1:17

Matthew counts fourteen generations from Abraham to David.

He counts fourteen from David until the people went to Babylon.

He counts fourteen more from that time to Christ.

**Help with a word**

Each generation is one step in a family.

Think of a grandparent, a parent, and a child.

**Pause and answer**

Who was Mary’s husband?

## An angel speaks to Joseph

## Matthew 1:18

This is how Jesus Christ came to be born.

Mary had promised to marry Joseph.

Before they had sex, Mary was already pregnant.

The baby came from the Holy Spirit.

## Matthew 1:19

Joseph wanted to do what was right before God.

He wanted to spare Mary public shame.

So Joseph planned to end their marriage promise quietly.

## Matthew 1:20

Joseph was still thinking about this.

An angel of the Lord came to Joseph in a dream.

The angel said, “Joseph, you belong to David’s family.

Do not fear taking Mary as your wife.

Her baby comes from the Holy Spirit.

## Matthew 1:21

“Mary will give birth to a son.

Give him the name Jesus.

He will save his people from their sins.”

**Help with these words**

An angel brings messages from God.

Sins are wrong things people do against God.

**Pause and answer**

Who spoke to Joseph in the dream?

## Joseph names the baby

## Matthew 1:22

Long ago, the Lord spoke through a prophet.

Now the prophet’s words came true.

## Matthew 1:23

The prophet said, “Look! A virgin will have a baby.

She will give birth to a son.

People will call him Immanuel.”

Immanuel means “God with us.”

**Help with these words**

A prophet speaks God’s message to people.

A virgin is someone who has never had sex.

## Matthew 1:24

Joseph woke up.

He did what the Lord’s angel told him.

Joseph took Mary as his wife.

## Matthew 1:25

Joseph did not have sex with Mary before the birth.

Mary gave birth to a son.

Joseph named him Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

What name did Joseph give the baby?
