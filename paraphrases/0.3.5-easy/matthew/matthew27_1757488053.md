# Matthew 27 — Jesus dies and Joseph buries him

**About this chapter**

Pilate hears the charges against Jesus.

Women who followed Jesus watch from a distance.

## The leaders hand Jesus over

## Matthew 27:1

Early in the morning, all the chief priests and elders met.

They made plans to have Jesus put to death.

## Matthew 27:2

They tied Jesus up and led him away.

They handed him over to Pilate, the governor.

**Pause and answer**

Who received Jesus from the leaders?

## Judas returns the silver

## Matthew 27:3

Judas saw that the leaders had condemned Jesus.

Judas felt deep regret for betraying him.

He returned the thirty pieces of silver to the chief priests and elders.

## Matthew 27:4

Judas said, “I have sinned.

I handed over an innocent man to die.”

They answered, “What is that to us?

That is your responsibility.”

## Matthew 27:5

Judas threw the silver into the temple and left.

Then he went away and hanged himself.

**Pause and answer**

What did Judas say about Jesus?

## A field becomes a burial place

## Matthew 27:6

The chief priests picked up the silver.

They said, “Our law forbids putting this money with the temple funds.

It paid for a man’s death.”

## Matthew 27:7

They talked it over and bought a potter’s field with the money.

It became a place to bury people from other lands.

**Help with a word**

A potter makes bowls and jars from clay.

## Matthew 27:8

That is why people still called it the Field of Blood when Matthew wrote.

## Matthew 27:9

So the words of Jeremiah the prophet came true.

“They took the thirty pieces of silver.

That was the price the people of Israel had set on him.

## Matthew 27:10

“They used the money to buy the potter’s field, just as the Lord commanded me.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the chief priests buy?

## Jesus stands before Pilate

## Matthew 27:11

Jesus stood before Pilate.

Pilate asked, “Are you the King of the Jews?”

Jesus answered, “You have said so.”

## Matthew 27:12

The chief priests and elders accused Jesus.

Jesus gave no answer.

## Matthew 27:13

Pilate asked, “Do you hear all the things they accuse you of doing?”

## Matthew 27:14

Jesus did not answer a single charge.

This greatly amazed Pilate.

**Pause and answer**

What amazed Pilate?

## Pilate offers the crowd a choice

## Matthew 27:15

At the feast the governor usually set one prisoner free.

The crowd chose the prisoner.

## Matthew 27:16

At that time a prisoner named Barabbas was well known for doing wrong.

## Matthew 27:17

The crowd gathered.

Pilate asked, “Whom shall I set free for you?

Barabbas, or Jesus who is called the Christ?”

## Matthew 27:18

Pilate knew why the leaders had handed Jesus over.

They were jealous of him.

## Matthew 27:19

Pilate sat in the judge’s seat.

His wife sent him a message.

“Have nothing to do with the case against that innocent man.

A dream about him caused me terrible distress today.”

**Pause and answer**

Who sent Pilate a message about a dream?

## The crowd calls for Barabbas

## Matthew 27:20

The chief priests and elders urged the crowd to ask for Barabbas.

They persuaded the people to ask for Jesus’ death.

## Matthew 27:21

Pilate asked, “Which prisoner shall I set free?”

The people answered, “Barabbas!”

## Matthew 27:22

Pilate asked, “Then what shall I do with Jesus, the one called Christ?”

They all shouted, “Put him on a cross!”

## Matthew 27:23

“Why?” asked Pilate.

“What wrong has he done?”

They shouted even louder, “Put him on a cross!”

## Matthew 27:24

Pilate saw that he could not calm the crowd.

A riot was starting.

He washed his hands with water in front of them.

He said, “I am innocent of this man’s death.

You take responsibility.”

**Help with this action**

Pilate washed his hands to show he wanted no blame for Jesus’ death.

## Matthew 27:25

All the people in that crowd answered, “Let us and our children answer for his death!”

## Matthew 27:26

Pilate set Barabbas free.

He had Jesus whipped.

Then he handed Jesus over to die on a cross.

**Pause and answer**

Which prisoner did Pilate set free?

## The soldiers mock Jesus

## Matthew 27:27

The governor’s soldiers took Jesus into their headquarters.

The whole group of soldiers gathered around him.

## Matthew 27:28

They took off his clothes and dressed him in a bright red robe.

## Matthew 27:29

They twisted thorny branches into a crown and put it on his head.

They placed a stick in his right hand.

They knelt before him and made fun of him.

“Hail, King of the Jews!” they said.

## Matthew 27:30

They spat on Jesus.

They took the stick and hit his head again and again.

## Matthew 27:31

Then they took off the robe and put his clothes back on him.

They led him away to put him on a cross.

**Pause and answer**

What did the soldiers put on Jesus’ head?

## Simon carries the cross

## Matthew 27:32

On the way they found Simon, a man from Cyrene.

They forced Simon to carry Jesus’ cross.

## Matthew 27:33

They came to a place called Golgotha.

Its name means “Place of the Skull.”

## Matthew 27:34

They gave Jesus wine mixed with something bitter called gall.

Jesus tasted it but refused to drink it.

## Matthew 27:35

The soldiers put Jesus on the cross.

They cast lots to decide who would get his clothes.

**Help with these words**

Casting lots means using chance to decide who gets something.

## Matthew 27:36

Then they sat down and kept watch over Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

Who carried Jesus’ cross on the way?

## People insult Jesus

## Matthew 27:37

Above his head they put a sign giving the charge against him.

It read, “This is Jesus, the King of the Jews.”

## Matthew 27:38

The soldiers put two robbers on crosses beside Jesus.

One was on his right, the other on his left.

## Matthew 27:39

People walking past shook their heads and insulted Jesus.

## Matthew 27:40

They said, “You said you could destroy the temple and rebuild it in three days!

Save yourself!

If you are the Son of God, come down from the cross!”

## Matthew 27:41

The chief priests, the teachers of the law, and the elders made fun of Jesus too.

## Matthew 27:42

They said, “He saved others, but he cannot save himself!

He is the King of Israel!

Let him come down from the cross.

Then we will believe him.

## Matthew 27:43

“He trusts God.

Let God rescue him now if God wants him.

He said, ‘I am the Son of God.’”

## Matthew 27:44

The robbers on the crosses beside Jesus also insulted him.

**Pause and answer**

What did the sign above Jesus say?

## Jesus cries out

## Matthew 27:45

From noon until three in the afternoon, the whole land was dark.

## Matthew 27:46

About three o’clock Jesus cried out loudly, “Eli, Eli, lema sabachthani?”

This means, “My God, my God, why have you left me?”

## Matthew 27:47

Some people nearby heard Jesus.

They said, “He is calling Elijah.”

## Matthew 27:48

One person ran to get a sponge.

He soaked it in sour wine, put it on a reed, and held it up for Jesus to drink.

## Matthew 27:49

The others said, “Leave him alone.

Let’s see if Elijah comes to save him.”

## Matthew 27:50

Jesus cried out loudly again.

He gave up his spirit and died.

**Pause and answer**

To whom did Jesus call when the land was dark?

## The ground shakes

## Matthew 27:51

Just then the temple curtain tore in two from top to bottom.

The ground shook and the rocks split.

## Matthew 27:52

Tombs broke open.

Many of God’s people who had died came back to life.

## Matthew 27:53

After Jesus rose from the dead, they came out of the tombs.

They went into Jerusalem, the holy city.

Many people saw them.

## Matthew 27:54

The Roman officer and the soldiers guarding Jesus felt the earthquake.

They saw what had happened and were terrified.

They said, “He really was the Son of God!”

**Pause and answer**

What did the officer and the soldiers say about Jesus?

## Women watch from a distance

## Matthew 27:55

Many women watched from a distance.

They had followed Jesus from Galilee to care for him.

## Matthew 27:56

Mary Magdalene was there.

So was Mary, the mother of James and Joseph.

The mother of Zebedee’s sons was there too.

**Pause and answer**

Where had the women followed Jesus from?

## Joseph lays Jesus in a tomb

## Matthew 27:57

That evening a rich man from Arimathea came.

His name was Joseph, and he too followed Jesus.

## Matthew 27:58

Joseph went to Pilate and asked for Jesus’ body.

Pilate ordered his men to give the body to Joseph.

## Matthew 27:59

Joseph took the body and wrapped it in clean linen cloth.

## Matthew 27:60

He laid Jesus in his own new tomb, cut into the rock.

He rolled a large stone across the entrance and left.

## Matthew 27:61

Mary Magdalene and the other Mary sat facing the tomb.

**Pause and answer**

Who sat facing the tomb?

## Guards at the tomb

## Matthew 27:62

The day after Preparation Day, the chief priests and Pharisees went to Pilate.

**Help with a name**

Preparation Day was the day before the Sabbath, the Jewish day of rest.

## Matthew 27:63

They said, “Sir, we remember what that deceiver said when he was alive.

He said, ‘After three days I will rise again.’

## Matthew 27:64

“Give orders to guard the tomb until the third day.

His followers might steal the body and say he rose from the dead.

That lie would be worse than the first.”

## Matthew 27:65

Pilate answered, “You have a guard.

Go and make the tomb as secure as you can.”

## Matthew 27:66

They went and sealed the stone.

They stationed guards at the tomb.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the leaders want guards at the tomb?
