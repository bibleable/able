# Matthew 3 — John baptizes Jesus

**About this chapter**

John tells people to get ready for the Lord.

Jesus comes to John at the Jordan River.

## John calls people to turn to God

## Matthew 3:1

In those days John the Baptist came to the desert in Judea.

John brought the people a message.

## Matthew 3:2

John said, “Turn away from sin.

The kingdom of heaven is near.”

**Help with these words**

Sin is wrongdoing against God.

Turning from sin means leaving wrongdoing and turning to God.

The kingdom of heaven means God ruling as King.

## Matthew 3:3

Long ago, the prophet Isaiah spoke about John.

Isaiah wrote, “Someone is calling in the desert.

‘Get the road ready for the Lord.

Make the paths straight for him.’”

**Help with this picture**

People prepare a road for a king to come.

John tells people to get ready to welcome the Lord.

**Pause and answer**

Where did John speak to the people?

## People come to the river

## Matthew 3:4

John wore clothes made from camel hair.

He wore a leather belt around his waist.

He ate locusts and wild honey.

**Help with a word**

Locusts are insects like large grasshoppers.

## Matthew 3:5

People came to John from Jerusalem and throughout Judea.

Others came from all around the Jordan River.

## Matthew 3:6

The people admitted the wrong things they had done.

John baptized them in the Jordan River.

**Help with a word**

John used water to baptize people.

Their baptism showed they wanted to turn to God.

**Pause and answer**

Where did John baptize the people?

## John warns the leaders

## Matthew 3:7

Many Pharisees and Sadducees came where John was baptizing people.

John told them, “You are a family of poisonous snakes!

Who warned you to run from God’s coming anger?

**Help with these words**

The Pharisees and Sadducees were two Jewish religious groups.

John compares these people to dangerous snakes.

## Matthew 3:8

“Grow fruit that shows you have turned away from sin.

## Matthew 3:9

“Do not tell yourselves, ‘Abraham is our father.’

I tell you, God can use these stones.

God can make children for Abraham from them.

## Matthew 3:10

“The axe is already at the roots of the trees.

Every tree that grows bad fruit or no fruit will fall.

Someone will cut it down and throw it into the fire.

**Help with this picture**

Good fruit shows that a tree is healthy.

Good actions show that a person has turned to God.

The axe is a warning that God will judge people.

**Pause and answer**

What can show that someone has turned to God?

## John speaks about the one who is coming

## Matthew 3:11

“I baptize you with water as you turn from sin.

Someone stronger than I am is coming after me.

I do not deserve even to carry his sandals.

He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and with fire.

## Matthew 3:12

“He holds a fork for tossing grain into the air.

He will clear the place where he separates the grain.

He will gather the wheat into his barn.

He will burn the chaff in a fire no one can put out.”

**About the picture**

![A stalk with ripe grain at the top.](../pictures/grain.svg)

Find the grain at the top of the stalk.

Farmers keep the grain for food.

Chaff is the light covering they separate from the grain.

John uses this picture to explain God’s judgment.

**Pause and answer**

What does the farmer put in the barn?

## Jesus comes to John

## Matthew 3:13

Then Jesus came from Galilee to the Jordan River.

Jesus wanted John to baptize him.

## Matthew 3:14

John tried to stop him.

John said, “I need you to baptize me.

Why are you coming to me?”

## Matthew 3:15

Jesus answered, “Let it happen this way now.

In this way we do everything God says is right.”

Then John agreed.

## Matthew 3:16

John baptized Jesus.

At once Jesus came up out of the water.

Suddenly heaven opened.

Jesus saw God’s Spirit come down like a dove.

God’s Spirit rested on Jesus.

**Help with a word**

A dove is a bird.

## Matthew 3:17

A voice from heaven said, “This is my Son.

I love him.

I am very pleased with him.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the voice call Jesus?
