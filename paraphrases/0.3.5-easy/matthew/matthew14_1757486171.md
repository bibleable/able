# Matthew 14 — Jesus feeds a crowd and walks on water

**About this chapter**

Jesus hears about John’s death.

Later he feeds a crowd and helps Peter on the lake.

## Herod hears about Jesus

## Matthew 14:1

Herod, the ruler of that region, heard news about Jesus.

## Matthew 14:2

Herod told his servants, “This is John the Baptist!

John has come back from the dead.

That is why he can do these powerful deeds.”

**About the story**

Herod thinks John has come back to life.

Matthew now tells what happened to John earlier.

**Pause and answer**

Who did Herod think Jesus was?

## Why Herod put John in prison

## Matthew 14:3

Earlier, Herod had arrested John.

He had John tied up and put in prison.

He did this because of Herodias, his brother Philip’s wife.

## Matthew 14:4

John kept telling Herod, “The law does not allow you to have her as your wife.”

## Matthew 14:5

Herod wanted to kill John, but he feared the people.

They believed John was a prophet.

**Help with a word**

A prophet speaks God’s message to people.

**Pause and answer**

Why was Herod afraid of the people?

## A promise at a birthday meal

## Matthew 14:6

On Herod’s birthday, the daughter of Herodias danced for the guests.

Herod was pleased with her dancing.

## Matthew 14:7

Herod made her a solemn promise.

He would give her whatever she asked for.

## Matthew 14:8

Her mother told her what to ask for.

The girl said, “Give me John the Baptist’s head here on a serving plate.”

**Pause and answer**

Who told the girl what to ask for?

## John’s followers tell Jesus

## Matthew 14:9

Herod felt deep sorrow.

But he had made his promise in front of his guests.

So he ordered his men to give her what she asked for.

## Matthew 14:10

Herod sent someone to cut off John’s head in prison.

## Matthew 14:11

Someone brought John’s head on a serving plate.

They gave it to the girl.

She carried it to her mother.

## Matthew 14:12

John’s followers came and took his body.

They buried John.

Then they went and told Jesus what had happened.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did John’s followers tell after they buried him?

## Jesus sees the crowd

## Matthew 14:13

Jesus heard what had happened to John.

He left quietly by boat for a place away from people.

But the crowds heard about it.

People walked from their towns to follow him.

## Matthew 14:14

Jesus stepped ashore and saw the large crowd.

He felt deep care for the people.

He healed those who were sick.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus do for the sick people on the shore?

## Five loaves and two fish

## Matthew 14:15

That evening the followers of Jesus came to him.

They said, “It is late, and we are far from the villages.

Send the people away so they can buy food.”

## Matthew 14:16

Jesus answered, “They do not need to leave.

You give them something to eat.”

## Matthew 14:17

His followers answered, “We have only five loaves of bread and two fish.”

## Matthew 14:18

Jesus said, “Bring them here to me.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus ask his followers to bring him?

## Everyone has enough to eat

## Matthew 14:19

Jesus told the people to sit on the grass.

He took the five loaves and the two fish.

He looked up to heaven and spoke a blessing.

Then Jesus broke the bread and gave it to his followers.

His followers gave the bread to the people.

**Help with these words**

Here a blessing is a prayer of praise to God.

## Matthew 14:20

Everyone ate until they had enough.

The followers picked up the pieces of bread left over.

The pieces filled twelve baskets.

## Matthew 14:21

About five thousand men ate that day.

Women and children ate too.

**Pause and answer**

Who handed the bread to the people?

## Jesus prays alone

## Matthew 14:22

At once Jesus told his followers to get into the boat.

They were to cross the lake ahead of him.

Jesus stayed to send the crowd home.

## Matthew 14:23

After the people left, Jesus went up the mountain to pray.

Evening came, and Jesus was there alone.

**Help with a word**

To pray means to talk to God.

## Matthew 14:24

By then the boat was far from land.

The wind blew against the boat.

Waves kept hitting it.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Jesus go to pray?

## Jesus walks toward the boat

## Matthew 14:25

In the last part of the night, Jesus came toward his followers.

He was walking on the lake.

## Matthew 14:26

His followers saw him walking on the water.

They cried out in fear, “It’s a ghost!”

## Matthew 14:27

Jesus spoke to them at once.

“Take courage!

It’s me.

Do not be afraid.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus say when his followers were afraid?

## Peter steps onto the water

## Matthew 14:28

Peter answered, “Lord, if it is you, give the word.

Tell me to come to you on the water.”

## Matthew 14:29

Jesus said, “Come.”

Peter stepped out of the boat.

He walked on the water toward Jesus.

## Matthew 14:30

Then Peter saw how strong the wind was.

He was afraid and began to sink.

He cried out, “Lord, save me!”

## Matthew 14:31

At once Jesus reached out and caught Peter.

Jesus said, “You trust me so little.

Why did you doubt?”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus do when Peter began to sink?

## The wind dies down

## Matthew 14:32

Jesus and Peter climbed into the boat.

Then the wind died down.

## Matthew 14:33

The people in the boat worshiped Jesus.

They said, “You really are the Son of God!”

**Pause and answer**

What did the people in the boat call Jesus?

## People bring the sick to Jesus

## Matthew 14:34

They crossed the lake and landed at Gennesaret.

## Matthew 14:35

The men there recognised Jesus.

They sent word to people throughout the area.

People brought everyone who was sick to Jesus.

## Matthew 14:36

They begged Jesus to let them touch just the edge of his cloak.

Everyone who touched him got well.

**Help with a word**

A cloak is a piece of clothing worn over other clothes.

**Pause and answer**

What did the people ask to touch?
