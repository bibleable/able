# Matthew 11 — John sends a question to Jesus

**About this chapter**

John sends messengers from prison.

Jesus speaks to the crowds about John.

## A question from prison

## Matthew 11:1

Jesus finished giving instructions to his twelve followers.

Then he went on to teach and preach in their towns.

## Matthew 11:2

John was in prison.

John heard about the things Christ was doing.

He sent his followers to Jesus.

**Help with a name**

Christ means the King God promised to send.

## Matthew 11:3

They asked, “Are you the one we were expecting?

Or should we look for someone else?”

## Matthew 11:4

Jesus answered, “Go back to John.

Tell him what you hear and see.

## Matthew 11:5

“Blind people see, and people who could not walk now walk.

People with leprosy become clean, and deaf people hear.

Dead people come back to life.

Poor people hear the good news.

## Matthew 11:6

“God blesses those who do not turn away because of me.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus tell the messengers to report?

## John prepares the way

## Matthew 11:7

John’s followers left.

Jesus spoke to the crowds about John.

“What did you go into the desert to see?

A reed bending in the wind?

**Help with a word**

A reed is a tall plant with a stem that bends.

**About the picture**

![A tall reed bends to one side.](../pictures/bending-reed.svg)

Trace the stem from the ground to its tip.

## Matthew 11:8

“Did you go to see a man in fine clothes?

People who wear fine clothes live in kings’ palaces.

## Matthew 11:9

“Did you go to see a prophet?

Yes, and John is more than a prophet.

## Matthew 11:10

“Scripture speaks about John in these words.

‘Look, I will send my messenger ahead of you.

He will prepare the way for you.’

**Help with these words**

A prophet speaks God’s message.

John also prepared people to welcome Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

What work did God give the messenger?

## Jesus tells people to listen

## Matthew 11:11

“I tell you truly, no one ever born has been greater than John the Baptist.

Yet even the least in the kingdom of heaven is greater than John.

## Matthew 11:12

“From the days of John the Baptist until now, the kingdom of heaven has faced violence.

Violent people try to take it by force.

## Matthew 11:13

“Until John came, the Law and all the Prophets spoke about what was to come.

## Matthew 11:14

“If you can accept this, John is the Elijah who was to come.

**Help with a name**

Elijah was a prophet who lived long before John.

John came to do the work people expected Elijah to do.

## Matthew 11:15

“If you have ears, listen!

**Pause and answer**

Which earlier prophet does Jesus name?

## Children in the marketplace

## Matthew 11:16

“What are the people of this time like?

They are like children sitting in the marketplace.

The children call to their playmates.

## Matthew 11:17

“‘We played the flute for you, but you did not dance.

We sang a sad funeral song, but you did not mourn.’

**Help with this picture**

The playmates refuse to join either song.

Jesus compares them to people who rejected both John and Jesus.

## Matthew 11:18

“John came fasting and keeping away from wine.

People said, ‘He has an evil spirit!’

## Matthew 11:19

“The Son of Man came eating and drinking.

People say, ‘Look at him!

He eats too much and drinks too much wine.

He is a friend of tax collectors and sinners!’

But what wisdom does shows that wisdom is right.”

**Help with a name**

Jesus calls himself the Son of Man.

**Pause and answer**

What did the children want their playmates to do when the flute played?

## Towns that saw Jesus’ miracles

## Matthew 11:20

Jesus spoke sternly to the towns where he had done most of his miracles.

The people had seen them and still had not turned from wrongdoing.

## Matthew 11:21

“Chorazin, trouble is coming for you!

Bethsaida, trouble is coming for you too!

Suppose Tyre and Sidon had seen the miracles you saw.

Long ago their people would have turned from wrongdoing.

They would have worn rough cloth and covered themselves with ashes.

**Help with this action**

People wore rough cloth and used ashes to show sorrow.

Here they would be sorry for doing wrong.

## Matthew 11:22

“I tell you, the day of judgment will be easier for Tyre and Sidon than for you.

## Matthew 11:23

“And you, Capernaum!

Do you expect to rise to heaven?

You will go down to Hades.

Suppose Sodom had seen the miracles you saw.

That city would still be here today.

**Help with a word**

Hades means the place of the dead.

## Matthew 11:24

“I tell you, on judgment day Sodom will fare better than you.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the people in these towns still need to do?

## Jesus speaks to his Father

## Matthew 11:25

Then Jesus said, “Father, I praise you.

You are Lord of heaven and earth.

You hid these things from the wise and well taught.

You showed them to little children.

## Matthew 11:26

“Yes, Father, this pleased you.

## Matthew 11:27

“My Father has put everything in my hands.

Only the Father knows the Son.

Only the Son knows the Father, along with those the Son chooses to show him to.

**Pause and answer**

Who showed these things to little children?

## Jesus offers rest

## Matthew 11:28

“Come to me, all who are tired from carrying heavy loads.

I will give you rest.

## Matthew 11:29

“Take my yoke and learn from me.

I am gentle and humble in heart.

You will find rest deep within you.

**Help with this picture**

A yoke is a wooden bar that helps animals pull a load.

Jesus invites people to learn from him as a gentle teacher.

## Matthew 11:30

“My yoke is easy to wear.

The load I give you is light.”

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus offer people who are tired?
