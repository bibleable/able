# Matthew 19 — Jesus welcomes children and answers a rich man

**About this chapter**

Jesus answers questions about marriage.

He welcomes children and speaks to a young man about eternal life.

## A question about marriage

## Matthew 19:1

When Jesus finished speaking, he left Galilee.

He went to the part of Judea across the Jordan River.

## Matthew 19:2

Large crowds followed Jesus.

He healed people there.

## Matthew 19:3

Some Pharisees came to test Jesus.

They asked, “Does the law let a man divorce his wife for any reason?”

**Help with a word**

Divorce means legally ending a marriage.

## Matthew 19:4

Jesus answered, “Have you read what God did at the beginning?

God made people male and female.

## Matthew 19:5

“God said, ‘This is why a man will leave his father and mother.

He will join his wife.

The two will become one flesh.’

## Matthew 19:6

“So the husband and wife are now one flesh.

No one should separate those whom God has joined together.”

**Help with these words**

“One flesh” describes the close bond of marriage.

Husband and wife share a life together.

**Pause and answer**

Who does Jesus say joins a husband and wife together?

## Why Moses allowed divorce

## Matthew 19:7

The Pharisees asked, “Then why did Moses give this command?

A man could give his wife divorce papers and send her away.”

## Matthew 19:8

Jesus answered, “Moses allowed you to divorce your wives because your hearts were hard.

It was different at the beginning.

**Help with this picture**

A hard heart pictures someone who refuses to listen to God.

## Matthew 19:9

“I tell you this about a man who divorces his wife and marries another woman.

He commits adultery, unless the divorce was for sexual wrongdoing.”

**Help with a word**

Adultery means breaking a marriage promise by having sex with someone else.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus say about the people’s hearts?

## A question about staying single

## Matthew 19:10

His followers said, “If marriage is like that, it is better to stay single.”

## Matthew 19:11

Jesus answered, “Only those who have received this gift can accept these words.

## Matthew 19:12

“Some men were born eunuchs.

Other men became eunuchs because of what people did to them.

Still others choose to live without sex for the sake of the kingdom of heaven.

Anyone who can accept this should accept it.”

**Help with a word**

Here Jesus uses “eunuch” for men whose bodies could not have sexual relations in the usual way.

He also speaks about people who choose a life without marriage to serve God.

**Pause and answer**

Why do some people choose to stay single?

## Jesus welcomes the children

## Matthew 19:13

People brought little children to Jesus.

They wanted Jesus to put his hands on the children and pray for them.

His followers scolded the people who brought them.

## Matthew 19:14

Jesus said, “Let the little children come to me.

Do not stand in their way.

The kingdom of heaven belongs to people like them.”

## Matthew 19:15

Jesus placed his hands on the children.

Then he went on from there.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus tell his followers to let the children do?

## A man asks about eternal life

## Matthew 19:16

A man came to Jesus with a question.

“Teacher, what good thing must I do to receive eternal life?”

**Help with these words**

Eternal life is life with God that never ends.

## Matthew 19:17

Jesus answered, “Why do you ask me about what is good?

Only One is good.

If you want to enter life, keep the commandments.”

## Matthew 19:18

The man asked, “Which commandments?”

Jesus answered, “Do not murder.

Do not commit adultery.

Do not steal.

Do not tell lies about someone when you are a witness.

## Matthew 19:19

“Honour your father and mother.

Love your neighbour as you love yourself.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus say about loving your neighbour?

## The young man faces a choice

## Matthew 19:20

The young man said, “I have kept all these commands.

What am I still missing?”

## Matthew 19:21

Jesus told him, “If you want to be perfect, go and sell what you own.

Give the money to poor people.

You will have treasure in heaven.

Then come and follow me.”

## Matthew 19:22

The young man owned many things.

When he heard this, he went away sad.

**Pause and answer**

Who was to receive the money from the sale?

## A camel and a needle

## Matthew 19:23

Jesus told his followers, “I tell you the truth.

It is hard for a rich person to enter the kingdom of heaven.

## Matthew 19:24

“I tell you again, think of a camel going through the eye of a needle.

Even that is easier than a rich person entering God’s kingdom.”

**About the picture**

![A sewing needle with a small hole at one end.](../pictures/needle.svg)

Find the tiny hole at the end.

That hole is called the eye of the needle.

A camel is far too big to fit through it.

## Matthew 19:25

His followers were very surprised.

They asked, “Then who can be saved?”

## Matthew 19:26

Jesus looked at them.

He said, “People cannot do this by themselves.

God can do all things.”

**Pause and answer**

Who can do what people cannot do by themselves?

## Peter asks about the future

## Matthew 19:27

Peter answered, “We have left everything to follow you.

What will we receive?”

## Matthew 19:28

Jesus told them, “I tell you the truth.

One day all things will be made new.

The Son of Man will sit on his glorious throne.

You who have followed me will sit on twelve thrones.

You will judge the twelve tribes of Israel.

**Help with these words**

Jesus calls himself the Son of Man.

A throne is a ruler’s seat.

The twelve tribes are the large family groups of Israel.

## Matthew 19:29

“Some have left homes or fields for my sake.

Some have left brothers, sisters, father, mother, wife, or children.

Everyone who has done this will receive a hundred times as much.

They will also receive eternal life.

## Matthew 19:30

“But many who are first will be last.

And many who are last will be first.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Peter and the others leave to follow Jesus?
