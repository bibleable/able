# Matthew 24 — Jesus tells his followers to keep watch

**About this chapter**

The followers ask Jesus what is coming.

Jesus tells them how to wait and stay ready.

## A question about the temple

## Matthew 24:1

Jesus left the temple and walked away.

His followers came up and pointed out the temple buildings.

## Matthew 24:2

Jesus asked, “Do you see all these buildings?

I tell you the truth.

Every stone will be thrown down.

No stone here will stay on top of another.”

## Matthew 24:3

Later Jesus sat on the Mount of Olives.

His followers came to speak with him privately.

They asked, “When will these things happen?

What sign will show that you are coming and this age is ending?”

**Pause and answer**

Where did the followers speak to Jesus privately?

## The beginning of trouble

## Matthew 24:4

Jesus answered, “Be careful that no one tricks you.

## Matthew 24:5

“Many people will come in my name.

Each will say, ‘I am the Christ.’

They will lead many people astray.

**Help with a name**

Christ means the King God promised to send.

## Matthew 24:6

“You will hear about wars.

You will also hear stories that wars may be coming.

Do not be afraid.

These things must happen, but the end is still to come.

## Matthew 24:7

“Nations will fight each other.

Kingdoms will fight each other.

In some places people will have too little food.

In other places the ground will shake.

## Matthew 24:8

“These things are only the beginning.

They are like the first pains before a baby is born.

**Help with this picture**

The first pains tell a mother that birth has begun.

Jesus says the troubles he describes are only the beginning.

**Pause and answer**

What will people falsely claim about themselves?

## Keep trusting through trouble

## Matthew 24:9

“Then people will hand you over to suffer and die.

People in every nation will hate you because you follow me.

## Matthew 24:10

“Many will turn away.

They will betray and hate one another.

## Matthew 24:11

“Many people will falsely claim to speak for God.

They will lead many others astray.

## Matthew 24:12

“More and more people will do evil.

Most people’s love will grow cold.

**Help with this picture**

Love growing cold means people stop caring for others.

## Matthew 24:13

“But God will save the person who stays faithful to the end.

## Matthew 24:14

“People will tell the good news of God’s kingdom throughout the world.

Every nation will hear the message.

Then the end will come.

**Pause and answer**

Who will hear the good news about God’s kingdom?

## Leave without delay

## Matthew 24:15

“The prophet Daniel spoke about something hateful that brings ruin.

You will see it standing in the holy place.”

Reader, pay attention to what this means.

**Help with these words**

The “holy place” means a place set apart for God.

Daniel warned of something there that deeply dishonours God.

## Matthew 24:16

Jesus continued, “Then the people in Judea must run to the mountains.

## Matthew 24:17

“Anyone on a rooftop must leave at once.

Do not go inside to get your things.

## Matthew 24:18

“Anyone working in a field must leave too.

Do not go back for your cloak.

## Matthew 24:19

“How hard those days will be for pregnant women and mothers nursing babies!

**Pause and answer**

Where does Jesus tell the people in Judea to go?

## A time of great suffering

## Matthew 24:20

“Pray that you will not have to flee in winter or on the Sabbath.

**Help with a word**

The Sabbath is the Jewish day of rest.

## Matthew 24:21

“People will suffer more than at any time since the world began.

Nothing like it will happen again.

## Matthew 24:22

“If those days went on, no one would survive.

But God will shorten them for the sake of the people he has chosen.

**Pause and answer**

For whose sake will God shorten those days?

## People who make false claims

## Matthew 24:23

“If anyone tells you, ‘The Christ is here!’ or ‘There he is!’ do not believe it.

## Matthew 24:24

“Some people will claim to be the Christ.

Others will falsely claim to speak for God.

They will do things that look like great signs and wonders.

They will try to fool even God’s chosen people, if that is possible.

## Matthew 24:25

“Remember, I have told you this before it happens.

## Matthew 24:26

“If someone says, ‘He is in the desert,’ do not go out there.

If someone says, ‘He is in a room inside,’ do not believe it.

## Matthew 24:27

“Lightning flashes from the east all the way to the west.

That is how it will be when the Son of Man comes.

**Help with this picture**

Jesus calls himself the Son of Man.

People across the sky’s whole span can see lightning flash.

Jesus says his coming will be plain to see.

## Matthew 24:28

“Where a dead body lies, vultures gather.

**Help with a word**

Vultures are large birds that feed on dead animals.

**Pause and answer**

What should the followers do if someone says the Christ is in the desert?

## The Son of Man comes

## Matthew 24:29

“Immediately after those days of suffering, the sun will grow dark.

The moon will give no light.

Stars will fall from the sky.

The powers of heaven will shake.

## Matthew 24:30

“Then the sign of the Son of Man will appear in the sky.

People of every nation on earth will mourn.

They will see the Son of Man coming on the clouds.

He will come with power and great glory.

## Matthew 24:31

“He will send his angels with a loud trumpet call.

They will gather the people he has chosen from every direction, from one end of the heavens to the other.

**Pause and answer**

Whom will the angels gather?

## New leaves on a fig tree

## Matthew 24:32

“Learn from the fig tree.

Its branches grow tender and put out new leaves.

Then you know summer is near.

**About the picture**

![New leaves opening at the tip of a twig.](../pictures/new-leaves.svg)

Find the new leaves at the tip.

The leaves help people recognise the change of season.

## Matthew 24:33

“In the same way, watch for all these things.

When you see them, you will know he is near.

He is right at the door.

**Help with this picture**

Someone at the door is very near.

Jesus uses this picture to help his followers understand the signs.

## Matthew 24:34

“I tell you the truth.

This generation will not pass away before all these things happen.

## Matthew 24:35

“Heaven and earth will pass away.

My words will last forever.

**Pause and answer**

What season is near when the new leaves open?

## The days of Noah

## Matthew 24:36

“No one knows that day or hour except the Father.

Even the angels in heaven and the Son do not know.

## Matthew 24:37

“When the Son of Man comes, it will be like the days of Noah.

## Matthew 24:38

“Before the flood, people ate and drank.

They married and gave their children in marriage.

They did these things until the day Noah entered the ark.

**Help with a word**

The ark was the large boat Noah entered before the flood.

## Matthew 24:39

“They knew nothing about what was coming.

Then the flood came and swept them all away.

That is how it will be when the Son of Man comes.

**Pause and answer**

Who knows the day and hour?

## Keep watch

## Matthew 24:40

“Two men will be working in a field.

One will be taken, and the other will stay.

## Matthew 24:41

“Two women will be grinding grain at a mill.

One will be taken, and the other will stay.

## Matthew 24:42

“So keep watch.

You do not know what day your Lord will come.

## Matthew 24:43

“Think of someone who owns a house.

Suppose he knew what time a thief would come at night.

He would stay awake and stop the thief from breaking in.

## Matthew 24:44

“You too must be ready.

The Son of Man will come when you do not expect him.

**Pause and answer**

Why does Jesus tell his followers to be ready?

## A servant who cares for others

## Matthew 24:45

“Think of a master who puts one servant in charge of his household.

The servant must give everyone food at the right time.

What would a faithful and wise servant do?

## Matthew 24:46

“When the master returns, he finds that servant giving out food.

That servant is blessed.

## Matthew 24:47

“I tell you the truth.

The master will put that servant in charge of everything he owns.

**Pause and answer**

What work does the master give the servant?

## A servant who hurts others

## Matthew 24:48

“But suppose the servant is wicked.

He thinks, ‘My master will be away a long time.’

## Matthew 24:49

“He starts beating the other servants.

He eats and drinks with people who get drunk.

## Matthew 24:50

“The master will come back on a day the servant does not expect.

The servant will not know the hour.

## Matthew 24:51

“The master will cut that servant to pieces.

He will give him a place among people who pretend to do right.

There people will cry and grind their teeth.”

**Help with these words**

Grinding teeth here shows deep distress.

**Pause and answer**

What does this servant expect his master to do?
