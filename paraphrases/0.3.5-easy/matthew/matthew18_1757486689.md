# Matthew 18 — Jesus teaches about care and forgiveness

**About this chapter**

Jesus tells his followers to care for the little ones.

Then Peter asks how often he should forgive.

## A child stands among the followers

## Matthew 18:1

The followers came to Jesus with a question.

“Who is greatest in the kingdom of heaven?”

## Matthew 18:2

Jesus called a little child over.

He had the child stand among them.

## Matthew 18:3

Jesus said, “I tell you the truth.

You must change and become like little children.

Otherwise you will never enter the kingdom of heaven.

## Matthew 18:4

“The greatest person in the kingdom of heaven is someone who is humble like this child.

**Help with a word**

Being humble includes letting go of the wish to be more important than others.

## Matthew 18:5

“Anyone who welcomes a child like this in my name welcomes me.

**Pause and answer**

Who did Jesus ask to stand among his followers?

## A serious warning about sin

## Matthew 18:6

“Think of someone who leads one of these little ones who trust me into sin.

That person would be better off drowning deep in the sea with a heavy millstone around their neck.

**Help with these words**

Sin means doing wrong against God.

A millstone is a heavy stone used to grind grain.

## Matthew 18:7

“What trouble comes to the world from things that lead people into sin!

These things are bound to come.

But what trouble awaits the person who brings them!”

## Matthew 18:8

Jesus spoke about a hand or foot that leads someone into sin.

He spoke of cutting it off and throwing it away.

He said it was better to enter life without it than to keep both hands and feet and go into the fire that never ends.

**Help with this picture**

Jesus uses a shocking picture to show how serious sin is.

He wants people to turn away from wrongdoing and keep their bodies safe.

## Matthew 18:9

Jesus used the same picture about an eye that leads someone into sin.

He spoke of throwing the eye away.

He said it was better to enter life with one eye than to keep both eyes and go into the fire of hell.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus warn people against doing to the little ones?

## One lost sheep

## Matthew 18:10

Jesus said, “Never look down on any of these little ones.

Their angels in heaven always see my Father’s face.

## Matthew 18:11

**About this verse number**

In the Berean Standard Bible, the reading continues at verse 12.

## Matthew 18:12

“What would a man do if one of his hundred sheep wandered away?

He would leave the ninety-nine on the hills.

He would search for the lost sheep.

## Matthew 18:13

“I tell you truly, if he finds it, he rejoices.

That one sheep brings him more joy than the ninety-nine that stayed.

## Matthew 18:14

“Your Father in heaven is like that.

He does not want even one of these little ones to perish.

**Help with this picture**

The man cares about the one sheep that went missing.

Jesus says his Father cares about each little one.

**Pause and answer**

Which sheep did the man go looking for?

## Speak to your brother

## Matthew 18:15

“If your brother does wrong to you, speak to him alone.

Tell him what he did.

If he listens, you have gained your brother back.

## Matthew 18:16

“If he will not listen, take one or two others along.

Scripture says two or three witnesses must confirm what happened.

## Matthew 18:17

“If he still refuses to listen, tell the church.

If he will not listen even to the church, treat him as you would a pagan or a tax collector.

**Help with these words**

Here the church means the people who follow Jesus.

A pagan worshiped other gods.

People often treated tax collectors as outsiders.

**Pause and answer**

Who should be there the first time you speak to your brother?

## Jesus promises to be with his followers

## Matthew 18:18

“I tell you the truth.

What you bind on earth will be bound in heaven.

What you set free on earth will be free in heaven.

**Help with this picture**

To bind is to tie up.

To loose is to set free.

Jesus uses these words about decisions his followers make together.

## Matthew 18:19

“Again I tell you the truth.

If two of you on earth agree about what to ask for, my Father in heaven will do it for you.

## Matthew 18:20

“Where two or three gather in my name, I am there with them.”

**Pause and answer**

Who promises to be with the people who gather?

## Peter asks how often to forgive

## Matthew 18:21

Peter came to Jesus and asked, “Lord, my brother may keep doing wrong to me.

How many times should I forgive him?

As many as seven times?”

## Matthew 18:22

Jesus answered, “I tell you, seventy-seven times!

## Matthew 18:23

“Here is what the kingdom of heaven is like.

A king wanted to collect what his servants owed him.

**Pause and answer**

How many times did Jesus tell Peter to forgive?

## The king releases the first servant

## Matthew 18:24

“As the king began, someone brought the first servant to him.

This servant owed him ten thousand talents.

**Help with these words**

A debt is what someone owes.

Ten thousand talents was an enormous amount of money.

## Matthew 18:25

“The first servant could not pay.

The king ordered people to sell the servant, his wife, and his children.

They must sell everything he owned to pay the debt.

## Matthew 18:26

“The first servant fell to his knees before the king.

He begged, ‘Give me time.

I will pay you back everything.’

## Matthew 18:27

“The king felt deep pity for the first servant.

He cancelled the whole debt and let him go.

**Pause and answer**

What happened to the debt the first servant owed the king?

## The first servant demands payment

## Matthew 18:28

“Outside, the first servant found a second servant.

The second servant owed him a hundred denarii.

The first servant grabbed him and began to choke him.

‘Pay me what you owe!’ he said.

**Help with these words**

Denarii were coins.

The second servant owed far less than the first.

**About the picture**

![Two coins.](../pictures/two-coins.svg)

Point to the coins.

The story is about money that two people owed.

## Matthew 18:29

“The second servant fell down and begged, ‘Give me time.

I will pay you back.’

## Matthew 18:30

“But the first servant refused.

He had the second servant put in prison until he could pay.

## Matthew 18:31

“The other servants saw what happened.

It upset them deeply.

They went and told the king the whole story.

**Pause and answer**

What did the second servant ask for?

## The king calls the first servant back

## Matthew 18:32

“The king sent for the first servant.

‘You wicked servant!’ the king said.

‘I cancelled your whole debt because you begged me.

## Matthew 18:33

“‘I showed you mercy.

Shouldn’t you have shown the other servant mercy too?’

## Matthew 18:34

“The king was angry.

He handed the first servant over to the jailers.

They were to torture him until he paid everything he owed.

## Matthew 18:35

“That is how my Father in heaven will treat each of you unless you forgive your brother from your heart.”

**Help with these words**

Forgiving from your heart means you mean the forgiveness you offer.

**Pause and answer**

What did the king expect the first servant to do for the second?
