# Matthew 12 — Jesus answers questions about his work

**About this chapter**

Jesus heals a man on the day of rest.

People disagree about where Jesus gets his power.

## Hungry followers pick grain

## Matthew 12:1

On the Sabbath, Jesus walked through fields of grain.

His followers were hungry.

They picked heads of grain and ate them.

**Help with a word**

The Sabbath is the Jewish day of rest.

**About the picture**

![A stalk with grain at the top.](../pictures/grain.svg)

Find the head at the top of the stalk.

This is the part the followers picked.

## Matthew 12:2

Some Pharisees saw this.

They told Jesus, “Look at what your followers are doing!

Our law forbids that on the Sabbath.”

**Help with a name**

The Pharisees were a Jewish religious group.

## Matthew 12:3

Jesus answered, “Have you read what David did?

David and the people with him were hungry.

## Matthew 12:4

“David went into God’s house.

He and his companions ate bread set apart for God.

The law allowed only priests to eat that bread.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the followers pick grain?

## Jesus speaks about mercy

## Matthew 12:5

“Have you read what the law says about the priests?

They work in the temple even on the Sabbath.

Yet they are innocent.

## Matthew 12:6

“I tell you, someone greater than the temple is here.

## Matthew 12:7

“God said, ‘I want mercy more than gifts offered in worship.’

If you understood this, you would not blame innocent people.

## Matthew 12:8

“The Son of Man is Lord of the Sabbath.”

**Help with a name**

Jesus calls himself the Son of Man.

Here he says he has authority over the Sabbath.

**Pause and answer**

What does God want more than gifts offered in worship?

## A man stretches out his hand

## Matthew 12:9

Jesus left the fields and entered their synagogue.

**Help with a word**

A synagogue is a place where Jewish people meet for worship.

## Matthew 12:10

A man there could hardly use one hand.

Some people wanted to accuse Jesus of breaking the law.

They asked him, “Does the law allow healing on the Sabbath?”

## Matthew 12:11

Jesus answered, “Suppose your sheep falls into a deep hole on the Sabbath.

Wouldn’t you reach down and lift it out?

## Matthew 12:12

“A person is worth much more than a sheep!

So the law allows us to do good on the Sabbath.”

## Matthew 12:13

Then Jesus told the man, “Stretch out your hand.”

The man stretched it out.

Now he could use it just like his other hand.

## Matthew 12:14

The Pharisees went outside.

They made plans to kill Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

What could the man do after Jesus healed him?

## God’s chosen servant

## Matthew 12:15

Jesus knew about their plans, so he left.

Large crowds followed Jesus, and he healed them all.

## Matthew 12:16

Jesus warned them, “Do not tell others about me.”

## Matthew 12:17

In this way the words of Isaiah the prophet came true.

## Matthew 12:18

God had said, “Here is the servant I chose.

I love him and take great joy in him.

I will put my Spirit on him.

He will tell all nations about justice.

**Help with a word**

Justice means treating people fairly and putting wrongs right.

## Matthew 12:19

“My servant will not argue or shout.

No one will hear him shouting in the streets.

## Matthew 12:20

“He will not break a bent and damaged reed.

He will not put out a lamp wick that barely burns.

He will keep going until justice wins.

**Help with these pictures**

A reed is a plant with a long stem.

A wick is the part of a lamp that burns.

Both pictures show how gently God’s servant treats the weak.

## Matthew 12:21

“People from every nation will put their hope in him.”

**Pause and answer**

What will the servant do with a damaged reed?

## People disagree about Jesus

## Matthew 12:22

Then people brought a man to Jesus.

An evil spirit troubled him.

The man could neither see nor speak.

Jesus healed him, and he could speak and see.

## Matthew 12:23

The crowds were amazed.

They asked, “Could Jesus be the Son of David?”

**Help with a name**

Son of David is a name for the promised King.

## Matthew 12:24

The Pharisees heard them.

They said, “Beelzebul rules the evil spirits.

Only Beelzebul gives this man power to drive them out.”

**Pause and answer**

What changed for the man Jesus healed?

## A kingdom fighting itself

## Matthew 12:25

Jesus knew their thoughts.

He said, “A kingdom that fights itself falls apart.

The same is true of a city or a family.

## Matthew 12:26

“Suppose Satan drives out Satan.

He would be fighting himself.

How could his kingdom last?

## Matthew 12:27

“You say Beelzebul helps me drive out evil spirits.

Then who helps your own people drive them out?

Your own people will judge you.

## Matthew 12:28

“But suppose God’s Spirit helps me drive them out.

Then God’s kingdom has already come to you.

**Help with these words**

God’s kingdom means God ruling as King.

**Pause and answer**

What happens to a family that keeps fighting itself?

## Jesus warns the Pharisees

## Matthew 12:29

“Think of someone who enters a strong man’s house.

How can he take anything unless he first ties up the strong man?

Only then can he take what is in the house.

**Help with this picture**

Jesus describes someone overcoming a strong man.

Jesus has been driving out evil spirits.

## Matthew 12:30

“Whoever is not with me is against me.

Whoever does not gather with me scatters.

**Help with these words**

To scatter means to spread things apart.

## Matthew 12:31

“So I tell you this about sin and words that insult God.

God will forgive people for all these things.

But God will not forgive insults against the Holy Spirit.

## Matthew 12:32

“God will forgive someone who speaks against the Son of Man.

But he will not forgive someone who speaks against the Holy Spirit.

That holds true now and in the age to come.

**Help with these words**

Speaking against God in this way is called blasphemy.

Jesus gave this warning after the Pharisees called his work the work of an evil spirit.

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus say about speaking against the Holy Spirit?

## Words show what fills the heart

## Matthew 12:33

“Make a tree good, and its fruit will be good.

Make it bad, and its fruit will be bad.

You know a tree by its fruit.

## Matthew 12:34

“You family of poisonous snakes!

How can you speak good words when you do evil?

Your mouth speaks what fills your heart.

**Help with this picture**

Your heart here means the person you are inside.

Jesus says that what is inside comes out in words.

## Matthew 12:35

“Good people store up good things inside.

They bring good things out of that store.

People who store up evil bring out evil.

## Matthew 12:36

“On judgment day, people must answer to God for every careless word.

## Matthew 12:37

“Your own words will show whether you are innocent or guilty.”

**Pause and answer**

What does fruit tell you about a tree?

## The sign of Jonah

## Matthew 12:38

Then some teachers of the law and some Pharisees spoke.

They said, “Teacher, we want you to show us a sign.”

**Help with a word**

Here a sign means an act showing that God sent Jesus.

## Matthew 12:39

Jesus answered, “People today do evil and are unfaithful to God.

They demand a sign.

They will receive only the sign of Jonah the prophet.

## Matthew 12:40

“Jonah spent three days and three nights inside a great fish.

The Son of Man will spend three days and three nights deep in the earth.

**Help with this picture**

Jesus points ahead to his burial.

## Matthew 12:41

“On judgment day the people of Nineveh will stand up.

They will speak against the people of this time.

The people of Nineveh turned from wrongdoing when Jonah spoke.

Now someone greater than Jonah is here.

## Matthew 12:42

“The Queen of the South will also stand on judgment day.

She will speak against the people of this time.

She came from far away to hear King Solomon’s wise words.

Now someone greater than Solomon is here.

**Pause and answer**

How did the people of Nineveh respond to Jonah?

## An evil spirit returns

## Matthew 12:43

“When an evil spirit leaves someone, it crosses dry places.

It looks for a place to rest but finds none.

## Matthew 12:44

“Then the spirit says, ‘I will return to the house I left.’

It finds the house empty, swept clean, and in order.

**Help with this picture**

In Jesus’ account the house stands for the person the spirit left.

## Matthew 12:45

“The spirit brings seven other spirits more evil than itself.

They all go in and live there.

The person is worse off than before.

That is how it will be for the people of this evil generation.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the spirit find when it came back?

## Jesus points to his followers

## Matthew 12:46

Jesus was still speaking to the crowds.

His mother and brothers stood outside.

They wanted to speak to him.

## Matthew 12:47

Someone told Jesus, “Your mother and brothers are standing outside.

They want to speak to you.”

## Matthew 12:48

Jesus answered, “Who is my mother?

Who are my brothers?”

## Matthew 12:49

Jesus pointed to his followers.

He said, “Here are my mother and my brothers.

## Matthew 12:50

“Whoever does what my Father in heaven wants is my brother, my sister, and my mother.”

**Pause and answer**

Who belongs to the family Jesus describes?
