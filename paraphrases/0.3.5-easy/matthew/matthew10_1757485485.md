# Matthew 10 — Jesus sends out the twelve

**About this chapter**

Jesus gives twelve followers work to do.

He tells them what to expect on the way.

## Jesus chooses twelve messengers

## Matthew 10:1

Jesus called his twelve followers to him.

He gave them power to drive out evil spirits.

He gave them power to heal every kind of sickness.

## Matthew 10:2

These are the names of the twelve apostles.

First came Simon, also called Peter, and his brother Andrew.

Then came James and John, the sons of Zebedee.

**Help with a word**

An apostle is someone Jesus sends to speak for him.

## Matthew 10:3

The others included Philip and Bartholomew, Thomas and Matthew.

Matthew had collected taxes.

There were also James, the son of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus.

## Matthew 10:4

Then came Simon the Zealot and Judas Iscariot.

Judas later handed Jesus over to his enemies.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus give the twelve power to do?

## What to say and do

## Matthew 10:5

Jesus sent these twelve out with instructions.

“Stay off the roads that lead to the Gentiles.

Do not enter any Samaritan town.

## Matthew 10:6

“Go to the lost sheep of Israel.

**Help with this picture**

Sheep can lose their way and need a shepherd.

Jesus means people in Israel who need his care and guidance.

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish.

## Matthew 10:7

“As you go, tell people this good news.

‘The kingdom of heaven is near.’

**Help with these words**

The kingdom of heaven means God ruling as King.

## Matthew 10:8

“Heal sick people and bring dead people back to life.

Make people with leprosy clean.

Drive out evil spirits.

You received these gifts freely.

Give freely too.

**Pause and answer**

What message did Jesus give the twelve?

## Where to stay

## Matthew 10:9

“Take no gold, silver, or copper money in your belts.

## Matthew 10:10

“Take no travel bag, extra shirt, sandals, or walking stick.

People who work deserve the food they need.

## Matthew 10:11

“When you enter a town or village, ask around.

Find someone worthy to welcome you.

Stay in that home until you leave town.

## Matthew 10:12

“When you enter the house, greet the people there.

## Matthew 10:13

“If the home is worthy, let your peace rest there.

If it is unworthy, let your peace return to you.

**Pause and answer**

How long should the twelve stay in one home?

## When a town refuses to listen

## Matthew 10:14

“Some people may refuse to welcome you or listen.

When you leave that house or town, shake the dust off your feet.

**Help with this action**

Leaving the dust behind showed that the messengers were moving on.

## Matthew 10:15

“I tell you truly, God will judge that town.

On that day even Sodom and Gomorrah will fare better.

**Help with these names**

Sodom and Gomorrah were cities God judged long ago.

**Pause and answer**

What should the twelve shake off their feet?

## When people bring you before rulers

## Matthew 10:16

“Look, I am sending you like sheep among wolves.

Be as alert as snakes and as innocent as doves.

**Help with these pictures**

Wolves can hurt sheep.

Jesus tells his followers to watch for danger and do no harm.

## Matthew 10:17

“Watch out for people who will hand you over to councils.

People will whip you in their places of worship.

## Matthew 10:18

“Because you follow me, people will take you before governors and kings.

You will speak about me to them and to the Gentiles.

## Matthew 10:19

“When they hand you over, do not worry.

You need not plan how to answer.

At that time you will receive the words to say.

## Matthew 10:20

“Your Father’s Spirit will speak through you.

**Pause and answer**

Who will help the twelve speak?

## When people turn against you

## Matthew 10:21

“A brother will hand over his brother to be killed.

A father will do the same to his child.

Children will turn against their parents and have them killed.

## Matthew 10:22

“Everyone will hate you because you belong to me.

But God will save whoever stays faithful to the end.

## Matthew 10:23

“When people mistreat you in one town, flee to the next.

I tell you truly, the Son of Man will come before you reach every town in Israel.

**Help with a name**

Jesus calls himself the Son of Man.

**Pause and answer**

Where should the twelve go if people mistreat them?

## A student and a teacher

## Matthew 10:24

“A student is not above the teacher.

A servant is not above the master.

## Matthew 10:25

“It is enough for students to be like their teacher.

It is enough for servants to be like their master.

People have called the head of the house Beelzebul.

Imagine what they will call the rest of his household!

**Help with this name**

Beelzebul is a name for the ruler of evil spirits.

People used that name to insult Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

Whom does a student learn to be like?

## Your Father knows you

## Matthew 10:26

“So do not fear those people.

Everything hidden will come into the open.

Every secret will become known.

## Matthew 10:27

“Tell people in daylight what I tell you in the dark.

What you hear in a whisper, announce from the rooftops.

## Matthew 10:28

“Do not fear people who can kill only your body.

They cannot kill your soul.

Fear the One who can destroy body and soul in hell.

## Matthew 10:29

“People sell two sparrows for one small coin.

Yet not one falls without your Father allowing it.

**Help with a word**

Sparrows are small birds.

## Matthew 10:30

“Your Father has even counted every hair on your head.

## Matthew 10:31

“So do not be afraid.

You matter more than many sparrows.

**Pause and answer**

What has your Father counted?

## Following Jesus can divide a family

## Matthew 10:32

“Tell others openly that you belong to me.

I too will say before my Father in heaven that you belong to me.

## Matthew 10:33

“But suppose you tell others you do not know me.

I too will deny knowing you before my Father in heaven.

## Matthew 10:34

“Do you think I came to bring peace on earth?

I came to bring a sword.

**Help with this picture**

A sword can divide things.

Jesus explains how people in one family can turn against each other over him.

## Matthew 10:35

“My coming will set a son against his father.

A daughter will turn against her mother.

A wife will turn against her husband’s mother.

## Matthew 10:36

“A person’s enemies will come from that person’s own family.

**Pause and answer**

Where does Jesus say disagreements can happen?

## What it costs to follow Jesus

## Matthew 10:37

“Anyone who loves father or mother more than me is unworthy of me.

Anyone who loves son or daughter more than me is unworthy of me.

## Matthew 10:38

“Anyone who refuses to take up their cross and follow me is unworthy of me.

**Help with this picture**

The Romans made prisoners carry a cross to their deaths.

Jesus speaks about staying faithful even when it costs your life.

## Matthew 10:39

“Whoever finds their life will lose it.

Whoever loses their life for my sake will find it.

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus ask a follower to carry?

## Welcome the people Jesus sends

## Matthew 10:40

“Whoever welcomes you welcomes me.

Whoever welcomes me welcomes the One who sent me.

## Matthew 10:41

“Welcome a prophet because that person is a prophet.

You will share a prophet’s reward.

Welcome someone because they do what is right.

You will share that person’s reward.

## Matthew 10:42

“Someone may give even a cup of cold water to one of these little ones because they follow me.

I tell you truly, the giver will keep their reward.”
