# Matthew 23 — Jesus warns leaders and grieves for Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

Jesus warns the crowds about leaders who want praise.

He tells his followers to serve others.

## What the leaders teach and do

## Matthew 23:1

Jesus spoke to the crowds and to his followers.

## Matthew 23:2

He said, “The teachers of the law and the Pharisees sit in Moses’ seat.

**Help with this picture**

Moses gave the people God’s law.

Sitting in his seat means having the job of teaching that law.

## Matthew 23:3

“So follow the teaching they give you.

But do not copy their actions.

They say one thing and do another.

## Matthew 23:4

“They tie up heavy loads for other people to carry.

They will not lift even one finger to help.

**Help with this picture**

The heavy loads are rules that are hard to follow.

**Pause and answer**

What do the leaders refuse to help people carry?

## Wanting people to notice

## Matthew 23:5

“They do everything to get people to notice them.

They make their prayer boxes wider.

They make the tassels on their clothes longer.

**Help with these words**

The small boxes held words from Scripture.

The tassels were threads hanging from the edges of clothing.

## Matthew 23:6

“At feasts they love the places of honour.

In the synagogues they want the best seats.

## Matthew 23:7

“They love it when people greet them in the marketplace.

They like people to call them ‘Rabbi.’

**Help with a word**

Rabbi means teacher.

**Pause and answer**

Which seats do the leaders want?

## The greatest person serves

## Matthew 23:8

“But do not ask people to call you ‘Rabbi.’

You have one Teacher.

You all belong to one family.

## Matthew 23:9

“Do not call anyone on earth your father.

You have one Father, and he is in heaven.

## Matthew 23:10

“Do not let people call you their instructor.

You have one Instructor, the Christ.

## Matthew 23:11

“The greatest among you must serve the others.

## Matthew 23:12

“People who lift themselves up will be brought low.

People who make themselves humble will be lifted up.

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus ask the greatest person to do?

## A door closed to others

## Matthew 23:13

“Trouble is coming for you, teachers of the law and Pharisees!

You pretend to do right.

You shut the door of the kingdom of heaven in people’s faces.

You do not enter.

You block people who want to go in.

**Help with this picture**

Jesus describes leaders keeping people from God’s rule as if they were shutting a door.

## Matthew 23:14

**About this verse number**

The BSB source has no verse text at this number.

## Matthew 23:15

“Trouble is coming for you, teachers of the law and Pharisees!

You pretend to do right.

You cross land and sea to gain one follower.

Then you lead that person twice as far down the road to hell as you have gone.

**Pause and answer**

What do the leaders do when people want to enter?

## Promises about the temple and altar

## Matthew 23:16

“Trouble is coming for you guides who cannot see the way!

You say, ‘A promise made by the temple counts for nothing.

But a promise made by the temple’s gold must be kept.’

**Help with these words**

A person could name something holy when making a serious promise.

## Matthew 23:17

“How foolish and blind you are!

Which matters more, the gold or the temple that makes the gold holy?

## Matthew 23:18

“You also say, ‘A promise made by the altar counts for nothing.

But a promise made by the gift on the altar must be kept.’

**Help with a word**

An altar was a place to bring gifts to God.

## Matthew 23:19

“You cannot see!

Which matters more, the gift or the altar that makes the gift holy?

**Pause and answer**

What makes the gift on the altar holy?

## Remember who lives in the temple

## Matthew 23:20

“Anyone who makes a promise by the altar includes everything on it.

## Matthew 23:21

“Anyone who makes a promise by the temple includes God, who lives there.

## Matthew 23:22

“Anyone who makes a promise by heaven includes God’s throne and God himself, who sits on it.

**Pause and answer**

Who sits on the throne in heaven?

## What matters most in the law

## Matthew 23:23

“Trouble is coming for you, teachers of the law and Pharisees!

You pretend to do right.

You give God a tenth of your mint, dill, and cumin.

Yet you leave out the most important parts of the law.

Treat people fairly, show mercy, and stay faithful.

Do these things and keep giving the tenth as well.

## Matthew 23:24

“You guides cannot see where you are going!

You strain a tiny fly from your drink.

Yet you swallow a camel!

**Help with this picture**

A gnat is a tiny fly.

A camel is a large animal.

Jesus says the leaders notice tiny details while missing something far bigger.

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus say matters more than measuring herbs?

## The inside of the cup

## Matthew 23:25

“Trouble is coming for you, teachers of the law and Pharisees!

You pretend to do right.

You wash the outside of the cup and dish.

Inside they are full of greed and the desire to please yourselves.

## Matthew 23:26

“Blind Pharisee!

Clean the inside of the cup and dish first.

Then the outside can be clean too.

**About the picture**

![A drinking cup.](../pictures/cup.svg)

Trace the outside edge of the cup.

Jesus uses the cup to speak about people.

God sees what they want inside as well as what they do.

## Matthew 23:27

“Trouble is coming for you, teachers of the law and Pharisees!

You pretend to do right.

You are like tombs painted white.

Outside they look beautiful.

Inside are dead people’s bones and all kinds of uncleanness.

## Matthew 23:28

“You too look good to other people.

Inside you are full of lies about who you are.

You are full of wrongdoing.

**Pause and answer**

Which part of the cup does Jesus say to clean first?

## The leaders speak about their ancestors

## Matthew 23:29

“Trouble is coming for you, teachers of the law and Pharisees!

You pretend to do right.

You build tombs for the prophets.

You decorate the monuments of people who did right.

## Matthew 23:30

“You say, ‘Our ancestors killed the prophets.

If we had lived then, we would never have helped them do it.’

## Matthew 23:31

“Your own words show whose children you are.

Your ancestors murdered the prophets.

## Matthew 23:32

“Go on, then, and finish the wrong your ancestors began!

**Help with these words**

Jesus warns that the leaders are continuing their ancestors’ wrongdoing.

**Pause and answer**

What did the leaders say their ancestors had done?

## Jesus will send messengers

## Matthew 23:33

“You snakes!

You family of poisonous snakes!

How will you escape the judgment of hell?

## Matthew 23:34

“So I am sending you prophets, wise people, and teachers.

You will kill some and put some to death on crosses.

You will whip others in your synagogues.

You will chase them from town to town.

## Matthew 23:35

“You will answer for the deaths of innocent people throughout the earth.

Think of Abel, who did right.

Think of Zechariah, son of Berechiah.

You murdered him between the temple and the altar.

## Matthew 23:36

“I tell you the truth.

All this will come upon the people of this generation.

**Pause and answer**

Whom does Jesus say he will send?

## Jesus speaks to Jerusalem

## Matthew 23:37

“Jerusalem, Jerusalem!

You kill the prophets.

You throw stones at the messengers God sends you.

So often I wanted to gather your children close.

A mother hen gathers her chicks under her wings like that.

But you would not come.

**Help with this picture**

A hen spreads her wings to shelter her chicks.

Jesus wanted to care for the people of Jerusalem like that.

## Matthew 23:38

“Look!

Your house is left empty.

## Matthew 23:39

“You will not see me again until you say these words.

‘God bless the one who comes in the Lord’s name!’”

**Pause and answer**

How does the hen care for her chicks?
