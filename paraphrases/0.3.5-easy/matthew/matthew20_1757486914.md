# Matthew 20 — Jesus tells a story about workers

**About this chapter**

A vineyard owner hires workers at different times.

Later, Jesus shows his followers what it means to serve.

## The owner hires workers

## Matthew 20:1

Jesus said, “Here is what the kingdom of heaven is like.

Early one morning, a landowner went to hire workers.

He needed people to work in his vineyard.

**Help with a word**

A vineyard is land where people grow grapes.

## Matthew 20:2

“The owner and the workers agreed on the pay.

Each worker would get one denarius for the day.

The owner sent them into his vineyard.

**Help with a word**

A denarius was a silver coin.

## Matthew 20:3

“Later that morning, the owner went to the marketplace.

He saw other people standing there without work.

## Matthew 20:4

“He told them, ‘Go and work in my vineyard too.

I will pay you what is fair.’

**Pause and answer**

What pay did the first workers agree to?

## More people are still waiting

## Matthew 20:5

“So they went.

At noon the owner went out and hired more workers.

In the middle of the afternoon, he did the same.

## Matthew 20:6

“Late that afternoon, he found others still waiting.

He asked, ‘Why have you stood here all day without work?’

## Matthew 20:7

“They answered, ‘No one has hired us.’

The owner said, ‘Go and work in my vineyard too.’

**Pause and answer**

Why were some people still waiting late in the day?

## The last workers get paid first

## Matthew 20:8

“That evening the owner spoke to the man in charge of the workers.

‘Call the workers and pay them,’ he said.

‘Start with the people I hired last.

Finish with the people I hired first.’

## Matthew 20:9

“The people hired late in the afternoon came forward.

Each received one denarius.

## Matthew 20:10

“Then the people hired early that morning came forward.

They expected more money.

But each received one denarius too.

**Pause and answer**

How much did each worker receive?

## The owner answers a complaint

## Matthew 20:11

“When they saw their pay, they complained to the owner.

## Matthew 20:12

“They said, ‘The last workers worked only one hour.

We worked hard through the heat of the day.

Yet you paid them as much as us.’

## Matthew 20:13

“The owner answered one worker, ‘Friend, I have treated you fairly.

Didn’t you agree to work for one denarius?

## Matthew 20:14

“‘Take your pay and go.

I want to give this last worker as much as you.

## Matthew 20:15

“‘May I do what I choose with my own money?

Are you jealous because I give freely?’

## Matthew 20:16

“So the last will be first.

And the first will be last.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did the first workers complain?

## Jesus tells the twelve what lies ahead

## Matthew 20:17

On the way up to Jerusalem, Jesus took his twelve followers aside.

He spoke to them alone.

## Matthew 20:18

“Look, we are going up to Jerusalem.

Someone will hand the Son of Man over to the chief priests and the teachers of the law.

They will sentence him to death.

**Help with a name**

Jesus calls himself the Son of Man.

## Matthew 20:19

“They will hand him over to the Gentiles.

Those men will mock him and whip him.

They will kill him on a cross.

On the third day, God will raise him to life.”

**Help with a word**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus say would happen on the third day?

## A mother asks for two places

## Matthew 20:20

Then the mother of Zebedee’s sons came to Jesus.

Her two sons came with her.

She knelt down to ask Jesus for something.

**Help with these names**

Zebedee’s sons were James and John.

## Matthew 20:21

Jesus asked, “What do you want?”

She answered, “When you rule as King, give my sons places beside you.

Let one sit at your right and one at your left.”

**Help with these places**

Seats beside a king were places of honour.

## Matthew 20:22

Jesus replied, “You do not know what you are asking.

Can you drink the cup I am going to drink?”

The brothers answered, “We can.”

**About the picture**

![A drinking cup.](../pictures/cup.svg)

Find the opening at the top of the cup.

Here drinking the cup means going through suffering, as Jesus will.

## Matthew 20:23

Jesus said, “You will indeed drink my cup.

But I cannot choose who sits at my right or left.

My Father has prepared those places for the people who will sit there.”

**Pause and answer**

Where did the mother want her sons to sit?

## Jesus teaches the twelve to serve

## Matthew 20:24

The other ten followers heard about this.

They were angry with the two brothers.

## Matthew 20:25

Jesus called them together.

“You know how the rulers of other nations treat people,” he said.

“Their leaders use their power to order people around.

## Matthew 20:26

“You must act differently.

Whoever wants to be great among you must serve the others.

## Matthew 20:27

“Whoever wants to be first must be your slave.

## Matthew 20:28

“The Son of Man came to serve others.

He did not come to have people serve him.

He came to give his life as a ransom for many.”

**Help with a word**

A ransom is a price paid to set someone free.

Jesus says his own life is that price.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus come to give for many people?

## Two men call to Jesus

## Matthew 20:29

Jesus and his followers were leaving Jericho.

A large crowd followed Jesus.

## Matthew 20:30

Two blind men sat beside the road.

They heard that Jesus was passing by.

They called out, “Lord, Son of David, have mercy on us!”

**Help with a name**

Son of David is a name for the promised King.

## Matthew 20:31

The crowd told the men to be quiet.

The two men called even louder.

“Lord, Son of David, have mercy on us!”

## Matthew 20:32

Jesus stopped and called to them.

He asked, “What do you want me to do for you?”

## Matthew 20:33

They answered, “Lord, let us see.”

## Matthew 20:34

Jesus felt deep care for them.

He touched their eyes.

At once the two men could see.

They followed Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

What did the two men ask Jesus to do?
