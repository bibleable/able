# Matthew 16 — Peter says who Jesus is

**About this chapter**

Jesus warns his followers about a teaching.

Then he asks who they believe he is.

## A sign from heaven

## Matthew 16:1

Some Pharisees and Sadducees came to test Jesus.

They asked him to show them a sign from heaven.

**Help with these words**

The Pharisees and Sadducees were two Jewish religious groups.

They wanted Jesus to do something that showed God had sent him.

## Matthew 16:2

Jesus answered, “In the evening you see a red sky.

You say, ‘The weather will be fine.’

## Matthew 16:3

“In the morning you see a red sky with dark clouds.

You say, ‘A storm is coming today.’

You understand what the sky tells you.

Yet you miss the signs of what is happening now.

## Matthew 16:4

“People today do wrong and are unfaithful to God.

They demand a sign.

The only sign they will receive is the sign of Jonah.”

Then Jesus left them.

**Help with this picture**

Jesus had spoken about Jonah’s three days inside a fish.

Jesus pointed ahead to his own burial.

**Pause and answer**

What did the leaders ask Jesus to show them?

## A warning about yeast

## Matthew 16:5

His followers crossed to the other shore.

They forgot to bring bread.

## Matthew 16:6

Jesus told them, “Watch out for the yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees.”

## Matthew 16:7

His followers talked among themselves.

They said, “He says this because we brought no bread.”

## Matthew 16:8

Jesus knew what they were saying.

“You trust me so little,” he said.

“Why are you talking about having no bread?

**Pause and answer**

What had the followers forgotten to bring?

## Jesus reminds them of two meals

## Matthew 16:9

“Do you still not understand?

Remember the five loaves for five thousand people.

How many baskets of food did you gather?

**About the picture**

![Five loaves of bread above two fish.](../pictures/loaves-and-fish.svg)

Count the loaves from the first meal Jesus mentions.

## Matthew 16:10

“Remember the seven loaves for four thousand people.

How many baskets did you gather then?

## Matthew 16:11

“Why do you think I am talking about bread?

Watch out for the yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees.”

## Matthew 16:12

Then his followers understood.

The warning was about what those groups taught.

Jesus had used yeast in bread as a picture.

**Help with this picture**

A little yeast spreads through dough.

Teaching can spread among people too.

**Pause and answer**

What did the yeast stand for?

## A question for Peter

## Matthew 16:13

Jesus came to the area around Caesarea Philippi.

He asked his followers, “Who do people say the Son of Man is?”

**Help with a name**

Jesus calls himself the Son of Man.

## Matthew 16:14

They answered, “Some say John the Baptist.

Others say Elijah.

Still others say Jeremiah or another prophet.”

## Matthew 16:15

Jesus asked, “And who do you say I am?”

## Matthew 16:16

Simon Peter answered, “You are the Christ.

You are the Son of the living God.”

**Help with a word**

Christ means the King God promised to send.

**Pause and answer**

Who did Peter say Jesus was?

## Jesus gives Peter a promise

## Matthew 16:17

Jesus replied, “God has blessed you, Simon, son of Jonah.

No human being showed you this.

My Father in heaven showed you.

## Matthew 16:18

“You are Peter.

On this rock I will build my church.

Even the gates of the place of the dead cannot overcome it.

**Help with this picture**

Peter’s name means “rock.”

Builders can set a building on rock to hold it steady.

Here the church means the people who belong to Jesus.

## Matthew 16:19

“I will give you the keys of the kingdom of heaven.

What you bind on earth will be bound in heaven.

What you set free on earth will be free in heaven.”

**Help with these pictures**

Keys let someone open or close a door.

Binding means tying something up.

Loosing means setting it free.

Jesus uses these pictures when he gives Peter authority.

## Matthew 16:20

Then Jesus gave his followers a firm warning.

They must tell no one that he was the Christ.

**Pause and answer**

Who showed Peter the answer?

## Jesus speaks about Jerusalem

## Matthew 16:21

From then on, Jesus began telling his followers what must happen.

He must go to Jerusalem.

The elders, chief priests, and law teachers would make him suffer greatly.

He would be killed.

On the third day he would come back to life.

## Matthew 16:22

Peter took Jesus aside and told him he was wrong.

“Lord, may this never happen!” Peter said.

“This will never happen to you!”

## Matthew 16:23

Jesus turned to Peter.

“Get behind me, Satan!” Jesus said.

“You are putting something in my way that could trip me.

You think about what people want.

You have missed what God wants.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus say would happen on the third day?

## What following Jesus costs

## Matthew 16:24

Then Jesus told his followers, “Do you want to follow me?

You must set aside your own wishes.

Take up your cross and follow me.

**Help with this picture**

The Romans made prisoners carry a cross to their deaths.

Jesus asks people to follow him even when it costs them their lives.

## Matthew 16:25

“Whoever tries to save their life will lose it.

Whoever loses their life for my sake will find it.

## Matthew 16:26

“What good is gaining the whole world if you lose your soul?

What could anyone give to buy their soul back?

## Matthew 16:27

“The Son of Man will come with his angels.

He will come in the glory of his Father.

Then he will repay each person for what they have done.

## Matthew 16:28

“I tell you truly, some people standing here will still be alive when they see the Son of Man coming as King.”

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus say a follower must take up?
