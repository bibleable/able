# Matthew 26 — Jesus shares a meal and faces arrest

**About this chapter**

Jesus eats the Passover meal with his followers.

Later, Judas leads a crowd to Jesus.

## Passover is near

## Matthew 26:1

Jesus finished teaching and spoke to his followers.

## Matthew 26:2

“You know the Passover is two days away.

Someone will hand the Son of Man over to die on a cross.”

**Help with these words**

At Passover, Jewish people remember how God freed their people from slavery in Egypt.

Jesus calls himself the Son of Man.

## Matthew 26:3

Meanwhile, the chief priests and elders met in a courtyard.

It belonged to Caiaphas, the high priest.

## Matthew 26:4

They planned to arrest Jesus secretly and kill him.

## Matthew 26:5

They said, “We must wait until after the feast.

The crowd might riot.”

**Pause and answer**

Where did the leaders meet?

## A woman brings perfume

## Matthew 26:6

Jesus was in Bethany at the house of Simon.

People called Simon “the Leper.”

## Matthew 26:7

As Jesus rested at the table, a woman came to him.

She carried a stone jar full of costly perfume.

She poured the perfume on his head.

**About the picture**

![A perfume jar.](../pictures/perfume-jar.svg)

Find the opening at the top of the jar.

The woman poured the perfume on Jesus’ head.

## Matthew 26:8

His followers saw this and became angry.

They asked, “Why waste the perfume?

## Matthew 26:9

“Someone could have sold it for a lot of money.

The money could have gone to poor people.”

**Pause and answer**

Why were the followers upset about the perfume?

## Jesus speaks up for the woman

## Matthew 26:10

Jesus knew what they were saying.

He asked, “Why are you troubling this woman?

She has done something beautiful for me.

## Matthew 26:11

“You will always have poor people among you.

You will not always have me here.

## Matthew 26:12

“She poured perfume on me to prepare my body for burial.

## Matthew 26:13

“I tell you the truth.

People will tell the good news throughout the world.

Wherever they tell it, they will remember what she did.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus say the perfume prepared his body for?

## Judas goes to the chief priests

## Matthew 26:14

Judas Iscariot was one of the twelve followers.

He went to the chief priests.

## Matthew 26:15

Judas asked, “What will you give me if I hand Jesus over to you?”

They set out thirty pieces of silver for him.

## Matthew 26:16

From then on, Judas looked for a chance to betray Jesus.

**Help with a word**

To betray Jesus meant to hand him over to his enemies.

**Pause and answer**

What did the chief priests offer Judas?

## The followers prepare the meal

## Matthew 26:17

The feast of bread made without yeast began.

The followers asked Jesus, “Where shall we prepare your Passover meal?”

## Matthew 26:18

Jesus sent them to a certain man in the city.

“Tell him the Teacher says, ‘My time is near.

I will eat the Passover meal with my followers at your house.’”

## Matthew 26:19

The followers did what Jesus said.

They prepared the Passover meal.

**Pause and answer**

Where were the followers to prepare the meal?

## One of the twelve will betray Jesus

## Matthew 26:20

That evening Jesus rested at the table with the twelve.

## Matthew 26:21

As they ate, Jesus said, “I tell you the truth.

One of you will betray me.”

## Matthew 26:22

Deeply sad, they asked him one by one, “Lord, you don’t mean me, do you?”

## Matthew 26:23

Jesus answered, “Someone who dipped his hand into the bowl with me will betray me.

## Matthew 26:24

“The Son of Man will die as Scripture says.

But what sorrow awaits the man who betrays him!

It would have been better for that man never to have been born.”

## Matthew 26:25

Judas, who would betray him, asked, “Rabbi, you don’t mean me, do you?”

Jesus answered, “You have said it yourself.”

**Help with a word**

Rabbi means teacher.

**Pause and answer**

How did the followers feel when Jesus spoke?

## Jesus shares bread and a cup

## Matthew 26:26

As they ate, Jesus took bread and spoke a blessing.

He broke the bread and gave it to his followers.

“Take this and eat,” he said.

“This is my body.”

## Matthew 26:27

Then Jesus took a cup and thanked God.

He gave it to them and said, “All of you, drink from it.

## Matthew 26:28

“This is my blood of the covenant.

I pour it out for many so their sins can be forgiven.

**Help with a word**

A covenant is a binding promise or agreement.

Jesus connects his death with God’s promise to forgive sins.

## Matthew 26:29

“I will not drink this wine again until the day I drink it anew with you in my Father’s kingdom.”

## Matthew 26:30

They sang a song of praise.

Then they went out to the Mount of Olives.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus call the bread?

## Peter makes a promise

## Matthew 26:31

Jesus told them, “Tonight all of you will turn away because of me.

Scripture says, ‘I will strike the shepherd.

The sheep will scatter.’

**Help with this picture**

Jesus speaks of himself as the shepherd.

His followers are like sheep that run away.

## Matthew 26:32

“But after I rise from the dead, I will go ahead of you into Galilee.”

## Matthew 26:33

Peter answered, “Even if everyone else turns away because of you, I never will.”

## Matthew 26:34

Jesus said, “I tell you the truth, Peter.

Tonight, before the rooster crows, you will deny me three times.”

**Help with a word**

To deny Jesus here means to say you do not know him.

## Matthew 26:35

Peter replied, “Even if I have to die with you, I will never deny you.”

All the other followers said the same.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus say Peter would do three times?

## Jesus asks his friends to stay awake

## Matthew 26:36

Jesus and his followers came to a place called Gethsemane.

Jesus said, “Sit here while I go over there to pray.”

## Matthew 26:37

Jesus took Peter and the two sons of Zebedee with him.

He began to feel deep sadness and distress.

**Help with these names**

The two sons of Zebedee were James and John.

## Matthew 26:38

Jesus told them, “My sorrow is so great I feel close to death.

Stay here and keep watch with me.”

## Matthew 26:39

Jesus went a little farther and fell facedown.

He prayed, “My Father, if possible, let this cup pass me by.

Yet let what you want happen, even if it differs from what I want.”

**Help with this picture**

The cup in this prayer means the suffering ahead of Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus ask his three followers to do?

## Jesus prays again

## Matthew 26:40

Jesus returned and found the three followers asleep.

He asked Peter, “Couldn’t you keep watch with me for one hour?

## Matthew 26:41

“Stay awake and pray so you will not give in to temptation.

Your spirit is willing, but your body is weak.”

**Help with a word**

Temptation is a pull to do what is wrong.

## Matthew 26:42

Jesus went away and prayed a second time.

“My Father, if I must drink this cup, let what you want happen.”

## Matthew 26:43

Again he returned and found them asleep.

They could hardly keep their eyes open.

## Matthew 26:44

Jesus left them and went away again.

A third time he prayed the same prayer.

**Pause and answer**

What were the followers doing when Jesus came back?

## Judas arrives

## Matthew 26:45

Jesus returned to his followers.

“Are you still sleeping and resting?” he asked.

“The time is near.

Someone is handing the Son of Man over to people who do wrong.

## Matthew 26:46

“Get up.

Let’s go!

The man who is betraying me is coming.”

## Matthew 26:47

Jesus was still speaking when Judas arrived.

Judas was one of the twelve.

The chief priests and elders had sent a large crowd with him.

They carried swords and clubs.

## Matthew 26:48

Judas had given them a sign.

“Arrest the man I kiss,” he had said.

## Matthew 26:49

Judas went straight to Jesus.

“Greetings, Rabbi!” he said, and kissed him.

## Matthew 26:50

Jesus answered, “Friend, do what you came to do.”

The men stepped forward, seized Jesus, and arrested him.

**Pause and answer**

How did Judas greet Jesus?

## Jesus tells a follower to put away his sword

## Matthew 26:51

One of the people with Jesus drew his sword.

He struck the high priest’s servant and cut off his ear.

## Matthew 26:52

Jesus told him, “Put your sword away.

All who take up the sword will die by the sword.

## Matthew 26:53

“Do you think I cannot ask my Father for help?

He would send me more than twelve armies of angels at once.

**Help with these words**

Jesus uses a word for large groups of soldiers, called legions.

Here he speaks of angels coming to his aid.

## Matthew 26:54

“But Scripture says this must happen.

How else would those words come true?”

## Matthew 26:55

Jesus asked the crowd, “Am I an outlaw that you come with swords and clubs to arrest me?

Every day I sat teaching in the temple courtyards.

You did not arrest me there.

## Matthew 26:56

“But all this happened so the prophets’ words would come true.”

Then all his followers left him and ran away.

**Pause and answer**

Whom could Jesus ask for help?

## Peter follows at a distance

## Matthew 26:57

The men who arrested Jesus took him to Caiaphas, the high priest.

The teachers of the law and the elders had gathered there.

## Matthew 26:58

Peter followed at a distance, right to the high priest’s courtyard.

He went inside and sat with the guards.

He wanted to see what would happen.

## Matthew 26:59

The chief priests and the whole council wanted to put Jesus to death.

They looked for witnesses who would tell lies about him.

## Matthew 26:60

Many people came forward and told lies.

Still, the council found no testimony it could use.

At last two people came forward.

## Matthew 26:61

They said, “This man said, ‘I can destroy God’s temple and build it again in three days.’”

**Pause and answer**

Where did Peter sit while he waited?

## The high priest questions Jesus

## Matthew 26:62

The high priest stood up and asked Jesus, “Won’t you answer?

What do you say about these men’s words against you?”

## Matthew 26:63

Jesus was silent.

The high priest said, “I put you under oath by the living God.

Tell us whether you are the Christ, the Son of God.”

**Help with these words**

An oath is a solemn promise to tell the truth.

Christ means the King God promised to send.

## Matthew 26:64

Jesus answered, “You have said it yourself.

I tell you all this.

From now on you will see the Son of Man at the right hand of the mighty God.

You will see him coming on the clouds of heaven.”

## Matthew 26:65

The high priest tore his clothes.

He said, “He has insulted God!

Why do we need more witnesses?

You just heard him say it.

**Help with this action**

The high priest tore his clothes to show his distress.

## Matthew 26:66

“What do you think?”

They answered, “He deserves to die.”

## Matthew 26:67

Then they spat in Jesus’ face and hit him.

Others slapped him.

## Matthew 26:68

They said, “Prophesy for us, Christ!

Tell us who hit you!”

**Pause and answer**

Who asked Jesus whether he was the Son of God?

## Peter says he does not know Jesus

## Matthew 26:69

Peter was sitting outside in the courtyard.

A servant girl came up to him.

She said, “You were with Jesus from Galilee too.”

## Matthew 26:70

Peter denied it in front of everyone.

He said, “I don’t know what you are talking about.”

## Matthew 26:71

Peter went out to the gateway.

Another servant girl saw him and told the people there, “This man was with Jesus from Nazareth.”

## Matthew 26:72

Again Peter denied it.

He swore, “I do not know the man!”

## Matthew 26:73

A little later, people standing nearby came up to Peter.

They said, “You must be one of them.

We can tell by the way you speak.”

## Matthew 26:74

Peter began to curse and swear.

“I do not know the man!” he said.

At once a rooster crowed.

## Matthew 26:75

Then Peter remembered what Jesus had said.

“Before the rooster crows, you will deny me three times.”

Peter went outside and cried bitterly.

**Pause and answer**

What sound reminded Peter of Jesus’ words?
