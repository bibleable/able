# Matthew 25 — Jesus tells his followers how to be ready

**About this chapter**

Jesus tells stories about waiting and using what someone entrusts to you.

Then he describes a King who remembers each act of care.

## Ten women wait with lamps

## Matthew 25:1

Jesus said, “At that time the kingdom of heaven will be like this.

Ten young women took their lamps to meet a bridegroom.

**Help with these words**

The bridegroom is the man getting married.

Matthew calls the ten women virgins.

They had never had sex.

## Matthew 25:2

“Five of the women were foolish.

Five were wise.

## Matthew 25:3

“The foolish women took their lamps without any extra oil.

## Matthew 25:4

“The wise women took their lamps and jars of extra oil.

**Help with a word**

These lamps burned oil to give light.

## Matthew 25:5

“The bridegroom took a long time to come.

All ten women grew sleepy and fell asleep.

**Pause and answer**

What did the wise women bring along with their lamps?

## The bridegroom arrives

## Matthew 25:6

“At midnight someone shouted, ‘The bridegroom is here!

Come out to meet him!’

## Matthew 25:7

“All ten women woke up.

They got the wicks of their lamps ready to burn.

## Matthew 25:8

“The foolish women asked the wise women for oil.

‘Our lamps are going out,’ they said.

## Matthew 25:9

“The wise women answered, ‘There may not be enough for all of us.

Go and buy some oil from the people who sell it.’

## Matthew 25:10

“While the five women went to buy oil, the bridegroom arrived.

The women who were ready went with him into the wedding feast.

Then the door was shut.

**Pause and answer**

Who went into the wedding feast with the bridegroom?

## The door is shut

## Matthew 25:11

“Later the other women came.

They called, ‘Lord, lord, open the door for us!’

## Matthew 25:12

“The bridegroom answered, ‘I tell you the truth.

I do not know you.’

## Matthew 25:13

“So keep watch.

You do not know the day or the hour.

**Pause and answer**

Why does Jesus tell his followers to keep watch?

## Three servants receive money

## Matthew 25:14

“Think of a man about to go on a journey.

He called his servants and put his property in their care.

## Matthew 25:15

“He gave the first servant five talents.

He gave the second servant two talents.

He gave the third servant one talent.

He gave each what that servant could manage.

Then the master left.

**Help with a word**

In this story a talent is a very large amount of money.

## Matthew 25:16

“The first servant put the five talents to work at once.

He earned five more.

## Matthew 25:17

“The second servant also put his two talents to work.

He earned two more.

## Matthew 25:18

“The third servant dug a hole in the ground.

He hid his master’s money there.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the third servant put the money?

## The first two servants report back

## Matthew 25:19

“After a long time the master returned.

He asked the servants what they had done with his money.

## Matthew 25:20

“The first servant brought five more talents.

He said, ‘Master, you trusted me with five talents.

Look, I earned five more.’

## Matthew 25:21

“The master answered, ‘Well done, good and faithful servant!

I could trust you with a few things.

Now I will put you in charge of many things.

Come and share my joy!’

## Matthew 25:22

“The second servant came and said, ‘Master, you trusted me with two talents.

Look, I earned two more.’

## Matthew 25:23

“The master answered, ‘Well done, good and faithful servant!

I could trust you with a few things.

Now I will put you in charge of many things.

Come and share my joy!’

**Pause and answer**

How did the master answer both servants?

## The third servant speaks

## Matthew 25:24

“Then the third servant came.

He said, ‘Master, I knew you were a hard man.

You harvest crops where you did not plant.

You gather grain where you did not scatter seed.

## Matthew 25:25

“‘I was afraid, so I hid your talent in the ground.

Here is what belongs to you.’

## Matthew 25:26

“The master answered, ‘You wicked, lazy servant!

You knew I harvest where I did not plant.

You knew I gather where I did not scatter seed.

## Matthew 25:27

“‘Then you should have left my money with the bankers.

When I came back, I would have received my money with interest.

**Help with a word**

Interest is extra money paid for the use of someone’s money.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the third servant hide the money?

## The master gives an order

## Matthew 25:28

“‘Take the talent from him.

Give it to the servant who has ten talents.

## Matthew 25:29

“‘Those who have will receive more.

They will have plenty.

Those who have nothing will lose even the little they have.

## Matthew 25:30

“‘Throw this worthless servant outside into the dark.

There people will cry and grind their teeth.’

**Pause and answer**

Who received the talent taken from the third servant?

## The King gathers the nations

## Matthew 25:31

“When the Son of Man comes in glory, all the angels will come with him.

He will sit on his glorious throne.

**Help with these words**

Jesus calls himself the Son of Man.

A throne is a king’s seat.

## Matthew 25:32

“People from every nation will gather before him.

He will separate them into two groups.

A shepherd separates sheep from goats like that.

## Matthew 25:33

“He will put the sheep on his right.

He will put the goats on his left.

**Pause and answer**

Where does the shepherd put the sheep?

## The King welcomes those on his right

## Matthew 25:34

“The King will tell the people on his right, ‘Come!

My Father has blessed you.

Receive the kingdom prepared for you since the world began.

## Matthew 25:35

“‘I was hungry, and you gave me food.

I was thirsty, and you gave me a drink.

I was a stranger, and you welcomed me in.

**About the picture**

![A drinking cup.](../pictures/cup.svg)

Point to the cup.

Giving a thirsty person a drink is one way to help.

## Matthew 25:36

“‘I needed clothes, and you gave me clothes.

I was sick, and you cared for me.

I was in prison, and you visited me.’

**Pause and answer**

What did the people do for a stranger?

## Serving the King

## Matthew 25:37

“Then the people who did right will ask, ‘Lord, when did we see you hungry and feed you?

When did we see you thirsty and give you a drink?

## Matthew 25:38

“‘When did we see you as a stranger and welcome you?

When did we see you needing clothes and give you clothes?

## Matthew 25:39

“‘When did we see you sick or in prison and visit you?’

## Matthew 25:40

“The King will answer, ‘I tell you the truth.

You helped one of the least of these brothers of mine.

Whatever you did for him, you did for me.’

**Help with these words**

The King speaks of people others may overlook.

He calls them his brothers.

**Pause and answer**

How had the people helped the King?

## The King speaks to those on his left

## Matthew 25:41

“Then the King will tell the people on his left, ‘Go away from me.

You are under a curse.

Go into the fire that burns forever.

It was prepared for the devil and his angels.

## Matthew 25:42

“‘I was hungry, and you gave me no food.

I was thirsty, and you gave me no drink.

## Matthew 25:43

“‘I was a stranger, and you did not welcome me in.

I needed clothes, and you gave me no clothes.

I was sick and in prison, and you did not care for me.’

## Matthew 25:44

“They too will ask, ‘Lord, when did we see you in need and fail to help?

Were you hungry or thirsty, a stranger or without clothes?

Were you sick or in prison?’

## Matthew 25:45

“The King will answer, ‘I tell you the truth.

Whatever you failed to do for one of the least of these, you failed to do for me.’

## Matthew 25:46

“These people will go away to punishment that lasts forever.

Those who did right will enter life that lasts forever.”

**Pause and answer**

What care did these people fail to give?
