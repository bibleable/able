# Jonah 2 — Jonah prays inside the fish

**About this chapter**

Jonah remembers sinking into the sea. From inside a great fish, he thanks God for hearing him.

## Jonah calls for help

## Jonah 2:1

Jonah was inside the fish.

There he prayed to the LORD his God.

## Jonah 2:2

Jonah said, “I was in deep trouble.

I called to the LORD, and He answered me.

I felt as if I were already in Sheol.

Sheol is the place of the dead.

I cried for help, and You heard my voice.

## Jonah 2:3

“You threw me into the deep sea.

The water rushed around me.

Your great waves broke over my head.

## Jonah 2:4

“I thought, ‘You have sent me away from Your sight.

Yet I will look toward Your holy temple again.’

## The LORD lifts Jonah up

## Jonah 2:5

“The water closed over me.

I was drowning.

Deep water surrounded me.

Seaweed wrapped around my head.

## Jonah 2:6

“I sank to the bottom of the mountains.

I felt trapped there forever, as if behind locked gates.

But You brought me up from the pit of death.

You saved my life, LORD my God!

## Jonah 2:7

“My life was slipping away.

Then I remembered You, LORD.

My prayer reached You in Your holy temple.

## Jonah gives thanks

## Jonah 2:8

“Some people hold on to worthless idols.

Idols are things people worship as gods.

Those people turn away from the LORD’s faithful love.

## Jonah 2:9

“But I will thank You aloud.

I will bring You a sacrifice, a gift for worship.

I will do what I promised You.

The LORD is the One who saves!”

## Jonah 2:10

Then the LORD gave the fish a command.

The fish vomited Jonah onto dry land.
