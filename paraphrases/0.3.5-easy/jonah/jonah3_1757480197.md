# Jonah 3 — Nineveh listens to God’s warning

**About this chapter**

The LORD sends Jonah to warn the people of Nineveh. The people hear the warning and change how they live.

## Jonah goes to Nineveh

## Jonah 3:1

The LORD spoke to Jonah a second time.

## Jonah 3:2

“Get up, Jonah!

Go to the great city of Nineveh.

Tell the people the message I give you.”

## Jonah 3:3

This time Jonah got up and obeyed the LORD.

He went to Nineveh.

The city was so large that the journey through it took three days.

## Jonah 3:4

On the first day, Jonah walked into the city.

He called out, “In forty days Nineveh will fall!”

## The people believe God

## Jonah 3:5

The people of Nineveh believed God.

They called for a fast.

A fast is a time when people go without food.

Everyone put on sackcloth, from the most important person to the least.

Sackcloth is rough cloth.

Wearing it showed how sorry they were.

![A piece of sackcloth with thick threads woven together.](../pictures/job-sackcloth.svg)

## Jonah 3:6

The king of Nineveh heard the news.

He stood up from his throne.

He took off his royal robe.

He put on sackcloth and sat in ashes.

## The king calls for change

## Jonah 3:7

The king sent an order throughout Nineveh.

“This order comes from the king and his officials.

No one may eat or drink anything.

Even the herds and flocks must go without food and water.

## Jonah 3:8

“Cover yourselves and your animals with sackcloth.

Everyone must call out to God with all their heart.

Each of you must stop doing evil.

Stop hurting people.

## Jonah 3:9

“Who knows what God will do?

God may turn from His great anger and spare us.

Then we will live.”

## God spares the city

## Jonah 3:10

God saw what the people did.

They had stopped doing evil.

God had warned them that disaster was coming.

Now He spared them from that disaster.
