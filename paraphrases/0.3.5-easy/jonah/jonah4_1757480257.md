# Jonah 4 — God cares about Nineveh

**About this chapter**

God has spared the city of Nineveh. Jonah goes outside the city. There God asks Jonah about a plant and the people of Nineveh.

## Jonah tells God why he ran away

## Jonah 4:1

Jonah was deeply upset that God had spared Nineveh.

He became angry.

## Jonah 4:2

Jonah prayed, “LORD, this is what I said back home!

This is why I hurried away toward Tarshish.

I knew You were kind and full of pity.

You are slow to get angry.

Your faithful love is great.

You can hold back the disaster You warned people about.

## Jonah 4:3

“Now, LORD, please take my life.

I would rather die than live.”

## Jonah 4:4

The LORD asked, “Jonah, is it right for you to be angry?”

## A plant gives Jonah shade

## Jonah 4:5

Jonah left Nineveh and went east of the city.

He made a shelter and sat in its shade.

He waited to see what would happen to Nineveh.

![A simple shelter with a roof of branches.](../pictures/feast-shelter.svg)

## Jonah 4:6

The LORD God made a vine grow over Jonah’s head.

Its leaves shaded Jonah and gave him relief.

Jonah was very glad to have the plant.

## Jonah 4:7

At dawn the next day, God sent a worm.

The worm attacked the plant, and the plant dried up.

## Jonah 4:8

As the sun rose, God sent a hot east wind.

The sun beat down on Jonah’s head.

He felt faint and wanted to die.

He said, “I would rather die than live.”

## God asks about the plant and the city

## Jonah 4:9

God asked, “Is it right to be angry about the plant?”

Jonah answered, “Yes! I am angry enough to die!”

## Jonah 4:10

The LORD said, “Jonah, you cared about that plant.

You did no work to look after it.

You did nothing to make it grow.

It grew in one night and died in one night.

## Jonah 4:11

“Think of Nineveh, that great city.

More than 120,000 people there cannot tell their right hand from their left.

There are many cattle there too.

Should I not care about Nineveh?”
