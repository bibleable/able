# Jonah 1 — Jonah takes a ship to Tarshish

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Jonah to go to Nineveh. Jonah boards a ship going elsewhere. When a storm comes, the sailors ask Jonah what has happened.

## The LORD sends Jonah to Nineveh

## Jonah 1:1

The LORD spoke to Jonah, the son of Amittai.

## Jonah 1:2

“Get up and go to the great city of Nineveh.

Speak out against the evil the people do.

I have seen it.”

## Jonah 1:3

Jonah got up, but he tried to run away from the LORD.

He went down to the port of Joppa.

There he found a ship sailing to Tarshish.

Jonah paid for the trip and got on board.

![A boat on the water.](../pictures/boat.svg)

## A storm threatens the ship

## Jonah 1:4

The LORD sent a strong wind over the sea.

A storm shook the ship.

It nearly broke apart.

## Jonah 1:5

The sailors were afraid.

Each sailor cried out to his own god.

They threw goods into the sea to make the ship lighter.

Meanwhile, Jonah had gone down to the bottom of the ship.

He lay there fast asleep.

## Jonah 1:6

The captain found Jonah.

“How can you sleep?” the captain asked.

“Get up and call to your God!

Maybe your God will think of us and save our lives.”

## The sailors question Jonah

## Jonah 1:7

The sailors said, “Let us cast lots.

Let us find out who brought this trouble on us.”

Casting lots was a way to choose someone, like drawing a name.

The lot pointed to Jonah.

## Jonah 1:8

The sailors asked Jonah, “Who brought this trouble on us?

What work do you do?

Where do you come from?

What is your country?

Who are your people?”

## Jonah 1:9

Jonah answered, “I am a Hebrew.

I worship the LORD, the God of heaven.

He made the sea and the dry land.”

## Jonah 1:10

Jonah had told them he was running away from the LORD.

Now the sailors were even more afraid.

They asked, “What have you done?”

## Jonah 1:11

The waves kept getting rougher.

The sailors asked Jonah, “What should we do with you so the sea will grow calm?”

## Jonah 1:12

Jonah answered, “Pick me up and throw me into the sea.

Then the sea will be calm for you.

I know this terrible storm is my fault.”

## The sailors try to reach land

## Jonah 1:13

The sailors tried hard to row back to land.

But the waves grew even wilder.

The sailors could not reach the shore.

## Jonah 1:14

Then the sailors called to the LORD.

“Please, LORD, do not let us die because of this man.

Please do not hold us guilty of killing an innocent man.

LORD, You have done what You chose to do.”

## Jonah 1:15

They lifted Jonah and threw him into the sea.

Then the wild sea grew calm.

## Jonah 1:16

The sailors felt great awe before the LORD.

They offered Him a sacrifice, a gift in worship.

They also made promises to Him.

## Jonah 1:17

The LORD sent a great fish to swallow Jonah.

Jonah stayed inside the fish for three days and three nights.
