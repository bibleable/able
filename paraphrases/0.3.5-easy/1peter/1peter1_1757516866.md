# 1 Peter 1 — God gives us a living hope

**About this chapter**

Peter writes to people living far from home.

He gives them a reason for hope even in grief.

## Peter greets people far from home

## 1 Peter 1:1

I am Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ.

An apostle is someone Jesus sends with his message.

I write to God’s chosen people living far from home.

You are scattered across Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia and Bithynia.

## 1 Peter 1:2

God the Father knew you beforehand and chose you.

His Spirit set you apart to obey Jesus Christ.

Peter pictures Jesus’ blood sprinkled on you.

This means Jesus gave his life to make you clean.

May God give you his kindness and peace in full measure.

**Pause and answer**

Who set these people apart to obey Jesus?

## A gift God keeps for you

## 1 Peter 1:3

Praise God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ!

God showed us great mercy and gave us new birth.

New birth means that God gives us new life.

Our hope is alive because God raised Jesus from death.

## 1 Peter 1:4

God is keeping an inheritance for you in heaven.

An inheritance is something a family member receives.

Nothing can spoil this gift.

It will never wear out or lose its beauty.

## 1 Peter 1:5

As you trust God, his power guards you.

God is ready to show you his salvation at the end.

Salvation means that God saves you.

**Pause and answer**

Where does God keep the gift he promises?

## Trusting Jesus through grief

## 1 Peter 1:6

This gives you great joy.

Yet for a little while, many troubles may bring you grief.

## 1 Peter 1:7

Fire tests gold and removes what is mixed with it.

Even that gold will perish.

Your faith is worth more than gold.

Troubles show that your trust in God is real.

Such faith will bring praise, glory and honor when Jesus Christ appears.

## 1 Peter 1:8

You love Jesus even though you have never seen him.

Even now, you trust him without seeing him.

Your joy is so wonderful that words cannot express it.

## 1 Peter 1:9

You are receiving what you trust God for.

He is saving you.

**Pause and answer**

Whom do the readers love even though they have never seen him?

## What the prophets wanted to know

## 1 Peter 1:10

Long ago, the prophets spoke for God.

They told of the kindness God would show you.

They searched carefully to understand how God would save you.

## 1 Peter 1:11

Christ’s Spirit was at work in those prophets.

The Spirit told them Christ would suffer.

Then great glory would follow.

The prophets tried to learn when and how this would happen.

## 1 Peter 1:12

God showed the prophets whom their words would serve.

They were speaking for your sake, beyond their own time.

Now people have told you this good news.

The Holy Spirit from heaven helped them tell you.

Even angels long to understand these things.

**Pause and answer**

Whose Spirit helped people tell the good news?

## Live as God’s children

## 1 Peter 1:13

So prepare your minds to act.

Think clearly and stay alert.

Put all your hope in the kindness God will show you when Jesus Christ appears.

## 1 Peter 1:14

Obey God as his children.

Once you did not know better.

Leave behind the desires that ruled you then.

## 1 Peter 1:15

The God who called you is holy.

Be holy in everything you do.

To be holy is to be set apart for God.

## 1 Peter 1:16

Scripture gives God’s reason.

“Be holy, because I am holy.”

## 1 Peter 1:17

When you pray, you call God your Father.

He judges everyone’s actions fairly.

While you live as strangers here, treat him with deep respect.

**Pause and answer**

How does the Father judge people’s actions?

## How Christ set you free

## 1 Peter 1:18

Your ancestors passed down an empty way of life.

You know that God paid to set you free from it.

Silver and gold wear out.

Neither could pay that price.

![Silver coins.](../pictures/ten-silver-coins.svg)

Look at the silver coins. Read on to find out what Christ gave.

## 1 Peter 1:19

Christ paid with his precious blood.

Picture a lamb without a single flaw, offered to God.

Like that lamb, Christ was without fault.

He gave his life for you.

## 1 Peter 1:20

God knew Christ before the world began.

In these last times, God made Christ known for your sake.

## 1 Peter 1:21

Through Christ you trust God.

God raised Christ from death and gave him glory.

So you put your trust and hope in God.

**Pause and answer**

What did God do after Christ died?

## Love one another deeply

## 1 Peter 1:22

You obeyed the truth and made your lives clean.

Now you have real love for your brothers and sisters.

Love one another deeply with a pure heart.

## 1 Peter 1:23

God has given you new birth.

Peter pictures this new life as growing from a seed.

This seed can never die.

God gives you life through his word, which lives and lasts.

## 1 Peter 1:24

Scripture compares people to grass.

All their glory is like flowers in a field.

The grass dries up.

The flowers fall.

## 1 Peter 1:25

Yet the Lord’s word lasts forever.

This is the good news you heard.

**Pause and answer**

What lasts even after the grass dries up?
