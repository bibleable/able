# 1 Peter 5 — Give God your worries

**About this chapter**

Peter asks leaders to care for God’s people.

He reminds everyone that God cares for them.

## Care for God’s people

## 1 Peter 5:1

I ask the elders among you to listen.

Elders are leaders who care for the church.

I am an elder too.

I witnessed Christ’s suffering.

I will also share in the glory he will reveal.

## 1 Peter 5:2

Care for God’s people as a shepherd cares for sheep.

Watch over the people God has placed among you.

Do this willingly, as God wants.

Serve eagerly, without trying to get rich from it.

![A sheep pen with an open gate.](../pictures/sheep-pen.svg)

Look at the gate. A shepherd watches over the sheep. Peter asks leaders to care for people.

## 1 Peter 5:3

Never use your power to boss people around.

Show the people in your care how to live.

## 1 Peter 5:4

Christ is the chief Shepherd.

When he comes, you will receive a crown of glory.

That crown pictures an honor that will never fade.

**Pause and answer**

How can a leader set an example?

## Be humble and give God your worries

## 1 Peter 5:5

Younger men, accept the guidance of your elders.

All of you, be humble toward each other.

Wear humility like clothing so people see it in your actions.

God stands against proud people.

He shows kindness to humble people.

## 1 Peter 5:6

Be humble under God’s powerful hand.

His hand is a picture of his power.

When the time is right, God will lift you up.

## 1 Peter 5:7

Give all your worries to God.

You can do this because he cares for you.

**Pause and answer**

Why can you give your worries to God?

## Stay alert and trust God

## 1 Peter 5:8

Keep a clear head and stay alert.

Your enemy, the devil, is like a roaring lion.

The lion prowls about, looking for someone to eat.

Peter means the devil is looking for someone to destroy.

## 1 Peter 5:9

Stand against the devil.

Hold firmly to your trust in God.

Remember your brothers and sisters throughout the world.

They face the same kinds of suffering.

## 1 Peter 5:10

You will suffer for a little while.

God is the source of all grace, his freely given kindness.

Through Christ, he called you to share his glory forever.

God himself will put you right again.

He will give you strength and help you stand firm.

He will set you on a foundation that holds.

## 1 Peter 5:11

Power belongs to God forever.

Amen.

**Pause and answer**

Who will give strength to people who suffer?

## Greetings at the end of the letter

## 1 Peter 5:12

Silvanus helped me write this short letter.

I trust him as a faithful brother.

I wrote to encourage you.

I assure you that this kindness truly comes from God.

Hold firmly to it.

## 1 Peter 5:13

The church in Babylon sends greetings.

God chose them just as he chose you.

Mark sends greetings too.

I call him my son.

## 1 Peter 5:14

Greet each other with a kiss that shows your love.

May all of you who belong to Christ have peace.

What does Peter ask for everyone who belongs to Christ?
