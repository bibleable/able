# 1 Peter 2 — God builds his people together

**About this chapter**

Peter pictures a builder fitting stones together.

He explains how people who belong to Jesus can live together.

## Grow like a baby drinking milk

## 1 Peter 2:1

So give up wanting to hurt people.

Stop tricking people or pretending to be good.

Let go of envy.

Stop telling lies that harm someone’s good name.

## 1 Peter 2:2

A newborn baby longs for milk.

Long for pure spiritual milk like that baby.

Peter pictures what feeds your faith as milk.

It helps you grow in the new life God gives you.

## 1 Peter 2:3

You have already tasted the Lord’s goodness.

That means you have experienced how good he is.

**Pause and answer**

What does milk help a newborn baby do?

## A house made of living stones

## 1 Peter 2:4

Come to Jesus.

Peter calls Jesus a living stone.

People rejected Jesus.

God chose him and treasures him.

## 1 Peter 2:5

You too are like living stones.

God fits you together to build a spiritual house.

Together you serve God as his holy priests.

Priests bring offerings to God.

Through Jesus Christ, you bring spiritual gifts of worship that please God.

## 1 Peter 2:6

In Scripture, God says he is laying a stone in Zion.

Zion is a name for Jerusalem.

God chose this precious stone as the cornerstone.

A cornerstone helps set the walls in place.

Anyone who trusts in Jesus will never face shame for trusting him.

![A cornerstone where two stone walls meet.](../pictures/cornerstone.svg)

Find the stone where the walls meet. Peter asks us to think of Jesus as that stone.

## 1 Peter 2:7

You who trust Jesus know how precious he is.

What about those who refuse to believe?

Scripture says the builders threw away a stone.

That very stone became the cornerstone.

## 1 Peter 2:8

Scripture also calls him a rock people trip over.

They stumble because they disobey God’s message.

This is the outcome appointed for them.

**Pause and answer**

Who is the cornerstone in Peter’s picture?

## A people who belong to God

## 1 Peter 2:9

God chose you to be his own people.

You serve the King as priests.

You are a nation set apart for God.

Tell others how good God is.

He called you out of darkness into his wonderful light.

That means he brought you from a life apart from him into his presence.

## 1 Peter 2:10

Once you did not belong together as a people.

Now you are God’s people.

Once you had not received mercy.

Now God has shown you mercy.

## 1 Peter 2:11

Dear friends, you live here as strangers far from home.

I urge you to turn away from wrong desires.

Those desires fight against your very life.

## 1 Peter 2:12

Live honorably among your neighbors who do not worship God.

They may accuse you of doing wrong.

Let them see the good you do.

Then they may praise God on the day he comes to visit.

**Pause and answer**

What can your neighbors see you do?

## Do good as free people

## 1 Peter 2:13

For the Lord’s sake, respect and obey human authorities.

This includes the king, who holds the highest office.

## 1 Peter 2:14

It also includes the governors the king sends.

They punish people who do wrong.

They praise people who do right.

## 1 Peter 2:15

God wants you to keep doing good.

This will silence foolish people who speak without knowing the truth.

## 1 Peter 2:16

Live as free people who serve God.

Never use your freedom to hide wrongdoing.

## 1 Peter 2:17

Treat every person with respect.

Love the whole family of believers.

Honor God with deep respect and awe.

Show respect to the king.

**Pause and answer**

Who should receive your respect?

## When servants suffer unfairly

## 1 Peter 2:18

Peter speaks to servants who work under masters.

Show your masters respect and follow their orders.

Peter says this includes gentle masters and harsh ones.

## 1 Peter 2:19

Someone may suffer unfairly yet keep God in mind.

God approves when that person endures pain for his sake.

## 1 Peter 2:20

Suppose you do wrong and someone beats you.

Bearing that pain earns you no praise.

But suppose you suffer because you did good.

God approves when you keep going through that suffering.

**Pause and answer**

What does God see when someone suffers for doing good?

## Follow the example Christ gave

## 1 Peter 2:21

God called you to follow Christ in this.

Christ suffered for you.

He left you an example to follow.

Peter pictures someone walking in the tracks Christ left behind.

## 1 Peter 2:22

Christ never sinned.

He never tried to deceive anyone with his words.

## 1 Peter 2:23

People insulted Christ.

He did not answer with insults.

He suffered without threatening anyone.

Christ trusted himself to God, who judges fairly.

## 1 Peter 2:24

Christ carried our sins in his own body on the wooden cross.

He did this so we could stop living for sin and do what is right.

Peter says that Christ’s wounds have brought you healing.

## 1 Peter 2:25

You were like sheep that wandered away.

Now you have come back to Christ.

Like a shepherd watching over sheep, Christ watches over your lives.

**Pause and answer**

What did Christ do when people insulted him?
