# 1 Peter 4 — Use your gifts to serve each other

**About this chapter**

Peter writes about loving people and welcoming guests.

He also writes to people who suffer for following Christ.

## Choose what God wants

## 1 Peter 4:1

Christ suffered in his body.

Be ready to face suffering with the same determination.

Whoever has suffered in the body has finished with sin.

## 1 Peter 4:2

So use the rest of your life to do what God wants.

Stop letting human desires decide how you live.

## 1 Peter 4:3

You once lived like people who worship other gods.

You have spent enough time chasing sexual pleasure and getting drunk.

You joined in wild drinking parties and group sex.

You worshiped idols, something God hates.

**Pause and answer**

What does Peter want his readers to do with the rest of their lives?

## When old companions insult you

## 1 Peter 4:4

Now your old companions think you are strange.

You have stopped rushing with them into that reckless life.

So they insult you.

## 1 Peter 4:5

They will have to answer to God for their actions.

God stands ready to judge the living and the dead.

## 1 Peter 4:6

This is why even people who have since died heard the good news.

They faced judgment in their bodies as other people do.

Yet they could live in the spirit as God intends.

**Pause and answer**

Who will judge the living and the dead?

## Love each other deeply

## 1 Peter 4:7

The end of all things is near.

Keep a clear head.

Stay alert so you can pray.

## 1 Peter 4:8

Above all, love each other deeply.

Love covers many sins.

When you love someone, you can forgive many wrongs.

## 1 Peter 4:9

Welcome each other into your homes.

Do this without complaining.

![A woven basket.](../pictures/basket.svg)

Imagine bringing food in this basket to share with a guest.

## 1 Peter 4:10

God shows his kindness through many different gifts.

Each of you has received a gift to care for.

Use yours to serve someone else.

## 1 Peter 4:11

When you speak, speak as someone bringing God’s words.

When you serve, use the strength God gives you.

Then people can praise God through Jesus Christ in everything.

Glory and power belong to him forever.

Amen.

**Pause and answer**

How can you use a gift God gave you?

## When people insult you for following Christ

## 1 Peter 4:12

Dear friends, you face a painful test.

Peter compares it to going through fire.

You need not think something strange is happening to you.

## 1 Peter 4:13

You share in Christ’s sufferings.

That gives you a reason for joy even now.

When Christ shows his glory, your joy will overflow.

## 1 Peter 4:14

People may insult you because you belong to Christ.

Even then, God blesses you.

God’s glorious Spirit rests on you.

## 1 Peter 4:15

None of you should suffer for murdering or stealing.

Do not give people cause to accuse you of wrongdoing.

Even interfering in other people’s business can do harm.

## 1 Peter 4:16

If you suffer for being a Christian, take courage.

You have no reason to feel ashamed.

Praise God that you bear Christ’s name.

**Pause and answer**

Why might someone suffer even when they have done good?

## Trust the God who made you

## 1 Peter 4:17

The time has come for God to judge his family first.

If he begins with us, what will happen to people who refuse to obey his good news?

## 1 Peter 4:18

Scripture asks what happens to those who reject God and keep sinning.

Even those who do right find the way to salvation hard.

## 1 Peter 4:19

So if you suffer while doing God’s will, trust the God who made you.

He is faithful.

Put your life in his care and keep doing good.

**Pause and answer**

Whom can you trust when you suffer?
