# 1 Peter 3 — Be ready to explain your hope

**About this chapter**

Peter writes to wives and husbands.

Then he asks everyone to do good, even when it costs them.

## The beauty God treasures

## 1 Peter 3:1

Peter writes to wives.

In the same way, accept your husband’s leadership.

Some husbands refuse to believe the message about Jesus.

How you live can win your husband over without a word.

## 1 Peter 3:2

Your husband can see how you live.

Let him see your pure conduct and deep respect.

## 1 Peter 3:3

Think about what makes a person beautiful.

Braided hair, gold jewelry and fine clothes decorate the outside.

## 1 Peter 3:4

Peter asks you to consider the person you are inside.

A gentle, quiet spirit has a beauty that lasts.

God treasures that beauty.

**Pause and answer**

What kind of beauty does God treasure?

## Sarah’s example and a word to husbands

## 1 Peter 3:5

Long ago, women who belonged to God made themselves beautiful this way.

They put their hope in God.

They accepted their husbands’ leadership.

## 1 Peter 3:6

Sarah obeyed Abraham and called him her lord.

You are like daughters of Sarah when you do right and refuse to let fear rule you.

## 1 Peter 3:7

Peter also speaks to husbands.

Live with your wife in a thoughtful way.

Peter pictures a delicate vessel, something you handle with care.

Honor your wife.

God gives both of you the same gift of life.

Treat her with honor so nothing gets in the way of your prayers.

**Pause and answer**

What gift do a husband and wife share?

## Look for ways to make peace

## 1 Peter 3:8

Now Peter speaks to everyone.

Seek agreement with each other.

Care when another person suffers.

Love each other like brothers and sisters.

Be kind and humble.

## 1 Peter 3:9

When people hurt you, do not hurt them back.

When they insult you, ask God to bless them.

God called you to do this.

He wants you to receive a blessing too.

## 1 Peter 3:10

Scripture speaks to anyone who wants to enjoy life and see good days.

Keep harmful words off your tongue.

Tell the truth.

## 1 Peter 3:11

Turn away from evil and do good.

Look for ways to make peace.

Keep working for peace.

## 1 Peter 3:12

The Lord watches over those who do right.

He listens when they pray.

The Lord stands against those who do evil.

![An eye that sees and an ear that hears.](../pictures/eye-and-ear.svg)

Look at the ear. Peter says the Lord listens when people pray.

**Pause and answer**

How can you answer someone who insults you?

## When someone asks why you have hope

## 1 Peter 3:13

Who will harm you if you are eager to do good?

## 1 Peter 3:14

Even if you suffer for doing right, God blesses you.

Do not fear what other people fear.

Stay steady.

## 1 Peter 3:15

Honor Christ as Lord deep in your heart.

Someone may ask why you have hope.

Be ready to give your reason.

Answer gently and show respect.

## 1 Peter 3:16

Keep your conscience clear.

That means you know you have acted honestly.

People may tell lies about you.

When they see how well you live as followers of Christ, they may feel ashamed.

## 1 Peter 3:17

You may suffer even when you do good.

If God allows this, it is better than suffering for doing evil.

**Pause and answer**

How should you speak when someone asks why you have hope?

## Christ brings you to God

## 1 Peter 3:18

Christ suffered for sins once for all.

Christ did what was right.

Yet he suffered for people who did wrong.

He did this to bring you to God.

People killed Christ’s body.

But he came alive in the Spirit.

## 1 Peter 3:19

In the Spirit, Christ also went to the spirits in prison.

He spoke his message to them.

## 1 Peter 3:20

Those spirits had disobeyed long ago, in Noah’s time.

God waited patiently while Noah built the ark, a large boat.

In that boat God brought eight people safely through the water.

## 1 Peter 3:21

That water is a picture of baptism.

In baptism a person goes into water.

Baptism now saves you through Jesus Christ’s return from death.

Washing dirt off your skin cannot do that.

In baptism you make a pledge to God.

You promise him a clear conscience.

## 1 Peter 3:22

Jesus Christ has gone into heaven.

He is at God’s right hand, the place of honor.

Angels and all who hold authority or power must obey him.

**Pause and answer**

How many people came safely through the water in the ark?
