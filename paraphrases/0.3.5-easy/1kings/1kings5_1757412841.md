# 1 Kings 5 — Solomon gets ready to build the temple

**About this chapter**

Solomon needs wood and stone for the temple. He asks Hiram for help, and the two kings agree on what each will supply.

## Two kings exchange messages

## 1 Kings 5:1

Hiram, king of Tyre, had always been David’s friend.

Hiram heard that Solomon had been anointed king in David’s place.

He sent messengers to Solomon.

## 1 Kings 5:2

Solomon sent this message back to Hiram.

## Solomon asks for cedar trees

## 1 Kings 5:3

“You know that my father David faced war on every side.

He could not build a house to honor the LORD his God while the wars lasted.

At last the LORD gave him victory over his enemies.

## 1 Kings 5:4

Now the LORD my God has given me peace on every side.

No enemy threatens me. No trouble stands in my way.

## 1 Kings 5:5

I plan to build a house to honor the LORD my God.

The LORD told my father David, ‘I will make your son king after you.

He will build a house to honor my name.’

**Reading help**

The house Solomon plans to build is the temple. People will worship the LORD there.

## 1 Kings 5:6

Please have your workers cut down cedar trees for me in Lebanon.

My workers will help yours.

I will pay your workers whatever you ask.

No one here cuts trees as well as the people of Sidon.”

## Hiram offers to send wood

## 1 Kings 5:7

Hiram was very glad to hear Solomon’s message.

He said, “Praise the LORD today!

He has given David a wise son to rule this great people.”

## 1 Kings 5:8

Hiram sent Solomon this answer.

“I have received your message.

I will supply the cedar and cypress wood you want.

## 1 Kings 5:9

My workers will bring the logs from Lebanon to the sea.

I will have the logs tied together as rafts.

They will float to the place you choose.

There I will have the logs taken apart.

Then your workers can carry them away.

In return, please send food for my household.”

**Reading help**

A raft can be made by tying logs side by side. The logs float together on the water.

![Logs tied side by side to form a raft on the water.](../pictures/hirams-timber-raft.svg)

## Solomon sends wheat and olive oil

## 1 Kings 5:10

Hiram supplied all the cedar and cypress wood Solomon wanted.

## 1 Kings 5:11

Every year Solomon sent food for Hiram’s household.

He sent 20,000 cors of wheat and 20,000 baths of pure olive oil.

**Reading help**

A cor measured dry food such as wheat. A bath measured liquid such as oil.

## 1 Kings 5:12

The LORD gave Solomon wisdom, just as he had promised.

Hiram and Solomon were at peace.

The two kings made a formal agreement called a treaty.

## Workers take turns in Lebanon

## 1 Kings 5:13

Solomon required 30,000 men from across Israel to work for him.

## 1 Kings 5:14

Each month Solomon sent 10,000 men to Lebanon.

Each group worked one month there, then spent two months at home.

Adoniram was in charge of these workers.

## Builders prepare the stone

## 1 Kings 5:15

Solomon also had 70,000 men to carry loads.

Another 80,000 cut stone in the mountains.

## 1 Kings 5:16

Besides these workers, Solomon had 3,300 men to oversee the work.

## 1 Kings 5:17

Solomon ordered the workers to cut large, costly stones from the rock.

They shaped the stones for the base of the temple.

## 1 Kings 5:18

Builders from Solomon and Hiram worked with people from Gebal.

Together they cut stone from the rock.

They prepared the wood and stone for building the temple.
