# 1 Kings 4 — Solomon rules Israel and teaches with wisdom

**About this chapter**

Solomon has people to help him govern and feed his household. People travel from other nations to hear what he can teach them.

## People who helped Solomon rule

## 1 Kings 4:1

Solomon was king over Israel.

## 1 Kings 4:2

These were the people who helped him rule.

Azariah, son of Zadok, was the priest.

## 1 Kings 4:3

Elihoreph and Ahijah wrote documents for the king.

Their father was Shisha.

Jehoshaphat, son of Ahilud, kept the king’s records.

## 1 Kings 4:4

Benaiah, son of Jehoiada, led the army.

Zadok and Abiathar served as priests.

## 1 Kings 4:5

Azariah, son of Nathan, was in charge of the governors.

Zabud, son of Nathan, was a priest.

He also gave the king advice.

## 1 Kings 4:6

Ahishar looked after the palace.

Adoniram, son of Abda, was in charge of people who had to work by force.

## Twelve governors take turns

## 1 Kings 4:7

Solomon had twelve governors throughout Israel.

They took turns supplying food for the king and his household.

Each governor supplied the food for one month every year.

**Reading help**

A governor looked after one part of the kingdom. That area was his district.

## 1 Kings 4:8

Here are their names and the places they governed.

Ben-hur governed the hill country of Ephraim.

## 1 Kings 4:9

Ben-deker governed Makaz, Shaalbim, Beth-shemesh, and Elon-beth-hanan.

## 1 Kings 4:10

Ben-hesed governed Arubboth.

His district included Socoh and all the land of Hepher.

## Districts along the coast and inland

## 1 Kings 4:11

Ben-abinadab governed Naphath-dor.

His wife was Taphath, one of Solomon’s daughters.

## 1 Kings 4:12

Baana, son of Ahilud, governed Taanach and Megiddo.

He also governed the whole area of Beth-shean.

It lay beside Zarethan, below Jezreel.

His district stretched from Beth-shean to Abel-meholah and beyond Jokmeam.

## 1 Kings 4:13

Ben-geber governed Ramoth-gilead.

His district included the villages of Jair in Gilead.

Jair was the son of Manasseh.

Ben-geber also governed Argob in Bashan.

Its 60 large cities had walls and bronze bars on their gates.

## More governors and their districts

## 1 Kings 4:14

Ahinadab, son of Iddo, governed Mahanaim.

## 1 Kings 4:15

Ahimaaz governed Naphtali.

His wife was Basemath, one of Solomon’s daughters.

## 1 Kings 4:16

Baana, son of Hushai, governed Asher and Aloth.

## 1 Kings 4:17

Jehoshaphat, son of Paruah, governed Issachar.

## 1 Kings 4:18

Shimei, son of Ela, governed Benjamin.

## 1 Kings 4:19

Geber, son of Uri, governed the land of Gilead.

His district included the lands of two former kings.

They were Sihon, king of the Amorites, and Og, king of Bashan.

Judah also had a governor.

## Food for Solomon’s household

## 1 Kings 4:20

Judah and Israel had as many people as grains of sand on a beach.

The people ate, drank, and celebrated.

## 1 Kings 4:21

Solomon ruled the kingdoms from the Euphrates River to the land of the Philistines and the border of Egypt.

Those kingdoms served Solomon throughout his life.

They sent him payments called tribute.

## 1 Kings 4:22

Each day Solomon’s household needed 30 cors of fine flour.

It also needed 60 cors of coarsely ground grain.

**Reading help**

A cor was a large measure for dry food such as flour or grain.

## 1 Kings 4:23

Each day it needed 10 fat oxen and 20 pasture-fed oxen.

It also needed 100 sheep.

There were deer, gazelles, roebucks, and fattened birds for food too.

## People live in peace

## 1 Kings 4:24

Solomon ruled all the kingdoms west of the Euphrates River, from Tiphsah to Gaza.

There was peace on every side.

## 1 Kings 4:25

While Solomon ruled, Judah and Israel lived in safety.

From Dan to Beersheba, people sat under their own grapevines and fig trees.

**Reading help**

Sitting under your own vine and fig tree is a picture of peace. People could stay on their land and enjoy what they grew.

## 1 Kings 4:26

Solomon had 4,000 stalls for the horses that pulled his chariots.

He had 12,000 horses.

## 1 Kings 4:27

Each month a different governor provided the king’s food.

Everyone who ate at Solomon’s table had what they needed.

## 1 Kings 4:28

Each governor also brought his share of barley and straw.

He took it where the chariot horses and other horses needed it.

![Grains growing at the top of a stalk.](../pictures/grain.svg)

**Reading help**

Barley is a grain. Straw is the dry stalk left after people collect the grain.

## People come to hear Solomon’s wisdom

## 1 Kings 4:29

God gave Solomon wisdom to understand things deeply.

His understanding was like the sand on a beach, too great to measure.

## 1 Kings 4:30

Solomon was wiser than all the wise men of the East.

His wisdom was greater than all the wisdom of Egypt.

## 1 Kings 4:31

Solomon was wiser than anyone else, even Ethan the Ezrahite.

He was wiser than Heman, Calcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol.

People in all the nearby nations heard about him.

## 1 Kings 4:32

Solomon composed 3,000 proverbs and 1,005 songs.

**Reading help**

A proverb is a short saying that teaches wisdom.

## 1 Kings 4:33

He taught about plants, from tall cedars in Lebanon to small hyssop plants growing in walls.

He also taught about animals, birds, reptiles, and fish.

## 1 Kings 4:34

Kings throughout the world heard about Solomon’s wisdom.

They sent people from every nation to listen to him.
