# 1 Kings 9 — The LORD answers Solomon

**About this chapter**

The LORD answers Solomon’s prayer and warns him about turning away. Later, Solomon builds cities and sends ships to bring back gold.

## The LORD hears Solomon’s prayer

## 1 Kings 9:1

Solomon finished building the LORD’s temple and his own palace.

He completed everything he had planned to do.

## 1 Kings 9:2

Then the LORD appeared to Solomon a second time.

He had appeared to Solomon before at Gibeon.

## 1 Kings 9:3

The LORD said, “I heard your prayer for help.

I have set apart this temple you built for me.

It will bear my name forever.

My eyes and my heart will always be there.

**Reading help**

The LORD speaks of his eyes and his heart. These words tell Solomon that the LORD will keep watching over the temple and caring about it.

## A promise about Solomon’s family

## 1 Kings 9:4

Live honestly before me as your father David did.

Follow all my commands and obey my laws.

## 1 Kings 9:5

If you do this, your family will rule Israel forever.

I promised your father David that one of his descendants would always sit on Israel’s throne.

## A warning about other gods

## 1 Kings 9:6

But you or your sons might turn away from me.

You might disobey my laws and commands.

You might go and serve and worship other gods.

## 1 Kings 9:7

If you do, I will remove Israel from the land I gave them.

I will send this temple away from my presence, though I set it apart for my name.

People of every nation will mock Israel.

## 1 Kings 9:8

The temple will become a pile of broken stones.

People passing by will be shocked and hiss at the sight.

They will ask, ‘Why did the LORD do this to the land and the temple?’

## 1 Kings 9:9

Others will answer, ‘The LORD brought their ancestors out of Egypt.

But the people left the LORD their God.

They worshiped and served other gods.

That is why the LORD brought this disaster on them.’”

## Hiram visits the towns in Galilee

## 1 Kings 9:10

Solomon spent 20 years building the LORD’s temple and the royal palace.

## 1 Kings 9:11

Afterward, Solomon gave King Hiram of Tyre 20 towns in Galilee.

Hiram had supplied all the cedar logs, cypress logs, and gold Solomon wanted.

![Logs tied side by side as a raft. Hiram supplied wood for Solomon’s buildings.](../pictures/hirams-timber-raft.svg)

## 1 Kings 9:12

Hiram traveled from Tyre to see the towns.

He was unhappy with what he saw.

## 1 Kings 9:13

Hiram asked, “My brother, what kind of towns have you given me?”

He called the area the Land of Cabul.

People still used that name when this account was written.

## 1 Kings 9:14

Hiram had sent Solomon 120 talents of gold.

**Reading help**

A talent was a large unit of weight. Here it tells how much the gold weighed.

## Solomon builds cities

## 1 Kings 9:15

Solomon forced people to work on his buildings.

They built the LORD’s temple, his palace, the supporting terraces, and Jerusalem’s wall.

They also worked on Hazor, Megiddo, and Gezer.

**Reading help**

Terraces are level areas built like wide steps. They help hold up the ground on a slope.

## 1 Kings 9:16

Earlier, Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, had captured Gezer.

He burned the city and killed the Canaanites living there.

He gave Gezer to his daughter as a wedding gift when she married Solomon.

## 1 Kings 9:17

Solomon rebuilt Gezer and Lower Beth-horon.

## 1 Kings 9:18

He also rebuilt Baalath and Tamar in the wilderness of Judah.

## 1 Kings 9:19

Solomon built cities to store supplies and keep chariots and horses.

He built what he wanted in Jerusalem, Lebanon, and throughout his kingdom.

## The people who did the work

## 1 Kings 9:20

Some people from other nations still lived in the land.

They were Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.

## 1 Kings 9:21

The Israelites had been unable to destroy these peoples completely.

Solomon forced their descendants to work for him.

This forced work continued when this account was written.

## 1 Kings 9:22

Solomon did not make the Israelites slaves.

They served as soldiers, servants, officers, and captains.

Some commanded the chariots and the soldiers on horseback.

## 1 Kings 9:23

Israelites also supervised Solomon’s building work.

There were 550 leaders in charge of the workers.

## The palace and the temple

## 1 Kings 9:24

Pharaoh’s daughter moved out of the City of David.

She went to live in the palace Solomon had built for her.

Then Solomon built the supporting terraces.

## 1 Kings 9:25

Three times a year Solomon gave burnt offerings and peace offerings on the altar he had built for the LORD.

He also burned incense before the LORD.

So Solomon completed the temple.

## Sailors bring gold from Ophir

## 1 Kings 9:26

Solomon gathered a group of ships at Ezion-geber.

This place was near Eloth in Edom, on the Red Sea.

## 1 Kings 9:27

Hiram sent sailors who knew the sea well.

They worked on the ships with Solomon’s men.

## 1 Kings 9:28

Together they sailed to Ophir.

They brought back 420 talents of gold and gave it to Solomon.
