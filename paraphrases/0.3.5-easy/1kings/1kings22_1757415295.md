# 1 Kings 22 — Micaiah warns Ahab

**About this chapter**

Two kings want to fight for a city. Micaiah warns Ahab about going to war. Later we hear what happened to each king’s family.

## Two kings talk about Ramoth-gilead

## 1 Kings 22:1

For three years, Aram and Israel had no war.

## 1 Kings 22:2

In the third year, King Jehoshaphat of Judah visited Ahab.

Ahab was king of Israel.

## 1 Kings 22:3

Ahab asked his servants, “Doesn’t Ramoth-gilead belong to us?

The king of Aram still holds that city.

We have done nothing to take it back.”

## 1 Kings 22:4

Ahab asked Jehoshaphat, “Will you fight with me at Ramoth-gilead?”

Jehoshaphat answered, “I will stand with you.

My people and my horses are yours too.”

## 1 Kings 22:5

Jehoshaphat added, “Please ask the LORD first.

Let us hear what the LORD says.”

Who did Jehoshaphat want to hear from first?

## Jehoshaphat asks to hear another prophet

## 1 Kings 22:6

Ahab gathered about 400 prophets.

He asked, “Should I fight for Ramoth-gilead or stay here?”

They answered, “Go.

The Lord will give you the city.”

## 1 Kings 22:7

Jehoshaphat asked, “Is there another prophet here?

Can we ask a prophet of the LORD?”

## 1 Kings 22:8

Ahab said, “We can ask the LORD through Micaiah.

He is the son of Imlah.

But I hate him.

His messages for me always bring bad news.”

Jehoshaphat said, “A king should not say that!”

## 1 Kings 22:9

Ahab called an official.

“Bring Micaiah son of Imlah here at once.”

## The prophets promise victory

## 1 Kings 22:10

The two kings wore their royal robes.

Each sat on his throne near Samaria’s city gate.

They sat where people separated grain from its husks.

All the prophets spoke in front of them.

## 1 Kings 22:11

Zedekiah son of Chenaanah had made two iron horns.

He said, “The LORD says you will strike Aram with these horns until you destroy them.”

**Reading help**

An animal can push with its horns. Zedekiah used the horns to picture the strength he said Ahab would have.

## 1 Kings 22:12

All the prophets gave the same message.

“Go to Ramoth-gilead and win!

The LORD will give the city to the king.”

## Micaiah tells the kings what he saw

## 1 Kings 22:13

The messenger who came for Micaiah said, “Listen.

Every prophet has given the king good news.

Please give him the same answer.”

## 1 Kings 22:14

Micaiah answered, “As surely as the LORD lives, I will say whatever the LORD tells me.”

## 1 Kings 22:15

When Micaiah arrived, Ahab asked him, “Should we fight for Ramoth-gilead or stay here?”

Micaiah answered, “Go and win.

The LORD will give the city to the king.”

## 1 Kings 22:16

Ahab said, “How many times must I ask you?

Promise to tell me only the truth in the LORD’s name.”

## 1 Kings 22:17

Then Micaiah said, “I saw Israel’s people across the hills.

They had scattered like sheep without a shepherd.

The LORD said, ‘These people have no leader.

Let each one go home in peace.’”

**Reading help**

A shepherd guides sheep. Micaiah saw that Israel’s people would lose their king.

## 1 Kings 22:18

Ahab turned to Jehoshaphat.

“Didn’t I tell you?

Micaiah always gives me bad news.”

## Micaiah describes the LORD on his throne

## 1 Kings 22:19

Micaiah continued, “Hear what the LORD says.

I saw the LORD sitting on his throne.

The whole army of heaven stood beside him.

Some stood on his right, others on his left.

## 1 Kings 22:20

The LORD asked, ‘Who will persuade Ahab to go to Ramoth-gilead?

Ahab will die there.’ Those standing there gave different answers.

## 1 Kings 22:21

Then a spirit came forward.

The spirit told the LORD, ‘I will persuade him.’ The LORD asked, ‘How?’

## 1 Kings 22:22

The spirit answered, ‘I will make all his prophets speak lies.’ The LORD said, ‘You will persuade him.

Go and do it.’

## 1 Kings 22:23

So, Ahab, the LORD has sent a lying spirit.

That spirit speaks through all these prophets of yours.

The LORD has said disaster will come upon you.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Micaiah say about the other prophets’ promise?

## Ahab orders Micaiah’s arrest

## 1 Kings 22:24

Zedekiah son of Chenaanah slapped Micaiah’s face.

Zedekiah asked, “Which way did the LORD’s Spirit go when he left me to speak to you?”

## 1 Kings 22:25

Micaiah answered, “One day you will hide in an inner room.

Then you will see.”

## 1 Kings 22:26

Ahab ordered, “Take Micaiah back to Amon, the city governor.

Take him to Joash, the king’s son, too.

## 1 Kings 22:27

Give them my order.

‘Put Micaiah in prison.

Give him only bread and water until I return safely.’”

## 1 Kings 22:28

Micaiah answered, “If you come back safely, the LORD has not spoken through me.” Then he said, “Listen carefully, everyone!”

## Two kings go into battle

## 1 Kings 22:29

Ahab and Jehoshaphat went to Ramoth-gilead.

## 1 Kings 22:30

Ahab told Jehoshaphat, “I will wear different clothes into battle.

You wear your royal robes.” Ahab changed his clothes and went into battle.

## 1 Kings 22:31

The king of Aram had given his 32 chariot commanders an order.

They were to fight only the king of Israel.

**Reading help**

A chariot was a cart that horses pulled.

## 1 Kings 22:32

The commanders saw Jehoshaphat.

They thought he was the king of Israel.

They turned to fight him, and Jehoshaphat cried out.

## 1 Kings 22:33

Then the commanders realized they had the wrong king.

They stopped chasing Jehoshaphat.

## Ahab dies

## 1 Kings 22:34

A man shot an arrow without aiming at Ahab.

The arrow struck Ahab through a gap in his armor.

Ahab told his driver, “Turn around!

Take me out of the battle.

I am badly hurt.”

## 1 Kings 22:35

The fighting lasted all day.

Men held Ahab upright in his chariot, facing Aram’s army.

His blood ran onto the floor of the chariot.

That evening Ahab died.

## 1 Kings 22:36

At sunset a shout went through the army.

“Everyone, go back to your city and your land!”

**Pause and answer**

Where did the shout tell the soldiers to go?

## 1 Kings 22:37

Ahab’s men took his body to Samaria.

They buried him there.

## 1 Kings 22:38

They washed Ahab’s chariot at the pool in Samaria.

Prostitutes bathed at that pool.

Dogs licked up Ahab’s blood, just as the LORD had said.

## Ahab’s son becomes king

## 1 Kings 22:39

Ahab built cities and a palace decorated with ivory.

The Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel tells about these and his other deeds.

## 1 Kings 22:40

Ahab died, as his ancestors had died.

His son Ahaziah became king in his place.

## Jehoshaphat rules Judah

## 1 Kings 22:41

Jehoshaphat son of Asa became king of Judah.

Ahab was then in his fourth year as Israel’s king.

## 1 Kings 22:42

Jehoshaphat was 35 when he became king.

He ruled in Jerusalem for 25 years.

His mother was Azubah, daughter of Shilhi.

## 1 Kings 22:43

Jehoshaphat followed the example of his father Asa.

He kept doing what the LORD said was right.

Yet the places of worship on the hills remained.

People still offered sacrifices and burned incense there.

**Reading help**

A sacrifice is a gift offered in worship. Incense gives off a smell when people burn it.

## 1 Kings 22:44

Jehoshaphat also made peace with the king of Israel.

## 1 Kings 22:45

The Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah tells more about Jehoshaphat.

It tells about his strength and the wars he fought.

## 1 Kings 22:46

Some men still practiced prostitution at places of worship.

They had been there since Asa was king.

Jehoshaphat sent those men out of the land.

## Jehoshaphat’s ships never sail

## 1 Kings 22:47

At that time Edom had no king of its own.

An official ruled there in place of a king.

## 1 Kings 22:48

Jehoshaphat built large trading ships called ships of Tarshish.

He wanted them to bring gold from Ophir.

The ships broke apart at Ezion-geber before they could sail.

## 1 Kings 22:49

Ahaziah son of Ahab said to Jehoshaphat, “Let my men sail with yours.” Jehoshaphat refused.

## 1 Kings 22:50

Jehoshaphat died.

His people buried him with his ancestors in the City of David.

His son Jehoram became king in his place.

## Ahaziah rules Israel

## 1 Kings 22:51

Ahab’s son Ahaziah became king of Israel in Jehoshaphat’s seventeenth year as king of Judah.

Ahaziah ruled in Samaria for two years.

![In Israel, Ahaziah became king after his father Ahab. In Judah, Jehoram became king after his father Jehoshaphat.](../pictures/kings-after-ahab-and-jehoshaphat.svg)

## 1 Kings 22:52

Ahaziah did what the LORD saw was evil.

He followed the example of his father and mother.

He also followed the example of Jeroboam son of Nebat.

Jeroboam had led Israel into sin.

## 1 Kings 22:53

Ahaziah served and worshiped the god Baal.

This made the LORD, Israel’s God, angry.

Ahaziah’s father had done the same thing.
