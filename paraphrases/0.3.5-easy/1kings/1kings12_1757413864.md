# 1 Kings 12 — The kingdom divides

**About this chapter**

Rehoboam is Solomon’s son. Jeroboam speaks with the people who want their work to be easier. Rehoboam must decide how to answer them.

## The people ask for easier work

## 1 Kings 12:1

Israel’s people gathered at Shechem to make Rehoboam king.

Rehoboam went there to meet them.

## 1 Kings 12:2

Jeroboam, son of Nebat, heard the news in Egypt.

He had lived there since he fled from King Solomon.

## 1 Kings 12:3

The people sent for Jeroboam.

Jeroboam and the people gathered before Rehoboam.

They said,

## 1 Kings 12:4

“Your father put a heavy yoke on us.

Make the work he demanded of us easier.

Then we will serve you.”

**Reading help**

A yoke is a wooden bar that helps animals pull a load. The people call their hard work a heavy yoke.

## 1 Kings 12:5

Rehoboam said, “Go away for three days.

Then come back to me.”

So the people left.

## Rehoboam asks for advice

## 1 Kings 12:6

Rehoboam asked the older men who had served his father Solomon, “How should I answer these people?”

## 1 Kings 12:7

They said, “Serve the people today.

Answer them with kind words.

Then they will always serve you.”

## 1 Kings 12:8

Rehoboam rejected the older men’s advice.

He turned to the young men who served him.

They had grown up with him.

## 1 Kings 12:9

He asked, “The people want me to lighten the yoke my father put on them.

How should we answer them?”

## The young men urge a harsh answer

## 1 Kings 12:10

The young men said, “They want a lighter yoke.

Tell them, ‘My little finger is thicker than my father’s waist!

## 1 Kings 12:11

My father gave you a heavy yoke.

I will make it heavier.

My father used whips. I will use scorpions.’”

**Reading help**

The young men want Rehoboam to boast that he is stronger than his father. Scorpions sting. His words threaten even more painful punishment.

## Rehoboam answers the people

## 1 Kings 12:12

On the third day Jeroboam and the people came back.

They came when Rehoboam had told them to come.

## 1 Kings 12:13

Rehoboam gave the people a harsh answer.

He refused to follow the older men’s advice.

## 1 Kings 12:14

He answered as the young men had advised.

“My father gave you a heavy yoke. I will make it heavier.

My father used whips. I will use scorpions.”

## 1 Kings 12:15

Rehoboam would not listen to the people.

The LORD had brought this about to keep his word.

Through Ahijah of Shiloh, he had given Jeroboam a promise.

![Ten pieces of Ahijah’s cloak set aside for Jeroboam.](../pictures/ahijahs-torn-cloak.svg)

**Reading help**

In chapter 11, Ahijah tore his cloak. He told Jeroboam to take ten pieces. They stood for ten tribes that Jeroboam would rule.

## Israel turns away from David’s family

## 1 Kings 12:16

The Israelites saw that Rehoboam would not listen.

They said, “What share do we have in the family of David, Jesse’s son?

People of Israel, go home!

David’s family can look after itself!”

Then they went home.

## 1 Kings 12:17

Rehoboam still ruled the Israelites in the towns of Judah.

## 1 Kings 12:18

Rehoboam sent Adoram, the man in charge of forced labor.

The Israelites threw stones at Adoram and killed him.

Rehoboam hurried into his chariot and escaped to Jerusalem.

## 1 Kings 12:19

Israel still refused to follow David’s family when this account was written.

## Israel makes Jeroboam king

## 1 Kings 12:20

The Israelites heard that Jeroboam had returned.

They called him to their gathering and made him king over Israel.

Only the tribe of Judah stayed loyal to David’s family.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did the Israelites choose as king when they left Rehoboam?

## 1 Kings 12:21

In Jerusalem, Rehoboam gathered 180,000 chosen fighters from Judah and Benjamin.

Solomon’s son Rehoboam wanted to fight Israel and get his kingdom back.

## God tells the people to go home

## 1 Kings 12:22

But God gave a message to Shemaiah, a man who spoke for God.

## 1 Kings 12:23

God told him, “Speak to Rehoboam, Solomon’s son, the king of Judah.

Speak to Judah, Benjamin, and the rest of the people too.

## 1 Kings 12:24

Tell them the LORD says, ‘Do not fight your fellow Israelites.

Go home, each of you. I have brought this about.’”

They listened to the LORD and went home.

**Pause and answer**

What did the fighters do when they heard the LORD’s message?

## Jeroboam worries about losing his kingdom

## 1 Kings 12:25

Jeroboam built up Shechem in the hills of Ephraim and lived there.

Then he went and built up Penuel.

## 1 Kings 12:26

Jeroboam thought, “David’s family might get the kingdom back.

## 1 Kings 12:27

My people may go to Jerusalem to offer sacrifices at the LORD’s temple.

They might become loyal to Rehoboam, king of Judah, again.

Then they will kill me and return to him.”

## 1 Kings 12:28

Jeroboam asked for advice. Then he made two gold calves.

He told the people, “Jerusalem is too far for you to go.

People of Israel, here are your gods who brought you out of Egypt.”

## Jeroboam sets up places of worship

## 1 Kings 12:29

Jeroboam put one calf in Bethel and the other in Dan.

## 1 Kings 12:30

This led the people into sin.

They went as far as Dan to worship before a calf.

## 1 Kings 12:31

Jeroboam also built places of worship on the hills.

He chose priests from all sorts of people outside the tribe of Levi.

## 1 Kings 12:32

Jeroboam set a feast like the one in Judah.

He chose the fifteenth day of the eighth month.

At Bethel he offered sacrifices to the calves he had made.

There he appointed priests for the places of worship on the hills.

## 1 Kings 12:33

Jeroboam himself had chosen that month for Israel’s feast.

On the fifteenth day he went to the altar he had built at Bethel.

He offered sacrifices there and burned incense.

**Pause and answer**

Why was Jeroboam afraid to let the people worship in Jerusalem?
