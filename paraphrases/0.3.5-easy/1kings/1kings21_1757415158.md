# 1 Kings 21 — Ahab takes Naboth’s vineyard

**About this chapter**

Naboth wants to keep his family’s vineyard. King Ahab wants the same land. After Naboth dies, the LORD sends Elijah to speak to Ahab.

## Naboth wants to keep his family land

## 1 Kings 21:1

Some time later, King Ahab wanted land in Jezreel.

Naboth owned a vineyard there.

It was next to Ahab’s palace.

![Grapes growing on a vine.](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

**Reading help**

A vineyard is a field where people grow grapes on vines.

## 1 Kings 21:2

Ahab said to Naboth, “Give me your vineyard.

It is close to my palace.

I want to grow vegetables there.

I can give you a better vineyard.

Or I can pay you its full price in silver.”

## 1 Kings 21:3

Naboth answered, “My family passed this land down to me.

May the LORD keep me from giving it to you!”

## 1 Kings 21:4

Ahab went home angry and upset.

Naboth had refused to give him his family land.

Ahab lay on his bed and turned his face away.

He refused to eat.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Naboth want to keep his land?

## Jezebel promises Ahab the vineyard

## 1 Kings 21:5

His wife Jezebel came in.

“Why are you so upset?” she asked.

“Why won’t you eat?”

## 1 Kings 21:6

Ahab told her, “I offered Naboth silver for his vineyard.

I offered him another vineyard in exchange.

Naboth said, ‘I will not give you my vineyard.’”

## 1 Kings 21:7

Jezebel said, “Aren’t you the king of Israel?

Get up. Eat something. Cheer up.

I will get Naboth’s vineyard for you.”

## Jezebel sends letters to the city leaders

## 1 Kings 21:8

Jezebel wrote letters in Ahab’s name.

She pressed Ahab’s royal seal onto the letters.

She sent them to the leaders in Naboth’s city.

**Reading help**

A royal seal made the king’s own mark. The mark made the letters look as if Ahab had sent them.

## 1 Kings 21:9

Jezebel wanted the leaders to call for a fast.

Naboth was to have a seat of honor.

**Reading help**

During a fast, people go without food for a time.

## 1 Kings 21:10

Jezebel wanted two men to tell a lie about Naboth.

They would say Naboth had cursed God and the king.

She wanted the people to kill Naboth with stones.

## 1 Kings 21:11

The leaders in Naboth’s city followed Jezebel’s orders.

## 1 Kings 21:12

They called for a fast.

They gave Naboth a seat of honor.

## 1 Kings 21:13

Two men sat across from Naboth.

In front of everyone, they told this lie.

“Naboth has cursed God and the king!”

The people took Naboth outside the city.

There they threw stones at him and killed him.

## 1 Kings 21:14

The leaders sent Jezebel a message.

They told her that the people had killed Naboth with stones.

## Ahab goes to the vineyard

## 1 Kings 21:15

Jezebel heard the news and told Ahab, “Get up.

Naboth refused to sell you his vineyard.

Now Naboth is dead. Go and take his vineyard.”

## 1 Kings 21:16

When Ahab heard that Naboth had died, he got up.

He went down to take Naboth’s vineyard.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Ahab go when he heard Naboth had died?

## The LORD sends Elijah to meet Ahab

## 1 Kings 21:17

Then the LORD spoke to Elijah, who came from Tishbe.

## 1 Kings 21:18

“Go and meet Ahab, Israel’s king, who rules in Samaria.

Ahab has gone to take Naboth’s vineyard.

You will find him there.

## 1 Kings 21:19

Give Ahab my message. ‘You murdered a man.

Now you have taken his land!’

Tell him, ‘Dogs licked up Naboth’s blood.

In that same place, dogs will lick up yours.’”

## 1 Kings 21:20

Ahab saw Elijah and said, “My enemy! You found me!”

Elijah answered, “Yes, I found you.

You have given yourself completely to doing evil.

The LORD has seen it.

## Elijah says what will happen to Ahab’s family

## 1 Kings 21:21

The LORD says, ‘I will bring disaster on you.

I will wipe out your family line.

Every male in your family in Israel will die.

This includes slaves and free men alike.

## 1 Kings 21:22

Your family will end like the family of Jeroboam, Nebat’s son.

It will end like the family of Baasha, Ahijah’s son.

You have made me angry.

You have led Israel into sin.’

**Reading help**

Jeroboam and Baasha had each been king of Israel. Neither family kept the throne.

## 1 Kings 21:23

The LORD also says, ‘Dogs will eat Jezebel’s body beside the wall of Jezreel.’

## 1 Kings 21:24

Dogs will eat the bodies of Ahab’s people who die in the city.

Birds will eat the bodies of those who die in the fields.”

## 1 Kings 21:25

No one else gave himself to evil as Ahab did.

The LORD saw it all.

Ahab’s wife Jezebel urged him on.

## 1 Kings 21:26

Ahab worshiped idols. The LORD hated what Ahab did.

The Amorites had worshiped idols too.

The LORD had driven them out before the Israelites.

## Ahab humbles himself before the LORD

## 1 Kings 21:27

When Ahab heard Elijah’s words, he tore his clothes.

He put on rough cloth and went without food.

He even lay down in the rough cloth.

He walked about quietly and humbly.

**Reading help**

The rough cloth was called sackcloth. Ahab wore it to show his sorrow.

## 1 Kings 21:28

Then the LORD spoke to Elijah again.

## 1 Kings 21:29

“Have you seen how Ahab has humbled himself before me?

Because of this, I will delay the disaster.

It will come on his family after Ahab dies, when his son is king.”

**Pause and answer**

When did the LORD say the disaster would come?
