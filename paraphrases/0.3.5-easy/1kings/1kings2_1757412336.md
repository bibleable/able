# 1 Kings 2 — David’s last instructions to Solomon

**About this chapter**

David tells Solomon how to live and rule. After David dies, Solomon must decide what to do about the people who supported his brother Adonijah.

## David tells Solomon to follow the LORD

## 1 Kings 2:1

David knew he would soon die.

He gave his son Solomon these instructions.

## 1 Kings 2:2

“Soon I will die, as everyone does.

Be strong. Show that you are ready to be a man.

## 1 Kings 2:3

Do what the LORD your God asks of you.

Follow his ways. Obey all his laws and commands.

You can read them in the Law of Moses.

Then you will do well wherever you go.

![A scroll unrolled to show its writing.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

**Reading help**

The Law of Moses is the teaching God gave Israel through Moses. People wrote these laws on scrolls.

## 1 Kings 2:4

Then the LORD will keep his promise to me.

He said, ‘Your descendants must follow me faithfully with their whole heart and soul.

If they do, someone from your family will always rule Israel.’

## David remembers Joab, Barzillai’s sons, and Shimei

## 1 Kings 2:5

You know what Joab, Zeruiah’s son, did to me.

He killed two army commanders, Abner and Amasa.

Abner was Ner’s son. Amasa was Jether’s son.

Joab took revenge for wartime deaths after peace had come.

Their blood stained his belt and sandals.

## 1 Kings 2:6

Use your wisdom in dealing with Joab.

Do not let this old man die in peace.

## 1 Kings 2:7

Show lasting kindness to the sons of Barzillai from Gilead.

Let them eat at your table.

They helped me when I fled from your brother Absalom.

**Pause and answer**

Why did David want Solomon to welcome Barzillai’s sons?

## 1 Kings 2:8

Remember Shimei, Gera’s son from Bahurim in Benjamin.

He shouted bitter curses at me as I went to Mahanaim.

Later he met me at the Jordan River.

There I promised by the LORD that I would never kill him.

## 1 Kings 2:9

But you must hold Shimei responsible.

You are wise. You know what to do.

See that this old man dies a violent death.”

## David dies after 40 years as king

## 1 Kings 2:10

Then David died.

The people buried him in the City of David.

## 1 Kings 2:11

David ruled Israel for 40 years.

He ruled seven years in Hebron and 33 in Jerusalem.

## 1 Kings 2:12

Solomon sat on his father David’s throne.

He now ruled with a firm hold on the kingdom.

## Adonijah asks Bathsheba for help

## 1 Kings 2:13

Adonijah, Haggith’s son, went to Bathsheba, Solomon’s mother.

She asked, “Do you come in peace?”

He said, “Yes, in peace.”

## 1 Kings 2:14

Adonijah said, “I have something to tell you.”

Bathsheba answered, “Go ahead.”

## 1 Kings 2:15

He said, “You know the kingdom was mine.

All Israel expected me to be king.

But the LORD gave the kingdom to my brother instead.

## 1 Kings 2:16

Now I have one request. Please do not refuse me.”

Bathsheba said, “Tell me what you want.”

## 1 Kings 2:17

Adonijah said, “Please speak to King Solomon.

He will listen to you.

Ask him to let me marry Abishag from Shunem.”

## 1 Kings 2:18

Bathsheba answered, “Very well. I will speak to the king for you.”

## Bathsheba brings the request to Solomon

## 1 Kings 2:19

Bathsheba went to Solomon to speak for Adonijah.

Solomon stood up and bowed to his mother.

Then he sat on his throne.

He had another throne brought for her.

Bathsheba sat on his right.

## 1 Kings 2:20

She said, “I have one small request.

Please do not refuse me.”

Solomon answered, “Ask, Mother. I will not refuse you.”

## 1 Kings 2:21

Bathsheba said, “Let your brother Adonijah marry Abishag from Shunem.”

## 1 Kings 2:22

Solomon answered, “Why ask for Abishag for Adonijah?

He is my older brother.

You might as well ask for the kingdom for him, for Abiathar the priest, and for Joab!”

**Reading help**

Abishag cared for David in his old age. Solomon hears his older brother’s request as another claim to David’s throne.

## Solomon gives orders about Adonijah and Abiathar

## 1 Kings 2:23

Solomon made a solemn promise by the LORD.

“May God punish me severely if Adonijah does not pay for this request with his life.

## 1 Kings 2:24

The LORD lives! He made me king in my father’s place.

He established my family as rulers, just as he promised.

As surely as the LORD lives, Adonijah will die today.”

## 1 Kings 2:25

Solomon gave the order to Benaiah, Jehoiada’s son.

Benaiah killed Adonijah.

## 1 Kings 2:26

Solomon told Abiathar the priest, “Return to your fields in Anathoth.

You deserve to die, but I will spare you today.

You carried the sacred chest of the Lord GOD before my father David.

You shared all his hardships.”

## 1 Kings 2:27

Solomon removed Abiathar from his work as the LORD’s priest.

This fulfilled what the LORD had said at Shiloh about Eli’s family.

## Joab goes to the LORD’s tent

## 1 Kings 2:28

Joab heard the news. He had supported Adonijah.

He had never joined Absalom.

Joab ran to the LORD’s tent.

There he held on to the horns of the altar.

**Reading help**

The altar was a place for offerings to God. Its corners had points shaped like horns. Joab held on to them because he wanted protection.

## 1 Kings 2:29

Solomon heard that Joab was beside the altar in the LORD’s tent.

He sent Benaiah, Jehoiada’s son, to put Joab to death.

## 1 Kings 2:30

Benaiah went into the tent.

He said, “The king tells you to come out.”

Joab answered, “No. I will die here.”

Benaiah went back and told Solomon what Joab had said.

## Solomon names the men Joab killed

## 1 Kings 2:31

Solomon said, “Do as Joab says. Kill him there and bury him.

Joab killed innocent men.

My father’s family and I must be free from blame for their deaths.

## 1 Kings 2:32

The LORD will hold Joab responsible for killing them.

My father David knew nothing about it.

Both men were better and more just than Joab.

Abner, Ner’s son, led Israel’s army.

Amasa, Jether’s son, led Judah’s army.

## 1 Kings 2:33

Joab and his descendants will always bear the blame for those deaths.

May the LORD give David’s family and his kingdom peace forever.”

## 1 Kings 2:34

Benaiah, Jehoiada’s son, went and killed Joab.

Joab was buried at his home in the wilderness.

## 1 Kings 2:35

Solomon chose Benaiah to lead the army in Joab’s place.

He chose Zadok to serve as priest in Abiathar’s place.

**Pause and answer**

Who took Joab’s place as leader of the army?

## Shimei promises to stay in Jerusalem

## 1 Kings 2:36

Solomon called Shimei to him.

“Build yourself a house in Jerusalem and live there.

Stay in the city.

## 1 Kings 2:37

If you leave and cross the Kidron Valley, you will die.

You will be responsible for your own death.”

## 1 Kings 2:38

Shimei answered, “That is fair. I will do as you say, my king.”

Shimei lived in Jerusalem for a long time.

## Shimei leaves for Gath

## 1 Kings 2:39

Three years later, two people Shimei held as slaves ran away.

They went to Achish, king of Gath, the son of Maacah.

Someone told Shimei, “They are in Gath.”

## 1 Kings 2:40

Shimei saddled his donkey and went to see Achish.

He found the two in Gath and brought them back.

## 1 Kings 2:41

Solomon heard that Shimei had left Jerusalem for Gath and returned.

## Solomon calls Shimei back

## 1 Kings 2:42

Solomon called Shimei to him.

“I made you promise by the LORD to stay here.

I warned you that you would die if you left.

You said that was fair and agreed to obey.

## 1 Kings 2:43

Why did you break your promise to the LORD?

Why did you disobey my order?”

## 1 Kings 2:44

Solomon continued, “You know how you harmed my father David.

The LORD will make you answer for what you did.

## 1 Kings 2:45

But the LORD will bless King Solomon.

David’s throne will stand firm before the LORD forever.”

## 1 Kings 2:46

Solomon gave Benaiah, Jehoiada’s son, the order.

Benaiah went out and killed Shimei.

Solomon now held the kingdom firmly in his hands.

**Reading help**

Holding the kingdom in his hands means that Solomon had firm control as king.

**Pause and answer**

What promise had Shimei made to Solomon?
