# 1 Kings 17 — Elijah and the widow of Zarephath

**About this chapter**

When there is no rain, Elijah gets food beside a stream and then in a widow’s home. Later the widow’s son becomes sick, and Elijah calls to the LORD.

## Elijah tells Ahab there will be no rain

## 1 Kings 17:1

Elijah the Tishbite lived in Gilead.

He told King Ahab, “I serve the LORD, the living God of Israel.

As surely as he lives, no dew or rain will fall in the coming years until I say so.”

**Reading help**

Dew is the tiny drops of water you can see on the ground in the morning.

## 1 Kings 17:2

Then the LORD spoke to Elijah.

## Ravens bring food by the stream

## 1 Kings 17:3

“Leave this place and go east.

Hide by the stream called Cherith, east of the Jordan River.

## 1 Kings 17:4

Drink water from the stream.

I have told the ravens to bring you food there.”

## 1 Kings 17:5

Elijah did what the LORD said.

He lived beside the stream called Cherith, east of the Jordan.

## 1 Kings 17:6

Every morning and evening the ravens brought Elijah bread and meat.

He drank water from the stream.

**Reading help**

Ravens are large black birds.

**Pause and answer**

What did the ravens bring Elijah?

## 1 Kings 17:7

After a while the stream dried up.

No rain had fallen on the land.

## The LORD sends Elijah to Zarephath

## 1 Kings 17:8

Then the LORD spoke to Elijah again.

## 1 Kings 17:9

“Go to Zarephath in the land of Sidon.

Stay there. I have told a widow there to feed you.”

## 1 Kings 17:10

Elijah went to Zarephath.

At the city gate he saw a widow gathering sticks.

He called to her, “Please bring me a little water in a cup.”

**Reading help**

A widow is a woman whose husband has died.

## 1 Kings 17:11

As she went to get the water, Elijah called after her.

“Please bring me a piece of bread too.”

## The widow has little food left

## 1 Kings 17:12

The widow answered, “As surely as the LORD your God lives, I have no bread.

I have one handful of flour in a jar.

I have a little oil in a jug.

I am gathering sticks to cook a meal for my son and me.

We will eat it. Then we will die.”

![The widow has a handful of flour in a jar and a little oil in a jug.](../pictures/widows-flour-and-oil.svg)

## 1 Kings 17:13

Elijah said, “Do not be afraid. Go and make the meal.

First make me a small loaf and bring it to me.

Then make some bread for yourself and your son.

## 1 Kings 17:14

The LORD, the God of Israel, says, ‘Your flour will not run out.

There will still be oil in your jug.

You will have both until the day the LORD sends rain on the land.’”

## There is food every day

## 1 Kings 17:15

The widow did what Elijah said.

Every day there was food for Elijah, the widow, and her family.

## 1 Kings 17:16

The flour in the jar did not run out.

The jug still had oil.

The LORD kept the promise he had made through Elijah.

## The widow’s son dies

## 1 Kings 17:17

Later, the widow’s son became sick.

He grew worse until he stopped breathing and died.

## 1 Kings 17:18

The widow asked Elijah, “Man of God, what have you done to me?

Did you come to bring up my sins and cause my son to die?”

## 1 Kings 17:19

Elijah said, “Give me your son.”

He took the boy from his mother’s arms.

He carried him upstairs to the room where he was staying.

Elijah laid the boy on his own bed.

## Elijah asks the LORD for the boy’s life

## 1 Kings 17:20

Elijah cried out, “LORD my God, this widow welcomed me into her home.

Have you brought this grief on her by causing her son to die?”

## 1 Kings 17:21

Elijah stretched himself out over the boy three times.

He cried out, “LORD my God, please give this boy his life back!”

## 1 Kings 17:22

The LORD listened to Elijah.

The boy came back to life.

## 1 Kings 17:23

Elijah carried the boy downstairs and gave him to his mother.

He said, “Look, your son is alive!”

## 1 Kings 17:24

The widow told Elijah, “Now I know you are a man of God.

The words the LORD gives you to speak are true.”
