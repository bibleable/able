# 1 Kings 19 — Elijah hears a quiet voice

**About this chapter**

Elijah runs away after Jezebel threatens him. He travels to a mountain, where the LORD speaks to him. Later Elijah meets Elisha in a field.

## Elijah flees from Jezebel

## 1 Kings 19:1

Ahab told Jezebel everything Elijah had done.

He told her that Elijah had killed all the prophets of Baal with a sword.

## 1 Kings 19:2

Jezebel sent Elijah a message.

“By this time tomorrow, you will be dead like those prophets.

May the gods punish me severely if I fail!”

## 1 Kings 19:3

Elijah was afraid and ran for his life.

He reached Beersheba in Judah and left his servant there.

## 1 Kings 19:4

Elijah walked for a day into the wilderness.

He sat under a broom tree and prayed to die.

He said, “LORD, I have had enough.

Take my life. I am no better than my ancestors.”

## An angel brings food and water

## 1 Kings 19:5

Elijah lay down under the broom tree and fell asleep.

An angel touched him and said, “Get up and eat.”

## 1 Kings 19:6

Elijah looked around.

Beside his head lay bread baked on hot coals and a jar of water.

He ate, drank, and lay down again.

## 1 Kings 19:7

The angel of the LORD came back and touched Elijah again.

“Get up and eat.

Otherwise the journey will be too much for you.”

## 1 Kings 19:8

Elijah got up and ate and drank.

The food gave him strength to walk for 40 days and 40 nights.

He reached Horeb, the mountain of God.

## The LORD asks Elijah a question

## 1 Kings 19:9

Elijah spent the night in a cave.

The LORD asked him, “What are you doing here, Elijah?”

## 1 Kings 19:10

Elijah answered, “I have served you with all my heart, LORD, God who commands armies.

The Israelites have broken their covenant with you.

They have torn down your altars.

They have killed your prophets with swords.

I am the only one left.

Now they want to kill me too.”

**Reading help**

A covenant is a solemn agreement. Elijah says the people have broken their agreement with the LORD.

## Elijah hears a quiet voice

## 1 Kings 19:11

The LORD said, “Go out and stand before me on the mountain.

I am about to pass by.”

A powerful wind tore into the mountains and broke rocks apart.

The LORD was not in the wind.

Then the ground shook in an earthquake.

The LORD was not in the earthquake.

## 1 Kings 19:12

After the earthquake came a fire.

The LORD was not in the fire.

After the fire came a quiet, gentle voice.

## 1 Kings 19:13

When Elijah heard it, he covered his face with his cloak.

He went out and stood at the entrance to the cave.

A voice asked, “What are you doing here, Elijah?”

## 1 Kings 19:14

Elijah answered, “I have served you with all my heart, LORD, God who commands armies.

The Israelites have broken their covenant with you.

They have torn down your altars.

They have killed your prophets with swords.

I am the only one left.

Now they want to kill me too.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Elijah hear after the fire?

## The LORD tells Elijah where to go

## 1 Kings 19:15

The LORD told Elijah, “Go back the way you came.

Go to the desert near Damascus.

When you arrive, anoint Hazael as king of Aram.

## 1 Kings 19:16

Anoint Jehu, son of Nimshi, as king of Israel.

Also anoint Elisha, son of Shaphat, from Abel-meholah.

Elisha will take your place as prophet.

**Reading help**

To anoint someone means to pour oil on them. Here it shows that God has chosen the person for a particular job.

## 1 Kings 19:17

Jehu will kill those who escape Hazael’s sword.

Elisha will kill those who escape Jehu’s sword.

## 1 Kings 19:18

Yet I have kept 7,000 people in Israel for myself.

They have never bowed down to Baal or kissed him.”

## Elisha leaves the field to follow Elijah

## 1 Kings 19:19

Elijah left and found Elisha, son of Shaphat, plowing a field.

Twelve teams of oxen were plowing.

Elisha worked with the twelfth team.

Elijah walked past and threw his cloak around Elisha.

![A cloak, an outer garment worn over other clothes.](../pictures/cloak.svg)

**Reading help**

Elijah puts his own cloak around Elisha. This shows that he is calling Elisha to follow him and become a prophet.

## 1 Kings 19:20

Elisha left the oxen and ran after Elijah.

“Please let me kiss my parents goodbye. Then I will follow you.”

Elijah answered, “Go back. What have I done to you?”

## 1 Kings 19:21

Elisha went back and killed his two oxen.

He cooked the meat over a fire made with their wooden equipment.

He gave the meat to the people, and they ate.

Then Elisha left to follow Elijah and serve him.
