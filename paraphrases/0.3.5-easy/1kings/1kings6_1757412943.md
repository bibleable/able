# 1 Kings 6 — Solomon builds the temple

**About this chapter**

Solomon builds a temple for the LORD. Inside, a long hall leads to a smaller room called the Most Holy Place.

## Solomon begins building

## 1 Kings 6:1

Solomon began building the LORD’s temple in his fourth year as king.

It was the 480th year since Israel had left Egypt.

The work began in Ziv, the second month.

## 1 Kings 6:2

The temple was 60 cubits long and 20 cubits wide.

It was 30 cubits high.

**Reading help**

A cubit was a measure of length, about the distance from an elbow to a fingertip.

## 1 Kings 6:3

A porch stood in front of the main hall.

It stretched 20 cubits across the front of the temple.

It reached out 10 cubits from the building.

## 1 Kings 6:4

Solomon had workers put narrow windows in frames high in the walls.

## Rooms along the outside walls

## 1 Kings 6:5

They built side rooms against the outside walls of the temple.

These rooms ran alongside the main hall and the inner room.

## 1 Kings 6:6

The bottom floor of side rooms was five cubits wide.

The middle floor was six cubits wide.

The top floor was seven cubits wide.

The floors rested on ledges along the outside walls.

The builders did not have to cut into the temple walls.

## 1 Kings 6:7

Workers shaped the stones where they dug them from the rock.

They brought the finished stones to the temple.

No one heard hammers, chisels, or other iron tools at the building site.

## Stairs and a cedar roof

## 1 Kings 6:8

The entrance to the bottom floor was on the south side.

Stairs led up to the middle floor, then to the top.

## 1 Kings 6:9

Solomon finished the temple building.

The workers covered it with cedar roof beams and boards.

## 1 Kings 6:10

The side rooms along the temple were each five cubits high.

Cedar beams joined them to the temple.

## The LORD speaks to Solomon

## 1 Kings 6:11

The LORD gave Solomon this message.

## 1 Kings 6:12

“You are building this temple.

Follow my laws and carry out all my commands.

Then through you I will keep my promise to your father David.

## 1 Kings 6:13

I will live among the people of Israel.

I will never leave my people.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the LORD ask Solomon to do?

## 1 Kings 6:14

Solomon built the temple and finished it.

## Two rooms inside the temple

## 1 Kings 6:15

Workers covered the inside walls with cedar boards from floor to ceiling.

They laid cypress boards on the floor.

## 1 Kings 6:16

They set apart the back 20 cubits for an inner room.

A wall of cedar boards reached from floor to ceiling.

This inner room was the Most Holy Place.

## 1 Kings 6:17

The main hall in front of it was 40 cubits long.

![Looking down from above, the main hall is 40 cubits long. Beyond it, the Most Holy Place is 20 cubits long.](../pictures/solomons-temple-rooms.svg)

## 1 Kings 6:18

Workers carved gourds and open flowers into the cedar boards.

Cedar covered all the stone inside.

**Reading help**

Gourds are fruits that grow on vines. The workers carved their shapes into the wood.

## Gold in the inner room

## 1 Kings 6:19

Solomon prepared the inner room for the ark of the LORD’s covenant.

**Reading help**

The ark was a sacred chest. It reminded Israel of the covenant, the solemn agreement God made with them.

## 1 Kings 6:20

The inner room was 20 cubits long, wide, and high.

Workers covered its inside walls with pure gold.

They covered the cedar altar with gold too.

## 1 Kings 6:21

Solomon had workers cover the inside of the temple with pure gold.

They stretched gold chains across the front of the gold-covered inner room.

## 1 Kings 6:22

They finished covering the whole inside of the temple with gold.

Gold also covered the entire altar belonging to the inner room.

## Two figures with wings

## 1 Kings 6:23

Workers made two figures called cherubim for the inner room.

They carved them from olive wood.

Each figure stood 10 cubits high.

**Reading help**

Cherubim are heavenly beings. These wooden figures showed them with wings.

## 1 Kings 6:24

Each wing of the first figure was five cubits long.

It measured 10 cubits from wingtip to wingtip.

## 1 Kings 6:25

The second figure also measured 10 cubits from wingtip to wingtip.

Both figures had the same size and shape.

## 1 Kings 6:26

Each figure stood 10 cubits tall.

## The wings meet in the middle

## 1 Kings 6:27

They placed the figures in the inner room with their wings spread.

One figure’s wing touched one wall.

The other figure’s wing touched the opposite wall.

Their other wingtips met in the middle.

## 1 Kings 6:28

Workers covered both figures with gold.

**Pause and answer**

## 1 Kings 6:29

Workers carved pictures on the walls of both rooms.

They carved cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers.

## 1 Kings 6:30

They covered the floors of both rooms with gold.

## Doors for the inner room

## 1 Kings 6:31

Solomon had workers make olive wood doors for the inner room.

The posts on each side of this doorway had five sides.

## 1 Kings 6:32

Workers carved cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers into the two doors.

They hammered gold over the cherubim and palm trees.

## Folding doors for the main hall

## 1 Kings 6:33

For the main hall entrance, they made olive wood doorposts.

These posts had four sides.

## 1 Kings 6:34

They made two doors from cypress wood.

Each door had two panels that folded.

## 1 Kings 6:35

They carved cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers into these doors too.

They hammered gold evenly over the carvings.

## The courtyard and the finished temple

## 1 Kings 6:36

Solomon built the inner courtyard with three rows of cut stone and one row of shaped cedar beams.

## 1 Kings 6:37

Workers laid the base of the LORD’s temple in Ziv.

That was the fourth year of Solomon’s rule.

## 1 Kings 6:38

They finished in Bul, the eighth month of Solomon’s eleventh year.

They had followed the plans down to the last detail.

Building the temple took seven years.
