# 1 Kings 1 — Solomon becomes king

**About this chapter**

David is old. His son Adonijah announces that he will be king. Bathsheba reminds David of his promise to her son Solomon.

## Abishag cares for David

## 1 Kings 1:1

King David was very old.

Even under blankets, he could not get warm.

## 1 Kings 1:2

His servants said, “Let us find a young woman who has never had sex.

She can look after you.

She can lie beside you to keep you warm.”

## 1 Kings 1:3

They searched throughout Israel for a beautiful young woman.

They found Abishag from Shunem and brought her to David.

## 1 Kings 1:4

Abishag was very beautiful.

She served David and cared for him.

David did not have sex with her.

## Adonijah says he will be king

## 1 Kings 1:5

Adonijah was the son of David and Haggith.

He began boasting, “I will be king!”

He got chariots and men on horses.

Fifty men ran ahead of him.

## 1 Kings 1:6

David had never corrected Adonijah or questioned his behavior.

Adonijah was very handsome.

He was David’s next son after Absalom.

## 1 Kings 1:7

Adonijah spoke with Joab, Zeruiah’s son, and Abiathar the priest.

They agreed to support him.

## 1 Kings 1:8

Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet did not join him.

Neither did Benaiah, Jehoiada’s son.

Shimei, Rei, and David’s strongest fighters stayed away too.

## Adonijah holds a feast

## 1 Kings 1:9

Adonijah offered sheep, oxen, and fattened calves as sacrifices.

He did this by the stone of Zoheleth near En-rogel.

He invited his brothers and the king’s servants from Judah.

## 1 Kings 1:10

But Adonijah left his brother Solomon out.

He also left out Nathan, Benaiah, and David’s strongest fighters.

**Reading help**

A priest leads people in worship. A prophet brings a message from God.

**Pause and answer**

Which brother did Adonijah leave out of the feast?

## Nathan speaks to Bathsheba

## 1 Kings 1:11

Nathan spoke to Bathsheba, Solomon’s mother.

“Have you heard? Haggith’s son Adonijah has made himself king.

King David knows nothing about it.

## 1 Kings 1:12

Please listen to my advice.

You can save your life and Solomon’s life.

## 1 Kings 1:13

Go to David now. Remind him of his promise.

He promised that your son Solomon would sit on his throne and rule after him.

Ask him why Adonijah has become king.

## 1 Kings 1:14

While you are still speaking, I will come in.

I will tell David that what you say is true.”

## Bathsheba reminds David of his promise

## 1 Kings 1:15

Bathsheba went to David’s bedroom.

Abishag from Shunem was caring for the old king.

## 1 Kings 1:16

Bathsheba bowed low before David.

He asked, “What would you like?”

## 1 Kings 1:17

She said, “My king, you made me a promise by the LORD your God.

You promised that my son Solomon would rule after you.

You said he would sit on your throne.

## 1 Kings 1:18

Now Adonijah has made himself king.

My king, you did not know about it.

## 1 Kings 1:19

He offered many oxen, fattened calves, and sheep.

He invited your other sons, Abiathar the priest, and Joab, who leads the army.

He did not invite Solomon.

## 1 Kings 1:20

My king, everyone in Israel is looking to you.

Tell them who will sit on your throne after you.

## 1 Kings 1:21

Otherwise, when you die, people will treat Solomon and me as criminals.”

**Reading help**

A throne is a king’s special seat. To sit on David’s throne means to rule after him.

## Nathan asks David who will rule

## 1 Kings 1:22

While Bathsheba was still speaking to David, Nathan arrived.

## 1 Kings 1:23

Someone told David, “Nathan the prophet is here.”

Nathan came in and bowed with his face to the ground.

## 1 Kings 1:24

Nathan asked, “My king, did you say Adonijah would rule after you?

Did you say he would sit on your throne?

## 1 Kings 1:25

Today he offered many oxen, fattened calves, and sheep.

He invited your sons, your army commanders, and Abiathar the priest.

They are eating and drinking with him.

They are shouting, ‘Long live King Adonijah!’

## 1 Kings 1:26

He did not invite me or Zadok the priest.

He left out Benaiah, Jehoiada’s son, and Solomon too.

## 1 Kings 1:27

My king, did you arrange this?

Why have I heard nothing from you about who will rule next?”

## David promises Solomon will be king

## 1 Kings 1:28

David said, “Call Bathsheba back.”

Bathsheba came and stood before him.

## 1 Kings 1:29

David made a solemn promise.

“As surely as the LORD lives, he has rescued me from every trouble.

## 1 Kings 1:30

Today I will keep my promise to you.

I swore by the LORD, the God of Israel.

Your son Solomon will sit on my throne.

He will be king after me.”

## 1 Kings 1:31

Bathsheba bowed with her face to the ground.

She said, “May my lord King David live forever!”

## David sends Solomon to Gihon

## 1 Kings 1:32

David called for Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah, Jehoiada’s son.

They came to see him.

## 1 Kings 1:33

David told them, “Take my servants with you.

Have Solomon ride my own mule down to Gihon.

## 1 Kings 1:34

There Zadok and Nathan must anoint Solomon king over Israel.

Blow the ram’s horn and shout, ‘Long live King Solomon!’

**Reading help**

To anoint someone means to pour oil on them. Here the oil shows the people that Solomon is their new king.

## 1 Kings 1:35

Come back with Solomon. He must sit on my throne.

I have chosen him to rule Israel and Judah in my place.”

## 1 Kings 1:36

Benaiah answered, “Amen! May the LORD your God say so too.

## 1 Kings 1:37

May the LORD be with Solomon as he was with you.

May he make Solomon’s kingdom even greater than yours, King David.”

## The people cheer for Solomon

## 1 Kings 1:38

Zadok, Nathan, and Benaiah went with the Cherethites and Pelethites.

They helped Solomon onto David’s mule and took him to Gihon.

## 1 Kings 1:39

Zadok took a horn filled with oil from the sacred tent.

He poured the oil on Solomon.

Then they blew the ram’s horn.

Everyone shouted, “Long live King Solomon!”

## 1 Kings 1:40

Everyone followed Solomon, playing flutes and celebrating.

Their shouts were so loud it seemed the ground would split.

## The guests hear the horn

## 1 Kings 1:41

Adonijah and his guests were finishing their feast.

They heard the ram’s horn.

Joab asked, “Why is the city so noisy?”

## 1 Kings 1:42

While Joab spoke, Jonathan arrived.

He was the son of Abiathar the priest.

Adonijah said, “Come in! You are a brave man.

You must have good news.”

## 1 Kings 1:43

Jonathan answered, “No. King David has made Solomon king.

## 1 Kings 1:44

David sent Zadok, Nathan, and Benaiah with Solomon.

The Cherethites and Pelethites went too.

They had Solomon ride David’s mule.

## Jonathan explains the noise

## 1 Kings 1:45

Zadok and Nathan anointed Solomon king at Gihon.

The people came back celebrating.

That is the noise you hear in the city.

## 1 Kings 1:46

Solomon is already sitting on the throne.

## 1 Kings 1:47

David’s servants came to congratulate him.

They said, ‘May God make Solomon even more famous than you.

May his kingdom be even greater than yours.’

David bowed in worship on his bed.

## 1 Kings 1:48

He said, ‘Praise the LORD, the God of Israel!

Today he gave me someone to sit on my throne.

I have seen it with my own eyes.’”

## Adonijah asks Solomon to spare him

## 1 Kings 1:49

Adonijah’s guests were terrified.

They got up and scattered.

## 1 Kings 1:50

Adonijah was afraid of Solomon.

He went to the altar and held on to its horns.

**Reading help**

An altar is a place for offerings to God. Its corners had points shaped like horns. Adonijah held these as he asked for protection.

## 1 Kings 1:51

Someone told Solomon, “Adonijah is afraid of you.

He is holding on to the horns of the altar.

He wants you to promise first that you will spare his life.”

## 1 Kings 1:52

Solomon answered, “If he does what is right, no one will harm a hair on his head.

But if he does evil, he will die.”

## 1 Kings 1:53

Solomon sent for Adonijah, and he left the altar.

Adonijah came and bowed before King Solomon.

Solomon told him, “Go home.”
