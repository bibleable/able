# 1 Kings 20 — Ahab lets Ben-hadad go

**About this chapter**

Two kings go to war. The LORD sends Ahab a promise before the fighting. Later a prophet tells Ahab a story about a prisoner.

## Ben-hadad makes demands

## 1 Kings 20:1

Ben-hadad was king of Aram. He gathered his whole army.

Thirty-two kings joined him with horses and chariots. They surrounded Samaria and attacked the city.

## 1 Kings 20:2

Ben-hadad sent messengers into Samaria to speak to Ahab, the king of Israel.

## 1 Kings 20:3

They gave Ahab this message from Ben-hadad.

“Your silver and gold are mine. I claim the best of your wives and children too.”

## 1 Kings 20:4

Ahab answered, “My lord the king, I agree. I belong to you, along with everything I own.”

## 1 Kings 20:5

The messengers came back. Ben-hadad had more to say.

“I have already asked for your silver, your gold, your wives, and your children.

## 1 Kings 20:6

About this time tomorrow, my servants will search your palace and your servants’ houses. They will carry off everything you value.”

## 1 Kings 20:7

Ahab called the leaders of Israel together.

“Look how this man is making trouble,” he said. “I already agreed when he asked for my wives, my children, my silver, and my gold.”

## 1 Kings 20:8

The leaders and the people all answered, “Do not agree to this.”

## 1 Kings 20:9

Ahab told the messengers, “Tell the king I will do what he first asked. I cannot agree to this new demand.”

They took his answer back to Ben-hadad.

## 1 Kings 20:10

Ben-hadad sent back a threat.

“May the gods punish me severely if I leave enough dust in Samaria for each of my men to pick up one handful!”

## 1 Kings 20:11

Ahab answered with a saying.

“A man putting on his armor should wait until he takes it off before he boasts.”

**Reading help**

Ahab means that Ben-hadad is talking as if he has already won.

## 1 Kings 20:12

Ben-hadad heard the answer while he was drinking with the other kings in their tents. At his command, his men took their places outside the city.

## A prophet gives Ahab a promise

## 1 Kings 20:13

A prophet came to Ahab with a message from the LORD.

“Do you see that huge army? Today I will give you victory over it. Then you will know that I am the LORD.”

## 1 Kings 20:14

“Who will do this?” Ahab asked.

“The LORD says the young officers who serve the district leaders will do it,” the prophet said.

“Who will start the battle?” asked Ahab.

“You will,” said the prophet.

## 1 Kings 20:15

Ahab gathered the 232 young officers. Then he gathered the other 7,000 soldiers of Israel.

## 1 Kings 20:16

They went out at noon. Ben-hadad and the 32 kings with him were in their tents getting drunk.

## 1 Kings 20:17

The young officers went first. Men watching for Ben-hadad told him, “People are coming out of Samaria.”

## 1 Kings 20:18

Ben-hadad said, “Whatever they have come for, peace or war, capture them alive.”

## 1 Kings 20:19

The young officers left the city. Israel’s army followed them.

## 1 Kings 20:20

Each officer struck down the man facing him. The soldiers of Aram ran away, and the Israelites chased them.

Ben-hadad escaped on horseback with his horsemen.

## 1 Kings 20:21

Ahab went out and attacked the horses and chariots. Many men from Aram died.

## 1 Kings 20:22

Later the prophet warned Ahab, “Get ready. Think carefully about what you need to do. The king of Aram will attack again in the spring.”

## The armies meet again at Aphek

## 1 Kings 20:23

Ben-hadad’s servants told him, “The gods of Israel are gods of the hills. That must be why Israel beat us. We think we can win on flat land.”

## 1 Kings 20:24

They advised him to put officers in charge of the soldiers in place of the kings.

## 1 Kings 20:25

They wanted him to replace all the soldiers, horses, and chariots he had lost. They felt sure they could win on flat land.

Ben-hadad agreed and did as they said.

## 1 Kings 20:26

In the spring Ben-hadad gathered his army. He went to Aphek to fight Israel.

## 1 Kings 20:27

The Israelites gathered food and supplies and went to meet them.

Israel’s camp looked like two little flocks of goats. Aram’s army covered the land around them.

## 1 Kings 20:28

A man of God brought Ahab this message from the LORD.

“The people of Aram say my power is only in the hills. They think I have no power in the valleys.

I will give you victory over this huge army. Then you will know that I am the LORD.”

## 1 Kings 20:29

The armies camped facing each other. On the seventh day they fought.

In one day Israel killed 100,000 soldiers from Aram who fought on foot.

## 1 Kings 20:30

The others ran into Aphek. The city wall fell on 27,000 of them.

Ben-hadad ran there too. He hid in an inner room.

## Ben-hadad asks to live

## 1 Kings 20:31

Ben-hadad’s servants said, “We have heard that Israel’s kings show mercy. Let us go to Ahab wearing rough cloth around our waists and ropes around our heads. Perhaps he will let you live.”

## 1 Kings 20:32

They dressed that way and went to Ahab.

“Your servant Ben-hadad asks you to let him live,” they said.

Ahab asked, “Is he still alive? He is my brother.”

## 1 Kings 20:33

The men had been hoping for a kind word. They answered at once, “Yes, your brother Ben-hadad!”

“Bring him here,” said Ahab. When Ben-hadad came, Ahab invited him into his chariot.

## 1 Kings 20:34

Ben-hadad said, “I will give back the cities my father took from your father. You can set up markets in Damascus as my father did in Samaria.”

Ahab said, “With this agreement I will let you go.” The two kings made the agreement, and Ahab sent him away.

## A prophet tells Ahab a story

## 1 Kings 20:35

The LORD spoke to a man who belonged to a group of prophets. Following the LORD’s word, the prophet asked a companion to hit him.

The companion refused.

## 1 Kings 20:36

The prophet said, “You have disobeyed the LORD. When you leave me, a lion will kill you.”

The man left. A lion found him and killed him.

## 1 Kings 20:37

The prophet met another man and asked him to hit him. This man hit the prophet and hurt him.

## 1 Kings 20:38

The prophet put a bandage over his eyes so the king would not recognize him. He waited by the road for Ahab.

## 1 Kings 20:39

As Ahab passed, the prophet called out to him and told him a story.

“I was in the battle. A man brought me a prisoner and told me to guard him. He said that if the prisoner escaped, I would have to die in his place or pay a talent of silver.”

**Reading help**

A talent was a unit of weight. A talent of silver was a very large payment.

## 1 Kings 20:40

“I was busy with other things,” the prophet continued. “The prisoner disappeared.”

Ahab said, “You have said what your own punishment must be.”

## 1 Kings 20:41

The prophet quickly took off the bandage. Ahab recognized him as one of the prophets.

## 1 Kings 20:42

The prophet told Ahab, “The LORD says, ‘I had decided that Ben-hadad must die. You let him go. Now you will lose your life in his place, and your people will suffer in place of his people.’”

## 1 Kings 20:43

Ahab went home to Samaria. He was unhappy and angry.
