# Obadiah 1 — The LORD calls Edom to account

**About this chapter**

Esau and Jacob were brothers. Edom was the nation that came from Esau. The people of Judah came from Jacob. When enemies attacked Jerusalem in Judah, Edom helped the attackers.

## Edom feels safe in the mountains

## Obadiah 1:1

The Lord GOD showed Obadiah a vision about Edom.

Obadiah says, “We heard a message from the LORD.

A messenger went to the nations with this call.

‘Get up! Let us fight against Edom!’”

## Obadiah 1:2

The LORD tells Edom, “I will make you small among the nations.

Other nations will look down on you.

## Obadiah 1:3

You live high up in gaps between the rocks.

You think, ‘Who could bring us down here?’

Your pride has fooled you.

## Obadiah 1:4

Think of an eagle flying high overhead.

Even if you built your nest among the stars, I would bring you down.”

The LORD has spoken.

## Even Edom’s friends will turn against it

## Obadiah 1:5

“Thieves who come at night usually take only what they want.

People picking grapes usually leave a few behind.

But look at the ruin coming to Edom!

## Obadiah 1:6

Enemies will search through everything Esau’s people own.

They will even find the treasures you hid.

## Obadiah 1:7

Your allies promised to stand beside you.

They will drive you to the edge of your land.

People you trusted will trick you and overpower you.

People who shared your bread will set a trap.

You will never see it coming.

## Obadiah 1:8

On that day I will destroy Edom’s wise people.

I will take away wisdom from Esau’s mountains,” says the LORD.

## Obadiah 1:9

“Teman, your strongest fighters will shake with fear.

Enemies will kill everyone in Esau’s mountains.

## What Edom did to Judah

## Obadiah 1:10

You attacked the people of Jacob, Esau’s brother.

Shame will cover you. Your nation will come to an end.

## Obadiah 1:11

Strangers entered Jerusalem and carried away its wealth.

They cast lots to decide who would get what.

You stood and watched. You were like one of them.

## Obadiah 1:12

You should never have taken pleasure in your relatives’ pain.

You should never have cheered when Judah fell.

You should never have bragged when Judah suffered.

## Obadiah 1:13

When disaster struck my people, you walked through their gate.

You enjoyed seeing their pain. You took their belongings.

You should never have done those things.

## Obadiah 1:14

People tried to escape. You waited where the roads met.

You blocked their way and handed survivors to the enemy.

You should never have done that.

## The LORD will judge the nations

## Obadiah 1:15

The day when the LORD judges all nations is near.

What you did to others will happen to you.

## Obadiah 1:16

You drank on my holy mountain.

All the nations will drink and keep drinking.

They will swallow until they disappear completely.”

Here drinking pictures receiving God’s judgment.

## Obadiah 1:17

But on Mount Zion, people will escape to safety.

This mountain in Jerusalem will be holy. It belongs to God.

Jacob’s people will take back their land.

## Obadiah 1:18

Jacob’s people and Joseph’s people will be like fire.

Esau’s people will be like dry stalks after harvest.

Fire burns dry stalks until nothing remains.

That is how completely Jacob’s people will defeat Esau’s people.

None of Esau’s people will survive. The LORD has spoken.

## People who lost their homes will receive land

## Obadiah 1:19

People from the Negev in the south will take Esau’s mountains.

People from the low hills will take the Philistines’ land.

Israel’s people will take the fields of Ephraim and Samaria.

Benjamin’s people will take Gilead.

## Obadiah 1:20

One group of Israelites had to live far from home.

They will take Canaanite land as far as Zarephath.

Others from Jerusalem had to live in Sepharad.

They will take the towns of the Negev.

## Obadiah 1:21

Leaders who rescue the people will climb Mount Zion.

From there they will rule over Esau’s mountains.

The kingdom will belong to the LORD.
