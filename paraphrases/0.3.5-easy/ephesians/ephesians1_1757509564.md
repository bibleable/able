# Ephesians 1 — God brings everything together in Christ

**About this chapter**

Paul writes to believers in Ephesus.

He tells them what he asks God to help them understand.

## Paul greets the believers

## Ephesians 1:1

I am Paul.

God chose me to be an apostle of Christ Jesus.

I write to God’s people in Ephesus who trust Christ Jesus.

**Help with these words**

An apostle is someone Jesus sends to speak for him.

## Ephesians 1:2

May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you grace and peace.

**Pause and answer**

Who chose Paul to be an apostle?

## God chose us in love

## Ephesians 1:3

Praise God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ!

In Christ, God has given us every spiritual blessing in heaven.

## Ephesians 1:4

Before God made the world, he chose us in Christ.

He chose us to be holy and free from blame before him.

God loved us.

## Ephesians 1:5

Long ago God decided to adopt us through Jesus Christ.

It pleased God to make us his children.

**Help with these words**

To adopt someone means to make them part of your family as your own child.

## Ephesians 1:6

So we praise God for his glorious grace.

God freely gave us this kindness through the Son he loves.

**Pause and answer**

When did God choose us in Christ?

## God makes his plan known

## Ephesians 1:7

Christ shed his blood for us.

Through him God sets us free and forgives our sins.

That is how great God’s kindness is.

## Ephesians 1:8

God poured out this kindness on us.

He acted with wisdom and understanding.

## Ephesians 1:9

God had a plan that was once hidden.

It pleased him to make that plan known to us.

Christ is at the center of it.

## Ephesians 1:10

When the time is right, God will bring everything together in Christ.

That includes everything in heaven and on earth.

**Pause and answer**

In whom will God bring everything together?

## The Spirit marks us as God’s own

## Ephesians 1:11

In Christ, God chose us to be his own.

He decided this long ago.

God carries out everything he plans.

## Ephesians 1:12

We were the first to put our hope in Christ.

God wanted our lives to bring him praise for his glory.

## Ephesians 1:13

You too heard the truth, the good news that saves you.

When you believed in Christ, God marked you as his own.

He gave you the Holy Spirit he had promised.

| What you heard | How you responded | What God gave you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The good news | You trusted Christ. | The Holy Spirit |

**Help with these words**

A seal left a mark showing who owned something.

Paul says the Spirit shows that believers belong to God.

## Ephesians 1:14

The Spirit guarantees that we will receive what God has promised.

We wait for God to set his own people fully free.

May God receive praise for his glory!

**Pause and answer**

How did God mark the believers as his own?

## Paul prays that they will know God better

## Ephesians 1:15

I heard how you trust the Lord Jesus.

I also heard how you love all God’s people.

## Ephesians 1:16

Ever since then, I keep thanking God for you.

I remember you when I pray.

## Ephesians 1:17

I pray to the glorious Father, the God of our Lord Jesus Christ.

May he give you wisdom and show you more of himself.

Then you will know him better.

## Ephesians 1:18

May God open the eyes of your heart.

May you know the hope he called you to.

May you see how precious his people are as his glorious inheritance.

**Help with these words**

The eyes of your heart is picture language for understanding something deep inside.

## Ephesians 1:19

May you know how great God’s power is for us who believe.

This is the same mighty power God showed in Christ.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul want the believers to know about God’s power?

## God raised Christ and gave him authority

## Ephesians 1:20

God raised Christ from the dead.

God seated Christ at his right hand in heaven.

**Help with these words**

The place at a ruler’s right hand was a place of honor and authority.

## Ephesians 1:21

Christ is far above every ruler and every power.

His name is greater than every other name, now and in the age to come.

## Ephesians 1:22

God put everything under Christ’s feet.

He gave Christ authority over everything for the good of the church.

## Ephesians 1:23

The church is Christ’s body.

It is the fullness of Christ, who fills everything everywhere.

**Pause and answer**

Who gave Christ authority over everything?
