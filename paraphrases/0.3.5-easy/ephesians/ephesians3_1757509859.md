# Ephesians 3 — Paul prays that believers will know Christ’s love

**About this chapter**

Paul explains the work God gave him among the Gentiles.

Then Paul tells the believers what he asks God to give them.

## God makes his plan known

## Ephesians 3:1

I, Paul, am in prison because I serve Christ Jesus for your sake.

I mean you Gentiles.

**Help with these words**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish.

## Ephesians 3:2

You have heard about the work God trusted me to do for you.

He gave me this work as a gift of his kindness.

## Ephesians 3:3

God showed me his hidden plan.

I have already written a little about it.

![A scroll with lines of writing](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## Ephesians 3:4

When you read my words, you can see what I understand about Christ and this plan.

## Ephesians 3:5

People in earlier times did not know it as we do now.

God’s Spirit has now shown it to his holy apostles and prophets.

## Ephesians 3:6

Through the good news, Gentiles share what God promised his people.

Gentile and Jewish believers belong to the same body.

In Christ Jesus they receive the same promise.

**Help with these words**

The body in this picture is the church.

People from different families belong together in Christ.

**Pause and answer**

Who belongs to the same body in Christ?

## Why Paul tells the good news

## Ephesians 3:7

God gave me the gift of serving this good news.

His power at work in me made this possible.

## Ephesians 3:8

I consider myself the least of all God’s people.

Yet God gave me the joy of telling Gentiles about Christ.

The riches Christ offers are more than anyone can measure.

## Ephesians 3:9

My work is to help everyone see how God carries out his plan.

God made all things.

For ages he kept this plan hidden.

## Ephesians 3:10

Now God uses the church to show his wisdom in all its variety.

Even the rulers and powers in heaven get to see it.

## Ephesians 3:11

This was God’s plan from all eternity.

He carried it out in Christ Jesus our Lord.

## Ephesians 3:12

We trust Christ and belong to him.

So we can come to God freely and with confidence.

## Ephesians 3:13

Please keep your courage when you hear what I suffer for you.

My suffering for you brings you honor.

**Pause and answer**

Why can believers come to God with confidence?

## Paul kneels to pray

## Ephesians 3:14

This is why I kneel before the Father.

## Ephesians 3:15

Every family in heaven and on earth receives its name from him.

## Ephesians 3:16

The riches of God’s glory have no limit.

I ask him to strengthen you deep inside through his Spirit.

## Ephesians 3:17

I pray that Christ will be at home in your hearts as you trust him.

May love hold you firm, like roots holding a tree.

## Ephesians 3:18

Together with all God’s people, may you grasp how wide and long, how high and deep Christ’s love is.

**Help with these words**

Paul uses words for measuring space to describe Christ’s love.

He wants believers to see how great that love is.

## Ephesians 3:19

May you know that love, even though no one can fully understand it.

May God fill you with all his fullness.

**Pause and answer**

Who does Paul ask to strengthen the believers?

## Paul praises God

## Ephesians 3:20

God’s power is already at work in us.

God can do far more than we ask.

He can do far more than we can imagine.

## Ephesians 3:21

May the church give God glory.

May God receive glory in Christ Jesus through every generation, forever.

Amen.

**Pause and answer**

How does God’s power compare with what we can imagine?
