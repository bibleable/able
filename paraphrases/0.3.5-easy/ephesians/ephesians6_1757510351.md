# Ephesians 6 — Stand firm and pray for each other

**About this chapter**

Paul speaks to people in different parts of a household.

Then he asks the church to stand firm and pray.

## Children and fathers

## Ephesians 6:1

Children, obey your parents in the Lord.

This is the right thing to do.

## Ephesians 6:2

God says, “Honor your father and mother.”

This is the first command that comes with a promise.

## Ephesians 6:3

God promises that things may go well for you and that you may live long on earth.

## Ephesians 6:4

Fathers, do not treat your children in ways that stir up anger.

Teach and guide them as the Lord instructs.

**Pause and answer**

How does Paul ask fathers to bring up their children?

## Slaves and masters

## Ephesians 6:5

Slaves, obey your earthly masters with respect and fear.

Be sincere, just as you are when you obey Christ.

## Ephesians 6:6

Do more than work well when someone is watching.

You serve Christ.

Do what God wants with all your heart.

## Ephesians 6:7

Serve willingly, as people working for the Lord.

## Ephesians 6:8

The Lord knows each good thing a person does.

He will reward that good, whether the person is enslaved or free.

## Ephesians 6:9

Masters, treat your slaves with the same goodwill.

Stop threatening them.

You and they have the same Master in heaven.

He has no favorites.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul tell masters to stop doing?

## Stand firm with God’s help

## Ephesians 6:10

Finally, draw strength from the Lord and his great power.

## Ephesians 6:11

Put on all the armor God gives you.

Then you can stand firm against the devil’s tricks.

**Help with these words**

Armor is what a soldier wears for protection.

Paul uses armor as a picture of the help God gives believers.

## Ephesians 6:12

Our struggle is against spiritual powers, rather than human beings.

We face rulers and powers of this dark world and evil spirits in the heavenly places.

## Ephesians 6:13

So take up all the armor God gives you.

Then you can resist evil when the hard day comes.

After doing everything you can, you will still be standing.

**Pause and answer**

Whose strength can believers draw on?

## The armor God gives

## Ephesians 6:14

Stand firm.

Wear truth like a belt around your waist.

Wear righteousness like armor that covers your chest.

**Help with these words**

Righteousness means being right with God and doing what is right.

## Ephesians 6:15

Put on shoes of readiness.

Be ready with the good news of peace.

## Ephesians 6:16

Trust God as you would take shelter behind a shield.

With this shield of faith you can put out every burning arrow of the evil one.

![A broad shield curves around the person behind it.](../pictures/shield-of-faith.svg)

Paul compares trusting God to taking shelter behind a shield.

## Ephesians 6:17

Wear salvation like a helmet.

Take the sword the Spirit gives you.

That sword is God’s word.

| Armor in the picture | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Belt | Truth |
| Chest armor | Righteousness |
| Shoes | Readiness with the good news of peace |
| Shield | Faith |
| Helmet | Salvation |
| Sword | God’s word |

**Help with these words**

Salvation means God saving his people.

## Keep praying for each other

## Ephesians 6:18

Pray with the Spirit’s help at all times.

Bring every kind of request to God.

Stay alert and keep praying for all God’s people.

## Ephesians 6:19

Please pray for me too.

Ask God to give me the words when I speak.

I want to explain his good news boldly, including what was once hidden.

## Ephesians 6:20

I am in chains because I speak as a messenger for that good news.

Pray that I will speak without fear, as I should.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul ask God to give him when he speaks?

## Tychicus brings news

## Ephesians 6:21

Our dear brother Tychicus serves the Lord faithfully.

He will tell you how I am and what I am doing.

## Ephesians 6:22

That is why I sent Tychicus to you.

He will bring news about us and give you courage.

## Ephesians 6:23

May God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you peace, brothers and sisters.

May they give you love together with faith.

## Ephesians 6:24

May God show grace to all who keep loving our Lord Jesus Christ forever.
