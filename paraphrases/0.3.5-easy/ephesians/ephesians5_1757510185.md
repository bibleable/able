# Ephesians 5 — Live in love and light

**About this chapter**

Paul asks believers to think about how they live and speak.

He describes the love Christ has for the church.

## Follow God’s example

## Ephesians 5:1

God loves you as his children.

So follow his example.

## Ephesians 5:2

Let love guide the way you live.

Christ loved us and gave his life for us.

His gift was like an offering whose sweet smell pleases God.

![A jar for something sweet-smelling](../pictures/perfume-jar.svg)

## Ephesians 5:3

You belong to God.

Among you there should be no sexual wrongdoing, impurity, or greed.

## Ephesians 5:4

Keep filthy words, foolish talk, and crude jokes out of your conversations.

Use your words to give thanks.

## Ephesians 5:5

You can be sure of this.

People who live for sexual wrongdoing, impurity, or greed have no share in the kingdom of Christ and God.

A greedy person worships what they want like a god.

## Ephesians 5:6

Do not let empty words fool you.

Such things bring God’s anger on those who disobey him.

## Ephesians 5:7

So do not join in what they do.

**Pause and answer**

Whose love does Paul ask the believers to follow?

## Live as people of the light

## Ephesians 5:8

Once you were darkness.

Now you are light because you belong to the Lord.

Live as people of the light.

## Ephesians 5:9

You can see what the light produces.

It brings out goodness, right living, and truth.

**Help with these words**

Here light pictures the life people have with the Lord.

Its fruit means the good things that grow out of that life.

## Ephesians 5:10

Find out what pleases the Lord.

## Ephesians 5:11

Refuse to join in the harmful things people do in darkness.

Bring those things into the light.

## Ephesians 5:12

What disobedient people do in secret is shameful even to speak about.

## Ephesians 5:13

Light makes hidden things visible.

Whatever the light shines on becomes light itself.

## Ephesians 5:14

That is why people say, “Wake up, sleeper!

Rise from the dead, and Christ will shine on you.”

**Pause and answer**

What does the light produce in people’s lives?

## Use your time wisely

## Ephesians 5:15

Think carefully about how you live.

Make wise choices.

## Ephesians 5:16

Use each opportunity well, because these days are full of evil.

## Ephesians 5:17

Leave foolish ways behind.

Learn what the Lord wants.

## Ephesians 5:18

Do not get drunk on wine.

That leads to reckless actions.

Let the Spirit fill you.

## Ephesians 5:19

Sing psalms, hymns, and songs from the Spirit to one another.

Make music for the Lord with all your heart.

**Help with these words**

Psalms are songs or prayers found in Scripture.

A hymn is a song of praise.

## Ephesians 5:20

Always thank God the Father for everything.

Give thanks in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**Pause and answer**

How can believers use music when they meet?

## Christ and the church

## Ephesians 5:21

Be willing to yield to one another because you honor Christ.

**Help with these words**

To yield means to let someone else take the lead.

## Ephesians 5:22

Wives, yield to your husbands as you do to the Lord.

## Ephesians 5:23

The husband is the head of his wife.

In the same way, Christ is the head of the church.

The church is Christ’s body, and Christ saves it.

## Ephesians 5:24

The church yields to Christ.

In the same way, wives should yield to their husbands in everything.

## Ephesians 5:25

Husbands, love your wives as Christ loved the church.

Christ gave himself up for the church.

## Ephesians 5:26

Christ did this to make the church holy.

He washed the church clean with water through his word.

## Ephesians 5:27

He wanted to bring the church to himself in all its beauty.

He pictures it without any stain or wrinkle.

The people who belong to him are to be holy and free from blame.

**Pause and answer**

How did Christ show his love for the church?

## Love and care in marriage

## Ephesians 5:28

Husbands should love their wives as they love their own bodies.

A man who loves his wife loves himself.

## Ephesians 5:29

People feed their bodies and take good care of them.

Christ cares for the church that way.

## Ephesians 5:30

We are parts of Christ’s body.

## Ephesians 5:31

Scripture says a man will leave his father and mother.

He will join his life to his wife’s life.

The two will become one flesh.

**Help with these words**

“One flesh” describes how closely marriage joins two people.

## Ephesians 5:32

There is a deep mystery here.

I am speaking about Christ and the church.

## Ephesians 5:33

This also applies to each married couple.

Each husband must love his wife as he loves himself.

Each wife must respect her husband.

What everyday act does Paul use to show how Christ cares for the church?
