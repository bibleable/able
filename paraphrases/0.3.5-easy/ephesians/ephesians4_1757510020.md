# Ephesians 4 — Speak the truth with love

**About this chapter**

Paul compares the church to a body growing strong.

He shows how believers can care for each other in daily life.

## Stay together in peace

## Ephesians 4:1

I am in prison because I belong to the Lord.

God called you to follow him.

I urge you to live in a way that honors his call.

## Ephesians 4:2

Be humble and gentle.

Be patient with each other.

Keep loving each other even when it is hard.

## Ephesians 4:3

The Spirit has joined you together.

Work hard to keep that unity by living in peace.

## Ephesians 4:4

There is one body and one Spirit.

When God called you, he gave you one shared hope.

## Ephesians 4:5

There is one Lord, one faith, and one baptism.

## Ephesians 4:6

There is one God and Father of all.

He rules over all, works through all, and is in all.

**Pause and answer**

How can the believers keep the unity the Spirit gives?

## Christ gives gifts to his people

## Ephesians 4:7

Christ has shown kindness to each of us.

He chose what gift each person would receive.

## Ephesians 4:8

Scripture tells of someone going up on high.

He led captives with him and gave gifts to people.

## Ephesians 4:9

What does it mean that he went up?

It means he also came down to the lower parts of the earth.

## Ephesians 4:10

The one who came down is Christ.

Christ went up above all the heavens to fill everything.

## Ephesians 4:11

Christ gave some people to serve as apostles or prophets.

Some tell others the good news.

Some care for the church as pastors and teachers.

**Help with these words**

An apostle is someone Jesus sends to speak for him.

A prophet speaks a message from God.

## Ephesians 4:12

They prepare God’s people to serve.

In this way Christ’s body grows strong.

## Ephesians 4:13

This continues until we all share one faith and know God’s Son together.

Then we will be fully grown, with Christ as the measure of our growth.

**Pause and answer**

What do these workers prepare God’s people to do?

## Tell the truth with love

## Ephesians 4:14

Then we will no longer be like little children.

We will no longer be tossed about like a boat in the waves.

Each new teaching comes like a gust of wind.

Clever people try to trick us with their lies.

![A boat like one that waves can toss about](../pictures/boat.svg)

## Ephesians 4:15

Let us speak the truth with love.

Then in every way we will grow more like Christ.

Christ is the head of the body.

## Ephesians 4:16

Christ joins the whole body together.

Every joint helps hold it in place.

As each part does its work, the whole body grows in love.

**Help with these words**

The body pictures the church.

Each person has a part to play in helping others grow.

**Pause and answer**

How does Paul ask believers to speak the truth?

## Leave the old way of life behind

## Ephesians 4:17

Speaking for the Lord, I insist on this.

Leave behind the way of life of Gentiles whose thinking leads nowhere.

## Ephesians 4:18

They have hardened their hearts and refuse to understand.

Their thinking has grown dark.

They live cut off from the life that comes from God.

## Ephesians 4:19

They no longer feel shame.

They give themselves over to their desires.

They do all kinds of impure things and still want more.

## Ephesians 4:20

You learned a different way of life when you came to know Christ.

## Ephesians 4:21

You heard about Jesus.

As his followers you learned the truth that is in him.

## Ephesians 4:22

You learned to take off your old self, like old clothes.

Leave behind that old way of living.

Its desires trick you and spoil your life.

## Ephesians 4:23

Let God make you new in the way you think.

## Ephesians 4:24

Put on your new self.

God made this new self to be like him.

Live in a way that is truly right and holy.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul mean by taking off the old self?

## Use your hands and words to help

## Ephesians 4:25

So give up lying.

Tell your neighbor the truth, because we belong to one another.

## Ephesians 4:26

When you feel angry, take care not to sin.

Deal with your anger before the sun goes down.

## Ephesians 4:27

Give the devil no chance to gain a hold on you.

## Ephesians 4:28

Anyone who has been stealing must stop.

Do honest work with your own hands.

Then you will have something to share with someone in need.

## Ephesians 4:29

Keep harmful words out of your mouth.

Think about what the listener needs.

Say something that helps that person grow stronger.

**Pause and answer**

What can someone do with the money they earn from honest work?

## Forgive as God forgave you

## Ephesians 4:30

Do not bring sorrow to God’s Holy Spirit.

Through the Spirit, God marked you as his own for the day he sets you fully free.

**Help with these words**

Paul uses the picture of a seal, a mark that shows who owns something.

God gives his Spirit to show that believers belong to him.

## Ephesians 4:31

Let go of bitterness, rage, and anger.

Stop shouting at people or speaking evil about them.

Give up every wish to hurt someone.

## Ephesians 4:32

Be kind to each other.

Care when someone hurts.

Forgive each other just as God forgave you through Christ.

**Pause and answer**

Whose forgiveness gives believers an example to follow?
