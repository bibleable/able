# Jude 1 — Hold on to your faith and show mercy

**About this chapter**

Jude warns about people who misuse God’s kindness.

He asks his friends to help people who doubt.

## Why Jude writes

## Jude 1:1

I am Jude, a servant of Jesus Christ.

James is my brother.

I write to you whom God has called.

God the Father loves you.

He keeps you safe in Jesus Christ.

## Jude 1:2

May you receive more and more mercy, peace and love.

Mercy is kindness shown to someone in need.

## Jude 1:3

Dear friends, I wanted to write about the salvation we share.

Salvation means that God saves us.

Now I must ask you to defend the faith.

The faith is the message God gave his people to believe.

He gave it to them once for all time.

## Jude 1:4

Some people who reject God slipped in among you unnoticed.

Long ago, their judgment was announced.

They use God’s kindness as an excuse for sexual wrongdoing.

They deny Jesus Christ, our only Master and Lord.

**Pause and answer**

What message does Jude ask his friends to defend?

## Remember what happened long ago

## Jude 1:5

You already know this, but remember what happened in Egypt.

Jesus led his people out of that land.

Later he destroyed those who refused to trust him.

## Jude 1:6

Some angels left their own place and the home God gave them.

God keeps them in darkness, bound with lasting chains.

They await judgment on the great day.

## Jude 1:7

Remember Sodom, Gomorrah and the towns around them.

Like those angels, the people crossed a boundary.

They sought sexual relations that were forbidden to them.

Their punishment warns us about the fire that lasts forever.

**Pause and answer**

Who led God’s people out of Egypt?

## How the intruders speak and act

## Jude 1:8

The people among you who rely on their dreams act much the same way.

They misuse their own bodies.

They reject authority and insult glorious beings in heaven.

## Jude 1:9

Michael, a chief angel, argued with the devil about Moses’ body.

Even Michael would not insult the devil or speak a false charge.

Michael said, “May the Lord correct you!”

## Jude 1:10

These people insult what they do not understand.

They act on urges like animals that cannot reason.

What they do by instinct will destroy them.

## Jude 1:11

What sorrow lies ahead for them!

They followed Cain’s example.

They rushed into Balaam’s error.

Like Korah, they rebelled and met ruin.

**Pause and answer**

Who did Michael ask to correct the devil?

## Hidden rocks and empty clouds

## Jude 1:12

They eat without shame at the meals where you share your love.

They care only for themselves.

They are like rocks hidden under water, dangerous to a boat.

They are like clouds the wind carries away without bringing rain.

They are like trees with no fruit at harvest.

Pull those trees up by the roots and they are twice dead.

![A boat.](../pictures/boat.svg)

Picture a rock just below the boat. The people on board may not see the danger.

## Jude 1:13

They are like wild waves throwing up foam.

What comes to the surface is their own shame.

They are like stars wandering from their course.

The deepest darkness awaits them forever.

**Pause and answer**

Why is a hidden rock dangerous to a boat?

## Remember the warnings

## Jude 1:14

Enoch belonged to the seventh generation, counting from Adam.

He spoke a message from God about people like these.

He said the Lord was coming with countless holy ones.

## Jude 1:15

The Lord will judge everyone.

He will show those who defy him what they have done wrong.

He will hold them to account for every cruel word against him.

## Jude 1:16

These people complain and find fault.

They follow their own desires and boast about themselves.

They praise others just to get what they want.

## Jude 1:17

Dear friends, remember what the apostles told you would happen.

Apostles are messengers our Lord Jesus Christ sent.

## Jude 1:18

They told you that people would mock the faith in the last days.

Those people would follow desires that go against God.

## Jude 1:19

These people split you into groups that oppose each other.

The things of this world guide them.

They do not have God’s Spirit.

**Pause and answer**

What did the apostles tell their readers to expect?

## Show mercy as you wait

## Jude 1:20

Dear friends, help each other grow strong in your holy faith.

Pray with the help of the Holy Spirit.

## Jude 1:21

Keep yourselves in God’s love.

Wait for our Lord Jesus Christ to show you mercy.

His mercy will bring you life that lasts forever.

## Jude 1:22

Show mercy to those who doubt.

## Jude 1:23

Others need rescuing, like someone you pull out of a fire.

Show mercy to still others, but take care.

Jude pictures sin as a stain that spreads even to clothing.

Hate the sin even as you help the person.

**Pause and answer**

How should you treat someone who doubts?

## Praise the God who keeps you safe

## Jude 1:24

God can keep you from stumbling.

He can bring you into his glorious presence free from fault.

You can stand before him with great joy.

## Jude 1:25

Give glory to the only God, our Savior, through Jesus Christ our Lord.

Greatness, power and the right to rule belong to God.

They were his before time began.

They are his now and will be his forever.

Amen.

**Pause and answer**

Who can keep you from stumbling?
