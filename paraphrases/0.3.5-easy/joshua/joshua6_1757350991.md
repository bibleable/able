# Joshua 6 — Jericho falls and Rahab lives

**About this chapter**

Israel marches around Jericho. Two men have promised to save Rahab and her family.

## Jericho shuts its gates

## Joshua 6:1

The people of Jericho shut their gates tight.

They were afraid of the Israelites.

Nobody went in or out.

## Joshua 6:2

The LORD told Joshua, “I have given you Jericho.

I have given you its king and its strong soldiers.”

## Joshua 6:3

The LORD told Joshua to march with all Israel’s soldiers.

They would go around the city once each day for six days.

## Joshua 6:4

Seven priests would each carry a ram’s horn ahead of the ark.

On day seven the people would circle Jericho seven times.

The priests would blow their horns.

**Reading help**

A ram is a male sheep.

People used its horn as a trumpet.

The ark was a special chest.

It held stone tablets of the covenant, the LORD’s solemn agreement with Israel.

## Joshua 6:5

The people would hear one long note from a horn.

Then everyone would shout loudly.

The city wall would fall.

Each person would rush straight into the city.

## The people walk around Jericho

## Joshua 6:6

Joshua, son of Nun, called the priests together.

He told them to pick up the ark of the covenant.

Seven priests were to carry horns ahead of the LORD’s ark.

## Joshua 6:7

Joshua told the people to start walking around Jericho.

Armed soldiers would go ahead of the LORD’s ark.

## Joshua 6:8

The seven priests went forward before the LORD.

Each priest blew his horn as he walked.

Other priests carried the LORD’s ark behind them.

## Joshua 6:9

Armed soldiers marched ahead of the priests with horns.

Other soldiers followed the ark.

The horns kept sounding.

## Joshua 6:10

Joshua had told the people to keep quiet.

They were to wait until Joshua told them to shout.

Until then, nobody was to say a word.

## Joshua 6:11

At Joshua’s word the priests carried the ark around Jericho once.

Then everyone went back to camp for the night.

## Six days pass

## Joshua 6:12

Joshua got up early the next morning.

The priests picked up the LORD’s ark.

## Joshua 6:13

The seven priests walked ahead of the ark again.

They kept blowing their seven horns.

Armed soldiers went first.

Other soldiers followed the ark while the horns sounded.

## Joshua 6:14

That second day they circled Jericho once and returned to camp.

For six days they went around once each day.

## Joshua calls for a shout

## Joshua 6:15

On day seven, they got up at first light.

They marched around Jericho in the same order.

Only on that day did they go around seven times.

## Joshua 6:16

After the seventh time around, the priests blew their horns.

Joshua called out, “Shout! The LORD has given you the city!”

## Joshua 6:17

Joshua told them that Jericho and everything in it belonged to the LORD for destruction.

But Rahab the prostitute and everyone in her house would live.

Rahab had hidden the spies Israel sent.

**Reading help**

A prostitute is someone who has sex for payment.

The spies were men Joshua sent to learn about Jericho in secret.

## Joshua 6:18

Joshua warned the people to leave those things alone.

Anyone who took them risked destruction too.

That person would bring disaster on the whole camp.

## Joshua 6:19

All the silver and gold belonged to the LORD.

So did everything made of bronze or iron.

These things must go into the LORD’s treasury.

**Reading help**

A treasury is a place to keep valuable things.

## The wall falls

## Joshua 6:20

The people heard the horns and gave a great shout.

The wall fell down.

Everyone rushed straight into Jericho, and Israel took the city.

## Joshua 6:21

The Israelites killed the people in the city with swords.

They killed men and women, young and old.

They also killed the oxen, sheep, and donkeys.

## The two men keep their promise

## Joshua 6:22

Joshua spoke to the two men who had explored Jericho.

“Go to Rahab’s house.

Bring her out with everyone there.

You promised her you would do this.”

## Joshua 6:23

The two young men brought Rahab out.

They brought her parents, her brothers, and everyone with her.

They settled her whole family outside Israel’s camp.

Where did the men take Rahab and her family?

## Joshua 6:24

The Israelites burned the city and everything in it.

They took the silver, gold, bronze, and iron to the treasury at the LORD’s house.

## Joshua 6:25

Joshua let Rahab live because she had hidden his spies.

He also spared her father’s family and everyone with her.

Rahab was still living among the Israelites when this account was written.

## Joshua warns against rebuilding Jericho

## Joshua 6:26

Then Joshua spoke a solemn curse.

“May the LORD curse whoever rebuilds Jericho.

When he lays its foundation, his oldest child will die.

When he puts up its gates, his youngest child will die.”

**Reading help**

A foundation is the base that holds up a building.

## Joshua 6:27

The LORD was with Joshua.

People throughout the land heard about Joshua.
