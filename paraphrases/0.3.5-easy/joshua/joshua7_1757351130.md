# Joshua 7 — Achan takes what belongs to the LORD

**About this chapter**

Achan hides things he took from Jericho. After Israel loses a battle, Joshua asks the LORD what happened.

## Achan takes things from Jericho

## Joshua 7:1

Israel broke faith with the LORD.

Achan took things from Jericho that the LORD had set apart.

Achan belonged to the tribe of Judah.

His father was Carmi.

Carmi’s father was Zabdi, and Zabdi’s father was Zerah.

The LORD was very angry with Israel.

## Israel loses at Ai

## Joshua 7:2

Joshua sent men from Jericho to look closely at Ai.

Ai was near Beth-aven, east of Bethel.

The men went there to learn about the town in secret.

## Joshua 7:3

They came back and told Joshua what they thought.

Few people lived in Ai.

The men said two or three thousand soldiers could take it.

They saw no reason to tire out everyone.

## Joshua 7:4

About three thousand men went up to Ai.

But Israel’s men ran away from the men of Ai.

## Joshua 7:5

The men of Ai chased them from the city gate to the stone quarries.

On the slopes the men of Ai killed about thirty-six Israelites.

When the people of Israel heard, they lost all courage.

**Reading help**

People cut stone from the ground at a quarry.

The account says the people’s hearts melted like water.

This means they felt afraid and helpless.

## Joshua asks the LORD for help

## Joshua 7:6

Joshua tore his clothes.

He lay face down before the ark of the LORD until evening.

The leaders of Israel lay face down too.

They all put dust on their heads to show their grief.

**Reading help**

The ark was the special chest that held the stone tablets of God’s covenant.

A covenant is a solemn agreement.

## Joshua 7:7

Joshua said, “Lord GOD, why did you bring us across the Jordan? Was it to let the Amorites destroy us? If only we had been content to stay across the river!”

## Joshua 7:8

“Lord, Israel has run from its enemies.

What can I say now?”

## Joshua 7:9

“The Canaanites and everyone else here will hear about this.

They will surround us and wipe us out.

Then what will you do to uphold the honor of your great name?”

## The LORD explains why Israel lost

## Joshua 7:10

The LORD said to Joshua, “Stand up! Why are you lying face down?”

## Joshua 7:11

“Israel has sinned.

The people have broken my covenant.

They have taken things I set apart for destruction.

They stole those things and lied about them.

They put them among their own belongings.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the people do with the stolen things?

## Joshua 7:12

“That is why Israel cannot stand against its enemies.

The people will run because they too face destruction.

Remove the things I set apart for destruction.

Unless you do, I will no longer be with you.”

## Joshua 7:13

The LORD told Joshua to get up and prepare the people.

They must set themselves apart for God before the next day.

The LORD, Israel’s God, said the forbidden things were still among them.

Until Israel removed them, Israel could not stand against its enemies.

## The LORD will show who took the things

## Joshua 7:14

In the morning the tribes must come forward.

The LORD would choose one tribe.

Its clans must come forward.

The LORD would choose one clan.

Its families must come forward.

The men in the chosen family must come forward one by one.

**Reading help**

A tribe was a large group with a shared ancestor.

Each tribe had smaller groups called clans.

Each clan included several families.

## Joshua 7:15

The LORD said the guilty man had broken his covenant.

What the man had done was shameful for Israel.

The people must burn the man and all that belonged to him.

## Achan comes forward

## Joshua 7:16

Joshua got up early the next morning.

He called Israel forward, one tribe at a time.

The LORD chose Judah.

## Joshua 7:17

Joshua called the clans of Judah forward.

The LORD chose the clan of Zerah.

From that clan the LORD chose Zabdi’s family.

## Joshua 7:18

The men of Zabdi’s family came forward one by one.

The LORD chose Achan, Carmi’s son.

Achan was Zabdi’s grandson and Zerah’s great-grandson, from Judah.

## Achan tells the truth

## Joshua 7:19

Joshua said, “My son, honor the LORD, Israel’s God.

Admit to God what you did.

Tell me everything.

Hide nothing from me.”

## Joshua 7:20

Achan answered, “It is true.

I sinned against the LORD, the God of Israel.

I will tell you what I did.”

## Joshua 7:21

“Among the things from Jericho I saw a beautiful cloak from Shinar.

I saw silver weighing two hundred shekels.

I also saw a gold bar weighing fifty shekels.

I wanted those things for myself, so I took them.

I hid them in the ground inside my tent.

The silver is underneath the other things.”

**Reading help**

A cloak is a piece of clothing worn over other clothes.

A shekel was a unit of weight.

## Joshua 7:22

Joshua sent messengers, and they ran to Achan’s tent.

They found everything there, with the silver underneath.

## Joshua 7:23

The messengers brought the things to Joshua and all Israel.

They laid everything out before the LORD.

## Israel takes Achan to the valley

## Joshua 7:24

Joshua and all Israel took Achan, Zerah’s descendant, to the Valley of Achor.

They brought the silver, the cloak, and the gold.

They also brought Achan’s sons and daughters.

They took his cattle, donkeys, sheep, tent, and everything else he owned.

## Joshua 7:25

Joshua asked Achan, “Why have you brought trouble on us? Today the LORD will bring trouble on you.” The Israelites killed Achan with stones.

Then they stoned the others and burned their bodies.

## Joshua 7:26

The people piled rocks over Achan.

The pile was still there when this account was written.

The LORD’s anger against Israel ended.

People still called that place the Valley of Achor.

**Reading help**

Achor means trouble.

The name reminded Israel of what happened there.
