# Joshua 10 — The LORD fights for Israel

**About this chapter**

Five kings attack Gibeon. Gibeon asks Israel for help. Joshua speaks to the LORD in front of the people.

## The king of Jerusalem hears about Gibeon

## Joshua 10:1

Adoni-zedek was king of Jerusalem.

He heard that Joshua had destroyed Ai and killed its king.

Joshua had done the same to Jericho and its king.

Now Gibeon had made peace with Israel.

Its people lived beside the Israelites.

## Joshua 10:2

This news frightened Adoni-zedek and his people.

Gibeon was an important city, like a city with a king.

It was larger than Ai.

Its men were strong fighters.

## Joshua 10:3

Adoni-zedek sent a message to four kings.

Hoham ruled Hebron, and Piram ruled Jarmuth.

Japhia ruled Lachish, and Debir ruled Eglon.

## Joshua 10:4

Adoni-zedek asked the kings to help him fight Gibeon.

He wanted to punish Gibeon for making peace with Joshua and Israel.

## Gibeon asks Joshua for help

## Joshua 10:5

The five Amorite kings joined their armies.

The kings of Jerusalem, Hebron, Jarmuth, Lachish, and Eglon all came.

They camped near Gibeon and attacked it.

## Joshua 10:6

The people of Gibeon sent word to Joshua at Gilgal.

“We are your servants.

Please do not leave us without help! Come quickly and save us! The Amorite kings from the hills have joined forces against us.”

## Joshua 10:7

Joshua left Gilgal with his whole army.

All his strongest fighters went with him.

## Joshua 10:8

The LORD told Joshua, “Do not fear those kings.

I have given you victory over them.

None of them will be able to stop you.”

## The LORD sends hail

## Joshua 10:9

Joshua marched all night from Gilgal.

The Amorites did not expect him to arrive then.

## Joshua 10:10

The LORD confused the Amorites.

Israel defeated them at Gibeon and killed many of them.

Israel chased them up the road to Beth-horon.

The fighting reached as far as Azekah and Makkedah.

## Joshua 10:11

The Amorites fled downhill from Beth-horon toward Azekah.

The LORD sent large hailstones down on them.

The hailstones killed more people than the Israelite swords did.

**Reading help**

Hailstones are pieces of ice that fall from the sky.

## The sun stands still

## Joshua 10:12

That day the LORD gave Israel victory over the Amorites.

Joshua spoke to the LORD where Israel could hear.

He said, “Sun, stand still over Gibeon.

Moon, stand still over the Valley of Aijalon.”

## Joshua 10:13

The sun stood still, and the moon stopped.

They stayed there until Israel had taken revenge on its enemies.

The Book of Jashar also describes that day.

The sun stayed in the middle of the sky.

About a whole day passed before it went down.

![The sun over Gibeon and the moon over the Valley of Aijalon](../pictures/gibeon-sun-moon.svg)

Point to the moon above the valley.

## Joshua 10:14

There had never been a day like it.

No day since had been like it either.

The LORD listened to Joshua and fought for Israel.

## Joshua 10:15

Then Joshua and all Israel returned to camp at Gilgal.

## The five kings hide

## Joshua 10:16

The five kings had run away.

They had hidden in a cave at Makkedah.

## Joshua 10:17

Someone told Joshua where the kings were hiding.

## Joshua 10:18

Joshua ordered men to block the cave opening with large stones.

Some men stayed to guard the kings.

## Joshua 10:19

Joshua told the rest of the army to keep going.

The fleeing soldiers were trying to reach their cities.

Joshua said the LORD had given Israel victory over them.

## Joshua 10:20

Joshua and Israel killed many of the fleeing soldiers.

The survivors reached cities with strong walls.

## Joshua 10:21

Israel’s whole army returned safely to Joshua at Makkedah.

No one dared to speak against Israel.

## Joshua faces the five kings

## Joshua 10:22

Joshua asked his men to open the cave.

He wanted them to bring him the five kings.

## Joshua 10:23

The men brought the five kings out.

They ruled Jerusalem, Hebron, Jarmuth, Lachish, and Eglon.

## Joshua 10:24

Joshua called all the men of Israel together.

At Joshua’s word, his commanders put their feet on the kings’ necks.

**Reading help**

This act showed that the kings had lost their power.

## Joshua 10:25

Joshua told his men to be strong and brave.

They had no need to fear or lose heart.

The LORD would defeat their other enemies too.

## Joshua 10:26

Then Joshua killed the five kings.

He hung their bodies on five trees until evening.

## Joshua 10:27

At sunset Joshua’s men took the bodies down at his command.

They threw them into the cave where the kings had hidden.

They closed the opening with large stones.

The stones were still there when this account was written.

## Makkedah and Libnah fall

## Joshua 10:28

That day Joshua took Makkedah.

Israel killed its king and everyone in the city.

No one survived.

The king of Jericho had met the same end.

## Joshua 10:29

Joshua and Israel went on to Libnah.

They fought against the city.

## Joshua 10:30

The LORD gave Israel victory over Libnah and its king.

Joshua and his men killed everyone there.

They killed Libnah’s king as they had killed Jericho’s king.

## Lachish and Eglon fall

## Joshua 10:31

Joshua and Israel moved from Libnah to Lachish.

They surrounded the city and fought against it.

## Joshua 10:32

The LORD gave Israel victory at Lachish.

Joshua took the city on the second day.

Israel killed everyone there, as at Libnah.

## Joshua 10:33

Horam, king of Gezer, came to help Lachish.

Joshua defeated Horam and the people who came with him.

None of them survived.

## Joshua 10:34

Joshua and Israel moved from Lachish to Eglon.

They surrounded Eglon and fought against it.

## Joshua 10:35

They took Eglon that day.

Joshua and his men killed everyone there, as at Lachish.

## Hebron and Debir fall

## Joshua 10:36

Joshua and Israel went up from Eglon to Hebron.

They fought against Hebron.

## Joshua 10:37

They took Hebron and its villages.

They killed the king and all the people.

As at Eglon, Joshua destroyed the city and left no survivors.

## Joshua 10:38

Then Joshua and Israel turned toward Debir.

They fought against the city.

**Reading help**

Here Debir is the name of a city.

Earlier in the chapter, Debir was also a king’s name.

## Joshua 10:39

They took Debir, its king, and its villages.

They killed everyone there.

Debir and its king met the same end as Hebron and Libnah.

## Joshua returns to Gilgal

## Joshua 10:40

Joshua conquered the hills, the Negev, the foothills, and the slopes.

He defeated all their kings.

He destroyed every living thing there.

This was what the LORD, Israel’s God, had commanded.

**Reading help**

The Negev is dry land in the south.

Foothills are low hills near higher hills or mountains.

## Joshua 10:41

Joshua took the land from Kadesh-barnea to Gaza.

He also took the region of Goshen as far as Gibeon.

## Joshua 10:42

Joshua defeated these kings and took their land in one campaign.

He won because the LORD, Israel’s God, fought for Israel.

**Reading help**

Here a campaign means a series of battles.

## Joshua 10:43

Then Joshua and all Israel went back to camp at Gilgal.
