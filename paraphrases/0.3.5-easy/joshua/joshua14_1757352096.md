# Joshua 14 — Caleb receives Hebron

**About this chapter**

Israel divides the land. Caleb reminds Joshua of a promise Moses made long ago.

## The families receive their land

## Joshua 14:1

Israel’s families received shares of land in Canaan.

Eleazar the priest and Joshua, son of Nun, divided the land.

The family leaders of Israel’s tribes helped them.

## Joshua 14:2

The leaders drew lots for nine and a half tribes.

This decided which land each tribe received.

The LORD had told Moses to do this.

## Joshua 14:3

Moses had already given land to two and a half tribes.

Their land was east of the Jordan River.

Moses gave the tribe of Levi no region of its own.

## Joshua 14:4

Joseph’s descendants formed two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim.

Levi’s families received towns where they could live.

Around the towns, their sheep and cattle could graze.

## Joshua 14:5

So the Israelites divided the land.

They did what the LORD had told Moses.

## Caleb remembers a promise

## Joshua 14:6

People from Judah came to Joshua at Gilgal.

Caleb, son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite, spoke to Joshua.

“You remember Kadesh-barnea.

There the LORD spoke to Moses, the man of God.

You know what he said about you and me.”

## Joshua 14:7

Caleb said, “I was forty years old then.

Moses, the LORD’s servant, sent me from Kadesh-barnea.

I went to find out what the land was like.

I came back and told Moses what I honestly believed.”

## Joshua 14:8

“The men who went with me frightened our people.

But I stayed faithful to the LORD my God.”

## Joshua 14:9

“That day Moses made me a solemn promise.

The land where I had walked would be mine.

My children after me would have it forever.

Moses promised this because I had followed the LORD with my whole heart.”

**Reading help**

Following the LORD with your whole heart means staying faithful to him in all you do.

## Caleb asks for the hills

## Joshua 14:10

“Forty-five years have passed since the LORD spoke to Moses.

Israel wandered in the wilderness during those years.

The LORD promised to keep me alive, and he has.

Today I am eighty-five years old.”

![At age 40 Caleb explored the land. At age 85 he asked for the hill country. Between them, 45 years passed.](../pictures/caleb-years.svg)

Point to Caleb’s age when Moses sent him.

## Joshua 14:11

“I am as strong as when Moses sent me.

I still have strength to go out and fight.

I have strength to come home again.”

## Joshua 14:12

“Give me the hill country the LORD promised me then.

You heard about the Anakim who lived there.

Their large cities had strong walls.

Perhaps the LORD will help me drive them out, as he said.”

## Joshua gives Hebron to Caleb

## Joshua 14:13

Joshua blessed Caleb, son of Jephunneh.

He gave Caleb the land of Hebron to keep.

## Joshua 14:14

Caleb had followed the LORD, Israel’s God, with his whole heart.

That is why Hebron became his family’s land.

It still belonged to them when this account was written.

**Reading help**

An inheritance is something a family receives and can pass on.

## Joshua 14:15

Hebron once had another name, Kiriath-arba.

It bore the name of Arba, the greatest man among the Anakim.

Then the fighting in the land stopped.
