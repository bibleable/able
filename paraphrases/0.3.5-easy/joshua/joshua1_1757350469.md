# Joshua 1 — Be strong and brave

**About this chapter**

Joshua takes up the work Moses left him. Before Israel crosses the Jordan, Joshua reminds the tribes east of the river how they can help their neighbors.

## The LORD speaks to Joshua

## Joshua 1:1

Moses, the LORD’s servant, had died.

Then the LORD spoke to Joshua, son of Nun.

Joshua had been Moses’ helper.

## Joshua 1:2

The LORD told Joshua it was time to lead.

Joshua and all the people must cross the Jordan River.

The LORD was giving the land across the river to Israel.

## Joshua 1:3

The LORD had promised Moses this land.

Now he told Joshua, “I have given you every place where you set foot.”

## Joshua 1:4

Their land would stretch from the wilderness and Lebanon to the great Euphrates River.

It would include all the land of the Hittites.

To the west it would reach the Great Sea.

## Joshua 1:5

No one would be able to stand against Joshua throughout his life.

The LORD promised to be with Joshua as he had been with Moses.

He would never leave Joshua.

## Courage to follow the law

## Joshua 1:6

The LORD told Joshua, “Be strong and brave.”

Joshua would give the people their shares of the land.

The LORD had sworn to give it to their ancestors.

## Joshua 1:7

Again the LORD told Joshua to be strong and very brave.

Joshua must follow the whole law Moses had given him.

He must stay on that path wherever he went.

Then he would do well.

**Reading help**

The path here means a way of living. Joshua would stay on it by doing what the law said.

## Joshua 1:8

Joshua must keep speaking the words of the Book of the Law.

He must think about them day and night.

Then he could do everything written there and succeed in his work.

![An open scroll with lines of writing](../pictures/scroll.svg)

The Book of the Law held the words Joshua was to remember.

**Pause and answer**

When should Joshua think about the law?

## Joshua 1:9

The LORD reminded Joshua, “Be strong and brave.”

Joshua need not lose heart or be afraid.

The LORD his God would be with him wherever he went.

## Get ready to cross the Jordan

## Joshua 1:10

Then Joshua gave orders to the officers of Israel.

## Joshua 1:11

The officers must go through the camp.

They must tell everyone to get food ready.

Within three days the people would cross the Jordan.

They would take the land the LORD was giving them.

**Pause and answer**

What did the officers tell the people to get ready?

## Joshua reminds the tribes of their promise

## Joshua 1:12

Joshua also spoke to Reuben, Gad and half the tribe of Manasseh.

## Joshua 1:13

He asked them to remember the orders Moses had given them.

Moses had told them the LORD would give them rest and land of their own.

## Joshua 1:14

Their wives, young children and animals could stay east of the Jordan.

Moses had given them land there.

But all their fighting men must cross ahead of the other Israelites.

They must go ready for battle and help the others.

## Joshua 1:15

They must help until the LORD gave the other tribes rest and land too.

Then the men could return to their own land east of the Jordan.

That was the land Moses had given them.

**Pause and answer**

When could the fighting men return home?

## The tribes answer Joshua

## Joshua 1:16

The people of those tribes answered Joshua.

They would do everything he commanded.

They would go wherever he sent them.

## Joshua 1:17

They promised to obey Joshua as they had obeyed Moses.

They asked that the LORD be with Joshua as he had been with Moses.

## Joshua 1:18

They said anyone who rebelled and refused Joshua’s orders would face death.

Then they told Joshua, “Be strong and brave!”
