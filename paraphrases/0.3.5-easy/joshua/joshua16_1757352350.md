# Joshua 16 — Joseph’s family receives land

**About this chapter**

Two branches of Joseph’s family receive land. This chapter describes the share belonging to Ephraim’s families.

## From the Jordan River to the sea

## Joshua 16:1

Joseph’s family received a share of the land.

Its border began at the Jordan River near Jericho.

It reached the waters of Jericho on the east.

From Jericho it climbed through the wilderness to the hills of Bethel.

**Reading help**

A border marks where one area ends.

## Joshua 16:2

Bethel had another name, Luz.

From Bethel the border reached the land of the Archites at Ataroth.

## Joshua 16:3

Then the border went downhill toward the west.

It reached the land of the Japhletites and the border of Lower Beth-horon.

It went on to Gezer and ended at the sea.

## Joshua 16:4

Ephraim and Manasseh were Joseph’s sons.

Their families received this land to keep.

## The borders of Ephraim’s land

## Joshua 16:5

Ephraim’s families had their own share of the land.

Their border ran from Ataroth-addar in the east to Upper Beth-horon.

## Joshua 16:6

The border continued toward the sea.

From Michmethath in the north, it turned east toward Taanath-shiloh.

It passed Taanath-shiloh and reached Janoah on the east.

## Joshua 16:7

From Janoah the border went down to Ataroth and Naarah.

It reached Jericho and ended at the Jordan River.

## Joshua 16:8

From Tappuah the border ran west to the stream called Kanah.

It ended at the sea.

This land belonged to the families of Ephraim.

## Towns inside Manasseh’s land

## Joshua 16:9

Ephraim’s families also received towns inside Manasseh’s land.

The villages belonging to those towns were theirs too.

## Joshua 16:10

Ephraim’s families did not drive the Canaanites out of Gezer.

The Canaanites stayed there among the families of Ephraim.

Ephraim’s people forced the Canaanites to work for them.

This was still true when this account was written.
