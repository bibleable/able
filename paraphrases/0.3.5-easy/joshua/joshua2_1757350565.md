# Joshua 2 — Rahab asks for a promise

**About this chapter**

Rahab shelters two visitors from Israel. She asks them to remember her family when Israel comes to Jericho.

## Two visitors in Jericho

## Joshua 2:1

Joshua, son of Nun, sent two spies from Shittim in secret.

They were to look over the land, especially Jericho.

In Jericho they stayed with a woman named Rahab.

Rahab was a prostitute.

**Reading help**

A spy gathers information secretly.

A prostitute is someone who has sex for payment.

## Joshua 2:2

Someone told the king of Jericho about the visitors.

Men from Israel had come that night to spy on the land.

## Joshua 2:3

The king sent Rahab a message.

He wanted her to bring the men out of her house.

He said they had come to spy on the whole land.

## Rahab hides the men

## Joshua 2:4

Rahab had already hidden the two men.

She told the messengers that the men had visited her.

She said she did not know where they came from.

## Joshua 2:5

Rahab said the men had left at dusk, just before the city gate closed.

She said she did not know where they went.

She urged the king’s men to hurry after them.

## Joshua 2:6

In fact, the spies were still on Rahab’s roof.

She had hidden them among the flax stalks she had laid there.

**Reading help**

Flax is a plant with long stems.

People used it to make linen cloth.

**Pause and answer**

Where were the spies when the king’s men left?

## Joshua 2:7

The king’s men searched along the road to the Jordan River crossings.

The city gate closed behind them.

## What Rahab believes

## Joshua 2:8

Before the spies lay down to sleep, Rahab went up to the roof.

## Joshua 2:9

Rahab told them, “I know the LORD has given you this land.”

She said everyone in the land was afraid of Israel.

## Joshua 2:10

The people had heard how the LORD dried up the Red Sea when Israel left Egypt.

They also knew about Sihon and Og, the Amorite kings across the Jordan.

Israel had destroyed both kings.

## Joshua 2:11

Rahab said the news had made the people lose courage.

She told the spies that the LORD their God ruled heaven and earth.

**Reading help**

Rahab said their hearts “melted.” This means they felt too afraid to be brave.

## A promise for Rahab’s family

## Joshua 2:12

Rahab had shown the spies kindness.

Now she asked them to swear by the LORD to show her family kindness.

She wanted a sign that she could trust their promise.

## Joshua 2:13

She asked them to spare her father, mother, brothers and sisters.

She asked them to save everyone in their families from death.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Rahab ask the spies to save?

## Joshua 2:14

The spies pledged their own lives for hers and her family’s.

Rahab must keep their visit secret.

When the LORD gave Israel the land, they would keep their promise.

## The window in the wall

## Joshua 2:15

Rahab’s house was part of the city wall.

She lowered the men on a rope through her window.

## Joshua 2:16

Rahab told the men to go to the hills.

They were to wait there three days while the search went on.

Then they could continue their journey.

## Joshua 2:17

The spies explained what Rahab must do for their promise to hold.

## The sign in the window

## Joshua 2:18

When Israel came into the land, a scarlet cord must hang in that same window.

Rahab must gather her parents, her brothers and her whole family inside her house.

## Joshua 2:19

The spies would answer for any harm to the people inside with Rahab.

Anyone who went out into the street would be responsible for their own life.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the spies promise to protect Rahab’s family?

## Joshua 2:20

If Rahab told anyone why they had come, their promise would no longer bind them.

## Joshua 2:21

Rahab agreed and sent the spies on their way.

After they left, she tied the scarlet cord in the window.

## The spies return to Joshua

## Joshua 2:22

The spies stayed in the hills for three days.

The king’s men searched along the road and returned without finding them.

## Joshua 2:23

The two spies came down from the hills and crossed the river.

They told Joshua, son of Nun, everything that had happened.

**Pause and answer**

Who heard the spies’ report?

## Joshua 2:24

The spies were sure the LORD had given Israel the whole land.

They told Joshua that everyone there was afraid of Israel.
