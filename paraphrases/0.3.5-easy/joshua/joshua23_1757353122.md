# Joshua 23 — Joshua tells Israel to stay faithful

**About this chapter**

Near the end of his life, Joshua gathers Israel. He reminds the people what the LORD has done for them.

## Joshua gathers Israel

## Joshua 23:1

The LORD had given Israel peace from the enemies around them.

A long time passed.

Joshua grew old.

## Joshua 23:2

Joshua called all Israel together.

The elders, leaders, judges, and officers came too.

Joshua told them, “I am old now.”

## Joshua 23:3

“You saw what the LORD your God did to those nations for you.

The LORD himself fought for you.”

## Joshua 23:4

“I gave each tribe its share of land to keep.

The land stretches west from the Jordan River to the Great Sea.

Some nations there have already fallen.

Others still live on the land I assigned to you.”

## Joshua 23:5

“The LORD your God will drive those nations out ahead of you.

Then you can take their land as he promised.”

## Joshua asks Israel to obey the law

## Joshua 23:6

“Be strong.

Obey everything in the Book of the Law of Moses.

Follow it without turning right or left.”

**Reading help**

Joshua speaks of obeying God as following a path.

Staying on that path means doing what God commands.

![An open scroll with lines of writing](../pictures/scroll.svg)

Point to the writing on the scroll.

Joshua told the people to obey the written law.

## Joshua 23:7

“Keep apart from the nations that still live among you.

Do not call on their gods.

Do not make promises in their gods’ names.

Do not serve those gods or bow down to them.”

## Joshua 23:8

“Stay faithful to the LORD your God.

You have done this until now.”

## Remember who fought for you

## Joshua 23:9

“The LORD has driven out great and powerful nations ahead of you.

No one has been able to stand against you.”

## Joshua 23:10

“One of you can make a thousand enemies run away.

The LORD your God fights for you, just as he promised.”

**Pause and answer**

Who fought for Israel?

## Joshua 23:11

“So take care to keep loving the LORD your God.”

## Joshua warns the people

## Joshua 23:12

Joshua warned Israel about turning away from the LORD.

The Israelites might join the nations still living among them.

They might marry into those families and make close ties with them.

## Joshua 23:13

Joshua said, “Then the LORD will stop driving those nations out.

They will be like traps that catch you.

They will be like whips against your sides and thorns in your eyes.

In the end you will perish from the good land the LORD gave you.”

**Reading help**

A trap holds something so it cannot get free.

Whips and thorns cause pain.

Joshua meant that these nations would bring Israel trouble and suffering.

## Remember what the LORD promised

## Joshua 23:14

“My life is coming to an end.

You know deep inside that the LORD kept every good promise he made to you.

Every promise came true.”

## Joshua 23:15

“The LORD gave you all the good things he promised.

He will also bring the trouble he warned you about.

He will destroy you from the good land he gave you.”

## Joshua 23:16

“The LORD your God commanded you to keep his covenant.

If you serve other gods and bow to them, you will break that covenant.

The LORD will be very angry with you.

You will soon perish from the good land he gave you.”

**Reading help**

A covenant is a solemn agreement.

Israel had agreed to serve the LORD.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, how could Israel stay faithful to the LORD?
