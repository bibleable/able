# Joshua 15 — The land Judah’s families received

**About this chapter**

Judah’s families receive land. Caleb’s daughter asks for water for the land her father gave her.

## Judah’s southern border

## Joshua 15:1

Judah’s families received land next to Edom.

Their far southern border reached the Wilderness of Zin.

## Joshua 15:2

The southern border began at a bay.

It was at the south end of the Salt Sea.

## Joshua 15:3

The border passed south of the Ascent of Akrabbim.

It continued to Zin, then south of Kadesh-barnea.

It passed Hezron, went up to Addar, and curved toward Karka.

## Joshua 15:4

From Azmon the border followed the Brook of Egypt to the sea.

That was Judah’s southern border.

## The border from the Jordan to the sea

## Joshua 15:5

The eastern border followed the Salt Sea to the Jordan River.

The northern border began at the bay where the Jordan enters the sea.

**Reading help**

A border marks where one area ends and another begins.

## Joshua 15:6

The northern border went up to Beth-hoglah.

It passed north of Beth-arabah.

Then it reached the Stone of Bohan.

Bohan was Reuben’s son.

## Joshua 15:7

From the Valley of Achor the border climbed to Debir.

It turned north toward Gilgal.

Gilgal faces the Ascent of Adummim, south of the deep valley.

The border followed the waters of En-shemesh to En-rogel.

## Joshua 15:8

The border climbed the Valley of Ben-hinnom.

It passed the southern slope of the Jebusite city, Jerusalem.

Then it climbed the hill west of the Valley of Hinnom.

This hill stands at the northern end of the Valley of Rephaim.

## Joshua 15:9

From the hilltop the border curved to the spring at the Waters of Nephtoah.

It reached the cities of Mount Ephron.

Then it turned toward Baalah, also called Kiriath-jearim.

## Joshua 15:10

From Baalah the border curved west to Mount Seir.

It followed the northern slope of Mount Jearim, also called Chesalon.

Then it went down to Beth-shemesh and across to Timnah.

## Joshua 15:11

The border reached Ekron’s northern slope and curved toward Shikkeron.

It passed Mount Baalah and Jabneel.

It ended at the sea.

## Joshua 15:12

The shore of the Great Sea marked the western border.

These borders enclosed the land of Judah’s families.

## Caleb receives Hebron

## Joshua 15:13

The LORD told Joshua to give Caleb land in Judah.

Caleb was the son of Jephunneh.

Joshua gave him Kiriath-arba, also called Hebron.

Arba was an ancestor of Anak.

## Joshua 15:14

Caleb drove three families of Anak out of Hebron.

These families came from Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai, sons of Anak.

## Joshua 15:15

Caleb then went to fight the people of Debir.

The city’s earlier name was Kiriath-sepher.

## Joshua 15:16

Caleb promised that whoever captured Kiriath-sepher could marry his daughter Acsah.

## Joshua 15:17

Othniel captured the city.

His father was Kenaz, Caleb’s brother.

Caleb gave Acsah to Othniel in marriage.

## Acsah asks for water

## Joshua 15:18

One day Acsah urged Othniel to ask Caleb for a field.

When Acsah got off her donkey, Caleb spoke to her.

He asked what she wanted.

## Joshua 15:19

Acsah asked her father for a blessing.

She said, “You gave me land in the Negev.

Please give me springs of water too.” Caleb gave her the upper springs and the lower springs.

**Reading help**

The Negev is dry land in the south.

A spring is a place where water flows from the ground.

## Cities in the south

## Joshua 15:20

These are the places Judah’s families received to keep.

## Joshua 15:21

These southern cities lay in the Negev near Edom.

Judah received Kabzeel, Eder and Jagur.

## Joshua 15:22

Judah received Kinah, Dimonah and Adadah.

## Joshua 15:23

Judah received Kedesh, Hazor and Ithnan.

## Joshua 15:24

Judah received Ziph, Telem and Bealoth.

## Joshua 15:25

Judah received Hazor-hadattah and Kerioth-hezron.

Another name for Kerioth-hezron was Hazor.

## Joshua 15:26

Judah received Amam, Shema and Moladah.

## Joshua 15:27

Judah received Hazar-gaddah, Heshmon and Beth-pelet.

## Joshua 15:28

Judah received Hazar-shual, Beersheba and Biziothiah.

## Joshua 15:29

Judah received Baalah, Iim and Ezem.

## Joshua 15:30

Judah received Eltolad, Chesil and Hormah.

## Joshua 15:31

Judah received Ziklag, Madmannah and Sansannah.

## Joshua 15:32

Judah received Lebaoth, Shilhim, Ain, and Rimmon.

This group had twenty-nine cities with their villages.

## Cities in the lower hills

## Joshua 15:33

Other cities lay in the lower hills.

Judah received Eshtaol, Zorah and Ashnah.

## Joshua 15:34

Judah received Zanoah, En-gannim, Tappuah and Enam.

## Joshua 15:35

Judah received Jarmuth, Adullam, Socoh and Azekah.

## Joshua 15:36

Judah received Shaaraim, Adithaim, and Gederah (also called Gederothaim).

This group had fourteen cities with their villages.

## Joshua 15:37

Judah received Zenan, Hadashah and Migdal-gad.

## Joshua 15:38

Judah received Dilan, Mizpeh and Joktheel.

## Joshua 15:39

Judah received Lachish, Bozkath and Eglon.

## Joshua 15:40

Judah received Cabbon, Lahmas and Chitlish.

## Joshua 15:41

Judah received Gederoth, Beth-dagon, Naamah, and Makkedah.

This group had sixteen cities with their villages.

## Joshua 15:42

Judah received Libnah, Ether and Ashan.

## Joshua 15:43

Judah received Iphtah, Ashnah and Nezib.

## Joshua 15:44

Judah received Keilah, Achzib, and Mareshah.

This group had nine cities with their villages.

## Cities near the sea

## Joshua 15:45

Judah received Ekron with its towns and villages.

## Joshua 15:46

From Ekron to the sea, Judah received all the cities near Ashdod.

Their villages came with them.

## Joshua 15:47

Judah received Ashdod and Gaza with their towns and villages.

This land reached the Brook of Egypt and the shore of the Great Sea.

## Cities in the hill country

## Joshua 15:48

Judah also received cities in the hill country.

Judah received Shamir, Jattir and Socoh.

## Joshua 15:49

Judah received Dannah and Kiriath-sannah.

Another name for Kiriath-sannah was Debir.

## Joshua 15:50

Judah received Anab, Eshtemoh and Anim.

## Joshua 15:51

Judah received Goshen, Holon, and Giloh.

This group had eleven cities with their villages.

## Joshua 15:52

Judah received Arab, Dumah and Eshan.

## Joshua 15:53

Judah received Janim, Beth-tappuah and Aphekah.

## Joshua 15:54

Judah received Humtah, Kiriath-arba, and Zior.

Another name for Kiriath-arba was Hebron.

This group had nine cities with their villages.

## Joshua 15:55

Judah received Maon, Carmel, Ziph and Juttah.

## Joshua 15:56

Judah received Jezreel, Jokdeam and Zanoah.

## Joshua 15:57

Judah received Kain, Gibeah, and Timnah.

This group had ten cities with their villages.

## Joshua 15:58

Judah received Halhul, Beth-zur and Gedor.

## Joshua 15:59

Judah received Maarath, Beth-anoth, and Eltekon.

This group had six cities with their villages.

## Joshua 15:60

Judah received Kiriath-baal and Rabbah.

Another name for Kiriath-baal was Kiriath-jearim.

This group had two cities with their villages.

## Cities in the wilderness

## Joshua 15:61

These cities lay in the wilderness.

Judah received Beth-arabah, Middin and Secacah.

## Joshua 15:62

Judah received Nibshan, the City of Salt, and En-gedi.

This group had six cities with their villages.

## The people of Jerusalem

## Joshua 15:63

Judah’s families could not drive the Jebusites out of Jerusalem.

When this account was written, the two peoples still lived there together.
