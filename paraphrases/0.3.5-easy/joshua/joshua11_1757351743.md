# Joshua 11 — The northern kings fight Israel

**About this chapter**

Kings from the north gather to fight Israel. Later, each tribe of Israel receives a share of the land.

## Jabin sends word to other kings

## Joshua 11:1

Jabin, king of Hazor, heard about Israel’s victories.

He sent word to Jobab, king of Madon.

He also sent word to the kings of Shimron and Achshaph.

## Joshua 11:2

Jabin’s message reached the kings in the northern mountains.

It reached the Arabah plain south of Chinnereth.

It reached the foothills and Naphoth-dor in the west.

## Joshua 11:3

The Canaanites in the east and west received the message.

So did the Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, and Jebusites in the hills.

The Hivites lived at the foot of Mount Hermon in Mizpah.

Jabin sent word to them too.

## The armies gather at Merom

## Joshua 11:4

The kings came with all their armies.

Their soldiers were like grains of sand on the shore.

They brought many horses and chariots.

**Reading help**

Think of trying to count the sand on a beach.

That picture tells us how many soldiers came.

A chariot was a cart that horses pulled.

## Joshua 11:5

The kings camped together by the waters of Merom.

They had come to fight Israel.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the kings camp together?

## Joshua 11:6

The LORD told Joshua, “Do not fear them.” By that time the next day, the LORD would give Israel victory.

The enemy soldiers would lie dead before Israel.

Joshua was to cripple their horses and burn their chariots.

## Israel defeats the northern armies

## Joshua 11:7

Joshua and his whole army came to the waters of Merom.

Their attack took the other armies by surprise.

## Joshua 11:8

The LORD gave Israel victory over those armies.

Israel chased them as far as Greater Sidon and Misrephoth-maim.

The chase also reached the Valley of Mizpeh in the east.

Israel killed them and left no survivors.

## Joshua 11:9

Joshua did what the LORD had told him.

He crippled the horses and burned the chariots.

## Joshua takes Hazor

## Joshua 11:10

Joshua turned back and took Hazor.

He killed its king with a sword.

Hazor had once led all these kingdoms.

## Joshua 11:11

The Israelites killed everyone in Hazor.

Nothing that breathed remained alive there.

Joshua burned the city.

## Joshua 11:12

Joshua took the other cities and captured their kings.

Israel killed the kings and the people with swords.

Moses, the LORD’s servant, had ordered their destruction.

## Joshua follows the orders Moses gave him

## Joshua 11:13

Some of the cities stood on raised ground.

Of those cities, Joshua burned only Hazor.

## Joshua 11:14

Israel kept the goods and animals from those cities.

But the Israelites killed all the people.

They let no one live.

## Joshua 11:15

The LORD had given his orders to Moses.

Moses had passed those orders on to Joshua.

Joshua did everything the LORD had commanded Moses.

## The land Joshua took

## Joshua 11:16

Joshua took the hill country, the whole Negev, and all Goshen.

He took the western foothills and the Arabah plain.

He also took Israel’s mountains and their foothills.

**Reading help**

The Negev is dry land in the south.

Foothills are the lower hills beside mountains.

## Joshua 11:17

This land stretched from Mount Halak, which rises toward Seir, to Baal-gad.

Baal-gad lay in the Valley of Lebanon below Mount Hermon.

Joshua captured and killed all the kings there.

![A mountain rises from lower ground](../pictures/mountain.svg)

Point to the bottom of the mountain.

This is what “the foot of Mount Hermon” means.

## Joshua 11:18

Joshua fought these kings for a long time.

## Joshua 11:19

Only the Hivites of Gibeon made peace with Israel.

Israel took all the other cities in battle.

## Joshua 11:20

The LORD hardened their hearts, so they fought Israel.

They received no mercy.

Israel destroyed them, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**Reading help**

Here “hardened their hearts” means the LORD made them unwilling to turn from fighting.

## The fighting ends

## Joshua 11:21

Joshua also fought the Anakim in the hills around Hebron, Debir, and Anab.

He destroyed the Anakim and their cities throughout the hills of Judah and Israel.

## Joshua 11:22

No Anakim remained in the land Israel held.

Some survived in Gaza, Gath, and Ashdod.

Those were the only places where Anakim remained.

## Joshua 11:23

Joshua took the whole land as the LORD had told Moses.

Joshua gave each tribe of Israel its share of the land.

It was theirs to pass on to their children.

Then the land had rest from war.

**Reading help**

“The land had rest” means the fighting stopped.
