# Joshua 8 — Israel takes Ai and hears the law

**About this chapter**

Israel returns to Ai. Later, families gather to hear Joshua read the law of Moses.

## The LORD speaks to Joshua

## Joshua 8:1

The LORD told Joshua, “Do not be afraid.

Do not lose heart.” Joshua was to take the whole army to Ai.

The LORD had given Joshua the king of Ai, the people, the city, and its land.

## Joshua 8:2

Ai and its king would share Jericho’s fate.

This time Israel could keep the animals and other goods.

The LORD told Joshua that some men would wait behind Ai.

**Reading help**

Jericho was the city Israel had already taken.

## Joshua 8:3

Joshua and the army set out for Ai.

Joshua chose thirty thousand brave soldiers.

He sent them out at night.

## Joshua speaks to the men

## Joshua 8:4

Joshua told those men to stay hidden near Ai.

They were to wait behind the city, ready to act.

## Joshua 8:5

Joshua explained what his own group would do.

They would approach Ai.

When the men of Ai came out, Joshua’s group would run away.

## Joshua 8:6

Joshua expected the men of Ai to follow.

They would think Israel was running away again.

The chase would take them away from their city.

**Pause and answer**

What did the men of Ai expect Israel to do?

## Joshua 8:7

Joshua said the men who were waiting would take Ai.

The LORD their God would give them the city.

## Joshua 8:8

Joshua said they must burn Ai after taking it.

These orders came from the LORD.

## Israel camps near Ai

## Joshua 8:9

The men Joshua sent waited between Bethel and Ai.

That place was west of Ai.

Joshua spent the night among the people.

## Joshua 8:10

Early the next morning Joshua gathered his men.

Joshua and Israel’s leaders went ahead of them to Ai.

## Joshua 8:11

Joshua’s men came near the city.

They camped north of Ai.

A valley lay between their camp and Ai.

## Joshua 8:12

Joshua had placed about five thousand men west of Ai.

They waited between Bethel and Ai.

## Joshua 8:13

Israel’s main camp was north of Ai.

Other soldiers guarded the rear on the west.

That night Joshua went into the valley.

## The king of Ai comes out

## Joshua 8:14

Early in the morning the king of Ai saw Israel.

He hurried out with his men to fight.

They met at the chosen place overlooking the Arabah plain.

The king knew nothing about the men behind Ai.

## Joshua 8:15

Joshua and his men acted as if they were losing.

They ran toward the wilderness.

## Joshua 8:16

All the men of Ai received the call to follow.

They chased Joshua away from the city.

## Joshua 8:17

The men of both Ai and Bethel joined the chase.

They left Ai wide open.

## Smoke rises from Ai

## Joshua 8:18

The LORD told Joshua to hold his spear toward Ai.

The LORD would give him the city.

Joshua held out his spear.

## Joshua 8:19

The waiting men got up when Joshua did this.

They rushed into Ai and took the city.

Soon the city was burning.

## Joshua 8:20

The men of Ai looked back.

Smoke from their city rose into the sky.

The Israelites who had run away now turned against them.

The men of Ai had nowhere to escape.

## Joshua 8:21

Joshua and his men saw the smoke.

They knew the other Israelites had taken Ai.

They turned and struck down the men chasing them.

## Joshua 8:22

The Israelites from the city also came out.

The men of Ai were caught between the two groups.

Israel killed them, and none got away.

## Joshua 8:23

But the Israelites captured the king of Ai alive.

They brought him to Joshua.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did the Israelites bring to Joshua alive?

## What happened to Ai

## Joshua 8:24

Israel killed everyone who had chased them into the fields and wilderness.

Then the Israelites went back into Ai.

They killed the people there too.

## Joshua 8:25

Twelve thousand men and women died that day.

They were all the people of Ai.

## Joshua 8:26

Joshua kept his spear stretched out.

He lowered it only after Israel had destroyed everyone in Ai.

## Joshua 8:27

Israel kept only the cattle and the goods from Ai.

The LORD had told Joshua they could keep these.

## Joshua 8:28

Joshua burned Ai and left it in ruins.

It still lay empty when this account was written.

## Joshua 8:29

Joshua hanged the king of Ai on a tree.

The body hung there until evening.

At sunset Joshua ordered his men to take it down.

They threw it down at the city gate and covered it with rocks.

The pile was still there when this account was written.

## Joshua builds an altar

## Joshua 8:30

Then Joshua built an altar on Mount Ebal.

He built it for the LORD, Israel’s God.

## Joshua 8:31

Moses, the LORD’s servant, had given Israel instructions.

Joshua followed them in building the altar.

The Book of the Law of Moses called for uncut stones.

Nobody had shaped the stones with an iron tool.

There the people gave the LORD burnt offerings and peace offerings.

**Reading help**

An altar is a place to give gifts to God.

A burnt offering was a gift burned up on the altar.

At a peace offering, worshipers also shared a meal.

## Joshua 8:32

While Israel watched, Joshua wrote on the stones.

He copied the law that Moses had written.

## Everyone hears the law

## Joshua 8:33

Priests from Levi’s tribe carried the LORD’s ark of the covenant.

The people stood on both sides, facing the priests.

Israel’s leaders, officers, and judges stood with them.

People from other lands stood beside those born in Israel.

Half the people stood in front of Mount Gerizim.

The other half stood in front of Mount Ebal.

Moses had told them to gather this way for Israel’s blessing.

**Reading help**

The ark was a special chest.

It held the stone tablets of the covenant, God’s solemn agreement with Israel.

## Joshua 8:34

Joshua read aloud everything in the Book of the Law.

He read the blessings and the curses.

**Reading help**

The blessings told of good things that would come through obedience.

The curses warned of trouble for disobeying God.

## Joshua 8:35

Everyone heard every word that Moses had commanded.

Women and young children listened too.

So did the people from other lands who lived among them.
