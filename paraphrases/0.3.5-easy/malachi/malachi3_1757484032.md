# Malachi 3 — Return to me, says the LORD

**About this chapter**

The LORD announces a messenger who will prepare the way.

He calls the people to bring the gifts they have kept back.

He also listens to those who honor him.

## The Lord will come to his temple

## Malachi 3:1

The LORD who commands heaven’s armies says,

“I will send my messenger ahead to prepare the way for me.

Then the Lord you are looking for will suddenly come to his temple.

He is the Messenger of the covenant, the one you long for.

He is coming.”

A covenant is a solemn agreement.

## Malachi 3:2

Who can stand before him when he comes?

He will be like the fire a worker uses to purify metal.

He will be like strong soap that cleans cloth.

## Malachi 3:3

A worker sits by the fire and removes impurities from silver.

In the same way, the Lord will purify Levi’s descendants.

He will make them pure like gold and silver.

Then they will offer gifts to the LORD as they should.

## Malachi 3:4

The LORD will be pleased with the gifts from Judah and Jerusalem.

They will please him as they did long ago.

## The LORD sees how people treat each other

## Malachi 3:5

The LORD says, “I will come to judge you.

I will quickly speak against those who use spells.

I will speak against those who break their marriage vows or lie under oath.

I see those who mistreat widows and children who have lost their fathers.

I see those who cheat workers out of their pay.

I see those who deny foreigners a fair hearing.

People who do these things have no fear of me.”

## The people ask how to return to the LORD

## Malachi 3:6

The LORD says, “I do not change.

That is why you descendants of Jacob are still here.

## Malachi 3:7

“Like your ancestors, you have turned away from my commands.

You have disobeyed them.

Come back to me, and I will come back to you.”

The people ask, “How can we come back?”

## Malachi 3:8

The LORD asks, “Would anyone rob God?

Yet you are robbing me.”

The people ask, “How are we robbing you?”

The LORD answers, “You are keeping back your tithes and offerings.”

A tithe is one share out of ten equal shares.

![One of ten equal boxes is marked to show a tithe.](../pictures/one-tenth.svg)

## Malachi 3:9

The LORD says, “A curse has come upon your whole nation.

Yet you still rob me.

## Malachi 3:10

“Bring the whole tithe into the storeroom.

Then there will be food in my house.

Put me to the test in this.

See how I will open the windows of heaven for you.

I will pour out more blessings than you can measure.

## Malachi 3:11

“I will stop the pests that eat your crops.

They will leave your harvest alone.

The vines in your fields will grow grapes.

## Malachi 3:12

“People everywhere will say that God has blessed you.

Your land will be a delight.”

## The LORD listens to what people say

## Malachi 3:13

The LORD says, “You have spoken harshly against me.”

The people ask, “What have we said against you?”

## Malachi 3:14

The LORD answers, “You have said, ‘Serving God does us no good.

What do we gain by following his commands?

What do we gain by showing sorrow before the LORD who commands heaven’s armies?

## Malachi 3:15

“‘Look at the proud! We call them blessed.

People do evil and still do well.

They even put God to the test and get away with it.’”

## Malachi 3:16

Then the people who respected the LORD spoke with each other.

The LORD listened and heard them.

In his presence someone wrote a record on a scroll.

It was a record of the people who respected the LORD and honored his name.

## Malachi 3:17

The LORD who commands heaven’s armies says,

“On the day I gather what I treasure, those people will be mine.

A father shows mercy to a son who serves him.

In the same way, I will show them mercy.

## Malachi 3:18

“Then you will again see the difference between those who do right and those who do evil.

You will know who serves God and who does not.”
