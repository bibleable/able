# Malachi 2 — Keep faith with God and one another

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells the priests why their teaching has hurt people.

Malachi also speaks to husbands who have broken their marriage promises.

## A warning to the priests

## Malachi 2:1

The LORD says, “Priests, this command is for you.

## Malachi 2:2

“Listen to me and take my words seriously.

Give my name the honor it deserves.

If you refuse, I will send a curse among you.

I will turn your blessings into curses.

I have already begun because you have ignored my words.”

The LORD who commands heaven’s armies says this.

## Malachi 2:3

“I will speak against your descendants.

I will smear your faces with dung from your festival sacrifices.

You will be carried away with the waste.

## Malachi 2:4

“Then you will know why I gave you this command.

I want my covenant with Levi to continue.”

A covenant is a solemn agreement.

Levi was an ancestor of the priests.

## Levi taught what was true

## Malachi 2:5

The LORD says, “Through my covenant I gave Levi life and peace.

I asked him to respect me.

Levi showed me deep respect and honored my name.

## Malachi 2:6

“He taught what was true.

He did not tell lies.

He lived at peace with me and did what was right.

He helped many people turn away from doing wrong.

## Malachi 2:7

“A priest should guard the knowledge God has given him.

People should be able to come to him for teaching.

He is a messenger of the LORD who commands heaven’s armies.

![The LORD gives the priest a message. The priest teaches the people.](../pictures/malachi-priest-teaches.svg)

## Malachi 2:8

“But you priests have left my path.

Your teaching has made many people stumble.”

Here stumbling means being led into wrongdoing.

The LORD says, “You have broken my covenant with Levi.

## Malachi 2:9

“You have disobeyed me.

You have favored some people over others when applying my law.

So I have let everyone see your shame.

The people now look down on you.”

## The people have broken their promises

## Malachi 2:10

Malachi asks, “Don’t we all have one Father?

One God made us all.

Why do we betray one another?

When we do that, we dishonor the covenant our ancestors received.”

## Malachi 2:11

Judah has broken faith with God.

Something terrible has happened in Israel and in Jerusalem.

Men of Judah have married women who worship a foreign god.

In doing so, they have dishonored the holy place the LORD loves.

## Malachi 2:12

May the LORD remove every man who does this from Jacob’s people.

Even bringing an offering to the LORD will not excuse him.

## Malachi 2:13

Here is something else you do.

You weep and groan until your tears cover the LORD’s altar.

An altar is the place where you bring gifts to God.

You weep because the LORD no longer welcomes your gifts.

## Malachi 2:14

You ask, “Why?”

The LORD witnessed the promises you made when you were young.

The woman you married is your companion.

You made a covenant with her, but you have betrayed her.

## Malachi 2:15

The LORD made husband and wife one.

They share in his Spirit.

Why did he make them one?

He wants children who follow him.

Watch over your heart.

Keep faith with the wife you married when you were young.

## Malachi 2:16

The LORD, the God of Israel, says, “I hate divorce.”

The LORD who commands heaven’s armies says that a man who divorces his wife covers his clothes with violence.

The picture shows the harm he carries with him.

Watch over your heart and keep your promise.

## The LORD hears what the people say

## Malachi 2:17

The LORD has grown tired of your words.

You ask, “What have we said to tire him?”

You say, “The LORD thinks people who do evil are good.

He is pleased with them.”

Or you ask, “Where is the God who judges fairly?”
