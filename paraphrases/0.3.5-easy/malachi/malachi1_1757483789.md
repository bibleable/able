# Malachi 1 — The LORD asks the priests to honor him

**About this chapter**

Israel’s people ask how the LORD has shown his love.

The priests ask how they have shown him disrespect.

The LORD answers both questions through Malachi.

## The LORD speaks about Jacob and Esau

## Malachi 1:1

The LORD gave Malachi a message for the people of Israel.

## Malachi 1:2

The LORD says, “I have loved you.”

The people ask, “How have you loved us?”

The LORD answers, “Esau and Jacob were brothers.

Yet I loved Jacob.

## Malachi 1:3

“I hated Esau.

I turned his mountains into empty, ruined land.

I left his land to the jackals that live in the desert.”

## Malachi 1:4

Esau’s people, the people of Edom, say,

“Our land lies in ruins, but we will rebuild.”

The LORD who commands heaven’s armies answers,

“They may build, but I will tear it down.

People will call Edom the land of evil.

They will say my anger against its people lasts forever.

## Malachi 1:5

“You Israelites will see this for yourselves.

You will say, ‘The LORD is great even beyond Israel’s borders!’”

## The LORD questions the priests about their gifts

## Malachi 1:6

The LORD who commands heaven’s armies speaks to the priests.

“A son honors his father.

A servant respects his master.

If I am your father, how do you honor me?

If I am your master, where is your respect for me?

You priests treat my name as if it means nothing.”

The priests ask, “How have we treated your name that way?”

## Malachi 1:7

The LORD answers, “You put food unfit for worship on my altar.”

An altar is the place where priests offer gifts to God.

The priests ask, “How have we dishonored you?”

The LORD answers, “You say my table deserves no respect.”

Here the LORD calls his altar a table.

## Malachi 1:8

The LORD says, “You offer blind animals as sacrifices.

That is wrong.

You also offer animals that limp or are sick.

That is wrong too.

Try giving those animals to your governor.

Would he be pleased with you?

Would he do you a favor?”

## Malachi 1:9

The LORD who commands heaven’s armies says,

“Go ahead and ask God to show you kindness.

These are the gifts you yourselves have brought.

Do you expect him to welcome you?”

## Malachi 1:10

The LORD says, “I wish one of you would shut the temple doors.

Then you would stop lighting useless fires on my altar.

I take no pleasure in you.

I will accept no gifts from you.”

## People everywhere will honor the LORD

## Malachi 1:11

The LORD who commands heaven’s armies says,

“From sunrise to sunset, nations everywhere will honor my name.

In every place people will burn incense in my honor.

They will bring me pure gifts.

For my name will be great among the nations.”

Incense gives off a sweet smell when it burns.

## Malachi 1:12

The LORD tells the priests, “Yet you dishonor my name.

You say my table is unclean.

You speak of its food as if it were worthless.

## Malachi 1:13

“You complain, ‘What a bother!’

You turn up your noses at this work.

You bring me stolen animals, animals that limp, and sick animals.

Should I accept gifts like these from you?”

## Malachi 1:14

The LORD says, “A man has a healthy male in his flock.

He promises to give that animal to me.

Then he offers an animal with something wrong with it instead.

A curse falls on the man who cheats like that.

I am a great King.

People in every nation must treat my name with deep respect.”
