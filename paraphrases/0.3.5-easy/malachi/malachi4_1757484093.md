# Malachi 4 — The LORD promises healing

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks through Malachi. He warns people who do evil. He promises healing to those who honor Him.

## A day like a burning fire

## Malachi 4:1

The LORD leads the armies of heaven.

He says, “Look! A day is coming.

It will burn like a very hot oven.

Proud people and those who do evil will be like dry stalks.

I will set them on fire.

No root or branch will be left.”

The fire shows how complete the LORD’s judgment will be.

## A day like the sunrise

## Malachi 4:2

“But for you who honor My name, the sun of righteousness will rise.

Its wings will bring healing.”

Think of sunlight spreading across the ground like wings.

The LORD will do what is right and bring healing.

“You will go out and jump for joy.

You will be like young calves running out of their pen.

![The bright sun shines over the hills.](../pictures/judges-song-sun.svg)

## Malachi 4:3

“You will walk over those who did evil.

They will be like ashes beneath your feet.

This will happen on the day I am preparing.”

The LORD who leads the armies of heaven says this.

## Remember Moses and look for Elijah

## Malachi 4:4

“Remember the law I gave My servant Moses.

At Mount Horeb, I gave Moses instructions for all Israel.

## Malachi 4:5

“Look! I will send you the prophet Elijah.

A prophet speaks for God.

Elijah will come before My great and fearsome day.

## Malachi 4:6

“Elijah will help fathers turn toward their children with love.

He will help children turn toward their fathers with love.

Otherwise I will come and strike the land with a curse.”
