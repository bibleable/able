# 2 Peter 2 — Watch out for false teachers

**About this chapter**

Peter warns about teachers who trick people.

He tells how God rescued Noah and Lot.

## Teachers who use lies

## 2 Peter 2:1

Long ago, some people falsely claimed to speak for God.

False teachers will come among you too.

They will quietly teach lies that ruin people.

They will even deny the Master who bought them.

By doing this, they will bring swift ruin on themselves.

## 2 Peter 2:2

Many will follow them and do shameful things.

Because of them, people will speak against the way of truth.

## 2 Peter 2:3

These teachers want what you have.

They will use lies to make money from you.

God passed judgment on them long ago.

That judgment still stands.

Their ruin is surely coming.

**Pause and answer**

Why do these teachers use lies?

## God rescued Noah and Lot

## 2 Peter 2:4

Think of the angels who sinned.

God did not spare them.

He sent them deep into hell.

There he holds them in chains in the dark until judgment.

## 2 Peter 2:5

God sent a flood upon the ancient world.

Its people lived without regard for God.

But God kept eight people safe.

One was Noah, who urged people to do right.

## 2 Peter 2:6

God also judged two cities, Sodom and Gomorrah.

He reduced them to ashes.

Their end warns those who live without regard for God.

## 2 Peter 2:7

Yet God rescued Lot.

Lot lived rightly.

His neighbors broke God’s law and did shameful things.

Their actions deeply troubled Lot.

## 2 Peter 2:8

Lot lived among them day after day.

He saw and heard the wrong things they did.

It hurt him deeply because he loved what was right.

## 2 Peter 2:9

So the Lord knows how to rescue his people from trouble.

He also knows how to hold those who do wrong.

They will face punishment on the day he judges them.

**Pause and answer**

Who did God rescue from Sodom?

## What the teachers do

## 2 Peter 2:10

This warning especially concerns those who follow corrupt desires and despise authority.

These teachers are bold and insist on their own way.

They insult glorious beings in heaven without fear.

## 2 Peter 2:11

Even angels, with much greater strength and power, hold back.

They do not bring insulting charges like that before the Lord.

## 2 Peter 2:12

These teachers insult what they do not understand.

Peter compares them to animals that act without thinking.

Such animals are born only to be caught and killed.

These teachers too will come to ruin.

## 2 Peter 2:13

The harm they have done will bring them harm in return.

They enjoy wild parties even in broad daylight.

They eat with you and enjoy deceiving you.

They bring shame to your gatherings.

## 2 Peter 2:14

They look for someone to commit adultery with.

They never have enough of sin.

They lure people who are unsure what to believe.

They have trained themselves to want more and more.

They are under a curse.

**Pause and answer**

What do the teachers do at meals with the believers?

## A donkey stops Balaam

## 2 Peter 2:15

They left the right path and followed Balaam’s example.

Balaam, son of Beor, loved getting paid to do wrong.

![Silver coins.](../pictures/ten-silver-coins.svg)

Look at the coins. Think about why Balaam wanted payment.

## 2 Peter 2:16

A donkey corrected Balaam for doing wrong.

Donkeys cannot usually speak.

But this donkey spoke with a human voice.

It stopped the prophet from carrying out his foolish plan.

## 2 Peter 2:17

These teachers are like springs with no water.

They are like mist a storm blows away.

People who come to them for help find nothing.

The deepest darkness awaits these teachers.

**Pause and answer**

What stopped Balaam from carrying out his plan?

## A false promise of freedom

## 2 Peter 2:18

Their words sound grand but have nothing to offer.

Some people are just getting free from those who live by lies.

The teachers lure them back by appealing to their desires for pleasure.

## 2 Peter 2:19

The teachers promise people freedom.

Yet their own wrongdoing rules them.

Whatever rules a person makes that person its slave.

## 2 Peter 2:20

Knowing our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ freed them from the world’s evil.

Suppose that evil traps them and rules them again.

Then they are worse off than before.

## 2 Peter 2:21

They learned the right way to live.

They received God’s holy command, then turned away from it.

They would have been better off never knowing that way.

## 2 Peter 2:22

Two old sayings describe what they have done.

A dog goes back to its own vomit.

A pig that someone washed rolls in the mud again.

Both pictures show what it means to return to old ways.

**Pause and answer**

What rules the teachers who promise freedom?
