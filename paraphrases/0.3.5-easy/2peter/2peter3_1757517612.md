# 2 Peter 3 — God keeps his promise

**About this chapter**

Peter asks his friends to remember God’s promise.

He explains why the Lord is patient.

## Remember what God’s messengers said

## 2 Peter 3:1

Dear friends, this is my second letter to you.

In both letters I remind you of things you know.

I want to help you think clearly and honestly.

## 2 Peter 3:2

Remember what God’s holy prophets said would happen.

Remember also what our Lord and Savior commanded.

Your apostles brought you his command.

Apostles are people Jesus sent with his message.

## 2 Peter 3:3

First, understand what will happen in the last days.

Some people will make fun of your hope.

They will follow their own wrong desires.

## 2 Peter 3:4

They will ask, “What happened to his promise to come?

Our ancestors died long ago.

Everything still goes on as it has since the world began.”

**Pause and answer**

What promise do some people make fun of?

## Remember the flood

## 2 Peter 3:5

These people choose to ignore what happened long ago.

God spoke, and the heavens came into being.

By his word God formed the earth out of water and through water.

## 2 Peter 3:6

Water then flooded the world.

The world of that time perished.

## 2 Peter 3:7

By that same word, God keeps today’s heavens and earth for fire.

He keeps them for the day of judgment.

That day will bring ruin to those who turn from God.

**Pause and answer**

What happened to the world in the time of the flood?

## Why the Lord is patient

## 2 Peter 3:8

Dear friends, remember how time looks to the Lord.

One day is like a thousand years to him.

A thousand years are also like one day.

## 2 Peter 3:9

Some people think the Lord is late keeping his promise.

The Lord is being patient with you.

He wants no one to perish.

He wants everyone to turn away from sin.

## 2 Peter 3:10

The day of the Lord will come unexpectedly, like a thief.

The heavens will disappear with a roar.

Fire will destroy the things the world is made of.

The earth and everything done on it will lie open for all to see.

**Pause and answer**

Why is the Lord patient?

## Look forward to a new earth

## 2 Peter 3:11

Since all these things will come apart, how should you live?

Live as people who belong to God.

Honor him in the things you do.

## 2 Peter 3:12

Look forward to God’s day and help it come sooner.

On that day fire will destroy the heavens.

The heat will melt the things the world is made of.

## 2 Peter 3:13

God has promised a new heaven and a new earth.

We look forward to that home.

What is right will have a lasting place there.

## 2 Peter 3:14

Dear friends, you are waiting for these things.

Make every effort to live at peace.

Live so the Lord can find you free from stain and blame.

A life free from stain means a life kept clean from sin.

**Pause and answer**

What home has God promised?

## Read Paul’s letters with care

## 2 Peter 3:15

Remember that our Lord’s patience gives people time to be saved.

Our dear brother Paul wrote to you about this too.

God gave Paul the wisdom to do so.

## 2 Peter 3:16

Paul speaks about these things throughout his letters.

Some parts are hard to understand.

People who lack understanding or firm convictions twist Paul’s words.

They twist the rest of Scripture too.

By doing this they bring ruin on themselves.

![A scroll for written words.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

Look at the scroll. Think of the words Paul sent to his readers.

## 2 Peter 3:17

Dear friends, now you know the danger ahead.

Be on your guard.

People who ignore God’s law could lead you into error.

Then you could lose your firm footing in the truth.

## 2 Peter 3:18

Keep growing in the grace of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.

Grace is his freely given kindness.

Keep getting to know him better.

May he receive glory now and forever.

Amen.

**Pause and answer**

What do some people do with parts of Paul’s letters that are hard to understand?
