# 2 Peter 1 — Remember what you have learned

**About this chapter**

Peter wants his readers to remember Jesus.

He tells them what he heard on a mountain.

## A faith as precious as ours

## 2 Peter 1:1

I am Simon Peter.

I serve Jesus Christ as his apostle.

An apostle is someone Jesus sends with his message.

Jesus Christ is our God and Savior.

Because he does what is right, you have received faith as precious as ours.

## 2 Peter 1:2

May you know God and our Lord Jesus better.

Through knowing them, may you receive more kindness and peace.

## 2 Peter 1:3

By his power, God has given us everything we need to live and honor him.

These gifts come through knowing the one who called us.

He called us by his own glory and goodness.

## 2 Peter 1:4

Through his glory and goodness, God gave us precious promises.

Wrong desires spoil life in this world.

You have escaped their hold.

Through his promises you can share in God’s own nature.

**Pause and answer**

What has God given us to help us live for him?

## Practice goodness, kindness and love

## 2 Peter 1:5

So make every effort to grow.

Along with trusting God, practice doing what is good.

As you do good, grow in knowledge too.

## 2 Peter 1:6

Along with knowledge, learn self-control.

Self-control means choosing what you do even when desires pull hard.

Learn also to keep going through trouble.

As you keep going, practice living in a way that honors God.

## 2 Peter 1:7

As you honor God, treat other believers like family.

Along with this kindness, practice love.

## 2 Peter 1:8

Let these qualities keep growing in you.

Then knowing our Lord Jesus Christ will make a difference.

Your life will produce good things.

**Pause and answer**

How can you show kindness to other believers?

## Remember what God has done

## 2 Peter 1:9

Anyone who lacks these qualities is like someone who cannot see ahead.

They have lost sight of what God did for them.

They have forgotten that God washed away their past sins.

## 2 Peter 1:10

So, brothers and sisters, make every effort to live this way.

Make sure of God’s call and choice of you.

As you practice these things, you will not stumble and fall away.

## 2 Peter 1:11

Our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ will welcome you richly into his kingdom.

His rule lasts forever.

**Pause and answer**

How long will the kingdom of Jesus last?

## Why Peter keeps reminding you

## 2 Peter 1:12

You already know these things.

You stand firmly in the truth you have received.

Even so, I will keep reminding you.

## 2 Peter 1:13

As long as I live, I should help you remember.

I think of my body as a tent.

A tent is a place to stay for a time.

![A tent beside a house.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

Point to the tent. Peter uses it to picture his body.

## 2 Peter 1:14

Our Lord Jesus Christ has made something clear to me.

I will soon put my tent aside.

I mean that I will soon die.

## 2 Peter 1:15

I want you to remember these things after my death.

I will do all I can to help you recall them whenever you need to.

**Pause and answer**

What does Peter mean by putting his tent aside?

## What we heard on the mountain

## 2 Peter 1:16

We told you about our Lord Jesus Christ’s power and his coming.

Nobody made up a clever story for us to repeat.

We saw his greatness with our own eyes.

## 2 Peter 1:17

God the Father gave Jesus honor and glory.

From his great glory the Father spoke about Jesus.

“This is my Son.

I love him.

He gives me great joy.”

## 2 Peter 1:18

We were there with Jesus on the holy mountain.

We ourselves heard that voice from heaven.

**Pause and answer**

Whose voice did Peter hear on the mountain?

## Words that guide us like a lamp

## 2 Peter 1:19

We can be sure of what the prophets said.

Prophets are people who spoke for God.

Pay attention to their words.

They guide you like a lamp in a dark place.

Keep listening until day breaks and the morning star rises in your hearts.

Picture daylight replacing the little light you needed at night.

## 2 Peter 1:20

Understand this first about the prophecies in Scripture.

No prophecy came from someone’s own explanation of things.

## 2 Peter 1:21

No one ever produced a prophecy just by wanting to.

The Holy Spirit guided people as they spoke.

Their message came from God.

**Pause and answer**

Who guided the people who spoke for God?
