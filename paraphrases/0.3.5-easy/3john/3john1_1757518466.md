# 3 John 1 — Gaius helps traveling believers

**About this chapter**

The elder writes to his friend Gaius.

Travelers have told the church how Gaius helped them.

## Good news about Gaius

## 3 John 1:1

I am the elder, a leader who cares for believers.

Gaius, my dear friend, I love you in the truth we share.

## 3 John 1:2

Dear friend, I pray that all goes well for you.

I pray for your health too.

I know you are already doing well in your life with God.

## 3 John 1:3

Some believers came and told me about you.

You hold firmly to the truth.

You keep living by it.

Hearing that gave me great joy.

## 3 John 1:4

I call the believers in my care my children.

Nothing gives me greater joy than hearing they walk in the truth.

That means they live by the truth they believe.

**Pause and answer**

What news about Gaius made the elder glad?

## Help the travelers on their way

## 3 John 1:5

Dear friend, you faithfully help the believers who visit you.

You do this even when they are strangers.

## 3 John 1:6

They told the church how you showed them love.

Please help them on the next part of their journey.

Give them the kind of help that honors God.

![A woven basket.](../pictures/basket.svg)

## 3 John 1:7

They set out to serve the name of Jesus.

They accepted nothing from people who did not share their faith.

## 3 John 1:8

So we ought to support travelers like these.

When we help them, we work together for the truth.

**Pause and answer**

How could Gaius share in the travelers’ work?

## Diotrephes turns people away

## 3 John 1:9

I wrote to the church about this.

But Diotrephes wants to be first among them.

He refuses to listen to what we say.

## 3 John 1:10

If I come, I will speak about what Diotrephes is doing.

He spreads hurtful lies about us.

He refuses to welcome the traveling believers.

He stops others who want to welcome them.

He even puts those people out of the church.

**Pause and answer**

What did Diotrephes do to people who wanted to welcome the visitors?

## Follow a good example

## 3 John 1:11

Dear friend, follow the example of people who do good.

Do not copy those who do evil.

Whoever does good belongs to God.

Whoever does evil has not seen God.

## 3 John 1:12

Everyone speaks well of Demetrius.

His life agrees with the truth.

We too speak well of him.

You know that what we say is true.

**Pause and answer**

Who had a good name among the believers?

## Greet each friend by name

## 3 John 1:13

I have much more to tell you.

I would rather put my pen and ink aside.

## 3 John 1:14

I hope to see you soon.

Then we can talk face to face.

Peace to you.

Your friends here send their greetings.

Please greet each of our friends there by name.

**Pause and answer**

How did the elder ask Gaius to greet their friends?
