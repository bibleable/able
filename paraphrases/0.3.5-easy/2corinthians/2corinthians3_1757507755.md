# 2 Corinthians 3 — God’s Spirit gives life

**About this chapter**

Paul asks the believers to picture a letter people can read.

Then he asks them to picture a cloth over someone’s face.

## A letter everyone can read

## 2 Corinthians 3:1

Are we starting to praise ourselves again?

Some people bring letters saying others can trust them.

Do we need such a letter for you?

Do we need one from you?

## 2 Corinthians 3:2

You yourselves are our letter.

We carry that letter in our hearts.

Everyone can see it and read it.

![An open scroll with lines of writing](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## 2 Corinthians 3:3

Anyone can see you are a letter from Christ.

That letter is the result of our work among you.

Christ wrote it with the Spirit of the living God instead of ink.

He wrote on human hearts instead of stone.

**Help with these words**

A letter tells people something about its writer.

Paul says the believers’ lives tell people about Christ.

**Pause and answer**

Who does Paul call a letter from Christ?

## God’s Spirit gives life

## 2 Corinthians 3:4

Through Christ we can say this confidently before God.

## 2 Corinthians 3:5

We cannot take credit for doing this work ourselves.

God gives us the ability to do it.

## 2 Corinthians 3:6

God made us able to serve his new covenant.

This covenant depends on the Spirit.

The written law brings death.

God’s Spirit gives life.

**Help with these words**

A covenant is a lasting agreement.

Here Paul is speaking about God’s agreement with his people.

**Pause and answer**

Who gives Paul the ability to do this work?

## The light on the face of Moses

## 2 Corinthians 3:7

Long ago God gave his people laws carved in stone.

Even the work of giving this law that brought death came with glory.

The face of Moses shone so brightly that the people of Israel could not keep looking at it.

Yet the light was fading.

## 2 Corinthians 3:8

How much greater is the glory of the Spirit’s work!

## 2 Corinthians 3:9

The law that declared people guilty came with glory.

The work that puts people right with God has far greater glory.

## 2 Corinthians 3:10

What once shone with glory seems to have no glory beside it.

The new glory is so much greater.

## 2 Corinthians 3:11

Even the glory that faded was wonderful.

How much greater is the glory that lasts!

**Pause and answer**

What happened to the light on the face of Moses?

## The Lord takes away the veil

## 2 Corinthians 3:12

This hope gives us courage to speak freely.

## 2 Corinthians 3:13

Moses used to cover his face with a veil.

It kept the people from watching the light fade away.

We can speak openly.

## 2 Corinthians 3:14

The people did not understand.

Even today, when they read the old covenant, it is as if the same veil is there.

Only Christ can take it away.

## 2 Corinthians 3:15

Even now, when they read what Moses wrote, a veil covers their hearts.

**Help with these words**

A veil is a cloth that covers the face.

Paul uses a covered heart as a picture of someone who cannot yet understand.

## 2 Corinthians 3:16

Whenever anyone turns to the Lord, the Lord removes the veil.

## 2 Corinthians 3:17

The Lord is the Spirit.

Where the Lord’s Spirit is, people are free.

## 2 Corinthians 3:18

With our faces uncovered, we all reflect the Lord’s glory.

The Lord, who is the Spirit, makes us more like him.

We share more and more in his glory.

**Pause and answer**

What happens when someone turns to the Lord?
