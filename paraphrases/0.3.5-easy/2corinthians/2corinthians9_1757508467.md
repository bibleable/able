# 2 Corinthians 9 — Give with joy

**About this chapter**

Paul wants the promised gift ready before he arrives.

He describes what giving can do for those who receive it.

## Get the promised gift ready

## 2 Corinthians 9:1

You already know about helping God’s people.

I hardly need to write to you about it.

## 2 Corinthians 9:2

I know how much you want to help.

I told the believers in Macedonia about you in Achaia.

You have been ready to give since last year.

Your eagerness moved most of them to help too.

## 2 Corinthians 9:3

Still, I am sending the brothers to you.

I want you to be ready, just as I said.

Then my praise for you will prove true.

## 2 Corinthians 9:4

Some people from Macedonia might come with me.

Imagine if they found the gift was not ready.

After trusting you so much, we would feel ashamed.

You would feel ashamed too.

## 2 Corinthians 9:5

So I asked the brothers to visit you ahead of me.

They can help you prepare the generous gift you promised.

That way you can give freely, without feeling forced.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Paul send the brothers ahead?

## Give what you decide to give

## 2 Corinthians 9:6

Think about a farmer planting seeds.

Plant only a few seeds and you gather a small crop.

Plant plenty of seeds and you gather a large crop.

![A stalk with ripe grain](../pictures/grain.svg)

**Help with these words**

Paul pictures giving as planting seeds.

A gift can lead to much good for others.

## 2 Corinthians 9:7

Each person should decide what to give.

Give willingly, without pressure or regret.

God loves a cheerful giver.

## 2 Corinthians 9:8

God can give you his kindness in full measure.

Then you will have what you need at all times.

You will have plenty for every kind of good work.

## 2 Corinthians 9:9

Scripture tells of giving freely to poor people.

It says that such righteousness lasts forever.

**Help with these words**

Righteousness means doing what is right in God’s sight.

**Pause and answer**

Who should decide what each person gives?

## Sharing leads people to thank God

## 2 Corinthians 9:10

God gives farmers seed to plant and bread to eat.

God will give you seed and increase your supply.

He will make a great harvest grow from the good you do.

## 2 Corinthians 9:11

God will enrich you in every way so you can always share.

When we bring your gift, people will thank God.

## 2 Corinthians 9:12

This work supplies what God’s people need.

It also gives many people a reason to thank God.

## 2 Corinthians 9:13

You say you believe the good news about Christ.

Your gift shows that you live by it.

God’s people will praise him for this.

They will praise him for your generosity to them and to others.

## 2 Corinthians 9:14

They will pray for you with deep affection.

They have seen God’s great kindness to you.

## 2 Corinthians 9:15

Thank God for his gift!

It is greater than words can say.

**Pause and answer**

When the people receive the gift, whom will they thank?
