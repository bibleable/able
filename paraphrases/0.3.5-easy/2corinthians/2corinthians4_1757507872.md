# 2 Corinthians 4 — God gives us strength to keep going

**About this chapter**

Paul explains why he keeps telling people about Jesus.

He remembers what God does for him in times of trouble.

## Paul speaks the truth openly

## 2 Corinthians 4:1

God showed us mercy and gave us this work.

That is why we keep going.

## 2 Corinthians 4:2

We have given up shameful things people do in secret.

We refuse to trick people or twist God’s words.

We speak the truth openly in God’s sight.

We leave it to each person’s conscience to judge us.

## 2 Corinthians 4:3

Some still find our good news hidden behind a veil.

It is hidden from those who are perishing.

## 2 Corinthians 4:4

The god of this age has blinded the minds of unbelievers.

They cannot see the light of the good news.

That light shows the glory of Christ.

Christ shows us what God is like.

**Help with these words**

Paul calls the evil one “the god of this age.”

Here blindness means being unable to understand the good news.

**Pause and answer**

How does Paul handle God’s words?

## God makes his light shine

## 2 Corinthians 4:5

The message we bring is that Jesus Christ is Lord.

We are your servants because of Jesus.

## 2 Corinthians 4:6

God once called light to shine out of darkness.

That same God made his light shine in our hearts.

Now we see God’s glory in the face of Jesus Christ.

## 2 Corinthians 4:7

This treasure is like something precious kept in clay jars.

The jars are like us.

Everyone can see where the great power comes from.

It comes from God.

![A plain clay jar](../pictures/clay-jar.svg)

**Help with these words**

A clay jar can crack or break.

Paul compares himself and his friends to jars because they can be hurt.

The treasure they carry is the good news about Christ.

**Pause and answer**

Where does the power Paul describes come from?

## Trouble does not crush us

## 2 Corinthians 4:8

Trouble presses us from every side.

It has not crushed us.

Sometimes we do not know what to do.

Even then we still have hope.

## 2 Corinthians 4:9

People hunt us down, yet God stays with us.

People knock us down, yet we survive.

## 2 Corinthians 4:10

Our bodies always carry the death of Jesus.

In this way our bodies also show his life.

**Help with these words**

Paul often faces death because he serves Jesus.

His suffering reminds people that Jesus died.

## 2 Corinthians 4:11

We are alive, yet we face death continually for Jesus’ sake.

In these bodies that can die, people can see Jesus’ life.

## 2 Corinthians 4:12

So death is at work in us.

Life is at work in you.

**Pause and answer**

Who stays with Paul and his companions in trouble?

## We trust God and speak

## 2 Corinthians 4:13

Someone wrote in Scripture, “I believed, so I spoke.”

We trust God in the same way.

So we speak too.

## 2 Corinthians 4:14

We know God raised the Lord Jesus from death.

God will also raise us with Jesus.

He will bring us together with you into his presence.

## 2 Corinthians 4:15

All this is for your good.

God’s kindness reaches more and more people.

So more people give thanks and honor God.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did God raise from death?

## What lasts forever

## 2 Corinthians 4:16

That is why we keep going.

Our bodies are wearing out.

Yet inside, God makes us new each day.

## 2 Corinthians 4:17

Our troubles now are light and last a short time compared with the glory ahead.

Through these troubles we gain glory that lasts forever.

Its greatness is beyond anything we can measure.

## 2 Corinthians 4:18

So we give our attention to what we cannot see.

What we see lasts only a while.

What we cannot see lasts forever.

**Pause and answer**

What does God do for Paul and his companions each day?
