# 2 Corinthians 13 — Paul prays for the church

**About this chapter**

Paul writes before his third visit to Corinth.

He ends his letter with a prayer for everyone there.

## Paul warns the church before he comes

## 2 Corinthians 13:1

This will be my third visit to you.

Scripture says that two or three witnesses must confirm each matter.

**Help with these words**

A witness tells what they know about an event.

## 2 Corinthians 13:2

I warned you during my second visit.

From far away I warn you again.

This is for those who sinned before and for everyone else.

When I return, I will deal firmly with anyone who keeps doing wrong.

## 2 Corinthians 13:3

You want proof that Christ speaks through me.

Christ acts with power among you.

## 2 Corinthians 13:4

Christ was weak when people nailed him to the cross.

Now he lives by God’s power.

We too share his weakness.

Yet when we deal with you, we will share his life through God’s power.

**Pause and answer**

By whose power does Christ live?

## Look at your own faith

## 2 Corinthians 13:5

Look closely at yourselves.

Do you trust Christ?

Put yourselves to the test.

Surely you know Jesus Christ is in you, unless you fail that test.

## 2 Corinthians 13:6

I hope you will see that we have passed the test.

## 2 Corinthians 13:7

We pray to God that you will keep from doing wrong.

We want you to do what is right.

That matters even if we seem to have failed the test ourselves.

## 2 Corinthians 13:8

We cannot work against the truth.

We can only work for it.

## 2 Corinthians 13:9

We are glad when you are strong, even when we are weak.

We pray that you will become all you should be.

## 2 Corinthians 13:10

That is why I write while I am away.

I hope I can be gentle when I come.

The Lord gave me authority to help you grow strong.

He never gave it to me to tear you down.

## Live in peace with each other

## 2 Corinthians 13:11

Finally, brothers and sisters, be joyful!

Work to get along fully with each other.

Encourage one another.

Seek agreement and live in peace.

Then the God of love and peace will be with you.

## 2 Corinthians 13:12

Greet one another with a holy kiss.

**Help with these words**

A holy kiss was a way believers greeted each other as family.

## 2 Corinthians 13:13

All God’s people here send you greetings.

## 2 Corinthians 13:14

May the Lord Jesus Christ show you his grace.

May God’s love be with you.

May you all share in the life the Holy Spirit gives you together.
