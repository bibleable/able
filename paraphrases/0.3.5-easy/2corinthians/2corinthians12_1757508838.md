# 2 Corinthians 12 — Christ helps Paul in his weakness

**About this chapter**

Paul remembers asking the Lord for help three times.

Then he tells the church why he wants to visit.

## A man hears words in Paradise

## 2 Corinthians 12:1

I must keep boasting, though it does no good.

Now I will tell you about visions from the Lord.

The Lord has shown things that were hidden.

**Help with these words**

A vision is something God lets a person see.

## 2 Corinthians 12:2

I know a man who belongs to Christ.

Fourteen years ago, God took him up to the third heaven.

Did his body go there too?

I do not know, but God knows.

## 2 Corinthians 12:3

I know this about that man.

Only God knows whether his body went with him.

## 2 Corinthians 12:4

God took him up to Paradise.

There he heard words too wonderful to repeat.

No one has permission to tell them.

## 2 Corinthians 12:5

I could boast about a man like that.

As for myself, I will speak only of my weaknesses.

## 2 Corinthians 12:6

Even if I chose to boast, my words would be true.

Speaking the truth would hardly make me foolish.

Still, I hold back.

Judge me by what you see me do and hear me say.

**Pause and answer**

Who knows whether the man’s body went to Paradise?

## Paul asks the Lord for help

## 2 Corinthians 12:7

The things the Lord showed me were extraordinary.

Something kept me from growing proud of them.

I call it a thorn in my flesh.

A messenger from Satan kept tormenting me.

**Help with these words**

A thorn is a sharp point on a plant.

Paul uses it as a picture of something that keeps hurting him.

He does not name the trouble.

## 2 Corinthians 12:8

Three times I begged the Lord to take it away.

## 2 Corinthians 12:9

The Lord told me, “My grace is enough for you.

My power shows itself fully when you are weak.”

So I gladly speak about my weaknesses.

Then Christ’s power can rest on me.

| Paul’s request | The Lord’s answer |
| --- | --- |
| Take the trouble away. | My grace is enough for you. |

## 2 Corinthians 12:10

For Christ’s sake, I can welcome weakness and insults.

I can face hardship, cruel treatment, and trouble.

When I am weak, Christ makes me strong.

**Pause and answer**

What did the Lord tell Paul when Paul asked for help?

## What the church saw Paul do

## 2 Corinthians 12:11

I sound foolish, but you drove me to this.

You should have spoken up for me.

I am nothing in myself.

Yet those so-called great apostles are no better than I am.

## 2 Corinthians 12:12

You saw the signs of a true apostle among you.

As I patiently kept working, you saw wonders and miracles.

## 2 Corinthians 12:13

Did I treat you worse than the other churches?

The only difference was that you never had to support me.

Please forgive me for that great wrong!

**Help with these words**

Paul speaks with irony here.

He calls it a wrong to make readers think about what he actually did.

He taught them without asking them to pay him.

**Pause and answer**

What signs did the believers see while Paul worked among them?

## Paul cares about the people themselves

## 2 Corinthians 12:14

Now I am ready to visit a third time.

Again, I will pay my own way.

I want you, not the things you own.

Parents save up for their children.

Children should not have to save up for their parents.

## 2 Corinthians 12:15

I will gladly spend everything I have for your sake.

I will even give myself for you.

If I love you more, will you love me less?

## 2 Corinthians 12:16

You know I never made you support me.

But perhaps you think I was clever and tricked you!

## 2 Corinthians 12:17

Did I use anyone I sent to take advantage of you?

## 2 Corinthians 12:18

I asked Titus to visit you.

I sent another believer with him.

Did Titus take advantage of you?

The same Spirit guided us.

Titus and I acted in the same way.

**Pause and answer**

What matters most to Paul when he thinks about his visit?

## What Paul fears he may find

## 2 Corinthians 12:19

Do you think we have been trying to defend ourselves to you?

We belong to Christ, and God hears us speak.

Dear friends, everything we say is to help you grow strong.

## 2 Corinthians 12:20

I fear I may find things I do not want to see.

You may dislike how I respond.

Will I find fights, jealousy, angry outbursts, and rivalry?

Will people spread lies, gossip, act proud, and cause confusion?

## 2 Corinthians 12:21

I fear God may humble me before you when I come again.

I may have to grieve for many who sinned before.

They have kept doing impure things, misusing sex, and giving themselves to uncontrolled desires.

They have not turned away from these things.

**Pause and answer**

Why might Paul grieve when he visits again?
