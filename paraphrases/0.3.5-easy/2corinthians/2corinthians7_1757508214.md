# 2 Corinthians 7 — Titus brings good news from Corinth

**About this chapter**

Paul had written a letter that made the believers sad.

Now Titus tells Paul how the believers responded.

## Paul asks the church to welcome him

## 2 Corinthians 7:1

Dear friends, remember what God has promised us.

Let us put away everything that stains our bodies or spirits.

Because we honor God, let us grow fully holy.

**Help with these words**

To be holy means to belong to God and live in his ways.

## 2 Corinthians 7:2

Make room for us in your hearts.

We have treated no one unfairly.

We have led no one into evil.

We have taken advantage of no one.

## 2 Corinthians 7:3

I say this without blaming you.

As I said before, we love you dearly.

We are with you in life and in death.

## 2 Corinthians 7:4

I trust you deeply.

I am proud of you.

You give me great courage.

Even in all our troubles, I am full of joy.

**Pause and answer**

How does Paul feel about the believers in Corinth?

## Titus brings news from Corinth

## 2 Corinthians 7:5

When we reached Macedonia, we could get no rest.

We faced trouble on every side.

Around us people were fighting.

Inside we felt afraid.

## 2 Corinthians 7:6

God comforts people who feel low.

God comforted us when Titus arrived.

## 2 Corinthians 7:7

You had comforted Titus, and his news comforted us too.

Titus said you missed me and felt deep sorrow.

He told us how much you cared about me.

That gave me even more joy.

| In Corinth | In Macedonia |
| --- | --- |
| The believers comfort Titus. | Titus brings their news to Paul. |

**Pause and answer**

Who came to Paul in Macedonia?

## Sorrow leads to a change

## 2 Corinthians 7:8

My letter made you sad for a little while.

At first I wished I had not written it.

Now I am glad I did.

## 2 Corinthians 7:9

Your pain itself gave me no joy.

I am glad because your sorrow led you to repent.

You felt sorrow as God intended.

So our letter did you no harm.

**Help with these words**

To repent means to turn away from wrong and turn back to God.

## 2 Corinthians 7:10

Sorrow from God leads people to repent.

This leads to salvation and leaves no regret.

The world’s kind of sorrow leads to death.

## 2 Corinthians 7:11

Look how seriously you took the wrong you saw.

You wanted to clear your names.

You felt angry and alarmed about the wrong.

You longed to see me and wanted to put things right.

You showed in every way that you were innocent in this matter.

**Pause and answer**

What did their sorrow lead the believers to do?

## The believers gave Titus joy

## 2 Corinthians 7:12

Someone did wrong and someone else suffered.

My main reason for writing was this.

Before God, you could see for yourselves how much you cared for us.

## 2 Corinthians 7:13

That gave us courage.

Seeing Titus so joyful made us even happier.

All of you helped him feel rested inside.

## 2 Corinthians 7:14

I had told Titus how proud I was of you.

You gave me no cause for shame.

We spoke truthfully to you.

Titus found that our words about you were true too.

## 2 Corinthians 7:15

Titus remembers how you all obeyed.

You welcomed him with such deep respect that you trembled.

Now he loves you even more.

## 2 Corinthians 7:16

It gives me joy to know I can trust you completely.

**Pause and answer**

What does Titus remember about his visit?
