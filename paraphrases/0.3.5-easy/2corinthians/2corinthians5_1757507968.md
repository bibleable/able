# 2 Corinthians 5 — God brings us back to himself

**About this chapter**

Paul compares our present life to living in a tent.

He tells why he hopes for a lasting home with God.

## A tent and a lasting home

## 2 Corinthians 5:1

Our body on earth is like a tent.

Someone can take down a tent.

Even if our body dies, we have a home in heaven.

God himself made that home to last forever.

![A tent beside a house](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

## 2 Corinthians 5:2

Here in our tent we groan.

We long to put on our heavenly home like clothing.

**Help with these words**

The tent pictures the body we have now.

The lasting home and the new clothes picture the life God will give us.

## 2 Corinthians 5:3

Once we put it on, we will be fully clothed.

## 2 Corinthians 5:4

Life in this tent weighs on us.

We groan because we long for more than taking off old clothes.

We want to put on what God gives us.

Then life will take the place of death.

## 2 Corinthians 5:5

God has prepared us for this very thing.

God gave us his Spirit as his pledge.

**Help with these words**

A pledge is something given now as a promise of what will come.

**Pause and answer**

What has God given us as his pledge?

## At home with the Lord

## 2 Corinthians 5:6

So we have courage at all times.

We know that while we live in this body, we are away from the Lord.

## 2 Corinthians 5:7

We live by trusting the Lord, even though we cannot see him.

## 2 Corinthians 5:8

We have courage.

We would rather leave this body and be home with the Lord.

## 2 Corinthians 5:9

Whether we stay or leave, we want to please him.

## 2 Corinthians 5:10

Each of us must stand before Christ as our judge.

Christ will give each person what their actions deserve.

He knows the good and bad we do in this body.

**Pause and answer**

Who will judge what each person has done?

## Christ’s love moves us

## 2 Corinthians 5:11

We know what it means to fear the Lord.

So we try to persuade people.

God knows what we are really like.

I hope you know it deep down too.

## 2 Corinthians 5:12

We have no need to praise ourselves to you again.

We want to give you reason to be proud of us.

Then you can answer people who only care how things look.

They give no thought to what is in someone’s heart.

## 2 Corinthians 5:13

If we seem out of our minds, it is for God.

If we are in our right minds, it is for you.

## 2 Corinthians 5:14

Christ’s love moves us to act.

We are sure that Christ died for everyone.

So all died with him.

## 2 Corinthians 5:15

Christ died for everyone so we would stop living just for ourselves.

Now we can live for Christ.

He died for us, and God raised him to life.

**Pause and answer**

For whom does Paul want us to live?

## God brings people back to himself

## 2 Corinthians 5:16

From now on we look beyond human ways of judging people.

Once we judged Christ that way too.

Now we see him differently.

## 2 Corinthians 5:17

When someone belongs to Christ, God makes that person new.

The old has gone.

Look, the new has come!

## 2 Corinthians 5:18

God did all this.

Through Christ, God brought us back to himself.

Then God gave us the work of helping others come back to him.

## 2 Corinthians 5:19

Through Christ, God was bringing the world back to himself.

He did not count people’s sins against them.

He trusted us with this message of peace with him.

## 2 Corinthians 5:20

We speak for Christ as his messengers.

God himself calls to you through us.

On Christ’s behalf we beg you to return to God and be at peace with him.

## 2 Corinthians 5:21

Christ never sinned.

Yet God made him to be sin for us.

Through him we can share in God’s own righteousness.

**Help with these words**

Righteousness means being right in God’s sight.

**Pause and answer**

Whose message do Paul and his friends bring?
