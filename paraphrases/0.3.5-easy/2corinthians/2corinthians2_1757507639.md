# 2 Corinthians 2 — Show him again that you love him

**About this chapter**

Paul writes about someone who caused the church pain.

He asks the believers to welcome that person with love.

## Paul explains why he wrote

## 2 Corinthians 2:1

I decided against another visit that would bring you pain.

## 2 Corinthians 2:2

If I make you sad, who will cheer me?

You are the very people I have hurt.

## 2 Corinthians 2:3

That is why I wrote to you as I did.

I hoped the people who bring me joy would not make me sad when I came.

I trusted that all of you would share my joy.

## 2 Corinthians 2:4

I was deeply troubled when I wrote to you.

I cried many tears.

I wanted you to know how much I love you.

I had no wish to hurt you.

**Pause and answer**

What did Paul want the believers to know when he wrote?

## Show your love again

## 2 Corinthians 2:5

The person who caused this pain has hurt you all to some degree.

I want to be fair about how much hurt he caused.

## 2 Corinthians 2:6

Most of you agreed to punish him.

That punishment is enough.

## 2 Corinthians 2:7

Now forgive him and comfort him.

Too much sadness could be more than he can bear.

## 2 Corinthians 2:8

Please show him again that you love him.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul ask the church to do for the man now?

## Paul also forgives

## 2 Corinthians 2:9

I wrote to see how you would meet this test.

Would you obey in everything?

## 2 Corinthians 2:10

When you forgive someone, I forgive that person too.

Whatever I have forgiven, I did it for your sake in Christ’s presence.

## 2 Corinthians 2:11

That way Satan cannot trick us.

We know how he tries to deceive us.

**Pause and answer**

When the church forgives someone, what does Paul do?

## Paul looks for Titus

## 2 Corinthians 2:12

I went to Troas to tell the good news about Christ.

There the Lord opened a door for my work.

**Help with these words**

An open door is Paul’s picture of a chance to tell people about Jesus.

## 2 Corinthians 2:13

But I could find no rest.

I could not find my brother Titus there.

So I said goodbye and left for Macedonia.

**Pause and answer**

Who did Paul hope to find in Troas?

## A scent that spreads

## 2 Corinthians 2:14

Thanks be to God!

God always leads us as captives in Christ’s victory procession.

Through us, people everywhere come to know Christ.

That knowledge spreads like a scent through the air.

**Help with these words**

Picture a victorious ruler leading a parade with captives.

Paul pictures himself as someone who belongs to Christ.

## 2 Corinthians 2:15

To God we are the sweet scent of Christ.

We carry it among people God is saving.

We also carry it among people who are perishing.

![A jar for perfume, which has a strong scent](../pictures/perfume-jar.svg)

**Help with these words**

A scent can reach many people from one place.

Paul uses this picture for the message about Christ spreading from person to person.

## 2 Corinthians 2:16

To those who are perishing, the smell brings death.

To those God is saving, the sweet scent brings life.

Who is fit for work like this?

## 2 Corinthians 2:17

Many sell God’s message to make money.

We speak honestly as people God has sent.

We belong to Christ, and God hears what we say.

**Pause and answer**

Who sent Paul and his companions to speak?
