# 2 Corinthians 6 — Open your hearts to us

**About this chapter**

Paul tells the people in Corinth what it costs to serve God.

He asks them to love him and his friends in return.

## God offers help today

## 2 Corinthians 6:1

We work together with God.

We urge you to put God’s gift of grace to use.

**Help with these words**

Grace is the kindness God gives freely.

## 2 Corinthians 6:2

God says, “When the time came to show kindness, I heard you.

When the day came to save you, I helped you.”

Look, now is the time of God’s kindness!

Today is the day of salvation!

**Pause and answer**

When does Paul say God offers to save people?

## Serving God through trouble

## 2 Corinthians 6:3

We take care to put nothing in anyone’s way.

We want no one to blame the work God gave us.

## 2 Corinthians 6:4

Everything we do shows that we serve God.

We keep going through troubles, hardships, and disasters.

## 2 Corinthians 6:5

People have beaten us and put us in prison.

We have faced riots.

We have worked hard, missed sleep, and gone hungry.

## 2 Corinthians 6:6

We serve with pure lives and with understanding.

We show patience and kindness.

The Holy Spirit is with us.

Our love is genuine.

## 2 Corinthians 6:7

We tell the truth and depend on God’s power.

Doing what is right is like having weapons in both hands.

**Help with these words**

Paul pictures doing right as protection when people attack him.

**Pause and answer**

What helps Paul and his friends tell the truth and keep serving?

## What people see in us

## 2 Corinthians 6:8

Some people honor us and others shame us.

Some spread lies about us and others praise us.

People call us liars even though we tell the truth.

## 2 Corinthians 6:9

Some treat us as strangers and others know us well.

We come close to death and still live on.

People punish us and we survive.

## 2 Corinthians 6:10

We feel sorrow, yet we always have joy.

We are poor, yet we make many people rich.

We have nothing, yet we have everything.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul still have when he feels sorrow?

## Open your hearts to us

## 2 Corinthians 6:11

Friends in Corinth, we have spoken freely to you.

Our hearts are wide open to you.

## 2 Corinthians 6:12

We have held back none of our love.

You have been holding back yours.

## 2 Corinthians 6:13

I speak to you as I would to my children.

Please give us the same love in return.

Open your hearts to us too.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul ask his friends to give in return?

## You belong to the living God

## 2 Corinthians 6:14

Do not bind yourselves in an uneven partnership with unbelievers.

How can right and wrong work together?

What do light and darkness share?

![A yoke joins animals for work.](../pictures/job-yoke.svg)

Paul uses the picture of being tied together to explain what an uneven partnership is like.

**Help with these words**

Paul uses the picture of a yoke.

A yoke is a wooden bar that joins two animals to pull together.

## 2 Corinthians 6:15

How could Christ be at peace with Belial?

What faith does a believer share with an unbeliever?

**Help with these words**

Belial is a name for the devil.

## 2 Corinthians 6:16

How could idols belong in God’s temple?

Together we are the temple of the living God.

God said he would live and walk among his people.

He would be their God, and they would be his people.

**Help with these words**

A temple is a place of worship.

Paul calls the believers a temple because God lives among them.

## 2 Corinthians 6:17

So the Lord says, “Come out from among them.

Live apart from them.

Keep away from what is unclean, and I will welcome you.”

## 2 Corinthians 6:18

The Lord who rules over all says, “I will be your Father.

You will be my sons and daughters.”

**Pause and answer**

What does God promise to be to his people?
