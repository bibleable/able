# 2 Corinthians 8 — Finish preparing your gift

**About this chapter**

The churches are collecting a gift for people in need.

Paul tells the believers in Corinth who will help them finish.

## The churches in Macedonia share what they have

## 2 Corinthians 8:1

Brothers and sisters, God has shown kindness to the churches in Macedonia.

We want you to know what happened there.

## 2 Corinthians 8:2

Trouble tested them severely.

They were very poor, yet they were full of joy.

They gave very generously.

## 2 Corinthians 8:3

I can tell you how they gave.

They gave all they could afford and even more.

They chose to do it themselves.

## 2 Corinthians 8:4

They begged us to let them take part.

They wanted to help God’s people in need.

## 2 Corinthians 8:5

They did more than we expected.

First they gave themselves to the Lord.

Then they gave themselves to help us, as God wanted.

**Pause and answer**

Who asked to take part in helping God’s people?

## Finish what you started

## 2 Corinthians 8:6

Titus helped you start collecting your gift.

So we asked Titus to help you finish.

## 2 Corinthians 8:7

You do so well in many things.

You trust God, speak well, and understand much.

You care deeply and show the love we encouraged in you.

Learn to give just as generously.

## 2 Corinthians 8:8

I give you no order about the gift.

Others have been eager to help.

Their example can show how genuine your love is.

## 2 Corinthians 8:9

You know how kind our Lord Jesus Christ has been.

Christ was rich, yet he became poor for you.

Through his poverty you could become rich.

**Help with these words**

Paul points to what Christ gave up for others.

The riches Christ gives include the life people receive from him.

## 2 Corinthians 8:10

Here is what I think will help you.

Last year you were the first to want to give.

You were also the first to start giving.

## 2 Corinthians 8:11

Now finish what you were so eager to start.

Give what you can afford.

![Two small coins](../pictures/two-coins.svg)

## 2 Corinthians 8:12

When someone wants to give, God welcomes the gift.

Give from what you have.

God does not ask for what you lack.

**Pause and answer**

What should a person use to decide how much to give?

## Share so everyone has enough

## 2 Corinthians 8:13

We want things to be fair.

Helping someone else should not leave you in need.

## 2 Corinthians 8:14

Right now you have extra, and they need help.

Later they may have extra when you need help.

Sharing this way makes things fair.

## 2 Corinthians 8:15

Scripture describes people who gathered food.

Those who gathered much had none left over.

Those who gathered little still had enough.

**Pause and answer**

If the believers in Corinth need help later, who could share with them?

## People the churches trust will carry the gift

## 2 Corinthians 8:16

Thank God for Titus!

God gave Titus the same concern for you that I have.

## 2 Corinthians 8:17

Titus welcomed our request to visit you.

He is eager to go and has chosen to come.

## 2 Corinthians 8:18

We are sending another believer with Titus.

All the churches praise his work telling the good news.

## 2 Corinthians 8:19

The churches chose him to travel with us.

Together we look after the gift.

We do this to honor the Lord and show how eager we are to help.

## 2 Corinthians 8:20

We want no one to find fault with how we handle this generous gift.

## 2 Corinthians 8:21

We take care to do what is right before the Lord.

We want people to see that we do right too.

**Pause and answer**

Who chose the believer who will travel with the gift?

## Welcome the messengers

## 2 Corinthians 8:22

We are sending another brother with them too.

Many times he has shown us how eager he is to help.

His great trust in you makes him even more eager.

## 2 Corinthians 8:23

Titus is my partner in the work among you.

The other brothers are messengers from the churches.

They bring honor to Christ.

## 2 Corinthians 8:24

Show these men your love where the churches can see it.

Then everyone will know why we are proud of you.

**Pause and answer**

Who works alongside Paul among the believers in Corinth?
