# 2 Corinthians 10 — Paul explains the work the Lord gave him

**About this chapter**

Some people in Corinth criticize Paul’s letters and his speaking.

Paul explains what the Lord sent him to do.

## Paul asks the believers to listen

## 2 Corinthians 10:1

I, Paul, ask you to listen.

Remember how gentle and kind Christ is.

In person I am humble among you.

From far away I speak boldly.

## 2 Corinthians 10:2

Some say we live by ordinary human standards.

I expect to speak firmly to those people.

Please give me no reason to do that when I come.

## 2 Corinthians 10:3

We live in human bodies like everyone else.

Yet we fight in a different way.

## 2 Corinthians 10:4

God gives us weapons that no human power can supply.

They have power to pull down strong forts.

## 2 Corinthians 10:5

We pull down arguments and proud claims that keep people from knowing God.

We bring every thought under Christ’s rule.

**Help with these words**

Paul pictures proud claims as strong forts.

Answering a false claim is like pulling down its walls.

## 2 Corinthians 10:6

When you have fully obeyed, we will be ready to deal with anyone who still disobeys.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul say his weapons pull down?

## Why the Lord gave Paul authority

## 2 Corinthians 10:7

You are judging by what you see on the outside.

Someone may feel sure they belong to Christ.

Let that person remember that we belong to Christ too.

## 2 Corinthians 10:8

I may say a great deal about the authority the Lord gave us.

I feel no shame about it.

The Lord gave us this authority to help you grow strong.

He never gave it to us to tear you down.

## 2 Corinthians 10:9

I have no wish to frighten you with my letters.

![Writing on a scroll](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## 2 Corinthians 10:10

Some people say this about me.

“Paul writes strong letters that carry weight.

But in person he makes little impression.

His words are hardly worth hearing.”

## 2 Corinthians 10:11

Those people should remember this.

When we come, our actions will match our letters.

**Pause and answer**

What did the Lord give Paul authority to do?

## The work God gave Paul

## 2 Corinthians 10:12

Some people praise themselves.

We would hardly dare compare ourselves with them!

They judge one another by standards they set themselves.

That shows how little they understand.

## 2 Corinthians 10:13

We will speak only about the work God gave us.

That work includes you in Corinth.

## 2 Corinthians 10:14

We came all the way to you.

In fact, we were the first to bring you the good news about Christ.

So our work there is within the limits God set.

## 2 Corinthians 10:15

We take no credit for work others have done.

We hope your faith will grow.

Then we can do much more of our work among you.

## 2 Corinthians 10:16

We hope to tell the good news beyond your region too.

Then we can speak about work we have done ourselves.

## 2 Corinthians 10:17

Scripture says that anyone who boasts should boast about the Lord.

## 2 Corinthians 10:18

Anyone can praise themselves.

What counts is the Lord’s praise.
