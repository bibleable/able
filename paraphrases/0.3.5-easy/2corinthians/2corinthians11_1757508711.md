# 2 Corinthians 11 — Paul speaks up for the church

**About this chapter**

Some teachers in Corinth boast about themselves.

Paul asks the believers to look at how those teachers treat them.

## Paul wants the church to stay faithful to Christ

## 2 Corinthians 11:1

Please put up with a little foolish talk from me.

Indeed, you are already doing that.

## 2 Corinthians 11:2

I care deeply that you stay faithful, just as God does.

I promised you to Christ like a bride promised to one husband.

I want to bring you to Christ pure and faithful.

**Help with these words**

The bride in this picture is the whole church.

Faithfulness means continuing to trust and love Christ.

## 2 Corinthians 11:3

The snake tricked Eve with clever words.

I am afraid someone may trick you too.

Then you may lose your pure and wholehearted love for Christ.

## 2 Corinthians 11:4

Someone may come teaching about a different Jesus from the one we told you about.

You may receive a different spirit or hear a different message.

You put up with all this far too easily.

## 2 Corinthians 11:5

I am as much an apostle as those so-called great apostles.

## 2 Corinthians 11:6

I may lack skill as a speaker.

But I know what I am talking about.

We have shown you that in every way.

**Pause and answer**

Whom does Paul want the believers to stay faithful to?

## Why Paul taught without pay

## 2 Corinthians 11:7

I humbled myself so you could be lifted up.

I taught you God’s good news without charging you.

Was that wrong?

## 2 Corinthians 11:8

You could say I robbed other churches!

I accepted their support while I worked for you.

**Help with these words**

Paul uses the word “robbed” to make a point.

Other churches gave him the help he needed to serve Corinth.

## 2 Corinthians 11:9

When I needed help among you, I asked none of you to support me.

The believers who came from Macedonia brought what I needed.

I will keep doing my work without burdening you.

## 2 Corinthians 11:10

Christ’s truth is in me.

No one in Achaia will silence me about teaching without pay.

## 2 Corinthians 11:11

Does that mean I do not love you?

God knows I love you!

## 2 Corinthians 11:12

I will keep working this way.

Some people boast that they work just as we do.

I will give them no chance to make that claim.

**Pause and answer**

Who supplied what Paul needed while he was in Corinth?

## Some teachers pretend

## 2 Corinthians 11:13

Those people are false apostles.

They deceive people by pretending Christ sent them.

## 2 Corinthians 11:14

Think of Satan himself.

He pretends to be an angel of light.

## 2 Corinthians 11:15

So his servants can pretend to serve what is right.

In the end they will receive what their actions deserve.

**Pause and answer**

What do the false apostles pretend?

## What the church has put up with

## 2 Corinthians 11:16

I say again, please do not take me for a fool.

But even if you do, listen to me anyway.

Let me boast a little too.

## 2 Corinthians 11:17

When I boast so boldly, I speak like a fool.

This is not how the Lord would speak.

## 2 Corinthians 11:18

Many people boast about things people admire.

So I will boast too.

## 2 Corinthians 11:19

You are so wise that you gladly put up with fools!

## 2 Corinthians 11:20

You let people treat you like slaves.

You let them use you and take what is yours.

You even let them act superior and slap your face.

## 2 Corinthians 11:21

How shameful of us to have been too weak for that!

Let me go on speaking like a fool.

Whatever anyone else dares to boast about, so do I.

**Help with these words**

Paul speaks sharply here.

He wants the church to see how wrong it was for those people to hurt them.

**Pause and answer**

How had some people treated the church?

## What Paul went through as Christ’s servant

## 2 Corinthians 11:22

Are those people Hebrews?

So am I.

Are they Israelites?

So am I.

Is Abraham their ancestor?

He is mine too.

## 2 Corinthians 11:23

Do they call themselves Christ’s servants?

I sound out of my mind, but I have served even more.

I worked harder and went to prison more often.

People beat me more severely.

Many times I came close to death.

## 2 Corinthians 11:24

Five times Jewish authorities had me whipped.

Each time I received thirty-nine lashes.

## 2 Corinthians 11:25

Three times people beat me with rods.

Once people threw stones at me.

Three times my ship broke apart at sea.

Once I spent a night and a day in the open sea.

## 2 Corinthians 11:26

I traveled often.

Rivers and robbers put me in danger.

I faced danger from my own people and from other nations.

I faced danger in cities, in the countryside, and at sea.

Even people pretending to be fellow believers put me in danger.

## 2 Corinthians 11:27

I worked until I was worn out.

I often went without sleep.

I knew hunger and thirst and often had no food.

I was cold and had nothing to cover me.

## 2 Corinthians 11:28

Besides all this, I worry about all the churches every day.

## 2 Corinthians 11:29

When someone is weak, I feel weak with them.

When someone leads a believer into sin, I feel deep grief.

**Pause and answer**

What weighs on Paul every day?

## Paul tells how he escaped

## 2 Corinthians 11:30

If I must boast, I will tell what shows my weakness.

## 2 Corinthians 11:31

God knows I am telling the truth.

He is the God and Father of the Lord Jesus.

He deserves praise forever.

## 2 Corinthians 11:32

In Damascus the governor served King Aretas.

The governor had people watch the city to arrest me.

## 2 Corinthians 11:33

But people lowered me in a basket through a window in the city wall.

That is how I escaped the governor.
