# 2 Corinthians 1 — God comforts us in trouble

**About this chapter**

Paul and Timothy write to believers in Corinth and nearby towns.

Paul remembers the people who prayed for him.

## Paul and Timothy send greetings

## 2 Corinthians 1:1

I am Paul.

God chose me to be an apostle of Christ Jesus.

Our brother Timothy joins me in writing this letter.

We write to God’s church in Corinth.

We also greet all God’s people throughout Achaia.

**Help with these words**

An apostle is someone Jesus sends to speak for him.

Achaia was the region around Corinth.

## 2 Corinthians 1:2

May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you grace and peace.

**Pause and answer**

Who joins Paul in sending this letter?

## God comforts us so we can comfort others

## 2 Corinthians 1:3

Praise God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.

He is a Father who cares when we hurt.

All comfort comes from him.

## 2 Corinthians 1:4

God comforts us in every trouble we face.

Then we can comfort someone else in trouble.

We give them the comfort God gave us.

## 2 Corinthians 1:5

We share deeply in Christ’s suffering.

Through Christ we also receive more than enough comfort.

## 2 Corinthians 1:6

When we suffer, it serves your comfort and salvation.

When God comforts us, you too receive comfort.

That helps you bear the same troubles we face.

**Help with these words**

Salvation means God saving people.

## 2 Corinthians 1:7

We have good reason to hope for you.

You share our troubles.

We know you will share our comfort too.

**Pause and answer**

When God comforts us, who else can we help?

## Paul remembers a time of danger

## 2 Corinthians 1:8

Brothers and sisters, we want you to know what happened in the province of Asia.

Our trouble was more than we could bear.

We thought we would never get out alive.

## 2 Corinthians 1:9

It felt as though someone had sentenced us to death.

This taught us to rely on God instead of ourselves.

God raises the dead to life.

## 2 Corinthians 1:10

God rescued us from that terrible danger.

He will rescue us again.

We have placed our hope in him.

## 2 Corinthians 1:11

You help us by praying for us.

Many people will thank God for answering their prayers for us.

**Pause and answer**

How can the people in Corinth help Paul and his companions?

## Paul wants the church to understand him

## 2 Corinthians 1:12

Here is what we take pride in.

Our consciences are clear about how we have lived.

God helped us live holy and honest lives among people, especially among you.

We relied on God’s kindness instead of human cleverness.

**Help with these words**

Your conscience is your sense of right and wrong.

## 2 Corinthians 1:13

We write words you can read and understand.

I hope you will come to understand us fully.

## 2 Corinthians 1:14

You already understand us in part.

When our Lord Jesus comes, you can take pride in us.

We will take pride in you too.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul hope the readers will understand more fully?

## Paul had planned two visits

## 2 Corinthians 1:15

I felt sure of this when I made my plans.

I wanted to visit you first.

That way you could receive a blessing twice.

## 2 Corinthians 1:16

I planned to see you on my way to Macedonia.

I planned to visit you again on my way back.

Then you could help me travel on to Judea.

| On the way there | On the way back |
| --- | --- |
| Corinth, then Macedonia | Corinth again, then Judea |

## 2 Corinthians 1:17

Do you think I made those plans carelessly?

Do I make plans like someone who says yes and means no?

## 2 Corinthians 1:18

God keeps his word.

Just as surely, our message to you does not say both yes and no.

**Pause and answer**

When did Paul plan to visit Corinth a second time?

## God keeps his promises

## 2 Corinthians 1:19

Silvanus, Timothy, and I told you about Jesus Christ.

Jesus is God’s Son.

In him God’s answer has always been yes.

## 2 Corinthians 1:20

Through Christ, God says yes to every promise he made.

Through Christ we say “Amen” and give God glory.

## 2 Corinthians 1:21

God helps us and you stand firm together in Christ.

God anointed us.

**Help with these words**

People poured oil on someone to set them apart for special work.

They called this anointing.

## 2 Corinthians 1:22

God marked us as his own, like someone using a seal.

God gave us his Spirit in our hearts.

The Spirit is his pledge of what is still to come.

**Help with these words**

A seal leaves a mark showing who something belongs to.

A pledge is something you give to show you will keep a promise.

**Pause and answer**

What has God given as his pledge?

## Why Paul stayed away

## 2 Corinthians 1:23

God knows I am telling the truth.

I stayed away from Corinth to spare you more pain.

## 2 Corinthians 1:24

We do not tell you what you must believe as though we ruled over you.

We work alongside you for your joy.

You stand firm because you trust Christ.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Paul stay away from Corinth?
