# 2 Timothy 2 — Pass on what you have learned

**About this chapter**

Paul asks Timothy to pass on what he has learned.

He shows Timothy how to teach even when people disagree.

## Teach people who can teach others

## 2 Timothy 2:1

Timothy, my child, draw strength from the grace Christ Jesus gives you.

## 2 Timothy 2:2

Many people heard what I taught you.

Pass that teaching on to people you can trust.

Choose people who can teach others too.

**Pause and answer**

Who should receive the teaching Timothy heard from Paul?

## A soldier, an athlete, and a farmer

## 2 Timothy 2:3

Share my hardships like a good soldier serving Christ Jesus.

## 2 Timothy 2:4

A soldier wants to please the person who enlisted him.

So he avoids getting tied up in work outside his service.

## 2 Timothy 2:5

Think also of an athlete competing for a crown.

To win the prize, the athlete must follow the rules.

## 2 Timothy 2:6

A farmer who works hard should receive the first share of the crop.

![A stalk of grain ready for harvest](../pictures/grain.svg)

## 2 Timothy 2:7

Think about these examples.

The Lord will help you understand them all.

**Pause and answer**

Who should receive the first share of the crop?

## Remember Jesus Christ

## 2 Timothy 2:8

Remember Jesus Christ.

He came from David’s family, and God raised him from death.

This is the good news I tell.

## 2 Timothy 2:9

Telling it has brought me suffering.

People have chained me like a criminal.

But no one can chain God’s message.

**Help with these words**

Chains can hold Paul in prison.

Other people can still hear God’s words and pass them on.

## 2 Timothy 2:10

So I endure all this for the people God has chosen.

I want them too to receive salvation in Christ Jesus and share his glory forever.

## 2 Timothy 2:11

Here is a saying you can trust.

If we died with Christ, we will also live with him.

## 2 Timothy 2:12

If we keep going, we will also rule with him.

If we deny knowing him, he will deny knowing us.

## 2 Timothy 2:13

Even if we break faith, Christ stays faithful.

He cannot go against who he is.

**Pause and answer**

What can still spread while Paul is in chains?

## Handle the truth with care

## 2 Timothy 2:14

Keep reminding believers of these things.

Warn them before God against fighting over words.

Such arguments do no good and ruin those who listen.

## 2 Timothy 2:15

Work hard to gain God’s approval.

Be a worker who can face him without shame.

Teach his true message accurately.

## 2 Timothy 2:16

Keep away from empty talk that dishonors God.

It leads people further from living for him.

## 2 Timothy 2:17

Such talk spreads like a disease that kills part of the body.

Hymenaeus and Philetus talk this way.

## 2 Timothy 2:18

They have left the truth.

They say the dead have already risen.

Their teaching is shaking some people’s faith.

**Help with these words**

The resurrection is God raising the dead to life.

## 2 Timothy 2:19

Yet the foundation God laid stands firm.

These words mark it as God’s own. “The Lord knows his own.”

It also says, “Everyone who calls on the Lord must turn away from evil.”

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul tell Timothy to do with God’s message?

## Ready for good work

## 2 Timothy 2:20

A large house has dishes of gold and silver.

It also has dishes of wood and clay.

Some are for special use and others for everyday tasks.

**Help with these words**

The Master in this picture is the Lord.

Paul compares a person ready to serve him to a dish ready for use.

## 2 Timothy 2:21

Leave behind whatever makes you unfit to serve.

Then you will be like a dish set apart for special use.

You will be useful to the Master and ready for every good work.

## 2 Timothy 2:22

Run from harmful desires that tempt you when you are young.

Seek to do right, trust God, love others, and live in peace.

Do this with people who sincerely call on the Lord.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul say a person can be ready for?

## Correct others gently

## 2 Timothy 2:23

Stay out of foolish arguments about things people do not understand.

You know such arguments start quarrels.

## 2 Timothy 2:24

Someone who serves the Lord must avoid quarrels.

Be kind to everyone, able to teach, and patient when wronged.

## 2 Timothy 2:25

Gently correct those who oppose you.

God may help them turn from wrong and recognize the truth.

## 2 Timothy 2:26

Then they can come to their senses and escape the devil’s trap.

He has held them captive to do what he wants.

**Pause and answer**

How should Timothy correct someone who opposes him?
