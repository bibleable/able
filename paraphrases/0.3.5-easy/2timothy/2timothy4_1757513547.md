# 2 Timothy 4 — Finish the work God gave you

**About this chapter**

Paul asks Timothy to keep teaching with patience.

He also asks him to come for a visit before winter.

## Teach with patience

## 2 Timothy 4:1

I give you this charge before God and Christ Jesus.

Christ Jesus will judge the living and the dead.

Remember that he will come and rule as King.

## 2 Timothy 4:2

Tell people God’s message.

Be ready when the time seems right and when it does not.

Point out wrong and warn people to turn from it.

Encourage people and teach them with great patience.

## 2 Timothy 4:3

A time will come when people refuse to listen to sound teaching.

They will gather teachers who tell them what they want to hear.

## 2 Timothy 4:4

They will stop listening to the truth.

They will turn to made-up stories.

## 2 Timothy 4:5

You, Timothy, must keep a clear head in every situation.

Keep going through hardship.

Tell people the good news.

Finish the work God gave you.

**Pause and answer**

How should Timothy teach someone who needs correction?

## Paul looks back on his life

## 2 Timothy 4:6

My life is already being poured out like a drink offered to God.

The time for me to leave this life is near.

## 2 Timothy 4:7

I have fought the good fight.

I have finished the race.

I have kept trusting God.

**Help with these words**

Paul pictures his life of service as a race.

He is near the end and has stayed faithful.

## 2 Timothy 4:8

The crown of righteousness is waiting for me.

The Lord judges fairly.

He will give me that crown on the day he comes.

He will also give it to everyone who longs to see him come.

**Pause and answer**

Who else will receive the crown Paul hopes for?

## Bring Mark and my cloak

## 2 Timothy 4:9

Do your best to come to me soon.

## 2 Timothy 4:10

Demas loved this present world and left me.

He went to Thessalonica.

Crescens went to Galatia, and Titus went to Dalmatia.

## 2 Timothy 4:11

Only Luke is here with me.

Find Mark and bring him with you.

Mark can help me in my work.

## 2 Timothy 4:12

I sent Tychicus to Ephesus.

## 2 Timothy 4:13

Bring the cloak I left with Carpus in Troas.

Bring my scrolls too, especially the ones made of parchment.

![A cloak like the coat Paul asked for](../pictures/cloak.svg)

**Help with these words**

A cloak is an outer coat.

Parchment is animal skin prepared for writing.

**Pause and answer**

Why does Paul want Timothy to bring Mark?

## The Lord stood by Paul

## 2 Timothy 4:14

Alexander, the man who works with copper, did me great harm.

The Lord will repay him for what he did.

## 2 Timothy 4:15

You too must be careful around Alexander.

He strongly opposed our message.

## 2 Timothy 4:16

The first time I spoke in my defense, everyone left me alone.

May God forgive them for leaving.

## 2 Timothy 4:17

But the Lord stayed with me and gave me strength.

I could tell the whole message for all the Gentiles to hear.

The Lord rescued me from the lion’s mouth.

**Help with these words**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish.

Paul pictures his escape from danger as a rescue from a lion’s mouth.

## 2 Timothy 4:18

The Lord will rescue me from every evil deed.

He will bring me safely into his kingdom in heaven.

May he receive glory forever and ever.

Amen.

**Pause and answer**

Who stayed with Paul when everyone else left?

## Come before winter

## 2 Timothy 4:19

Greet Prisca and Aquila for me.

Greet everyone in the household of Onesiphorus.

## 2 Timothy 4:20

Erastus stayed in Corinth.

Trophimus was sick, so I left him in Miletus.

## 2 Timothy 4:21

Do your best to come before winter.

Eubulus, Pudens, Linus, and Claudia send greetings.

All the other believers here greet you too.

## 2 Timothy 4:22

May the Lord be with you in spirit, Timothy.

May God show grace to all of you.

**Pause and answer**

When does Paul hope Timothy will arrive?
