# 2 Timothy 3 — Hold on to what you learned

**About this chapter**

Paul warns Timothy about people who mislead others.

He reminds Timothy where he first learned to trust God.

## Hard times will come

## 2 Timothy 3:1

Timothy, remember this.

In the last days, hard times will come.

## 2 Timothy 3:2

People will care only about themselves and their money.

They will boast and act proud.

They will insult others and disobey their parents.

They will show no thanks and have no respect for what is holy.

## 2 Timothy 3:3

They will show no love and refuse to forgive.

They will tell lies about others and lose control of themselves.

They will act cruelly and hate what is good.

## 2 Timothy 3:4

They will betray people who trust them.

They will act without thinking and think too highly of themselves.

They will love pleasure more than they love God.

## 2 Timothy 3:5

They will look as if they serve God.

Yet they will deny the power of living for him.

Keep away from such people.

**Pause and answer**

What will these people love more than God?

## People who use their teaching to control others

## 2 Timothy 3:6

Some of these people quietly gain a place in people’s homes.

There they gain control over women who are easy to mislead.

These women feel the weight of their sins.

Many desires pull them in different directions.

## 2 Timothy 3:7

They keep learning, yet they never come to know the truth.

## 2 Timothy 3:8

Jannes and Jambres stood against Moses.

In the same way, these men stand against the truth.

Their thinking has gone wrong.

Their faith fails the test.

## 2 Timothy 3:9

They will not get much further.

Everyone will see how foolish they are, just as people saw it with Jannes and Jambres.

**Pause and answer**

Who stood against Moses?

## Timothy remembers Paul’s example

## 2 Timothy 3:10

You know what I taught and how I lived.

You saw what I aimed for and how I trusted God.

You saw my patience and love.

You saw me keep going.

## 2 Timothy 3:11

You know how people made me suffer in Antioch, Iconium, and Lystra.

I went through terrible trouble there.

Yet the Lord rescued me each time.

## 2 Timothy 3:12

People will persecute everyone who wants to live for God in Christ Jesus.

**Help with these words**

To persecute people means to treat them cruelly because of what they believe.

## 2 Timothy 3:13

Evil people and people who pretend to be trustworthy will grow worse.

They fool others, and others fool them.

**Pause and answer**

Who rescued Paul when people made him suffer?

## Keep learning from Scripture

## 2 Timothy 3:14

Timothy, hold on to what you learned and came to believe.

You know the people who taught you.

## 2 Timothy 3:15

You have known the holy writings since you were very small.

They can give you the wisdom to trust Christ Jesus for salvation.

![A scroll open for reading](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## 2 Timothy 3:16

All Scripture comes from the breath of God.

It teaches us and shows us where we are wrong.

It corrects us and trains us to do right.

**Help with these words**

Scripture means the holy writings.

“God’s breath” tells us that the message comes from God.

## 2 Timothy 3:17

In this way Scripture prepares the person who serves God.

It gives them what they need for every good work.

**Pause and answer**

What can Scripture help you do when you are wrong?
