# 2 Timothy 1 — Keep using the gift God gave you

**About this chapter**

Paul writes to Timothy from prison.

He remembers the people who taught Timothy to trust God and the friend who came looking for Paul.

## Paul remembers Timothy and his family

## 2 Timothy 1:1

I am Paul.

God chose me to be an apostle of Christ Jesus.

God promised us life in Christ Jesus.

**Help with these words**

An apostle is someone Jesus sends to speak for him.

## 2 Timothy 1:2

Timothy, I love you like my own child.

May God our Father and Christ Jesus our Lord give you grace, mercy, and peace.

## 2 Timothy 1:3

I serve God with a clear conscience, as my ancestors did.

Night and day I remember you in my prayers.

I thank God for you.

## 2 Timothy 1:4

I remember your tears.

I long to see you.

Seeing you would fill me with joy.

## 2 Timothy 1:5

I remember how sincerely you trust God.

Your grandmother Lois trusted him first.

Your mother Eunice trusted him too.

I am sure that the same faith lives in you.

| Timothy’s grandmother | Timothy’s mother | Paul’s friend |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lois | Eunice | Timothy |

**Pause and answer**

Who in Timothy’s family trusted God before him?

## Use the gift God gave you

## 2 Timothy 1:6

So remember the gift God gave you when I laid my hands on you.

Care for that gift as you would fan a fire into flame.

**Help with these words**

A fire can burn brighter when air reaches it.

Paul wants Timothy to keep using his gift so it grows stronger.

## 2 Timothy 1:7

God gave us a spirit of power, love, and self-control.

We need not let fear rule us.

## 2 Timothy 1:8

Speak about our Lord without shame.

Do not be ashamed of me in prison for him.

Rely on God’s power and share in suffering for the good news.

## 2 Timothy 1:9

God saved us and called us to live holy lives.

We did nothing to earn this.

Before time began, God planned to give us this grace in Christ Jesus.

## 2 Timothy 1:10

Now our Savior Christ Jesus has come and shown us that grace.

Christ broke the power of death.

Through the good news he showed us life that never ends.

## 2 Timothy 1:11

God sent me as an apostle to announce and teach this good news.

**Pause and answer**

What gives Timothy strength to share in suffering?

## Keep the good teaching safe

## 2 Timothy 1:12

That is why I suffer.

Even so, I feel no shame.

I know the one I trust.

I am sure he can guard what I have placed in his care until that day.

**Help with these words**

Here “that day” means the day when Christ comes as judge.

## 2 Timothy 1:13

Follow the example of the sound teaching you heard from me.

Hold to it with the faith and love you have in Christ Jesus.

## 2 Timothy 1:14

God trusted you with something precious.

Guard it with the help of the Holy Spirit who lives in us.

**Pause and answer**

Who helps Timothy guard what God gave him?

## A friend looks for Paul

## 2 Timothy 1:15

You know everyone in the province of Asia turned away from me.

Phygelus and Hermogenes were among them.

## 2 Timothy 1:16

May the Lord show mercy to the household of Onesiphorus.

Onesiphorus often gave me fresh courage.

He felt no shame about visiting me in chains.

## 2 Timothy 1:17

When Onesiphorus came to Rome, he kept looking until he found me.

## 2 Timothy 1:18

May the Lord show him mercy on that day.

You know how much Onesiphorus helped me in Ephesus.

**Pause and answer**

What did Onesiphorus do when he reached Rome?
