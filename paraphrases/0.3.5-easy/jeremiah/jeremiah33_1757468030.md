# Jeremiah 33 — Joy will return to Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

Jerusalem is under attack. God tells Jeremiah that people will celebrate weddings there again. God also makes a promise about a ruler from David's family.

## God promises to heal his people

## Jeremiah 33:1

The guards still held Jeremiah in their courtyard.

The LORD spoke to Jeremiah a second time.

## Jeremiah 33:2

The LORD said, “I made the earth.

I shaped it and set it firmly in place.

My name is the LORD.

## Jeremiah 33:3

“Call to me, and I will answer you.

I will show you great things you do not know.

You could never discover them on your own.”

## Jeremiah 33:4

The LORD, the God of Israel, spoke about Jerusalem.

People had torn down houses and royal palaces there.

They needed to defend the city against swords and ramps.

The attackers used ramps to reach the city walls.

## Jeremiah 33:5

The LORD said, “The soldiers of Babylon are coming to fight.

Those places will fill with the bodies of the dead.

In my great anger, I will strike those people down.

I have turned away from this city because its people do such evil.

## Jeremiah 33:6

“Yet I will bring healing to this city.

I will make its people well again.

They will know deep peace and learn they can trust my word.

## Jeremiah 33:7

“I will bring the people of Judah and Israel back from captivity.

I will build them up again as they were before.

## Jeremiah 33:8

“They chose to disobey me in many ways.

I will wash away their guilt.

I will forgive all their sins against me.

## Jeremiah 33:9

“This city will bring me joy.

People everywhere will hear what I do for Jerusalem.

They will praise me and honor my name.

They will tremble with wonder at all the good things I give the city.”

## People will celebrate weddings again

## Jeremiah 33:10

The LORD said, “You say this land is empty.

Look at Judah's towns and Jerusalem's streets.

No people or animals live there now.

Yet you will hear voices there again.

## Jeremiah 33:11

“You will hear people celebrating.

You will hear brides and grooms at their weddings.

People will bring gifts of thanks to my temple.

They will say, ‘Thank the LORD who commands heaven's armies.

The LORD is good. His faithful love lasts forever.’

I will bring the people back from captivity.

Their land will be as it was before.”

## Jeremiah 33:12

The LORD who commands heaven's armies said, “This land and its towns lie empty.

Yet grass will grow there for the sheep.

Shepherds will lead their flocks there to rest.

## Jeremiah 33:13

“Picture shepherds counting their sheep as each one passes.

You will see them in the hills and foothills.

You will see them in the dry southern region called the Negev.

They will be in Benjamin and the towns around Jerusalem.

They will be in Judah's towns too.”

## A ruler from David's family

## Jeremiah 33:14

The LORD said, “I made a good promise to Israel and Judah.

The time is coming when I will keep it.

## Jeremiah 33:15

“In those days a new branch will grow from David's family.”

The branch is a picture of a new ruler.

God said, “This ruler will do what is right.

He will treat people fairly throughout the land.

![A branch with new growth. God promises a new ruler from David's family.](../pictures/branch.svg)

## Jeremiah 33:16

“In those days I will save Judah.

The people of Jerusalem will live in safety.

People will call Jerusalem ‘The LORD Our Righteousness.’

That name points to the LORD who makes us right with him.

## Jeremiah 33:17

“There will always be a man from David's family to rule Israel.

## Jeremiah 33:18

“There will always be priests from Levi's family to serve me.

They will burn offerings on the altar.

They will burn gifts of grain and bring other sacrifices.”

## God's promise is as sure as day and night

## Jeremiah 33:19

The LORD spoke to Jeremiah again.

## Jeremiah 33:20

“I made a covenant with day and night.

A covenant is a firm promise.

Could you break it and stop day and night from coming on time?

## Jeremiah 33:21

“Only then could you break my promise to David my servant.

Only then could David lack a son to rule after him.

My promise to the priests from Levi's family is just as firm.

## Jeremiah 33:22

“No one can count all the stars in the sky.

No one can measure all the sand by the sea.

That is how large I will make David's family.

I will also give the Levites who serve me a great many descendants.”

## God will show his people mercy

## Jeremiah 33:23

The LORD spoke to Jeremiah once more.

## Jeremiah 33:24

“Have you heard what these people say?

They say I have rejected the two families I chose.

They look down on my people.

They no longer see them as a nation.

## Jeremiah 33:25

“I made my promise about day and night.

I set the order for the heavens and the earth.

## Jeremiah 33:26

“My promise to Jacob's family and David's family is just as sure.

I will keep choosing rulers from David's family.

They will lead the descendants of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

I will bring my people back from captivity.

I will show them mercy.”
