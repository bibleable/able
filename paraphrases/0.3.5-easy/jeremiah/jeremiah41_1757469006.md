# Jeremiah 41 — Johanan brings the captives back

**About this chapter**

Ishmael kills Gedaliah, the governor of Judah. He takes the people of Mizpah away. Johanan sets out to find them.

## Gedaliah shares a meal

## Jeremiah 41:1

In the seventh month, Ishmael came to Mizpah with ten men.

Ishmael was Nethaniah’s son and Elishama’s grandson.

He belonged to the royal family.

He had served as one of the king’s chief officers.

Gedaliah son of Ahikam ate a meal with them.

## Jeremiah 41:2

Ishmael and his ten men got up.

They killed Gedaliah with their swords.

Gedaliah was Shaphan’s grandson.

Babylon’s king had chosen him to govern Judah.

## Jeremiah 41:3

Ishmael also killed the people of Judah who were with Gedaliah.

He killed the Chaldean soldiers there too.

These soldiers served Babylon.

## A group of men arrives

## Jeremiah 41:4

Two days after Gedaliah’s death, the news had not spread.

## Jeremiah 41:5

Eighty men came from Shechem, Shiloh, and Samaria.

They had shaved their beards and torn their clothes.

They had cut their skin to show their sorrow.

They brought grain and frankincense to the LORD’s house.

Frankincense gives off a sweet smell when it burns.

## Jeremiah 41:6

Ishmael went out from Mizpah to meet the men.

He wept as he walked.

He told them, “Come to Gedaliah son of Ahikam.”

## Jeremiah 41:7

The men followed him into the city.

There Ishmael and his men killed the visitors.

They threw their bodies into a cistern.

A cistern is a pit for storing water.

## Jeremiah 41:8

Ten visitors asked Ishmael to spare them.

They said, “We have food hidden in a field.

We have wheat, barley, oil, and honey.”

Ishmael let those ten live.

![A head of grain, like the grain the men had stored.](../pictures/grain.svg)

## Jeremiah 41:9

The cistern was large.

Long ago King Asa had ordered people to dig it.

Asa feared an attack from King Baasha of Israel.

Now Ishmael filled the cistern with the people he had killed.

## The people leave Mizpah

## Jeremiah 41:10

Ishmael took everyone left in Mizpah prisoner.

The king’s daughters were among them.

Nebuzaradan, the captain of the guard, had put Gedaliah in charge of them.

Ishmael took them toward the land of Ammon.

## Jeremiah 41:11

Johanan son of Kareah heard what Ishmael had done.

The other army commanders heard too.

## Jeremiah 41:12

Johanan and the commanders took all their men to fight Ishmael.

They found him near the large pool at Gibeon.

## Jeremiah 41:13

The prisoners saw Johanan and the army commanders.

They were glad to see them.

## Jeremiah 41:14

All the prisoners from Mizpah turned around.

They went over to Johanan.

## Jeremiah 41:15

Ishmael and eight of his men escaped.

They went to the people of Ammon.

## The people stop near Bethlehem

## Jeremiah 41:16

Johanan and the commanders led the people away from Gibeon.

These were the people Ishmael took after he killed Gedaliah.

They included soldiers, women, children, and royal officials.

## Jeremiah 41:17

They stopped at Geruth Chimham, near Bethlehem.

They planned to go on to Egypt.

## Jeremiah 41:18

They were afraid of the Chaldeans.

Babylon’s king had chosen Gedaliah to govern Judah.

Now Ishmael had killed him.
