# Jeremiah 16 — God tells Jeremiah why Judah will lose its home

**About this chapter**

God tells Jeremiah to stay away from funerals and feasts. He explains why the people will have to leave their land. He also promises to bring them home again.

## Instructions for Jeremiah

## Jeremiah 16:1

Jeremiah said, “The LORD spoke to me.”

## Jeremiah 16:2

The LORD said, “Jeremiah, do not marry here.

Do not have sons or daughters in this place.”

## Jeremiah 16:3

Then the LORD spoke about the children born there.

He also spoke about their mothers and fathers.

## Jeremiah 16:4

“Deadly diseases will take their lives.

No one will mourn them or bury them.

Their bodies will lie like dung on the ground.

Others will die in war or from hunger.

Birds and wild animals will feed on their bodies.

## Jeremiah 16:5

“Stay away from houses where families gather for a funeral meal.

Do not go there to grieve or comfort them.

I have taken my peace away from this people.

I have withdrawn my faithful love and tender care.

## Jeremiah 16:6

“People will die here, from the greatest to the least.

No one will bury them or mourn for them.

No one will cut themselves or shave their head as a sign of grief.

## Jeremiah 16:7

“No one will bring food to comfort a grieving family.

Even those who lose a parent will receive no cup to drink.

## Jeremiah 16:8

“Stay away from houses where people are celebrating too.

Do not sit down to eat and drink with them.”

## Jeremiah 16:9

The LORD commands heaven's armies. He is the God of Israel.

He said, “In your lifetime you will see the celebrations stop.

I will silence the sounds of joy here.

You will no longer hear brides and grooms celebrating.”

## The people ask what they have done

## Jeremiah 16:10

The LORD told Jeremiah, “Give the people this message.

They will ask, ‘Why does the LORD say such trouble is coming?

What have we done wrong against our God?’

## Jeremiah 16:11

“Tell them my answer. ‘Your ancestors left me.

They followed other gods and worshiped and served them.

They turned away from me and ignored my teaching.

## Jeremiah 16:12

“‘You have done even worse than your ancestors.

Each of you insists on doing the evil you want to do.

You refuse to listen to me.

## Jeremiah 16:13

“‘So I will drive you out of this land.

You will go where neither you nor your ancestors have lived.

There you will serve other gods day and night.

I will show you no favor.’”

## God promises a journey home

## Jeremiah 16:14

The LORD said, “People now say, ‘As surely as the LORD lives!

He brought the Israelites out of Egypt.’

The day will come when they use different words.

## Jeremiah 16:15

“They will say, ‘As surely as the LORD lives!

He brought the Israelites home from the north.

He brought them back from every land where he had sent them.’

I will bring them home to the land I gave their ancestors.

## God sees what the people do

## Jeremiah 16:16

“But first I will send many fishermen to catch them.

Then I will send hunters to search the mountains and hills.

They will find people even in cracks in the rocks.”

The fishermen and hunters picture people coming to capture them.

![A fishing net. Jeremiah compares the capture of the people to catching fish.](../pictures/net.svg)

## Jeremiah 16:17

God said, “I see everything my people do.

They cannot hide from me.

I see the wrong they have done.

## Jeremiah 16:18

“First I will repay them double for their sins.

They have spoiled my land with their lifeless idols.

They have filled the land that belongs to me with things I hate.”

## People from far away will come to God

## Jeremiah 16:19

Jeremiah prayed, “LORD, you give me strength.

You protect me like a strong fortress.

When trouble comes, I find safety with you.

People will come to you from earth's farthest places.

They will say, ‘Our ancestors handed down lies to us.

Their idols were worthless. They could never help us.

## Jeremiah 16:20

“‘Can people make their own gods?

The things we make are not gods.’”

## Jeremiah 16:21

The LORD said, “Now I will teach them.

I will show them how great my power is.

Then they will know my name is the LORD.”
