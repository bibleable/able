# Jeremiah 47 — An army comes like a flood

**About this chapter**

The LORD gives Jeremiah a message about the Philistines. Jeremiah describes an army coming into their land. It will spread like water pouring over a riverbank.

## The people hear the army coming

## Jeremiah 47:1

Jeremiah was a prophet, someone who spoke for God.

The LORD gave Jeremiah a message about the Philistines.

This happened before Pharaoh attacked the city of Gaza.

Pharaoh was the king of Egypt.

## Jeremiah 47:2

The LORD says, “Look! Water is rising in the north.

It rushes over the riverbanks.

It covers the land and everything on it.

It fills the cities where the people live.

People everywhere in the land cry out in grief.”

The flood is a picture of the coming army.

![A river within its banks, then spreading over the land.](../pictures/river-overflows.svg)

## Jeremiah 47:3

The people hear horses pounding the ground.

War chariots rumble past on rattling wheels.

Fathers drop their hands, helpless.

They do not turn back for their sons.

## The Philistine cities grieve

## Jeremiah 47:4

The day has come for all the Philistines to face destruction.

No friends will be left to help Tyre and Sidon.

These are two cities on the coast.

The Philistines came from the shores of Caphtor.

The LORD is about to destroy those who remain.

## Jeremiah 47:5

People in Gaza shave their heads to show their grief.

The city of Ashkelon falls silent.

Jeremiah asks the people left in the valley,

“How long will you wound yourselves in grief?”

## A cry for the fighting to stop

## Jeremiah 47:6

Someone cries out to the LORD’s sword as if it could hear.

“Sword of the LORD, how long until you rest?

Go back into your sheath.

Be still!”

A sheath is a cover for a sword.

The cry asks for the killing to stop.

## Jeremiah 47:7

How can the sword rest?

The LORD gave it a command.

He sent it against Ashkelon and the land along the sea.
