# Jeremiah 48 — Grief for Moab

**About this chapter**

Moab trusts its wealth and its soldiers. God says that its towns will fall. Even as he announces judgment, he speaks of his grief for the people.

## Cries rise from Moab's towns

## Jeremiah 48:1

The LORD, Israel's God, commands heaven's armies. He says,

“What sorrow for Nebo! The attackers will ruin it.

They will capture Kiriathaim and bring it shame.

They will tear down the fortress.

## Jeremiah 48:2

“No one praises Moab anymore.

People in Heshbon plot against it.

They say, ‘Come, let us end Moab as a nation.’

People of Madmen, war will reach you too.

Your town will fall silent.

## Jeremiah 48:3

“Listen to the cry from Horonaim!

‘Everything lies broken! What terrible destruction!’

## Jeremiah 48:4

“Moab will fall to pieces.

Even its little children will cry out.

## Jeremiah 48:5

“People weep bitterly as they climb the road to Luhith.

On the way down to Horonaim they cry over the ruins.

## Jeremiah 48:6

“Run for your lives! Escape to the desert.

Live there like a juniper bush standing alone.

## Jeremiah 48:7

“You trusted the things you made and the wealth you stored.

Yet enemies will capture you.

They will carry off Chemosh, your god.

His priests and officials will go with him.

## Jeremiah 48:8

“The attacker will come to every town. None will escape.

Even the valley and the high plain will lie in ruins.

The LORD has said this.

## Jeremiah 48:9

“Scatter salt on Moab's land. It will lie waste.

No one will live in its empty towns.”

## Jeremiah 48:10

A curse falls on those who neglect the work the LORD gives them.

In this judgment, a curse falls on those who hold back their swords.

## The wine jar will be emptied

## Jeremiah 48:11

From its earliest days Moab has lived undisturbed.

Think of wine resting in a jar with sediment at the bottom.

No one has poured the wine from jar to jar.

Its taste and smell have stayed the same.

In the same way, Moab's people have never gone into exile.

![A clay jar. Moab's settled life is compared to wine left in one jar.](../pictures/clay-jar.svg)

## Jeremiah 48:12

The LORD says, “One day I will send people to pour Moab out.

They will empty the jars and smash them.”

## Jeremiah 48:13

Moab will feel ashamed for trusting Chemosh.

Israel felt that same shame over its worship at Bethel.

## Jeremiah 48:14

“How can you boast, ‘Our soldiers are strong and ready for battle’?

## Jeremiah 48:15

“Enemies have invaded Moab's towns and brought the land to ruin.

Its finest young men have died in the fighting.”

The King who says this is the LORD who commands heaven's armies.

## Jeremiah 48:16

Moab's time of trouble is near.

Suffering is coming fast.

## News reaches the towns on the plain

## Jeremiah 48:17

All you neighbors who know Moab, mourn for it!

Say, “The ruler's staff has snapped.

The staff that showed such power and honor lies broken.”

## Jeremiah 48:18

People of Dibon, leave your place of honor.

Sit on the dry ground.

Moab's attacker has reached you and torn down your forts.

## Jeremiah 48:19

People of Aroer, stand beside the road and watch.

Ask the men and women who flee, “What happened?”

## Jeremiah 48:20

Moab has fallen in disgrace. Weep and cry aloud!

Tell the people by the Arnon River, “Moab lies in ruins!”

## Jeremiah 48:21

Judgment has come on the towns of the high plain.

It has reached Holon, Jahzah, and Mephaath.

## Jeremiah 48:22

It has reached Dibon, Nebo, and Beth-diblathaim.

## Jeremiah 48:23

It has reached Kiriathaim, Beth-gamul, and Beth-meon.

## Jeremiah 48:24

Kerioth and Bozrah face it too.

Every town of Moab, near and far, faces judgment.

## Jeremiah 48:25

The LORD says, “Moab's horn is cut off. Its arm is broken.”

These are pictures of Moab losing its strength.

## Moab's pride gives way to grief

## Jeremiah 48:26

“Make Moab drunk, for it boasted against the LORD.

Like someone rolling in their own vomit, Moab will face people's laughter.

## Jeremiah 48:27

“Moab, you laughed at Israel. Did you catch Israel stealing?

Yet whenever you spoke of Israel, you shook your head.

## Jeremiah 48:28

“People of Moab, leave your towns. Live among the rocks.

Be like a dove nesting at the mouth of a cave.”

## Jeremiah 48:29

We have heard how proud Moab is.

Its people boast and think themselves better than others.

## Jeremiah 48:30

The LORD says, “I know how they look down on others.

Their boasts achieve nothing. Their deeds are just as empty.

## Jeremiah 48:31

“So I weep aloud for all of Moab.

I groan for the men of Kir-heres.

## Jeremiah 48:32

“Grapevines of Sibmah, I weep for you even more than for Jazer.

Your branches stretched to the sea and reached Jazer.

Now the attacker has taken your summer fruit and grapes.

## Jeremiah 48:33

“Joy has gone from Moab's orchards and fields.

I have stopped the wine flowing from the presses.

No one tramples grapes with glad shouts.

The shouts you hear now are cries of distress.

## Jeremiah 48:34

“People cry from Heshbon to Elealeh, all the way to Jahaz.

Others cry from Zoar to Horonaim and Eglath-shelishiyah.

Even the waters of Nimrim have dried up.

## Jeremiah 48:35

“I will put an end to those who offer gifts at Moab's hilltop shrines.

They burn sweet-smelling incense to their gods.

## Jeremiah 48:36

“My heart sounds like a flute playing a funeral song.

I grieve for Moab and for the men of Kir-heres.

The wealth they gathered is gone.

## Jeremiah 48:37

“People shave their heads and trim their beards in grief.

Their hands bear cuts. They wear rough cloth around their waists.

## Jeremiah 48:38

“People mourn on the flat roofs and in the town squares.

I have broken Moab like a jar no one wants.”

## Jeremiah 48:39

Moab lies shattered. Hear the people weep!

They turn away in shame.

Their neighbors mock them and shudder at what has happened.

## The last warning and a promise

## Jeremiah 48:40

The LORD says, “Look! An eagle swoops down.

It spreads its wings over Moab.”

The eagle pictures the attacking enemy.

## Jeremiah 48:41

The enemy has taken Kirioth and captured the forts.

Moab's soldiers will tremble like a woman giving birth.

## Jeremiah 48:42

Moab boasted against the LORD.

That is why it will fall as a nation.

## Jeremiah 48:43

The LORD says, “People of Moab, terror awaits you.

A pit and a trap await you too.

## Jeremiah 48:44

“Anyone running from danger will fall into the pit.

Anyone climbing out will land in the trap.

I will bring Moab's time of punishment.

## Jeremiah 48:45

“People fleeing to Heshbon stand helpless in its shadow.

Fire spreads from Heshbon, from the city of King Sihon.

The flames burn the heads of Moab's noisy fighters.

## Jeremiah 48:46

“What sorrow for you, Moab! The people of Chemosh are lost.

Enemies have taken your sons and daughters away as prisoners.

## Jeremiah 48:47

“Yet in days to come I will bring Moab's people back from captivity.”

Here ends the LORD's message of judgment against Moab.
