# Jeremiah 6 — The people refuse to hear the warning

**About this chapter**

A horn sounds to warn people of danger. God tells Jerusalem to listen and choose the good way. The people refuse.

## Danger comes from the north

## Jeremiah 6:1

People of Benjamin, run to safety! Leave Jerusalem!

Blow a ram's horn in Tekoa to warn the people.

Raise a signal above Beth-haccherem.

An enemy from the north will bring terrible destruction.

## Jeremiah 6:2

The LORD says, “Zion is like a beautiful, delicate daughter.

Yet I will bring her to ruin.”

Zion is another name for Jerusalem.

## Jeremiah 6:3

Leaders and their armies will come like shepherds with flocks.

They will set up tents all around Jerusalem.

Each group will take its own patch of ground.

## Jeremiah 6:4

The attackers say, “Get ready to fight!

Let's go up at noon!”

Then they say, “Daylight is slipping away.

Look how long the shadows have grown!

## Jeremiah 6:5

“Come on! Let's attack at night.

Let's tear down the city's strong buildings!”

## Jeremiah 6:6

The LORD commands heaven's armies. He says,

“The attackers will cut down trees and build a ramp against Jerusalem.

This city must answer for the way its people mistreat others.

## Jeremiah 6:7

“Water keeps flowing from a well.

In the same way, evil keeps coming from Jerusalem.

I hear the sounds of violence and destruction.

Everywhere I look, people are sick or wounded.

## Jeremiah 6:8

“Jerusalem, take my warning seriously.

Otherwise I will turn away from you.

I will leave your land empty, with no one living there.”

## Who will listen?

## Jeremiah 6:9

The LORD who commands heaven's armies says,

“Go over what is left of Israel.

Go back as a grape picker does.

Reach along the branches for every last grape.”

The people still in the land are like those last grapes.

![Grapes on a vine. A picker goes back over the branches to find what is left.](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

## Jeremiah 6:10

Jeremiah asks, “Who will listen if I give this warning?

The people have closed their ears.

The LORD's words offend them.

They take no joy in hearing him.

## Jeremiah 6:11

“I am full of the LORD's anger.

I am tired of holding it in.”

The LORD says, “Pour it out.

It will reach the children in the street and the young men gathered there.

Enemies will take husbands and wives, even the very old.

## Jeremiah 6:12

“Other people will take their homes, fields, and wives.

I will act against the people who live here.

## Jeremiah 6:13

“From the least to the greatest, they want more for themselves.

Even the prophets and priests tell lies.

## Jeremiah 6:14

“My people are like someone with a deep wound.

Their leaders barely treat it.

They keep saying, ‘Peace! Peace!’ when there is no peace.

## Jeremiah 6:15

“Do they feel shame over the terrible things they do?

They do not even blush.

When I punish them, they too will fall.”

## A choice at the crossroads

## Jeremiah 6:16

The LORD says, “Stand where the roads meet. Look around.

Ask about the old paths. Ask, ‘Which way is good?’

Walk that way, and you will find rest deep inside.”

The good path means living the way God taught them.

But the people said, “We will not take that path.”

## Jeremiah 6:17

God said, “I gave you lookouts.

I told you to listen when they blew the warning horn.

But you said, ‘We will not listen!’

## Jeremiah 6:18

“Nations, listen. People gathered together, hear what will happen to them.

## Jeremiah 6:19

“Listen, all the earth! I will bring disaster on these people.

Their own plans have brought this on them.

They ignored my words and rejected my teaching.

## Jeremiah 6:20

“What use do I have for sweet-smelling incense from Sheba?

Why bring sweet cane from a faraway land?

I will not accept your burnt offerings.

Your sacrifices give me no pleasure.”

## Jeremiah 6:21

The LORD says, “I will put obstacles in their path.

Parents and children alike will stumble.

Friends and neighbors will die.”

## Jerusalem hears about the enemy

## Jeremiah 6:22

The LORD says, “An army is coming from the north.

A powerful nation is on the move from far away.

## Jeremiah 6:23

“They carry bows and spears. They show no mercy.

They roar like the sea.

Their riders line up for battle against you, Jerusalem.”

## Jeremiah 6:24

“We have heard the news. Our hands hang limp.

Pain grips us like a woman giving birth.”

## Jeremiah 6:25

Stay out of the fields. Keep off the roads.

The enemy carries swords. Terror surrounds us.

## Jeremiah 6:26

My people, put on rough mourning clothes and roll in ashes.

Weep as you would for an only son.

The enemy will come suddenly to destroy us.

## God asks Jeremiah to test the people

## Jeremiah 6:27

The LORD told Jeremiah, “A worker tests rock to find the metal inside.

I have chosen you to test my people.

Find out how they live.

## Jeremiah 6:28

“They stubbornly turn against me.

They go around spreading lies about others.

They are like bronze and iron mixed into silver.

All of them have become corrupt.”

## Jeremiah 6:29

A worker blows air into the fire to heat the metal.

The lead burns away, but the silver still is not pure.

In the same way, the evil among the people remains.

## Jeremiah 6:30

People call them silver that failed the test.

The LORD has rejected them.

**Think about it**

**What did the people say when God asked them to take the good path?**
