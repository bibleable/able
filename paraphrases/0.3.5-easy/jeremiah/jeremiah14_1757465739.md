# Jeremiah 14 — The jars come back empty

**About this chapter**

Rain has stopped falling in Judah. People and animals cannot find water. Jeremiah speaks to God about the people and the promises their prophets have made.

## Judah has no rain

## Jeremiah 14:1

The LORD spoke to Jeremiah about the drought.

A drought is a long time without enough rain.

## Jeremiah 14:2

“The people of Judah mourn.

People at the town gates grow weak.

They weep for their land.

Cries rise from Jerusalem.

## Jeremiah 14:3

“The nobles send servants to fetch water.

The servants look in the pits that store water.

The pits are dry. The servants bring their jars home empty.

They cover their heads in shame.

![A clay jar like the ones the servants brought home empty.](../pictures/clay-jar.svg)

## Jeremiah 14:4

“The dry ground has cracked because no rain has fallen.

The farmers cover their heads in shame too.

## Jeremiah 14:5

“A mother deer gives birth in the field.

Even she leaves her newborn because there is no grass.

## Jeremiah 14:6

“Wild donkeys stand on the bare hilltops.

They gasp for air like jackals, a kind of wild dog.

Without grass to eat, even their eyes grow weak.”

## A prayer for help

## Jeremiah 14:7

“LORD, the wrong we have done speaks against us.

Help us for the sake of your name.

Again and again we have turned against you.

We have sinned against you.

## Jeremiah 14:8

“You are Israel's hope. You save us in trouble.

Why do you seem like a stranger here?

Why are you like a traveler who stays only one night?

## Jeremiah 14:9

“Why do you seem like someone caught by surprise?

Why do you seem like a soldier unable to save us?

Yet you are here with us, LORD.

We are the people who bear your name.

Please do not leave us.”

## Jeremiah 14:10

The LORD said about the people, “They love to wander away.

They make no effort to stop.”

So the LORD would not accept them.

He would remember their wrongdoing and punish their sins.

## The prophets promise peace

## Jeremiah 14:11

The LORD said to me, “Jeremiah, do not pray for things to go well for these people.

## Jeremiah 14:12

“They may fast, going without food to seek my help.

Even then I will not listen to their cries.

They may bring burnt offerings and gifts of grain.

I will not accept those gifts.

War, hunger, and disease will bring their lives to an end.”

## Jeremiah 14:13

I answered, “Lord GOD, listen to what the prophets tell them!

They say you have promised lasting peace here.

They say the people will never face war or hunger.”

## Jeremiah 14:14

The LORD answered, “These prophets tell lies in my name.

I never sent them or chose them for this work.

I never spoke to them.

They claim to see visions and tell the future.

Their messages come from their own confused minds.

## Jeremiah 14:15

“They say, ‘War and hunger will never touch this land.’

Yet war and hunger will kill those very prophets.

I never sent them to speak for me.

## Jeremiah 14:16

“War and hunger will also kill those who hear them.

Their bodies will lie in Jerusalem's streets.

No one will bury them or their wives or children.

I will bring the evil they have done back on them.”

## Tears for the people

## Jeremiah 14:17

The LORD told Jeremiah, “Say these words to them.

‘My tears keep falling, day and night.

My people are like a young woman with a terrible wound.

A crushing blow has struck her.

## Jeremiah 14:18

“‘In the fields I see people who died in the fighting.

In the city I see people wasting away from hunger.

Prophets and priests travel to a land they do not know.’”

## The people put their hope in God

## Jeremiah 14:19

“LORD, have you given up on Judah completely?

Do you hate Zion, your city?

Why have you struck us so hard we cannot recover?

We hoped for peace, but nothing good came.

We waited for healing. All we found was terror.

## Jeremiah 14:20

“LORD, we admit we have done evil.

Our ancestors did wrong too.

We have sinned against you.

## Jeremiah 14:21

“For the sake of your name, please do not reject us.

Do not let people dishonor your glorious throne.

Remember your covenant, the promise you made to us.

Please keep that promise.

## Jeremiah 14:22

“Can the idols of other nations bring rain?

Can the sky send showers on its own?

LORD our God, you are the one who sends rain.

We put our hope in you.

You have done all these things.”
