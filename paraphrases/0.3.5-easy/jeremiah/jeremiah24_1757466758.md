# Jeremiah 24 — Two baskets of figs

**About this chapter**

The LORD shows Jeremiah two baskets of fruit. He uses the baskets to explain what will happen to two groups of people from Judah.

## Jeremiah sees the baskets

## Jeremiah 24:1

Nebuchadnezzar was the king of Babylon.

He took Jeconiah, the king of Judah, away to Babylon.

Jeconiah was the son of Jehoiakim.

Nebuchadnezzar also took officials and skilled workers from Jerusalem.

Some of those workers made things out of metal.

After this, the LORD showed me two baskets of figs.

They stood in front of the LORD’s temple.

## Jeremiah 24:2

One basket held very good figs.

They were like the first figs to ripen.

The other basket held rotten figs.

They were too bad to eat.

![One basket holds plump figs. The other holds shriveled, spotted figs.](../pictures/two-baskets-of-figs.svg)

## Jeremiah 24:3

The LORD asked, “Jeremiah, what do you see?”

“Figs!” I answered.

“The good figs are very good.

The bad figs are so bad that nobody can eat them.”

## What the good figs mean

## Jeremiah 24:4

Then the LORD spoke to me.

## Jeremiah 24:5

The LORD, the God of Israel, said, “Think of the good figs.

That is how I see the people I sent away from Judah.

They now live in Babylonia, the land of the Chaldeans.

## Jeremiah 24:6

“I will watch over them and care for them.

I will bring them back to this land.

I will build them up and let them stand.

I will plant them and let them take root.”

Like plants with strong roots, the people will have a lasting home.

## Jeremiah 24:7

“I will give them hearts that know Me.

They will know that I am the LORD.

They will be My people, and I will be their God.

They will come back to Me with all their heart.

## What the bad figs mean

## Jeremiah 24:8

“Now think of the figs that nobody can eat.

That is how I will treat Zedekiah, king of Judah, and his officials.

I will also treat the remaining people of Jerusalem that way.

Some stayed in this land. Others live in Egypt.

## Jeremiah 24:9

“Wherever I send them, the kingdoms of the earth will recoil at the sight of them.

People will mock them and treat them with shame.

People will use their name when they curse others.

## Jeremiah 24:10

“I will send war, hunger, and deadly disease against them.

They will die in the land I gave them and their ancestors.”
