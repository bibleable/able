# Jeremiah 17 — A tree with roots by the stream

**About this chapter**

God compares someone who trusts him to a tree beside water. Jeremiah asks God for help. Then God sends him to Jerusalem's gates with a message about the Sabbath.

## Judah's sin leaves a deep mark

## Jeremiah 17:1

The LORD said, “Judah's sin is carved deep into their hearts.

It is like writing cut with an iron tool tipped with diamond.

The marks are even on the horns of their altars.”

These horns were the raised corners of places of worship.

## Jeremiah 17:2

“Even their children remember those altars and the Asherah poles.

The poles were used to worship the goddess Asherah.

They stood beside green trees and on high hills.

## Jeremiah 17:3

“My mountain stands in the open country.

Throughout your land you have sinned at hilltop places of worship.

I will let enemies take your wealth and treasures.

## Jeremiah 17:4

“You will lose the land I gave you.

I will make you serve your enemies in an unfamiliar land.

You have stirred up my anger like a fire.

It will keep burning forever.”

## A desert bush and a tree beside water

## Jeremiah 17:5

The LORD said, “A person who turns away from me is under a curse

when that person relies on human strength alone.

## Jeremiah 17:6

“Such a person is like a bush in the desert.

It grows in dry, salty ground where no one lives.

When good things come, that person misses them.

## Jeremiah 17:7

“I bless the person who trusts me.

That person can depend on me.

## Jeremiah 17:8

“Think of a tree growing beside a stream.

Its roots reach toward the water.

Even in hot weather its leaves stay green.

A whole year can pass without rain.

Still the tree bears fruit.”

This is what it is like to trust the LORD.

## God knows what is in the heart

## Jeremiah 17:9

“The human heart can fool us more than anything else.

Its sickness is beyond cure. Who can understand it?

## Jeremiah 17:10

“I, the LORD, see what is in each heart.

I know what each person thinks.

I repay each one for how they live and act.

## Jeremiah 17:11

“Think of a partridge sitting on another bird's eggs.

That is what it is like to get rich by cheating.

Halfway through life that person will lose the riches.

In the end everyone will see how foolish that person was.”

## Jeremiah prays to the LORD

## Jeremiah 17:12

Our holy place is like a glorious throne.

It has stood high from the beginning.

## Jeremiah 17:13

LORD, Israel puts its hope in you.

Shame comes on those who leave you.

Their names are like words written in dust.

They have left you, the spring of living water.

Like fresh water from a spring, you give life.

## Jeremiah 17:14

Heal me, LORD, and I will be well.

Save me, and I will be safe.

You are the one I praise.

## Jeremiah 17:15

People keep asking me, “Where is the LORD's promised message?

Let us see it come true now!”

## Jeremiah 17:16

I have stayed where you sent me as a shepherd.

I have kept caring for your people.

I never wanted that terrible day to come.

You heard every word I spoke before you.

## Jeremiah 17:17

Please do not fill me with fear.

When disaster comes, I turn to you for shelter.

## Jeremiah 17:18

Let those who hunt me feel shame.

Please spare me that shame.

Let fear fall on them and spare me from terror.

Let the day of disaster come upon them.

Let twice the ruin fall on them.

## A message at Jerusalem's gates

## Jeremiah 17:19

The LORD told me, “Stand at the People's Gate.

Judah's kings use it to enter and leave Jerusalem.

Go to all the other gates too.

## Jeremiah 17:20

“Say, ‘Kings of Judah, listen to the LORD.

Everyone from Judah and Jerusalem who uses these gates, listen.

## Jeremiah 17:21

“‘Take care to obey me on the Sabbath.

The Sabbath is the day set apart for rest.

Do not carry loads through Jerusalem's gates on that day.

## Jeremiah 17:22

“‘Do not carry loads out of your homes either.

Put your work aside. Keep this day holy.

That is what I told your ancestors to do.

## Jeremiah 17:23

“‘But they stubbornly refused to listen.

They would not let me correct them.

## Jeremiah 17:24

“‘Now listen carefully to me.

On the Sabbath, carry no loads through these gates.

Keep the day holy by resting from work.

## Jeremiah 17:25

“‘Then kings and princes will still pass through these gates.

Kings from David's family will rule here.

They and their officials will ride in chariots and on horses.

The people of Judah and Jerusalem will come with them.

People will always live in this city.

## Jeremiah 17:26

“‘People will come to my temple from Judah's towns and from around Jerusalem.

They will come from Benjamin, the foothills, and the hill country.

They will come from the dry southern land called the Negev.

They will bring burnt offerings, other sacrifices, and gifts of grain.

They will bring sweet-smelling frankincense and gifts to thank me.

## Jeremiah 17:27

“‘But suppose you keep carrying loads through the gates on the Sabbath.

If you refuse to keep the day holy, I will set the gates on fire.

The fire will burn Jerusalem's strong buildings.

No one will be able to put it out.’”
