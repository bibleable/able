# Jeremiah 44 — The people in Egypt refuse to listen

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah speaks to people from Judah who now live in Egypt. They remember having plenty of food in Judah. Jeremiah asks them to remember why they lost their homes.

## Remember what happened in Judah

## Jeremiah 44:1

The LORD gave Jeremiah a message for the Jews in Egypt.

Some lived in Migdol, Tahpanhes, and Memphis.

Others lived in the region of Pathros.

## Jeremiah 44:2

The LORD, Israel’s God, leads the armies of heaven.

He said, “You saw the disaster I brought on Jerusalem.

You saw what happened to the other cities of Judah.

Look at them now. They stand empty and broken.”

## Jeremiah 44:3

“The people there made me angry by doing evil.

They burned incense to other gods and served them.”

Incense gives off a sweet smell when it burns.

Neither the people nor their ancestors had known those gods.

## Jeremiah 44:4

The LORD said, “Again and again I sent my servants, the prophets.

They spoke for me. They said, ‘Stop doing this thing I hate.’”

## Jeremiah 44:5

“But the people refused to listen.

They kept doing wrong.

They kept burning incense to other gods.”

## Jeremiah 44:6

The LORD said his anger burned like a fire.

It reached Judah’s cities and Jerusalem’s streets.

That was why the cities now lay in ruins.

## The LORD warns the people in Egypt

## Jeremiah 44:7

The LORD asked, “Why bring such harm on yourselves?

What will happen to Judah’s men and women?

What will happen to the children and babies?

Who will be left?”

## Jeremiah 44:8

“You have come to live in Egypt.

Here you burn incense to other gods.

Why make me angry by doing this?

You will perish. People everywhere will speak of you with scorn.

They will use your name when they curse someone.”

## Jeremiah 44:9

“Remember the wrong your ancestors did in Judah.

Remember what the kings and their wives did.

Have you forgotten what you and your wives did there?

You did wrong even in Jerusalem’s streets.”

## Jeremiah 44:10

The LORD said the people still refused to humble themselves.

They showed him no respect.

They ignored the laws he gave them and their ancestors.

## Jeremiah 44:11

Israel’s God said, “I have decided to bring disaster.

I will cut off the people of Judah.”

## Jeremiah 44:12

He spoke about those who chose to live in Egypt.

War and hunger would kill them, from the least to the greatest.

People would shudder at what happened to them.

Others would curse them, insult them, and put them to shame.

## Jeremiah 44:13

The LORD said, “I punished Jerusalem.

I will also punish those living in Egypt.

War, hunger, and deadly sickness will come.”

## Jeremiah 44:14

The people wanted to return to Judah and live there.

But only a few would escape and return home.

## The people give Jeremiah their answer

## Jeremiah 44:15

A large crowd stood before Jeremiah in Egypt.

The women were there, including women from Pathros.

The men knew their wives burned incense to other gods.

Together the people answered Jeremiah.

## Jeremiah 44:16

“You brought us a message from the LORD.

We refuse to listen to you!”

## Jeremiah 44:17

“We promised to burn incense to the Queen of Heaven.”

That was their name for a goddess they worshiped.

“We will pour out drinks as gifts to her.

Our families, kings, and leaders did this in Judah and Jerusalem.

We did it too. We had plenty to eat then.

Life was good. No disaster came.”

## Jeremiah 44:18

“We stopped burning incense and pouring out drinks for her.

Since then we have gone without the things we need.

War and hunger have been killing us.”

## Jeremiah 44:19

The women added, “Our husbands knew what we did.

We made cakes shaped like the goddess.

We burned incense and poured out drinks for her.”

## Jeremiah explains why Judah lies empty

## Jeremiah 44:20

Jeremiah answered the whole crowd, both men and women.

## Jeremiah 44:21

He said, “The LORD remembers the incense you burned in Judah and Jerusalem.

Your ancestors, kings, leaders, and neighbors burned it too.

Did you think the LORD had forgotten?”

## Jeremiah 44:22

“The LORD could bear your evil deeds no longer.

Look at your land today. No one lives there.

People shudder at the sight.

They speak of your land when they curse someone.”

## Jeremiah 44:23

“You burned incense to other gods.

You sinned against the LORD.

You ignored what he said and disobeyed his laws.

That is why this disaster came.”

## The LORD gives a sign

## Jeremiah 44:24

Jeremiah spoke again to everyone, including the women.

“People of Judah in Egypt, hear what the LORD says.”

## Jeremiah 44:25

Israel’s God said, “You and your wives made promises.

You promised incense and drink offerings to the Queen of Heaven.

You have done what you promised.

Go ahead, then. Keep those promises!”

## Jeremiah 44:26

“Now hear my promise, you people of Judah in Egypt.

I swear by my own great name.

None of you here will use my name to make a promise again.

No one will say, ‘As surely as the Lord GOD lives.’”

## Jeremiah 44:27

“I am watching them. I will bring disaster on them.

War and hunger will keep taking their lives in Egypt.”

## Jeremiah 44:28

“Only a few will escape the fighting.

They will return from Egypt to Judah.

Then the people who came here will know whose word comes true.

Will it be my word or theirs?”

## Jeremiah 44:29

The LORD said, “I will give you a sign.

It will show you that I will punish you here.

You can be sure my warning will come true.”

## Jeremiah 44:30

“Hophra is Pharaoh, the king of Egypt.

I will hand him over to enemies who want him dead.

Remember Zedekiah, the king of Judah.

I handed him over to Nebuchadnezzar, the king of Babylon.

That king wanted Zedekiah dead too.”
