# Jeremiah 30 — God promises freedom to Israel and Judah

**About this chapter**

God tells Jeremiah to write down a promise. Israel and Judah face terrible trouble. God will rescue them and bring them home.

## Write down my words

## Jeremiah 30:1

The LORD spoke to Jeremiah.

## Jeremiah 30:2

The LORD, the God of Israel, said,

“Write in a book every word I have spoken to you.

## Jeremiah 30:3

“One day I will free my people, Israel and Judah.

I will bring them back to the land I gave their ancestors.

That land will be theirs again.”

## God will free his frightened people

## Jeremiah 30:4

The LORD gave this message about Israel and Judah.

## Jeremiah 30:5

The LORD said, “Listen to those cries of fear!

The people feel no peace.

## Jeremiah 30:6

“Can a man give birth?

Look at the men holding their stomachs.

They bend over like women about to give birth.

Their faces have gone pale with fear.

## Jeremiah 30:7

“What a terrible day! No other day will be like it.

Jacob's people, the people of Israel, will suffer greatly.

Yet I will rescue them from this trouble.”

## Jeremiah 30:8

The LORD who commands heaven's armies said, “That day I will break the yoke off their necks.

I will tear away the ropes that hold them.”

A yoke is a bar laid across an animal's neck.

Here the yoke means the power others hold over Israel.

God said, “Foreigners will no longer make my people slaves.

![A wooden yoke. God promises to break the power of those who enslave his people.](../pictures/job-yoke.svg)

## Jeremiah 30:9

“My people will serve me, the LORD their God.

They will also serve David their king.

I will raise up this king for them.

## Jeremiah 30:10

“People of Jacob, you are my servants. Do not be afraid.

I will rescue you, even from far away.

I will free your children from the land that holds them captive.

You will come home and live in peace.

No one will frighten you.

## Jeremiah 30:11

“I am with you to save you.

I scattered you among other nations.

I will bring those nations to an end.

I will let your people live on.

I will still punish you fairly for the wrong you did.”

## God promises to heal a deep wound

## Jeremiah 30:12

The LORD spoke about his people's suffering as a wound.

He said, “Your wound is deep. No one can cure it.

## Jeremiah 30:13

“No one speaks up for you.

No one has medicine for your sores.

No one can make you well.

## Jeremiah 30:14

“Those who once loved you have forgotten you.

They no longer come looking for you.

I struck you as an enemy would.

My punishment was harsh because your sins were many and serious.

## Jeremiah 30:15

“Why do you cry out about your wound?

No one can stop your pain.

I brought this trouble on you because of all your sins.

## Jeremiah 30:16

“Yet those who swallow you up will face destruction themselves.

Enemies will carry all your attackers away from their homes.

Those who took your goods by force will lose their own.

Those who raided you will face raids themselves.

## Jeremiah 30:17

“People call you an outcast, Zion.

They say no one cares what happens to you.

But I will heal your wounds and give you back your health.”

## Families will celebrate in the rebuilt city

## Jeremiah 30:18

The LORD said, “I will show kindness to Jacob's families.

I will help them rebuild their homes.

They will build their city again where its ruins stand.

The palace will stand in its proper place.

## Jeremiah 30:19

“You will hear the people giving thanks and celebrating.

I will help their families grow in number.

I will honor them. No one will look down on them.

## Jeremiah 30:20

“Their children will live as they did long ago.

As a people they will stand firm before me.

I will punish everyone who mistreats them.

## Jeremiah 30:21

“Their ruler will come from their own people.

I will invite him to come near me.

Who would dare come to me on his own?

## Jeremiah 30:22

“You will be my people.

I will be your God.”

## A storm shows the force of God's anger

## Jeremiah 30:23

Look! The LORD's anger is like a fierce storm.

Like a twisting wind, it sweeps down on those who do evil.

## Jeremiah 30:24

The LORD's anger will last until his work is done.

He will carry out everything he has planned.

In days to come you will understand.
