# Jeremiah 5 — A search for an honest person

**About this chapter**

God tells Jeremiah to search Jerusalem for someone who seeks the truth. Jeremiah looks among poor people and powerful leaders. God sees how people gain wealth by cheating their neighbors.

## Search the streets of Jerusalem

## Jeremiah 5:1

The LORD said, “Walk through Jerusalem. Look closely.

Search the open squares where people meet.

Can you find even one person who treats others fairly and seeks the truth?

If you can, I will forgive the city.

## Jeremiah 5:2

“People say, ‘As surely as the LORD lives!’

They use my name to promise they are telling the truth.

Yet they lie.”

## Jeremiah 5:3

Jeremiah said, “LORD, you look for truth.

You struck the people, but they felt no pain.

Even when you brought them to ruin, they refused to learn.

Their faces were hard like stone. They refused to change.”

## Jeremiah 5:4

I thought, “These people are poor.

Perhaps they act foolishly because they do not know the LORD's way.

They do not know what their God calls fair.

## Jeremiah 5:5

“I will speak to the leaders.

Surely they know how God wants them to live.”

But every leader had also turned against God.

They were like animals that broke free from a yoke and chains.

## Jeremiah 5:6

A lion from the forest will strike them down.

A desert wolf will attack them.

A leopard will wait outside their towns.

It will tear apart anyone who goes out.

Again and again the people have turned against God.

## The people turn away from God

## Jeremiah 5:7

The LORD asked, “Why should I forgive you?

Your children left me. They made promises in the names of false gods.

I gave them all they needed.

Yet they broke their marriage promises and went to prostitutes.

## Jeremiah 5:8

“They were like well-fed male horses calling for a mate.

Each man wanted his neighbor's wife.

## Jeremiah 5:9

“Should I let these things go unpunished?

Should I let a nation do this without answering to me?

## Jeremiah 5:10

“Go through the vineyards and strip off the branches.

Leave some of the vines standing.

The branches you strip away do not belong to me.”

The vineyard pictures God's people under attack.

## Jeremiah 5:11

The LORD said, “Both Israel and Judah have been unfaithful to me.”

## Jeremiah 5:12

The people lied about the LORD. They said,

“He will do nothing. No harm will reach us.

We will never face war or hunger.

## Jeremiah 5:13

“The prophets' warnings are only wind.

God has given them no message.

Let those troubles come upon the prophets themselves!”

## An army will come from far away

## Jeremiah 5:14

The LORD commands heaven's armies. He answered those words.

He told Jeremiah, “My words in your mouth will be like fire.

The people will be like wood that burns.”

The judgment Jeremiah announces will reach the people.

## Jeremiah 5:15

The LORD said, “People of Israel, I am bringing a nation against you.

It is an ancient nation from far away.

When its people speak, you will not understand their language.

## Jeremiah 5:16

“Their soldiers are strong.

They carry arrows in cases called quivers.

Those quivers are like open graves.

Their arrows bring death.

## Jeremiah 5:17

“They will eat your crops and your stored food.

They will take the lives of your sons and daughters.

They will eat your sheep and cattle, your grapes and figs.

You trust your cities with their strong walls.

The enemy will bring those cities down in battle.

## Jeremiah 5:18

“Yet even then I will let some of you live.

## Jeremiah 5:19

“The people will ask, ‘Why has our God done this to us?’

Jeremiah, give them my answer.

‘In your own land you left me and served foreign gods.

Now you will serve foreigners in a land far from home.’”

## The sea has a boundary

## Jeremiah 5:20

God said, “Announce this to Jacob's family.

Let everyone in Judah hear it.

## Jeremiah 5:21

“Listen, you foolish people who understand so little!

You have eyes, yet you miss what is in front of you.

You have ears, yet you refuse to listen.

## Jeremiah 5:22

“Do you have no fear of me?

I put the sand in place as a boundary for the sea.

The waves rise and roar.

They cannot break through the boundary I set.

![Sand at the edge of the sea. God points to the shore as a boundary for the waves.](../pictures/sand-by-the-sea.svg)

## Jeremiah 5:23

“Yet these people stubbornly refuse to obey me.

They have turned around and gone their own way.

## Jeremiah 5:24

“They never say, ‘Let us honor the LORD our God.

He sends the autumn and spring rains in season.

He makes sure the weeks for gathering crops come in their time.’

## Jeremiah 5:25

“Your sins have kept these gifts from you.

Your wrongdoing has cost you the good things I give.

## People get rich by cheating others

## Jeremiah 5:26

“Some of my people watch for someone to trap.

They wait like hunters who catch birds.

But the ones they trap are people.

## Jeremiah 5:27

“A hunter's cage fills up with birds.

In the same way, these people's homes fill up with things they gained by lying.

That is how they became rich and powerful.

## Jeremiah 5:28

“They have grown fat and comfortable.

They outdo each other in doing evil.

They refuse to help children without fathers get a fair hearing.

They do nothing to defend the rights of poor people.

## Jeremiah 5:29

“Should I leave such things unpunished?

Should I let a nation do this without answering to me?

## Jeremiah 5:30

“Something terrible has happened in this land.

## Jeremiah 5:31

“The prophets give false messages.

The priests use their own authority to rule.

My people like it that way.

But what will you do when the end comes?”
