# Jeremiah 40 — Jeremiah stays with the people in Judah

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah can choose where to live. He goes to Mizpah, where Gedaliah leads the people who are still in Judah.

## The captain frees Jeremiah

## Jeremiah 40:1

The LORD spoke to Jeremiah after these events.

At Ramah, Jeremiah was in chains with other prisoners.

They came from Jerusalem and other places in Judah.

Soldiers were taking the prisoners to Babylon.

Nebuzaradan, the captain of the guard, found Jeremiah there and freed him.

## Jeremiah 40:2

The captain told Jeremiah, “The LORD your God said he would bring disaster on this place.”

## Jeremiah 40:3

“Now the LORD has done what he said.

You and your people disobeyed the LORD.

That is why this happened to you.”

## Jeremiah 40:4

The captain said, “Today I free you.”

He took the chains off Jeremiah’s wrists.

“Come to Babylon with me if you wish.

I will take care of you there.

Or you may stay. Choose where you want to go.”

## Jeremiah 40:5

Before Jeremiah left, the captain suggested a place.

“You could stay with Gedaliah and the people of Judah.”

Gedaliah was Ahikam’s son and Shaphan’s grandson.

Babylon’s king had put Gedaliah over Judah’s cities.

The captain said Jeremiah could also choose another place.

Then he gave Jeremiah food and a gift.

He let Jeremiah go.

## Jeremiah 40:6

Jeremiah went to Gedaliah at Mizpah.

He stayed with the people still living in Judah.

## People gather at Mizpah

## Jeremiah 40:7

Judah’s army commanders and their men were in the countryside.

They heard that Babylon’s king had put Gedaliah in charge.

Some of Judah’s poorest people still lived there.

Gedaliah would care for these men, women, and children.

## Jeremiah 40:8

The commanders brought their men to Gedaliah at Mizpah.

Ishmael son of Nethaniah came.

So did Johanan and Jonathan, the sons of Kareah.

Seraiah son of Tanhumeth came too.

Ephai’s sons came from Netophah.

Jezaniah, whose father came from Maacah, also came.

## Jeremiah 40:9

Gedaliah made a solemn promise to them.

He said, “You can serve the Chaldeans without fear.”

The Chaldeans were the people who ruled Babylon.

“Stay here and serve Babylon’s king. You will do well.”

## Jeremiah 40:10

Gedaliah said, “I will stay in Mizpah.

I will speak for you when the Chaldeans come.

Gather grapes for wine. Gather summer fruit and oil.

Fill your storage jars. Live in the cities you have taken.”

## Jeremiah 40:11

People from Judah heard the news in other lands.

Some lived in Moab, Ammon, or Edom.

They heard that Babylon’s king had left people in Judah.

They heard that he had put Gedaliah in charge.

## Jeremiah 40:12

So they returned from the places where others had driven them.

They went to Gedaliah at Mizpah in Judah.

They gathered plenty of grapes and summer fruit.

## Johanan warns Gedaliah

## Jeremiah 40:13

Johanan and the other army commanders came to Mizpah.

They had news for Gedaliah.

## Jeremiah 40:14

They asked, “Do you know what King Baalis of Ammon has done?

He has sent Ishmael son of Nethaniah to kill you.”

Gedaliah did not believe them.

## Jeremiah 40:15

Then Johanan spoke to Gedaliah alone.

“Let me kill Ishmael. No one will know.

If Ishmael kills you, the people here will scatter.

Why should the people left in Judah die?”

## Jeremiah 40:16

Gedaliah told Johanan, “Do not do that!

What you say about Ishmael is a lie.”
