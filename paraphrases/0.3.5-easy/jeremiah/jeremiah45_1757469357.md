# Jeremiah 45 — A promise for Baruch

**About this chapter**

Baruch writes down Jeremiah’s words. Baruch feels worn out. The LORD gives Jeremiah a message for him.

## Baruch writes on a scroll

## Jeremiah 45:1

Jehoiakim was king of Judah.

His father was Josiah.

This happened in Jehoiakim’s fourth year as king.

Jeremiah was a prophet, someone who spoke for God.

As Jeremiah spoke, Baruch wrote down his words.

Baruch was the son of Neriah.

He wrote on a scroll.

A scroll is a long sheet you can roll up.

![A scroll unrolled so someone can read or write on it.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

Then Jeremiah spoke to Baruch.

## Jeremiah 45:2

“Baruch, the LORD is the God of Israel.

He has a message for you.

## Baruch is tired and sad

## Jeremiah 45:3

“The LORD says, ‘Baruch, I heard what you said.

You said you were already hurting.

You said I had given you even more sorrow.

You were tired from groaning.

You could find no rest.’”

## The LORD promises to keep Baruch alive

## Jeremiah 45:4

The LORD told Jeremiah to give Baruch these words.

“Across this land, I will tear down what I built.

I will pull up what I planted.”

God speaks of the land like a building and a garden.

What He helped grow there will come down.

## Jeremiah 45:5

“Baruch, are you seeking great things for yourself?

Stop seeking them.

I will bring disaster on every living creature.

But wherever you go, I will let you escape alive.”

Baruch’s own life will be his prize.
