# Jeremiah 51 — A scroll sinks in the Euphrates

**About this chapter**

Babylon has hurt many nations. God promises to defend his people. Jeremiah gives a man named Seraiah a scroll to read aloud in Babylon.

## God remembers his people

## Jeremiah 51:1

The LORD says, “I will stir up a destroyer against Babylon,

against the people of the place called Leb-kamai.

## Jeremiah 51:2

“Strangers will come from every side when disaster strikes.

They will empty the land like wind scattering husks from grain.

## Jeremiah 51:3

“Babylon's archers will have no time to bend their bows or put on armor.

The attackers will spare none of the young soldiers.

The whole army faces destruction.

## Jeremiah 51:4

“Babylonian soldiers will fall in their own land.

In their own streets they will die of their wounds.

## Jeremiah 51:5

“Israel and Judah carry great guilt before Israel's holy God.

Yet the LORD who commands heaven's armies is still their God.

He has never left them.”

## Jeremiah 51:6

Run from Babylon! Save your lives!

Do not share in Babylon's punishment.

The LORD will repay Babylon for what it did.

## Jeremiah 51:7

Babylon was like a gold cup in the LORD's hand.

The nations drank its wine and lost their senses.

That is how Babylon affected the whole earth.

## Jeremiah 51:8

Suddenly Babylon falls and breaks apart. Weep for it!

Bring balm to ease its pain. Perhaps its wounds can heal.

## Jeremiah 51:9

“We tried to heal Babylon. Nothing helped.

Leave it! Let each of us go home.

The judgment against it reaches all the way to the clouds.”

## Jeremiah 51:10

“The LORD has shown that our cause is just.

Come to Zion. Let's tell what our God has done.”

## The LORD calls the Medes

## Jeremiah 51:11

The archers ready their arrows.

The LORD has stirred the kings of the Medes to act.

He plans to destroy Babylon for what it did to his temple.

## Jeremiah 51:12

A flag goes up against Babylon's walls.

Guards and lookouts take their places. Soldiers wait in hiding.

The LORD carries out the plan he announced against Babylon.

## Jeremiah 51:13

Babylon, you live by many waters and have great wealth.

Yet your life is like a thread someone has cut.

Your end has come.

## Jeremiah 51:14

The LORD who commands heaven's armies swears by his own name,

“Soldiers will fill your land like a swarm of locusts.

You will hear their shouts of victory.”

## God made the world

## Jeremiah 51:15

The LORD made the earth by his power.

In his wisdom he set the world firmly in place.

By his understanding he spread out the sky.

## Jeremiah 51:16

At his thunder the waters in the sky roar.

He brings clouds from the ends of the earth.

He sends lightning with rain.

He brings out the wind as if from storerooms.

## Jeremiah 51:17

People understand so little!

Goldsmiths have reason to feel ashamed of their idols.

They pour melted metal into shapes that look like gods.

Yet those shapes cannot breathe.

## Jeremiah 51:18

These idols are worthless. People can laugh at them.

When judgment comes, the idols will be gone.

## Jeremiah 51:19

Jacob's people belong to the one who made everything.

He chose Israel as his own people.

His name is the LORD who commands heaven's armies.

## A weapon and a burning mountain

## Jeremiah 51:20

The LORD says, “You are like a club in my hand.

With you I break nations and bring down kingdoms.

## Jeremiah 51:21

“With you I strike down horses and riders,

chariots and their drivers.

## Jeremiah 51:22

“With you I strike down men and women,

old people and children, young men and young women.

## Jeremiah 51:23

“With you I strike down shepherds and sheep,

farmers and oxen, governors and officials.

## Jeremiah 51:24

“You will see me repay Babylon and all the people of Chaldea.

I will answer the evil they did in Zion.

## Jeremiah 51:25

“Babylon, you loom like a mountain that destroys the earth.

I am against you. I will roll you down over the cliffs.

All that will remain is a burnt mountain.

## Jeremiah 51:26

“No one will take a stone from you for a building's corner.

No one will take a stone for its base.

You will lie empty forever.”

## Messengers run to Babylon's king

## Jeremiah 51:27

Raise a flag! Blow a ram's horn so the nations hear.

Call Ararat, Minni, and Ashkenaz together against Babylon.

A captain will lead them. Horses will come like swarming locusts.

## Jeremiah 51:28

The kings of the Medes are called to join them.

Their governors, officials, and the lands they rule must get ready.

## Jeremiah 51:29

The earth shakes and twists.

The LORD's plan for Babylon stands.

He will leave its land without people.

## Jeremiah 51:30

Babylon's soldiers have stopped fighting. They stay in their forts.

Their strength is gone. The houses are burning.

The bars that held the city gates shut are broken.

## Jeremiah 51:31

One runner meets another. More messengers rush to the king.

The news is the same. Enemies hold the whole city.

## Jeremiah 51:32

They hold the river crossings. The marshes are on fire.

Babylon's soldiers are terrified.

## Jeremiah 51:33

The LORD, Israel's God, commands heaven's armies.

He compares Babylon to a floor where people trample grain at harvest.

The time for Babylon to be trampled is near.

## Jerusalem asks God for justice

## Jeremiah 51:34

Jerusalem says, “Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon's king, crushed me.

He put me aside like an empty bowl.

Like a monster he swallowed my best food and spat me out.

## Jeremiah 51:35

“Make Babylon answer for hurting my people.

Make the people of Chaldea answer for the blood they shed.”

## Jeremiah 51:36

The LORD answers, “I will speak up for you.

I will punish Babylon for what it did to you.

I will dry up its sea and its springs.

## Jeremiah 51:37

“Babylon will be a heap of broken stones.

Jackals, a kind of wild dog, will live there.

People will shudder and mock. No one will live there.

## Jeremiah 51:38

“The Babylonians roar together like young lions.

Hear them growl like lion cubs.

## Jeremiah 51:39

“While they are excited, I will give them a feast.

They will get drunk and celebrate.

Then they will sleep forever. They will never wake up.

## Jeremiah 51:40

“I will lead them away like lambs going to slaughter,

like rams and male goats.”

## Leave Babylon and remember Jerusalem

## Jeremiah 51:41

Sheshach, another name for Babylon, has fallen!

Nations once praised it. Now they look on it with horror.

## Jeremiah 51:42

The attack overwhelms Babylon like a roaring sea.

Its waves cover the city.

## Jeremiah 51:43

The towns lie deserted. The ground is dry.

No one lives there or even passes through.

## Jeremiah 51:44

The LORD says, “I will punish Bel, Babylon's god.

I will make him give back what he swallowed.

Nations will stop flocking to him. Even Babylon's wall will fall.

## Jeremiah 51:45

“Leave Babylon, my people! Each of you, save your life.

Get away from the LORD's fierce anger.

## Jeremiah 51:46

“Take courage when rumors reach you.

One year you will hear one report, the next year another.

People will speak of violence and rulers fighting each other.

## Jeremiah 51:47

“The time will come for me to punish Babylon's idols.

Shame will cover its land. Its dead will lie where they fall.

## Jeremiah 51:48

“Attackers will come against Babylon from the north.

Then heaven and earth and everything in them will shout for joy.

## Jeremiah 51:49

“Babylon killed people throughout the earth.

Babylon too must fall for killing the people of Israel.

## Jeremiah 51:50

“You who survived the fighting, leave right away!

Even from far away, remember the LORD. Think of Jerusalem.”

## Jeremiah 51:51

“We feel ashamed when we hear the insults.

Foreigners have entered the holy rooms of the LORD's temple.

Disgrace covers our faces.”

## Babylon's walls will fall

## Jeremiah 51:52

The LORD says, “I will punish Babylon's idols.

Throughout the land the wounded will groan.

## Jeremiah 51:53

“Even if Babylon builds its fortress up to the sky,

I will send attackers who can reach it there.”

## Jeremiah 51:54

Listen to the cry from Babylon!

Hear the crash as Chaldea falls!

## Jeremiah 51:55

The LORD brings Babylon down. Its mighty voice falls silent.

The attackers roar like great waves.

Their noise echoes through the city.

## Jeremiah 51:56

A destroyer comes against Babylon.

Its soldiers become prisoners. Their bows lie broken.

The LORD sees that justice is done. He will repay in full.

## Jeremiah 51:57

“I will make the princes and wise advisers drunk.

So too the governors, officials, and soldiers.

They will sleep forever and never wake.”

The King who says this is the LORD who commands heaven's armies.

## Jeremiah 51:58

He says, “Babylon's thick walls will fall flat.

Fire will burn up its high gates.

People from many nations wore themselves out building them.

All their work will go up in flames.”

## Jeremiah sends a scroll with Seraiah

## Jeremiah 51:59

In King Zedekiah's fourth year as ruler of Judah, he traveled to Babylon.

His officer Seraiah went with him to arrange supplies.

Seraiah was Neriah's son and Mahseiah's grandson.

Jeremiah gave Seraiah instructions.

## Jeremiah 51:60

Jeremiah had written on a single scroll, a long rolled sheet.

It held all his words about the disaster coming on Babylon.

![A scroll unrolled to show its writing. Seraiah was to read Jeremiah's message aloud.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## Jeremiah 51:61

Jeremiah told Seraiah, “When you reach Babylon, read every word aloud.

## Jeremiah 51:62

“Then say, ‘LORD, you have promised to destroy this place.

No people or animals will stay here.

It will lie empty forever.’

## Jeremiah 51:63

“When you finish reading, tie a stone to the scroll.

Throw it into the Euphrates River.

## Jeremiah 51:64

“Say, ‘Babylon will sink just like this scroll.

It will never rise again after the disaster the LORD brings.

Its people will wear themselves out.’”

Here Jeremiah's words end.
