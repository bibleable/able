# Jeremiah 1 — The LORD gives Jeremiah words to speak

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah thinks he is too young to speak for God.

The LORD tells him where to go and what to say.

The LORD also promises to stay with him.

## When Jeremiah spoke for the LORD

## Jeremiah 1:1

These are the words of Jeremiah, son of Hilkiah.

His family belonged to the priests in Anathoth.

Anathoth was a town in the land of Benjamin.

## Jeremiah 1:2

The LORD began speaking to Jeremiah when Josiah ruled Judah.

It was the thirteenth year of Josiah’s reign.

Josiah’s father was Amon.

## Jeremiah 1:3

The LORD continued speaking to Jeremiah when Josiah’s son Jehoiakim was king.

Later, another son of Josiah ruled Judah.

His name was Zedekiah.

Jeremiah heard from the LORD until Zedekiah’s eleventh year as king.

In the fifth month of that year, enemies took Jerusalem’s people away from home.

## Jeremiah says he is too young

## Jeremiah 1:4

I am Jeremiah.

The LORD spoke to me.

## Jeremiah 1:5

He said, “I knew you before I formed you inside your mother.

Before you were born, I chose you for a special task.

I made you a prophet to the nations.”

A prophet speaks God’s message to people.

## Jeremiah 1:6

I answered, “Oh, Lord GOD, I do not know how to speak.

I am only a child!”

## Jeremiah 1:7

The LORD told me, “Do not say you are only a child.

Go to everyone I send you to.

Tell them everything I tell you to say.

## Jeremiah 1:8

“Do not be afraid of them.

I am with you, and I will rescue you.”

## Jeremiah 1:9

Then the LORD reached out and touched my mouth.

He said, “I have put my words in your mouth.”

The LORD himself gave me the message to speak.

## Jeremiah 1:10

The LORD said, “Today I give you authority over nations and kingdoms.

Your work is to pull up roots and tear down.

It is to destroy and bring down rulers.

It is also to build and to plant.”

These pictures describe the message Jeremiah would speak to nations.

Some would fall, and others would grow.

## The almond branch and the boiling pot

## Jeremiah 1:11

The LORD asked me, “Jeremiah, what do you see?”

I answered, “I see a branch from an almond tree.”

## Jeremiah 1:12

The LORD said, “You have seen well.

I am watching to make sure my words come true.”

## Jeremiah 1:13

Again the LORD asked me, “What do you see?”

I answered, “I see a boiling pot.

It is tipping toward us from the north.”

## Jeremiah 1:14

The LORD said, “Trouble will pour out from the north.

It will reach everyone who lives in this land.”

The tipping pot was a picture of that danger.

## Jeremiah 1:15

The LORD said, “I will call the families and kingdoms of the north.

Their kings will set their thrones at Jerusalem’s gates.

They will attack the walls around Jerusalem.

They will also attack Judah’s other cities.

## Jeremiah 1:16

“I will judge my people for the evil they have done.

They have left me.

They burned incense to other gods.

They bowed down to things they made with their own hands.”

Incense gives off a sweet smell as it burns.

## The LORD promises to protect Jeremiah

## Jeremiah 1:17

The LORD told me, “Get ready and stand up.

Tell the people everything I command you.

Do not let them frighten you.

If you do, I will make you tremble in front of them.

## Jeremiah 1:18

“Today I make you like a city with strong defenses.

You will be like an iron pillar and bronze walls.

You will stand against Judah’s kings, officials, priests, and people.”

A strong wall stands firm when enemies attack.

The LORD would help Jeremiah stand firm too.

## Jeremiah 1:19

The LORD said, “They will fight against you.

But they will never defeat you.

I am with you, and I will rescue you.”
