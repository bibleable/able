# Jeremiah 21 — A choice for the people of Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

Babylon’s army surrounds Jerusalem.

King Zedekiah hopes the LORD will send the army away.

Jeremiah brings the LORD’s answer.

## The king sends two men to Jeremiah

## Jeremiah 21:1

King Zedekiah sent two men to Jeremiah.

One was Pashhur, the son of Malchijah.

The other was Zephaniah, a priest and the son of Maaseiah.

The LORD spoke to Jeremiah when these men came.

## Jeremiah 21:2

The men said, “Please ask the LORD about us.

King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon is fighting against us.

The LORD has done wonderful things for us before.

Perhaps he will do something like that again.

Then Nebuchadnezzar might take his army away.”

## The LORD answers King Zedekiah

## Jeremiah 21:3

Jeremiah answered the men.

“Take this message back to Zedekiah.

## Jeremiah 21:4

“The LORD, the God of Israel, says,

‘You are fighting the king of Babylon and his soldiers.

Those soldiers, the Chaldeans, surround your city walls.

I will turn your own weapons against you.

I will gather the enemy soldiers in the middle of Jerusalem.

![A closed gate in a city wall. The enemy army waited outside Jerusalem’s walls.](../pictures/asa-city-gate.svg)

## Jeremiah 21:5

‘I myself will fight against you.

I will reach out my strong arm against you.

I am very angry.

## Jeremiah 21:6

‘I will strike the people and animals in this city.

A terrible disease will spread among them, and they will die.

## Jeremiah 21:7

‘Some people will survive the disease, the fighting, and the hunger.

Then I will hand them over to Nebuchadnezzar.

I will hand over King Zedekiah and his officers too.

Their enemies want them dead.

Nebuchadnezzar will kill them with the sword.

He will show them no pity.’”

## The LORD gives the people a choice

## Jeremiah 21:8

Jeremiah gave the messengers these words for the people.

“The LORD says, ‘I am giving you a choice.

One way leads to life.

The other leads to death.

## Jeremiah 21:9

‘Stay in this city, and fighting, hunger, or disease will kill you.

Go out and surrender to the Chaldeans, and you will live.

Your own life will be your prize from this war.

## Jeremiah 21:10

‘I have decided to bring disaster on this city.

I will hand it over to the king of Babylon.

He will burn it down.’”

## The LORD calls the royal family to judge fairly

## Jeremiah 21:11

Jeremiah also had a message for Judah’s royal family.

“Listen to what the LORD says.

## Jeremiah 21:12

‘You belong to King David’s family.

Every morning, give people a fair hearing.

When someone robs another person, rescue the victim from the robber.

If you keep doing evil, my anger will burn like fire.

No one will be able to put it out.

## Jeremiah 21:13

‘You live on high, rocky ground above the valley.

You say, “Who can attack us up here?

Who can get into our homes?”

But I, the LORD, am against you.

## Jeremiah 21:14

‘I will punish you for what you have done.

I will set fire to your forest.

The fire will burn up everything around you.’”
