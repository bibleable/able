# Jeremiah 19 — A broken jar shows what will happen to Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

The LORD sends Jeremiah to a valley outside Jerusalem.

He tells Jeremiah to take a clay jar with him.

The jar will help the leaders understand the LORD’s warning.

## The LORD sends Jeremiah to the valley

## Jeremiah 19:1

The LORD told Jeremiah, “Go to a potter and buy a clay jar.

Take some elders of the people with you.

Take some leaders of the priests too.”

A potter makes jars and other things from clay.

![A clay jar like the one Jeremiah was told to buy.](../pictures/clay-jar.svg)

## Jeremiah 19:2

The LORD said, “Go out to the Valley of Ben-hinnom.

Go near the entrance to the Potsherd Gate.

There, speak the words I give you.”

A potsherd is a broken piece of pottery.

## Jeremiah 19:3

Jeremiah was to say, “Kings of Judah and people of Jerusalem, listen!

This message comes from the LORD who commands heaven’s armies.

He is the God of Israel.

He says, ‘I will bring disaster on this place.

The news will make the ears of everyone who hears it ring.’”

The news would shock people that deeply.

## Why the LORD will judge the city

## Jeremiah 19:4

The LORD said, “The people have left me.

They have turned this place into a place for foreign gods.

Here they burn incense to gods they never knew before.

Their ancestors and the kings of Judah never knew those gods either.

The people have killed innocent people and filled this place with their blood.”

Incense gives off a sweet smell as it burns.

## Jeremiah 19:5

The LORD said, “They built places to worship Baal.

There they burned their children as offerings to Baal.

I never commanded that.

I never spoke of it.

It never even entered my mind.

## Jeremiah 19:6

“The time will come when this place will have a new name.

People now call it Topheth or the Valley of Ben-hinnom.

They will call it the Valley of Slaughter.”

Slaughter means a great killing.

## Jeremiah 19:7

The LORD said, “Here I will ruin the plans of Judah and Jerusalem.

Their enemies want to kill them.

I will let those enemies kill them with swords.

I will leave their bodies for birds and wild animals to eat.

## Jeremiah 19:8

“I will leave the city in ruins.

People passing by will be horrified.

They will mock the city when they see all the damage.

## Jeremiah 19:9

“Enemies who want them dead will surround the city.

The people inside will be desperate for food.

I will bring them to the point of eating their own sons and daughters.

They will even eat one another.”

## What the broken jar means

## Jeremiah 19:10

The LORD told Jeremiah, “Smash the jar while the men with you watch.

## Jeremiah 19:11

“Tell them this is what the LORD who commands heaven’s armies says.

‘I will shatter this nation and this city like a clay jar.

No one can put that jar back together.

People will bury the dead at Topheth until no space remains.

## Jeremiah 19:12

‘This is what I will do to this place and its people.

I will make Jerusalem like Topheth.

## Jeremiah 19:13

‘People burned incense on the flat roofs of their houses.

There they worshiped the stars and other lights in the sky.

They poured out drinks as gifts to other gods.

Those houses will be unclean like Topheth.

This includes the houses of Judah’s kings.’”

## Jeremiah speaks at the temple

## Jeremiah 19:14

The LORD had sent Jeremiah to speak at Topheth.

Afterward Jeremiah came back to the LORD’s temple.

He stood in the courtyard and spoke to all the people.

## Jeremiah 19:15

“This is what the LORD who commands heaven’s armies, the God of Israel, says.

‘I warned this city and its villages about the disasters to come.

Now I will bring every one of those disasters upon them.

They have stubbornly refused to listen to my words.’”
