# Jeremiah 7 — Jeremiah speaks at the temple gate

**About this chapter**

People come to Jerusalem's temple to worship. At the gate, Jeremiah gives them a message from God about how they treat their neighbors.

## Change what you do

## Jeremiah 7:1

The LORD gave Jeremiah a message.

## Jeremiah 7:2

“Stand at the gate of my temple,” the LORD said.

“Tell the people of Judah who come through these gates to worship me,

‘Listen to what the LORD says.’”

![A gateway. Jeremiah stood at the temple gate to speak to people coming to worship.](../pictures/asa-city-gate.svg)

## Jeremiah 7:3

The LORD, the God of Israel, commands heaven's armies.

He said, “Change how you live and what you do.

Then I will let you stay here.

## Jeremiah 7:4

“You keep saying, ‘The LORD's temple! The LORD's temple!

This is the LORD's temple!’

You fool yourselves if you think those words will keep you safe.

## Jeremiah 7:5

“Really change what you do.

Treat one another fairly.

## Jeremiah 7:6

“Stop mistreating foreigners, children without fathers, and widows.

Stop killing innocent people here.

Stop following other gods. You are hurting yourselves.

## Jeremiah 7:7

“Then I will let you stay here.

This is the land I gave your ancestors for all time.

## The temple cannot hide injustice

## Jeremiah 7:8

“Look at the lies you trust! They cannot help you.

## Jeremiah 7:9

“You steal and murder. You are unfaithful in marriage.

You lie after promising to tell the truth.

You burn incense to Baal.

You follow gods you never knew before.

## Jeremiah 7:10

“Then you stand before me in the temple that bears my name.

You say, ‘We are safe!’

Do you think you can go on doing these terrible things?

## Jeremiah 7:11

“Do you think my temple is a hiding place for robbers?

I have seen what you do.

## Jeremiah 7:12

“Go to Shiloh. It was the first place where I put my name.

See what I did there because Israel did evil.

## Jeremiah 7:13

“Now you too have done evil.

I spoke again and again. You refused to listen.

I called to you. You gave no answer.

## Jeremiah 7:14

“You trust this temple that bears my name.

I gave this place to you and your ancestors.

Now I will do to it what I did to Shiloh.

## Jeremiah 7:15

“I will send you away from me.

That is what I did to your relatives, the people of Ephraim.

## Families worship other gods

## Jeremiah 7:16

“Jeremiah, do not pray for these people.

Do not beg me to help them. I will not listen.

## Jeremiah 7:17

“Look at Judah's towns and the streets of Jerusalem.

Do you see what the people are doing?

## Jeremiah 7:18

“The children gather wood. The fathers light a fire.

The women knead dough and bake cakes for a goddess called the Queen of Heaven.

They pour out drinks as gifts to other gods.

These things stir up my anger.

## Jeremiah 7:19

“But who suffers because of what they do?

They bring shame on themselves.”

## Jeremiah 7:20

The Lord GOD said, “My fierce anger will fall on this place.

People and animals, trees and crops will all suffer.

My anger will burn like a fire no one can put out.”

## God asks his people to listen

## Jeremiah 7:21

The LORD who commands heaven's armies is Israel's God.

He said, “Go ahead. Put your burnt offerings with your other sacrifices.

Eat the meat yourselves!

## Jeremiah 7:22

“When I led your ancestors out of Egypt,

I had more to tell them than how to offer sacrifices.

## Jeremiah 7:23

“I told them, ‘Obey me. I will be your God.

You will be my people.

Follow all my instructions so things may go well for you.’

## Jeremiah 7:24

“They refused to listen.

They stubbornly did the evil they wanted to do.

With every step they went farther from me.

## Jeremiah 7:25

“Ever since your ancestors left Egypt, I have sent you my servants the prophets.

Again and again they brought you my words.

## Jeremiah 7:26

“Yet the people refused to listen to me.

They grew stubborn and did even worse than their ancestors.

## Jeremiah 7:27

“Jeremiah, tell them everything I have said.

They will refuse to listen to you too.

When you call to them, they will give no answer.

## Jeremiah 7:28

“Tell them, ‘You refused to listen to the LORD your God.

You refused to let him correct you.

Truth has died among you. No one speaks it anymore.

## Jeremiah 7:29

“‘Cut off your hair and throw it away as a sign of grief.

Sing a song of sorrow on the bare hills.

The LORD is angry with this generation and has turned away from it.’

## Grief in the valley

## Jeremiah 7:30

“The people of Judah have done evil in my sight.

They brought things I hate into the temple that bears my name.

They spoiled my holy place.

## Jeremiah 7:31

“They built places of worship at Topheth in the Valley of Ben-hinnom.

There they burned their own children as sacrifices.

I never gave that command. I never intended such a thing.

## Jeremiah 7:32

“Soon people will have another name for Topheth and that valley.

They will call it the Valley of Slaughter.

They will bury the dead at Topheth until it is full.

## Jeremiah 7:33

“Birds and wild animals will feed on the bodies.

No one will be there to drive them away.

## Jeremiah 7:34

“I will silence the celebrations in Judah's towns and Jerusalem's streets.

People will no longer hear the happy voices of brides and grooms.

The land will lie in ruins.”
