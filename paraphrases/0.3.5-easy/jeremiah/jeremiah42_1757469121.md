# Jeremiah 42 — The people ask Jeremiah to pray

**About this chapter**

The people must decide where to live. They ask Jeremiah to find out what the LORD wants them to do.

## The people make a promise

## Jeremiah 42:1

The army commanders came with Johanan son of Kareah.

Jezaniah son of Hoshaiah came too.

All the people joined them, whoever they were.

## Jeremiah 42:2

They spoke to Jeremiah, the LORD’s prophet.

“Please pray to the LORD your God for us.

Once there were many of us.

You can see how few of us are left.”

## Jeremiah 42:3

“Ask the LORD your God where we should go.

Ask him what we should do.”

## Jeremiah 42:4

Jeremiah said, “I hear you.

I will pray to the LORD your God for you.

I will tell you everything he says.

I will keep nothing back.”

## Jeremiah 42:5

The people told Jeremiah, “The LORD hears our promise.

If we break it, he can speak against us.

We promise to do everything he tells you.”

## Jeremiah 42:6

“We will obey the LORD our God.

We will obey even if his answer is hard to hear.

We want things to go well for us as we obey him.”

## The LORD tells them to stay

## Jeremiah 42:7

Ten days later, the LORD spoke to Jeremiah.

## Jeremiah 42:8

Jeremiah called Johanan and the other commanders.

He called all the people, whatever their place in the community.

## Jeremiah 42:9

Jeremiah said, “You asked me to take your request to the LORD.

Here is the answer from the LORD, Israel’s God.”

## Jeremiah 42:10

The LORD said, “Stay here in Judah.

I will build you up. I will plant you here.”

He meant he would help them grow strong in their land.

He would bring the disaster he had sent to an end.

## Jeremiah 42:11

“You are afraid of the king of Babylon.

Do not fear him. I am with you.

I will save you from his power.”

## Jeremiah 42:12

“I will show you mercy.

Babylon’s king will also show you mercy.

He will let you return to your land.”

## The LORD warns them about Egypt

## Jeremiah 42:13

Jeremiah said, “Suppose you say, ‘We refuse to stay here.’

Then you will be disobeying the LORD your God.”

## Jeremiah 42:14

“You may say, ‘We want to live in Egypt.

There we will see no war.

We will hear no horn warning us of battle.

There we will have enough bread to eat.’”

## Jeremiah 42:15

Jeremiah said, “People left in Judah, listen!

Israel’s God leads the armies of heaven.

Here is his answer if you insist on living in Egypt.”

## Jeremiah 42:16

“The war you fear will reach you in Egypt.

You dread being hungry. Hunger will reach you there too.

You will die in Egypt.”

## Jeremiah 42:17

“Everyone who insists on living there will die.

War, hunger, and deadly sickness will come.

No one who goes will escape the disaster I send.”

## Jeremiah 42:18

The LORD said, “I showed my anger against Jerusalem.

I will show that same anger if you go to Egypt.

People will shudder at what happens to you.

They will use your name when they curse.

They will insult you. You will never see Judah again.”

## Jeremiah reminds them of their promise

## Jeremiah 42:19

Jeremiah said, “People left in Judah, the LORD has spoken.

He told you, ‘Do not go to Egypt.’

Remember that I warned you today.”

## Jeremiah 42:20

“You fooled yourselves when you asked me to pray.

You said, ‘Ask the LORD our God for us.

Tell us everything he says. We will do it.’”

## Jeremiah 42:21

“Today I have told you his answer.

Yet you have disobeyed the LORD your God.

You have ignored the message he sent me to bring.”

## Jeremiah 42:22

“So be sure of this.

You want to go and live in Egypt.

War, hunger, and deadly sickness will kill you there.”
