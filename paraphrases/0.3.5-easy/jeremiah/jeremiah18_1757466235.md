# Jeremiah 18 — Jeremiah watches a potter reshape the clay

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah watches a potter make a pot. God uses what Jeremiah sees to explain why Judah can still change course. Later, people plot against Jeremiah, and he cries out to God.

## A pot takes a new shape

## Jeremiah 18:1

The LORD spoke to Jeremiah.

## Jeremiah 18:2

“Go down to the potter's house now.

I will give you my message there.”

A potter makes pots out of clay.

## Jeremiah 18:3

I went down to the potter's house.

The potter was working at his wheel.

The wheel turned the clay as he shaped it.

## Jeremiah 18:4

Something went wrong with the pot in his hands.

He used the same clay to make a different pot.

He shaped it the way he thought best.

![A clay jar, like the pots a potter shapes by hand.](../pictures/clay-jar.svg)

## Jeremiah 18:5

Then the LORD spoke to me.

## Jeremiah 18:6

“People of Israel, think about what the potter does with clay.

Can I not do the same with you?

You are in my hands as clay is in the potter's hands.

## God calls Judah to change

## Jeremiah 18:7

“I may warn a nation or kingdom,

‘I am going to tear you down and destroy you.’

## Jeremiah 18:8

“But suppose the people hear my warning and stop doing evil.

Then I will hold back the trouble I planned to bring.

## Jeremiah 18:9

“At another time I may tell a nation or kingdom,

‘I will build you up and help you stand firm.’

## Jeremiah 18:10

“But suppose those people do evil and refuse to listen to me.

Then I will hold back the good I planned for them.

## Jeremiah 18:11

“Jeremiah, tell the people of Judah and Jerusalem what I say.

‘I am making plans to bring disaster on you.

Each of you must turn away from evil now.

Change the way you live and what you do.’

## Jeremiah 18:12

“But they will say, ‘What is the use?

We will follow our own plans.

Each of us will do the evil we insist on doing.’”

## The people leave the good road

## Jeremiah 18:13

The LORD said, “Ask the other nations.

Who has ever heard of anything like this?

My people Israel have done something terrible.

## Jeremiah 18:14

“Does the snow leave Lebanon's rocky mountainsides?

Do the cool streams flowing from far away dry up?

## Jeremiah 18:15

“Yet my people have forgotten me.

They burn sweet-smelling incense to idols that cannot help them.

Worshiping idols is like leaving a good, familiar road.

My people stumble along rough side paths.

## Jeremiah 18:16

“They have left their land in ruins.

People will look down on it for years to come.

Those who pass by will shake their heads in horror.

## Jeremiah 18:17

“When the enemy comes, I will scatter my people like an east wind.

When trouble comes, I will turn my back on them.”

God meant he would turn away when they needed help.

## People plan to accuse Jeremiah

## Jeremiah 18:18

Some people said, “Let's make a plan against Jeremiah.

We still have priests to teach us the law.

We have wise people to give advice.

We have prophets to give us messages from God.

Let's speak against Jeremiah and ignore everything he says.”

## Jeremiah asks God to hear him

## Jeremiah 18:19

Jeremiah prayed, “Pay attention to me, LORD.

Listen to what my accusers are saying.

## Jeremiah 18:20

“Should people pay back kindness with harm?

They have dug a pit to trap me.

Remember how I stood before you and spoke up for them.

I asked you to turn your anger away from them.

## Jeremiah 18:21

“Now let hunger take their children.

Let war come upon them.

Let the women lose their children and husbands.

Let disease take the husbands.

Let the young men die in battle.

## Jeremiah 18:22

“Let cries rise from their houses when attackers come without warning.

These people dug a pit to catch me.

They hid traps for my feet.

## Jeremiah 18:23

“LORD, you know every plan they made to kill me.

Do not wipe away their guilt or overlook their sin.

Let them fall before you.

Deal with them when you act in anger.”
