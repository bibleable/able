# Jeremiah 23 — God promises a king who will do right

**About this chapter**

God speaks about leaders as shepherds who should care for sheep. He promises a good king. He also warns the people about prophets who speak lies in his name.

## God will gather his scattered sheep

## Jeremiah 23:1

The LORD said, “Trouble is coming to the shepherds!

They destroy and scatter the sheep in my pasture.”

The shepherds are Israel's leaders. The sheep are God's people.

## Jeremiah 23:2

The LORD, the God of Israel, told the leaders,

“You drove my people away and scattered them.

You did nothing to care for them.

Now I will deal with you for the evil you did.

## Jeremiah 23:3

“I sent my people away to many lands.

I myself will gather those who are left.

Like sheep returning to pasture, they will come home.

They will have children, and their families will grow.

## Jeremiah 23:4

“I will choose new leaders to care for them.

My people will no longer live in fear.

No one will go missing.”

## A new king from David's family

## Jeremiah 23:5

The LORD said, “A day is coming when I will raise up a righteous Branch for David.”

A new branch grows from an old tree.

God meant a new king would come from David's family.

“He will rule wisely. He will do what is right.

He will see that people receive justice in the land.

![A leafy branch. The new branch pictures a king from David's family.](../pictures/branch.svg)

## Jeremiah 23:6

“In his days I will save Judah.

Israel's people will live in safety.

People will call him ‘The LORD Our Righteousness.’

That name points to the LORD who makes us right with him.

## Jeremiah 23:7

“Today people say, ‘As surely as the LORD lives!

He brought Israel's people out of Egypt.’

One day they will use different words.

## Jeremiah 23:8

“They will say, ‘As surely as the LORD lives!

He led Israel's families home from the north.

He brought them back from every land where he had sent them.’

They will live on their own land again.”

## Jeremiah grieves over the prophets

## Jeremiah 23:9

Jeremiah spoke about the prophets.

“My heart is breaking. My whole body shakes.

I stagger like a man who has drunk too much wine.

The LORD's holy words have shaken me.

## Jeremiah 23:10

“Many people in this land are unfaithful to their husbands or wives.

The land suffers under a curse.

Even the grass in the wilderness has dried up.

People do evil and use their power to do wrong.”

## Jeremiah 23:11

The LORD said, “Both prophets and priests have turned against me.

Even in my temple I have seen their evil deeds.

## Jeremiah 23:12

“Their path will be like a slippery road in the dark.

They will stumble and fall.

When the time comes to punish them, I will bring disaster.

## Jeremiah 23:13

“I saw the prophets in Samaria do something offensive.

They spoke for Baal, a false god.

They led my people Israel away from me.

## Jeremiah 23:14

“The prophets in Jerusalem do something horrible too.

They sleep with other people's spouses and live by lies.

They encourage people to keep doing evil.

So no one gives up their wrongdoing.

To me these prophets are like the wicked people of Sodom.

Jerusalem's people are like the people of Gomorrah.”

## Jeremiah 23:15

The LORD who commands heaven's armies said,

“I will give those prophets bitter plants to eat and poisoned water to drink.

The prophets of Jerusalem have taught people throughout the land to live without regard for me.”

## The prophets give false hope

## Jeremiah 23:16

The LORD who commands heaven's armies said, “Do not listen to these prophets.

They fill you with hopes that will come to nothing.

The visions they describe come from their own minds.

I did not show them those things.

## Jeremiah 23:17

“To people who look down on me they say,

‘The LORD promises you peace.’

To those who stubbornly go their own way they say,

‘Nothing bad will happen to you.’

## Jeremiah 23:18

“Which of them has stood in my council?

That is where they would hear my plans.

Which of them has listened to my words and obeyed?”

## Jeremiah 23:19

The LORD's anger comes like a fierce storm.

Like a whirlwind, it sweeps down on people who do evil.

## Jeremiah 23:20

His anger will last until he carries out his plans.

In days to come you will understand this clearly.

## Jeremiah 23:21

God said, “These prophets ran to give you their message.

Yet I never sent them.

They spoke in my name. Yet I never spoke to them.

## Jeremiah 23:22

“If they had stood in my council, they would have told you my words.

They would have turned my people away from doing evil.”

## God knows where their words come from

## Jeremiah 23:23

The LORD asked, “Am I God only here nearby?

I am God far away too.

## Jeremiah 23:24

“Where could anyone hide so I could not see them?

I fill the heavens and the earth.

## Jeremiah 23:25

“I hear the prophets who tell lies in my name.

They keep saying, ‘I had a dream! I had a dream!’

## Jeremiah 23:26

“How long will they go on like this?

They tell people lies they have dreamed up themselves.

## Jeremiah 23:27

“They think sharing their dreams will make my people forget me.

Their ancestors forgot me when they worshiped Baal.

## Jeremiah 23:28

“A prophet who has a dream can tell the dream.

Anyone who has my word must tell it truthfully.

Can straw compare to grain?”

Straw is the dry stalk. Grain feeds people.

The prophets' dreams cannot take the place of God's word.

## Jeremiah 23:29

The LORD asked, “Isn't my word like fire?

Isn't it like a hammer that breaks a rock?”

Fire and a hammer show the power of God's word.

## Jeremiah 23:30

The LORD said, “I oppose prophets who copy each other's words.

They say those words came from me.

## Jeremiah 23:31

“I oppose prophets who make their own speeches and say,

‘The LORD has spoken.’

## Jeremiah 23:32

“I oppose prophets who tell people false dreams.

Their careless lies lead my people astray.

I never sent them or gave them orders.

They do my people no good at all.”

## Ask what the LORD has said

## Jeremiah 23:33

The LORD told Jeremiah, “A prophet, priest, or someone else may ask,

‘What is the burden of the LORD?’

A burden is a heavy load. Here it can mean a message from God.

Answer them, ‘What burden? The LORD says he will leave you.’

## Jeremiah 23:34

“If anyone says, ‘This is the burden of the LORD,’

I will punish that person and their household.

## Jeremiah 23:35

“When speaking to family or friends, ask,

‘What answer did the LORD give?’

Or ask, ‘What did the LORD say?’

## Jeremiah 23:36

“Stop using the words ‘the burden of the LORD.’

Each person's own words become their burden.

You twist the words of the living God, your God who commands heaven's armies.

## Jeremiah 23:37

“When you speak to a prophet, ask,

‘How did the LORD answer you? What did he say?’

## Jeremiah 23:38

“I warned you against saying, ‘This is the burden of the LORD.’

Suppose you still insist on saying it.

## Jeremiah 23:39

“Then I will forget you and send you away from me.

I will cast away your city too.

That is the city I gave you and your ancestors.

## Jeremiah 23:40

“I will bring lasting shame and disgrace upon you.

No one will ever forget it.”
