# Jeremiah 26 — Jeremiah speaks in his own defense

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah brings a warning to the temple in Jerusalem. Some people want him put to death. The officials hear what Jeremiah has to say.

## God sends Jeremiah to the temple courtyard

## Jeremiah 26:1

Jehoiakim, son of Josiah, had just begun to rule Judah.

The LORD gave Jeremiah a message.

## Jeremiah 26:2

The LORD said, “Stand in the temple courtyard.

People come there from towns all over Judah to worship.

Tell them everything I told you to say.

Do not leave out a single word.

## Jeremiah 26:3

“Perhaps they will listen.

Perhaps each person will stop doing wrong.

Then I can hold back the trouble I planned because of their evil deeds.

## Jeremiah 26:4

“Tell them, ‘I am the LORD. Listen to me.

Follow the teaching I have given you.

## Jeremiah 26:5

“‘Again and again I sent my servants the prophets to you.

They spoke my words, but you refused to listen.

Hear what they say now.

## Jeremiah 26:6

“‘If you keep refusing, this temple will become like Shiloh.’

Shiloh was a place of worship that came to ruin.

God said, ‘People everywhere will use Jerusalem's name as a curse.

They will wish the same ruin on others.’”

## The priests and prophets accuse Jeremiah

## Jeremiah 26:7

The priests, the prophets, and all the people were listening.

They heard Jeremiah give this warning in the LORD's temple.

## Jeremiah 26:8

Jeremiah finished telling them everything the LORD had commanded.

Then the priests, the prophets, and the people grabbed him.

They shouted, “You must die!

## Jeremiah 26:9

“How dare you say the LORD gave you this message?

You say this temple will become like Shiloh.

You say Jerusalem will lie empty with nobody living here!”

The people crowded around Jeremiah in the temple.

## Jeremiah 26:10

Judah's officials heard what was happening.

They went from the palace up to the temple.

They took their seats at the entrance called the New Gate.

![A gateway. The officials sat at the entrance of the temple’s New Gate to hear the case.](../pictures/asa-city-gate.svg)

## Jeremiah 26:11

The priests and prophets spoke to the officials and the people.

“This man deserves to die!

You yourselves heard him speak against our city.”

## Jeremiah answers the charge

## Jeremiah 26:12

Jeremiah spoke to the officials and everyone gathered there.

“The LORD sent me to warn this temple and this city.

You heard the message he gave me.

## Jeremiah 26:13

“Change how you live. Stop doing wrong.

Listen to the LORD your God and obey him.

Then he may hold back the trouble he announced.

## Jeremiah 26:14

“You have me in your power.

Do to me what you think is right.

## Jeremiah 26:15

“But remember this. If you kill me, you kill an innocent man.

You, this city, and its people will share the guilt.

The LORD really did send me.

He told me to speak these words to you.”

## Jeremiah 26:16

The officials and all the people answered the priests and prophets.

“Jeremiah does not deserve to die.

He spoke to us in the name of the LORD our God.”

## The elders remember Micah

## Jeremiah 26:17

Some of the elders, leaders from the land, stood up.

They spoke to everyone gathered there.

## Jeremiah 26:18

“Remember Micah from Moresheth, when Hezekiah was king of Judah.

Micah gave all Judah a message from the LORD who commands heaven's armies.

He said, ‘People will plow Zion like a field.

Jerusalem will be a pile of broken stones.

Trees will grow on the hill where the temple stands.’

## Jeremiah 26:19

“Did King Hezekiah or anyone in Judah kill Micah? No.

Hezekiah honored the LORD and asked for his favor.

The LORD held back the trouble he had announced.

If we kill Jeremiah, we will bring great harm on ourselves!”

## What happened to Uriah and Jeremiah

## Jeremiah 26:20

Another prophet also spoke in the LORD's name.

He was Uriah, son of Shemaiah, from Kiriath-jearim.

Like Jeremiah, Uriah warned Jerusalem and the whole land.

## Jeremiah 26:21

King Jehoiakim, his warriors, and his officials heard Uriah.

The king wanted Uriah dead.

Uriah heard this and was afraid. He fled to Egypt.

## Jeremiah 26:22

King Jehoiakim sent Elnathan, son of Achbor, to Egypt.

Other men went with Elnathan.

## Jeremiah 26:23

They brought Uriah back to King Jehoiakim.

The king had him killed with a sword.

His men threw Uriah's body where the common people were buried.

## Jeremiah 26:24

But Ahikam, son of Shaphan, stood up for Jeremiah.

With Ahikam's help, Jeremiah stayed safe.

The people who wanted to kill him could not take him.
