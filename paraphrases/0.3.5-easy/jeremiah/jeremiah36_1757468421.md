# Jeremiah 36 — Baruch writes God's words on a scroll

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah speaks God's message, and Baruch writes it down. The people hear the message. Then someone reads it to the king.

## God wants the people to hear his warning

## Jeremiah 36:1

It was Jehoiakim’s fourth year as king of Judah.

He was the son of King Josiah.

That year the LORD spoke to Jeremiah.

## Jeremiah 36:2

“Get a scroll,” the LORD said.

A scroll is a long sheet that rolls up.

“Write down everything I told you about Israel, Judah, and all the nations.

Start with my first words to you when Josiah was king.

Include everything up to today.

## Jeremiah 36:3

“The people of Judah will hear what trouble I plan to bring.

Perhaps each person will stop doing wrong.

Then I will forgive their sins.”

## Jeremiah 36:4

Jeremiah called Baruch, the son of Neriah.

Jeremiah repeated everything the LORD had told him.

Baruch wrote those words on a scroll.

![A scroll like the one Baruch wrote on.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## Jeremiah 36:5

Jeremiah told Baruch, “I cannot go into the LORD's temple.

## Jeremiah 36:6

“So you must go on a day of fasting.

On that day people go without food before God.

Read aloud the LORD's words that you wrote down.

Let everyone from the towns of Judah hear them.

## Jeremiah 36:7

“Perhaps the people will ask the LORD for help.

Perhaps each person will stop doing wrong.

The LORD has warned them how angry he is with them.”

## Jeremiah 36:8

Baruch did everything Jeremiah the prophet told him.

He read the LORD's words aloud in the temple.

## Baruch reads at the temple

## Jeremiah 36:9

This happened in the ninth month of Jehoiakim's fifth year as king.

The people had heard a call to fast before the LORD.

It was for everyone in Jerusalem.

It included visitors from all the towns of Judah.

## Jeremiah 36:10

Baruch read Jeremiah's words so everyone could hear.

He read from Gemariah's room in the upper courtyard.

It stood beside the temple's New Gate.

Gemariah's father was Shaphan the scribe.

A scribe was someone who wrote official records.

## Jeremiah 36:11

Micaiah heard all the LORD's words from the scroll.

Micaiah was Gemariah's son and Shaphan's grandson.

## Jeremiah 36:12

Micaiah went down to the scribe's room in the palace.

The officials were sitting there together.

Elishama the scribe was there, with Delaiah son of Shemaiah.

Elnathan son of Achbor and Gemariah son of Shaphan were there.

So were Zedekiah son of Hananiah and the other officials.

## Jeremiah 36:13

Micaiah told the officials everything he had heard.

He repeated what Baruch had read to the people.

## The officials listen to the scroll

## Jeremiah 36:14

The officials sent Jehudi to Baruch.

Jehudi's father was Nethaniah.

His grandfather was Shelemiah, and his great-grandfather was Cushi.

Jehudi said, “Bring the scroll you read to the people.”

Baruch took the scroll and went to the officials.

## Jeremiah 36:15

The officials said, “Please sit down and read to us.”

So Baruch read the scroll aloud.

## Jeremiah 36:16

The officials heard the whole message.

They looked at each other in fear.

They said, “We must tell the king all of this.”

## Jeremiah 36:17

They asked Baruch, “How did you write these words?

Did Jeremiah speak them to you?”

## Jeremiah 36:18

Baruch said, “Yes. Jeremiah spoke every word to me.

I wrote the words on the scroll with ink.”

## Jeremiah 36:19

The officials told Baruch, “You and Jeremiah must hide.

Keep your hiding place secret.”

## The king burns the scroll

## Jeremiah 36:20

The officials left the scroll in Elishama's room.

Then they went to the king in the courtyard.

They told the king everything.

## Jeremiah 36:21

The king sent Jehudi for the scroll.

Jehudi brought it from Elishama's room.

He read it aloud to the king.

The officials standing beside the king heard it too.

## Jeremiah 36:22

It was the ninth month.

The king sat in the rooms he used in winter.

A fire burned in a pot in front of him.

## Jeremiah 36:23

Jehudi would read three or four columns of writing.

Then King Jehoiakim would cut off that part with a scribe's knife.

He would throw that piece into the fire.

He kept doing this until the whole scroll burned up.

## Jeremiah 36:24

The king and his servants heard the warning without fear.

None of them tore their clothes.

People sometimes tore their clothes to show deep sorrow.

## Jeremiah 36:25

Elnathan, Delaiah, and Gemariah begged the king to spare the scroll.

The king would not listen.

## Jeremiah 36:26

The king ordered three men to arrest Baruch and Jeremiah.

One was Jerahmeel, the king's son.

The others were Seraiah son of Azriel and Shelemiah son of Abdeel.

But the LORD had hidden Baruch and Jeremiah.

## Baruch writes the words again

## Jeremiah 36:27

The king had burned the words Baruch wrote down for Jeremiah.

Then the LORD spoke to Jeremiah again.

## Jeremiah 36:28

“Take another scroll.

Write the same words that were on the first scroll.

Those are the words Jehoiakim burned.

## Jeremiah 36:29

“Give my message about Jehoiakim, king of Judah.

Tell him, ‘You burned the scroll.

You asked why I had Jeremiah write about Babylon's king.

The scroll said Babylon's king would destroy this land.

He would leave it without people or animals.’

## Jeremiah 36:30

“So here is what I say about Jehoiakim.

No one from his family will sit on David's throne to rule.

People will throw his dead body outside.

It will lie in the heat by day and the frost by night.

## Jeremiah 36:31

“I will punish Jehoiakim, his family after him, and his servants for doing wrong.

I warned them and the people of Jerusalem and Judah.

They refused to listen.

So I will bring all that trouble on them.”

## Jeremiah 36:32

Jeremiah gave another scroll to Baruch, the son of Neriah.

Jeremiah spoke, and Baruch wrote down every word from the burned scroll.

Baruch also wrote many more words like them.
