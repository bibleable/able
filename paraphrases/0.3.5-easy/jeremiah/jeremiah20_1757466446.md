# Jeremiah 20 — Jeremiah cannot keep God’s message to himself

**About this chapter**

A priest punishes Jeremiah for speaking God’s message.

Jeremiah tells the LORD how it feels when friends turn against him.

He praises the LORD, then cries out in deep sorrow.

## A priest punishes Jeremiah

## Jeremiah 20:1

Pashhur, son of Immer, was a priest.

He was the chief official in the LORD’s temple.

He heard Jeremiah speak God’s warning to the people.

## Jeremiah 20:2

Pashhur ordered men to beat Jeremiah and put him in the stocks.

Stocks were a wooden frame that held a prisoner in place.

They held Jeremiah at the Upper Gate of Benjamin beside the temple.

## Jeremiah 20:3

The next day Pashhur let Jeremiah out of the stocks.

Jeremiah told him, “The LORD has a new name for you.

He calls you Magor-missabib.”

The name means “terror on every side.”

![Pashhur’s new name, Magor-missabib, means terror on every side.](../pictures/pashhur-new-name.svg)

## Jeremiah 20:4

Jeremiah said, “The LORD says, ‘I will make you a terror to yourself and your friends.

You will see enemies kill your friends with swords.

I will hand Judah over to the king of Babylon.

He will take people away to Babylon and kill people with the sword.

## Jeremiah 20:5

‘I will give Jerusalem’s wealth to its enemies.

They will take the things people made and everything of value.

They will seize the treasures of Judah’s kings too.

They will carry it all to Babylon.

## Jeremiah 20:6

‘Pashhur, enemies will take you and your whole household prisoner.

You told your friends lies and claimed to speak for me.

You and those friends will die in Babylon.

People will bury you there.’”

## Jeremiah tells the LORD how people mock him

## Jeremiah 20:7

LORD, you deceived me, and I believed you.

You were stronger than I was, and you won.

Everyone laughs at me all day long.

## Jeremiah 20:8

Whenever I speak, I have to shout the warning,

“Violence! Destruction!”

People insult me all day for speaking your message.

## Jeremiah 20:9

Sometimes I say, “I will stop talking about the LORD.

I will no longer speak in his name.”

Then his message feels like a fire inside me.

It burns deep in my bones.

Trying to keep it in wears me out.

I cannot keep silent.

## Jeremiah 20:10

I hear people whisper, “Terror on every side!

Report him! Let’s report him!”

Even the friends I trust watch for me to slip.

They say, “Maybe we can trick him.

Then we can defeat him and take our revenge.”

## Jeremiah puts his case in God’s hands

## Jeremiah 20:11

But the LORD stands beside me like a mighty warrior.

Those who hunt me will stumble.

They will fail, and their failure will bring them deep shame.

No one will ever forget their disgrace.

## Jeremiah 20:12

LORD, you command heaven’s armies.

You test those who do right.

You know what people think and what is in their hearts.

I have put my case in your hands.

Let me see you repay those who hurt me.

## Jeremiah 20:13

Sing to the LORD!

Praise him!

He rescues people in need from those who do evil.

## Jeremiah grieves over the day he was born

## Jeremiah 20:14

I curse the day I was born!

May no one bless the day my mother gave birth to me.

## Jeremiah 20:15

I curse the man who brought my father the news.

He told him, “You have a son!”

My father was so happy to hear it.

## Jeremiah 20:16

May that man end up like the cities the LORD destroyed without pity.

May he hear cries of alarm in the morning.

May he hear shouts of battle at noon.

## Jeremiah 20:17

Why did he let me live inside my mother?

If he had killed me there, her womb would have been my grave.

She would have carried me forever.

## Jeremiah 20:18

Why was I born only to see trouble and sorrow?

Why must my life end in shame?
