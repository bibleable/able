# Jeremiah 31 — God promises to bring his people home

**About this chapter**

God promises to gather the scattered families of Israel. He will comfort people who weep. He will make a new covenant with Israel and Judah.

## The people will dance and plant again

## Jeremiah 31:1

The LORD said, “At that time I will be the God of every family in Israel.

They will be my people.”

## Jeremiah 31:2

The LORD said, “Some of my people survived the fighting.

They went looking for a place to rest.

In the wilderness they found kindness from me.”

## Jeremiah 31:3

Long ago the LORD appeared to us.

He said, “My love for you lasts forever.

With faithful love I have drawn you close.

## Jeremiah 31:4

“Israel, you are like a young woman to me.

I will build you up again.

You will play your tambourines and dance for joy.”

A tambourine is a small drum you can shake or tap.

## Jeremiah 31:5

“You will plant grapevines on the hills of Samaria again.

The farmers who plant them will enjoy the grapes.

![A vine bearing grapes like those the farmers will enjoy.](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

## Jeremiah 31:6

“One day the lookouts on Ephraim's hills will call,

‘Come! Let us go up to Zion to worship the LORD our God.’”

Zion is in Jerusalem.

## God will guide the travelers home

## Jeremiah 31:7

The LORD said, “Sing for joy for Jacob's people, Israel!

Shout for the nation that leads the nations!

Let people hear your praise.

Say, ‘LORD, save the people of Israel who are left!’

## Jeremiah 31:8

“I will bring them from the north.

I will gather them from earth's farthest places.

Blind people and people who have trouble walking will come.

Pregnant women and women giving birth will come too.

A great crowd will come home together.

## Jeremiah 31:9

“They will weep and ask me for help.

I will guide them beside streams of water.

Their path will be level so they do not stumble.

I am Israel's father.

Ephraim is like my firstborn son.”

## Jeremiah 31:10

Nations, listen to the LORD.

Tell people even on faraway coasts,

“God scattered the people of Israel. He will gather them again.

He will guard them as a shepherd guards his sheep.

## Jeremiah 31:11

“The LORD has set Jacob's people free.

He rescued them from an enemy stronger than they were.

## Jeremiah 31:12

“They will shout for joy on the heights of Zion.

Their faces will shine because the LORD gives so much.

They will have grain, fresh wine, oil, and young sheep and cattle.

Their lives will be like a garden with plenty of water.

They will thrive instead of wasting away.”

## Jeremiah 31:13

The LORD said, “Young women will dance for joy.

Young men and old men will rejoice too.

I will comfort those who mourn.

Their sorrow will turn to joy.

## Jeremiah 31:14

“I will give the priests more than enough.

My people will be satisfied with my good gifts.”

## God hears Rachel and Ephraim weep

## Jeremiah 31:15

The LORD said, “Listen to the bitter crying in Ramah.

Rachel is weeping for her children. They are gone.

She refuses all comfort.”

Rachel was an ancestor of the people who had been taken away.

## Jeremiah 31:16

The LORD told Rachel, “You can stop weeping now.

I will reward you for your work.

Your children will return from the enemy's land.

## Jeremiah 31:17

“You have hope for the future.

Your children will come back to their own land.

## Jeremiah 31:18

“I have heard the people of Ephraim groaning.

They say, ‘We were like a calf that resists training.

You corrected us firmly.

Bring us back so we can return to you.

You are the LORD our God.

## Jeremiah 31:19

“‘When we came back, we were sorry for our sins.

When we understood, we struck our thighs in grief.

We felt shame for the things we did when young.’

## Jeremiah 31:20

“Ephraim is my precious son, a child I delight in.

Even when I speak against him, I remember him.

I long for him and care deeply for him.

## The road back to Judah

## Jeremiah 31:21

“Put signs along the road.

Remember the path you took when you left.

Israel, my young daughter, come back to your towns.

## Jeremiah 31:22

“My daughter, you have turned away from me.

How long will you wander?

The LORD has made something new happen in the land.

A woman will give shelter to a man.”

## Jeremiah 31:23

The LORD commands heaven's armies. He is the God of Israel.

He said, “I will bring the captives home.

In Judah's towns people will speak this blessing again.

‘May the LORD bless you, holy mountain,

a place where people do what is right.’

## Jeremiah 31:24

“People will live together in Judah and its towns.

Farmers and shepherds who travel with their flocks will live there.

## Jeremiah 31:25

“I will give rest to those who are tired.

I will give strength to those who are weak.”

## Jeremiah 31:26

Then I, Jeremiah, woke up and looked around.

My sleep had been sweet.

## Each person answers for their own actions

## Jeremiah 31:27

The LORD said, “A farmer scatters seed across a field.

In the same way, I will fill Israel and Judah with people and animals.

## Jeremiah 31:28

“I watched over them as I pulled up and tore down.

I brought ruin on them.

Now I will watch over them to build and plant.

## Jeremiah 31:29

“In those days people will stop using this saying,

‘The parents ate sour grapes.

Their children's teeth feel the sourness.’

## Jeremiah 31:30

“Each person will die for their own wrongdoing.

The one who eats the grapes will taste the sourness.”

## God promises a new covenant

## Jeremiah 31:31

The LORD said, “The time is coming when I will make a new covenant with Israel and Judah.”

A covenant is a firm promise that binds people together.

## Jeremiah 31:32

“When I led their ancestors out of Egypt, I took them by the hand.

I made a covenant with them then.

They broke it, though I cared for them like a husband.

The new covenant will be different.

## Jeremiah 31:33

“Here is the promise I will make to Israel.

I will put my law in their minds.

I will write it on their hearts.

My teaching will be part of who they are.

I will be their God. They will be my people.

## Jeremiah 31:34

“No one will need to tell a neighbor or a brother, ‘Know the LORD.’

They will all know me, from the least to the greatest.

I will forgive the wrong they have done.

I will never again hold their sins against them.”

## God's promise to Israel will last

## Jeremiah 31:35

The LORD gives the sun to light the day.

He sets the moon and stars to light the night.

He stirs up the sea until its waves roar.

His name is the LORD who commands heaven's armies.

## Jeremiah 31:36

The LORD said, “Only if this order disappears could Israel's children cease to be a nation before me.

## Jeremiah 31:37

“Can anyone measure the whole sky?

Can anyone explore all the foundations under the earth?

Only then would I reject all Israel's children for the wrong they have done.”

## Jerusalem will belong to God

## Jeremiah 31:38

The LORD said, “One day people will rebuild Jerusalem for me.

It will reach from Hananel's Tower to the Corner Gate.

## Jeremiah 31:39

“They will measure straight out to the hill of Gareb.

Then the boundary will turn toward Goah.

## Jeremiah 31:40

“The whole valley with its dead bodies and ashes will belong to me.

So will all the fields as far as the Kidron Valley.

Even the corner of the Horse Gate in the east will be holy to me.

No one will ever tear the city down again.”
