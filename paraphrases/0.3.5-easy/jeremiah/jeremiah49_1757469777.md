# Jeremiah 49 — Messages for nations around Israel

**About this chapter**

God speaks about Ammon, Edom, Damascus, Kedar, Hazor, and Elam. He tells people who feel safe why their wealth, armies, and strong cities will fail them.

## Ammon has taken Israel's land

## Jeremiah 49:1

The LORD asked the people of Ammon, “Does Israel have no children left to inherit the land?

Why has Milcom, Ammon's god, taken over the land of Gad?

Why do his people live in Israel's towns?

## Jeremiah 49:2

“One day I will raise a battle cry against Rabbah in Ammon.

Rabbah will become a pile of stones. Its villages will burn.

Israel will drive out the people who took its land.

## Jeremiah 49:3

“People of Heshbon, weep! Ai lies in ruins.

People of Rabbah, cry out! Wear rough clothes to show your grief.

Run back and forth inside your walls.

Enemies will carry Milcom away, along with his priests and officials.

## Jeremiah 49:4

“Ammon, why boast about the crops your valleys grow?

You have been unfaithful. You trust your wealth and ask,

‘Who could ever attack me?’”

## Jeremiah 49:5

The Lord GOD, who commands heaven's armies, said,

“Your neighbors will bring you terror on every side.

Each of you will run away.

No one will gather the people who escape.

## Jeremiah 49:6

“Yet afterward I will bring the people of Ammon back from captivity.”

## Edom cannot hide

## Jeremiah 49:7

The LORD who commands heaven's armies spoke about Edom.

“Where are the wise people of Teman?

Can no one there give good advice anymore?

## Jeremiah 49:8

“People of Dedan, run away and hide!

The time has come for me to punish Esau's people, the people of Edom.

## Jeremiah 49:9

“Grape pickers usually leave some grapes on the branches.

Thieves at night take only what they want.

![Grapes on a vine. God asks Edom to think of the grapes left after picking.](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

## Jeremiah 49:10

“But I will take everything from Esau's people.

I will uncover every hiding place. No one can hide.

Esau's children, relatives, and neighbors will perish.

Esau's people will be gone.

## Jeremiah 49:11

“Leave your children who have lost their parents with me.

I will keep them alive. Your widows can trust me.”

## Jeremiah 49:12

The LORD said, “Think of punishment as a cup to drink.

Even those who did not deserve it had to drink.

How can you expect to escape? You must drink too.

## Jeremiah 49:13

“I swear by my own name that Bozrah will lie empty and ruined.

People will mock it and use its name as a curse.

All its towns will stay in ruins forever.”

## Edom's high cliffs cannot keep it safe

## Jeremiah 49:14

Jeremiah said, “I heard a message from the LORD.

A messenger has gone to the nations to call them together.

‘Get ready to march against Edom! Get up and fight!’”

## Jeremiah 49:15

God said to Edom, “I will make you small among the nations.

People will look down on you.

## Jeremiah 49:16

“You frighten others. That has made you proud.

You think you are safe in your rocky cliffs and mountain heights.

Even an eagle's high nest would not keep you safe from me.

I will bring you down.”

## Jeremiah 49:17

The LORD said, “Travelers will shudder when they see Edom.

They will mock you when they see the harm you have suffered.

## Jeremiah 49:18

“Sodom, Gomorrah, and their neighbors fell.

Like them, Edom will have no one left living there.

## Jeremiah 49:19

“A lion comes out of the bushes by the Jordan River.

It rushes into a green pasture. That is how suddenly Edom's enemy will come.

In a moment I will drive Edom from its land.

I will choose whom to put in charge.

Who is like me? Who can call me to account?

What shepherd can stand against me?”

## Jeremiah 49:20

Hear what the LORD has planned for Edom and Teman.

The people are like a flock of sheep.

Enemies will drag away even the smallest.

Their pasture will lie in ruins.

## Jeremiah 49:21

When Edom falls, the earth will shake.

People as far away as the Red Sea will hear the cry.

## Jeremiah 49:22

Look! The enemy swoops like an eagle spreading its wings over Bozrah.

That day Edom's strongest soldiers will tremble.

Their distress will be like the pain of giving birth.

## News of an attack on Damascus

## Jeremiah 49:23

Hamath and Arpad have heard terrible news.

Their people feel ashamed. They churn like a rough sea.

Nothing can calm their fear.

## Jeremiah 49:24

The people of Damascus have lost their strength. They turn to flee.

Panic seizes them. They hurt like a woman giving birth.

## Jeremiah 49:25

The LORD asks, “Why have people not left this famous city,

the city that brings me joy?

## Jeremiah 49:26

“Its young men will fall in its streets.

On that day all its soldiers will fall silent.”

## Jeremiah 49:27

“I will set the walls of Damascus on fire.

The fire will burn up King Ben-hadad's strong buildings.”

## Kedar and Hazor lose what they own

## Jeremiah 49:28

King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon defeated Kedar and the kingdoms of Hazor.

The LORD said, “March against Kedar.

Bring ruin on these people of the east.”

## Jeremiah 49:29

The attackers will take their tents, flocks, and tent curtains.

They will carry off their belongings and their camels.

They will shout, “Danger is all around you!”

## Jeremiah 49:30

“People of Hazor, run quickly and hide!” says the LORD.

“King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon has made plans against you.”

## Jeremiah 49:31

The LORD tells the attackers, “Go against these people who live at ease.

They live by themselves and feel safe.

They have no gates and no bars to lock them.

## Jeremiah 49:32

“Enemies will take their camels and large herds.

These people cut the hair on the sides of their heads.

I will scatter them in every direction.

Trouble will come on them from every side.

## Jeremiah 49:33

“Jackals, a kind of wild dog, will live in Hazor.

The place will lie empty forever. No people will live there.”

## God speaks about Elam

## Jeremiah 49:34

Zedekiah had just become king of Judah.

The LORD gave Jeremiah the prophet a message about Elam.

## Jeremiah 49:35

The LORD who commands heaven's armies said,

“Elam's soldiers rely on their bows for strength.

I will break Elam's bow.

## Jeremiah 49:36

“I will bring winds from all four directions against Elam.

I will scatter the people in every direction.

People driven from Elam will go to every nation.

## Jeremiah 49:37

“I will break their strength before enemies who want them dead.

In my fierce anger I will bring disaster on them.

War will follow them until I bring them to an end.

## Jeremiah 49:38

“I will set my throne in Elam.”

God meant that he would rule there.

“I will destroy its king and officials.

## Jeremiah 49:39

“Yet in days to come, I will bring Elam's people back from captivity.”
