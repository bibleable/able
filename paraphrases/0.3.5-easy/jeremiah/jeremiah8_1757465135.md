# Jeremiah 8 — Jeremiah grieves for his wounded people

**About this chapter**

God asks why the people of Jerusalem refuse to turn back to him. Their leaders tell them all is well. Jeremiah sees how deeply his people are hurting.

## The graves will lie open

## Jeremiah 8:1

The LORD said, “At that time people will open the graves in Jerusalem.

They will take out the bones of Judah's kings and officials.

They will take out the bones of priests, prophets, and other people too.

## Jeremiah 8:2

“The bones will lie under the sun, moon, and stars.

These people loved and worshiped those lights in the sky.

They looked to them for guidance.

No one will gather the bones to bury them again.

They will lie like dung on the ground.

## Jeremiah 8:3

“I will drive the survivors of these people into other lands.

Wherever they go, they will wish they were dead.”

These are the words of the LORD who commands heaven's armies.

## Why will Jerusalem not turn back?

## Jeremiah 8:4

The LORD told Jeremiah to give the people this message.

“When people fall down, don't they get up?

When someone takes a wrong turn, don't they turn back?

## Jeremiah 8:5

“Then why do the people of Jerusalem keep turning away from me?

They hold on to lies. They refuse to come back.

## Jeremiah 8:6

“I have listened closely. Their words are dishonest.

No one stops to ask, ‘What wrong have I done?’

No one is sorry. Each rushes along their own path like a horse charging into battle.

## Jeremiah 8:7

“Even a stork knows the season for its journey.

Doves, swifts, and thrushes know when to fly away.

Yet my people do not know what I ask of them.

## The leaders promise peace

## Jeremiah 8:8

“You say, ‘We are wise. We have the LORD's law.’

But the scribes have used their pens to spread lies.”

Scribes were people who wrote down the law.

## Jeremiah 8:9

“Those who call themselves wise will feel shame.

They will be afraid and find themselves trapped.

They have rejected my words. What wisdom do they have?

## Jeremiah 8:10

“I will give their wives to other men.

Their fields will have new owners.

From the least to the greatest, everyone wants more for themselves.

Prophets and priests alike deceive the people.

## Jeremiah 8:11

“My people are like someone with a deep wound.

Their leaders treat the wound as if it were small.

They say, ‘Peace! Peace!’ Yet there is no peace.

## Jeremiah 8:12

“Do they feel shame for the terrible things they do?

They feel none. They do not even blush.

So they will fall with the others.

When I punish them, they will go down.

## Jeremiah 8:13

“I will take away their harvest.

They will find no grapes on the vines.

They will find no figs on the trees.

Even the leaves will dry up.

They will lose what I gave them.”

![Grapes hanging on a vine. God warns that the people will look for grapes and find none.](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

## The people hear the enemy coming

## Jeremiah 8:14

“Why are we still sitting here? Come together!

Let us run to the towns with strong walls.

We will die there. The LORD our God has judged us.

We sinned against him. He has given us poisoned water to drink.

## Jeremiah 8:15

“We hoped for peace. Nothing good came.

We waited for healing. All we found was terror.

## Jeremiah 8:16

“Listen! We can hear enemy horses snorting in Dan.

The ground shakes as their powerful horses neigh.

The enemy is coming to destroy the land and everything in it.

They will destroy the city and the people who live there.”

## Jeremiah 8:17

The LORD said, “I am sending poisonous snakes among you.

No one can charm these snakes to quiet them.

They will bite you.”

## Jeremiah asks why his people have no healing

## Jeremiah 8:18

My sorrow feels like a wound that will never heal.

My heart is faint.

## Jeremiah 8:19

Listen! My people are crying out from far away.

“Is the LORD still in Zion? Is our King still there?”

The LORD asks, “Why have they made me angry?

They carved idols and worshiped worthless gods from other lands.”

## Jeremiah 8:20

The people cry, “Harvest is over. Summer has ended.

Still no one has saved us.”

## Jeremiah 8:21

My people's suffering breaks my heart.

I grieve for them. What has happened fills me with horror.

## Jeremiah 8:22

Is there no healing balm in Gilead?

Balm is something people put on a wound.

Is there no doctor there?

Then why are my people still hurting?
