# Jeremiah 13 — Jeremiah hides a linen cloth

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah wears a cloth around his waist. Then God tells him to hide it. What happens to the cloth helps Jeremiah understand God's message for Judah.

## Jeremiah hides the cloth in the rocks

## Jeremiah 13:1

Jeremiah said, “The LORD told me to buy a linen cloth to wear around my waist.

He said, ‘Keep it out of the water.’”

## Jeremiah 13:2

I bought the cloth as the LORD told me.

I wrapped it around my waist.

## Jeremiah 13:3

Then the LORD spoke to me again.

## Jeremiah 13:4

“Take the cloth you bought, the one you are wearing.

Go to Perath now.

Hide the cloth in a crack in the rocks.”

## Jeremiah 13:5

I went to Perath.

I hid the cloth as the LORD had told me.

## Jeremiah 13:6

Many days later the LORD spoke again.

“Go back to Perath.

Get the cloth I told you to hide.”

## Jeremiah 13:7

I dug it out of its hiding place at Perath.

The cloth was ruined. I could no longer use it.

## God explains the ruined cloth

## Jeremiah 13:8

Then the LORD spoke to me.

## Jeremiah 13:9

“The people of Judah are proud.

The people of Jerusalem are very proud.

I will ruin their pride just as that cloth was ruined.

## Jeremiah 13:10

“These people refuse to listen to my words.

They insist on having their own way.

They follow other gods and worship them.

They will be like this ruined cloth that serves no purpose.

## Jeremiah 13:11

“You wore that cloth close around your waist.

I brought Israel and Judah close to me like that.

I wanted them to be my people.

Through them others would know my name and praise and honor me.

But they would not listen.

## Wine for every wineskin

## Jeremiah 13:12

“Tell them, ‘The LORD, the God of Israel, says every wineskin will be full of wine.’

A wineskin is a bag that holds wine.

They will answer, ‘Of course! Everyone knows that.’

![A wineskin used to hold wine.](../pictures/job-wineskin.svg)

## Jeremiah 13:13

“Then give them my message.

‘I will make the people of this land stagger like drunk people.

This will happen to the kings who sit on David's throne.

It will happen to the priests, prophets, and everyone in Jerusalem.

## Jeremiah 13:14

“‘I will make them crash against each other, fathers and sons alike.

My pity and compassion will not stop me from bringing them to ruin.’”

## Listen before darkness comes

## Jeremiah 13:15

Listen carefully. Put your pride aside.

The LORD has spoken.

## Jeremiah 13:16

Honor the LORD your God before he brings darkness.

Picture walking in the mountains at dusk.

Your feet stumble. You wait for light.

Yet God turns the light to deep darkness.

## Jeremiah 13:17

Jeremiah said, “If you refuse to listen, I will weep alone because of your pride.

I will shed many tears for the LORD's flock.”

The flock means God's people. Enemies will take them captive.

## A warning to the rulers and Jerusalem

## Jeremiah 13:18

Tell the king and his mother, the queen,

“Come down and sit in a low place.

Your splendid crowns have fallen from your heads.”

## Jeremiah 13:19

The towns in the Negev, the dry south, are shut tight.

No one can open their gates.

Enemies have taken all Judah's people away as prisoners.

## Jeremiah 13:20

Jerusalem, look up! See who is coming from the north.

Where is the flock you were so proud of?

Where are the people God gave you to care for?

## Jeremiah 13:21

You taught other nations to be your close friends.

What will you say when God makes them your rulers?

Pain will seize you like a woman giving birth.

## Jeremiah 13:22

Jerusalem, you ask, “Why has this happened to me?”

Your enemies have torn off your clothes and left you exposed.

This is a picture of your shame because of your many sins.

## Jeremiah 13:23

Can someone from Ethiopia change the color of their skin?

Can a leopard change its spots?

You have made a habit of doing evil.

Now doing good seems as impossible for you as changing those spots.

## Jeremiah 13:24

The LORD said, “I will scatter you like chaff in a desert wind.”

Chaff is the light husk around grain.

The wind blows it away.

## Jeremiah 13:25

“This is the future I have set for you.

You forgot me and put your trust in lies.

## Jeremiah 13:26

“I will lift your skirts over your face.

Everyone will see your shame.

## Jeremiah 13:27

“I saw you on the hills and in the fields.

You were like an unfaithful wife seeking other lovers.

You called out for them like a horse seeking a mate.

You sold yourself without shame. I saw everything you did.

What trouble you face, Jerusalem!

How long will you stay unclean?”
