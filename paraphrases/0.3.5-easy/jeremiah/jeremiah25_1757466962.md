# Jeremiah 25 — Seventy years under Babylon's rule

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah has warned Judah for many years. Now he tells the people how long Babylon will rule. A cup shows that God will also judge the other nations.

## The people have refused to listen

## Jeremiah 25:1

The LORD gave Jeremiah a message about everyone in Judah.

It was the fourth year of King Jehoiakim, son of Josiah.

In Babylon, Nebuchadnezzar was in his first year as king.

## Jeremiah 25:2

Jeremiah the prophet spoke to the people of Judah.

He also spoke to everyone in Jerusalem.

## Jeremiah 25:3

He said, “I first heard from the LORD when Josiah ruled Judah.

Josiah, son of Amon, was in his thirteenth year as king.

For 23 years now the LORD has spoken to me.

Again and again I told you his words.

But you refused to listen.

## Jeremiah 25:4

“The LORD also sent his servants the prophets many times.

You would not even stop to hear them.

## Jeremiah 25:5

“They said, ‘Each of you must stop doing evil.

Then you can stay in the land the LORD gave you and your ancestors forever.’

## Jeremiah 25:6

“God told you, ‘Stop following and worshiping other gods.

Stop making me angry with the things you make.

Then I will do you no harm.’

## Jeremiah 25:7

“But the LORD says, ‘You hurt yourselves by refusing to listen.

The things you made have made me angry.’

## Babylon will rule for 70 years

## Jeremiah 25:8

“The LORD commands heaven's armies.

He says, ‘You have disobeyed my words.

## Jeremiah 25:9

“‘So I will call the peoples of the north.

I will send for my servant Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon.

They will attack you and the nations around you.

I will leave these lands in lasting ruin.

Those who see them will shudder and mock.

## Jeremiah 25:10

“‘No one will hear laughter or the joy of a wedding.

No one will hear stones grinding grain for bread.

No lamp will shine in the houses.

## Jeremiah 25:11

“‘This whole land will lie in ruins.

These nations will serve Babylon's king for 70 years.

## Jeremiah 25:12

“‘When those years end, I will punish Babylon's king and people.

They too are guilty.

Their land, the land of the Chaldeans, will lie in ruins forever.

## Jeremiah 25:13

“‘Everything I said against that land will happen.

Jeremiah wrote these warnings in this book.

He spoke them against all the nations.

## Jeremiah 25:14

“‘Many nations and powerful kings will make the Babylonians their slaves.

I will repay the Babylonians for what they have done.’”

## A cup for the nations

## Jeremiah 25:15

The LORD, the God of Israel, told me,

“Take this cup of wine from my hand.

Make every nation I send you to drink from it.”

The wine in the cup is a picture of God's anger.

Drinking it means facing his judgment.

![A cup like the one in Jeremiah's message about God's judgment.](../pictures/cup.svg)

## Jeremiah 25:16

God said, “They will drink and stumble.

They will lose their senses because I will send war among them.”

## Jeremiah 25:17

So I took the cup from the LORD's hand.

Every nation he sent me to had to drink.

## Jeremiah 25:18

Jerusalem and Judah's towns drank, with their kings and officials.

They became ruins that people still see with horror.

People mock them and use their names in curses.

## Jeremiah 25:19

Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, drank too.

So did his officials, leaders, and all his people.

## Jeremiah 25:20

People from many tribes drank, and all the kings of Uz.

All the Philistine kings drank.

They ruled Ashkelon, Gaza, Ekron, and what remained of Ashdod.

## Jeremiah 25:21

Edom, Moab, and the people of Ammon drank.

## Jeremiah 25:22

All the kings of Tyre and Sidon drank.

So did the kings along the coasts across the sea.

## Jeremiah 25:23

Dedan, Tema, and Buz drank.

So did the people who cut the hair at their temples.

## Jeremiah 25:24

All the kings of Arabia drank.

So did the kings of the tribes living in the desert.

## Jeremiah 25:25

All the kings of Zimri, Elam, and Media drank.

## Jeremiah 25:26

The northern kings drank one after another, near and far.

The cup was for every kingdom on earth.

Last of all, the king of Sheshach would drink.

Sheshach is another name for Babylon.

## No nation can refuse the cup

## Jeremiah 25:27

The LORD told me to speak for him.

“Tell them that Israel's God, who commands heaven's armies, says,

‘Drink until you are drunk and sick.

You will fall and never get up again.

That is what the war I send will do to you.’

## Jeremiah 25:28

“If they refuse the cup, tell them what I say.

‘You must drink it.

## Jeremiah 25:29

“‘I am already bringing trouble on Jerusalem, the city that bears my name.

How could you escape punishment?

I am calling for war against people everywhere.’

## God will judge the earth

## Jeremiah 25:30

“Tell them everything I have said.

Say, ‘The LORD will roar from his holy home above.

His voice will thunder over the land he tends like a pasture.

Workers shout as they press grapes with their feet.

So the LORD will shout to everyone on earth.

## Jeremiah 25:31

“‘People at the ends of the earth will hear the noise.

The LORD has brought a charge against the nations.

He will judge everyone.

Those who do evil will die by the sword.’”

## Jeremiah 25:32

The LORD who commands heaven's armies said, “Look!

Trouble is spreading from one nation to the next.

It is like a great storm rising far away.”

## Jeremiah 25:33

That day, people the LORD has struck down will lie everywhere.

No one will mourn them or gather them for burial.

Their bodies will lie like dung on the ground.

## The leaders cry out

## Jeremiah 25:34

Cry out, you shepherds! Roll in the dust and weep!

These shepherds are the leaders who care for the people.

The time has come for them to die.

They will fall like a fine clay pot that shatters.

## Jeremiah 25:35

The leaders will find nowhere to run.

There will be no escape for them.

## Jeremiah 25:36

Listen to the leaders weeping.

They cry like shepherds watching their pasture disappear.

The LORD is destroying their land.

## Jeremiah 25:37

The fields where they once lived in peace are silent.

The LORD's fierce anger has brought this about.

## Jeremiah 25:38

The LORD is like a lion leaving its den.

The enemy's sword has ruined the land.

This has happened because of the LORD's great anger.
