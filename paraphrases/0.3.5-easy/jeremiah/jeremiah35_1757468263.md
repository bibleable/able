# Jeremiah 35 — A family obeys Jonadab

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah offers wine to a family called the Rechabites. Their answer helps explain what God wants the people of Judah to hear.

## Jeremiah offers the family some wine

## Jeremiah 35:1

Jehoiakim, the son of Josiah, was king of Judah.

During his reign, the LORD spoke to Jeremiah.

## Jeremiah 35:2

The LORD said, “Go and speak to the Rechabite family.

Bring them to a room in my temple.

Offer them some wine.”

## Jeremiah 35:3

So I, Jeremiah, gathered the whole Rechabite family.

I brought Jaazaniah, his brothers, and all his sons.

Jaazaniah's father was named Jeremiah.

His grandfather was Habazziniah.

## Jeremiah 35:4

I brought the family to the LORD's temple.

We went into a room used by Hanan's sons.

Hanan, son of Igdaliah, was a man who served God.

The officials used a room nearby.

Below the officials' room was the doorkeeper's room.

The doorkeeper was Maaseiah, son of Shallum.

## Jeremiah 35:5

I set jugs of wine before the men.

I put out cups and said, “Have some wine.”

## The family explains its way of life

## Jeremiah 35:6

The men answered, “We do not drink wine.

Long ago Jonadab, son of Rechab, gave our family a command.

He said, ‘Never drink wine.

This command is for you and everyone born into your family.

## Jeremiah 35:7

“‘Do not build houses or plant seeds.

Do not plant a vineyard, a field of grapevines.

Live in tents all your lives.

Then you can live a long time in the land where you move from place to place.’

## Jeremiah 35:8

“We have obeyed everything Jonadab told our family.

None of us has ever drunk wine.

That includes our wives, our sons, and our daughters.

## Jeremiah 35:9

“We have never built houses to live in.

We own no vineyards, fields, or crops.

## Jeremiah 35:10

“We have lived in tents.

We have done everything Jonadab told us to do.

![A tent beside a house. Jonadab told his family to live in tents.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

## Jeremiah 35:11

“Then King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon marched into our land.

We said, ‘Let's go to Jerusalem for safety.

We can escape the armies from Babylon and Aram there.’

That is why we have stayed in Jerusalem.”

## God asks Judah to listen

## Jeremiah 35:12

Then the LORD spoke to Jeremiah.

## Jeremiah 35:13

The LORD commands heaven's armies.

He is the God of Israel.

He told Jeremiah to speak to Judah and Jerusalem.

“Ask the people, ‘Will you learn from my correction?

Will you obey what I say?’

## Jeremiah 35:14

“Jonadab told his sons never to drink wine.

His family still obeys him to this day.

I have spoken to you again and again.

Yet you have refused to obey me.

## Jeremiah 35:15

“Again and again I sent my servants, the prophets.

They brought you this message from me.

‘Each of you must stop doing wrong.

Change what you do. Stop serving other gods.

Then you can live in the land I gave your families long ago.’

But you refused to listen to me.

## Jeremiah 35:16

“Jonadab's family did what Jonadab told them.

Yet these people have refused to listen to me.”

## Jeremiah 35:17

The LORD, the God of Israel, commands heaven's armies.

He said, “I warned Judah and everyone in Jerusalem about trouble.

Now I will bring all that trouble on them.

I spoke, but they refused to obey.

I called, but they gave me no answer.”

## God's promise to Jonadab's family

## Jeremiah 35:18

Then Jeremiah spoke to the Rechabite family.

“The LORD commands heaven's armies. He is Israel's God.

He says, ‘You listened to Jonadab, your ancestor.

You did everything he told your family to do.

## Jeremiah 35:19

“‘Because you obeyed, I make this promise.

There will always be a man from Jonadab's family who stands before me to serve me.’”
