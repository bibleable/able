# Jeremiah 32 — Jeremiah buys a field

**About this chapter**

Jerusalem is under attack. Yet God tells Jeremiah to buy a field. God promises that one day people will buy land there again.

## The king keeps Jeremiah under guard

## Jeremiah 32:1

The LORD spoke to Jeremiah in King Zedekiah's tenth year as king of Judah.

It was Nebuchadnezzar's eighteenth year as king of Babylon.

## Jeremiah 32:2

Babylon's army had surrounded Jerusalem.

Jeremiah the prophet was a prisoner in the palace courtyard.

The king's guards stayed there.

## Jeremiah 32:3

King Zedekiah had put Jeremiah there because of his message.

The king asked, “Why do you speak this way?

You say the LORD will give Jerusalem to Babylon's king.

You say that king will capture it.

## Jeremiah 32:4

“You say I will have no way to escape Babylon's soldiers.

They will hand me over to their king.

I will look him in the eye and speak with him.

## Jeremiah 32:5

“You say he will take me to Babylon.

I will stay there until the LORD deals with me.

You say the LORD has warned us about fighting Babylon.

If we fight, we will lose.”

## Jeremiah buys his cousin's field

## Jeremiah 32:6

Jeremiah told what happened next.

“The LORD gave me this message.

## Jeremiah 32:7

“‘Your uncle Shallum's son Hanamel will come to you.

He will ask you to buy his field in Anathoth.

As his relative, you have the right to buy it.’”

## Jeremiah 32:8

My cousin Hanamel came to the guards' courtyard just as the LORD had said.

He asked, “Please buy my field in Anathoth, in Benjamin.

You have the right to inherit it and buy it back.

Buy it for yourself.”

Then I knew the message really came from the LORD.

## Jeremiah 32:9

So I bought my cousin's field in Anathoth.

I weighed out 17 shekels of silver.

A shekel was a unit of weight.

## Jeremiah 32:10

I signed and sealed the deed.

A deed is a written record of who owns the land.

I called people to watch as witnesses.

I weighed the silver on scales.

## Jeremiah 32:11

I took both copies of the deed.

The sealed copy held the terms of the sale.

The other copy was open for people to read.

## Jeremiah 32:12

I gave the deed to Baruch, son of Neriah and grandson of Mahseiah.

My cousin Hanamel watched.

So did the witnesses who signed the deed.

The other Jews sitting in the courtyard saw it too.

## Jeremiah 32:13

While everyone watched, I told Baruch what to do.

## Jeremiah 32:14

“The LORD commands heaven's armies. He is Israel's God.

He says to take both copies of the deed.

Put the sealed copy and the open copy in a clay jar.

That will keep them safe for a long time.

![A clay jar like the one for keeping both copies of the deed safe.](../pictures/clay-jar.svg)

## Jeremiah 32:15

“The LORD who commands heaven's armies has made a promise.

People will buy houses and fields in this land again.

They will buy vineyards, where grapes grow.”

## Jeremiah prays about the land

## Jeremiah 32:16

After I gave Baruch the deed, I prayed to the LORD.

## Jeremiah 32:17

“Lord GOD, you made the heavens and the earth.

You used your great power to make them.

Nothing is too hard for you.

## Jeremiah 32:18

“You show faithful love to thousands.

When parents do wrong, their children too face the consequences.

You are the great and mighty God.

Your name is the LORD who commands heaven's armies.

## Jeremiah 32:19

“Your plans are wise. Your deeds are powerful.

You see how each person lives.

You repay each one according to what they do.

## Jeremiah 32:20

“You did wonders in Egypt that showed your power.

You still do wonders in Israel and among other nations.

Even today, people know your name because of them.

## Jeremiah 32:21

“You led your people Israel out of Egypt.

The wonders you did showed your great strength.

People trembled with fear at what you did.

## Jeremiah 32:22

“You gave Israel the land you promised their ancestors.

People called it a land flowing with milk and honey.

It was a rich land with plenty of food.

## Jeremiah 32:23

“Your people came and took possession of it.

But they refused to listen to you or follow your law.

They failed to do what you commanded.

That is why you have brought this trouble on them.

## Jeremiah 32:24

“Look at the ramps Babylon's soldiers built against Jerusalem's walls.

War, hunger, and disease have put the city in their hands.

You see it, LORD. What you said has come true.

## Jeremiah 32:25

“Yet you told me to buy the field with silver.

You told me to have witnesses watch the sale.

And now Babylon's soldiers have the city in their hands!”

## God answers Jeremiah

## Jeremiah 32:26

Then the LORD answered Jeremiah.

## Jeremiah 32:27

“I am the LORD, the God of all living things.

Is anything too hard for me?

## Jeremiah 32:28

“I will give this city to Babylon's soldiers.

Their king, Nebuchadnezzar, will capture it.

## Jeremiah 32:29

“The soldiers fighting Jerusalem will enter and burn it down.

They will burn the houses too.

On the roofs the people burned incense to Baal.

Incense gives off sweet-smelling smoke.

They poured out drinks as gifts to other gods.

That made me angry.

## Jeremiah 32:30

“From their earliest days, Israel and Judah have done evil.

They keep making me angry by what they do.

## Jeremiah 32:31

“Since the day people built Jerusalem, it has made me angry.

Now I will remove this city from my sight.

## Jeremiah 32:32

“Israel and Judah have angered me by doing evil.

Their kings, officials, priests, and prophets have all done it.

So have the people of Judah and Jerusalem.

## Jeremiah 32:33

“They turned their backs on me.

I taught them again and again.

They refused to listen or accept correction.

## Jeremiah 32:34

“They put things I hate in the temple that bears my name.

They spoiled the holy place where people worship me.

## Jeremiah 32:35

“In the Valley of Ben-hinnom they built places to worship Baal.

There they offered their sons and daughters in the fire to Molech.

I never commanded this. I never intended it.

This terrible act led Judah into sin.

## God promises to bring his people home

## Jeremiah 32:36

“I am the LORD, the God of Israel.

You say war, hunger, and disease will give Jerusalem to Babylon's king.

Now listen to my promise about this city.

## Jeremiah 32:37

“In my great anger I sent my people away.

I will gather them from all those lands.

I will bring them home to live here safely.

## Jeremiah 32:38

“They will be my people.

I will be their God.

## Jeremiah 32:39

“I will give them one purpose and one way to live.

Together they will always honor me with deep respect.

That will be good for them and their children.

## Jeremiah 32:40

“I will make a covenant that lasts forever.

A covenant is a firm promise.

I will always do good to them.

I will help them honor me deep in their hearts.

Then they will stay close to me.

## Jeremiah 32:41

“It will give me joy to do them good.

With all my heart I will settle them in this land.

They will have roots here like a plant in the ground.

## Jeremiah 32:42

“I brought great trouble on these people.

Just as surely, I will give them every good thing I promised.

## Jeremiah 32:43

“You say, ‘This land is empty of people and animals.

Babylon's army holds it.’

Yet people will buy fields here again.

## Jeremiah 32:44

“People will pay silver for fields.

They will sign and seal deeds while witnesses watch.

This will happen in Benjamin and around Jerusalem.

It will happen in the towns of Judah too.

People will buy fields in the hills and foothills.

They will buy fields in the dry south, the Negev.

For I will bring my people back from captivity.

I, the LORD, have said this.”
