# Jeremiah 34 — The people break their promise to free their slaves

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah brings King Zedekiah news about Jerusalem. The people of Jerusalem free the men and women they held as slaves. Then they force them to come back.

## A message for King Zedekiah

## Jeremiah 34:1

Nebuchadnezzar was king of Babylon.

His whole army attacked Jerusalem and the cities around it.

Forces from the kingdoms he ruled and from other nations fought with him.

During the fighting, the LORD spoke to Jeremiah.

## Jeremiah 34:2

The LORD, the God of Israel, sent Jeremiah to Zedekiah, king of Judah.

“Tell him what I say.

I will give Jerusalem to the king of Babylon.

He will burn the city down.

## Jeremiah 34:3

“You will have no way to escape.

His soldiers will capture you and bring you to him.

You will look him in the eye.

You will speak to him face to face.

Then you will go to Babylon.

## Jeremiah 34:4

“Yet listen to me, Zedekiah, king of Judah.

No one will kill you with a sword.

## Jeremiah 34:5

“You will die in peace.

People burned sweet-smelling spices to mourn the kings before you.

They will burn spices for you too.

They will cry out in grief, ‘Oh, our master!’

I, the LORD, have said this.”

## Jeremiah 34:6

Jeremiah the prophet went to King Zedekiah in Jerusalem.

He told the king everything the LORD had said.

## Jeremiah 34:7

Babylon's army was still fighting against Jerusalem.

It was also attacking Lachish and Azekah.

These were the last other cities in Judah whose walls still protected them.

## The people take back their promise

## Jeremiah 34:8

King Zedekiah made a covenant with everyone in Jerusalem.

A covenant is a serious promise.

They promised to announce freedom for people they held as slaves.

After this, the LORD spoke to Jeremiah.

## Jeremiah 34:9

The promise covered Hebrew men and women.

Each person who held them as slaves must free them.

No one could keep a fellow Jew as a slave.

## Jeremiah 34:10

The officials and people who made the promise obeyed it.

They let the men and women go free.

## Jeremiah 34:11

Later they changed their minds.

They took back the very people they had freed.

They forced those men and women to be slaves again.

## God reminds Judah of his covenant

## Jeremiah 34:12

Then the LORD gave Jeremiah this message.

## Jeremiah 34:13

“I am the LORD, the God of Israel.

Your ancestors were slaves in Egypt.

When I brought them out, I made a covenant with them.

## Jeremiah 34:14

“I told them, ‘Suppose a fellow Hebrew sells himself to work for you.

He may serve you for six years.

In the seventh year you must let him go free.’

But your ancestors refused to listen.

![Six years of work. In the seventh year the worker must go free.](../pictures/jeremiah-seventh-year.svg)

## Jeremiah 34:15

“Recently you changed your ways and pleased me.

Each of you announced freedom for your neighbor.

You made that promise before me in my temple.

## Jeremiah 34:16

“Then you changed your minds again.

You had freed these men and women.

They could go wherever they wanted.

But you forced them to be your slaves again.

By doing this, you showed disrespect for my name.

## The LORD tells the people what will happen

## Jeremiah 34:17

“I am the LORD. You have disobeyed me.

You have denied freedom to your relatives and neighbors.

Now I announce what freedom will mean for you.

I will let war, sickness, and hunger take your lives.

Kingdoms everywhere will be horrified at what happens to you.

## Jeremiah 34:18

“When you made your promise, you cut a calf in two.

You walked between the pieces before me.

Now you have broken that promise.

I will treat those who broke it like that calf.

## Jeremiah 34:19

“Officials from Judah and Jerusalem walked between the pieces.

Palace officials, priests, and people throughout the land did too.

## Jeremiah 34:20

“I will give them over to enemies who want to kill them.

Birds and wild animals will feed on their bodies.

## Jeremiah 34:21

“Babylon's army has pulled away from you.

But those soldiers still want to kill your leaders.

I will hand King Zedekiah and his officials over to them.

## Jeremiah 34:22

“I, the LORD, will order that army back to Jerusalem.

The soldiers will attack Jerusalem and capture it.

They will burn it down.

I will leave Judah's cities empty.

No one will live there.”
