# Jeremiah 39 — Jerusalem falls to Babylon

**About this chapter**

Babylon’s army breaks into Jerusalem.

King Zedekiah tries to escape.

Jeremiah stays with his people, and the LORD promises to rescue Ebed-melech.

## The army enters Jerusalem

## Jeremiah 39:1

Zedekiah was in his ninth year as king of Judah.

In the tenth month, King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon came with his whole army.

They surrounded Jerusalem and kept people from leaving.

## Jeremiah 39:2

By Zedekiah’s eleventh year as king, the army broke through the city wall.

It happened on the ninth day of the fourth month.

## Jeremiah 39:3

Babylon’s officials came in and sat at the Middle Gate.

Nergal-sharezer from Samgar was there.

So was Nebo-sarsekim, an official called the Rabsaris.

Another man named Nergal-sharezer held the title of Rabmag.

The king’s other officials were there too.

## Jeremiah 39:4

Zedekiah and his soldiers saw the officials and fled.

At night they went through the king’s garden.

They left through the gate between the two walls.

They took the road toward the Arabah.

## Babylon captures Zedekiah

## Jeremiah 39:5

The Chaldeans, the soldiers from Babylon, chased them.

They caught Zedekiah on the plains near Jericho.

They took him to Nebuchadnezzar at Riblah in the land of Hamath.

There Nebuchadnezzar passed judgment on Zedekiah.

## Jeremiah 39:6

At Riblah, Nebuchadnezzar killed Zedekiah’s sons while Zedekiah watched.

Nebuchadnezzar also killed all the nobles of Judah.

## Jeremiah 39:7

Then Nebuchadnezzar blinded Zedekiah.

He put Zedekiah in bronze chains to take him to Babylon.

## Some people go to Babylon and others stay

## Jeremiah 39:8

The Chaldeans burned the king’s palace and the people’s homes.

They tore down Jerusalem’s walls.

## Jeremiah 39:9

Nebuzaradan was captain of the Babylonian king’s guard.

He took the people still in the city to Babylon.

He also took those who had left Judah’s side to join him.

## Jeremiah 39:10

Nebuzaradan let some poor people stay in Judah.

They had owned no land.

Now he gave them fields and vineyards.

A vineyard is land where people grow grapes.

## The king orders his officers to protect Jeremiah

## Jeremiah 39:11

King Nebuchadnezzar had given Nebuzaradan orders about Jeremiah.

## Jeremiah 39:12

The king said, “Take Jeremiah into your care.

Keep him safe from harm.

Do whatever he asks of you.”

## Jeremiah 39:13

Nebuzaradan carried out the order with the other Babylonian commanders.

Among them were Nebushazban the Rabsaris and Nergal-sharezer the Rabmag.

## Jeremiah 39:14

The commanders sent for Jeremiah from the courtyard where guards held him.

They put him in Gedaliah’s care so Gedaliah could take him home.

Gedaliah was Ahikam’s son and Shaphan’s grandson.

So Jeremiah stayed among his own people.

## An earlier promise to Ebed-melech

## Jeremiah 39:15

Earlier, while the guards still held Jeremiah in the courtyard, the LORD spoke to him.

## Jeremiah 39:16

The LORD said, “Go to Ebed-melech, the man from Cush.

Tell him this message comes from the God of Israel.

I am the LORD who commands heaven’s armies.

I warned that disaster would come to this city.

You will see my words come true with your own eyes.

## Jeremiah 39:17

“But on that day I will save you.

The men you fear will have no power over you.

## Jeremiah 39:18

“I will rescue you.

No one will kill you with a sword.

Because you trusted me, you will escape with your life.”
