# Jeremiah 27 — Jeremiah wears a yoke

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah wears a yoke to show what God is telling the kings. They must accept the rule of the king of Babylon.

## A message for five kings

## Jeremiah 27:1

Zedekiah son of Josiah had just become king of Judah.

The LORD spoke to Jeremiah.

## Jeremiah 27:2

Jeremiah said, “The LORD told me to make a yoke with leather straps.

He told me to wear it on my neck.”

A yoke is a bar that animals wear to pull a load.

![Two oxen with a wooden yoke across their necks.](../pictures/job-yoke.svg)

## Jeremiah 27:3

The LORD said, “Messengers have come to King Zedekiah in Jerusalem.

They come from Edom, Moab, Ammon, Tyre, and Sidon.

Send my words back to their kings.

## Jeremiah 27:4

“Tell the messengers to take this message to their masters.

It comes from the LORD, the God of Israel.

I command heaven's armies.

## Jeremiah 27:5

“By my great power I made the earth.

I made the people and animals that live on it.

I give the earth to whomever I choose.

## Jeremiah 27:6

“Now I have given these lands to my servant Nebuchadnezzar.

He is the king of Babylon.

Even the wild animals are under his rule.

## Jeremiah 27:7

“All nations will serve him, his son, and his grandson.

Then it will be Babylon's turn.

Many nations and powerful kings will make Babylon serve them.

## What the yoke means

## Jeremiah 27:8

“Some nations may refuse to put their necks under Nebuchadnezzar's yoke.

That means they refuse to serve him as king.

I will punish them with war, hunger, and sickness.

Through Nebuchadnezzar I will bring those nations to an end.

## Jeremiah 27:9

“Some of your prophets say, ‘You will never serve Babylon's king.’

Others say the same thing by telling fortunes or explaining dreams.

Some claim to speak to the dead or use magic.

Do not listen to them.

## Jeremiah 27:10

“Their message is a lie.

If you follow it, you will lose your land.

I will drive you out, and you will die.

## Jeremiah 27:11

“But suppose a nation accepts the king's yoke and serves him.

I will let those people stay on their land.

They can farm it and live there.”

## Jeremiah warns King Zedekiah

## Jeremiah 27:12

I gave King Zedekiah of Judah the same message.

“Accept the yoke of Babylon's king.

Serve him and his people, and you will live.

## Jeremiah 27:13

“Why should you and your people die?

The LORD has warned every nation that refuses to serve Babylon.

War, hunger, and sickness will come upon them.

## Jeremiah 27:14

“Some prophets tell you, ‘You must not serve Babylon's king.’

They are telling you a lie. Do not listen to them.

## Jeremiah 27:15

“The LORD says, ‘I never sent those prophets.

Yet they tell lies and say the words are mine.

So I will drive you from your land.

You and those prophets will die.’”

## What will happen to the things in the temple

## Jeremiah 27:16

Then I spoke to the priests and all the people.

“The LORD says your prophets are telling you a lie.

They say, ‘The things Babylon took from the LORD's temple will come back very soon.’

## Jeremiah 27:17

“Do not listen to them.

Serve the king of Babylon and live.

Why let Jerusalem become a pile of ruins?

## Jeremiah 27:18

“Suppose they really are prophets who have heard from the LORD.

Let them ask the LORD who commands heaven's armies for help.

Some things still remain in the temple, the palace, and Jerusalem.

Let them pray that those things stay here.

## Jeremiah 27:19

“The LORD has spoken about what remains in the city.

There are pillars, stands, and the basin called the Sea.

The Sea is a large bowl for water.

There are other things here too.

## Jeremiah 27:20

“King Nebuchadnezzar left these things behind when he took King Jeconiah away.

Jeconiah, son of Jehoiakim, had ruled Judah.

Nebuchadnezzar took him from Jerusalem to Babylon.

He also took all the nobles of Judah and Jerusalem.

## Jeremiah 27:21

“The LORD commands heaven's armies. He is Israel's God.

He says this about the things still in Jerusalem,

including those in the temple and in the king's palace.

## Jeremiah 27:22

“‘People will take those things to Babylon.

They will stay there until I turn my attention to them again.

Then I will bring them back here to their place.’”
