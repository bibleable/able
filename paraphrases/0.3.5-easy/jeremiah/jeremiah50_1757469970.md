# Jeremiah 50 — Babylon falls and God's people come home

**About this chapter**

Babylon took people from Israel and Judah captive. Jeremiah announces that Babylon itself will fall. God promises to defend the people their captors refuse to free.

## Announce the news

## Jeremiah 50:1

The LORD spoke through Jeremiah the prophet about Babylon.

Babylon was in the land of the Chaldeans.

## Jeremiah 50:2

“Tell the nations! Raise a flag so people will notice.

Hold nothing back. Say, ‘Babylon has fallen!

Bel, the god called Marduk, has come to shame.

Babylon's idols lie broken in pieces.’

## Jeremiah 50:3

“A nation from the north will attack Babylon.

Its land will lie empty.

People and animals alike will flee.

## Jeremiah 50:4

“In those days Israel and Judah will come together.

They will weep as they go to seek the LORD their God.

## Jeremiah 50:5

“They will ask the way to Zion and turn toward it.

There they will join themselves to me in a covenant,

a promise that lasts forever. They will never forget it.

## A scattered flock finds its way home

## Jeremiah 50:6

“My people are like lost sheep.

Their leaders are like shepherds who led them the wrong way.

They wandered from hill to hill and forgot their resting place.

![A sheep pen, a place where a flock can rest together.](../pictures/sheep-pen.svg)

## Jeremiah 50:7

“Everyone who found them attacked them.

Their enemies said, ‘We have done nothing wrong.

These people sinned against the LORD, who cared for them like sheep in a good pasture.

Their ancestors put their hope in him.’

## Jeremiah 50:8

“Leave Babylon! Get out of the land of the Chaldeans.

Lead the way like goats at the head of a flock.

## Jeremiah 50:9

“I am bringing powerful nations together from the north.

They will line up for battle and capture Babylon.

Their arrows will find their mark, like skilled soldiers who always succeed.

## Jeremiah 50:10

“The attackers will take Chaldea's wealth.

Each will carry away as much as they want.

## Babylon loses its power

## Jeremiah 50:11

“You who took what belonged to my people sang for joy.

You kicked up your heels like a young cow treading grain.

You called out like a spirited horse.

## Jeremiah 50:12

“But Babylon, the land that gave you birth, will suffer shame.

It will become the least of the nations,

a dry and empty desert.

## Jeremiah 50:13

“My anger will leave Babylon empty of people.

Travelers will shudder at the ruin and hiss in scorn.

## Jeremiah 50:14

“Archers, gather around Babylon.

Let your arrows fly, for Babylon has sinned against the LORD.

## Jeremiah 50:15

“Shout the battle cry from every side!

Babylon raises its hands in surrender.

Its towers have fallen. Its walls lie broken.

The LORD is calling Babylon to account.

Treat it as it treated others.

## Jeremiah 50:16

“No one will sow seed or cut the ripe grain in Babylon.

When the enemy comes with swords, people will flee.

Each will go back to their own land and people.

## Jeremiah 50:17

“Israel is like a flock scattered by lions.

The king of Assyria was the first lion to attack.

Then Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, came like a lion crushing bones.”

## God forgives those he saves

## Jeremiah 50:18

The LORD, the God of Israel, commands heaven's armies.

He says, “I punished the king of Assyria.

I will punish Babylon's king and his land too.

## Jeremiah 50:19

“I will bring Israel home like sheep returning to pasture.

Carmel and Bashan will give them food.

They will have plenty on the hills of Ephraim and Gilead.

## Jeremiah 50:20

“In those days people may search for Israel's guilt.

They will find none. They may look for Judah's sins.

Those too will be gone. I will forgive the people I keep safe.”

## The hammer breaks

## Jeremiah 50:21

The LORD calls the attackers to Merathaim and Pekod.

He commands them to destroy the land and its people.

“Carry out everything I have commanded,” he says.

## Jeremiah 50:22

Listen to the battle! Hear the crash of destruction!

## Jeremiah 50:23

Babylon once struck the whole world like a hammer.

Now the hammer itself lies in pieces.

The nations look on Babylon in horror.

## Jeremiah 50:24

God says, “Babylon, you stepped into my trap without knowing it.

You set yourself against me. Now you are a prisoner.”

## Jeremiah 50:25

The Lord GOD, who commands heaven's armies, opens his store of weapons.

He brings them out in anger.

He has work to do in the land of the Chaldeans.

## Jeremiah 50:26

Attackers come from far away.

God calls them to break open Babylon's grain stores.

The heaps of wreckage will look like heaps of grain.

He commands them to leave no survivors.

## Jeremiah 50:27

Babylon's strong men are like young bulls going to slaughter.

What sorrow for them! Their time of punishment has come.

## Jeremiah 50:28

Listen to those who have escaped from Babylon!

In Zion they tell what the LORD their God has done.

He has punished Babylon for what it did to his temple.

## Jeremiah 50:29

“Call the archers to surround Babylon. Let no one escape.

Pay Babylon back for what it did to others.

In its pride it stood against the LORD, Israel's holy God.

## Jeremiah 50:30

“Its young men will fall in the streets.

Its soldiers will fall silent on that day,” says the LORD.

## Jeremiah 50:31

The Lord GOD who commands heaven's armies says,

“Proud Babylon, I stand against you.

The time has come for me to punish you.

## Jeremiah 50:32

“You will trip and fall. No one will help you up.

I will start a fire in your cities.

It will burn up everything around you.”

## The LORD will defend the captives

## Jeremiah 50:33

The LORD who commands heaven's armies says,

“Israel and Judah both suffer under their captors.

The people who hold them refuse to let them go.”

## Jeremiah 50:34

But the one who comes to free them is strong.

His name is the LORD who commands heaven's armies.

He will speak up for them and win their case.

He will give the earth rest while Babylon is in turmoil.

## Jeremiah 50:35

The LORD announces war against the Chaldeans.

The sword will reach Babylon's people, officials, and wise advisers.

## Jeremiah 50:36

War will expose the foolish words of its false prophets.

Even its strongest fighters will shake with fear.

## Jeremiah 50:37

War will strike its horses, chariots, and foreign troops.

Those troops will lose their courage.

Enemies will break into the treasuries and take the wealth.

## Jeremiah 50:38

Drought will dry up Babylon's waters.

The land is full of carved gods.

Its people have lost their senses over these idols.

## Jeremiah 50:39

Desert animals will live there, including hyenas and ostriches.

Generation after generation, no people will make their homes there.

## Jeremiah 50:40

The LORD says, “Remember how God destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah and their neighbors.

Babylon too will be a place where no one lives.”

## The king hears the army coming

## Jeremiah 50:41

Look! An army comes from the north.

A great nation and many kings are coming from far away.

## Jeremiah 50:42

Their riders carry bows and spears. They show no mercy.

Their voices roar like the sea.

They line up on horseback, ready to attack Babylon.

## Jeremiah 50:43

Babylon's king hears the news. His hands hang limp.

Pain grips him like a woman giving birth.

## Jeremiah 50:44

The LORD says, “A lion springs from the bushes by the Jordan into a green pasture.

Just as suddenly, I will drive Babylon from its land.

I will choose whom to put in charge.

Who is like me? Who can call me to account?

What shepherd can stand against me?”

## Jeremiah 50:45

Listen to what the LORD has planned for Babylon, the land of the Chaldeans.

Its people are like a flock whose smallest lambs are dragged away.

Even their pasture will lie in ruins.

## Jeremiah 50:46

When Babylon falls, the earth will shake.

People in other nations will hear the cry.
