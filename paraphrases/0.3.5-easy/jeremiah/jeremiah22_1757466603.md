# Jeremiah 22 — God tells Judah's kings to do what is right

**About this chapter**

God sends Jeremiah to the palace. He tells the king to protect people who need help. He also says what will happen to three kings of Judah.

## A message at the palace gates

## Jeremiah 22:1

The LORD told Jeremiah, “Go down to the palace of Judah's king.

Give this message there.

## Jeremiah 22:2

“Say, ‘King of Judah, you sit on David's throne.

Listen to what the LORD says.

Your officials and everyone entering these gates must listen too.

## Jeremiah 22:3

“‘Do what is fair and right.

Help people who have been robbed escape those who hurt them.

Treat foreigners, children without fathers, and widows fairly.

Do not hurt them. Do not kill innocent people here.

## Jeremiah 22:4

“‘If you obey, kings will keep ruling from David's throne.

They will ride horses and chariots through these palace gates.

Their officials and their people will come with them.

## Jeremiah 22:5

“‘But if you disobey, this palace will become a pile of stones.

I, the LORD, swear by my own name that this will happen.’”

## Why the palace will fall

## Jeremiah 22:6

The LORD said to the royal house of Judah,

“To me you are like Gilead and the high mountains of Lebanon.

Yet I will leave you as empty as a desert.

You will be like towns where no one lives.

## Jeremiah 22:7

“I will send armed men to tear you down.

They will cut down your finest cedar wood.

They will throw it into the fire.

## Jeremiah 22:8

“People from many nations will pass Jerusalem.

They will ask each other, ‘This was a great city.

Why did the LORD do this to it?’

## Jeremiah 22:9

“Others will answer, ‘The people broke their covenant with the LORD.’

A covenant is a firm promise.

‘They left their God to worship and serve other gods.’”

## Shallum will never see home again

## Jeremiah 22:10

Save your bitter tears for the king who had to leave home.

You grieve for the dead king, but think of the one taken away.

He will never see his own land again.

## Jeremiah 22:11

Shallum was the son of King Josiah.

He became king of Judah after his father died.

Now Shallum had left Jerusalem.

The LORD said, “He will never come back.

## Jeremiah 22:12

“He will die in the land where his captors took him.

He will never see Judah again.”

## Jehoiakim builds a palace without paying the workers

## Jeremiah 22:13

The LORD said, “Trouble is coming to the king who builds his palace by doing wrong.

He makes his own people build the rooms upstairs.

He refuses to pay them for their work.

## Jeremiah 22:14

“He says, ‘I want a great palace with large rooms upstairs.’

He has windows cut in the walls.

He covers the walls with cedar wood and paints them bright red.

## Jeremiah 22:15

“Does having the finest cedar make you a king?

Your father Josiah had enough to eat and drink.

He did what was fair and right.

Things went well for him.

## Jeremiah 22:16

“He stood up for people who were poor and needed help.

Things went well for him then.

That is what it means to know me.

## Jeremiah 22:17

“But you think only of getting rich by cheating others.

You kill innocent people.

You use threats and force to take what others have.”

## Jeremiah 22:18

The LORD spoke about Jehoiakim, son of Josiah, king of Judah.

“No one will weep for him as for a brother or sister.

No one will cry, ‘Our master! His greatness is gone!’

## Jeremiah 22:19

“They will treat his body like a dead donkey.

They will drag him outside Jerusalem's gates and leave him there.”

## The people lose those they depended on

## Jeremiah 22:20

“Go to Lebanon and cry out!

Call out from Bashan and the hills of Abarim!

The nations you loved and trusted have all been crushed.

## Jeremiah 22:21

“I warned you when you felt safe.

You answered, ‘I will not listen.’

Ever since you were young, you have disobeyed me.

## Jeremiah 22:22

“The wind will sweep away your shepherds.”

The shepherds are the people's leaders.

“Enemies will take away the allies you loved.

Then you will feel shame for the harm you have done.

## Jeremiah 22:23

“You live among cedars as if in a nest in Lebanon.

Now pain will come on you.

You will groan like a woman giving birth.”

## A warning to King Coniah

## Jeremiah 22:24

The LORD told Coniah, son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah,

“As surely as I live, I would pull you off my right hand,

even if you were my signet ring.”

A signet ring made a mark that showed the king's authority.

## Jeremiah 22:25

“I will hand you to the very people you fear.

They want to take your life.

They are King Nebuchadnezzar and his soldiers from Babylon.

## Jeremiah 22:26

“I will send you and your mother far away.

Neither of you was born in that land.

Both of you will die there.

## Jeremiah 22:27

“You will long to come home.

Yet you will never return.”

## Jeremiah 22:28

Is Coniah like a broken clay pot nobody wants?

Why must he and his children leave their home?

Why are they being thrown into a land they do not know?

## Jeremiah 22:29

O land, land, land!

Listen to what the LORD says!

## Jeremiah 22:30

The LORD said, “Record Coniah's name as if he had no children.

He will have no success in his lifetime.

None of his descendants will succeed him on David's throne.

None will rule Judah.”
