# Jeremiah 9 — Tears for Judah

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah grieves for the people who have died. God speaks about neighbors who betray each other. He tells the people what it means to know him.

## Tears that keep flowing

## Jeremiah 9:1

If only my head were a spring full of water!

Then tears could flow from my eyes day and night.

I would weep for all my people who have been killed.

## Jeremiah 9:2

If only I had a traveler's shelter in the wilderness!

I would leave my people and stay there.

They are unfaithful in marriage. They betray those who trust them.

## Jeremiah 9:3

The LORD said, “They aim their tongues like bows.

Their lies hurt people, and lies win out over truth.

They go from one evil deed to the next.

They give no thought to me.

## Jeremiah 9:4

“Watch out even for your neighbor.

Even a brother may trick you.

Friends spread lies that hurt your name.

## Jeremiah 9:5

“People betray their friends. No one tells the truth.

They have practiced telling lies.

They wear themselves out doing wrong.

## Jeremiah 9:6

“You live among people who deceive each other.

They cling to their lies and refuse to know me.”

## God sees the harm their words cause

## Jeremiah 9:7

The LORD who commands heaven's armies said,

“I will test my people like a worker testing metal.

The worker heats the metal to remove what spoils it.

What else can I do with my people?

## Jeremiah 9:8

“Their words are like arrows that kill.

A man greets his neighbor with words of peace.

All the while he is planning to trap him.

## Jeremiah 9:9

“Should I let them go unpunished for these things?

Should I let such a nation do this without answering to me?”

## Why the land lies empty

## Jeremiah 9:10

I will weep for the mountains.

I will sing a sad song for the wilderness pastures.

The grass has burned up. No one passes through.

I hear no cattle calling.

The birds and the wild animals have all gone.

## Jeremiah 9:11

The LORD said, “I will turn Jerusalem into a pile of stones.

Jackals, a kind of wild dog, will live there.

I will leave Judah's towns empty of people.”

## Jeremiah 9:12

Who is wise enough to understand what happened?

Has the LORD told anyone who can explain it?

Why is the land so ruined and dry?

Why can no one cross it?

## Jeremiah 9:13

The LORD answered, “I gave them my teaching.

They left it behind.

They refused to listen to me or do what I said.

## Jeremiah 9:14

“They stubbornly did what they wanted.

Their ancestors taught them to worship false gods called Baals.

They followed those gods.”

## Jeremiah 9:15

The LORD, the God of Israel, commands heaven's armies.

He said, “I will give them wormwood, a bitter plant, to eat.

I will give them poisoned water to drink.

## Jeremiah 9:16

“I will scatter them among nations they have never known.

Even their ancestors never knew those nations.

War will follow them until they are gone.”

## Call for the women who sing songs of grief

## Jeremiah 9:17

The LORD who commands heaven's armies said,

“Think about what is happening. Send for the women who lead the mourning.

Call the women who know these songs best.”

## Jeremiah 9:18

Let them hurry here and sing for us.

Let their song bring tears to our eyes.

Let our tears run down our faces.

## Jeremiah 9:19

Listen to the crying in Zion.

“Our city lies in ruins! What shame we feel!

Our homes are torn down.

We have had to leave our land.”

## Jeremiah 9:20

Women, listen closely to the LORD's words.

Teach your daughters how to cry out in grief.

Teach one another these songs of sorrow.

## Jeremiah 9:21

Death has come like someone climbing through our windows.

Even our strongest buildings cannot keep it out.

Children are gone from the streets.

Young men are gone from the town squares.

## Jeremiah 9:22

Tell the people what the LORD says.

“Dead bodies will lie like dung in the open fields.

Think of stalks of grain left behind the worker who cut them.

No one will come to gather them up.”

## What matters to the LORD

## Jeremiah 9:23

The LORD said, “Wise people, do not boast about your wisdom.

Strong people, do not boast about your strength.

Rich people, do not boast about your wealth.

## Jeremiah 9:24

“If you boast, let it be because you understand and know me.

I am the LORD. I show faithful love on earth.

I treat people fairly and do what is right.

These things bring me joy.

## Jeremiah 9:25

“A time is coming when I will punish people who have only the outward sign of circumcision.”

Circumcision is a mark on a man's body.

## Jeremiah 9:26

God named Egypt, Judah, Edom, Ammon, and Moab.

He included the desert people who cut the hair at the sides of their heads.

In God's eyes these nations lack the sign that matters.

Even the people of Israel are uncircumcised in their hearts.

Their hearts are closed to him.
