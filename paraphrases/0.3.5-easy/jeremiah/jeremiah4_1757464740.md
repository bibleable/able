# Jeremiah 4 — A warning horn sounds in Judah

**About this chapter**

God calls Israel to return to him. A horn warns Judah that an enemy is coming. Jeremiah describes the fear he feels and the ruined land he sees.

## Prepare your hearts

## Jeremiah 4:1

The LORD said, “Israel, if you want to return, come back to me.

Take away the idols I hate. Stay faithful to me.

## Jeremiah 4:2

“When you promise something in my name, say,

‘As surely as the LORD lives.’

Speak truthfully. Be fair. Do what is right.

Then I will bless the nations, and they will praise me.”

## Jeremiah 4:3

The LORD told the people of Judah and Jerusalem,

“Break up the hard ground before you plant.

Do not scatter seed among thorns.”

Like farmers preparing the soil, the people must prepare to change.

## Jeremiah 4:4

“Give your hearts to me, people of Judah and Jerusalem.

Circumcision is a mark on the body.

I ask you to cut away what keeps your hearts from me.

Otherwise your evil deeds will stir up my anger.

It will burn like a fire no one can put out.”

## Sound the alarm

## Jeremiah 4:5

Spread this news in Judah. Announce it in Jerusalem.

“Blow the ram's horn so the whole land hears.

Call everyone together. Run to the towns with strong walls!”

![A curved ram's horn. People blew the horn to warn others of danger.](../pictures/sheba-and-joabs-horn.svg)

## Jeremiah 4:6

“Raise a flag pointing the way to Zion.

Find shelter now. Do not wait!

I am bringing terrible destruction from the north.

## Jeremiah 4:7

“An enemy has set out like a lion leaving its hiding place.

He destroys nations. Now he is coming to ruin your land.

Your towns will lie in ruins. No one will live there.”

## Jeremiah 4:8

Put on rough clothes to show your grief.

Weep and cry aloud.

The LORD is still fiercely angry with us.

## Jeremiah 4:9

The LORD said, “On that day the king will lose courage.

So will the officials. The priests will shake with fear.

The prophets will be stunned.”

## Jeremiah 4:10

Jeremiah said, “Oh, Lord GOD! You have misled the people of Jerusalem.

You said, ‘You will have peace.’

Yet a sword is at our throats!”

## The army comes like a hot wind

## Jeremiah 4:11

The people of Jerusalem will hear this message.

“A burning wind blows toward my people from the bare desert hills.

Farmers use a breeze to separate grain from its husks.

## Jeremiah 4:12

“The wind I send is far too strong for that.

Now I announce my judgment against them.”

## Jeremiah 4:13

Look! The enemy rolls in like storm clouds.

His chariots rush like a whirlwind.

His horses race faster than eagles fly.

What will become of us? We are ruined!

## Jeremiah 4:14

Jerusalem, wash the evil from your heart so you can be saved.

How long will you hold on to harmful thoughts?

## Jeremiah 4:15

Listen! A voice from Dan brings news.

The warning of disaster comes from the hills of Ephraim.

## Jeremiah 4:16

Warn the nations. Tell Jerusalem,

“An army is coming from a faraway land.

Hear the soldiers shout against the towns of Judah!”

## Jeremiah 4:17

The LORD said, “They surround Jerusalem like men watching a field.

This is happening because the city turned against me.

## Jeremiah 4:18

“The things you have done brought this on you.

How bitter this punishment is!

You feel its pain deep in your heart.”

## Jeremiah hears the battle horn

## Jeremiah 4:19

“I am in agony! My chest hurts.

My heart pounds. I cannot keep quiet.

I hear the horn calling people to battle.

## Jeremiah 4:20

“Each report brings news of another disaster.

The whole land lies in ruins.

In a moment my tents fall and their curtains come down.

## Jeremiah 4:21

“How long must I watch that warning flag?

How long must I hear that horn?”

## Jeremiah 4:22

The LORD said, “My people do not know me.

They act like foolish children who do not understand.

They know how to do evil very well.

They do not know how to do good.”

## Jeremiah looks at the land

## Jeremiah 4:23

I looked at the earth. It was empty and shapeless.

I looked up. There was no light in the sky.

## Jeremiah 4:24

I looked at the mountains. They shook.

Every hill swayed.

## Jeremiah 4:25

I looked again. I saw no people.

Even the birds had flown away.

## Jeremiah 4:26

I looked at the land that once grew plenty of food.

Now it was a desert. Every city lay in ruins.

This had happened before the LORD in his fierce anger.

## Jeremiah 4:27

The LORD said, “The whole land will lie empty.

Yet I will stop short of destroying it completely.

## Jeremiah 4:28

“The earth will mourn, and the sky will turn dark.

I have spoken. I have made my plan.

I will carry it out.”

## Jerusalem cries for help

## Jeremiah 4:29

People hear riders and archers coming. Everyone runs.

Some hide in thick bushes. Others climb among the rocks.

No one stays behind in the cities.

## Jeremiah 4:30

Jerusalem, your city lies in ruins. What will you do?

You dress in bright red and wear gold jewelry.

You paint your eyes to make them look larger.

It does you no good. The lovers you trusted despise you.

They want to kill you.

## Jeremiah 4:31

I hear a cry like a woman having her first child.

It is Zion, the city of Jerusalem, crying out in pain.

She gasps for breath and stretches out her hands.

“Help! My strength is gone. My killers are here!”
