# Jeremiah 11 — The people break their covenant with God

**About this chapter**

God reminds Judah of the promise made when he brought their ancestors out of Egypt. Later, he warns Jeremiah that people in Anathoth want to kill him.

## Remember the covenant

## Jeremiah 11:1

The LORD gave Jeremiah a message.

## Jeremiah 11:2

“Listen to the words of this covenant.

A covenant is a serious promise that binds people together.

Tell the people of Judah and Jerusalem what it says.

## Jeremiah 11:3

“Tell them, ‘The LORD, the God of Israel, says this.

Anyone who disobeys this covenant comes under a curse.

## Jeremiah 11:4

“‘I gave your ancestors these commands when I led them out of Egypt.

Their suffering there was like the heat of a furnace for iron.

I said, “Listen to me. Do everything I tell you.

You will be my people. I will be your God.”

## Jeremiah 11:5

“‘I promised your ancestors a land rich in milk and honey.

I did this to keep that promise.

You live in that rich land today.’”

I, Jeremiah, answered, “Amen, LORD.” I agreed with him.

## A warning for every town

## Jeremiah 11:6

The LORD told me, “Speak in Judah's towns and Jerusalem's streets.

Tell the people, ‘Listen to the covenant and do what it says.’

## Jeremiah 11:7

“Ever since I brought your ancestors out of Egypt, I have warned them.

Again and again I told them, ‘Obey me.’

## Jeremiah 11:8

“But they would not listen.

Each person stubbornly did the evil they wanted to do.

They broke the covenant I told them to keep.

So I brought on them the troubles it warned them about.”

## Jeremiah 11:9

The LORD said to me, “The people of Judah and Jerusalem have joined together against me.

## Jeremiah 11:10

“They have gone back to the sins of their ancestors.

Like them, they refuse to listen to my words.

They have gone after other gods to worship them.

Both Israel and Judah have broken my covenant with their ancestors.

## Jeremiah 11:11

“So I will bring trouble they cannot escape.

They will cry out to me, but I will not listen.

## Jeremiah 11:12

“People in Judah and Jerusalem will call to their other gods.

They burned incense to those gods as an act of worship.

Those gods cannot save them when trouble comes.

## Jeremiah 11:13

“Judah, you have as many gods as you have towns.

Jerusalem has as many shameful altars as it has streets.

On those altars you burn incense to Baal.

## The olive tree and its branches

## Jeremiah 11:14

“Jeremiah, do not pray for these people.

Do not cry out to me on their behalf.

When trouble comes, I will not listen to their cries.

## Jeremiah 11:15

“My people, I love you. Yet you plan evil and carry it out.

What right do you have to come into my temple?

Can meat set apart for worship save you from disaster?

You celebrate when you do wrong.”

## Jeremiah 11:16

The LORD once called his people a healthy olive tree.

Its fruit was beautiful and good.

Now a loud roar will sound.

The LORD will set the tree on fire.

Its branches will burn up.

![A leafy branch. God compares his people to the branches of an olive tree.](../pictures/branch.svg)

## Jeremiah 11:17

The LORD who commands heaven's armies planted you like that tree.

Now he has announced trouble for you.

Israel and Judah brought this on themselves.

They made him angry by burning incense to Baal.

## Jeremiah learns of a plot against him

## Jeremiah 11:18

I learned about the danger because the LORD told me.

LORD, you showed me what those people were doing.

## Jeremiah 11:19

I had been like a gentle lamb on its way to be killed.

I had no idea what people were planning against me.

They said, “Let's destroy the tree and its fruit.”

They meant, “Let's kill Jeremiah so no one remembers his name.”

## Jeremiah 11:20

LORD, you command heaven's armies. You judge fairly.

You know what people feel and think.

I have placed my case in your hands.

Let me see you punish those who want to kill me.

## Jeremiah 11:21

The LORD spoke about the people of Anathoth who wanted me dead.

They had threatened me, “Stop speaking in the LORD's name.

If you keep speaking, we will kill you.”

## Jeremiah 11:22

The LORD who commands heaven's armies answered, “I will punish them.

Their young men will die in battle.

Their sons and daughters will die of hunger.

## Jeremiah 11:23

“No one will be left.

When the time comes for punishment, I will bring disaster on the people of Anathoth.”
