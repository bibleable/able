# Jeremiah 29 — A letter to the people in Babylon

**About this chapter**

Jeremiah sends a letter from Jerusalem. It tells the people in Babylon how to live far from home. God promises to bring them back.

## Jeremiah sends a letter

## Jeremiah 29:1

King Nebuchadnezzar had forced people to leave Jerusalem for Babylon.

These people were called exiles.

Jeremiah the prophet wrote to their surviving leaders, priests, and prophets.

His letter was for all the other exiles too.

## Jeremiah 29:2

King Jeconiah and his mother had already gone into exile.

So had the palace officials and the leaders of Judah and Jerusalem.

Skilled workers, including people who made things from metal, had gone too.

## Jeremiah 29:3

King Zedekiah of Judah sent two men to Babylon's king, Nebuchadnezzar.

The men were Elasah son of Shaphan and Gemariah son of Hilkiah.

Jeremiah gave them the letter to carry.

## Jeremiah 29:4

The letter gave God's message to everyone in exile in Babylon.

It came from the LORD, the God of Israel.

He commands heaven's armies.

## Make a home and pray for your city

## Jeremiah 29:5

“Build houses and live in them.

Plant gardens and eat what you grow.

## Jeremiah 29:6

“Marry and have sons and daughters.

Help your sons and daughters marry and have children too.

Let your families grow while you live there.

## Jeremiah 29:7

“Work for the good of the city where I sent you.

Pray to me for that city.

When the city does well, you will do well too.”

## Jeremiah 29:8

The LORD, the God of Israel, gave this warning.

“Some among you speak as prophets or claim to tell the future.

Do not let them trick you.

Pay no attention to the dreams you ask them to tell you about.

## Jeremiah 29:9

“They claim to speak for me, but they tell you lies.

I did not send them.”

## God promises to bring his people back

## Jeremiah 29:10

The LORD said, “When Babylon's 70 years are over, I will come to help you.

I will keep my promise and bring you home.

## Jeremiah 29:11

“I know the plans I have for you.

I plan for your good and want to keep you from harm.

I want to give you hope for the future.

## Jeremiah 29:12

“Then you will call to me.

You will come and pray to me.

I will listen to you.

## Jeremiah 29:13

“You will look for me with all your heart.

When you do, you will find me.

## Jeremiah 29:14

“Yes, you will find me. I will free you.

I sent you away to many lands.

I will gather you from all those places.

I will bring you back to the home you had to leave.”

## A warning about those who stayed in Jerusalem

## Jeremiah 29:15

You may say, “The LORD has given us prophets here in Babylon.”

## Jeremiah 29:16

Now hear what the LORD says about the king on David's throne.

His message is also about your relatives still in Jerusalem.

They stayed there when you went into exile.

## Jeremiah 29:17

The LORD who commands heaven's armies said,

“I will send war, hunger, and sickness against them.

They will be like figs so rotten no one can eat them.”

![Two baskets of figs. The spoiled fruit pictures what will happen to the people in Jerusalem.](../pictures/two-baskets-of-figs.svg)

## Jeremiah 29:18

“I will pursue them with war, hunger, and sickness.

Kingdoms everywhere will be horrified at what happens to them.

In the lands where I send them, people will mock them.

People will use their name to wish harm on others.

## Jeremiah 29:19

“I sent my servants the prophets again and again.

Yet the people of Jerusalem refused to listen to me.

You who went into exile refused to listen too.”

## Two men tell lies in God's name

## Jeremiah 29:20

Hear what the LORD says, all you exiles.

He sent you from Jerusalem to Babylon.

## Jeremiah 29:21

The LORD, the God of Israel, spoke about two men.

One was Ahab son of Kolaiah.

The other was Zedekiah son of Maaseiah.

“They tell you lies and say the words are mine.

I will hand them over to King Nebuchadnezzar.

You will see him put them to death.

## Jeremiah 29:22

“The exiles from Judah will remember what happened.

When they wish someone harm, they will say,

‘May the LORD treat you like Zedekiah and Ahab.

Babylon's king burned them in the fire.’

## Jeremiah 29:23

“These two men have done a terrible wrong in Israel.

They slept with other men's wives.

They spoke lies in my name.

I never told them to say those things.

I know what they did. I myself saw it.”

## Shemaiah tries to silence Jeremiah

## Jeremiah 29:24

God gave Jeremiah a message for Shemaiah the Nehelamite.

## Jeremiah 29:25

The LORD, the God of Israel, said to Shemaiah,

“You sent letters on your own authority.

You wrote to the people of Jerusalem and all the priests.

You wrote to Zephaniah the priest, son of Maaseiah.

This is what you told him.”

## Jeremiah 29:26

Shemaiah wrote, “The LORD made you priest in Jehoiada's place.

You are in charge of keeping order in the temple.

If someone you think is mad acts like a prophet, lock him up.

Put his feet in wooden stocks and iron around his neck.

## Jeremiah 29:27

“Jeremiah from Anathoth acts like a prophet among you.

Why have you said nothing to stop him?

## Jeremiah 29:28

“He sent us this message in Babylon.

‘You will be here a long time.

Build houses and settle down.

Plant gardens and eat what you grow.’”

## Jeremiah 29:29

Zephaniah the priest read Shemaiah's letter aloud to Jeremiah.

## Jeremiah 29:30

Then the LORD spoke to Jeremiah.

## Jeremiah 29:31

“Write to all the exiles about Shemaiah the Nehelamite.

Shemaiah spoke as a prophet, but I never sent him.

He made you trust a lie.

## Jeremiah 29:32

“I will punish Shemaiah and his descendants.

No one from his family will remain among my people.

He will never see the good I will do for them.

He taught people to turn against me.”
