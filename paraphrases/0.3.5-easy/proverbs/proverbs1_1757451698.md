# Proverbs 1 — Listen to wisdom

**About this chapter**

Solomon teaches people how to make wise choices. A father warns his son about robbers. Then Wisdom speaks to a whole city.

## What wise sayings teach

## Proverbs 1:1

These are Solomon’s proverbs.

A proverb is a short, wise saying.

Solomon was David’s son and king of Israel.

## Proverbs 1:2

These sayings help you grow wise.

They teach you to accept correction.

They help you understand what wise people say.

## Proverbs 1:3

They teach you how to live wisely.

You learn to do right and treat people fairly.

## Proverbs 1:4

People with little experience can learn to spot danger.

Young people can learn to think before they act.

## Proverbs 1:5

Even wise people can listen and learn more.

People with good judgment can still ask for advice.

## Proverbs 1:6

Then they can understand wise sayings and stories.

They can work out what a riddle means.

## Proverbs 1:7

Knowledge begins with deep respect for the LORD.

Foolish people hate wisdom.

They refuse to learn when someone corrects them.

## Listen to your parents

## Proverbs 1:8

My son, listen to what your father teaches.

Remember what your mother teaches you.

## Proverbs 1:9

Their teaching is like a wreath on your head.

It is like a necklace around your neck.

Both are gifts people wear to show honor.

Your parents’ teaching brings you honor.

## Proverbs 1:10

My son, people who do wrong may invite you along.

Tell them no.

## What the robbers promise

## Proverbs 1:11

They may say, “Come with us! Let’s hide and attack someone who did nothing wrong.

## Proverbs 1:12

“Let’s swallow people whole, like the grave.” They mean they want to take people’s lives.

## Proverbs 1:13

The robbers say, “We will find valuable things.

We will fill our houses with what we steal.

## Proverbs 1:14

“Join us! We will share the money.”

## Who gets caught?

## Proverbs 1:15

My son, stay away from that road.

Take no part in what the robbers do.

## Proverbs 1:16

They rush to do wrong.

They hurry to kill.

## Proverbs 1:17

Imagine spreading a net where a bird can see it.

The bird sees the trap and stays away.

![A net made of crossing ropes.](../pictures/net.svg)

**Look at the net**

Trace one rope with your finger or your eyes. Imagine a bird seeing those ropes before it lands.

## Proverbs 1:18

The robbers hide to attack someone else.

But they set a trap for themselves.

Their own lives are in danger.

## Proverbs 1:19

That is what happens to people greedy for stolen goods.

What they take can cost them their lives.

## Wisdom calls to the city

Wisdom means knowing how to live well. Here the writer pictures Wisdom as a woman. Listen to what she tells the people.

## Proverbs 1:20

Wisdom calls out in the street.

She speaks where people gather in the square.

## Proverbs 1:21

She calls out above the noise of the crowd.

At the city gates, everyone can hear her speak.

## Proverbs 1:22

Wisdom asks, “How long will you refuse to learn?

How long will you enjoy making fun of others?

How long will you hate learning anything?”

## Proverbs 1:23

Wisdom says, “I told you where you went wrong.

If you had turned back, I would have shared my spirit with you.

I would have helped you understand my words.”

## The people turn away

## Proverbs 1:24

“I called, but you refused to listen.

I held out my hand.

No one took it.

## Proverbs 1:25

“You ignored all my advice.

You wanted no one to correct you.

## Proverbs 1:26

“So when trouble comes, I will laugh.

When fear comes, I will make fun of you.

## Proverbs 1:27

“Fear will come like a sudden storm.

Trouble will sweep through like a fierce wind.

Pain and fear will be too much for you.”

## What follows their choices

## Proverbs 1:28

Wisdom says, “Then those people will call to me.

I will give no answer.

They will look hard for me.

They will never find me.

## Proverbs 1:29

“They hated learning.

They chose to show the LORD no respect.

## Proverbs 1:30

“They would take none of my advice.

They hated hearing that they had done wrong.

## Proverbs 1:31

“So they will eat the fruit of their choices.” Wisdom means their own plans will bring them trouble.

They will have to live with the results.

## Proverbs 1:32

Wisdom says, “People who turn away from learning lose their lives.

Foolish people think they are safe.

Ignoring danger destroys them.

## Proverbs 1:33

“But whoever listens to me will live in safety.

They can rest without fear of harm.”
