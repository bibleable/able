# Proverbs 23 — Listen and choose wisely

**About this chapter**

A teacher speaks to his son about food, money, and wine. He asks his son to think beyond what looks good at first.

## At a ruler’s table

## Proverbs 23:1

Suppose a ruler invites you to a meal.

Take a good look at what lies before you.

## Proverbs 23:2

If you have a big appetite, hold yourself back.

The teacher uses a sharp picture to say how important this is.

## Proverbs 23:3

Do not long for the ruler’s finest food.

A fine meal can give you the wrong idea about your host.

## Riches that fly away

## Proverbs 23:4

Do not wear yourself out trying to get rich.

Be wise enough to stop.

## Proverbs 23:5

Look at your riches, and suddenly they are gone.

Picture money growing wings and soaring away like an eagle.

## Proverbs 23:6

Avoid a meal with someone who hates to share.

Do not long for that person’s finest food.

## Proverbs 23:7

Your host says, “Eat and drink.” Inside, he is counting the cost of every bite.

He wishes you would leave the food alone.

## Proverbs 23:8

Even the little you ate will come back up.

Your kind words to the host will go to waste.

## Protect a child’s land

## Proverbs 23:9

Someone determined to act foolishly will scorn wise words.

Save your advice when speaking to such a person.

## Proverbs 23:10

Leave old boundary stones in place.

Do not take land from children whose father has died.

## Proverbs 23:11

Their defender is strong.

God himself will take their side against you.

## Listen and learn

## Proverbs 23:12

Give your full attention to what you are taught.

Listen to people who can help you learn.

## Proverbs 23:13

Take care to correct a child.

The teacher describes a rod used for discipline.

He speaks of correction that leaves the child alive.

## Proverbs 23:14

The teacher pictures that correction saving the child from Sheol.

Sheol means the place of the dead.

## Proverbs 23:15

My son, when you grow wise, I feel glad.

## Proverbs 23:16

When I hear you speak honestly, I feel joy deep inside.

## Choose what to follow

## Proverbs 23:17

Do not envy people who do wrong.

Keep showing deep respect for the LORD all day.

## Proverbs 23:18

There is a future for you.

You have reason to keep hoping.

## Proverbs 23:19

Listen, my son, and grow wise.

Set your heart on going the right way.

## Proverbs 23:20

Stay away from groups that drink too much wine or stuff themselves with meat.

## Proverbs 23:21

Those habits can leave people poor.

They grow drowsy and end up wearing rags.

## What brings parents joy

## Proverbs 23:22

Listen to your father who gave you life.

Keep treating your mother with respect as she grows old.

## Proverbs 23:23

Think of truth as something worth buying.

Never sell it.

Hold on to wisdom, instruction, and understanding.

## Proverbs 23:24

A father takes great joy in a son who does right.

A wise son brings his father delight.

## Proverbs 23:25

May your father and mother be glad.

May the woman who gave you birth rejoice.

## A warning about being unfaithful

## Proverbs 23:26

My son, give me your full attention.

Watch how I live and take delight in my ways.

## Proverbs 23:27

The teacher compares paying for sex to falling into a deep pit.

Sleeping with someone else’s wife is like falling into a narrow well.

Both pictures show how hard it is to get free.

## Proverbs 23:28

The unfaithful woman waits like a robber.

More and more men break their promises with her.

## What happens after too much wine

## Proverbs 23:29

Who feels miserable and sad?

Who keeps fighting and complaining?

Who has wounds for no good reason and red eyes?

## Proverbs 23:30

It is the people who stay late drinking wine.

They keep looking for mixed drinks to taste.

## Proverbs 23:31

Do not let the red wine hold your gaze.

It sparkles in the cup and slips down easily.

![A drinking cup with a handle.](../pictures/cup.svg)

**Picture the cup**

Imagine light catching the wine in the cup. Then read the next verse. The teacher pictures a snake to explain what the drinker cannot see in the cup.

## Proverbs 23:32

In the end that wine bites like a poisonous snake.

What seemed pleasant can hurt you.

## Proverbs 23:33

You will see strange things.

You will think and say things that make no sense.

## Proverbs 23:34

You will feel like someone sleeping on a tossing sea.

Imagine lying high on a ship’s mast as it sways.

The mast is the tall pole that holds the sail.

## Proverbs 23:35

The drinker says, “They hit me, but I felt nothing.

They beat me, but I did not even know it.

When can I wake up and find another drink?”
