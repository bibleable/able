# Proverbs 2 — Search for wisdom like treasure

**About this chapter**

A father tells his son how to find wisdom. Wisdom helps a person see danger and choose what is right.

## Ask and keep looking

## Proverbs 2:1

My son, accept what I teach you.

Keep my commands in your memory.

## Proverbs 2:2

Listen carefully to wise words.

Give your attention to understanding them.

## Proverbs 2:3

Speak up and ask for wisdom.

Ask for help to understand.

## Proverbs 2:4

Search for wisdom as you would search for silver.

Keep looking as if someone had hidden a treasure.

![Nine silver coins together and one coin apart.](../pictures/ten-silver-coins.svg)

**Look for the silver**

Find the coin that sits apart. Imagine searching for it if someone had hidden it. The father wants his son to search that carefully for wisdom.

## Proverbs 2:5

Then you will understand deep respect for the LORD.

You will come to know God.

## The LORD gives wisdom

## Proverbs 2:6

The LORD gives wisdom.

His words help you learn and understand.

## Proverbs 2:7

The LORD keeps good advice ready for honest people.

He protects them like a shield.

A shield takes the blow when someone strikes.

## Proverbs 2:8

The LORD guards the paths where people do what is fair.

He watches over people who stay faithful to him.

## Proverbs 2:9

Then you will understand how to do right.

You will know how to treat people fairly.

You will recognize a good choice.

## Wisdom helps you recognize danger

## Proverbs 2:10

Wisdom will become part of you.

You will enjoy what you learn.

## Proverbs 2:11

Thinking before you act will protect you.

Understanding what is happening will keep you safe.

## Proverbs 2:12

Wisdom will help you turn away from evil.

It will protect you from people who twist the truth.

## Proverbs 2:13

These people leave a straight path to walk in darkness.

This means they give up doing what is right.

## Proverbs 2:14

They enjoy doing wrong.

They take pleasure in turning good things into evil.

## Proverbs 2:15

Their paths twist and turn.

They trick people by hiding what they really mean.

## A warning about an unfaithful wife

## Proverbs 2:16

Wisdom will also protect you from another man’s wife when she tries to talk you into sleeping with her.

## Proverbs 2:17

She leaves the husband she married when young.

She forgets the promise she made before God.

## Proverbs 2:18

The road to her house leads down to death.

Following her means joining those who have died.

## Proverbs 2:19

Those who go to her never come back.

They never reach the path that leads to life.

## Follow people who do good

## Proverbs 2:20

So follow the example of people who do good.

Keep doing what is right, as they do.

## Proverbs 2:21

Honest people will live in the land.

Those who live without blame will stay there.

## Proverbs 2:22

Those who do evil will lose their place there.

Those who break faith are like plants pulled up by the roots.

They too will have to leave the land.

**Pause and answer**

Who gives wisdom to the son who asks for it?
