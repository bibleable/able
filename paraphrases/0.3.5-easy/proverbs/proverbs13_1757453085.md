# Proverbs 13 — Walk with wise people

**About this chapter**

Solomon asks us to think about whose advice we hear. The people we spend time with can shape our choices.

## Listening and speaking

## Proverbs 13:1

A wise son listens when his father corrects him.

Someone who mocks others refuses to hear correction.

## Proverbs 13:2

Good words can bring good things to the speaker.

People who break trust want violence.

## Proverbs 13:3

Choosing your words carefully can protect your life.

Speaking without thinking can ruin you.

## Work and honesty

## Proverbs 13:4

Someone who avoids work wants much but gets nothing.

Someone who works steadily has enough to be satisfied.

## Proverbs 13:5

People who do right hate lies.

Wicked people bring shame and disgrace.

## Proverbs 13:6

Doing right protects an honest person.

Doing wrong brings a sinner down.

## Wealth and light

## Proverbs 13:7

One person acts rich but owns nothing.

Another acts poor but owns a great deal.

## Proverbs 13:8

A rich person may pay money to save his life.

A poor person hears no such demand.

## Proverbs 13:9

The life of a good person is like a bright light.

The lamp of a wicked person goes out.

![A flame shines from a clay oil lamp in the dark.](../pictures/davids-lamp.svg)

**Look at the flame**

Find the light at the end of the lamp. Imagine how dark the room would be if it went out. Solomon uses those two pictures to describe two ways of living.

## Proverbs 13:10

Thinking you always know best leads to quarrels.

Listening to advice helps you grow wise.

## Proverbs 13:11

Money gained by cheating slips away.

Money earned by hard work grows.

## Waiting and learning

## Proverbs 13:12

Waiting a long time for what you hope for hurts.

Getting what you long for is like a tree that gives life.

It gives you fresh strength.

## Proverbs 13:13

Ignoring instruction comes at a cost.

Respecting a command brings a reward.

## Proverbs 13:14

A wise person’s teaching is like a spring of fresh water.

It helps you live.

It guides you away from deadly traps.

## Proverbs 13:15

People value someone who shows good judgment.

People who break trust have a hard road to walk.

## Advice and correction

## Proverbs 13:16

A careful person learns the facts before acting.

A foolish person’s actions show how little they understand.

## Proverbs 13:17

A messenger who does wrong gets into trouble.

A messenger people can trust helps put things right.

## Proverbs 13:18

Ignoring correction brings poverty and shame.

People honor someone who listens and learns.

## Proverbs 13:19

Getting what you long for feels sweet.

Foolish people hate the thought of giving up evil.

## Friends, family, and food

## Proverbs 13:20

Spend time with wise people and you will grow wise.

Keeping company with foolish people can ruin you.

## Proverbs 13:21

Trouble follows people who keep doing wrong.

Those who do right receive good things.

## Proverbs 13:22

A good person leaves something for the grandchildren to inherit.

A sinner’s wealth ends up with those who do right.

## Proverbs 13:23

A poor family’s unplowed field could grow plenty of food.

When people act unfairly, that family can lose it all.

## Proverbs 13:24

Solomon pictures a parent’s correction as a rod.

A father who never corrects his son shows hatred.

A loving father takes care to teach and correct him.

## Proverbs 13:25

Those who do right eat until they are full.

Wicked people go hungry.

**Pause and answer**

Who can help you grow wise when you spend time together?
