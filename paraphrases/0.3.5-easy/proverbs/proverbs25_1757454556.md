# Proverbs 25 — A cup of water for an enemy

**About this chapter**

Solomon asks us to picture familiar things, including honey, rain clouds, and cold water. Each picture helps explain a choice.

## Sayings copied for a king

## Proverbs 25:1

Here are more wise sayings from Solomon.

Men who served Hezekiah, king of Judah, copied them.

## Proverbs 25:2

God shows his greatness by keeping some things hidden.

Kings gain honor by searching for answers.

## Proverbs 25:3

The sky is high above us.

The earth goes deep beneath us.

Who can fully know what a king is thinking?

## Proverbs 25:4

A metalworker removes the waste from melted silver.

Then the worker can shape the silver into a bowl.

## Proverbs 25:5

In the same way, remove wicked people from the king’s court.

Then the king can rule firmly and fairly.

## A place of honor

## Proverbs 25:6

When you stand before a king, do not boast.

Leave the places of honor for those invited there.

## Proverbs 25:7

It is better to hear, “Come up here!” Imagine the shame of being sent back in front of a prince.

Now think about something you saw with your own eyes.

## Proverbs 25:8

Even then, do not hurry to take your neighbor to court.

What will you do if your neighbor shows you were wrong?

## Speaking with care

## Proverbs 25:9

Speak directly to your neighbor about your disagreement.

Keep other people’s secrets out of it.

## Proverbs 25:10

Someone who hears you tell a secret may shame you.

People may remember for years that you broke their trust.

## Proverbs 25:11

The right word at the right time is a beautiful gift.

Picture gold apples set in silver.

## Proverbs 25:12

A wise person may tell you where you went wrong.

If you listen, that correction is like a fine gold earring.

## Promises and patience

## Proverbs 25:13

A faithful messenger refreshes the people who sent the message.

Imagine the coolness of snow on a hot harvest day.

## Proverbs 25:14

Clouds gather and wind blows, but no rain comes.

That is like someone boasting about gifts they never give.

## Proverbs 25:15

With patience you can persuade a ruler.

Gentle words can break through resistance as strong as bone.

## Knowing when to stop

## Proverbs 25:16

If you find honey, eat only what you need.

Too much will make you sick.

## Proverbs 25:17

Give your neighbor time between visits.

If you are always there, your neighbor may come to hate your visits.

## Proverbs 25:18

A lie told against your neighbor in court can wound like a club, a sword, or a sharp arrow.

## Proverbs 25:19

Trusting an unreliable person in trouble is like biting with a broken tooth.

It is like standing on an injured foot.

What you counted on gives way.

## Care for someone who is hurting

## Proverbs 25:20

Singing to someone deep in grief can bring more pain.

It is like taking away a coat on a cold day.

It can sting like vinegar in a wound.

## Proverbs 25:21

If your enemy is hungry, give them food.

If your enemy is thirsty, give them water.

![A drinking cup with a handle.](../pictures/cup.svg)

**Picture offering the cup**

Imagine filling this cup for someone who has hurt you. The saying asks you to notice that person’s thirst and give them a drink.

## Proverbs 25:22

The saying pictures burning coals on the enemy’s head.

Your kindness can make your enemy feel shame for the harm they did.

The LORD will reward you.

## What gives strength and what gives way

## Proverbs 25:23

A north wind brings rain.

Speaking badly about someone behind their back brings angry looks.

## Proverbs 25:24

A quiet corner of a roof is better than a whole house shared with a wife who keeps quarreling.

## Proverbs 25:25

Good news from far away brings fresh strength.

It is like cold water when you are tired.

## Proverbs 25:26

Think of a spring stirred up with mud.

Think of a well whose water is no longer clean.

An honest person who gives in to evil is like that water.

## Proverbs 25:27

Eating too much honey is harmful.

So is seeking praise for yourself.

## Proverbs 25:28

A city with broken walls has no protection.

A person who cannot control their temper is like that city.

**Pause and answer**

What can you give an enemy who is thirsty?
