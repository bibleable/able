# Proverbs 22 — A good name and a fair hearing

**About this chapter**

A generous person shares bread. A judge hears a poor person’s case. Both have a choice about how to treat a neighbor.

## What gives a person value

## Proverbs 22:1

A good name means people think well of you.

It is worth more than great riches.

Their respect is better than silver or gold.

## Proverbs 22:2

Rich people and poor people share something important.

The LORD made them all.

## Proverbs 22:3

A careful person sees danger and takes shelter.

Someone with little experience keeps going and gets hurt.

## Proverbs 22:4

Being humble and deeply respecting the LORD brings a reward.

It brings wealth, honor, and life.

## The path a child learns

## Proverbs 22:5

The path of someone who does wrong has thorns and traps.

People who value their lives stay away from that path.

## Proverbs 22:6

Teach children the right way to live.

Even when they are old, they will keep following it.

## Proverbs 22:7

Rich people have power over poor people.

Someone who borrows money must serve the lender.

## Proverbs 22:8

Treating others unfairly is like planting seeds of trouble.

Trouble will be your harvest.

The stick you used in anger will break.

## Sharing bread and speaking kindly

## Proverbs 22:9

Blessing comes to the generous person who shares bread with someone poor.

## Proverbs 22:10

Send away the person who keeps mocking others.

Then the fighting, the quarrels, and the insults stop.

## Proverbs 22:11

Someone who values a pure heart and speaks kindly will have the king as a friend.

## Proverbs 22:12

The LORD watches over what is true.

He makes the words of dishonest people come to nothing.

## Excuses and traps

## Proverbs 22:13

Someone who avoids work says, “There is a lion outside!

It will kill me in the street!”

## Proverbs 22:14

The words of a woman who breaks her marriage promise are like a deep pit.

Someone under the LORD’s anger falls into that trap.

## Proverbs 22:15

Children can hold tightly to foolish ways.

The saying pictures correction as a rod that drives foolishness away.

## Proverbs 22:16

Some people get rich by taking advantage of poor people.

Others give gifts to people who are already rich.

Both ways lead to poverty.

## Why the teacher wrote these sayings

## Proverbs 22:17

Listen closely to what wise people say.

Think about what I am teaching you.

## Proverbs 22:18

Take pleasure in remembering their words.

Keep them ready so you can say them yourself.

## Proverbs 22:19

Today I am teaching you, yes, you.

I want you to trust the LORD.

## Proverbs 22:20

I have written thirty sayings for you.

They offer advice and help you learn.

## Proverbs 22:21

They teach you words you can trust.

Then you can give a true answer to those who sent you.

## A fair hearing for the poor

## Proverbs 22:22

Never rob someone because they are poor.

At the city gate, leaders hear people’s cases.

Never use that place to crush someone who is suffering.

## Proverbs 22:23

The LORD himself will argue their case.

He will take from those who robbed them.

## Proverbs 22:24

Avoid close friendship with someone who loses their temper.

Do not keep company with someone quick to anger.

## Proverbs 22:25

You may learn to act the same way.

Then your own anger can trap you.

## Debts, land, and work

## Proverbs 22:26

Do not pledge to pay back someone else’s debt.

## Proverbs 22:27

What if you have nothing to pay with?

The lender could take even the bed you sleep on.

## Proverbs 22:28

Leave the old boundary stone where your ancestors put it.

It marks where one family’s land ends and another’s begins.

![A stone marks the line between two fields.](../pictures/field-boundary.svg)

**Find the boundary**

Trace the line through the stone. Imagine moving the stone farther into the next field. Someone would lose land that belongs to their family.

## Proverbs 22:29

Notice someone who does skilled work.

That skill will bring the worker before kings, beyond serving people no one knows.

**Pause and answer**

Who speaks up for poor people when someone robs them?
