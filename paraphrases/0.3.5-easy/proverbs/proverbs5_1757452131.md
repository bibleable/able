# Proverbs 5 — Be faithful to your wife

**About this chapter**

A father speaks to his son about married love. He warns his son about the cost of breaking trust.

## Listen before you act

## Proverbs 5:1

My son, listen to the wisdom I teach you.

Pay attention to what I have learned.

## Proverbs 5:2

Then you can think before you act.

Your words will show what you know.

## Words that sound sweet

## Proverbs 5:3

Another man’s wife may invite you to sleep with her.

Her words taste sweet as honey.

They slip out as smoothly as oil.

She makes the invitation sound good.

## Proverbs 5:4

But the end is bitter as a bitter herb.

It cuts like a sword with two sharp edges.

What sounded sweet brings pain.

## Proverbs 5:5

Her steps lead down to death.

They go straight to the place of the dead.

## Proverbs 5:6

She gives no thought to the path of life.

She wanders without knowing she has lost her way.

## Stay away from her door

## Proverbs 5:7

So listen to me now, my sons.

Keep following what I tell you.

## Proverbs 5:8

Stay far away from that woman.

Keep away from her door.

## Proverbs 5:9

Otherwise others may take your best strength.

A cruel person may take years of your life.

## Proverbs 5:10

Strangers may enjoy everything you own.

Your hard work may make someone else’s family rich.

## Regret at the end of life

## Proverbs 5:11

At the end of your life you will groan.

Your body will have no strength left.

## Proverbs 5:12

Then you will say, “I hated learning from correction!

I hated hearing that I had done wrong.

## Proverbs 5:13

“I refused to listen to my teachers.

I paid no attention to those who guided me.

## Proverbs 5:14

“Now I have almost lost everything.

All my people have gathered, and they can see it.”

## Water from your own well

## Proverbs 5:15

Drink the water you store at home.

Draw fresh water from your own well.

The father uses water to speak about married love.

## Proverbs 5:16

Would you let your spring water run into the streets?

Would you let it flow through the town square?

![A tipped cup spills water across the ground.](../pictures/water-spilled-on-the-ground.svg)

**Follow the water**

Trace the water from the cup onto the ground. Now picture water flowing out into the street. The father asks his son to think about what belongs within his marriage.

## Proverbs 5:17

Keep that water for yourselves.

This means keeping sexual love between you and your wife.

## Find joy in your wife

## Proverbs 5:18

May God bless your fountain.

Find joy in the wife you married when young.

## Proverbs 5:19

She is lovely like a deer that moves lightly.

May her breasts always give you pleasure.

May her love always hold your attention.

## Proverbs 5:20

My son, why let another man’s wife draw you away?

Why hold another woman against your chest?

## The LORD sees each choice

## Proverbs 5:21

The LORD sees where each person goes.

He looks closely at everything each person does.

## Proverbs 5:22

A wicked person gets caught in his own wrong actions.

His sins hold him like ropes he cannot escape.

## Proverbs 5:23

He refuses to accept correction, and so he dies.

His own foolish choices lead him astray.

**Pause and answer**

With whom does the father tell his son to find joy?
