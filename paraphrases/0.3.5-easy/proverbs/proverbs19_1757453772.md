# Proverbs 19 — Kindness to someone in need

**About this chapter**

A poor man looks for his friends. They are nowhere to be found. Solomon asks us to see what it means to help someone like him.

## Honest words and hasty choices

## Proverbs 19:1

A poor person who lives honestly is better off than a fool who twists the truth.

## Proverbs 19:2

Wanting to act is not enough.

Learn what you need to know first.

If you rush ahead, you can miss your goal.

## Proverbs 19:3

A person can ruin his own plans by acting foolishly.

Then he gets angry with the LORD.

## When friends disappear

## Proverbs 19:4

Rich people attract many friends.

A poor person may lose even the friend they have.

## Proverbs 19:5

A witness who lies will face punishment.

Telling lies will not help anyone escape.

## Proverbs 19:6

Many people try to please a prince.

Everyone wants to be friends with someone who gives gifts.

## Proverbs 19:7

A poor man’s own brothers turn against him.

His friends stay even farther away.

He goes looking for them to ask for help.

He cannot find them anywhere.

## Wisdom and patience

## Proverbs 19:8

Learning wisdom is a way to care for yourself.

Hold on to what you understand, and you will do well.

## Proverbs 19:9

A witness who lies will face punishment.

Someone who keeps telling lies will perish.

## Proverbs 19:10

Solomon sees fine living as out of place for a fool.

He finds it even more startling when a slave rules over princes.

## Proverbs 19:11

Understanding helps you be patient.

You show good character when you let an insult pass.

## Proverbs 19:12

An angry king frightens people like a roaring lion.

A king’s kindness refreshes them like dew on grass.

Dew is the water that gathers in drops overnight.

## Choices at home

## Proverbs 19:13

A foolish son can ruin his father.

A wife who keeps quarreling is like water dripping without a pause.

## Proverbs 19:14

A father can leave his children houses and money.

A wife with good judgment is a gift from the LORD.

## Proverbs 19:15

Laziness makes a person sleepy.

Someone who keeps avoiding work goes hungry.

## Proverbs 19:16

Keeping a command protects your life.

Careless choices can lead to death.

## Kindness and correction

## Proverbs 19:17

Helping someone who is poor is like lending to the LORD.

The LORD will repay the person who helps.

![Two small copper coins side by side.](../pictures/two-coins.svg)

**Look at the coins**

Imagine giving these coins to someone who needs food. The saying pictures the LORD himself receiving the loan.

## Proverbs 19:18

Correct your son while there is hope.

Do not let your neglect help bring about his death.

## Proverbs 19:19

Someone who loses his temper must face the cost.

If you keep rescuing him, you will have to do it again.

## Proverbs 19:20

Listen to advice.

Accept correction.

You can grow wise for the rest of your life.

## Plans and lasting love

## Proverbs 19:21

You may have many plans in mind.

What the LORD intends to do will happen.

## Proverbs 19:22

People long for love they can count on.

It is better to be poor than to tell lies.

## Proverbs 19:23

Deep respect for the LORD leads to life.

You can rest content and safe from harm.

## Proverbs 19:24

Picture someone too lazy to finish a meal.

His hand is in the dish.

He will not even lift the food to his mouth.

## Learning from a warning

## Proverbs 19:25

When someone who mocks others gets a beating, onlookers learn caution.

Someone with good judgment learns from a word of correction.

## Proverbs 19:26

A son may attack his father or drive his mother from her home.

By doing this he brings shame and disgrace.

## Proverbs 19:27

My son, keep listening to instruction.

If you stop, you will wander away from what you have learned.

## Proverbs 19:28

A dishonest witness makes a joke of justice.

Wicked people swallow wrongdoing as eagerly as food.

## Proverbs 19:29

Those who mock others face judgment.

Those who act foolishly face blows on the back.

**Pause and answer**

Who promises to repay the person who helps someone poor?
