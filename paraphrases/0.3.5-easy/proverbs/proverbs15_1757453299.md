# Proverbs 15 — A gentle answer

**About this chapter**

Solomon asks us to notice how we answer each other. One reply can make a quarrel louder. Another can help it end.

## Words that calm anger

## Proverbs 15:1

A gentle answer can calm someone who is angry.

Harsh words make anger grow.

## Proverbs 15:2

Wise people speak in ways that help others value knowledge.

Foolish people pour out foolish words.

## Proverbs 15:3

The LORD sees everywhere.

He watches people who do evil and people who do good.

## Proverbs 15:4

Kind words are like a tree that gives life.

Twisted words can crush a person’s spirit.

## Listening and praying

## Proverbs 15:5

A foolish person rejects a father’s correction.

A careful person listens and learns.

## Proverbs 15:6

The home of a good person holds great treasure.

What wicked people earn brings them trouble.

## Proverbs 15:7

Wise people share what they know.

Foolish people have no wisdom in their hearts to share.

## Proverbs 15:8

The LORD hates the offerings that wicked people bring him.

He delights in the prayers of honest people.

## Proverbs 15:9

The LORD hates the way wicked people live.

He loves people who keep trying to do right.

## What the LORD sees

## Proverbs 15:10

Anyone who leaves the right path faces hard correction.

Someone who hates correction will die.

## Proverbs 15:11

The LORD can see even Sheol and Abaddon.

Sheol means the place of the dead.

Abaddon means destruction.

How easily the LORD can see into our hearts!

## Proverbs 15:12

Someone who mocks others dislikes hearing what needs to change.

That person avoids asking wise people for advice.

## Joy and sorrow

## Proverbs 15:13

Joy shows in a person’s face.

Deep sadness can leave someone feeling crushed.

## Proverbs 15:14

Someone with good judgment goes looking for knowledge.

Foolish people feed on foolishness.

They keep taking it in as if it were food.

## Proverbs 15:15

For people who suffer under others, every day is hard.

For someone with a cheerful heart, every day feels like a feast.

## Proverbs 15:16

It is better to own little and deeply respect the LORD than to have great wealth and constant trouble.

## Proverbs 15:17

A plate of vegetables shared with love is a better meal than rich meat shared with hatred.

## Patience in a quarrel

## Proverbs 15:18

Someone quick to anger starts fights.

A patient person helps people settle a quarrel.

## Proverbs 15:19

For someone who avoids work, the path feels blocked by thorns.

For an honest person, the path is like an open road.

## Proverbs 15:20

A wise son makes his father glad.

A foolish son looks down on his mother.

## Proverbs 15:21

Someone with poor judgment enjoys doing foolish things.

Someone with understanding takes a straight path.

That means choosing what is right.

## Advice and timely words

## Proverbs 15:22

Plans fail when nobody gives advice.

Plans succeed when many people help you think them through.

## Proverbs 15:23

It feels good to give a helpful answer.

What a gift to hear it just when you need it!

## Proverbs 15:24

Wise people follow a path that leads upward to life.

It leads away from the place of the dead.

## A widow’s land

## Proverbs 15:25

The LORD tears down the house of the proud.

He protects the land of a widow.

A widow is a woman whose husband has died.

![A stone marks the line between two fields.](../pictures/field-boundary.svg)

**Find the stone**

Follow the line between the fields. Imagine someone moving the stone to take part of the widow’s land. The saying tells us that the LORD guards her boundary.

## Proverbs 15:26

The LORD hates plans to do harm.

He takes pleasure in words from a pure heart.

## Proverbs 15:27

Dishonest gain brings trouble on the whole family.

Someone who refuses bribes will live.

A bribe is a gift meant to buy an unfair decision.

## Proverbs 15:28

Good people think about how to answer.

Wicked people blurt out hurtful things.

## Good news and good advice

## Proverbs 15:29

The LORD is far from wicked people.

He listens when people who do right pray.

## Proverbs 15:30

A bright look can lift someone’s spirits.

Good news gives strength to the whole body.

## Proverbs 15:31

A wise warning can save your life.

Listen to it, and you will be at home among wise people.

## Proverbs 15:32

Ignoring correction shows little care for yourself.

Listening when someone corrects you helps you understand.

## Proverbs 15:33

Deep respect for the LORD teaches you wisdom.

Humility comes before honor.

Being humble means knowing you still have something to learn.

**Pause and answer**

What kind of answer can calm an angry person?
