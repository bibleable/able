# Proverbs 3 — Trust the LORD and help your neighbor

**About this chapter**

A father tells his son to trust the LORD. He shows what wisdom looks like when a neighbor needs help.

## Keep the teaching close

## Proverbs 3:1

My son, remember what I teach you.

Keep my commands close to your heart.

## Proverbs 3:2

These teachings will help you live a long life.

They will bring you peace.

## Proverbs 3:3

Keep loving people.

Be someone they can trust.

Wear love and faithfulness like a necklace.

Write them on your heart.

That means remembering them wherever you go.

## Proverbs 3:4

Then God and other people will look on you with kindness.

They will think well of you.

## Let the LORD guide you

## Proverbs 3:5

Trust the LORD with your whole heart.

You cannot depend on your own understanding alone.

## Proverbs 3:6

Turn to the LORD in everything you do.

He will make your path straight.

That means he will guide you.

## Proverbs 3:7

Never assume you already know best.

Show deep respect for the LORD.

Turn away from doing wrong.

## Proverbs 3:8

This will bring healing to your body.

It will give you fresh strength.

## Honor the LORD

## Proverbs 3:9

Honor the LORD with what you own.

Give him the first part of each crop you gather.

![A green stalk with ripe grain at the top.](../pictures/grain.svg)

**Look at the grain**

Find the ripe grain at the top of the stalk. Imagine gathering the first handful to give to the LORD.

## Proverbs 3:10

Then your barns will be full.

Your great tubs will overflow with new wine.

## Proverbs 3:11

My son, accept the LORD’s teaching when you do wrong.

Listen when he corrects you.

## Proverbs 3:12

The LORD corrects the person he loves.

A father does this for a son who brings him joy.

## What wisdom gives

## Proverbs 3:13

How happy is the person who finds wisdom!

How happy is the person who learns to understand!

## Proverbs 3:14

Wisdom brings you more good than silver does.

What you gain from wisdom is worth more than pure gold.

## Proverbs 3:15

Even rubies are worth less than wisdom.

Rubies are precious red stones.

Nothing you want is worth as much as wisdom.

## Proverbs 3:16

Picture Wisdom as a woman bringing gifts.

In her right hand she holds long life.

In her left hand she holds wealth and honor.

## Proverbs 3:17

It is a joy to follow her ways.

Every path she shows you leads to peace.

## Proverbs 3:18

Wisdom is like a tree whose fruit gives life.

Happy are those who hold on to wisdom.

## The LORD made the earth

## Proverbs 3:19

The LORD used wisdom when he made the earth.

He used understanding when he set the heavens in place.

## Proverbs 3:20

The LORD knew how to open the deep waters.

He made drops of water fall from the clouds.

## Walk and rest in safety

## Proverbs 3:21

My son, keep these lessons in mind.

Think carefully before you choose what to do.

Learn to tell good choices from bad ones.

## Proverbs 3:22

These skills give you life.

They bring you honor, like a beautiful necklace.

## Proverbs 3:23

Then you can walk safely along your way.

Your foot will stay steady.

## Proverbs 3:24

When you lie down, you can rest without fear.

You will sleep peacefully.

## Proverbs 3:25

You need not live in fear of sudden danger.

You need not fear the ruin that comes to wicked people.

## Proverbs 3:26

You can count on the LORD.

He will keep your foot clear of the trap.

## Give help today

## Proverbs 3:27

When someone deserves your help, give what you can.

## Proverbs 3:28

You may already have what your neighbor needs.

Give it today.

Why send your neighbor away until tomorrow?

## Proverbs 3:29

Your neighbor trusts you enough to live beside you.

Never plan to harm your neighbor.

## Proverbs 3:30

Never accuse someone who has done you no harm when you have no reason to accuse them.

## Choose whose example to follow

## Proverbs 3:31

Do not envy someone who uses violence.

Do not do what that person does.

## Proverbs 3:32

The LORD hates it when people twist what is right.

He is a friend to honest people.

## Proverbs 3:33

The LORD brings a curse on the home of wicked people.

A curse here means trouble sent by the LORD.

He blesses the home of those who do right.

## Proverbs 3:34

The LORD laughs at those who make fun of others.

He shows kindness to humble people.

Humble people do not think they are above others.

## Proverbs 3:35

Wise people will receive honor.

Foolish people will face shame for all to see.

**Pause and answer**

When should you help a neighbor if you already have what they need?
