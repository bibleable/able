# Proverbs 20 — An honest measure

**About this chapter**

Solomon takes us to a market where buyers and sellers bargain. He asks us to think about honesty in what we weigh, say, and promise.

## Avoiding a quarrel

## Proverbs 20:1

Wine can lead you to mock others.

Strong drink can lead you into fights.

It is foolish to let drink lead you astray.

## Proverbs 20:2

A king can frighten people like a roaring lion.

Someone who provokes his anger risks losing their life.

## Proverbs 20:3

Settling a quarrel brings honor.

Any fool can start a fight.

## Proverbs 20:4

A farmer who avoids work misses the time to plow.

At harvest the farmer looks for food and finds nothing.

## What people carry inside

## Proverbs 20:5

What someone intends can be like water deep in a well.

A person with understanding can draw those thoughts out.

## Proverbs 20:6

Many people say, “You can count on my love.” How many keep that promise?

## Proverbs 20:7

Honest people keep doing what is right.

Their children are blessed after them.

## Proverbs 20:8

A king sits on his throne to judge.

He looks closely to find and remove what is wrong.

## Proverbs 20:9

Who can say, “My heart is pure.

I am clean from all my sin”?

## Honest measures and honest words

## Proverbs 20:10

Some sellers use one weight to buy and another to sell.

They also cheat by changing the measure.

The LORD hates both ways of cheating.

## Proverbs 20:11

Even young people show who they are by what they do.

Their actions show whether they are honest and good.

## Proverbs 20:12

The LORD made ears for hearing.

He also made eyes for seeing.

## Proverbs 20:13

If you choose sleep over work, you will grow poor.

Get up and work, and you will have food.

## Proverbs 20:14

A buyer says, “This is worth nothing!” Then the buyer leaves, boasting about the bargain.

## Proverbs 20:15

There is plenty of gold and many precious red stones.

Words from someone who knows the truth are a rare treasure.

## Promises and secrets

## Proverbs 20:16

Suppose someone promises to pay a stranger’s debt.

Take that person’s coat as a pledge.

Keep something of value if the promise is for a foreigner.

## Proverbs 20:17

Food bought by cheating may taste sweet at first.

Later it feels like chewing small stones.

Dishonesty turns pleasure into pain.

## Proverbs 20:18

Ask for advice when you make plans.

A leader going to war needs wise guidance.

## Proverbs 20:19

Someone who gossips keeps telling other people’s secrets.

Avoid someone who talks that way.

## What to leave to the LORD

## Proverbs 20:20

Someone curses their father or mother.

Picture that person’s lamp going out in the darkest night.

The saying warns that their life will come to an end.

## Proverbs 20:21

Someone may hurry to get the property a family leaves behind.

That inheritance will bring no blessing in the end.

## Proverbs 20:22

When someone harms you, do not plan revenge.

Wait for the LORD.

He will save you.

## Proverbs 20:23

The LORD hates it when sellers use unequal weights.

It is wrong to cheat with scales.

## Proverbs 20:24

The LORD directs our steps.

How can anyone fully understand the way their life will go?

## Think before making a promise

## Proverbs 20:25

Think before promising something to God.

A hasty promise can trap you.

Only afterward may you realize what you have promised.

## Proverbs 20:26

A wise king separates out those who do evil.

Solomon pictures a heavy wheel rolling over grain to separate it.

That picture shows how firmly the king deals with wrongdoing.

## Proverbs 20:27

Your spirit is like a lamp the LORD uses.

Its light searches the deepest parts of who you are.

![A bright flame burns at the tip of a clay lamp.](../pictures/davids-lamp.svg)

**Find the flame**

Imagine holding the lamp in a dark room. Its light shows what was hard to see. Solomon uses a lamp to describe how the LORD sees into a person.

## Proverbs 20:28

Faithful love and honesty keep a king safe.

They help him continue to rule.

## Proverbs 20:29

Young people can take pride in their strength.

Gray hair brings honor to older people.

## Proverbs 20:30

Solomon compares painful punishment to scrubbing away evil.

He speaks of blows that leave wounds and reach deep inside a person.

**Pause and answer**

What does Solomon tell you to do before making a promise to God?
