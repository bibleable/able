# Proverbs 27 — A friend’s honest words

**About this chapter**

Solomon describes a friend who tells a hard truth and a neighbor who is close enough to help. He also asks a shepherd to look carefully at the flock.

## Boasting and anger

## Proverbs 27:1

Do not boast about what you will do tomorrow.

You do not know what the day will bring.

## Proverbs 27:2

Let someone else praise you.

Even a stranger can speak for you.

Leave praise for others to give.

## Proverbs 27:3

A stone is heavy.

So is a load of sand.

The trouble a fool causes is harder to bear than either.

## Proverbs 27:4

Rage is cruel.

Anger can sweep over you like a flood.

Jealousy is even harder to stand against.

## What a friend can give

## Proverbs 27:5

It is better when someone openly corrects you than when someone loves you but never shows it.

## Proverbs 27:6

A friend may say something true that hurts.

You can trust that friend.

An enemy may kiss you to hide the truth.

## Proverbs 27:7

When you are full, even honey can seem unpleasant.

When you are hungry, even bitter food tastes sweet.

## Proverbs 27:8

Someone who wanders away from home is like a bird far from its nest.

## Proverbs 27:9

Scented oil and incense bring joy.

Incense gives off a sweet smell as it burns.

A friend’s advice can bring that same joy.

## Proverbs 27:10

Stay close to your friend and your father’s friend.

When trouble comes, a nearby neighbor can help you sooner than a brother who lives far away.

## Taking care in what you do

## Proverbs 27:11

My son, act wisely and make me glad.

Then I can answer someone who makes fun of me.

## Proverbs 27:12

A careful person sees danger and takes shelter.

Someone with little experience keeps going and suffers for it.

## Proverbs 27:13

Suppose someone promises to pay a stranger’s debt.

Take that person’s coat as a pledge.

Keep something of value when the promise is for a foreigner.

## Proverbs 27:14

Imagine shouting a blessing to your neighbor early in the morning.

To your neighbor it may sound like a curse.

## What everyday things can teach

## Proverbs 27:15

On a rainy day the roof keeps dripping.

Living with someone who keeps picking fights feels like that.

The saying pictures a wife who keeps arguing.

## Proverbs 27:16

Trying to hold her back is like holding back the wind.

It is like trying to grip oil in your hand.

## Proverbs 27:17

One piece of iron can sharpen another.

Friends can sharpen each other’s thinking in the same way.

## Proverbs 27:18

Someone who tends a fig tree gets to eat its fruit.

Someone who cares for a master well receives honor.

## Proverbs 27:19

Still water shows you your face.

In the same way, what you hold in your heart shows who you are.

## What tests a person

## Proverbs 27:20

Sheol is the place of the dead.

Abaddon means destruction.

These never have enough.

People too can keep wanting more of what they see.

## Proverbs 27:21

A worker tests silver and gold with heat.

Praise tests a person.

How you respond shows what you are like.

## Proverbs 27:22

Picture grain crushed with a heavy tool in a bowl.

Even such pounding would not take foolishness out of someone determined to keep it.

## Look after the flock

## Proverbs 27:23

Know how each of your sheep is doing.

Look carefully after all the animals in your herds.

![A sheep pen with an open gate.](../pictures/sheep-pen.svg)

**Find the open gate**

Imagine the sheep coming through the gate one by one. A shepherd can look at each sheep and see how it is doing.

## Proverbs 27:24

Money does not last forever.

Even a king cannot be sure his family will always wear the crown.

## Proverbs 27:25

Cut and gather the hay.

Fresh grass grows again.

Gather the grass on the hills too.

## Proverbs 27:26

Your lambs will give you wool for clothing.

You can sell goats to buy a field.

## Proverbs 27:27

Your goats will give enough milk for you and your household.

There will be milk for the women who work for you too.

**Pause and answer**

Who can help when your brother lives far away?
