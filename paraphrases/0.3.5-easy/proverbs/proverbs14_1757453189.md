# Proverbs 14 — Wisdom at home and among neighbors

**About this chapter**

Solomon looks at daily life, from a farmer’s field to a king’s court. He asks how our choices affect the people around us.

## Building a home

## Proverbs 14:1

A wise woman builds up her home.

A foolish woman tears hers down with her own hands.

Her choices can hurt her whole family.

## Proverbs 14:2

People who live honestly show deep respect for the LORD.

People who cheat show that they despise him.

## Proverbs 14:3

A fool’s proud words bring punishment, like a blow from a stick.

Wise people choose words that protect them.

## Proverbs 14:4

Without oxen, the feed box stands empty.

But a strong ox pulls the plow.

Its strength helps the farmer grow a big crop.

## Words and judgment

## Proverbs 14:5

An honest witness tells the truth.

A dishonest witness tells one lie after another.

## Proverbs 14:6

Someone who mocks others looks for wisdom in vain.

Someone with good judgment learns easily.

## Proverbs 14:7

Leave the company of someone who talks foolishly.

You will learn nothing from those words.

## Proverbs 14:8

Wise people think about where their choices lead.

Foolish people fool themselves.

## Proverbs 14:9

Foolish people laugh at the idea of putting a wrong right.

Honest people want good for each other.

## What the heart knows

## Proverbs 14:10

You know the pain deep inside you.

A stranger cannot fully share the joy you feel either.

## Proverbs 14:11

The home of wicked people will come to ruin.

Even in a tent, an honest family will thrive.

![A tent beside a house.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

**Find the tent**

Look at the tent beside the house. Solomon says the family in the tent can thrive. Think about how that family lives.

## Proverbs 14:12

A road can seem right to someone who takes it.

Yet that road can end in death.

## Proverbs 14:13

Someone who laughs may still feel pain inside.

A time of joy can end in sorrow.

## Proverbs 14:14

Someone who turns away from doing right must live with the results.

Good people receive the good their choices bring.

## Think before taking a step

## Proverbs 14:15

An inexperienced person may believe every word.

A careful person thinks before taking the next step.

## Proverbs 14:16

Wise people see danger and turn away from evil.

Foolish people rush ahead without care.

## Proverbs 14:17

Someone who gets angry quickly acts foolishly.

People hate someone who secretly plans harm.

## Proverbs 14:18

People who believe anything end up with foolish ideas.

Careful people gain knowledge and wear it like a crown.

## Proverbs 14:19

Evil people will bow down before good people.

They will bow at the gates of those who do right.

## How neighbors treat each other

## Proverbs 14:20

Even neighbors turn against a poor person.

A rich person has many eager friends.

## Proverbs 14:21

Looking down on a neighbor is a sin.

Happy are those who show kindness to poor people.

## Proverbs 14:22

People who plan harm lose their way.

People who plan good find love they can count on.

## Proverbs 14:23

Every kind of work brings some gain.

Only talking about work leaves a person poor.

## Proverbs 14:24

Wealth brings honor to wise people, like a crown.

Foolish people work and produce more foolishness.

## Truth and a place of safety

## Proverbs 14:25

A witness who tells the truth can save lives.

A witness who lies misleads people.

## Proverbs 14:26

Someone who deeply respects the LORD can feel safe.

That person’s children also have a place of safety.

## Proverbs 14:27

Deep respect for the LORD is like a spring of fresh water.

It gives life and helps you avoid deadly traps.

## Proverbs 14:28

A king with many people has great honor.

A ruler without people to lead faces ruin.

## Patience and kindness

## Proverbs 14:29

A patient person shows deep understanding.

Someone who loses their temper quickly shows how foolish anger can be.

## Proverbs 14:30

A peaceful heart gives life to the body.

Wanting what others have can eat away at you.

Solomon pictures envy as rot deep in the bones.

## Proverbs 14:31

People who mistreat the poor insult God, who made them.

People who help someone in need honor God.

## Proverbs 14:32

Wicked people fall because of their own wrongdoing.

Good people have a place of safety even in death.

## What brings a nation honor

## Proverbs 14:33

Wisdom is at home in people with good judgment.

Even among foolish people, wisdom makes herself known.

## Proverbs 14:34

A nation gains honor when its people do right.

Sin brings shame to any people.

## Proverbs 14:35

A king is pleased with a wise servant.

A servant who acts shamefully makes the king angry.

**Pause and answer**

When someone shows kindness to a person in need, whom do they honor?
