# Proverbs 11 — An honest weight and an open hand

**About this chapter**

Solomon asks us to picture a seller weighing goods and another selling grain. Their choices matter to everyone who comes to buy food.

## Honesty guides your steps

## Proverbs 11:1

A seller uses scales to weigh what you buy.

The LORD hates it when sellers use false weights to cheat you.

He takes pleasure in an honest weight.

## Proverbs 11:2

Pride brings shame.

A humble person grows wise.

## Proverbs 11:3

Honest people let honesty guide their choices.

People who break trust ruin themselves by cheating.

## Proverbs 11:4

When the day of anger comes, wealth cannot save you.

Doing right can save you from death.

## Proverbs 11:5

Doing right shows an honest person where to go.

Wicked people fall because of the harm they do.

## Proverbs 11:6

Honest people escape danger by doing right.

Dishonest people get caught by their own desires.

## What happens to a city

## Proverbs 11:7

When a wicked person dies, his hopes die too.

The strength he counted on is gone.

## Proverbs 11:8

A good person escapes trouble.

A wicked person falls into that trouble instead.

## Proverbs 11:9

Someone who has no regard for God can ruin a neighbor with words.

Good people escape harm through what they know.

## Proverbs 11:10

When good people do well, the whole city celebrates.

When wicked people die, the people shout for joy.

## Proverbs 11:11

When honest people bless a city, it grows strong.

Wicked people can tear it apart with their words.

## Whom can you trust?

## Proverbs 11:12

Someone who insults a neighbor shows poor judgment.

A person with understanding knows when to keep quiet.

## Proverbs 11:13

Someone who gossips tells other people your secrets.

Someone you can trust keeps your secret.

## Proverbs 11:14

Without good advice a nation falls.

Many people giving wise advice can help save it.

## Proverbs 11:15

Promise to pay a stranger’s debt, and you will suffer for it.

You are safer if you refuse to take on that debt.

## What kindness brings

## Proverbs 11:16

People honor a woman who treats others with kindness.

Men who show no mercy gain only money.

## Proverbs 11:17

When you show kindness, you also help yourself.

When you act cruelly, you bring trouble on yourself.

## Proverbs 11:18

The reward for doing evil turns out to be empty.

Doing right is like planting seeds.

The harvest is a reward you can count on.

## Proverbs 11:19

Someone who truly does right finds life.

Someone who keeps chasing evil finds death.

## What pleases the LORD

## Proverbs 11:20

The LORD hates it when people plan crooked things.

He takes pleasure in people who live without blame.

## Proverbs 11:21

You can be sure the wicked will face punishment.

The children of those who do right will escape.

## Proverbs 11:22

Picture a gold ring in a pig’s nose.

Solomon compares it to a beautiful woman who shows poor judgment.

Beauty alone cannot help her choose well.

## Proverbs 11:23

Good people’s desires lead to good things.

What wicked people hope for brings anger upon them.

## Giving and keeping

## Proverbs 11:24

One person gives freely and gains even more.

Another keeps what others should have and becomes poor.

## Proverbs 11:25

A generous person will do well.

When you give others fresh strength, you too will find fresh strength.

## Proverbs 11:26

People curse the person who keeps all the grain back.

They bless the person who sells it to them.

![A head of ripe grain on a stalk.](../pictures/grain.svg)

**Look at the grain**

Find the seeds at the top. People can grind these seeds to make bread. Imagine coming to buy grain for your family and finding someone willing to sell it.

## Proverbs 11:27

Work to find what is good, and people will welcome you.

Go looking for evil, and it will find you.

## What lasts

## Proverbs 11:28

Someone who trusts in riches will fall.

Those who do right grow strong like green leaves.

## Proverbs 11:29

Bring trouble on your family, and you will inherit the wind.

That means you will have nothing to keep.

A foolish person will end up serving a wise one.

## Proverbs 11:30

Good people are like a tree whose fruit gives life.

What they do helps others live.

A wise person wins people over.

## Proverbs 11:31

Even people who do right receive what their actions deserve on earth.

How much more will those who ignore God and do wrong!

**Pause and answer**

What does someone you can trust do with your secret?
