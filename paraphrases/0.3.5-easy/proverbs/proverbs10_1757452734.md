# Proverbs 10 — Wise choices in work and speech

**About this chapter**

Solomon gives short sayings about everyday choices. He asks us to notice what happens when people work, speak, or listen.

## Family and work

## Proverbs 10:1

These are short sayings from Solomon.

A wise son makes his father glad.

A foolish son brings his mother sorrow.

## Proverbs 10:2

Treasure gained by doing wrong cannot help you.

Doing right can save you from death.

## Proverbs 10:3

The LORD feeds those who do right.

He refuses to give wicked people what they crave.

## Proverbs 10:4

Avoiding work leads to poverty.

Steady work brings wealth.

## Proverbs 10:5

A wise son gathers crops in summer.

A son who sleeps through harvest brings shame.

![Ripe grain at the top of a green stalk.](../pictures/grain.svg)

**Find the ripe grain**

Look at the top of the stalk. Harvest is the time to gather ripe crops. The wise son gathers the grain while it is ready.

## What people remember

## Proverbs 10:6

Blessings rest on those who do right.

Wicked people use words to hide their plans for violence.

## Proverbs 10:7

Remembering a good person brings a blessing.

A wicked person’s name rots away.

People lose all respect for that name.

## Proverbs 10:8

Wise people listen when someone gives them instructions.

Foolish people keep talking until they ruin themselves.

## Proverbs 10:9

Honest people can go about their lives safely.

People who cheat will have their secrets uncovered.

## Words can hurt or give life

## Proverbs 10:10

Someone who winks to trick others causes pain.

Foolish people keep talking until they ruin themselves.

## Proverbs 10:11

A good person’s words are like a spring of water.

Those words give life.

Wicked people use words to hide their plans for violence.

## Proverbs 10:12

Hatred starts quarrels.

Love forgives all kinds of wrongs.

## Proverbs 10:13

People with good judgment speak wisely.

People with poor judgment face punishment.

Solomon pictures a rod striking someone’s back.

## Proverbs 10:14

Wise people keep what they learn for later.

A foolish person can bring disaster just by speaking.

## Money and the path to life

## Proverbs 10:15

Money protects rich people like a city wall.

Poor people can lose everything because they have so little.

## Proverbs 10:16

The work of good people helps them live.

What wicked people gain brings them punishment.

## Proverbs 10:17

Listen when someone corrects you.

That keeps you on the path to life.

Ignore correction and you lose your way.

## When to speak

## Proverbs 10:18

Someone who hides hatred behind friendly words is lying.

Someone who spreads lies about others acts foolishly.

## Proverbs 10:19

Keep talking and sooner or later you will say something wrong.

A wise person knows when to stop.

## Proverbs 10:20

An honest person’s words are like the finest silver.

What a wicked person holds in their heart has little value.

## Proverbs 10:21

Good people speak words that feed many others.

Their words help others live.

Foolish people die because they lack good judgment.

## Blessing and hope

## Proverbs 10:22

The LORD’s blessing brings wealth.

He adds no sorrow to that gift.

## Proverbs 10:23

A foolish person takes pleasure in doing shameful things.

A person with understanding finds joy in wisdom.

## Proverbs 10:24

What wicked people fear will happen to them.

Those who do right will receive what they long for.

## Proverbs 10:25

A fierce wind sweeps past.

The wicked are gone.

Those who do right stand firm forever.

## Proverbs 10:26

Vinegar stings your teeth.

Smoke stings your eyes.

That is how it feels to send someone who avoids work.

## Where the LORD’s way leads

## Proverbs 10:27

Deep respect for the LORD adds years to life.

Wicked people’s lives will be short.

## Proverbs 10:28

Those who do right find joy in what they hope for.

The hopes of wicked people come to nothing.

## Proverbs 10:29

Those who live honestly find shelter in the LORD’s way.

Those who do evil face ruin.

## Proverbs 10:30

Those who do right will always stand firm.

Wicked people will lose their place in the land.

## Proverbs 10:31

Good people speak words of wisdom.

Solomon pictures a tongue cut out.

Someone who twists the truth will lose the power to speak.

## Proverbs 10:32

Good people know what is right to say.

Wicked people twist their words.

**Pause and answer**

What does a wise person do when someone gives instructions?
