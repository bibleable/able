# Proverbs 7 — A warning from the window

**About this chapter**

A father tells his sons what he saw from his window. A young man followed a married woman home. He did not see where his choice would lead.

## Keep wisdom close

## Proverbs 7:1

My son, remember my words.

Keep my commands like something precious.

## Proverbs 7:2

Follow my commands and live.

Guard my teaching as carefully as you protect your eyes.

## Proverbs 7:3

Tie my words to your fingers.

Write them on your heart.

That means keeping them where you will always remember them.

## Proverbs 7:4

Call Wisdom your sister.

Treat understanding like a close relative.

Welcome wise advice as you would welcome family.

## Proverbs 7:5

Wisdom can protect you when another man’s wife tries to draw you in with sweet words.

## What the father saw

## Proverbs 7:6

I stood at a window in my house.

I looked through the strips of wood across the window.

## Proverbs 7:7

I saw a group of young people with little experience.

One young man caught my eye.

He was making a foolish choice.

## Proverbs 7:8

He crossed the street near the woman’s corner.

He walked down the road toward her house.

## Proverbs 7:9

The sun had gone down.

The last light faded and the street grew dark.

## The woman at the corner

## Proverbs 7:10

A woman came out to meet him.

She wore clothes like a woman who sells sex.

She had a plan to trick him.

## Proverbs 7:11

She spoke loudly and refused to listen to anyone.

She seldom stayed at home.

## Proverbs 7:12

Sometimes she waited in the street.

Sometimes she waited in the open square.

She watched for someone at every corner.

## Proverbs 7:13

She grabbed the young man and kissed him.

Then she spoke to him without shame.

## What she promised

## Proverbs 7:14

She said, “Today I gave God the offerings I promised.” These were called peace offerings.

The person who brought one could share in the meal.

## Proverbs 7:15

The woman said, “So I came looking for you.

Now I have found you!

## Proverbs 7:16

“I have covered my bed with colored cloth.

It is fine linen from Egypt.

## Proverbs 7:17

“My bed smells sweet.

I used myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon.” These give off sweet smells.

## Proverbs 7:18

She said, “Come to bed with me.

Let us enjoy holding each other until morning.

## Her husband is away

## Proverbs 7:19

“My husband is away from home.

He has gone on a long trip.

## Proverbs 7:20

“He took a bag of money with him.

He will come home when the moon is full.”

## A trap he cannot see

## Proverbs 7:21

She kept talking until she won him over.

She said things he wanted to hear.

## Proverbs 7:22

Suddenly he followed her.

He was like an ox on its way to be killed.

He was like a deer leaping into a trap.

## Proverbs 7:23

An arrow strikes the deer deep inside its body.

A bird flies straight into a trap.

Like them, the young man cannot see the danger.

He has no idea it will cost him his life.

![Ropes cross to form a net.](../pictures/net.svg)

**Look at the net**

Follow one rope to where it crosses another. Imagine a bird flying into those ropes. The father uses a trap to explain the danger his son might miss.

## The father speaks to his sons

## Proverbs 7:24

Now listen to me, my sons.

Pay attention to what I say.

## Proverbs 7:25

Do not set your heart on following her.

Stay away from the paths she takes.

## Proverbs 7:26

She has already brought many people down.

Many have lost their lives.

## Proverbs 7:27

Her house is like a road leading down to Sheol.

Sheol means the place of the dead.

Following her leads to death.

**Pause and answer**

What danger did the young man fail to see?
