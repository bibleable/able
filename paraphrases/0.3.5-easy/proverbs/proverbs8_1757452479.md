# Proverbs 8 — Wisdom calls to everyone

**About this chapter**

The writer pictures Wisdom as a woman who speaks. Wisdom tells people how to live well. She remembers the joy of being with the LORD when he made the world.

## A voice at the city gates

## Proverbs 8:1

Can you hear Wisdom calling?

She raises her voice so people can learn to understand.

## Proverbs 8:2

Wisdom stands on high ground above the road.

She stands where the roads meet.

## Proverbs 8:3

She goes to the gates of the city.

She calls to people as they come in.

## Proverbs 8:4

Wisdom says, “People everywhere, I am calling to you!”

## What Wisdom says

## Proverbs 8:5

If you have little experience, learn to think ahead.

If you make foolish choices, learn to understand.

## Proverbs 8:6

Listen.

I have good and important things to say.

I will tell you what is right.

## Proverbs 8:7

I speak the truth.

I hate using words to do harm.

## Proverbs 8:8

Everything I say is right.

I never twist my words to mislead you.

## Proverbs 8:9

People who understand can see my meaning clearly.

People who have learned the truth recognize my words as right.

## A choice worth more than silver

## Proverbs 8:10

If you must choose between silver and my teaching, choose my teaching.

Choose knowledge even over pure gold.

![Nine silver coins together and one coin apart.](../pictures/ten-silver-coins.svg)

**Look at the silver**

Find the single coin. Then look at the whole group. Wisdom says her teaching is worth more than the silver you see.

## Proverbs 8:11

Wisdom is worth more than rubies.

Rubies are precious red stones.

Nothing you long for is worth as much as wisdom.

## Proverbs 8:12

I am Wisdom.

Careful thought lives with me like a friend.

I know how to think through a choice.

## Proverbs 8:13

To have deep respect for the LORD is to hate evil.

I hate pride that looks down on others.

I hate cruel deeds and words that twist the truth.

## Help for people who lead

## Proverbs 8:14

I give good advice.

I know how to judge well.

I have understanding and strength.

## Proverbs 8:15

Kings rule with my help.

I help rulers make fair laws.

## Proverbs 8:16

Princes lead with my help.

So do all the leaders who govern fairly.

## Proverbs 8:17

I love those who love me.

Those who seek me early will find me.

## What Wisdom gives

## Proverbs 8:18

I bring riches and honor.

I bring wealth that lasts and a way to live rightly.

## Proverbs 8:19

Picture my gifts as fruit you can gather.

My fruit is worth more than pure gold.

My harvest is worth more than the finest silver.

## Proverbs 8:20

The path I walk is right.

At every step I do what is fair.

## Proverbs 8:21

I give wealth to those who love me.

I fill their storehouses with treasure.

## Before the earth began

## Proverbs 8:22

The LORD created me first.

He made me before his other works of long ago.

## Proverbs 8:23

The LORD gave me my place at the very beginning.

I was there before the earth began.

## Proverbs 8:24

I was born before there were deep waters.

No spring had begun to pour out water.

## Proverbs 8:25

I was born before the mountains stood in place.

There were no hills yet.

## Proverbs 8:26

The LORD had not yet made land or fields.

He had not made even a speck of dust.

## Beside the LORD as he worked

## Proverbs 8:27

I was there when the LORD put the sky in place.

He drew a circle across the deep waters.

Picture the line where sea and sky seem to meet.

## Proverbs 8:28

He set the clouds above.

Water rushed up from springs deep below.

## Proverbs 8:29

He told the sea where to stop.

The water could go no farther.

He marked out the base on which the earth stands.

## Proverbs 8:30

I worked beside him like a skilled builder.

Day after day I brought him joy.

I was always glad to be with him.

## Proverbs 8:31

I took joy in the whole world he made.

The people in it gave me joy.

## Waiting at Wisdom’s door

## Proverbs 8:32

So now, my sons, listen to me.

Happy are those who follow my ways.

## Proverbs 8:33

Pay attention when I teach you.

Take my words to heart and grow wise.

## Proverbs 8:34

Happy is the person who listens to me.

Picture someone watching at my door every day.

That person waits at the doorway to hear me.

## Proverbs 8:35

Whoever finds me finds life.

The LORD looks on that person with kindness.

## Proverbs 8:36

Whoever misses me does harm to himself.

Those who hate me love death.

**Pause and answer**

What does Wisdom tell you to choose when someone offers you silver or her teaching?
