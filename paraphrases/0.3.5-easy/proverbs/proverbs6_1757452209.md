# Proverbs 6 — Learn from the ant and the lamp

**About this chapter**

A father warns his son about promises, quarrels, and sex outside marriage. He points to an ant and a lamp to teach wisdom.

## A promise to pay

## Proverbs 6:1

My son, suppose your neighbor owes money.

You shake hands with a stranger.

You promise to pay if your neighbor cannot.

## Proverbs 6:2

Now your own words have caught you in a trap.

You must do what you promised.

## Proverbs 6:3

My son, your neighbor now has power over you.

Go to your neighbor and put your pride aside.

Keep asking for release from your promise.

## Proverbs 6:4

Go before you lie down to sleep.

Do not wait until morning.

## Proverbs 6:5

Get free like a gazelle escaping a hunter.

A gazelle is a swift animal like a deer.

Get free like a bird escaping a trap.

## Watch the ant

## Proverbs 6:6

If you keep putting off work, watch an ant.

Learn wisdom from what it does.

## Proverbs 6:7

The ant has no one giving it orders.

No one stands over it to watch it work.

## Proverbs 6:8

Yet the ant stores food during summer.

It gathers food when the crops are ripe.

## Proverbs 6:9

How long will you lie there avoiding work?

When will you get up?

## Proverbs 6:10

You say, “Just a little more sleep.” You fold your hands for a little more rest.

## Proverbs 6:11

Then poverty can come suddenly, like a robber.

You can find yourself without the things you need.

## A person who starts quarrels

## Proverbs 6:12

A dishonest person goes around twisting the truth.

## Proverbs 6:13

He winks to send secret signals.

He makes signs with his feet and fingers.

## Proverbs 6:14

He quietly plans how to hurt others.

Again and again he starts quarrels between people.

## Proverbs 6:15

So trouble will strike him suddenly.

In a moment he will fall beyond recovery.

## Seven things the LORD hates

## Proverbs 6:16

The LORD hates six things.

In fact, there are seven things he cannot stand.

## Proverbs 6:17

Looking down on others.

Telling lies.

Killing innocent people.

## Proverbs 6:18

Making plans to do evil.

Rushing to do wrong.

## Proverbs 6:19

Lying when you give evidence about someone.

Turning brothers against each other.

## Teaching that gives light

## Proverbs 6:20

My son, do what your father has told you.

Remember what your mother taught you.

## Proverbs 6:21

Tie their words close to your heart.

Wear them like a necklace.

This means keeping their teaching with you always.

## Proverbs 6:22

Their words will guide you as you walk.

They will guard you as you sleep.

You will remember their words when you wake.

## Proverbs 6:23

Their teaching is like a lamp.

Its light shows you where to step.

When your parents correct you, they show you the way to life.

![A flame burns at the tip of a clay lamp in the dark.](../pictures/davids-lamp.svg)

**Look for the flame**

Imagine carrying this lamp along a dark path. Its light helps you see your next step. A teaching you remember can help you choose what to do next.

## A warning about an affair

## Proverbs 6:24

Their teaching will guard you against another man’s wife who tries to draw you in with smooth words.

## Proverbs 6:25

She may look beautiful.

Do not dwell on wanting her.

Do not let her glance draw you in.

## Proverbs 6:26

Paying someone for sex can leave you poor.

An affair with another man’s wife can cost you your life.

## Proverbs 6:27

Could you hold fire against your chest without burning your clothes?

## Proverbs 6:28

Could you walk on hot coals without burning your feet?

## Proverbs 6:29

So it is when a man sleeps with another man’s wife.

He will face punishment for what he does.

## What the wrongdoer loses

## Proverbs 6:30

People understand why a hungry person might steal food.

They do not look down on him for being hungry and stealing to eat.

## Proverbs 6:31

Yet if they catch him, he must repay seven times what he stole.

It may take everything he owns.

## Proverbs 6:32

A man who sleeps with another man’s wife shows poor judgment.

By doing this, he destroys himself.

## Proverbs 6:33

He will suffer wounds and shame.

The disgrace will stay with him.

## Proverbs 6:34

The husband will be jealous and furious.

When he takes revenge, he will show no mercy.

## Proverbs 6:35

The husband will refuse any payment to settle the matter.

Even costly gifts will not change his mind.
