# Proverbs 12 — Words that hurt and words that heal

**About this chapter**

Solomon describes a person hearing an insult and another weighed down by worry. In both moments, a few words can make a difference.

## Taking correction

## Proverbs 12:1

People who love learning welcome correction.

Refusing to hear where you went wrong is foolish.

## Proverbs 12:2

The LORD looks kindly on a good person.

He judges those who make plans to hurt others.

## Proverbs 12:3

Doing evil gives no one a firm place to stand.

Those who do right are like trees with strong roots.

No one can pull them up.

## Proverbs 12:4

A wife of good character brings her husband honor, like a crown.

A wife who brings shame is like sickness deep in his bones.

## What plans and words can do

## Proverbs 12:5

Good people make fair plans.

Wicked people give advice that tricks others.

## Proverbs 12:6

Wicked people use words to set a deadly trap.

Honest people can speak up and save a life.

## Proverbs 12:7

Wicked people fall and are gone.

The family of those who do right stands firm.

## Proverbs 12:8

People praise someone who shows good judgment.

They have little respect for someone who twists the truth.

## Food, work, and care for animals

## Proverbs 12:9

Someone others overlook may still have a servant to help.

That is better than acting important while going hungry.

## Proverbs 12:10

A good person takes care of an animal’s needs.

Even what wicked people call kindness can be cruel.

![A sheep pen with an open gate.](../pictures/sheep-pen.svg)

**Find the gate**

Trace the way into the pen. A shepherd can lead the sheep inside to keep them safe. That is one way to care for an animal.

## Proverbs 12:11

Those who work their land have plenty to eat.

Those who spend their days chasing empty dreams show poor judgment.

## Proverbs 12:12

Wicked people want the things other people have stolen.

Good people are like plants whose roots keep growing.

## Listening before speaking

## Proverbs 12:13

An evil person gets caught by his own hurtful words.

A good person finds a way out of trouble.

## Proverbs 12:14

Good words bear fruit.

That means they bring good things to the speaker.

People also gain from the work of their hands.

## Proverbs 12:15

Foolish people think their own way is always right.

Wise people listen to advice.

## Proverbs 12:16

A foolish person shows anger at once.

A wise person can let an insult pass.

## Words that heal

## Proverbs 12:17

An honest witness tells what really happened.

A dishonest witness tries to fool people.

## Proverbs 12:18

Careless words can hurt like a sword.

A wise person’s words help people heal.

## Proverbs 12:19

The truth stands forever.

A lie lasts only a moment.

## Proverbs 12:20

People who plan harm think of ways to deceive others.

People who help others make peace find joy.

## Keeping your word

## Proverbs 12:21

Those who do right come to no harm.

The lives of wicked people are full of trouble.

## Proverbs 12:22

The LORD hates lies.

People who keep their word give him joy.

## Proverbs 12:23

A wise person knows when to keep quiet about something.

A foolish person talks and shows everyone how little they understand.

## Proverbs 12:24

People who work hard will lead others.

Those who avoid work will have to work under orders.

## A kind word for someone who worries

## Proverbs 12:25

Worry weighs a person down.

A kind word can lift that person’s spirits.

## Proverbs 12:26

A good person chooses friends with care.

Wicked people lose their way by following their own habits.

## Proverbs 12:27

A lazy hunter leaves the catch uncooked.

A hardworking person values what they have.

## Proverbs 12:28

Doing right is like walking a path that leads to life.

Another path leads to death.

**Pause and answer**

What can cheer someone who feels weighed down by worry?
