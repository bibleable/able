# Proverbs 9 — Two invitations

**About this chapter**

The writer pictures wisdom and foolishness as two women. Both call to people passing by. Listen to what each woman offers her guests.

## Wisdom sets the table

## Proverbs 9:1

Wisdom has built a house.

She carved seven tall posts to hold it up.

## Proverbs 9:2

Wisdom has prepared the meat and mixed the wine.

She has set the table for her guests.

## Proverbs 9:3

Wisdom sends her servant women out with invitations.

She calls from the high places in the city.

## Wisdom invites guests

## Proverbs 9:4

She calls to people still learning to make good choices.

“Come in here!”

## Proverbs 9:5

Wisdom says, “Come and eat my bread.

Drink the wine I have mixed.

![A drinking cup with a handle.](../pictures/cup.svg)

**Picture a place at the table**

Look at the cup. Imagine Wisdom setting it beside the bread for a guest. She welcomes people who still have much to learn.

## Proverbs 9:6

“Leave your foolish ways behind and live.

Let understanding guide your steps.”

## How people respond to correction

## Proverbs 9:7

Someone who mocks others may insult you for correcting them.

Speaking up about a wicked person’s deeds can bring you disgrace.

## Proverbs 9:8

Correct someone who mocks you, and they will hate you.

Correct a wise person, and they will love you for it.

## Proverbs 9:9

Teach a wise person, and they will grow wiser.

A person who does right keeps learning.

## Where wisdom begins

## Proverbs 9:10

Wisdom begins with deep respect for the LORD.

You gain understanding by knowing the Holy One.

The Holy One is God.

## Proverbs 9:11

Wisdom adds days to your life.

It helps you live for many years.

## Proverbs 9:12

When you choose wisdom, you gain from that choice.

If you mock wisdom, you yourself must face the results.

## Folly sits by her door

## Proverbs 9:13

Now picture a woman named Folly.

Folly means acting foolishly.

She is loud and easily fooled.

She understands nothing.

## Proverbs 9:14

Folly sits on a seat by her door.

Her house is high above the city.

## Proverbs 9:15

People are walking straight along their way.

Folly calls to them as they pass.

## What Folly offers

## Proverbs 9:16

She calls to people still learning to make good choices.

“Come in here!”

## Proverbs 9:17

Folly says, “Water tastes sweet when you steal it.

Bread tastes good when you eat it in secret.” She makes doing wrong sound inviting.

## Proverbs 9:18

The people she calls have no idea who is inside.

The dead are there.

Her guests are deep in Sheol, the place of the dead.

**Pause and answer**

Which woman asks her guests to leave their foolish ways behind?
