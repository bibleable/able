# Proverbs 26 — When gossip stops

**About this chapter**

A door swings back and forth. A fire burns until the wood runs out. These everyday sights help explain what people do with their time and words.

## When honor is out of place

## Proverbs 26:1

Snow is out of place in summer.

Rain is unwelcome at harvest.

Giving honor to someone who insists on acting foolishly is just as unfitting.

## Proverbs 26:2

A sparrow flutters past.

A swallow darts away.

A curse you did nothing to deserve will not land on you.

## Proverbs 26:3

A whip guides a horse.

A bridle guides a donkey.

The saying pictures a rod on the back of someone who refuses to learn.

## When to answer

## Proverbs 26:4

When someone talks foolishly, do not copy their way of speaking.

You could end up acting just like them.

## Proverbs 26:5

Yet sometimes a foolish claim needs an answer.

Show where it goes wrong.

Otherwise the speaker may think it was wise.

## Proverbs 26:6

Sending a message by an unreliable fool harms the sender.

The saying pictures someone cutting off their own feet or swallowing violence.

## Proverbs 26:7

Legs that hang limp cannot carry someone forward.

A wise saying cannot help when a fool repeats it without understanding.

## When foolishness brings harm

## Proverbs 26:8

Honoring a fool is like tying a stone into a sling.

A stone tied there cannot fly free.

## Proverbs 26:9

A thorn can pierce the hand of someone drunk.

A wise saying in a fool’s mouth can cause harm too.

## Proverbs 26:10

Hiring a fool or just anyone passing by puts others at risk.

It is like an archer whose arrows wound people at random.

## Proverbs 26:11

A dog goes back to its own vomit.

In the same way, a fool keeps repeating a foolish act.

## Proverbs 26:12

Someone may think they are already wise.

There is more hope for a fool than for someone like that.

## Excuses for staying put

## Proverbs 26:13

Someone avoiding work says, “A lion is in the road!

There is a fierce lion in the town square!”

## Proverbs 26:14

A door swings on its hinges and stays in place.

A person rolls over in bed and stays there too.

## Proverbs 26:15

Picture someone with a hand in the food dish.

Even lifting the food to their mouth seems too much work.

## Proverbs 26:16

Someone who avoids work may think they know more than seven people who give thoughtful answers.

## How quarrels grow

## Proverbs 26:17

Suppose you walk past two people arguing.

Getting mixed up in their fight is like grabbing a dog by the ears.

## Proverbs 26:18

Picture someone recklessly throwing burning sticks and shooting deadly arrows.

## Proverbs 26:19

Someone who tricks a neighbor can be just as dangerous.

Then the person says, “I was only joking!”

## Proverbs 26:20

A fire goes out when the wood runs out.

A quarrel dies down when the gossip stops.

## Proverbs 26:21

Charcoal keeps embers hot.

Wood feeds a fire.

Someone who loves to argue keeps a quarrel going.

## Proverbs 26:22

Gossip can seem like tasty food.

You take it in eagerly, and it stays deep inside you.

## What friendly words can hide

## Proverbs 26:23

A smooth coating can cover an ordinary clay pot.

In the same way, warm words can cover cruel thoughts.

![A plain brown clay jar with a narrow neck.](../pictures/clay-jar.svg)

**Look at the jar**

Imagine covering the plain clay with a shiny coating. The jar would look different, but the clay underneath would stay the same. This is how the saying describes someone hiding hatred with warm words.

## Proverbs 26:24

Someone can hide hatred behind friendly words.

Inside, that person is planning to deceive you.

## Proverbs 26:25

When that person speaks kindly, do not trust those words.

The saying pictures seven hateful things hidden in that heart.

## Proverbs 26:26

Lies can hide hatred for a time.

But the whole gathering will see the harm that person has done.

## Proverbs 26:27

Someone who digs a pit for another will fall in.

Someone who rolls a stone will find it rolling back on them.

Harm can come back on the person who planned it.

## Proverbs 26:28

Someone who tells lies shows hatred for the people those lies hurt.

False praise can ruin a person too.
