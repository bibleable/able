# Proverbs 24 — What a field can teach

**About this chapter**

A teacher walks past a field. Weeds cover the ground, and the wall lies in pieces. He stops to think about what the field can teach him.

## Choosing your company

## Proverbs 24:1

Do not envy people who do evil.

Do not wish you could join them.

## Proverbs 24:2

They plan to hurt people.

They talk about the trouble they will cause.

## What wisdom builds

## Proverbs 24:3

Wisdom helps you build a home.

Understanding helps you make it stand firm.

## Proverbs 24:4

Knowledge helps you fill its rooms with beautiful, valuable things.

## Proverbs 24:5

Wisdom makes a person strong.

Learning gives a person even greater strength.

## Proverbs 24:6

A ruler needs good advice before going to war.

Listening to many advisers helps bring victory.

## Plans and choices

## Proverbs 24:7

Wisdom is out of reach for someone who refuses to learn.

That person has nothing to say when people meet to decide important matters.

## Proverbs 24:8

If you keep planning harm, people will know you for it.

They will call you someone who plots evil.

## Proverbs 24:9

Making foolish plans to do wrong is a sin.

People hate it when someone mocks them.

## Help when lives are in danger

## Proverbs 24:10

If trouble makes you give up, it shows how little strength you have.

## Proverbs 24:11

Rescue people who are being led away to die.

Hold back those stumbling toward their death.

## Proverbs 24:12

You might say, “We knew nothing about it.” But God knows what is in your heart.

He guards your life and knows what you knew.

He will repay each person for what they do.

## Honey and hope

## Proverbs 24:13

My son, eat honey.

It is good.

Honey from the honeycomb tastes sweet.

## Proverbs 24:14

Wisdom brings that kind of sweetness to your life.

Find wisdom and you have a future.

Your hope will last.

## Proverbs 24:15

You who plan harm, leave the home of an honest person alone.

Do not wait outside to attack.

Do not wreck the place where that person rests.

## Proverbs 24:16

Someone who does right may fall seven times.

Each time they get up again.

Wicked people fall when trouble comes.

## When an enemy falls

## Proverbs 24:17

When your enemy falls, do not celebrate.

When your enemy stumbles, take no pleasure in it.

## Proverbs 24:18

The LORD will see your delight and disapprove.

He may turn his anger away from your enemy.

## Proverbs 24:19

Do not let people who do evil fill you with worry.

Do not wish you were like them.

## Proverbs 24:20

They have no future.

Their life is like a lamp whose flame goes out.

![A small flame shines from a clay lamp in the dark.](../pictures/davids-lamp.svg)

**Find the light**

Look at the flame. Imagine it going out. The teacher uses that darkness to picture the end of a life spent doing evil.

## Respect and fair judgment

## Proverbs 24:21

My son, show deep respect for the LORD and the king.

Keep away from people who rebel against them.

## Proverbs 24:22

The LORD and the king can bring ruin suddenly.

Who knows how great that ruin can be?

## Proverbs 24:23

Here are more sayings from wise people.

A judge must hear each case fairly.

It is wrong to favor one side.

## Proverbs 24:24

Suppose a judge tells a guilty person, “You are innocent.” Whole nations will speak out against that judge.

People will call down trouble on the judge.

## Proverbs 24:25

Things go well for judges who find the guilty person guilty.

Those judges will receive a great blessing.

## Honest words and honest work

## Proverbs 24:26

An honest answer is like a kiss on the lips.

It shows that the speaker cares for you.

## Proverbs 24:27

Finish the work outside first.

Get your field ready.

Then build your house.

## Proverbs 24:28

Never speak against your neighbor in court without good reason.

Never use lies to mislead people.

## Proverbs 24:29

Do not say, “I will do to my neighbor what he did to me.

I will pay him back.”

## A lesson among the weeds

## Proverbs 24:30

I walked past a field owned by someone who avoided work.

I passed grapevines belonging to someone who showed poor judgment.

## Proverbs 24:31

Thorns had grown everywhere.

Prickly weeds covered the soil.

The stone wall had fallen apart.

## Proverbs 24:32

I stopped and looked.

As I thought about what I saw, I learned a lesson.

## Proverbs 24:33

You say, “A little more sleep.” You fold your hands to rest a little longer.

## Proverbs 24:34

Then poverty comes suddenly, like a robber.

Like a thief, it leaves you without what you need.

**Pause and answer**

What did the teacher see growing in the neglected field?
