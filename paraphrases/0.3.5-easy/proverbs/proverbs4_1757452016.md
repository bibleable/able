# Proverbs 4 — Stay on the wise path

**About this chapter**

A father passes on what his own father taught him. He compares the choices we make to paths we walk.

## A father remembers being a son

## Proverbs 4:1

My sons, listen to me, your father.

Pay attention so you can understand.

## Proverbs 4:2

What I teach you is good.

Keep doing what I tell you.

## Proverbs 4:3

I too was once a young boy.

I was my mother’s only child.

## Proverbs 4:4

My father taught me these words.

“Keep my words close to your heart.

Do what I tell you and you will live.

## What the grandfather taught

## Proverbs 4:5

“Learn to be wise.

Learn to understand.

Remember my words and follow them.

## Proverbs 4:6

“Stay close to Wisdom and she will protect you.

Love her and she will watch over you.”

**Who is Wisdom?**

The grandfather speaks of wisdom as a woman. Learning from her means learning to choose what is good.

## Proverbs 4:7

My father said, “Wisdom matters most.

So learn to be wise.

Whatever else you gain, make sure you gain understanding.

## Proverbs 4:8

“Value Wisdom and she will lift you up.

Hold her close and she will bring you honor.

## Proverbs 4:9

“She will place a wreath on your head.

She will give you a beautiful crown.” A wreath and a crown are signs of honor.

## Hold on to what you learn

## Proverbs 4:10

Now, my son, listen to me.

Take my words to heart and you will live many years.

## Proverbs 4:11

I will teach you to make wise choices.

That is how I lead you along straight paths.

## Proverbs 4:12

When you walk, nothing will block your steps.

When you run, you will keep your footing.

## Proverbs 4:13

Hold on to what you learn.

Guard it well.

Your life depends on it.

## Stay away from the path of evil

## Proverbs 4:14

Keep off the path of people who do evil.

That means refusing to join in what they do.

## Proverbs 4:15

Stay away from that path.

Turn aside and go on your way.

## Proverbs 4:16

Those people cannot sleep until they do wrong.

They lie awake until they make someone fall.

## Proverbs 4:17

Doing wrong is their daily bread.

Hurting people is the wine they drink.

They make violence part of every day.

## Morning light and deep darkness

## Proverbs 4:18

The path of those who do right is like morning light.

The light grows brighter until the sun is high overhead.

![The bright sun shines over the hills.](../pictures/judges-song-sun.svg)

**Look at the hills**

Find the hills under the bright sun. Imagine walking there at first light. As the sky gets brighter, more of the path comes into view.

## Proverbs 4:19

People who do evil walk as if in deep darkness.

They trip and cannot see what made them fall.

## Guard your heart

## Proverbs 4:20

My son, pay attention to my words.

Listen closely to what I say.

## Proverbs 4:21

Keep my words in front of you.

Remember them deep in your heart.

## Proverbs 4:22

My words give life to those who find them.

They bring health to the whole body.

## Proverbs 4:23

Guard your heart with great care.

Water flows from a spring.

In the same way, how you live flows from your heart.

Pay attention to what you think and love.

## Choose your words and your steps

## Proverbs 4:24

Keep lies out of your mouth.

Speak honestly, without twisting the truth.

## Proverbs 4:25

Look straight ahead.

Keep your eyes on where you are going.

## Proverbs 4:26

Make the ground level where you will walk.

Then your steps will be steady.

Think carefully about the choices ahead of you.

## Proverbs 4:27

Keep to that path.

Do not turn right or left.

Turn away from doing evil.

What happens to the light on the path of people who do right?
