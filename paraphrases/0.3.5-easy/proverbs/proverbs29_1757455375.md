# Proverbs 29 — A king who listens to the poor

**About this chapter**

These sayings take us into a courtroom and a king’s house. Notice whose words a ruler listens to and whose case a good person takes seriously.

## Who makes the people glad?

## Proverbs 29:1

Someone may hear many warnings and still refuse to change.

Then ruin can come suddenly, beyond repair.

## Proverbs 29:2

When good people thrive, the people celebrate.

When wicked people rule, the people groan.

## Proverbs 29:3

A son who loves wisdom gives his father joy.

Someone who pays for sex again and again wastes his wealth.

## Proverbs 29:4

A king who deals fairly keeps the country steady.

A ruler who keeps demanding payment tears it apart.

## A trap made of words

## Proverbs 29:5

Someone who gives a neighbor false praise sets a trap.

Picture a net spread where the neighbor will walk.

![Ropes cross each other to form a net.](../pictures/net.svg)

**Look at the net**

Follow a rope to where it crosses another. Imagine a foot caught in those ropes. False praise can draw someone into trouble before they see the danger.

## Proverbs 29:6

People who do evil get caught by their own wrongdoing.

Those who do right can sing for joy.

## Proverbs 29:7

Good people pay attention when poor people need justice.

Wicked people do not care about their case.

## Anger in the city

## Proverbs 29:8

People who mock others can stir up a whole city.

Wise people help the anger die down.

## Proverbs 29:9

A wise person takes a fool to court.

There is shouting and laughing.

The two never settle the matter.

## Proverbs 29:10

Violent people hate someone who has done nothing wrong.

Honest people care what happens to that person.

## Proverbs 29:11

A fool lets every angry feeling spill out.

A wise person holds anger back.

## What a ruler listens to

## Proverbs 29:12

When a ruler listens to lies, all the officials who serve that ruler become corrupt.

## Proverbs 29:13

A poor person and someone who mistreats the poor share one gift.

The LORD gives both of them light to see.

## Proverbs 29:14

A king may hear a poor person’s case and judge fairly.

Such a king’s rule will stand for all time.

## Learning from correction

## Proverbs 29:15

The saying pictures correction as a rod.

Correction helps a child learn wisdom.

A child left without guidance brings shame to the mother.

## Proverbs 29:16

When wicked people thrive, more people rebel.

Those who do right will live to see the wicked fall.

## Proverbs 29:17

Correct your son and he will bring you peace.

You will take great joy in him.

## Proverbs 29:18

When people receive no message from God, they stop holding themselves back.

Happy are those who follow God’s teaching.

## Proverbs 29:19

Words alone may fail to correct a servant.

The servant may understand and still refuse to act.

## Words spoken in haste

## Proverbs 29:20

Think about someone who speaks before thinking.

There is more hope for a fool than for that person.

## Proverbs 29:21

A servant given everything he wants from childhood will bring sorrow in the end.

## Proverbs 29:22

An angry person starts quarrels.

Someone who quickly loses their temper does wrong again and again.

## Proverbs 29:23

Pride brings a person down.

A humble person receives honor.

## Fear and trust

## Proverbs 29:24

Someone who helps a thief puts their own life at risk.

Even when called on to swear to the truth, that person stays silent.

## Proverbs 29:25

Fear of other people can catch you like a trap.

Trust the LORD, and he will keep you safe.

## Proverbs 29:26

Many people try to win a ruler’s favor.

The LORD is the one who gives a person justice.

## Proverbs 29:27

People who do right cannot stand someone who acts unjustly.

Wicked people cannot stand someone who lives honestly.

**Pause and answer**

How does a good king judge a poor person’s case?
