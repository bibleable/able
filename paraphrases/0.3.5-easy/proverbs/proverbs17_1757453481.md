# Proverbs 17 — A friend loves at all times

**About this chapter**

Solomon pictures a quiet table and a house full of arguing. He describes what helps people live together as family and friends.

## A peaceful home

## Proverbs 17:1

A dry piece of bread is enough at a peaceful table.

That is better than a feast where everyone fights.

## Proverbs 17:2

A wise servant may lead a son who brings shame.

The servant may even receive a share of the family property, like one of the brothers.

## Proverbs 17:3

A metalworker heats silver in a pot and gold in a furnace.

The heat shows how pure the metal is.

The LORD tests what is in our hearts.

## Proverbs 17:4

Someone who does evil listens eagerly to harmful words.

A liar likes to hear words that hurt people.

## Honor and honesty

## Proverbs 17:5

Mocking poor people insults God, who made them.

Anyone who enjoys another person’s disaster will face punishment.

## Proverbs 17:6

Grandchildren bring honor to their grandparents, like a crown.

Children take pride in their fathers.

## Proverbs 17:7

Fine speeches sound strange coming from a fool.

Lies sound even worse coming from a ruler.

## Proverbs 17:8

Someone who pays a bribe thinks it works like magic.

A bribe is a gift to buy an unfair favor.

The giver sees it opening doors everywhere.

## What keeps friends together

## Proverbs 17:9

Letting a wrong rest can help love grow.

Bringing it up again and again can separate close friends.

## Proverbs 17:10

A single correction reaches a person who wants to understand.

Even a hundred blows teach a fool less.

## Proverbs 17:11

An evil person wants only to rebel.

A harsh messenger will come against that person.

## Proverbs 17:12

Imagine meeting a mother bear whose cubs are gone.

Even that is better than meeting a fool bent on foolishness.

## Leave the quarrel

## Proverbs 17:13

When someone repays kindness with harm, trouble stays in that person’s home.

## Proverbs 17:14

Starting a quarrel is like letting floodwater loose.

Leave the argument before it bursts out.

![Water spills from a tipped cup and spreads across the ground.](../pictures/water-spilled-on-the-ground.svg)

**Follow the water**

Trace where the water goes after it leaves the cup. Now imagine a whole flood rushing out. A quarrel can spread beyond the people who started it.

## Proverbs 17:15

A judge may let a guilty person go free.

A judge may declare an innocent person guilty.

The LORD hates both decisions.

## Proverbs 17:16

Why put money in the hand of a fool to buy wisdom?

The fool has no wish to learn.

## Friends and neighbors

## Proverbs 17:17

A friend loves you at all times.

A brother is there to help when trouble comes.

## Proverbs 17:18

Someone shakes hands and promises to pay a neighbor’s debt.

Taking on that debt shows poor judgment.

## Proverbs 17:19

People who love doing wrong love a fight.

Someone who builds a high gate to show off invites ruin.

## Proverbs 17:20

Someone who keeps twisting what is right finds no good.

Someone who tells lies falls into trouble.

## Sorrow and joy

## Proverbs 17:21

A father grieves when his child grows up foolish.

His child’s foolishness brings him no joy.

## Proverbs 17:22

Joy does a person good, like medicine.

Deep sadness can drain a person’s strength.

## Proverbs 17:23

A wicked person secretly accepts a bribe.

The payment keeps someone from getting a fair decision.

## Proverbs 17:24

A person with good judgment keeps wisdom in view.

A foolish person looks everywhere else, even to the ends of the earth.

## Knowing when to speak

## Proverbs 17:25

A foolish son brings his father sorrow.

His choices bring pain to the mother who gave him birth.

## Proverbs 17:26

Punishing an innocent person is wrong.

So is whipping a leader for acting honestly.

## Proverbs 17:27

A person with knowledge chooses when to speak.

A person with understanding stays calm.

## Proverbs 17:28

Even a fool can seem wise by keeping quiet.

People may think a silent person has good judgment.

**Pause and answer**

When does a friend show love?
