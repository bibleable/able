# Proverbs 28 — Courage to do what is right

**About this chapter**

A poor person can see what a rich person misses. A ruler can protect a country or leave its people hungry. These sayings ask us to look closely at how people use power.

## Courage and leadership

## Proverbs 28:1

Wicked people run even when nobody is chasing them.

People who do right are as brave as a lion.

## Proverbs 28:2

When a country rebels, ruler follows ruler.

A leader with good sense and knowledge keeps order.

## Proverbs 28:3

A leader who has nothing may still mistreat poor people.

Such a leader is like rain that beats down the crops.

It leaves people with nothing to eat.

![Ripe grain at the top of a green stalk.](../pictures/grain.svg)

**Look at the stalk**

Imagine the grain standing ready to gather. Now imagine hard rain flattening the crop. The saying compares a harsh leader to rain that destroys a family’s food.

## People who listen to the law

## Proverbs 28:4

People who abandon the law praise those who do wrong.

People who keep the law stand against them.

## Proverbs 28:5

People who do evil fail to understand what is fair.

People who seek the LORD can understand justice fully.

## Proverbs 28:6

It is better to be poor and honest than to grow rich while living dishonestly.

## Proverbs 28:7

A son with good judgment follows the law.

A son who joins people who gorge themselves brings his father shame.

## Money and good judgment

## Proverbs 28:8

Someone may grow rich by charging borrowers more and more.

That wealth will go to someone who helps poor people.

## Proverbs 28:9

Suppose someone refuses to listen to God’s teaching.

Even that person’s prayer displeases God.

## Proverbs 28:10

Someone who leads honest people into wrongdoing falls into his own trap.

People who live blamelessly will receive good things.

## Proverbs 28:11

A rich person may think he is wise.

A poor person with good judgment can see through him.

## Admitting what went wrong

## Proverbs 28:12

When honest people succeed, there is much to celebrate.

When wicked people gain power, others go into hiding.

## Proverbs 28:13

Hiding your sins will not help you do well.

Admit what you did and turn away from it.

You will find mercy.

## Proverbs 28:14

Happy is the person who always shows deep respect for God.

Someone who stubbornly refuses to listen falls into trouble.

## Power and its dangers

## Proverbs 28:15

A wicked ruler terrifies people who cannot defend themselves.

That ruler is like a roaring lion or a charging bear.

## Proverbs 28:16

A ruler with poor judgment treats people harshly.

A ruler who refuses dishonest gain will live longer.

## Proverbs 28:17

Someone guilty of taking a life runs under the weight of that guilt.

His flight ends in the grave.

No one should help him escape.

## Proverbs 28:18

Honest people walk in safety.

Those who act dishonestly can fall without warning.

## Work and the rush for wealth

## Proverbs 28:19

A farmer who works the land has plenty to eat.

Someone who chases empty dreams ends up very poor.

## Proverbs 28:20

Someone who stays faithful receives many blessings.

Someone in a rush to get rich will face punishment.

## Proverbs 28:21

It is wrong to take sides unfairly.

Yet some people will do wrong for even a piece of bread.

## Proverbs 28:22

A person who hates to share rushes to get rich.

He does not see the poverty ahead.

## Correction, trust, and generosity

## Proverbs 28:23

An honest correction may be hard to hear.

Later people value it more than praise meant to please them.

## Proverbs 28:24

Someone steals from a parent and says, “I did nothing wrong.” That person belongs alongside someone who destroys what others have.

## Proverbs 28:25

Greed starts quarrels.

Someone who trusts the LORD will do well.

## Proverbs 28:26

Trusting only your own judgment is foolish.

Choosing a wise path keeps you safe.

## Proverbs 28:27

Give to people who are poor, and you will have enough.

Turn away from their need, and many will curse you.

## Proverbs 28:28

When wicked people take power, others hide.

When those rulers die, people who do right can thrive.
