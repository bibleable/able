# Genesis 50 — Joseph keeps his promise and comforts his brothers

**About this chapter**

Joseph takes his father’s body home to Canaan.

When his brothers come to him afraid, Joseph speaks kindly to them.

## Joseph asks to bury his father in Canaan

## Genesis 50:1

Joseph leaned over his father’s face.

He cried and kissed him.

## Genesis 50:2

Joseph told his doctors to embalm his father, Jacob.

The doctors did as he asked.

**Reading help**

Jacob was also called Israel. To embalm a body means to treat it so it will last after death.

## Genesis 50:3

It took the doctors forty days to embalm Jacob.

The Egyptians mourned for him for seventy days.

## Genesis 50:4

When the time of mourning ended, Joseph spoke to Pharaoh’s officials.

“If you are willing, please speak to Pharaoh for me.

## Genesis 50:5

My father made me promise him something.

He said, ‘I am about to die.

Bury me in the tomb I dug for myself in Canaan.’

Please let me go and bury my father. Then I will return.”

## Genesis 50:6

Pharaoh answered, “Go and bury your father.

Keep the promise you made to him.”

**Pause and answer**

What promise did Joseph want to keep?

## Jacob’s family travels to the burial place

## Genesis 50:7

Joseph set out to bury his father.

Pharaoh’s officials went with him.

They included the leaders of Pharaoh’s household and the leaders of Egypt.

## Genesis 50:8

Joseph’s household, his brothers, and his father’s household went too.

Only their children, flocks, and herds stayed in Goshen.

## Genesis 50:9

People riding in chariots and on horses went with Joseph.

A very large group traveled together.

## Genesis 50:10

Across the Jordan River, they reached the threshing floor of Atad.

There they cried out loudly in grief.

Joseph mourned for his father there for seven days.

**Reading help**

A threshing floor was a flat place where people separated grain from its stalks.

## Genesis 50:11

The Canaanites saw them crying at Atad.

They said, “The Egyptians are mourning here with deep sorrow.”

That is how the place across the Jordan got its name, Abel-mizraim.

## Genesis 50:12

Jacob’s sons did what their father had asked.

## Genesis 50:13

They carried Jacob to Canaan and buried him in the cave at Machpelah.

The cave was in a field near Mamre.

Abraham had bought this burial place from Ephron the Hittite.

## Genesis 50:14

After the burial, Joseph returned to Egypt.

His brothers and everyone who had gone with him returned too.

**Pause and answer**

## Joseph’s brothers ask him to forgive them

## Genesis 50:15

Now their father was dead. Joseph’s brothers were afraid.

They said, “What if Joseph is still angry with us?

He may pay us back for all the harm we did.”

## Genesis 50:16

They sent Joseph a message.

“Before he died, your father told us what to say to you.

## Genesis 50:17

He said, ‘Please forgive your brothers for their sin.

They did wrong when they hurt you.’

We serve your father’s God. Please forgive us for what we did.”

Joseph cried when he heard their message.

## Genesis 50:18

Then his brothers came and bowed down before him.

They said, “We are your slaves!”

## Genesis 50:19

Joseph answered, “Do not be afraid. Can I take God’s place?

## Genesis 50:20

You meant to harm me. God meant it for good.

He brought us to this day and kept many people alive.

## Genesis 50:21

Do not be afraid. I will give you and your children what you need.”

Joseph spoke kindly to his brothers and comforted them.

**Pause and answer**

What did Joseph promise to do for his brothers and their children?

## Joseph asks his family to remember God’s promise

## Genesis 50:22

Joseph and his father’s household stayed in Egypt.

Joseph lived to be 110 years old.

## Genesis 50:23

Joseph saw three generations of Ephraim’s family grow up.

Manasseh had a son named Machir.

Joseph held Machir’s children on his knees as they grew.

## Genesis 50:24

Joseph told his brothers, “I am about to die.

God will surely come to help you.

He will lead you out of Egypt.

He will bring you to the land he promised Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.”

## Genesis 50:25

Joseph asked the sons of Israel to make a solemn promise.

He said, “God will surely come to help you.

When he does, carry my bones away from here.”

## Genesis 50:26

Joseph died at the age of 110.

People embalmed his body and put it in a coffin in Egypt.

| Whose body? | What the family did |
| --- | --- |
| Jacob’s | His sons buried him in Canaan. |
| Joseph’s | They placed him in a coffin in Egypt. He had asked them to take his bones when they left. |
