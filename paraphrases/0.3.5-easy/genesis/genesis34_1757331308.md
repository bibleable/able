# Genesis 34 — Shechem harms Dinah, and her brothers take revenge

**About this chapter**

Shechem forces Dinah to have sex with him.

Her brothers learn what happened. Two of them kill the men of the city.

| Person | Family |
| --- | --- |
| Dinah | Daughter of Leah and Jacob |
| Simeon and Levi | Brothers of Dinah |
| Shechem | Son of Hamor |

## Shechem harms Dinah

## Genesis 34:1

Dinah was the daughter of Leah and Jacob.

She went to visit the young women who lived nearby.

![Leah and Jacob are Dinah’s parents.](../pictures/dinahs-parents.svg)

The story begins with their daughter Dinah visiting the women nearby.

## Genesis 34:2

Shechem saw Dinah. His father was Hamor, the Hivite ruler of the area.

Shechem took Dinah and forced her to have sex with him.

## Genesis 34:3

Shechem felt drawn to Dinah, Jacob’s daughter.

He loved her and spoke gently to her.

## Genesis 34:4

Shechem told his father Hamor, “Get me this young woman as my wife.”

## Genesis 34:5

Jacob heard what Shechem had done to Dinah.

Jacob’s sons were in the fields with his animals.

Jacob said nothing until his sons came home.

## Hamor asks for a marriage

## Genesis 34:6

Hamor, Shechem’s father, came to speak with Jacob.

## Genesis 34:7

When Jacob’s sons heard the news, they came back from the fields.

They felt deep sorrow and anger.

Shechem had done a terrible wrong in Israel.

No one should have done that to Dinah.

## Genesis 34:8

Hamor told them, “My son Shechem longs for Dinah.

Please let him marry her.

## Genesis 34:9

Let our families marry each other.

Our sons can marry your daughters.

Your sons can marry our daughters.

## Genesis 34:10

Come and live among us. There is land for you here.

Travel freely and buy property of your own.”

## Genesis 34:11

Shechem spoke to Dinah’s father and brothers.

“Please do this for me. I will give you whatever you ask.

## Genesis 34:12

Name the price you want for the marriage.

Ask for a costly gift too. I will pay it.

Just let me marry her.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Shechem ask Dinah’s family to let him do?

## Dinah’s brothers give a dishonest answer

## Genesis 34:13

Shechem had forced their sister Dinah to have sex with him.

So Jacob’s sons tried to trick Shechem and Hamor with their answer.

## Genesis 34:14

The brothers said, “We cannot give our sister to a man who is not circumcised.

That would bring shame on us.

## Genesis 34:15

We will agree if you do one thing.

Every man and boy among you must be circumcised like us.

**Reading help**

Circumcision means removing the foreskin, the skin covering the tip of the penis.

For Jacob’s family, it was a sign of their agreement with God.

## Genesis 34:16

Then our sons and daughters can marry yours.

We will live with you and become one people.

## Genesis 34:17

If you refuse, we will take our sister and leave.”

## Genesis 34:18

Hamor and Shechem liked what the brothers said.

## Genesis 34:19

Shechem wanted to marry Dinah, so he agreed at once.

His father’s household respected Shechem more than anyone else in the family.

**Pause and answer**

What condition did Dinah’s brothers set?

## Hamor speaks at the city gate

## Genesis 34:20

Hamor and Shechem went to the city gate.

There they spoke to the men of the city.

## Genesis 34:21

They said, “These people want peace with us.

Our land has plenty of room for them.

Let them live and trade here.

Our sons and daughters can marry theirs.

## Genesis 34:22

They will live with us as one people on one condition.

All our men must be circumcised like them.

## Genesis 34:23

Think of their flocks, their other animals, and everything they own.

Will all these things not become ours?

Let us agree so they will stay.”

## Genesis 34:24

All the men at the gate listened to Hamor and Shechem.

Every man and boy in the city underwent circumcision.

**Pause and answer**

What did Hamor and Shechem hope their people would gain?

## Simeon and Levi take Dinah from Shechem’s house

## Genesis 34:25

Three days later, the men were still in pain.

Dinah’s brothers Simeon and Levi entered the city with swords.

The people expected no attack.

The two brothers killed every male in the city.

## Genesis 34:26

Simeon and Levi killed Hamor and Shechem.

They took Dinah out of Shechem’s house and left.

## Genesis 34:27

Jacob’s other sons came and saw the dead.

Because of what Shechem had done to their sister, they robbed the city.

## Genesis 34:28

They took the flocks, herds, and donkeys.

They took everything they found in the city and fields.

## Genesis 34:29

They took the women and children away as captives.

They carried off the people’s possessions and emptied their houses.

**Pause and answer**

Who took Dinah out of Shechem’s house?

## Jacob speaks to Simeon and Levi

## Genesis 34:30

Jacob told Simeon and Levi, “You have brought trouble on me.

Now the Canaanites and Perizzites who live here will hate me.

There are so few of us.

If they join together against us, they could kill my whole family.”

## Genesis 34:31

The brothers answered, “Should Shechem have treated our sister like a prostitute?”

**Reading help**

A prostitute is someone who has sex for payment.

The brothers were speaking about how Shechem treated Dinah.

He had forced her to have sex.
