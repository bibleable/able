# Genesis 11 — Babel and the family of Abram

**About this chapter**

People build a city together.

Later the family record follows Shem’s descendants to Abram.

## The people build a city

## Genesis 11:1

Everyone on earth spoke the same language.

They used the same words.

## Genesis 11:2

People traveled east and found flat land in Shinar.

They settled there.

## Genesis 11:3

They said to each other, “Let us make bricks.

Let us bake them until they are hard.”

They built with bricks in place of stones.

They used sticky tar to hold the bricks together.

## Genesis 11:4

They said, “Let us build a city for ourselves.

Let us build a tower that reaches to the sky.

Then people will know our name.

We will stay together instead of scattering across the earth.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did the people want to build the tower?

## The people scatter

## Genesis 11:5

The LORD came down to see the city and tower that the people were building.

## Genesis 11:6

The LORD said, “They are one people with one language.

This is what they have begun to do.

Nothing they plan will be beyond their reach.

## Genesis 11:7

“Let us go down and mix up their language.

Then they will no longer understand one another.”

## Genesis 11:8

The LORD scattered the people from there across the earth.

They stopped building the city.

## Genesis 11:9

That is why the city is called Babel.

There the LORD mixed up the language of the whole world.

From there he scattered the people across the earth.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the people stop building?

## From Shem to Eber

## Genesis 11:10

Here is the family record of Shem.

Two years after the flood, Shem became the father of Arphaxad.

Shem was 100 years old.

## Genesis 11:11

After Arphaxad was born, Shem lived another 500 years.

Shem had other sons and daughters.

## Genesis 11:12

Arphaxad was 35 when he became the father of Shelah.

## Genesis 11:13

After Shelah was born, Arphaxad lived another 403 years.

Arphaxad had other sons and daughters.

## Genesis 11:14

Shelah was 30 when he became the father of Eber.

## Genesis 11:15

After Eber was born, Shelah lived another 403 years.

Shelah had other sons and daughters.

Who was Eber’s father?

## Genesis 11:16

## Genesis 11:17

After Peleg was born, Eber lived another 430 years.

Eber had other sons and daughters.

Peleg was 30 when he became the father of Reu.

## Genesis 11:19

After Reu was born, Peleg lived another 209 years.

Peleg had other sons and daughters.

## Genesis 11:20

Reu was 32 when he became the father of Serug.

## Genesis 11:21

After Serug was born, Reu lived another 207 years.

Reu had other sons and daughters.

## From Serug to Terah

## Genesis 11:22

Serug was 30 when he became the father of Nahor.

## Genesis 11:23

After Nahor was born, Serug lived another 200 years.

Serug had other sons and daughters.

## Genesis 11:24

Nahor was 29 when he became the father of Terah.

## Genesis 11:25

After Terah was born, Nahor lived another 119 years.

Nahor had other sons and daughters.

## Genesis 11:26

When Terah was 70, he became the father of Abram, Nahor and Haran.

**Pause and answer**

What were the names of Terah’s sons?

## Terah takes his family from Ur

## Genesis 11:27

Here is Terah’s family record.

Terah’s sons were Abram, Nahor and Haran.

Haran became the father of Lot.

## Genesis 11:28

Haran died while his father Terah was still alive.

Haran died in his homeland, in Ur of the Chaldeans.

## Genesis 11:29

Abram married Sarai.

Nahor married Milcah.

Haran was the father of Milcah and Iscah.

**Reading help**

Haran is the name of Lot’s father. In the next part of the story, Haran is also the name of a place.

## Genesis 11:30

Sarai could not have children.

She had no children.

## Genesis 11:31

Terah took his son Abram and Abram’s wife Sarai with him.

He also took his grandson Lot, the son of Haran.

They left Ur of the Chaldeans to go to Canaan.

On the way they reached a place called Haran and settled there.

Terah lived 205 years.
