# Genesis 16 — The LORD hears Hagar

**About this chapter**

Hagar leaves Sarai’s home.

At a spring in the desert, an angel calls Hagar by name.

## Sarai asks Abram for a family

## Genesis 16:1

Abram’s wife Sarai had no children.

An Egyptian woman named Hagar was her servant.

## Genesis 16:2

Sarai said to Abram, “The LORD has kept me from having children.

Please sleep with my servant.

Perhaps I can have a family through her.”

Abram agreed to Sarai’s request.

## Genesis 16:3

Abram had lived in Canaan for ten years.

Then Sarai gave Hagar, her Egyptian servant, to Abram as his wife.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Hagar come from originally?

## Hagar runs away

## Genesis 16:4

Abram slept with Hagar, and Hagar became pregnant.

When Hagar knew she was pregnant, she looked down on Sarai.

## Genesis 16:5

Sarai told Abram, “I blame you for this wrong.

I gave you my servant.

Since Hagar learned she was pregnant, she has looked down on me.

May the LORD judge between you and me.”

## Genesis 16:6

Abram answered, “Hagar is your servant.

Do what you want with her.”

Sarai treated Hagar harshly, and Hagar ran away.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Hagar run away?

## The angel finds Hagar

## Genesis 16:7

The angel of the LORD found Hagar by a spring in the desert.

The spring was beside the road to Shur.

## Genesis 16:8

The angel said, “Hagar, servant of Sarai, where have you come from?

Where are you going?”

Hagar answered, “I am running away from Sarai, my mistress.”

## Genesis 16:9

The angel of the LORD told Hagar, “Go back to Sarai.

Accept her authority over you.”

## Genesis 16:10

Then the angel said, “I will give you many descendants.

No one will be able to count them.”

**Pause and answer**

What promise did the angel make about Hagar’s descendants?

## A name for Hagar’s child

## Genesis 16:11

The angel told Hagar, “You are pregnant.

You will have a son.

Name him Ishmael because the LORD has heard your cry of pain.

## Genesis 16:12

“He will be like a wild donkey.

He will be against everyone, and everyone will be against him.

He will live at odds with all his brothers.”

**Reading help**

The angel uses a word picture. He compares Ishmael to a wild donkey and describes the conflict he will face.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the angel tell Hagar to name her son Ishmael?

## The God who sees Hagar

## Genesis 16:13

Hagar gave a name to the LORD who spoke to her.

She called him “the God who sees me.”

She said, “Here I have seen the one who sees me!”

## Genesis 16:14

That is why people called the well Beer-lahai-roi.

It lies between Kadesh and Bered.

**Reading help**

| At the spring | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| The LORD heard Hagar. | The angel told her to name her son Ishmael. |
| The LORD saw Hagar. | Hagar called him the God who sees her. |

## Genesis 16:15

Hagar gave birth to Abram’s son.

Abram named the boy Ishmael.

## Genesis 16:16

Abram was eighty-six years old when Hagar gave birth to Ishmael.

**Pause and answer**

What name did Hagar give the LORD?
