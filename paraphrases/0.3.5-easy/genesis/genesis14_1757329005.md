# Genesis 14 — Abram brings Lot home

**About this chapter**

Four kings carry Lot away from Sodom.

Someone escapes and brings the news to Abram.

## Four kings face five

## Genesis 14:1

Four kings joined together.

Amraphel ruled Shinar, and Arioch ruled Ellasar.

Chedorlaomer ruled Elam, and Tidal ruled Goiim.

## Genesis 14:2

They went to war against five kings.

Bera ruled Sodom, and Birsha ruled Gomorrah.

Shinab ruled Admah, and Shemeber ruled Zeboiim.

The fifth king ruled Bela, also called Zoar.

## Genesis 14:3

These five kings joined forces in the Valley of Siddim.

This is the place called the Salt Sea.

## Genesis 14:4

For twelve years the five kings had served Chedorlaomer.

In the thirteenth year they turned against him.

## Chedorlaomer and his allies advance

## Genesis 14:5

In the fourteenth year Chedorlaomer and his allies went out to fight.

They defeated the Rephaites at Ashteroth-karnaim and the Zuzites at Ham.

They defeated the Emites at Shaveh-kiriathaim.

## Genesis 14:6

They defeated the Horites around Mount Seir.

They went as far as El-paran, near the desert.

## Genesis 14:7

Then they turned back and attacked En-mishpat, also called Kadesh.

They took all the land of the Amalekites.

They also defeated the Amorites who lived at Hazazon-tamar.

## Genesis 14:8

The kings of Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, Zeboiim and Bela marched out.

Bela was also called Zoar.

The five kings got ready to fight in the Valley of Siddim.

## Genesis 14:9

They faced Chedorlaomer of Elam and Tidal of Goiim.

Amraphel of Shinar and Arioch of Ellasar fought alongside Chedorlaomer.

Four kings faced five.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the five kings get ready to fight?

## The kings take Lot away

## Genesis 14:10

The Valley of Siddim was full of pits of sticky tar.

The kings of Sodom and Gomorrah fled.

Some men fell into the pits.

The survivors escaped to the hills.

## Genesis 14:11

The four kings took everything from Sodom and Gomorrah.

They took all the food too, then left.

## Genesis 14:12

Abram’s nephew Lot lived in Sodom.

The kings took Lot away with everything he owned.

**Pause and answer**

Why was Lot in Sodom?

## Abram hears the news

## Genesis 14:13

One man escaped and told Abram the Hebrew what had happened.

Abram lived near the oak trees of Mamre the Amorite.

Mamre and his brothers Eshcol and Aner had an agreement to stand with Abram.

## Genesis 14:14

Abram heard that Lot was a captive.

He called together 318 trained men born in his household.

They went after the kings as far as Dan.

## Genesis 14:15

That night Abram divided his men into groups.

They defeated Chedorlaomer’s army and chased it to Hobah, north of Damascus.

## Genesis 14:16

Abram brought back all the stolen goods.

He brought back Lot and Lot’s belongings.

He also brought back the women and the other people.

**Pause and answer**

Who came back with Abram besides Lot?

## Melchizedek blesses Abram

## Genesis 14:17

Abram returned after defeating Chedorlaomer and his allies.

The king of Sodom met Abram in the Valley of Shaveh.

People also called it the King’s Valley.

## Genesis 14:18

Melchizedek, king of Salem, brought out bread and wine.

Melchizedek was a priest of God Most High.

## Genesis 14:19

Melchizedek blessed Abram.

He said, “May God Most High bless Abram.

God made heaven and earth.

## Genesis 14:20

“Praise God Most High!

He gave you victory over your enemies.”

Abram gave Melchizedek a tenth of everything.

## Abram keeps his promise

## Genesis 14:21

The king of Sodom told Abram, “Give me the people.

Keep the goods for yourself.”

## Genesis 14:22

Abram answered, “I raised my hand and made a promise to the LORD.

He is God Most High, who made heaven and earth.

## Genesis 14:23

“I will take nothing of yours, not even a thread or a sandal strap.

Then you can never say you made Abram rich.

## Genesis 14:24

“Allow for the food my men have already eaten.

Give Aner, Eshcol and Mamre their share.

They went with me, and they may keep what belongs to them.”
