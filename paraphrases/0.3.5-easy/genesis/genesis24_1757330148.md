# Genesis 24 — Rebekah agrees to go with Abraham’s servant

**About this chapter**

Abraham sends a servant to find a wife for Isaac.

At a well, the servant meets Rebekah.

## Abraham asks his servant to promise

## Genesis 24:1

Abraham was now very old.

The LORD had blessed him in every way.

## Genesis 24:2

Abraham’s chief servant looked after everything Abraham owned.

Abraham told him, “Put your hand under my thigh.

## Genesis 24:3

Make me a promise before the LORD.

He is the God of heaven and earth.

I live among the Canaanites.

Do not choose a Canaanite wife for my son.

## Genesis 24:4

Go back to my own country.

Find a wife for Isaac among my relatives.”

## Genesis 24:5

The servant asked, “What if she refuses to come here?

Should I take Isaac back to your old country?”

## Genesis 24:6

Abraham answered, “Never take Isaac back there.

## Genesis 24:7

The LORD is the God of heaven.

He brought me away from my father’s home and country.

He promised to give this land to my descendants.

The LORD will send his angel ahead of you.

Then you can find my son a wife there.

## Genesis 24:8

If she refuses to come, you are free from your promise.

But do not take Isaac back there.”

## Genesis 24:9

The servant put his hand under Abraham’s thigh.

That was how he made his solemn promise to Abraham.

## The servant prays at a well

## Genesis 24:10

The servant took ten of Abraham’s camels.

He brought many good things from Abraham’s home.

He traveled to Nahor’s town in Aram-naharaim.

## Genesis 24:11

Toward evening, the servant stopped outside the town.

He made the camels kneel by the well.

This was when the women came to get water.

## Genesis 24:12

The servant prayed, “LORD, God of my master Abraham, help me today.

Help me find a wife for Isaac.

Show kindness to Abraham.

## Genesis 24:13

Here I stand beside the spring.

The young women from town are coming for water.

## Genesis 24:14

I will ask one to lower her jar for me.

May she say, ‘Drink. I will water your camels too.’

Let her be the one you chose for your servant Isaac.

Then I will know you have shown Abraham kindness.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the servant hope the woman would offer?

## Rebekah brings water for the camels

## Genesis 24:15

Before he finished praying, Rebekah came with a jar on her shoulder.

Her father was Bethuel. His parents were Milcah and Nahor.

Nahor was Abraham’s brother.

![A clay jar with a narrow neck and a wide body.](../pictures/clay-jar.svg)

Rebekah carried a jar to the spring to draw water.

## Genesis 24:16

Rebekah was beautiful. She had never had sex with anyone.

She went down to the spring and filled her jar.

Then she came back up.

## Genesis 24:17

The servant ran to meet her.

“Please give me a little water from your jar,” he said.

## Genesis 24:18

“Drink, sir,” Rebekah answered.

She quickly lowered the jar and gave him a drink.

## Genesis 24:19

Then Rebekah said, “I will get water for your camels too.

I will keep going until they have had enough.”

## Genesis 24:20

Rebekah quickly poured the water into the animals’ drinking trough.

She ran back to the well for more.

She brought water for all the camels.

## Genesis 24:21

The servant watched in silence.

Had the LORD helped him find Isaac’s wife?

**Pause and answer**

How did Rebekah help the camels?

## The servant learns who Rebekah is

## Genesis 24:22

When the camels finished drinking, the servant took out a gold ring.

It weighed half a shekel.

He also took out two gold bracelets for Rebekah.

Together they weighed ten shekels.

**Reading help**

A shekel was a measure of weight.

## Genesis 24:23

The servant asked, “Who is your father?

Does your father have room for us tonight?”

## Genesis 24:24

Rebekah answered, “My father is Bethuel.

His parents are Milcah and Nahor.”

## Genesis 24:25

She added, “We have plenty of straw and animal feed.

We also have room for you tonight.”

## Genesis 24:26

The servant bowed down and worshiped the LORD.

## Genesis 24:27

“Praise the LORD, the God of my master Abraham!” he said.

“You have stayed faithful to Abraham and shown him kindness.

You led me straight to his relatives.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did the servant praise God?

## Laban welcomes the visitors

## Genesis 24:28

Rebekah ran home.

She told her mother’s household what had happened.

## Genesis 24:29

Rebekah had a brother named Laban.

Laban hurried out to the servant at the spring.

## Genesis 24:30

Laban had seen the ring and Rebekah’s bracelets.

He had heard what the servant said to her.

Laban found the servant standing beside the camels.

## Genesis 24:31

Laban said, “The LORD has blessed you. Come in!

Why stand out here?

I have made room for you and the camels.”

## Genesis 24:32

The servant went to the house.

People took the loads off the camels.

They brought straw and feed for the animals.

They brought water for the visitors to wash their feet.

## Genesis 24:33

They brought the servant a meal.

He said, “First I must tell you why I came.”

Laban answered, “Please tell us.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the servant want to do before eating?

## The servant explains his promise

## Genesis 24:34

The servant began, “I work for Abraham.

## Genesis 24:35

The LORD has blessed my master and made him rich.

Abraham has sheep, cattle, camels, and donkeys.

He has silver and gold, and male and female servants.

## Genesis 24:36

Sarah, Abraham’s wife, had a son in her old age.

Abraham has given his son everything he owns.

## Genesis 24:37

Abraham made me promise him something.

I must not find his son a wife among the Canaanites where he lives.

## Genesis 24:38

I must visit his father’s family instead.

I must find a wife among Abraham’s relatives.

## Genesis 24:39

I asked Abraham, ‘What if she will not come with me?’

## Genesis 24:40

Abraham told me, ‘I have followed the LORD.

He will send his angel with you.

He will help you find my son a wife among my father’s family.

## Genesis 24:41

If my relatives refuse when you visit them, you are free from your promise.’”

**Pause and answer**

Where did Abraham send his servant to find Isaac a wife?

## The servant tells the family about Rebekah

## Genesis 24:42

The servant continued, “Today at the spring I prayed.

I asked the LORD, the God of Abraham, to help me.

## Genesis 24:43

I stood by the spring and waited for a young woman.

I would ask for a little water from her jar.

## Genesis 24:44

What if she offered water for my camels too?

I asked the LORD to show me Isaac’s future wife that way.

## Genesis 24:45

Before I finished praying silently, Rebekah came.

She carried a jar on her shoulder.

She went down to the spring for water.

I asked her for a drink.

## Genesis 24:46

She quickly lowered her jar.

‘Drink,’ she said. ‘I will water your camels too.’

I drank, and she gave my camels water.

## Genesis 24:47

I asked who her father was.

She said, ‘Bethuel. His parents are Nahor and Milcah.’

Then I put the ring on her nose.

I put the bracelets on her wrists.

## Genesis 24:48

I bowed down and worshiped the LORD, Abraham’s God.

I praised him for leading me along the right road.

I had found Abraham’s brother’s granddaughter for Isaac.

## Genesis 24:49

Will you show Abraham kindness and keep faith with him?

Please tell me your answer.

If you refuse, I will know to go elsewhere.”

**Pause and answer**

Whose granddaughter was Rebekah?

## Rebekah’s family answers

## Genesis 24:50

Laban and Bethuel answered, “This comes from the LORD.

The decision is his.

## Genesis 24:51

Here is Rebekah. Take her with you.

Let her marry Isaac, as the LORD has said.”

## Genesis 24:52

When Abraham’s servant heard this, he bowed to the ground before the LORD.

## Genesis 24:53

The servant gave Rebekah silver and gold jewelry and clothes.

He gave valuable gifts to her brother and mother too.

## Genesis 24:54

The visitors ate and drank and stayed the night.

In the morning the servant said, “Let me return to Abraham.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the servant do when he heard the family’s answer?

## Rebekah gives her own answer

## Genesis 24:55

Rebekah’s brother and mother said, “Let her stay about ten days.

Then she can go.”

## Genesis 24:56

The servant answered, “Please let me leave without delay.

The LORD has helped me do what I came for.

Let me go back to my master.”

## Genesis 24:57

The family said, “We will ask Rebekah what she thinks.”

## Genesis 24:58

They called Rebekah. “Will you go with this man?” they asked.

“I will go,” Rebekah answered.

## Genesis 24:59

So the family sent Rebekah and her nurse on their way.

They went with Abraham’s servant and his men.

## Genesis 24:60

The family blessed Rebekah. “Our sister,” they said,

“may thousands upon thousands of people come from your family.

May they take control of their enemies’ cities.”

## Genesis 24:61

Rebekah and her servant girls got ready.

They climbed onto the camels and followed Abraham’s servant.

So he set out with Rebekah.

**Pause and answer**

What did Rebekah tell her family?

## Isaac meets Rebekah

## Genesis 24:62

Isaac lived in the Negev.

He had just come back from Beer-lahai-roi.

## Genesis 24:63

Early one evening Isaac went into a field to think quietly.

He looked up and saw camels coming.

## Genesis 24:64

Rebekah looked up and saw Isaac.

She got down from her camel.

## Genesis 24:65

“Who is that man coming to meet us?” she asked.

The servant answered, “He is my master.”

Rebekah covered herself with her veil, a piece of cloth.

## Genesis 24:66

The servant told Isaac everything he had done.

## Genesis 24:67

Isaac took Rebekah into his mother Sarah’s tent.

He married Rebekah and loved her.

After his mother’s death, Isaac found comfort with Rebekah.
