# Genesis 29 — Jacob meets Rachel, and Leah names her sons

**About this chapter**

Jacob meets Rachel at a well.

Later, he marries her older sister Leah and then Rachel.

The LORD sees how much Leah is hurting.

## Jacob comes to a well

## Genesis 29:1

Jacob went on with his journey.

He reached the land of the people of the east.

## Genesis 29:2

Jacob saw a well in a field.

Three flocks of sheep lay beside it.

The sheep drank water from this well.

A large stone covered the opening.

## Genesis 29:3

The shepherds usually waited for all the flocks to arrive.

Then they rolled the stone away and watered the sheep.

Afterward, they rolled the stone back over the well.

## Genesis 29:4

Jacob asked the shepherds, “Friends, where are you from?”

“We come from Haran,” they answered.

## Genesis 29:5

Jacob asked, “Do you know Laban, Nahor’s grandson?”

“Yes, we know him,” they said.

## Genesis 29:6

“Is Laban well?” Jacob asked.

“Yes,” they said. “Here comes his daughter Rachel with his sheep.”

## Rachel brings her father’s sheep

## Genesis 29:7

Jacob said, “There is plenty of daylight left.

It is too early to gather the animals for the night.

Give the sheep water. Then take them back to eat grass.”

## Genesis 29:8

The shepherds answered, “We have to wait for all the flocks.

Then we can roll the stone away and give the sheep water.”

## Genesis 29:9

While Jacob was speaking, Rachel arrived with her father’s sheep.

She was their shepherd.

## Genesis 29:10

Jacob saw Rachel and the sheep that belonged to Laban.

Laban was the brother of Jacob’s mother.

Jacob rolled the stone away and watered his uncle’s sheep.

## Genesis 29:11

Then Jacob kissed Rachel and began to cry aloud.

## Genesis 29:12

Jacob told Rachel, “I am Rebekah’s son.

Your father and I are relatives.”

Rachel ran to tell her father.

**Pause and answer**

How did Jacob help Rachel at the well?

## Jacob offers to work for Laban

## Genesis 29:13

Laban heard that his sister’s son had come.

He ran to Jacob, hugged him, and kissed him.

Laban took Jacob home. Jacob told him everything that had happened.

## Genesis 29:14

Laban said, “You really are my own flesh and blood!”

Jacob stayed with Laban for a month.

**Reading help**

Laban meant, “You are part of my family.”

## Genesis 29:15

Then Laban said, “You are my relative.

Why should you work for me without pay?

Tell me what I should give you for your work.”

## Genesis 29:16

Laban had two daughters. Leah was the older daughter.

Rachel was the younger daughter.

## Genesis 29:17

Leah had weak eyes.

Rachel had a lovely figure and was beautiful.

## Genesis 29:18

Jacob loved Rachel. He told Laban, “I will work for you for seven years.

Then let me marry your younger daughter Rachel.”

## Genesis 29:19

Laban said, “I would rather you marry her than another man.

Stay here with me.”

## Genesis 29:20

Jacob worked seven years for Rachel.

He loved her so much that the years seemed like days.

**Pause and answer**

How long did Jacob offer to work to marry Rachel?

## Jacob discovers that he has married Leah

## Genesis 29:21

Jacob said to Laban, “I have finished my years of work.

Let me marry Rachel now and sleep with her.”

## Genesis 29:22

Laban invited all the men of the place.

He prepared a wedding feast.

## Genesis 29:23

That evening Laban brought his daughter Leah to Jacob.

Jacob had sex with Leah.

## Genesis 29:24

Laban gave Leah a woman named Zilpah to serve her.

## Genesis 29:25

In the morning Jacob saw that his wife was Leah!

He asked Laban, “What have you done to me?

I worked for you so I could marry Rachel.

Why did you trick me?”

## Genesis 29:26

Laban answered, “Here the older daughter marries first.

That is our custom.

## Genesis 29:27

Finish this week of wedding celebrations.

Then you can marry Rachel too.

In return, work for me another seven years.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jacob discover the morning after the feast?

## Jacob marries Rachel

## Genesis 29:28

Jacob agreed. He finished the week of celebrations.

Then Laban gave Rachel to Jacob as his wife.

## Genesis 29:29

Laban gave Rachel a woman named Bilhah to serve her.

## Genesis 29:30

Jacob had sex with Rachel too.

He loved Rachel more than Leah.

Jacob worked another seven years for Laban.

**Pause and answer**

When did Jacob marry Rachel?

## Leah gives birth to four sons

## Genesis 29:31

The LORD saw that Leah was unloved.

He made it possible for Leah to have children.

Rachel could not have children at that time.

## Genesis 29:32

Leah became pregnant and gave birth to a son.

She named him Reuben.

She said, “The LORD has seen how I suffer.

Surely my husband will love me now.”

## Genesis 29:33

Leah became pregnant again and had another son.

She said, “The LORD heard that I am unloved.

That is why he gave me this son too.”

She named him Simeon.

## Genesis 29:34

Leah became pregnant and had a third son.

She said, “Now my husband will feel close to me.

I have given him three sons.”

The boy’s name was Levi.

## Genesis 29:35

Leah became pregnant and had another son.

“This time I will praise the LORD,” she said.

She named him Judah.

Then Leah stopped having children.
