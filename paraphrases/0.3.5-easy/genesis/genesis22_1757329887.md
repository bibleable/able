# Genesis 22 — God provides a ram for Abraham

**About this chapter**

God tests Abraham.

Abraham and his son Isaac go to a mountain together.

## God calls Abraham

## Genesis 22:1

Some time later, God tested Abraham.

God called, “Abraham!”

Abraham answered, “Here I am.”

## Genesis 22:2

God said, “Take Isaac, your only son, whom you love.

Go to the land of Moriah.

I will show you a mountain there.

Give Isaac to me there as a burnt offering.”

**Reading help**

A burnt offering was a gift to God.

The person giving it burned the whole gift on an altar.

An altar is a place for giving offerings to God.

## Genesis 22:3

Abraham got up early the next morning.

He put a saddle on his donkey.

He split wood for the burnt offering.

Then Abraham left for the place God had named.

Isaac and two servants went with him.

## Genesis 22:4

On the third day, Abraham looked up.

He could see the place far away.

## Genesis 22:5

Abraham told his servants, “Stay here with the donkey.

Isaac and I will go over there to worship God.

Then we will come back to you.”

**Pause and answer**

## Isaac asks about the lamb

## Genesis 22:6

Abraham gave Isaac the wood to carry.

Abraham carried the fire and the knife for the offering.

Father and son walked on together.

## Genesis 22:7

Isaac called, “Father!”

Abraham answered, “Here I am, my son.”

Isaac said, “We have the fire and the wood.

But where is the lamb for the burnt offering?”

## Genesis 22:8

Abraham answered, “God himself will give us the lamb for the offering, my son.”

They kept walking together.

**Pause and answer**

What did Isaac ask his father about?

## The angel stops Abraham

## Genesis 22:9

They reached the place God had shown Abraham.

Abraham built an altar and put the wood on it.

He tied Isaac and laid him on the wood.

## Genesis 22:10

Abraham reached for the knife.

He took it to kill his son.

## Genesis 22:11

Just then the angel of the LORD called from heaven.

“Abraham! Abraham!”

Abraham answered, “Here I am.”

## Genesis 22:12

The angel said, “Do not hurt the boy.

Do nothing to him.

Now I know that you fear God.

You were willing to give me your only son.”

**Reading help**

Here, to fear God means to treat God with deep respect.

## Genesis 22:13

Abraham looked up. Behind him he saw a ram.

A ram is a male sheep.

Its horns had caught in thick bushes.

Abraham took the ram and gave it as a burnt offering in place of Isaac.

![Isaac is spared. Abraham gives the ram as the offering.](../pictures/ram-in-isaacs-place.svg)

The angel stopped Abraham before he hurt Isaac.

## Genesis 22:14

Abraham named the place “The LORD Will Provide.”

People still say, “On the LORD’s mountain, the LORD will give what is needed.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Abraham offer in Isaac’s place?

## The LORD makes Abraham a promise

## Genesis 22:15

The angel of the LORD called from heaven again.

## Genesis 22:16

The angel gave Abraham this message from the LORD.

“I make this promise in my own name.

You obeyed me. You were willing to give me your only son.

## Genesis 22:17

So I will certainly bless you.

Your family will grow through many generations.

Picture the stars in the sky and the grains of sand on the shore.

That is how many descendants you will have.

Your descendants will take control of their enemies’ cities.

## Genesis 22:18

Through your family I will bless every nation on earth.

I will do this because you obeyed me.”

## Genesis 22:19

Abraham returned to his servants.

Together they went back to Beersheba.

Abraham made his home there.

**Pause and answer**

Who will receive a blessing through Abraham’s family?

## Abraham hears news of his brother’s family

## Genesis 22:20

Some time later, Abraham heard news about his brother Nahor.

Milcah had given birth to sons in Nahor’s family.

## Genesis 22:21

Uz was their first son. Buz was his brother.

Another son was Kemuel. Kemuel became Aram’s father.

## Genesis 22:22

Their other sons were Chesed, Hazo, Pildash, Jidlaph, and Bethuel.

## Genesis 22:23

Bethuel became Rebekah’s father.

In all, Milcah and Nahor had eight sons.

## Genesis 22:24

Nahor also had a concubine named Reumah.

A concubine was a partner with fewer rights than a wife.

Reumah’s children were Tebah, Gaham, Tahash, and Maacah.
