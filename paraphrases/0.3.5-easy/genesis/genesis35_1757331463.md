# Genesis 35 — Jacob returns to Bethel

**About this chapter**

Jacob returns to the place where God spoke to him.

Later, Rachel gives birth on the road to Bethlehem.

## Jacob prepares to go to Bethel

## Genesis 35:1

God told Jacob, “Go up to Bethel and live there.

Build an altar there for me.

I appeared to you there when you ran from Esau.”

## Genesis 35:2

Jacob spoke to his family and everyone with them.

“Get rid of the foreign gods you have with you.

Make yourselves clean and change your clothes.

## Genesis 35:3

Then we will go to Bethel.

I will build an altar to God there.

God answered me when I was in trouble.

He has been with me everywhere I have gone.”

## Genesis 35:4

They gave Jacob all the idols they had worshiped.

They also gave him their earrings.

Jacob buried everything under the oak tree near Shechem.

## Genesis 35:5

As they left, God filled the nearby towns with fear.

So nobody chased Jacob’s sons.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Jacob want to build an altar at Bethel?

## Jacob builds the altar

## Genesis 35:6

Jacob and everyone with him came to Luz in Canaan.

Luz was another name for Bethel.

## Genesis 35:7

Jacob built an altar there. He called the place El-bethel.

Here God had appeared to him as he fled from Esau.

**Reading help**

An altar is a place to give offerings to God.

El-bethel means “God of Bethel.”

## Genesis 35:8

Deborah died. She was the nurse who had cared for Rebekah.

The family buried Deborah under the oak tree below Bethel.

Jacob named the tree Allon-bacuth, which means “oak of weeping.”

**Pause and answer**

Who was Deborah?

## God blesses Jacob

## Genesis 35:9

After Jacob returned from Paddan-aram, God appeared to him again.

God blessed him.

## Genesis 35:10

God said, “Your name has been Jacob.

From now on your name will be Israel.”

So God named him Israel.

![Jacob receives a new name, Israel.](../pictures/jacob-named-israel.svg)

God calls Jacob by his new name.

## Genesis 35:11

God said, “I am God Almighty.

Have children, and let your family grow.

Whole nations will come from your family.

Some of your descendants will be kings.

## Genesis 35:12

I gave this land to Abraham and Isaac.

I will give it to you and your descendants too.”

## Genesis 35:13

Then God went up from the place where they had spoken.

## Genesis 35:14

Jacob stood a stone upright where God had spoken to him.

He poured a drink on it as a gift to God.

Then he poured oil on the stone.

## Genesis 35:15

Jacob called the place Bethel. God had spoken to him there.

**Pause and answer**

What land did God promise to Jacob and his descendants?

## Rachel gives birth on the road

## Genesis 35:16

Jacob’s family left Bethel.

They were still some distance from a place called Ephrath.

Rachel began to give birth. The birth was difficult.

## Genesis 35:17

A midwife was there to help Rachel give birth.

She said, “Do not be afraid. You are having another son.”

## Genesis 35:18

As Rachel was dying, she named her son Ben-oni.

His father named him Benjamin.

## Genesis 35:19

Rachel died. The family buried her beside the road to Ephrath.

Another name for Ephrath is Bethlehem.

## Genesis 35:20

Jacob stood a stone upright on Rachel’s grave.

The stone still marked her grave when this account was written.

**Pause and answer**

What name did Jacob give Rachel’s baby?

## Jacob’s twelve sons

## Genesis 35:21

Israel, also called Jacob, traveled on.

He set up his tent beyond the Tower of Eder.

## Genesis 35:22

While they lived there, Reuben had sex with Bilhah.

Bilhah was his father’s concubine. Jacob heard what Reuben had done.

Jacob had twelve sons.

**Reading help**

A concubine was a partner with fewer rights than a wife.

## Genesis 35:23

Leah’s sons were Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun.

Reuben was Jacob’s oldest son.

## Genesis 35:24

Rachel’s sons were Joseph and Benjamin.

## Genesis 35:25

Bilhah, Rachel’s servant, had two sons. They were Dan and Naphtali.

## Genesis 35:26

Zilpah, Leah’s servant, had two sons. They were Gad and Asher.

These are the sons of Jacob, born to him in Paddan-aram.

**Reading help**

This list groups the family together. Earlier in this chapter, Rachel gives birth to Benjamin on the road to Ephrath.

| Mother | Her sons |
| --- | --- |
| Leah | Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun |
| Rachel | Joseph, Benjamin |
| Bilhah | Dan, Naphtali |
| Zilpah | Gad, Asher |

**Pause and answer**

Who were Benjamin’s parents?

## Jacob and Esau bury Isaac

## Genesis 35:27

Jacob came home to his father Isaac at Mamre.

Mamre was near Kiriath-arba, also called Hebron.

Both Abraham and Isaac had lived there.

## Genesis 35:28

Isaac lived for 180 years.

## Genesis 35:29

Isaac took his last breath after a long life.

He joined his people who had died before him.

His sons Esau and Jacob buried him.
