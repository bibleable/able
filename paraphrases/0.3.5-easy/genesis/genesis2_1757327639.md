# Genesis 2 — God plants a garden in Eden

**About this chapter**

God rests on the seventh day.

Then we hear how the man and the woman came to live in Eden.

## God rests

## Genesis 2:1

God had finished making the heavens and the earth.

Everything in them was complete.

## Genesis 2:2

By the seventh day, God had finished his work.

On that day he rested from all his work.

## Genesis 2:3

God blessed the seventh day and made it holy.

He set it apart because on that day he rested from creating.

**Pause and answer**

Why did God set apart the seventh day?

## The man comes to life

## Genesis 2:4

Here is the account of the heavens and the earth when the LORD God made them.

## Genesis 2:5

No bushes had appeared in the fields.

No field plants had begun to grow.

The LORD God had not yet sent rain.

There was no one to work the ground.

## Genesis 2:6

Water came up from springs in the earth.

It watered the whole surface of the ground.

## Genesis 2:7

The LORD God shaped a man from the dust of the ground.

God breathed life into the man’s nose.

The man became a living person.

**Pause and answer**

What did God use to shape the man?

## The garden and its rivers

## Genesis 2:8

The LORD God planted a garden in Eden, in the east.

He put the man he had made there.

## Genesis 2:9

The LORD God made trees grow from the ground.

They were beautiful and gave good food.

In the middle of the garden stood the tree of life.

The tree of the knowledge of good and evil stood there too.

## Genesis 2:10

A river flowed out of Eden and watered the garden.

From there it divided into four rivers.

## Genesis 2:11

The first river is the Pishon.

It winds through the whole land of Havilah.

That land has gold.

## Genesis 2:12

Its gold is pure.

Havilah also has bdellium and onyx.

**Reading help**

Bdellium is a substance that comes from trees. Onyx is a precious stone.

## Genesis 2:13

The second river is the Gihon.

It winds through the whole land of Cush.

## Genesis 2:14

The third river is the Tigris.

It flows east of Assyria.

The fourth river is the Euphrates.

**Reading help**

**Follow the water**

Eden → Garden → Four rivers

A river branch is a stream that flows away from the main river.

**Pause and answer**

What watered the garden?

## God gives the man work and a command

## Genesis 2:15

The LORD God put the man in the garden of Eden.

The man was to work the garden and care for it.

## Genesis 2:16

The LORD God told him, “You are free to eat from the trees in the garden.

## Genesis 2:17

“But you must not eat from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.

On the day you eat from it, you will surely die.”

**Pause and answer**

Which tree did God tell the man not to eat from?

## The man names the animals

## Genesis 2:18

The LORD God said, “It is not good for the man to be alone.

I will make a helper who is right for him.”

## Genesis 2:19

The LORD God formed the wild animals and birds from the ground.

He brought them to the man to see what he would call them.

Each creature kept the name the man gave it.

## Genesis 2:20

The man named the farm animals, birds and wild animals.

Among them Adam found no helper who was right for him.

**Pause and answer**

Who gave the animals their names?

## God makes the woman

## Genesis 2:21

The LORD God made the man fall deeply asleep.

While he slept, God took one of his ribs.

God closed the flesh where the rib had been.

## Genesis 2:22

The LORD God made a woman from that rib.

He brought her to the man.

## Genesis 2:23

The man said, “At last!

Her bones come from my bones.

Her flesh comes from my flesh.

I will call her ‘woman’ because she came from man.”

## Genesis 2:24

That is why a man leaves his father and mother.

He joins his life to his wife’s life.

Together they become one flesh.

**Reading help**

“One flesh” describes how closely husband and wife are joined.

## Genesis 2:25

The man and his wife were both naked.

Neither felt ashamed.

**Pause and answer**

What did God use to make the woman?
