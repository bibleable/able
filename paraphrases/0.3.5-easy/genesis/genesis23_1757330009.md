# Genesis 23 — Abraham buys a place to bury Sarah

**About this chapter**

Sarah dies in Canaan.

Abraham asks the people there for a place to bury her.

## Abraham mourns for Sarah

## Genesis 23:1

Sarah lived to be 127 years old.

## Genesis 23:2

Sarah died in Kiriath-arba, also called Hebron.

This town was in Canaan.

Abraham went to mourn for Sarah. He wept for her.

## Genesis 23:3

Then Abraham got up from beside Sarah’s body.

He spoke to the people there, the Hittites.

## Genesis 23:4

“I come from another land. I live among you as a stranger.

Please let me have a burial place here.

Then I can bury my wife.”

## Genesis 23:5

The Hittites answered Abraham.

## Genesis 23:6

“Listen, sir. God has chosen you from among us.

Choose the best of our tombs to bury your wife.

Any of us would let you use his tomb.”

**Reading help**

A tomb is a place to bury someone who has died.

A cave can serve as a tomb.

**Pause and answer**

What did Abraham ask the Hittites for?

## Abraham asks to buy a cave

## Genesis 23:7

Abraham stood up and bowed before the Hittites.

## Genesis 23:8

Abraham said, “If you agree that I may bury my wife here, please hear me.

Speak to Ephron for me. He is Zohar’s son.

## Genesis 23:9

Ask Ephron to sell me the cave of Machpelah.

It belongs to him. It is at the end of his field.

Let me pay him the full price while you watch.

Then I will have a place to bury my wife.”

## Genesis 23:10

Ephron was sitting among the other Hittites.

Ephron answered Abraham where everyone could hear.

The Hittites had gathered at the city gate.

## Genesis 23:11

Ephron said, “No, sir. Please listen.

I give you the field and its cave.

My people here have heard me offer it to you.

Bury your wife there.”

**Pause and answer**

Who owned the cave Abraham wanted to buy?

## Abraham pays Ephron

## Genesis 23:12

Abraham bowed again before the Hittites.

## Genesis 23:13

Everyone heard Abraham say to Ephron, “Please hear me.

I will pay you for the field.

Please take my payment so I can bury my wife there.”

## Genesis 23:14

Ephron answered Abraham.

## Genesis 23:15

“Listen, sir. The land is worth 400 shekels of silver.

What is that between you and me?

Go and bury your wife.”

## Genesis 23:16

Abraham agreed to pay the price the Hittites had heard.

He weighed out 400 shekels of silver for Ephron.

He used the same measure that traders used.

**Reading help**

Here a shekel tells how much the silver weighs.

**Pause and answer**

How much silver did Abraham pay Ephron?

## Abraham buries Sarah

## Genesis 23:17

The sale included Ephron’s field at Machpelah near Mamre.

It included the cave and every tree inside the field’s boundaries.

## Genesis 23:18

Abraham now owned it all.

All the Hittites gathered at the city gate witnessed the sale.

## Genesis 23:19

Then Abraham buried Sarah in the cave at Machpelah.

The field was near Mamre, at Hebron in Canaan.

## Genesis 23:20

The Hittites recognized the field and cave as Abraham’s property.

Abraham now had a place to bury his dead.
