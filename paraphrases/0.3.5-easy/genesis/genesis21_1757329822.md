# Genesis 21 — Sarah has a son, and God hears Ishmael

**About this chapter**

Sarah laughs for joy when Isaac is born.

Later, Hagar and her son Ishmael need water.

## Sarah laughs with joy

## Genesis 21:1

The LORD cared for Sarah.

The LORD did what he had promised her.

## Genesis 21:2

Sarah became pregnant. She gave birth to a son.

Abraham was already old.

Their son came at the time God had promised.

## Genesis 21:3

Abraham named their son Isaac.

## Genesis 21:4

When Isaac was eight days old, Abraham circumcised him.

God had told Abraham to do this.

**Reading help**

Circumcision means removing the foreskin from the penis.

For Abraham’s family, this marked their agreement with God.

## Genesis 21:5

Abraham was 100 years old when Isaac was born.

## Genesis 21:6

Sarah said, “God has made me laugh!

Everyone who hears the news will laugh with me.”

## Genesis 21:7

Sarah said, “Who would have told Abraham I would feed a baby at my breast?

Yet here is our son, even though Abraham is old!”

**Pause and answer**

What made Sarah laugh with joy?

## Sarah asks Abraham to send Ishmael away

## Genesis 21:8

Isaac grew. In time, he stopped drinking his mother’s milk.

That day Abraham held a big feast.

## Genesis 21:9

At the feast, Sarah saw Ishmael making fun of Isaac.

Ishmael was Abraham’s son by Hagar, the Egyptian woman.

## Genesis 21:10

Sarah told Abraham, “Send this slave woman and her son away!

When you die, your property will go to Isaac.

I do not want her son to share it.”

## Genesis 21:11

These words upset Abraham deeply.

Ishmael was his son too.

## Genesis 21:12

God told Abraham, “Do not be so troubled about Ishmael and Hagar.

Listen to what Sarah tells you.

The family line I promised you will continue through Isaac.

## Genesis 21:13

Ishmael is also your son.

I will make his family into a nation.”

**Pause and answer**

What did God promise about Ishmael’s family?

## Hagar runs out of water

## Genesis 21:14

Early the next morning, Abraham got up.

He put bread and a leather bag of water on Hagar’s shoulders.

Then he sent Hagar and Ishmael away.

Hagar wandered in the desert near Beersheba.

## Genesis 21:15

At last, the water bag was empty.

Hagar left Ishmael under a bush.

## Genesis 21:16

Hagar sat about as far away as an arrow can fly.

She said, “I cannot watch my boy die.”

Hagar sat there and cried aloud.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Hagar leave Ishmael?

## God hears Ishmael

## Genesis 21:17

God heard Ishmael’s voice.

God’s angel called to Hagar from heaven.

“What is wrong, Hagar? Do not be afraid.

God has heard your boy right where he lies.

## Genesis 21:18

Get up. Help the boy up.

Hold his hand.

I will make his family into a great nation.”

## Genesis 21:19

Then God helped Hagar see a well.

Hagar filled her water bag at the well.

She gave Ishmael a drink.

## Genesis 21:20

God was with Ishmael as he grew up.

Ishmael lived in the desert.

He became very good at shooting arrows.

## Genesis 21:21

Ishmael lived in the desert of Paran.

His mother found him a wife from Egypt.

## A king asks Abraham for a promise

## Genesis 21:22

At that time King Abimelech came to Abraham.

Phicol, the leader of his army, came too.

They said, “God is with you in everything you do.”

## Genesis 21:23

Abimelech said, “Make me a promise here before God.

Deal honestly with me, my children, and their children.

I have shown you kindness.

Show kindness to me and to this land where you live.”

## Genesis 21:24

Abraham answered, “I make that promise before God.”

## Genesis 21:25

Then Abraham complained to Abimelech about a well.

The king’s servants had taken it.

## Genesis 21:26

Abimelech said, “I do not know who did this.

You never told me.

Today is the first I have heard of it.”

**Pause and answer**

What had the king’s servants taken?

## Seven lambs mark an agreement

## Genesis 21:27

Abraham gave Abimelech some sheep and cattle.

The two men made a covenant, a firm agreement.

## Genesis 21:28

Abraham set seven young female sheep apart from his flock.

## Genesis 21:29

Abimelech asked, “Why did you set these seven lambs apart?”

## Genesis 21:30

Abraham said, “Take these seven lambs from me.

By taking them, you agree that I dug this well.”

![Seven numbered marks stand for the seven lambs Abraham sets apart.](../pictures/seven-lambs-for-the-well.svg)

Each circle stands for one lamb. The gift confirms that Abraham dug the well.

## Genesis 21:31

The two men made their promises there before God.

That is why people called the place Beersheba.

## Genesis 21:32

After the agreement, Abimelech and Phicol left Beersheba.

They went back to the land of the Philistines.

## Genesis 21:33

Abraham planted a tree called a tamarisk in Beersheba.

There Abraham prayed to the LORD, the God who lives forever.

## Genesis 21:34

Abraham lived in the land of the Philistines a long time.
