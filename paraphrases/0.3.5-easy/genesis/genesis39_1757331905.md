# Genesis 39 — The LORD stays with Joseph in Egypt

**About this chapter**

Joseph works in Potiphar’s house.

Later, Potiphar sends him to prison.

The LORD stays with Joseph in both places.

## Potiphar puts Joseph in charge

## Genesis 39:1

The Ishmaelites took Joseph to Egypt.

An Egyptian named Potiphar bought Joseph from them.

Potiphar led the guards for Pharaoh, the king of Egypt.

## Genesis 39:2

The LORD was with Joseph.

Joseph did well as he worked in Potiphar’s house.

## Genesis 39:3

Potiphar saw that the LORD was with Joseph.

The LORD helped Joseph succeed in everything he did.

## Genesis 39:4

Potiphar was pleased with Joseph and chose him to serve him personally.

He put Joseph in charge of his household.

He trusted Joseph with everything he owned.

## Genesis 39:5

From then on, the LORD blessed Potiphar’s household because of Joseph.

The LORD blessed everything in Potiphar’s home and fields.

## Genesis 39:6

Potiphar left everything he owned in Joseph’s care.

Potiphar only concerned himself with the food he ate.

Joseph was strong and handsome.

## Joseph refuses Potiphar’s wife

## Genesis 39:7

After a while, Potiphar’s wife began to want Joseph.

She said, “Have sex with me.”

## Genesis 39:8

Joseph refused. He told her, “My master trusts me.

He leaves everything in the house to me.

I look after everything he owns.

## Genesis 39:9

No one in this house has more authority than I do.

My master has kept nothing from me except you.

You are his wife.

How could I do such an evil thing and sin against God?”

## Genesis 39:10

Day after day, Potiphar’s wife kept asking Joseph.

He refused to have sex with her.

He even refused to stay near her.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Joseph say he would refuse?

## Joseph runs outside

## Genesis 39:11

One day Joseph went into the house to work.

None of the other servants were inside.

## Genesis 39:12

Potiphar’s wife grabbed Joseph by his cloak.

She said, “Have sex with me!”

Joseph ran outside, leaving his cloak in her hand.

**Reading help**

A cloak is a piece of clothing worn over other clothes.

## Genesis 39:13

She saw that Joseph had run out of the house.

She still held his cloak.

**Pause and answer**

What did Joseph leave behind when he ran?

## Potiphar’s wife accuses Joseph

## Genesis 39:14

Potiphar’s wife called the servants and told them a lie.

“Look! My husband brought this Hebrew here to shame us.

Joseph came to have sex with me. I screamed loudly.

## Genesis 39:15

When he heard me cry for help, he left his cloak beside me.

Then he ran outside.”

## Genesis 39:16

Potiphar’s wife kept the cloak beside her until Potiphar came home.

## Genesis 39:17

She told Potiphar the same story.

“The Hebrew slave you brought here came to shame me.

## Genesis 39:18

When I cried for help, he left his cloak beside me and ran outside.”

## Genesis 39:19

Potiphar’s wife said, “This is what your slave did to me.”

Potiphar became very angry when he heard her story.

**Pause and answer**

Who told Potiphar the story about the cloak?

## The LORD stays with Joseph in prison

## Genesis 39:20

Potiphar sent Joseph to prison.

This was where the king kept his prisoners.

## Genesis 39:21

The LORD was with Joseph and showed him kindness.

The LORD helped Joseph gain the trust of the man in charge of the prison.

## Genesis 39:22

The man put all the prisoners in Joseph’s care.

Joseph looked after all the work in the prison.

## Genesis 39:23

The man trusted Joseph to do the work without checking on him.

The LORD was with Joseph.

The LORD helped him succeed in everything he did.

| Where Joseph lived | What Joseph looked after |
| --- | --- |
