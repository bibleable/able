# Genesis 44 — Judah offers to stay in Benjamin’s place

**About this chapter**

Joseph tells his house manager to hide a silver cup in Benjamin’s sack.

Judah has promised his father to bring Benjamin home.

## Joseph gives orders about his cup

## Genesis 44:1

Joseph spoke to the man who managed his house.

“Fill their sacks with as much food as they can carry.

Put each man’s silver at the top of his sack.

## Genesis 44:2

Put my silver cup in the youngest brother’s sack.

Put it at the top, beside the silver for his grain.”

The manager did what Joseph told him.

## Genesis 44:3

At dawn the brothers set off with their donkeys.

## Genesis 44:4

They were still near the city when Joseph told his manager, “Hurry after them.

When you catch up, ask them why they repaid kindness with evil.

## Genesis 44:5

Say, ‘This is the cup my master drinks from.

He uses it to look for signs about hidden things.

What you have done is wrong!’”

## Genesis 44:6

The manager caught up with the brothers.

He told them what Joseph had said.

## The manager searches the sacks

## Genesis 44:7

The brothers asked, “Sir, why do you say this?

We would never do such a thing.

## Genesis 44:8

We found silver at the top of our sacks before.

We brought it all the way back from Canaan.

Why would we steal silver or gold from your master?”

## Genesis 44:9

They added, “If one of us has the cup, let him die.

The rest of us will be your slaves.”

## Genesis 44:10

The manager answered, “Very well. Whoever has the cup will be my slave.

The rest of you will be free of blame.”

## Genesis 44:11

Each brother quickly set his sack down and opened it.

## Genesis 44:12

The manager searched the sacks, from the oldest brother to the youngest.

He found the cup in Benjamin’s sack.

The manager found Joseph’s silver cup in Benjamin’s sack.

## Genesis 44:13

The brothers tore their clothes in distress.

They loaded their donkeys and went back to the city.

## Judah speaks for all his brothers

## Genesis 44:14

Joseph was still at home when Judah and his brothers arrived.

They fell to the ground before him.

## Genesis 44:15

Joseph asked, “What have you done?

Surely you know a man like me can discover hidden things.”

## Genesis 44:16

Judah answered, “Sir, what can we say?

How can we show we are innocent?

God has brought our wrongdoing to light.

We will all be your slaves, including the brother who had the cup.”

## Genesis 44:17

Joseph said, “I would never keep all of you.

Only the man who had the cup will be my slave.

The rest of you may go home to your father in peace.”

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Joseph say he would keep?

## Judah reminds Joseph of their first visit

## Genesis 44:18

Judah came closer to Joseph.

“Sir, please let me speak to you. Please do not be angry.

You have as much power as Pharaoh himself.

## Genesis 44:19

You asked us, ‘Do you have a father or another brother?’

## Genesis 44:20

We answered, ‘Our father is old.

Our youngest brother was born when our father was already old.

His brother is dead. He is the only son his mother has left.

Our father loves him.’

## Genesis 44:21

You told us, ‘Bring him here so I can see him myself.’

## Genesis 44:22

We said, ‘The boy cannot leave his father.

If he leaves, his father will die.’

## Genesis 44:23

You told us, ‘Bring your youngest brother with you.

Otherwise you cannot see me again.’”

**Reading help**

Judah is speaking about Benjamin. Benjamin and Joseph had the same mother.

Judah still does not know he is speaking to Joseph.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the brothers first hesitate to bring Benjamin?

## Judah tells Joseph what their father said

## Genesis 44:24

Judah continued, “We went home and told our father your words.

## Genesis 44:25

Later our father said, ‘Go back and buy us some food.’

## Genesis 44:26

We said, ‘We can only go if our youngest brother comes too.

Without him, the governor will not see us.’

## Genesis 44:27

Our father said, ‘You know my wife Rachel had two sons.

## Genesis 44:28

One went away. I thought a wild animal had torn him to pieces.

I have never seen him since.

## Genesis 44:29

Now you want to take this son too.

If anything happens to him, I will die of sorrow in my old age.’”

## Judah offers to take Benjamin’s place

## Genesis 44:30

Judah said, “Think what will happen if I go home without Benjamin.

My father loves Benjamin so much that his life depends on him.

## Genesis 44:31

When my father sees Benjamin is missing, he will die.

We will have caused our old father to die of sorrow.

## Genesis 44:32

I promised my father I would keep Benjamin safe.

I said, ‘If I fail to bring him home, I will be to blame for the rest of my life.’

## Genesis 44:33

Please let me stay as your slave in Benjamin’s place.

Let him go home with his brothers.

## Genesis 44:34

How can I go home to my father without him?

I could not bear to see my father suffer like that.”
