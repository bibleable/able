# Genesis 20 — God tells Abimelech to return Sarah

**About this chapter**

Abraham and Sarah stay in Gerar.

The king takes Sarah into his home.

Then God speaks to the king in a dream.

## The king takes Sarah

## Genesis 20:1

Abraham traveled south to a region called the Negev.

He settled between Kadesh and Shur.

For a time, Abraham stayed in Gerar.

## Genesis 20:2

Abraham said about his wife Sarah, “She is my sister.”

Abimelech was the king of Gerar.

He sent for Sarah and took her into his home.

## Genesis 20:3

One night God came to Abimelech in a dream.

God said, “You have taken a married woman.

Because of this, you will die.”

## God tells the king what to do

## Genesis 20:4

Abimelech had not had sex with Sarah.

He asked, “Lord, will you destroy an innocent nation?

## Genesis 20:5

Abraham told me Sarah was his sister.

Sarah herself said Abraham was her brother.

I honestly thought I had done nothing wrong.”

## Genesis 20:6

God answered Abimelech in the dream.

“Yes, I know you meant no harm.

So I kept you from doing wrong against me.

That is why I kept you from touching Sarah.

## Genesis 20:7

Now give Abraham his wife back.

Abraham is a prophet. He will pray for you.

Then you will live.

If you keep Sarah, you will certainly die.

Everyone in your household will die too.”

**Reading help**

A prophet is someone who speaks for God.

When Abraham prays for someone, he asks God to help that person.

**Pause and answer**

What did God tell Abimelech to do?

## Abraham explains why he was afraid

## Genesis 20:8

Early the next morning, Abimelech called all his servants.

He told them everything that had happened.

The servants were very afraid.

## Genesis 20:9

Then Abimelech called Abraham.

“What have you done to us?” the king asked.

“What wrong have I done to you?

You have brought great guilt on me and my people.

You should never have done this to me.”

## Genesis 20:10

Abimelech asked Abraham, “Why did you do this?”

## Genesis 20:11

Abraham answered, “I thought nobody here respected God.

I thought someone would kill me to take my wife.

## Genesis 20:12

Sarah really is my sister.

We have the same father and different mothers.

Later, Sarah became my wife.

## Genesis 20:13

God sent me away from my father’s home.

That was when I asked Sarah for this kindness.

Wherever we went, I wanted her to tell people I was her brother.”

**Pause and answer**

What was Abraham afraid someone would do?

## Sarah returns and Abraham prays

## Genesis 20:14

Abimelech gave Abraham sheep and cattle.

He also gave Abraham male and female servants.

Then Abimelech returned Sarah to her husband.

Follow Sarah’s return after God speaks to Abimelech.

## Genesis 20:15

Abimelech told Abraham, “You can live anywhere you like in my land.”

## Genesis 20:16

Abimelech told Sarah, “I am giving your brother a thousand pieces of silver.

This shows everyone with you that you are free from blame.”

## Genesis 20:17

Then Abraham prayed to God.

God healed Abimelech, his wife, and his female servants.

The women could have children again.

## Genesis 20:18

Earlier, the LORD had kept every woman in Abimelech’s household from having children.

The LORD had done this because Abimelech had taken Sarah, Abraham’s wife.
