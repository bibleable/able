# Genesis 17 — God gives Abram a new name

**About this chapter**

God makes promises to Abram and his family. God also gives Abram and Sarai new names.

## God calls Abram Abraham

## Genesis 17:1

Abram was 99 years old when the LORD appeared to him.

The LORD said, “I am God Almighty. Live close to me and do what is right.

## Genesis 17:2

I will make a covenant with you. I will give you many descendants.”

**Reading help**

A covenant is a lasting agreement. Your descendants are your children and the people born into your family after them.

## Genesis 17:3

Abram bowed with his face to the ground. God spoke to him again.

## Genesis 17:4

God said, “Here is my part of our covenant. You will be the father of many nations.

## Genesis 17:5

Your new name is Abraham. I have made you the father of many nations.

## Genesis 17:6

I will give you a very large family. Whole nations will come from your family. Some of your descendants will be kings.

## Genesis 17:7

My covenant with you and your descendants will last forever. I will be your God and their God.

## Genesis 17:8

You live as a stranger in Canaan now. I will give all this land to you and your descendants forever. I will be their God.”

**Pause and answer**

What new name did God give Abram?

## A sign of the covenant

## Genesis 17:9

God said to Abraham, “You and your descendants must keep my covenant.

## Genesis 17:10

Every man and boy among you must be circumcised.

## Genesis 17:11

That means cutting away the foreskin, the skin that covers the tip of the penis. This will be the sign of the covenant between us.

## Genesis 17:12

In every generation, circumcise each baby boy when he is eight days old. This includes boys born in your household and those bought from foreigners, even if they are outside your family.

## Genesis 17:13

Every male born in your household or bought with your money must be circumcised. The mark on your bodies will be a sign of my lasting covenant.

## Genesis 17:14

Any man or boy who stays uncircumcised must be separated from his people. He has broken my covenant.”

**Pause and answer**

How old should a baby boy be when he receives the sign?

## God makes a promise about Sarah

## Genesis 17:15

God said to Abraham, “Call your wife Sarah from now on. Her name used to be Sarai.

![Sarai receives a new name, Sarah.](../pictures/sarai-named-sarah.svg)

Both names belong to Abraham’s wife.

## Genesis 17:16

I will bless Sarah. She will have a son with you. Nations will come from her family. Some of her descendants will be kings.”

## Genesis 17:17

Abraham bowed with his face to the ground. He laughed and thought, “Can a man have a child at 100? Can Sarah give birth at 90?”

## Genesis 17:18

Abraham said to God, “Please let Ishmael live with your blessing!”

## Genesis 17:19

God answered, “Sarah really will have a son with you. Name him Isaac. My covenant with Isaac and his descendants will last forever.

## Genesis 17:20

I have heard what you asked for Ishmael. I will bless him and give him many descendants. He will be the father of twelve rulers. His family will become a great nation.

## Genesis 17:21

I will make my covenant with Isaac. Sarah will give birth to him at this time next year.”

## Genesis 17:22

When God finished speaking, he went up and left Abraham.

**Pause and answer**

What name did God give the son Sarah would have?

## Abraham does what God asks

## Genesis 17:23

That very day Abraham circumcised his son Ishmael and every man and boy in his household. This included those born there and those he had bought. Abraham did what God had told him.

## Genesis 17:24

Abraham was 99 when he was circumcised.

## Genesis 17:25

His son Ishmael was 13.

## Genesis 17:26

Abraham and Ishmael were circumcised on the same day.

## Genesis 17:27

All the men in the household were circumcised with Abraham. Some had been born there. Others had been bought from foreigners.
