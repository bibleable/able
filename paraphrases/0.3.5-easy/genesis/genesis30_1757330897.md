# Genesis 30 — Jacob’s family and flocks grow

**About this chapter**

Rachel longs for a child.

As Jacob’s family grows, he wants to provide for them.

## Bilhah gives birth to two sons

## Genesis 30:1

Rachel had no children with Jacob. She envied her sister Leah.

Rachel told Jacob, “Give me children, or I will die!”

## Genesis 30:2

Jacob grew angry. “Do I have God’s power?” he asked.

“God is the one who has kept you from having children.”

## Genesis 30:3

Rachel said, “Here is Bilhah, my servant. Have sex with her.

She can give birth to children for me.

That way I too can have a family.”

## Genesis 30:4

Rachel gave Bilhah to Jacob as a wife.

Jacob had sex with Bilhah.

## Genesis 30:5

Bilhah became pregnant and gave birth to Jacob’s son.

## Genesis 30:6

Rachel said, “God has decided in my favor.

He heard me ask for help and gave me a son.”

Rachel named him Dan.

## Genesis 30:7

Bilhah became pregnant again. She had a second son with Jacob.

## Genesis 30:8

Rachel said, “I have struggled hard against my sister and won.”

She named him Naphtali.

## Zilpah gives birth to two sons

## Genesis 30:9

Leah saw that she had stopped having children.

She gave Jacob her servant Zilpah as a wife.

## Genesis 30:10

Zilpah gave birth to Jacob’s son.

## Genesis 30:11

Leah said, “What good fortune!” She named him Gad.

## Genesis 30:12

Then Zilpah gave birth to a second son with Jacob.

## Genesis 30:13

Leah said, “How happy I am! The women call me happy.”

Leah named him Asher.

## Rachel asks Leah for some plants

## Genesis 30:14

At wheat harvest time, Reuben found plants called mandrakes.

He brought them from the field to his mother Leah.

Rachel asked Leah, “Please give me some of your son’s mandrakes.”

## Genesis 30:15

Leah answered, “You have already taken my husband away.

Do you want my son’s mandrakes too?”

Rachel said, “Give me the mandrakes.

In return, Jacob can spend tonight with you.”

## Genesis 30:16

That evening Jacob came back from the field.

Leah went to meet him. “Come with me,” she said.

“I paid for this night with my son’s mandrakes.”

Jacob had sex with Leah that night.

## Leah and Rachel have children

## Genesis 30:17

God listened to Leah. She became pregnant again.

She gave birth to her fifth son with Jacob.

## Genesis 30:18

Leah said, “I gave my servant to my husband.

Now God has rewarded me.” She named the boy Issachar.

## Genesis 30:19

Leah became pregnant again.

She gave birth to her sixth son with Jacob.

## Genesis 30:20

Leah said, “God has given me a good gift.

Now my husband will honor me. I have given him six sons.”

She named the boy Zebulun.

## Genesis 30:21

Later Leah had a daughter. She named her Dinah.

## Genesis 30:22

Then God remembered Rachel and listened to her.

He made it possible for her to have a child.

## Genesis 30:23

Rachel became pregnant and gave birth to a son.

She said, “God has taken away my shame.”

## Genesis 30:24

Rachel named him Joseph.

She said, “May the LORD give me another son.”

## Jacob asks to go home

## Genesis 30:25

After Joseph was born, Jacob spoke to Laban.

“Let me go back to my own country.

## Genesis 30:26

Let my wives and children go with me.

I worked for you to have this family.

You know how hard I worked.”

## Genesis 30:27

Laban answered, “If you are pleased with me, please stay.

I have looked for signs to learn what God is doing.

That is how I learned the LORD blessed me because of you.”

## Genesis 30:28

Laban added, “Tell me what pay you want.

I will give it to you.”

## Genesis 30:29

Jacob answered, “You know how I have worked for you.

You know how well your animals have done in my care.

## Genesis 30:30

You had very little before I came.

Now you have many times more than you had then.

The LORD blessed you wherever I went.

When can I start providing for my own family?”

**Pause and answer**

Why did Laban want Jacob to stay?

## Jacob and Laban agree on Jacob’s pay

## Genesis 30:31

Laban asked, “What can I give you?”

Jacob answered, “You need not give me anything now.

Agree to this, and I will keep tending your flocks.

## Genesis 30:32

Let me go through your flocks today.

Let me take out the sheep with spots or speckles.

Let me take the dark lambs too.

I will also take the goats with spots or speckles.

Those animals will be my pay.

## Genesis 30:33

Later you can check and see that I am honest.

Suppose you find a goat of mine without spots or speckles.

Or suppose you find a lamb of mine that is not dark.

You can count those animals as stolen.”

## Genesis 30:34

Laban said, “I agree. We will do as you say.”

## Genesis 30:35

That day Laban took out the male goats with streaks or spots.

He took the female goats with speckles or spots.

He took every goat with white on it and every dark lamb.

Laban put his sons in charge of those animals.

## Genesis 30:36

Laban put three days of travel between himself and Jacob.

Jacob looked after the rest of Laban’s flocks.

## Jacob’s flocks grow

## Genesis 30:37

Jacob cut fresh branches from poplar, almond, and plane trees.

He peeled off strips of bark.

The white wood underneath showed through.

## Genesis 30:38

Jacob put the branches where the animals drank water.

The animals came to drink when they were ready to mate.

## Genesis 30:39

They mated in front of the branches.

Their young had streaks, speckles, or spots.

## Genesis 30:40

Jacob separated the young animals.

He turned the others toward Laban’s dark sheep with streaks.

Jacob kept his own animals apart from Laban’s.

## Genesis 30:41

When the stronger females were ready to mate, Jacob set out the branches.

The animals could see them as they drank and mated.

## Genesis 30:42

When the animals were weak, Jacob left the branches out of the troughs.

The weaker animals went to Laban. The stronger ones went to Jacob.

## Genesis 30:43

Jacob became very rich. He had large flocks and many servants, both women and men.

He also had camels and donkeys.
