# Genesis 6 — God tells Noah to build an ark

**About this chapter**

God sees the violence on earth.

He tells Noah how to keep his family and the animals alive.

## People spread across the earth

## Genesis 6:1

More and more people lived on the earth.

Daughters were born to them.

## Genesis 6:2

The sons of God saw that the women were beautiful.

They married the women they chose.

## Genesis 6:3

The LORD said, “My Spirit will not struggle with people forever.

Their lives come to an end.

Their days will be 120 years.”

## Genesis 6:4

The Nephilim lived on earth then and later too.

The sons of God slept with the daughters of men.

Their children became powerful men, famous long ago.

**Pause and answer**

How long did the LORD say people’s days would be?

## God sees how people live

## Genesis 6:5

The LORD saw how much evil people did on earth.

Even the things they thought and planned were always evil.

## Genesis 6:6

The LORD regretted making people on the earth.

He felt deep sorrow.

## Genesis 6:7

The LORD said, “I will wipe the people I made off the earth.

The animals, crawling creatures and birds will go too.

I am sorry I made them.”

## Genesis 6:8

But the LORD looked kindly on Noah.

**Pause and answer**

How did the LORD feel about making people?

## Noah walks with God

## Genesis 6:9

Here is Noah’s story.

Noah did what was right.

Among the people of his time, he lived without blame.

Noah walked with God.

**Reading help**

Walking with God means living in close friendship with him.

## Genesis 6:10

Noah had three sons.

Their names were Shem, Ham and Japheth.

## Genesis 6:11

God saw what the earth had become.

Wrongdoing had spoiled it.

Violence was everywhere.

## Genesis 6:12

God looked at the earth and saw the damage.

All the creatures on earth had gone wrong in the way they lived.

**Pause and answer**

What were the names of Noah’s three sons?

## God describes the ark

## Genesis 6:13

God told Noah, “I have decided to put an end to all living creatures.

Because of them the earth is full of violence.

I will destroy them and the earth.

## Genesis 6:14

“Build an ark from gopher wood.

Make rooms inside.

Cover the inside and outside with pitch.

**Reading help**

An ark is a large boat. Pitch is a sticky coating that keeps water out.

## Genesis 6:15

“Make the ark 300 cubits long.

Make it 50 cubits wide and 30 cubits high.

## Genesis 6:16

“Make a roof for the ark.

Finish the walls one cubit below the top.

Put a door in the side.

Build three decks, one below, one in the middle and one above.

**Reading help**

**Picture the three decks**

| Level of the ark | Where it was |
| --- | --- |
| Upper deck | Under the roof |
| Middle deck | Between the other two decks |
| Lower deck | At the bottom |

A cubit is about the length from your elbow to your fingertips.

**Pause and answer**

Where did God tell Noah to put the door?

## Food and shelter for people and animals

## Genesis 6:17

“I will send a flood over the earth.

It will destroy every creature under the sky that breathes.

Everything on earth will die.

## Genesis 6:18

“But I will make a covenant with you.

You and your wife will enter the ark.

Your sons and their wives will enter with you.

**Reading help**

A covenant is a binding agreement.

## Genesis 6:19

“Bring two of every kind of living creature into the ark.

Bring a male and a female to keep them alive with you.

## Genesis 6:20

“Every kind of bird and land animal will come to you in pairs.

So will every kind of crawling creature.

Keep them alive.

## Genesis 6:21

“Gather every kind of food that you and the animals eat.

Store it for all of you.”

## Genesis 6:22

Noah did everything exactly as God had told him.

**Pause and answer**

Who would eat the food Noah stored?
