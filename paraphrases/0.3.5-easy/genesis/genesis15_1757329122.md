# Genesis 15 — Abram looks up at the stars

**About this chapter**

Abram asks who will receive what he owns.

The LORD takes him outside to look at the stars.

## Who will inherit from Abram?

## Genesis 15:1

After this the LORD spoke to Abram in a vision.

“Do not be afraid, Abram.

I am your shield.

I am your very great reward.”

**Reading help**

A shield protects a person from harm. The LORD promises to protect Abram.

## Genesis 15:2

Abram answered, “Lord GOD, what can you give me?

I still have no child.

Eliezer of Damascus will inherit my household.”

## Genesis 15:3

Abram said, “You have given me no children.

A servant in my house will receive what I own.”

**Pause and answer**

Who does Abram expect to inherit his household?

## More descendants than Abram can count

## Genesis 15:4

The LORD answered, “Eliezer will not inherit from you.

Your own child will inherit from you.”

## Genesis 15:5

The LORD took Abram outside.

“Look up at the sky,” he said.

“Count the stars if you can.

That is how many descendants you will have.”

![Bright stars scattered across a dark sky.](../pictures/abram-looks-at-stars.svg)

The stars help Abram picture how large his family will become.

**Reading help**

✦　✦　✦　✦　✦

Picture these few stars in a sky full of stars. Abram’s descendants are his children and the generations after them.

## Genesis 15:6

Abram believed the LORD.

The LORD counted that trust as righteousness.

He saw Abram as right with him.

**Pause and answer**

What does the LORD ask Abram to count?

## Abram asks about the land

## Genesis 15:7

The LORD said, “I brought you out of Ur of the Chaldeans.

I am the LORD.

I brought you here to give you this land.”

## Genesis 15:8

Abram asked, “Lord GOD, how can I know this land will be mine?”

## Genesis 15:9

The LORD told him to bring a young cow, a goat and a ram.

Each animal was to be three years old.

Abram was also to bring a turtledove and a young pigeon.

## Genesis 15:10

Abram brought them all.

He cut the three larger animals in half.

He laid each half opposite the other.

He left the birds whole.

## Genesis 15:11

Birds that eat meat flew down to the dead animals.

Abram drove the birds away.

**Pause and answer**

What does Abram do when birds come near the animals?

## The LORD tells Abram what lies ahead

## Genesis 15:12

As the sun went down, Abram fell deeply asleep.

Suddenly he was overcome by darkness and great fear.

## Genesis 15:13

The LORD told Abram, “This is certain.

Your descendants will live as strangers in another land.

People there will enslave them and treat them cruelly for four hundred years.

## Genesis 15:14

“I will judge the nation that makes them slaves.

Then your descendants will leave with many possessions.

## Genesis 15:15

“You will die in peace at a good old age.

You will join your ancestors, and people will bury you.

## Genesis 15:16

“In the fourth generation your descendants will come back here.

The wrongdoing of the Amorites has not yet reached its full measure.”

**Pause and answer**

When will Abram’s descendants come back?

## The LORD makes a covenant

## Genesis 15:17

After sunset, when it was dark, a smoking firepot appeared.

A burning torch appeared too.

Both passed between the halves of the animals.

## Genesis 15:18

That day the LORD made a covenant with Abram.

He said, “I have given this land to your descendants.

It stretches from the river of Egypt to the great river Euphrates.

**Reading help**

A covenant is a binding agreement.

## Genesis 15:19

“It is the land of the Kenites, Kenizzites and Kadmonites.

## Genesis 15:20

“It is also the land of the Hittites, Perizzites and Rephaites.

## Genesis 15:21

“It includes the land of the Amorites, Canaanites, Girgashites and Jebusites.”

**Pause and answer**

Which two rivers mark the ends of the land in the promise?
