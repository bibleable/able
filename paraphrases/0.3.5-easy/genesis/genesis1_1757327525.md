# Genesis 1 — God makes the heavens and the earth

**About this chapter**

God speaks, and things begin to happen.

Listen for what God sees at the end of his work.

## Day one — Light and darkness

## Genesis 1:1

In the beginning, God made the heavens and the earth.

## Genesis 1:2

The earth had no shape and was empty.

Darkness covered the deep water.

God’s Spirit hovered over the water.

## Genesis 1:3

God said, “Let there be light.”

Light appeared.

## Genesis 1:4

God saw that the light was good.

God separated the light from the darkness.

## Genesis 1:5

God called the light “day.”

He called the darkness “night.”

Evening came, then morning.

That was the first day.

**Pause and answer**

What did God call the light?

## Day two — The sky

## Genesis 1:6

God said, “Let there be a space between the waters.

Let it keep the waters apart.”

## Genesis 1:7

God made that space.

He separated the water below from the water above.

It happened as God said.

## Genesis 1:8

God called the space “sky.”

Evening came, then morning.

That was the second day.

**Pause and answer**

What did God call the space between the waters?

## Day three — Land, seas and plants

## Genesis 1:9

God said, “Let the water below the sky gather in one place.

Let dry land appear.”

It happened as God said.

## Genesis 1:10

God called the dry land “earth.”

He called the gathered waters “seas.”

God saw that it was good.

## Genesis 1:11

God said, “Let plants grow on the land.

Let plants make seeds.

Let fruit trees grow fruit with seeds inside, each of its own kind.”

It happened as God said.

## Genesis 1:12

Plants grew from the land.

Each kind of plant made its own seeds.

Each kind of fruit tree grew fruit with seeds inside.

God saw that it was good.

![A stalk of grain with seeds at the top.](../pictures/grain.svg)

Look at the seeds. They can grow into more plants of the same kind.

## Genesis 1:13

Evening came, then morning.

That was the third day.

**Pause and answer**

What grew on the dry land?

## Day four — Lights in the sky

## Genesis 1:14

God said, “Let lights shine in the sky.

Let them separate day from night.

Let them mark seasons, days and years.

## Genesis 1:15

“Let the lights shine on the earth.”

It happened as God said.

## Genesis 1:16

God made two great lights.

The greater light ruled the day.

The smaller light ruled the night.

God also made the stars.

## Genesis 1:17

God placed the lights in the sky to shine on the earth.

## Genesis 1:18

They ruled over day and night.

They separated light from darkness.

God saw that it was good.

## Genesis 1:19

Evening came, then morning.

That was the fourth day.

**Pause and answer**

Which light ruled the day?

## Day five — Sea creatures and birds

## Genesis 1:20

God said, “Let the water fill with living creatures.

Let birds fly across the open sky above the earth.”

## Genesis 1:21

God made the great sea creatures.

He made every kind of creature that moved in the water.

The water was full of life.

God also made every kind of bird with wings.

God saw that it was good.

## Genesis 1:22

God blessed them.

He said, “Have many young and fill the seas.

Let there be many birds on the earth.”

## Genesis 1:23

Evening came, then morning.

That was the fifth day.

**Pause and answer**

Where did God tell the birds to fly?

## Day six — Animals on land

## Genesis 1:24

God said, “Let the land bring forth living creatures of every kind.

Let there be farm animals, creatures that crawl, and wild animals.”

It happened as God said.

## Genesis 1:25

God made every kind of wild animal and farm animal.

He made every kind of creature that crawls on land.

God saw that it was good.

**Pause and answer**

Who made the animals that crawl on land?

## Day six — People and their work

## Genesis 1:26

God said, “Let us make human beings in our image, like us.

Let them rule over the fish, the birds and the farm animals.

Let them rule over all the earth and everything that crawls on it.”

## Genesis 1:27

So God made human beings in his own image.

He made them male and female.

**Reading help**

An image shows something about the one it represents.

God gives people a place in his world as his representatives.

## Genesis 1:28

God blessed them.

He said, “Have children and grow in number.

Fill the earth and bring it under your rule.

Rule over the fish, the birds and every creature that moves on the earth.”

## Genesis 1:29

God said, “I give you every plant on earth that makes seeds.

I give you every tree that grows fruit with seeds inside.

These are your food.

## Genesis 1:30

“I give green plants for food to the wild animals.

I give them to the birds and the creatures that crawl.

They are food for everything that breathes.”

It happened as God said.

## Genesis 1:31

God looked at everything he had made.

It was very good.

Evening came, then morning.

That was the sixth day.

**Pause and answer**

What did God say about everything he had made?
