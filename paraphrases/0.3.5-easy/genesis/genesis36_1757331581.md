# Genesis 36 — Esau’s family and the leaders of Edom

**About this chapter**

Esau moves away from his brother Jacob.

His family grows in the land of Edom.

## Esau’s family in Canaan

## Genesis 36:1

This is the story of Esau and his family.

Esau also had the name Edom.

## Genesis 36:2

Esau married women in Canaan.

Adah was the daughter of Elon the Hittite.

Oholibamah was the daughter of Anah.

Her grandfather was Zibeon the Hivite.

## Genesis 36:3

Another wife was Basemath, Ishmael’s daughter.

Nebaioth was her brother.

## Genesis 36:4

Adah and Esau had a son named Eliphaz.

Basemath had a son named Reuel.

## Genesis 36:5

Oholibamah had three sons named Jeush, Jalam, and Korah.

All these sons of Esau were born in Canaan.

| Mother | Her sons |
| --- | --- |
| Adah | Eliphaz |
| Basemath | Reuel |
| Oholibamah | Jeush, Jalam, and Korah |

## Esau moves to Mount Seir

## Genesis 36:6

Esau took his wives, sons, daughters, and everyone in his household.

He took all his animals and everything he owned in Canaan.

He moved far away from his brother Jacob.

## Genesis 36:7

The brothers had too much to stay together.

There was too little land to feed all their animals.

## Genesis 36:8

So Esau, also called Edom, settled near Mount Seir.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the brothers need more land?

## Esau’s sons and grandsons

## Genesis 36:9

Here is the family of Esau near Mount Seir.

The people called the Edomites came from his family.

## Genesis 36:10

Esau and Adah’s son was Eliphaz.

Esau and Basemath’s son was Reuel.

## Genesis 36:11

Eliphaz had sons named Teman, Omar, Zepho, Gatam, and Kenaz.

## Genesis 36:12

Timna was a concubine of Eliphaz, Esau’s son.

She gave birth to another son, Amalek.

These sons of Eliphaz were Adah’s grandsons.

**Reading help**

A concubine was a partner with fewer rights than a wife.

## Genesis 36:13

Reuel had four sons named Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah.

They were grandsons of Esau’s wife Basemath.

## Genesis 36:14

Esau’s wife Oholibamah was Anah’s daughter and Zibeon’s granddaughter.

Her sons with Esau were Jeush, Jalam, and Korah.

**Pause and answer**

Who was Amalek’s mother?

## Leaders in Esau’s family

## Genesis 36:15

Some of Esau’s family became chiefs, or leaders of family groups.

Esau’s oldest son was Eliphaz.

His sons included the chiefs Teman, Omar, Zepho, and Kenaz.

## Genesis 36:16

Korah, Gatam, and Amalek were chiefs too.

These grandsons of Adah led groups in Edom.

They belonged to the family of Eliphaz.

## Genesis 36:17

Reuel’s sons were chiefs in Edom too.

Their names were Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah.

They were grandsons of Esau’s wife Basemath.

## Genesis 36:18

Jeush, Jalam, and Korah were chiefs.

Their mother was Esau’s wife Oholibamah, the daughter of Anah.

## Genesis 36:19

These were the chiefs in the family of Esau.

Esau also had the name Edom.

Which son of Esau was the oldest?

## Seir’s family

## Genesis 36:20

Seir the Horite had sons who lived in this land.

Their names included Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, and Anah.

## Genesis 36:21

Seir’s other sons were Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan.

These sons were chiefs of the Horite people in Edom.

## Genesis 36:22

Lotan’s sons were Hori and Hemam.

Timna was Lotan’s sister.

## Genesis 36:23

Shobal’s sons were Alvan, Manahath, Ebal, Shepho, and Onam.

## Genesis 36:24

Zibeon’s sons were Aiah and Anah.

Anah once took his father’s donkeys to graze in the wilderness.

There he found springs of hot water.

## Genesis 36:25

Anah’s children were Dishon and a daughter named Oholibamah.

## Genesis 36:26

Dishon’s sons were Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran.

## Genesis 36:27

Ezer’s sons were Bilhan, Zaavan, and Akan.

## Genesis 36:28

Dishan’s sons were Uz and Aran.

## Genesis 36:29

The Horite chiefs included Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, and Anah.

## Genesis 36:30

Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan were chiefs too.

Each led a group of Horites in the land of Seir.

**Pause and answer**

## Kings in Edom

## Genesis 36:31

Edom already had kings before Israel had its first king.

Here are the names of those kings of Edom.

## Genesis 36:32

Bela, son of Beor, ruled Edom.

His city was Dinhabah.

## Genesis 36:33

When Bela died, Jobab became king.

Jobab was Zerah’s son and came from Bozrah.

## Genesis 36:34

When Jobab died, Husham became king.

Husham came from the land of the Temanites.

## Genesis 36:35

When Husham died, Hadad became king.

Hadad was the son of Bedad.

Hadad defeated the people of Midian in Moab.

His city was Avith.

## Genesis 36:36

When Hadad died, Samlah from Masrekah became king.

## Genesis 36:37

When Samlah died, Shaul became king.

Shaul came from Rehoboth on the Euphrates River.

## Genesis 36:38

When Shaul died, Baal-hanan became king.

Baal-hanan was the son of Achbor.

## Genesis 36:39

When Baal-hanan son of Achbor died, another Hadad became king.

His city was Pau. His wife was Mehetabel.

She was Matred’s daughter and Me-zahab’s granddaughter.

**Pause and answer**

Who became king after Bela died?

## Chiefs and their lands

## Genesis 36:40

Here are Esau’s chiefs, grouped by family and place.

Their names included Timna, Alvah, and Jetheth.

## Genesis 36:41

Oholibamah, Elah, and Pinon were chiefs too.

## Genesis 36:42

So were Kenaz, Teman, and Mibzar.

## Genesis 36:43

Magdiel and Iram were also chiefs.

These chiefs led the people in the places they owned in Edom.

The Edomites all came from Esau’s family.
