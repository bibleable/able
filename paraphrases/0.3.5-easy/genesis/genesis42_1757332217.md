# Genesis 42 — Joseph’s brothers come to buy grain

**About this chapter**

Jacob’s family needs food.

Ten of his sons travel to Egypt.

The man who sells them grain knows who they are.

## Jacob sends ten sons to Egypt

## Genesis 42:1

Jacob learned there was grain in Egypt.

He asked his sons, “Why are you standing there looking at each other?”

## Genesis 42:2

Jacob said, “I hear Egypt has grain.

Go and buy some for us so we can stay alive.”

## Genesis 42:3

Ten of Joseph’s brothers went to Egypt to buy grain.

## Genesis 42:4

Jacob kept Joseph’s brother Benjamin at home.

“I am afraid something might happen to him,” Jacob said.

## Genesis 42:5

The famine had reached Canaan, where Jacob lived.

So his sons joined the other people coming to buy grain.

## Joseph knows his brothers

## Genesis 42:6

Joseph governed Egypt. He sold grain to its people.

His brothers came and bowed with their faces to the ground.

## Genesis 42:7

Joseph knew his brothers at once. He treated them like strangers.

He spoke harshly. “Where do you come from?”

They answered, “From Canaan. We came to buy food.”

## Genesis 42:8

Joseph recognized his brothers. They did not recognize him.

## Genesis 42:9

Joseph remembered his dreams about them.

“You are spies!” he said.

“You have come to find out where our land is weak.”

## Genesis 42:10

They answered, “No, my lord. We came to buy food.

## Genesis 42:11

We all have the same father. We are honest men.

We are not spies.”

## Genesis 42:12

Joseph said, “You came to look for weak places in our land.”

## Genesis 42:13

They answered, “There are twelve brothers in our family.

Our father lives in Canaan. Our youngest brother is with him.

One of our brothers is gone.”

**Pause and answer**

Who recognized whom when the brothers bowed down?

## Joseph tests what they say

## Genesis 42:14

Joseph said, “It is as I said. You are spies!

## Genesis 42:15

I will test your story. I swear by Pharaoh’s life.

You cannot leave unless your youngest brother comes here.

## Genesis 42:16

Send one man to get your brother.

The rest of you will stay in prison.

Then I will know whether your words are true.

If you have lied, I swear by Pharaoh’s life that you are spies!”

## Genesis 42:17

Joseph kept them all in prison for three days.

## Genesis 42:18

On the third day Joseph said, “I fear God.

Do what I say and you will live.

## Genesis 42:19

If you are honest, leave one brother here in prison.

The rest can go. Carry grain home to your hungry families.

## Genesis 42:20

Then bring your youngest brother to me.

That will prove you told the truth and save your lives.”

The brothers agreed.

**Pause and answer**

Who did Joseph want them to bring back?

## The brothers remember how Joseph begged for help

## Genesis 42:21

The brothers said to each other, “We deserve this because of what we did to our brother.

We saw how much he suffered. He begged for help.

We would not listen. Now we are in trouble.”

## Genesis 42:22

Reuben answered, “I told you not to wrong the boy.

You would not listen. Now we must answer for his life.”

## Genesis 42:23

The brothers spoke through an interpreter when they talked to Joseph.

They did not know Joseph could understand their words.

**Reading help**

An interpreter speaks both languages and tells each person what the other said.

## Genesis 42:24

Joseph turned away and cried. Then he turned back and spoke to them.

He chose Simeon to stay. He had Simeon tied up while his brothers watched.

**Pause and answer**

What did the brothers think Joseph needed an interpreter for?

## One brother finds his silver

## Genesis 42:25

Joseph told his workers to fill the sacks with grain.

Each man’s silver was to go back into his sack.

Joseph also told them to give the men food for the journey.

The workers did as Joseph said.

## Genesis 42:26

The brothers loaded the grain on their donkeys and left.

## Genesis 42:27

They stopped for the night. One brother opened his sack to feed his donkey.

Right at the top he saw his silver.

## Genesis 42:28

“Someone put my silver back!” he told his brothers.

“Here it is in my sack!”

They shook with fear and asked each other, “What has God done to us?”

**Pause and answer**

Who gave the order to put the silver back?

## The brothers tell Jacob about the governor

## Genesis 42:29

The brothers reached their father Jacob in Canaan.

They told him everything that had happened.

## Genesis 42:30

“The man who governs Egypt spoke harshly to us.

He accused us of being spies.

## Genesis 42:31

We told him, ‘We are honest men. We are not spies.

## Genesis 42:32

We are twelve brothers with one father.

One brother is gone. The youngest is with our father in Canaan.’

## Genesis 42:33

The governor said, ‘I want to know if you are honest.

Leave one brother with me.

Take food home to your hungry families.

## Genesis 42:34

Bring your youngest brother back to me.

Then I will know you are telling the truth about who you are.

I will give your brother back. You can trade here.’”

**Pause and answer**

What did the brothers say the governor would do for Simeon?

## Jacob fears losing Benjamin

## Genesis 42:35

The men emptied their sacks.

Each man found his own bag of silver inside.

Jacob and his sons saw the silver and were frightened.

## Genesis 42:36

Jacob said, “You are taking my sons away from me!

Joseph is gone. Simeon is gone too.

Now you want to take Benjamin. Everything is going against me!”

## Genesis 42:37

Reuben told Jacob, “Put Benjamin in my care.

I will bring him back to you.

If I fail, you may kill my two sons.”

## Genesis 42:38

Jacob answered, “My son will not go with you.

His brother is dead. Benjamin is the only one left.

I am an old man. If harm comes to him on the journey, I will go down to Sheol in sorrow.”

**Reading help**

Joseph and Benjamin had the same mother, Rachel.

Jacob believed Joseph was dead. Sheol means the place of the dead.

| Simeon | A prisoner in Egypt |
| Benjamin | At home with Jacob in Canaan |
