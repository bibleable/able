# Genesis 33 — Esau runs to welcome Jacob

**About this chapter**

Jacob sees his brother Esau coming with 400 men.

Jacob steps ahead of his family to meet him.

## The brothers meet

## Genesis 33:1

Jacob looked up. Esau was coming with 400 men.

Jacob gathered the children around their mothers.

The mothers were Leah, Rachel, and the two women servants.

## Genesis 33:2

The two servants and their children went first.

Next came Leah and her children.

Rachel and Joseph came last.

## Genesis 33:3

Jacob went ahead of them all.

As he came toward Esau, Jacob bowed to the ground seven times.

## Genesis 33:4

Esau ran to meet Jacob.

He put his arms around Jacob’s neck and kissed him.

Both brothers cried.

**Pause and answer**

How did Esau greet Jacob?

## Esau meets Jacob’s family

## Genesis 33:5

Esau looked up and saw the women and children.

“Who are these people with you?” he asked.

Jacob answered, “God has been kind to me, your servant.

These are the children God has given me.”

## Genesis 33:6

The two women servants came forward with their children.

They bowed down.

## Genesis 33:7

Then Leah and her children came forward and bowed.

Last of all, Joseph and Rachel came and bowed.

**Pause and answer**

Who came forward with Rachel?

## Jacob asks Esau to accept his gift

## Genesis 33:8

Esau asked, “Why did you send all those herds to meet me?”

Jacob said, “My lord, I hoped you would welcome me.”

## Genesis 33:9

Esau said, “My brother, I already have plenty.

Keep what is yours.”

## Genesis 33:10

Jacob urged him, “Please! You have welcomed me so kindly.

Please take this gift from me.

Seeing your face is like seeing the face of God.”

## Genesis 33:11

Jacob said, “Please accept the gift I sent you.

God has been kind to me. I have everything I need.”

Jacob kept asking until Esau accepted the gift.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Jacob want Esau to accept his gift?

## Jacob asks to travel slowly

## Genesis 33:12

Esau said, “Let us go. I will lead the way.”

## Genesis 33:13

Jacob answered, “My lord, you know the children tire easily.

The sheep and cows are feeding their young.

I must look after them.

If we rush them for even one day, the animals will all die.

## Genesis 33:14

Please go ahead of me, your servant.

I will travel as slowly as the animals and children need.

I will come to you in Seir.”

## Genesis 33:15

Esau offered, “Let me leave some of my men with you.”

Jacob answered, “Why do that, my lord?

Your kindness is enough for me.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did Jacob want to travel slowly?

## Jacob makes a home in Canaan

## Genesis 33:16

That day Esau started back to Seir.

## Genesis 33:17

Jacob went to Succoth. He built a house for himself.

He also built shelters for his animals.

That is why the place had the name Succoth.

**Reading help**

Succoth means “shelters.”

## Genesis 33:18

Jacob had traveled from Paddan-aram.

He reached the city of Shechem safely, in Canaan.

He set up camp just outside the city.

## Genesis 33:19

Jacob bought the land where he had put his tent.

He paid 100 pieces of silver to the sons of Hamor.

Hamor was the father of a man named Shechem.

## Genesis 33:20

Jacob built an altar there.

He called it El-Elohe-Israel.

**Reading help**

An altar is a place to give offerings to God.

The name Jacob chose means “God, the God of Israel.”

Israel was the name Jacob received before he met Esau.
