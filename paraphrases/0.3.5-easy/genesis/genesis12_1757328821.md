# Genesis 12 — Abram leaves Haran

**About this chapter**

The LORD makes a promise to Abram.

Follow Abram and Sarai as they travel to Canaan and Egypt.

## The LORD calls Abram

## Genesis 12:1

The LORD said to Abram, “Leave your country and your relatives.

Leave your father’s household.

Go to the land I will show you.

## Genesis 12:2

“I will make a great nation from your family.

I will bless you and make your name great.

You will be a blessing to others.

## Genesis 12:3

“I will bless those who bless you.

I will curse those who curse you.

Through you I will bless every family on earth.”

**Pause and answer**

Whom will the LORD bless through Abram?

## Abram reaches Canaan

## Genesis 12:4

Abram went as the LORD told him.

Lot went with him.

Abram was 75 years old when he left Haran.

## Genesis 12:5

Abram took his wife Sarai and his nephew Lot.

They took everything they owned and the people they had gathered in Haran.

They set out for Canaan and arrived there.

## Genesis 12:6

Abram traveled through Canaan to the oak tree of Moreh at Shechem.

The Canaanite people already lived in the land.

**Pause and answer**

Who already lived in Canaan?

## Abram builds altars

## Genesis 12:7

The LORD appeared to Abram.

He said, “I will give this land to your descendants.”

Abram built an altar there to the LORD who had appeared to him.

**Reading help**

Descendants are a person’s children and the generations that follow them. An altar is a place for giving an offering to God.

## Genesis 12:8

Then Abram went to the hills east of Bethel.

He set up his tent between Bethel and Ai.

Bethel lay to the west and Ai to the east.

There Abram built an altar and called on the LORD.

**Reading help**

![A tent beside a house.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

Point to the tent. Abram could take his tent along when he moved.

## Genesis 12:9

Abram kept traveling toward the Negev.

**Pause and answer**

Who promised land to Abram’s descendants?

## Abram asks Sarai to say she is his sister

## Genesis 12:10

There was a famine in the land.

Food was so scarce that Abram went down to Egypt to live for a while.

## Genesis 12:11

As they neared Egypt, Abram told Sarai, “I know you are beautiful.

## Genesis 12:12

“When the Egyptians see you, they will say you are my wife.

Then they will kill me and let you live.

## Genesis 12:13

“Please say you are my sister.

Then they will treat me well because of you.

They will let me live.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did Abram leave Canaan for Egypt?

## Sarai goes to the palace

## Genesis 12:14

When Abram entered Egypt, the Egyptians saw how beautiful Sarai was.

## Genesis 12:15

Pharaoh’s officials saw Sarai and praised her beauty to Pharaoh.

They took Sarai to his palace.

**Reading help**

Pharaoh was the title of the king of Egypt.

## Genesis 12:16

Pharaoh treated Abram well because of Sarai.

Abram received sheep, cattle, male and female donkeys, and camels.

He also gained men and women as servants.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the officials take Sarai?

## Pharaoh calls for Abram

## Genesis 12:17

Because of Sarai, Abram’s wife, the LORD sent terrible plagues on Pharaoh and his household.

## Genesis 12:18

Pharaoh called Abram to him.

“What have you done to me?” he asked.

“Why didn’t you tell me Sarai was your wife?

## Genesis 12:19

“You said she was your sister.

That is why I took her as my wife.

Here is your wife.

Take her and go!”

## Genesis 12:20

Pharaoh gave his men orders about Abram.

They sent Abram away with his wife and everything he owned.

**Pause and answer**

Who went with Abram when he left Egypt?
