# Genesis 19 — Lot leaves Sodom

**About this chapter**

Two angels visit Lot in Sodom.

They lead Lot and his family out of danger.

## Lot welcomes two visitors

## Genesis 19:1

Two angels reached Sodom that evening.

Lot was sitting at the city gate.

Lot stood to greet them.

He bowed with his face to the ground.

## Genesis 19:2

Lot said, “My lords, please stay at my house.

You can wash your feet and spend the night.

Then you can leave early tomorrow.”

The angels answered, “We will sleep in the town square.”

## Genesis 19:3

Lot kept asking them to come.

So the angels went home with Lot.

Lot made a feast and baked bread without yeast.

The angels ate the meal.

## A crowd surrounds Lot’s house

## Genesis 19:4

Before bedtime, all the men of Sodom gathered.

Young men and old men surrounded the house.

## Genesis 19:5

They shouted to Lot, “Where are tonight’s visitors?

Send them outside. We want to force them to have sex with us.”

## Genesis 19:6

Lot went outside to speak to the men.

He closed the door behind him.

## Genesis 19:7

Lot begged them, “Please, my brothers!

Do not do this evil thing!

## Genesis 19:8

I have two daughters who have never had sex.

Let me bring them out to you.

You can do what you want to them.

Please leave my guests alone.

They came to my house for protection.”

## Genesis 19:9

The crowd shouted, “Move away!

You came here from another land.

Now you act like our judge!

We will hurt you even more than your guests.”

The men pushed against Lot.

They moved forward to break down the door.

## Genesis 19:10

The angels reached outside and pulled Lot in.

Then they shut the door.

## Genesis 19:11

The angels made the men outside blind.

Young and old kept feeling for the door.

They searched until they wore themselves out.

## The angels tell Lot to leave

## Genesis 19:12

The angels asked Lot, “Who else belongs to your family here?

Do you have sons, daughters, or sons-in-law here?

Bring your whole family out of the city.

## Genesis 19:13

We are about to destroy this place.

The LORD has heard many cries against its people.

So the LORD sent us to destroy it.”

## Genesis 19:14

Lot went to the men his daughters would marry.

He said, “Get up! Leave this place!

The LORD is about to destroy the city.”

The men thought Lot was joking.

## Genesis 19:15

At dawn, the angels urged Lot to hurry.

“Get up! Take your wife and your two daughters.

Leave now, or you will die when the city faces punishment.”

## Genesis 19:16

Lot still waited.

The LORD cared deeply about Lot and his family.

So the angels took each one by the hand.

They led all four safely outside the city.

## Lot asks to go to a small town

## Genesis 19:17

Outside the city, one angel said, “Run for your lives!

Keep looking ahead. Do not look back.

Do not stop on the flat land here.

Run to the mountains, or you will die!”

## Genesis 19:18

Lot answered, “Please, my lords!

## Genesis 19:19

You have been very kind to me.

You have saved my life.

But I cannot reach the mountains in time.

I will die on the way.

## Genesis 19:20

Look! I can reach that little town nearby.

Please let me run there. It is so small!

Then I will live.”

## Genesis 19:21

The angel answered, “I will do what you ask.

I will spare that town.

## Genesis 19:22

Hurry there! I must wait until you reach it.”

That is how the town got its name, Zoar.

## Abraham sees smoke rising

## Genesis 19:24

Then the LORD sent sulfur and fire from heaven.

They fell like rain on Sodom and Gomorrah.

## Genesis 19:25

The LORD destroyed those cities and the whole plain.

The people in the cities died.

Everything growing there died too.

## Genesis 19:26

Lot’s wife looked back.

She became a tall column of salt.

## Genesis 19:27

Early the next morning, Abraham got up.

He went back to the place where he had stood before the LORD.

## Genesis 19:28

Abraham looked toward Sodom, Gomorrah, and the whole plain.

Smoke rose from the land.

It looked like smoke from a great oven.

## Genesis 19:29

When God destroyed those cities, God remembered Abraham.

God led Lot safely away from the cities where Lot had lived.

## Lot and his daughters live in a cave

## Genesis 19:30

Lot was afraid to stay in Zoar.

He left with his two daughters.

They lived in a cave in the mountains.

## Genesis 19:31

One day the older daughter told her sister, “Our father is old.

People everywhere have children together.

But there is no man here to have children with us.

## Genesis 19:32

Let us give our father wine until he is drunk.

Then we can have sex with him.

That way our father’s family will continue.”

## Genesis 19:33

That night the daughters got their father drunk.

The older daughter had sex with him.

Lot did not know when she lay down.

He did not know when she got up.

## Genesis 19:34

The next day the older sister said, “Last night I had sex with our father.

Tonight let us get him drunk again.

Then you can have sex with him.

That way our father’s family will continue.”

## Genesis 19:35

That night they got Lot drunk again.

The younger daughter had sex with him.

Lot did not know when she lay down.

He did not know when she got up.

## Genesis 19:36

Both daughters became pregnant.

Lot was the father of both babies.

## Genesis 19:37

The older daughter had a son named Moab.

The people called Moabites came from his family.

## Genesis 19:38

The younger daughter had a son named Ben-ammi.

The people called Ammonites came from his family.
