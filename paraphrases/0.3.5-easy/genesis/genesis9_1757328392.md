# Genesis 9 — God puts a rainbow in the clouds

**About this chapter**

God speaks to Noah and his sons after the flood.

He tells them what the rainbow will mean.

## God blesses Noah’s family

## Genesis 9:1

God blessed Noah and his sons.

He said, “Have children and grow in number.

Fill the earth.

## Genesis 9:2

“The animals on land will fear you.

So will the birds, the crawling creatures and the fish.

I have put them in your hands.

## Genesis 9:3

“Every creature that lives and moves can be food for you.

I gave you green plants before.

Now I give you all these creatures too.

**Pause and answer**

What could Noah’s family now eat as well as plants?

## God calls people to account for life

## Genesis 9:4

“But do not eat meat with its lifeblood still in it.

## Genesis 9:5

“If a person or animal takes your life, I will require its life in return.

Anyone who kills another person must answer to me.

## Genesis 9:6

“If someone sheds a person’s blood, another person will shed the killer’s blood.

For God made human beings in his own image.

## Genesis 9:7

“As for you, have children and grow in number.

Spread across the earth and fill it.”

**Pause and answer**

Why does God say a human life matters?

## A promise to people and animals

## Genesis 9:8

Then God spoke to Noah and the sons with him.

## Genesis 9:9

“I am making my covenant with you.

It is also for those who come after you in your family.

**Reading help**

A covenant is a binding agreement. Here God makes a promise to Noah’s family and all living creatures.

## Genesis 9:10

“It includes every living creature that came out of the ark with you.

It includes the birds, the farm animals and the wild animals.

## Genesis 9:11

“This is my covenant with you.

Never again will floodwaters wipe out all life.

Never again will a flood destroy the earth.”

**Pause and answer**

Which creatures does God include in his covenant?

## The rainbow is the sign

## Genesis 9:12

God said, “I am giving you a sign of my covenant.

This covenant is for you and every living creature with you.

It is for all generations to come.

## Genesis 9:13

“I have put my rainbow in the clouds.

It is the sign of my covenant with the earth.

**Reading help**

🌈 A rainbow is a band of colors in the sky. Picture it in the clouds as you read God’s promise.

## Genesis 9:14

“When I bring clouds over the earth, the rainbow will appear in them.

## Genesis 9:15

“Then I will remember my covenant with you and every kind of living creature.

Never again will a flood wipe out all life.

## Genesis 9:16

“I will see the rainbow in the clouds.

I will remember my lasting covenant with every living creature on earth.”

## Genesis 9:17

God told Noah, “The rainbow is the sign of the covenant I have made with every creature on earth.”

**Pause and answer**

What will God remember when he sees the rainbow?

## Noah and his sons

## Genesis 9:18

Shem, Ham and Japheth were Noah’s sons who came out of the ark.

Ham was the father of Canaan.

## Genesis 9:19

From Noah’s three sons came the people who spread across the whole earth.

## Genesis 9:20

Noah was a farmer.

He planted a vineyard, a field of grapevines.

## Genesis 9:21

Noah drank wine from his grapes and became drunk.

Inside his tent he took off his clothes.

## Genesis 9:22

Ham, Canaan’s father, saw his father naked.

Ham went outside and told his two brothers.

## Genesis 9:23

Shem and Japheth put a piece of clothing across their shoulders.

They walked backward into the tent and covered their father.

They kept their faces turned away so they would not see him naked.

**Pause and answer**

How did Shem and Japheth cover their father?

## Noah speaks about his family

## Genesis 9:24

Noah woke up after drinking the wine.

He learned what his youngest son had done to him.

## Genesis 9:25

Noah said, “May Canaan be under a curse!

May he be the lowest servant among his brothers.”

## Genesis 9:26

Noah also said, “Praise the LORD, the God of Shem!

May Canaan serve Shem.

## Genesis 9:27

“May God give Japheth more land.

May he live in Shem’s tents, and may Canaan serve him.”

## Genesis 9:28

Noah lived 350 more years after the flood.

## Genesis 9:29

Noah lived 950 years in all.

Then he died.

**Pause and answer**

How long did Noah live after the flood?
