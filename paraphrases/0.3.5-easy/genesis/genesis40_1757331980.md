# Genesis 40 — Joseph explains two dreams in prison

**About this chapter**

Two men in prison have dreams on the same night.

Joseph listens to each man and explains his dream.

## Two of Pharaoh’s workers go to prison

## Genesis 40:1

Some time later, two men upset the king of Egypt.

One served the king his drinks. The other baked his bread.

## Genesis 40:2

Pharaoh, the king, was angry with both men.

They were his chief cupbearer and his chief baker.

## Genesis 40:3

Pharaoh put the men in prison at the guard captain’s house.

Joseph was a prisoner there too.

## Genesis 40:4

The guard captain put Joseph in charge of the two men.

Joseph looked after them. They stayed in prison for some time.

## Genesis 40:5

One night the cupbearer and the baker each had a dream.

Each dream had its own meaning.

**Pause and answer**

Who looked after the two men in prison?

## Joseph sees that the men are upset

## Genesis 40:6

Joseph came to see them in the morning.

He saw how upset they were.

## Genesis 40:7

Joseph asked the two men, “Why do you look so sad today?”

## Genesis 40:8

They answered, “We both had dreams.

There is nobody here to explain them.”

Joseph said, “It is God who gives the meaning of dreams.

Tell me what you dreamed.”

**Pause and answer**

Who did Joseph say gives dreams their meaning?

## The cupbearer dreams about grapes

## Genesis 40:9

The chief cupbearer told Joseph, “In my dream I saw a grapevine.

## Genesis 40:10

It had three branches. Buds appeared and flowers opened.

Then bunches of grapes grew ripe.

## Genesis 40:11

I had Pharaoh’s cup in my hand.

I picked the grapes and squeezed the juice into his cup.

Then I put the cup in Pharaoh’s hand.”

## Genesis 40:12

Joseph answered, “This is what your dream means.

The three branches stand for three days.

## Genesis 40:13

Within three days Pharaoh will give you your job back.

You will put the cup in his hand again.

You will serve him as you did before.

## Genesis 40:14

When things go well for you, please remember me.

Be kind to me. Tell Pharaoh about me.

Ask him to let me out of prison.

## Genesis 40:15

People took me by force from the land of the Hebrews.

Here in Egypt I have done nothing to deserve prison.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Joseph ask the cupbearer to do for him?

## The baker dreams about baskets

## Genesis 40:16

The chief baker heard the good news for the cupbearer.

He told Joseph, “I had a dream too.

I carried three baskets of white bread on my head.

## Genesis 40:17

The top basket held all kinds of baked food for Pharaoh.

Birds were eating the food from that basket.”

## Genesis 40:18

Joseph answered, “This is what your dream means.

The three baskets stand for three days.

## Genesis 40:19

Within three days Pharaoh will have your head cut off.

He will hang your body on a tree.

Then birds will eat the flesh of your body.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the three baskets stand for?

## Pharaoh decides what happens to the men

## Genesis 40:20

The third day was Pharaoh’s birthday.

He held a feast for all his officials.

Before them all, Pharaoh singled out the chief cupbearer and the chief baker.

## Genesis 40:21

Pharaoh gave the cupbearer his job back.

Once again he put the cup in Pharaoh’s hand.

## Genesis 40:22

Pharaoh had the chief baker hanged.

It happened just as Joseph had explained.

## Genesis 40:23

The chief cupbearer forgot all about Joseph.

| Man | What he saw in his dream | What happened on the third day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cupbearer | Three branches on a grapevine | Pharaoh gave him his job back. |
| Baker | Three baskets on his head | Pharaoh had him hanged. |
