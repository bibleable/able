# Genesis 7 — Noah and his family enter the ark

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Noah when the rain will come.

Follow what happens to the ark as the water rises.

## God tells Noah which animals to bring

## Genesis 7:1

The LORD told Noah, “Go into the ark with your whole family.

Among the people living now, I have seen that you do what is right.

## Genesis 7:2

“Take seven pairs of each kind of clean animal.

Take one pair of each kind of unclean animal.

Each pair is a male and its mate.

## Genesis 7:3

“Take seven pairs of each kind of bird, male and female.

Then their young can live throughout the earth.

**Reading help**

Clean and unclean name two groups of animals. Noah would later offer clean animals to God.

## Genesis 7:4

“Seven days from now I will send rain.

It will fall for forty days and forty nights.

I will wipe from the earth every living thing I made.”

## Genesis 7:5

Noah did everything the LORD told him.

**Pause and answer**

How many pairs of each kind of bird was Noah to bring?

## People and animals enter

## Genesis 7:6

Noah was 600 years old when the flood came.

## Genesis 7:7

Noah and his wife went into the ark.

His sons and their wives went with them to escape the flood.

## Genesis 7:8

All kinds of creatures came.

There were clean and unclean animals.

There were birds and creatures that crawl along the ground.

## Genesis 7:9

They came to Noah and entered the ark in pairs.

Each pair was male and female, just as God had told Noah.

**Pause and answer**

How did the animals enter the ark?

## Water comes from below and above

## Genesis 7:10

After seven days the flood came upon the earth.

## Genesis 7:11

Noah was 600 years old.

It was the seventeenth day of the second month.

Water burst out from deep under the earth.

The sky opened like gates letting water rush through.

## Genesis 7:12

Rain fell on the earth for forty days and forty nights.

## Genesis 7:13

On the very day the flood began, Noah entered the ark.

His wife and his sons Shem, Ham and Japheth went in.

His sons’ three wives went in too.

## Genesis 7:14

With them came every kind of wild animal and farm animal.

Crawling creatures, birds and every kind of winged creature came too.

## Genesis 7:15

Every kind of creature that breathed came to Noah.

They went into the ark two by two.

## Genesis 7:16

Male and female went in, just as God had told Noah.

Then the LORD shut Noah in.

**Pause and answer**

Who shut Noah in?

## The water lifts the ark

## Genesis 7:17

For forty days the flood kept coming.

The rising water lifted the ark high above the ground.

## Genesis 7:18

The water rose higher and spread across the earth.

The ark floated on its surface.

## Genesis 7:19

At last the water covered all the high mountains under the sky.

## Genesis 7:20

The water rose fifteen cubits above the mountaintops.

**Reading help**

Fifteen cubits is about seven meters.

**Pause and answer**

What lifted the ark off the ground?

## Who remains alive?

## Genesis 7:21

Every living thing that moved on the earth outside the ark died.

The birds, farm animals, wild animals and swarming creatures died.

The people died too.

## Genesis 7:22

Everything on dry land that breathed through its nose died.

## Genesis 7:23

The flood swept away people, farm animals, crawling creatures and birds.

Only Noah and those with him in the ark remained alive.

## Genesis 7:24

The floodwaters covered the earth for 150 days.

**Reading help**

| What happened | How long? |
| --- | --- |
| From God’s warning to the flood | 7 days |
| Rain fell | 40 days and nights |
| Floodwaters covered the earth | 150 days |

The rainy days are part of the flood.

**Pause and answer**

Who stayed alive through the flood?
