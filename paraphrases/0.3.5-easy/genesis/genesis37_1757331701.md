# Genesis 37 — Joseph dreams, and his brothers sell him

**About this chapter**

Joseph tells his brothers about two dreams.

Later, his father sends him to see how his brothers are doing.

## Jacob gives Joseph a robe

## Genesis 37:1

Jacob lived in Canaan, where his father had lived.

## Genesis 37:2

Here is the story of Jacob’s family.

His son Joseph was 17. He helped care for the flock.

He worked with the sons of Bilhah and Zilpah, his father’s wives.

Joseph brought his father a bad report about these brothers.

## Genesis 37:3

Jacob, also called Israel, loved Joseph more than his other sons.

Jacob was already old when Joseph was born.

He made Joseph a robe of many colors.

## Genesis 37:4

The brothers saw that their father loved Joseph most.

They hated Joseph. They could not speak kindly to him.

**Pause and answer**

What gift did Jacob make for Joseph?

## Joseph tells his family about two dreams

## Genesis 37:5

Joseph had a dream and told his brothers about it.

They hated him even more.

## Genesis 37:6

Joseph said, “Listen to my dream.

## Genesis 37:7

We were in a field, tying grain stalks into bundles.

Suddenly my bundle stood up straight.

Your bundles gathered around mine and bowed down to it.”

## Genesis 37:8

His brothers asked, “Do you mean to be our king?

Are you really going to rule over us?”

His dream and his words made them hate him more.

## Genesis 37:9

Joseph told his brothers about a second dream.

“This time the sun, the moon, and eleven stars bowed down to me!”

## Genesis 37:10

Joseph told his father as well as his brothers.

Jacob scolded him. “What kind of dream is that?

Will your mother, your brothers, and I really bow down to you?”

## Genesis 37:11

His brothers felt jealous of Joseph.

His father kept thinking about what Joseph had said.

| Dream | What Joseph saw |
| --- | --- |
| In the field | His brothers’ grain bundles bowed to his bundle. |
| In the sky | The sun, moon, and eleven stars bowed to Joseph. |

**Pause and answer**

What did the brothers think the first dream meant?

## Joseph looks for his brothers

## Genesis 37:12

Later, the brothers took their father’s flocks to graze near Shechem.

## Genesis 37:13

Jacob told Joseph, “Your brothers are with the flocks at Shechem.

Get ready. I am sending you to them.”

Joseph answered, “I am ready.”

## Genesis 37:14

Jacob said, “See how your brothers and the flocks are doing.

Then come and tell me.”

Joseph left the valley of Hebron and reached Shechem.

## Genesis 37:15

A man found Joseph wandering in a field.

He asked, “What are you looking for?”

## Genesis 37:16

Joseph answered, “I am looking for my brothers.

Please tell me where they have taken the flocks to graze.”

## Genesis 37:17

The man said, “They left here.

I heard them say they were going to Dothan.”

Joseph went on and found his brothers in Dothan.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Joseph finally find his brothers?

## Reuben wants to bring Joseph home

## Genesis 37:18

The brothers saw Joseph while he was still far away.

Before he reached them, they planned to kill him.

## Genesis 37:19

They said to each other, “Here comes that dreamer!

## Genesis 37:20

Let us kill him and throw him into a pit.

We can say a wild animal ate him.

Then we will see what happens to his dreams!”

## Genesis 37:21

Reuben heard them and tried to save Joseph.

He said, “Let us leave him alive.”

## Genesis 37:22

Reuben said, “Do not kill him. Put him in this pit in the wilderness.

Do not hurt him.”

Reuben planned to rescue Joseph and bring him back to his father.

## Genesis 37:23

When Joseph reached them, his brothers took off his robe.

It was the robe of many colors.

## Genesis 37:24

They threw Joseph into the pit.

The pit was empty. There was no water in it.

**Pause and answer**

What did Reuben plan to do for Joseph?

## The brothers sell Joseph

## Genesis 37:25

The brothers sat down to eat.

They saw Ishmaelite traders coming from Gilead.

Their camels carried spices, balm, and myrrh to Egypt.

**Reading help**

Balm and myrrh are substances that come from plants.

## Genesis 37:26

Judah said, “What do we gain if we kill our brother and hide what we did?

## Genesis 37:27

Let us sell him to the Ishmaelites and leave him alive.

Joseph is our brother, part of our own family.”

His brothers agreed.

## Genesis 37:28

When the Midianite traders passed by, the brothers pulled Joseph out.

They sold him to the Ishmaelites for twenty shekels of silver.

The Ishmaelites took Joseph to Egypt.

**Reading help**

A shekel was a measure of weight for silver.

## Genesis 37:29

Reuben came back and found the pit empty.

He tore his clothes in distress.

## Genesis 37:30

Reuben went to his brothers. “The boy is gone!” he said.

“What am I going to do?”

**Pause and answer**

Where did the traders take Joseph?

## Jacob thinks Joseph has died

## Genesis 37:31

The brothers killed a young goat.

They dipped Joseph’s robe in the goat’s blood.

## Genesis 37:32

They sent the colorful robe to their father with a message.

“We found this. Look closely. Is it your son’s robe?”

## Genesis 37:33

Jacob knew the robe at once. “It belongs to my son!” he said.

“A wild animal must have torn Joseph apart and eaten him!”

## Genesis 37:34

Jacob tore his clothes and wrapped rough cloth around his waist.

For many days he grieved for Joseph.

## Genesis 37:35

His sons and daughters tried to comfort him.

Jacob refused their comfort. “I will grieve for my son until I go down to Sheol,” he said.

Jacob wept for Joseph.

**Reading help**

Sheol is a Bible word for the place of the dead.

## Genesis 37:36

Meanwhile, in Egypt, the Midianites sold Joseph to Potiphar.

Potiphar served Pharaoh, the king of Egypt.

He was the captain of Pharaoh’s guards.

Why did Jacob believe Joseph had died?
