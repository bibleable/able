# Genesis 47 — Joseph gives his family a home in Goshen

**About this chapter**

Jacob’s family finds a place to live in Egypt.

As the hunger continues, Egyptians come to Joseph for food.

## The brothers ask to live in Goshen

## Genesis 47:1

Joseph told Pharaoh, “My father and brothers have come from Canaan.

They brought their flocks, herds, and everything they own.

They are in Goshen now.”

## Genesis 47:2

Joseph chose five of his brothers to meet Pharaoh.

## Genesis 47:3

Pharaoh asked, “What work do you do?”

They answered, “Sir, we are shepherds, just as our fathers were.”

## Genesis 47:4

They told Pharaoh, “We have come to stay for a while.

The famine in Canaan is severe.

There is no grass for our flocks.

Please let us live in Goshen.”

## Genesis 47:5

Pharaoh told Joseph, “Your father and brothers have come to you.

## Genesis 47:6

You can settle them in the best part of Egypt.

Let them live in Goshen.

Choose any of them who are especially good with animals.

Put them in charge of my animals.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did the brothers want to leave Canaan?

## Jacob meets Pharaoh

## Genesis 47:7

Joseph brought his father Jacob to meet Pharaoh.

Jacob gave Pharaoh a blessing.

## Genesis 47:8

Pharaoh asked Jacob, “How old are you?”

## Genesis 47:9

Jacob answered, “I have traveled from place to place for 130 years.

My years have been few and hard.

My fathers lived and traveled longer than I have.”

## Genesis 47:10

Jacob blessed Pharaoh again. Then Jacob left.

## Genesis 47:11

Joseph did what Pharaoh had told him to do.

He gave his father and brothers land in the district of Rameses.

It was some of the best land in Egypt.

## Genesis 47:12

Joseph gave food to his father and his brothers.

He provided food for everyone in their families.

**Pause and answer**

How old was Jacob when he met Pharaoh?

## The people run out of money

## Genesis 47:13

The famine had left the whole region without food.

Both Egypt and Canaan suffered greatly.

## Genesis 47:14

People from Egypt and Canaan bought grain from Joseph.

Joseph collected all their money and took it to Pharaoh’s palace.

## Genesis 47:15

When the money in both lands ran out, the Egyptians came to Joseph.

“Give us food,” they said. “We have no money left.

Why should we die here in front of you?”

## Genesis 47:16

Joseph answered, “Bring me your animals.

I will give you food in exchange for them.”

## Genesis 47:17

They brought their horses, their flocks and herds, and their donkeys.

That year Joseph gave them food in exchange for all their animals.

**Pause and answer**

What did the people give Joseph after their money ran out?

## Pharaoh gains the fields

## Genesis 47:18

The next year the people came back to Joseph.

“Sir, you know our money is gone,” they said.

“Our animals are yours. Only our bodies and our land are left.

## Genesis 47:19

Why should we die and our fields lie empty?

Buy us and our land in exchange for food.

We will be Pharaoh’s slaves. The land will be his.

Give us seed so we can live and keep growing crops.”

## Genesis 47:20

The famine was so severe that the Egyptians sold their fields.

Joseph bought the land for Pharaoh. It now belonged to Pharaoh.

## Genesis 47:21

Joseph made the people serve Pharaoh as slaves throughout Egypt.

## Genesis 47:22

The priests kept their land. Pharaoh had given it to them.

He supplied their food, so they could keep their fields.

**Pause and answer**

Why could the priests keep their land?

## Joseph divides the harvest into five shares

## Genesis 47:23

Joseph told the people, “Today I bought you and your land for Pharaoh.

Here is seed. Plant it in the fields.

## Genesis 47:24

When you gather the crops, divide them into five equal shares.

Give one share to Pharaoh. Keep the other four.

Use some for seed. Feed yourselves, your households, and your children.”

![Five equal boxes show one share for Pharaoh and four for the growers.](../pictures/five-crop-shares.svg)

Look at the four shares together. The families keep these for food and seed.

## Genesis 47:25

The people said, “You have saved our lives!

You have shown us kindness. We will serve Pharaoh.”

## Genesis 47:26

Joseph made this a law in Egypt.

Pharaoh would receive one fifth of the harvest.

The law still stood when this account was written.

Only the priests kept land of their own.

| Who received the harvest? | How many of the five equal shares? |
| --- | --- |
| Pharaoh | 1 share |
| The people who grew the crops | 4 shares for seed and food |

## Jacob asks Joseph for a promise

## Genesis 47:27

The Israelites settled in Goshen in Egypt.

They gained property there. Their families grew very large.

## Genesis 47:28

Jacob lived in Egypt for 17 years.

He lived to be 147 years old.

## Genesis 47:29

As Jacob came near death, he called for Joseph.

“Please show me kindness and keep your word,” Jacob said.

“Put your hand under my thigh and promise me this.

Do not bury me in Egypt.

## Genesis 47:30

When I die, carry me out of Egypt.

Bury me where my fathers are buried.”

Joseph said, “I will do what you ask.”

## Genesis 47:31

“Give me your solemn promise,” Jacob said.

Joseph gave his promise.

Then Jacob bowed in worship at the head of his bed.
