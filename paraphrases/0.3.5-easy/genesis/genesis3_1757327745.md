# Genesis 3 — The man and the woman hide from God

**About this chapter**

A snake questions the woman about God’s words.

Listen for what each person says when God calls.

## The snake questions God’s words

## Genesis 3:1

The snake was more cunning than any wild animal the LORD God had made.

It asked the woman, “Did God really tell you that you must not eat from any tree in the garden?”

## Genesis 3:2

The woman told the snake, “We may eat fruit from the garden trees.

## Genesis 3:3

“But God told us about the tree in the middle of the garden.

He said we must not eat its fruit or touch it.

If we do, we will die.”

## Genesis 3:4

The snake told her, “You certainly will not die!

## Genesis 3:5

“God knows what will happen when you eat it.

Your eyes will open.

You will be like God, knowing good and evil.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the snake say would happen if they ate the fruit?

## They eat the fruit and hide

## Genesis 3:6

The woman saw that the fruit looked good to eat.

The tree was beautiful, and she wanted wisdom.

She took some fruit and ate it.

Her husband was with her.

She gave him some, and he ate it.

## Genesis 3:7

Then both their eyes opened.

They realized they were naked.

They sewed fig leaves together to cover themselves.

**Reading help**

Their eyes opening means they now understood something about themselves.

## Genesis 3:8

A breeze was blowing through the garden.

The man and his wife heard the LORD God as he walked there.

They hid from him among the trees.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the man and the woman hide?

## God asks what happened

## Genesis 3:9

The LORD God called to the man, “Where are you?”

## Genesis 3:10

The man answered, “I heard your voice in the garden.

I was afraid because I was naked.

So I hid.”

## Genesis 3:11

God asked, “Who told you that you were naked?

Did you eat from the tree I told you not to eat from?”

## Genesis 3:12

The man answered, “You gave me the woman.

She gave me fruit from the tree, and I ate it.”

## Genesis 3:13

Then the LORD God asked the woman, “What have you done?”

She replied, “The snake tricked me, and I ate.”

**Pause and answer**

Who did the woman say had tricked her?

## God speaks to the snake and the woman

## Genesis 3:14

The LORD God told the snake, “Because you did this, you are cursed above all farm animals and wild animals.

You will crawl on your belly.

You will eat dust all your life.

## Genesis 3:15

“I will make you and the woman enemies.

Your offspring and hers will be enemies too.

He will crush your head, and you will strike his heel.”

## Genesis 3:16

God told the woman, “I will greatly increase your pain when you give birth.

Having children will hurt.

You will long for your husband, and he will rule over you.”

**Pause and answer**

What did God say the snake would do all its life?

## Adam must work the ground

## Genesis 3:17

God told Adam, “You listened to your wife.

You ate fruit from the tree I forbade you to eat from.

Because of you, the ground is under a curse.

All your life you will work hard to get food from it.

## Genesis 3:18

“The ground will grow thorns and prickly weeds.

You will eat plants from the fields.

**Reading help**

![A stalk of grain.](../pictures/grain.svg)

Look at the grain. Picture Adam working to grow food among thorny plants.

## Genesis 3:19

“You will sweat as you work for your bread.

This will last until you return to the ground.

I made you from its dust.

You are dust and will return to dust.”

**Pause and answer**

What would grow among Adam’s food plants?

## The way to the tree of life

## Genesis 3:20

Adam named his wife Eve.

She would be the mother of all the living.

## Genesis 3:21

The LORD God made clothes from animal skins.

He dressed Adam and his wife.

## Genesis 3:22

The LORD God said, “The man has become like one of us.

He knows good and evil.

Now he might reach out for fruit from the tree of life.

If he eats it, he will live forever.”

## Genesis 3:23

So the LORD God sent the man out of Eden.

He had to work the ground from which God had made him.

## Genesis 3:24

After sending the man away, God set cherubim east of the garden.

A flaming sword turned in every direction there.

They guarded the way to the tree of life.

**Reading help**

Cherubim are heavenly beings. Here God gives them the work of guarding the way.

**Pause and answer**

Which tree did the guards protect?
