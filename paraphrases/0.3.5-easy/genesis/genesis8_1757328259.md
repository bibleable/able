# Genesis 8 — Noah waits for dry ground

**About this chapter**

From inside the ark, Noah watches for dry land.

Notice what the dove brings back.

## The ark comes to rest

## Genesis 8:1

God remembered Noah and all the animals with him in the ark.

God sent a wind across the earth.

The water began to go down.

## Genesis 8:2

Water stopped rushing up from deep underground.

The sky stopped pouring down rain.

## Genesis 8:3

The water kept going down.

By the end of 150 days, it was lower.

## Genesis 8:4

On day seventeen of the seventh month, the ark stopped floating.

It came to rest on the mountains of Ararat.

## Genesis 8:5

The water kept going down until the tenth month.

On the first day of that month, the mountaintops appeared.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the ark come to rest?

## Noah sends out two birds

## Genesis 8:6

Forty days later, Noah opened the window he had made in the ark.

## Genesis 8:7

Noah sent out a raven.

The raven flew back and forth until the water dried up.

## Genesis 8:8

Then Noah sent out a dove.

He wanted to know if the water had gone down enough to uncover the ground.

## Genesis 8:9

Water still covered the land.

The dove could find nowhere to rest her feet.

She flew back to Noah.

He reached out and took her back inside.

## Genesis 8:10

Noah waited seven more days.

Then he sent the dove out again.

## Genesis 8:11

That evening she came back with a fresh olive leaf in her beak.

She had picked it from a tree.

Now Noah knew the water had gone down.

## Genesis 8:12

Noah waited another seven days and sent the dove out.

This time she did not come back.

**Follow the dove**

| Trip | What Noah saw |
| --- | --- |
| First | The dove came back to the ark. |
| Second | She returned with an olive leaf. |
| Third | She did not return. |

**Pause and answer**

What did the dove bring back on her second trip?

## God tells Noah to leave the ark

## Genesis 8:13

Noah was now 601 years old.

On the first day of the first month, the floodwater had dried up.

Noah took the covering off the ark.

He could see that the surface of the ground was dry.

## Genesis 8:14

By day twenty-seven of the second month, the ground was dry all through.

## Genesis 8:15

Then God spoke to Noah.

## Genesis 8:16

“Come out of the ark with your wife.

Bring your sons and their wives.

## Genesis 8:17

“Bring out every living creature with you.

Bring the birds, farm animals and creatures that crawl.

Let them spread across the earth and have many young.”

## Genesis 8:18

So Noah left the ark with his wife, his sons and their wives.

## Genesis 8:19

Every kind of creature came out of the ark.

Birds, crawling creatures and all the other animals came out, kind by kind.

**Pause and answer**

Who told Noah when to leave the ark?

## Noah gives thanks and the LORD makes a promise

## Genesis 8:20

Noah built an altar to the LORD.

He chose some of every kind of clean animal and clean bird.

He burned them on the altar as gifts to the LORD.

**Reading help**

An altar is a place for giving an offering to God.

## Genesis 8:21

The LORD was pleased with the smell of the offering.

He said to himself, “People’s hearts turn toward evil from their youth.

Even so, I will never again curse the ground because of them.

I will never again destroy every living creature as I have done.

## Genesis 8:22

“As long as the earth lasts, planting and harvest will continue.

Cold and heat, summer and winter, day and night will continue.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the LORD say would continue as long as the earth lasts?
