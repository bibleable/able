# Genesis 38 — Judah recognizes what he gave Tamar

**About this chapter**

Tamar marries Judah’s oldest son.

Later, three things that belong to Judah help Tamar show who fathered her children.

## Judah has three sons

## Genesis 38:1

About that time, Judah moved away from his brothers.

He settled near Hirah, a man from Adullam.

## Genesis 38:2

There Judah met the daughter of Shua, a Canaanite.

Judah married her and had sex with her.

## Genesis 38:3

She became pregnant and had a son.

Judah named him Er.

## Genesis 38:4

She had another son and named him Onan.

## Genesis 38:5

At Chezib she had a third son.

She named him Shelah.

**Pause and answer**

What did Judah name his oldest son?

## Tamar loses her husband

## Genesis 38:6

Judah found a wife for Er, his oldest son.

Her name was Tamar.

## Genesis 38:7

The LORD saw that Er did evil.

So the LORD put Er to death.

## Genesis 38:8

Judah told Onan, “Have sex with your brother’s widow.

As his brother, you owe him this.

Have a child with Tamar to carry on your brother’s family.”

## Genesis 38:9

Onan knew the child would count as his brother’s child.

Whenever he had sex with Tamar, he let his semen fall on the ground.

He did this to avoid having a child for his brother.

## Genesis 38:10

The LORD saw that Onan’s action was evil.

So the LORD put Onan to death too.

## Genesis 38:11

Judah told Tamar, “Stay at your father’s house as a widow until Shelah grows up.”

Judah thought, “Shelah might die like his brothers.”

So Tamar went to live with her father.

**Reading help**

A widow is a woman whose husband has died.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Judah tell Tamar to wait for?

## Tamar waits beside the road

## Genesis 38:12

Much later, Judah’s wife, the daughter of Shua, died.

After Judah mourned her, he went to Timnah with his friend Hirah.

Judah’s workers were there cutting wool from his sheep.

## Genesis 38:13

Someone told Tamar, “Judah, your husband’s father, is going to Timnah.

He is going there for the sheep shearing.”

## Genesis 38:14

Shelah had grown up, but Judah had not let him marry Tamar.

Tamar took off her widow’s clothes.

She covered her face with a veil to hide who she was.

She sat at the entrance to Enaim, on the road to Timnah.

## Genesis 38:15

Judah saw her covered face.

He thought she was a prostitute.

**Reading help**

A prostitute is someone who has sex for payment.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Judah fail to recognize Tamar?

## Judah leaves three things with Tamar

## Genesis 38:16

Judah went over to Tamar. He did not know who she was.

“Let me have sex with you,” he said.

Tamar asked, “What will you give me?”

## Genesis 38:17

Judah said, “I will send a young goat from my flock.”

Tamar answered, “Leave me something to keep until you send it.”

## Genesis 38:18

Judah asked, “What should I leave?”

Tamar answered, “Your seal, your cord, and your walking stick.”

Judah gave them to her. Then he had sex with her.

Tamar became pregnant by Judah.

**Reading help**

A seal made a mark that showed who owned it.

## Genesis 38:19

Tamar left. She took off the veil.

She put her widow’s clothes back on.

**Pause and answer**

Which three things did Judah leave with Tamar?

## Hirah looks for the woman

## Genesis 38:20

Judah sent Hirah with the young goat.

Hirah was to get Judah’s things back from the woman.

But Hirah could not find her.

## Genesis 38:21

Hirah asked the local men, “Where is the shrine prostitute who sat by the road at Enaim?”

They answered, “No shrine prostitute has been here.”

**Reading help**

A shrine is a place of worship.

Hirah thought the woman had a connection to a shrine.

## Genesis 38:22

Hirah went back and told Judah, “I could not find her.

The men there said no shrine prostitute had been there.”

## Genesis 38:23

Judah said, “Let her keep my things.

People will laugh at us if we keep looking.

I sent the goat, but you could not find her.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Hirah tell Judah when he came back?

## Judah admits he was wrong

## Genesis 38:24

About three months later, someone told Judah, “Tamar has been selling sex.

Now she is pregnant.”

Judah said, “Bring her out and burn her to death!”

## Genesis 38:25

As people brought Tamar out, she sent word to Judah.

“The man who owns these things made me pregnant,” she said.

“Look closely. Whose seal, cord, and walking stick are these?”

## Genesis 38:26

Judah recognized his things. He said, “Tamar has done what is right more than I have.

I did not let my son Shelah marry her.”

Judah never had sex with Tamar again.

**Pause and answer**

What wrong did Judah admit?

## Tamar gives birth to twins

## Genesis 38:27

When the time came for Tamar to give birth, she was carrying twins.

## Genesis 38:28

One baby put out a hand.

The midwife tied a bright red thread around his wrist.

“This one came out first,” she said.

## Genesis 38:29

The baby pulled his hand back. His brother was born first.

The midwife said, “You have broken out first!”

The family named him Perez.

## Genesis 38:30

Then the baby with the red thread was born.

The family named him Zerah.

| Baby | What happened at the birth |
| --- | --- |
| Perez | His whole body came out first. |
| Zerah | His hand came out first. He was born second. |
