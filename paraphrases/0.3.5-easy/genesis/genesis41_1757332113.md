# Genesis 41 — Joseph stores grain for the hungry years

**About this chapter**

Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, has two dreams.

Joseph explains them and helps Egypt prepare for hunger.

## Pharaoh dreams about cows and grain

## Genesis 41:1

Two full years passed. Then Pharaoh had a dream.

He was standing beside the Nile River.

## Genesis 41:2

Seven healthy, well-fed cows came out of the river.

They ate grass among the tall reeds.

## Genesis 41:3

Then seven sick, thin cows came out of the Nile.

They stood beside the healthy cows on the riverbank.

## Genesis 41:4

The thin cows ate the seven healthy cows.

Then Pharaoh woke up.

## Genesis 41:5

Pharaoh fell asleep again and had a second dream.

He saw seven full, ripe heads of grain on one stalk.

## Genesis 41:6

Then seven thin heads of grain grew.

The hot wind from the east had dried them out.

## Genesis 41:7

The thin heads swallowed the seven full heads.

Pharaoh woke up and realized he had been dreaming.

## Genesis 41:8

In the morning Pharaoh felt troubled.

He called all Egypt’s magicians and wise men.

He told them his dreams. Nobody could explain them.

**Pause and answer**

What happened to the healthy cows in Pharaoh’s dream?

## The cupbearer remembers Joseph

## Genesis 41:9

The chief cupbearer, who served Pharaoh his drinks, spoke up.

“Today I remember what I did wrong.

## Genesis 41:10

You were angry with me and the chief baker.

You sent us to prison under the guard captain’s care.

## Genesis 41:11

One night we each had a dream.

Each dream had its own meaning.

## Genesis 41:12

A young Hebrew man was there with us.

He worked for the guard captain.

We told him our dreams. He explained each one.

## Genesis 41:13

Everything happened as he said it would.

You gave me my job back. You had the baker hanged.”

## Genesis 41:14

Pharaoh sent for Joseph. The guards hurried him out of prison.

Joseph shaved and changed his clothes. Then he went to Pharaoh.

## Genesis 41:15

Pharaoh said, “Nobody can explain my dream.

I hear you can tell what a dream means.”

## Genesis 41:16

Joseph answered, “I cannot do that on my own.

God will give you the right answer.”

**Pause and answer**

Who did Joseph say would give Pharaoh the answer?

## Pharaoh tells Joseph what he saw

## Genesis 41:17

Pharaoh told Joseph, “In my dream I stood beside the Nile.

## Genesis 41:18

Seven healthy, well-fed cows came up from the river.

They began to graze among the reeds.

## Genesis 41:19

Then seven more cows came out, sick and thin.

I have never seen such ugly cows anywhere in Egypt!

## Genesis 41:20

Those thin cows ate the first seven cows.

## Genesis 41:21

Yet you would never know they had eaten anything.

They looked as ugly as before. Then I woke up.

## Genesis 41:22

I also dreamed I saw seven full, ripe heads of grain.

They grew on one stalk.

## Genesis 41:23

Seven more heads of grain grew after them.

They were thin and dry from the hot east wind.

## Genesis 41:24

The thin heads swallowed the seven full ones.

I told the magicians. None could explain it to me.”

**Pause and answer**

How did the thin cows look after they ate?

## Joseph explains both dreams

## Genesis 41:25

Joseph said, “Both dreams have the same meaning.

God has shown you what he is about to do.

## Genesis 41:26

The seven healthy cows stand for seven years.

The seven ripe heads of grain stand for those same years.

## Genesis 41:27

The seven thin cows stand for seven years of famine.

The seven dried heads of grain mean the same thing.

**Reading help**

A famine is a time when there is too little food for people to eat.

## Genesis 41:28

As I told you, God has shown you what he will do.

## Genesis 41:29

For seven years, food will grow in plenty throughout Egypt.

## Genesis 41:30

Then seven years of famine will come.

People will forget how much food they once had.

The famine will ruin the land.

## Genesis 41:31

The hunger will be so great that nobody will remember the good years.

## Genesis 41:32

You dreamed this in two ways because God has firmly decided it.

God will do it soon.”

| What Pharaoh saw | What it meant for Egypt |
| --- | --- |
| Seven healthy cows and seven full heads of grain | Seven years with plenty of food |
| Seven thin cows and seven dried heads of grain | Seven years with too little food |

**Pause and answer**

Why did both dreams have the same meaning?

## Joseph tells Pharaoh how to prepare

## Genesis 41:33

Joseph continued, “Choose a wise man who has good judgment.

Put him in charge of Egypt.

## Genesis 41:34

Choose officials to collect grain during the seven good years.

From every five equal shares of the harvest, collect one.

## Genesis 41:35

Give the officials authority to gather the extra food.

Let them store the grain safely in the cities.

## Genesis 41:36

That food will be ready for the seven years of famine.

Then the people of Egypt can survive.”

## Genesis 41:37

Pharaoh and all his officials liked the plan.

## Genesis 41:38

Pharaoh asked them, “Can we find anyone else like Joseph?

God’s Spirit is in him.”

**Pause and answer**

How much of each harvest did Joseph say to collect?

## Pharaoh puts Joseph in charge

## Genesis 41:39

Pharaoh told Joseph, “God has shown you all this.

Nobody else has your wisdom or your good judgment.

## Genesis 41:40

You will be in charge of my household.

My people must obey you.

Only I, the king, will have more power than you.”

## Genesis 41:41

Pharaoh said, “I put you in charge of all Egypt.”

## Genesis 41:42

Pharaoh took his royal ring off his finger.

He put it on Joseph’s finger.

He dressed Joseph in fine linen clothes.

He put a gold chain around Joseph’s neck.

## Genesis 41:43

Pharaoh had Joseph ride in his second royal chariot.

Men went ahead of him, calling, “Bow down!”

Pharaoh put Joseph over all Egypt.

## Genesis 41:44

Pharaoh told Joseph, “I am the king.

Throughout Egypt, nobody may act without your permission.”

## Genesis 41:45

Pharaoh gave Joseph the name Zaphenath-paneah.

He gave Joseph a wife named Asenath.

Her father was Potiphera, the priest of On.

Joseph took charge of all Egypt.

**Pause and answer**

Who still had more power than Joseph?

## Joseph gathers grain and names his sons

## Genesis 41:46

Joseph was 30 when he began serving Pharaoh.

He left the king and traveled throughout Egypt.

## Genesis 41:47

For seven years the fields produced great harvests.

## Genesis 41:48

Joseph collected the extra food during all seven years.

Each city stored grain from the fields around it.

## Genesis 41:49

The grain was like sand by the sea.

There was so much that Joseph stopped measuring it.

## Genesis 41:50

Before the famine, Asenath and Joseph had two sons.

Asenath was the daughter of Potiphera, the priest of On.

## Genesis 41:51

Joseph named his first son Manasseh.

He said, “God has made me forget all my troubles and everyone in my father’s house.”

## Genesis 41:52

Joseph named his second son Ephraim.

He said, “God has helped me thrive in the land where I suffered.”

**Pause and answer**

Where did Joseph store the grain from each area?

## People come to Egypt for food

## Genesis 41:53

Egypt’s seven years of great harvests ended.

## Genesis 41:54

The seven years of famine began, just as Joseph had said.

Every country had too little food.

But Egypt still had food stored throughout the land.

## Genesis 41:55

Soon people all over Egypt were very hungry.

They cried out to Pharaoh for food.

Pharaoh told them, “Go to Joseph. Do what he tells you.”

## Genesis 41:56

The famine spread across the land. Hunger in Egypt was severe.

Joseph opened all the storehouses and sold grain to the Egyptians.

## Genesis 41:57

People from every nation came to Joseph in Egypt to buy grain.

The famine was severe everywhere.

How did the stored grain help people during the famine?
