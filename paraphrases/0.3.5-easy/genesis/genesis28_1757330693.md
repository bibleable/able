# Genesis 28 — Jacob dreams of a ladder to heaven

**About this chapter**

Jacob leaves home to visit his mother’s family.

On the way, God speaks to Jacob in a dream.

## Isaac blesses Jacob before he leaves

## Genesis 28:1

Isaac called Jacob and blessed him.

He told Jacob, “Do not marry a Canaanite woman.

## Genesis 28:2

Go now to Paddan-aram.

Go to the home of Bethuel, your mother’s father.

Marry one of the daughters of your uncle Laban.

Laban is your mother’s brother.

## Genesis 28:3

May God Almighty bless you with many children.

May your family grow into many peoples.

## Genesis 28:4

May God give you the blessing he gave Abraham.

May your children and their families share that blessing.

You live here now as a stranger.

May this land God gave Abraham become yours.”

## Genesis 28:5

Isaac sent Jacob to Laban in Paddan-aram.

Laban was the son of Bethuel the Aramean.

Laban’s sister Rebekah was the mother of Jacob and Esau.

**Pause and answer**

Whose home did Isaac tell Jacob to visit?

## Esau marries Mahalath

## Genesis 28:6

Esau heard that Isaac had blessed Jacob.

Isaac had sent Jacob to Paddan-aram to find a wife.

He had told Jacob, “Do not marry a Canaanite woman.”

## Genesis 28:7

Esau heard that Jacob obeyed both his parents.

Jacob had gone to Paddan-aram.

## Genesis 28:8

Esau saw that his father did not want his sons to marry Canaanite women.

## Genesis 28:9

So Esau went to Ishmael, Abraham’s son.

Esau married Ishmael’s daughter Mahalath. She was Nebaioth’s sister.

Esau already had other wives.

**Pause and answer**

Who was Mahalath’s father?

## Jacob dreams of a ladder

## Genesis 28:10

Jacob left Beersheba and headed toward Haran.

## Genesis 28:11

When the sun went down, Jacob stopped for the night.

He put a stone under his head.

Then he lay down to sleep.

## Genesis 28:12

Jacob dreamed about a ladder.

Its feet rested on the ground. Its top reached heaven.

God’s angels went up and down the ladder.

## Genesis 28:13

The LORD stood at the top.

He said, “I am the LORD.

I am the God of your grandfather Abraham and your father Isaac.

You are lying on land I will give to you and your descendants.

## Genesis 28:14

Your descendants will be like the dust on the ground.

They will spread west and east, north and south.

Through you and your family I will bless every family on earth.

## Genesis 28:15

I am with you. I will watch over you wherever you go.

I will bring you back to this land.

I will stay with you until I have done everything I promised.”

**Reading help**

Dust has more tiny grains than anyone can count.

God was telling Jacob how large his family would grow.

**Pause and answer**

What did God promise to do wherever Jacob went?

## Jacob stands the stone upright

## Genesis 28:16

Jacob woke up. He said, “The LORD is here!

I did not know it.”

## Genesis 28:17

Jacob was afraid. He said, “What an amazing place!

This is the house of God!

This is the gate of heaven!”

## Genesis 28:18

Early the next morning, Jacob picked up the stone from under his head.

He stood it upright as a marker.

Then he poured oil on top of it.

## Genesis 28:19

Jacob called the place Bethel.

Until then, people had called the town Luz.

**Reading help**

Bethel means “house of God.”

| When | How Jacob used the stone |
| --- | --- |
| At night | He rested his head on it. |
| In the morning | He stood it upright and poured oil on it. |

**Pause and answer**

What did Jacob do with the stone in the morning?

## Jacob makes a promise

## Genesis 28:20

Jacob made a solemn promise.

He said, “If God stays with me and watches over me on this journey,

if he gives me food and clothes,

## Genesis 28:21

and if I reach my father’s home safely, then the LORD will be my God.

## Genesis 28:22

This stone I stood upright will mark God’s house.

God, I will give you a tenth of everything you give me.”

![One of ten equal squares is marked.](../pictures/one-tenth.svg)

Jacob promises God one share out of every ten.

**Reading help**

A tenth is one share out of ten equal shares.

**Pause and answer**

What share did Jacob promise to give God?
