# Genesis 4 — Cain and Abel

**About this chapter**

Two brothers bring gifts to the LORD.

When Cain grows angry, the LORD speaks to him.

## Two brothers bring gifts

## Genesis 4:1

Adam and his wife Eve slept together.

Eve became pregnant and gave birth to Cain.

She said, “With the LORD’s help, I have had a son.”

## Genesis 4:2

Later Eve gave birth to Cain’s brother Abel.

Abel cared for sheep.

Cain grew crops.

## Genesis 4:3

One day Cain brought the LORD some of his crops as a gift.

## Genesis 4:4

Abel brought the best parts from the first lambs born to his sheep.

The LORD was pleased with Abel and his gift.

## Genesis 4:5

The LORD was not pleased with Cain or his gift.

Cain became very angry.

His face showed how unhappy he was.

**Pause and answer**

What gift did Abel bring?

## The LORD warns Cain

## Genesis 4:6

The LORD asked Cain, “Why are you angry?

Why do you look so unhappy?

## Genesis 4:7

“If you do what is right, I will accept you.

If you refuse, sin crouches at your door.

It wants to take hold of you.

You must rule over it.”

**Reading help**

Picture an animal waiting to spring. The LORD uses this picture to warn Cain about sin.

## Genesis 4:8

Cain said to Abel, “Let us go to the field.”

There Cain attacked his brother Abel and killed him.

**Pause and answer**

What did the LORD tell Cain to do about sin?

## The LORD asks about Abel

## Genesis 4:9

The LORD asked Cain, “Where is your brother Abel?”

Cain answered, “I do not know!

Is it my job to watch over my brother?”

## Genesis 4:10

The LORD asked, “What have you done?

Your brother’s blood cries out to me from the ground.

**Reading help**

The blood crying out means the LORD knows Abel was killed and will answer that wrong.

## Genesis 4:11

“The ground opened its mouth to receive the blood you spilled.

Now you are under a curse.

You must leave that ground.

## Genesis 4:12

“When you work the soil, it will no longer give you crops.

You will be on the run, wandering from place to place.”

**Pause and answer**

What would happen when Cain worked the soil?

## A mark to protect Cain

## Genesis 4:13

Cain told the LORD, “This punishment is too much for me.

## Genesis 4:14

“Today you are driving me from this land.

I will be hidden from your face.

I will wander from place to place.

Whoever finds me will kill me.”

## Genesis 4:15

The LORD said, “That will not happen.

If anyone kills Cain, the punishment for it will be seven times as great.”

The LORD put a mark on Cain.

It would keep anyone who found Cain from killing him.

## Genesis 4:16

Cain went away from the LORD’s presence.

He settled in the land of Nod, east of Eden.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the LORD put a mark on Cain?

## Cain’s family

## Genesis 4:17

Cain and his wife slept together.

She became pregnant and gave birth to Enoch.

Cain built a city and named it after his son Enoch.

## Genesis 4:18

Enoch became the father of Irad.

Irad became the father of Mehujael.

Mehujael became the father of Methusael.

Methusael became the father of Lamech.

## Genesis 4:19

Lamech married two women, Adah and Zillah.

## Genesis 4:20

Adah gave birth to Jabal.

From Jabal came the people who lived in tents and raised animals.

**Reading help**

![A tent and a house.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

Find the tent. Jabal’s family lived in tents and raised animals.

## Genesis 4:21

Jabal’s brother was Jubal.

From Jubal came those who played the harp and flute.

## Genesis 4:22

Zillah gave birth to Tubal-cain.

He made all kinds of tools from bronze and iron.

His sister was Naamah.

**Pause and answer**

Who made tools from bronze and iron?

## Lamech speaks, and Eve has another son

## Genesis 4:23

Lamech said, “Adah and Zillah, listen to me.

My wives, hear what I say.

I killed a young man because he struck and wounded me.

## Genesis 4:24

“If Cain is avenged seven times, then Lamech will be avenged seventy-seven times.”

## Genesis 4:25

Adam and Eve slept together again.

Eve gave birth to a son and named him Seth.

She said, “Cain killed Abel.

God has given me another child in Abel’s place.”

## Genesis 4:26

Seth also had a son.

He named him Enosh.

At that time people began calling on the name of the LORD.

**Pause and answer**

What did Eve name her next son?
