# Esther 5 — Esther invites the king and Haman

**About this chapter**

Esther goes to the king and invites him to a feast. Haman goes too. On the way home, Haman sees Mordecai.

## The king welcomes Esther

## Esther 5:1

On the third day, Esther put on her royal robes.

She stood in the palace’s inner courtyard, across from the king’s rooms.

The king sat on his throne facing the entrance.

## Esther 5:2

The king saw Queen Esther and welcomed her.

He held out his gold scepter, his royal staff.

Esther came closer and touched its tip.

![A gold scepter with a rounded tip. The king held his scepter out to welcome Esther.](../pictures/esther-gold-scepter.svg)

## Esther 5:3

The king asked, “What is it, Queen Esther?

What would you like me to give you?

I will give you even half my kingdom.”

## A feast today and another tomorrow

## Esther 5:4

Esther answered, “If it pleases you, come to my feast today.

I have prepared it for you.

Please bring Haman too.”

## Esther 5:5

The king said, “Hurry and bring Haman.

Let us do what Esther asks.”

The king and Haman went to Esther’s feast.

## Esther 5:6

While they drank wine, the king asked Esther again.

“What do you want me to give you?

Ask me.

I will give you even half my kingdom.”

## Esther 5:7

Esther answered, “Here is what I ask.

## Esther 5:8

“If you are pleased with me and want to grant my request, come with Haman tomorrow.

I will prepare another feast for you both.

Then I will answer your question.”

When did Esther say she would answer the king?

## Haman sees Mordecai at the gate

## Esther 5:9

Haman left the feast feeling joyful.

At the king’s gate, Haman saw Mordecai sitting there.

Mordecai did not stand up or shake with fear.

Haman was furious with Mordecai.

## Esther 5:10

Haman held his anger in check and went home.

He called his friends and his wife, Zeresh.

## Esther 5:11

Haman boasted about his riches and his many sons.

He told them how the king had honored him.

The king had placed him above the other officials and servants.

## Haman tells his family and friends

## Esther 5:12

Haman said, “Queen Esther invited only me to join the king at her feast.

Tomorrow I will go with the king again.

## Esther 5:13

“But none of this makes me happy while I see Mordecai the Jew sitting at the king’s gate.”

## Esther 5:14

Zeresh and all Haman’s friends suggested putting up tall gallows.

These were for hanging a person.

They wanted the gallows fifty cubits high, about twenty-three metres.

They told Haman, “In the morning, ask the king to have Mordecai hanged.

Then enjoy the feast with the king.”

Haman liked their advice and had the gallows built.
