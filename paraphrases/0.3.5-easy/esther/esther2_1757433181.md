# Esther 2 — Esther becomes queen

**About this chapter**

Esther leaves the home where Mordecai raised her. After Esther becomes queen, Mordecai sends her news that the king needs to hear.

## The king looks for a new queen

## Esther 2:1

Later, King Xerxes grew calm.

He remembered Vashti, what she had done, and the order he had given against her.

## Esther 2:2

The king’s attendants had an idea.

They suggested searching for beautiful young women for the king.

They were to look for virgins, women who had never had sex.

## Esther 2:3

The attendants suggested choosing officials in every province.

The officials would bring the women to the fortress in Susa.

There the women would live in the harem.

This was the part of the palace where the women lived.

Hegai, the king’s official in charge, would care for them.

They would receive beauty treatments there.

## Esther 2:4

The attendants said, “Let the woman who pleases you become queen in Vashti’s place.”

The king liked their idea and put it into action.

## Mordecai raises Esther

## Esther 2:5

Mordecai, a Jewish man, lived in the fortress of Susa.

He belonged to the tribe of Benjamin.

His father was Jair, son of Shimei.

Shimei’s father was Kish.

## Esther 2:6

Mordecai’s family had come from Jerusalem.

Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, had taken captives from there.

He forced them from their homes along with Jeconiah, king of Judah.

## Esther 2:7

Mordecai had a cousin named Hadassah, also called Esther.

Esther was a beautiful young woman.

When her parents died, Mordecai took her into his home.

He raised her as his own daughter.

**Pause and answer**

Who raised Esther after her parents died?

## Esther goes to the palace

## Esther 2:8

When people heard the king’s order, officials brought many young women to Susa.

They brought Esther to the palace too.

Hegai took care of Esther and the other women.

## Esther 2:9

Hegai liked Esther and wanted to help her.

He quickly gave her beauty treatments and special food.

He chose seven women from the palace to serve her.

He moved them with Esther to the best rooms in the harem.

## Esther 2:10

Esther kept quiet about her family and her people.

Mordecai had asked her to keep this secret.

## Esther 2:11

Every day Mordecai walked outside the courtyard where the women lived.

He wanted to hear how Esther was.

He wanted to know what was happening to her.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Mordecai walk near the courtyard each day?

## The women prepare to meet the king

## Esther 2:12

Each woman had twelve months of beauty treatments before she visited King Xerxes.

For six months, the women used oil scented with myrrh.

For the next six months, they used perfumes and cosmetics.

![A small jar for perfume, with a narrow neck.](../pictures/perfume-jar.svg)

## Esther 2:13

When her turn came, each woman could ask for things to take along.

She could bring those things from the harem to the king’s palace.

## Esther 2:14

She went to the king in the evening.

In the morning, she went to a second harem.

Shaashgaz, a royal official, cared for the king’s concubines there.

Concubines were partners of the king with lower rank than a wife.

She would see the king again only if he liked her and called for her by name.

## Xerxes chooses Esther

## Esther 2:15

Esther’s father, Abihail, was Mordecai’s uncle.

Mordecai had adopted Esther as his daughter.

When Esther’s turn came, she listened to Hegai.

She asked only for what Hegai suggested.

Everyone who saw Esther liked her.

## Esther 2:16

Esther went to King Xerxes at his palace.

This happened in the seventh year of his rule.

It was Tebeth, the tenth month.

## Esther 2:17

The king loved Esther more than all the other women.

He favored her above all the others who had come to him.

Xerxes placed the royal crown on Esther’s head.

He made her queen in Vashti’s place.

## Esther 2:18

The king gave a great feast in Esther’s honor.

All his officials and servants came.

For a time he excused the provinces from paying taxes.

He also gave generous gifts.

## Mordecai hears a plan against the king

## Esther 2:19

The young women gathered a second time.

Mordecai was sitting at the king’s gate.

## Esther 2:20

Esther still kept her family and her people secret.

She followed Mordecai’s instructions just as she had done while growing up in his care.

## Esther 2:21

While Mordecai sat at the king’s gate, two guards grew angry.

These royal attendants, Bigthan and Teresh, guarded the entrance.

Together they planned to kill King Xerxes.

## Esther 2:22

Mordecai found out and told Queen Esther.

Esther warned the king.

She told him the news came from Mordecai.

## Esther 2:23

An investigation showed that Mordecai’s report was true.

Both guards died by hanging on the gallows.

In the king’s presence, a writer recorded what happened.

The record was called the Book of the Chronicles.
