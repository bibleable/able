# Esther 4 — Esther decides to go to the king

**About this chapter**

Mordecai asks Esther to speak for her people. Esther must decide whether to enter the king’s courtyard without an invitation.

## Mordecai grieves at the king’s gate

## Esther 4:1

Mordecai heard about the order to kill the Jews.

He tore his clothes and put on sackcloth and ashes.

Sackcloth was rough cloth people wore to show their sorrow.

Mordecai went into the middle of the city.

He cried out loudly in deep grief.

## Esther 4:2

Mordecai stopped at the king’s gate.

The law kept anyone wearing sackcloth from going through.

## Esther 4:3

The king’s order reached one province after another.

Wherever it went, Jewish people wept and cried out in grief.

They fasted, going without food.

Many lay down in sackcloth and ashes.

## Esther sends Hathach to Mordecai

## Esther 4:4

The women and men who served Esther told her about Mordecai.

Queen Esther was deeply upset.

She sent clothes for Mordecai to wear in place of the sackcloth.

Mordecai refused the clothes.

## Esther 4:5

Esther called Hathach, a palace attendant the king had assigned to her.

She sent Hathach to find out what was wrong.

She wanted to know why Mordecai was grieving.

## Esther 4:6

Hathach found Mordecai in the city square outside the king’s gate.

## Esther 4:7

Mordecai told Hathach everything that had happened to him.

Haman had offered silver for the king’s treasury to have the Jews killed.

Mordecai told Hathach exactly how much Haman had offered.

## Esther 4:8

Mordecai gave Hathach a copy of the order from Susa.

It called for the death of the Jews.

Mordecai asked Hathach to show it to Esther and explain it.

He urged Esther to go to the king.

She should beg the king to spare her people.

## Esther 4:9

Hathach went back to Esther.

He told her what Mordecai had said.

**Pause and answer**

What did Mordecai want Esther to ask the king?

## Esther explains the danger

## Esther 4:10

Esther gave Hathach a message to take to Mordecai.

## Esther 4:11

Esther said, “The king’s officials and people throughout his kingdom know the law.

It applies to every man and woman.

Anyone who enters the inner courtyard without the king’s invitation must die.

The king can hold out his gold scepter, his royal staff.

Only then may the person live.

The king has not called for me in thirty days.”

## Esther 4:12

Mordecai heard Esther’s message.

**Pause and answer**

## Mordecai asks Esther to speak

## Esther 4:13

Mordecai sent Esther this answer.

“You live in the king’s palace.

Do you think you alone will escape when the other Jews die?

## Esther 4:14

“If you stay silent now, help for the Jews will come from somewhere else.

But you and your father’s family will die.

Who knows?

Perhaps you became queen for just such a time as this.”

## Esther asks her people to fast

## Esther 4:15

Esther sent her answer to Mordecai.

## Esther 4:16

“Gather all the Jews you can find in Susa.

Ask them to fast for me.

For three days and nights, go without food and drink.

The women who serve me will fast with me too.

Then I will go to the king, even though it is against the law.

If I die, I die.”

## Esther 4:17

Mordecai went and did everything Esther had asked.
