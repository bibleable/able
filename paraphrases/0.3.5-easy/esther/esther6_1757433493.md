# Esther 6 — The king honors Mordecai

**About this chapter**

During the night, the king hears how Mordecai once helped him. When Haman arrives, the king asks him for advice.

## The king hears what Mordecai did

## Esther 6:1

That night King Xerxes could not sleep.

He asked his servants to bring the Book of Records.

This book, called the Chronicles, told what had happened during his reign.

The servants read it aloud to him.

![An open scroll shows lines of writing, like the record read aloud to the king.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## Esther 6:2

They read about Bigthana and Teresh.

These two royal attendants guarded the entrance to the king’s rooms.

They had planned to kill King Xerxes.

Mordecai had reported their plan.

## Esther 6:3

The king asked, “How have we honored Mordecai for this?”

His attendants answered, “We have done nothing for him.”

## Haman comes to see the king

## Esther 6:4

The king asked, “Who is in the courtyard?”

Haman had just stepped into the outer courtyard.

He wanted the king to have Mordecai hanged.

Haman had already prepared the gallows for him.

## Esther 6:5

The attendants answered, “Haman is standing in the courtyard.”

The king said, “Bring him in.”

## Esther 6:6

Haman came in.

The king asked, “I want to honor a man.

What should I do for him?”

Haman thought, “Who would the king want to honor more than me?”

**Pause and answer**

Who did Haman think the king wanted to honor?

## Haman describes a royal honor

## Esther 6:7

Haman answered the king.

“Here is what you could do for the man you want to honor.

## Esther 6:8

“Bring a royal robe you have worn.

Bring a horse you have ridden.

Choose one that wears the royal crest on its head.”

The crest was a sign that the horse belonged to the king.

## Esther 6:9

Haman continued, “Give the robe and horse to one of your highest princes.

Have the prince dress the man in the robe.

Let the man ride the horse through the city square.

Have the prince call out ahead of him, ‘This is how the king honors someone who pleases him!’”

## Mordecai rides through the city

## Esther 6:10

The king told Haman, “Hurry!

Take the robe and the horse to Mordecai the Jew.

He is sitting at my gate.

Do everything for him that you just suggested.”

## Esther 6:11

Haman took the horse and dressed Mordecai in the robe.

Mordecai rode through the city square.

Haman called out ahead of him, “This is how the king honors someone who pleases him!”

**Pause and answer**

Who rode the king’s horse through the square?

## Haman hurries home

## Esther 6:12

Then Mordecai went back to the king’s gate.

Haman covered his head to show his grief.

He hurried home.

## Esther 6:13

Haman told his wife Zeresh and his friends everything that had happened.

His advisers and Zeresh said, “Mordecai is Jewish.

You have already begun to lose to him.

You cannot defeat him.

He will overcome you.”

## Esther 6:14

They were still talking when the king’s attendants arrived.

They hurried Haman to the feast Esther had prepared.
