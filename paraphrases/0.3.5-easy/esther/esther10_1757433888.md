# Esther 10 — Mordecai uses his position to help his people

**About this chapter**

The book ends by describing King Xerxes’ rule and what Mordecai did for his people.

## The king and his records

## Esther 10:1

King Xerxes made people throughout his land pay tribute.

Tribute was a payment the ruler required.

Even people on the farthest shores had to pay.

## Esther 10:2

The royal records told of the great things Mordecai did.

They also told how the king gave Mordecai great power.

The book was called the Chronicles of the Kings of Media and Persia.

![An open scroll with lines of writing, like the record of Mordecai’s work.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## Mordecai works for peace

## Esther 10:3

Only King Xerxes ranked higher than Mordecai the Jew.

Mordecai was a leader among the Jews.

Many of his own people thought highly of him.

He worked to make life better for his people.

He spoke words of peace to them all.
