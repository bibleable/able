# Esther 8 — A new order for the Jews

**About this chapter**

Haman is dead, but his written order still puts the Jews in danger. Esther goes back to the king to ask for help.

## The king gives Mordecai his ring

## Esther 8:1

That same day, King Xerxes gave Queen Esther Haman’s property.

Haman had been the enemy of the Jews.

Esther told the king that Mordecai was her relative.

Then Mordecai came before the king.

## Esther 8:2

The king had taken back his signet ring from Haman.

Now he gave the ring to Mordecai.

It made the king’s mark on official letters.

Esther put Mordecai in charge of Haman’s property.

## Esther asks the king to act

## Esther 8:3

Esther spoke to the king again.

She fell at his feet and wept.

Haman the Agagite had plotted to destroy the Jews.

Esther begged the king to stop that plan.

## Esther 8:4

The king held out his gold scepter to Esther.

This was the royal staff he held in his hand.

Esther got up and stood before him.

![The king’s gold scepter. He held it out to Esther, and she stood up.](../pictures/esther-gold-scepter.svg)

## Esther 8:5

Esther said, “My king, if you are pleased with me and think it right, please give a written order.

Cancel the letters from Haman, son of Hammedatha the Agagite.

He wrote them to destroy the Jews in all your provinces.

## Esther 8:6

“How could I bear to see my people suffer like this?

How could I watch my own family die?”

**Pause and answer**

What did Esther want the king to cancel?

## The king allows a new order

## Esther 8:7

King Xerxes answered Queen Esther and Mordecai the Jew.

“I have given Haman’s property to Esther.

Haman attacked the Jews.

That is why he died on the gallows.

## Esther 8:8

“Write whatever you think best about the Jews in my name.

Seal the order with my ring.

Once an order bears my name and seal, no one can cancel it.”

## Esther 8:9

The royal writers came at once.

It was day twenty-three of Sivan, the third month.

They wrote Mordecai’s orders for the Jews and the regional rulers, governors, and princes.

The orders covered all 127 provinces from India to Cush.

The writers used each province’s writing and each people’s language.

The Jews received the orders in their own writing and language too.

## Esther 8:10

Mordecai wrote in the name of King Xerxes.

He sealed the letters with the king’s ring.

Messengers carried them on fast horses bred from the king’s mares.

**Pause and answer**

Whose ring did Mordecai use to seal the letters?

## What the letters say

## Esther 8:11

The letters allowed the Jews in every city to gather and defend themselves.

The king allowed them to kill all the forces that attacked them.

The order included women and children.

It also allowed the Jews to take their enemies’ belongings.

## Esther 8:12

The order applied on just one day throughout the kingdom of Xerxes.

That was day thirteen of Adar, the twelfth month.

## Esther 8:13

Every province was to publish a copy for everyone to hear.

Then the Jews could be ready on that day to pay their enemies back for the harm they had done.

## Esther 8:14

At the king’s command, the messengers hurried away on royal horses.

People in the fortress of Susa also heard the order.

## The people celebrate

## Esther 8:15

Mordecai left the king wearing royal clothes of blue and white.

He wore a large gold crown and a purple robe of fine linen.

The people of Susa shouted for joy.

## Esther 8:16

For the Jews, it was as if light had come.

They felt joy, and people treated them with honor.

## Esther 8:17

Wherever the king’s order reached, the Jews held feasts and celebrated.

This happened in cities throughout the provinces.

Many other people became Jews because they were afraid of the Jews.
