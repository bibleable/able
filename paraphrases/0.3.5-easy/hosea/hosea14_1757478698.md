# Hosea 14 — Israel, come back to the LORD

**About this chapter**

Hosea gives the people words to pray. The LORD promises to love them. He describes Israel growing again like plants after a dry night.

## Words to say to the LORD

## Hosea 14:1

People of Israel, come back to the LORD your God.

Your own sins have made you stumble and fall.

## Hosea 14:2

Come back and tell the LORD what you did wrong.

Say to Him, “Please forgive all our sins.

Be kind to us and welcome us back.

We will offer You praise with our own lips.

## Hosea 14:3

“Assyria cannot save us.

We will give up riding horses into battle.

Our own hands made the things we worshiped.

We will never call those things our gods again.

You show tender care to children who have no father.”

## The LORD promises to love Israel

## Hosea 14:4

The LORD says, “My people have turned away from Me.

I will heal them of their habit of leaving Me.

I will love them freely.

I am no longer angry with them.

## Hosea 14:5

“I will be like dew for Israel.”

Dew is water that gathers on plants overnight.

“Israel will bloom like a lily.

Israel will grow strong roots like Lebanon’s tall cedar trees.

## Hosea 14:6

“New branches will grow.

Israel will be as beautiful as an olive tree.

Israel will smell as sweet as the cedars of Lebanon.

## Hosea 14:7

“People will come back to live in Israel’s shade.

They will grow grain.

They will bloom like grapevines.

People will speak well of Israel, just as they praise Lebanon’s wine.”

Israel is like a plant growing well again.

## The LORD watches over His people

## Hosea 14:8

The LORD calls His people Ephraim, another name for Israel.

“Ephraim, what do I have to do with idols?

I am the One who answers you.

I watch over you.

I am like a green cypress tree.

The fruit you grow comes from Me.”

## Hosea 14:9

A wise person will think about these words.

Someone with good judgment will learn what they mean.

The LORD shows people the right way to live.

People who do right walk in His ways.

Those who refuse to follow Him stumble there.
