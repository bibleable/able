# Hosea 1 — The names of Hosea’s children

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks through Hosea. Each time a child is born in Hosea’s family, the LORD gives the child a name. Each name carries a message for Israel.

## Hosea marries Gomer

## Hosea 1:1

The LORD gave His message to Hosea, the son of Beeri.

During Hosea’s time, four kings ruled Judah, one after another.

Their names were Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah.

Jeroboam, the son of Jehoash, ruled Israel during this time.

## Hosea 1:2

When the LORD first spoke through Hosea, He said,

“Go and marry a woman who sells sex.

Have children in a family marked by unfaithfulness.

The people of this land have turned away from Me.”

The LORD compared Israel to someone who breaks a marriage promise.

## Hosea 1:3

Hosea married Gomer, the daughter of Diblaim.

Gomer became pregnant and gave birth to a son.

## Their first son and their daughter

## Hosea 1:4

The LORD told Hosea, “Name him Jezreel.”

Jezreel was a place where people had been killed.

“Soon I will punish Jehu’s family for those killings.

I will bring the kingdom of Israel to an end.

## Hosea 1:5

“On that day I will break Israel’s bow in the Valley of Jezreel.”

A broken bow is a picture of an army losing its power.

## Hosea 1:6

Next Gomer gave birth to a daughter.

The LORD told Hosea, “Name her Lo-ruhamah.”

Her name means “No Mercy.”

“I will no longer show mercy to the people of Israel.

I will no longer forgive them.

## Hosea 1:7

“But I will show mercy to the people of Judah.

I, the LORD their God, will save them.

They will need no bows, swords, or battles for this.

They will need no horses or riders.”

## Their second son

## Hosea 1:8

In time Gomer stopped nursing Lo-ruhamah.

Then Gomer became pregnant again and had a son.

## Hosea 1:9

The LORD said, “Name him Lo-ammi.”

His name means “Not My People.”

“You are not My people, and I am not your God.”

## A promise for the future

## Hosea 1:10

One day the people of Israel will be like grains of sand by the sea.

There will be too many to measure or count.

In that very place they once heard, “You are not My people.”

There they will hear, “You are children of the living God.”

## Hosea 1:11

The people of Judah and Israel will gather together.

They will choose one leader for themselves.

Together they will go up out of the land.

What a great day it will be for Jezreel!
