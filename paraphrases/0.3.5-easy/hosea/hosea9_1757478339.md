# Hosea 9 — Israel will leave the LORD’s land

**About this chapter**

Hosea tells Israel why the harvest is no time to celebrate.

The people have turned away from God.

They will lose their homes and wander among other nations.

## The harvest will fail

## Hosea 9:1

People of Israel, do not celebrate like the other nations.

You have been unfaithful to your God.

On every threshing floor you have loved the pay of a prostitute.

A threshing floor is where farmers separate grain from stalks.

Hosea compares Israel to someone who takes money for sex.

Israel has turned to other gods for the harvest.

## Hosea 9:2

The grain from the threshing floor will not feed the people.

They will press grapes, but the new wine will run out.

## Hosea 9:3

The people will have to leave the LORD’s land.

Hosea calls Israel by the name Ephraim.

Ephraim will return to Egypt.

In Assyria they will eat food God’s law calls unclean.

## Hosea 9:4

They will no longer pour out wine for the LORD.

Their sacrifices will give him no pleasure.

Their food will be like bread in a house of mourning.

Anyone who eats it will be unclean for worship.

They will keep their bread to feed themselves.

They will bring none of it into the LORD’s house.

## Hosea 9:5

What will you do when the day comes for the LORD’s festival?

## Hosea 9:6

Even if you escape the destruction, Egypt will gather you in.

People will bury you in Memphis, a city in Egypt.

Prickly weeds will grow over your precious silver.

Thorns will cover your tents.

## Israel turns against God’s messenger

## Hosea 9:7

Israel, know this.

The time has come to answer for your sins.

You call the prophet a fool.

You say the person moved by God’s Spirit has lost his mind.

That shows how greatly you have sinned.

It shows how much hatred you carry.

## Hosea 9:8

A watchman looks out for danger and warns the people.

The prophet keeps watch over Ephraim with my God.

Yet people set traps for the prophet wherever he goes.

They hunt him like someone trapping a bird.

Even in God’s house he meets hatred.

## Hosea 9:9

The people have done terrible wrong.

They have become as corrupt as the people at Gibeah long ago.

God will remember their guilt and punish their sins.

## The LORD remembers Israel’s ancestors

## Hosea 9:10

The LORD says, “Finding Israel was like finding grapes in the desert.

Seeing your ancestors was like seeing the first figs on a young tree.

They were a joy to me.

But they went to Baal-peor.

There they gave themselves to a shameful god.

They became as hateful as the idol they loved.”

## Hosea 9:11

Ephraim’s glory will fly away like a bird.

Women will no longer conceive or carry children.

No babies will be born.

## Hosea 9:12

The LORD says, “Even if they raise children, I will take every child from them.

How terrible it will be when I turn away!”

## Hosea 9:13

I saw Ephraim settled in a green meadow like Tyre.

Yet Ephraim’s people will bring out their children to be killed.

## Hosea 9:14

LORD, what will you give them?

Let pregnancies end before the babies can be born.

Let mothers’ breasts give no milk.

## The people will lose their home

## Hosea 9:15

The LORD says, “At Gilgal I saw all their evil.

There I came to hate them.

Because of their wicked deeds, I will drive them from my house.

I will no longer love them.

Every one of their leaders rebels against me.

## Hosea 9:16

“Ephraim is like a plant that someone has struck down.

Its root has dried up.

It can no longer grow fruit.

Even if the people have children, I will kill their dear little ones.”

## Hosea 9:17

My God will reject them because they disobeyed him.

They will wander from country to country.
