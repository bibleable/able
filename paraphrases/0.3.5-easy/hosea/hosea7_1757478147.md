# Hosea 7 — Israel looks elsewhere for help

**About this chapter**

Hosea describes anger that grows like a fire. When Israel needs help, the people turn to neighboring countries. The LORD remembers how He cared for them.

## The LORD sees what is happening

## Hosea 7:1

The LORD says, “When I set out to heal Israel, I see Ephraim’s sins.

I see the crimes in Samaria too.”

Ephraim is another name for northern Israel.

Samaria was its capital.

“People deceive each other.

Thieves break into homes.

Robbers attack people in the streets.

## Hosea 7:2

“They never stop to think that I remember every wrong they do.

Their deeds surround them.

I see it all.

## Hosea 7:3

“The evil they do pleases the king.

The lies they tell please the princes.

## Anger burns like an oven

## Hosea 7:4

“They are all unfaithful like people who break their marriage promises.

Think of a baker who heats an oven and kneads dough.

The oven stays hot while the dough rises.

The baker does not even have to stir the fire.

![Dough rises while the baker waits.](../pictures/yeast-and-dough.svg)

## Hosea 7:5

“On the king’s feast day, the princes grow hot with wine.

The king joins hands with people who mock him.

## Hosea 7:6

“The people plotting against him lie in wait.

Their hearts are like that oven.

All night their anger burns like hot coals.

In the morning it bursts into flames.

## Hosea 7:7

“They burn with anger and destroy their rulers.

Their kings fall one after another.

None of them calls to Me.”

## Israel grows weak without noticing

## Hosea 7:8

Ephraim mixes with the other nations.

Ephraim is like flatbread that nobody turns over.

One side burns while the other stays uncooked.

## Hosea 7:9

Foreigners drain Israel’s strength, but Israel does not notice.

Israel is like someone whose hair is turning gray without their knowing it.

## Hosea 7:10

Israel’s pride shows that the people are guilty.

Even now they will not return to the LORD their God.

They do not seek Him.

## The people call to other nations

## Hosea 7:11

Ephraim is like a dove that does not know where to fly.

The people call to Egypt for help.

Then they turn to Assyria.

## Hosea 7:12

The LORD says, “As they go, I will spread My net over them.

I will bring them down like birds.

When I hear them gathering like a flock, I will punish them.

## Hosea 7:13

“What trouble awaits them! They have wandered away from Me.

They face ruin because they have turned against Me.

I want to rescue them, yet they tell lies about Me.

## Hosea 7:14

“They cry loudly on their beds.

Yet they do not call to Me with all their hearts.

They wound themselves as they plead for grain and new wine.

They keep turning away from Me.

## Hosea 7:15

“I trained them and made their arms strong.

Yet they make plans against Me.

## Hosea 7:16

“They turn elsewhere instead of coming to Me, the Most High.

They are like a bow that misses its mark.

Their leaders will die by the sword because of the curses they speak.

People in Egypt will mock them for this.”
