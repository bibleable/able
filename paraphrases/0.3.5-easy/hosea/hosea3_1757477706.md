# Hosea 3 — Hosea shows love to his wife again

**About this chapter**

Hosea tells what the LORD asked him to do. His marriage shows how the LORD loves the people of Israel.

## The LORD speaks to Hosea

## Hosea 3:1

The LORD said to me, “Go and love your wife again.

Another man loves her.

She has been unfaithful to you.

Love her as I love the people of Israel.

They turn to other gods.

They love giving raisin cakes to idols.”

An idol is an object people worship.

## Hosea gives silver and grain

## Hosea 3:2

So I paid to bring my wife back.

I gave fifteen shekels of silver.

A shekel was a measure of weight.

I also gave one and a half large measures of barley.

Barley is a kind of grain.

![A stalk with a head of ripe grain.](../pictures/grain.svg)

## Hosea 3:3

I told her, “Stay with me for many days.

Do not have sex with anyone else.

I will be faithful to you too.”

## Israel will return to the LORD

## Hosea 3:4

Israel will also wait for many days.

The people will have no king or prince.

They will offer no sacrifices.

They will have no stone pillars for worship.

They will have no ephod, a special garment priests wear.

They will have no idols.

## Hosea 3:5

After that, the people of Israel will turn back.

They will seek the LORD their God.

They will also seek David their king.

In the last days, they will come trembling to the LORD.

They will come to receive His goodness.
