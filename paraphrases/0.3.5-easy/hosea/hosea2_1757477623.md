# Hosea 2 — God promises to love his people again

**About this chapter**

God speaks of Israel as a wife who has left her husband. Her lovers stand for the gods Israel worships. God remembers his people and promises to bring them home.

## Israel has forgotten who gave her food

## Hosea 2:1

The LORD says, “Call your brothers ‘My people.’

Call your sisters ‘My loved one.’

## Hosea 2:2

“Speak to your mother about what she has done.

Tell her, ‘She is no longer my wife.

I am no longer her husband.’

She must stop giving herself to other lovers.

## Hosea 2:3

“Otherwise I will leave her as naked as a newborn.

She will be like a desert where nothing grows.

I will let her die of thirst.

## Hosea 2:4

“I will withhold pity from her children.

She had those children while she was unfaithful.

## Hosea 2:5

“Their mother sold herself to lovers.

She became pregnant and brought shame on herself.

She said, ‘I will go after my lovers.

They give me bread and water, wool and linen cloth.

They give me oil and things to drink.’

## Hosea 2:6

“So I will block her path with thorn bushes.

I will put a wall around her.

She will be unable to find her way.

## Hosea 2:7

“She will search for her lovers but never catch up with them.

Then she will say, ‘I will go back to my first husband.

Life was better with him.’

## Hosea 2:8

“She never recognized who gave her grain, fresh wine, and oil.

I did. I also gave her plenty of silver and gold.

Yet my people used those gifts to make things for Baal, a false god.

## The harvest and the feasts will stop

## Hosea 2:9

“At harvest I will take back my grain.

When the wine is ready, I will take that too.

I will take back the wool and linen I gave her for clothes.

## Hosea 2:10

“Her lovers will see the shame she tried to hide.

No one will rescue her from my hands.

## Hosea 2:11

“I will put an end to her joyful gatherings.

Her feasts at each new moon will stop.

So will her Sabbath celebrations and all her other festivals.

## Hosea 2:12

“She says her lovers paid her with grapevines and fig trees.

I will ruin those vines and trees.

They will grow wild, and animals will eat them.

## Hosea 2:13

“She put on rings and jewelry and ran after her lovers.

She burned sweet-smelling incense to the gods called Baals.

I will punish her for those days of worship.

She forgot me,” says the LORD.

## A way back to hope

## Hosea 2:14

“Now I will draw her to me.

I will lead her into the wilderness and speak gently to her.

## Hosea 2:15

“There I will give back her vineyards.

In the Valley of Achor, where there was trouble, I will open a way to hope.

She will answer me as she did in her youth, when I led her out of Egypt.

## Hosea 2:16

“On that day, Israel, you will call me ‘my husband.’

You will stop calling me ‘my master.’

## Hosea 2:17

“I will take the names of the Baals from your lips.

You will stop calling on them.

## Hosea 2:18

“I will make a covenant, a firm promise, for my people.

It will include wild animals, birds, and creatures that crawl.

I will put an end to bows, swords, and war in the land.

My people will lie down safely.

## Hosea 2:19

“I will promise to take you as my bride forever.

I will do what is right and fair.

I will show you faithful love and tender care.

## Hosea 2:20

“I will keep my marriage promise to you.

You will know me, the LORD.”

## God gives the land what it needs

## Hosea 2:21

The LORD says, “On that day I will answer the sky.

The sky in turn will answer the earth.”

It will send the rain the soil needs.

## Hosea 2:22

“The earth will answer the grain, the grapes, and the olives.”

The soil will help them grow.

“There will be grain, new wine, and oil for Jezreel.”

## Hosea 2:23

“I will plant my people in the land as my own.

I will show love to the one called ‘No Compassion.’

To those called ‘Not My People,’ I will say, ‘You are my people.’

They will answer, ‘You are my God.’”
