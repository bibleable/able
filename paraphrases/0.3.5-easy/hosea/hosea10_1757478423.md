# Hosea 10 — It is time to seek the LORD

**About this chapter**

Israel has good land and good harvests.

But the people use their wealth to worship idols.

Hosea uses pictures from farming to call Israel back to the LORD.

## Good harvests and empty promises

## Hosea 10:1

Israel was like a vine full of grapes.

The people grew plenty of food for themselves.

The more they grew, the more altars they built.

An altar is a place to offer gifts in worship.

Better harvests meant finer stone pillars for their worship.

![A vine bearing grapes, like Hosea’s picture of Israel with its rich harvests.](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

## Hosea 10:2

The people have been dishonest.

Now they must answer for what they have done.

The LORD will tear down their altars.

He will smash their stone pillars.

## Hosea 10:3

Then the people will say, “We have no king.

We have shown the LORD no respect.

What could a king do for us anyway?”

## Hosea 10:4

They make agreements and swear to keep them.

But their promises are lies.

So judgment grows like poisonous weeds between rows of crops.

It spreads harm through the land.

## Israel loses its idol and its king

## Hosea 10:5

The people of Samaria fear for the calf at Beth-aven.

This calf is a statue they worship.

Enemies will carry it away.

The people and their priests once celebrated its glory.

Now they will weep over losing it.

## Hosea 10:6

Enemies will carry the calf to Assyria.

It will be a payment to Assyria’s great king.

Ephraim is another name for Israel here.

The people of Israel will feel shame over their wooden idols.

## Hosea 10:7

Think of a twig floating on water.

The water sweeps it away.

That is how enemies will sweep away Samaria and its king.

## Hosea 10:8

Israel sinned by worshiping at the hilltop shrines of Aven.

Enemies will wreck those places of worship.

Thorns and prickly weeds will cover the altars.

In fear, the people will beg the mountains, “Cover us!”

They will cry to the hills, “Fall on us!”

## The LORD calls Israel to change

## Hosea 10:9

The LORD says, “Israel, you sinned long ago at Gibeah.

You have gone on sinning ever since.

Remember how war caught up with the evildoers at Gibeah.

## Hosea 10:10

“I will punish the people when I choose.

Nations will gather against them.

Those nations will take them prisoner for their two sins.

## Hosea 10:11

“Ephraim is like a young cow trained to thresh grain.”

To thresh means to separate grain from its stalks.

The LORD says, “She likes that work.

But I will put a yoke on her fine neck.”

A yoke is a wooden bar for pulling a load.

The LORD says, “I will harness Ephraim for work.

Judah will plow.

Jacob’s people will break up the hard ground.

## Hosea 10:12

“Plant what is right, as a farmer plants seed.

Then you will gather a harvest of faithful love.

Break up the ground you have left unplowed.”

The picture calls the people to change how they live.

It is time to seek the LORD.

Keep seeking him until he comes.

He will send what is right like rain on the land.

## Israel trusted its army

## Hosea 10:13

You planted evil and gathered a harvest of injustice.

You ate the fruit of lies.

That is the life your dishonest ways have produced.

You trusted your own plans and your many strong soldiers.

## Hosea 10:14

So your people will hear the roar of battle.

Enemies will tear down all your forts.

Remember how Shalman destroyed Beth-arbel in battle.

His forces killed mothers and children together.

## Hosea 10:15

People of Bethel, this will happen to you too.

You have done great evil.

At dawn Israel’s king will meet his end.
