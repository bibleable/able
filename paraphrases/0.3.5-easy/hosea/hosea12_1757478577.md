# Hosea 12 — Come back and act fairly

**About this chapter**

Hosea speaks to people who cheat others and boast about their wealth. He reminds them of their ancestor Jacob and calls them back to God.

## Chasing the wind

## Hosea 12:1

Ephraim is another name for the northern people of Israel.

They chase the east wind all day.

It is as if they think wind can feed them.

They tell more lies and hurt more people.

They make an agreement with Assyria.

They also send olive oil to Egypt.

## Hosea 12:2

The LORD also brings a charge against Judah.

He will punish the people of Jacob for what they have done.

## Jacob asks for God’s favor

## Hosea 12:3

Before Jacob was born, he grabbed his brother’s heel.

When Jacob grew strong, he wrestled with God.

## Hosea 12:4

Jacob wrestled with the angel and won.

Jacob wept and begged for God’s favor.

At Bethel, Jacob met God and spoke with Him.

## Hosea 12:5

This is the LORD, the God who leads heaven’s armies.

The LORD is the name people remember Him by.

## Hosea 12:6

So come back to your God.

Keep loving people and treating them fairly.

Always wait for your God.

## Cheating and boasting

## Hosea 12:7

A trader likes to cheat people with his scales.

His scales show the wrong weight.

## Hosea 12:8

The people of Ephraim boast, “Look how rich we are!

We earned this wealth ourselves.

No one can find anything sinful in our work.”

## Hosea 12:9

The LORD says, “I have been your God since you lived in Egypt.

I will make you live in tents again.

You stayed in tents like these at your festival.

![A tent beside a house.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

## Hosea 12:10

“I spoke through the prophets, people who brought you My words.

I showed them many visions.

Through them I told stories to teach you.”

## Remember who cared for Israel

## Hosea 12:11

The people of Gilead do evil.

They will come to nothing.

At Gilgal, people offer bulls on altars.

Those altars will become piles of stones in plowed fields.

## Hosea 12:12

Jacob fled to the land of Aram.

Israel was another name for Jacob.

There he worked to be able to marry.

His work was looking after sheep.

## Hosea 12:13

Later the LORD sent a prophet to lead Israel’s people out of Egypt.

Through that prophet, the LORD kept the people safe.

## Hosea 12:14

The people of Ephraim have made their Lord very angry.

He will hold them responsible for the people they killed.

He will repay them for treating Him with scorn.
