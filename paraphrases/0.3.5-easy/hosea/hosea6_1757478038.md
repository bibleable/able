# Hosea 6 — The LORD asks for lasting love

**About this chapter**

The people speak about coming back to the LORD. Then the LORD speaks about their love. It disappears like mist in the morning.

## Come back to the LORD

## Hosea 6:1

The people say, “Come, let us return to the LORD.

He has torn us, but He will heal us.

He has hurt us, but He will bandage our wounds.

## Hosea 6:2

“After two days He will bring us back to life.

On the third day He will raise us up.

Then we can live close to Him.

## Hosea 6:3

“Let us keep trying to know the LORD.

We can count on the sun to rise.

We can count on the LORD to come to us.

He will come like spring rain that waters the ground.”

![The sun shines over the hills.](../pictures/judges-song-sun.svg)

## Love that disappears

## Hosea 6:4

The LORD answers, “Ephraim, what shall I do with you?

Judah, what shall I do with you?

Your love for Me is like the morning mist.

It disappears like drops of dew early in the day.”

Ephraim is another name for the people of northern Israel.

## Hosea 6:5

“So I sent My prophets to speak to you.

My words cut you down.

My words brought death.

My judgments flash out like lightning.”

Prophets are people who speak for God.

## Hosea 6:6

“I want you to show mercy more than I want sacrifices.

Show mercy by treating others with kindness.

Knowing Me matters more than burning gifts on an altar.

## Hosea 6:7

“Like Adam, they broke the covenant.

A covenant is a binding promise.

There they broke their promise to Me.

## Violence in the land

## Hosea 6:8

“People in the city of Gilead do evil.

Bloody footprints mark the ground there.

## Hosea 6:9

“A group of priests hides and waits like robbers.

They murder people on the road to Shechem.

These priests have done terrible things.

## Hosea 6:10

“I have seen something horrible among the people of Israel.

Ephraim acts like someone who sells sex.

The people have been unfaithful to Me.

Israel has become unclean in My sight.

## Hosea 6:11

“Judah, I have set a harvest time for you too.

It will come when I bring My people back from captivity.”

Captivity means others held the people and kept them from going home.
