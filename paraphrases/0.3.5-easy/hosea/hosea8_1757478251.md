# Hosea 8 — Israel forgets the LORD

**About this chapter**

Israel calls the LORD their God. But the people worship things they have made and pay other nations to help them. Hosea warns them about what will follow.

## Sound the warning

## Hosea 8:1

The LORD says, “Blow the ram’s horn to warn the people!

An eagle hangs over My house.

My people have broken their covenant with Me.

A covenant is a solemn promise.

They have turned against My law.”

## Hosea 8:2

The people of Israel cry out, “Our God, we know You!”

## Hosea 8:3

But Israel has turned away from what is good.

An enemy will chase them.

## A calf made by human hands

## Hosea 8:4

The LORD says, “They chose kings without asking Me.

They chose leaders I did not approve.

They used silver and gold to make idols.

These things they worship will bring them to ruin.”

## Hosea 8:5

Samaria, the LORD rejects the calf you worship.

He says, “I am very angry with these people.

How long will they keep doing wrong?”

## Hosea 8:6

Someone in Israel made that calf with their own hands.

How could it be God?

The calf of Samaria will break into pieces.

## A harvest lost

## Hosea 8:7

It is as if the people planted wind.

What will they harvest? A fierce windstorm!

Their choices will bring even greater trouble.

Their grain does not grow tall.

The shoots produce no grain to grind into flour.

Even if grain grew, strangers would eat it.

## Hosea 8:8

Other nations have swallowed Israel up.

They treat Israel like a pot nobody wants.

## Hosea 8:9

The people went to Assyria like a wild donkey wandering alone.

Ephraim, another name for Israel, has paid for lovers.

This means Israel has paid other nations to be its friends.

## Hosea 8:10

The LORD says, “They pay other nations for help.

Now I will gather them up.

A powerful king will weigh them down.

They will begin to grow weak.

## Altars and strong cities

## Hosea 8:11

“Ephraim built many altars to offer sacrifices for sin.

Yet at those very altars the people kept sinning.

## Hosea 8:12

“I wrote down important teachings for them in My law.

They treated those teachings as if they came from strangers.

## Hosea 8:13

“They offer Me animals as gifts.

They eat the meat.”

The LORD does not accept their offerings.

He will remember the wrong they have done.

He will punish their sins.

They will go back to Egypt.

## Hosea 8:14

Israel has forgotten the God who made them.

They have built palaces.

Judah has built many cities with strong defenses.

The LORD says, “I will send fire on their cities.

It will burn up their strongholds.”
