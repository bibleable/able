# Hosea 5 — The LORD calls His people to admit their wrongs

**About this chapter**

The LORD warns Israel and Judah through Hosea. The people turn to another king for help. The LORD waits for them to admit what they have done.

## The LORD speaks to Israel and its leaders

## Hosea 5:1

The LORD says, “Priests, listen!

People of Israel, pay attention!

You in the king’s family, listen too!

I will judge you for what you have done.

At Mizpah you have been like a trap.

On Mount Tabor you have been like a net spread to catch people.

## Hosea 5:2

“Those who rebel against Me have gone far in killing others.

I will punish them all.

## Hosea 5:3

“I know Ephraim well.”

Ephraim is another name for the northern kingdom of Israel.

“Israel can hide nothing from Me.

Ephraim, you have turned to prostitution.

You have made yourselves unclean.”

## Hosea 5:4

Hosea compares Israel to someone who breaks a marriage promise.

The people keep choosing to be unfaithful to God.

What they do keeps them from returning to Him.

They do not know the LORD.

## The people look for the LORD

## Hosea 5:5

Israel’s pride shows that the people are guilty.

Their own sins make them trip and fall.

Even Judah falls with them.

## Hosea 5:6

They take their sheep and cattle and look for the LORD.

But they cannot find Him.

He has drawn away from them.

## Hosea 5:7

They have broken faith with the LORD.

Their children were born of unfaithfulness.

When the New Moon festival comes, they and their land will face ruin.

## A warning for Israel and Judah

## Hosea 5:8

Sound the ram’s horn in Gibeah!

Blow the trumpet in Ramah!

Shout the battle cry in Beth-aven!

Lead the way, people of Benjamin!

## Hosea 5:9

The LORD says, “When the day of punishment comes, Ephraim will lie in ruins.

Tribes of Israel, what I tell you is certain.

## Hosea 5:10

“Judah’s leaders are like people who move boundary stones.”

A boundary stone marks where one person’s field ends.

Moving it lets someone take a neighbor’s land.

“My anger will pour over those leaders like water.

![A boundary marker shows where one field ends and another begins.](../pictures/field-boundary.svg)

## Hosea 5:11

“Ephraim insists on following worthless idols.

So judgment weighs heavily on the people and crushes them.

## Assyria cannot heal them

## Hosea 5:12

“I am like a moth eating away at Ephraim.

I am like rot spreading through Judah.

## Hosea 5:13

“Ephraim saw that he was sick.

Judah saw his wound.

Ephraim sent to the great king of Assyria for help.

But that king cannot cure your sickness or heal your wound.

## Hosea 5:14

“I will be like a lion to Ephraim.

I will be like a young lion to Judah.

I will tear them apart and carry them away.

No one will be able to rescue them.

## Hosea 5:15

“Then I will go back to My place.

I will wait until they admit their guilt and seek Me.

In their suffering they will look for Me with all their heart.”
