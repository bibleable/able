# Hosea 11 — I taught you to walk

**About this chapter**

The LORD remembers caring for Israel like a parent. Even after the people turn away, He longs to bring them home.

## The LORD remembers His child

## Hosea 11:1

The LORD says, “When Israel was a child, I loved him.

I called My son out of Egypt.”

God calls the people of Israel His son.

## Hosea 11:2

“The more I called, the farther My people went from Me.

They gave sacrifices to false gods called the Baals.

They burned sweet-smelling incense before carved idols.

## Hosea 11:3

“I taught Ephraim to walk.

I held them by their arms.

I healed them, but they never knew who healed them.”

Ephraim is another name for the people of northern Israel.

## Hosea 11:4

“I guided them gently, as if love were a rope leading them.

I lifted the yoke from their necks.

I bent down to feed them.”

A yoke is a wooden bar a work animal carries.

Lifting it shows how gently the LORD cared for His people.

## The people refuse to come back

## Hosea 11:5

“They refused to turn back to Me.

So they will go back to Egypt.

Assyria will rule over them.

## Hosea 11:6

“Enemies with swords will sweep through their cities.

They will break the bars that hold the gates shut.

My people’s own plans will bring them to ruin.

## Hosea 11:7

“My people are determined to turn away from Me.

They call out to the Most High God.

But I will not lift them up.

## The LORD asks how He could give them up

## Hosea 11:8

“Ephraim, how could I give you up?

Israel, how could I let you go?

How could I treat you like Admah or Zeboiim?”

Those two cities were destroyed.

“My heart changes within Me.

I feel such tenderness for you!

## Hosea 11:9

“I will hold back My fierce anger.

I will not destroy Ephraim again.

I am God. I am not human.

I am the Holy One here among you.

I will not come to you in anger.”

## The children come home

## Hosea 11:10

The people will follow the LORD.

His call will sound like a lion’s roar.

When He roars, His children will come trembling from the west.

## Hosea 11:11

“They will come trembling like birds from Egypt.

They will come like doves from Assyria.

I will settle them in their homes,” says the LORD.

## Hosea 11:12

“Ephraim surrounds Me with lies.

The people of Israel try to deceive Me.

Judah still follows God.

Judah stays faithful to the Holy One.”
