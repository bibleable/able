# Nehemiah 9 — The people remember and pray

**About this chapter**

The Israelites admit the wrong they have done. In their prayer they remember how God cared for their families long ago.

## The people tell God what they have done

## Nehemiah 9:1

On day twenty-four of the seventh month, the Israelites gathered.

They went without food that day.

They wore rough cloth and put dust on their heads.

These actions showed their sorrow.

## Nehemiah 9:2

The Israelites stood apart from the foreigners.

They told God what they had done wrong.

They also spoke of the wrong their ancestors had done.

## Nehemiah 9:3

They stood and read from the Law of the LORD their God.

They read for a quarter of the day.

For another quarter, they admitted their sins and worshiped God.

## Nehemiah 9:4

Some Levites stood on a raised platform.

They were Jeshua, Bani, Kadmiel, Shebaniah, Bunni, Sherebiah, Bani, and Chenani.

They called out loudly to the LORD their God.

## The Levites praise God

## Nehemiah 9:5

Then Jeshua, Kadmiel, Bani, and Hashabneiah spoke with the other Levites named here.

These were Sherebiah, Hodiah, Shebaniah, and Pethahiah.

They said, “Stand and praise the LORD your God forever!”

Then they prayed to God.

Your name deserves more praise than anyone can give.

## Nehemiah 9:6

You alone are the LORD.

You made the heavens and everything in them.

You made the earth, the seas, and everything in them.

You give life to everything.

All the beings in heaven worship you.

## Nehemiah 9:7

LORD, you chose Abram.

You led him out of Ur in the land of the Chaldeans.

You gave him the name Abraham.

## Nehemiah 9:8

You saw that Abraham was faithful to you.

You made a covenant with him, a solemn promise.

You promised land to the people who would come from his family.

Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Jebusites, and Girgashites lived there.

You kept your promise because you do what is right.

What did God promise Abraham?

## God leads the people out of Egypt

## Nehemiah 9:9

You saw our ancestors suffer in Egypt.

You heard them cry out at the Red Sea.

## Nehemiah 9:10

Pharaoh, his officials, and his people treated our ancestors with pride.

You knew what they had done.

You did wonders that showed your power against them.

People still know your name because of those wonders.

## Nehemiah 9:11

You divided the sea in front of our ancestors.

They walked across on dry ground.

You threw those who chased them into the deep water.

They sank like a stone in rough water.

## Nehemiah 9:12

By day you led our ancestors with a tall column of cloud.

By night a column of fire lit their way.

**Pause and answer**

What lit the way at night?

## God gives laws and food

## Nehemiah 9:13

You came down on Mount Sinai.

You spoke to our ancestors from heaven.

The laws you gave them were fair and true.

Your commands were good.

## Nehemiah 9:14

You taught them about your holy Sabbath, the day of rest.

You gave them your laws through your servant Moses.

## Nehemiah 9:15

When they were hungry, you gave them bread from heaven.

When they were thirsty, you gave them water from a rock.

You told them to take the land you had promised them.

## God stays with his people

## Nehemiah 9:16

But our ancestors became proud and stubborn.

They refused to do what you commanded.

## Nehemiah 9:17

They refused to listen.

They forgot the wonders you had done among them.

They chose a leader to take them back into slavery in Egypt.

But you forgive and show kindness.

You care about people who suffer.

You are slow to anger and full of faithful love.

You stayed with them.

## Nehemiah 9:18

They even made a metal calf.

They said, “This is your God who brought you out of Egypt!” They insulted you terribly.

## Nehemiah 9:19

Even then, you cared for them in the wilderness.

You kept the cloud ahead of them by day.

The fire still lit their way by night.

## Nehemiah 9:20

You gave your good Spirit to teach them.

You kept giving them manna, the food from heaven.

You gave them water to drink.

## Nehemiah 9:21

For forty years you cared for them in the wilderness.

They had everything they needed.

Their clothes lasted, and their feet did not swell.

What did God give the people to eat in the wilderness?

## God gives the people land

## Nehemiah 9:22

You gave them kingdoms and peoples.

You gave them their shares throughout the land.

They took the land of Sihon, king of Heshbon.

They also took the land of Og, king of Bashan.

## Nehemiah 9:23

Their children and grandchildren became as many as the stars.

You brought them to the land you had promised.

You had told their ancestors to enter and take it.

## Nehemiah 9:24

Their families entered and took the land.

You defeated the Canaanites who lived there.

You gave Israel power over the kings and their people.

Israel could do with them as it wished.

## Nehemiah 9:25

They took cities with strong walls and good farmland.

They found houses full of goods and wells already dug.

They found grapevines, olive trees, and plenty of fruit trees.

They ate their fill and grew fat.

They enjoyed all the good things you gave them.

## The people cry out for help

## Nehemiah 9:26

But they turned against you and ignored your Law.

Your prophets urged them to come back to you.

The people killed those prophets and spoke terribly against you.

## Nehemiah 9:27

So you let enemies take power over them.

Those enemies treated them cruelly.

In their trouble our ancestors cried out to you.

You heard them from heaven and showed them mercy.

You sent people to rescue them from their enemies.

## Nehemiah 9:28

As soon as they had peace, they did wrong again.

You left them under their enemies’ rule.

Again they cried out, and you heard from heaven.

In your mercy you rescued them many times.

**Pause and answer**

What did God do when the people cried out again?

## Nehemiah 9:29

You urged them to follow your Law again.

But they were proud and disobeyed your commands.

You gave them laws to live by.

Anyone who follows those laws will live.

Still they turned away and refused to obey.

## Nehemiah 9:30

For many years you were patient with them.

Your Spirit warned them through your prophets.

They would not listen.

So you let nearby nations take power over them.

## Nehemiah 9:31

Yet you showed great mercy.

You let your people live and stayed with them.

You are a kind God who cares for us.

## The people bring their own trouble to God

## Nehemiah 9:32

Our God, you are great and mighty.

We stand in awe of you.

In your love you keep your covenant.

Please take our suffering seriously.

Our kings, leaders, priests, prophets, ancestors, and all your people have suffered.

This trouble began under the kings of Assyria.

It continues today.

## Nehemiah 9:33

In all our trouble, you have acted fairly.

You have been faithful.

We have done wrong.

## Nehemiah 9:34

Our kings, leaders, priests, and ancestors disobeyed your Law.

They ignored your commands and your warnings.

## Nehemiah 9:35

You gave them their own kingdom and many good things.

They had room to live and good land to farm.

Even then they refused to serve you.

They kept doing wrong.

## Nehemiah 9:36

Now we are slaves here!

You gave our ancestors this land to enjoy its good food.

Here we are, slaves in that very land.

## Nehemiah 9:37

Our land grows plenty of food.

But its harvest goes to the kings you put over us because of our sins.

They do as they please with us and our animals.

We are in deep trouble.

## Nehemiah 9:38

Because of all this, we make a firm agreement.

We put it in writing.

Our leaders, Levites, and priests put their names on it.

Their seals show that they agree.
