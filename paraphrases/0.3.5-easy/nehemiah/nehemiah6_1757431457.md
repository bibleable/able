# Nehemiah 6 — Nehemiah stays to finish the wall

**About this chapter**

Nehemiah tells how his enemies tried to frighten him. He tells how the people finished Jerusalem’s wall.

## Four invitations

## Nehemiah 6:1

We had rebuilt the wall and closed every gap.

I still had to hang the doors in the gates.

Sanballat, Tobiah, and Geshem the Arab heard the news.

Our other enemies heard it too.

## Nehemiah 6:2

Sanballat and Geshem sent me a message.

“Come meet us in a village on the plain of Ono.”

They planned to hurt me there.

## Nehemiah 6:3

I sent messengers back with my answer.

“My work here is important.

I cannot come down.

Why should the work stop while I visit you?”

## Nehemiah 6:4

Four times they sent the same message.

Four times I gave the same answer.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Nehemiah stay at the wall?

## A letter accuses Nehemiah

## Nehemiah 6:5

Sanballat sent the message a fifth time.

This time his young servant carried a letter.

The letter had no seal to keep it closed.

![An open scroll shows lines of writing.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## Nehemiah 6:6

The letter said, “People in other nations are saying this about you.

Geshem says it too.

You and the Jews plan to turn against the king.

That is why you are building the wall.

People say you will become the Jews’ king.

## Nehemiah 6:7

“They say you have chosen prophets in Jerusalem to announce, ‘Judah has a king!’

Soon the king will hear these stories.

Come and talk this over with us.”

## Nehemiah 6:8

I sent Sanballat my answer.

“None of these stories are true.

You made them up yourself.”

## Nehemiah 6:9

Our enemies wanted to frighten us.

They thought fear would make us too weak to finish.

I prayed, “My God, give my hands strength.”

I was asking God to help me keep working.

**Pause and answer**

What did Nehemiah ask God to help him do?

## Shemaiah tells Nehemiah to hide

## Nehemiah 6:10

Later I visited Shemaiah, who had to stay home.

His father was Delaiah, and his grandfather was Mehetabel.

Shemaiah said, “Meet me inside the temple, God’s house.

Let us shut the doors.

Men are coming tonight to kill you!”

## Nehemiah 6:11

I answered, “Should someone like me run away?

Should I enter the temple to save my own life?

I will stay away from the temple.”

## Nehemiah 6:12

I realized God had never sent Shemaiah.

Tobiah and Sanballat had paid him to speak against me.

Shemaiah spoke as though his message came from God.

## Nehemiah 6:13

They paid him to scare me into doing wrong.

If I did what he said, I would sin.

Then they could ruin my good name.

## Nehemiah 6:14

I prayed, “My God, remember what Tobiah and Sanballat did.

Remember Noadiah too.

She was a prophet who tried to frighten me.

Remember the other prophets who tried to frighten me.”

**Pause and answer**

Who paid Shemaiah to frighten Nehemiah?

## The wall is complete

## Nehemiah 6:15

We finished the wall in fifty-two days.

We finished on day twenty-five of the month called Elul.

## Nehemiah 6:16

All our enemies heard the news.

The nations around us grew afraid and lost courage.

They saw that our God had done this work.

**Pause and answer**

How long did the people take to finish the wall?

## Tobiah keeps sending letters

## Nehemiah 6:17

During that time the nobles of Judah wrote often to Tobiah.

The nobles were people from leading families.

Tobiah kept sending letters back.

## Nehemiah 6:18

Many people in Judah had made solemn promises to Tobiah.

Tobiah had married the daughter of Shecaniah, son of Arah.

Tobiah’s son Jehohanan had married the daughter of Meshullam, son of Berechiah.

## Nehemiah 6:19

The nobles kept telling me about Tobiah’s good deeds.

They also told Tobiah what I said.

Tobiah sent me letters to frighten me.

**Pause and answer**

Who told Tobiah what Nehemiah said?
