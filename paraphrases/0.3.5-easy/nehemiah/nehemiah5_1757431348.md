# Nehemiah 5 — Families ask for help

**About this chapter**

Nehemiah tells what happened when families ran out of food. He also tells how he served as governor of Judah.

## Families need food

## Nehemiah 5:1

About that time, the men and their wives cried out.

They complained about other Jews who had treated them badly.

## Nehemiah 5:2

Some said, “We have many sons and daughters.

We need grain to eat so we can live.”

![A stalk of grain with seeds at the top.](../pictures/grain.svg)

The families needed the seeds for food.

## Nehemiah 5:3

Others said, “There is so little food.

To get grain, we must borrow against our fields, vineyards, and homes.”

A vineyard is a place where grapes grow.

If families could not repay, the lender could keep their property.

## Nehemiah 5:4

Still others said, “The king makes us pay a tax on our fields and vineyards.

We borrowed money to pay it.

## Nehemiah 5:5

“We are Jews like them.

Our children are just like their children.

Yet we have to give our sons and daughters as slaves.

Some of our daughters are already slaves.

We cannot buy them back.

Other people now have our fields and vineyards.”

**Pause and answer**

Why could the families no longer buy their daughters back?

## Nehemiah calls the leaders together

## Nehemiah 5:6

I heard what these families said.

I became very angry.

## Nehemiah 5:7

I thought carefully about what to do.

Then I spoke firmly to the nobles and officials.

These men were leaders among the Jews.

I said, “You charge your own people interest!”

Interest is the extra payment a lender asks for.

I called a large meeting to challenge these leaders.

## Nehemiah 5:8

I said, “Foreigners bought some of our fellow Jews as slaves.

We did all we could to buy them back.

Now you sell your own people!

Must we buy them back again?”

The leaders could find nothing to say.

## Nehemiah 5:9

I said, “What you are doing is wrong.

Show respect for our God by the way you live.

Then our foreign enemies will have no reason to mock us for this.

**Pause and answer**

What extra payment did the leaders ask for?

## The leaders promise to give things back

## Nehemiah 5:10

“My brothers, my servants, and I have also lent people money and grain.

Let us all stop charging interest.

## Nehemiah 5:11

“Give back their fields, vineyards, olive trees, and houses today.

Give back the interest you charged them too.

Return the money, grain, new wine, and oil.”

## Nehemiah 5:12

The leaders answered, “We will give it back.

We will ask them for nothing more.

We will do what you say.”

I called the priests over.

In front of the priests, I made the leaders promise before God.

## Nehemiah 5:13

I shook out the folds of my robe.

I said, “May God shake out anyone who breaks this promise.

May that person lose his home and everything he owns.”

Everyone said, “Amen.”

Here “Amen” means “May it be so.”

They praised the LORD.

The people did what they had promised.

**Pause and answer**

What did the leaders promise to give back?

## Nehemiah feeds people at his table

## Nehemiah 5:14

King Artaxerxes made me governor of Judah.

I served from his twentieth year as king until his thirty-second.

For those twelve years, my brothers and I took none of the food set aside for the governor.

## Nehemiah 5:15

Earlier governors made life hard for the people.

They took bread, wine, and forty shekels of silver.

A shekel was a way to measure silver by weight.

Their servants also treated the people badly.

I respected God too much to act that way.

## Nehemiah 5:16

I worked hard to rebuild the wall.

All my servants came to work there too.

We took no land for ourselves.

## Nehemiah 5:17

At my table I fed 150 Jews, including officials.

Guests from nearby nations also ate with us.

## Nehemiah 5:18

Each day my cooks prepared one ox, six fine sheep, and some birds.

Every ten days I received plenty of different wines.

Still, I never demanded the governor’s share of food.

The people already had enough to bear.

## Nehemiah 5:19

My God, remember all I have done for these people.

Please show me kindness.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Nehemiah give up the governor’s share of food?
