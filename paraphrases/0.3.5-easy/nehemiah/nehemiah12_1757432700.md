# Nehemiah 12 — Two choirs give thanks for the wall

**About this chapter**

The chapter names priests and Levites who served God. Then Nehemiah tells how two choirs walked along Jerusalem’s wall to thank God.

## The priests who returned

## Nehemiah 12:1

Priests and Levites returned with Zerubbabel and Jeshua.

Zerubbabel was the son of Shealtiel.

The priests included Seraiah, Jeremiah, and Ezra.

## Nehemiah 12:2

Amariah, Malluch, and Hattush were also among the priests.

## Nehemiah 12:3

Shecaniah, Rehum, and Meremoth were also among the priests.

## Nehemiah 12:4

Iddo, Ginnethon, and Abijah were also among the priests.

## Nehemiah 12:5

Mijamin, Maadiah, and Bilgah were also among the priests.

## Nehemiah 12:6

Shemaiah, Joiarib, and Jedaiah were also among the priests.

## Nehemiah 12:7

Sallu, Amok, Hilkiah, and Jedaiah complete this list.

These men led their fellow priests in Jeshua’s time.

## Levites and the family of Jeshua

## Nehemiah 12:8

The Levites included Jeshua, Binnui, Kadmiel, Sherebiah, Judah, and Mattaniah.

Mattaniah and his companions led songs of thanks to God.

## Nehemiah 12:9

Bakbukiah and Unni served with them.

They stood opposite them during worship.

## Nehemiah 12:10

Jeshua was Joiakim’s father.

Joiakim was Eliashib’s father.

Eliashib was Joiada’s father.

## Nehemiah 12:11

Joiada was Jonathan’s father.

Jonathan was Jaddua’s father.

## Leaders of priestly families

## Nehemiah 12:12

In Joiakim’s time, each family of priests had a leader.

Meraiah led Seraiah’s family.

Hananiah led Jeremiah’s family.

## Nehemiah 12:13

Meshullam led Ezra’s family.

Jehohanan led Amariah’s family.

## Nehemiah 12:14

Jonathan led Malluchi’s family.

Joseph led Shebaniah’s family.

## Nehemiah 12:15

Adna led Harim’s family.

Helkai led Meraioth’s family.

## Nehemiah 12:16

Zechariah led Iddo’s family.

Meshullam led Ginnethon’s family.

## Nehemiah 12:17

Zichri led Abijah’s family.

The record also names Miniamin’s family.

It names Piltai for Moadiah’s family.

## More families and their leaders

## Nehemiah 12:18

Shammua led Bilgah’s family.

Jonathan led Shemaiah’s family.

## Nehemiah 12:19

Mattenai led Joiarib’s family.

Uzzi led Jedaiah’s family.

## Nehemiah 12:20

Kallai led Sallai’s family.

Eber led Amok’s family.

## Nehemiah 12:21

Hashabiah led Hilkiah’s family.

Nethanel led Jedaiah’s family.

## Nehemiah 12:22

Records named the family leaders of the Levites and priests.

These records cover the time of Eliashib, Joiada, Johanan, and Jaddua, when Darius the Persian ruled.

## Nehemiah 12:23

The Book of the Chronicles listed leaders from Levi’s family.

Its list reached the time of Johanan, son of Eliashib.

## Singers and gatekeepers

## Nehemiah 12:24

Hashabiah, Sherebiah, and Jeshua led the Levites.

This Jeshua was Kadmiel’s son.

Their companions stood facing them.

The two groups took turns praising and thanking God.

David, the man of God, had given this instruction.

## Nehemiah 12:25

Mattaniah, Bakbukiah, Obadiah, Meshullam, Talmon, and Akkub were gatekeepers.

They guarded the storerooms by the gates.

## Nehemiah 12:26

These men served in the time of Joiakim.

His father was Jeshua, son of Jozadak.

They also served in the time of Nehemiah and Ezra.

Nehemiah was governor.

Ezra was a priest and a scribe who worked with the sacred writings.

## The people prepare for the celebration

## Nehemiah 12:27

The people invited Levites from all their homes to Jerusalem.

They came to dedicate the wall, giving thanks to God for it.

They sang joyful songs with cymbals, harps, and lyres.

Harps and lyres make music with strings.

![A harp with strings like those musicians pluck to make music.](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

## Nehemiah 12:28

Singers gathered from the land around Jerusalem.

Some came from the villages of the Netophathites.

## Nehemiah 12:29

Others came from Beth-gilgal and the fields of Geba and Azmaveth.

The singers had built their own villages around Jerusalem.

## Nehemiah 12:30

The priests and Levites first purified themselves.

They followed the ceremony for becoming clean for worship.

Then they purified the people, the gates, and the wall.

## The first choir turns right

## Nehemiah 12:31

I, Nehemiah, brought Judah’s leaders up onto the wall.

I formed two large choirs to sing thanks to God.

The first choir walked right along the wall toward the Dung Gate.

## Nehemiah 12:32

Hoshaiah followed with half the leaders of Judah.

## Nehemiah 12:33

Azariah, Ezra, and Meshullam went with them.

## Nehemiah 12:34

Judah, Benjamin, Shemaiah, and Jeremiah went too.

## Nehemiah 12:35

Some priests carried trumpets.

Zechariah also went.

His father was Jonathan, son of Shemaiah.

Shemaiah’s father was Mattaniah, son of Micaiah.

Micaiah’s father was Zaccur, son of Asaph.

## Nehemiah 12:36

Zechariah’s companions were Shemaiah, Azarel, Milalai, Gilalai, Maai, Nethanel, Judah, and Hanani.

They carried the instruments David, the man of God, had chosen.

Ezra the scribe walked in front of the group.

## Nehemiah 12:37

At the Fountain Gate, they climbed the steps of the City of David.

The steps led up to the wall.

They passed above David’s house and reached the Water Gate on the east.

## The second choir turns left

## Nehemiah 12:38

The second choir turned left.

I followed with half the people along the wall.

We passed the Tower of the Ovens and reached the Broad Wall.

## Nehemiah 12:39

We went over the Gate of Ephraim, the Jeshanah Gate, and the Fish Gate.

We passed the Tower of Hananel and the Tower of the Hundred.

We reached the Sheep Gate and stopped at the Gate of the Guard.

## Both choirs sing at God’s house

## Nehemiah 12:40

Both choirs stood in God’s house.

I stood there too, with the half of the officials who had followed me.

## Nehemiah 12:41

The priests stood there with their trumpets.

They were Eliakim, Maaseiah, Miniamin, Micaiah, Elioenai, Zechariah, and Hananiah.

## Nehemiah 12:42

Maaseiah, Shemaiah, Eleazar, Uzzi, Jehohanan, Malchijah, Elam, and Ezer also stood there.

Then Jezrahiah led the choirs as they sang out.

## Nehemiah 12:43

That day the people offered great sacrifices to God.

They celebrated because God had given them great joy.

The women and children celebrated too.

Even people far away could hear Jerusalem celebrating.

## The people provide food for those who serve

## Nehemiah 12:44

That day some men took charge of the storerooms.

They gathered gifts and supplies from the village fields.

These included the first crops and the tithe, one tenth of the harvest.

The Law set aside these shares for priests and Levites.

Judah’s people were glad for the work those men did.

## Nehemiah 12:45

The priests and Levites served God and led the ceremonies for becoming clean.

The singers and gatekeepers also did their work.

They followed the instructions of David and his son Solomon.

## Nehemiah 12:46

Long ago, when David and Asaph lived, leaders directed the singers.

They led songs of praise and thanks to God.

## Nehemiah 12:47

In the time of Zerubbabel and Nehemiah, all Israel gave daily shares for the singers and gatekeepers.

The people also set aside daily shares for the Levites.

The Levites set aside shares each day for Aaron’s family.
