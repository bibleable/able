# Nehemiah 13 — Nehemiah returns to Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

When Nehemiah returns, he finds a man’s belongings in a temple storeroom. Nehemiah tells how he put the room back to use and what else he did in Jerusalem.

## The people hear the Law

## Nehemiah 13:1

The people listened as someone read the Book of Moses aloud.

They heard a law about the Ammonites and Moabites.

No Ammonite or Moabite could ever join God’s gathered people.

## Nehemiah 13:2

Those nations had failed to give Israel food and water.

They had paid Balaam to curse the Israelites.

But our God turned that curse into a blessing.

## Nehemiah 13:3

As soon as the Israelites heard this law, they acted.

They kept everyone of foreign descent out of their community.

**Pause and answer**

What did God do with Balaam’s curse?

## Tobiah uses a temple storeroom

## Nehemiah 13:4

Earlier, Eliashib the priest had managed the storerooms in God’s house.

Tobiah was a relative of Eliashib.

## Nehemiah 13:5

Eliashib gave Tobiah a large room.

It had held grain offerings and things used for worship.

It also held frankincense, which gives a sweet smell when burned.

People had stored grain, new wine, and oil there.

A tenth was for the Levites, singers, and gatekeepers.

The room also held gifts for the priests.

## Nehemiah 13:6

I, Nehemiah, was away from Jerusalem when this happened.

I had gone back to Artaxerxes, king of Babylon.

It was his thirty-second year as king.

Later, the king gave me permission to leave.

## Nehemiah 13:7

I returned to Jerusalem.

There I learned what Eliashib had done wrong.

He had given Tobiah a room in the temple courtyards.

## Nehemiah 13:8

I was very upset.

I threw all Tobiah’s belongings out of the room.

## Nehemiah 13:9

I ordered people to make the rooms clean for worship.

Then I had them bring back the temple supplies.

They also brought back the grain offerings and frankincense.

## The Levites receive their food again

## Nehemiah 13:10

I learned that the Levites had received no share of food.

So the Levites and singers had left their temple work.

Each had gone back to work in his fields.

## Nehemiah 13:11

I spoke sharply to the officials.

I asked, “Why have you neglected God’s house?”

Then I gathered the Levites and singers.

I put them back at their places of work.

## Nehemiah 13:12

People throughout Judah brought supplies to the storerooms.

They brought a tenth of their grain, new wine, and oil.

## Nehemiah 13:13

I chose men whom people trusted to manage the storerooms.

They were Shelemiah the priest, Zadok the scribe, and Pedaiah the Levite.

Hanan helped them.

His father was Zaccur, son of Mattaniah.

These men gave the other Levites their shares.

## Nehemiah 13:14

My God, remember me for this.

Remember how I lovingly cared for your house and its worship.

**Pause and answer**

Why had the Levites gone back to their fields?

## Traders sell food on the Sabbath

## Nehemiah 13:15

The Sabbath was the weekly day of rest.

On that day I saw people in Judah pressing grapes with their feet to make wine.

They loaded donkeys with grain, wine, grapes, and figs.

They brought all kinds of goods into Jerusalem.

I warned them against selling food on the Sabbath.

![Six days for work, followed by the seventh day for rest.](../pictures/sabbath-week.svg)

## Nehemiah 13:16

Men from Tyre lived in Jerusalem too.

They brought in fish and other goods.

On the Sabbath they sold them to people from Judah.

## Nehemiah 13:17

I spoke firmly to Judah’s leaders.

I asked, “Why do this wrong?

You treat the Sabbath like any other day.

## Nehemiah 13:18

“Your ancestors did the same thing.

That is why God brought disaster on us and this city.

Now you are making God angry with Israel again.

You are treating the Sabbath as an ordinary day.”

## The gates close for the Sabbath

## Nehemiah 13:19

Evening shadows fell across Jerusalem’s gates just before the Sabbath.

I ordered the gates shut until the Sabbath ended.

Some of my servants guarded the gates.

They kept loads of goods outside on the Sabbath.

## Nehemiah 13:20

Once or twice, traders spent the night outside Jerusalem.

They had all kinds of goods to sell.

## Nehemiah 13:21

I warned them, “Why spend the night by the wall?

If you do it again, I will use force against you.”

After that, the traders stayed away on the Sabbath.

## Nehemiah 13:22

I told the Levites to make themselves clean for worship.

They were to guard the gates to keep the Sabbath holy.

My God, remember me for this too.

Show me mercy because your faithful love is so great.

## Nehemiah speaks about marriages

## Nehemiah 13:23

In those days I saw some Jewish men who had married women from Ashdod, Ammon, and Moab.

## Nehemiah 13:24

Half their children spoke the language of Ashdod or another people.

Those children could not speak the language of Judah.

## Nehemiah 13:25

I scolded the men and called down curses on them.

I beat some of them and pulled out their hair.

I made them promise before God.

I said, “Do not let your daughters marry these peoples’ sons.

Do not marry their daughters or choose them for your sons.

## Nehemiah 13:26

“Remember how King Solomon of Israel sinned in this way.

Among many nations there was no king like Solomon.

God loved him and made him king over all Israel.

Yet his foreign wives led him into sin.

## Nehemiah 13:27

“Now I hear you have also married foreign women.

How could you do this wrong and be unfaithful to our God?”

**Pause and answer**

Which king did Nehemiah ask the men to remember?

## Nehemiah assigns work at the temple

## Nehemiah 13:28

Eliashib the high priest had a son named Jehoiada.

One of Jehoiada’s sons married the daughter of Sanballat the Horonite.

So I drove Jehoiada’s son away from me.

## Nehemiah 13:29

My God, remember what they have done.

They dishonored the work of the priests.

They broke the covenant, the solemn agreement for priests and Levites.

## Nehemiah 13:30

I cleared away everything foreign from the priests and Levites to make them clean for worship.

I gave each priest and Levite a particular job.

## Nehemiah 13:31

I arranged for people to bring wood at the set times.

I also arranged for gifts from the first crops.

My God, remember me and show me kindness.
