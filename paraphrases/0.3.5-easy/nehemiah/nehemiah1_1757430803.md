# Nehemiah 1 — Nehemiah hears news of Jerusalem and prays

**About this chapter**

Nehemiah works for the king in Susa. Visitors tell him about the people and the broken wall in Jerusalem. Nehemiah tells what he did when he heard the news.

## Visitors bring news from Judah

## Nehemiah 1:1

I am Nehemiah, son of Hacaliah. This is my account.

In the twentieth year of the king’s rule, in the month called Chislev, I was in the fortress at Susa.

## Nehemiah 1:2

My brother Hanani came with some men from Judah.

I asked about the Jews who had survived the exile.

I also asked about Jerusalem.

## Nehemiah 1:3

They said, “The survivors in Judah face great trouble and shame.

Jerusalem’s wall is broken down. Fire has destroyed its gates.”

**Reading help**

Exile means being forced to live away from your home country. The visitors told Nehemiah about the survivors living in Judah.

**Pause and answer**

What had happened to Jerusalem’s gates?

## Nehemiah grieves and prays

## Nehemiah 1:4

When I heard this, I sat down and cried.

For days I grieved. I went without food and prayed to the God of heaven.

## Nehemiah 1:5

I prayed, “LORD, God of heaven, you are great and worthy of deep respect.

You keep your covenant. You show faithful love to those who love you and obey your commands.

**Reading help**

A covenant is a serious promise. Nehemiah begins his prayer by remembering that God keeps his promises.

## Nehemiah admits his people’s sin

## Nehemiah 1:6

Please look at me and listen to my prayer.

I am your servant. Day and night I pray for your servants, the people of Israel.

I admit that we have sinned against you.

My family and I have sinned too.

## Nehemiah 1:7

We have done wrong against you.

We have disobeyed the commands and laws you gave your servant Moses.

## Nehemiah remembers God’s promise

## Nehemiah 1:8

Please remember what you told Moses.

You said, ‘If you are unfaithful to me, I will scatter you among the nations.

## Nehemiah 1:9

But if you come back to me and obey my commands, I will gather you again.

Even if you are far away at the ends of the earth, I will bring you back.

I will bring you to the place I chose for my name.’

**Pause and answer**

What did God promise to do if his scattered people came back to him?

## Nehemiah 1:10

These are your servants, your own people.

You used your great power to set them free.

## Nehemiah asks for help today

## Nehemiah 1:11

Lord, please listen to my prayer.

Listen also to your other servants who gladly honor you.

Help me succeed today. Let this man show me kindness.”

At that time I was the king’s cupbearer.

![A drinking cup. Nehemiah served the king his drink.](../pictures/cup.svg)

**Reading help**

A cupbearer served drinks to the king. When Nehemiah asks for kindness from “this man,” he means the king.

**Pause and answer**

What did Nehemiah ask God to help him with that day?
