# Nehemiah 3 — Neighbors rebuild the wall together

**About this chapter**

Families and groups repair different sections of Jerusalem’s wall. The list starts at the Sheep Gate. It follows one group after another until it reaches that gate again.

## The Sheep Gate and the Fish Gate

## Nehemiah 3:1

Eliashib, the chief priest, rebuilt the Sheep Gate with the other priests.

They set up its doors and dedicated the gate to God.

They built as far as the Tower of the Hundred and the Tower of Hananel.

They dedicated that part of the wall to God too.

## Nehemiah 3:2

Men from Jericho built beside Eliashib.

Zaccur, son of Imri, built the next section.

## Nehemiah 3:3

The sons of Hassenaah rebuilt the Fish Gate.

They put its beams and doors in place.

They fitted the bolts and bars to close it.

**Reading help**

A beam is a long piece of wood that supports part of a building. Bolts and bars held the gate doors shut.

## Nehemiah 3:4

Meremoth, son of Uriah and grandson of Hakkoz, repaired the next section.

Beside him worked Meshullam, son of Berechiah and grandson of Meshezabel.

Zadok, son of Baana, repaired the section beside Meshullam.

## Nehemiah 3:5

People from Tekoa repaired the next section.

Their nobles refused to help the men leading the work.

## From the Jeshanah Gate to the Broad Wall

## Nehemiah 3:6

Joiada, son of Paseah, and Meshullam, son of Besodeiah, repaired the Jeshanah Gate.

They put its beams and doors in place.

They fitted the bolts and bars.

## Nehemiah 3:7

Melatiah from Gibeon and Jadon from Meronoth worked next to them.

Men from Gibeon and Mizpah helped.

The governor west of the Euphrates River ruled over those men.

## Nehemiah 3:8

Uzziel, son of Harhaiah, worked next to them. He made things from gold.

Next came Hananiah, who made perfume.

They strengthened Jerusalem’s defenses as far as the Broad Wall.

## Families help repair the wall

## Nehemiah 3:9

Rephaiah, son of Hur, repaired the next section.

He ruled half the district of Jerusalem.

## Nehemiah 3:10

Jedaiah, son of Harumaph, repaired the wall across from his house.

Hattush, son of Hashabneiah, worked beside him.

## Nehemiah 3:11

Malchijah, son of Harim, and Hasshub, son of Pahath-moab, repaired another section.

They also repaired the Tower of the Ovens.

## Nehemiah 3:12

Shallum, son of Hallohesh, ruled the other half of Jerusalem’s district.

He repaired the next section with his daughters.

**Pause and answer**

Who worked with Shallum on his section of the wall?

## The Valley Gate, Dung Gate, and Fountain Gate

## Nehemiah 3:13

Hanun and the people of Zanoah rebuilt the Valley Gate.

They fitted its doors, bolts, and bars.

They also repaired about 450 metres of wall, all the way to the Dung Gate.

![A line connects the Valley Gate and the Dung Gate, about 450 metres apart along the repaired wall.](../pictures/valley-gate-to-dung-gate.svg)

## Nehemiah 3:14

Malchijah, son of Rechab, ruled the district of Beth-haccherem.

He rebuilt the Dung Gate and fitted its doors, bolts, and bars.

## Nehemiah 3:15

Shallun, son of Col-hozeh, ruled the district of Mizpah.

He rebuilt the Fountain Gate and put a roof over it.

He fitted its doors, bolts, and bars.

He repaired the wall by the Pool of Shelah near the king’s garden.

His work reached the stairs leading down from the City of David.

## Workers from nearby districts

## Nehemiah 3:16

Nehemiah, son of Azbuk, ruled half the district of Beth-zur.

He repaired the next section, across from the tombs of David.

His work reached the pool people had dug and the House of the Mighty.

**Reading help**

This worker is Nehemiah, son of Azbuk. The Nehemiah who spoke to the king was the son of Hacaliah.

## Nehemiah 3:17

Levites repaired the next section under Rehum, son of Bani.

Hashabiah ruled half the district of Keilah.

He repaired the next section on behalf of his district.

## Nehemiah 3:18

Other men from Keilah worked beside them under Binnui, son of Henadad.

Binnui ruled the other half of the district.

## Nehemiah 3:19

Ezer, son of Jeshua, ruled Mizpah. He repaired another section near a bend in the wall.

It faced the path up to the building where weapons were kept.

## The wall beside the houses

## Nehemiah 3:20

Baruch, son of Zabbai, worked hard on the next section.

He repaired the wall from the bend to the door of Eliashib’s house.

Eliashib was the chief priest.

## Nehemiah 3:21

Meremoth, son of Uriah and grandson of Hakkoz, repaired another section.

He worked from Eliashib’s door to the end of the house.

## Nehemiah 3:22

Priests who lived nearby repaired the next section.

## Nehemiah 3:23

Benjamin and Hasshub worked in front of their house.

Next came Azariah, son of Maaseiah and grandson of Ananiah.

He repaired the wall beside his house.

## Nehemiah 3:24

Binnui, son of Henadad, repaired another section.

It ran from Azariah’s house to the bend and the corner.

## The Water Gate and the hill of Ophel

## Nehemiah 3:25

Palal, son of Uzai, worked across from the bend.

He faced the tower that stood out from the king’s upper palace, near the guards’ courtyard.

Pedaiah, son of Parosh, worked next to him.

## Nehemiah 3:26

Temple servants who lived on the hill of Ophel worked with Pedaiah.

They repaired the wall across from the Water Gate on the east and the tower that stood out.

## Nehemiah 3:27

People from Tekoa repaired another section beside them.

It ran from across the great tower to the wall of Ophel.

## From the Horse Gate back to the Sheep Gate

## Nehemiah 3:28

Above the Horse Gate, priests repaired the wall.

Each priest worked in front of his own house.

**Pause and answer**

Where did each priest work above the Horse Gate?

## Nehemiah 3:29

Zadok, son of Immer, worked across from his house.

Next came Shemaiah, son of Shecaniah, who guarded the East Gate.

## Nehemiah 3:30

Hananiah, son of Shelemiah, and Hanun, the sixth son of Zalaph, repaired another section.

Meshullam, son of Berechiah, worked beside them, across from his own room.

## Nehemiah 3:31

Malchijah, a goldsmith, worked next to him.

He repaired as far as the house of the temple servants and merchants, across from the Inspection Gate.

His work reached the upper room above the corner.

## Nehemiah 3:32

Goldsmiths and merchants repaired the wall from that upper room to the Sheep Gate.

**Pause and answer**

Which gate appears at both the beginning and the end of the list?
