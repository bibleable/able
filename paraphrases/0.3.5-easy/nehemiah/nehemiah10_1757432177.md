# Nehemiah 10 — The people promise to follow God’s Law

**About this chapter**

The agreement begins with a list of names. Then the people say how they will keep the Sabbath and care for God’s house.

## The governor and the priests

## Nehemiah 10:1

The leaders put their seals on the written agreement.

A seal was a mark that showed their agreement.

Governor Nehemiah, son of Hacaliah, added his seal.

Zedekiah also added his seal.

## Nehemiah 10:2

Seraiah, Azariah, and Jeremiah added their seals.

## Nehemiah 10:3

Pashhur, Amariah, and Malchijah added their seals.

## Nehemiah 10:4

Hattush, Shebaniah, and Malluch added their seals.

## Nehemiah 10:5

Harim, Meremoth, and Obadiah added their seals.

## Nehemiah 10:6

Daniel, Ginnethon, and Baruch added their seals.

## Nehemiah 10:7

Meshullam, Abijah, and Mijamin added their seals.

## Nehemiah 10:8

Maaziah, Bilgai, and Shemaiah added their seals.

This was the list of the priests.

## The people make their promise

## Nehemiah 10:28

The rest of the people joined in.

They included priests, Levites, gatekeepers, singers, and temple servants.

Others had left the surrounding peoples to follow God’s Law.

Their wives joined them.

So did all their sons and daughters who could understand.

## Nehemiah 10:29

Together with their leaders, the people made a solemn promise.

God had given his Law through his servant Moses.

They promised to obey every command of the LORD their Lord.

Here is what they promised.

## Nehemiah 10:30

We will not arrange marriages between our daughters and the men of the surrounding peoples.

Nor will we arrange for our sons to marry their daughters.

## Nehemiah 10:31

The Sabbath is our weekly day of rest.

Traders may come with grain or other goods to sell.

We will buy nothing from them on Sabbaths or holy days.

Every seventh year we will leave our fields unplanted.

That year we will cancel every debt.

People who owe us will owe us nothing.

## Money and wood for worship

## Nehemiah 10:32

Each year we will give a third of a shekel for God’s house.

A shekel is a measure of weight used for money.

## Nehemiah 10:33

This gift will pay for the bread set before God.

It will provide the regular grain offerings and burnt offerings.

It will provide offerings for Sabbaths, new moons, and feasts.

It will also provide holy offerings and offerings for sin.

The sin offerings are for making peace with God for Israel.

Our gifts will support all the work at God’s house.

## Nehemiah 10:34

Our families drew lots, including the priests, Levites, and other people.

This way, each family learned its turn to bring wood.

Each year we will bring wood to God’s house at those times.

The wood will burn on the altar of the LORD our God.

This is what the Law says.

## The first crops and the tenth share

## Nehemiah 10:35

Each year we will bring the first crops from our land.

We will bring the first fruit from every fruit tree.

We will take these gifts to the LORD’s house.

## Nehemiah 10:36

We will also bring our firstborn sons, as the Law says.

We will bring the first young born to our animals, including our herds and flocks.

We will bring them to the priests who serve at God’s house.

## Nehemiah 10:37

We will take the first of our dough and grain offerings to the priests.

We will bring the first fruit from all our trees.

We will also bring new wine and oil.

These gifts go to the storerooms at God’s house.

One tenth of what we grow belongs to the Levites.

A tenth is one share out of ten equal shares.

The Levites will collect it in every town where we work.

## Nehemiah 10:38

A priest from Aaron’s family will go with the Levites when they collect the tenth.

The Levites will take one tenth of what they receive to God’s house.

They will put it in the storerooms for the treasury.

## Nehemiah 10:39

The Israelites and the Levites will bring grain, new wine, and oil to the storerooms.

There we keep the things used for worship in God’s holy place.

The priests on duty, the gatekeepers, and the singers stay there.

We will take care of our God’s house.
