# Nehemiah 11 — Who lived in Jerusalem?

**About this chapter**

Some families moved to Jerusalem. Others stayed on their family land. This list tells where people lived and what work some of them did.

## One in ten moves to Jerusalem

## Nehemiah 11:1

The leaders settled in Jerusalem, the holy city.

The other people drew lots to choose who would move there.

For every ten people, one went to Jerusalem.

The other nine stayed in their towns.

![One of ten boxes has a check mark. It shows the one person chosen to move.](../pictures/one-tenth.svg)

## Nehemiah 11:2

Some men offered to live in Jerusalem.

The people asked God to bless those men.

**Pause and answer**

Out of every ten people, how many moved to Jerusalem?

## Nehemiah 11:3

Here are the leaders from the provinces who lived in Jerusalem.

Other people lived on their own land in Judah’s villages.

They included Israelites, priests, Levites, and temple servants.

Families of Solomon’s servants lived there too.

## Families of Judah and Benjamin

## Nehemiah 11:4

Some families from Judah and Benjamin settled in Jerusalem.

Athaiah came from Judah, from the family of Perez.

His father was Uzziah, and his grandfather was Zechariah.

Zechariah’s father was Amariah.

Amariah’s father was Shephatiah, son of Mahalalel.

## Nehemiah 11:5

Maaseiah came from the family of Shelah.

His father was Baruch, son of Col-hozeh.

Col-hozeh’s father was Hazaiah, son of Adaiah.

Adaiah’s father was Joiarib, son of Zechariah.

## Nehemiah 11:6

From the family of Perez, 468 brave men settled in Jerusalem.

## Nehemiah 11:7

Sallu came from Benjamin’s family.

His father was Meshullam, son of Joed.

Joed’s father was Pedaiah, son of Kolaiah.

Kolaiah’s father was Maaseiah, son of Ithiel.

Ithiel’s father was Jeshaiah.

## Nehemiah 11:8

Gabbai and Sallai followed Sallu.

The men from Benjamin numbered 928.

## Nehemiah 11:9

Joel, son of Zichri, was their leader.

Judah, son of Hassenuah, led the city’s Second District.

A district is one part of a city.

**Pause and answer**

Who led the men from Benjamin?

## Priests in Jerusalem

## Nehemiah 11:10

The priests included Jedaiah, son of Joiarib.

Jachin was also a priest.

## Nehemiah 11:11

Seraiah was the chief official at God’s house.

His father was Hilkiah, son of Meshullam.

Meshullam’s father was Zadok, son of Meraioth.

Meraioth’s father was Ahitub.

## Nehemiah 11:12

Their fellow priests who worked at the temple numbered 822.

Another priest was Adaiah, son of Jeroham.

Jeroham’s father was Pelaliah, son of Amzi.

Amzi’s father was Zechariah, son of Pashhur.

Pashhur’s father was Malchijah.

## Nehemiah 11:13

The family leaders who served with Adaiah numbered 242.

Another priest was Amashai, son of Azarel.

Azarel’s father was Ahzai, son of Meshillemoth.

Meshillemoth’s father was Immer.

## Nehemiah 11:14

With Amashai served 128 strong, brave men.

Zabdiel, son of Haggedolim, led them.

## Levites and gatekeepers

## Nehemiah 11:15

Shemaiah was one of the Levites.

His father was Hasshub, son of Azrikam.

Azrikam’s father was Hashabiah, son of Bunni.

## Nehemiah 11:16

Shabbethai and Jozabad were leaders among the Levites.

They looked after the work outside God’s house.

## Nehemiah 11:17

Mattaniah led the people in prayer and thanks to God.

His father was Mica, son of Zabdi.

Zabdi’s father was Asaph.

Bakbukiah was second among the men serving with Mattaniah.

Abda also served.

His father was Shammua, son of Galal.

Galal’s father was Jeduthun.

## Nehemiah 11:18

In all, 284 Levites lived in Jerusalem, the holy city.

## Nehemiah 11:19

Akkub, Talmon, and their fellow gatekeepers watched the gates.

There were 172 gatekeepers.

**Pause and answer**

What work did Mattaniah lead?

## Where people lived and served

## Nehemiah 11:20

The other Israelites, including priests and Levites, lived throughout Judah’s villages.

Each lived on land passed down through the family.

## Nehemiah 11:21

The temple servants lived on the hill of Ophel.

Ziha and Gishpa led them.

## Nehemiah 11:22

Uzzi led the Levites in Jerusalem.

His father was Bani, son of Hashabiah.

Hashabiah’s father was Mattaniah, son of Mica.

Uzzi came from Asaph’s family.

This family led the singing for worship in God’s house.

## Nehemiah 11:23

The king had given an order for the singers.

It set out their work for each day.

## Nehemiah 11:24

Pethahiah spoke for the king in all matters concerning the people.

His father was Meshezabel.

His family came from Zerah, son of Judah.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the temple servants live?

## Towns of Judah

## Nehemiah 11:25

Some people of Judah settled in towns with fields nearby.

They lived in Kiriath-arba, Dibon, Jekabzeel, and the surrounding villages.

## Nehemiah 11:26

Others lived in Jeshua, Moladah, and Beth-pelet.

## Nehemiah 11:27

Some lived in Hazar-shual.

Others lived in Beersheba and its villages.

## Nehemiah 11:28

Some lived in Ziklag.

Others lived in Meconah and its villages.

## Nehemiah 11:29

People of Judah also lived in En-rimmon, Zorah, and Jarmuth.

## Nehemiah 11:30

Some lived in Zanoah and Adullam and their villages.

Others lived in Lachish and its fields.

Still others lived in Azekah and its villages.

Their homes stretched from Beersheba to the Valley of Hinnom.

## Towns of Benjamin

## Nehemiah 11:31

Families of Benjamin from Geba lived in Michmash and Aija.

They also lived in Bethel and its villages.

## Nehemiah 11:32

Some lived in Anathoth, Nob, and Ananiah.

## Nehemiah 11:33

Others lived in Hazor, Ramah, and Gittaim.

## Nehemiah 11:34

Some lived in Hadid, Zeboim, and Neballat.

## Nehemiah 11:35

Others lived in Lod and Ono.

Some lived in the Valley of the Craftsmen.

Craftsmen make things by hand.

## Nehemiah 11:36

Some groups of Levites from Judah settled in Benjamin’s land.
