# Nehemiah 2 — Nehemiah asks the people to rebuild the wall

**About this chapter**

The king sees that Nehemiah is sad and asks him why. Later in Jerusalem, Nehemiah goes out at night to see the damage for himself.

## The king sees that Nehemiah is sad

## Nehemiah 2:1

It was the month of Nisan in King Artaxerxes’ twentieth year as king.

I brought the king his wine. He had never seen me sad before.

## Nehemiah 2:2

The king asked, “Why do you look so sad? You are not sick.

Something must be troubling you.”

I was very afraid.

## Nehemiah 2:3

I answered, “May the king live forever! How could I be happy?

The city where my ancestors are buried lies in ruins.

Fire has destroyed its gates.”

**Reading help**

Nehemiah is telling the story. The city where his ancestors are buried is Jerusalem.

## Nehemiah asks to go to Judah

## Nehemiah 2:4

The king asked, “What would you like me to do?”

I prayed to the God of heaven.

## Nehemiah 2:5

Then I answered, “If it pleases you, and you are pleased with my service, please send me to Judah.

Let me rebuild the city where my ancestors are buried.”

## Nehemiah 2:6

The queen sat beside the king.

He asked, “How long will your journey take? When will you return?”

The king was willing to send me. I gave him a time for my return.

## The king gives Nehemiah letters

## Nehemiah 2:7

I also asked, “Would you give me letters for the governors west of the Euphrates River?

Then they can let me travel safely through their lands to Judah.

## Nehemiah 2:8

Please give me a letter for Asaph too. He looks after your forest.

I need wood for the gates of the fortress by the temple.

I also need wood for the city wall and the house where I will live.”

My God helped me, and the king gave me what I asked for.

## Nehemiah 2:9

I gave the king’s letters to the governors west of the Euphrates.

The king had also sent army officers and men on horses with me.

## Nehemiah 2:10

Sanballat the Horonite and Tobiah, an Ammonite official, heard the news.

They were very upset that someone had come to help the Israelites.

## Nehemiah looks at the wall at night

## Nehemiah 2:11

I arrived in Jerusalem and stayed there three days.

## Nehemiah 2:12

Then I went out at night with a few men.

God had given me an idea for helping Jerusalem. I had told no one about it.

The animal I rode was the only one we took.

## Nehemiah 2:13

That night I went out through the Valley Gate.

I went toward the Well of the Serpent and the Dung Gate.

I looked closely at the broken walls and burned gates.

## Nehemiah 2:14

I went on to the Fountain Gate and the King’s Pool.

There was no room for my animal to get through.

## Nehemiah 2:15

I went up the valley that night to look at the wall.

Then I turned back and entered the city through the Valley Gate.

**Pause and answer**

Which gate did Nehemiah use to leave the city and come back?

## The people agree to rebuild

## Nehemiah 2:16

The officials did not know where I had gone or why.

I had yet to tell the Jewish people, the priests, the nobles, the officials, or the other workers.

## Nehemiah 2:17

Then I told them, “You can see our trouble. Jerusalem lies in ruins.

Fire has destroyed its gates. Come, let us rebuild Jerusalem’s wall.

Then we will no longer live in disgrace.”

## Nehemiah 2:18

I told them how kindly God had helped me.

I told them what the king had said.

They answered, “Let us start rebuilding.” They got ready for this good work.

**Reading help**

Nehemiah describes God’s help as God’s kind hand on him. The king listened to his request and gave him the letters he needed.

## Three men challenge the builders

## Nehemiah 2:19

Sanballat, Tobiah, and Geshem the Arab heard about our plan.

They made fun of us. “What are you doing?” they asked.

“Are you turning against the king?”

## Nehemiah 2:20

I answered, “The God of heaven will help us succeed.

We are his servants, and we will start rebuilding.

You have no share in Jerusalem. You have no right to claim it.”
