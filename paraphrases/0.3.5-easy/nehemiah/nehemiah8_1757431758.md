# Nehemiah 8 — Ezra reads and the people listen

**About this chapter**

The people gather to hear God’s Law. When they understand the words, they share food and celebrate together.

## The people ask Ezra to read

## Nehemiah 8:1

Everyone gathered in the open space by the Water Gate.

They asked Ezra to bring the Book of the Law of Moses.

This book held the commands the LORD gave Israel.

Ezra was a scribe, someone who studied and copied these writings.

## Nehemiah 8:2

Ezra was also a priest.

On the first day of the seventh month, he brought the book.

Men and women gathered with everyone who could listen and understand.

## Nehemiah 8:3

Ezra faced the crowd by the Water Gate.

He read aloud from first light until noon.

Everyone listened closely to the words of the Law.

## Nehemiah 8:4

Ezra stood on a high wooden platform.

People had built it for the reading.

On his right stood Mattithiah, Shema, Anaiah, Uriah, Hilkiah, and Maaseiah.

On his left stood Pedaiah, Mishael, Malchijah, Hashum, Hash-baddanah, Zechariah, and Meshullam.

## The teachers explain the words

## Nehemiah 8:5

Ezra stood high enough for everyone to see him.

When Ezra opened the book, everyone stood up.

## Nehemiah 8:6

Ezra praised the LORD, the great God.

Everyone raised their hands and said, “Amen! Amen!”

By saying “Amen,” they agreed with Ezra’s praise.

Then they bowed with their faces to the ground.

They worshiped the LORD.

## Nehemiah 8:7

The people stayed in their places.

Levites taught them the Law.

The teachers included Jeshua, Bani, Sherebiah, Jamin, Akkub, and Shabbethai.

Hodiah, Maaseiah, Kelita, Azariah, Jozabad, Hanan, and Pelaiah also taught.

## Nehemiah 8:8

The Levites read aloud from the Book of God’s Law.

They explained what the words meant.

This helped the people understand what they heard.

## The people share their food

## Nehemiah 8:9

The people wept when they heard the Law.

Nehemiah the governor spoke to them.

Ezra and the Levites who taught the people spoke too.

They said, “This day is holy to the LORD your God.

This day belongs to him.

Do not mourn or cry.”

## Nehemiah 8:10

Nehemiah said, “Go and enjoy rich food and sweet drinks.

Send some to anyone who has nothing ready to eat.

Today is holy to our Lord.

Do not be sad.

The joy of the LORD gives you strength.”

## Nehemiah 8:11

The Levites helped the people grow calm.

They said, “Be still.

Today is holy.

Do not be sad.”

## Nehemiah 8:12

Then everyone ate and drank.

They sent food to others.

They celebrated with great joy because they understood the teaching.

## The leaders learn about shelters

## Nehemiah 8:13

On the second day, all the family leaders gathered around Ezra.

The priests and Levites joined them.

Together they studied the words of the Law.

## Nehemiah 8:14

They found a command the LORD had given through Moses.

During the feast in the seventh month, the Israelites should live in shelters.

These shelters were called booths.

## Nehemiah 8:15

They were to announce this in Jerusalem and all their towns.

“Go to the hills and bring back branches.

Gather them from olive, wild olive, myrtle, and palm trees.

Bring branches from other leafy trees too.

Make shelters as the Law says.”

## The people celebrate in shelters

## Nehemiah 8:16

The people brought back branches and made shelters.

Some built them on rooftops and in their own courtyards.

Others built them in the courtyards of God’s house.

They also used the squares by the Water Gate and the Gate of Ephraim.

## Nehemiah 8:17

All who had returned from exile built shelters and lived in them.

These people had come home after others had forced them away.

Israel had not celebrated like this since the days of Joshua, son of Nun.

The people were full of joy.

## Nehemiah 8:18

Ezra read from the Book of God’s Law every day of the feast.

The people celebrated for seven days.

On the eighth day, they gathered as the Law commanded.
