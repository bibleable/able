# Ezra 5 — The builders keep working while the governor writes to Darius

**About this chapter**

Work on the temple begins again. The governor asks who gave permission. He sends the builders’ answer to King Darius.

## Two prophets help the builders

## Ezra 5:1

Two prophets spoke to the Jews in Judah and Jerusalem.

They were Haggai and Zechariah, son of Iddo.

They brought a message from the God of Israel, who ruled over the people.

## Ezra 5:2

Zerubbabel, son of Shealtiel, and Jeshua, son of Jozadak, began rebuilding God’s temple in Jerusalem again.

God’s prophets stayed with them and helped.

## The governor asks about the work

## Ezra 5:3

Tattenai was governor of the land west of the Euphrates River.

He came with Shethar-bozenai and other officials.

They asked the Jews, “Who gave you permission to rebuild this temple?”

## Ezra 5:4

They also asked, “What are the names of the men building it?”

## Ezra 5:5

God watched over the Jewish leaders.

The officials let the work continue while they sent a report to King Darius and waited for his written reply.

**Reading help**

When the passage says God’s eye was on the leaders, it means God saw what was happening and cared for them.

**Pause and answer**

## The officials write to King Darius

## Ezra 5:6

Tattenai, Shethar-bozenai, and the other officials west of the Euphrates sent Darius a letter.

Here is what they wrote.

## Ezra 5:7

“To King Darius. May you have peace in every way.

![A scroll unrolled so that someone can read the writing.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

**Reading help**

In this letter, “we” first means the officials writing to Darius. In verse 11 they quote the Jewish leaders. There, “we” means the people rebuilding the temple.

## Ezra 5:8

We went to Judah and visited the temple of the great God.

The people are building with large stones.

They are putting wooden beams into the walls.

They are working hard, and the building is going well.

## Ezra 5:9

We asked the leaders, ‘Who gave you permission to rebuild this temple?’

## Ezra 5:10

We also asked for their names.

We wanted to write down the leaders’ names for you.

## The letter gives the leaders’ answer

## Ezra 5:11

The leaders answered, ‘We serve the God of heaven and earth.

Many years ago a great king of Israel built this temple and finished it.

Now we are rebuilding it.

## Ezra 5:12

Our ancestors made the God of heaven angry.

So God let Nebuchadnezzar, the Babylonian king, take them captive.

Nebuchadnezzar destroyed this temple and took our people to Babylon.

## Ezra 5:13

Then Cyrus became king over Babylon.

In his first year he gave an order to rebuild God’s temple.

**Pause and answer**

Which king did the leaders say gave permission to rebuild?

## The leaders recall what Cyrus ordered

## Ezra 5:14

Nebuchadnezzar had taken gold and silver objects from the temple in Jerusalem.

He had put them in the temple in Babylon.

Cyrus took them out and gave them to Sheshbazzar.

Cyrus made Sheshbazzar governor.

## Ezra 5:15

Cyrus told him to take the objects back to the temple in Jerusalem.

He ordered the people to rebuild God’s temple where it had stood before.

## Ezra 5:16

So Sheshbazzar came to Jerusalem and laid the temple foundation.

The work began then. The building is still unfinished.’

**Reading help**

A foundation is the base that supports a building. [Read about the day the builders laid the foundation.](ezra3_1757429749.md)

## The officials ask for a decision

## Ezra 5:17

King Darius, if you agree, please have someone search the royal records in Babylon.

Did King Cyrus give the order to rebuild God’s temple in Jerusalem?

Please send us your decision.”
