# Ezra 1 — Cyrus sends the temple gifts back to Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

Families who have lived far from home prepare to return to Jerusalem. King Cyrus asks them to rebuild the temple. They take back the treasures that once belonged there.

## Cyrus writes to the people

## Ezra 1:1

Cyrus was in his first year as king of Persia.

The LORD had spoken through the prophet Jeremiah.

Now the LORD moved Cyrus to act, just as he had promised.

Cyrus sent an announcement throughout his kingdom. He also put it in writing.

![An unrolled scroll showing lines of writing.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

**Reading help**

A king could have his message written on a scroll. People could unroll it and read his words aloud.

## Ezra 1:2

King Cyrus wrote, “The LORD is the God of heaven.

He has given me all the kingdoms of the earth.

He has chosen me to build him a temple in Jerusalem, in Judah.

## Ezra 1:3

Who among you belongs to his people?

May your God be with you.

Go to Jerusalem and build the temple of the LORD, the God of Israel.

He is the God who is in Jerusalem.

## Ezra 1:4

Wherever any of his people still live, their neighbors must help them.

Give them silver, gold, supplies, and animals.

You may also give a gift of your own choice for God’s temple in Jerusalem.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Cyrus ask God’s people to build?

## Families get ready to go

## Ezra 1:5

God moved people to go and rebuild the LORD’s temple in Jerusalem.

Family leaders from Judah and Benjamin got ready to go.

Priests and Levites got ready too.

**Reading help**

The Levites belonged to the tribe of Levi. They helped with worship at the temple.

## Ezra 1:6

Their neighbors helped them. They gave silver and gold objects, supplies, animals, and other valuable things.

They also gave gifts of their own choice.

## Cyrus returns what Nebuchadnezzar took

## Ezra 1:7

King Cyrus brought out objects that belonged to the LORD’s temple.

Years before, King Nebuchadnezzar had taken them from Jerusalem.

He had put them in the temple of his own gods.

**Reading help**

Babylon’s soldiers had taken the temple objects when Jerusalem fell. [Read about the objects they took in 2 Kings 25.](../2kings/2kings25_1757418583.md)

## Ezra 1:8

At Cyrus’s order, Mithredath brought the objects out.

Mithredath looked after the king’s treasure.

He counted out the objects and gave them to Sheshbazzar, the prince of Judah.

**Pause and answer**

Who counted the objects before handing them to Sheshbazzar?

## Sheshbazzar takes the treasures to Jerusalem

## Ezra 1:9

The list included 30 gold dishes and 1,000 silver dishes.

It also listed 29 silver utensils.

## Ezra 1:10

There were 30 gold bowls and 410 matching silver bowls.

The list also named 1,000 other objects.

## Ezra 1:11

There were 5,400 gold and silver objects in all.

Sheshbazzar took them all with him when the exiles went from Babylon to Jerusalem.

**Reading help**

Exiles are people forced to live away from their own land. Now these families could return.
