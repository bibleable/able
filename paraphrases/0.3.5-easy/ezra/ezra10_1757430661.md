# Ezra 10 — The people gather in Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

Shecaniah proposes sending away the foreign wives and their children. Ezra calls the people together. Family leaders hear the cases, and the chapter records the men’s names.

## Shecaniah speaks to Ezra

## Ezra 10:1

Ezra lay face down in front of God’s temple.

He cried as he prayed and confessed his people’s sins.

Many men, women, and children gathered around him.

They cried bitterly too.

## Ezra 10:2

Shecaniah, son of Jehiel from Elam’s family, spoke to Ezra.

“We have been unfaithful to our God.

We have married foreign women from the peoples around us.

Even now there is hope for Israel.

## Ezra 10:3

Let us make a covenant before our God.

Let us send away these wives and their children.

We will follow your advice and the advice of those who tremble at God’s command.

Let us act according to the law.

## Ezra 10:4

Stand up, Ezra. This is your responsibility.

We will support you. Be strong and act.”

## The people promise to act

## Ezra 10:5

Ezra stood up. He asked the leading priests, the Levites, and all Israel to promise to do what Shecaniah had said.

They made that promise under oath.

**Reading help**

An oath is a solemn promise. Here the people promise before God to carry out the plan.

## Ezra 10:6

Ezra left the front of the temple.

He went to the room of Jehohanan, son of Eliashib.

There he ate no food and drank no water.

He was grieving because the people who had returned from exile had been unfaithful.

## A meeting in heavy rain

## Ezra 10:7

An announcement went out across Judah and Jerusalem.

Everyone who had returned from exile had to gather in Jerusalem.

## Ezra 10:8

The leaders and elders gave them three days to come.

Anyone who stayed away would lose all his property.

He would also lose his place in the community.

## Ezra 10:9

The men of Judah and Benjamin came within three days.

On the twentieth day of the ninth month, they sat in the square outside God’s temple.

They trembled because of the matter before them and the heavy rain.

## Ezra 10:10

Ezra the priest stood up. He said, “You have been unfaithful to God by marrying foreign women.

You have added to Israel’s guilt.

## Ezra 10:11

Admit your sin to the LORD, the God of your ancestors.

Do what he wants. Separate yourselves from the peoples around you and from your foreign wives.”

## The people ask for time

## Ezra 10:12

The whole gathering answered loudly, “We must do as you say!

## Ezra 10:13

But there are many of us. This is the rainy season.

We cannot stay out in the open.

We have done great wrong in this matter.

Dealing with it will take more than a day or two.

## Ezra 10:14

Let our leaders speak for all of us.

Set a time for each man who has married a foreign woman to come.

Let him come with the elders and judges from his town.

Keep doing this until God’s fierce anger turns away from us.”

## Ezra 10:15

Jonathan son of Asahel and Jahzeiah son of Tikvah opposed the plan.

Meshullam and Shabbethai the Levite supported those two men.

## Family leaders hear the cases

## Ezra 10:16

The people who had returned from exile followed the plan.

Ezra chose family leaders by name to represent their families.

They began looking into the marriages on the first day of the tenth month.

## Ezra 10:17

By the first day of the first month, they had heard every case involving a man who had married a foreign woman.

## The priests on the list

## Ezra 10:18

The list included priests from the families of Jeshua, son of Jozadak, and his brothers.

Their names were Maaseiah, Eliezer, Jarib, and Gedaliah.

## Ezra 10:19

These men promised to send their wives away.

They brought a ram from the flock as an offering for their guilt.

**Reading help**

A ram is a male sheep. These priests brought one as a guilt offering because they had done wrong.

## Ezra 10:20

The list names these men from Immer’s family.

Hanani and Zebadiah.

## Ezra 10:21

The list names these men from Harim’s family.

Maaseiah, Elijah, Shemaiah, Jehiel, and Uzziah.

## Ezra 10:22

The list names these men from Pashhur’s family.

Elioenai, Maaseiah, Ishmael, Nethanel, Jozabad, and Elasah.

## Levites, a singer, and gatekeepers

## Ezra 10:23

These Levites were on the list. Their names were Jozabad, Shimei, Kelaiah, Pethahiah, Judah, and Eliezer.

Kelaiah also went by the name Kelita.

Eliashib was a singer.
