# Ezra 7 — Ezra goes to Jerusalem to teach God’s law

**About this chapter**

Ezra knows God’s law and wants to teach it. King Artaxerxes gives him a letter that provides for the journey and the temple. At the end, Ezra tells how God helped him.

## Ezra’s family goes back to Aaron

## Ezra 7:1

Many years later, Artaxerxes was king of Persia.

Ezra came from a family of priests. His father was Seraiah.

Before Seraiah came Azariah, and before Azariah came Hilkiah.

## Ezra 7:2

The family line goes back from Hilkiah to Shallum, then to Zadok, then to Ahitub.

## Ezra 7:3

Before Ahitub came Amariah, then another Azariah, then Meraioth.

## Ezra 7:4

Before Meraioth came Zerahiah, then Uzzi, then Bukki.

## Ezra 7:5

Before Bukki came Abishua, then Phinehas, then Eleazar.

Eleazar’s father was Aaron, the chief priest.

**Reading help**

This list starts with Ezra and goes back through earlier generations. It shows how Ezra belongs to Aaron’s family.

## Ezra studies, follows, and teaches the law

## Ezra 7:6

This was the Ezra who came from Babylon.

He was a scribe who knew the Law of Moses well.

The LORD, the God of Israel, had given this law.

The LORD helped Ezra, and the king gave Ezra everything he asked for.

**Reading help**

A scribe worked with written records. Ezra studied and taught God’s written law.

## Ezra 7:7

In the seventh year of Artaxerxes’ rule, Ezra traveled to Jerusalem with other Israelites.

Among them were priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, and temple servants.

## Ezra 7:8

Ezra reached Jerusalem in the fifth month of that year.

## Ezra 7:9

He had left Babylon on the first day of the first month.

He reached Jerusalem on the first day of the fifth month.

God had been kind to him and helped him.

## Ezra 7:10

Ezra had made up his mind to study the LORD’s law.

He wanted to put it into practice and teach its rules to the people of Israel.

## The king gives Ezra a letter

## Ezra 7:11

King Artaxerxes gave a letter to Ezra, the priest and scribe.

Ezra knew the commands the LORD had given Israel.

Here is what the king wrote.

## Ezra 7:12

“Greetings from Artaxerxes, king of kings, to Ezra.

You are a priest and a scribe who knows the law of the God of heaven.

![An unrolled scroll with lines of writing.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

**Reading help**

The king is speaking in this letter. When he says “you,” he first speaks to Ezra. In verse 21 he speaks to the people in charge of his money and supplies.

## Ezra 7:13

Any Israelites in my kingdom who want to go to Jerusalem may go with you.

This includes priests and Levites.

## Ezra 7:14

My seven advisers and I are sending you.

Find out how people in Judah and Jerusalem are living.

Use the law of your God that you have with you.

## Gifts for worship in Jerusalem

## Ezra 7:15

Take the silver and gold my advisers and I have freely given to Israel’s God.

His house is in Jerusalem.

## Ezra 7:16

Take all the silver and gold you can gather throughout Babylon.

Take the gifts the people and priests freely bring for God’s house in Jerusalem.

## Ezra 7:17

Use the money to buy the bulls, rams, and lambs you need.

Buy grain and drink offerings to go with them.

Present the offerings on the altar at God’s house in Jerusalem.

## Ezra 7:18

You and your brothers may decide how to use the silver and gold left over.

Do what your God wants.

## Ezra 7:19

Take all the objects people gave you for temple worship.

Present them to God in Jerusalem.

## Ezra 7:20

If the temple needs anything else, you may pay for it with money from my treasury.

## Orders for the treasurers

## Ezra 7:21

I, King Artaxerxes, give this order to the treasurers west of the Euphrates River.

Give Ezra what he asks for without delay.

He is the priest who knows the law of the God of heaven.

## Ezra 7:22

Give him up to 100 talents of silver and 100 cors of wheat.

Give him up to 100 baths of wine and 100 baths of olive oil.

Give him as much salt as he needs.

**Reading help**

People weighed silver in talents. They measured grain in cors and liquids in baths. Here “bath” means a measure of how much a container holds.

**Pause and answer**

## Ezra 7:23

Take care to do everything the God of heaven commands for his house.

Why bring his anger on my kingdom or my sons?

## Ezra 7:24

You must not charge taxes or fees to those who serve at God’s house.

This includes priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, temple servants, and all other workers there.

## Ezra will choose judges and teach

## Ezra 7:25

Ezra, use the wisdom your God has given you.

Choose officials and judges for the people west of the Euphrates who know God’s laws.

Teach those laws to anyone who does not know them.

## Ezra 7:26

Strictly punish anyone who disobeys your God’s law and the king’s law.

The punishment may be death, exile, loss of property, or prison.”

## Ezra gives thanks

## Ezra 7:27

Ezra said, “Praise the LORD, the God of our ancestors!

He moved the king to honor the LORD’s house in Jerusalem.

## Ezra 7:28

God helped me win the support of the king, his advisers, and his powerful officials.

The LORD my God was helping me, so I took courage.

I gathered leaders from Israel to go with me to Jerusalem.”
