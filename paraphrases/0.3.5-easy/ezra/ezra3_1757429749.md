# Ezra 3 — The people rebuild the altar and begin the temple

**About this chapter**

The families gather in Jerusalem to worship. First they rebuild the altar. Later, when the temple foundation is ready, some people cheer and others weep.

## The people gather in Jerusalem

## Ezra 3:1

By the seventh month, the Israelites were living in their towns.

Then they came together in Jerusalem.

## Ezra 3:2

Jeshua, son of Jozadak, and the other priests began building an altar.

Zerubbabel, son of Shealtiel, and those with him helped.

They built it to give burnt offerings to the God of Israel.

This was what God’s servant Moses had written in the law.

## Ezra 3:3

They feared the people who lived around them.

Even so, they built the altar on its old base.

Every morning and evening they burned offerings to the LORD there.

**Reading help**

An altar is a place to bring offerings to God. For a burnt offering, the priests burned the gift on the altar.

## The people celebrate the feast

## Ezra 3:4

They celebrated the Feast of Tabernacles as the law directed.

Each day they brought the number of burnt offerings set for that day.

![A temporary shelter with a roof made of branches.](../pictures/feast-shelter.svg)

**Reading help**

At the Feast of Tabernacles the people stayed in shelters. This helped them remember the shelters their ancestors lived in after leaving Egypt.

## Ezra 3:5

After the feast, they kept bringing the regular burnt offerings.

They brought offerings at each new moon and at the LORD’s other sacred festivals.

People also brought gifts they freely chose to give the LORD.

## Ezra 3:6

Their burnt offerings had begun on the first day of the seventh month.

The workers had yet to lay the foundation of the LORD’s temple.

## Workers prepare to rebuild the temple

## Ezra 3:7

The people paid the workers who shaped stone and wood.

They gave food, drink, and oil to people from Sidon and Tyre.

In return, those people brought cedar logs from Lebanon by sea to Joppa.

Cyrus, king of Persia, had given permission for this.

## Ezra 3:8

In the second year after their return, the second month came.

Zerubbabel, Jeshua, and the others began work on the temple in Jerusalem.

The priests, the Levites, and all who had returned from exile joined them.

They chose Levites aged 20 or older to oversee the work.

## Ezra 3:9

Several families of Levites worked together to guide the builders.

They included Jeshua with his sons and brothers, and Kadmiel with his sons, from Yehudah’s family.

The sons of Henadad joined them with their sons and brothers.

## The people sing to the LORD

## Ezra 3:10

The builders laid the foundation of the LORD’s temple.

The priests put on their special clothes and took their trumpets.

Levites from Asaph’s family took their cymbals.

They stood ready to praise the LORD as King David had instructed.

**Reading help**

A foundation is the base that holds up a building. Cymbals are metal plates that musicians strike together.

## Ezra 3:11

The singers took turns answering each other in song.

They praised the LORD and thanked him.

“He is good. His faithful love for Israel lasts forever.”

All the people shouted praise to the LORD because the temple foundation was in place.

## Some people cry and others cheer

## Ezra 3:12

Many older priests, Levites, and family leaders remembered the first temple.

When they saw the new foundation, they cried aloud.

Many others shouted for joy.

## Ezra 3:13

The shouts and the crying were so loud that no one could tell the sounds apart.

People far away could hear them.
