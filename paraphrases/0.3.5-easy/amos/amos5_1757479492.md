# Amos 5 — Let justice flow like a river

**About this chapter**

Amos sings a song of grief over Israel. God sees how the poor are treated when they ask for justice. He calls his people to seek him and live.

## A song of grief for Israel

## Amos 5:1

People of Israel, listen to this song.

It is a song of sorrow about you.

## Amos 5:2

“Israel is like a young woman who has fallen.

She lies alone on her own land.

No one lifts her up. She will never rise again.”

## Amos 5:3

The Lord GOD says, “An Israelite city sends out a thousand soldiers.

Only a hundred will be left.

Another city sends a hundred. Only ten will be left.”

## Seek the LORD and live

## Amos 5:4

The LORD says to Israel, “Come looking for me, and live!

## Amos 5:5

“Do not go to Bethel to find help.

Do not go to Gilgal or travel to Beersheba.

Enemies will take the people of Gilgal away.

Nothing will be left of Bethel.”

## Amos 5:6

Seek the LORD and live.

Otherwise he will sweep through Joseph's people like a fire.

Joseph's people are the people of Israel.

No one in Bethel will be able to put the fire out.

## Amos 5:7

Some of you make justice as bitter as wormwood, a bitter plant.

You throw aside what is right.

## Amos 5:8

Look at the stars called the Pleiades and Orion.

The LORD made them. He turns darkness into dawn.

He turns day into night.

He calls for the waters of the sea and pours them over the earth.

His name is the LORD.

## Amos 5:9

In a flash he brings ruin on the strong.

Even their forts fall.

## Give poor people a fair hearing

## Amos 5:10

People hear court cases at the city gate.

Some of you hate anyone who speaks up there against wrongdoing.

You despise the person who tells the truth.

## Amos 5:11

You crush poor people by demanding grain from them as a tax.

You have built stone houses, but you will never live in them.

You have planted fine vineyards, but you will never drink their wine.

## Amos 5:12

“I know how often you have done wrong.

You take money to rule against people who are in the right.

When poor people come to court, you deny them justice.”

## Amos 5:13

In such evil times, a person with good sense keeps quiet.

## Amos 5:14

Look for what is good. Turn away from evil so you may live.

Then the LORD, who commands heaven's armies, will be with you, just as you say he is.

## Amos 5:15

Hate evil. Love what is good.

Give people a fair hearing at the city gate.

Perhaps the LORD who commands heaven's armies will show kindness to those of Joseph's people who are left.

## No safe place to hide

## Amos 5:16

The Lord, the LORD who commands heaven's armies, says,

“People will weep in every town square.

In every street they will cry, ‘What sorrow!’

They will call the farmers to grieve with them.

They will call the singers of funeral songs to weep aloud.

## Amos 5:17

“People will weep in every vineyard.

For I, the LORD, will pass among you.”

## Amos 5:18

You long for the Day of the LORD, when he comes to act.

What sorrow that day will bring you!

You expect light, but you will find darkness.

## Amos 5:19

Imagine running away from a lion and meeting a bear.

Or imagine getting home and leaning your hand against the wall.

There a snake bites you.

## Amos 5:20

That is what the Day of the LORD will be like for you.

It will be dark all around, without a glimmer of light.

## What God wants from his people

## Amos 5:21

The LORD says, “I hate your religious feasts.

When you gather for worship, it is like a smell I cannot stand.

## Amos 5:22

“You bring me burnt offerings and gifts of grain.

I will not accept them.

You bring well-fed cattle as peace offerings.

I will pay no attention to them.

## Amos 5:23

“Take the noise of your songs away!

I will not listen to your harps.

## Amos 5:24

“Let justice roll on like a river.

Let right actions flow like a stream that never dries up.”

God wants people to keep treating one another fairly.

![Water flows beside a tree. Amos compares doing right to a stream that keeps flowing.](../pictures/jeremiah-tree-by-water.svg)

## Amos 5:25

God asks, “People of Israel, was it sacrifices and offerings you brought me during those forty years in the wilderness?

## Amos 5:26

“You carried Sakkuth, whom you called your king.

You carried Kaiwan, your star god.

You made these idols yourselves.

## Amos 5:27

“So I will send you away from home.

You will live as captives beyond Damascus.”

The LORD who commands heaven's armies has said this.
