# Amos 6 — A warning to leaders who feel safe

**About this chapter**

Amos speaks to leaders who enjoy fine food and music while their people suffer. He warns them that they will be the first to lose their homes.

## The leaders feel safe

## Amos 6:1

Trouble is coming to you who live in comfort in Zion!

Zion is another name for Jerusalem.

On Mount Samaria, you leaders also feel safe.

You are important people in a nation that calls itself first.

The people of Israel come to you for help.

## Amos 6:2

Go and look at Calneh.

Then go to the great city of Hamath.

Go down to Gath, a city of the Philistines.

Are you better than those kingdoms?

Do they have more land than you?

## Amos 6:3

You act as if disaster were far away.

Yet you bring closer the day when violent people rule.

## Feasts while Israel suffers

## Amos 6:4

You sleep on beds decorated with ivory.

You stretch out on couches.

You feast on lambs from your flocks and calves from your stalls.

## Amos 6:5

You make up songs as you play the harp.

Like David, you invent instruments for making music.

![A harp like the instrument David played.](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

## Amos 6:6

You drink whole bowls of wine.

You rub the finest oils on your skin.

Yet you feel no grief as Joseph’s people fall into ruin.

Joseph’s people here means the people of Israel.

## Amos 6:7

So you will be the first ones the enemy takes away.

Your feasts will end.

Your days of lying on couches will be over.

## The city and its houses

## Amos 6:8

The Lord GOD makes a solemn promise in His own name.

The LORD who leads heaven’s armies says, “I hate the pride of Jacob’s people.

I hate the forts they trust in.

I will hand their city and everything in it to the enemy.”

## Amos 6:9

Even if ten men remain in one house, they too will die.

## Amos 6:10

A relative will come to carry out the bodies and burn them.

He will call to someone inside, “Is anyone else with you?”

“No one,” the person will answer.

The relative will say, “Quiet! We must not call on the LORD by name.”

## Amos 6:11

The LORD will give the order.

The great house will break into pieces.

The small house will become a pile of broken stones.

## The LORD answers their boasting

## Amos 6:12

Do horses race along cliffs?

Does anyone use oxen to plow the sea?

Yet you have done something just as senseless.

You have made justice like poison.

What should have been good and right tastes bitter, like the plant called wormwood.

## Amos 6:13

You celebrate your victory at Lo-debar.

You boast, “We took Karnaim by our own strength!”

## Amos 6:14

The LORD who leads heaven’s armies says, “People of Israel, I will bring a nation against you.

They will treat you cruelly throughout your land.

This will reach from Lebo-hamath to the stream in the Arabah.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the leaders fail to grieve over while they feasted?
