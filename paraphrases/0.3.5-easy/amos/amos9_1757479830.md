# Amos 9 — The LORD promises Israel a home again

**About this chapter**

Amos sees the Lord standing beside an altar.

The Lord says no hiding place can keep people from him.

Then he promises to bring his people home.

## No one can hide from the Lord

## Amos 9:1

I saw the Lord standing beside the altar.

An altar is a place where people offer gifts to God.

The Lord said, “Strike the tops of the pillars!

Make the bases of the doorways shake.

Bring the pillars down on the people’s heads.

I will kill the rest with the sword.

Even those who run will not escape.

## Amos 9:2

“Suppose they dig down to Sheol, the place of the dead.

I will reach down and take them.

Suppose they climb up to heaven.

I will pull them down from there.

## Amos 9:3

“They might hide on top of Mount Carmel.

I will search for them there and catch them.

They might hide on the bottom of the sea.

I will tell the sea serpent to bite them there.

## Amos 9:4

“Their enemies might take them away as prisoners.

Even there I will command that they die by the sword.

I will keep my eyes on them to bring them harm.”

## The LORD rules over the earth

## Amos 9:5

The Lord GOD commands heaven’s armies.

He touches the earth, and it melts.

All who live there mourn.

The whole land rises and falls like the Nile River in Egypt.

## Amos 9:6

Amos pictures the sky as a great roof resting on the earth.

God builds his rooms high in the heavens.

He calls for the waters of the sea.

He pours them out over the earth.

His name is the LORD.

## Amos 9:7

The LORD asks, “People of Israel, are you so different from the people of Cush in my eyes?

I brought Israel out of Egypt.

I also brought the Philistines from Caphtor.

I brought the people of Aram from Kir.

## Amos 9:8

“I see this kingdom and the evil it does.

I will wipe the kingdom from the earth.

Yet I will spare some of Jacob’s family.”

Jacob’s family is the people of Israel.

## Amos 9:9

The LORD says, “At my command, Israel will shake among all the nations.

Think of someone shaking grain in a sieve.

Not even a pebble will fall to the ground.”

A sieve has small holes that let some things pass through.

It holds back the larger pieces.

## Amos 9:10

The LORD says, “Some among my people keep doing evil.

They say, ‘Disaster will never come near us.’

All those sinners will die by the sword.”

## The LORD promises to rebuild

## Amos 9:11

The LORD says, “When that day comes, I will raise David’s fallen tent.

I will close the gaps and rebuild the ruined parts.

It will stand again as it did long ago.”

The fallen tent is a picture of David’s kingdom.

## Amos 9:12

The LORD says, “Then my people will take possession of what remains of Edom.

They will also take possession of the nations that bear my name.”

The LORD himself will do this.

## Amos 9:13

The LORD says, “A time is coming when the harvest will last until plowing begins again.

People will still be pressing grapes when others begin sowing seed.

Sweet wine will drip from the mountains and flow down every hill.”

This pictures a harvest so rich that people can hardly gather it all.

## Amos 9:14

The LORD says, “I will bring my people Israel back from captivity.

They will rebuild the ruined cities and live in them.

They will plant grapevines and drink the wine they make.

They will grow gardens and eat their own fruit.

## Amos 9:15

“I will plant my people firmly in the land I gave them.

No one will ever pull them up by the roots again.”

Like plants with strong roots, the people will stay in their land.

This is the promise of the LORD your God.
