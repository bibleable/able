# Amos 3 — Amos explains why he must speak

**About this chapter**

Amos asks people to think about sounds they know. A horn warns a city of danger. Amos brings Israel a warning from the LORD.

## A message for the family God brought out of Egypt

## Amos 3:1

People of Israel, listen to the LORD’s message against you.

It is for the whole family He brought out of Egypt.

## Amos 3:2

The LORD says, “Of all the families on earth, I chose you to be close to Me.

That is why I will hold you responsible for all the wrong you have done.”

## What does a warning tell you?

## Amos 3:3

Can two people walk together unless they agree where to go?

## Amos 3:4

Does a lion roar in the forest when it has no prey?

Does a young lion growl in its den when it has caught nothing?

A den is the place where a lion rests.

## Amos 3:5

Does a bird enter a trap if nothing draws it there?

Does the trap spring shut when there is nothing to catch?

## Amos 3:6

When someone blows a ram’s horn in a city, the people shake with fear.

They know the sound warns of danger.

When disaster comes to a city, has the LORD not brought it about?

![A curved ram’s horn. A person blows into the narrow end to sound a warning.](../pictures/sheba-and-joabs-horn.svg)

## Amos 3:7

The Lord GOD tells His servants what He plans to do before He acts.

These servants are the prophets, who speak His words to the people.

## Amos 3:8

The lion has roared. Who would not be afraid?

The Lord GOD has spoken. How could His prophet keep quiet?

## Witnesses come to Samaria

## Amos 3:9

Send this message to the forts of Ashdod and Egypt.

“Gather on the mountains around Samaria.

See the trouble in the city.

See how cruelly the people there treat others.”

## Amos 3:10

The LORD says, “They no longer know how to do right.

They fill their forts with things they have taken by force.”

## Amos 3:11

So the Lord GOD says, “An enemy will surround your land.

That enemy will tear down your defenses.

Your enemy will take the treasures from your forts.”

## Amos 3:12

The LORD says, “Think of a shepherd who finds a lion eating a sheep.

All he can pull from its mouth is two legs or part of an ear.

So little will be left for the people rescued in Samaria.

They will have just the corner of a bed or a cushion from a couch.

## The altars and houses will fall

## Amos 3:13

“Listen, and tell what you have seen against Jacob’s family.”

This is the word of the Lord GOD, who leads heaven’s armies.

## Amos 3:14

“When I punish Israel for its sins, I will destroy the altars at Bethel.

The points called horns on the altar will break off.

They will fall to the ground.

## Amos 3:15

“I will tear down the houses people use in winter and in summer.

The houses decorated with ivory will fall too.

The great houses will be gone.”

The LORD has spoken.
