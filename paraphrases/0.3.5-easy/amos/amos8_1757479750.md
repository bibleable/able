# Amos 8 — A basket of summer fruit

**About this chapter**

Amos sees summer fruit ready to gather. The LORD tells him that Israel’s time has come. He names the ways traders cheat people who are already poor.

## What Amos sees

## Amos 8:1

The Lord GOD showed me a basket of summer fruit.

## Amos 8:2

He asked, “Amos, what do you see?”

I answered, “A basket of summer fruit.”

The LORD said, “The end has come for My people Israel.

I will spare them no longer.”

## Amos 8:3

The Lord GOD says, “On that day the singing in the temple will turn to weeping.

Dead bodies will lie everywhere.

People will carry them out in silence.”

## Traders cheat people who need food

## Amos 8:4

Listen, you who crush people in need!

You ruin the lives of the poor in this land.

## Amos 8:5

You say, “When will the New Moon festival end?

We want to sell grain again.

When will the Sabbath day of rest be over?

We want to sell wheat.

Let us give people a smaller measure of grain.

Let us weigh out more of their silver than they owe.

Our scales will help us cheat them.

## Amos 8:6

“We can buy poor people for silver.

We can buy a person in need for the price of a pair of sandals.

We can even sell the husks along with the wheat.”

## The LORD remembers

## Amos 8:7

The LORD has sworn by the Pride of Jacob, the one Israel takes pride in.

“I will never forget what these people have done.

## Amos 8:8

“Will the land not shake because of this?

Everyone who lives there will grieve.

The ground will rise and fall like the water of the Nile River in Egypt.

## Amos 8:9

“On that day I will make the sun go down at noon.

I will make the earth dark in broad daylight,” says the Lord GOD.

## Amos 8:10

“Your feasts will become times of grief.

Your songs will become cries of sorrow.

You will wear rough cloth and shave your heads to show your grief.

It will be like grieving for an only son.

That day will end in bitter sorrow.

## People search for a word from the LORD

## Amos 8:11

“A time is coming when I will send a famine.

Usually a famine means people cannot find enough food.

This time people will hunger to hear My words.

## Amos 8:12

“They will stumble from sea to sea.

They will wander from north to east.

They will search for a word from Me and find none.

## Amos 8:13

“On that day even the beautiful young women and the young men will faint from thirst.

## Amos 8:14

“Some swear oaths by the idol that brings guilt on Samaria.

Some swear by the god worshiped at Dan.

Others swear by the way people worship at Beersheba.

Those people will fall and never rise again.”
