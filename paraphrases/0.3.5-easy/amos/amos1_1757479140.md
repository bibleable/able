# Amos 1 — Amos tells why the LORD will punish five neighbors of Israel

**About this chapter**

Amos cared for sheep.

The LORD gave Amos a message about Israel.

Here Amos tells what the LORD says about Israel’s neighbors.

## Amos hears the LORD speak

## Amos 1:1

These are the words of Amos.

Amos tended sheep with other shepherds in Tekoa.

God showed Amos what would happen to Israel.

This happened two years before an earthquake shook the land.

Uzziah was king of Judah then.

Jeroboam, the son of Jehoash, was king of Israel.

![Sheep in a pen like the sheep Amos cared for.](../pictures/sheep-pen.svg)

## Amos 1:2

Amos said, “The LORD roars from Zion.”

Zion is in Jerusalem.

The roar pictures the LORD’s powerful voice.

When the LORD speaks, the shepherds’ fields dry up.

Even plants on top of Mount Carmel dry up.

## Damascus crushed the people of Gilead

## Amos 1:3

The LORD says, “Damascus has sinned three times, even four.”

That means the people kept doing wrong.

The LORD says, “I have decided to punish Damascus.

I will carry out my decision.

They crushed the people of Gilead with iron threshing sleds.”

Farmers dragged these heavy tools over stalks to separate the grain.

## Amos 1:4

The LORD says, “I will send fire on Hazael’s royal house.

The fire will burn down the forts of Ben-hadad.

## Amos 1:5

“I will break down the gates of Damascus.

I will remove the ruler of the Valley of Aven.

I will also remove the ruler of Beth-eden.

Enemies will take the people of Aram away to Kir.”

## Gaza handed people over to Edom

## Amos 1:6

The LORD says, “Gaza has sinned three times, even four.

I have decided to punish Gaza.

I will carry out my decision.

They took whole communities away from their homes.

They handed those people over to Edom.

## Amos 1:7

“So I will send fire on Gaza’s walls.

The fire will burn down the city’s forts.

## Amos 1:8

“I will remove the ruler of Ashdod.

I will also remove the ruler of Ashkelon.

I will strike the city of Ekron.

Even the Philistines who are left will die.”

This is what the Lord GOD says.

## Tyre broke a promise

## Amos 1:9

The LORD says, “Tyre has sinned three times, even four.

I have decided to punish Tyre.

I will carry out my decision.

They handed a whole community of captives over to Edom.

They broke their promise to treat others as brothers.

## Amos 1:10

“So I will send fire on Tyre’s walls.

The fire will burn down the city’s forts.”

## Edom refused to show mercy

## Amos 1:11

The LORD says, “Edom has sinned three times, even four.

I have decided to punish Edom.

I will carry out my decision.

The people of Edom chased their own relatives with swords.

They refused to show them mercy.

They held on to their anger.

They would not let it die down.

## Amos 1:12

“So I will send fire on Teman.

The fire will burn down the forts of Bozrah.”

## Ammon attacked families to take their land

## Amos 1:13

The LORD says, “The people of Ammon have sinned three times, even four.

I have decided to punish Ammon.

I will carry out my decision.

They cut open pregnant women in Gilead.

They did this to take more land.

## Amos 1:14

“So I will start a fire in the walls of Rabbah.

The fire will burn down the city’s forts.

Soldiers will shout as they go into battle.

A fierce wind will blow when the storm comes.

## Amos 1:15

“Enemies will take Ammon’s king away from his land.

They will take his princes away with him.”

The LORD has spoken.
