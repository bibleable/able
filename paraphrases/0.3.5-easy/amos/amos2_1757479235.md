# Amos 2 — The LORD speaks against people who sell the poor

**About this chapter**

Amos brings a message from the LORD.

The LORD speaks to Moab, Judah, and Israel.

He tells each nation why he will punish them.

## Moab dishonored a dead king

## Amos 2:1

The LORD says, “Moab has sinned three times, even four.”

That means the people kept doing wrong.

The LORD says, “I have decided to punish Moab.

I will carry out my decision.

Moab’s people burned the bones of Edom’s king.

They burned them until only white powder remained.

## Amos 2:2

“So I will send fire on Moab.

The fire will burn down the forts in Kerioth.

Moab’s people will die amid the noise of battle.

Soldiers will shout and blow a ram’s horn.”

A ram’s horn made a loud battle signal.

## Amos 2:3

The LORD says, “I will kill Moab’s ruler.

I will also kill all the officials with him.”

## Judah rejected the LORD’s teaching

## Amos 2:4

The LORD says, “Judah has sinned three times, even four.

I have decided to punish Judah.

I will carry out my decision.

Judah’s people rejected my teaching.

They disobeyed my commands.

They believed the same lies their ancestors believed.

Those lies led them away from me.

## Amos 2:5

“So I will send fire on Judah.

It will burn down the forts in Jerusalem.”

## Israel mistreats people in need

## Amos 2:6

The LORD says, “Israel has sinned three times, even four.

I have decided to punish Israel.

I will carry out my decision.

They sell innocent people for silver.

They sell people in need for a pair of sandals.

## Amos 2:7

“They trample poor people’s heads as if they were dust.

They shove people in need out of their way.

A father and son have sex with the same young woman.

By doing this, they dishonor my holy name.

## Amos 2:8

“People who borrow money leave their clothes as a promise to repay.

The lenders spread those clothes beside every altar.

They lie down on them there.”

An altar is a place where people offer gifts to God.

The LORD says, “They make people pay fines.

Then they drink the wine from those fines in my house.”

## The LORD reminds Israel how he helped

## Amos 2:9

The LORD says, “I destroyed the Amorites before you.

They stood tall like cedar trees.

They were strong like oak trees.

Yet I destroyed their fruit above and their roots below.”

Picture a tree with nothing left to grow again.

That is how completely the LORD defeated the Amorites.

## Amos 2:10

The LORD says, “I brought you out of Egypt.

I led you through the desert for forty years.

Then you could take the land of the Amorites.

## Amos 2:11

“I chose some of your sons to be prophets.”

Prophets speak God’s message to the people.

The LORD says, “I chose some young men to be Nazirites.”

Nazirites made a special promise to God.

Part of that promise was to drink no wine.

The LORD asks, “People of Israel, isn’t this true?

## Amos 2:12

“Yet you made the Nazirites drink wine.

You told the prophets to stop speaking my message.”

## Even the fastest runners cannot escape

## Amos 2:13

The LORD says, “I am about to crush you where you stand.

Think of the weight of a cart full of grain.”

![Grain like the crop that fills the heavy cart.](../pictures/grain.svg)

## Amos 2:14

The LORD says, “Even fast runners will find no escape.

Strong people will find their strength fails them.

Even great warriors cannot save their own lives.

## Amos 2:15

“Soldiers with bows will have to give way.

Fast runners cannot get away.

Even riders on horses cannot save their lives.

## Amos 2:16

“On that day even the bravest soldiers will run away naked.”

The LORD has spoken.
