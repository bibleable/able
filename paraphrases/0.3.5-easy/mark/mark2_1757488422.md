# Mark 2 — Jesus forgives and calls people

**About this chapter**

Jesus heals a man who cannot walk and calls Levi to follow him.

People question Jesus about meals, fasting, and the Sabbath.

## Four men bring a man to Jesus

## Mark 2:1

A few days later, Jesus returned to Capernaum.

People heard that he was at home.

## Mark 2:2

A crowd filled the house.

Even the space outside the door was full.

Jesus told the people God’s message.

## Mark 2:3

Four men brought a man who was paralysed.

They carried him to Jesus.

**Help with a word**

Being paralysed means being unable to move part or all of the body.

**About the picture**

![A sleeping mat with a rolled end.](../pictures/mat.svg)

Find the mat.

Four men carried a man on a mat.

## Mark 2:4

The four men could not get through the crowd.

So they made a hole in the roof above Jesus.

They lowered the man through it on his mat.

**About this section**

How did the four men get past the crowd?

## Jesus forgives the man

## Mark 2:5

Jesus saw their trust in him.

He said to the man, “Son, your sins are forgiven.”

**Help with a word**

Sins are wrong things people do against God.

## Mark 2:6

Some teachers of God's law were sitting there.

They were thinking to themselves.

## Mark 2:7

They thought, “Why does this man speak like that?

He is blaspheming!

Who can forgive sins except God alone?”

**Help with “blaspheming”**

This means speaking in a way that dishonors God.

This is the teachers’ accusation.

## Mark 2:8

At once Jesus knew what the teachers were thinking.

Jesus asked, “Why are you thinking these things?”

## Mark 2:9

Jesus asked, “Which is easier to say to this man?

‘Your sins are forgiven’?

Or ‘Get up, take your mat, and walk’?”

## Mark 2:10

Jesus said, “I want you to know this.

The Son of Man can forgive sins here on earth.”

Then Jesus spoke to the man on the mat.

**Help with “Son of Man”**

Jesus uses this title for Himself.

## Mark 2:11

Jesus said, “Get up.

Pick up your mat and go home.”

## Mark 2:12

At once, the man got up and took his mat.

He walked out in front of everyone.

They were all amazed and praised God.

They said, “We have never seen anything like this!”

**About this section**

What did the man carry home?

## Jesus eats at Levi’s house

## Mark 2:13

Jesus went out beside the sea again.

All the people came to him.

He taught them there.

## Mark 2:14

As Jesus walked along, he saw Levi, the son of Alphaeus.

Levi sat at the place where he collected taxes.

Jesus said, “Follow me.”

Levi got up and followed him.

## Mark 2:15

Jesus was eating at Levi's house.

Many tax collectors and sinners ate with Jesus and his followers.

Many of these people followed Jesus.

**Help with words**

Tax collectors collected money that people had to pay to the government.

Sinners means people known for breaking God's law here.

## Mark 2:16

Some teachers of God's law who were Pharisees saw Jesus eating with these people.

They asked his followers, “Why does he eat with tax collectors and sinners?”

**Help with a word**

Pharisees were a Jewish religious group.

## Mark 2:17

Jesus heard them.

He said, “Healthy people do not need a doctor.

Sick people do.

I came to call sinners, rather than people who are right with God.”

**Help with this picture**

Jesus uses the need for a doctor to explain his work among sinners.

**About this section**

What did Levi do when Jesus called him?

## People ask about fasting

## Mark 2:18

John’s followers and the Pharisees often fasted.

People asked Jesus, “John’s followers fast.

The followers of the Pharisees fast too.

Why don’t your followers fast?”

**Help with a word**

Fasting means choosing to go without food for a time, often for prayer.

## Mark 2:19

Jesus answered, “Can wedding guests fast while the bridegroom is with them?

They cannot fast while he is with them.”

**Help with a word**

The bridegroom is the man getting married.

## Mark 2:20

Jesus said, “One day someone will take the bridegroom away.

Then the guests will fast.”

**Help with this picture**

Jesus compares his presence with his followers to a bridegroom being with wedding guests.

**About this section**

When did Jesus say the wedding guests would fast?

## Jesus talks about cloth and wine

## Mark 2:21

Jesus said, “Think about a tear in old clothes.

No one covers it with new cloth that can shrink.

The new patch would pull away.

Then the tear would get bigger.”

## Mark 2:22

Jesus said, “No one puts new wine in old leather bags.

The wine would burst the bags.

That would ruin both the wine and the bags.

People put new wine in new bags.”

**Help with this picture**

New wine gives off gas as it develops.

New leather bags can stretch, but old ones may split.

Where do people put new wine?

## Jesus talks about the day of rest

## Mark 2:23

One Sabbath, Jesus walked through fields of grain.

His followers began picking heads of grain as they walked.

**Help with words**

The Sabbath is the Jewish day of rest.

Heads of grain are the parts of a plant that hold its seeds.

## Mark 2:24

The Pharisees said to Jesus, “Look!

Why are your followers doing something the law does not allow on the Sabbath?”

## Mark 2:25

Jesus answered, “Have you never read what David did?

He and his companions were hungry and needed food.”

## Mark 2:26

Jesus said, “When Abiathar was high priest, David entered God's house.

David ate the bread set apart for God.

Only priests were allowed to eat it.

David also gave some to his companions.”

## Mark 2:27

Jesus said, “The Sabbath was made for people.

People were not made for the Sabbath.”

## Mark 2:28

Jesus said, “So the Son of Man is Lord even of the Sabbath.”
