# 1 Thessalonians 5 — Stay alert and care for each other

**About this chapter**

Paul teaches the church how to wait for the Lord.

He gives examples of ways to care for each other.

## The day of the Lord

## 1 Thessalonians 5:1

Brothers and sisters, we need not write about dates or times.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:2

You already know how the day of the Lord will come.

It will come as unexpectedly as a thief at night.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:3

People will say, “We are safe and at peace.”

Then destruction will come suddenly, like the first pains of childbirth.

They will have no escape.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:4

Brothers and sisters, you do not live in darkness.

That day need not surprise you like a thief.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:5

You all belong to the light and the day.

None of us belongs to the night or the darkness.

**Pause and answer**

Why does Paul compare that day to a thief’s arrival?

## Stay alert and encourage each other

## 1 Thessalonians 5:6

So let us stay alert and clearheaded.

Others are like people who sleep.

**Help with these words**

Staying awake here means being ready for the Lord as you live each day.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:7

People usually sleep at night.

Those who get drunk do so at night.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:8

We belong to the day, so let us stay clearheaded.

Wear faith and love like armor that protects your chest.

Wear your hope of salvation like a helmet.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:9

God chose to save us through our Lord Jesus Christ.

He did not choose us to suffer his anger.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:10

Jesus died for us.

Whether we live or die, we can live together with him.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:11

So encourage each other and help each other grow strong.

Keep doing this, just as you are doing now.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul compare to a helmet?

## Care for people according to their needs

## 1 Thessalonians 5:12

Brothers and sisters, notice those who work hard among you.

They care for you in the Lord and correct you when needed.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:13

Love them and respect them deeply for their work.

Live in peace with each other.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:14

Warn people who disrupt the community.

Give courage to those who feel afraid.

Help those who are weak.

Be patient with everyone.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:15

See that no one pays back harm with harm.

Always seek what is good for each other and for everyone.

**Pause and answer**

How should the believers treat someone who feels afraid?

## Give thanks and hold on to good things

## 1 Thessalonians 5:16

Keep rejoicing at all times.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:17

Keep praying.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:18

Thank God whatever happens.

This is what God wants for you in Christ Jesus.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:19

Do not put out the Spirit’s fire.

**Help with these words**

Paul compares the Spirit’s work to a fire.

He asks the believers to welcome the Spirit’s work among them.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:20

Listen respectfully when people speak messages they believe come from God.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:21

Test everything you hear.

Hold on to what is good.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:22

Keep away from every kind of evil.

**Pause and answer**

After testing what you hear, what should you hold on to?

## Paul’s prayer and last request

## 1 Thessalonians 5:23

May the God of peace make every part of you holy.

May he keep your spirit, soul, and body free from blame when our Lord Jesus Christ comes.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:24

You can trust the God who calls you.

He will do it.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:25

Brothers and sisters, pray for us too.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:26

Greet all the believers with a holy kiss.

**Help with these words**

A holy kiss was a way believers greeted each other as family.

## 1 Thessalonians 5:27

Before the Lord, I give you this charge.

Read this letter aloud to all the believers.
