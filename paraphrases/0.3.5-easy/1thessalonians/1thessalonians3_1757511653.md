# 1 Thessalonians 3 — Timothy brings good news

**About this chapter**

Paul wants to know how his friends are doing.

Timothy visits them and brings word back.

## Timothy goes to help

## 1 Thessalonians 3:1

At last we could bear the waiting no longer.

We agreed to stay by ourselves in Athens.

## 1 Thessalonians 3:2

We sent our brother Timothy to you.

He works alongside us for God, telling the good news about Christ.

We sent him to strengthen your faith and give you courage.

## 1 Thessalonians 3:3

We wanted none of you to lose heart in these troubles.

You know such troubles were appointed for us.

## 1 Thessalonians 3:4

When we were with you, we often warned you about this.

We told you people would make us suffer for our faith.

As you know, that is what happened.

## 1 Thessalonians 3:5

I could wait no longer to learn how your faith was holding up.

So I sent Timothy.

I feared the tempter had drawn you away from God.

Then our work among you would have come to nothing.

**Help with these words**

The tempter is Satan.

To tempt someone means to try to draw them into doing wrong.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Paul send Timothy?

## Timothy brings good news

## 1 Thessalonians 3:6

Timothy has just returned from visiting you.

He brought good news about your faith and love.

You remember us with affection.

You long to see us as much as we long to see you.

## 1 Thessalonians 3:7

Brothers and sisters, we are suffering, and people are treating us cruelly.

But hearing that you still trust God has comforted us.

## 1 Thessalonians 3:8

We feel alive again because you stand firm in the Lord.

## 1 Thessalonians 3:9

You bring us great joy when we come before God.

How can we ever thank God enough for you?

## 1 Thessalonians 3:10

Night and day we pray with all our hearts.

We ask to see you face to face.

We want to help your faith grow where you still need help.

**Pause and answer**

What news comforted Paul and his friends?

## A prayer for growing love

## 1 Thessalonians 3:11

May God our Father and our Lord Jesus open the way for us to visit you.

## 1 Thessalonians 3:12

May the Lord make your love grow and overflow.

May you love one another and everyone else.

That is how much we love you.

![A drinking cup](../pictures/cup.svg)

**Help with these words**

Paul pictures love like water filling a cup and running over its edge.

He wants their love to reach beyond their own group.

## 1 Thessalonians 3:13

May the Lord make your hearts strong.

Then you can stand holy and free from blame before God our Father when our Lord Jesus comes with all his holy ones.

Amen.

**Pause and answer**

Who else does Paul want the believers to love besides each other?
