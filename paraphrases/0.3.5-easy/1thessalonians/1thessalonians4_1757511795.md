# 1 Thessalonians 4 — Encourage each other with hope

**About this chapter**

Paul writes about love and daily work.

He also comforts people who have lost someone they love.

## Live in a way that pleases God

## 1 Thessalonians 4:1

Brothers and sisters, we taught you how to please God.

You already live this way.

In the name of the Lord Jesus, we urge you to keep growing.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:2

You remember the instructions we gave you.

The Lord Jesus gave us the authority to teach them.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:3

God wants you to be holy.

Keep away from sexual wrongdoing.

**Help with these words**

To be holy means to belong to God and live in his ways.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:4

Each of you must learn to control your own body.

Treat your body with holiness and honor.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:5

Do not let sexual desire rule you.

That is how people who do not know God live.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:6

Never wrong another believer or take advantage of them in sexual matters.

The Lord will punish those who do these things.

We gave you this serious warning before.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:7

God called us to live holy lives and leave impurity behind.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:8

If you reject this instruction, you reject God himself.

He is the one who gives you his Holy Spirit.

**Pause and answer**

Who gives the believers his Holy Spirit?

## Keep showing love

## 1 Thessalonians 4:9

You already know about loving each other as family.

God himself has taught you to love each other.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:10

You show that love to believers throughout Macedonia.

Brothers and sisters, we urge you to love them even more.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:11

Make it your aim to live quietly.

Attend to your own affairs.

Work with your own hands, as we taught you.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:12

Then you will act well toward people outside the church.

You will be able to provide for your own needs.

**Pause and answer**

Where do the believers already show love to others?

## Hope when someone dies

## 1 Thessalonians 4:13

Brothers and sisters, we want you to know about believers who have died.

Then, even as you grieve, you can have hope.

**Help with these words**

Paul calls death “sleep” in this passage.

He expects God to raise those who have died.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:14

We believe Jesus died and rose again.

So we believe God will bring with Jesus those who died trusting him.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:15

We tell you what the Lord has said.

Some of us may still be alive when he comes.

We will not go ahead of those who have died.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:16

The Lord himself will come down from heaven.

A loud command will sound.

People will hear the chief angel’s voice and God’s trumpet.

Those who died belonging to Christ will rise first.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:17

Then God will lift those of us still alive into the clouds with them.

Together we will meet the Lord in the air.

We will always be with the Lord.

## 1 Thessalonians 4:18

Use these words to give each other courage.

**Pause and answer**

Who will rise first when the Lord comes?
