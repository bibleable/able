# 1 Thessalonians 2 — Paul remembers his visit

**About this chapter**

Paul reminds the believers how he and his friends cared for them.

He tells them how much he wants to visit again.

## Why we spoke boldly

## 1 Thessalonians 2:1

Brothers and sisters, you know our visit to you did some good.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:2

You know how badly people treated us in Philippi.

They hurt us and shamed us.

Then people opposed us when we came to you.

God gave us courage to tell you his good news.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:3

We never tried to trick you.

We had no hidden purpose or wish to deceive you.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:4

God saw fit to trust us with his good news.

When we speak, we aim to please God.

God knows what is in our hearts.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:5

You know we never praised you just to get something.

We never used our message to hide greed.

God knows this is true.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:6

We never looked for praise from you or anyone else.

As messengers sent by Christ, we could have claimed honor.

**Pause and answer**

Who gave Paul and his friends courage to speak?

## How we cared for you

## 1 Thessalonians 2:7

We were gentle with you, like a mother nursing her children.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:8

We loved you dearly.

We gladly shared God’s good news with you.

We were glad to share our very lives too.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:9

Brothers and sisters, remember how hard we worked.

We worked night and day while telling you God’s good news.

That way none of you had to support us.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:10

You believers saw how we lived, and God saw too.

We lived in a way that was holy, right, and free from blame.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:11

You know we cared for each one of you as a father cares for his children.

| A nursing mother | A caring father |
| --- | --- |
| Treats her children gently | Encourages each child |

## 1 Thessalonians 2:12

We encouraged and comforted you.

We urged you to live in a way that honors God.

God calls you to share his kingdom and glory.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Paul and his friends work night and day?

## You received God’s word

## 1 Thessalonians 2:13

We keep thanking God for the way you listened.

You heard the message from us and recognized it as God’s own word.

That word is at work in you who believe.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:14

Brothers and sisters, your experience was like that of the churches in Judea that belong to Christ Jesus.

People from your own community made you suffer.

Jewish opponents in Judea made the believers there suffer too.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:15

Those opponents killed the Lord Jesus and their own prophets.

They drove us out too.

They displease God and act against the good of everyone.

**Help with these words**

A prophet speaks a message from God.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:16

They try to stop us from telling Gentiles how God can save them.

They keep adding to their sins until the measure is full.

God’s anger has come upon them in full.

**Help with these words**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish.

**Pause and answer**

Whose message did the believers hear through Paul and his friends?

## Paul longs to return

## 1 Thessalonians 2:17

Brothers and sisters, we had to leave you for a little while.

We still held you close in our hearts.

Being apart made us want to see you even more.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:18

We wanted to come to you.

I, Paul, tried again and again.

But Satan stood in our way.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:19

When our Lord Jesus comes, we will stand before him.

Who will be our hope, our joy, and our crown?

You will!

**Help with these words**

A winner could receive a crown as a sign of honor.

Paul calls his friends his crown because they give him joy and make him proud.

## 1 Thessalonians 2:20

You are our glory and our joy.

**Pause and answer**

Whom does Paul long to see again?
