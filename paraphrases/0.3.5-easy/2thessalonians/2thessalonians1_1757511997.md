# 2 Thessalonians 1 — God sees you when you suffer

**About this chapter**

Paul and his friends write again to the church in Thessalonica.

They thank God for believers who keep trusting him through trouble.

## Your faith and love keep growing

## 2 Thessalonians 1:1

Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy send this letter.

We greet the church in Thessalonica.

You belong to God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

## 2 Thessalonians 1:2

May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you grace and peace.

**Help with these words**

Grace is kindness God gives freely.

## 2 Thessalonians 1:3

Brothers and sisters, we owe God thanks for you.

Your trust in him keeps growing.

Each of you loves the others more and more.

## 2 Thessalonians 1:4

We tell God’s other churches how proud we are of you.

People hurt you and make life hard for you.

Still you keep going and trusting God.

| What Paul heard from Thessalonica | What Paul told the other churches |
| --- | --- |
| The believers faced trouble. | They kept trusting God. |

**Pause and answer**

What two things keep growing among the believers?

## God judges fairly

## 2 Thessalonians 1:5

All this shows that God judges fairly.

You suffer because you belong to his kingdom.

God will count you worthy of that kingdom.

**Help with these words**

God’s kingdom means the people and the world under his rule.

## 2 Thessalonians 1:6

It is right for God to bring trouble on those who trouble you.

## 2 Thessalonians 1:7

It is also right for God to give you relief, and us too.

This will happen when the Lord Jesus appears from heaven with his powerful angels.

## 2 Thessalonians 1:8

Jesus will come in blazing fire.

He will punish those who refuse to know God and obey the good news about our Lord Jesus.

## 2 Thessalonians 1:9

Their punishment will be destruction that lasts forever.

They will be shut out from the Lord’s presence and from the glory of his power.

## 2 Thessalonians 1:10

On that day his people will bring him glory.

Everyone who has trusted him will look on him with wonder.

That includes you, because you believed what we told you.

**Pause and answer**

Who will give the suffering believers relief?

## What we pray for you

## 2 Thessalonians 1:11

That is why we always pray for you.

May God count you worthy of the life he called you to.

May his power bring every good thing you want to do to completion.

May he finish the work you do because you trust him.

## 2 Thessalonians 1:12

Then your lives will bring honor to the name of our Lord Jesus.

You too will share in his glory.

This comes through the kindness of our God and the Lord Jesus Christ.

**Pause and answer**

Whose power can help the believers finish their good work?
