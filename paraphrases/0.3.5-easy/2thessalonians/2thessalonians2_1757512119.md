# 2 Thessalonians 2 — Hold on to what you learned

**About this chapter**

A report about the Lord’s return has upset the church.

Paul reminds his friends what he told them in person.

## A warning about a false report

## 2 Thessalonians 2:1

Brothers and sisters, our Lord Jesus Christ will come.

He will gather us together to be with him.

Here is what we ask of you.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:2

Someone may say the day of the Lord has already come.

They may claim a spirit told them so.

They may repeat a message or show you a letter they say came from us.

Do not let this shake or frighten you.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:3

Do not let anyone trick you.

First people will rebel against God.

Then the man who defies God’s law will be revealed.

Destruction awaits that man.

**Help with these words**

To rebel means to turn against someone’s rule.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:4

He will oppose everything people call a god or worship.

He will claim to be greater than all of them.

He will even sit in God’s temple and call himself God.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:5

Remember that I told you this when I was with you.

**Pause and answer**

What false report had frightened the believers?

## Evil is already at work

## 2 Thessalonians 2:6

You know what is holding that man back now.

He will appear at the proper time.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:7

Already, the power that defies God’s law is secretly at work.

The one holding it back will keep doing so until he is taken out of the way.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:8

Then the man who defies God’s law will appear.

The Lord Jesus will kill him with his breath.

The glory of Jesus at his coming will bring that man to nothing.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:9

When that man comes, Satan will work through him.

He will use all kinds of powers, signs, and false wonders.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:10

He will use every kind of evil trick on those who are perishing.

They refused to love the truth that could have saved them.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:11

Because of this, God will send them a powerful delusion.

They will believe the lie.

**Help with these words**

A delusion is a false belief that seems true.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:12

God will judge all who refused to believe the truth and took pleasure in evil.

**Pause and answer**

Who will put an end to the man who defies God’s law?

## Hold on to what we taught you

## 2 Thessalonians 2:13

Brothers and sisters, the Lord loves you.

We should always thank God for you.

From the beginning God chose to save you.

His Spirit makes you holy, and you believe the truth.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:14

God called you through the good news we brought you.

He wants you to share the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:15

So stand firm, brothers and sisters.

Hold on to what we taught you in person and by letter.

| In person | By letter |
| --- | --- |
| The believers heard Paul teach. | The believers read what Paul taught. |

## 2 Thessalonians 2:16

Think of our Lord Jesus Christ and God our Father.

God loved us and freely gave us comfort that lasts forever.

He gave us good reason to hope.

## 2 Thessalonians 2:17

May they give you courage and strength in every good thing you say and do.

**Pause and answer**

How did the believers receive the teaching Paul asks them to keep?
