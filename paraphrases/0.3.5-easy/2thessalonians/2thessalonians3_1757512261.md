# 2 Thessalonians 3 — Keep doing good

**About this chapter**

Paul asks his friends to pray for him.

He reminds them how he worked to pay for his food.

## Pray for the message to spread

## 2 Thessalonians 3:1

Brothers and sisters, please pray for us.

Ask that the Lord’s message will spread quickly.

May people honor it just as you did.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:2

Pray that God will rescue us from people who do evil.

Some people refuse to believe.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:3

You can trust the Lord.

He will give you strength and guard you from the evil one.

**Help with these words**

The evil one is another name for the devil.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:4

We trust the Lord that you are doing what we tell you.

We trust that you will keep doing it.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:5

May the Lord guide your hearts into God’s love.

May he help you keep going as Christ did.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul ask the church to pray about the Lord’s message?

## Remember how we worked

## 2 Thessalonians 3:6

Brothers and sisters, we give you this command in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.

Keep away from believers who refuse to live responsibly according to what we taught you.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:7

You saw how we lived among you.

We did our share of the work.

You know how to follow our example.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:8

We paid for the food we ate.

We worked hard night and day.

We wanted to spare you the cost of feeding us.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:9

We had the right to ask for your support.

We chose to work to give you an example to follow.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:10

You remember the rule we gave when we were there.

Anyone who refuses to work should not eat.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Paul and his friends pay for their food?

## Keep doing good

## 2 Thessalonians 3:11

We hear some of you are avoiding your own work.

Instead, you spend your time interfering in other people’s business.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:12

In the Lord Jesus Christ we urge those people to change.

We command them to work quietly and earn their own food.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:13

Brothers and sisters, keep doing good even when you feel tired.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:14

Notice if someone refuses to obey what we say in this letter.

Stop keeping company with that person so they will feel ashamed.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:15

Remember that person still belongs to your family of believers.

Warn them as a brother or sister.

Never treat them as an enemy.

**Pause and answer**

How should believers speak to someone who ignores the letter?

## Paul sends his greeting

## 2 Thessalonians 3:16

May the Lord of peace himself give you peace at all times and in every way.

May the Lord be with all of you.

## 2 Thessalonians 3:17

I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand.

This is how I write.

My handwriting marks every letter as mine.
