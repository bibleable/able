# Luke 3 — John prepares the people for Jesus

**About this chapter**

John tells people how to turn back to God.

Luke tells about Jesus’ baptism and traces his family line.

## God speaks to John

## Luke 3:1

Tiberius Caesar was in his fifteenth year as emperor.

Pontius Pilate governed Judea, and Herod ruled Galilee.

Herod’s brother Philip ruled Ituraea and Trachonitis.

Lysanias ruled Abilene.

## Luke 3:2

Annas and Caiaphas were the high priests at that time.

John, Zechariah’s son, was in the desert.

There God spoke to him.

## Luke 3:3

John went throughout the area around the Jordan River.

He told people to turn away from sin and be baptized so God would forgive their sins.

**Help with these words**

Sin is wrongdoing against God.

Baptism uses water.

Here it shows that people are turning back to God.

**Pause and answer**

Where was John when God spoke to him?

## A road made ready

## Luke 3:4

The prophet Isaiah had written these words.

“Someone is calling out in the desert.

‘Prepare the way for the Lord.

Make straight paths for him.

## Luke 3:5

“‘Every valley will be filled in.

Every mountain and hill will be made low.

Crooked roads will become straight.

Rough roads will become smooth.

## Luke 3:6

“‘All people will see how God saves.’”

**Help with this picture**

People might prepare a road for a king to come.

John was preparing people to welcome the Lord.

**Pause and answer**

Who is coming along the road in Isaiah’s picture?

## John asks for good fruit

## Luke 3:7

Crowds came for John to baptize them.

John said, “You family of poisonous snakes!

Who warned you to flee from God’s coming anger?

## Luke 3:8

“Grow fruit that shows you have turned from sin.

Do not tell yourselves, ‘Abraham is our father.’

God can make children for Abraham from these stones.

## Luke 3:9

“The axe is ready at the roots of the trees.

Every tree without good fruit will be cut down.

It will be thrown into the fire.”

**Help with this picture**

Good fruit shows that a tree is healthy.

Good actions show that a person has turned back to God.

**Pause and answer**

What does good fruit stand for in John’s warning?

## People ask what to do

## Luke 3:10

The people asked John, “What should we do, then?”

## Luke 3:11

John answered, “If you have two shirts, give one to someone who has none.

If you have food, share it too.”

## Luke 3:12

Tax collectors also came to be baptized.

They asked, “Teacher, what should we do?”

## Luke 3:13

John answered, “Collect only the amount you have permission to collect.”

## Luke 3:14

Some soldiers asked, “And what should we do?”

John answered, “Do not force people to give you money.

Do not tell lies about them to get money.

Be content with your pay.”

**Pause and answer**

What could someone with two shirts do?

## John speaks about the one who is coming

## Luke 3:15

The people were waiting for the Christ.

Everyone wondered whether John might be the Christ.

**Help with a word**

Christ means the King God promised to send.

## Luke 3:16

John told them all, “I baptize you with water.

But someone more powerful than I am is coming.

I do not deserve even to untie his sandals.

He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and with fire.

## Luke 3:17

“He holds a fork for tossing grain into the air.

He will clear the place where he separates the grain.

He will gather the wheat into his barn.

He will burn the chaff in a fire no one can put out.”

**About the picture**

![A stalk with ripe grain at the top.](../pictures/grain.svg)

Find the grain at the top of the stalk.

Chaff is the light covering around the grain.

John uses the farmer’s work as a picture of judgment.

## Luke 3:18

John urged the people to listen in many other ways too.

He told them the good news.

**Pause and answer**

What will the farmer gather into his barn?

## Herod puts John in prison

## Luke 3:19

John also spoke against Herod, the ruler of Galilee.

He challenged Herod about Herodias, his brother’s wife.

He spoke against all the other wrong things Herod had done.

## Luke 3:20

Herod did one more wrong thing.

He locked John in prison.

**Pause and answer**

Who put John in prison?

## A voice from heaven

## Luke 3:21

When the people were being baptized, Jesus was baptized too.

As Jesus prayed, heaven opened.

## Luke 3:22

The Holy Spirit came down on Jesus in a form like a dove.

A voice from heaven said, “You are my Son.

I love you.

I am very pleased with you.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the voice call Jesus?

## The family record begins with Joseph

## Luke 3:23

Jesus was about thirty when he began his work.

People thought Jesus was Joseph’s son.

Joseph was Heli’s son.

**Help with the family record**

Luke goes back through the family one generation at a time.

Each line names a son and then his father.

## Luke 3:24

Heli was the son of Matthat.

Matthat was the son of Levi.

Levi was the son of Melchi.

Melchi was the son of Jannai.

Jannai was the son of Joseph.

## Luke 3:25

Joseph was the son of Mattathias.

Mattathias was the son of Amos.

Amos was the son of Nahum.

Nahum was the son of Esli.

Esli was the son of Naggai.

## Luke 3:26

Naggai was the son of Maath.

Maath was the son of Mattathias.

Mattathias was the son of Semein.

Semein was the son of Josech.

Josech was the son of Joda.

**Pause and answer**

About how old was Jesus when he began his work?

## From Joda back to David

## Luke 3:27

Joda was the son of Joanan.

Joanan was the son of Rhesa.

Rhesa was the son of Zerubbabel.

Zerubbabel was the son of Shealtiel.

Shealtiel was the son of Neri.

## Luke 3:28

Neri was the son of Melchi.

Melchi was the son of Addi.

Addi was the son of Cosam.

Cosam was the son of Elmadam.

Elmadam was the son of Er.

## Luke 3:29

Er was the son of Joshua.

Joshua was the son of Eliezer.

Eliezer was the son of Jorim.

Jorim was the son of Matthat.

Matthat was the son of Levi.

## Luke 3:30

Levi was the son of Simeon.

Simeon was the son of Judah.

Judah was the son of Joseph.

Joseph was the son of Jonam.

Jonam was the son of Eliakim.

## Luke 3:31

Eliakim was the son of Melea.

Melea was the son of Menna.

Menna was the son of Mattatha.

Mattatha was the son of Nathan.

Nathan was the son of David.

**Pause and answer**

Who was Nathan’s father in this list?

## From David back through Abraham

## Luke 3:32

David was the son of Jesse.

Jesse was the son of Obed.

Obed was the son of Boaz.

Boaz was the son of Sala.

Sala was the son of Nahshon.

## Luke 3:33

Nahshon was the son of Amminadab.

Amminadab was the son of Admin.

Admin was the son of Arni.

Arni was the son of Hezron.

Hezron was the son of Perez.

Perez was the son of Judah.

## Luke 3:34

Judah was the son of Jacob.

Jacob was the son of Isaac.

Isaac was the son of Abraham.

Abraham was the son of Terah.

Terah was the son of Nahor.

**Pause and answer**

Who was Isaac’s father?

## The record reaches Adam

## Luke 3:35

Nahor was the son of Serug.

Serug was the son of Reu.

Reu was the son of Peleg.

Peleg was the son of Eber.

Eber was the son of Shelah.

## Luke 3:36

Shelah was the son of Cainan.

Cainan was the son of Arphaxad.

Arphaxad was the son of Shem.

Shem was the son of Noah.

Noah was the son of Lamech.

## Luke 3:37

Lamech was the son of Methuselah.

Methuselah was the son of Enoch.

Enoch was the son of Jared.

Jared was the son of Mahalalel.

Mahalalel was the son of Cainan.

## Luke 3:38

Cainan was the son of Enosh.

Enosh was the son of Seth.

Seth was the son of Adam.

Adam was the son of God.

**Pause and answer**

Who is the last person Luke calls the son of God in this list?
