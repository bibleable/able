# Luke 4 — Jesus reads good news to the people

**About this chapter**

Jesus answers the devil in the desert.

In Nazareth he reads aloud from Isaiah.

## A stone and a loaf of bread

## Luke 4:1

Jesus left the Jordan River, full of the Holy Spirit.

The Spirit led him into the desert.

## Luke 4:2

For forty days the devil tried to make Jesus disobey God.

Jesus ate nothing during those days.

At the end, he was hungry.

## Luke 4:3

The devil said, “If you are God’s Son, tell this stone to turn into bread.”

## Luke 4:4

Jesus answered, “Scripture says, ‘People cannot live on bread alone.’”

**Help with a word**

Scripture means the sacred writings that tell us God’s words.

**Pause and answer**

What did the devil tell Jesus to do with the stone?

## The devil offers kingdoms

## Luke 4:5

The devil took Jesus up to a high place.

In a moment he showed Jesus all the kingdoms of the world.

## Luke 4:6

The devil said, “I will give you power to rule all these kingdoms.

You can have all their glory too.

This power was given to me.

I can give it to anyone I choose.

## Luke 4:7

“If you worship me, it will all be yours.”

## Luke 4:8

Jesus answered, “Scripture says, ‘Worship the Lord your God.

Serve him alone.’”

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Jesus say to worship?

## On top of the temple

## Luke 4:9

Then the devil took Jesus to Jerusalem.

He set Jesus on the highest point of the temple.

“If you are God’s Son, throw yourself down,” he said.

## Luke 4:10

“Scripture says, ‘God will order his angels to guard you carefully.

## Luke 4:11

“‘They will lift you up in their hands.

Your foot will never strike a stone.’”

## Luke 4:12

Jesus answered, “Scripture also says, ‘Do not put the Lord your God to the test.’”

## Luke 4:13

The devil finished testing Jesus in all these ways.

He left Jesus until another chance came.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus say about testing God?

## Jesus opens a scroll in Nazareth

## Luke 4:14

Jesus returned to Galilee with the power of the Holy Spirit.

News about Jesus spread all around the area.

## Luke 4:15

Jesus taught in the synagogues.

Everyone praised him.

**Help with a word**

A synagogue is a place where Jewish people meet for worship.

## Luke 4:16

Then Jesus came to Nazareth, where he had grown up.

On the Sabbath he went to the synagogue as usual.

He stood up to read aloud.

## Luke 4:17

Someone handed Jesus the scroll of the prophet Isaiah.

Jesus unrolled it and found these words.

**About the picture**

![A scroll open between two rolled ends.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

Find the part between the rolled ends.

That is where Jesus could see the words to read.

**Pause and answer**

Whose words did Jesus find in the scroll?

## Good news for people in need

## Luke 4:18

“The Spirit of the Lord is on me.

He has chosen me to bring good news to poor people.

He sent me to announce freedom for prisoners.

He sent me to tell blind people they will see again.

He sent me to free people others hold down.

**Help with a word**

Isaiah uses the word “anointed.”

Anointing someone with oil was a sign of choosing them for special work.

## Luke 4:19

“He sent me to announce the year of the Lord’s kindness.”

## Luke 4:20

Jesus rolled up the scroll and gave it back to the helper.

Then Jesus sat down.

Everyone in the synagogue was watching him.

## Luke 4:21

Jesus began, “Today, as you listen, these words of Scripture have come true.”

**Pause and answer**

What good news did Jesus read about prisoners?

## People ask about Joseph’s son

## Luke 4:22

Everyone spoke well of Jesus.

They were amazed at his gracious words.

They asked, “Isn’t this Joseph’s son?”

## Luke 4:23

Jesus said, “You will surely quote this saying to me.

‘Doctor, heal yourself!’

You will say, ‘We heard what you did in Capernaum.

Do the same here in your own town.’”

**Help with this saying**

The people want Jesus to do in his hometown what he did elsewhere.

## Luke 4:24

He added, “I tell you the truth.

People do not welcome a prophet in his own town.

**Pause and answer**

Where had the people heard about Jesus doing great things?

## A widow and a man from Syria

## Luke 4:25

“Think of the many widows in Israel when Elijah lived.

No rain fell for three and a half years.

There was little food anywhere in the land.

## Luke 4:26

“Yet God sent Elijah to none of those widows.

God sent him to a widow in Zarephath, in Sidon.

## Luke 4:27

“Many people in Israel had leprosy when the prophet Elisha lived.

None of them were made clean.

Only Naaman, a man from Syria, was made clean.”

**Help with these words**

Leprosy was a name for serious skin diseases.

Here “made clean” means the disease was gone.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Naaman come from?

## The crowd drives Jesus away

## Luke 4:28

Everyone in the synagogue grew angry when they heard this.

## Luke 4:29

They got up and drove Jesus out of town.

Their town stood on a hill.

They took him to its edge to throw him off the cliff.

## Luke 4:30

But Jesus walked through the crowd and went on his way.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus do when the crowd took him to the cliff?

## An evil spirit leaves a man

## Luke 4:31

Jesus went down to Capernaum, a town in Galilee.

On the Sabbath he began teaching the people.

## Luke 4:32

His teaching amazed them.

They could hear that he spoke with authority.

## Luke 4:33

There in the synagogue was a man with an evil spirit.

He cried out loudly,

## Luke 4:34

“Ha!

What do you want with us, Jesus of Nazareth?

Have you come to destroy us?

I know who you are!

You are the Holy One of God!”

## Luke 4:35

Jesus spoke sharply to the spirit.

“Be quiet!

Come out of him!”

The spirit threw the man down in front of everyone.

Then it left him without hurting him.

## Luke 4:36

Everyone was amazed.

They asked each other, “What kind of message is this?

He has the right and the power to command evil spirits.

He speaks, and they come out!”

## Luke 4:37

News about Jesus spread throughout the area.

**Pause and answer**

What happened when Jesus ordered the spirit to come out?

## Jesus cares for sick people

## Luke 4:38

Jesus left the synagogue and went to Simon’s house.

The mother of Simon’s wife had a high fever.

The people there asked Jesus to help her.

## Luke 4:39

Jesus stood over her and ordered the fever to leave.

The fever left her.

She got up at once and began to serve them.

## Luke 4:40

At sunset people brought everyone who was ill to Jesus.

They had many different illnesses.

Jesus put his hands on each person and healed them.

## Luke 4:41

Evil spirits came out of many people.

The spirits shouted, “You are the Son of God!”

Jesus ordered them to stop speaking.

They knew he was the Christ.

**Help with a name**

Christ means the King God promised to send.

**Pause and answer**

What did the woman do when her fever left?

## Other towns need the good news

## Luke 4:42

At dawn Jesus went to a place where he could be alone.

The crowds looked for him.

When they found him, they tried to keep him there.

## Luke 4:43

Jesus said, “I must tell other towns the good news about God’s kingdom too.

That is why God sent me.”

**Help with these words**

God’s kingdom means God ruling as King.

## Luke 4:44

Jesus continued to preach in the synagogues of Judea.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Jesus say he must leave?
