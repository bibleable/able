# Luke 22 — Jesus shares a meal, prays, and is arrested

**About this chapter**

Jesus shares the Passover meal with his followers.

That night Judas brings a crowd to arrest Jesus.

## Judas makes an agreement

## Luke 22:1

The Feast of Unleavened Bread was near.

People called this feast Passover.

**Help with these words**

At Passover, Jewish families remember how God freed their people from slavery in Egypt.

Unleavened bread is bread made without yeast.

## Luke 22:2

The chief priests and teachers of God’s law were afraid of the people.

They looked for a way to kill Jesus.

## Luke 22:3

Then Satan entered Judas Iscariot.

Judas was one of Jesus’ twelve closest followers.

## Luke 22:4

Judas went to the chief priests and temple officers.

They discussed how Judas could hand Jesus over to them.

## Luke 22:5

The leaders were pleased.

They agreed to give Judas money.

## Luke 22:6

Judas agreed.

He waited for a time when no crowd was around.

Then he could hand Jesus over.

**Pause and answer**

When did Judas plan to hand Jesus over?

## Peter and John prepare the meal

## Luke 22:7

The day of Unleavened Bread came.

On that day people sacrificed a lamb for Passover.

## Luke 22:8

Jesus sent Peter and John ahead.

He said, “Go and prepare the Passover meal for us.”

## Luke 22:9

They asked, “Where do you want us to prepare it?”

## Luke 22:10

Jesus answered, “When you enter the city, a man will meet you.

He will be carrying a water jug.

Follow him into the house he enters.

## Luke 22:11

“Tell the owner, ‘The Teacher asks where the guest room is.

He wants to eat the Passover meal with his followers.’

## Luke 22:12

“The owner will show you a large room upstairs.

It already has furniture.

Prepare the meal there.”

## Luke 22:13

Peter and John went.

They found everything just as Jesus had said.

They prepared the Passover meal.

**Pause and answer**

What was the man carrying who led Peter and John to the house?

## Jesus shares the Passover meal

## Luke 22:14

When it was time, Jesus took his place at the table.

His apostles joined him.

**Help with these words**

The apostles were followers Jesus chose and sent out.

## Luke 22:15

Jesus said, “I have wanted very much to eat this Passover meal with you before I suffer.

## Luke 22:16

“I tell you, I will not eat it again until it comes to fulfilment in God’s kingdom.”

## Luke 22:17

Jesus took a cup and thanked God.

He said, “Take this and share it among you.

**About the picture**

![A drinking cup with a handle.](../pictures/cup.svg)

Point to the cup.

At the meal, Jesus asked the apostles to share what was in the cup.

## Luke 22:18

“I tell you, I will not drink wine again until God’s kingdom comes.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus ask the apostles to share?

## Bread and a cup to remember Jesus

## Luke 22:19

Jesus took bread and thanked God.

He broke the bread and gave it to them.

He said, “This is my body, which I give for you.

Do this to remember me.”

## Luke 22:20

After supper Jesus took the cup in the same way.

He said, “This cup is the new covenant in my blood.

I pour out my blood for you.

**Help with these words**

A covenant is a lasting agreement.

Jesus speaks of his blood because he will give his life for them.

## Luke 22:21

“Look at the table.

The hand of the man who will betray me is here with mine.

## Luke 22:22

“The Son of Man will go to his death as God has planned.

But how terrible for the man who betrays him!”

**Help with these words**

Son of Man is a name Jesus uses for himself.

## Luke 22:23

The followers began asking each other who would do this.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus ask his followers to remember when they broke the bread?

## A leader serves others

## Luke 22:24

The followers also argued about which of them was the greatest.

## Luke 22:25

Jesus said, “The kings of the Gentiles order their people about.

Their rulers call themselves people who do good for others.

## Luke 22:26

“You must act differently.

The greatest among you should take the place of the youngest.

The leader should serve the others.

## Luke 22:27

“Who seems greater at a meal?

Is it the guest or the person serving the food?

People say the guest is greater.

Yet I am here among you as one who serves.

**Pause and answer**

How did Jesus say a leader should treat others?

## Jesus promises his followers a place

## Luke 22:28

“You stayed with me through hard times.

## Luke 22:29

“My Father gave me a kingdom.

Now I give you a kingdom.

## Luke 22:30

“You will eat and drink at my table in my kingdom.

You will sit on thrones and judge the twelve tribes of Israel.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Jesus say his followers would eat with him?

## Jesus prays for Simon Peter

## Luke 22:31

“Simon, Simon! Satan has asked to sift all of you like wheat.

**Help with these words**

Simon is another name for Peter.

People shake wheat in a sieve to separate the grain from other bits.

Jesus uses that shaking to describe how Satan wants to test the followers.

## Luke 22:32

“But Simon, I have prayed for you.

I have asked that you will keep trusting me.

When you turn back to me, help the other followers stand firm.”

## Luke 22:33

Peter said, “Lord, I am ready to go to prison with you.

I am ready to die with you.”

## Luke 22:34

Jesus answered, “Peter, today you will say three times that you do not know me.

This will happen before the rooster crows.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus ask Peter to do when he turned back?

## Jesus speaks about what is coming

## Luke 22:35

Then Jesus asked his followers, “I sent you out without a money bag, a travel bag, or sandals.

Did you need anything you did not have?”

They answered, “Nothing.”

## Luke 22:36

Jesus said, “This time, take your money bag and your travel bag.

Anyone without a sword should sell his cloak and buy one.

## Luke 22:37

“The Scriptures say, ‘People counted him among those who broke the law.’

I tell you, those words must come true about me.

What the Scriptures say about me is happening now.”

## Luke 22:38

The followers said, “Look, Lord, here are two swords.”

Jesus answered, “That is enough.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus say people would count him among?

## Jesus prays to his Father

## Luke 22:39

Jesus went out to the Mount of Olives as usual.

His followers went with him.

## Luke 22:40

When they arrived, Jesus told them, “Pray that you will not give in to temptation.”

## Luke 22:41

Jesus walked a short way from them, about as far as someone could throw a stone.

He knelt down and prayed.

## Luke 22:42

“Father, if you are willing, take this cup away from me.

Yet do what you want, even if I want something else.”

**Help with these words**

Here Jesus calls the suffering ahead of him a cup.

He asks his Father if he can be spared that suffering.

**Pause and answer**

Whose will did Jesus ask his Father to carry out?

## Jesus returns to his followers

## Luke 22:43

An angel from heaven appeared to Jesus and gave him strength.

## Luke 22:44

Jesus was in great distress.

He prayed even harder.

His sweat fell to the ground like drops of blood.

## Luke 22:45

Jesus got up from prayer and found his followers asleep.

Grief had worn them out.

## Luke 22:46

Jesus asked, “Why are you sleeping?

Get up and pray so you will not give in to temptation.”

**Pause and answer**

Why had the followers fallen asleep?

## Judas arrives with a crowd

## Luke 22:47

While Jesus was speaking, a crowd arrived.

Judas, one of the twelve, led them.

Judas came close to kiss Jesus.

## Luke 22:48

Jesus asked, “Judas, are you handing over the Son of Man with a kiss?”

## Luke 22:49

The people with Jesus saw what was about to happen.

They asked, “Lord, should we use our swords?”

## Luke 22:50

One of them struck the high priest’s servant.

He cut off the servant’s right ear.

## Luke 22:51

Jesus said, “Stop! That is enough!”

He touched the man’s ear and healed him.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus do for the wounded servant?

## Jesus speaks to the leaders

## Luke 22:52

Jesus spoke to the chief priests, temple officers, and elders who had come for him.

“Have you come with swords and clubs as if I were a criminal?

## Luke 22:53

“I was with you in the temple courts every day.

You never seized me there.

But this is your hour, when darkness has power.”

**Help with these words**

Jesus speaks of darkness to describe the power of evil.

**Pause and answer**

Where had the leaders seen Jesus every day?

## Peter sits by the fire

## Luke 22:54

They seized Jesus and took him to the high priest’s house.

Peter followed some distance behind.

## Luke 22:55

People lit a fire in the middle of the courtyard.

Peter sat with them around the fire.

## Luke 22:56

A servant girl saw Peter in the firelight.

She looked closely and said, “This man was with Jesus too.”

## Luke 22:57

Peter denied it.

He said, “Woman, I do not know him.”

## Luke 22:58

A little later someone else saw Peter.

“You are one of them too,” the man said.

Peter answered, “Man, I am not.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Peter tell the servant girl?

## The rooster crows

## Luke 22:59

About an hour later another man spoke up.

“I am sure this man was with Jesus.

He comes from Galilee too.”

## Luke 22:60

Peter answered, “Man, I do not know what you mean.”

While Peter was still speaking, the rooster crowed.

## Luke 22:61

The Lord Jesus turned and looked at Peter.

Peter remembered what Jesus had told him.

“Before the rooster crows today, you will deny me three times.”

## Luke 22:62

Peter went outside and cried bitterly.

**Pause and answer**

What did Peter remember when Jesus looked at him?

## The guards mock Jesus

## Luke 22:63

The men guarding Jesus made fun of him and beat him.

## Luke 22:64

They covered his eyes and kept asking, “Speak as a prophet!

Tell us who hit you!”

## Luke 22:65

They said many other things to insult him.

**Pause and answer**

What did the guards cover so Jesus could not see?

## The council questions Jesus

## Luke 22:66

At daybreak the elders gathered for a council meeting.

The chief priests and teachers of God’s law were there.

They brought Jesus before the council.

## Luke 22:67

They said, “If you are the Christ, tell us.”

Jesus answered, “If I tell you, you will not believe me.

## Luke 22:68

“If I ask you a question, you will not answer.

## Luke 22:69

“But from now on the Son of Man will sit at the right hand of the mighty God.”

**Help with these words**

The Christ is the King God chose.

The seat at God’s right hand is a place of honour and power.

## Luke 22:70

They all asked, “Are you, then, the Son of God?”

Jesus answered, “You say that I am.”

## Luke 22:71

The council members said, “Why do we need any more witnesses?

We heard him say it ourselves.”

**Pause and answer**

What did all the council members ask Jesus?
