# Luke 11 — Jesus teaches his followers to pray

**About this chapter**

Jesus teaches a prayer and tells a story about asking for help.

Later, at a meal, he speaks about what God sees inside people.

## Teach us to pray

## Luke 11:1

One day Jesus finished praying.

One follower said, “Lord, teach us to pray.

John taught his followers to pray.”

## Luke 11:2

Jesus told them, “When you pray, say these words.

‘Father, may everyone honour your holy name.

May your kingdom come.

## Luke 11:3

“‘Give us the bread we need each day.

## Luke 11:4

“‘Forgive our sins, for we forgive everyone who wrongs us.

Do not lead us into temptation.’”

**Help with these words**

Sin is wrongdoing against God.

Temptation is a pull to do wrong.

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus teach his followers to ask for each day?

## A friend needs bread at midnight

## Luke 11:5

Then Jesus told them a story.

“Suppose you go to a friend’s house at midnight.

You say, ‘Friend, lend me three loaves of bread.

## Luke 11:6

“‘A friend on a journey has arrived at my house.

I have nothing to give him to eat.’

## Luke 11:7

“Your friend answers from inside, ‘Do not bother me.

The door is shut.

My children and I are in bed.

I cannot get up to give you anything.’

## Luke 11:8

“Friendship alone might not make him get up.

But you keep asking.

So he will get up and give you all you need.

**Pause and answer**

Why does the visitor need bread?

## Ask your Father

## Luke 11:9

“So I tell you, ask and you will receive.

Look and you will find.

Knock and the door will open for you.

## Luke 11:10

“Everyone who asks receives.

Everyone who looks finds.

The door opens for everyone who knocks.

## Luke 11:11

“Suppose your son asks you for a fish.

Would you give him a snake instead?

## Luke 11:12

“If he asks for an egg, would you give him a scorpion?

**Help with a word**

A scorpion is a small creature with a sting in its tail.

## Luke 11:13

“Even though you do wrong, you give your children good gifts.

How much more will your Father in heaven give the Holy Spirit to those who ask him!”

**Pause and answer**

What gift does Jesus say the Father will give?

## A man begins to speak

## Luke 11:14

Jesus drove an evil spirit out of a man who could not speak.

When the spirit left him, the man spoke.

The crowds were amazed.

## Luke 11:15

Some said, “Beelzebul, the ruler of evil spirits, gives him power to drive out spirits.”

## Luke 11:16

Others wanted to test Jesus.

They demanded a sign from heaven.

**Pause and answer**

What happened when the spirit left the man?

## A kingdom fighting itself

## Luke 11:17

Jesus knew their thoughts.

He said, “A kingdom that fights itself falls apart.

A family that fights itself falls apart too.

## Luke 11:18

“If Satan fights himself, how can his kingdom last?

You say Beelzebul helps me drive out evil spirits.

## Luke 11:19

“If Beelzebul helps me, who helps your own people drive them out?

Your own people will judge you.

## Luke 11:20

“But if I drive out evil spirits by the finger of God, then God’s kingdom has come to you.

**Help with this picture**

The finger of God here means God’s power at work.

**Pause and answer**

What happens to a kingdom that fights itself?

## A stronger man comes

## Luke 11:21

“Think of a strong man guarding his house.

With all his weapons, he keeps his things safe.

## Luke 11:22

“Then a stronger man comes and overpowers him.

He takes the armour the first man trusted.

He shares out what he has taken.

## Luke 11:23

“Whoever is not with me is against me.

Whoever does not gather with me scatters.

**Pause and answer**

What happens to the armour the first man trusted?

## A spirit returns

## Luke 11:24

“When an evil spirit leaves a person, it wanders through dry places.

It looks for rest but finds none.

It says, ‘I will go back to the house I left.’

## Luke 11:25

“It comes back and finds the house swept and tidy.

**Help with this picture**

The spirit calls the person it left a house.

## Luke 11:26

“It brings seven other spirits more evil than itself.

They go in and live there.

That person ends up worse off than before.”

**Pause and answer**

How does the person fare after the spirits return?

## People ask for a sign

## Luke 11:27

A woman in the crowd called out to Jesus.

“God has blessed the mother who gave birth to you and nursed you!”

## Luke 11:28

Jesus answered, “Even more blessed are those who hear God’s word and obey it.”

## Luke 11:29

As the crowd grew, Jesus said, “People of this generation do evil.

They demand a sign.

The only sign they will receive is the sign of Jonah.

## Luke 11:30

“Jonah was a sign to the people of Nineveh.

In the same way the Son of Man will be a sign to the people of this generation.

**Help with a name**

Jesus calls himself the Son of Man.

## Luke 11:31

“The Queen of the South travelled far to hear Solomon’s wisdom.

On judgment day she will stand up and speak against this generation.

Someone greater than Solomon is here now.

## Luke 11:32

“The people of Nineveh turned from wrongdoing when Jonah preached.

On judgment day they will stand up and speak against this generation.

Someone greater than Jonah is here now.

**Pause and answer**

What did the people of Nineveh do when they heard Jonah?

## A lamp on a stand

## Luke 11:33

“No one lights a lamp to hide it in a cellar or under a basket.

People set a lamp on a stand.

Then everyone who comes in can see its light.

## Luke 11:34

“Your eye is like a lamp for your body.

When your eyes are good, your whole body is full of light.

When your eyes are bad, your body is dark.

## Luke 11:35

“Take care that the light inside you is not darkness.

## Luke 11:36

“If light fills your whole body, no part stays dark.

You will shine as though a lamp were shining on you.”

**Help with this picture**

A lamp helps a person see the way.

Jesus asks people to pay attention to what fills them inside.

**Pause and answer**

Where do people put a lamp so others can see?

## A meal at a Pharisee’s home

## Luke 11:37

While Jesus was speaking, a Pharisee invited him to eat.

Jesus went inside and took his place at the table.

## Luke 11:38

The Pharisee was surprised.

Jesus had not washed before the meal.

**Help with this custom**

The Pharisee expected a special washing before a meal.

## Luke 11:39

The Lord said, “You Pharisees wash the outside of cups and dishes.

Yet inside you are greedy and full of wrongdoing.

**About the picture**

![A drinking cup.](../pictures/cup.svg)

Trace the outside edge of the cup.

Jesus uses the cup to speak about who people are inside.

## Luke 11:40

“How foolish!

God made the outside.

Didn’t he make the inside too?

## Luke 11:41

“Give from within you to people in need.

Then everything will be clean for you.

**Pause and answer**

What surprised the man who invited Jesus?

## What the Pharisees overlooked

## Luke 11:42

“Trouble is coming for you Pharisees!

You give God a tenth of your mint, rue, and other herbs.

Yet you pass over justice and love for God.

Practise these things and keep giving the tenth too.

**Help with these words**

Mint and rue are plants with small leaves.

Justice includes treating people fairly.

## Luke 11:43

“Trouble is coming for you Pharisees!

You want the best seats in the synagogues.

You love it when people greet you in the marketplace.

## Luke 11:44

“Trouble is coming for you!

You are like graves without markers.

People walk over them without knowing they are there.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus say the Pharisees passed over?

## Loads too heavy to carry

## Luke 11:45

An expert in God’s law said, “Teacher, those words insult us too.”

## Luke 11:46

Jesus answered, “Trouble is coming for you experts in the law too!

You give people heavy loads to carry.

You will not lift one finger to help them.

**Help with this picture**

The heavy loads stand for rules people struggle to keep.

## Luke 11:47

“Trouble is coming for you!

Your ancestors killed the prophets, and you build the prophets’ tombs.

## Luke 11:48

“You show that you agree with what your ancestors did.

They killed the prophets, and you build their tombs.

**Pause and answer**

What help did the law experts refuse to give?

## A key taken away

## Luke 11:49

“So God in his wisdom said, ‘I will send them prophets and apostles.

They will kill some and mistreat others.’

## Luke 11:50

“The people of this generation will answer for the blood of all the prophets killed since the world began.

## Luke 11:51

“That includes Abel and goes all the way to Zechariah.

Zechariah was killed between the altar and the holy place.

I tell you, this generation will answer for it all.

## Luke 11:52

“Trouble is coming for you experts in the law!

You have taken away the key to knowledge.

You did not go in yourselves.

You blocked the way for people who were going in.”

**Help with this picture**

A key opens a door.

These teachers kept others from understanding God’s message.

## Luke 11:53

As Jesus left, the teachers of the law and the Pharisees turned fiercely against him.

They pressed him with questions about many things.

## Luke 11:54

They were waiting for him to say something they could use against him.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the leaders keep asking Jesus questions?
