# Luke 24 — Jesus is alive and walks with two followers

**About this chapter**

Women visit the tomb where Jesus lay.

Later, two travellers meet Jesus on the road to Emmaus.

## The women find the tomb open

## Luke 24:1

Very early on the first day of the week, the women went to the tomb.

They brought the spices they had prepared.

## Luke 24:2

The stone had rolled away from the entrance.

**About the picture**

![An open tomb with the round stone to one side.](../pictures/tomb.svg)

Point to the opening where the women went in.

## Luke 24:3

The women went inside.

The body of the Lord Jesus was gone.

## Luke 24:4

The women wondered what had happened.

Suddenly two men in shining clothes stood beside them.

**Pause and answer**

What did the women find when they went inside the tomb?

## The women remember Jesus’ words

## Luke 24:5

The women were so afraid they bowed their faces to the ground.

The two men asked, “Why look for someone alive in a place for the dead?

## Luke 24:6

“Jesus is not here.

He has risen!

Remember what he told you in Galilee.

## Luke 24:7

“He said that people would hand the Son of Man over to sinful men.

They would put him to death on a cross.

On the third day he would rise again.”

**Help with these words**

Son of Man is a name Jesus uses for himself.

## Luke 24:8

Then the women remembered Jesus’ words.

**Pause and answer**

What had Jesus said would happen on the third day?

## Peter runs to the tomb

## Luke 24:9

They came back from the tomb.

They told the eleven apostles and the others everything that had happened.

## Luke 24:10

Mary Magdalene, Joanna, and Mary the mother of James were there.

They and the other women told the apostles what they had seen and heard.

## Luke 24:11

The apostles thought the women’s words made no sense.

They did not believe them.

## Luke 24:12

But Peter got up and ran to the tomb.

He bent down and saw only the linen cloths.

He went away wondering what had happened.

**Pause and answer**

What did Peter see inside the tomb?

## Two followers walk to Emmaus

## Luke 24:13

That same day two followers walked towards a village called Emmaus.

It was about seven miles from Jerusalem.

## Luke 24:14

The two talked about everything that had happened.

## Luke 24:15

As they talked it over, Jesus came up beside them.

He walked with them.

## Luke 24:16

They could see him, but they could not recognise him.

## Luke 24:17

Jesus asked, “What are you talking about as you walk?”

The two stopped.

Their faces were sad.

**Pause and answer**

Who joined the two followers on the road?

## Cleopas and his companion tell their story

## Luke 24:18

One of them was named Cleopas.

He asked, “Are you the only visitor in Jerusalem who hasn’t heard what happened there these last few days?”

## Luke 24:19

Jesus asked, “What happened?”

They answered, “It was about Jesus of Nazareth.

He was a prophet.

His words and deeds were powerful before God and all the people.

## Luke 24:20

“Our chief priests and rulers handed Jesus over to die.

They had him put to death on a cross.

## Luke 24:21

“We had hoped he would set Israel free.

Today is the third day since it happened.

**Pause and answer**

What had the two followers hoped Jesus would do?

## The travellers tell Jesus what the women saw

## Luke 24:22

“Some women in our group amazed us.

They went to the tomb early this morning.

## Luke 24:23

“They could not find his body.

They told us angels had appeared to them.

The angels said Jesus was alive.

## Luke 24:24

“Some of our friends went to the tomb.

They found it just as the women had said.

But they did not see Jesus.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the angels tell the women?

## Jesus explains the Scriptures

## Luke 24:25

Jesus said, “How foolish you are!

You are so slow to believe all that the prophets said!

## Luke 24:26

“Didn’t the Christ have to suffer these things before entering his glory?”

**Help with these words**

The Christ is the King God chose.

His glory is the greatness and honour that belong to him.

## Luke 24:27

Jesus began with the writings of Moses and the prophets.

He explained what the Scriptures said about him throughout those writings.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus explain to the two followers?

## The travellers recognise Jesus

## Luke 24:28

They came near the village.

Jesus seemed ready to walk on.

## Luke 24:29

The two urged him, “Stay with us.

It is almost evening.

The day is nearly over.”

So Jesus went in to stay with them.

## Luke 24:30

At the table Jesus took bread and blessed it.

He broke the bread and gave it to them.

## Luke 24:31

Then their eyes were opened and they recognised Jesus.

At that moment he disappeared from their sight.

## Luke 24:32

They asked each other, “Didn’t our hearts burn within us on the road, while he spoke and explained the Scriptures?”

**Help with these words**

The travellers use “burn” to describe how deeply Jesus’ words moved them.

**Pause and answer**

When did the two followers recognise Jesus?

## The two return to Jerusalem

## Luke 24:33

The two got up at once and returned to Jerusalem.

They found the eleven apostles gathered with the others.

## Luke 24:34

The people there said, “The Lord really has risen!

Simon has seen him!”

**Help with these words**

Simon is another name for Peter.

## Luke 24:35

Then the two travellers told what happened on the road.

They explained how they recognised Jesus when he broke the bread.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the two travellers go to share their news?

## Jesus shows his hands and feet

## Luke 24:36

While they were talking, Jesus himself stood among them.

He said, “Peace be with you.”

## Luke 24:37

They were startled and afraid.

They thought they saw a spirit.

## Luke 24:38

Jesus asked, “Why are you afraid?

Why do you doubt that it is me?

## Luke 24:39

“Look at my hands and feet.

It really is me.

Touch me and see.

A spirit has no flesh or bones.

You can see that I have both.”

## Luke 24:40

Then Jesus showed them his hands and feet.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus show his frightened followers?

## Jesus eats with his followers

## Luke 24:41

They were so happy and amazed they still could hardly believe it.

Jesus asked, “Do you have anything here to eat?”

## Luke 24:42

They gave him a piece of grilled fish.

## Luke 24:43

Jesus took the fish and ate it while they watched.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus eat while his followers watched?

## Jesus gives his followers a message

## Luke 24:44

Jesus said, “Remember what I told you when I was with you.

The Law of Moses, the Prophets, and the Psalms all speak about me.

Everything they say about me must come true.”

## Luke 24:45

Then Jesus helped them understand the Scriptures.

**Help with these words**

The Scriptures are the holy writings.

The Psalms are songs and prayers in those writings.

## Luke 24:46

He said, “The Scriptures say the Christ will suffer.

He will rise from the dead on the third day.

## Luke 24:47

“In his name, people will bring this message to every nation, starting in Jerusalem.

Turn away from sin and receive God’s forgiveness.

## Luke 24:48

“You have seen these things for yourselves.

You are my witnesses.

## Luke 24:49

“I will send you what my Father promised.

Stay in the city until you receive power from heaven.”

**Pause and answer**

Where did Jesus tell his followers to wait?

## Jesus blesses his followers

## Luke 24:50

Jesus led them out as far as Bethany.

He raised his hands and blessed them.

## Luke 24:51

While Jesus was blessing them, he left them.

God took him up into heaven.

## Luke 24:52

They worshipped Jesus.

Then they went back to Jerusalem full of joy.

## Luke 24:53

They kept praising God in the temple.

**Pause and answer**

What did the followers do in the temple?
