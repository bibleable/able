# Luke 10 — Jesus sends out pairs and tells a story about a neighbour

**About this chapter**

Jesus sends followers ahead in pairs.

Later he tells how a traveller helped an injured man.

## Jesus sends seventy-two followers

## Luke 10:1

The Lord chose seventy-two other followers.

He sent them ahead in pairs.

They went to every place Jesus planned to visit.

## Luke 10:2

Jesus told them, “There is a great harvest to gather.

There are only a few workers.

Ask the Lord of the harvest to send more workers into his fields.

**Help with this picture**

A harvest is a crop ready to gather.

Jesus is sending people to share God’s message.

He asks them to pray for more helpers.

## Luke 10:3

“Go now.

I am sending you like lambs among wolves.

**Help with this picture**

Wolves can hurt lambs.

Jesus warns his followers that they will face danger.

## Luke 10:4

“Take no money purse, travel bag, or sandals.

Do not stop to greet people on the road.

**Pause and answer**

How many followers travelled together in each group?

## When people welcome you

## Luke 10:5

“When you enter a house, first say, ‘Peace to this house.’

## Luke 10:6

“If someone there welcomes peace, your peace will rest on that person.

If not, your peace will come back to you.

## Luke 10:7

“Stay in that same house.

Eat and drink what they give you.

Workers deserve their pay.

Do not move from house to house.

## Luke 10:8

“When a town welcomes you, eat whatever people serve you.

## Luke 10:9

“Heal the sick people there.

Tell them, ‘God’s kingdom has come near to you.’

**Help with these words**

God’s kingdom means God ruling as King.

**Pause and answer**

What greeting did Jesus give his followers to use?

## When a town turns them away

## Luke 10:10

“If a town refuses to welcome you, go into its streets.

Tell the people this.

## Luke 10:11

“‘We wipe even your town’s dust off our feet as a sign against you.

Yet be sure that God’s kingdom has come near.’

## Luke 10:12

“I tell you, on judgment day it will be easier for Sodom than for that town.

**Pause and answer**

What message should the people still hear about God’s kingdom?

## Jesus warns the towns

## Luke 10:13

“Trouble is coming for you, people of Chorazin and Bethsaida!

Think of the miracles you saw.

Had Tyre and Sidon seen them, their people would have turned from wrongdoing long ago.

They would have sat in rough cloth and ashes.

**Help with this action**

Wearing rough cloth and sitting in ashes showed sorrow for wrongdoing.

## Luke 10:14

“On judgment day it will be easier for Tyre and Sidon than for you.

## Luke 10:15

“And you, Capernaum, do you expect to rise to heaven?

You will go down to Hades, the place of the dead.

## Luke 10:16

“Whoever listens to you listens to me.

Whoever turns you away turns me away.

Whoever turns me away turns away the One who sent me.”

**Pause and answer**

Who else is turned away when someone turns Jesus away?

## The seventy-two return

## Luke 10:17

The seventy-two came back full of joy.

They said, “Lord, even the evil spirits obey us when we speak in your name!”

## Luke 10:18

Jesus told them, “I saw Satan fall from heaven like lightning.

## Luke 10:19

“I have given you authority to walk on snakes and scorpions.

I have given you authority over all the enemy’s power.

Nothing will harm you.

## Luke 10:20

“Do not rejoice because the spirits obey you.

Rejoice because your names are written in heaven.”

**Pause and answer**

What reason for joy did Jesus give the seventy-two?

## Jesus thanks his Father

## Luke 10:21

Then the Holy Spirit filled Jesus with joy.

Jesus said, “I praise you, Father, Lord of heaven and earth.

You hid these things from the wise and the learned.

You showed them to little children.

Yes, Father, this pleased you.

## Luke 10:22

“My Father has trusted me with everything.

Only the Father knows who the Son is.

Only the Son knows who the Father is, along with those the Son chooses to show him to.”

## Luke 10:23

Jesus turned to his followers and spoke to them privately.

“God has blessed you by letting you see these things.

## Luke 10:24

“Many prophets and kings longed to see what you see.

They never saw it.

They longed to hear what you hear.

They never heard it.”

**Pause and answer**

Who longed to see what the followers saw?

## A question about eternal life

## Luke 10:25

One day an expert in God’s law stood up to test Jesus.

He asked, “Teacher, what must I do to receive eternal life?”

**Help with these words**

Eternal life is life with God that never ends.

## Luke 10:26

Jesus asked, “What does the law say?

How do you understand it?”

## Luke 10:27

The expert answered, “Love the Lord your God with all your heart and soul.

Love him with all your strength and all your mind.

Love your neighbour as you love yourself.”

## Luke 10:28

Jesus told him, “You have answered correctly.

Do this and you will live.”

**Pause and answer**

How does the law say to love your neighbour?

## Two travellers pass by

## Luke 10:29

The expert wanted to show he was in the right.

So he asked Jesus, “Who is my neighbour?”

## Luke 10:30

Jesus answered with a story.

“A man was travelling down from Jerusalem to Jericho.

Robbers took his clothes and beat him.

Then they went away, leaving him close to death.

## Luke 10:31

“A priest happened to be going down that road.

He saw the injured man and passed on the other side.

## Luke 10:32

“A Levite came to the same spot.

He too saw the man and passed on the other side.

**Help with a word**

Levites belonged to the family group that helped with worship in Israel.

**Pause and answer**

What did the priest do when he saw the injured man?

## A Samaritan stops to help

## Luke 10:33

“Then a Samaritan travelling that road came to the man.

When he saw the man, he felt deep care for him.

**Help with a name**

The Samaritan came from Samaria.

Many Jews and Samaritans did not get along.

## Luke 10:34

“The Samaritan went over and poured oil and wine on the wounds.

He wrapped the wounds in bandages.

He put the man on his own animal.

He took him to an inn and cared for him.

## Luke 10:35

“The next day the Samaritan gave the innkeeper two silver coins.

‘Care for him,’ he said.

‘When I come back, I will pay any extra cost.’

**About the picture**

![Two coins side by side.](../pictures/two-coins.svg)

Point to one coin, then the other.

The Samaritan paid the innkeeper two silver coins, called denarii.

**Pause and answer**

What did the Samaritan ask the innkeeper to do?

## Jesus asks who acted as a neighbour

## Luke 10:36

“Which of these three acted as a neighbour to the man the robbers attacked?”

## Luke 10:37

The expert answered, “The one who showed him mercy.”

Jesus told him, “Go and do the same.”

**Help with a word**

Here the Samaritan showed mercy by stopping to care for someone in distress.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus tell the expert to do?

## Martha welcomes Jesus and Mary listens

## Luke 10:38

As they travelled, Jesus came to a village.

A woman named Martha welcomed him into her home.

## Luke 10:39

Martha had a sister named Mary.

Mary sat at the Lord’s feet and listened to him.

## Luke 10:40

Martha had so much to prepare that it took all her attention.

She came to Jesus and said, “Lord, my sister has left me to serve alone.

Don’t you care?

Tell her to help me!”

## Luke 10:41

The Lord answered, “Martha, Martha, you are worried and upset about many things.

## Luke 10:42

“Only one thing is needed.

Mary has chosen something good.

No one will take it away from her.”

**Pause and answer**

What was Mary doing while Martha got things ready?
