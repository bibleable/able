# Luke 23 — Jesus dies and Joseph lays him in a tomb

**About this chapter**

Pilate hears the charges against Jesus.

Jesus dies on a cross.

Joseph asks to bury him.

## The council accuses Jesus

## Luke 23:1

The whole council stood up.

They took Jesus to Pilate, the governor.

## Luke 23:2

The council members accused Jesus.

They said, “This man turns our people against their rulers.

He tells them not to pay taxes to Caesar.

He says he is the Christ, a king.”

**Help with these words**

Caesar was the Roman emperor.

The Christ is the King God chose.

## Luke 23:3

Pilate asked Jesus, “Are you the King of the Jews?”

Jesus answered, “You have said so.”

## Luke 23:4

Pilate told the chief priests and the crowd, “I find no reason to charge this man with a crime.”

## Luke 23:5

But they kept saying, “His teaching stirs up the people all over Judea.

He began in Galilee and came all the way here.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Pilate tell the crowd after questioning Jesus?

## Pilate sends Jesus to Herod

## Luke 23:6

When Pilate heard this, he asked, “Does Jesus come from Galilee?”

## Luke 23:7

Pilate learned that Jesus came from the area Herod ruled.

Herod was in Jerusalem then.

So Pilate sent Jesus to Herod.

## Luke 23:8

Herod was very pleased to see Jesus.

He had heard about Jesus and had long wanted to meet him.

Herod hoped to see Jesus do a miracle.

## Luke 23:9

Herod asked Jesus many questions.

Jesus gave no answer.

## Luke 23:10

The chief priests and teachers of God’s law stood there.

They kept making angry charges against Jesus.

## Luke 23:11

Herod and his soldiers made fun of Jesus.

They put a fine robe on him.

Then they sent him back to Pilate.

## Luke 23:12

Herod and Pilate had been enemies.

That day they became friends.

**Pause and answer**

What had Herod hoped to see Jesus do?

## Pilate reports what he found

## Luke 23:13

Pilate called the chief priests, the other leaders, and the people together.

## Luke 23:14

Pilate said, “You brought Jesus to me.

You said he was turning people against their rulers.

I questioned him in front of you.

I find him innocent of these charges.

## Luke 23:15

“Herod also found no reason to condemn Jesus.

He sent Jesus back to us.

Jesus has done nothing that deserves death.

## Luke 23:16

“So I will punish him and let him go.”

## Luke 23:17

**About this verse number**

In the Berean Standard Bible, the reading continues at verse 18.

**Pause and answer**

What did Pilate say he planned to do after punishing Jesus?

## The crowd asks for Barabbas

## Luke 23:18

They all shouted together, “Take Jesus away!

Let Barabbas go!”

## Luke 23:19

Barabbas was in prison for fighting against the rulers in the city and for murder.

## Luke 23:20

Pilate wanted to let Jesus go.

So he spoke to the crowd again.

## Luke 23:21

But they kept shouting, “Crucify him! Crucify him!”

**Help with these words**

To crucify someone means to put them to death on a cross.

## Luke 23:22

Pilate spoke to them a third time.

“What wrong has this man done?

I find no reason to put him to death.

I will punish him and let him go.”

**Pause and answer**

Whom did the crowd ask Pilate to release?

## Pilate gives his decision

## Luke 23:23

But the crowd kept shouting for Jesus to die on a cross.

At last their shouts won out.

## Luke 23:24

Pilate decided to give them what they demanded.

## Luke 23:25

He freed Barabbas, the prisoner they had asked for.

Barabbas had fought against the rulers and had killed someone.

Pilate handed Jesus over to die as they wanted.

**Pause and answer**

What did Pilate decide to do with Jesus?

## Simon carries the cross

## Luke 23:26

The soldiers led Jesus away.

Simon, a man from Cyrene, was coming in from the countryside.

They seized Simon and made him carry the cross behind Jesus.

## Luke 23:27

A large crowd followed Jesus.

Among them were women crying aloud in grief for him.

**Pause and answer**

Who carried the cross behind Jesus?

## Jesus speaks to the women

## Luke 23:28

Jesus turned to them.

He said, “Women of Jerusalem, weep for yourselves and your children instead of weeping for me.

## Luke 23:29

“Days are coming when people will say, ‘How fortunate are the women who could never have children, who never gave birth or nursed a baby!’

## Luke 23:30

“Then people will call to the mountains, ‘Fall on us!’

They will call to the hills, ‘Cover us!’

## Luke 23:31

“If people do these things when the wood is green, what will happen when it is dry?”

**Help with these words**

Green wood is harder to burn than dry wood.

Jesus uses this picture to warn that worse suffering is coming.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Jesus tell the women to weep for?

## Jesus prays for forgiveness

## Luke 23:32

The soldiers also led away two men who had committed crimes.

They were going to put them to death with Jesus.

## Luke 23:33

They reached a place called the Skull.

There the soldiers put Jesus on a cross.

They put the two other men on crosses beside him.

One was on his right and one on his left.

## Luke 23:34

Jesus said, “Father, forgive them.

They do not know what they are doing.”

The soldiers cast lots to decide who would get each piece of Jesus’ clothing.

**Help with these words**

Casting lots was a way of choosing by chance.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus ask his Father to do?

## The sign above Jesus

## Luke 23:35

The people stood watching.

The leaders made fun of Jesus.

“He saved others,” they said.

“Let him save himself if he is the Christ, the one God chose.”

## Luke 23:36

The soldiers made fun of Jesus too.

They came up and offered him sour wine.

## Luke 23:37

They said, “If you are the King of the Jews, save yourself!”

## Luke 23:38

A sign above Jesus said, “This is the King of the Jews.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the sign above Jesus say?

## A man asks Jesus to remember him

## Luke 23:39

One of the men on a cross insulted Jesus.

He said, “Aren’t you the Christ?

Save yourself and us!”

## Luke 23:40

But the other man told him to stop.

“Don’t you fear God?” he asked.

“You face the same sentence as Jesus.

## Luke 23:41

“We deserve this punishment for what we did.

But Jesus has done nothing wrong.”

## Luke 23:42

Then the man said, “Jesus, remember me when you come into your kingdom.”

## Luke 23:43

Jesus answered, “I tell you the truth.

Today you will be with me in Paradise.”

**Help with these words**

Paradise is a place of blessing with God.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus promise the man who asked him to remember him?

## Luke 23:44

It was about noon.

Darkness covered the whole land until about three in the afternoon.

## Luke 23:45

The sun stopped shining.

The temple curtain tore down the middle.

## Luke 23:46

Jesus called out in a loud voice, “Father, I place my spirit in your hands.”

Then Jesus took his last breath and died.

## The people who saw Jesus die

## Luke 23:47

The Roman officer saw what had happened and praised God.

He said, “This man really did what was right.”

## Luke 23:48

The people who had gathered saw what happened.

They went home beating their chests in grief.

## Luke 23:49

The people who knew Jesus watched from a distance.

The women who had followed Jesus from Galilee were there too.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the people who knew Jesus stand?

## Joseph asks for Jesus’ body

## Luke 23:50

Joseph was a member of the council.

He was a good man who did what was right.

## Luke 23:51

Joseph had disagreed with the council’s decision and what they did.

He came from Arimathea, a town in Judea.

He was waiting for God’s kingdom.

## Luke 23:52

Joseph went to Pilate and asked for Jesus’ body.

## Luke 23:53

Joseph took Jesus’ body down from the cross.

He wrapped it in linen cloth.

He laid it in a tomb cut into rock.

No one had ever been buried there.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Joseph lay Jesus’ body?

## The women see the tomb

## Luke 23:54

It was the day people prepared for the Sabbath.

The Sabbath was about to begin.

**Help with these words**

The Sabbath is the Jewish day of rest.

## Luke 23:55

The women from Galilee who had come with Jesus followed Joseph.

They saw the tomb.

They saw how Joseph laid Jesus’ body inside.

## Luke 23:56

Then the women went back and prepared spices and perfumes.

On the Sabbath they rested, as God had commanded.
