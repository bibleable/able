# Luke 17 — Forgiveness, thanks, and the coming of the Son of Man

**About this chapter**

Jesus teaches his followers to forgive.

Ten men ask Jesus for help.

Later, Jesus tells his followers what to remember when he comes.

## Forgive the person who comes back

## Luke 17:1

Jesus told his followers, “Things will come that lead people into sin.

But how terrible for the person who leads them into sin!

## Luke 17:2

“Suppose someone leads even one of these little ones into sin.

It would be better for that person to have a heavy millstone around their neck and be thrown into the sea.

**Help with these words**

A millstone is a heavy stone used to grind grain.

Jesus uses its weight to show how serious it is to lead someone into sin.

## Luke 17:3

“Watch what you do.

If another follower sins, tell them what is wrong.

If they turn away from that sin, forgive them.

## Luke 17:4

“Suppose they sin against you seven times in one day.

Seven times they come back and say, ‘I turn away from my sin.’

You must forgive them.”

**Pause and answer**

What should you do when the person comes back and turns from sin?

## The apostles ask for more faith

## Luke 17:5

The apostles asked the Lord Jesus, “Help us trust you more!”

## Luke 17:6

Jesus answered, “Suppose your faith is as small as a mustard seed.

You could tell this mulberry tree, ‘Pull up your roots.

Plant yourself in the sea.’

The tree would obey you.

**Help with these words**

The apostles were followers Jesus chose to send out.

Faith means trusting God.

A mustard seed is very small.

**Pause and answer**

What tiny thing does Jesus use to describe faith?

## A servant finishes the work

## Luke 17:7

“Suppose your servant comes home from working in the field.

The servant has been ploughing or looking after sheep.

Would you say, ‘Come and eat at once’?

## Luke 17:8

“Wouldn’t you say, ‘Make my meal.

Get ready to serve me while I eat and drink.

After that, you can eat and drink’?

## Luke 17:9

“Does the master thank the servant for doing what the master asked?

## Luke 17:10

“It is the same for you.

When you have done everything God asks, say this.

‘We are servants who deserve no special praise.

We have only done our duty.’”

**Pause and answer**

What do the servants say when they finish their work?

## Ten men ask Jesus for help

## Luke 17:11

Jesus was travelling to Jerusalem.

He passed between Samaria and Galilee.

## Luke 17:12

As Jesus entered a village, ten men met him.

They had a skin disease called leprosy.

They stood some distance away.

## Luke 17:13

The men called out, “Jesus, Master, have mercy on us!”

## Luke 17:14

Jesus saw the men and said, “Go and show yourselves to the priests.”

As the men went, their skin became clean again.

**Help with these words**

Priests could check someone’s skin and say it was clean.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Jesus tell the men to visit?

## One man comes back

## Luke 17:15

One man saw that he was well.

He came back, praising God in a loud voice.

## Luke 17:16

He bowed with his face to the ground at Jesus’ feet.

He thanked Jesus.

This man came from Samaria.

**About the picture**

Each dot stands for one man.

Follow the arrow back to Jesus.

## Luke 17:17

Jesus asked, “Didn’t all ten become clean?

Where are the other nine?

## Luke 17:18

“Was this man from another people the only one who came back to praise God?”

## Luke 17:19

Jesus told the man, “Get up and go.

Your faith has made you well.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did the man come back to Jesus?

## The Pharisees ask about God’s kingdom

## Luke 17:20

The Pharisees asked Jesus, “When will God’s kingdom come?”

Jesus answered, “You cannot tell by watching for signs.

## Luke 17:21

“People will not say, ‘Look! Here it is!’

They will not say, ‘There it is!’

God’s kingdom is already among you.”

**Help with these words**

God’s kingdom means God ruling as king.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Jesus say God’s kingdom was?

## Lightning across the sky

## Luke 17:22

Then Jesus spoke to his followers.

“A time will come when you will long to see even one day of the Son of Man.

But you will not see it.

**Help with these words**

Son of Man is a name Jesus uses for himself.

## Luke 17:23

“People will say, ‘Look! There he is!’

Or they will say, ‘Here he is!’

Do not go running after them.

## Luke 17:24

“Lightning flashes across the whole sky.

It lights up the sky from end to end.

That is how it will be when the Son of Man comes.

## Luke 17:25

“But first the Son of Man must suffer many things.

The people of this generation will reject him.

**Pause and answer**

What lights up the sky from end to end?

## Remember Noah and Lot

## Luke 17:26

“Think of the days when Noah lived.

It will be like that in the days of the Son of Man.

## Luke 17:27

“People ate and drank.

People married and arranged marriages.

They kept doing these things until Noah entered the ark.

Then the flood came and swept them all away.

**Help with these words**

The ark was the large boat Noah built.

## Luke 17:28

“It was the same when Lot lived.

People ate and drank.

They bought and sold things.

They planted crops and built houses.

## Luke 17:29

“Then Lot left the city of Sodom.

That day fire and burning sulphur fell from the sky like rain.

Everyone in the city died.

## Luke 17:30

“It will be like that on the day people see the Son of Man.

**Pause and answer**

What were people doing before Lot left Sodom?

## Leave your things behind

## Luke 17:31

“On that day someone might be on the roof.

They must leave their things in the house.

Someone working in a field must not turn back for anything either.

## Luke 17:32

“Remember Lot’s wife!

**Help with these words**

Lot’s wife looked back as she left Sodom.

## Luke 17:33

“Anyone who tries to save their life will lose it.

Anyone who loses their life will keep it.

**Pause and answer**

What should the person in the field leave behind?

## Two people side by side

## Luke 17:34

“I tell you, two people will share one bed that night.

One will be taken and the other left.

## Luke 17:35

“Two women will grind grain together.

One will be taken and the other left.”

## Luke 17:36

**About this verse number**

In the Berean Standard Bible, the reading continues at verse 37.

## Luke 17:37

The followers asked, “Where, Lord?”

Jesus answered, “Where a dead body lies, vultures gather.”

**Help with these words**

Vultures are large birds that feed on dead animals.

**Pause and answer**

What happens to the two women grinding grain?
