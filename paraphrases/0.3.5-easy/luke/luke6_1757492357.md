# Luke 6 — Jesus chooses twelve and teaches people to love their enemies

**About this chapter**

Jesus prays through the night before he chooses twelve apostles.

Then he teaches a crowd how to treat other people.

## Grain on the day of rest

## Luke 6:1

One Sabbath Jesus walked through fields of grain.

His followers picked some heads of grain.

They rubbed them in their hands and ate the grain.

**Help with a word**

The Sabbath is the Jewish day of rest.

**About the picture**

![A stalk with grain at the top.](../pictures/grain.svg)

Point to the head of grain.

This is the part the followers picked.

## Luke 6:2

Some Pharisees asked, “Why are you doing something our law forbids on the Sabbath?”

## Luke 6:3

Jesus answered, “Have you read what David did when he and his friends were hungry?

## Luke 6:4

“David went into God’s house and took bread set apart for God.

He ate some and shared it with his friends.

The law allowed only priests to eat that bread.”

## Luke 6:5

Then Jesus said, “The Son of Man is Lord of the Sabbath.”

**Help with a name**

Jesus calls himself the Son of Man.

**Pause and answer**

What did David share with his hungry friends?

## Jesus helps a man use his hand

## Luke 6:6

On another Sabbath Jesus taught in a synagogue.

A man there could not use his right hand properly.

## Luke 6:7

The teachers of the law and the Pharisees watched Jesus closely.

They wanted a reason to accuse him.

Would he heal the man on the Sabbath?

## Luke 6:8

Jesus knew their thoughts.

He told the man, “Get up and stand here among us.”

The man got up and stood there.

## Luke 6:9

Jesus asked them, “What does the law allow on the Sabbath?

Should we do good or do harm?

Should we save a life or destroy it?”

## Luke 6:10

Jesus looked around at everyone.

He told the man, “Stretch out your hand.”

The man stretched it out, and his hand was well again.

## Luke 6:11

The teachers of the law and the Pharisees were furious.

They began to discuss what they could do to Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus ask the man to stretch out?

## Jesus chooses twelve apostles

## Luke 6:12

One day Jesus went up a mountain to pray.

He spent the whole night talking to God.

## Luke 6:13

At daylight Jesus called his followers together.

He chose twelve of them and called them apostles.

**Help with a word**

An apostle is someone Jesus sends to speak for him.

## Luke 6:14

Jesus chose Simon, whom he named Peter, and Simon’s brother Andrew.

He chose James and John, and Philip and Bartholomew.

## Luke 6:15

He chose Matthew and Thomas, and James the son of Alphaeus.

He also chose Simon, called the Zealot.

## Luke 6:16

He chose Judas the son of James, and Judas Iscariot.

Judas Iscariot later betrayed Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus do the night before he chose the twelve?

## A crowd gathers on level ground

## Luke 6:17

Jesus came down with them and stood on level ground.

Many of his followers were there.

A great crowd came from throughout Judea and Jerusalem.

Others came from the coast near Tyre and Sidon.

## Luke 6:18

They came to hear Jesus and to be healed of their illnesses.

Jesus also healed people troubled by evil spirits.

## Luke 6:19

Everyone tried to touch Jesus.

Power came from him and healed them all.

**Pause and answer**

Why did people try to touch Jesus?

## Jesus speaks to people who are hurting

## Luke 6:20

Jesus looked at his followers and said, “God blesses you who are poor.

God’s kingdom belongs to you.

## Luke 6:21

“God blesses you who are hungry now.

You will have enough to eat.

God blesses you who cry now.

You will laugh.

## Luke 6:22

“God blesses you when people hate you because of the Son of Man.

They may shut you out, insult you, and call you evil.

## Luke 6:23

“When that happens, be glad and jump for joy.

You have a great reward in heaven.

Their ancestors treated God’s prophets the same way.

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus promise people who are hungry now?

## Jesus warns people who are comfortable

## Luke 6:24

“But trouble is coming for you who are rich.

You have already had your comfort.

## Luke 6:25

“Trouble is coming for you who have plenty to eat now.

You will be hungry.

Trouble is coming for you who laugh now.

You will grieve and cry.

## Luke 6:26

“Trouble is coming when everyone speaks well of you.

Their ancestors praised false prophets that way.

**Help with these words**

False prophets claim to speak for God but lead people astray.

**Pause and answer**

Who received praise from the people’s ancestors?

## Love your enemies

## Luke 6:27

“To you who are listening I say, love your enemies.

Do good to people who hate you.

## Luke 6:28

“Ask God to bless those who curse you.

Pray for those who treat you badly.

## Luke 6:29

“If someone hits you on one cheek, turn the other cheek too.

If someone takes your coat, let them have your shirt too.

## Luke 6:30

“Give to everyone who asks you.

If someone takes what belongs to you, do not demand it back.

## Luke 6:31

“Treat other people the way you want them to treat you.

**Pause and answer**

Whom does Jesus tell his followers to pray for?

## Give without expecting repayment

## Luke 6:32

“Do you expect praise for loving people who love you?

Even sinners love people who love them.

## Luke 6:33

“Do you expect praise for helping people who help you?

Even sinners do that.

## Luke 6:34

“Do you expect praise for lending when you expect repayment?

Even sinners lend to each other and expect everything back.

## Luke 6:35

“Love your enemies and do good to them.

Lend without expecting anything back.

Then your reward will be great.

You will be children of the Most High.

He is kind even to ungrateful people and people who do evil.

**Help with a name**

The Most High is a name for God.

## Luke 6:36

“Show mercy, just as your Father shows mercy.

**Pause and answer**

How does God treat people who are ungrateful?

## Forgive and give generously

## Luke 6:37

“Do not judge others, and you will not be judged.

Do not condemn others, and you will not be condemned.

Forgive others, and you will be forgiven.

## Luke 6:38

“Give, and you will receive.

Picture a measure packed down, shaken, and filled to overflowing.

That much will be poured into your lap.

The measure you use for others will be used for you.”

**Help with this picture**

Think of someone measuring grain into a container.

Shaking it makes room for even more grain.

Jesus uses the full measure to teach about giving generously.

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus ask you to do when someone needs forgiveness?

## Look at your own actions first

## Luke 6:39

Jesus told them a story.

“Can someone who cannot see lead another who cannot see?

Wouldn’t both fall into a hole?

## Luke 6:40

“A student is not above the teacher.

When students finish their training, they will be like their teacher.

## Luke 6:41

“Why notice a speck in your brother’s eye when you miss the huge piece of wood in your own?

## Luke 6:42

“How can you say, ‘Brother, let me take that speck out’?

You cannot even see the wood in your own eye!

You pretend to see clearly.

Take the wood out of your own eye first.

Then you can see to remove your brother’s speck.

**Help with this picture**

A speck is tiny, like a bit of dust.

Jesus tells people to face their own wrongdoing before correcting someone else.

**Pause and answer**

Whose eye needs attention first?

## Fruit shows what kind of tree it is

## Luke 6:43

“A good tree cannot grow bad fruit.

A bad tree cannot grow good fruit.

## Luke 6:44

“You know each tree by its fruit.

People do not pick figs from thorn bushes.

They do not pick grapes from prickly bushes either.

## Luke 6:45

“Good people bring good things from the good stored in their hearts.

People who store up evil bring out evil.

What fills your heart comes out in your words.

**Pause and answer**

What shows what fills a person’s heart?

## Two builders face a flood

## Luke 6:46

“Why do you call me ‘Lord, Lord’ and still ignore what I say?

## Luke 6:47

“Think of someone who comes to me, listens, and does what I say.

Let me show you what that person is like.

## Luke 6:48

“A builder dug deep and laid the base of his house on rock.

Floodwater rushed against the house.

The house stood firm because the builder had built it well.

**Help with a word**

The base that holds up a building is its foundation.

## Luke 6:49

“Now think of someone who hears my words and ignores them.

That person is like a builder who built straight on the ground without laying a base.

Floodwater rushed against that house, and it fell at once.

The whole house was wrecked.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did the first house stay standing?
