# Luke 8 — Jesus tells a story about seeds and helps people in trouble

**About this chapter**

Jesus explains what it means to listen to God’s message.

He calms a storm and helps people on both shores of a lake.

## Women help Jesus and his followers

## Luke 8:1

Soon Jesus travelled from town to town and village to village.

He told people the good news about God ruling as King.

His twelve apostles went with him.

## Luke 8:2

Some women went too.

They had been freed from evil spirits or healed of sickness.

One was Mary Magdalene.

Seven evil spirits had left her.

## Luke 8:3

Joanna and Susanna were there, along with many other women.

Joanna’s husband, Chuza, managed Herod’s household.

The women used their own money to support Jesus and his followers.

**Pause and answer**

How did the women support Jesus and his followers?

## Seed falls in four places

## Luke 8:4

People from many towns came to Jesus.

A large crowd gathered, and Jesus told them this story.

## Luke 8:5

“A farmer went out to scatter seed.

Some seed fell on the path.

People stepped on it, and birds ate it.

## Luke 8:6

“Some seed fell on rocky ground.

The plants came up but dried out without water.

## Luke 8:7

“Some seed fell among thorns.

The thorns grew with the young plants and crowded them out.

## Luke 8:8

“Some seed fell on good soil.

It grew into a crop a hundred times what the farmer planted.”

Then Jesus called out, “If you have ears to hear, listen!”

**About the picture**

![A stalk with ripe grain at the top.](../pictures/grain.svg)

Find the grain at the top.

The seed in good soil grew into a crop like this.

**Pause and answer**

Which ground gave the farmer a large crop?

## The seed is God’s message

## Luke 8:9

His followers asked Jesus what the story meant.

## Luke 8:10

Jesus answered, “God has let you know the secrets of his kingdom.

I tell others stories, so that they may look without seeing.

They may listen without understanding.

## Luke 8:11

“This is what the story means.

The seed stands for God’s message.

## Luke 8:12

“Think of the seed on the path.

People hear the message, but the devil takes it from their hearts.

He does this to keep them from believing and being saved.

## Luke 8:13

“Think of the seed on rocky ground.

People hear the message and gladly accept it.

But it never takes root in them.

They believe for a while and turn away when trouble tests them.

**Pause and answer**

What happens when people pictured by rocky ground face trouble?

## Thorns and good soil

## Luke 8:14

“Think of the seed among thorns.

People hear the message, then go about their lives.

Worries, riches, and pleasures crowd it out.

Their crop never ripens.

## Luke 8:15

“Think of the seed on good soil.

People hear the message with good and honest hearts.

They hold on to it.

They keep going and produce a crop.

**Help with this picture**

A crop shows that a plant has grown.

Jesus uses it to show what happens when people keep living by God’s message.

**Pause and answer**

What do the people pictured by good soil do with God’s message?

## A lamp on a stand

## Luke 8:16

“No one lights a lamp and covers it with a jar.

No one puts a lit lamp under a bed.

People set it on a stand.

Then everyone who comes in can see the light.

## Luke 8:17

“Everything hidden will come into the light.

People will learn what was secret.

## Luke 8:18

“So take care how you listen.

Those who have will receive more.

Those who have nothing will lose even what they think they have.”

**Pause and answer**

Why do people put a lamp on a stand?

## Jesus speaks about his family

## Luke 8:19

Jesus’ mother and brothers came to see him.

The crowd was so large they could not reach him.

## Luke 8:20

Someone told Jesus, “Your mother and brothers are standing outside.

They want to see you.”

## Luke 8:21

Jesus answered, “My mother and brothers are those who hear God’s message and do what it says.”

**Pause and answer**

Whom does Jesus call his family?

## A storm on the lake

## Luke 8:22

One day Jesus told his followers, “Let’s cross the lake.”

They got into a boat together and set off.

## Luke 8:23

Jesus fell asleep as they sailed.

A fierce wind swept down onto the lake.

Water poured into the boat.

They were in great danger.

## Luke 8:24

His followers woke Jesus, shouting, “Master, Master, we’re going to die!”

Jesus got up and ordered the wind and wild water to stop.

The storm died down and the lake was calm.

## Luke 8:25

Jesus asked, “Where is your faith?”

Afraid and amazed, they asked each other, “Who is this?

Even the wind and the water obey him!”

**Pause and answer**

What happened when Jesus spoke to the wind and water?

## A man meets Jesus on the shore

## Luke 8:26

They sailed to the land of the Gerasenes.

It was across the lake from Galilee.

## Luke 8:27

Jesus stepped ashore and met a man from the town.

Evil spirits had taken hold of him.

For a long time he had worn no clothes.

He lived among the tombs instead of in a house.

**Help with a word**

A tomb is a place where people bury the dead.

## Luke 8:28

The man saw Jesus, cried out, and fell before him.

He shouted, “Jesus, Son of the Most High God, what do you want with me?

Please do not torture me!”

## Luke 8:29

Jesus had ordered the evil spirit to leave him.

Many times the spirit had seized the man.

People had bound him with chains and iron bands.

He broke the chains, and the spirit drove him into lonely places.

**Pause and answer**

Where had the man been living?

## The spirits leave the man

## Luke 8:30

Jesus asked, “What is your name?”

“Legion,” he answered, because many evil spirits had entered him.

## Luke 8:31

The spirits kept begging Jesus not to send them into the Abyss.

**Help with these words**

A legion was a large group of soldiers.

Here the name shows there were many spirits.

The Abyss means a deep pit the spirits feared.

## Luke 8:32

Many pigs were feeding on the hillside.

The spirits begged Jesus to let them enter the pigs.

Jesus allowed it.

## Luke 8:33

The spirits left the man and entered the pigs.

The herd rushed down the steep bank into the lake and drowned.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the spirits ask to go?

## People come to see the man

## Luke 8:34

The people caring for the pigs saw what happened.

They ran to tell people in town and in the countryside.

## Luke 8:35

People went to see for themselves.

They found the man sitting at Jesus’ feet.

He was wearing clothes and thinking clearly.

The people were afraid.

## Luke 8:36

Those who had seen it told the others how the man had been healed.

## Luke 8:37

Everyone in that region was so afraid that they asked Jesus to leave.

Jesus got into the boat to go back.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the people find the man sitting?

## Jesus sends the man home

## Luke 8:38

The man who was free of the spirits begged to go with Jesus.

But Jesus sent him home.

## Luke 8:39

Jesus said, “Go home and tell what God has done for you.”

The man went all over town telling what Jesus had done for him.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus ask the man to tell people?

## Jairus asks for help

## Luke 8:40

When Jesus returned, the crowd welcomed him.

Everyone had been waiting for him.

## Luke 8:41

A synagogue leader named Jairus came and fell at Jesus’ feet.

He begged Jesus to come to his house.

**Help with a word**

A synagogue is a place where Jewish people gather for worship.

## Luke 8:42

His only daughter was about twelve years old.

She was dying.

Jesus went with Jairus, and the crowd pressed close around Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Jairus ask Jesus to come to his house?

## A woman touches Jesus’ cloak

## Luke 8:43

A woman in the crowd had been bleeding for twelve years.

She had spent all her money on doctors.

No one could heal her.

## Luke 8:44

She came up behind Jesus and touched the edge of his cloak.

Her bleeding stopped at once.

**About the picture**

![A cloak worn over other clothes.](../pictures/cloak.svg)

Point to the lower edge of the cloak.

The woman reached for the edge of Jesus’ cloak.

## Luke 8:45

Jesus asked, “Who touched me?”

Everyone said they hadn’t.

Peter said, “Master, people are pressing against you on every side.”

## Luke 8:46

Jesus said, “Someone touched me.

I know power has gone out from me.”

## Luke 8:47

The woman saw that she could no longer go unnoticed.

Trembling, she came and fell before Jesus.

In front of everyone, she said why she had touched him.

She told them she had been healed at once.

## Luke 8:48

Jesus said, “Daughter, your faith has healed you.

Go in peace.”

**Pause and answer**

What happened when the woman touched the cloak?

## News from Jairus’s house

## Luke 8:49

While Jesus spoke, someone came from Jairus’s house.

He told Jairus, “Your daughter has died.

You need not trouble the Teacher any more.”

## Luke 8:50

Jesus heard this and told Jairus, “Do not be afraid.

Just believe, and she will be well.”

## Luke 8:51

At the house Jesus allowed only five people to go in with him.

They were Peter, John, James, and the girl’s father and mother.

## Luke 8:52

Everyone was weeping and grieving for the girl.

Jesus said, “Stop crying.

She is asleep, not dead.”

**Help with these words**

Jesus speaks of the girl as someone who will wake up.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus tell Jairus when the news came?

## The girl gets up

## Luke 8:53

The people laughed at Jesus because they knew she had died.

## Luke 8:54

Jesus took her hand and called, “Child, get up!”

## Luke 8:55

Her spirit returned, and she got up at once.

Jesus told them to give her something to eat.

## Luke 8:56

Her parents were amazed.

Jesus told them to tell no one what had happened.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus ask the family to give the girl?
