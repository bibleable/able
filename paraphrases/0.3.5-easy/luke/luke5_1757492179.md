# Luke 5 — Jesus calls Simon and Levi to follow him

**About this chapter**

Simon hears Jesus speak from his fishing boat.

Later, Levi invites Jesus to share a meal.

## Jesus teaches from Simon’s boat

## Luke 5:1

Jesus stood beside the Lake of Gennesaret.

People crowded close to hear God’s message.

## Luke 5:2

Jesus saw two boats at the edge of the lake.

The fishermen had left their boats to wash their nets.

**About the picture**

![A fishing net made of crossing ropes.](../pictures/net.svg)

Trace one rope across the net.

The fishermen lowered nets like this into the water.

## Luke 5:3

Jesus got into Simon’s boat.

He asked Simon to move a little way from shore.

Then Jesus sat down and taught the people from the boat.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the crowd come close to Jesus?

## Two boats fill with fish

## Luke 5:4

When Jesus finished speaking, he told Simon, “Go into deep water.

Lower your nets there to catch fish.”

## Luke 5:5

Simon answered, “Master, we worked hard all night.

We caught nothing.

But because you say so, I will lower the nets.”

## Luke 5:6

They lowered the nets and caught so many fish that the nets began to tear.

## Luke 5:7

They signalled to their partners in the other boat for help.

Their partners came.

The fish filled both boats, and the boats began to sink.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Simon lower his nets again?

## The fishermen follow Jesus

## Luke 5:8

Simon Peter saw what had happened.

He fell at Jesus’ knees.

“Lord, go away from me,” he said.

“I am a man who has sinned.”

**Help with a name**

Simon Peter is the same man whose boat Jesus used.

## Luke 5:9

The catch of fish amazed Simon and everyone with him.

## Luke 5:10

It amazed his partners James and John, the sons of Zebedee.

Jesus told Simon, “Do not be afraid.

From now on you will catch people.”

**Help with this picture**

Simon used to gather fish in a net.

Jesus will give him the work of bringing people to Jesus.

## Luke 5:11

They brought the boats ashore.

Then they left everything and followed Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

What did the fishermen do after bringing their boats ashore?

## Jesus touches a man with leprosy

## Luke 5:12

In one town Jesus met a man covered with leprosy.

The man saw Jesus and fell facedown.

He begged, “Lord, you can make me clean if you want to.”

**Help with these words**

Leprosy was a name for serious skin diseases.

Being clean again meant the man could take part in worship with others.

## Luke 5:13

Jesus reached out and touched the man.

“I want to,” Jesus said.

“Be clean!”

At once the disease left the man.

## Luke 5:14

Jesus told him, “Tell no one about this.

Go and show yourself to the priest.

Bring the offering Moses commanded for someone who has become clean.

This will show them what has happened.”

## Luke 5:15

Yet the news about Jesus spread even more.

Large crowds came to hear him.

Sick people came for healing.

## Luke 5:16

Jesus often went away to the desert to pray.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Jesus tell the man to visit?

## A mat comes through the roof

## Luke 5:17

One day Jesus was teaching.

Some Pharisees and teachers of God’s law sat nearby.

People had come from Jerusalem and every village in Galilee and Judea.

The Lord’s power was there for Jesus to heal sick people.

## Luke 5:18

Some men carried a man who could not move on a mat.

They tried to bring him inside and lay him before Jesus.

## Luke 5:19

The crowd blocked their way.

So the men went up on the roof.

They lowered the man on his mat through the roof tiles.

He came down among the people, right in front of Jesus.

**About the picture**

![A mat spread out flat.](../pictures/mat.svg)

Find the mat.

The men lowered the man while he lay on his mat.

## Luke 5:20

Jesus saw how they trusted him.

He told the man, “Friend, your sins are forgiven.”

**Help with a word**

Sins are wrong things people do against God.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the men go up on the roof?

## The man carries his mat home

## Luke 5:21

The teachers of the law and the Pharisees thought, “Who is this man who insults God?

Only God can forgive sins.”

## Luke 5:22

Jesus knew what they were thinking.

He asked, “Why are you thinking these things?

## Luke 5:23

“Which is easier to say?

‘Your sins are forgiven’ or ‘Get up and walk’?

## Luke 5:24

“I want you to know that the Son of Man has the right to forgive sins on earth.”

Then Jesus told the man on the mat, “Get up.

Pick up your mat and go home.”

**Help with a name**

Jesus calls himself the Son of Man.

## Luke 5:25

At once the man stood up in front of them.

He picked up the mat he had been lying on.

He went home praising God.

## Luke 5:26

Everyone was amazed and praised God.

Filled with wonder and fear, they said, “We have seen wonderful things today!”

**Pause and answer**

What did the man carry home?

## Levi welcomes Jesus to a meal

## Luke 5:27

After this Jesus went out and saw Levi, a tax collector.

Levi sat where people came to pay their taxes.

Jesus told him, “Follow me.”

## Luke 5:28

Levi got up, left everything, and followed Jesus.

## Luke 5:29

Levi gave a great feast for Jesus at his home.

Many tax collectors and other people ate with them.

## Luke 5:30

The Pharisees and their teachers of the law complained to Jesus’ followers.

“Why do you eat and drink with tax collectors and sinners?”

## Luke 5:31

Jesus answered, “Healthy people do not need a doctor.

Sick people do.

## Luke 5:32

“I came to call sinners to turn away from sin.

I did not come to call those who do right.”

**Help with this picture**

A doctor helps sick people.

Jesus helps people turn away from wrongdoing and turn back to God.

**Pause and answer**

What did Levi do when Jesus said to follow him?

## A question about fasting

## Luke 5:33

They said to Jesus, “John’s followers often fast and pray.

The Pharisees’ followers do too.

Your followers keep eating and drinking.”

**Help with a word**

To fast means choosing to go without food for a time.

## Luke 5:34

Jesus answered, “Can you make wedding guests fast while the groom is with them?

## Luke 5:35

“One day the groom will be taken away from them.

Then they will fast.”

**Help with this picture**

The groom is the man getting married.

Jesus compares his friends to guests celebrating while the groom is with them.

**Pause and answer**

When does Jesus say the wedding guests will fast?

## New cloth and new wine

## Luke 5:36

Jesus also told them this story.

“Nobody tears cloth from new clothes to patch old clothes.

That would leave a tear in the new clothes.

The new patch would not match the old clothes.

## Luke 5:37

“Nobody pours new wine into old leather bags.

The new wine would burst the old bags.

The wine would spill, and the bags would be ruined.

## Luke 5:38

“People put new wine into new leather bags.

**Help with these words**

The leather bags were called wineskins.

New wine changes as it stands, so the bag needs room to stretch.

## Luke 5:39

“Someone who has been drinking old wine wants no new wine.

That person says, ‘The old wine is better.’”

**Pause and answer**

What happens if someone puts new wine into old leather bags?
