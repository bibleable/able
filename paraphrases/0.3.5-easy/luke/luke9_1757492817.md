# Luke 9 — Jesus sends the twelve and sets out for Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

Jesus sends his followers to tell the good news.

As they travel, he teaches them what following him will cost.

## Jesus sends the twelve

## Luke 9:1

Jesus called his twelve apostles together.

He gave them power and authority over all evil spirits.

He also gave them power to heal diseases.

## Luke 9:2

Then Jesus sent them to tell people about God’s kingdom and heal the sick.

**Help with these words**

God’s kingdom means God ruling as King.

## Luke 9:3

“Take nothing for the journey,” Jesus said.

“Take no walking stick, bag, bread, or money.

Do not take an extra shirt.

## Luke 9:4

“When someone welcomes you into a house, stay there until you leave that area.

## Luke 9:5

“If a town will not welcome you, shake the dust off your feet as you leave.

This will be a sign against them.”

**Help with this action**

Shaking off the dust showed that the messengers were leaving and the town had refused their message.

## Luke 9:6

The twelve set out and went from village to village.

Everywhere they told the good news and healed people.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus send the twelve to do for sick people?

## Herod wants to see Jesus

## Luke 9:7

Herod, the ruler, heard what was happening.

He was puzzled.

Some people said John had risen from the dead.

## Luke 9:8

Others said Elijah had appeared.

Still others said a prophet from long ago had risen.

## Luke 9:9

Herod said, “I had John’s head cut off.

Who is this man I keep hearing about?”

Herod kept trying to see Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

What puzzled Herod about the reports he heard?

## People follow Jesus to Bethsaida

## Luke 9:10

The apostles returned and told Jesus everything they had done.

He took them away privately to a town called Bethsaida.

## Luke 9:11

The crowds found out and followed Jesus.

He welcomed them and told them about God’s kingdom.

He healed the people who needed healing.

## Luke 9:12

Late in the day, the twelve came to Jesus.

“We are far from the villages,” they said.

“Send the people to nearby villages and farms.

They need food and places to stay.”

## Luke 9:13

Jesus said, “You give them something to eat.”

They answered, “We have only five loaves and two fish.

Would you have us buy food for everyone?”

**About the picture**

![Five loaves above two fish.](../pictures/loaves-and-fish.svg)

Count the loaves.

Then count the fish.

**Pause and answer**

What food did the twelve have?

## Jesus shares the food

## Luke 9:14

About five thousand men were there.

Jesus told his followers, “Have them sit in groups of about fifty.”

## Luke 9:15

The followers did as Jesus asked.

Everyone sat down.

## Luke 9:16

Jesus took the five loaves and two fish.

He looked up to heaven and spoke a blessing.

He broke the food into pieces.

Then he gave it to his followers to serve the people.

## Luke 9:17

Everyone ate until they had enough.

The followers gathered the pieces left over.

They filled twelve baskets.

**Pause and answer**

How much food was left over?

## Peter answers Jesus

## Luke 9:18

One day Jesus prayed away from the crowds.

His followers were with him.

He asked them, “Who do the crowds say I am?”

## Luke 9:19

They answered, “Some say John the Baptist.

Others say Elijah.

Others say a prophet from long ago has risen.”

## Luke 9:20

Jesus asked, “Who do you say I am?”

Peter answered, “You are the Christ sent by God.”

**Help with a name**

Christ means the King God promised to send.

## Luke 9:21

Jesus firmly told them to tell no one about this.

## Luke 9:22

“The Son of Man must suffer greatly,” Jesus said.

“The elders, the chief priests, and the law teachers will reject him.

He must be killed.

On the third day he will rise to life.”

**Help with a name**

Jesus calls himself the Son of Man.

**Pause and answer**

Who did Peter say Jesus was?

## What following Jesus costs

## Luke 9:23

Jesus told them all, “Anyone who wants to follow me must put aside their own wishes.

They must take up their cross every day and follow me.

**Help with this picture**

The Romans made prisoners carry a cross to their deaths.

Jesus asks people to stay faithful to him whatever the cost.

## Luke 9:24

“Whoever tries to save their life will lose it.

Whoever loses their life for my sake will save it.

## Luke 9:25

“What good is gaining the whole world if you lose your very self?

## Luke 9:26

“Someone may be ashamed of me and my words.

The Son of Man will be ashamed of that person when he comes.

He will come in his own glory and in the glory of the Father and the holy angels.

## Luke 9:27

“I tell you the truth.

Some people standing here will still be alive when they see God’s kingdom.”

**Pause and answer**

How often does Jesus say to take up the cross?

## Jesus prays on the mountain

## Luke 9:28

About eight days later, Jesus took Peter, John, and James up a mountain to pray.

## Luke 9:29

As Jesus prayed, his face changed.

His clothes shone bright white.

## Luke 9:30

Suddenly Moses and Elijah were there, talking with Jesus.

## Luke 9:31

Moses and Elijah appeared in glory.

They spoke with Jesus about his coming departure in Jerusalem.

**Help with a word**

Departure means leaving.

Jesus had told his followers he would die and rise again.

## Luke 9:32

Peter and the others had fallen asleep.

When they woke, they saw Jesus shining in glory.

They saw the two men standing with him.

**Pause and answer**

Who spoke with Jesus on the mountain?

## A voice from the cloud

## Luke 9:33

Moses and Elijah began to leave.

Peter told Jesus, “Master, it is good to be here.

Let us make three shelters.

One for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah.”

Peter did not know what he was saying.

## Luke 9:34

As Peter spoke, a cloud surrounded them.

They were afraid as they entered the cloud.

## Luke 9:35

A voice from the cloud said, “This is my Son.

I have chosen him.

Listen to him!”

## Luke 9:36

When the voice stopped, only Jesus was there with them.

For the time being, the followers told no one what they had seen.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did the voice tell the followers to listen to?

## A father asks for help

## Luke 9:37

The next day they came down the mountain.

A large crowd met Jesus.

## Luke 9:38

A man called out, “Teacher, please look at my son!

He is my only child.

## Luke 9:39

“A spirit seizes him and he suddenly screams.

His body shakes hard and his mouth foams.

The spirit keeps hurting him and hardly ever leaves him.

## Luke 9:40

“I begged your followers to drive it out.

They could not.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did the father call out to Jesus?

## Jesus gives the boy back to his father

## Luke 9:41

Jesus answered, “You people have turned away and do not believe!

How long must I stay and put up with this?”

Then he said to the father, “Bring your son here.”

## Luke 9:42

As the boy came near, the spirit threw him down.

His body shook hard.

Jesus ordered the evil spirit out and healed the boy.

He gave the boy back to his father.

## Luke 9:43

Everyone was amazed at the greatness of God.

As they wondered at what Jesus was doing, Jesus spoke to his followers.

## Luke 9:44

“Listen carefully to this.

Someone is about to hand the Son of Man over to men.”

## Luke 9:45

The meaning was hidden from them.

They did not understand and were afraid to ask Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

What happened to the boy when Jesus spoke to the spirit?

## Jesus welcomes a child

## Luke 9:46

The followers began arguing about which of them would be greatest.

## Luke 9:47

Jesus knew what they were thinking.

He had a little child stand beside him.

## Luke 9:48

Jesus said, “Welcome this child in my name, and you welcome me.

Welcome me, and you welcome the One who sent me.

The least important among you is the greatest.”

**Pause and answer**

Who stood beside Jesus while the followers argued about greatness?

## John tries to stop someone

## Luke 9:49

John said, “Master, we saw someone driving out evil spirits in your name.

We tried to stop him because he does not travel with us.”

## Luke 9:50

Jesus answered, “Do not stop him.

Whoever is not against you is on your side.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus tell John to do about the man?

## A village refuses to welcome Jesus

## Luke 9:51

The time was near for Jesus to be taken up to heaven.

Jesus made up his mind to go to Jerusalem.

## Luke 9:52

He sent messengers ahead.

They went to a Samaritan village to prepare for him.

## Luke 9:53

The people there would not welcome Jesus because he was going to Jerusalem.

## Luke 9:54

James and John saw this.

They asked, “Lord, shall we call down fire from heaven to destroy them?”

## Luke 9:55

Jesus turned and told James and John they were wrong.

## Luke 9:56

Jesus and his followers went on to another village.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus and his followers do after the village turned them away?

## Three conversations on the road

## Luke 9:57

On the road, someone said, “Jesus, I will follow you wherever you go.”

## Luke 9:58

Jesus answered, “Foxes have holes, and birds have nests.

But the Son of Man has nowhere to rest his head.”

## Luke 9:59

Jesus told another man, “Follow me.”

The man answered, “Lord, first let me bury my father.”

## Luke 9:60

Jesus said, “Let the dead bury their own dead.

You go and tell people about God’s kingdom.”

**Help with these words**

Jesus tells this man to leave the burial to others.

He gives him a message to share now.

## Luke 9:61

Another person said, “Lord, I will follow you.

First let me say goodbye to my family.”

## Luke 9:62

Jesus answered, “Someone who starts to plough and keeps looking back is not fit for God’s kingdom.”

**Help with this picture**

A farmer uses a plough to make a line in the soil.

Looking ahead helps the farmer keep that line straight.

Jesus asks his followers to keep their attention on following him.

**Pause and answer**

What work did Jesus give the man who wanted to bury his father?
