# Luke 20 — Jesus answers questions in the temple

**About this chapter**

The leaders ask who gave Jesus the right to teach.

Later, people ask Jesus about taxes and life after death.

## Who sent John?

## Luke 20:1

One day Jesus taught in the open courts of the temple.

He told the people the good news.

The chief priests, teachers of God’s law, and elders came to him.

## Luke 20:2

They asked, “What right do you have to do these things?

Who gave you that right?”

## Luke 20:3

Jesus answered, “I have a question for you too.

Tell me this.

## Luke 20:4

“Who sent John to baptize people?

Did God send him, or did people send him?”

**Help with these words**

John baptized people in water as they turned back to God.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Jesus ask the leaders about?

## The leaders discuss their answer

## Luke 20:5

The leaders talked among themselves.

They said, “Suppose we say, ‘God sent John.’

Jesus will ask why we did not believe John.

## Luke 20:6

“But suppose we say, ‘People sent John.’

The crowd will throw stones at us.

They are sure John spoke for God.”

## Luke 20:7

So the leaders answered, “We do not know who sent John.”

## Luke 20:8

Jesus replied, “Then I will not tell you who gave me the right to do these things.”

**Pause and answer**

What answer did the leaders give Jesus?

## The owner sends three servants

## Luke 20:9

Jesus told the people a story.

“A man planted a vineyard.

He rented it to some farmers.

Then he went away for a long time.

**Help with these words**

A vineyard is a place where people grow grapes.

## Luke 20:10

“When the grapes were ripe, the owner sent a servant.

The servant came to collect the owner’s share of the grapes.

The farmers beat him and sent him away with nothing.

## Luke 20:11

“The owner sent another servant.

The farmers beat and insulted him.

They sent him away with nothing.

## Luke 20:12

“The owner sent a third servant.

The farmers hurt him and threw him out.

**Pause and answer**

What did the owner send his servants to collect?

## The owner sends his son

## Luke 20:13

“The owner thought, ‘What shall I do?

I will send my son, whom I love.

Perhaps they will respect him.’

## Luke 20:14

“The farmers saw the son coming.

They said to each other, ‘One day this vineyard will be his.

Let us kill him so it will be ours.’

## Luke 20:15

“They threw the son out of the vineyard and killed him.

What will the owner do to those farmers?

## Luke 20:16

“He will come and kill them.

He will give the vineyard to others.”

The people cried, “May that never happen!”

**Pause and answer**

Whom did the owner send after the three servants?

## The stone the builders turned down

## Luke 20:17

Jesus looked straight at them.

He asked, “Then what do these words in Scripture mean?

‘The builders turned down a stone.

That stone became the cornerstone.’

**Help with these words**

A cornerstone is an important stone at the corner of a building.

Jesus uses the stone as a picture of himself.

The leaders want to turn him away.

## Luke 20:18

“Anyone who falls on that stone will break into pieces.

Anyone the stone falls on will be crushed.”

## Luke 20:19

The teachers of God’s law and the chief priests understood.

Jesus had told the story about them.

They wanted to arrest him at once.

But they were afraid of the people.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the leaders want to arrest Jesus?

## The spies ask about taxes

## Luke 20:20

The leaders watched Jesus and sent spies.

The spies pretended that their questions were honest.

They wanted Jesus to say something they could use against him.

Then they could hand him over to the governor.

## Luke 20:21

The spies said, “Teacher, we know you teach what is right.

You do not favour one person over another.

You teach the truth about how God wants us to live.

## Luke 20:22

“Does our law allow us to pay taxes to Caesar?”

## Luke 20:23

Jesus knew they were trying to trick him.

He spoke to the spies.

## Luke 20:24

“Show me a silver coin called a denarius.

Whose face and whose words are on it?”

They answered, “Caesar’s.”

**Help with these words**

Caesar was the title of the Roman emperor.

## Luke 20:25

Jesus said, “Give Caesar what belongs to Caesar.

Give God what belongs to God.”

## Luke 20:26

The spies could not trap Jesus with his words in front of the people.

His answer amazed them.

They had nothing to say.

**Pause and answer**

Whose face was on the coin?

## A question about seven brothers

## Luke 20:27

Then some Sadducees came with a question.

The Sadducees said that people who die never rise again.

## Luke 20:28

They said, “Teacher, Moses wrote this law for us.

Suppose a man dies and leaves a wife but no children.

His brother must marry the widow.

Their children will carry on the dead brother’s family.

**Help with these words**

A widow is a woman whose husband has died.

## Luke 20:29

“Now suppose there were seven brothers.

The first married and died without children.

## Luke 20:30

“Then the second brother married the widow.

## Luke 20:31

“Then the third brother married her.

The same thing happened to all seven brothers.

They all died without children.

## Luke 20:32

“Last of all, the woman died.

## Luke 20:33

“When the dead rise again, whose wife will she be?

All seven brothers married her.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the Sadducees want to know about the woman?

## Jesus speaks about life after death

## Luke 20:34

Jesus answered, “People marry in this life.

## Luke 20:35

“But God counts some people worthy to rise from the dead and share in the life to come.

In that life they do not marry.

**Help with these words**

Resurrection means God raising the dead to life.

## Luke 20:36

“They cannot die again.

In that way they are like the angels.

They are God’s children because God has raised them to life.

## Luke 20:37

“Even Moses showed that the dead rise again.

Think of what Moses wrote about the burning bush.

Moses called the Lord ‘the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.’

**About the picture**

![Abraham, his son Isaac, and Isaac’s son Jacob.](../pictures/abraham-family.svg)

Follow the names from Abraham down to Jacob.

Jesus names all three when he speaks about the living God.

## Luke 20:38

“God is the God of the living.

To God they are all alive.”

## Luke 20:39

Some teachers of God’s law said, “Teacher, you answered well!”

## Luke 20:40

No one dared to ask Jesus another question.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus say about death in the life to come?

## David calls the Christ his Lord

## Luke 20:41

Then Jesus asked, “Why do people call the Christ the Son of David?

**Help with these words**

Christ means the King God chose.

“Son of David” means someone from King David’s family.

## Luke 20:42

“In the book of Psalms, David calls him ‘my Lord.’

David says that God spoke these words to his Lord.

‘Sit at my right hand

## Luke 20:43

until I put your enemies under your feet like a footstool.’

**Help with these words**

Sitting at God’s right hand is a place of honour.

The footstool is a picture of God putting enemies under the King’s rule.

## Luke 20:44

“David calls the Christ his Lord.

So how can the Christ be David’s son?”

**Pause and answer**

What name did David use for the Christ?

## Jesus warns his followers

## Luke 20:45

All the people were listening.

Jesus spoke to his followers.

## Luke 20:46

“Watch out for the teachers of God’s law.

They like walking around in long robes.

They love it when people greet them in the market.

They want the best seats in the meeting houses.

They want the places of honour at feasts.

## Luke 20:47

“They cheat widows and take their homes.

Then they say long prayers to impress people.

God will judge these men more severely.”

**Pause and answer**

Whose homes did these teachers take?
