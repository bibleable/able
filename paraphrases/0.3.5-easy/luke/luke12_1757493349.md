# Luke 12 — Jesus teaches his friends to trust God and be ready

**About this chapter**

Jesus speaks about possessions, worry, and trust.

He tells a story about servants waiting for their master.

## Hidden words come to light

## Luke 12:1

Thousands of people gathered.

They began stepping on one another.

Jesus spoke first to his followers.

“Watch out for the yeast of the Pharisees.

I mean pretending to be good.

**Help with this picture**

A little yeast spreads through dough.

Jesus warns that pretending can spread among people too.

## Luke 12:2

“Everything covered up will come into the open.

Every secret will become known.

## Luke 12:3

“People will hear in daylight what you said in the dark.

Words whispered in private rooms will be shouted from rooftops.

**Pause and answer**

What happens to words whispered in private?

## God remembers the sparrows

## Luke 12:4

“My friends, do not fear people who kill the body.

After that, they can do nothing more.

## Luke 12:5

“I will tell you whom to fear.

Fear God, who has authority to send you to hell after death.

Yes, fear him.

## Luke 12:6

“Five sparrows sell for two small coins.

Yet God remembers every one of those birds.

**About the picture**

![Two small coins side by side.](../pictures/two-coins.svg)

Point to the two coins.

Even the five birds sold for this small price matter to God.

## Luke 12:7

“God has even counted every hair on your head.

Do not be afraid.

You matter more than many sparrows.

**Pause and answer**

What has God counted on your head?

## Help when you must speak

## Luke 12:8

“Tell people openly that you belong to me.

Then the Son of Man will say before God’s angels that you belong to him.

**Help with a name**

Jesus calls himself the Son of Man.

## Luke 12:9

“But suppose you tell people you do not know me.

Then I will deny knowing you before God’s angels.

## Luke 12:10

“God will forgive someone who speaks against the Son of Man.

But God will not forgive someone who speaks with contempt against the Holy Spirit.

## Luke 12:11

“People will bring you before synagogue leaders, rulers, and other authorities.

Do not worry about how to defend yourselves.

## Luke 12:12

“At that time the Holy Spirit will teach you what to say.”

**Pause and answer**

Who will help the followers know what to say?

## A question about family property

## Luke 12:13

Someone in the crowd called out, “Teacher, tell my brother to share the property our family left us.”

## Luke 12:14

Jesus answered, “Who gave me the job of judging between you or dividing your property?”

## Luke 12:15

Then Jesus told the people, “Watch out for every kind of greed.

A person’s life is more than all the things they own.”

**Help with a word**

Greed means always wanting more for yourself.

**Pause and answer**

What did the man want his brother to share?

## A rich man plans bigger barns

## Luke 12:16

Jesus told them a story.

“A rich man’s land grew a very large crop.

## Luke 12:17

“The man thought, ‘Where can I put all these crops?

I have no room for them.’

## Luke 12:18

“Then he said, ‘I know what to do.

I will pull down my barns and build bigger ones.

I will store all my grain and goods there.

## Luke 12:19

“‘Then I can say to myself, “You have plenty stored for many years.

Rest now.

Eat, drink, and enjoy yourself.”’

## Luke 12:20

“But God said to him, ‘You fool!

Tonight your life will end.

Who will own everything you stored away?’

## Luke 12:21

“That is how it is for those who save treasures only for themselves and are not rich toward God.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did the man want bigger barns?

## Look at the ravens

## Luke 12:22

Then Jesus said to his followers, “Do not worry about what you will eat.

Do not worry about clothes for your body.

## Luke 12:23

“Your life is more than food.

Your body is more than clothes.

## Luke 12:24

“Look at the ravens.

They do not plant seeds or gather crops.

They have no barns to store food.

Yet God feeds them.

You matter much more than birds!

**Help with a word**

Ravens are large black birds.

## Luke 12:25

“Can worry add even one hour to your life?

## Luke 12:26

“If you cannot do even that small thing, why worry about the rest?

**Pause and answer**

Who feeds the ravens?

## Look at the lilies

## Luke 12:27

“Look at the lilies growing.

They do not work or make thread for clothes.

Yet even King Solomon in his finest clothes was never dressed like one of these flowers.

## Luke 12:28

“God dresses the grass in the field like that.

Today it grows.

Tomorrow people burn it in an oven.

How much more will God clothe you!

You trust him so little.

## Luke 12:29

“Do not keep worrying about what you will eat or drink.

## Luke 12:30

“People of the nations chase after all these things.

Your Father knows you need them.

## Luke 12:31

“Seek his kingdom, and you will receive these things too.

**Pause and answer**

Who knows what the followers need?

## Treasure that lasts

## Luke 12:32

“Do not be afraid, little flock.

Your Father is glad to give you the kingdom.

**Help with this picture**

A shepherd cares for a flock of sheep.

Jesus speaks tenderly to his followers when he calls them his little flock.

## Luke 12:33

“Sell what you own and give to poor people.

Get money bags that never wear out.

That treasure in heaven never runs out.

No thief can reach it.

No moth can ruin it.

## Luke 12:34

“Where your treasure is, your heart will be too.

**Help with this picture**

What you treasure shows what you care about most.

**Pause and answer**

Whom does Jesus tell his followers to help?

## Servants wait for their master

## Luke 12:35

“Keep your clothes ready for work and your lamps burning.

## Luke 12:36

“Be like servants waiting for their master after a wedding feast.

They are ready to open the door as soon as he knocks.

## Luke 12:37

“Blessed are the servants the master finds awake!

I tell you truly, the master will dress for work.

He will have the servants sit at the table.

Then he will serve them himself.

## Luke 12:38

“He may come in the middle of the night or even later.

Blessed are those servants if he finds them awake.

**Pause and answer**

Who serves the meal when the master finds his servants awake?

## Peter asks about the story

## Luke 12:39

“Think about someone who owns a house.

If he knew when a thief was coming, he would prevent the break-in.

## Luke 12:40

“You too must be ready.

The Son of Man will come when you do not expect him.”

## Luke 12:41

Peter asked, “Lord, is that story for us or for everyone?”

## Luke 12:42

The Lord answered, “Think of a master who chooses a manager.

Who can he trust to give the servants their food on time?

Who will do this work wisely?

## Luke 12:43

“Blessed is the servant whose master comes home and finds him doing that work.

## Luke 12:44

“I tell you truly, the master will put him in charge of everything he owns.

**Pause and answer**

What job did the master give the manager?

## The manager who mistreats others

## Luke 12:45

“But suppose the manager thinks, ‘My master will be away a long time.’

He starts beating the other servants, both men and women.

He eats and drinks until he gets drunk.

## Luke 12:46

“His master will return on a day he does not expect.

It will be an hour he never saw coming.

The master will cut him to pieces.

He will give him a place with those who do not believe.

## Luke 12:47

“A servant may know what the master wants but fail to get ready or obey.

That servant will receive many blows.

## Luke 12:48

“Another servant may do wrong without knowing what the master wanted.

That servant will receive fewer blows.

Much is expected of people who have received much.

Still more is expected of those trusted with much.

**Pause and answer**

What did the manager think about his master’s return?

## A family takes different sides

## Luke 12:49

“I came to light a fire on the earth.

How I wish it were burning already!

## Luke 12:50

“I have a baptism to go through.

I feel deep distress until it is over.

**Help with this picture**

Jesus speaks of the suffering ahead of him as a baptism.

He knows he must go through it.

## Luke 12:51

“Do you think I came to bring peace on earth?

I tell you, people will be divided.

## Luke 12:52

“Five people in one family will take different sides.

Three will stand against two, and two against three.

## Luke 12:53

“Fathers and sons will stand against each other.

Mothers and daughters will stand against each other.

A wife and her husband’s mother will stand against each other.”

**Pause and answer**

How many people are in the family Jesus describes?

## Reading the sky

## Luke 12:54

Jesus then spoke to the crowds.

“You see a cloud rising in the west.

You say, ‘Rain is coming,’ and it rains.

## Luke 12:55

“When the wind blows from the south, you say, ‘It will be hot.’

And it is hot.

## Luke 12:56

“You pretend to understand!

You can read the signs in the earth and sky.

Why can’t you understand what is happening now?

**Help with this comparison**

The people recognise signs of changing weather.

Jesus wants them to recognise the importance of what is happening among them.

**Pause and answer**

What did people expect when they saw a cloud rising in the west?

## On the way to court

## Luke 12:57

“Why don’t you decide for yourselves what is right?

## Luke 12:58

“Suppose someone is taking you to court.

Try hard to settle things on the way.

Otherwise that person may drag you before the judge.

The judge may hand you to an officer.

The officer may put you in prison.

## Luke 12:59

“I tell you, you will stay there until you pay the last coin.”

**Pause and answer**

When should the two people try to settle their disagreement?
