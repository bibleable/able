# Luke 13 — Jesus asks for change and sets a woman free

**About this chapter**

Jesus tells a story about giving a fig tree time to grow fruit.

On the Sabbath he helps a woman stand up straight.

## Jesus hears about people who died

## Luke 13:1

Some people told Jesus what Pilate had done.

He had people from Galilee killed as they offered sacrifices.

Their blood mixed with the blood of their offerings.

## Luke 13:2

Jesus asked, “Do you think they suffered because they sinned more than everyone else in Galilee?

## Luke 13:3

“No, I tell you.

But unless you turn from sin, you too will all perish.

**Help with these words**

To turn from sin means to stop doing wrong and turn back to God.

## Luke 13:4

“Think of the eighteen people who died when the tower at Siloam fell on them.

Had they sinned more than everyone else in Jerusalem?

## Luke 13:5

“No, I tell you.

But unless you turn from sin, you too will all perish.”

**Pause and answer**

What change did Jesus ask his listeners to make?

## A gardener asks for more time

## Luke 13:6

Then Jesus told a story.

“A man had a fig tree in his vineyard.

He looked for figs but found none.

## Luke 13:7

“He told the gardener, ‘For three years I have looked for figs on this tree.

I have never found any.

Cut it down.

Why should it use up the soil?’

## Luke 13:8

“The gardener answered, ‘Sir, leave it for one more year.

I will dig around it and add manure to feed the soil.

## Luke 13:9

“‘If the tree grows figs next year, good.

If it does not, you can cut it down.’”

**Help with this picture**

The gardener asks for time for the tree to change and grow fruit.

Jesus tells this story after asking people to change how they live.

**Pause and answer**

How did the gardener plan to care for the tree?

## A woman stands up straight

## Luke 13:10

One Sabbath, Jesus was teaching in a synagogue.

**Help with these words**

The Sabbath is the Jewish day of rest.

A synagogue is a place where Jewish people meet for worship.

## Luke 13:11

A woman there had been bent over for eighteen years.

A spirit had caused this, and she could not stand straight.

## Luke 13:12

Jesus saw the woman and called her over.

He said, “Woman, you are free from the condition that bent you over.”

## Luke 13:13

Jesus placed his hands on her.

At once she stood up straight and praised God.

**Pause and answer**

What did the woman do when she stood up straight?

## Jesus answers the synagogue leader

## Luke 13:14

The synagogue leader was angry because Jesus had healed on the Sabbath.

He told the crowd, “There are six days for work.

Come for healing on those days instead of the Sabbath.”

## Luke 13:15

The Lord answered, “You pretend to do right!

Even on the Sabbath you untie your ox or donkey.

You lead the animal to water.

## Luke 13:16

“This woman belongs to Abraham’s family.

Satan kept her bound for eighteen long years.

Shouldn’t she be set free on the Sabbath?”

**Help with this picture**

Jesus compares untying an animal to freeing the woman from what held her bent over.

## Luke 13:17

Jesus’ answer put his opponents to shame.

The whole crowd rejoiced at the wonderful things Jesus was doing.

**Pause and answer**

What did people untie on the Sabbath to give it water?

## A seed and some yeast

## Luke 13:18

Jesus asked, “What is God’s kingdom like?

What can I compare it to?

## Luke 13:19

“It is like a mustard seed a man planted in his garden.

The seed grew into a tree.

Birds made nests in its branches.”

**About the picture**

![A branch with leaves.](../pictures/branch.svg)

Trace the branch out toward its leaves.

In Jesus’ story a tiny seed grows branches big enough for birds to nest in.

## Luke 13:20

Again Jesus asked, “What can I compare God’s kingdom to?

## Luke 13:21

“It is like yeast a woman mixed into three measures of flour.

The yeast worked through the whole batch of dough.”

**Help with a word**

Yeast makes dough rise.

Jesus uses the seed and the yeast to show how God’s kingdom grows.

**Pause and answer**

What did the birds do in the branches?

## The narrow door

## Luke 13:22

Jesus travelled from town to town and village to village.

He taught as he went toward Jerusalem.

## Luke 13:23

Someone asked, “Lord, will only a few people be saved?”

Jesus answered the people,

## Luke 13:24

“Try with all your strength to enter through the narrow door.

Many will try to go in but will be unable.

## Luke 13:25

“The owner of the house will get up and shut the door.

You will stand outside knocking and calling, ‘Lord, open the door for us!’

But the owner will say, ‘I do not know where you come from.’

**Help with this picture**

Jesus speaks of entering God’s kingdom as going through a door.

He urges the people listening to act while it is open.

## Luke 13:26

“Then you will say, ‘We ate and drank with you.

You taught in our streets.’

## Luke 13:27

“The owner will answer, ‘I tell you, I do not know where you come from.

Go away from me, all of you who do evil.’

**Pause and answer**

What did the people outside ask the owner to do?

## A place at the table

## Luke 13:28

“You will see Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and all the prophets in God’s kingdom.

But you yourselves will be thrown out.

Then you will cry and grind your teeth.

## Luke 13:29

“People will come from east and west, north and south.

They will take their places at the table in God’s kingdom.

## Luke 13:30

“Some who are last will be first.

Some who are first will be last.”

**Pause and answer**

Where will the people at the table come from?

## A warning about Herod

## Luke 13:31

Just then some Pharisees came to Jesus.

They warned him, “Leave this place!

Herod wants to kill you.”

## Luke 13:32

Jesus answered, “Go and tell that fox, ‘Today and tomorrow I will keep driving out evil spirits and healing people.

On the third day I will finish my work.’

**Help with a word**

Jesus calls Herod a fox.

This is a sharp way to speak about a dangerous, crafty ruler.

## Luke 13:33

“I must keep going today, tomorrow, and the next day.

Surely Jerusalem is the place where a prophet must die!

**Pause and answer**

What work did Jesus say he would continue doing?

## Jesus speaks to Jerusalem

## Luke 13:34

“Jerusalem, Jerusalem!

You kill the prophets and stone the messengers God sends you.

So often I wanted to gather your children close.

A mother hen gathers her chicks under her wings like that.

But you would not come.

**Help with this picture**

A hen brings her chicks close to protect them.

Jesus wanted to care for the people of Jerusalem like that.

## Luke 13:35

“Look, your house is left empty.

You will not see me again until you say, ‘God bless the one who comes in the Lord’s name!’”

**Pause and answer**

How does a mother hen shelter her chicks?
