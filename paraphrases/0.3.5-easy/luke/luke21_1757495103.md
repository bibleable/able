# Luke 21 — The widow’s gift and Jesus’ call to keep watch

**About this chapter**

Jesus sees a widow give all she has.

Later, his followers ask what will happen to the temple.

## A widow gives two coins

## Luke 21:1

Jesus looked up.

He saw rich people putting money into the temple offering box.

## Luke 21:2

Then Jesus saw a poor widow.

She put in two small copper coins.

**Help with these words**

A widow is a woman whose husband has died.

**About the picture**

![Two small copper coins lying side by side.](../pictures/two-coins.svg)

Point to each coin the widow gave.

## Luke 21:3

Jesus said, “I tell you the truth.

This poor widow has given more than all the others.

## Luke 21:4

“The others gave money they could spare.

But this woman is poor.

She gave all the money she had to live on.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the widow have left to live on after she gave her coins?

## Jesus speaks about the temple stones

## Luke 21:5

Some followers talked about the temple.

They admired its beautiful stones and the gifts people had given to God.

Jesus spoke to them.

## Luke 21:6

“You see these stones here.

One day they will all fall to the ground.

No stone will be left on top of another.”

## Luke 21:7

They asked, “Teacher, when will this happen?

What sign will show us it is about to happen?”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus say would happen to the temple stones?

## Jesus warns his followers about false claims

## Luke 21:8

Jesus answered, “Watch out so no one fools you.

Many people will come using my name.

They will say, ‘I am the one!’

They will say, ‘The time is near!’

Do not follow them.

## Luke 21:9

“You will hear about wars and people fighting their rulers.

Do not be afraid.

These things must happen first.

The end will not come at once.”

## Luke 21:10

Then Jesus said, “Nations will fight other nations.

Kingdoms will fight other kingdoms.

## Luke 21:11

“There will be great earthquakes.

In some places people will have little food.

Terrible diseases will spread.

People will see frightening things and great signs in the sky.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus tell his followers to do if someone claimed to be him?

## Jesus promises help when his followers speak

## Luke 21:12

“But before all this, people will seize you and hurt you because you follow me.

They will hand you over to leaders in the meeting houses.

They will put you in prison.

They will take you before kings and governors because of me.

## Luke 21:13

“That will be your chance to tell them about me.

## Luke 21:14

“Decide now that you will not worry about what to say in your defence.

## Luke 21:15

“I will give you words to say.

I will give you wisdom.

No one against you will be able to defeat your answer or prove it wrong.

**Pause and answer**

Who promised to help the followers know what to say?

## Keep trusting Jesus

## Luke 21:16

“Even parents, brothers, other relatives, and friends will turn you in.

People will kill some of you.

## Luke 21:17

“Everyone will hate you because you follow me.

## Luke 21:18

“Yet not even one hair on your head will be lost.

**Help with these words**

Jesus has just said that some followers will die.

The picture of a single hair shows how completely God cares for them.

## Luke 21:19

“Keep trusting me through it all.

That is how you will gain your life.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus ask his followers to keep doing?

## Jesus warns Jerusalem

## Luke 21:20

“When you see armies all around Jerusalem, you will know the city will soon lie in ruins.

## Luke 21:21

“Then the people in Judea must flee to the mountains.

The people in Jerusalem must leave the city.

People in the countryside must stay outside the city.

## Luke 21:22

“Those will be days of punishment.

Everything the Scriptures say about those days will come true.

## Luke 21:23

“How hard those days will be for pregnant women and mothers nursing babies!

There will be great suffering in the land.

God’s anger will come against this people.

## Luke 21:24

“Enemies will kill people with swords.

They will take prisoners away to many nations.

The Gentiles will trample Jerusalem underfoot until their time is over.

**Help with these words**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish.

Trampling a city underfoot is a picture of ruling over it by force.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Jesus tell the people in Judea to go?

## Look up when the Son of Man comes

## Luke 21:25

“There will be signs in the sun, moon, and stars.

On earth the sea will roar and the waves will rise.

Whole nations will be afraid and bewildered.

## Luke 21:26

“People will faint with fear as they wait for what is coming on the earth.

Even the powers in the heavens will shake.

## Luke 21:27

“Then people will see the Son of Man coming in a cloud.

He will come with power and great glory.

**Help with these words**

Son of Man is a name Jesus uses for himself.

## Luke 21:28

“When these things begin, stand up and lift your heads.

The time when God will set you free is near.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did Jesus tell his followers to lift their heads?

## New leaves show that summer is near

## Luke 21:29

Jesus told them this story.

“Look at the fig tree and all the other trees.

## Luke 21:30

“You see new leaves opening on their branches.

Then you know summer is near.

**About the picture**

![New leaves opening on a twig.](../pictures/new-leaves.svg)

Find the new leaves at the end of the twig.

## Luke 21:31

“In the same way, when you see these things happen, you will know God’s kingdom is near.

## Luke 21:32

“I tell you the truth.

This generation will still be here when all these things happen.

## Luke 21:33

“Heaven and earth will pass away.

My words will last forever.

**Pause and answer**

What do new leaves tell people about summer?

## Keep watch and pray

## Luke 21:34

“Watch yourselves.

Wild living, getting drunk, and everyday worries can take up all your attention.

Then that day may catch you suddenly, like a trap.

**Help with these words**

A trap can catch an animal before it knows what is happening.

Jesus warns his followers that the day can come just as suddenly.

## Luke 21:35

“That day will come upon everyone on earth.

## Luke 21:36

“So keep watch all the time.

Pray for strength to escape what is coming.

Pray for strength to stand before the Son of Man.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus tell his followers to pray for?

## People come early to hear Jesus

## Luke 21:37

Each day Jesus taught at the temple.

Each evening he went out to the Mount of Olives.

He spent the night there.

## Luke 21:38

Early each morning all the people came to the temple to hear Jesus.

**Pause and answer**

When did people come to hear Jesus teach?
