# Luke 19 — Jesus visits Zacchaeus and comes to Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

Jesus stops under a tree to speak to Zacchaeus.

Later, people welcome Jesus as their King.

## Zacchaeus climbs a tree

## Luke 19:1

Jesus entered Jericho and walked through the city.

## Luke 19:2

A rich man named Zacchaeus lived there.

He was in charge of other tax collectors.

**Help with these words**

Tax collectors took tax money from people for the government.

## Luke 19:3

Zacchaeus wanted to see who Jesus was.

But Zacchaeus was short.

He could not see over the crowd.

## Luke 19:4

So Zacchaeus ran ahead and climbed a sycamore tree.

Jesus was going to pass that way.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Zacchaeus climb the tree?

## Jesus goes to Zacchaeus’s house

## Luke 19:5

Jesus reached the tree and looked up.

“Zacchaeus,” he said, “come down quickly.

I must stay at your house today.”

## Luke 19:6

Zacchaeus hurried down.

He was glad to welcome Jesus.

## Luke 19:7

Everyone who saw this complained.

They said, “Jesus has gone to stay with a sinner!”

## Luke 19:8

Zacchaeus stood up and said, “Look, Lord!

I give half of what I own to poor people.

If I cheated anyone, I will pay back four times what I took.”

## Luke 19:9

Jesus told him, “Today salvation has come to this house.

Zacchaeus too belongs to Abraham’s family.

## Luke 19:10

“The Son of Man came to look for the lost and save them.”

**Help with these words**

Son of Man is a name Jesus uses for himself.

Jesus calls people who have wandered away from God “lost.”

Salvation means God saving people.

**Pause and answer**

What did Zacchaeus promise anyone he had cheated?

## A man leaves money with ten servants

## Luke 19:11

Jesus was near Jerusalem.

The people listening thought God’s kingdom would appear at once.

So Jesus told them a story.

## Luke 19:12

“A man from a family of high rank left home.

He went to a distant country to receive the right to rule as king.

Then he planned to return.

## Luke 19:13

“Before he left, he called ten servants.

He gave each servant one mina.

He said, ‘Use this money to do business until I return.’

**Help with these words**

A mina was an amount of money.

## Luke 19:14

“But the people of his country hated him.

They sent messengers after him to say, ‘We do not want this man as our king.’

**Pause and answer**

What did the man ask his servants to do with the money?

## Two servants report what they earned

## Luke 19:15

“The man became king and came back.

He called the servants who had received his money.

He wanted to know how much each had earned.

## Luke 19:16

“The first servant came and said, ‘Master, your one mina earned ten more.’

## Luke 19:17

“The king said, ‘Well done! You are a good servant.

I could trust you with something very small.

Now you will be in charge of ten cities.’

## Luke 19:18

“The second servant said, ‘Master, your one mina earned five more.’

## Luke 19:19

“The king told him, ‘You will be in charge of five cities.’

**Pause and answer**

How many cities did the king give the second servant to rule?

## One servant brings back the money in a cloth

## Luke 19:20

“Another servant came and said, ‘Master, here is your mina.

I kept it wrapped in a cloth.

## Luke 19:21

“‘I was afraid because you are a harsh man.

You take out money you never put in.

You gather crops you never planted.’

## Luke 19:22

“The king answered, ‘You wicked servant!

I will judge you by your own words.

You say I am harsh?

You say I take out money I never put in and gather crops I never planted?

## Luke 19:23

“‘Then why didn’t you put my money in the bank?

When I came back, I could have collected it with interest.’

**Help with these words**

Interest is extra money a bank pays you for keeping your money there.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the servant keep the mina?

## The king gives his decision

## Luke 19:24

“The king told the people standing there, ‘Take his mina.

Give it to the servant who has ten.’

## Luke 19:25

“They said, ‘Master, he already has ten!’

## Luke 19:26

“The king answered, ‘Anyone who has something will receive more.

But someone who has nothing will lose even the little he has.

## Luke 19:27

“‘As for my enemies who refused to have me as king, bring them here.

Kill them in front of me.’”

**Pause and answer**

Who gave the orders at the end of the story?

## Two followers fetch a young donkey

## Luke 19:28

After telling this story, Jesus walked on ahead towards Jerusalem.

## Luke 19:29

Jesus came near Bethphage and Bethany, by the Mount of Olives.

He sent two of his followers ahead.

## Luke 19:30

Jesus said, “Go into the village ahead of you.

As you enter, you will find a young donkey tied there.

No one has ever ridden it.

Untie it and bring it here.

## Luke 19:31

“If someone asks why, say, ‘The Lord needs it.’”

## Luke 19:32

The two followers went.

They found the donkey just as Jesus had said.

## Luke 19:33

They began to untie the young donkey.

Its owners asked, “Why are you untying it?”

## Luke 19:34

The followers answered, “The Lord needs it.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the followers tell the owners of the donkey?

## The people welcome Jesus as King

## Luke 19:35

They led the young donkey to Jesus.

They spread their cloaks on its back.

Then they helped Jesus onto the donkey.

## Luke 19:36

As Jesus rode along, people spread their cloaks on the road.

**About the picture**

![A cloak that a person could wear over other clothes.](../pictures/cloak.svg)

This is a cloak.

People took off cloaks like this and spread them on the road to welcome Jesus.

## Luke 19:37

Jesus came near the path down the Mount of Olives.

The whole crowd of followers began to praise God.

They called out with joy because of all the miracles they had seen.

## Luke 19:38

They shouted, “Blessed is the King who comes in the Lord’s name!

Peace in heaven!

Glory to God in the highest heaven!”

## Luke 19:39

Some Pharisees in the crowd said to Jesus, “Teacher, tell your followers to stop!”

## Luke 19:40

Jesus answered, “I tell you, if they keep quiet, the stones themselves will shout.”

**Help with these words**

Jesus pictures even the stones joining in the praise.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the followers praise God?

## Jesus cries over Jerusalem

## Luke 19:41

Jesus came near Jerusalem and saw the city.

He began to cry over it.

## Luke 19:42

Jesus said, “If only you knew today what would bring you peace!

But now you cannot see it.

## Luke 19:43

“The time will come when your enemies build a wall around you.

They will close you in on every side.

## Luke 19:44

“They will bring you and the children within your walls down to the ground.

They will not leave one stone on another.

This will happen because you did not recognise the time when God came to you.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus wish the people of Jerusalem knew?

## Jesus teaches in the temple

## Luke 19:45

Jesus went into the open courts of the temple.

He began to drive out the people selling things there.

## Luke 19:46

Jesus told them, “The Scriptures say, ‘My house will be a house of prayer.’

But you have made it a hideout for robbers.”

**Help with these words**

The Scriptures are the holy writings.

God calls the temple his house.

Prayer means talking to God.

## Luke 19:47

Jesus taught in the temple every day.

The chief priests, the teachers of God’s law, and the leaders of the people wanted to kill Jesus.

## Luke 19:48

But the leaders could not find a way to do it.

The people listened closely to every word Jesus said.

**Pause and answer**

What did the people do while Jesus taught?
