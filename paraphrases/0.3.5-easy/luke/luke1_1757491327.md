# Luke 1 — Gabriel brings news to Zechariah and Mary

**About this chapter**

Gabriel tells Zechariah that Elizabeth will have a son named John.

Mary hears that she will have a son named Jesus.

## Luke writes to Theophilus

## Luke 1:1

Many people have written about the things that have come true among us.

## Luke 1:2

We learned these things from people who saw them from the beginning.

They served God by sharing his message.

## Luke 1:3

Theophilus, I write to you with respect.

I too have checked everything carefully from the beginning.

I have decided to write it down in order for you.

## Luke 1:4

Then you can be sure of the things you have learned.

**Pause and answer**

How did Luke prepare to write his account?

## Zechariah and Elizabeth

## Luke 1:5

When Herod was king of Judea, a priest named Zechariah lived there.

He belonged to the group of priests called Abijah’s group.

His wife Elizabeth came from Aaron’s family.

## Luke 1:6

God saw that both of them did what was right.

They faithfully followed all the Lord’s commands and rules.

## Luke 1:7

They had no children because Elizabeth could not have a child.

Both Zechariah and Elizabeth were old.

## Luke 1:8

One day Zechariah’s group was on duty.

Zechariah was doing his work as a priest before God.

**Pause and answer**

What work did Zechariah do?

## An angel in the temple

## Luke 1:9

The priests chose by lot who would burn incense.

This time they chose Zechariah to go inside the Lord’s temple.

**Help with these words**

Choosing by lot means making a choice by drawing marked objects.

Incense gives off a sweet smell when it burns.

## Luke 1:10

When it was time to burn the incense, the people prayed outside.

## Luke 1:11

An angel of the Lord appeared to Zechariah.

The angel stood on the right side of the incense altar.

## Luke 1:12

Zechariah was startled to see the angel.

He was very afraid.

**Pause and answer**

Where were the people praying?

## A son named John

## Luke 1:13

The angel said, “Do not be afraid, Zechariah.

God has heard your prayer.

Your wife Elizabeth will have a son.

Give him the name John.

## Luke 1:14

“John will bring you great joy.

Many people will celebrate his birth.

## Luke 1:15

“The Lord will call John great.

John must never drink wine or any strong drink.

God will fill John with the Holy Spirit even before he is born.

## Luke 1:16

“John will help many people in Israel turn back to the Lord their God.

## Luke 1:17

“John will go ahead of the Lord with the spirit and power Elijah had.

He will turn fathers’ hearts toward their children.

He will help disobedient people learn the wisdom of those who do right.

In this way John will get people ready for the Lord.”

**Help with a name**

Elijah was a prophet who spoke God’s message long before John.

**Pause and answer**

What name did the angel give the baby?

## Gabriel answers Zechariah

## Luke 1:18

Zechariah asked, “How can I be sure?

I am old, and so is my wife.”

## Luke 1:19

The angel answered, “I am Gabriel.

I stand before God.

God sent me to bring you this good news.

## Luke 1:20

“You did not believe my words.

Now you will be unable to speak until these things happen.

What I have told you will come true at the right time.”

**Pause and answer**

Why would Zechariah be unable to speak for a time?

## Elizabeth receives good news

## Luke 1:21

Outside, the people waited for Zechariah.

They wondered why he stayed so long in the temple.

## Luke 1:22

At last Zechariah came out, but he could not speak.

The people realised he had seen a vision inside the temple.

He kept making signs to them.

**Help with a word**

A vision is something God lets someone see.

## Luke 1:23

When Zechariah finished his days of service, he went home.

## Luke 1:24

After this, Elizabeth became pregnant.

For five months she stayed away from other people.

She said,

## Luke 1:25

“The Lord has done this for me.

He has shown me kindness.

He has taken away the shame I felt among people.”

**Pause and answer**

How did Zechariah communicate with the people outside?

## Gabriel visits Mary

## Luke 1:26

When Elizabeth was six months pregnant, God sent Gabriel to Nazareth.

Nazareth was a town in Galilee.

## Luke 1:27

Gabriel came to Mary, a virgin promised in marriage to Joseph.

Joseph belonged to King David’s family.

**Help with a word**

A virgin is someone who has never had sex.

## Luke 1:28

Gabriel greeted Mary.

“The Lord has shown you great kindness,” he said.

“The Lord is with you.”

## Luke 1:29

The greeting troubled Mary greatly.

She wondered what Gabriel’s words meant.

**Pause and answer**

In which town did Gabriel visit Mary?

## The name Jesus

## Luke 1:30

Gabriel said, “Do not be afraid, Mary.

God has shown you kindness.

## Luke 1:31

“You will become pregnant and give birth to a son.

Give him the name Jesus.

## Luke 1:32

“He will be great.

People will call him the Son of the Most High.

The Lord God will give him the throne of his ancestor David.

**Help with these words**

The Most High is a name for God.

David’s throne pictures his place as king.

## Luke 1:33

“He will rule over Jacob’s family forever.

His kingdom will never end.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Gabriel say about how long Jesus would rule?

## Mary answers Gabriel

## Luke 1:34

Mary asked Gabriel, “How can this happen?

I have never had sex.”

## Luke 1:35

Gabriel replied, “The Holy Spirit will come upon you.

The power of the Most High will cover you.

So the holy child you bear will be called the Son of God.

## Luke 1:36

“Your relative Elizabeth is going to have a son in her old age.

People said she could not have a child.

Now she is six months pregnant.

## Luke 1:37

“Every word God speaks will come true.”

## Luke 1:38

Mary answered, “I am the Lord’s servant.

May what you have said happen to me.”

Then Gabriel left her.

**Pause and answer**

What news did Gabriel give Mary about Elizabeth?

## Mary greets Elizabeth

## Luke 1:39

Mary got ready and hurried to a town in the hills of Judah.

## Luke 1:40

She entered Zechariah’s house and greeted Elizabeth.

## Luke 1:41

Elizabeth heard Mary’s greeting.

The baby jumped inside Elizabeth, and the Holy Spirit filled her.

## Luke 1:42

Elizabeth called out, “God has blessed you among women!

God has blessed the baby you are carrying!

## Luke 1:43

“What an honour for me!

Why should the mother of my Lord come to see me?

## Luke 1:44

“As soon as I heard your greeting, my baby jumped for joy.

## Luke 1:45

“God has blessed you because you believed him.

You trusted that the Lord would do what he told you.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Elizabeth’s baby do when Mary spoke?

## Mary praises God

## Luke 1:46

Mary said, “With all my heart I praise the Lord.

## Luke 1:47

“Deep inside, I rejoice in God my Saviour.

## Luke 1:48

“He has looked kindly on me, his lowly servant.

From now on people in every generation will call me blessed.

## Luke 1:49

“The Mighty One has done great things for me.

His name is holy.

## Luke 1:50

“From one generation to the next, God shows mercy to those who fear him.

**Pause and answer**

What did Mary call God when she spoke about her joy?

## God remembers his promise

## Luke 1:51

“God has done powerful things with his arm.

He has scattered those who think proudly of themselves.

**Help with this picture**

Mary speaks of God’s arm to describe God’s strength.

## Luke 1:52

“He has brought rulers down from their thrones.

He has lifted up the humble.

## Luke 1:53

“He has filled hungry people with good things.

He has sent the rich away empty.

## Luke 1:54

“He has helped his servant Israel.

He has remembered to show mercy,

## Luke 1:55

“just as he promised our ancestors.

He promised Abraham and his family mercy forever.”

**About the picture**

![Abraham, his son Isaac, and Isaac’s son Jacob.](../pictures/abraham-family.svg)

Follow the arrows from Abraham to his grandson.

Mary remembers God’s promise to Abraham’s family.

## Luke 1:56

Mary stayed with Elizabeth about three months.

Then Mary went home.

**Pause and answer**

What did God give to hungry people?

## Elizabeth names her son

## Luke 1:57

When the time came, Elizabeth gave birth to a son.

## Luke 1:58

Elizabeth’s neighbours and relatives heard about the Lord’s great kindness to her.

They shared her joy.

## Luke 1:59

When the boy was eight days old, people came for his circumcision.

They wanted to name him Zechariah after his father.

**Help with a word**

Circumcision is a sign of God’s covenant with Abraham’s family.

It involves removing the fold of skin at the tip of a boy’s penis.

## Luke 1:60

Elizabeth said, “No.

His name will be John.”

## Luke 1:61

They said, “No one in your family has that name.”

**Pause and answer**

What name did Elizabeth choose?

## Zechariah writes the name

## Luke 1:62

They made signs to Zechariah.

They wanted to know what name he had chosen.

## Luke 1:63

Zechariah asked for a writing tablet.

He wrote, “His name is John.”

Everyone was amazed.

## Luke 1:64

At once Zechariah could speak again.

He began to praise God.

## Luke 1:65

All the neighbours were filled with wonder and fear.

Throughout the hills of Judea, people talked about what had happened.

## Luke 1:66

Those who heard kept thinking about it.

They asked, “What will this child become?”

The Lord’s hand was with John.

**Help with this picture**

The Lord’s hand means God was present to help John.

**Pause and answer**

What did Zechariah do when he could speak again?

## Zechariah praises God

## Luke 1:67

The Holy Spirit filled John’s father, Zechariah.

Zechariah spoke a message from God.

## Luke 1:68

“Praise the Lord, the God of Israel!

He has come to his people and set them free.

## Luke 1:69

“From the family of David his servant, God has raised up a mighty Saviour for us.

**Help with this picture**

Zechariah calls the Saviour a “horn of salvation.”

A strong animal’s horn pictures the power to rescue people.

## Luke 1:70

“Long ago God promised this through his holy prophets.

## Luke 1:71

“He promised to save us from our enemies and from everyone who hates us.

**Pause and answer**

From whose family would the Saviour come?

## A promise to Abraham

## Luke 1:72

“He promised to show mercy to our ancestors.

He remembers his holy covenant.

**Help with a word**

A covenant is a binding promise or agreement.

## Luke 1:73

“God made a solemn promise to our father Abraham.

## Luke 1:74

“He promised to rescue us from our enemies.

Then we could serve him without fear.

## Luke 1:75

“We could live holy lives and do right before him every day.

**Pause and answer**

How would the people serve God after he rescued them?

## Zechariah speaks to John

## Luke 1:76

“And you, my child, will be called a prophet of the Most High.

You will go ahead of the Lord to prepare his way.

## Luke 1:77

“You will tell his people how God saves them by forgiving their sins.

## Luke 1:78

“Our God shows us such tender mercy.

A light like the dawn will come to us from heaven.

## Luke 1:79

“It will shine on people living in darkness and in the shadow of death.

It will guide our feet onto the path of peace.”

**Help with this picture**

At dawn people can see their way again.

Zechariah pictures God guiding people toward peace.

## Luke 1:80

John grew up and became strong in spirit.

He lived in the desert until he appeared publicly before the people of Israel.

**Pause and answer**

What would John tell people about forgiveness?
