# Luke 2 — Jesus is born in Bethlehem and grows up in Nazareth

**About this chapter**

Shepherds visit the newborn Jesus.

Years later, Mary and Joseph look for Jesus in Jerusalem.

## Joseph and Mary travel to Bethlehem

## Luke 2:1

Caesar Augustus ruled the Roman Empire.

He ordered a count of all the people in his empire.

## Luke 2:2

This was the first such count while Quirinius governed Syria.

## Luke 2:3

Everyone went to their own town to give their names.

## Luke 2:4

Joseph left Nazareth in Galilee and travelled to Bethlehem in Judea.

Bethlehem was David’s town.

Joseph went there because he belonged to David’s family.

## Luke 2:5

Mary went with Joseph to give their names.

She had promised to marry Joseph and was expecting a baby.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Joseph go to Bethlehem?

## Mary lays her baby in a manger

## Luke 2:6

While they were in Bethlehem, it was time for the baby to come.

## Luke 2:7

Mary gave birth to her first child, a son.

She wrapped him snugly in strips of cloth.

There was no room for them at the inn.

So she laid him in a manger.

**Help with a word**

A manger is a feeding box for animals.

**About the picture**

![An open wooden feeding box with hay.](../pictures/manger.svg)

Point to the open top of the manger.

Mary laid Jesus in a feeding box like this.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Mary lay her baby?

## Shepherds hear good news

## Luke 2:8

Nearby, shepherds were living out in the fields.

That night they were watching over their sheep.

## Luke 2:9

An angel of the Lord stood before the shepherds.

The Lord’s glory shone around them, and they were terrified.

## Luke 2:10

The angel said, “Do not be afraid.

I bring good news for all the people.

This news will give them great joy.

## Luke 2:11

“Today in David’s town a Saviour has been born for you.

He is Christ the Lord!

**Help with these names**

A saviour is someone who rescues people.

Christ means the King God promised to send.

## Luke 2:12

“Here is how you will know him.

You will find a baby wrapped in strips of cloth.

He will be lying in a manger.”

**Pause and answer**

How would the shepherds recognise the baby?

## The shepherds go to Bethlehem

## Luke 2:13

Suddenly a great crowd of angels from heaven joined the angel.

They praised God together.

## Luke 2:14

“Glory to God in the highest heaven!

Peace on earth to the people God looks on with favour!”

## Luke 2:15

The angels left and went back to heaven.

The shepherds said, “Let’s go to Bethlehem.

Let’s see what the Lord has told us about.”

## Luke 2:16

They hurried and found Mary and Joseph.

They saw the baby lying in the manger.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did the shepherds find with the baby?

## Mary remembers the shepherds’ words

## Luke 2:17

After seeing the baby, the shepherds told others what the angel had said about him.

## Luke 2:18

Everyone who heard the shepherds was amazed.

## Luke 2:19

Mary kept all these things in her heart.

She thought about them often.

## Luke 2:20

The shepherds went back, praising God.

Everything they had heard and seen was just as the angel had told them.

**Pause and answer**

What did Mary do with what she heard?

## Mary and Joseph present Jesus to the Lord

## Luke 2:21

Eight days later, it was time for the baby’s circumcision.

His parents named him Jesus.

The angel had given him that name before Mary became pregnant.

## Luke 2:22

The time for purification set by the law of Moses had passed.

Mary and Joseph took Jesus to Jerusalem.

They brought him there to present him to the Lord.

**Help with a word**

Purification was part of the worship that the law required after childbirth.

## Luke 2:23

The Lord’s law says, “Set apart every firstborn son for the Lord.”

## Luke 2:24

They also came to give the offering named in the law.

The law said, “A pair of turtledoves or two young pigeons.”

**Pause and answer**

What name did Mary and Joseph give the baby?

## Simeon holds Jesus

## Luke 2:25

Simeon lived in Jerusalem.

He loved God and did what was right.

He waited for God to comfort Israel.

The Holy Spirit was with him.

## Luke 2:26

The Holy Spirit had made Simeon a promise.

Before he died, he would see the Christ the Lord had sent.

## Luke 2:27

The Spirit led Simeon into the temple courtyards.

Just then the parents brought Jesus in to do what the law required.

## Luke 2:28

Simeon held Jesus in his arms and praised God.

**Pause and answer**

What had the Holy Spirit promised Simeon?

## Simeon praises God

## Luke 2:29

He said, “Lord, you rule over all.

Now your servant can die in peace, as you promised.

## Luke 2:30

“With my own eyes I have seen how you will save us.

## Luke 2:31

“You have prepared this for all peoples to see.

## Luke 2:32

“This child is a light to show the other nations your way.

He brings honour to your people Israel.”

**Help with this picture**

A light helps people see where to go.

Simeon says Jesus will make God known beyond Israel.

**Pause and answer**

Who would see the light Simeon spoke about?

## Simeon speaks to Mary

## Luke 2:33

Mary and Joseph marvelled at what Simeon said about Jesus.

## Luke 2:34

Simeon blessed them.

Then he told Mary, “Because of this child, many in Israel will fall and many will rise.

He will be a sign from God that people speak against.

## Luke 2:35

“What people really think will come to light.

And a sword will pierce your own soul too.”

**Help with this picture**

The sword pictures deep sorrow that Mary herself will feel.

**Pause and answer**

Who heard Simeon’s words about her own sorrow?

## Anna thanks God

## Luke 2:36

Anna, a prophet, was there too.

She was Phanuel’s daughter, from the tribe of Asher.

She was very old.

She had lived with her husband for seven years.

## Luke 2:37

After her husband died, Anna lived as a widow.

She was now eighty-four years old.

She never left the temple.

She worshiped there day and night, fasting and praying.

**Help with a word**

Fasting means choosing to go without food for a time.

## Luke 2:38

Anna came up just then and thanked God.

People were waiting for God to set Jerusalem free.

Anna spoke to them all about the child.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Anna tell about Jesus?

## Jesus grows up in Nazareth

## Luke 2:39

Jesus’ parents did everything the Lord’s law required.

Then they returned to Nazareth, their town in Galilee.

## Luke 2:40

Jesus grew strong and full of wisdom.

God showed him favour.

## Luke 2:41

Every year Jesus’ parents went to Jerusalem for the Passover feast.

**Help with a name**

At Passover, Jewish people remember how God freed their people from slavery in Egypt.

## Luke 2:42

When Jesus was twelve, they went to the feast as usual.

**Pause and answer**

How old was Jesus when this family visit to the feast took place?

## Mary and Joseph look for Jesus

## Luke 2:43

After the feast, his parents started home.

Jesus stayed in Jerusalem.

His parents did not know he had stayed.

## Luke 2:44

They thought he was travelling with their group.

After a day’s journey, they looked for him among relatives and friends.

## Luke 2:45

They could not find him.

So they went back to Jerusalem to look for him.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Mary and Joseph go to look for Jesus after checking with their group?

## Jesus sits with the teachers

## Luke 2:46

After three days they found him in the temple courtyards.

Jesus was sitting with the teachers.

He listened to them and asked questions.

## Luke 2:47

Everyone who heard Jesus was amazed at his understanding and his answers.

## Luke 2:48

His parents were astonished to see him there.

Mary said, “My child, why have you done this to us?

Your father and I have been so worried looking for you.”

**Pause and answer**

What was Jesus doing with the teachers?

## Jesus goes home with his parents

## Luke 2:49

Jesus asked, “Why were you looking for me?

Didn’t you know I had to be in my Father’s house?”

**Help with these words**

Jesus calls the temple his Father’s house.

He is speaking about God.

## Luke 2:50

His parents did not understand what Jesus meant.

## Luke 2:51

Jesus went back to Nazareth with them and obeyed them.

Mary kept all these things in her heart.

## Luke 2:52

Jesus grew taller and wiser.

He grew in favour with God and with people.

**Pause and answer**

How did Jesus treat his parents when they went home?
