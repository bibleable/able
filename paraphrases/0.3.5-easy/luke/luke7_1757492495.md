# Luke 7 — Jesus helps a widow and forgives a woman

**About this chapter**

An officer asks for help for his servant.

Later, at a meal, Jesus tells a story about forgiveness.

## An officer sends elders to Jesus

## Luke 7:1

Jesus finished speaking to the people who had gathered to listen.

Then he went to Capernaum.

## Luke 7:2

A Roman army officer lived there.

A servant he valued greatly was sick and near death.

**Help with a word**

Luke calls the officer a centurion.

A centurion led Roman soldiers.

## Luke 7:3

The officer heard about Jesus.

He sent Jewish elders to ask Jesus to come and heal his servant.

## Luke 7:4

The elders came to Jesus and begged him to help.

They said, “This officer deserves your help.

## Luke 7:5

“He loves our people.

He built our synagogue.”

**Pause and answer**

Whom did the officer first send to Jesus?

## Friends bring the officer’s message

## Luke 7:6

Jesus went with the elders.

As Jesus neared the house, the officer sent friends with a message.

“Lord, please do not trouble yourself.

I do not deserve to have you in my home.

## Luke 7:7

“That is why I did not come to you myself.

Just say the word, and my servant will get well.

## Luke 7:8

“I take orders, and I give soldiers orders too.

I tell one to go, and he goes.

I tell another to come, and he comes.

My servant does what I tell him to do.”

## Luke 7:9

The officer’s words amazed Jesus.

Jesus turned to the crowd following him.

“I tell you, even in Israel I have never found such great faith.”

## Luke 7:10

The messengers went back to the house.

They found the servant well.

**Pause and answer**

What did the friends find when they went home?

## Jesus sees a mother at the town gate

## Luke 7:11

Soon afterward Jesus went to a town called Nain.

His followers and a large crowd went with him.

## Luke 7:12

At the town gate, people were carrying out a young man who had died.

He was his mother’s only son.

Her husband had already died.

Many people from the town were with her.

**Help with a word**

A widow is a woman whose husband has died.

## Luke 7:13

The Lord saw the mother and felt deep care for her.

He said, “Do not cry.”

## Luke 7:14

Jesus stepped forward and touched the coffin.

The people carrying it stopped.

He said, “Young man, I tell you, get up!”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus say to the young man?

## The young man speaks

## Luke 7:15

The dead man sat up and began to speak.

Jesus gave him back to his mother.

## Luke 7:16

Everyone was filled with wonder and fear.

They praised God and said, “A great prophet has come among us!

God has come to help his people!”

## Luke 7:17

News about Jesus spread through Judea and all the land around it.

**Pause and answer**

What did the crowd do when the young man sat up?

## John sends two messengers

## Luke 7:18

John’s followers told him everything that had happened.

## Luke 7:19

John called two of his followers and sent them to the Lord.

“Ask him, ‘Are you the one we were expecting?

Or should we look for someone else?’”

## Luke 7:20

The two men came to Jesus.

They said, “John the Baptist sent us to ask this question.

‘Are you the one we were expecting?

Or should we look for someone else?’”

## Luke 7:21

At that very time Jesus healed many sick and suffering people.

He freed people from evil spirits.

He helped many blind people see.

## Luke 7:22

Jesus answered, “Go and tell John what you have seen and heard.

Blind people see, and people who could not walk now walk.

People with leprosy become clean, and deaf people hear.

Dead people come back to life.

Poor people hear the good news.

**Help with these words**

Leprosy was a name for serious skin diseases.

Here becoming clean means the disease was gone.

## Luke 7:23

“God blesses those who do not turn away because of me.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus tell the messengers to report?

## John prepares the way

## Luke 7:24

John’s messengers left.

Jesus spoke to the crowds about John.

“What did you go into the desert to see?

A reed bending in the wind?

**About the picture**

![A reed bending to one side.](../pictures/bending-reed.svg)

Follow the long stem to its tip.

## Luke 7:25

“Did you go to see a man in fine clothes?

People who wear fine clothes and live in luxury live in palaces.

## Luke 7:26

“Did you go to see a prophet?

Yes, and John is more than a prophet.

## Luke 7:27

“Scripture says this about John.

‘I will send my messenger ahead of you.

He will get the way ready for you.’

## Luke 7:28

“I tell you, no one ever born has been greater than John.

Yet even the least in God’s kingdom is greater than John.”

**Pause and answer**

What work did God give John?

## Two responses to John

## Luke 7:29

All the people who heard Jesus agreed that God’s way was right.

Even the tax collectors agreed.

John had baptized them.

## Luke 7:30

The Pharisees and teachers of the law had refused John’s baptism.

They rejected what God wanted for them.

**Pause and answer**

Who had refused John’s baptism?

## Children in the marketplace

## Luke 7:31

Jesus asked, “What are the people of this time like?

What can I compare them to?

## Luke 7:32

“They are like children sitting in the marketplace, calling to each other.

‘We played the flute for you, but you did not dance.

We sang a funeral song, but you did not cry.’

**Help with this picture**

The other children refuse to join either song.

Jesus compares them to people who found fault with both John and Jesus.

## Luke 7:33

“John the Baptist came eating no bread and drinking no wine.

You say, ‘He has an evil spirit!’

## Luke 7:34

“The Son of Man came eating and drinking.

You say, ‘He eats too much and drinks too much wine!

He is a friend of tax collectors and sinners!’

**Help with a name**

Jesus calls himself the Son of Man.

## Luke 7:35

“But all the children of wisdom show that wisdom is right.”

**Help with this picture**

Wisdom’s “children” are people who live by wisdom.

Their lives show that wisdom is right.

**Pause and answer**

What did the children want their playmates to do when they played the flute?

## A woman brings perfume

## Luke 7:36

A Pharisee invited Jesus to a meal.

Jesus went to his house and rested at the table.

## Luke 7:37

A woman in that town had lived a sinful life.

She heard that Jesus was eating at the Pharisee’s house.

She brought a stone jar of perfume.

**About the picture**

![A jar for holding perfume.](../pictures/perfume-jar.svg)

Find the opening where the perfume comes out.

## Luke 7:38

She stood behind Jesus, at his feet, and cried.

Her tears wet his feet.

She wiped them with her hair.

She kissed his feet and poured perfume on them.

## Luke 7:39

The Pharisee who invited Jesus saw this.

He thought, “If Jesus were a prophet, he would know who is touching him.

He would know that she is a sinner.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the woman use to dry Jesus’ feet?

## Two men owe money

## Luke 7:40

Jesus said, “Simon, I have something to tell you.”

Simon answered, “Tell me, Teacher.”

## Luke 7:41

Jesus said, “Two men owed money to the same lender.

One owed five hundred silver coins.

The other owed fifty.

**Help with a word**

The silver coins were called denarii.

A debt is money someone owes.

## Luke 7:42

“Neither man could pay.

The lender cancelled both debts.

Which man will love the lender more?”

## Luke 7:43

Simon answered, “I suppose the one who owed more.”

Jesus said, “You are right.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the lender do when neither man could pay?

## Jesus speaks to Simon about the woman

## Luke 7:44

Jesus turned toward the woman and spoke to Simon.

“Do you see this woman?

You gave me no water for my feet when I came in.

She wet my feet with tears and dried them with her hair.

## Luke 7:45

“You did not greet me with a kiss.

She has kept kissing my feet since I came in.

## Luke 7:46

“You gave me no oil for my head.

She poured perfume on my feet.

## Luke 7:47

“I tell you, her many sins have been forgiven.

She has shown great love.

Someone who has been forgiven little shows little love.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the woman pour on Jesus’ feet?

## Jesus speaks to the woman

## Luke 7:48

Jesus told the woman, “Your sins are forgiven.”

## Luke 7:49

The others at the table wondered, “Who is this man who even forgives sins?”

## Luke 7:50

Jesus told the woman, “Your faith has saved you.

Go in peace.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus tell the woman about her sins?
