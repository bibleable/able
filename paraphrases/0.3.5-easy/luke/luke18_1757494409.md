# Luke 18 — Jesus teaches about prayer and listens to a blind man

**About this chapter**

A widow keeps asking a judge to help her.

Later, a man beside the road keeps calling to Jesus.

## A widow keeps asking for justice

## Luke 18:1

Jesus told his followers a story about prayer.

He wanted them to keep praying, even when they felt discouraged.

## Luke 18:2

“In one town there lived a judge.

He had no fear of God.

He had no respect for people.

## Luke 18:3

“A widow lived in that town.

She kept coming to the judge.

She kept saying, ‘Someone has wronged me.

Please give me a fair decision.’

**Help with these words**

A widow is a woman whose husband has died.

## Luke 18:4

“For a time the judge refused.

Then he thought, ‘I have no fear of God.

I have no respect for people.

## Luke 18:5

“‘But this widow keeps coming to me.

I will see that she gets justice.

Otherwise her visits will wear me out.’”

**Pause and answer**

What did the widow keep asking the judge to do?

## God hears people who call for justice

## Luke 18:6

The Lord Jesus said, “Listen to what the unfair judge said.

## Luke 18:7

“Think about the people God has chosen.

They call out to God day and night.

Won’t God give them justice?

Will God put off helping them?

## Luke 18:8

“I tell you, God will act quickly to give them justice.

But when the Son of Man comes, will he find people on earth who still trust God?”

**Help with these words**

Son of Man is a name Jesus uses for himself.

**Pause and answer**

Who hears the people calling day and night?

## Two men go to pray

## Luke 18:9

Some people were sure they were right with God.

They looked down on others.

Jesus told them this story.

## Luke 18:10

“Two men went to the temple to pray.

One was a Pharisee.

The other collected taxes.

## Luke 18:11

“The Pharisee stood by himself and prayed.

‘God, I thank you that I am different from other people.

They cheat people, do wrong, or break their marriage promises by having sex with someone else.

I am different from this tax collector too.

## Luke 18:12

“‘Twice a week I fast.

I give you a tenth of everything I get.’

**Help with these words**

To fast means to go without food for a time.

A tenth is one part out of ten equal parts.

## Luke 18:13

“The tax collector stood far away.

He would not even look up to heaven.

He beat his chest and said, ‘God, I have sinned.

Please have mercy on me!’

**Help with these words**

Beating his chest showed how sorry the man was.

He asked God to show him kindness and forgive him.

## Luke 18:14

“I tell you, the tax collector went home right with God.

The Pharisee did not.

God will bring down those who put themselves above others.

God will honour those who are humble.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the tax collector ask God to give him?

## Jesus welcomes children

## Luke 18:15

People even brought babies to Jesus so he could lay his hands on them.

His followers saw this and told the people off.

## Luke 18:16

But Jesus called the children to him.

He said, “Let the little children come to me.

Do not stop them.

God’s kingdom belongs to people like these children.

## Luke 18:17

“I tell you the truth.

You must receive God’s kingdom like a little child to enter it.”

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Jesus want his followers to let through?

## A ruler asks about life with God

## Luke 18:18

A ruler asked Jesus, “Good Teacher, what must I do to receive life that lasts forever?”

## Luke 18:19

Jesus answered, “Why do you call me good?

Only God is good.

## Luke 18:20

“You know God’s commands.

Be faithful in marriage.

Do not murder.

Do not steal.

Do not tell lies about another person.

Honour your father and mother.”

## Luke 18:21

The ruler said, “I have obeyed all these commands since I was young.”

## Luke 18:22

Jesus heard him and said, “You still need one thing.

Sell everything you own.

Give the money to people who are poor.

Then you will have treasure in heaven.

Come and follow me.”

## Luke 18:23

The ruler became very sad when he heard this.

He owned a great deal.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Jesus tell the ruler to give his money to?

## A camel and a needle

## Luke 18:24

Jesus saw how sad the ruler was.

Jesus said, “It is hard for rich people to enter God’s kingdom!

## Luke 18:25

“It is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for a rich person to enter God’s kingdom.”

**About the picture**

![A sewing needle with a small hole at one end.](../pictures/needle.svg)

Find the small hole where the thread goes.

Jesus calls this the eye of the needle.

A camel is far too big to fit through.

## Luke 18:26

The people listening asked, “Then who can be saved?”

## Luke 18:27

Jesus answered, “God can do what people cannot do.”

**Pause and answer**

Who can do what people cannot do?

## Peter remembers what he left

## Luke 18:28

Peter said, “Look, we left everything we had to follow you.”

## Luke 18:29

Jesus answered, “I tell you the truth.

Some people leave their home, wife, brothers, parents, or children for the sake of God’s kingdom.

## Luke 18:30

“They will receive many times more in this life.

In the life to come, they will live forever.”

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus promise in the life to come?

## Jesus tells the twelve what will happen

## Luke 18:31

Jesus took his twelve closest followers aside.

He said, “We are going up to Jerusalem.

Everything the prophets wrote about the Son of Man will happen.

## Luke 18:32

“People will hand him over to people who are not Jewish.

They will make fun of him.

They will insult him and spit on him.

## Luke 18:33

“They will beat him with a whip and kill him.

On the third day he will rise again.”

## Luke 18:34

The twelve followers did not understand any of this.

The meaning was hidden from them.

They could not grasp what Jesus meant.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus say would happen on the third day?

## A man calls out beside the road

## Luke 18:35

Jesus came near the town of Jericho.

A blind man sat beside the road, asking for money.

## Luke 18:36

The man heard a crowd going past.

He asked what was happening.

## Luke 18:37

People told him, “Jesus from Nazareth is passing by.”

## Luke 18:38

The man called out, “Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!”

**Help with these words**

David was a king of Israel long ago.

The man calls Jesus “Son of David” because Jesus comes from David’s family.

## Luke 18:39

The people at the front told him to be quiet.

But he called even louder, “Son of David, have mercy on me!”

**Pause and answer**

What did the man do when people told him to be quiet?

## Jesus listens to the man

## Luke 18:40

Jesus stopped.

He told the people to bring the man to him.

When the man came close, Jesus asked him a question.

## Luke 18:41

“What do you want me to do for you?”

The man answered, “Lord, let me see again.”

## Luke 18:42

Jesus said, “See again!

Your faith has made you well.”

## Luke 18:43

At once the man could see.

He followed Jesus and praised God.

When the people saw this, they all praised God too.

**Pause and answer**

What did the man ask Jesus to do for him?
