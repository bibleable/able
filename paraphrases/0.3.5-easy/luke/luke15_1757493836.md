# Luke 15 — Lost and found

**About this chapter**

Jesus tells stories about a lost sheep and a lost coin.

Then he tells a story about a father and two sons.

## People complain about Jesus

## Luke 15:1

Tax collectors and other people known as sinners gathered around Jesus.

They came to listen to him.

**Help with these words**

Tax collectors took tax money from people for the government.

Sin means doing wrong against God.

## Luke 15:2

The Pharisees and the teachers of God’s law complained.

They said, “Jesus welcomes sinners.

He even eats with them!”

## Luke 15:3

So Jesus told them a story.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jesus do that upset the people who complained?

## A man looks for one sheep

## Luke 15:4

“Suppose you have a hundred sheep.

One sheep goes missing.

You leave the other ninety-nine where they are eating grass.

You search until you find the missing sheep.

## Luke 15:5

“You are glad to find your sheep.

You lift it onto your shoulders.

## Luke 15:6

“When you get home, you call your friends and neighbours together.

You say, ‘Come and be glad with me!

I found my lost sheep!’

## Luke 15:7

“Here is what I tell you.

When one sinner turns back to God, heaven celebrates.

There is more joy over that one person than over ninety-nine people who already do right and have no need to turn back.

**Help with these words**

To repent means to turn away from wrongdoing and turn back to God.

**Pause and answer**

What does the man do when one sheep goes missing?

## A woman looks for one coin

## Luke 15:8

“Suppose a woman has ten silver coins.

She loses one coin.

She lights a lamp and sweeps her house.

She looks carefully until she finds the coin.

## Luke 15:9

“Then she calls her friends and neighbours together.

She says, ‘Come and be glad with me!

I found my lost coin!’

**About the picture**

![Nine silver coins in a group and one silver coin apart.](../pictures/ten-silver-coins.svg)

Point to the coin on its own.

The woman searched for one coin like this.

## Luke 15:10

“It is the same when one sinner turns back to God.

There is joy in front of God’s angels.”

**Pause and answer**

How does the woman feel when she finds her coin?

## The younger son leaves home

## Luke 15:11

Then Jesus told another story.

“A father had two sons.

## Luke 15:12

“The younger son said, ‘Father, give me my share of what you own.’

So the father divided what he owned between his sons.

## Luke 15:13

“A few days later, the younger son gathered his things.

He went to live in a country far away.

There he wasted his money on wild living.

## Luke 15:14

“The younger son spent everything he had.

Then food became scarce throughout that country.

The son had nothing to live on.

## Luke 15:15

“He went to work for a man from that country.

The man sent him into his fields to feed pigs.

## Luke 15:16

“The pigs ate seed pods.

The son was so hungry he wanted those pods.

But no one gave him anything.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the younger son want the pigs’ food?

## The younger son decides to go home

## Luke 15:17

“Then the younger son began to think clearly.

He said to himself, ‘My father’s workers have plenty to eat.

Here I am dying of hunger!

## Luke 15:18

“‘I will get up and go back to my father.

I will tell him I have sinned against God and against him.

## Luke 15:19

“‘I do not deserve to be his son anymore.

I will ask him to take me on as a worker.’

**Pause and answer**

What work did the younger son plan to ask for?

## The father runs to meet his son

## Luke 15:20

“So the younger son got up and went home.

His father saw him coming from far away.

The father felt deeply for his son.

He ran to his son, hugged him, and kissed him.

## Luke 15:21

“The son said, ‘Father, I have sinned against God and against you.

I do not deserve to be your son anymore.’

## Luke 15:22

“But the father called to his servants.

‘Quick! Bring the best robe and dress him in it.

Put a ring on his finger.

Put sandals on his feet.

**Help with these words**

A robe is a long piece of clothing.

## Luke 15:23

“‘Bring the calf we have been fattening.

Kill it for the meal.

Let us eat and celebrate!

## Luke 15:24

“‘My son was dead and is alive again!

He was lost, and now we have found him!’

So they began to celebrate.

**Help with these words**

The father speaks as if his son had died.

His son was gone from the family.

Now the father has his son home again.

**Pause and answer**

How did the father welcome his son?

## The older son stays outside

## Luke 15:25

“The older son had been out in the field.

As he came near the house, he heard music and dancing.

## Luke 15:26

“He called a servant over.

He asked, ‘What is happening?’

## Luke 15:27

“The servant answered, ‘Your brother came home safe and well.

That is why your father killed the fattened calf.’

## Luke 15:28

“The older son grew angry.

He refused to go inside.

So his father came outside and begged him to come in.

**Pause and answer**

Who went outside to speak to the older son?

## The father answers the older son

## Luke 15:29

“The older son said, ‘I have worked for you all these years.

I have always done what you told me.

You never gave me even a young goat for a meal with my friends.

## Luke 15:30

“‘This son of yours wasted your money on women he paid for sex.

Then he comes home, and you kill the fattened calf for him!’

## Luke 15:31

“The father answered, ‘Son, you are always with me.

Everything I have is yours.

## Luke 15:32

“‘But it was right to celebrate and be glad.

This brother of yours was dead and is alive again.

He was lost, and now we have found him.’”

**Pause and answer**

Why did the father want to celebrate?
