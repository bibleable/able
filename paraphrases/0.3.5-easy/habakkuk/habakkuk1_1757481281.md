# Habakkuk 1 — How long must I call for help?

**About this chapter**

Habakkuk sees people hurting each other and asks God for help. When the LORD tells him about Babylon, Habakkuk has another question.

## Habakkuk cries out to the LORD

## Habakkuk 1:1

Habakkuk was a prophet.

This is the message he received in a vision.

## Habakkuk 1:2

Habakkuk asked, “LORD, how long must I call for help?

You do not seem to hear me.

I cry out, ‘People are hurting each other!’

Why do You not save us?

## Habakkuk 1:3

“Why do You make me watch people do evil?

Why do You let them keep doing wrong?

Everywhere I look, people destroy things and hurt each other.

The fighting and arguing never stop.

## Habakkuk 1:4

“No one makes sure the law is obeyed.

People who do wrong surround those who do right.

Even the decisions that judges make are unfair.”

## The LORD speaks about Babylon

## Habakkuk 1:5

The LORD answered, “Look closely at the nations around you.

You will be amazed.

I am doing something in your lifetime.

Even if someone told you, you would hardly believe it.

## Habakkuk 1:6

“I am raising up the Chaldeans, the people of Babylon.

They are cruel and act in haste.

They march across the earth and seize other people’s homes.

## Habakkuk 1:7

“People tremble in fear of them.

The Chaldeans take power for themselves.

They decide for themselves what is right.

## Habakkuk 1:8

“Their horses run faster than leopards.

They are fiercer than wolves hunting at night.

Their riders charge from far away.

They swoop down like a vulture diving for food.

## Habakkuk 1:9

“They all come to do violence.

Their great crowds sweep forward like an east wind.

They take more prisoners than anyone can count, like grains of sand.

## Habakkuk 1:10

“They laugh at kings and mock rulers.

Even a strong fort makes them laugh.

They heap up earth against its walls and capture it.

## Habakkuk 1:11

“Then they sweep past like the wind.

They are guilty.

They worship their own strength.”

## Habakkuk asks why the LORD is silent

## Habakkuk 1:12

Habakkuk said, “LORD, my God, my Holy One, You have always lived.

We will not die.

You have chosen the Chaldeans to carry out Your judgment.

You are our Rock.

You have given them power to correct us.

## Habakkuk 1:13

“You are too pure to look on evil with approval.

You cannot stand wrongdoing.

Why, then, do You let these people act so falsely?

They destroy people who do more right than they do.

Why do You keep silent?

## People caught like fish

## Habakkuk 1:14

“You have made people like fish in the sea.

They are like little creatures with no ruler to protect them.

## Habakkuk 1:15

“Our enemy is like a fisherman.

He pulls people up with a hook.

He gathers them in his net.

What joy he takes in his catch!

![A fishing net. Habakkuk compares people taken prisoner to fish caught in a net.](../pictures/net.svg)

## Habakkuk 1:16

“He even makes offerings to his net.

He burns sweet-smelling incense to it.

His catch has made him wealthy and given him rich food.

## Habakkuk 1:17

“Will he keep emptying his net to fill it again?

Will he keep killing people of other nations without mercy?”
