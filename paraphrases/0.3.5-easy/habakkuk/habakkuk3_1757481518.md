# Habakkuk 3 — Even then I will rejoice in the LORD

**About this chapter**

Habakkuk sings a prayer to God.

He remembers how God came to save his people.

Habakkuk is afraid, but he still finds joy in God.

## Habakkuk asks the LORD for mercy

## Habakkuk 3:1

This is the prayer of Habakkuk the prophet.

A prophet speaks God’s message to the people.

The song begins with the word “Shigionoth.”

We do not know exactly what that word meant.

## Habakkuk 3:2

LORD, I have heard what you have done.

Your deeds fill me with wonder.

Do those great things again in our lifetime.

Let us see what you can do.

Even when you are angry, please show mercy.

## God comes in glory

## Habakkuk 3:3

God came from Teman.

He is the Holy One who came from Mount Paran.

Here the song says “Selah.”

This word appears three times in the song.

God’s glory filled the sky.

Praise for him filled the earth.

## Habakkuk 3:4

God shone as brightly as the sun.

Light flashed from his hand.

His power lay hidden there.

## Habakkuk 3:5

Deadly sickness went ahead of God.

Fever followed behind him.

Habakkuk pictures sickness moving wherever God goes.

## Habakkuk 3:6

God stood and measured the earth.

At his look the nations shook with fear.

Ancient mountains broke apart.

Hills that seemed to last forever fell down.

God’s ways last forever.

## Habakkuk 3:7

I saw trouble in the tents of Cushan.

The tent curtains of Midian shook.

Even in their homes the people trembled.

## Habakkuk sings about God riding to the rescue

## Habakkuk 3:8

LORD, were you angry with the rivers?

Was it the streams that made you angry?

Did the sea make you furious?

You rode out with your horses and your war chariots.

You rode out to save.

## Habakkuk 3:9

You lifted your bow.

You called for many arrows.

Selah.

You split the earth open with rivers.

## Habakkuk 3:10

The mountains shook when they saw you.

Water rushed past in floods.

The deep sea roared.

Its waves rose high like hands reaching up.

## Habakkuk 3:11

The sun and moon stood still in the sky.

Your arrows flashed as they flew.

Your spear shone bright.

## Habakkuk 3:12

In anger you marched across the earth.

You beat down nations as a farmer beats grain.

## Habakkuk 3:13

You came to save your people.

You came to save the one you chose and anointed.

To anoint someone was to mark them with oil.

You crushed the leader of the wicked people.

You stripped him bare from head to foot.

Selah.

## Habakkuk 3:14

The enemy’s warriors rushed out to scatter us.

They took pleasure in attacking weak people in secret.

You pierced their leader’s head with his own spear.

## Habakkuk 3:15

Your horses trampled through the sea.

They stirred up the deep water.

## Habakkuk waits and trusts God

## Habakkuk 3:16

When I heard this, I shook inside.

My lips trembled at the sound.

My bones felt as if they were rotting away.

I shook where I stood.

Yet I will wait patiently.

Trouble will come to the people who invade us.

## Habakkuk 3:17

The fig trees may have no buds.

The vines may have no grapes.

The olive trees may give no olives.

The fields may grow no food.

Our sheep pens may be empty.

There may be no cattle in our stalls.

## Habakkuk 3:18

Even then I will rejoice in the LORD.

My joy comes from God, who saves me.

## Habakkuk 3:19

The Lord GOD gives me strength.

A deer steps safely on high ground.

God makes my feet steady like a deer’s feet.

He helps me walk on the heights.
