# Habakkuk 2 — Habakkuk waits for the LORD’s answer

**About this chapter**

Habakkuk waits on the city wall for the LORD’s answer.

The LORD tells him to write down what he sees.

The message warns a proud ruler who takes what belongs to others.

## Habakkuk waits for an answer

## Habakkuk 2:1

I will stand at my post on the city wall.

I will watch and wait for God to speak.

If he corrects me, I want to know how to answer.

## Habakkuk 2:2

The LORD answered me, “Write down the vision.

Make the words clear on tablets.

Then a messenger can run with the news.

## Habakkuk 2:3

“The vision tells how things will end.

What it shows is true.

Even if the wait seems long, keep waiting.

It will happen at the time I have set.

## Habakkuk 2:4

“Look at the proud man.

What is in his heart is wrong.

But the person who does right will live by faith.”

Faith means trusting God.

## Wealth cannot satisfy the proud ruler

## Habakkuk 2:5

Wealth lets the proud ruler down.

He never rests.

Like death, he always has room for more.

He takes nation after nation.

He claims all their people as his own.

## Habakkuk 2:6

Those people will mock him with these words.

“How terrible for you! You pile up things that belong to others.

You borrow more and more to make yourself rich.

How long can this go on?”

## Habakkuk 2:7

The people you owe will suddenly rise against you.

Then they will take what you have.

## Habakkuk 2:8

You robbed many nations.

The people who are left will rob you.

You killed people and attacked their land and cities.

You hurt everyone who lived there.

## A stolen fortune cannot keep a family safe

## Habakkuk 2:9

How terrible for the man who cheats to enrich his family!

He wants his home to be like a nest high on a cliff.

He thinks trouble cannot reach him there.

## Habakkuk 2:10

You planned to destroy many peoples.

Your plans brought shame on your own family.

Now you will lose your own life.

## Habakkuk 2:11

The stones in your wall will cry out.

The wooden beams will answer them.

Even your house is pictured as a witness to what you did.

## A city built by violence will not last

## Habakkuk 2:12

How terrible for someone who kills people to build a city!

He founds his town by doing evil.

## Habakkuk 2:13

People wear themselves out building for nothing.

What they build only feeds a fire.

This is what the LORD who commands heaven’s armies has decided.

## Habakkuk 2:14

People everywhere will know the LORD’s glory.

That knowledge will fill the earth as water fills the sea.

## Those who shame others will face shame

## Habakkuk 2:15

How terrible for you when you pour your neighbors wine!

You keep pouring from your wine bag until they are drunk.

You do this so you can look at their naked bodies.

## Habakkuk 2:16

Now shame will take the place of your honor.

You too must drink and expose your uncircumcised body.

The LORD holds a cup in his right hand.

Now it is your turn to drink from it.

The cup pictures his judgment.

Your honor will turn to disgrace.

## Habakkuk 2:17

The violence you did to Lebanon will fall on you.

You will tremble because of the animals you destroyed.

You killed people and attacked their land and cities.

You hurt everyone who lived there.

## An idol cannot answer

## Habakkuk 2:18

What help can a carved idol give?

Its maker trusts something his own hands have made.

The image teaches lies, yet it cannot even speak.

## Habakkuk 2:19

How terrible for someone who tells wood, “Wake up!”

He says to silent stone, “Get up!”

Can that stone tell anyone what to do?

Gold and silver cover it, but it has no breath.

## Habakkuk 2:20

The LORD is in his holy temple.

Let everyone on earth be silent before him.
