# Judges 16 — Samson tells Delilah his secret

**About this chapter**

Delilah asks Samson what makes him strong. Later, in a Philistine temple, Samson asks God for strength again.

## Samson leaves Gaza

## Judges 16:1

One day Samson went to Gaza.

He saw a woman there who was a prostitute.

He went in to spend the night with her.

## Judges 16:2

The people of Gaza heard that Samson was there.

Men gathered near the city gate and waited quietly.

They planned to kill Samson at dawn.

## Judges 16:3

Samson got up at midnight.

He pulled up the city gates, posts, and bar together.

He carried them on his shoulders to a mountaintop overlooking Hebron.

## Delilah asks about Samson’s strength

## Judges 16:4

Later Samson fell in love with Delilah.

She lived in the Valley of Sorek.

## Judges 16:5

The Philistine rulers came to Delilah.

They wanted her to learn why Samson was so strong.

They wanted to capture him and keep him tied up.

Each ruler promised her 1,100 shekels of silver.

**Reading help**

A shekel was a unit of weight.

The rulers were offering Delilah a large payment.

## Judges 16:6

Delilah asked Samson, “What makes you so strong? How could someone tie you up and hold you?”

## Judges 16:7

Samson said that seven fresh bowstrings could hold him.

He said he would be as weak as any other man.

## Judges 16:8

The Philistine rulers brought Delilah seven bowstrings that had never dried.

Delilah tied Samson with them.

## Judges 16:9

Men were hiding in her room.

Delilah called, “Samson, the Philistines are here!” Samson snapped the strings as easily as burned thread.

No one had learned his secret.

## Delilah keeps asking

## Judges 16:10

Delilah said, “You made fun of me.

You lied to me.

Please tell me what could hold you.”

## Judges 16:11

Samson said that new ropes could hold him.

He said ropes that no one had used would make him weak.

## Judges 16:12

Delilah tied Samson with new ropes.

Again men were hiding in her room.

She called, “Samson, the Philistines are here!” Samson broke the ropes like threads.

## Judges 16:13

Delilah said, “You keep making fun of me and telling lies.

Tell me what could hold you.” Samson said he would grow weak if she wove his seven braids into the threads on a loom and fastened them there.

## Judges 16:14

While Samson slept, Delilah wove his braids into the threads.

She fastened them with a pin.

She called, “Samson, the Philistines are here!” He woke and pulled the pin, loom, and threads loose.

**Reading help**

A loom is a frame people use to weave cloth.

## Samson tells the truth

## Judges 16:15

Delilah asked, “How can you say you love me when you keep this from me? Three times you have made fun of me.

You still have not told me what makes you strong.”

## Judges 16:16

Day after day she kept asking him.

At last Samson felt he could bear it no longer.

## Judges 16:17

Samson told her everything.

“No one has ever cut my hair.

Even before my birth, I was a Nazirite set apart for God.

If someone shaves my head, my strength will leave me.

I will be as weak as any other man.”

**Reading help**

A Nazirite was someone set apart to serve God in a special way.

Samson’s uncut hair was a sign that he belonged to God.

## Judges 16:18

Delilah knew Samson had finally told her the truth.

She sent word for the Philistine rulers to return.

They came with the silver.

## Samson loses his strength

## Judges 16:19

Samson fell asleep on Delilah’s lap.

She called a man to shave off Samson’s seven braids.

Delilah began to overpower Samson, and his strength left him.

## Judges 16:20

Delilah called, “Samson, the Philistines are here!” Samson woke up and thought he could break free again.

He did not know the LORD had left him.

## Judges 16:21

The Philistines captured Samson and put out his eyes.

In Gaza they held him in prison with bronze chains.

They made him grind grain.

Point to the grains at the top.

Samson had to crush grain into flour in prison.

## Judges 16:22

But Samson’s hair began to grow again.

## The Philistines gather in their temple

## Judges 16:23

The Philistine rulers gathered to offer a great sacrifice to their god Dagon.

They celebrated and said, “Our god gave us victory over Samson.”

## Judges 16:24

When the people saw Samson, they praised Dagon.

They said, “Our god gave us our enemy.

Samson ruined our land and killed many of our people.”

## Judges 16:25

As they celebrated, they called for Samson to entertain them.

They brought him from prison and made him perform.

They stood him between the pillars of the temple.

## Judges 16:26

A servant held Samson’s hand.

Samson asked the servant to guide him to the pillars.

He said he wanted to feel them and lean against them.

## Judges 16:27

The temple was full of men and women.

All the Philistine rulers were there.

About three thousand people watched Samson from the roof.

## Samson prays one last time

## Judges 16:28

Samson cried out, “Lord GOD, please remember me.

Please give me strength just once more.

I want to pay the Philistines back for my two eyes.”

## Judges 16:29

Samson reached out and took hold of the two pillars beside him.

## Judges 16:30

Samson said, “Let me die with the Philistines.” He pushed with all his strength.

The temple fell on the rulers and the people inside.

More people died with Samson than he had killed during his life.

## Judges 16:31

Samson’s brothers and his father’s family came for his body.

They buried him in the tomb of his father Manoah, between Zorah and Eshtaol.

Samson had led Israel as judge for twenty years.
