# Judges 1 — Israel’s tribes after Joshua dies

**About this chapter**

After Joshua dies, Judah and Simeon help each other. Many tribes settle in places where other peoples still live.

## Judah and Simeon go together

## Judges 1:1

After Joshua died, the Israelites asked the LORD a question.

“Which tribe should go first to fight the Canaanites for us?”

## Judges 1:2

The LORD answered, “Judah will go first.

I have given them the land.”

## Judges 1:3

Judah’s men asked the men of Simeon for help.

“Come with us to the land given to Judah.

Help us fight the Canaanites there.

Then we will help you in your land.” The men of Simeon went with them.

**Pause and answer**

What did Judah promise to do for Simeon?

## Judges 1:4

The LORD gave Judah victory over the Canaanites and Perizzites.

At Bezek the men of Israel killed ten thousand men.

## Adoni-bezek at Bezek

## Judges 1:5

At Bezek they found Adoni-bezek and fought him.

They defeated the Canaanites and Perizzites there.

## Judges 1:6

Adoni-bezek ran away, but the men chased and caught him.

They cut off his thumbs and big toes.

## Judges 1:7

Adoni-bezek said, “Seventy kings picked up scraps under my table.

I had cut off their thumbs and big toes.

Now God has repaid me for what I did.” The men brought Adoni-bezek to Jerusalem, where he died.

## Judges 1:8

Judah’s men fought against Jerusalem and took the city.

They killed people there with swords and burned the city.

## Judah goes to Hebron and Debir

## Judges 1:9

Then Judah’s men went down to fight other Canaanites.

These Canaanites lived in the hills, the Negev, and the lower hills.

## Judges 1:10

Judah fought the Canaanites in Hebron.

Hebron’s earlier name was Kiriath-arba.

Judah struck down Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai.

## Judges 1:11

From Hebron they went to fight the people of Debir.

Debir’s earlier name was Kiriath-sepher.

## Judges 1:12

Caleb promised that the man who took Kiriath-sepher could marry his daughter Acsah.

## Judges 1:13

Othniel took the city.

His father was Kenaz, Caleb’s younger brother.

Caleb gave Acsah to Othniel in marriage.

## Acsah asks for springs

## Judges 1:14

One day Acsah urged Othniel to ask Caleb for a field.

When Acsah got off her donkey, Caleb asked what she wanted.

## Judges 1:15

Acsah said, “Give me a blessing.

You gave me land in the Negev.

Please give me springs of water too.” Caleb gave Acsah both the upper and lower springs.

**Reading help**

The Negev is dry land in the south.

A spring is a place where water flows from the ground.

![Water flows from an upper spring and a lower spring](../pictures/acsah-springs.svg)

Point to the two springs Caleb gave Acsah.

## Judah and Simeon fight at Zephath

## Judges 1:16

Moses’ father-in-law was a Kenite.

His descendants left the City of Palms with Judah’s men.

They went to the Wilderness of Judah near Arad in the Negev.

They settled among the people there.

## Judges 1:17

Judah’s men went with the men of Simeon to Zephath.

They fought the Canaanites there and destroyed the city completely.

After that, people called the city Hormah.

## Judges 1:18

Judah also took Gaza, Ashkelon, and Ekron.

Judah took the land around each city too.

## People in the hills and on the plains

## Judges 1:19

The LORD was with Judah.

Judah took the hill country.

But the people on the plains had iron chariots.

Judah could not drive those people out.

**Reading help**

A chariot was a cart that horses pulled in battle.

A plain is a wide stretch of flat land.

## Judges 1:20

Judah gave Hebron to Caleb, as Moses had promised.

Caleb drove out the families of Anak’s three sons.

## Judges 1:21

Benjamin’s people could not drive the Jebusites out of Jerusalem.

The Jebusites still lived there with Benjamin’s people when this account was written.

## Joseph’s descendants take Bethel

## Judges 1:22

Joseph’s descendants went to fight against Bethel.

The LORD was with them.

## Judges 1:23

They sent men to look at Bethel in secret.

Bethel used to be called Luz.

## Judges 1:24

These men saw a man leaving the city.

They asked him to show them a way in.

They promised to treat him kindly.

## Judges 1:25

The man showed them the entrance.

Joseph’s people killed the people of the city with swords.

They let the man and his whole family go free.

## Judges 1:26

The man went to the land of the Hittites.

He built a city there and called it Luz.

It still had that name when this account was written.

## Canaanites remain in many towns

## Judges 1:27

Manasseh’s people could not drive everyone out of their land.

Canaanites stayed in Beth-shean, Taanach, Dor, Ibleam, Megiddo, and their villages.

They were determined to stay.

## Judges 1:28

Later Israel grew stronger and forced the Canaanites to work.

Israel never drove them all out.

**Reading help**

Forced labor means making people work against their will.

## Judges 1:29

Ephraim’s people did not drive the Canaanites out of Gezer.

Both peoples continued to live in Gezer.

## Judges 1:30

Zebulun’s people did not drive the Canaanites out of Kitron and Nahalol.

The Canaanites stayed, and Zebulun forced them to work.

## Judges 1:31

Asher’s people did not drive out the people of Acco, Sidon, or Ahlab.

People also stayed in Achzib, Helbah, Aphik, and Rehob.

## Judges 1:32

So Asher’s people lived among the Canaanites already there.

## Judges 1:33

Naphtali’s people did not drive out the people of Beth-shemesh and Beth-anath.

Naphtali’s people lived among the Canaanites.

They forced the people of those two towns to work.

## The Amorites keep Dan in the hills

## Judges 1:34

The Amorites forced Dan’s people into the hills.

They kept Dan’s people from coming down to the plain.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the Amorites make Dan’s people stay?

## Judges 1:35

The Amorites were determined to stay in Mount Heres, Aijalon, and Shaalbim.

Later Joseph’s descendants grew stronger.

They forced the Amorites to work for them.

## Judges 1:36

The Amorites’ border ran from the Ascent of Akrabbim to Sela and beyond.
