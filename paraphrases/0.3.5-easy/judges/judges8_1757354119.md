# Judges 8 — Gideon refuses to rule Israel

**About this chapter**

Gideon leads tired men across the Jordan. After the fighting, Israel asks him to become their ruler.

## Gideon answers Ephraim

## Judges 8:1

The men of Ephraim argued angrily with Gideon.

They asked, “Why did you leave us out? Why didn’t you call us when you went to fight Midian?”

## Judges 8:2

Gideon answered, “What have I done compared with you? Even the grapes Ephraim gathers afterward are better than my family’s whole harvest.”

**Reading help**

Gideon belonged to Abiezer’s family.

After a harvest, people gather grapes left on the vines.

Gideon used these grapes to praise what Ephraim did later in the battle.

![Grapes growing on a vine](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

Point to the grapes Gideon used as a picture.

## Judges 8:3

“God let you capture Midian’s two princes, Oreb and Zeeb.

What did I do that was as great as that?” When the men heard Gideon’s answer, their anger eased.

## The tired men ask for bread

## Judges 8:4

Gideon and his three hundred men crossed the Jordan River.

They were very tired but kept chasing the enemy.

## Judges 8:5

Gideon asked the men of Succoth for bread.

“My men are worn out.

We are still chasing Zebah and Zalmunna, the kings of Midian.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did Gideon ask for bread?

## Judges 8:6

The leaders of Succoth answered, “Have you caught those kings yet? Why should we give your army bread?”

## Judges 8:7

Gideon answered, “The LORD will give me those two kings.

Then I will punish you with thorn bushes from the wilderness.”

## Judges 8:8

Gideon went on to Penuel and asked for bread there.

The men of Penuel gave him the same answer.

## Judges 8:9

Gideon told them, “When I return after winning the battle, I will tear down this tower.”

## Gideon captures two kings

## Judges 8:10

Zebah and Zalmunna were at Karkor with about fifteen thousand men.

These were all who remained of the eastern armies.

A hundred and twenty thousand men who carried swords had died.

## Judges 8:11

Gideon traveled along the traders’ road east of Nobah and Jogbehah.

His attack surprised the army.

## Judges 8:12

The two kings of Midian ran away.

Gideon chased and captured Zebah and Zalmunna.

Their whole army scattered.

## Gideon returns to Succoth and Penuel

## Judges 8:13

After the battle Gideon, son of Joash, came back along the road up to Heres.

## Judges 8:14

Gideon captured a young man from Succoth and questioned him.

The young man wrote down the names of Succoth’s leaders and elders.

There were seventy-seven names.

## Judges 8:15

Gideon showed the men of Succoth the two kings.

“Here are Zebah and Zalmunna,” he said.

“You mocked me for asking for bread before I caught them.”

## Judges 8:16

Gideon seized the town elders.

He punished the men of Succoth with thorns from the wilderness.

## Judges 8:17

Gideon tore down the tower of Penuel.

He killed the men of that town.

## Gideon asks about his brothers

## Judges 8:18

Gideon asked the kings about the men they had killed at Tabor.

The kings answered, “They looked like you.

Each one looked like a king’s son.”

## Judges 8:19

Gideon said, “They were my brothers.

We had the same mother.

As surely as the LORD lives, I would have spared you if you had spared them.”

## Judges 8:20

Gideon told his oldest son, Jether, to kill the kings.

Jether was still young and afraid.

He left his sword where it was.

## Judges 8:21

The kings told Gideon to kill them himself.

They said a grown man had a grown man’s strength.

Gideon killed both kings.

He took the curved ornaments from their camels’ necks.

## Israel asks Gideon to rule

## Judges 8:22

The Israelites told Gideon, “You saved us from Midian.

Rule over us.

Let your son and grandson rule after you.”

## Judges 8:23

Gideon answered, “I will not rule over you.

My son will not rule over you.

The LORD will rule over you.”

## Judges 8:24

Gideon asked each man for one earring from the things he had taken.

Their enemies, the Ishmaelites, had worn gold earrings.

## Judges 8:25

The men gladly agreed.

They spread a piece of clothing on the ground.

Each man dropped an earring onto it.

## Israel worships the ephod

## Judges 8:26

The gold earrings weighed 1,700 shekels.

This weight left out the curved ornaments and hanging jewels.

It also left out the kings’ purple clothes and the camels’ neck chains.

**Reading help**

A shekel was a unit of weight.

## Judges 8:27

Gideon used the gold to make an ephod.

He put it in Ophrah, his hometown.

All Israel went there to worship it.

The ephod became a trap for Gideon and his family.

**Reading help**

An ephod was an object used in worship.

Here “a trap” means that worshiping it drew people away from the LORD.

## Judges 8:28

Israel defeated Midian.

Midian did not rise against Israel again.

The land had peace for forty years during Gideon’s lifetime.

## Gideon’s family

## Judges 8:29

Gideon went home and settled there.

He was Joash’s son and was also called Jerubbaal.

## Judges 8:30

Gideon had many wives and seventy sons.

## Judges 8:31

A woman in Shechem was Gideon’s concubine.

She also had a son by Gideon.

Gideon named him Abimelech.

**Reading help**

A concubine was a woman in a lasting relationship with a man.

She had lower status than a wife.

## Judges 8:32

Gideon lived to be very old.

When he died, his family buried him in his father Joash’s tomb.

It was in Ophrah, where Abiezer’s family lived.

## Israel forgets

## Judges 8:33

As soon as Gideon died, Israel turned to other gods.

They worshiped the Baals and chose Baal-berith as their god.

## Judges 8:34

Israel forgot the LORD their God.

He had rescued them from enemies on every side.

## Judges 8:35

Gideon had done many good things for Israel.

Yet the Israelites showed no kindness to his family.

In your own words, how did Israel treat Gideon’s family after he died?
