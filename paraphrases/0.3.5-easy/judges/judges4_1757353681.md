# Judges 4 — Deborah, Barak, and Jael

**About this chapter**

Deborah speaks to Barak for the LORD. Later, Barak meets Jael while searching for Sisera.

## Israel cries out for help

## Judges 4:1

Ehud died.

Then the Israelites again did what the LORD saw as evil.

## Judges 4:2

The LORD gave Jabin power over Israel.

Jabin was king of Canaan and ruled from Hazor.

Sisera led his army.

Sisera lived in Harosheth-hagoyim.

## Judges 4:3

Jabin had nine hundred iron chariots.

He treated the Israelites cruelly for twenty years.

The Israelites cried out to the LORD for help.

**Reading help**

A chariot was a cart that horses pulled in battle.

## The people come to Deborah

## Judges 4:4

Deborah was Israel’s judge at that time.

She was a prophet, someone who spoke God’s message.

Her husband was Lappidoth.

## Judges 4:5

Deborah sat under a tree called the Palm of Deborah.

It stood between Ramah and Bethel in the hills of Ephraim.

Israelites came to her to settle their disagreements.

## Deborah brings Barak a message

## Judges 4:6

Deborah sent for Barak, son of Abinoam.

He came from Kedesh in Naphtali.

Deborah told him what the LORD, Israel’s God, commanded.

Barak must take ten thousand men to Mount Tabor.

They were to come from Naphtali and Zebulun.

## Judges 4:7

The LORD would bring Sisera to the Kishon River with his chariots and soldiers.

The LORD would give Barak victory over Sisera.

## Judges 4:8

Barak told Deborah, “I will go if you come with me.

If you stay here, I will stay too.”

## Judges 4:9

Deborah answered, “I will go with you.

But you will not receive the honor for this.

The LORD will give a woman victory over Sisera.” Then Deborah went with Barak to Kedesh.

**Reading help**

Receiving honor means people give you credit for what happened.

## Judges 4:10

At Kedesh, Barak called the men of Zebulun and Naphtali.

Ten thousand men followed him.

Deborah went with him too.

## Two places near Kedesh and Mount Tabor

## Judges 4:11

Heber the Kenite had moved away from his people.

The Kenites were descendants of Hobab, the father of Moses’ wife.

Heber set up his tent near Kedesh, beside the great tree of Zaanannim.

## Judges 4:12

Sisera heard that Barak, son of Abinoam, had gone up Mount Tabor.

## Judges 4:13

Sisera called his whole army from Harosheth-hagoyim to the Kishon River.

All nine hundred iron chariots came too.

## Sisera’s army loses the battle

## Judges 4:14

Deborah told Barak, “Get up! Today the LORD is giving you victory over Sisera.

The LORD has already gone ahead of you.” Barak went down Mount Tabor.

His ten thousand men followed.

## Judges 4:15

As Barak advanced, the LORD defeated Sisera’s army in battle.

Sisera’s chariot drivers and soldiers fled.

Sisera left his chariot and ran away on foot.

## Judges 4:16

Barak chased the chariots and soldiers to Harosheth-hagoyim.

All the men in Sisera’s army died in battle.

## Sisera comes to Jael’s tent

## Judges 4:17

Sisera ran to Jael’s tent.

Jael was the wife of Heber the Kenite.

Heber’s family was at peace with Jabin, king of Hazor.

## Judges 4:18

Jael went out to meet Sisera.

She invited him inside and told him not to fear.

Sisera entered her tent.

Jael covered him with a blanket.

## Judges 4:19

Sisera was thirsty and asked Jael for water.

Jael opened a container of milk and gave him a drink.

Then she covered him again.

## Judges 4:20

Sisera asked Jael to stand at the tent entrance.

If anyone asked whether a man was inside, she was to say no.

## Judges 4:21

Sisera was tired and fell asleep.

While he slept, Jael killed him with a tent peg.

## Jael shows Barak what happened

## Judges 4:22

Barak came looking for Sisera.

Jael went out and offered to show him the man he wanted.

Barak followed Jael inside and saw Sisera lying dead.

## Judges 4:23

That day God gave Israel victory over Jabin, king of Canaan.

## Judges 4:24

The Israelites grew stronger and stronger.

At last they destroyed King Jabin.
