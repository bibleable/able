# Judges 21 — Israel grieves for Benjamin

**About this chapter**

Six hundred men of Benjamin survived the war. The other tribes fear that Benjamin will disappear. Their leaders make decisions that bring harm to more families.

## Israel remembers a promise

## Judges 21:1

At Mizpah the men of Israel made a promise.

Each man promised, “I will never give my daughter in marriage to a man of Benjamin.”

## Judges 21:2

Now the people went to Bethel.

They sat before God until evening.

They cried out loud.

## Judges 21:3

They asked, “LORD, you are Israel’s God.

Why has this happened to Israel?

Today one of our tribes is missing!”

## Judges 21:4

Early the next day, the people built an altar.

They gave burnt offerings and peace offerings there.

**Reading help**

An altar was a place to give gifts to God.

The people burned a whole animal as a burnt offering.

A peace offering included a meal people shared.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the people sit and cry?

## The people remember another promise

## Judges 21:5

The Israelites asked who had missed the meeting at Mizpah.

They had made another serious promise there.

They would kill anyone who missed that meeting before the LORD.

## Judges 21:6

The Israelites grieved for their relatives from Benjamin.

They said, “Today Israel has lost a tribe.

## Judges 21:7

How can the men who survived find wives?

We made a promise in the LORD’s name.

We cannot give them our daughters as wives.”

## Judges 21:8

They asked again who had missed the meeting.

Nobody from the town of Jabesh-gilead had come.

## Judges 21:9

The Israelites knew this because they counted the people.

No one from Jabesh-gilead was there.

## Israel sends men to Jabesh-gilead

## Judges 21:10

The people sent 12,000 brave men to Jabesh-gilead.

They ordered the men to kill the people there.

That order included women and children.

## Judges 21:11

The order called for the death of every man and boy.

It also called for the death of every woman who had had sex with a man.

## Judges 21:12

The men found 400 young women who had never had sex with a man.

They took these women to the camp at Shiloh.

Shiloh was in the land of Canaan.

## Israel offers peace to Benjamin

## Judges 21:13

The men of Benjamin were at the rock of Rimmon.

The people of Israel sent them a message of peace.

## Judges 21:14

The men of Benjamin came back.

The Israelites gave them the women from Jabesh-gilead as wives.

Some men of Benjamin still had no wives.

## Judges 21:15

The Israelites grieved for Benjamin.

The LORD had left a gap among Israel’s tribes.

**Reading help**

The gap was the place Benjamin had among the tribes.

The Israelites feared the whole tribe would die out.

## Judges 21:16

Israel’s leaders said, “The women of Benjamin are dead.

How can the rest of the men find wives?”

## Judges 21:17

The leaders wanted the men of Benjamin to have children.

Those children could inherit Benjamin’s land.

Then the tribe would live on.

## Judges 21:18

The leaders said, “We cannot give them our daughters.”

The Israelites had called down a curse on anyone who gave a man of Benjamin a wife.

**Reading help**

Here a curse means asking for harm to come to someone.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the leaders want Benjamin to have children?

## Women are taken from Shiloh

## Judges 21:19

The leaders remembered the feast at Shiloh.

People gathered there every year to honor the LORD.

Shiloh was north of Bethel and south of Lebonah.

It lay east of the road from Bethel to Shechem.

![Shiloh lies between Lebonah to the north and Bethel to the south.](../pictures/shiloh-between-two-towns.svg)

## Judges 21:20

The leaders told the men of Benjamin to hide among the grapevines.

## Judges 21:21

The young women of Shiloh would come out to dance.

The leaders told each man to take a woman by force as his wife.

The men would take the women back to Benjamin.

## Judges 21:22

The leaders expected the women’s fathers and brothers to complain.

The leaders would ask the families to accept what happened.

They would say, “We found too few wives during the war.

You did not give these men your daughters.

So you did not break your promise.”

## Judges 21:23

The men of Benjamin did what the leaders said.

They seized women who were dancing and carried them away.

The men went back to their own land.

They rebuilt their towns and lived there.

## The people go home

## Judges 21:24

The other Israelites went home too.

Each went back to his tribe and family.

Each returned to his own land.

## Judges 21:25

In those days Israel had no king.

Each person did what seemed right to that person.

**Pause and answer**

Who decided what seemed right in those days?
