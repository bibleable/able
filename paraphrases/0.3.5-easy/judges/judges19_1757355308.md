# Judges 19 — A woman suffers violence in Gibeah

**About this chapter**

A woman travels from Bethlehem with a Levite and his servant. In Gibeah, men rape her. The account ends with Israel asking how to respond.

## The woman returns to Bethlehem

## Judges 19:1

Israel had no king in those days.

A Levite lived far back in the hills of Ephraim.

A woman from Bethlehem in Judah became his concubine.

**Reading help**

A Levite was a member of Israel’s tribe of Levi.

A concubine was a partner with lower standing than a wife.

## Judges 19:2

She was unfaithful to him and left him.

She went back to her father’s house in Bethlehem.

She stayed there for four months.

## Judges 19:3

Her husband went to speak kindly to her and bring her back.

He took his servant and two donkeys.

The woman brought him into her father’s house.

Her father was glad to welcome him.

## Her father asks the travelers to stay

## Judges 19:4

Her father asked the Levite to stay.

For three days the Levite ate, drank, and slept there.

## Judges 19:5

Early on the fourth day the travelers prepared to leave.

The woman’s father said to the Levite, “Eat some bread for strength.

Then you can go.”

## Judges 19:6

The two men sat down to eat and drink.

Her father asked the Levite to stay another night and enjoy himself.

## Judges 19:7

The Levite got up to leave.

Her father persuaded him to stay that night.

## Judges 19:8

Early on the fifth day the Levite got up to leave.

The woman’s father asked him to eat first.

The men ate together and stayed until late afternoon.

## Judges 19:9

The Levite stood up to leave with the woman and his servant.

Her father said, “The day is almost over.

Stay tonight and enjoy yourself here.

You can start for home early tomorrow.”

## The travelers pass Jebus

## Judges 19:10

This time the Levite would not stay.

They left with the two donkeys saddled.

They came near Jebus, also called Jerusalem.

## Judges 19:11

Daylight was fading near Jebus.

The servant asked the Levite to spend the night in this Jebusite city.

## Judges 19:12

The Levite said, “No Israelites live in that city.

We will go on to Gibeah.”

## Judges 19:13

He said, “Let us try to reach Gibeah or Ramah.

We can spend the night in one of those towns.”

## Judges 19:14

They kept going.

The sun set as they neared Gibeah in Benjamin’s land.

## An old man meets them in Gibeah

## Judges 19:15

They went into Gibeah to spend the night.

The Levite sat in the town square.

No one invited the travelers home.

## Judges 19:16

An old man came back from working in the fields that evening.

He came from the hills of Ephraim.

He now lived in Gibeah among the people of Benjamin.

## Judges 19:17

The old man saw the traveler in the square.

He asked where he had come from and where he was going.

## Judges 19:18

The Levite said, “We came from Bethlehem in Judah.

We are traveling to the hills of Ephraim, where I live.

I am going to the house of the LORD.

No one has invited us in.”

## Judges 19:19

“We have straw and feed for the donkeys.

We have bread and wine for all three of us.

We have all the supplies we need.”

## The old man offers a place to stay

## Judges 19:20

The old man said, “Peace to you.

I will give you whatever you need.

Please do not sleep in the square.”

## Judges 19:21

He took the travelers home and fed their donkeys.

The travelers washed their feet, ate, and drank.

## Men threaten the travelers

## Judges 19:22

While they were enjoying the evening, men surrounded the house.

They pounded on the door.

They demanded that the old man bring out the Levite.

They wanted to rape him.

## Judges 19:23

The old man went outside.

He begged them to stop.

“This man is a guest in my house.

Do not do this terrible thing.”

## Judges 19:24

The old man offered them his own daughter, who was a virgin, and the Levite’s concubine.

He said they could do what they wanted to the women.

He asked them to leave the Levite alone.

## Judges 19:25

The men refused to listen.

The Levite took the woman and sent her outside to them.

The men raped and hurt her throughout the night.

At dawn they let her go.

## The woman returns to the doorway

## Judges 19:26

The woman went back to the house where the Levite was staying.

She fell at the door and lay there until daylight.

## Judges 19:27

In the morning the Levite opened the doors to leave.

The woman lay in the doorway.

Her hands rested on the threshold.

**Reading help**

The threshold is the part of the doorway at ground level.

## Judges 19:28

He told her, “Get up.

Let us go.” She did not answer.

He put her on his donkey and went home.

## People across Israel hear what happened

## Judges 19:29

At home the Levite cut her body into twelve pieces.

He sent the pieces throughout Israel.

## Judges 19:30

Everyone who saw this said, “Nothing like this has happened since Israel left Egypt.

Think about it.

Talk together and say what we should do.”
