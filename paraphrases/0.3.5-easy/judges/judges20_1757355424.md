# Judges 20 — Israel fights the tribe of Benjamin

**About this chapter**

After a woman is killed in Gibeah, the other tribes of Israel ask Benjamin to hand over the men who hurt her. Benjamin refuses. War breaks out within Israel.

## Israel meets at Mizpah

## Judges 20:1

People came from all over Israel, from Dan in the north to Beersheba in the south.

People came from Gilead too.

They met together before the LORD at Mizpah.

## Judges 20:2

The leaders of all the tribes stood before God’s people.

There were 400,000 men on foot carrying swords.

## Judges 20:3

The people of Benjamin heard about the meeting.

At Mizpah the Israelites asked how this terrible thing had happened.

**Reading help**

Benjamin was one of Israel’s tribes. Here the name Israel often means the other tribes together.

## Judges 20:4

The dead woman’s husband was a Levite.

He told them, “We came to Gibeah in the land of Benjamin to spend the night.

## Judges 20:5

Men from the town came around the house that night.

They wanted to kill me.

They raped my partner, and she died.

## Judges 20:6

I cut up her body and sent the pieces throughout Israel.

I wanted you to know about the terrible thing those men had done.

## Judges 20:7

You are the people of Israel.

Tell us what you think we should do.”

**Pause and answer**

## Israel asks Benjamin to hand over the men

## Judges 20:8

Everyone stood up together.

They said, “None of us will go home.

## Judges 20:9

We will draw lots to decide who should go against Gibeah.

**Reading help**

To draw lots was to make a choice by picking a marked object.

## Judges 20:10

One man in every ten will collect food for the army.

The others will go to Gibeah to punish the people for what they did.”

## Judges 20:11

The men of Israel gathered together against Gibeah.

They had all agreed to do this.

## Judges 20:12

The tribes sent messengers throughout Benjamin.

They asked, “How could such an evil thing happen among your people?

## Judges 20:13

Give us the men who did it.

We will put them to death and remove this evil from Israel.”

The people of Benjamin refused to listen to the other Israelites.

**Pause and answer**

How did Benjamin answer the other tribes?

## The armies gather

## Judges 20:14

Men from the towns of Benjamin gathered at Gibeah to fight the other Israelites.

## Judges 20:15

That day 26,000 men with swords came from the towns of Benjamin.

Another 700 men had been chosen from Gibeah.

## Judges 20:16

Among Benjamin’s soldiers were 700 left-handed men.

They could throw a stone with a sling and hit a mark as small as a hair.

## Judges 20:17

The other tribes of Israel gathered 400,000 men.

Each man had a sword and had fought before.

## Judges 20:18

The Israelites went to Bethel to ask God which tribe should fight Benjamin first.

The LORD answered, “Judah will go first.”

## Israel loses many men

## Judges 20:19

The next morning the Israelites went and camped near Gibeah.

## Judges 20:20

The men of Israel got ready to fight the men of Benjamin at Gibeah.

## Judges 20:21

The men of Benjamin came out of Gibeah.

That day they killed 22,000 Israelites.

## Judges 20:22

The army of Israel found the courage to fight again.

The men took their places where they had stood on the first day.

## Judges 20:23

The Israelites wept before the LORD until evening.

They asked, “Should we fight our own brothers from Benjamin again?”

The LORD told them to go.

## Judges 20:24

On the second day the Israelites went to fight Benjamin again.

## Judges 20:25

The men of Benjamin came out of Gibeah.

They killed 18,000 more Israelites.

All those Israelites had carried swords.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the Israelites call the people of Benjamin their brothers?

## The people weep at Bethel

## Judges 20:26

All the people of Israel went to Bethel.

They sat before the LORD and cried.

They went without food until evening.

They gave the LORD burnt offerings and peace offerings.

## Judges 20:27

The Israelites asked the LORD what to do.

In those days the sacred chest called the ark of God’s covenant was at Bethel.

**Reading help**

A covenant is a solemn promise. The chest reminded the people of God’s promise to Israel.

## Judges 20:28

Phinehas the priest served there.

His father was Eleazar, and his grandfather was Aaron.

The people asked, “Should we fight our brothers from Benjamin again, or should we stop?”

The LORD said, “Go and fight.

Tomorrow I will give you victory over them.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the LORD promise the third time?

## The third day at Gibeah

## Judges 20:29

Some of Israel’s men hid near Gibeah.

## Judges 20:30

On the third day the Israelites came back to fight at Gibeah as they had before.

## Judges 20:31

The men of Benjamin came out and fought farther from the town.

They killed about thirty Israelites in the fields and on the roads to Bethel and Gibeah.

## Judges 20:32

The men of Benjamin thought they were winning again.

The Israelites had meant for the men of Benjamin to follow them away from Gibeah.

## Judges 20:33

Israelite soldiers gathered at a place called Baal-tamar.

Other Israelites came out of hiding near Gibeah.

## Judges 20:34

Ten thousand men chosen from Israel attacked Gibeah.

The fighting was fierce.

Benjamin’s men still did not see the danger.

## Judges 20:35

The LORD gave Israel victory over Benjamin.

That day the Israelites killed 25,100 men of Benjamin who carried swords.

## Benjamin sees the smoke

The account now goes back to tell how the men of Benjamin learned they had lost.

## Judges 20:36

Then the men of Benjamin knew they had lost.

Earlier the Israelites had fallen back because they were counting on the men near Gibeah.

## Judges 20:37

Those men rushed into Gibeah.

They killed the people there.

## Judges 20:38

The men of Israel had agreed that smoke rising above Gibeah would be a sign for them.

## Judges 20:39

When they saw it, the Israelites would turn back.

Until then the men of Benjamin thought they were winning, because they had killed about thirty Israelites.

## Judges 20:40

The men of Benjamin looked behind them.

Smoke was rising from the whole town.

## Judges 20:41

The Israelites turned back to face them.

The men of Benjamin were terrified when they saw what was happening.

## Judges 20:42

They ran toward the wilderness.

The fighting followed them there.

Men coming from the towns killed those who were fleeing.

## Judges 20:43

The Israelites surrounded the men of Benjamin.

They chased them and caught up with them east of Gibeah.

## Six hundred men escape

## Judges 20:44

Eighteen thousand men of Benjamin died there.

They had all been brave fighters.

## Judges 20:45

Others ran toward a place in the wilderness called the rock of Rimmon.

Israel’s men killed 5,000 on the roads.

They caught up with the others at Gidom and killed 2,000 more.

## Judges 20:46

About 25,000 men of Benjamin died that day.

They had all been brave men who carried swords.

## Judges 20:47

Six hundred men escaped to the rock of Rimmon.

They stayed there for four months.

## Judges 20:48

The Israelites went back to the towns of Benjamin.

They killed the people and the animals they found.

They burned every town they came to.
