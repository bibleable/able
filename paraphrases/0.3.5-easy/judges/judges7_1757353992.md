# Judges 7 — The LORD saves Israel with three hundred men

**About this chapter**

Gideon has fewer men than before. A dream in Midian’s camp gives him courage.

## Many men go home

## Judges 7:1

Early in the morning, Gideon and his men camped by the spring of Harod.

Gideon also had the name Jerubbaal.

Midian’s camp lay north of them, in the valley near the hill of Moreh.

## Judges 7:2

The LORD told Gideon, “You have too many men.

If I give them victory, Israel might boast.

They might say, ‘We saved ourselves.’”

## Judges 7:3

The LORD told Gideon to make an announcement.

Anyone shaking with fear could leave Mount Gilead.

Twenty-two thousand men went home.

Ten thousand stayed.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the LORD want Gideon to take fewer men?

## The LORD chooses three hundred men

## Judges 7:4

The LORD told Gideon, “There are still too many men.

Take them to the water.

There I will choose who goes with you and who stays behind.”

## Judges 7:5

Gideon led the men to the water.

The LORD told Gideon to put them in two groups.

Some lapped the water with their tongues like dogs.

Others knelt down to drink.

## Judges 7:6

Three hundred men lifted water to their mouths with their hands and lapped it.

All the others knelt down to drink.

## Judges 7:7

The LORD said, “I will use the three hundred who lapped the water.

I will save you and give you victory over Midian.

Send the others home.”

## Judges 7:8

Gideon kept the three hundred and sent the rest back to their tents.

The three hundred took the food and rams’ horns the others had brought.

Below them in the valley lay Midian’s camp.

**Reading help**

A ram is a male sheep.

People blew into a ram’s horn to make a loud sound.

## Gideon and Purah hear a dream

## Judges 7:9

That night the LORD told Gideon to go down against the camp.

The LORD promised him victory.

## Judges 7:10

The LORD knew Gideon might be afraid to go.

He told Gideon he could take his servant Purah with him.

## Judges 7:11

The LORD said, “Listen to what the men are saying.

Then you will have courage.” Gideon and Purah went where the armed guards were at the edge of the camp.

## Judges 7:12

Midian, Amalek, and the peoples from the east filled the valley like a swarm of locusts.

Their camels were as hard to count as grains of sand on a beach.

## Judges 7:13

Gideon heard a man tell his friend a dream.

A round loaf of barley bread rolled into Midian’s camp.

It hit a tent and knocked it over.

The tent fell flat.

![In the dream a loaf of bread rolls toward a tent, and the tent falls flat](../pictures/gideon-bread-dream.svg)

Point to the loaf in the dream.

Then find the fallen tent.

## Judges 7:14

The friend said the dream meant Gideon would win.

“Gideon is an Israelite, the son of Joash.

God has given him victory over Midian and this whole camp.”

**Reading help**

The bread and the tent were pictures in a dream.

The friend understood the fallen tent to mean defeat for Midian.

## Gideon returns to his people

## Judges 7:15

Gideon heard the dream and what it meant.

He bowed down and worshiped God.

Back at Israel’s camp, Gideon told the men to get up.

The LORD had given them victory over Midian.

**Pause and answer**

What did Gideon do when he heard what the dream meant?

## Judges 7:16

Gideon divided the three hundred men into three groups.

Each man received a ram’s horn and a large jar with a torch inside.

## Judges 7:17

Gideon told his men to watch him.

At the edge of the camp, they would do what he did.

## Judges 7:18

Gideon told them all to sound their horns with his group.

Their shout would be, “For the LORD and for Gideon!”

## The horns sound

## Judges 7:19

Gideon and the hundred men with him reached the camp during the night.

They blew their horns and broke their jars.

## Judges 7:20

All three groups sounded their horns and broke their jars.

With torches shining and horns sounding, the men shouted, “A sword for the LORD and for Gideon!”

## Judges 7:21

The Israelites stood in place around the camp.

The whole Midianite army cried out and ran away.

## Judges 7:22

As the three hundred horns sounded, the LORD turned the men in Midian’s camp against each other.

They fought each other with swords.

The army fled to Beth-shittah toward Zererah.

They reached the edge of Abel-meholah, near Tabbath.

## Other tribes join Israel

## Judges 7:23

Men from Naphtali, Asher, and all of Manasseh answered the call.

They chased the Midianites.

## Judges 7:24

Gideon sent messengers through Ephraim’s hills to ask for help.

Ephraim’s men came down and took control of the Jordan River crossings as far as Beth-barah.

## Judges 7:25

They captured two Midianite princes, Oreb and Zeeb.

They killed Oreb at the rock of Oreb and Zeeb at the winepress of Zeeb.

They kept chasing the Midianites.

They brought the princes’ heads to Gideon across the Jordan.

**Reading help**

A winepress was a place where people crushed grapes.
