# Judges 10 — Israel turns back to the LORD

**About this chapter**

Tola and Jair lead Israel. Later the people worship other gods, then ask the LORD for help.

## Tola leads Israel

## Judges 10:1

After Abimelech’s time, Tola came to rescue Israel.

Tola belonged to the tribe of Issachar.

His father was Puah, and his grandfather was Dodo.

Tola lived in Shamir in the hills of Ephraim.

## Judges 10:2

Tola led Israel as judge for twenty-three years.

When he died, the people buried him in Shamir.

**Reading help**

Here a judge is a leader who helps Israel.

## Jair leads Israel

## Judges 10:3

Jair from Gilead became judge after Tola.

Jair led Israel for twenty-two years.

## Judges 10:4

Jair had thirty sons.

They rode thirty donkeys and had thirty towns in Gilead.

People still called those towns Havvoth-jair when this account was written.

## Judges 10:5

When Jair died, the people buried him in Kamon.

## Israel serves other gods

## Judges 10:6

Again the LORD saw Israel doing evil.

The Israelites served the Baals and the Ashtoreths.

They worshiped the gods of Aram, Sidon, and Moab.

They also worshiped the gods of the Ammonites and Philistines.

They left the LORD and stopped serving him.

## Judges 10:7

The LORD was very angry with Israel.

He gave the Philistines and Ammonites power over them.

**Reading help**

The account says the LORD’s anger burned.

That means he was very angry.

## Judges 10:8

That very year these enemies began treating Israel cruelly.

For eighteen years all the Israelites in Gilead suffered.

Gilead was east of the Jordan River, in the land of the Amorites.

## Judges 10:9

The Ammonites also crossed the Jordan.

They fought Judah, Benjamin, and the people of Ephraim.

The Israelites were in great trouble.

## Israel asks the LORD for help

## Judges 10:10

The Israelites cried to the LORD for help.

“We have sinned against you.

We left you, our God, to serve the Baals.”

## Judges 10:11

The LORD reminded them of the Egyptians, Amorites, Ammonites, and Philistines who had harmed them.

## Judges 10:12

The Sidonians, Amalekites, and Maonites had also treated them cruelly.

The LORD said, “You cried out to me.

Didn’t I rescue you from them?”

## Judges 10:13

“But you left me and served other gods.

So I will no longer rescue you.”

## Judges 10:14

“Go and call out to the gods you chose.

Let them rescue you now that you are in trouble.”

## The people change what they do

## Judges 10:15

The Israelites told the LORD, “We have sinned.

Do what you think is right with us.

But please rescue us today!”

## Judges 10:16

The Israelites got rid of the foreign gods among them.

They began to serve the LORD again.

The LORD could no longer bear to see Israel suffer.

## Gilead needs a leader

## Judges 10:17

The Ammonites gathered for war and camped in Gilead.

The Israelites gathered and camped at Mizpah.

## Judges 10:18

The leaders of Gilead spoke to one another.

They agreed that whoever first led the fight against the Ammonites would lead everyone in Gilead.
