# Judges 18 — Dan takes Micah’s idols and priest

**About this chapter**

The tribe of Dan looks for a place to live. On the way, the people meet the priest who lives with Micah.

## Five men look for land

## Judges 18:1

Israel had no king in those days.

The tribe of Dan was looking for land to live in.

Dan had not yet taken possession of its share of Israel’s land.

## Judges 18:2

Dan’s families chose five brave men from Zorah and Eshtaol.

They sent them to explore the land.

The five went into the hills of Ephraim.

They spent the night at Micah’s house.

## Judges 18:3

There they recognized the young Levite’s voice.

They went to him and asked, “Who brought you here? Why are you here? What work do you do?”

## Judges 18:4

The young man told them what Micah had done for him.

He said, “Micah hired me to be his priest.”

## The five men ask about their journey

## Judges 18:5

The five men asked him, “Please ask God about our journey.

Will it go well?”

## Judges 18:6

The priest said, “Go in peace.

The LORD is watching over your journey.”

## Judges 18:7

The five men reached Laish.

Like the Sidonians, the people there lived quietly and felt safe.

They had all they needed.

No cruel ruler oppressed them.

Sidon was far away, and they had no allies.

**Reading help**

Allies are people who have agreed to help each other.

## The men report what they found

## Judges 18:8

The men returned to Zorah and Eshtaol.

Their relatives asked what they had found.

## Judges 18:9

The five men said, “We saw a very good land.

Why wait? Let us go and take it.”

## Judges 18:10

“The people there expect no trouble.

There is plenty of room.

God has given you this land.

It has everything you need.”

## Dan’s people come to Micah’s house

## Judges 18:11

Six hundred men of Dan left Zorah and Eshtaol.

They carried weapons for battle.

## Judges 18:12

They camped near Kiriath-jearim in Judah.

The place west of that city became known as Mahaneh-dan.

It still had that name when this account was written.

## Judges 18:13

From there they went into the hills of Ephraim.

They came to Micah’s house.

## Judges 18:14

The five who had explored Laish spoke to their relatives.

“One house here has an ephod and household gods.

It also has a carved image and a metal idol.

Think about what to do.”

**Reading help**

An ephod was an object used in worship.

An idol is an object people worship.

## Judges 18:15

They left the road and went to Micah’s house.

The young Levite lived there, and they greeted him.

## The priest leaves with them

## Judges 18:16

The six hundred armed men of Dan stood at the gate.

## Judges 18:17

The priest stood with the men at the gate.

The five who had explored the land went inside.

They took the carved image, ephod, household gods, and metal idol.

## Judges 18:18

As the men took these things from Micah’s house, the priest spoke.

“What are you doing?” he asked.

## Judges 18:19

They told him, “Be quiet and come with us.

Be a father and a priest to us.

Would you rather serve one household or a whole tribe of Israel?”

## Judges 18:20

The priest was glad.

He took the ephod, the household gods, and the carved image.

He went with the people of Dan.

## Judges 18:21

The people set out again.

Their small children, animals, and belongings went ahead of them.

## Micah follows the people of Dan

## Judges 18:22

By then they were some distance from Micah’s house.

Micah’s neighbors gathered and caught up with the people of Dan.

## Judges 18:23

Micah and his neighbors called out.

The people of Dan turned around.

They asked Micah, “What is wrong? Why have you brought all these people?”

## Judges 18:24

Micah replied, “You took the gods I made.

You took my priest too.

What do I have left? How can you ask what is wrong?”

## Judges 18:25

The men of Dan warned Micah to stop shouting.

They said angry men might attack him.

Micah and his family could all die.

## Judges 18:26

Dan’s people went on.

Micah saw they were too strong for him.

He turned back and went home.

## Laish gets a new name

## Judges 18:27

The people of Dan took Micah’s idols and his priest to Laish.

The people there lived quietly and expected no attack.

Dan’s men killed people with their swords and burned the city.

## Judges 18:28

Laish lay in a valley near Beth-rehob.

Sidon was far away.

The people of Laish had no allies to rescue them.

Dan’s people rebuilt the city and lived there.

## Judges 18:29

The city once called Laish now had a new name, Dan.

The tribe named it after their ancestor Dan, a son of Israel.

## The carved image stays in Dan

## Judges 18:30

The people of Dan set up idols for themselves.

Jonathan was the son of Gershom and the grandson of Moses.

Jonathan and his sons served as priests for Dan until enemies took the people of the land captive.

## Judges 18:31

Dan’s people set up the carved image Micah had made.

It stayed there as long as God’s house was in Shiloh.
