# Judges 14 — Samson tells a riddle

**About this chapter**

Samson tells a riddle at his wedding feast. The answer comes from something he saw on the way to Timnah.

## Samson wants to marry

## Judges 14:1

Samson went down to Timnah.

He saw a young Philistine woman there.

## Judges 14:2

Samson went home and told his parents about her.

He asked them to arrange for him to marry her.

## Judges 14:3

His parents asked, “Could you marry someone from our family or our people? Why choose a wife from the uncircumcised Philistines?” Samson told his father, “Please arrange it.

She is the woman I want.”

**Reading help**

Circumcision was a sign of God’s covenant with Israel.

Samson’s parents used this word to speak about the difference between their people and the Philistines.

## Judges 14:4

His parents did not know the LORD was behind this.

The Philistines ruled Israel at that time.

The LORD was looking for a chance to act against them.

## A lion near Timnah

## Judges 14:5

Samson and his parents went down to Timnah.

Near the fields where grapes grew, a young lion rushed at Samson.

It roared.

## Judges 14:6

The Spirit of the LORD gave Samson great strength.

With his bare hands, Samson tore the lion apart as easily as a young goat.

Samson kept this from his parents.

## Judges 14:7

Samson continued to Timnah and spoke with the woman.

He liked her.

## Samson finds honey

## Judges 14:8

Later Samson came back to marry her.

He turned off the road to see the dead lion.

Inside its body he found a swarm of bees and their honey.

## Judges 14:9

Samson scooped up honey and ate it as he walked.

He gave some to his parents, and they ate it.

He kept quiet about where the honey came from.

## A riddle at the wedding feast

## Judges 14:10

Samson’s father went to see the woman.

Samson prepared a wedding feast there.

That was the custom for a man getting married.

## Judges 14:11

When the Philistines saw Samson, they chose thirty men to keep him company.

## Judges 14:12

Samson said, “Let me tell you a riddle.

You have the seven days of the feast to solve it.

If you do, I will give you thirty linen garments and thirty sets of clothes.”

**Reading help**

A riddle is a puzzle told in words.

Linen is a kind of cloth.

## Judges 14:13

“If you cannot solve it, you must give me those things.” The men answered, “Let us hear your riddle.”

## Judges 14:14

Samson said, “From the eater came something to eat.

From the strong came something sweet.” For three days the men tried to explain it.

They could not.

## The men threaten Samson’s wife

## Judges 14:15

On the fourth day the men threatened Samson’s wife.

They told her to get the answer from Samson.

Otherwise they would burn her and her father’s family to death.

They asked, “Did you invite us here to rob us?”

## Judges 14:16

Samson’s wife cried and told him, “You hate me! If you loved me, you would explain the riddle you told my people.” Samson answered, “I have kept it even from my parents.

Why should I tell you?”

## Judges 14:17

Samson’s wife cried during the seven days of the feast.

She kept asking until Samson told her on the seventh day.

Then she told her people the answer.

## The men give their answer

## Judges 14:18

Before sunset that day the men answered Samson.

“What is sweeter than honey? What is stronger than a lion?” Samson said, “You plowed with my young cow.

That is how you solved my riddle!”

**Reading help**

The lion was the strong eater.

The honey inside it was the sweet food.

Samson compared his wife to a young cow pulling a plow.

He meant the men had used her to get the answer.

## Judges 14:19

The Spirit of the LORD came on Samson with power.

Samson went to Ashkelon and killed thirty men there.

He took their clothes and gave them to the men who had answered the riddle.

Still angry, Samson went back to his father’s house.

## Judges 14:20

One of Samson’s wedding companions became the woman’s husband.
