# Judges 13 — An angel announces Samson’s birth

**About this chapter**

A visitor tells Manoah’s wife she will have a son. She and Manoah learn what God has planned for him.

## A message for Manoah’s wife

## Judges 13:1

Again Israel did what the LORD saw as evil.

So the LORD let the Philistines rule Israel for forty years.

## Judges 13:2

Manoah lived in Zorah and belonged to the tribe of Dan.

His wife had no children.

She had been unable to have a child.

## Judges 13:3

The angel of the LORD appeared to Manoah’s wife.

He said, “You have been unable to have children.

But you will become pregnant and have a son.”

## Judges 13:4

“Take care to avoid wine and other strong drinks.

Eat no food that God’s law calls unclean.”

## Judges 13:5

“You will have a son.

No one is to shave his head.

Even before birth, the boy will be a Nazirite dedicated to God.

He will begin to free Israel from the Philistines.”

**Reading help**

A Nazirite was a person set apart to serve God in a special way.

Leaving the hair uncut was one sign of this.

## She tells Manoah

## Judges 13:6

The woman told Manoah, “A man of God came to me.

He looked like an angel of God.

The sight filled me with awe.

I did not ask where he came from.

He did not tell me his name.”

## Judges 13:7

“He said I would become pregnant and have a son.

He told me to avoid wine and strong drinks.

I must eat no food that God’s law calls unclean.

Our boy will be a Nazirite dedicated to God from before birth until he dies.”

## The visitor returns

## Judges 13:8

Manoah prayed, “Lord, please send the man of God back to us.

Let him teach us how to raise our son.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Manoah want the visitor to teach them?

## Judges 13:9

God heard Manoah’s prayer.

The angel came back while the woman sat in a field.

Manoah was elsewhere.

## Judges 13:10

She ran to tell Manoah.

“The man who came the other day is here again!”

## Judges 13:11

Manoah followed his wife to the man.

Manoah asked, “Did you speak to my wife?” The man answered, “Yes, I did.”

## Manoah asks about the boy

## Judges 13:12

Manoah asked, “When what you say comes true, how should the boy live? What work should he do?”

## Judges 13:13

The angel of the LORD answered Manoah.

“Your wife must follow all the instructions I gave her.”

## Judges 13:14

“She must eat nothing that grows on a grapevine.

She must avoid wine and strong drinks.

She must eat no food that God’s law calls unclean.

She must do everything I told her.”

![Grapes growing on a vine](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

Point to the fruit that the angel told Manoah’s wife to avoid.

## Manoah offers a meal

## Judges 13:15

Manoah said to the angel, “Please stay.

We will prepare a young goat for you to eat.”

## Judges 13:16

The angel said, “Even if I stay, I will not eat your food.

If you give a burnt offering, give it to the LORD.” Manoah still did not know who the visitor was.

**Reading help**

A burnt offering was a gift burned on an altar for God.

## Judges 13:17

Manoah asked the visitor his name.

“When your words come true, we want to honor you.”

## Judges 13:18

The angel answered, “Why ask my name? It is too wonderful for you to understand.”

## The angel goes up in the flame

## Judges 13:19

Manoah took a young goat and a gift of grain.

He offered them to the LORD on a rock.

As Manoah and his wife watched, the LORD did something wonderful.

## Judges 13:20

A flame rose from the altar toward the sky.

The angel of the LORD went up in the flame.

Manoah and his wife saw it happen.

They fell with their faces to the ground.

## Judges 13:21

The visitor did not appear to them again.

Then Manoah understood.

Their visitor had been the angel of the LORD.

## Judges 13:22

Manoah told his wife, “We have seen God! We are going to die!”

## Judges 13:23

His wife answered, “If the LORD meant to kill us, why would he accept our gifts? He accepted the burnt offering and the grain.

He showed us all these things.

He even spoke to us this way.”

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, why did Manoah’s wife believe they would live?

## Samson is born

## Judges 13:24

The woman had a son.

She named him Samson.

Samson grew, and the LORD blessed him.

## Judges 13:25

At Mahaneh-dan the Spirit of the LORD began to move Samson to act.

This place lay between Zorah and Eshtaol.
