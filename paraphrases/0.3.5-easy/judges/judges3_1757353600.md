# Judges 3 — Othniel, Ehud, and Shamgar rescue Israel

**About this chapter**

Israel calls to the LORD for help. Three leaders rescue the people at different times.

## Nations remain in Canaan

## Judges 3:1

Some Israelites had never fought in the wars in Canaan.

The LORD left some nations there to test those Israelites.

## Judges 3:2

Later generations of Israelites would learn what war was like.

This especially mattered for those who had never fought.

## Judges 3:3

The Philistines with their five rulers remained.

So did all the Canaanites and the Sidonians.

The Hivites lived in the mountains of Lebanon.

Their land stretched from Mount Baal-hermon to Lebo-hamath.

## Judges 3:4

The LORD had given Israel’s ancestors commands through Moses.

With these nations still there, would Israel obey those commands?

## Judges 3:5

The Israelites lived among the Canaanites, Hittites, and Amorites.

They also lived among the Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.

## Judges 3:6

Israelite men married women from those peoples.

Israelite families gave their daughters in marriage to the men there.

The Israelites began to serve the gods of those peoples.

## Othniel rescues Israel

## Judges 3:7

The LORD saw the evil Israel did.

The people forgot the LORD their God.

They worshiped the Baals and the Asherahs.

**Reading help**

The Baals and the Asherahs were gods worshiped by Israel’s neighbors.

## Judges 3:8

The LORD was very angry with Israel.

He gave Cushan-rishathaim, king of Aram-naharaim, power over Israel.

Israel served that king for eight years.

## Judges 3:9

The Israelites cried to the LORD for help.

The LORD chose Othniel to rescue them.

Othniel’s father was Kenaz, Caleb’s younger brother.

## Judges 3:10

The Spirit of the LORD came upon Othniel.

Othniel became Israel’s judge and went to war.

The LORD gave Othniel victory over Cushan-rishathaim, king of Aram.

**Reading help**

Here a judge is a leader God chooses to help Israel.

## Judges 3:11

Israel had peace for forty years.

Then Othniel, son of Kenaz, died.

## Eglon rules over Israel

## Judges 3:12

Again the LORD saw Israel doing evil.

Because of this, he gave Eglon power over Israel.

Eglon was king of Moab.

## Judges 3:13

Eglon joined forces with the Ammonites and Amalekites.

Together they defeated Israel and took the City of Palms.

## Judges 3:14

Israel served Eglon, king of Moab, for eighteen years.

## Judges 3:15

Again Israel cried to the LORD for help.

This time the LORD chose Ehud to rescue them.

Ehud was left-handed and came from Benjamin’s tribe.

His father was Gera.

Israel sent Ehud to take tribute to King Eglon.

**Reading help**

Tribute is a payment people give to a ruler who has power over them.

## Ehud visits King Eglon

## Judges 3:16

Ehud took along a short sword he had made.

It had two sharp edges.

## Judges 3:17

Ehud presented the tribute to Eglon, king of Moab.

Eglon was a very heavy man.

## Judges 3:18

When Ehud finished, he sent the tribute carriers away.

## Judges 3:19

Near Gilgal, Ehud reached a place where idols stood.

He turned back and told the king he had a secret message.

The king called for silence.

His attendants left.

## Judges 3:20

Eglon was sitting in his cool upstairs room.

Ehud said, “I have a message from God for you.” Eglon stood up.

## Eglon dies

## Judges 3:21

Ehud struck Eglon with his sword.

## Judges 3:22

The sword stayed in Eglon’s body.

Eglon lost control of his bowels.

## Judges 3:23

Ehud left the upstairs room.

Its doors were locked behind him.

## Judges 3:24

Eglon’s servants found the doors locked.

They thought the king wanted privacy to use the toilet.

## Judges 3:25

The servants waited and grew worried.

At last they opened the doors with a key.

They found their king dead on the floor.

## Judges 3:26

While the servants waited, Ehud got away.

He passed the idols and reached Seirah.

## Israel follows Ehud

## Judges 3:27

Ehud blew a ram’s horn in the hills of Ephraim.

The Israelites came down from the hills with him.

Ehud led them.

## Judges 3:28

Ehud said, “Follow me.

The LORD has given you victory over Moab.” Israel followed Ehud to the Jordan River.

They took control of the crossings that led to Moab.

They let no one cross.

## Judges 3:29

Israel killed about ten thousand Moabites.

These were strong, brave men.

None escaped.

## Judges 3:30

That day Israel defeated Moab.

Then Israel had peace for eighty years.

## Shamgar rescues Israel

## Judges 3:31

After Ehud came Shamgar, son of Anath.

Shamgar struck down six hundred Philistines with an oxgoad.

He too rescued Israel.

**Reading help**

An oxgoad was a stick farmers used to guide oxen.
