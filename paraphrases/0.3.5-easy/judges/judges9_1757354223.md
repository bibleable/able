# Judges 9 — Abimelech becomes king at Shechem

**About this chapter**

Shechem makes Abimelech king. His brother Jotham tells a story to warn the people about their choice.

## Abimelech asks his relatives for help

## Judges 9:1

Abimelech went to Shechem to see his mother’s brothers.

He spoke to them and the rest of her family.

His father was Gideon, also called Jerubbaal.

## Judges 9:2

Abimelech asked them to speak to Shechem’s leaders.

“Would you prefer one ruler or all seventy sons of Gideon? Remember, I am your relative.”

## Judges 9:3

His uncles gave the leaders his message.

The leaders wanted Abimelech to rule.

“He is one of our family,” they said.

## Judges 9:4

They gave him silver from the temple of Baal-berith.

The silver weighed seventy shekels.

Abimelech used the silver to hire reckless men who would follow him.

## Jotham survives

## Judges 9:5

Abimelech went to his father’s home in Ophrah.

There he murdered his brothers, Gideon’s seventy sons, on one stone.

But Jotham, the youngest, hid and survived.

## Judges 9:6

The leaders of Shechem and Beth-millo gathered at Shechem.

Beside the oak tree near the pillar, they made Abimelech king.

## Judges 9:7

Jotham heard what had happened.

From Mount Gerizim he called out to Shechem’s leaders.

“Listen to me, and may God listen to you.”

## Jotham tells a story about trees

## Judges 9:8

Jotham told them a story.

The trees wanted to choose a king.

They asked the olive tree to rule them.

## Judges 9:9

The olive tree answered, “My oil honors God and people.

Should I stop giving oil to rule the trees?”

## Judges 9:10

Next the trees asked the fig tree to rule them.

## Judges 9:11

The fig tree answered, “Should I stop growing sweet, good fruit to rule the trees?”

## Judges 9:12

Then the trees asked the grapevine to rule them.

## Judges 9:13

The grapevine answered, “My wine brings joy to God and people.

Should I stop giving wine to rule the trees?”

![Grapes growing on a vine](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

Point to the fruit the vine wanted to keep giving.

## Judges 9:14

At last all the trees asked the thornbush to rule them.

## Judges 9:15

The thornbush answered, “If you truly want me as king, come into my shade for safety.

If you do not, may fire come from me.

May it burn up the tall cedars of Lebanon.”

**Reading help**

The trees speak because Jotham is telling a story.

The thornbush offers shelter, then threatens to burn the trees.

**Pause and answer**

Which plant agreed to rule?

## Jotham explains his warning

## Judges 9:16

Jotham asked the leaders, “Were you honest and faithful when you made Abimelech king? Did you treat my father and his family as they deserved?”

## Judges 9:17

“My father fought for you.

He risked his life to free you from Midian.”

## Judges 9:18

“But you turned against his family.

You killed his seventy sons on one stone.

You chose Abimelech, his servant woman’s son, as your king because he is your relative.”

## Judges 9:19

“If you treated my father’s family honestly, may you find joy in Abimelech.

May he find joy in you.”

## Judges 9:20

“But if you did wrong, may fire come from Abimelech and destroy the leaders of Shechem and Beth-millo.

May fire come from those leaders and destroy Abimelech too.”

**Reading help**

The fire in Jotham’s warning pictures the harm Abimelech and the leaders would do to each other.

## Judges 9:21

Jotham feared his brother Abimelech.

He escaped to Beer and lived there.

## Shechem turns against its king

## Judges 9:22

Abimelech ruled Israel for three years.

## Judges 9:23

Then God sent a spirit that stirred up hatred between Abimelech and Shechem’s leaders.

The leaders betrayed Abimelech.

## Judges 9:24

Abimelech had murdered Gideon’s seventy sons.

Shechem’s leaders had helped him.

God would hold both Abimelech and the leaders responsible for those deaths.

## Judges 9:25

Shechem’s leaders set men against Abimelech in the hills.

Those men robbed people traveling on the road.

Word reached Abimelech.

## Gaal speaks at a feast

## Judges 9:26

Gaal, son of Ebed, came to Shechem with his brothers.

Shechem’s leaders put their trust in Gaal.

## Judges 9:27

The people picked grapes and pressed out the juice with their feet.

They held a feast in their god’s temple.

As they ate and drank, they cursed Abimelech.

## Judges 9:28

Gaal asked, “Why should Shechem serve Abimelech? He is Gideon’s son, and Zebul is his officer.

Serve the people of Hamor, the father of Shechem.

Why serve Abimelech?”

## Judges 9:29

Gaal wished he could lead the people.

He said he would remove Abimelech and challenge his army to fight.

## Zebul sends word to Abimelech

## Judges 9:30

Zebul, the city’s governor, heard what Gaal said.

Zebul was furious.

## Judges 9:31

Zebul secretly sent word to Abimelech.

Gaal and his brothers were turning the city against him.

## Judges 9:32

Zebul wanted Abimelech and his men to come that night.

They were to wait outside the city.

## Judges 9:33

Zebul expected Gaal to come out in the morning.

He urged Abimelech to fight Gaal then.

## Judges 9:34

Abimelech came by night with his army in four groups.

They waited outside Shechem.

## Gaal sees men on the mountains

## Judges 9:35

Gaal stood at the city gate.

Abimelech and his men came into view.

## Judges 9:36

Gaal told Zebul, “Look! People are coming down the mountains.” Zebul said Gaal was seeing shadows that looked like people.

## Judges 9:37

Gaal looked again.

People were coming down from the middle of the land.

Another group was coming from the Diviners’ Oak.

## Judges 9:38

Zebul said, “What happened to your boasting? You asked why anyone should serve Abimelech.

Here are the men you mocked.

Go and fight them!”

## Gaal flees

## Judges 9:39

Gaal led Shechem’s leaders out to fight Abimelech.

## Judges 9:40

Abimelech chased Gaal, and Gaal ran away.

Many people from Shechem lay wounded along the way to the city gate.

## Judges 9:41

Abimelech stayed in Arumah.

Zebul forced Gaal and his brothers to leave Shechem.

## Abimelech destroys Shechem

## Judges 9:42

The next day people from Shechem went into the fields.

Abimelech heard about it.

## Judges 9:43

Abimelech divided his men into three groups.

They attacked the people who had come out of the city.

## Judges 9:44

Abimelech and some of his men reached the city gate.

The other two groups killed the people in the fields.

## Judges 9:45

Abimelech fought all day and took the city.

He killed its people, tore down the city, and scattered salt over it.

## The people in Shechem’s tower die

## Judges 9:46

The leaders in Shechem’s tower heard the news.

They went into an inner room of the temple of El-berith.

## Judges 9:47

Abimelech learned that the leaders from the tower had gathered there.

## Judges 9:48

Abimelech and his men went up Mount Zalmon.

He cut a tree branch and carried it on his shoulder.

He told his men to follow his example.

## Judges 9:49

The men followed him with branches.

They set the place on fire.

About a thousand men and women from Shechem’s tower died there.

## Abimelech dies at Thebez

## Judges 9:50

Next Abimelech camped outside Thebez and captured it.

## Judges 9:51

Thebez had a strong tower.

The people and their leaders ran inside.

They locked the door and went up to the roof.

## Judges 9:52

Abimelech came to the tower.

He intended to burn it.

## Judges 9:53

A woman dropped a millstone.

It struck Abimelech’s head and broke his skull.

**Reading help**

A millstone is a heavy stone used to grind grain.

## Judges 9:54

Abimelech called the man who carried his armor.

He feared people would say a woman had killed him.

At his command, the man killed him with a sword.

## Judges 9:55

When the Israelites saw that Abimelech was dead, they went home.

## Jotham’s words come true

## Judges 9:56

In this way God repaid Abimelech for the evil he had done to his father.

Abimelech had murdered his seventy brothers.

## Judges 9:57

God also made the people of Shechem face the harm they had done.

Jotham’s words against them came true.

In your own words, how did things end for Abimelech and Shechem?
