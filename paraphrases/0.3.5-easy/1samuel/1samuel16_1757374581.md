# 1 Samuel 16 — The LORD chooses David

**About this chapter**

Samuel goes to Bethlehem to meet Jesse’s sons. God chooses the youngest son, David. Later Saul sends for David to play music for him.

## Samuel goes to Bethlehem

## 1 Samuel 16:1

The LORD asked Samuel, “How long will you grieve for Saul?

I have rejected him as king of Israel.

Fill your horn with oil.

Go to Jesse in Bethlehem.

I have chosen one of his sons to be king.”

## 1 Samuel 16:2

Samuel asked, “How can I go?

If Saul hears about it, he will kill me.”

The LORD answered, “Take a young cow with you.

Tell the people you have come to offer it to the LORD.

## 1 Samuel 16:3

Invite Jesse to the sacrifice.

I will show you what to do.

Pour oil on the man I point out to you.”

**Reading help**

Samuel carried oil in a hollow animal horn. Pouring oil on someone to show God had chosen him was called anointing.

## 1 Samuel 16:4

Samuel obeyed the LORD and went to Bethlehem.

The town leaders shook with fear when they met him.

They asked, “Have you come in peace?”

## 1 Samuel 16:5

Samuel said, “Yes, I come in peace.

I have come to offer a sacrifice to the LORD.

Prepare yourselves for worship and come with me.”

Samuel helped Jesse and his sons prepare for worship.

He invited them to the sacrifice.

## Samuel meets seven of Jesse’s sons

## 1 Samuel 16:6

When they arrived, Samuel saw Jesse’s son Eliab.

Samuel thought, “Surely this is the man the LORD has chosen.”

## 1 Samuel 16:7

The LORD told Samuel, “Do not judge by his looks or his height.

I have not chosen him.

People see what a person looks like.

I see the heart.”

**Reading help**

Here “the heart” means who someone is on the inside. The LORD knows what a person wants and cares about.

## 1 Samuel 16:8

Jesse called his son Abinadab to stand before Samuel.

Samuel said, “The LORD has not chosen this one either.”

## 1 Samuel 16:9

Then Jesse brought his son Shammah.

Samuel said, “The LORD has not chosen this one either.”

## 1 Samuel 16:10

Jesse brought seven sons to stand before Samuel.

Samuel said, “The LORD has chosen none of these men.”

**Pause and answer**

What could the LORD see that Samuel could not see?

## Jesse sends for his youngest son

## 1 Samuel 16:11

Samuel asked Jesse, “Do you have any other sons?”

Jesse answered, “My youngest is out caring for the sheep.”

Samuel said, “Send for him.

We will wait for him before we sit down to eat.”

## 1 Samuel 16:12

Jesse sent for his youngest son.

The young man came in.

He had a reddish complexion and beautiful eyes.

He was handsome.

The LORD said, “Stand up and pour oil on him.

He is the one.”

## 1 Samuel 16:13

Samuel took the horn and poured oil on David’s head.

David’s brothers watched.

From that day on, the LORD’s Spirit was powerfully at work in David.

Then Samuel left for Ramah.

**Pause and answer**

Where was David when his father sent for him?

## Saul asks for a musician

## 1 Samuel 16:14

The LORD’s Spirit left Saul.

A spirit from the LORD began to trouble him deeply.

## 1 Samuel 16:15

Saul’s servants said, “A spirit from God is troubling you.

## 1 Samuel 16:16

Give us permission to find a skilled harp player.

When the spirit troubles you, he can play for you.

Then you will feel better.”

**Reading help**

A harp is a musical instrument. The player makes music by plucking its strings.

![A harp has strings that a musician plucks to make music.](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

## 1 Samuel 16:17

Saul told his servants, “Find someone who plays well.

Bring him to me.”

## 1 Samuel 16:18

One servant said, “I have seen a son of Jesse from Bethlehem who plays the harp.

He is a strong, brave fighter.

He speaks well and is handsome.

The LORD is with him.”

## David plays for Saul

## 1 Samuel 16:19

Saul sent messengers to Jesse.

“Send me your son David, the one who tends the sheep.”

## 1 Samuel 16:20

Jesse loaded a donkey with bread.

He also sent wine in a bag made of animal skin, and a young goat.

David took these gifts to Saul.

## 1 Samuel 16:21

David came and began to serve Saul.

Saul loved him very much.

David became the man who carried Saul’s armor.

## 1 Samuel 16:22

Saul sent a message to Jesse.

“Please let David stay and serve me.

I am pleased with him.”

## 1 Samuel 16:23

Whenever the spirit from God troubled Saul, David played his harp.

Saul felt better, and the spirit that troubled him left.
