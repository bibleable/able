# 1 Samuel 27 — David lives among the Philistines

**About this chapter**

David fears that Saul will kill him. He takes his family to Gath. There King Achish comes to trust David.

## David leaves Israel

## 1 Samuel 27:1

David thought, “One day Saul will kill me.

My best hope is to escape to Philistine land.

Then Saul will stop searching for me throughout Israel.

I will be safe from him.”

## 1 Samuel 27:2

David and his 600 men went to Gath.

Its king was Achish, the son of Maoch.

## 1 Samuel 27:3

David and his men settled in Gath with Achish.

Each man brought his family.

David brought his two wives.

Ahinoam came from Jezreel.

Abigail came from Carmel and had been Nabal’s wife before he died.

## 1 Samuel 27:4

Saul heard that David had fled to Gath.

Then Saul stopped searching for him.

## Achish gives David a town

## 1 Samuel 27:5

David said to Achish, “If you are pleased with me, please give me a place to live in a country town.

Why should I, your servant, live here in the king’s city with you?”

## 1 Samuel 27:6

That day Achish gave David the town of Ziklag.

When this account was written, Ziklag still belonged to the kings of Judah.

## 1 Samuel 27:7

David lived in Philistine land for one year and four months.

## David gives Achish a false report

## 1 Samuel 27:8

David and his men attacked the Geshurites, the Girzites, and the Amalekites.

These peoples had lived there since ancient times.

Their land stretched as far as Shur and Egypt.

## 1 Samuel 27:9

Wherever David attacked, he left no man or woman alive.

David took sheep, cattle, donkeys, camels, and clothes.

Then he went back to Achish.

## 1 Samuel 27:10

Achish would ask, “Where did you attack today?”

Sometimes David said he had attacked the Negev of Judah.

Other times he named the Negev of Jerahmeel or the Negev of the Kenites.

**Reading help**

The Negev is a dry region in the south.

David named places that were different from the places he had attacked.

## 1 Samuel 27:11

David brought no survivors back to Gath.

He said they would tell people what he had done.

This continued throughout David’s time in Philistine land.

## 1 Samuel 27:12

Achish trusted David.

He thought, “David’s own people in Israel must hate him now.

He will serve me for the rest of his life.”
