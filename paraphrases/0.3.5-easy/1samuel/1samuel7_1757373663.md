# 1 Samuel 7 — Samuel prays for Israel

**About this chapter**

The Israelites turn back to the LORD. When the Philistines come, the Israelites ask Samuel to pray for them.

## The ark stays at Kiriath-jearim

## 1 Samuel 7:1

Men from Kiriath-jearim came for the LORD’s ark.

They carried this sacred chest to Abinadab’s house on the hill.

They set apart Abinadab’s son Eleazar for a special task.

Eleazar would guard the ark.

## 1 Samuel 7:2

Twenty years passed with the ark at Kiriath-jearim.

All the people of Israel grieved and longed for the LORD.

## Israel turns back to the LORD

## 1 Samuel 7:3

Samuel spoke to all Israel.

“If you truly want to return to the LORD, put away the foreign gods you worship.

Put away the images of Ashtoreth too.

Give yourselves fully to the LORD.

Serve him alone.

Then he will rescue you from the Philistines.”

## 1 Samuel 7:4

The Israelites put away their images of Baal and Ashtoreth.

They served only the LORD.

**Reading help**

Baal and Ashtoreth were gods other peoples worshiped.

**Pause and answer**

What did the Israelites put away?

## The people gather at Mizpah

## 1 Samuel 7:5

Samuel said, “Bring everyone in Israel together at Mizpah.

I will pray to the LORD for you.”

## 1 Samuel 7:6

The people gathered at Mizpah.

They drew water and poured it out before the LORD.

They went without food that day.

They admitted, “We have sinned against the LORD.”

Samuel served as Israel’s judge there at Mizpah.

**Reading help**

A judge helped lead Israel and settled disputes.

## 1 Samuel 7:7

The Philistines heard about the meeting at Mizpah.

Their rulers marched toward Israel.

When the Israelites heard this, they were afraid.

## 1 Samuel 7:8

The Israelites begged Samuel, “Keep calling to the LORD our God for us.

Ask him to save us from the Philistines.”

## The LORD answers Samuel

## 1 Samuel 7:9

Samuel took a lamb still drinking its mother’s milk.

He gave the whole lamb to the LORD as a burnt offering.

Samuel cried out to the LORD for Israel.

The LORD answered him.

## 1 Samuel 7:10

The Philistines came to fight while Samuel offered the lamb.

The LORD sent loud thunder against the Philistines.

The thunder confused them, and they ran from Israel.

## 1 Samuel 7:11

Israel’s men ran out of Mizpah after the Philistines.

They struck down Philistines as far as the land below Beth-car.

**Pause and answer**

What made the Philistines turn and run?

## A stone to remember the LORD’s help

## 1 Samuel 7:12

Samuel set up a stone between Mizpah and Shen.

He named it Ebenezer.

He said, “The LORD has helped us this far.”

**Reading help**

Ebenezer means “stone of help.”

The stone reminded the people who had helped them.

![A stone stands between Mizpah and Shen. Samuel named it Ebenezer to remember the LORD’s help.](../pictures/ebenezer-stone.svg)

## 1 Samuel 7:13

After this defeat, the Philistines stopped invading Israel’s land.

The LORD opposed the Philistines for the rest of Samuel’s life.

## 1 Samuel 7:14

Israel got back the towns the Philistines had taken between Ekron and Gath.

Israel also freed the land around those towns from Philistine rule.

Israel and the Amorites lived at peace.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Samuel name the stone Ebenezer?

## Samuel visits the towns of Israel

## 1 Samuel 7:15

Samuel served as Israel’s judge for the rest of his life.

## 1 Samuel 7:16

Every year Samuel visited Bethel, Gilgal, and Mizpah in turn.

He served as judge in each place.

## 1 Samuel 7:17

Then Samuel returned to his home in Ramah.

He served as judge there too.

In Ramah Samuel built an altar to the LORD.

**Reading help**

An altar was a place to offer gifts to God.
