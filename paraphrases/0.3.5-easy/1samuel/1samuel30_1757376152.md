# 1 Samuel 30 — David brings the families home

**About this chapter**

David returns to Ziklag and finds that the families are gone. He asks the LORD what to do. After the rescue, the men disagree about who should share the goods.

## The families are gone

## 1 Samuel 30:1

On the third day David and his men reached Ziklag.

The Amalekites had attacked the southern region called the Negev.

They had attacked Ziklag and burned it down.

## 1 Samuel 30:2

The Amalekites had taken the women and everyone else away as prisoners.

They took young and old alike.

They left no one dead.

## 1 Samuel 30:3

David and his men found their town burned down.

Their wives, sons, and daughters were gone.

## 1 Samuel 30:4

David and his men cried aloud.

They cried until they had no strength left.

## 1 Samuel 30:5

The Amalekites had taken both of David’s wives.

One was Ahinoam from Jezreel.

The other was Abigail, the widow of Nabal from Carmel.

**Pause and answer**

Why did David and his men cry?

## David asks the LORD for help

## 1 Samuel 30:6

Each man grieved for his sons and daughters.

The men talked about killing David with stones.

David was deeply troubled, but he found strength in the LORD his God.

## 1 Samuel 30:7

David spoke to Abiathar the priest, the son of Ahimelech.

“Please bring me the ephod.”

Abiathar brought it to him.

**Reading help**

An ephod was a garment a priest wore when serving God.

## 1 Samuel 30:8

David asked the LORD, “Should I go after these men?

Will I catch up with them?”

The LORD answered, “Go after them.

You will catch up and rescue the prisoners.”

## Some men stay at the stream

## 1 Samuel 30:9

David and his 600 men reached a stream called Besor.

Some men stayed there.

## 1 Samuel 30:10

Two hundred men were too tired to cross the stream.

David went on with the other 400.

**Pause and answer**

Why did 200 men stay at the stream?

## An Egyptian man gets food and water

## 1 Samuel 30:11

David’s men found an Egyptian man in a field.

They brought him to David and gave him food and water.

## 1 Samuel 30:12

They gave him a piece of fig cake and two bunches of raisins.

He felt stronger after he ate.

For three days and three nights he had gone without food or water.

**Reading help**

Dried figs could be pressed together into a cake. Raisins are dried grapes.

## 1 Samuel 30:13

David asked, “Who is your master? Where are you from?”

The man answered, “I am from Egypt.

My master is an Amalekite, and I am his slave.

Three days ago I got sick, and he left me behind.

## 1 Samuel 30:14

We attacked the land of the Cherethites in the Negev.

We also attacked Judah and Caleb’s part of the Negev.

We burned Ziklag down.”

## David finds the Amalekites

## 1 Samuel 30:15

David asked, “Will you take me to those men?”

The man said, “Promise me before God that you will spare my life.

Promise you will not give me back to my master.

Then I will take you to them.”

**Pause and answer**

What promises did the Egyptian ask David to make?

## 1 Samuel 30:16

The Egyptian led David to the Amalekites.

They were eating and drinking all across the open country.

They celebrated because they had taken so much from Philistine land and Judah.

## 1 Samuel 30:17

David fought them from twilight until the following evening.

Only 400 young men escaped.

They rode away on camels.

## David brings everyone back

## 1 Samuel 30:18

David rescued his two wives.

He got back everything the Amalekites had taken.

## 1 Samuel 30:19

Every son and daughter came back.

Everyone, young and old, was safe.

David also recovered all the stolen goods.

**Pause and answer**

Who was missing after the rescue?

## 1 Samuel 30:20

David took all the flocks and herds.

His men drove them ahead of the other animals.

They called out, “These belong to David!”

## The men disagree about sharing

## 1 Samuel 30:21

David returned to the 200 men at the Besor stream.

They had been too tired to go farther.

They came out to meet David and his men.

David greeted them.

## 1 Samuel 30:22

Some of the men who went with David spoke cruelly.

“Those men stayed behind. Why share the goods with them?

Give them their wives and children and send them away.”

## 1 Samuel 30:23

David said, “My brothers, the LORD gave us these things.

You must not treat his gifts that way.

He kept us safe and gave us victory over the raiders.

## 1 Samuel 30:24

Who would agree to what you suggest?

A man who stayed with the supplies gets the same share as a man who fought.

They will share equally.”

## 1 Samuel 30:25

David made this a rule for Israel.

People still followed it when this account was written.

## David sends gifts to Judah

## 1 Samuel 30:26

Back in Ziklag, David sent some of the goods to his friends among Judah’s leaders.

He said, “Here is a gift for you.

It comes from what we took from the LORD’s enemies.”

## 1 Samuel 30:27

David sent gifts to people in Bethel, Ramoth Negev, and Jattir.

## 1 Samuel 30:28

He sent gifts to people in Aroer, Siphmoth, and Eshtemoa.

## 1 Samuel 30:29

He sent gifts to Racal and to the towns of the Jerahmeelites and Kenites.

## 1 Samuel 30:30

He sent gifts to people in Hormah, Bor-ashan, and Athach.

## 1 Samuel 30:31

He sent gifts to Hebron and to all the other places where he and his men had traveled.
