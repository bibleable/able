# 1 Samuel 28 — Saul asks Samuel what to do

**About this chapter**

Saul is afraid of the Philistine army. He wants advice from Samuel, who has died. At night Saul goes to a woman at Endor.

## Achish calls David to battle

## 1 Samuel 28:1

The Philistines gathered their army to fight Israel.

King Achish told David, “You and your men must come with me to battle.”

## 1 Samuel 28:2

David answered, “Then you will see what I can do.”

Achish said, “Good. You will guard me for the rest of your life.”

## Saul fears the Philistine army

## 1 Samuel 28:3

Samuel had already died.

All Israel had mourned for him.

They buried him in his hometown of Ramah.

Saul had removed the mediums and spiritists from the land.

**Reading help**

Mediums and spiritists were people who tried to speak with the dead.

## 1 Samuel 28:4

The Philistines camped at Shunem.

Saul gathered Israel’s army and camped at Gilboa.

## 1 Samuel 28:5

Saul saw the Philistine army.

He shook with fear.

## 1 Samuel 28:6

Saul asked the LORD what to do.

The LORD gave him no answer through dreams, the Urim, or prophets.

**Reading help**

The Urim were used by priests to ask God for guidance. Prophets spoke God’s messages to the people.

**Pause and answer**

What answer did Saul receive when he asked the LORD for help?

## Saul goes to Endor

## 1 Samuel 28:7

Saul told his servants, “Find a woman who is a medium.

I want to ask her for help.”

They answered, “There is a medium at Endor.”

## 1 Samuel 28:8

Saul changed clothes so people would not recognize him.

He took two men with him.

They went to the woman at night.

Saul asked her to call up the spirit of someone he would name.

## 1 Samuel 28:9

The woman answered, “You know what Saul has done.

He has killed mediums and spiritists in this land.

Are you trying to get me killed?”

## 1 Samuel 28:10

Saul made her a promise in the LORD’s name.

“As surely as the LORD lives, no one will punish you for this.”

## The woman sees Samuel

## 1 Samuel 28:11

The woman asked, “Whom shall I bring up for you?”

Saul answered, “Bring up Samuel.”

## 1 Samuel 28:12

When the woman saw Samuel, she cried out.

She said, “You tricked me! You are Saul!”

## 1 Samuel 28:13

The king said, “Do not be afraid.

What do you see?”

She answered, “I see a god coming up out of the ground.”

## 1 Samuel 28:14

Saul asked, “What does he look like?”

She said, “An old man is coming up.

He is wearing a robe.”

Then Saul knew it was Samuel.

Saul bowed with his face to the ground to show respect.

**Pause and answer**

How did the woman describe the person she saw?

## Samuel answers Saul

## 1 Samuel 28:15

Samuel asked Saul, “Why have you disturbed me by bringing me up?”

Saul answered, “I am in deep trouble.

The Philistines are fighting me, and God has turned away from me.

He no longer answers through prophets or dreams.

I called you to tell me what to do.”

## 1 Samuel 28:16

Samuel asked, “Why ask me?

The LORD has turned away from you and become your enemy.

## 1 Samuel 28:17

The LORD has done what he told you through me.

He has taken the kingdom from you and given it to David, your neighbor.

**Reading help**

Earlier Samuel’s robe tore when Saul grabbed it. Samuel used the torn cloth to explain that Saul would lose the kingdom.

![Samuel’s robe with a piece torn from its edge. The torn cloth pictured Saul losing the kingdom.](../pictures/samuels-torn-robe.svg)

## 1 Samuel 28:18

You disobeyed the LORD when he was angry with Amalek.

You did not carry out his judgment against them.

That is why the LORD has done this to you today.

## 1 Samuel 28:19

The LORD will let the Philistines defeat you and Israel.

Tomorrow you and your sons will be with me among the dead.

The LORD will give the Philistines victory over Israel’s army.”

**Pause and answer**

Who did Samuel say would rule Israel?

## Saul falls to the ground

## 1 Samuel 28:20

Samuel’s words terrified Saul.

At once he fell flat on the ground.

He had no strength left.

He had eaten nothing all that day and night.

## 1 Samuel 28:21

The woman came to Saul and saw how upset he was.

She said, “I risked my life to do what you asked.

## 1 Samuel 28:22

Now please listen to me.

Let me give you a little bread.

Eat so you have the strength to leave.”

## The woman prepares food

## 1 Samuel 28:23

Saul said, “I will not eat.”

His servants and the woman kept urging him.

At last Saul listened.

He got up from the ground and sat on the bed.

## 1 Samuel 28:24

The woman quickly killed a calf she had raised for meat.

She worked flour into dough and baked bread without yeast.

## 1 Samuel 28:25

She served the meal to Saul and his servants.

They ate, then got up and left that night.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the woman want Saul to eat before leaving?
