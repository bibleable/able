# 1 Samuel 4 — The Philistines take the ark of God

**About this chapter**

Israel’s leaders bring the ark from Shiloh to their camp. After the battle, a messenger runs back to Shiloh with news for Eli.

## Israel loses a battle

## 1 Samuel 4:1

Samuel’s words reached people throughout Israel.

The Israelites went to fight the Philistines.

Israel camped at Ebenezer.

The Philistines camped at Aphek.

## 1 Samuel 4:2

The Philistines fought Israel and won.

They killed about 4,000 Israelites in the battle.

## The leaders send for the ark

## 1 Samuel 4:3

Israel’s soldiers came back to camp.

Their leaders asked, “Why did the LORD let the Philistines defeat us today?

Let us bring the ark of the LORD’s covenant from Shiloh.

It can go with us and save us from our enemies.”

**Reading help**

The ark was a sacred chest. A covenant is a solemn promise. The ark reminded Israel of God’s covenant with his people.

## 1 Samuel 4:4

The Israelites sent men to Shiloh for the ark.

The men brought back the ark of the LORD of Hosts.

The LORD rules as king between the cherubim.

Eli’s sons, Hophni and Phinehas, came with the ark.

**Reading help**

Cherubim are heavenly beings. Two figures of cherubim stood on the ark’s cover, with their wings spread over it.

## 1 Samuel 4:5

When the ark arrived, all the Israelites shouted.

Their shout was so loud that the ground shook.

**Pause and answer**

What did the Israelites do when the ark arrived?

## The Philistines hear the shouting

## 1 Samuel 4:6

The Philistines heard the shout.

They asked, “Why are the Hebrews shouting in their camp?”

Then they learned that the LORD’s ark had arrived.

## 1 Samuel 4:7

The Philistines were afraid.

They said, “Gods have come into their camp!

What trouble we are in!

Nothing like this has happened before.

## 1 Samuel 4:8

Who can save us from these powerful gods?

These gods brought all kinds of terrible plagues on the Egyptians in the wilderness.”

**Reading help**

The Philistines called the Israelites Hebrews. Here the Philistines are telling each other what they believe about Israel’s God.

## 1 Samuel 4:9

The Philistines urged each other, “Be brave and fight!

The Hebrews served us.

If we lose, we will have to serve them.”

## The Philistines win again

## 1 Samuel 4:10

The Philistines fought and defeated Israel.

The Israelites ran back to their tents.

The Philistines killed 30,000 Israelite soldiers who fought on foot.

## 1 Samuel 4:11

The Philistines took the ark of God.

Eli’s sons, Hophni and Phinehas, both died.

**Pause and answer**

Who took the ark of God?

## A messenger runs to Shiloh

## 1 Samuel 4:12

That day a man from the tribe of Benjamin ran to Shiloh from the battle.

His clothes were torn.

He had dirt on his head.

**Reading help**

People tore their clothes and put dirt on their heads to show grief.

## 1 Samuel 4:13

Eli sat on his chair beside the road.

He was waiting because he feared for the ark of God.

The messenger went into town and told the news.

The whole town cried out.

## 1 Samuel 4:14

Eli heard the cries.

He asked, “Why is everyone shouting?”

The messenger hurried over to tell Eli.

## 1 Samuel 4:15

Eli was 98 years old.

His eyes stared straight ahead.

He could no longer see.

## Eli hears about his sons and the ark

## 1 Samuel 4:16

The man told Eli, “I ran from the battle today.

I have just arrived.”

Eli asked, “What happened, my son?”

## 1 Samuel 4:17

The messenger said, “Israel ran from the Philistines.

Many of our people died.

Both your sons, Hophni and Phinehas, are dead.

The Philistines took the ark of God.”

## 1 Samuel 4:18

When Eli heard about the ark, he fell backward off his chair by the town gate.

Eli was old and heavy.

He broke his neck and died.

Eli had led Israel as a judge for 40 years.

**Pause and answer**

What news did Eli hear just before he fell?

## Phinehas’s wife names her son

## 1 Samuel 4:19

Phinehas’s wife was about to have a baby.

She heard that the Philistines had taken God’s ark.

She heard that Eli and her husband had died.

Her birth pains began.

She collapsed and gave birth.

## 1 Samuel 4:20

As she was dying, the women helping her said, “Do not be afraid.

You have a son!”

She paid no attention to them and gave no answer.

## 1 Samuel 4:21

She named her boy Ichabod.

She said, “The glory has left Israel.”

She said this because the Philistines had taken the ark, and Eli and Phinehas had died.

**Reading help**

The name Ichabod recalls the mother’s words about glory. The ark was a sign of God’s presence with Israel. She grieved when the enemy took it away.

## 1 Samuel 4:22

She said again, “The glory has left Israel.

The Philistines have taken the ark of God.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did the baby’s mother say the glory had left Israel?
