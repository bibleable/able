# 1 Samuel 2 — Hannah praises God and Samuel grows up

**About this chapter**

Hannah praises the LORD for helping people who are weak. Samuel serves God at Shiloh. Eli’s sons take what people bring for God.

## Hannah thanks the LORD

## 1 Samuel 2:1

Hannah prayed, “LORD, you fill me with joy.

You have made me strong.

I can speak boldly to my enemies.

I am glad because you saved me.

## 1 Samuel 2:2

No one is holy like you, LORD.

You alone are God.

No rock is like our God.

**Reading help**

Hannah calls God a rock. A rock is firm and strong. Hannah trusts God to keep her safe.

## 1 Samuel 2:3

Stop bragging about how great you are.

The LORD knows what you do.

He judges your actions fairly.

## The LORD gives strength to the weak

## 1 Samuel 2:4

Strong fighters lose their bows when the bows break.

People who were weak gain strength.

## 1 Samuel 2:5

People who had plenty must now work for bread.

People who were starving have enough to eat.

A woman who could have no children gives birth to seven.

A mother of many sons grows weak.

## 1 Samuel 2:6

The LORD brings death and gives life.

He brings people down to the place of the dead.

He also raises people up.

## 1 Samuel 2:7

The LORD makes people poor or rich.

He brings some people low.

He lifts others up.

## 1 Samuel 2:8

He lifts poor people from the dust.

He helps those who sit among the ashes.

He gives them places of honor beside princes.

The earth rests on foundations that belong to the LORD.

He set the world on them.

## Hannah prays about God’s king

## 1 Samuel 2:9

The LORD guards those who stay faithful to him.

Wicked people die in darkness.

No one wins by strength alone.

## 1 Samuel 2:10

The LORD will thunder from heaven against his enemies.

He will crush those who fight him.

He will judge people everywhere on earth.

He will give strength to his king.

He will make his chosen king powerful.”

**Reading help**

In Israel people poured oil on a king’s head to show God had chosen him. The Bible calls this king God’s anointed one.

## Eli’s sons take meat from the offerings

## 1 Samuel 2:11

Elkanah went home to Ramah.

Samuel stayed with Eli the priest.

There the boy began to serve the LORD.

## 1 Samuel 2:12

Eli’s sons did evil.

They had no respect for the LORD.

## 1 Samuel 2:13

They ignored the way priests should treat the people.

When someone offered a sacrifice, the priest’s servant came near.

He held a fork with three points while the meat boiled.

## 1 Samuel 2:14

The servant pushed the fork into the cooking pot.

Whatever came up on the fork went to the priest.

The priests treated everyone who came to Shiloh this way.

## 1 Samuel 2:15

Sometimes the servant came before anyone burned the fat.

He said, “Give the priest raw meat to roast.

He will refuse boiled meat.”

## 1 Samuel 2:16

The person might say, “First let the fat burn for the LORD.

Then take as much as you want.”

The servant would say, “Give it to me now.

If you refuse, I will take it by force.”

## 1 Samuel 2:17

The LORD saw how serious their sin was.

These young men treated his offering as worthless.

## Hannah brings Samuel a robe

## 1 Samuel 2:18

Samuel was still a boy.

He wore a linen ephod as he served the LORD.

**Reading help**

An ephod was a piece of clothing worn for serving God. Samuel’s ephod was made from a cloth called linen.

## 1 Samuel 2:19

Each year Hannah made a little robe for Samuel.

She brought it when she and Elkanah came to offer their sacrifice.

![Hannah makes a little robe and brings it to Samuel each year.](../pictures/hannah-brings-a-robe.svg)

## 1 Samuel 2:20

Eli would bless Elkanah and Hannah.

He said, “May the LORD give you children together in place of the boy Hannah gave to him.”

Then they would go home.

## 1 Samuel 2:21

The LORD cared for Hannah.

In time she had three more sons and two daughters.

Samuel grew up serving the LORD at Shiloh.

What did Hannah bring Samuel each year?

## Eli speaks to his sons

## 1 Samuel 2:22

Eli was very old.

He heard how his sons treated the Israelites.

He also heard that his sons were having sex with women who served at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.

**Reading help**

The Tent of Meeting was a place where people came to worship God.

## 1 Samuel 2:23

Eli asked his sons, “Why do you do these things?

People keep telling me about the evil you do.

## 1 Samuel 2:24

My sons, the LORD’s people are telling each other terrible things about you.

## 1 Samuel 2:25

If someone wrongs another person, God can speak for him.

But who can speak for someone who wrongs the LORD?”

Eli’s sons refused to listen to their father.

The LORD had decided to put them to death.

## 1 Samuel 2:26

Samuel kept growing taller.

The LORD was pleased with him.

People thought well of Samuel too.

**Pause and answer**

How did Eli’s sons respond to their father?

## A messenger comes to Eli

## 1 Samuel 2:27

A man of God came to Eli with this message from the LORD.

“I made myself known to your family long ago.

They lived in Egypt under Pharaoh’s rule.

## 1 Samuel 2:28

Out of all Israel’s tribes, I chose your ancestor as my priest.

He offered sacrifices on my altar.

He burned incense and wore an ephod before me.

I gave his family the food offerings from Israel.

**Reading help**

Incense gives off a sweet smell when it burns.

## 1 Samuel 2:29

Why do you treat the offerings at my house with such disrespect?

You honor your sons more than you honor me.

You eat the best parts of my people’s gifts and grow fat.

## 1 Samuel 2:30

I am the LORD, the God of Israel.

I said your family would serve me forever.

Now I say that can no longer be.

I will honor those who honor me.

Those who despise me will lose their honor.

## The LORD tells Eli what will happen

## 1 Samuel 2:31

Soon I will take away your family’s strength.

No one in your family will reach old age.

## 1 Samuel 2:32

You will see trouble in my house.

Even when Israel does well, no one in your family will grow old.

## 1 Samuel 2:33

Some in your family will still serve at my altar.

You will strain your eyes and grieve because of them.

Men with swords will kill your descendants.

## 1 Samuel 2:34

Here is a sign for you.

Your sons Hophni and Phinehas will die on the same day.

## 1 Samuel 2:35

I will choose a faithful priest for myself.

He will do what I want him to do.

I will give him a family that lasts.

His family will always serve before my chosen king.

## 1 Samuel 2:36

Everyone left in your family will bow before him.

They will ask for a piece of silver or some bread.

They will beg for a priest’s work so they can eat.”
