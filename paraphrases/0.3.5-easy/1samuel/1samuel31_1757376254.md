# 1 Samuel 31 — The people of Jabesh bury Saul and his sons

**About this chapter**

Saul and three of his sons die on Mount Gilboa. When news reaches Jabesh, men from the town go to bring their bodies back for burial.

## Israel loses the battle

## 1 Samuel 31:1

The Philistines fought Israel, and Israel’s soldiers ran away.

Many Israelites died on Mount Gilboa.

## 1 Samuel 31:2

The Philistines chased Saul and his sons.

They killed three of Saul’s sons. Their names were Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua.

## 1 Samuel 31:3

The fighting around Saul grew fierce.

Philistine archers caught up with Saul and wounded him badly.

**Reading help**

Archers were soldiers who used bows and arrows.

## Saul dies on Mount Gilboa

## 1 Samuel 31:4

Saul spoke to the man who carried his armor.

He asked the man to kill him with his sword.

Saul feared the Philistines would torture and kill him.

The man was terrified and refused.

Then Saul took his own life.

## 1 Samuel 31:5

The man who carried Saul’s armor saw that Saul was dead.

He too took his own life and died beside Saul.

## 1 Samuel 31:6

Saul, his three sons, the man who carried his armor, and all the men with Saul died that day.

## People flee from their towns

## 1 Samuel 31:7

Israelites lived along the valley and across the Jordan River.

They saw that Israel’s army had fled.

They learned that Saul and his sons were dead.

They left their towns and ran away.

The Philistines came to live in those towns.

## 1 Samuel 31:8

The next day the Philistines came to take things from the dead.

They found Saul and his three sons on Mount Gilboa.

## 1 Samuel 31:9

The Philistines cut off Saul’s head and took his armor.

They sent messengers throughout their land.

The messengers told the news in the temples and among the people.

## 1 Samuel 31:10

The Philistines put Saul’s armor in the temple of the Ashtoreths.

They hung his body on the wall of Beth-shan.

**Reading help**

The Philistines worshiped the Ashtoreths in this temple.

## Men from Jabesh bring the bodies back

## 1 Samuel 31:11

The people of Jabesh-gilead heard what the Philistines had done to Saul.

## 1 Samuel 31:12

The brave men of Jabesh traveled all night.

They took the bodies of Saul and his sons down from the wall of Beth-shan.

They brought the bodies back to Jabesh and burned them there.

## 1 Samuel 31:13

The men buried the bones under a tamarisk tree in Jabesh.

They fasted for seven days.

**Reading help**

The people went without food as they mourned.
