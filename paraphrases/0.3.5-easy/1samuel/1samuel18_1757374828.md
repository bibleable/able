# 1 Samuel 18 — Jonathan loves David, but Saul fears him

**About this chapter**

Jonathan makes a promise to David. The people praise David, and Saul begins to fear that David will become king.

## Jonathan makes a promise to David

## 1 Samuel 18:1

David finished speaking with King Saul.

Saul’s son Jonathan felt very close to David.

Jonathan loved David as much as he loved himself.

## 1 Samuel 18:2

From that day on, Saul kept David with him.

Saul no longer let David go home to his father.

## 1 Samuel 18:3

Jonathan made a covenant with David because he loved him as himself.

**Reading help**

A covenant is a solemn promise. Jonathan made this promise as David’s friend.

## 1 Samuel 18:4

Jonathan took off his robe and gave it to David.

He also gave David his tunic, sword, bow, and belt.

**Reading help**

A tunic was a piece of clothing worn under a robe.

## 1 Samuel 18:5

David did well at everything Saul sent him to do.

Saul put David in charge of his fighting men.

The people and Saul’s officers were glad about this.

**Pause and answer**

What did Jonathan give David to show his friendship?

## A song makes Saul angry

## 1 Samuel 18:6

After David killed Goliath, the soldiers came home.

Women from towns all over Israel came out to welcome King Saul.

They danced and sang for joy.

They played tambourines and other instruments.

## 1 Samuel 18:7

As the women danced, they sang, “Saul has killed his thousands!

David has killed his tens of thousands!”

## 1 Samuel 18:8

The song made Saul angry.

He said, “They give David credit for tens of thousands.

They give me credit for only thousands.

What is left for David to take but my kingdom?”

## 1 Samuel 18:9

From that day on, Saul watched David with jealousy.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the women’s song make Saul angry?

## David gets away from Saul

## 1 Samuel 18:10

The next day a spirit from God troubled Saul deeply.

Saul began to prophesy inside his house.

David played the harp as he usually did.

Saul had a spear in his hand.

**Reading help**

To prophesy means to speak as a prophet does.

## 1 Samuel 18:11

Saul threw the spear, hoping to pin David to the wall.

Twice David got away from Saul.

## 1 Samuel 18:12

Saul feared David because the LORD was with David.

The LORD had left Saul.

## David leads the soldiers

## 1 Samuel 18:13

Saul sent David away to lead a thousand men.

David led the men into battle and brought them back.

## 1 Samuel 18:14

David kept doing well because the LORD was with him.

## 1 Samuel 18:15

Saul saw how well David did.

That made Saul afraid of him.

## 1 Samuel 18:16

The people of Israel and Judah loved David.

He led them into battle and brought them home.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the people love David?

## Saul offers Merab in marriage

## 1 Samuel 18:17

Saul told David, “You may marry my older daughter Merab.

Be brave and fight the LORD’s battles for me.”

Saul hoped the Philistines would kill David.

Then Saul would not have to kill David himself.

## 1 Samuel 18:18

David asked, “Who am I to marry the king’s daughter?

What is so important about my father’s family in Israel?”

## 1 Samuel 18:19

The time came for Merab to marry David.

But Saul gave Merab in marriage to Adriel from Meholah.

## Michal loves David

## 1 Samuel 18:20

Saul’s daughter Michal loved David.

Saul was pleased when he heard this.

![Saul is the father of Jonathan, Merab, and Michal. Merab is the older daughter.](../pictures/sauls-children-and-david.svg)

## 1 Samuel 18:21

Saul thought he could use the marriage as a trap.

He still hoped the Philistines would kill David.

He said to David, “Here is another chance to marry my daughter.”

## 1 Samuel 18:22

Saul told his servants to speak to David in private.

“Tell him the king is pleased with him.

Tell him all my servants love him.

Encourage him to marry my daughter.”

## 1 Samuel 18:23

The servants spoke to David.

He asked, “Do you think marrying the king’s daughter is a small thing?

I am poor, and people think little of me.”

## 1 Samuel 18:24

The servants told Saul what David had said.

**Pause and answer**

Why did David think marrying the king’s daughter would be difficult?

## David marries Michal

## 1 Samuel 18:25

Saul wanted revenge on his enemies.

He sent word that David could marry Michal for a price.

The only payment he wanted was a hundred Philistine foreskins.

Saul hoped the Philistines would kill David.

**Reading help**

A foreskin is the fold of skin at the tip of the penis. Saul demanded these body parts as proof that Philistines had died.

## 1 Samuel 18:26

The servants told David the price.

David was pleased that he could marry the king’s daughter.

He acted before the wedding day.

## 1 Samuel 18:27

David and his men killed two hundred Philistines.

David brought their foreskins to the king as the full marriage payment.

Then Saul gave Michal to David, and they married.

## Saul’s fear grows

## 1 Samuel 18:28

Saul saw that the LORD was with David.

He also saw that Michal loved David.

## 1 Samuel 18:29

Saul grew even more afraid of David.

From then on, Saul was David’s enemy.

**Pause and answer**

How did Saul feel when he saw that the LORD was with David?

## 1 Samuel 18:30

The Philistine commanders kept coming out to fight.

Each time, David did better than all of Saul’s other officers.

People spoke of David with great respect.
