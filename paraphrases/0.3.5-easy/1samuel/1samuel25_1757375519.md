# 1 Samuel 25 — Abigail asks David to spare her household

**About this chapter**

Nabal insults the men David sends to ask for food. David wants revenge. Abigail takes food to David and asks him to listen before anyone dies.

## Israel mourns for Samuel

## 1 Samuel 25:1

Samuel died.

People from all Israel gathered to mourn for him.

They buried him at his home in Ramah.

Then David went down to the wilderness of Paran.

## 1 Samuel 25:2

A rich man from Maon kept his flocks at Carmel.

He owned 1,000 goats and 3,000 sheep.

He was at Carmel to have the sheep’s wool cut.

## 1 Samuel 25:3

His name was Nabal. He belonged to Caleb’s family.

His wife Abigail was wise and beautiful.

Nabal was harsh and treated people badly.

## David sends a greeting

## 1 Samuel 25:4

David was in the wilderness when he heard about the sheep shearing.

## 1 Samuel 25:5

David sent ten young men to Carmel.

He said, “Greet Nabal for me.

## 1 Samuel 25:6

Wish him a long life.

Ask for peace for him, his family, and everything he owns.

## 1 Samuel 25:7

Tell him I heard that his workers are cutting the wool.

We treated his shepherds well.

Nothing of theirs went missing while they were with us at Carmel.

## 1 Samuel 25:8

His men can tell him this themselves.

Ask him to welcome you on this feast day.

Ask him to share whatever he can spare with you and with me, his son David.”

**Reading help**

Jesse was David’s father. David called himself Nabal’s son as a way to show respect.

## Nabal refuses to share

## 1 Samuel 25:9

The ten men gave Nabal the whole message from David.

Then they waited for his answer.

## 1 Samuel 25:10

Nabal said, “Who is David? Who is this son of Jesse?

Plenty of servants run away from their masters these days.

## 1 Samuel 25:11

I have bread, water, and meat for my workers.

Why give their food to men who come from who knows where?”

## 1 Samuel 25:12

David’s men went back and told him everything Nabal had said.

## 1 Samuel 25:13

David told his men to put on their swords.

They did, and David put on his own sword.

About 400 men followed him.

Two hundred stayed with the supplies.

## A servant warns Abigail

## 1 Samuel 25:14

One of Nabal’s servants went to Abigail.

He said, “David sent men from the wilderness to greet our master.

Nabal shouted at them.

## 1 Samuel 25:15

David’s men were very good to us.

They left us in peace out in the fields.

Nothing went missing the whole time we were with them.

## 1 Samuel 25:16

They were like a wall around us, day and night, while we watched the sheep.

**Reading help**

The servant compares David’s men to a wall. He means they kept the shepherds safe.

## 1 Samuel 25:17

Please think what you can do.

Our master and everyone in his house are in danger.

He behaves so badly that nobody can speak to him.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did the servant ask Abigail for help?

## Abigail brings food

## 1 Samuel 25:18

Abigail hurried to load donkeys with food.

She took 200 loaves, two bags of wine, and five sheep prepared for eating.

She added five measures of roasted grain, 100 bunches of raisins, and 200 fig cakes.

**Reading help**

Each measure of grain was called a seah. The wine bags were made from animal skins.

## 1 Samuel 25:19

She told her servants, “Go ahead. I will follow you.”

She said nothing to Nabal.

## 1 Samuel 25:20

Abigail rode her donkey into a narrow mountain valley.

There she met David and his men coming down toward her.

## 1 Samuel 25:21

David had just said, “All my care for this man’s property was wasted.

Nothing of his went missing.

I did good to him, and he did evil to me.

## 1 Samuel 25:22

May God punish me severely if I leave a single male in Nabal’s household alive by morning.”

## Abigail asks David to listen

## 1 Samuel 25:23

Abigail saw David and quickly got off her donkey.

She bowed with her face to the ground.

## 1 Samuel 25:24

At David’s feet she said, “My lord, blame me alone.

Please listen to what I have to say.

## 1 Samuel 25:25

Pay no attention to Nabal’s insults.

His name means ‘fool,’ and he acts like one.

I did not see the men you sent.

## 1 Samuel 25:26

As surely as the LORD lives and you live, the LORD has kept you from killing people for revenge.

May your enemies and everyone who wants to harm you end up like Nabal.

## 1 Samuel 25:27

Please give the food I brought to the men with you.

## Abigail asks David to think about his future

## 1 Samuel 25:28

Please forgive me.

You fight the LORD’s battles.

He will give you a family of kings that lasts.

May you do no evil as long as you live.

## 1 Samuel 25:29

Someone may hunt you and try to kill you.

The LORD your God will keep your life safe like something precious tied in a bundle.

He will throw your enemies’ lives away like stones from a sling.

**Reading help**

Abigail pictures two things. People tie up what they want to keep safe. A stone thrown from a sling flies far away. Abigail trusts God to protect David and deal with his enemies.

![A cord holds a bundle closed. Abigail uses this picture to say that the LORD will keep David’s life safe.](../pictures/abigails-bundle.svg)

## 1 Samuel 25:30

The LORD will do the good things he promised you.

He will make you ruler over Israel.

## 1 Samuel 25:31

Then you will have no cause to grieve over killing innocent people or taking revenge.

When the LORD does good to you, please remember me.”

## David listens to Abigail

## 1 Samuel 25:32

David said, “Praise the LORD, the God of Israel!

He sent you to meet me today.

## 1 Samuel 25:33

May God bless you for your wise judgment.

You stopped me from killing people to get revenge.

## 1 Samuel 25:34

The LORD, the God of Israel, has kept me from harming you.

As surely as he lives, if you had not come quickly, no male in Nabal’s household would have survived the night.”

## 1 Samuel 25:35

David accepted Abigail’s gift.

He said, “Go home in peace.

I have heard you and will do what you ask.”

**Pause and answer**

What did David thank Abigail for stopping him from doing?

## Abigail goes home

## 1 Samuel 25:36

At home Nabal was enjoying a feast fit for a king.

He was very drunk.

Abigail waited until morning to tell him anything.

## 1 Samuel 25:37

In the morning, when Nabal was sober, Abigail told him what had happened.

His heart failed him, and he lay still like a stone.

## 1 Samuel 25:38

About ten days later, the LORD struck Nabal, and he died.

## 1 Samuel 25:39

David heard that Nabal had died.

He said, “Praise the LORD! He defended me when Nabal insulted me.

He kept me from doing wrong.

He made Nabal answer for his own evil.”

Then David sent men to ask Abigail to marry him.

## Abigail agrees to marry David

## 1 Samuel 25:40

David’s servants came to Abigail at Carmel.

They said, “David has sent us to bring you to him as his wife.”

## 1 Samuel 25:41

Abigail stood up and bowed with her face to the ground.

She said, “I am ready to serve.

I will even wash the feet of David’s servants.”

## 1 Samuel 25:42

Abigail quickly got on a donkey.

Five of her women servants went with her.

She followed David’s messengers and became his wife.

**Pause and answer**

How did Abigail answer David’s invitation?

## 1 Samuel 25:43

David had also married Ahinoam from Jezreel.

Ahinoam and Abigail were both his wives.

## 1 Samuel 25:44

Saul had given his daughter Michal, David’s wife, to another man.

His name was Palti son of Laish, from Gallim.
