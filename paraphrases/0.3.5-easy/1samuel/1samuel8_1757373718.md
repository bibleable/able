# 1 Samuel 8 — Israel asks for a king

**About this chapter**

Israel’s leaders want a king. Samuel warns the people about what a king will take from their families.

## Samuel’s sons judge unfairly

## 1 Samuel 8:1

When Samuel grew old, he chose his sons to be judges over Israel.

## 1 Samuel 8:2

His first son was Joel.

His second son was Abijah.

They served as judges in Beersheba.

## 1 Samuel 8:3

Samuel’s sons acted differently from their father.

They took money from people who wanted them to decide a case their way.

So their decisions were unfair.

**Reading help**

Money or a gift given to a judge to get an unfair decision is called a bribe.

## The leaders ask Samuel for a king

## 1 Samuel 8:4

All the leaders of Israel met together.

They went to see Samuel at Ramah.

## 1 Samuel 8:5

They said, “You are old.

Your sons do not act as you do.

Choose a king to judge us.

The other nations have kings.”

**Pause and answer**

Why were the leaders unhappy with Samuel’s sons?

## 1 Samuel 8:6

Samuel was unhappy when they asked for a king to judge them.

So Samuel prayed to the LORD.

## The LORD tells Samuel to warn the people

## 1 Samuel 8:7

The LORD told Samuel, “Listen to everything the people say.

They are turning away from me as their king.

That is why they have turned away from you.

## 1 Samuel 8:8

I brought them out of Egypt.

Ever since then, they have left me to serve other gods.

Now they are treating you the same way.

## 1 Samuel 8:9

Listen to them.

But give them a serious warning.

Tell them how their king will treat them.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the LORD tell Samuel to explain to the people?

## A king will take sons and daughters

## 1 Samuel 8:10

Samuel told the people who wanted a king everything the LORD had said.

## 1 Samuel 8:11

Samuel said, “This is what your king will do.

He will take your sons to serve him.

Some will work with his chariots and horses.

Others will run ahead of his chariots.

**Reading help**

A chariot was a cart that horses pulled.

## 1 Samuel 8:12

Some will lead groups of a thousand or fifty.

Others will plow his land and gather his crops.

Others will make weapons and equipment for his chariots.

## 1 Samuel 8:13

He will take your daughters to work for him.

Some will make perfume.

Others will cook or bake.

## A king will take land and food

## 1 Samuel 8:14

He will take your best fields.

He will take your best vineyards and olive groves.

He will give these lands to his servants.

## 1 Samuel 8:15

He will take a tenth of your grain and grapes.

He will give this share to his officials and servants.

**Reading help**

Imagine dividing the harvest into ten equal shares. The king takes one share. That is a tenth.

![Ten equal boxes represent the harvest. The marked box shows the one share the king takes.](../pictures/one-tenth.svg)

## 1 Samuel 8:16

He will take the men and women who serve you.

He will take your best cattle and donkeys.

All of them will work for him.

## 1 Samuel 8:17

He will take a tenth of your flocks.

You yourselves will become his slaves.

**Pause and answer**

How much of the people’s flocks would the king take?

## 1 Samuel 8:18

Then you will beg the LORD for help because of the king you chose.

On that day the LORD will give you no answer.”

## The people still want a king

## 1 Samuel 8:19

The people refused to listen to Samuel.

They said, “We must have a king!

## 1 Samuel 8:20

Then we will be like all the other nations.

Our king will judge us.

He will lead us out and fight our battles.”

## 1 Samuel 8:21

Samuel listened to everything the people said.

Then he repeated their words to the LORD.

## 1 Samuel 8:22

The LORD said to Samuel, “Listen to them.

Choose a king for them.”

Then Samuel told the men of Israel, “Each of you, go home to your own town.”
