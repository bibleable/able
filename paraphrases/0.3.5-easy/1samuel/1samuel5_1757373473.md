# 1 Samuel 5 — The Philistines move the ark from city to city

**About this chapter**

The Philistines put God’s sacred chest beside a statue. The statue falls. As the chest moves from city to city, people grow afraid.

## The ark arrives in Ashdod

## 1 Samuel 5:1

The Philistines had taken the ark of God from Israel.

They carried the ark from Ebenezer to Ashdod.

**Reading help**

The ark was Israel’s sacred chest.

It reminded the people of God’s promise to them.

## 1 Samuel 5:2

In Ashdod the Philistines carried the ark into Dagon’s temple.

Dagon was a god the Philistines worshiped.

They set the ark beside Dagon’s statue.

## Dagon’s statue falls twice

## 1 Samuel 5:3

Early the next morning, the people of Ashdod got up.

Dagon’s statue lay face down before the LORD’s ark.

The people lifted the statue back into place.

## 1 Samuel 5:4

Early the following morning, they found the statue face down again.

Its head and both hands had broken off.

The head and hands lay across the bottom of the doorway.

Only the body remained in one piece.

## 1 Samuel 5:5

After that, Dagon’s priests stepped over that part of the doorway.

Everyone else entering the temple in Ashdod did the same.

People still did this when this account was written.

## The rulers send the ark to Gath

## 1 Samuel 5:6

The LORD’s hand was heavy on Ashdod and the nearby towns.

He brought great suffering on the people there.

Lumps called tumors grew on their bodies.

**Reading help**

The LORD’s “heavy hand” means he used his power against the people.

## 1 Samuel 5:7

The men of Ashdod saw what was happening.

They said, “The ark of Israel’s God cannot stay here.

Israel’s God has used his power against us and against Dagon.”

## 1 Samuel 5:8

They called all the Philistine rulers together.

They asked, “What shall we do with the ark of Israel’s God?”

The rulers answered, “Take it to Gath.”

So the people carried the ark away.

## 1 Samuel 5:9

When the ark came to Gath, the LORD acted against that city too.

People all over the city panicked.

The LORD struck men both young and old with tumors.

**Pause and answer**

Which city did the rulers choose after Ashdod?

## The people of Ekron ask the rulers for help

## 1 Samuel 5:10

The people of Gath sent the ark to Ekron.

When it arrived, the people of Ekron cried out.

“They have brought the ark of Israel’s God here to kill us and our people!”

![The Philistines took the ark first to Ashdod, then to Gath, then to Ekron.](../pictures/ark-in-three-philistine-cities.svg)

## 1 Samuel 5:11

The people of Ekron called all the Philistine rulers together.

They said, “Send the ark of Israel’s God back where it belongs.

Then it will not kill us and our people.”

People were dying, and the whole city was in a panic.

God had used his great power against the city.

## 1 Samuel 5:12

Even the people who survived suffered from tumors.

The cries of the city rose to heaven.
