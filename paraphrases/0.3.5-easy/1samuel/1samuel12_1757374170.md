# 1 Samuel 12 — Samuel promises to keep praying for Israel

**About this chapter**

Israel now has a king. Samuel reminds the people how the LORD has cared for them. When they become afraid, Samuel promises to keep praying for them.

## Samuel asks how he has treated the people

## 1 Samuel 12:1

Samuel said to all Israel, “I listened to everything you asked of me.

I have given you a king.

## 1 Samuel 12:2

Your king now leads you.

I am old, and my hair is gray.

My sons are here among you.

I have led you since I was young.

## 1 Samuel 12:3

Here I am before the LORD and his chosen king.

Speak up if I took your ox or donkey.

Tell me if I cheated you or treated you cruelly.

Did I take money to overlook a wrong?

Tell me, and I will give back what I took.”

## 1 Samuel 12:4

The people answered, “You have never cheated us or treated us cruelly.

You have taken nothing from anyone.”

## 1 Samuel 12:5

Samuel said, “You have found nothing that I took from you.

Today the LORD and his chosen king are witnesses to your words.”

The people answered, “The LORD is a witness.”

**Reading help**

A witness can tell others what happened. Here the LORD and Saul have heard what the people said about Samuel.

**Pause and answer**

## Samuel remembers the rescue from Egypt

## 1 Samuel 12:6

Samuel told the people, “The LORD chose Moses and Aaron.

The LORD brought your ancestors out of Egypt.

## 1 Samuel 12:7

Stand here before the LORD.

I will remind you of the good and right things he did for you and your ancestors.

## 1 Samuel 12:8

Jacob went to Egypt.

Later your ancestors there cried out to the LORD.

The LORD sent Moses and Aaron to lead them out of Egypt.

So your ancestors came to live here.

## The people cried out for help

## 1 Samuel 12:9

But they forgot the LORD their God.

So he gave their enemies power over them.

Sisera, who led Hazor’s army, fought them.

The Philistines and the king of Moab also fought them.

## 1 Samuel 12:10

Your ancestors cried out to the LORD.

They said, ‘We have sinned.

We left you and worshiped other gods, the Baals and Ashtoreths.

Save us from our enemies so we can serve you.’

## 1 Samuel 12:11

The LORD sent Jerubbaal, Barak, Jephthah, and me, Samuel.

He saved you from the enemies all around you.

Then you lived in safety.

**Reading help**

Jerubbaal was another name for Gideon.

**Pause and answer**

What did the LORD do when the people cried out to him?

## Samuel speaks about the king

## 1 Samuel 12:12

The LORD your God was your king.

But you saw Nahash, king of the Ammonites, coming against you.

You told me, ‘We must have a king to rule us.’

## 1 Samuel 12:13

Here is the king you chose and asked for.

The LORD has given him to you.

## 1 Samuel 12:14

Honor the LORD, serve him, and obey his commands.

If you and your king follow the LORD your God this way, all will be well.

## 1 Samuel 12:15

But if you refuse to obey his commands, the LORD will oppose you.

He did the same to your ancestors.

**Pause and answer**

Who must obey the LORD along with the people?

## Thunder and rain at harvest time

## 1 Samuel 12:16

Now stand here and watch.

The LORD is about to do something great.

## 1 Samuel 12:17

Today people are gathering the wheat harvest.

I will ask the LORD to send thunder and rain.

Then you will see how wrong it was to ask the LORD for a king.”

## 1 Samuel 12:18

Samuel called out to the LORD.

That day the LORD sent thunder and rain.

All the people felt great fear of the LORD and of Samuel.

## 1 Samuel 12:19

The people begged Samuel, “Pray to the LORD your God for us so we may live!

We have sinned in many ways.

Now we have done wrong again by asking for a king.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the people ask Samuel to do?

## Samuel promises to pray and teach

## 1 Samuel 12:20

Samuel answered, “Do not be afraid.

You have done wrong, but keep following the LORD.

Serve him with all your heart.

**Reading help**

To serve God with all your heart means to give yourself fully to him.

## 1 Samuel 12:21

Stay away from worthless things.

They have no power to help you or save you.

## 1 Samuel 12:22

The LORD was pleased to make you his own people.

For the honor of his great name, he will never leave his people.

## 1 Samuel 12:23

If I stopped praying for you, I would sin against the LORD.

I will keep praying for you.

I will keep teaching you to do what is good and right.

## 1 Samuel 12:24

Above all, honor the LORD.

Serve him faithfully with all your heart.

Remember the great things he has done for you.

**Pause and answer**

What did Samuel promise to keep doing for the people?

## 1 Samuel 12:25

But if you keep doing evil, you and your king will all be swept away.”

**Reading help**

Here “swept away” means that both the people and their king would face destruction.
