# 1 Samuel 19 — Jonathan and Michal help David

**About this chapter**

Saul wants David dead. Two of Saul’s children help David. Later David goes to Samuel and tells him what has happened.

## Jonathan warns David

## 1 Samuel 19:1

Saul told his son Jonathan and all his servants to kill David.

Jonathan loved his friend David.

## 1 Samuel 19:2

Jonathan warned him, “My father Saul wants to kill you.

Be careful tomorrow morning.

Find a place to hide.

## 1 Samuel 19:3

I will stand with my father in the field near you.

I will ask him about you.

Then I will tell you what I learn.”

## Jonathan speaks to his father

## 1 Samuel 19:4

Jonathan told Saul how much good David had done.

He said, “David serves you and has done you no wrong.

You must not do wrong to him.

Think how much he has helped you.

## 1 Samuel 19:5

David risked his life when he killed Goliath.

That day the LORD saved all Israel.

You saw it and were glad.

Why kill an innocent man for no reason?”

**Reading help**

Jonathan calls David innocent because David has done nothing to deserve death.

## 1 Samuel 19:6

Saul listened to Jonathan and made a solemn promise.

“As surely as the LORD lives, no one will kill David.”

## 1 Samuel 19:7

Jonathan called David and told him everything Saul had said.

Then Jonathan brought David back to Saul.

David served Saul as before.

**Pause and answer**

What promise did Saul make after he listened to Jonathan?

## David escapes from Saul

## 1 Samuel 19:8

War broke out again.

David fought the Philistines.

He defeated them, and they ran from him.

## 1 Samuel 19:9

Saul sat in his house holding his spear.

A spirit from the LORD came and troubled Saul deeply.

David was playing the harp.

## 1 Samuel 19:10

Saul tried to pin David to the wall with his spear.

David got away, and the spear hit the wall.

That night David fled.

## Michal helps David leave

## 1 Samuel 19:11

Saul sent men to watch David’s house.

They were to kill David in the morning.

Michal, David’s wife, warned him, “Run for your life tonight, or tomorrow you will die!”

## 1 Samuel 19:12

Michal lowered David through a window.

David ran away to safety.

**Pause and answer**

Who warned David that his life was in danger that night?

## 1 Samuel 19:13

Michal laid a household idol in the bed.

She put goat hair on its head and covered it with clothing.

**Reading help**

A household idol was a figure that people kept in their home and worshiped.

## 1 Samuel 19:14

Saul sent his men to take David away.

Michal told them, “He is sick.”

## Saul questions Michal

## 1 Samuel 19:15

Saul sent the men back to see David.

“Bring him to me in his bed so I can kill him.”

## 1 Samuel 19:16

The men went in.

They found the idol in bed with goat hair on its head.

## 1 Samuel 19:17

Saul asked Michal, “Why did you trick me?

You let my enemy escape!”

Michal answered, “David said he would kill me unless I helped him get away.”

## David goes to Samuel

## 1 Samuel 19:18

After David escaped, he went to Samuel at Ramah.

David told Samuel everything Saul had done to him.

Then David and Samuel went to stay at Naioth.

**Pause and answer**

What did David tell Samuel?

## 1 Samuel 19:19

Someone told Saul, “David is at Naioth in Ramah.”

## 1 Samuel 19:20

Saul sent men to take David away.

They saw Samuel leading a group of prophets.

The prophets were speaking messages from God.

Then God’s Spirit came upon Saul’s men.

They too began to speak messages from God.

**Reading help**

To speak a message from God is to prophesy. A person who gives such messages is called a prophet.

## 1 Samuel 19:21

Saul heard what happened and sent a second group.

Those men also began to speak messages from God.

Saul sent a third group.

The same thing happened to them.

## Saul goes to Ramah himself

## 1 Samuel 19:22

Then Saul himself went toward Ramah.

At the large water pit in Secu, he asked, “Where are Samuel and David?”

Someone answered, “At Naioth in Ramah.”

## 1 Samuel 19:23

Saul set out for Naioth.

God’s Spirit came upon Saul too.

He spoke messages from God all the way to Naioth.

## 1 Samuel 19:24

Saul took off his robes and spoke messages from God in front of Samuel.

He fell to the ground and lay naked all that day and night.

That is why people say, “Is Saul one of the prophets too?”
