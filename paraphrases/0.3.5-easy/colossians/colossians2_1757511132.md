# Colossians 2 — Keep living each day with Christ

**About this chapter**

Paul writes to people who have never met him.

He wants them to recognize ideas that could lead them away from Christ.

## Paul cares for believers he has never met

## Colossians 2:1

I want you to know how hard I struggle for you.

I struggle for the people in Laodicea too.

I care about all who have never met me in person.

## Colossians 2:2

I want you all to take courage and stay close in love.

I want you to understand fully and be sure of what you know.

Then you will know God’s mystery, Christ himself.

## Colossians 2:3

In Christ lie all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge.

## Colossians 2:4

I tell you this so that nobody can fool you with clever words.

## Colossians 2:5

I am far away, yet in spirit I am with you.

I am glad you stand together and trust Christ so firmly.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul say can be found in Christ?

## Stay rooted in Christ

## Colossians 2:6

You welcomed Christ Jesus as your Lord.

Keep living each day with him.

## Colossians 2:7

Let your roots go down into Christ.

Let him build you up.

Stand firm in the faith you learned.

Give thanks to God freely and often.

**Help with these words**

Roots hold a plant in the ground and help it grow.

Paul pictures a believer depending on Christ in the same way.

## Colossians 2:8

Watch out for people who try to trap you with empty ideas and lies.

Their teaching comes from human traditions and this world’s spiritual powers.

It does not come from Christ.

## Colossians 2:9

Everything that makes God who he is lives in Christ in bodily form.

## Colossians 2:10

In Christ you have all you need to be complete.

Christ rules over every ruler and power.

**Pause and answer**

How does Paul picture someone who keeps trusting Christ?

## God gives life and cancels our debt

## Colossians 2:11

Christ gave you a circumcision that no human hands could perform.

He stripped away your old sinful nature.

**Help with these words**

Circumcision usually means cutting away the foreskin of the penis.

Here Paul uses the word for a change Christ makes inside a person.

## Colossians 2:12

In baptism you shared in Christ’s burial.

You also rose with him because you trusted God’s power.

God is the one who raised Christ from the dead.

## Colossians 2:13

You were dead in your sins, still living under the power of your old sinful nature.

God gave you life with Christ.

He forgave us all our sins.

## Colossians 2:14

The written record of our debt stood against us.

God canceled that debt.

He took the record away and nailed it to the cross.

![A scroll with lines of writing](../pictures/scroll.svg)

**Help with these words**

A debt is something you owe.

Paul compares God forgiving sins to someone canceling a debt.

## Colossians 2:15

God stripped the rulers and powers of their strength.

Through the cross he defeated them for everyone to see.

**Pause and answer**

What did God do with the record that stood against us?

## Stay close to Christ

## Colossians 2:16

Let nobody judge you by what you eat or drink.

Let nobody judge you about a feast, a new moon celebration, or a Sabbath day.

## Colossians 2:17

These things were like a shadow of what was coming.

Christ himself is the reality the shadow points to.

## Colossians 2:18

Some people act humble and worship angels.

They dwell on visions they say they have seen.

Their own thinking makes them proud without good reason.

Do not let them rob you of your prize.

## Colossians 2:19

They have lost touch with Christ, the head of the body.

He holds the whole body together through its joints and connecting bands.

God makes the body grow.

**Help with these words**

The body here means the people who belong to Christ.

**Pause and answer**

Who makes the whole body grow?

## Rules about things that wear out

## Colossians 2:20

You died with Christ to this world’s spiritual powers.

Why follow their rules as if you still belonged to the world?

## Colossians 2:21

People tell you, “Do not handle this!

Do not taste that!

Do not touch!”

## Colossians 2:22

The things those rules deal with get used up.

The rules come from what people command and teach.

## Colossians 2:23

Such rules may sound wise.

People invent ways to worship, act humble, and treat their bodies harshly.

None of this stops them from giving in to selfish desires.

**Pause and answer**

Where do these rules come from?
