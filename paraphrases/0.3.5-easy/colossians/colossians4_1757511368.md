# Colossians 4 — Pray for us and share the news

**About this chapter**

Paul asks his friends to pray for him in prison.

He tells them who will bring news and where to send his letter.

## Masters also answer to God

## Colossians 4:1

Masters, give your slaves what is right and fair.

Remember that you also have a Master in heaven.

**Pause and answer**

Why should masters act fairly?

## Pray for a chance to speak

## Colossians 4:2

Keep praying.

Stay alert as you pray, and give thanks.

## Colossians 4:3

Please pray for us too.

Ask God to open a door for our message.

We want to tell people what God has made known about Christ.

That message is why I am in chains.

**Help with these words**

An open door pictures a chance to tell people the good news.

## Colossians 4:4

Pray that I will explain it clearly, as I should.

## Colossians 4:5

Act wisely toward people outside the church.

Make good use of every opportunity.

## Colossians 4:6

Speak kindly at all times.

Let your words be like food seasoned with salt.

Then you will know how to answer each person.

**Help with these words**

Salt gives food flavor.

Paul wants people to take care with the words they offer a listener.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul ask his friends to pray about his speaking?

## Two friends bring news

## Colossians 4:7

Tychicus will tell you all the news about me.

He is our dear brother.

He serves the Lord faithfully alongside me.

## Colossians 4:8

I sent Tychicus so you could hear how we are.

I also want him to give you courage.

## Colossians 4:9

Onesimus is coming with him.

He is one of your own people, a faithful brother we love.

Together they will tell you everything happening here.

**Pause and answer**

Who is coming with Tychicus?

## Greetings from Paul’s friends

## Colossians 4:10

Aristarchus is in prison with me and sends his greetings.

Mark, the cousin of Barnabas, greets you too.

You already have instructions about Mark.

Welcome him if he comes.

## Colossians 4:11

Jesus, also called Justus, sends greetings too.

These three are my only Jewish fellow workers for God’s kingdom.

They have been a comfort to me.

## Colossians 4:12

Epaphras sends greetings.

He comes from your community and serves Christ Jesus.

He keeps praying hard for you.

He asks that you grow strong in faith and stand sure of all that God wants.

## Colossians 4:13

I have seen how hard Epaphras works for you.

He works hard for the people in Laodicea and Hierapolis too.

## Colossians 4:14

Luke, the doctor we love, sends greetings.

Demas greets you too.

**Pause and answer**

What does Epaphras keep doing for the people in Colossae?

## Read the letters in both churches

## Colossians 4:15

Greet the believers in Laodicea.

Greet Nympha and the church that meets in her home.

## Colossians 4:16

Read this letter aloud among yourselves.

Then have the church in Laodicea read it too.

You in turn should read the letter from Laodicea.

| In Colossae | In Laodicea |
| --- | --- |
| Read this letter and the letter from Laodicea. | Read the letter sent to Colossae too. |

## Colossians 4:17

Give Archippus this message.

“Finish the work the Lord has given you.”

## Colossians 4:18

I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand.

Remember that I am in chains.

May God show you his grace.

**Pause and answer**

Where else does Paul want this letter read?
