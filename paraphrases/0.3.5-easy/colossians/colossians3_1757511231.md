# Colossians 3 — Put on love

**About this chapter**

Paul compares changing how we live to changing clothes.

He describes what love looks like at home and at work.

## Look to Christ

## Colossians 3:1

God raised you to life with Christ.

So seek what matters in heaven.

Christ sits there at God’s right hand.

**Help with these words**

A place at a king’s right hand was a place of honor and authority.

## Colossians 3:2

Give your attention to what matters in heaven.

Look beyond the things of earth.

## Colossians 3:3

You died, and your life is now hidden with Christ in God.

**Help with these words**

Paul is speaking to living people.

Their old life has ended, and their new life belongs to Christ.

## Colossians 3:4

Christ is your life.

When Christ appears, you too will appear with him in glory.

**Pause and answer**

Where does Paul say Christ sits?

## Leave the old ways behind

## Colossians 3:5

Put an end to the wrong desires that once ruled you.

Leave behind sexual wrongdoing, impurity, lust, and evil desires.

Give up greed too.

Greedy people worship what they want like a god.

## Colossians 3:6

Because of such things, God’s anger is coming on those who disobey him.

## Colossians 3:7

You once lived that way too.

That was how you acted among them.

## Colossians 3:8

Now put aside anger and fits of rage.

Give up the wish to hurt others.

Stop telling lies about people or using filthy words.

## Colossians 3:9

Tell each other the truth.

You have taken off your old self and its habits like old clothes.

## Colossians 3:10

You have put on your new self.

God made you and keeps making you new.

He gives you understanding so you become more like him.

## Colossians 3:11

Among you are Greeks and Jews, circumcised and uncircumcised people.

Some come from foreign lands, including Scythia.

Some are slaves and others are free.

Christ is everything, and Christ is in you all.

**Help with these words**

Circumcision was a mark on the body of Jewish men.

Scythians were a people from outside the Greek world.

**Pause and answer**

What does taking off the old clothes mean in Paul’s picture?

## Put on love

## Colossians 3:12

God chose you, set you apart, and loves you.

Put on kindness as you would put on clothes.

Care when others hurt.

Be humble, gentle, and patient.

![A cloak to put on](../pictures/cloak.svg)

## Colossians 3:13

Be patient with each other’s faults.

When someone gives you a reason to complain, forgive them.

Remember how the Lord forgave you.

## Colossians 3:14

Over all these clothes, put on love.

Love holds you together in complete unity.

## Colossians 3:15

Let Christ’s peace guide your hearts.

God called you to peace as parts of one body.

Be thankful.

**Help with these words**

The body here means all the people who belong to Christ.

## Colossians 3:16

Give Christ’s message a lasting home among you.

Teach each other wisely and warn each other when needed.

Sing psalms, hymns, and songs from the Spirit.

Sing with thankful hearts to God.

## Colossians 3:17

Whatever you say or do, do it in the name of the Lord Jesus.

Through Jesus give thanks to God the Father.

**Pause and answer**

Whose forgiveness gives you an example to follow?

## Life with your family

## Colossians 3:18

Wives, yield to your husbands as is fitting for those who belong to the Lord.

## Colossians 3:19

Husbands, love your wives.

Never treat them harshly.

## Colossians 3:20

Children, obey your parents in everything.

This pleases the Lord.

## Colossians 3:21

Fathers, do not keep stirring up anger in your children.

They may lose heart.

**Pause and answer**

How should husbands treat their wives?

## Serve the Lord in your work

## Colossians 3:22

Slaves, obey your earthly masters in everything.

Do more than try to please them while they watch.

Serve honestly, with deep respect for the Lord.

## Colossians 3:23

Whatever your work, do it with your whole heart.

Remember that you work for the Lord.

## Colossians 3:24

The Lord will give you the inheritance he promised as your reward.

Christ is the Lord you serve.

## Colossians 3:25

Anyone who does wrong will answer for it.

God shows no favoritism.

**Pause and answer**

Who does Paul say will give the reward?
