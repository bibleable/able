# Colossians 1 — Christ has first place in everything

**About this chapter**

Epaphras brought Paul news from Colossae.

Paul tells the believers there why he thanks God for them.

## News from Epaphras

## Colossians 1:1

I am Paul.

God chose me to be an apostle of Christ Jesus.

Our brother Timothy joins me in writing.

**Help with these words**

An apostle is someone Jesus sends to speak for him.

## Colossians 1:2

We greet God’s people in Colossae, our faithful brothers and sisters in Christ.

May God our Father give you grace and peace.

## Colossians 1:3

Whenever we pray for you, we thank God.

He is the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.

## Colossians 1:4

We heard that you trust Christ Jesus.

We heard that you love all God’s people.

## Colossians 1:5

Your trust and love grow from your hope.

God keeps what you hope for safe in heaven.

You heard about this hope in the good news, the message of truth.

## Colossians 1:6

That good news has reached you.

Around the world it keeps growing and bearing fruit.

It has done the same among you since you first heard it and understood God’s kindness.

![Grapes growing on a vine](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

**Help with these words**

A plant bears fruit as it grows.

Here the fruit is the good the message does in people’s lives.

## Colossians 1:7

Our dear friend Epaphras taught you this good news.

He serves beside us and faithfully does Christ’s work on our behalf.

## Colossians 1:8

Epaphras told us about the love the Spirit gives you.

**Pause and answer**

## What Paul asks God to give them

## Colossians 1:9

Since we heard about you, we keep praying for you.

We ask God to show you fully what he wants.

May his Spirit give you wisdom and understanding.

## Colossians 1:10

Then you can live in a way that honors the Lord.

You can please him in everything you do.

May every good thing you do bear fruit.

May you come to know God better.

## Colossians 1:11

We ask God to strengthen you with his great power.

Then you can be patient and keep going.

## Colossians 1:12

Give thanks with joy to the Father.

He has made you fit to share what belongs to his people in the light.

## Colossians 1:13

God rescued us from the power of darkness.

He brought us into the kingdom of the Son he loves.

## Colossians 1:14

Through his Son we have been set free.

God has forgiven our sins.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul ask God to help the believers understand?

## Who God’s Son is

## Colossians 1:15

We cannot see God.

His Son shows us what God is like.

The Son is the firstborn over everything God made.

**Help with these words**

Here “firstborn” describes the Son’s place of honor over all creation.

## Colossians 1:16

Through the Son, God made all things in heaven and on earth.

That includes things we see and things we cannot see.

Every throne, ruler, and power owes its existence to him.

God made all things through his Son and for his Son.

## Colossians 1:17

The Son was there before anything else.

He holds everything together.

## Colossians 1:18

The church is his body, and he is its head.

He is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead.

So he has first place in everything.

## Colossians 1:19

God was pleased to have all his fullness live in his Son.

## Colossians 1:20

Through his Son, God made peace.

Jesus shed his blood on the cross.

Through him God brings all things on earth and in heaven back to himself.

**Pause and answer**

Who holds everything together?

## Hold on to the good news

## Colossians 1:21

Once you were far from God.

Your thoughts turned against him, and you did evil things.

## Colossians 1:22

Now God has brought you back through Christ’s death in a human body.

He wants to bring you into his presence holy, without fault or blame.

## Colossians 1:23

Keep trusting Christ and standing firm.

Hold on to the hope you heard in the good news.

That message has gone out to every creature under heaven.

I, Paul, have become its servant.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul ask the believers to hold on to?

## Why Paul works so hard

## Colossians 1:24

I rejoice even as I suffer for you.

In my body I bear what remains of Christ’s sufferings for his body, the church.

## Colossians 1:25

God gave me the work of serving the church.

My task is to tell you God’s whole message.

## Colossians 1:26

For many generations God kept this mystery hidden.

Now he has made it known to his people.

## Colossians 1:27

God wanted his people to know how glorious this mystery is among the Gentiles.

Christ is in you.

Because of him, you can hope to share in glory.

**Help with these words**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish.

## Colossians 1:28

Christ is the one we tell people about.

We warn and teach everyone with all the wisdom we have.

We want to bring each person before God fully grown in Christ.

## Colossians 1:29

That is why I work so hard.

Christ gives me his strength, and his power works in me.
