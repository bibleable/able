# Zechariah 10 — The LORD calls His people home

**About this chapter**

Zechariah tells the people who sends the rain their crops need. The LORD promises to care for His scattered people and bring their families home.

## Ask the LORD for rain

## Zechariah 10:1

When spring comes, ask the LORD for rain.

The LORD makes the storm clouds.

He sends showers so that everyone can grow food in the fields.

## Zechariah 10:2

Idols give people lies.

Those who claim to tell the future see things that are not real.

They tell people false dreams.

Their comforting words give no help.

So the people wander like sheep without a shepherd.

They suffer because no one guides them.

## The LORD cares for Judah

## Zechariah 10:3

The LORD says, “I am angry with the shepherds.

I will punish those leaders.”

The people of Judah are like a flock of sheep.

The LORD who leads heaven’s armies cares for them.

He will make them strong like a king’s horse in battle.

## Zechariah 10:4

From Judah will come the cornerstone and the tent peg.

A cornerstone holds a place of strength in a building.

A tent peg holds a tent in place.

The battle bow and all the rulers will also come from Judah.

![A cornerstone at the corner of a wall.](../pictures/cornerstone.svg)

## Zechariah 10:5

Judah’s people will be like strong soldiers.

They will tread their enemies down in the muddy streets.

They will fight because the LORD is with them.

They will put enemy riders to shame.

## Families will rejoice

## Zechariah 10:6

The LORD says, “I will make the people of Judah strong.

I will rescue the people of Joseph.

I care about their suffering, so I will bring them back.

It will be as if I had never turned them away.

I am the LORD their God.

I will answer them.

## Zechariah 10:7

“Ephraim’s people will be strong like a warrior.

They will be as glad as people celebrating with wine.

Their children will see it and be happy.

They will rejoice in the LORD.

## A call to come home

## Zechariah 10:8

“I will whistle to call My people together.

I have set them free.

There will be as many of them as before.

## Zechariah 10:9

“Though I scatter them among nations like seed, they will remember Me.

Even in faraway lands they will think of Me.

They and their children will live and come back.

## Zechariah 10:10

“I will bring them back from Egypt.

I will gather them from Assyria.

I will take them to Gilead and Lebanon.

So many will come that the land will fill up.

## Zechariah 10:11

“They will pass through trouble like people crossing a sea.

They will strike its waves.

Even the deepest parts of the Nile River will dry up.

Proud Assyria will be humbled.

Egypt will lose its power to rule.

## Zechariah 10:12

“I, the LORD, will give My people strength.

They will live their lives in My name.”

The LORD has said this.
