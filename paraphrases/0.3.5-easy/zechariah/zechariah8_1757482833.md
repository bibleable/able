# Zechariah 8 — Children will play in Jerusalem again

**About this chapter**

God promises to live among his people again. Zechariah describes old people resting in the streets and children playing there. People from other nations will come to Jerusalem to seek God.

## God will bring his people home

## Zechariah 8:1

The LORD who commands heaven's armies spoke to me again.

## Zechariah 8:2

He said, “I love Zion deeply.

I care fiercely about what happens to my city.”

## Zechariah 8:3

The LORD said, “I will return to Zion.

I will live in Jerusalem.

People will call Jerusalem the City of Truth.

They will call my mountain the Holy Mountain.”

## Zechariah 8:4

The LORD who commands heaven's armies said,

“Old men and women will sit along Jerusalem's streets again.

They will have lived long enough to need walking sticks.

## Zechariah 8:5

“The streets will be full of boys and girls playing.”

## Zechariah 8:6

The LORD asked, “To the few of you who are left, this may seem impossible. Is it impossible for me?”

## Zechariah 8:7

He said, “I will rescue my people from lands in the east and the west.

## Zechariah 8:8

“I will bring them home to live in Jerusalem.

They will be my people. I will be their God.

I will keep my word and do what is right.”

## Take courage and keep building

## Zechariah 8:9

The LORD who commands heaven's armies said, “Keep your hands strong for the work.

The builders laid the foundation for my temple.

The prophets were there when that work began.

You are hearing their words now.

## Zechariah 8:10

“Before that, people earned nothing for their work.

They earned nothing from the work of their animals either.

Enemies made it unsafe to come and go.

I had turned neighbors against each other.

## Zechariah 8:11

“But now I will treat the people who are left differently.

## Zechariah 8:12

“The seed you plant will grow well.

Vines will bear grapes. The soil will produce crops.

Dew will fall from the sky.

I will give all these things to my people to keep.

![Grapes growing on a vine. God promises the people a harvest.](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

## Zechariah 8:13

“Families of Judah and Israel, other nations used your name in curses.

I will save you. You will become a blessing.

Take courage and keep working.”

## Tell the truth and judge fairly

## Zechariah 8:14

The LORD who commands heaven's armies said,

“Your ancestors made me angry.

I decided to bring disaster on you, and I carried out that decision.

## Zechariah 8:15

“Now I have decided to do good to Jerusalem and Judah again.

Do not be afraid.

## Zechariah 8:16

“Here is what I ask of you. Tell each other the truth.

When you hear cases at the city gates, judge fairly.

Give decisions that help people live in peace.

## Zechariah 8:17

“Do not plan to hurt your neighbor.

Do not delight in lying after promising to tell the truth.

I hate these things,” says the LORD.

## Days of fasting will become feasts

## Zechariah 8:18

The LORD who commands heaven's armies spoke to me again.

## Zechariah 8:19

“You fast during the fourth, fifth, seventh, and tenth months.”

To fast means to go without food for a time.

“Those days will become joyful feasts for the people of Judah.

So love truth and peace.”

## People from many cities will come

## Zechariah 8:20

The LORD said, “People will come from many cities.

## Zechariah 8:21

“People from one city will visit another and say,

‘Come with us now! Let us ask the LORD for his favor.

Let us seek the LORD who commands heaven's armies.

I am going myself.’

## Zechariah 8:22

“Many peoples and powerful nations will come to Jerusalem.

They will seek me and ask for my favor.”

## Zechariah 8:23

The LORD who commands heaven's armies said,

“In those days, ten men from nations with different languages will take hold of the robe of a Jewish person.

They will say, ‘Let us go with you.

We have heard that God is with you.’”
