# Zechariah 7 — The LORD asks His people to show mercy

**About this chapter**

Visitors ask whether they should keep fasting. The LORD asks them to remember how He told their ancestors to treat other people.

## A question from Bethel

## Zechariah 7:1

The LORD spoke to Zechariah in King Darius’s fourth year as king.

It was the fourth day of Chislev, the ninth month.

## Zechariah 7:2

The people of Bethel sent Sharezer and Regem-melech with their men.

They went to ask the LORD for His favor.

## Zechariah 7:3

They spoke to the priests at the temple and to the prophets.

“For many years we have wept and fasted in the fifth month.

Should we keep doing this?”

To fast means to go without food for a time.

## Who was the fast for?

## Zechariah 7:4

Then the LORD who leads heaven’s armies spoke to me.

## Zechariah 7:5

He said, “Ask the priests and everyone in the land this question.

For seventy years you fasted and grieved in the fifth and seventh months.

Was it really for Me that you fasted?

## Zechariah 7:6

“When you ate and drank, who was it for?

Was it just for yourselves?

## Zechariah 7:7

“Remember what I said through the prophets long ago.

Jerusalem and the towns around it were full of people then.

They had plenty to live on.

People also lived in the southern region called the Negev and in the foothills.

Did the prophets not give you this same message?”

## Treat people fairly

## Zechariah 7:8

Then the LORD spoke to Zechariah again.

## Zechariah 7:9

The LORD who leads heaven’s armies said, “Judge each case fairly.

Keep loving one another.

Show kindness when someone is hurting.

## Zechariah 7:10

“Do not mistreat women whose husbands have died.

Do not mistreat children who have lost their fathers.

Do not mistreat foreigners or people who are poor.

Do not make plans to harm one another.”

## The people refused to listen

## Zechariah 7:11

But the people turned their backs.

They covered their ears.

They refused to listen.

## Zechariah 7:12

Their hearts became as hard as flint, a very hard stone.

This means they stubbornly refused to change.

They would not listen to God’s law.

The LORD had sent His words by His Spirit through the earlier prophets.

The people ignored those words too.

So the LORD who leads heaven’s armies became very angry.

## Zechariah 7:13

The LORD said, “When I called to them, they would not listen.

So when they called to Me, I would not listen.

## Zechariah 7:14

“I scattered them like a powerful wind blowing in circles.

They went to live among nations they did not know.

The land they left behind lay empty.

No one came or went.

This is how they turned a pleasant land into a lonely, empty place.”
