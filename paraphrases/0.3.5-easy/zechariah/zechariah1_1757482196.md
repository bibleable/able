# Zechariah 1 — God promises to return to Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

The LORD asks his people to return to him. Later, Zechariah sees riders among the trees at night. He hears a promise of mercy for Jerusalem.

## Return to me

## Zechariah 1:1

Darius was in his second year as king.

In the eighth month, the LORD spoke to Zechariah the prophet.

Zechariah was the son of Berechiah and the grandson of Iddo.

## Zechariah 1:2

The LORD said, “I was very angry with your ancestors.

## Zechariah 1:3

“Give the people this message from the LORD who commands heaven's armies.

‘Return to me, and I will return to you.’

## Zechariah 1:4

“Remember what happened to your ancestors.

Earlier prophets brought them my message.

They said, ‘Change your ways. Stop doing evil.’

But your ancestors paid no attention to me.

## Zechariah 1:5

“Where are your ancestors now?

And what about the prophets? Do they live forever?

## Zechariah 1:6

“My servants the prophets told your ancestors my words and commands.

What I warned them about came true.

Then your ancestors turned back and said,

‘The LORD who commands heaven's armies did what he said he would do.

He dealt with us according to what we had done.’”

## Riders among the trees

## Zechariah 1:7

Later that same year, the LORD spoke to Zechariah again.

It was the twenty-fourth day of Shebat, the eleventh month.

## Zechariah 1:8

Zechariah said, “That night I saw a man on a red horse.

He had stopped among myrtle trees on low ground.

Behind him were red, reddish-brown, and white horses.”

## Zechariah 1:9

I asked the angel who was talking with me, “Sir, what are these?”

The angel said, “I will show you.”

## Zechariah 1:10

The man among the myrtle trees explained,

“The LORD sent these riders to travel around the earth and see what is happening.”

## Zechariah 1:11

The riders reported to the LORD's angel among the trees.

“We have traveled around the earth.

Everywhere we went, the land was quiet and at rest.”

## The angel asks about Jerusalem

## Zechariah 1:12

Then the LORD's angel asked, “LORD who commands heaven's armies,

you have been angry with Jerusalem and Judah's towns for seventy years.

How much longer before you show them mercy?”

## Zechariah 1:13

The LORD answered the angel who was talking with me.

His words were kind and brought comfort.

## Zechariah 1:14

The angel told me, “Give the people this message.

The LORD who commands heaven's armies says,

‘I care deeply about Jerusalem, about Zion, my city.

## Zechariah 1:15

“‘I am very angry with the nations that now live at ease.

I was only a little angry with my people.

But those nations made my people's suffering worse.’

## Zechariah 1:16

“The LORD says, ‘I will come back to Jerusalem with mercy.

People will rebuild my house, the temple.

They will stretch a measuring line across Jerusalem.’”

Builders use a line to measure where to build.

## Zechariah 1:17

The angel told me to announce another promise.

“The LORD's cities will again have more than enough good things.

He will comfort Zion again and choose Jerusalem as his own.”

## Four horns and four workers

## Zechariah 1:18

Then I looked up and saw four horns.

![Four curved horns. In Zechariah's vision they stand for nations that scattered the people.](../pictures/zechariah-four-horns.svg)

## Zechariah 1:19

I asked the angel, “What do these horns mean?”

He answered, “They stand for the powers that scattered the people of Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem.”

## Zechariah 1:20

Then the LORD showed me four skilled workers.

## Zechariah 1:21

I asked, “What have these workers come to do?”

He answered, “Those horns are the nations that used their strength against Judah.

They scattered the people. No one dared lift their head.

Now the workers have come to frighten those powers and bring them down.”
