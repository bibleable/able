# Zechariah 13 — The LORD answers His people

**About this chapter**

Zechariah tells what the LORD promises for a coming day. The LORD will take away sin. After a time of great trouble, people will call to Him and He will answer.

## A spring of water

## Zechariah 13:1

“On that day a spring will open.

Water will flow for David’s family and the people of Jerusalem.

It will wash away their sin and everything that makes them unclean.”

The flowing water shows how God will make His people clean.

## Zechariah 13:2

The LORD who leads heaven’s armies says, “I will erase the names of the idols from the land.

No one will remember those false gods.

I will also remove the false prophets.

I will drive out the spirit that makes people unclean.”

## People stop claiming to speak for God

## Zechariah 13:3

If a man still claims to speak for the LORD, his own parents will answer him.

They will say, “You told lies in the LORD’s name.

You cannot stay alive.”

When he gives that false message, his parents will stab him.

## Zechariah 13:4

On that day these prophets will feel ashamed of their visions.

They once wore rough, hairy coats to fool people.

They wanted people to think they spoke for God.

They will stop wearing those coats.

## Zechariah 13:5

Each will say, “I am no prophet.

I work the land.

Someone bought me as a servant when I was young.”

## Zechariah 13:6

Someone will ask, “How did you get those wounds on your chest?”

The man will answer, “I got them at my friends’ house.”

## The shepherd and the sheep

## Zechariah 13:7

The LORD who leads heaven’s armies speaks to a sword.

“Wake up, sword, against My shepherd!

He is the man who stands beside Me.

Strike the shepherd, and the sheep will scatter.

I will turn against the little ones.”

Sheep scatter when they lose the shepherd who keeps them together.

## Zechariah 13:8

The LORD says, “Across the land, two out of every three will die.

One out of every three will remain.

## Zechariah 13:9

“I will bring those who remain through fire.

People heat silver to make it pure.

They test gold in the fire.

That is how I will purify and test My people.

They will call to Me, and I will answer.

I will say, ‘They are My people.’

They will say, ‘The LORD is our God.’”
