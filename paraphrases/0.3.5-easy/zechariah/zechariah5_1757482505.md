# Zechariah 5 — The flying scroll and the basket

**About this chapter**

Zechariah describes two visions. In the first, he sees a scroll flying. In the second, he sees a basket carried away.

## A scroll flies over the land

## Zechariah 5:1

I looked up again.

This time I saw a scroll flying.

A scroll is a long sheet people can write on and roll up.

![An open scroll with lines of writing.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## Zechariah 5:2

The angel asked me, “What do you see?”

I said, “I see a flying scroll.

It is twenty cubits long and ten cubits wide.”

A cubit was about the length from an elbow to the fingertips.

## Zechariah 5:3

The angel said, “This scroll carries a curse across the whole land.”

A curse here is a message of punishment.

“One side says that every thief must leave the land.

The other side says the same about people who lie under oath.”

To lie under oath is to promise to tell the truth and then lie.

## Zechariah 5:4

The LORD who leads heaven’s armies says, “I will send out the curse.

It will enter the house of anyone who steals.

It will enter the house of anyone who uses My name to swear a lie.

It will stay there and destroy the house.

Even the wood and stones will be gone.”

## A woman in a basket

## Zechariah 5:5

Then the angel came forward again.

He said, “Look up. See what is coming.”

## Zechariah 5:6

I asked, “What is it?”

The angel answered, “A basket that people use for measuring.

It shows the wrong that people do throughout the land.”

## Zechariah 5:7

Then the lid lifted off the basket.

The lid was made of lead, a heavy metal.

A woman sat inside the basket.

## Zechariah 5:8

The angel said, “This is Wickedness.”

In this vision, the woman stands for the evil people do.

The angel pushed her down into the basket.

He pressed the heavy lid back into place.

## Two women carry the basket away

## Zechariah 5:9

I looked up and saw two women coming.

They had wings like the wings of a stork, a large bird.

The wind filled their wings.

They lifted the basket high above the ground.

## Zechariah 5:10

I asked the angel, “Where are they taking the basket?”

## Zechariah 5:11

The angel answered, “They are taking it to the land of Shinar.

They will build a house for it there.

When the house is ready, they will set the basket on a raised stand.”
