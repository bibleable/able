# Zechariah 12 — The LORD defends Jerusalem and its people mourn

**About this chapter**

Armies will come against Jerusalem.

The LORD promises to defend the city.

He also tells why families throughout the land will weep.

## The LORD speaks about Jerusalem

## Zechariah 12:1

This is the LORD’s message about Israel.

The LORD stretches out the sky.

He sets the earth firmly in place.

He forms the spirit within each person.

## Zechariah 12:2

The LORD says, “I will make Jerusalem like a cup of wine.

The peoples around the city will stagger like people who drank too much.”

The cup pictures what will happen to Jerusalem’s enemies.

Their armies will surround Jerusalem and the rest of Judah.

![A cup like the one in the LORD’s message about Jerusalem.](../pictures/cup.svg)

## Zechariah 12:3

The LORD says, “On that day all nations will gather against Jerusalem.

I will make Jerusalem like a heavy stone.

Anyone who tries to lift it will get badly hurt.”

So the nations will hurt themselves by attacking Jerusalem.

## Zechariah 12:4

The LORD says, “I will terrify every enemy horse.

I will make the riders lose their minds.

I will watch over the people of Judah.

But I will make all the enemy horses blind.

## Zechariah 12:5

“Then each leader of Judah will think this.

‘The people of Jerusalem give me strength.

Their God is the LORD who commands heaven’s armies.’”

## The LORD will protect Judah and Jerusalem

## Zechariah 12:6

The LORD says, “Judah’s families will be like a pot of burning coals in a woodpile.

They will be like a flaming torch among bundles of grain.”

Fire spreads quickly through dry wood or grain.

In the same way, Judah will defeat the peoples on every side.

The people of Jerusalem will stay safe in their city.

## Zechariah 12:7

The LORD will first save the people in Judah’s tents.

David’s royal family will share the honor with Judah.

So will the people of Jerusalem.

Neither group will have greater honor than Judah.

## Zechariah 12:8

On that day the LORD will defend Jerusalem’s people.

Even the weakest person will be strong like King David.

David’s family will be like God among the people.

They will lead like the LORD’s angel going ahead of them.

## Zechariah 12:9

The LORD says, “On that day I will act.

I will destroy all the nations that attack Jerusalem.”

## The people will mourn for the one they pierced

## Zechariah 12:10

The LORD says, “I will give David’s family a spirit of grace and prayer.

I will give it to Jerusalem’s people too.

They will turn to me in prayer for my kindness.

They will look at me, the one they pierced.”

To pierce someone means to wound them with something sharp.

They will weep for him like parents grieving for an only child.

Their sorrow will be as deep as losing a firstborn son.

## Zechariah 12:11

On that day people throughout Jerusalem will cry aloud.

Their cries will be like the cries at Hadad-rimmon on the plain of Megiddo.

## Zechariah 12:12

Families across the land will mourn.

Each family group will mourn on its own.

The men of David’s family will mourn, and their wives will mourn separately.

The men of Nathan’s family and their wives will do the same.

## Zechariah 12:13

The men of Levi’s family will mourn.

Their wives will mourn separately.

The men of Shimei’s family and their wives will do the same.

## Zechariah 12:14

All the other families will mourn too.

Each family will grieve on its own, with the wives mourning separately.
