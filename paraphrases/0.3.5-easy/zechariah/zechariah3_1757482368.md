# Zechariah 3 — Clean clothes for Joshua

**About this chapter**

Zechariah tells what God showed him in a vision. In the vision, Joshua receives clean clothes. The change of clothes shows that God has taken away Joshua’s guilt.

## Satan accuses Joshua

## Zechariah 3:1

The angel showed me Joshua, the high priest.

As high priest, Joshua led the priests in worship.

Joshua stood before the angel of the LORD.

Satan stood on Joshua’s right, ready to accuse him.

## Zechariah 3:2

The LORD said to Satan, “The LORD speaks against you, Satan!

The LORD chose Jerusalem, and He speaks against you.

Joshua is like a burning stick pulled from a fire.”

This means Joshua has been rescued from danger.

## Clean clothes for Joshua

## Zechariah 3:3

Joshua stood before the angel in very dirty clothes.

## Zechariah 3:4

The angel spoke to those standing there.

“Take off Joshua’s dirty clothes.”

Then the angel told Joshua, “I have taken away your guilt.

I will give you fine clothes to wear.”

## Zechariah 3:5

Then I said, “Put a clean turban on his head.”

A turban is cloth wrapped around the head.

They put the clean turban on Joshua and dressed him.

The angel of the LORD stood beside them.

## Joshua hears the LORD’s promise

## Zechariah 3:6

Then the angel of the LORD gave Joshua instructions.

## Zechariah 3:7

The angel said, “Here is what the LORD says.”

The LORD leads the armies of heaven.

“Joshua, follow My ways and do what I tell you.

Then you will be in charge of My temple.

You will look after its courtyards, the open spaces around it.

I will give you a place among those standing here.

## Zechariah 3:8

“Listen, Joshua, you and the men sitting before you.

You are a sign of what I am going to do.

I will bring My servant, whom I call the Branch.

## Zechariah 3:9

“Look at the stone I have put before Joshua.

That one stone has seven eyes.

I will carve words into the stone.

In a single day I will take away the guilt of this land.

## Zechariah 3:10

“On that day each of you will invite a neighbor over.

You will sit together under your grapevine and fig tree.”

The LORD who leads the armies of heaven promises this.
