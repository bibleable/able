# Zechariah 9 — A humble king will bring peace

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks about cities near Israel.

He promises to watch over his people.

Jerusalem’s king will come riding a young donkey.

## The LORD speaks against the northern cities

## Zechariah 9:1

The LORD gives a message against the land of Hadrach.

The message also reaches Damascus.

People everywhere are looking to the LORD.

So are all the tribes of Israel.

## Zechariah 9:2

The message includes Hamath, which borders Damascus.

It includes Tyre and Sidon too, clever as their people are.

## Zechariah 9:3

The people of Tyre built a strong fortress.

They piled up silver like dust.

Gold was as plentiful as dirt in their streets.

## Zechariah 9:4

The Lord will take away Tyre’s wealth.

He will throw its riches into the sea.

Fire will burn up the city.

## The LORD speaks about the Philistine cities

## Zechariah 9:5

Ashkelon’s people will see this and be afraid.

The people of Gaza and Ekron will suffer terribly.

Ekron will lose hope.

Gaza will lose its king.

No one will live in Ashkelon.

## Zechariah 9:6

People of mixed ancestry will live in Ashdod.

The LORD says, “I will put an end to the Philistines’ pride.

## Zechariah 9:7

“I will take the blood from their mouths.

I will take the forbidden food from between their teeth.”

The Philistines who remain will belong to our God.

They will be like one of Judah’s family groups.

The people of Ekron will have a place among them like the Jebusites.

## Zechariah 9:8

The LORD says, “I will camp around my house to guard it.

I will protect it from armies marching back and forth.

No cruel ruler will overrun my people again.

I am watching with my own eyes.”

## Jerusalem welcomes its king

## Zechariah 9:9

People of Zion, rejoice!

People of Jerusalem, shout for joy!

Look! Your king is coming to you.

He does what is right, and he has won the victory.

He is humble and rides a young donkey.

## Zechariah 9:10

The LORD says, “I will take away Ephraim’s war chariots.

I will take away Jerusalem’s war horses.

The bows used in battle will break.”

The king will announce peace to the nations.

He will rule from sea to sea.

His rule will reach from the Euphrates River to the ends of the earth.

## The LORD promises to free the prisoners

## Zechariah 9:11

The LORD says, “Blood sealed my covenant with you.”

A covenant is a solemn agreement.

“Because of that agreement, I will free your prisoners from the dry pit.

## Zechariah 9:12

“Prisoners, you still have hope!

Come back to your place of safety.

Today I promise to give you back twice what you lost.

## Zechariah 9:13

“Judah will be my bow.

Ephraim will be the arrow I fit to it.

People of Zion, I will stir up your sons against the sons of Greece.

You will be like a strong warrior’s sword.”

These pictures show the LORD working through his people.

## The LORD will save his people

## Zechariah 9:14

The LORD will appear above his people.

His arrow will flash like lightning.

The Lord GOD will blow a ram’s horn.

He will come in swirling winds from the south.

## Zechariah 9:15

The LORD who commands heaven’s armies will protect his people.

They will overcome their enemies with stones from slings.

They will drink and shout as if drinking wine.

Zechariah pictures bowls full of blood for sprinkling at the altar.

He pictures the corners of the altar wet with blood.

That is what the battle will be like.

## Zechariah 9:16

On that day the LORD their God will save them.

They are his people, like sheep in his care.

They will shine across his land like jewels in a crown.

## Zechariah 9:17

How beautiful his people will be!

Grain will help the young men grow strong.

New wine will help the young women thrive.
