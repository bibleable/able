# Ezekiel 15 — Wood from a vine

**About this chapter**

The LORD asks Ezekiel to think about wood from a vine. He uses the wood to explain what will happen to Jerusalem.

## Can you make anything from this wood?

## Ezekiel 15:1

Ezekiel said, “The LORD spoke to me.”

## Ezekiel 15:2

The LORD asked, “Son of man, think about a vine.

It grows among the trees in the forest.

Is its wood better than the branches on those trees?”

“Son of man” is what the LORD calls Ezekiel.

![Grapes hanging from a vine.](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

## Ezekiel 15:3

“Can you make anything useful from the vine’s wood?

Could you even make a peg from it?

A peg would give you somewhere to hang a pot.

## Ezekiel 15:4

“No. People throw the wood into a fire for fuel.

The fire burns away both ends.

The heat turns the middle black.

What can you make from it now?

## Ezekiel 15:5

“You could make nothing useful when the wood was whole.

You can do even less with the burned piece.”

## What the vine wood means for Jerusalem

## Ezekiel 15:6

Then the Lord GOD said, “Think of that forest vine.

I gave its wood to the fire for fuel.

In the same way, I will give up the people of Jerusalem.

## Ezekiel 15:7

“I will turn against them.

Even if they escape one fire, another will burn them up.

When I turn against them, you will know I am the LORD.

## Ezekiel 15:8

“The people have been unfaithful to Me.

That is why I will leave their land empty and ruined.”

The Lord GOD said this.
