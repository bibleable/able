# Ezekiel 14 — The LORD asks Israel to turn back to him

**About this chapter**

Israel’s leaders come to Ezekiel for an answer. The LORD knows what they worship. He also explains what even three good men could do for Jerusalem.

## The leaders come to Ezekiel

## Ezekiel 14:1

Some leaders of Israel came to see me.

They sat down in front of me.

## Ezekiel 14:2

Then the LORD spoke to me.

## Ezekiel 14:3

The LORD called me “son of man.”

He said, “These men have given their hearts to idols.”

An idol is something a person worships as a god.

Their idols were like stones in their path.

They kept tripping over them and doing wrong.

God asked, “Should these men come to me for advice?”

## Ezekiel 14:4

The Lord GOD told me what to say.

“Suppose someone in Israel holds on to idols.

Those idols keep leading that person into sin.

Then the person comes to a prophet to ask for my answer.

My answer will deal with all those idols.”

## Ezekiel 14:5

“Their idols have drawn the Israelites far from me.

I want to win back their hearts.”

## Ezekiel 14:6

The Lord GOD told Israel, “Turn back to me.

Turn away from your idols.

Stop doing the things I hate.”

## Ezekiel 14:7

“This goes for Israelites and foreigners who live among you.

Someone may turn away from me and worship idols.

That person may still ask a prophet for my advice.

I myself will answer that person.”

## Ezekiel 14:8

“I will act against that person.

I will remove that person from among my people.

Others will speak of what happened as a warning.

Then you will know that I am the LORD.”

## Ezekiel 14:9

“Suppose a prophet feels drawn to give a false message.

I, the LORD, am the one who drew him into speaking.

I will act against that prophet.

I will destroy him from among my people Israel.”

## Ezekiel 14:10

The prophet will answer for giving the message.

The person who asked will answer for asking.

Both will face the same punishment.

## Ezekiel 14:11

“Then Israel will stop wandering away from me.

My people will stop making themselves unclean by doing wrong.

They will be my people. I will be their God.”

## Three men who did right

## Ezekiel 14:12

The LORD gave me another message.

## Ezekiel 14:13

“Son of man, suppose the people of a land betray me.

Suppose I cut off their food supply.

A famine would come. A famine is a severe lack of food.

People and animals would die of hunger.”

![An empty bowl shows what it means to have no food.](../pictures/jerusalem-empty-bowl.svg)

## Ezekiel 14:14

“Suppose Noah, Daniel, and Job lived in that land.

All three men did what was right.

Even so, they could save only their own lives.”

## Ezekiel 14:15

“Or suppose I send wild animals through the land.

Families would lose their children. The land would lie empty.

People would be afraid to travel through it.”

## Ezekiel 14:16

The Lord GOD said, “As surely as I live, even those three men could not save their sons or daughters.

Only the three men would survive. The land would lie empty.”

## Ezekiel 14:17

“Or suppose I send war against that land.

I give the order for swords to sweep through it.

People and animals die.”

## Ezekiel 14:18

The Lord GOD gave his word again.

“Even those three men could not save their sons or daughters.

Only the three men would survive.”

## Ezekiel 14:19

“Or suppose I send a plague into that land.”

A plague is a sickness that spreads among many people.

God spoke of showing his anger through bloodshed.

People and animals would die.

## Ezekiel 14:20

The Lord GOD said, “As surely as I live, even Noah, Daniel, and Job could not save their children.

By doing right they could save only their own lives.”

## Some people will come out of Jerusalem

## Ezekiel 14:21

The Lord GOD said Jerusalem would face all four judgments.

War and famine would come.

Wild animals and deadly sickness would come too.

How much worse that would be for people and animals there!

## Ezekiel 14:22

“Yet some sons and daughters will survive.

People will bring them out of Jerusalem to you.

You will see how the survivors live and what they do.

That will bring you comfort after the disaster I sent.”

## Ezekiel 14:23

“When you see how they act, you will understand.

I had reason for everything I did in Jerusalem.”

The Lord GOD gave this message.

**Think about it**

**Whose lives could Noah, Daniel, and Job save by doing right?**
