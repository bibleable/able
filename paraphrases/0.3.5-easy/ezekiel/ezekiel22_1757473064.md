# Ezekiel 22 — The LORD calls Jerusalem to account

**About this chapter**

God tells Ezekiel to name the wrongs people have done in Jerusalem. He compares the city to a furnace. Then he describes how the leaders and the people have hurt others.

## God speaks to the city

## Ezekiel 22:1

The LORD spoke to me.

## Ezekiel 22:2

He called me “son of man.”

He asked, “Will you judge this city where people shed blood?

Tell Jerusalem plainly about all the terrible things the people do.”

## Ezekiel 22:3

The Lord GOD gave me this message for Jerusalem.

“Within your walls, people kill others.

You make idols to worship. This makes you unclean.

You bring disaster on yourself.”

## Ezekiel 22:4

“You are guilty of murder. Your idols have made you unclean.

Your own deeds have brought the end near.

I have let other nations insult you and laugh at you.”

## Ezekiel 22:5

“People near and far will mock you.

They know your bad name. Your city is in turmoil.”

## The harm people do inside Jerusalem

## Ezekiel 22:6

“Look at the princes of Israel in your city.

Each uses his power to kill.”

## Ezekiel 22:7

“People treat their fathers and mothers with scorn.

They take advantage of foreigners who live among you.

They mistreat children without fathers and women whose husbands have died.”

## Ezekiel 22:8

“You show no respect for the things I set apart as holy.

You dishonor my Sabbaths, the days of rest.”

## Ezekiel 22:9

“Some people tell lies about others to get them killed.

Some eat meals at places of idol worship on the mountains.

People also do shameful things in your city.”

## Ezekiel 22:10

“Men have sex with their fathers’ wives.

They force themselves on women during their periods.”

## Ezekiel 22:11

“One man has sex with his neighbor’s wife.

Another sexually abuses his daughter-in-law.

Another violates his own sister, his father’s daughter.”

## Ezekiel 22:12

“People accept money to kill someone.

You lend money and demand too much in return.

You use threats to take money from your neighbors.

You have forgotten me,” said the Lord GOD.

## Ezekiel 22:13

“I strike my hands together in anger.

I see how you make money by doing wrong.

I see the blood you have shed.”

## Ezekiel 22:14

“Will you still be brave when I deal with you?

Will your hands still be strong?

I, the LORD, have spoken. I will act.”

## Ezekiel 22:15

“I will scatter you among the nations.

You will live in many lands.

I will remove what has made you unclean.”

## Ezekiel 22:16

“The nations will see the shame you have brought on yourselves.

Then you will know that I am the LORD.”

## Jerusalem is like a furnace

## Ezekiel 22:17

The LORD gave me another message.

## Ezekiel 22:18

“Son of man, the people of Israel have become like dross to me.”

Dross is the waste left when workers purify metal.

God pictured copper, tin, iron, and lead together in a furnace.

A furnace is a place where a fire burns hot enough to melt metal.

To God the people were like the waste from silver.

## Ezekiel 22:19

The Lord GOD said, “Because you have become like dross, I will gather you inside Jerusalem.”

## Ezekiel 22:20

“A worker gathers silver, copper, iron, lead, and tin.

A blast of heat melts the metals in the furnace.

In my anger I will gather you and leave you there.

You will melt like the metal.”

## Ezekiel 22:21

“I will gather you together inside the city.

My anger will be like fire blowing on you.”

## Ezekiel 22:22

“Just as silver melts in a furnace, you will melt in Jerusalem.

Then you will know that I, the LORD, have acted in anger.”

## No one stands up for the land

## Ezekiel 22:23

The LORD spoke to me again.

## Ezekiel 22:24

He told me, “Son of man, speak to the land.

When my anger comes, you are still unclean.

No rain has fallen on you.”

## Ezekiel 22:25

The princes plot together like a roaring lion tearing its prey.

They kill people and take their treasures.

Because of them, many women have lost their husbands.

## Ezekiel 22:26

God said, “The priests twist my law and dishonor my holy things.

They treat holy things like things for everyday use.

They ignore the difference between clean and unclean for worship.

They pay no attention to my Sabbaths. They dishonor me.”

## Ezekiel 22:27

The officials are like wolves tearing the animals they catch.

They kill people and destroy lives to get rich.

## Ezekiel 22:28

The prophets cover up these deeds.

It is like putting a white coating over a weak wall.

They claim to see visions and predict things that are lies.

They say, “The Lord GOD says this.” But God has said nothing.

![A white coating hides damage in a wall. The prophets tried to hide the wrong that people did.](../pictures/whitewashed-wall.svg)

## Ezekiel 22:29

People threaten others to take their money. They rob them.

They mistreat poor people and those who need help.

They cheat foreigners who live among them. No one gives them justice.

## Ezekiel 22:30

God said, “I looked for someone to repair the wall.

I wanted someone to stand before me in the gap on behalf of the land.

Then I would spare it. But I found no one.”

Standing in the gap meant speaking up for the people before God.

## Ezekiel 22:31

“So my anger poured out on them like a fire that burns everything up.

I made them answer for what they had done.”

The Lord GOD had spoken.
