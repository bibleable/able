# Ezekiel 6 — The LORD speaks to the mountains of Israel

**About this chapter**

Israel’s people worship idols on the mountains.

An idol is an object people worship as a god.

The LORD tells Ezekiel what will happen to those places.

He also tells him about the people who will survive.

## Ezekiel must face the mountains

## Ezekiel 6:1

I am Ezekiel.

The LORD spoke to me.

## Ezekiel 6:2

The LORD called me “son of man.”

He said, “Turn and face the mountains of Israel.

Speak my message against them.”

![A mountain like the ones Ezekiel had to face.](../pictures/mountain.svg)

## Ezekiel 6:3

The LORD told me what to say.

“Mountains of Israel, hear what the Lord GOD says!

He speaks to the mountains and the hills.

He speaks to the deep, narrow ravines and the valleys too.”

The Lord GOD says, “I will send an army against you.

I will destroy the places where people worship on the hills.

## The places of worship will fall

## Ezekiel 6:4

“Enemies will tear down your altars.

An altar is a place where people offer gifts in worship.

Enemies will smash the altars where you burn incense.

Incense gives off a sweet smell as it burns.

I will throw your dead down in front of your idols.

## Ezekiel 6:5

“I will lay the dead people of Israel before their idols.

I will scatter your bones around your altars.

## Ezekiel 6:6

“Wherever you live, enemies will wreck your cities.

They will tear down your places of worship on the hills.

They will ruin your altars and treat them with contempt.

They will smash your idols to pieces.

They will tear down your incense altars.

Everything you made for worship will be gone.

## Ezekiel 6:7

“People will fall dead among you.

Then you will know that I am the LORD.

## Some people will survive

## Ezekiel 6:8

“Yet I will let some of you live.

Enemies will scatter you among many lands and nations.

Some of you will escape their swords.

## Ezekiel 6:9

“Enemies will take the survivors away as prisoners.

In those lands the survivors will remember me.

They will remember how deeply they hurt me.

They broke faith with me like an unfaithful husband or wife.

They turned away from me and longed for their idols.

They will feel disgust with themselves for the evil they did.

## Ezekiel 6:10

“Then they will know that I am the LORD.

They will see that I meant what I said.

The disaster I warned them about will come.”

## Ezekiel must show his grief

## Ezekiel 6:11

The Lord GOD said to me, “Clap your hands.

Stamp your feet and cry out in sorrow.

Grieve over the terrible things Israel’s people have done.

War, hunger, and disease will kill them.

## Ezekiel 6:12

“Disease will kill those who are far away.

Enemy swords will kill those who are near.

Those left behind will starve.

That is how I will show them my great anger.

## Ezekiel 6:13

“You will see the dead lying among their idols.

They will lie around their altars on every high hill.

They will lie on mountaintops and under leafy trees and oaks.

In all those places they burned sweet incense for their idols.

Then you will know that I am the LORD.

## Ezekiel 6:14

“I will reach out my hand to strike them.

I will lay waste to the land wherever they live.

From the desert to Diblah, the land will lie empty.

Then they will know that I am the LORD.”
