# Ezekiel 12 — Ezekiel packs a bag and leaves his home

**About this chapter**

The LORD asks Ezekiel to act out a message. People watch him pack for a journey. Later they see how his hands shake when he eats.

## A bag for leaving home

## Ezekiel 12:1

The LORD spoke to me.

## Ezekiel 12:2

He called me “son of man.”

“You live among people who keep turning against me.

They have eyes, but they refuse to see.

They have ears, but they refuse to listen.”

They paid no attention to what God showed them.

## Ezekiel 12:3

“Pack a bag as if you were going into exile.”

Exile means having to live away from your own land.

“Go from your home to another place in daylight.

Let the people watch. Perhaps then they will understand.”

## Ezekiel 12:4

“Carry your bags outside while it is still light.

In the evening, leave like someone going into exile.

Make sure the people see you go.”

## Ezekiel 12:5

“While they watch, dig a hole through the wall.

Carry your things out through the hole.”

## Ezekiel 12:6

“As daylight fades, lift your bags onto your shoulder.

Cover your face so you cannot see the land.

I have made you a sign for Israel.”

My actions would show what was going to happen.

## Ezekiel 12:7

I did what God told me.

During the day I brought my bags outside.

That evening I dug through the wall with my hands.

As the light faded, I put my things on my shoulder.

The people watched me carry them out.

## What Ezekiel’s journey means

## Ezekiel 12:8

The next morning the LORD spoke to me.

## Ezekiel 12:9

“Son of man, the people of Israel resist me.

Haven’t they asked you, ‘What are you doing?’”

## Ezekiel 12:10

The Lord GOD told me how to answer.

“This message is about the prince in Jerusalem.

It is also about all the Israelites there.”

## Ezekiel 12:11

“Tell them, ‘I am a sign for you.’”

My journey showed what would happen to those people.

Enemies would take them away as prisoners.

## Ezekiel 12:12

As the light faded, the prince would shoulder his bags.

People would dig through the wall to bring him out.

He would cover his face and could not see the land.

## Ezekiel 12:13

The LORD said, “I will catch him in my net.”

The prince would have no way to escape.

God would bring him to Babylon, the land of the Chaldeans.

The prince would never see Babylon, yet he would die there.

## Ezekiel 12:14

God would scatter the prince’s helpers and soldiers in every direction.

He would send enemies with swords after them.

## Ezekiel 12:15

“I will scatter them among many nations.

Then they will know that I am the LORD.”

## Ezekiel 12:16

“A few will survive the war, hunger, and deadly sickness.

Wherever they go, they will tell people what they did.

They will speak of the terrible things they did in Israel.

Then they will know that I am the LORD.”

## Eating a meal in fear

## Ezekiel 12:17

The LORD gave me another message.

## Ezekiel 12:18

“Son of man, shake as you eat your bread.

Tremble with fear as you drink your water.”

## Ezekiel 12:19

I was to tell the people what this meant.

The Lord GOD was speaking about Jerusalem and Israel.

The people there would eat and drink in fear.

They had filled their land with violence.

Because of this, they would lose everything in it.

## Ezekiel 12:20

Their cities, now full of people, would lie in ruins.

The land would stand empty.

God said, “Then you will know that I am the LORD.”

## The people think there is plenty of time

## Ezekiel 12:21

The LORD spoke to me again.

## Ezekiel 12:22

“Son of man, why do people in Israel repeat this saying?

‘Time goes by. What the prophets see never happens.’”

The prophets spoke about visions, things God showed them.

## Ezekiel 12:23

The Lord GOD said, “I will put an end to that saying.

People in Israel will stop repeating it.

Tell them the time is near.

What I have shown in visions will happen.”

## Ezekiel 12:24

People in Israel would stop claiming to see false visions.

They would stop giving predictions just to please people.

## Ezekiel 12:25

The LORD said, “What I say will happen without delay.

You have turned against me. Yet I will do what I say in your own lifetime.”

## Ezekiel 12:26

Once more the LORD spoke to me.

## Ezekiel 12:27

“Son of man, listen to what the Israelites say about you.

‘His vision is about something many years away.

He is telling us about the distant future.’”

## Ezekiel 12:28

The Lord GOD gave me this answer for them.

“I will wait no longer.

I will do what I have said.”
