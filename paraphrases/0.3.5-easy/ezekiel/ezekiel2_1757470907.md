# Ezekiel 2 — God sends Ezekiel to speak

**About this chapter**

Ezekiel tells what he saw and heard. God tells him to speak to the people of Israel, even if they refuse to listen.

## God helps Ezekiel stand

## Ezekiel 2:1

God said to me, “Son of man, stand up.

I will speak to you.”

“Son of man” is the name God uses for me here.

## Ezekiel 2:2

As God spoke, His Spirit came into me.

The Spirit helped me to my feet.

I heard God speaking to me.

## Ezekiel 2:3

God said, “Son of man, I am sending you to the people of Israel.

They have turned against Me.

Their ancestors did the same thing.

Even today, the people still turn against Me.

## Ezekiel 2:4

“These people are stubborn.

They refuse to change.

I am sending you to them.

Tell them, ‘This is what the Lord GOD says.’

## Ezekiel 2:5

“They may listen or they may refuse.

They have often refused to obey Me.

Either way, they will know a prophet has been among them.”

A prophet is someone who speaks for God.

## Speak even if they refuse to listen

## Ezekiel 2:6

God said, “Son of man, do not fear these people.

Do not fear what they say.

Being with them is like standing among thorn bushes.

It is like living among scorpions.”

Thorns can scratch, and scorpions can sting.

“Even when these people turn against Me, stand firm.

Do not let their words frighten you.

## Ezekiel 2:7

“Tell them what I say.

Speak whether they listen or refuse.

They have turned against Me.

## A hand holds out a scroll

## Ezekiel 2:8

“Son of man, you must listen to Me.

Do not turn against Me as they have done.

Open your mouth.

Eat what I give you.”

## Ezekiel 2:9

I looked and saw a hand reaching toward me.

The hand held a scroll.

A scroll is a long sheet you can roll up.

![A scroll unrolled to show the writing.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## Ezekiel 2:10

God unrolled the scroll in front of me.

I saw writing on both sides.

The words spoke of people weeping and grieving.

They told of terrible trouble.
