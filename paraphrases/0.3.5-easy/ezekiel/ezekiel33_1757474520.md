# Ezekiel 33 — A watchman warns the people

**About this chapter**

A watchman sounds a horn when danger comes. God tells Ezekiel to warn Israel. Later a man brings news from Jerusalem.

## A watchman sounds the horn

## Ezekiel 33:1

The LORD spoke to me again.

## Ezekiel 33:2

He called me “son of man.”

He told me to give my people this example.

“Suppose I bring an enemy army against a land.

The people choose one man to be their watchman.”

A watchman looks out for danger.

## Ezekiel 33:3

“The watchman sees the enemy coming.

He blows a ram’s horn to warn everyone.”

![A ram’s horn. Its sound warns people that danger is near.](../pictures/sheba-and-joabs-horn.svg)

## Ezekiel 33:4

“Suppose someone hears the horn and ignores it.

The enemy comes and kills that person.

That person is responsible for their own death.”

## Ezekiel 33:5

“The person heard the horn and ignored the warning.

Taking the warning seriously would have saved their life.”

## Ezekiel 33:6

“But suppose the watchman sees the army and stays silent.

The people hear no warning. The enemy kills someone.

That person dies in their sin.

I will hold the watchman responsible for that death.”

## Ezekiel must warn Israel

## Ezekiel 33:7

God said, “I have made you a watchman for Israel.

Listen to what I say.

Give my people the warning you hear from me.”

## Ezekiel 33:8

“Suppose I tell someone doing evil, ‘You will die.’

You must warn that person to turn from evil.

If you keep quiet, that person will die in their sin.

I will hold you responsible for their death.”

## Ezekiel 33:9

“But suppose you warn the person, and they keep doing evil.

They will die for their own sin.

You will have saved your life.”

## Ezekiel 33:10

God told me what Israel’s people were saying.

“Our sins weigh us down. We are wasting away.

How can we live?”

## Ezekiel 33:11

The Lord GOD told me to give them his answer.

“As surely as I live, I take no joy in the death of people who do evil.

I want them to turn back and live.

Turn back, people of Israel! Leave your evil ways!

Why should you die?”

## A person can turn back

## Ezekiel 33:12

God told me, “Tell your people what happens when someone changes.

Suppose someone who did right begins to do wrong.

Their earlier good deeds cannot save them now.

Suppose someone who did evil turns away from it.

Their earlier sins need no longer bring them down.”

## Ezekiel 33:13

“I may tell a good person, ‘You will live.’

But suppose they count on their good deeds and do evil.

I will no longer count those good deeds in their favor.

They will die for the wrong they have done.”

## Ezekiel 33:14

“I may tell someone doing evil, ‘You will die.’

But suppose that person turns away from sin.

They begin to do what is fair and right.”

## Ezekiel 33:15

“They give back what a borrower left as a promise to pay.

They repay what they stole.

They follow the laws that lead to life and stop doing evil.

That person will live.”

## Ezekiel 33:16

“I will no longer hold their past sins against them.

They have done what is fair and right. They will live.”

## Ezekiel 33:17

“Yet your people say, ‘The Lord is unfair.’

Look at what the people themselves do. They act unfairly.”

## Ezekiel 33:18

“If someone stops doing right and does evil, they will die for it.”

## Ezekiel 33:19

“If someone turns from evil and does what is right, they will live.”

## Ezekiel 33:20

“People of Israel, you keep saying that I am unfair.

I will judge each of you by what you do.”

## News comes from Jerusalem

## Ezekiel 33:21

It was the twelfth year of our exile.

Enemies had taken us away from our homeland.

On day five of the tenth month, a man came to me.

He had escaped from Jerusalem.

He said, “The enemy has captured the city!”

## Ezekiel 33:22

The evening before, I had felt the LORD’s power upon me.

He had opened my mouth before the man arrived that morning.

I could speak again.

## Ezekiel 33:23

Then the LORD gave me a message.

## Ezekiel 33:24

He said, “Some people live among the ruins in Israel.

They say, ‘Abraham was only one man, yet he possessed this land.

There are many of us. Surely the land is ours!’”

## Ezekiel 33:25

The Lord GOD told me to give them his answer.

“You eat meat with the blood still in it.

You worship idols. You kill people.

Why should the land belong to you?”

## Ezekiel 33:26

“You trust in your swords. You do things I hate.

You have sex with your neighbors’ wives.

Why should the land belong to you?”

## Ezekiel 33:27

The Lord GOD said, “As surely as I live, enemies will kill those in the ruins.

Wild animals will eat those in the open fields.

Deadly sickness will reach the people in forts and caves.

They will die there.”

## Ezekiel 33:28

“I will leave the land empty.

The strength you boast about will be gone.

Israel’s mountains will be so deserted that nobody will pass through.”

## Ezekiel 33:29

“I will leave the land empty because of the terrible things they did.

Then they will know that I am the LORD.”

## Listening to Ezekiel

## Ezekiel 33:30

God said, “Your people talk about you beside the walls.

They talk in the doorways of their homes.

They tell each other, ‘Come and hear the LORD’s message!’”

## Ezekiel 33:31

“They come and sit in front of you as usual.

They hear your words but do nothing about them.

They speak of love while looking for ways to cheat people for money.”

## Ezekiel 33:32

“To them you are like someone singing a love song.

They enjoy the beautiful voice and the skillful playing.

They listen to your words, yet they do not act on them.”

## Ezekiel 33:33

“What you have told them will happen.

Then they will know a prophet was among them.”

A prophet is someone God sends to speak for him.
