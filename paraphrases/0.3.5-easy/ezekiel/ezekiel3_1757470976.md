# Ezekiel 3 — Ezekiel receives God’s words and must warn Israel

**About this chapter**

In his vision, Ezekiel sees a scroll in God’s hand. God tells him what to do with it. Later, by the river, God explains Ezekiel’s work.

## Ezekiel eats the scroll

## Ezekiel 3:1

God called me “son of man.”

He said, “Eat this scroll.

Then go and speak to the people of Israel.”

A scroll is a long sheet of writing rolled up.

![A scroll unrolled to show the writing.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## Ezekiel 3:2

I opened my mouth.

God gave me the scroll to eat.

## Ezekiel 3:3

God said, “Eat the scroll I am giving you.

Fill your stomach with it.”

I ate it. In my mouth it tasted sweet like honey.

## Ezekiel 3:4

Then God said, “Go to the people of Israel.

Tell them my words.”

## Ezekiel 3:5

“I am sending you to Israel.

You already know the language these people speak.”

## Ezekiel 3:6

“There are many peoples whose words you cannot understand.

If I sent you to them, they would listen.”

## Ezekiel 3:7

“But Israel will refuse to listen to you.

They refuse to listen to me.

They have made up their minds to disobey.”

## Ezekiel 3:8

“I will make your face as hard as theirs.

I will make your forehead as hard as theirs.”

God would help me stand firm when they opposed me.

## Ezekiel 3:9

“Your forehead will be like a diamond, harder than flint.”

Flint is a hard stone.

“Even when these people rebel, do not be afraid.

Do not lose courage when you face them.”

## Ezekiel 3:10

“Listen carefully to every word I say.

Keep my words in your heart.”

God wanted me to remember what he said.

## Ezekiel 3:11

“Go to your own people who live in exile.”

Enemies had taken them away from their own land.

“Tell them, ‘Here is what the Lord GOD says.’

Speak whether they listen or refuse.”

## Seven days by the river

## Ezekiel 3:12

Then the Spirit lifted me up.

Behind me I heard a great rumbling sound.

I heard words praising the LORD’s glory in his dwelling place.

## Ezekiel 3:13

I heard the living creatures’ wings brush against each other.

I heard the wheels beside them.

Together they made that great rumbling sound.

## Ezekiel 3:14

The Spirit lifted me and carried me away.

I felt bitter and angry as I went.

I could feel the LORD’s great power upon me.

## Ezekiel 3:15

I came to Tel-abib beside the Kebar River.

Some of my people lived there in exile.

I sat among them for seven days.

What I had seen and heard was almost more than I could bear.

## A watchman gives a warning

## Ezekiel 3:16

At the end of those seven days, the LORD spoke to me.

## Ezekiel 3:17

“Son of man, I have made you a watchman for Israel.”

A watchman warns people when danger is coming.

“Listen to me. Give the people my warning.”

## Ezekiel 3:18

“Suppose I tell someone doing evil, ‘You will die.’

You must warn that person to turn back and live.

If you keep quiet, that person will die in their sin.

I will hold you responsible for their death.”

## Ezekiel 3:19

“But suppose you warn someone who does evil.

They hear you and still choose to do wrong.

They will die for their sin.

You will have saved your own life.”

## Ezekiel 3:20

“Suppose someone who did right begins to do evil.

I put something in their path that makes them stumble.

If you give no warning, that person will die in their sin.

The good they once did will no longer count for them.

I will hold you responsible for their death.”

## Ezekiel 3:21

“Now suppose you warn a person who does right.

They listen to you and stay away from sin.

They will live because they listened.

You too will have saved your life.”

## God tells Ezekiel when to speak

## Ezekiel 3:22

There by the river I felt the LORD’s power upon me.

He said, “Get up. Go to the open plain.

I will speak with you there.”

## Ezekiel 3:23

I went out to the plain.

There I saw the glory of the LORD.

It was like the glory I saw by the Kebar River.

I fell with my face to the ground.

## Ezekiel 3:24

The Spirit came into me and stood me on my feet.

God told me, “Go home and shut yourself inside.”

## Ezekiel 3:25

“People will tie you with ropes.

You will be unable to go out among them.”

## Ezekiel 3:26

“I will make your tongue stick to the roof of your mouth.

You will be silent.

The people keep disobeying, but you cannot speak to correct them.”

## Ezekiel 3:27

“When I speak to you, I will open your mouth.

Tell them, ‘Here is what the Lord GOD says.’

Let those who will listen hear you.

Let those who refuse go on refusing.

These people have turned against me.”
