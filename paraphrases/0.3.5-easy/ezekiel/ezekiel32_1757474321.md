# Ezekiel 32 — A song of grief for Egypt

**About this chapter**

Ezekiel sings about Pharaoh, the king of Egypt. Later the LORD tells Ezekiel to grieve for Egypt’s people. They will join other powerful nations that have already fallen.

## A great creature caught in a net

## Ezekiel 32:1

The LORD spoke to me in the twelfth year.

It was the first day of the twelfth month.

## Ezekiel 32:2

He called me “son of man.”

He told me to sing a lament for Pharaoh.

A lament is a song that expresses grief.

“Pharaoh, among the nations you are like a lion.

You are also like a great creature in the sea.

You thrash about and stir up mud in your rivers.”

## Ezekiel 32:3

The Lord GOD said, “I will spread my net over you.

People from many nations will haul you out.”

Even a ruler as strong as Pharaoh could be caught.

## Ezekiel 32:4

“I will throw you onto an open field and leave you there.

Birds will settle on you.

Wild animals will eat until they are full.”

## Ezekiel 32:5

“I will leave your flesh on the mountains.

Your remains will fill the valleys.”

## Ezekiel 32:6

“Your blood will soak the ground as far as the mountains.

It will fill the deep gullies.”

## Darkness over Egypt

## Ezekiel 32:7

“When I put out your light, I will cover the sky.

The stars will grow dark.

A cloud will cover the sun. The moon will give no light.”

## Ezekiel 32:8

“I will darken every light that shines above you.

Darkness will cover your land,” said the Lord GOD.

## Ezekiel 32:9

“Your fall will trouble people in many nations.

Even people in lands you have never known will fear.”

## Ezekiel 32:10

“Many will shudder at what happens to you.

Their kings will see me raise my sword.

On the day you fall, they will tremble.

Each king will fear for his own life.”

## Ezekiel 32:11

The Lord GOD said, “The king of Babylon will come against you with a sword.”

## Ezekiel 32:12

His soldiers were strong and showed no mercy.

They would strike down the crowds of Egypt.

They would destroy everything that made Egypt proud.

## Ezekiel 32:13

God said, “I will kill Egypt’s cattle beside the water.

No feet will stir up mud there again.

No hooves will disturb the water.”

## Ezekiel 32:14

“Then I will let the water settle.

The rivers will flow as smoothly as oil.”

## Ezekiel 32:15

“I will empty Egypt of everything that fills it.

I will strike down the people living there.

When the land lies empty, they will know I am the LORD.”

## Ezekiel 32:16

The Lord GOD said women from many nations would sing this lament.

They would sing it for Egypt and all its people.

## Egypt goes down to the place of the dead

## Ezekiel 32:17

In the twelfth year the LORD spoke to me again.

It was the fifteenth day of the month.

## Ezekiel 32:18

God told me to weep aloud for Egypt’s people.

I was to announce that Egypt would go down into the earth.

The daughters of other powerful nations would go there too.

They would join those in the Pit, the place of the dead.

## Ezekiel 32:19

God asked Egypt, “Are you more beautiful than the others?

Go down and lie among the uncircumcised.”

The uncircumcised were men without the physical mark of circumcision.

In this song, lying among them is a sign of disgrace.

## Ezekiel 32:20

The sword was ready for Egypt.

Egypt’s people would fall among those killed in battle.

Their enemies would drag them all away.

## Ezekiel 32:21

From Sheol, the place of the dead, mighty chiefs would speak.

They would say, “Egypt and its helpers have come down here.

They lie with the uncircumcised, among those killed in battle.”

## Nations that once frightened others

## Ezekiel 32:22

Assyria is there with its whole army.

Graves surround Assyria’s grave.

All those soldiers died by the sword.

## Ezekiel 32:23

Deep in the Pit lie the graves of Assyria’s soldiers.

Once they frightened people throughout the land of the living.

Now the soldiers lie dead around Assyria’s grave.

## Ezekiel 32:24

Elam is there too, with its people buried all around.

Once they spread fear among the living.

Then enemies killed them in battle.

They went down uncircumcised into the earth.

Now they share the disgrace of those in the Pit.

## Ezekiel 32:25

Elam has a resting place among the dead.

The graves of its many soldiers surround it.

Enemies killed them with swords. They lie there uncircumcised.

Once people were terrified of them.

Now they lie in shame among the dead.

## Ezekiel 32:26

Meshech and Tubal are there with all their people.

Their graves lie all around them.

They spread fear among the living.

Enemies killed them in battle. They too lie uncircumcised.

## Ezekiel 32:27

They do not share the graves of the warriors of long ago.

Those warriors once filled the living with fear.

People buried them with their weapons.

Swords lay under their heads and shields over their bones.

## Ezekiel 32:28

God told Pharaoh, “You too will fall.

You will lie among the uncircumcised, with those killed in battle.”

## Ezekiel 32:29

Edom is there with all its kings and princes.

Even those strong rulers lie among those killed in battle.

They lie uncircumcised with the others in the Pit.

## Ezekiel 32:30

The rulers from the north and the people of Sidon are there.

Once their power made people tremble.

Now they lie uncircumcised among the dead.

They share the shame of those in the Pit.

## Ezekiel 32:31

The Lord GOD said Pharaoh would see them there.

Pharaoh and his whole army would die in battle.

Seeing the others would bring Pharaoh some comfort over his loss.

## Ezekiel 32:32

The Lord GOD said, “I will spread fear among the living.”

Pharaoh and all his people would lie among the uncircumcised.

They would rest with those killed in battle.
