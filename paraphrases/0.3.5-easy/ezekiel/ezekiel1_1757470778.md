# Ezekiel 1 — Ezekiel sees the glory of the LORD by the river

**About this chapter**

Far from home, Ezekiel sees four living creatures. He describes what he sees beside them and above them. Then he hears a voice.

## God speaks to Ezekiel by the river

## Ezekiel 1:1

It was the thirtieth year, day five of the fourth month.

I was beside the Kebar River with the other exiles.

Exiles are people forced to live away from home.

The heavens opened. God showed me visions.

A vision is something God lets a person see.

## Ezekiel 1:2

That day was in the fifth year of King Jehoiachin’s exile.

## Ezekiel 1:3

My name is Ezekiel. I am a priest, the son of Buzi.

The LORD spoke directly to me by the Kebar River.

I was in the land of the Chaldeans.

There I felt the LORD’s power come upon me.

## Four living creatures come out of the storm

## Ezekiel 1:4

I looked north. A swirling wind was coming.

I saw a great cloud with bright light all around it.

Fire flashed back and forth inside the cloud.

The center shone with a golden light.

## Ezekiel 1:5

In the fire I saw four living creatures.

Their bodies looked like human bodies.

## Ezekiel 1:6

Each creature had four faces and four wings.

## Ezekiel 1:7

Their legs were straight.

Their feet looked like the hooves of a young cow.

They shone like polished bronze, a kind of metal.

## Ezekiel 1:8

Each creature had hands like human hands.

I could see them beneath the wings on all four sides.

All four creatures had faces and wings.

## Ezekiel 1:9

Their wings touched one another.

Each creature moved straight ahead without turning around.

## Ezekiel 1:10

Each creature had a human face.

On its right was the face of a lion.

On its left was the face of an ox.

It also had the face of an eagle.

## Ezekiel 1:11

The creatures spread two wings upward.

These wings touched the wings of the creatures on either side.

Each creature covered its body with its other two wings.

## Ezekiel 1:12

Wherever the spirit led them, the creatures went.

They went straight ahead without turning.

## Ezekiel 1:13

Between the creatures I saw what looked like glowing coals.

They also looked like burning torches.

Bright fire moved back and forth between the creatures.

Lightning flashed out of the fire.

## Ezekiel 1:14

The creatures darted back and forth.

They moved as quickly as flashes of lightning.

## Wheels move beside the creatures

## Ezekiel 1:15

Then I looked at the ground.

Beside each of the four-faced creatures stood a wheel.

## Ezekiel 1:16

All four wheels looked alike.

They shone like a jewel called beryl.

Each looked like one wheel inside another wheel.

## Ezekiel 1:17

The wheels could move in any of four directions.

They did not need to turn to face another way.

## Ezekiel 1:18

The outer edges of the wheels rose high above me.

I was filled with wonder.

Eyes covered the edges of all four wheels.

## Ezekiel 1:19

When the creatures moved, the wheels moved beside them.

When the creatures rose from the ground, the wheels rose too.

## Ezekiel 1:20

Wherever the spirit led, the creatures went.

The wheels rose beside them.

The spirit of the living creatures was in the wheels.

## Ezekiel 1:21

They moved together. They stopped together.

They rose from the ground together.

The same spirit was in the creatures and the wheels.

## A voice comes from above

## Ezekiel 1:22

Above the creatures’ heads I saw something like a wide surface.

It gleamed like crystal. The sight filled me with awe.

## Ezekiel 1:23

Beneath it, the creatures stretched their wings toward one another.

Each also had two wings that covered its body.

## Ezekiel 1:24

When they moved, their wings roared like rushing water.

They sounded like the voice of God Almighty.

They also sounded like a great army.

When the creatures stopped, they lowered their wings.

## Ezekiel 1:25

They stood still with their wings down.

A voice came from above that shining surface.

## Ezekiel 1:26

High above them I saw what looked like a throne.

A throne is a king’s seat.

It looked like sapphire, a blue jewel.

Someone who looked like a man sat on it.

## Ezekiel 1:27

From his waist upward, he gleamed with a golden light.

It looked as if fire glowed inside that light.

Below his waist, he also looked like fire.

Bright light surrounded him.

## Ezekiel 1:28

The light looked like a rainbow in a cloud on a rainy day.

This is how the glory of the LORD appeared to me.

I fell with my face to the ground.

Then I heard a voice speaking.
