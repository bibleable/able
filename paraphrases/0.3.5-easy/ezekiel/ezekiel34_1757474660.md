# Ezekiel 34 — The LORD looks for his scattered sheep

**About this chapter**

The LORD calls the people of Israel his flock. Their leaders should have cared for them as shepherds care for sheep. The LORD tells Ezekiel what he will do for his flock.

## The shepherds leave the sheep hungry

## Ezekiel 34:1

The LORD spoke to me.

## Ezekiel 34:2

He called me “son of man.”

He told me to speak against Israel’s shepherds.

These shepherds were the leaders of Israel.

“Trouble is coming to you! You feed only yourselves.

Shouldn’t shepherds feed their flock?”

## Ezekiel 34:3

“You eat the fat and wear clothes made from the wool.

You kill the well-fed sheep for meat.

Yet you leave the flock hungry.”

## Ezekiel 34:4

“You gave no help to the weak or sick sheep.

You did not wrap the wounds of the injured.

You left the wandering sheep far from home.

You never searched for the lost ones.

You used force and treated them cruelly.”

## Ezekiel 34:5

The sheep had no one to care for them.

They scattered. Wild animals found them and ate them.

## Ezekiel 34:6

“My sheep wandered over mountains and high hills.

They scattered across the earth.

No one went to look for them.”

## The LORD takes the flock away from the shepherds

## Ezekiel 34:7

The LORD said, “You shepherds, listen to my words.”

## Ezekiel 34:8

“As surely as I live, my flock has no shepherd.

Wild animals hunt my sheep and eat them.

My shepherds never went looking for my flock.

They fed only themselves.”

## Ezekiel 34:9

Again God said, “You shepherds, listen to my words!”

## Ezekiel 34:10

“I am against you. I will take my flock back.

You will lose your work as shepherds.

You will no longer feed yourselves from my flock.

I will rescue my sheep from your mouths.”

God would keep the leaders from using his people for their own gain.

## The LORD finds and cares for his sheep

## Ezekiel 34:11

The Lord GOD said, “I myself will look for my sheep.

I will find them.”

## Ezekiel 34:12

“When sheep scatter, their shepherd goes looking for them.

That is how I will search for my people.

They scattered on a dark, cloudy day.

I will rescue them wherever they are.”

## Ezekiel 34:13

“I will gather them from the nations.

I will bring them home to their own land.

I will feed them on Israel’s mountains and in its deep valleys.

I will feed them wherever people live in that land.”

## Ezekiel 34:14

“My sheep will have good grass to eat.

They will feed high on Israel’s mountains.

They will have rich grass and a place to rest.”

## Ezekiel 34:15

“I myself will care for my sheep.

I will lead them to lie down and rest.”

![A sheep pen with an open gate, where a shepherd can bring the flock to rest.](../pictures/sheep-pen.svg)

## Ezekiel 34:16

“I will find the lost sheep. I will bring back those that wandered.

I will wrap the wounds of the injured.

I will help the weak grow strong.

But I will destroy the fat, strong sheep.

I will judge my flock fairly.”

## Some sheep push the others away

## Ezekiel 34:17

The Lord GOD spoke to his flock.

“I will judge between one sheep and another.

I will judge between the rams and the goats.”

A ram is a male sheep.

## Ezekiel 34:18

“Some of you eat the best grass.

Why do you then walk all over the rest?

You drink the clear water.

Why stir up mud with your feet?”

## Ezekiel 34:19

“Why should my other sheep eat grass you have crushed?

Why should they have to drink muddy water?”

## Ezekiel 34:20

The Lord GOD said, “I myself will judge between the fat sheep and the thin sheep.”

## Ezekiel 34:21

“You shove the weak sheep with your sides and shoulders.

You push them with your horns until they scatter.”

## Ezekiel 34:22

“I will rescue my flock.

No one will be able to hunt them down again.

I will judge between one sheep and another.”

## One shepherd and a promise of peace

## Ezekiel 34:23

“I will give them one shepherd, my servant David.

He will feed them. He will care for them.”

## Ezekiel 34:24

“I, the LORD, will be their God.

My servant David will be a prince among them.

I, the LORD, have spoken.”

## Ezekiel 34:25

“I will make a covenant of peace with them.”

A covenant is a firm promise.

God would remove the wild animals from the land.

People could live safely in the wilderness.

They could even sleep in the forest.

## Ezekiel 34:26

“I will bless my people and the places around my hill.

I will send rain when the land needs it.

Those showers will be my gift to them.”

## Ezekiel 34:27

The trees will bear fruit. The fields will grow food.

God’s people will live safely on their land.

God said he would break their yoke.

A yoke is a bar used to control working animals.

God meant he would free his people from slavery.

Then they would know that he is the LORD.

## Ezekiel 34:28

“Other nations will no longer hunt them down.

Wild animals will no longer eat them.

My people will be safe. No one will frighten them.”

## Ezekiel 34:29

“I will give them a garden famous for what it grows.

My people will no longer starve in their land.

Other nations will no longer insult them.”

## Ezekiel 34:30

“Then Israel will know that I am with them.

I am the LORD their God, and they are my people.”

## Ezekiel 34:31

The Lord GOD said, “You are my flock.

You are the sheep I feed in my pasture.

You are my people, and I am your God.”
