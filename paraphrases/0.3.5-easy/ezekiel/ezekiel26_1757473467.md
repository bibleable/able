# Ezekiel 26 — Tyre will become a bare rock

**About this chapter**

Tyre is a wealthy city by the sea. Its people are glad that Jerusalem lies in ruins. The LORD tells Ezekiel what will happen to Tyre.

## Tyre hopes to gain from Jerusalem’s fall

## Ezekiel 26:1

The LORD spoke to me in the twelfth year.

It was the first day of the eleventh month.

## Ezekiel 26:2

He called me “son of man.”

He told me what Tyre had said about Jerusalem.

“Jerusalem was a gate for the nations. Now that gate lies broken!

The way is open to me. Now I will grow rich.”

Tyre expected trade that once went through Jerusalem to come its way.

## Ezekiel 26:3

The Lord GOD said, “Tyre, I am against you.

I will bring many nations against you.

They will come like waves rolling in from the sea.”

## Ezekiel 26:4

“They will tear down your walls and towers.

I will scrape away your soil. Only bare rock will remain.”

## Ezekiel 26:5

“Out in the sea, people will spread fishing nets on that rock.

Other nations will carry off your goods.

I, the Lord GOD, have spoken.”

## Ezekiel 26:6

“Enemies will kill the people in your villages on the mainland.

Then they will know that I am the LORD.”

## The king of Babylon comes from the north

## Ezekiel 26:7

The Lord GOD said, “I will bring Nebuchadnezzar against Tyre from the north.”

Nebuchadnezzar was king of Babylon. He ruled over other kings.

He would come with many soldiers, horses, riders, and chariots.

Chariots were carts that horses pulled in battle.

## Ezekiel 26:8

The king’s soldiers would kill people in the mainland villages.

They would surround Tyre, build a ramp, and raise their shields.

## Ezekiel 26:9

They would use heavy rams to strike the city walls.

They would tear down the towers with axes.

## Ezekiel 26:10

Dust from all those horses would cover Tyre.

The king would enter its gates like an army entering a broken city.

The noise of riders, wagons, and chariots would shake the walls.

## Ezekiel 26:11

Horses would trample every street.

Soldiers would kill the people, and the great pillars would fall.

## Ezekiel 26:12

The attackers would take Tyre’s riches and goods for sale.

They would pull down the walls and the beautiful houses.

They would throw stones, wood, and soil into the sea.

## Ezekiel 26:13

The LORD said, “I will silence your songs.

No one will hear your lyres anymore.”

A lyre is a musical instrument with strings.

## Ezekiel 26:14

“I will make you a bare rock where people spread fishing nets.

No one will rebuild you. I, the LORD, have spoken.”

## Rulers by the sea mourn for Tyre

## Ezekiel 26:15

The Lord GOD asked Tyre, “Won’t the coastlands shake when you fall?

They will hear the wounded groan as people die in your city.”

## Ezekiel 26:16

The rulers along the sea will step down from their thrones.

They will take off their robes with their fine stitching.

They will sit on the ground and tremble.

What happens to Tyre will terrify them.

## Ezekiel 26:17

They will sing a song of grief for Tyre.

“Famous city, home of sailors, how you have fallen!

You and your people were mighty on the sea.

You filled others with fear.”

## Ezekiel 26:18

“Now the coastlands shake because you have fallen.

People on the islands are afraid because you are gone.”

## The city lies empty

## Ezekiel 26:19

The Lord GOD said, “I will leave you empty like other deserted cities.

I will bring the deep sea over you.

Its great waters will cover you.”

## Ezekiel 26:20

“I will bring you down to the Pit.”

The Pit is a name for the place of the dead.

Tyre would join those who died long ago.

It would lie like old ruins deep in the earth.

No one would live there or see its former beauty among the living.

## Ezekiel 26:21

“What happens to you will fill people with horror.

You will be gone. People will search for you.

They will never find you,” said the Lord GOD.
