# Ezekiel 9 — A mark for those who grieve

**About this chapter**

Ezekiel describes a vision of Jerusalem. Before judgment comes on the city, the LORD sees the people who grieve over the wrong done there.

## The men at the temple

## Ezekiel 9:1

In my vision I heard God call out loudly.

He called the men who would carry out judgment on the city.

Each was to come with a weapon in his hand.

## Ezekiel 9:2

I saw six men coming from the Upper Gate.

That gate faces north.

Each man carried a weapon.

Another man came with them.

He wore linen clothes and carried writing tools at his side.

They stood beside the bronze altar, the place for offerings.

## The man with the writing kit

## Ezekiel 9:3

The glory of Israel’s God rose from above the cherubim.

Cherubim are heavenly beings.

God’s glory moved to the doorway of the temple.

God called the man in linen who carried the writing tools.

![The temple, where Ezekiel saw God’s glory move to the doorway.](../pictures/solomons-temple-rooms.svg)

## Ezekiel 9:4

The LORD told him to go through Jerusalem.

Some people there groaned with grief at the terrible things others had done.

The man was to put a mark on their foreheads.

## Judgment on Jerusalem

## Ezekiel 9:5

Then I heard the LORD tell the other men to follow.

They were to kill the people without the mark and show no pity.

## Ezekiel 9:6

The judgment included old men, young men, young women, women and children.

The men were to leave everyone with the mark alone.

The LORD told them to start at His holy place.

They began with the elders in front of the temple.

## Ezekiel 9:7

The LORD told them to fill the temple courtyards with the dead.

This would make the temple unclean.

Then the men went out and killed people throughout the city.

## Ezekiel asks about the people who are left

## Ezekiel 9:8

While this was happening, I stood alone.

I fell facedown and cried out to the Lord GOD.

“You are pouring out Your anger on Jerusalem.

Will You destroy everyone left in Israel?”

## Ezekiel 9:9

God answered, “The people of Israel and Judah have done terrible wrong.

People have shed blood throughout the land.

The people of the city have twisted what is right.

They say, ‘The LORD has left this land.

He does not see us.’

## Ezekiel 9:10

“I will show them no pity.

I will not spare them.

I will hold them responsible for what they have done.”

## Ezekiel 9:11

Then the man in linen came back with his writing tools.

He told the LORD, “I have done what You told me to do.”
