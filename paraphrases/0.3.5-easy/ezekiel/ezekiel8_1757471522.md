# Ezekiel 8 — God shows Ezekiel what people worship in the temple

**About this chapter**

Ezekiel sits at home with the leaders of Judah. In a vision, God shows him the temple in Jerusalem. Ezekiel tells us what he sees there.

## A vision of Jerusalem

## Ezekiel 8:1

It was day five of the sixth month, in the sixth year.

I sat in my house with the leaders of Judah.

There I felt the power of the Lord GOD come upon me.

## Ezekiel 8:2

I looked up. I saw someone who looked like a man.

Below his waist he looked like fire.

Above his waist he shone with a golden light.

## Ezekiel 8:3

He reached out with what looked like a hand.

He took hold of my hair.

God’s Spirit lifted me between earth and sky.

In a vision, the Spirit carried me to Jerusalem.

A vision is something God shows a person.

I saw the north gate of the temple’s inner courtyard.

An idol stood at the entrance.

An idol is an object people worship as a god.

This idol made God jealous because his people worshiped it.

## Ezekiel 8:4

There I saw the glory of Israel’s God.

I had seen that same glory on the plain.

God’s glory showed me how great he is.

## Ezekiel 8:5

God called me “son of man.”

He said, “Look toward the north.”

I looked north of the Altar Gate.

There at the entrance stood the idol.

## Ezekiel 8:6

God asked, “Son of man, do you see what Israel is doing?

My people do terrible things in my holy place.

They are driving me far away from it.

You will see even worse things.”

## A doorway in the wall

## Ezekiel 8:7

God brought me to the courtyard entrance.

I saw a hole in the wall.

## Ezekiel 8:8

God said, “Son of man, dig through the wall.”

I dug through it and found a doorway.

## Ezekiel 8:9

God said, “Go inside.

See the terrible things the people are doing here.”

## Ezekiel 8:10

I went in. Pictures covered the walls.

People had carved crawling creatures and all kinds of unclean animals.

They had carved pictures of all Israel’s idols too.

## Ezekiel 8:11

Seventy leaders of Israel stood facing the pictures.

Jaazaniah son of Shaphan stood among them.

Each man held a small container for burning incense.

Sweet-smelling smoke rose in a cloud.

## Ezekiel 8:12

God asked, “Son of man, do you see these leaders?

Each worships his own idol in the dark.

They say, ‘The LORD does not see us.

The LORD has left this land.’”

## Ezekiel 8:13

God said to me again, “You will see them do even worse things.”

## Women at the gate and men in the courtyard

## Ezekiel 8:14

God brought me to the north gate of the LORD’s temple.

Women sat there weeping for Tammuz.

Tammuz was a god they worshiped.

## Ezekiel 8:15

God asked, “Son of man, do you see this?

You will see even worse things.”

## Ezekiel 8:16

God led me into the inner courtyard.

About twenty-five men stood between the temple porch and the altar.

Their backs were toward the temple. Their faces were toward the east.

They bowed down to worship the sun.

## Ezekiel 8:17

God asked, “Son of man, do you see this?

The people of Judah do terrible things here.

Across the land they also hurt people.

Again and again they make me angry.

Look! They even hold a branch to their noses.”

## Ezekiel 8:18

God said, “I will act in anger.

I will show them no pity. I will not spare them.

Even if they shout to me, I will not listen.”
