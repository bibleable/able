# Ezekiel 44 — The east gate stays shut and the priests serve

**About this chapter**

In his vision, Ezekiel returns to the east gate. The LORD explains who will care for the temple and who may come near his table.

## The east gate stays shut

## Ezekiel 44:1

The man brought me back to the outer gate of the holy place.

This gate faced east. It was shut.

## Ezekiel 44:2

The LORD said, “Keep this gate shut.

No one may open it or come in through it.

I, the LORD, the God of Israel, entered through it.

That is why it must stay shut.”

## Ezekiel 44:3

“Only the prince may sit inside the gateway to eat in my presence.

He must come in through the porch of the gateway.

He must leave by that same porch.”

## Ezekiel 44:4

The man took me through the north gate to the temple front.

I saw the glory of the LORD filling the temple.

I fell with my face to the ground.

## Ezekiel 44:5

The LORD called me “son of man.”

“Look carefully. Listen closely to all my rules for the temple.

Notice the way in and every way out.”

## The LORD speaks to Israel about his holy place

## Ezekiel 44:6

God told me to speak to the Israelites who had turned against him.

He said, “I have had enough of the terrible things you do.”

## Ezekiel 44:7

“You let foreigners stay in my holy place.

They were uncircumcised in heart and body.”

They did not bear the sign of God’s covenant on their bodies.

Their hearts were closed to him too.

“You made my temple unclean while offering me the fat and blood.

You broke your covenant, your solemn agreement with me.”

## Ezekiel 44:8

“I put you in charge of my holy things.

You left that work to others.

You put them in charge of my holy place.”

## Ezekiel 44:9

The Lord GOD said, “No foreigner uncircumcised in heart and body may enter my holy place.

This also applies to foreigners living among the Israelites.”

## The work of the Levites

## Ezekiel 44:10

Some Levites, people from the tribe of Levi, turned away from God.

When Israel worshiped idols, these Levites followed the idols too.

They would answer for what they had done.

## Ezekiel 44:11

God said, “They will still have work in my holy place.

They will look after the gates and serve the people.

They will kill the animals people bring for burnt offerings and other sacrifices.”

## Ezekiel 44:12

“Those Levites served idols. They led Israel into sin.

I raised my hand and swore an oath.

They must answer for the wrong they did.”

## Ezekiel 44:13

“They may no longer come near me to serve as priests.

They may not approach my holy things, even the most holy things.

They will bear the shame of what they did.”

## Ezekiel 44:14

“Yet I will give them charge of the temple work.

They will see to the tasks that need doing there.”

## The priests from Zadok’s family

## Ezekiel 44:15

Other Levites were priests from the family of Zadok.

They cared for God’s holy place when Israel turned away.

God said, “They may come near to serve me.

They will stand before me and offer the fat and blood.”

## Ezekiel 44:16

“They alone may enter my holy place and approach my table.

They will serve me and do the work I give them.”

## Ezekiel 44:17

These priests must put on linen clothes to enter the inner courtyard.

Linen is cloth made from the flax plant.

At its gates or inside the temple, they must never wear wool.

## Ezekiel 44:18

They must wrap linen turbans around their heads.

They must also wear linen underclothes.

Their clothes must keep them from getting sweaty.

## Ezekiel 44:19

Before joining the people in the outer courtyard, they must change.

They must leave their holy clothes in the holy rooms.

Then those clothes would not pass on holiness to anyone who touched them.

## Ezekiel 44:20

The priests must keep their hair neatly trimmed.

They must neither shave their heads nor let their hair grow long.

## Ezekiel 44:21

A priest must not drink wine before entering the inner courtyard.

## Ezekiel 44:22

A priest may marry an Israelite woman who has never had sex.

He may also marry the widow of another priest.

He must not marry another widow or a divorced woman.

## The priests teach and judge

## Ezekiel 44:23

“The priests must teach my people what is holy.”

Holy things are set apart for God. Other things are for everyday use.

The priests must also teach what is clean or unclean for worship.

## Ezekiel 44:24

“When people have a dispute, the priests must judge it by my laws.

At all my feasts they must follow my rules.

They must keep my Sabbaths holy.”

The Sabbath was the weekly day of rest.

## Ezekiel 44:25

Being near a dead person makes a priest unclean for worship.

He may go near his father or mother when they die.

He may also go near his son, daughter, brother, or unmarried sister.

## Ezekiel 44:26

Once the priest is clean again, he must count seven days.

## Ezekiel 44:27

When he returns to serve in the inner courtyard, he must bring a sin offering.

## Food and land for the priests

## Ezekiel 44:28

The LORD said, “I myself am the priests’ inheritance.”

An inheritance is a share that belongs to someone.

“Give them no share of land in Israel. I am their share.”

## Ezekiel 44:29

The priests may eat from the grain offerings.

They may eat from the sin offerings and guilt offerings too.

Whatever Israel sets apart completely for the LORD will belong to them.

## Ezekiel 44:30

Give the priests the best of the first things you harvest.

Give them the best part of every contribution.

Give the priest the first batch of dough you make.

Then a blessing will rest on your home.

## Ezekiel 44:31

A priest must never eat a bird or animal found dead.

He must never eat one torn by wild animals.
