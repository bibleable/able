# Ezekiel 36 — God promises Israel a home and a new heart

**About this chapter**

Israel’s cities stand empty. The LORD tells Ezekiel to speak to the land as if it could hear him. Then he tells the people how he will bring them home and change them inside.

## Other nations have taken Israel’s land

## Ezekiel 36:1

The LORD called me “son of man.”

He said, “Speak my message to the mountains of Israel.

Say, ‘Mountains of Israel, listen to the LORD.’”

## Ezekiel 36:2

The Lord GOD said, “Your enemy shouted with joy.

‘Those ancient hills belong to us now!’”

## Ezekiel 36:3

God told me to say, “Enemies have trampled you on every side.

They have stripped you bare. Other nations have taken you.

People tell lies about you.”

## Ezekiel 36:4

The Lord GOD spoke to the mountains and hills.

He spoke to the deep gullies and the valleys.

He spoke to the ruined places and the empty cities.

Neighboring nations had taken what they wanted and laughed at the land.

## Ezekiel 36:5

God said, “I am angry with Edom and the other nations.

They were glad to take my land for themselves.

They showed nothing but scorn as they seized its pastures.”

## Ezekiel 36:6

God told me to speak to Israel’s hills and valleys again.

“The other nations have insulted you.

I care deeply about what happens to you.

That is why I speak with such anger.”

## Ezekiel 36:7

The Lord GOD said, “I have raised my hand to make a promise.

The nations around you will know what it means to suffer insults.”

## The land will feed its people again

## Ezekiel 36:8

“Mountains of Israel, your trees will grow branches and fruit.

That fruit will feed my people.

They will soon come home.”

## Ezekiel 36:9

“I am on your side. I am turning toward you.

People will work your soil and plant seeds.”

## Ezekiel 36:10

“I will bring the whole people of Israel back in great numbers.

They will live in your cities and rebuild the ruins.”

## Ezekiel 36:11

“People and animals will fill the land.

Families and flocks will grow.

You will have people living here as you once did.

You will do even better than before.

Then you will know that I am the LORD.”

## Ezekiel 36:12

“My people will walk on your soil again.

The land will be theirs to pass on to their children.

You will no longer take their children from them.”

## Ezekiel 36:13

People had said to the land, “You eat people up!

You rob the nation of its children.”

They meant that people kept losing their lives there.

## Ezekiel 36:14

The Lord GOD told the land, “You will no longer swallow up people.

Families will no longer lose children because of you.”

## Ezekiel 36:15

“I will put an end to the other nations’ insults.

You will no longer have to bear their scorn.

You will no longer bring about your people’s downfall.”

## Why Israel had to leave

## Ezekiel 36:16

The LORD gave me another message.

## Ezekiel 36:17

“Son of man, my people made their land unclean by doing wrong.”

God compared this to a rule about worship in Israel.

During her period, a woman was unclean for worship for a time.

God used that familiar rule to describe the state of the land.

## Ezekiel 36:18

“My people shed blood in the land.

They worshiped idols there and made the land unclean.

That is why I acted in anger against them.”

## Ezekiel 36:19

“I scattered them across many countries.

I judged them for the things they had done.”

## Ezekiel 36:20

Wherever Israel went, other nations treated God’s holy name with disrespect.

They said, “These people belong to the LORD.

Yet they had to leave his land!”

## Ezekiel 36:21

God said, “I care about my holy name.

Israel has brought shame on my name in those countries.”

## God gives his people a new heart

## Ezekiel 36:22

God told me to say, “People of Israel, I will act for the honor of my holy name.

You brought shame on my name among the nations.

I am doing this for my name’s sake.”

## Ezekiel 36:23

“I will show those nations how holy my name is.

They will see my holiness in what I do among you.

Then they will know that I am the LORD.”

## Ezekiel 36:24

“I will gather you from all those countries.

I will bring you back to your own land.”

## Ezekiel 36:25

“I will sprinkle clean water on you.

I will wash away everything that made you unclean.

I will cleanse you of your worship of idols.”

## Ezekiel 36:26

“I will give you a new heart and a new spirit.

I will take away your heart of stone.

I will give you a heart of flesh.”

A stone cannot respond. A living heart can.

God would change them so they could respond to him.

## Ezekiel 36:27

“I will put my Spirit within you.

I will help you follow my laws.

You will take care to do what I command.”

## Ezekiel 36:28

“You will live in the land I gave your ancestors.

You will be my people. I will be your God.”

## Ezekiel 36:29

“I will save you from everything that makes you unclean.

I will make the grain grow in abundance.

I will keep hunger from sweeping through your land.”

![A stalk of grain. God promises that the fields will grow food again.](../pictures/grain.svg)

## Ezekiel 36:30

“Your trees will bear plenty of fruit.

Your fields will give you plenty of food.

Other nations will stop mocking you because your people are starving.”

## Ezekiel 36:31

“Then you will remember how you lived.

You will feel disgust with yourselves for the evil you did.”

## Ezekiel 36:32

“Understand why I am acting. I am doing this for my holy name.

People of Israel, be ashamed of what you have done.”

## Empty cities will fill with people

## Ezekiel 36:33

The Lord GOD said, “When I cleanse you from your sins, people will rebuild your ruined cities.

They will live in those cities again.”

## Ezekiel 36:34

Travelers once saw empty fields wherever they went.

Now farmers will work that land again.

## Ezekiel 36:35

People will say, “This land once lay empty.

Now it looks like the garden of Eden!

Those cities were piles of broken stones.

Now they have strong walls and people living in them.”

## Ezekiel 36:36

God said, “The nations left around you will know who did this.

I rebuilt what lay in ruins.

I planted again where nothing grew.

I am the LORD. I have spoken, and I will do it.”

## Ezekiel 36:37

The Lord GOD said, “Once again I will hear my people’s prayer.

I will make them grow in number like a flock.”

## Ezekiel 36:38

At feasts in Jerusalem, people brought many sheep to offer to God.

Crowds of people will fill the ruined cities like those flocks.

God said, “Then they will know that I am the LORD.”
