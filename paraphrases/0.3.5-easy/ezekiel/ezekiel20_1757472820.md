# Ezekiel 20 — Israel’s leaders come to ask the LORD

**About this chapter**

Israel’s leaders come to Ezekiel with a question. The LORD asks them to remember Egypt, the wilderness, and the land he gave their families.

## The leaders sit before Ezekiel

## Ezekiel 20:1

It was day ten of the fifth month, in the seventh year.

Some leaders of Israel came to ask the LORD for guidance.

They sat down in front of me.

## Ezekiel 20:2

Then the LORD spoke to me.

## Ezekiel 20:3

He called me “son of man.”

He said, “Tell these leaders what the Lord GOD says.

Have you come to ask me for guidance?

As surely as I live, I will not answer your questions.”

## Ezekiel 20:4

“Will you judge them, son of man? Will you judge them?

Tell them about the terrible things their ancestors did.”

## God remembers Israel in Egypt

## Ezekiel 20:5

God said, “I chose Israel when Jacob’s descendants lived in Egypt.

I made myself known to them.

I raised my hand to make a solemn promise.

I told them, ‘I am the LORD your God.’”

## Ezekiel 20:6

“I promised to lead them out of Egypt.

I had chosen a land for them, the finest of all lands.

It flowed with milk and honey.”

This meant the land could provide plenty of good food.

## Ezekiel 20:7

“I told each of them to throw away the idols they looked to.

‘Do not make yourselves unclean by worshiping Egypt’s idols.

I am the LORD your God.’”

## Ezekiel 20:8

“They turned against me and refused to listen.

They kept the idols of Egypt.

In Egypt I decided to show them my fierce anger.”

## Ezekiel 20:9

“Yet the nations around them were watching.

I acted to keep those nations from dishonoring my name.

They saw me make myself known by leading Israel out.”

## The parents in the wilderness

## Ezekiel 20:10

“I brought my people out of Egypt into the wilderness.”

## Ezekiel 20:11

“I gave them my laws and taught them my commands.

Those who obey them will live.”

## Ezekiel 20:12

“I gave them the Sabbaths, the days of rest.

These days were a sign between us.

They showed that I, the LORD, set my people apart as holy.”

## Ezekiel 20:13

“Even in the wilderness Israel turned against me.

They rejected the laws that lead to life.

They treated my Sabbaths like any other day.

In my anger I decided to put an end to them there.”

## Ezekiel 20:14

“Yet I acted to protect my name.

The other nations had seen me bring Israel out of Egypt.”

## Ezekiel 20:15

“In the wilderness I raised my hand and made a promise.

That generation would never enter the land I had given them.

It was the finest of lands, flowing with milk and honey.”

## Ezekiel 20:16

“They refused to obey my commands and dishonored my Sabbaths.

They still wanted to worship their idols.”

## Ezekiel 20:17

“Even so, I felt pity for them.

I spared them from destruction in the wilderness.”

## God speaks to their children

## Ezekiel 20:18

“In the wilderness I spoke to their children.

‘Do not follow your parents’ rules or copy their ways.

Do not make yourselves unclean with their idols.’”

## Ezekiel 20:19

“‘I am the LORD your God.

Follow my laws. Do what I tell you.’”

## Ezekiel 20:20

“‘Keep my Sabbaths holy. They will be a sign between us.

You will know that I am the LORD your God.’”

## Ezekiel 20:21

“The children also turned against me.

They disobeyed the laws that lead to life and dishonored my Sabbaths.

I decided to show them my fierce anger there in the wilderness.”

## Ezekiel 20:22

“Yet I held back my hand to protect my name.

The nations had seen me bring Israel out of Egypt.”

## Ezekiel 20:23

“But I raised my hand in the wilderness and made another promise.

I would scatter them among different nations.”

## Ezekiel 20:24

“They rejected my laws and dishonored my Sabbaths.

They kept looking to their parents’ idols.”

## Ezekiel 20:25

“So I gave them over to bad rules.

Those rules could never give them life.”

## Ezekiel 20:26

“They offered their firstborn children in fire.

Because of those gifts I declared the people unclean.

I would bring them to ruin.

Then they would know that I am the LORD.”

## The people in the promised land and the leaders today

## Ezekiel 20:27

God told me, “Tell Israel how else their ancestors insulted me.

They broke faith with me.”

## Ezekiel 20:28

“I brought them into the land I had promised.

They chose high hills and leafy trees as places to worship.

There they offered sacrifices that made me angry.

They burned sweet-smelling incense and poured out drinks as offerings.”

## Ezekiel 20:29

“I asked, ‘What is this high place where you go?’”

People still called it Bamah, meaning “high place.”

## Ezekiel 20:30

God had a question for the people of Israel now.

“Will you make yourselves unclean as your ancestors did?

Will you betray me by worshiping their idols?”

## Ezekiel 20:31

“You still offer your sons in fire as gifts to idols.

You still make yourselves unclean.

And now you come to ask me for guidance?

As surely as I live, I will not answer your questions.”

## Ezekiel 20:32

“You want to be like the nations around you.

You say, ‘Let us serve gods of wood and stone.’

What you have in mind will never happen.”

## God will gather and judge his people

## Ezekiel 20:33

“As surely as I live, I will rule over you.

I will reach out my strong arm and show my anger.”

## Ezekiel 20:34

“With that same power and anger I will gather you.

I will bring you out of the countries where I scattered you.”

## Ezekiel 20:35

“I will lead you into the wilderness among the nations.

There I will judge you face to face.”

## Ezekiel 20:36

“I judged your ancestors in the wilderness of Egypt.

I will judge you too.”

## Ezekiel 20:37

“I will make you pass under my rod.”

This pictures a shepherd checking the sheep one by one.

“I will bind you to my covenant, my solemn agreement with you.”

![A sheep pen with an open gate. A shepherd can check the sheep as they pass through.](../pictures/sheep-pen.svg)

## Ezekiel 20:38

“I will separate out those who keep rebelling against me.

I will bring them out of the lands where they live.

But they will never enter Israel.

Then you will know that I am the LORD.”

## Israel will worship on God’s mountain

## Ezekiel 20:39

“People of Israel, go on serving your idols for now.

Afterward you will listen to me.

Your gifts and idols will no longer dishonor my holy name.”

## Ezekiel 20:40

“On my holy mountain, high in Israel, you will all serve me.

There I will accept you.

I will ask for your best gifts and all your holy offerings.”

## Ezekiel 20:41

“I will gather you from the countries where I scattered you.

I will welcome you like an offering with a pleasing smell.

The nations will see through you that I am holy.”

## Ezekiel 20:42

“I will bring you back to Israel.

This is the land I promised your ancestors.

Then you will know that I am the LORD.”

## Ezekiel 20:43

“There you will remember the deeds that made you unclean.

You will feel disgust with yourselves for the evil you did.”

## Ezekiel 20:44

“You will see that I acted for the honor of my name.

I will not give you the punishment your evil deeds deserved.

Then, people of Israel, you will know that I am the LORD.”

## A warning for the southern forest

## Ezekiel 20:45

The LORD spoke to me again.

## Ezekiel 20:46

He said, “Son of man, face south.

Speak my warning against the forest of the Negev.”

The Negev was the southern region.

## Ezekiel 20:47

“Tell the forest to listen to the Lord GOD.

‘I will set you on fire.

Both green trees and dry trees will burn.

No one will put out the flames.

People’s faces will feel the burning heat from south to north.’”

## Ezekiel 20:48

“Everyone will see that I, the LORD, started the fire.

No one will put it out.”

## Ezekiel 20:49

I said, “Ah, Lord GOD! Listen to what they say about me.

‘Isn’t he just telling us stories?’”

They heard the picture of the forest but questioned my warning.
