# Ezekiel 7 — Silver and gold cannot save Israel

**About this chapter**

The LORD tells Ezekiel that the time has come to judge Israel. People have filled the land with violence. Even their greatest treasures cannot keep them safe.

## The end has come

## Ezekiel 7:1

The LORD gave me a message.

## Ezekiel 7:2

He called me “son of man.”

The Lord GOD told me to speak to Israel.

“The end has come! It has reached every part of your land.”

## Ezekiel 7:3

“Now the end has come for you.

I will act in anger and judge what you have done.

I will repay you for the things you did that I hate.”

## Ezekiel 7:4

“I will show no pity. I will not spare you.

I will punish you for the terrible things you do.

Then you will know that I am the LORD.”

## Ezekiel 7:5

The Lord GOD said, “Disaster is coming!

Nothing like it has happened before.”

## Ezekiel 7:6

“The end has come! The end has come!

It is here. It rises up against you.”

## Ezekiel 7:7

“People of this land, disaster has reached you.

The day is almost here.

On the mountains you will hear cries of fear.

The joyful shouting will stop.”

## Ezekiel 7:8

“Very soon I will act in fierce anger.

I will judge you for the way you live.

I will repay you for the things you do that I hate.”

## Ezekiel 7:9

“I will show no pity. I will not spare you.

I will punish you for your evil deeds.

Then you will know who has struck you.

It is I, the LORD.”

## Violence brings punishment

## Ezekiel 7:10

“Look! The day has come. Disaster is here.”

God described a stick growing buds and pride coming into flower.

The people’s pride had grown. Now their punishment was ready.

## Ezekiel 7:11

Their violence had become like a stick used to punish them.

None of the crowd would remain.

All their wealth and precious things would disappear.

## Ezekiel 7:12

“The time has come. The day is here.

Someone buying land has no reason to celebrate.

Someone selling it has no reason to grieve over the sale.

My anger will reach them all.”

## Ezekiel 7:13

Even if both people lived, the seller would never get the land back.

What God showed Ezekiel about the people would happen.

No one could keep their life by doing wrong.

## Ezekiel 7:14

They blew the trumpet to call people to battle.

Everything was ready. Yet no one went out to fight.

God’s anger was against them all.

## Ezekiel 7:15

Outside the city, enemies would kill people with swords.

Inside the city, people would die of hunger and sickness.

## The people lose their strength and their wealth

## Ezekiel 7:16

Some people would escape to the mountains.

Their cries would sound like the low calls of doves.

Each person would grieve over the wrong they had done.

## Ezekiel 7:17

Their hands would hang limp.

Their knees would feel like water.

Fear would leave them too weak to stand firm.

## Ezekiel 7:18

They would wear rough cloth to show their grief.

They would shave their heads.

People would see the fear and shame on their faces.

## Ezekiel 7:19

They would throw their silver into the streets.

Even their gold would seem too unclean to touch.

Silver and gold could never save them from the LORD’s anger.

They could not eat their wealth to fill their empty stomachs.

Their love of wealth had led them into sin.

![Silver pieces. The people could not eat silver when they were hungry.](../pictures/ten-silver-coins.svg)

## Ezekiel 7:20

The people took pride in their beautiful jewelry.

They used it to make idols, images they worshiped as gods.

God hated these idols.

He would make the people see them as unclean.

## Ezekiel 7:21

God would let foreigners carry those treasures away.

Wicked people would take them and treat them with disrespect.

## Ezekiel 7:22

God said, “I will turn away.

Violent men will enter the place I treasure.”

They would dishonor God’s holy place.

## Even the leaders have no answer

## Ezekiel 7:23

“Make a chain!” God said.

People had killed others throughout the land.

Violence filled the city.

## Ezekiel 7:24

God would bring people from the cruelest nations.

They would take the houses for themselves.

God would end the pride of the powerful.

The invaders would dishonor the holy places.

## Ezekiel 7:25

Great fear and pain were coming.

The people would look for peace and find none.

## Ezekiel 7:26

One disaster would follow another.

People would hear one frightening report after another.

They would ask a prophet what God had shown him.

The priests would have no teaching to give.

The elders would have no advice.

## Ezekiel 7:27

The king would grieve. The prince would lose all hope.

The people’s hands would shake.

God said, “I will deal with them according to their actions.

I will judge them by the standards they use for others.

Then they will know that I am the LORD.”

**Think about it**

**Why would silver and gold be useless when the people were hungry?**
