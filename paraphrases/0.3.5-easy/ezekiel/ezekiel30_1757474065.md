# Ezekiel 30 — Egypt and its helpers will fall

**About this chapter**

Egypt has strong cities and friends in other lands. The LORD tells Ezekiel what will happen to them when Babylon’s king comes.

## Trouble comes to Egypt and its neighbors

## Ezekiel 30:1

The LORD spoke to me again.

## Ezekiel 30:2

He called me “son of man.”

He told me to give the people his message.

“Cry out in grief! What a terrible day it will be!”

## Ezekiel 30:3

“The Day of the LORD is near.”

This is the day when the LORD will judge the nations.

Clouds will darken the sky. Trouble will come to those lands.

## Ezekiel 30:4

War will come to Egypt. Many people there will die.

Enemies will take Egypt’s riches and tear down its foundations.

The people of Cush will be in great distress.

## Ezekiel 30:5

People from Cush, Put, Lud, and Libya will die in battle with Egypt.

People from many other lands will die too.

So will men from the land of the covenant.

A covenant is a solemn agreement.

## Ezekiel 30:6

The LORD said, “The nations that help Egypt will fall.

Egypt takes pride in its strength, but that strength will fail.”

From Migdol to Syene, people will die in battle.

## Ezekiel 30:7

Their lands will lie empty like other empty lands.

Their cities will stand in ruins like other ruined cities.

## Ezekiel 30:8

The LORD said, “I will set Egypt on fire.

All those who help Egypt will lose their strength.

Then they will know that I am the LORD.”

## Ezekiel 30:9

“On that day I will send messengers in ships.”

The people of Cush think they are safe.

But the news from Egypt will terrify them.

The day of Egypt’s defeat will surely come.

![A boat on the water. The messengers travel by water to bring news to Cush.](../pictures/boat.svg)

## Babylon’s king comes to Egypt

## Ezekiel 30:10

The Lord GOD said, “I will use Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon.

He will put an end to Egypt’s great crowds.”

## Ezekiel 30:11

Nebuchadnezzar and his people will come to destroy the land.

They are crueler than any other nation.

They will draw their swords against Egypt.

People will lie dead throughout the land.

## Ezekiel 30:12

“I will dry up the streams.

I will sell the land to people who do evil.

People from other countries will ruin the land and everything in it.

I, the LORD, have spoken.”

## Egypt’s cities face judgment

## Ezekiel 30:13

The Lord GOD said, “I will destroy the idols in Memphis.”

Idols are images that people worship as gods.

“Egypt will have no prince. I will fill the land with fear.”

## Ezekiel 30:14

“I will ruin the region of Pathros.

I will set fire to Zoan. I will judge Thebes.”

## Ezekiel 30:15

“I will show my anger against Pelusium.”

Pelusium was a city that helped defend Egypt.

“I will bring death to the crowds in Thebes.”

## Ezekiel 30:16

“I will set Egypt on fire.”

The people of Pelusium will suffer terribly.

Enemies will break into Thebes.

Every day will bring trouble to Memphis.

## Ezekiel 30:17

Young men in On and Pi-beseth will die in battle.

Enemies will take the people of those cities away as prisoners.

## Ezekiel 30:18

Daylight will turn dark in Tahpanhes.

The LORD says he will break Egypt’s yoke.

A yoke lets someone control animals as they work.

Egypt will lose the power it used to control others.

A cloud will cover the city. Enemies will take its daughters away.

## Ezekiel 30:19

The LORD said, “This is how I will judge Egypt.

Then they will know that I am the LORD.”

## A broken arm shows how Pharaoh will lose power

## Ezekiel 30:20

In the eleventh year, the LORD spoke to me again.

It was day seven of the first month.

## Ezekiel 30:21

“Son of man, I have broken the arm of Pharaoh, Egypt’s king.

No one has wrapped his arm so it can heal.

No one has held it steady with a splint.

His arm has no strength to hold a sword.”

The broken arm was a picture of Pharaoh losing power.

## Ezekiel 30:22

The Lord GOD said, “I am against Pharaoh.

I will break both his arms.

Even the arm that is still strong will break.

His sword will drop from his hand.”

## Ezekiel 30:23

“I will scatter the Egyptians across many countries.

They will live among other nations.”

## Ezekiel 30:24

“I will give the king of Babylon strong arms.

I will put my sword in his hand.

But I will break Pharaoh’s arms.”

Before Babylon’s king, Pharaoh will groan like a dying man.

## Ezekiel 30:25

Babylon’s king will grow strong. Pharaoh’s arms will hang limp.

The LORD will put his sword in the hand of Babylon’s king.

The king will use it against Egypt.

Then the people will know who the LORD is.

## Ezekiel 30:26

The LORD said, “I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations.

They will live in many different lands.

Then they will know that I am the LORD.”
