# Ezekiel 35 — The LORD answers Edom’s hatred

**About this chapter**

Edom wanted Israel’s land and was glad when Israel suffered. The LORD tells Ezekiel what He heard and how He will judge Edom.

## A message for Mount Seir

## Ezekiel 35:1

The LORD spoke to me, Ezekiel.

## Ezekiel 35:2

“Son of man, turn toward Mount Seir.

Speak My message against it.”

Mount Seir was in Edom.

This message was for the people who lived there.

![A mountain, like the high land named in Ezekiel’s message.](../pictures/mountain.svg)

## Ezekiel 35:3

“Tell them what the Lord GOD says.

Mount Seir, I am against you.

I will stretch out My hand against you.

I will leave your land empty and ruined.

## Ezekiel 35:4

“I will bring down your cities.

Your land will lie empty.

Then you will know that I am the LORD.

## Edom held on to hatred

## Ezekiel 35:5

“You have hated the people of Israel for a long time.

When disaster struck them, you handed them over to be killed.

This was when their punishment was reaching its end.

## Ezekiel 35:6

“As surely as I live, you too will face bloodshed.

You did not turn away in horror when others were killed.

Now that same violence will follow you.”

The Lord GOD has said this.

## Ezekiel 35:7

“I will leave Mount Seir empty and ruined.

No one will travel back and forth through it.

## Ezekiel 35:8

“Those killed in battle will cover your mountains.

They will fall on the hills and in the valleys.

They will fall in the deep, narrow places between the hills.

## Ezekiel 35:9

“Your land will stay empty forever.

No one will live in your cities.

Then you will know that I am the LORD.

## The LORD heard Edom boast

## Ezekiel 35:10

“You said, ‘Both nations and their lands will be ours.

We will take them for ourselves.’

Yet I, the LORD, was there.”

## Ezekiel 35:11

The Lord GOD says, “As surely as I live, I will judge you.

You acted in anger because you hated those people.

You wanted what was theirs.

I will deal with you according to what you did.

When I judge you, I will make Myself known among them.

## Ezekiel 35:12

“You spoke about the mountains of Israel with scorn.

You said, ‘They lie empty! The land is ours for the taking!’

Then you will know that I, the LORD, heard every word.

## Ezekiel 35:13

“You boasted against Me.

You spoke against Me again and again.

I heard you Myself.”

## Edom rejoiced over Israel’s loss

## Ezekiel 35:14

The Lord GOD says, “While the whole earth celebrates, I will leave your land empty.

## Ezekiel 35:15

“You were glad when the land Israel’s families had received lay in ruins.

Now I will do the same to you.

Mount Seir and all Edom will lie in ruins.

Then people will know that I am the LORD.”
