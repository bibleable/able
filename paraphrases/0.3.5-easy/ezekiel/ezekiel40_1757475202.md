# Ezekiel 40 — A man measures the temple gates

**About this chapter**

God shows Ezekiel a vision. In the vision, Ezekiel follows a man through the temple gates. The man shows him the size of each place.

## God takes Ezekiel to a mountain

## Ezekiel 40:1

We had lived away from home for 25 years.

It was 14 years since enemies destroyed Jerusalem.

On the tenth day of the first month, the LORD took hold of me by his power.

He took me there.

## Ezekiel 40:2

God showed me the land of Israel in a vision.

A vision is something God lets someone see.

He set me on a very high mountain.

On its south slope, some buildings looked like a city.

## Ezekiel 40:3

God took me to those buildings.

A man stood in the gateway.

He shone like bronze, a kind of metal.

He held a linen cord and a measuring rod.

## Ezekiel 40:4

The man called me “son of man.”

He told me, “Look closely and listen carefully.

Pay attention to everything I show you.

That is why you came here.

Tell the people of Israel everything you see.”

## The east gate

## Ezekiel 40:5

I saw a wall around the temple grounds.

The man's rod was 6 long cubits long.

A cubit was the length from elbow to fingertip.

A long cubit added the width of a hand.

The man measured the wall with his rod.

It was 1 rod thick and 1 rod high.

![A measuring rod divided into 6 equal parts. Each part is 1 long cubit.](../pictures/ezekiel-measuring-rod.svg)

## Ezekiel 40:6

The man climbed the steps of the east gate.

He measured the floor at the entrance.

It was 1 rod deep.

## Ezekiel 40:7

Rooms lined the passage through the gate.

Each room measured 1 rod by 1 rod.

There were 5 cubits between the rooms.

A covered porch faced inward.

The floor at its entrance was 1 rod deep.

## Ezekiel 40:8

Then the man measured the gateway's inner porch.

## Ezekiel 40:9

The porch was 8 cubits deep.

Its doorposts were 2 cubits thick.

The porch faced the temple.

## Ezekiel 40:10

The east gate had 3 rooms on each side.

All 6 rooms were the same size.

The gateposts on both sides matched too.

## Ezekiel 40:11

The man measured the entrance to the gateway.

It was 10 cubits wide and 13 cubits long.

## Ezekiel 40:12

Each room had a low wall in front.

The wall was 1 cubit high.

Each room measured 6 cubits by 6 cubits.

## Ezekiel 40:13

The man measured across the gateway at roof level.

He measured between two rooms facing each other.

From doorway to doorway it was 25 cubits.

## Ezekiel 40:14

The gateposts were 60 cubits high.

The gateway reached the gatepost by the courtyard.

A courtyard is an open space between walls or buildings.

## Ezekiel 40:15

The man measured from the entrance to the far end of the inner porch.

It was 50 cubits.

## Ezekiel 40:16

The rooms and side pillars had windows with sloping edges.

These windows faced into the gateway.

Windows lined the inside of the porches too.

Each side pillar had a picture of a palm tree.

## The outer courtyard and its gates

## Ezekiel 40:17

The man took me into the outer courtyard.

A paved walkway went around the courtyard.

There were 30 rooms facing the walkway.

## Ezekiel 40:18

The walkway ran beside the gateways.

It went back as far as the gateways did.

This was the lower walkway.

## Ezekiel 40:19

The man measured from the front of the lower gate to the outside of the inner courtyard.

It was 100 cubits on the east side.

It was also 100 cubits on the north side.

## Ezekiel 40:20

The outer courtyard had a gate facing north.

The man measured its length and width.

## Ezekiel 40:21

This gate had 3 rooms on each side.

Its rooms, side pillars and porch matched the east gate.

The whole gateway was 50 cubits long and 25 cubits wide.

## Ezekiel 40:22

Its windows, porch and palm tree pictures matched the east gate's in size.

There were 7 steps up to the gate.

The porch stood across from the steps.

## Ezekiel 40:23

An inner gate faced the outer north gate.

The east side had gates facing each other too.

The man measured 100 cubits between the north gates.

## Ezekiel 40:24

The man led me to the south side.

I saw a gate facing south.

He measured its side pillars and porch.

They matched the others in size.

## Ezekiel 40:25

Windows lined the gateway and its porch, like the others.

The gateway was 50 cubits long and 25 cubits wide.

## Ezekiel 40:26

There were 7 steps up to the gate.

The porch stood across from the steps.

Each side had a palm tree picture on its pillar.

## Ezekiel 40:27

The inner courtyard also had a gate facing south.

The man measured 100 cubits between the south gates.

## The gates of the inner courtyard

## Ezekiel 40:28

The man led me through the inner south gate.

We entered the inner courtyard.

He measured the gate. It matched the others in size.

## Ezekiel 40:29

Its rooms, side pillars and porch matched the others.

Windows lined the gateway and the porch.

The gateway was 50 cubits long and 25 cubits wide.

## Ezekiel 40:30

The porches around the inner courtyard were 25 cubits long.

Each was 5 cubits deep.

## Ezekiel 40:31

The south gate's porch faced the outer courtyard.

Its side pillars had pictures of palm trees.

There were 8 steps up to this gate.

## Ezekiel 40:32

The man took me to the inner courtyard's east side.

He measured the gate. Its size matched the others.

## Ezekiel 40:33

Its rooms, side pillars and porch matched the others.

Windows lined both the gateway and the porch.

The gateway was 50 cubits long and 25 cubits wide.

## Ezekiel 40:34

Its porch faced the outer courtyard.

The pillars on both sides had pictures of palm trees.

There were 8 steps up to this gate.

## Ezekiel 40:35

The man took me to the inner north gate.

He measured it. Its size matched the others.

## Ezekiel 40:36

Its rooms, side pillars and porch matched the others.

It had windows all around.

The gateway was 50 cubits long and 25 cubits wide.

## Ezekiel 40:37

Its porch faced the outer courtyard.

The pillars on both sides had pictures of palm trees.

There were 8 steps up to this gate.

## Tables for preparing offerings

## Ezekiel 40:38

Each inner gateway had a room beside its porch.

A doorway led into the room.

Here the priests would wash animals for burnt offerings.

These were gifts they burned on the altar for God.

## Ezekiel 40:39

Inside the porch were 2 tables on each side.

Here the priests would kill the animals for burnt offerings.

They would also prepare sin offerings and guilt offerings here.

These were gifts people brought when they had done wrong.

## Ezekiel 40:40

More tables stood outside, up by the north gate entrance.

There were 2 on each side of the porch.

## Ezekiel 40:41

There were 4 tables inside and 4 outside.

The priests would use all 8 to kill the animals for offerings.

## Ezekiel 40:42

There were also 4 tables of neatly cut stone.

Each was 1½ cubits long and 1½ cubits wide.

Each stood 1 cubit high.

These held the tools for killing animals for burnt offerings and other gifts.

## Ezekiel 40:43

Hooks with two points lined the room.

Each hook was as long as a hand is wide.

The priests would put the meat on the tables.

## Rooms for the priests

## Ezekiel 40:44

I saw two rooms in the inner courtyard, outside the inner gate.

The room by the north gate faced south.

The room by the south gate faced north.

## Ezekiel 40:45

The man told me about the room facing south.

It was for priests who cared for the temple.

## Ezekiel 40:46

The room facing north was for priests who cared for the altar.

These priests came from Zadok's family.

Of all the Levites, only Zadok's family could come near the LORD to serve him.

The Levites were people from the family of Levi.

## Ezekiel 40:47

The man measured the inner courtyard.

It was square, 100 cubits long and 100 cubits wide.

The altar stood in front of the temple.

## The temple porch

## Ezekiel 40:48

The man took me to the temple porch.

He measured the pillars on either side.

Each measured 5 cubits across.

The entrance was 14 cubits wide.

Its side walls measured 3 cubits on each side.

## Ezekiel 40:49

The porch was 20 cubits wide and 12 cubits deep.

There were 10 steps up to it.

A column stood beside each side pillar.
