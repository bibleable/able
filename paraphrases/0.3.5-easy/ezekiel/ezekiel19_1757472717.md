# Ezekiel 19 — A song of grief for Israel’s princes

**About this chapter**

God gives Ezekiel a song about Israel’s rulers. First the song tells of two young lions taken away. Then it tells of a vine that loses the branches a king could use.

## A lioness loses her first cub

## Ezekiel 19:1

God told Ezekiel, “Sing a lament for the princes of Israel.”

A lament is a song that expresses grief.

## Ezekiel 19:2

The song begins, “Princes, what was your mother like?

She was like a lioness among lions.

She lay down among the young lions.

There she raised her cubs.”

## Ezekiel 19:3

One cub grew into a young lion.

He learned to tear apart the animals he caught.

Then he began to eat people.

The song uses this lion to describe a ruler who hurt people.

## Ezekiel 19:4

Other nations heard about him.

They caught the lion in a deep pit.

They used hooks to lead him away to Egypt.

## Another lion goes to Babylon

## Ezekiel 19:5

The lioness waited, but her cub did not return.

At last she lost hope.

She raised another cub until he grew strong.

## Ezekiel 19:6

This young lion prowled among the other lions.

He too learned to tear apart his prey.

Then he began to eat people.

## Ezekiel 19:7

He tore down forts and wrecked cities.

The whole land shook when he roared.

## Ezekiel 19:8

People came against him from lands on every side.

They threw a net over him.

They caught him in their pit.

## Ezekiel 19:9

They used hooks to pull him into a cage.

They took him to the king of Babylon.

There they kept him prisoner.

His roar no longer sounded in Israel’s mountains.

## A vine loses its strong branches

## Ezekiel 19:10

The song continues, “Your mother was like a grapevine beside water.

The vine grew in your vineyard.

With plenty of water, it grew many branches and much fruit.

![Bunches of grapes growing on a vine.](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

## Ezekiel 19:11

“Its branches were strong enough to make a king’s scepter.”

A scepter is a staff a king holds to show he rules.

The vine rose above the thick branches around it.

Everyone could see how tall and leafy it was.

## Ezekiel 19:12

In anger, someone tore up the vine by its roots.

They threw it to the ground.

The east wind dried up its fruit.

They stripped off its strong branches.

The branches dried up, and fire burned them.

## Ezekiel 19:13

Now the vine grows in the wilderness.

The ground there is dry and needs water.

## Ezekiel 19:14

Fire spread from the vine’s main branch and burned its fruit.

No branch strong enough for a king’s scepter remains.

This song is a lament.

People will sing it when they grieve.
