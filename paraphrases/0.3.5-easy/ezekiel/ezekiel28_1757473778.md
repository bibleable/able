# Ezekiel 28 — The ruler of Tyre grows proud

**About this chapter**

The ruler of Tyre believes his riches and wisdom make him a god. The LORD sends Ezekiel to answer him. Later the LORD speaks about Sidon and promises Israel a safe home.

## A ruler calls himself a god

## Ezekiel 28:1

The LORD spoke to me.

## Ezekiel 28:2

He called me “son of man.”

He told me to give the ruler of Tyre this message.

“You are proud. You say, ‘I am a god!

Here in the middle of the sea, I sit on a god’s throne.’

You think like someone who believes he is a god.

Yet you are human.”

## Ezekiel 28:3

“You think you are even wiser than Daniel!

What secret could anyone hide from you?”

## Ezekiel 28:4

“You have used your wisdom and understanding to gain wealth.

You have filled your storehouses with gold and silver.”

## Ezekiel 28:5

“You are skilled at buying and selling.

Your wealth has grown, and so has your pride.”

## Ezekiel 28:6

The Lord GOD told the ruler why judgment was coming.

“You think you have the mind of a god.”

## Ezekiel 28:7

“I will bring people from other nations against you.

They will show you no mercy.

They will draw their swords and ruin all the splendor your wisdom brought you.”

## Ezekiel 28:8

“They will send you down to the Pit.”

The Pit means the place of the dead.

“You will die a violent death in the middle of the sea.”

## Ezekiel 28:9

“When your attackers strike you, will you still say, ‘I am a god’?

In their hands you will be a man who can die.”

## Ezekiel 28:10

“Foreigners will kill you.

You will die in disgrace, like those called uncircumcised.”

Circumcision was a sign on a man’s body.

“I, the Lord GOD, have spoken.”

## A song of grief for the king of Tyre

## Ezekiel 28:11

The LORD spoke to me again.

## Ezekiel 28:12

He told me to sing a lament for the king of Tyre.

A lament is a song of grief.

God said, “You were a picture of perfection.

You were full of wisdom and entirely beautiful.”

## Ezekiel 28:13

“You were in Eden, God’s garden. You wore precious stones.

There were rubies, topaz, diamonds, beryl, onyx, and jasper.

There were sapphires, turquoise, and emeralds.

Gold held the stones in place.

All this was ready on the day I made you.”

## Ezekiel 28:14

“I chose you as a guardian cherub.”

A cherub is a heavenly being who serves God.

“You stood on God’s holy mountain.

You walked among stones that shone like fire.”

## Ezekiel 28:15

“From the day I made you, you did what was right.

Then evil appeared in you.”

## Ezekiel 28:16

“As your trade grew, you became violent. You sinned.

I drove you from my mountain in disgrace.

Guardian cherub, I sent you away from the fiery stones.”

## Ezekiel 28:17

“You grew proud of your beauty.

Your splendor spoiled your wisdom.

I threw you to the ground. Kings saw what happened to you.”

## Ezekiel 28:18

“You cheated people in trade and did many other wrong things.

That dishonored your holy places.

I brought fire out from within you. It burned you up.

Everyone watching saw you turn to ashes on the ground.”

## Ezekiel 28:19

“All the nations that know you look on in horror.

You have come to a terrible end. You are gone forever.”

## A message for Sidon

## Ezekiel 28:20

The LORD spoke to me once more.

## Ezekiel 28:21

He said, “Son of man, turn toward Sidon.

Speak my message against that city.”

## Ezekiel 28:22

“Sidon, I am against you.

I will show my glory in your city.

When I judge you, people will see that I am holy.

They will know that I am the LORD.”

## Ezekiel 28:23

“I will send a deadly sickness. Blood will flow in your streets.

Enemies will attack from every side. People will die inside the city.

Then they will know that I am the LORD.”

## Ezekiel 28:24

Israel’s neighbors looked down on the people of Israel.

They hurt them like sharp thorns that prick the skin.

God promised that those neighbors would no longer hurt Israel.

“Then they will know that I am the Lord GOD.”

## A home for Israel

## Ezekiel 28:25

The Lord GOD said, “My people Israel now live scattered among many nations.

I will gather them together.

Those nations will see that I am holy by what I do for Israel.

My people will live in the land I gave my servant Jacob.”

## Ezekiel 28:26

“They will live safely there. They will build houses and plant vineyards.”

A vineyard is a place where people grow grapes.

“I will judge the neighbors who looked down on them.

My people will be safe. They will know I am the LORD their God.”
