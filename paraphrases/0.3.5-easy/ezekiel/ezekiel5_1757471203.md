# Ezekiel 5 — Three parts of Ezekiel’s hair tell Jerusalem’s story

**About this chapter**

Ezekiel has made a model of Jerusalem. Now God tells him to use his hair to show what will happen to the people.

## Three equal parts

## Ezekiel 5:1

God called me “son of man.”

He said, “Use a sharp sword like a barber’s razor.

Shave the hair from your head and beard.

Weigh the hair on scales.”

The scales would help me make three equal parts.

## Ezekiel 5:2

God told me what to do when I finished acting out the siege.

A siege is when an army surrounds a city.

I was to burn one part inside the model city.

I was to cut another part with the sword around the city.

I was to let the wind carry the last part away.

God said he would send a sword after the scattered people.

## Ezekiel 5:3

God told me to keep a few hairs.

I was to tuck them into a fold in my clothes.

## Ezekiel 5:4

I was to take a few of those hairs out again.

I was to throw them into the fire.

God said fire would spread from there to all Israel.

## Why Jerusalem faces judgment

## Ezekiel 5:5

The Lord GOD explained, “This city stands for Jerusalem.

I placed Jerusalem in the middle of many nations.

Other lands surround it.”

## Ezekiel 5:6

“The people of Jerusalem turned against my laws.

They did worse things than their neighbors did.

They refused to live by my laws.”

## Ezekiel 5:7

The Lord GOD spoke to the people of Jerusalem.

“You have rebelled more than the nations around you.

You have refused to follow my laws.

You have not even kept the laws your neighbors follow.”

## Ezekiel 5:8

“I myself am against you, Jerusalem.

I will judge you. The other nations will see it.”

## Ezekiel 5:9

“You have done things I hate.

What I will do to you has never happened before.

I will never do it again.”

## Ezekiel 5:10

“Fathers among you will eat their sons.

Sons will eat their fathers.

I will judge you.

I will scatter the survivors in every direction.”

## What the three parts mean

## Ezekiel 5:11

The Lord GOD made a solemn promise.

“As surely as I live, you have made my holy place unclean.

You brought idols there and did things I hate.”

Idols are things people worship as gods.

God said he would take away his favor.

He would show no pity or spare them.

## Ezekiel 5:12

One third of the people would die inside Jerusalem.

Sickness or hunger would kill them there.

Another third would die in battle outside the walls.

God would scatter the last third in every direction.

Enemies with swords would chase them.

## Ezekiel 5:13

God said, “When I have punished them, my anger will end.

Then they will know that I, the LORD, have spoken.”

They would know how deeply the LORD cared about their unfaithfulness.

## Other nations will see

## Ezekiel 5:14

God told Jerusalem, “I will leave your city in ruins.

The nations around you will look down on you.

Everyone who passes by will see you.”

## Ezekiel 5:15

“The nations will insult you and mock you.

They will be horrified. What happens to you will warn them.

They will see how fiercely I punish you in my anger.

I, the LORD, have spoken.”

## Ezekiel 5:16

God compared the hunger he would send to deadly arrows.

People would grow hungrier and hungrier.

God would cut off their food supply.

## Ezekiel 5:17

God said, “Hunger and wild animals will take your children.

Deadly sickness and violence will spread among you.

I will bring war against you.

I, the LORD, have spoken.”
