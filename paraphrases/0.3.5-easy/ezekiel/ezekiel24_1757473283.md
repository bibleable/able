# Ezekiel 24 — A cooking pot and a sign of grief

**About this chapter**

On the day an army surrounds Jerusalem, God gives Ezekiel a story about a pot. Later, Ezekiel loses his wife. The people ask why he grieves in silence.

## Write down this day

## Ezekiel 24:1

The LORD spoke to me in the ninth year.

It was the tenth day of the tenth month.

## Ezekiel 24:2

God called me “son of man.”

“Write down today’s date.

Today the king of Babylon has begun a siege of Jerusalem.”

A siege is when an army surrounds a city.

## Ezekiel 24:3

God told me to tell a story to the people who disobeyed him.

The Lord GOD said, “Put the cooking pot on the fire.

Pour water into it.”

## Ezekiel 24:4

“Put the good pieces of meat into the pot.

Put in the thigh and the shoulder.

Fill the pot with the best bones.”

## Ezekiel 24:5

“Choose the best animal in the flock.

Pile fuel under the pot. Bring the water to a boil.

Let the bones cook in it.”

## The rust that stays on the pot

## Ezekiel 24:6

The Lord GOD said, “Trouble is coming to the city where people shed blood.”

Jerusalem was like a pot with rust that would not come off.

“Take the pieces out one by one.

Take them all. There is no need to draw lots to choose them.”

## Ezekiel 24:7

The city had killed people. Their blood was still there.

It lay on bare rock for everyone to see.

No one had covered it with dust.

## Ezekiel 24:8

God said, “I left that blood on bare rock.

I would not let anyone cover it.

In my anger I will punish the city for those deaths.”

## Ezekiel 24:9

The Lord GOD said, “Trouble is coming to this city of bloodshed!

I myself will pile the wood high.”

## Ezekiel 24:10

“Pile up the logs. Light the fire.

Cook the meat well and mix in the spices.

Let the bones burn.”

## Ezekiel 24:11

“Leave the empty pot on the coals.

Let the copper grow hot until it glows.

Let the heat melt away the filth and burn away the rust.”

## Ezekiel 24:12

Every effort to clean the pot had failed.

Even the fire had left thick rust on it.

## Ezekiel 24:13

God spoke to Jerusalem. “Your shameful deeds have made you unclean.

I tried to cleanse you, but you refused.

You will remain unclean until my anger against you has passed.”

## Ezekiel 24:14

“I, the LORD, have spoken. The time is coming when I will act.

I will not hold back, show pity, or change my mind.

I will judge you for what you have done.”

## Ezekiel loses his wife

## Ezekiel 24:15

Then the LORD spoke to me again.

## Ezekiel 24:16

“Son of man, the one you love to look upon will die.

I am about to take her from you with a sudden blow.

Do not mourn aloud or let your tears fall.”

## Ezekiel 24:17

“Groan quietly. Leave off the usual signs of mourning.

Wrap your turban around your head. Tie on your sandals.

Leave your lips uncovered. Do not eat the food people bring to mourners.”

## Ezekiel 24:18

I spoke to the people in the morning.

That evening my wife died.

The next morning I did what God had told me.

## Ezekiel 24:19

The people asked me, “What does the way you are acting mean for us?

Please tell us.”

## Ezekiel 24:20

I answered, “The LORD gave me a message for you.”

## Ezekiel 24:21

The Lord GOD told Israel, “I am about to let my sanctuary be dishonored.”

The sanctuary was the temple, God’s holy place.

It was their pride and joy. They loved to look at it.

“The sons and daughters you left behind will die in battle.”

## Ezekiel 24:22

I told them, “You will do as I have done.

You will leave your lips uncovered.

You will not eat the food people bring to mourners.”

## Ezekiel 24:23

“You will keep your turbans and sandals on.

You will not mourn aloud or weep.

Your sins will wear you down.

You will groan among yourselves.”

## Ezekiel 24:24

God said, “Ezekiel is a sign for you.

You will do what he has done.

When this happens, you will know that I am the Lord GOD.”

## Someone will bring the news

## Ezekiel 24:25

God spoke to me about the day the people would lose their stronghold.

They counted on it to keep them safe.

It filled them with pride and joy.

They would also lose their sons and daughters.

## Ezekiel 24:26

God said, “Someone who escapes will come to you with the news.”

## Ezekiel 24:27

“On that day you will be able to speak to the survivor.

Your time of silence will end.

You will be a sign to the people.

Then they will know that I am the LORD.”
