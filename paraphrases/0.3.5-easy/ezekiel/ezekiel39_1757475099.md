# Ezekiel 39 — Gog falls on the mountains of Israel

**About this chapter**

Ezekiel tells Gog what will happen to his army. The LORD also tells Israel why the people had to leave their land and how he will bring them home.

## The LORD speaks to Gog

## Ezekiel 39:1

God called me “son of man.”

He told me to give Gog this message.

“Gog, chief ruler of Meshech and Tubal, I am against you.”

## Ezekiel 39:2

“I will turn you around and lead you on.

I will bring you from the far north.

I will send you against Israel’s mountains.”

## Ezekiel 39:3

“I will knock the bow from your left hand.

The arrows will fall from your right hand.”

## Ezekiel 39:4

“You will die on the mountains of Israel.

Your soldiers and the nations with you will die there too.

Wild animals and hungry birds will feed on you.”

## Ezekiel 39:5

“You will fall in the open fields.

I, the Lord GOD, have spoken.”

## Ezekiel 39:6

“I will send fire on Magog.

Fire will also reach people who feel safe along the coasts.

They will know that I am the LORD.”

## Ezekiel 39:7

“My people Israel will know my holy name.

I will no longer let people dishonor it.

The nations will know that I am the LORD.

I am the Holy One in Israel.”

## Ezekiel 39:8

“The day I told you about is coming.

What I said will happen.”

## Israel clears the land after the battle

## Ezekiel 39:9

The people in Israel’s cities will go out to make fires.

They will burn the small shields and the large shields.

They will burn bows, arrows, clubs, and spears.

These weapons will fuel their fires for seven years.

## Ezekiel 39:10

They will have no need to gather wood in the fields.

They will have no need to cut trees in the forest.

The weapons will give them all the fuel they need.

The people who robbed them will now lose their own goods to Israel.

## Ezekiel 39:11

God said, “I will give Gog a burial place in Israel.”

It will be in the Valley of the Travelers, east of the sea.

People will bury Gog and his whole army there.

The graves will block the travelers’ way.

People will call it the Valley of Hamon-gog.

Hamon-gog means Gog’s great crowd.

## Ezekiel 39:12

For seven months the Israelites will bury the dead.

They will do this to make the land clean again.

## Ezekiel 39:13

Everyone in the land will help bury them.

God said, “When I show my glory, people will honor Israel for this work.”

## Ezekiel 39:14

After the seven months, men will begin a regular search.

They will travel across the land looking for the invaders’ remains.

The work of burial will continue until the land is clean.

## Ezekiel 39:15

When someone finds a human bone, they will set a marker beside it.

The marker will show the gravediggers where to look.

They will bury the bone in the Valley of Hamon-gog.

![A stone marker stands beside a bone so the gravediggers can find it.](../pictures/marker-by-a-bone.svg)

## Ezekiel 39:16

A city there will have the name Hamonah, meaning a great crowd.

This is how the people will make the land clean.

## A feast for the birds and wild animals

## Ezekiel 39:17

God told me to call the birds and the wild animals.

“Come from every direction. Gather on the mountains of Israel.

I am preparing a great feast of sacrifice for you.

You will eat flesh and drink blood.”

## Ezekiel 39:18

“The mighty soldiers will be your food.

You will drink the blood of rulers.

It will be like feasting on the fat animals of Bashan.

There will be as much as rams, lambs, goats, and bulls could provide.”

## Ezekiel 39:19

“At the sacrifice I prepare, you will eat fat until you are full.

You will drink blood until you are drunk.”

## Ezekiel 39:20

“At my table you will have more than enough to eat.

You will feed on horses and their riders.

You will feed on strong soldiers and warriors of every kind.”

The Lord GOD said this about Gog’s defeat.

## The LORD brings his people home

## Ezekiel 39:21

“I will show the nations my glory.

They will see how I use my power to carry out my judgment.”

## Ezekiel 39:22

“From that day on, Israel will know I am the LORD their God.”

## Ezekiel 39:23

“The nations will understand why Israel went into exile.”

Exile means having to live away from home.

God said, “My people were unfaithful to me.

I turned away and let their enemies take hold of them.

They fell in battle.”

## Ezekiel 39:24

“I turned away from them.

I dealt with them according to the wrong they had done.

They had made themselves unclean.”

## Ezekiel 39:25

“Now I will bring Jacob’s people back from captivity.”

Jacob’s people are the people of Israel.

God will show mercy to them all.

He will guard the honor of his holy name.

## Ezekiel 39:26

“They will live safely in their land. No one will frighten them.

They will forget their shame and the times they betrayed me.”

## Ezekiel 39:27

“I will gather them from the lands of their enemies.

When I bring them home, many nations will see how holy I am.”

## Ezekiel 39:28

“I sent them away to live among other nations.

Now I will gather them in their own land.

I will leave no one behind.

Then they will know I am the LORD their God.”

## Ezekiel 39:29

“I will no longer turn my face away from them.

I will pour out my Spirit on the people of Israel.”

The Lord GOD made this promise.
