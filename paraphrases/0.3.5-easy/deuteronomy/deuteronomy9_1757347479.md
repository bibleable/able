# Deuteronomy 9 — Moses remembers praying for Israel

**About this chapter**

Moses reminds Israel why the LORD is giving them land. He remembers a time when the people disobeyed and he prayed for them.

## Across the Jordan

## Deuteronomy 9:1

Moses called Israel to listen.

They were about to cross the Jordan River.

They would take land from nations larger and stronger than Israel.

Those nations had large cities with walls that seemed to reach the sky.

## Deuteronomy 9:2

The descendants of Anak were strong and tall.

Israel had heard people ask who could stand against them.

## Deuteronomy 9:3

The LORD their God would cross ahead of Israel.

Moses compared him to a fire that burns everything in its path.

He would overcome those nations and bring them down before Israel.

Israel would quickly drive them out and destroy them, as the LORD had promised.

## Why Israel would receive the land

## Deuteronomy 9:4

Moses warned Israel against taking credit for the land.

They might think the LORD had given it to them because they were good.

The LORD was driving out those nations because of the evil they had done.

## Deuteronomy 9:5

Israel’s own goodness had not earned them the land.

The other nations had done evil.

The LORD was also keeping his promise to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob.

**Pause and answer**

To whom had the LORD promised the land?

## Deuteronomy 9:6

The LORD was giving Israel good land.

Their own actions had not earned that gift.

Moses called them a stiff-necked people.

**Reading help**

“Stiff-necked” means stubborn. The people kept refusing to listen.

## Moses remembers Horeb

## Deuteronomy 9:7

Moses asked Israel to remember how they had angered the LORD in the wilderness.

From Egypt to this place, they had kept disobeying him.

## Deuteronomy 9:8

At Horeb the people made the LORD angry enough to destroy them.

## Deuteronomy 9:9

Moses climbed the mountain to get the stone tablets.

They held the words of the LORD’s covenant with Israel.

Moses stayed there for 40 days and 40 nights.

He ate no bread and drank no water.

**Reading help**

A tablet is a flat piece of stone with writing on it. A covenant is a solemn agreement.

## Deuteronomy 9:10

The LORD gave Moses two stone tablets.

God himself had written on them with his finger.

The people had gathered at the mountain and heard the LORD speak from the fire.

Those same words were on the tablets.

**Pause and answer**

Who wrote on the stone tablets?

## Deuteronomy 9:11

At the end of those 40 days and 40 nights, the LORD gave Moses the tablets of the covenant.

## The LORD sends Moses down

## Deuteronomy 9:12

The LORD told Moses to go down at once.

The people Moses had led out of Egypt had done wrong.

They had quickly turned away from the LORD’s commands.

They had made an image out of melted metal.

## Deuteronomy 9:13

The LORD told Moses he had seen how stubborn the people were.

## Deuteronomy 9:14

The LORD told Moses to leave him alone.

He would destroy Israel and erase even their name from the earth.

Then Moses’ family would become a larger, stronger nation.

## The stone tablets break

## Deuteronomy 9:15

Moses went back down the mountain as it blazed with fire.

He carried the two tablets of the covenant in his hands.

![A mountain](../pictures/mountain.svg)

Trace the way down the mountain with your finger. Moses carried the tablets down.

## Deuteronomy 9:16

Moses saw the metal calf the people had made.

They had sinned against the LORD their God.

They had quickly turned away from what he had commanded.

**Reading help**

A calf is a young cow. The people had made an idol shaped like a calf.

## Deuteronomy 9:17

Moses threw down the two tablets.

The people saw the stone tablets break into pieces.

**Pause and answer**

What happened to the tablets when Moses threw them down?

## Moses prays for the people and Aaron

## Deuteronomy 9:18

Moses lay down before the LORD for another 40 days and 40 nights.

Again he ate no bread and drank no water.

The people had done evil and made the LORD angry.

## Deuteronomy 9:19

Moses feared the LORD would destroy Israel in his anger.

But once again the LORD listened to Moses.

## Deuteronomy 9:20

The LORD was angry enough to destroy Aaron too.

Moses also prayed for Aaron.

**Pause and answer**

Who else did Moses pray for?

## Deuteronomy 9:21

Moses took the calf the people had sinned by making.

He burned it and ground it into fine dust.

He threw the dust into the stream flowing down the mountain.

## Israel refuses to obey

## Deuteronomy 9:22

Israel also angered the LORD at Taberah, Massah and Kibroth-hattaavah.

## Deuteronomy 9:23

At Kadesh-barnea the LORD told Israel to take the land he had given them.

The people refused.

They would neither trust the LORD nor do what he said.

## Deuteronomy 9:24

Moses said Israel had disobeyed the LORD ever since he first knew them.

## What Moses asked the LORD

## Deuteronomy 9:25

Moses explained why he lay before the LORD for 40 days and 40 nights.

The LORD had said he would destroy Israel.

## Deuteronomy 9:26

Moses asked the Lord GOD to spare his own people.

The LORD had used his great power to free them from Egypt.

**Pause and answer**

What did Moses ask the LORD to do for Israel?

## Deuteronomy 9:27

Moses asked the LORD to remember his servants Abraham, Isaac and Jacob.

He asked him to look past Israel’s stubbornness and sin.

## Deuteronomy 9:28

Moses asked what the Egyptians might say if Israel died.

They might say the LORD could not bring Israel into the land he had promised.

They might say he hated Israel and took them into the wilderness to kill them.

## Deuteronomy 9:29

Moses reminded the LORD that Israel belonged to him.

The LORD himself had brought them out by his great power.
