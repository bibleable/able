# Deuteronomy 4 — Remember what the LORD has done

**About this chapter**

Moses asks Israel to remember the voice they heard at Horeb. He tells them to teach their children what happened there.

## Listen to the laws

## Deuteronomy 4:1

Moses asked Israel to listen to the laws he taught.

By following them, the people could live and enter the land.

The LORD, the God of their ancestors, was giving it to them.

## Deuteronomy 4:2

They must keep the commands Moses brought from the LORD.

They must never add to them or take anything away.

## Deuteronomy 4:3

Israel had seen what the LORD did at Baal-peor.

The LORD destroyed everyone among them who followed Baal of Peor.

## Deuteronomy 4:4

But some stayed faithful to the LORD their God.

Every one of those people was still alive.

## Deuteronomy 4:5

The LORD had told Moses to teach Israel these laws.

The people must follow them in their new land.

## Other nations will see

## Deuteronomy 4:6

Israel must follow the laws carefully.

Other peoples would hear the laws and see how Israel lived.

They would say that this great nation showed wisdom and understanding.

## Deuteronomy 4:7

What other great nation had a god so near?

The LORD was near whenever Israel called to him.

## Deuteronomy 4:8

What other great nation had laws this fair?

Moses was giving Israel all these laws that day.

## Tell your children what you saw

## Deuteronomy 4:9

The people must take care to remember what they had seen.

They must remember it all their lives.

They must teach it to their children and grandchildren.

**Pause and answer**

Whom should the people teach?

## Deuteronomy 4:10

At Horeb the LORD had told Moses to gather the people.

The LORD wanted them to hear his words.

They would learn to honor him deeply all their lives.

They must teach their children those words too.

## Deuteronomy 4:11

The people stood at the foot of the mountain.

Flames rose high into the sky.

Black clouds and deep darkness covered the mountain.

![A mountain](../pictures/mountain.svg)

Point to the bottom of the mountain. The people stood there.

## Deuteronomy 4:12

The LORD spoke from the fire.

Israel heard his words.

They heard a voice but saw no shape.

## The LORD gives his covenant

## Deuteronomy 4:13

The LORD told them about his covenant.

He gave them the Ten Commandments to obey.

He wrote them on two flat pieces of stone.

**Reading help**

A covenant is a solemn agreement. The LORD told his people how to live as his people.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the LORD write the Ten Commandments?

## Deuteronomy 4:14

The LORD also told Moses to teach Israel the laws.

They must follow them after crossing the Jordan into their land.

## Israel must never make idols

## Deuteronomy 4:15

Moses reminded Israel to be careful.

At Horeb they saw no shape when the LORD spoke from the fire.

## Deuteronomy 4:16

They must never do wrong by making an idol.

They must never make one shaped like a man or woman.

**Reading help**

An idol is an object people make and worship as a god.

## Deuteronomy 4:17

They must never make an idol shaped like an animal or a flying bird.

## Deuteronomy 4:18

They must never make one shaped like a crawling creature or a fish.

## Deuteronomy 4:19

They would see the sun, moon and stars above them.

The LORD had given these lights to all peoples.

Israel must never bow down to worship them.

## Israel belongs to the LORD

## Deuteronomy 4:20

Moses compared Egypt to a furnace for heating iron.

Life there had been terribly hard for Israel.

The LORD brought them out to be his own people.

They still belonged to him.

## Deuteronomy 4:21

Moses said the LORD was angry with him because of the people.

The LORD swore that Moses would never cross the Jordan into that good land.

It was the land the LORD was giving Israel.

## Deuteronomy 4:22

Moses would die on this side of the Jordan.

The people would cross and take the good land.

## Deuteronomy 4:23

They must remember the covenant the LORD made with them.

They must never make any idol the LORD had forbidden.

## Deuteronomy 4:24

Moses compared the LORD to a fire that burns everything up.

The LORD is a jealous God.

He calls his people to worship him alone.

## Moses warns Israel

## Deuteronomy 4:25

Moses warned them about the years ahead.

They would live in the land and have children and grandchildren.

If they made idols, they would do evil and anger the LORD.

## Deuteronomy 4:26

Moses called heaven and earth to witness his warning.

If Israel did this, they would soon lose the land across the Jordan.

Destruction would come quickly.

**Reading help**

A witness hears or sees something happen. Moses spoke as if heaven and earth could hear his warning.

## Deuteronomy 4:27

The LORD would scatter the people among other nations.

Only a few would survive in those places.

## Deuteronomy 4:28

There they would serve gods people made from wood and stone.

These gods could never see, hear, eat or smell.

**Pause and answer**

What were the idols made from?

## Israel can return to the LORD

## Deuteronomy 4:29

Even there, the people could look for the LORD their God.

If they sought him with their whole heart and soul, they would find him.

**Reading help**

Looking for God with your whole heart and soul means turning to him with all that you are.

## Deuteronomy 4:30

In the days ahead these troubles would come upon them.

Then they would return to the LORD and listen to him.

## Deuteronomy 4:31

The LORD their God shows mercy.

He would never abandon them or destroy them.

He would remember the covenant he swore to their ancestors.

## Remember Egypt and the fire

## Deuteronomy 4:32

Moses asked Israel to think back to when God first made people.

Had anyone, anywhere, ever heard of anything so great?

## Deuteronomy 4:33

Had any other people heard God speak from a fire and lived?

## Deuteronomy 4:34

Had any other god taken a people out of another nation?

Israel saw what the LORD did for them in Egypt.

He brought trials, signs, wonders, war and great terrors.

He used his great power to free Israel.

## Deuteronomy 4:35

The LORD showed Israel these things so they would know him.

The LORD alone is God.

## Deuteronomy 4:36

He spoke from heaven to teach and correct them.

On earth they saw his great fire.

From the fire they heard his words.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the people hear the LORD’s words come from?

## The LORD loved their ancestors

## Deuteronomy 4:37

The LORD loved their ancestors and chose their descendants.

He himself brought Israel out of Egypt by his great power.

## Deuteronomy 4:38

He drove out nations larger and stronger than Israel.

He brought Israel into their land.

He gave it to Israel to keep, as they could now see.

## Deuteronomy 4:39

Moses told them to remember this deep within themselves.

The LORD alone is God in heaven and on earth.

## Deuteronomy 4:40

Moses told them to keep the LORD’s commands.

Then they and their children could do well.

They could live long in the land the LORD was giving them forever.

## Three cities for safety

## Deuteronomy 4:41

Moses set apart three cities east of the Jordan.

## Deuteronomy 4:42

Someone might kill a neighbor by accident, without any earlier hatred.

That person could run to one of these cities to stay alive.

## Deuteronomy 4:43

Bezer belonged to Reuben, on high, flat land in the wilderness.

Ramoth belonged to Gad, in Gilead.

Golan belonged to Manasseh, in Bashan.

**Pause and answer**

Which city belonged to Gad?

## Where Moses taught the law

## Deuteronomy 4:44

Moses gave the Israelites the law.

## Deuteronomy 4:45

These were the teachings, commands and rules Moses gave them after they left Egypt.

## Deuteronomy 4:46

Israel was in the valley east of the Jordan, opposite Beth-peor.

This land had belonged to Sihon, the Amorite king in Heshbon.

Moses and Israel had defeated him after leaving Egypt.

## Deuteronomy 4:47

Israel took the lands of Sihon and Og, king of Bashan.

Both were Amorite kings east of the Jordan.

## Deuteronomy 4:48

The land began at Aroer beside the Arnon Valley.

It reached Mount Siyon, also called Hermon.

## Deuteronomy 4:49

It included all the Arabah east of the Jordan.

It reached the Sea of the Arabah below the slopes of Pisgah.
