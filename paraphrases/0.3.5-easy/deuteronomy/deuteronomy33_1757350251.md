# Deuteronomy 33 — Moses blesses the tribes of Israel

**About this chapter**

Before he dies, Moses asks God to bless the tribes. He speaks of their land, their families and the work they will do.

## The LORD comes to his people

## Deuteronomy 33:1

Before Moses died, he blessed the people of Israel.

Moses was a man of God.

## Deuteronomy 33:2

Moses pictured the LORD coming from Sinai.

His light rose over Israel from Seir like the dawn.

He shone from Mount Paran with countless holy ones around him.

Fire blazed at his right hand.

## Deuteronomy 33:3

LORD, you love your people.

You hold all your holy ones in your hand.

They sit at your feet to hear your words.

## Deuteronomy 33:4

Moses gave us your law.

It belongs to all the people of Jacob.

## Deuteronomy 33:5

The tribal leaders gathered, and the tribes of Israel came together.

Then the LORD became King in Jeshurun.

**Reading help**

Jeshurun is another name for Israel in this song.

## Reuben and Judah

## Deuteronomy 33:6

Moses asked that Reuben’s tribe live on and remain strong in numbers.

## Deuteronomy 33:7

Moses asked the LORD to hear Judah’s cry and bring Judah back to his people.

Judah fought for himself, but he needed the LORD’s help against his enemies.

## Levi serves the LORD

## Deuteronomy 33:8

Moses asked the LORD to give Levi the Thummim and Urim.

The LORD had tested his faithful servant at Massah.

He had challenged him at the waters of Meribah.

**Reading help**

The Urim and Thummim were sacred objects that priests used when asking God for a decision.

## Deuteronomy 33:9

Levi kept the LORD’s word and honored his covenant.

Loyalty to the LORD came before loyalty to parents, brothers or sons.

## Deuteronomy 33:10

The Levites would teach the LORD’s law to Jacob’s people, Israel.

They would offer incense and whole burnt offerings on his altar.

**Pause and answer**

What would the Levites teach?

## Deuteronomy 33:11

Moses asked the LORD to bless what Levi had and accept his work.

He asked the LORD to defeat Levi’s enemies so they could never rise again.

## Benjamin rests safely

## Deuteronomy 33:12

Moses called Benjamin the one the LORD loved.

He asked that Benjamin might rest safely on the LORD’s shoulders.

The LORD would shelter Benjamin all day.

**Reading help**

The shoulders are a picture of someone carrying a loved one safely.

## Gifts for Joseph

## Deuteronomy 33:13

Moses asked the LORD to bless Joseph’s land.

May dew fall on it from above.

May water rise from deep below.

## Deuteronomy 33:14

May the sun help its crops grow.

May each season bring a rich harvest.

![A stalk of ripe grain](../pictures/grain.svg)

Grain was one of the crops a family could gather at harvest.

**Pause and answer**

What did Moses ask each season to bring?

## Deuteronomy 33:15

May the ancient mountains and the hills that stand through the ages give their best.

## Deuteronomy 33:16

May Joseph receive the best gifts of the land.

May the God who appeared in the burning bush show him favor.

May these blessings crown Joseph, a prince among his brothers.

## Deuteronomy 33:17

Moses compared Joseph’s strength to a bull with the horns of a wild ox.

He pictured those horns pushing back nations to the ends of the earth.

They stood for the tens of thousands of Ephraim and the thousands of Manasseh.

## Zebulun and Issachar

## Deuteronomy 33:18

Moses asked Zebulun to rejoice on his journeys.

He asked Issachar to rejoice in his tents.

## Deuteronomy 33:19

These tribes would invite people to a mountain.

There they would offer sacrifices that pleased God.

The sea would give them plenty, and the sand held hidden treasures.

## Gad, Dan and Naphtali

## Deuteronomy 33:20

Moses praised the one who gave Gad more land.

He pictured Gad as a fierce lion, able to tear its prey apart.

## Deuteronomy 33:21

Gad chose good land where a ruler’s share lay waiting.

Gad joined the leaders of Israel.

Gad carried out the LORD’s laws and did what was just.

## Deuteronomy 33:22

Moses pictured Dan as a young lion leaping out of Bashan.

## Deuteronomy 33:23

Moses said Naphtali enjoyed the LORD’s favor and blessing.

Naphtali would take possession of the sea and the south.

## The blessing for Asher

## Deuteronomy 33:24

Moses asked that Asher receive the greatest blessing among the sons.

May his brothers favor him.

May he have so much oil he could dip his foot in it.

## Deuteronomy 33:25

May iron and bronze bars secure Asher’s gates.

May his strength last all his days.

**Pause and answer**

How long did Moses want Asher’s strength to last?

## A safe home for Israel

## Deuteronomy 33:26

No one is like the God of Jeshurun.

Moses pictured God riding across the sky in splendor to help Israel.

## Deuteronomy 33:27

The God who lives forever is a safe home for Israel.

His arms support his people forever.

He drove out their enemy and told Israel to destroy that enemy.

**Reading help**

God’s arms are a picture of the support he gives his people.

## Deuteronomy 33:28

So Jacob’s family could live in safety and peace.

Their land gave them grain and new wine.

Dew fell from the sky.

## Deuteronomy 33:29

Moses called Israel blessed because the LORD had saved them.

The LORD was their shield and the sword they took pride in.

Their enemies would shrink back in fear.

Israel would walk over their enemies’ high places.
