# Deuteronomy 28 — Blessings and warnings for Israel

**About this chapter**

Moses tells Israel what obedience would mean for their families and fields. He also warns them of hunger, war and exile if they turn away from the LORD.

## If Israel obeys

## Deuteronomy 28:1

Moses told Israel to listen to the LORD their God.

If they carefully obeyed every command, the LORD would raise Israel above every nation.

## Deuteronomy 28:2

If Israel obeyed the LORD, all these blessings would reach them.

## Deuteronomy 28:3

The LORD would bless them in town and in the country.

## Deuteronomy 28:4

He would bless their children and their crops.

He would bless their calves and lambs.

## Deuteronomy 28:5

He would bless their baskets and their bowls for making bread.

![A basket](../pictures/basket.svg)

Families used baskets to carry food.

**Pause and answer**

What bowl did families use to make bread?

## Deuteronomy 28:6

He would bless them when they came in and when they went out.

## Enemies flee and the harvest grows

## Deuteronomy 28:7

The LORD would defeat the enemies who attacked Israel.

They would come against Israel from one direction.

They would run away in seven directions.

## Deuteronomy 28:8

The LORD would bless their barns and all their work.

He would bless them in the land he was giving them.

## Deuteronomy 28:9

The LORD had promised to set Israel apart as his holy people.

He would do this if they kept his commands and lived as he taught them.

## Deuteronomy 28:10

People everywhere would see that Israel belonged to the LORD.

They would look on Israel with awe.

## Deuteronomy 28:11

In the land he promised their ancestors, the LORD would give them plenty.

Their families would grow, their animals would have young, and their fields would yield crops.

## Rain at the right time

## Deuteronomy 28:12

The sky would be like a storehouse the LORD opened.

He would send rain at the right time and bless their work.

Israel would lend to many nations without needing to borrow.

**Pause and answer**

What would the LORD send at the right time?

## Deuteronomy 28:13

If Israel obeyed, the LORD would make them the head instead of the tail.

Moses meant Israel would lead and keep rising.

## Deuteronomy 28:14

Israel must follow all the commands Moses gave that day.

They must never leave that path to serve other gods.

## If Israel disobeys

## Deuteronomy 28:15

Moses also warned Israel what would happen if they disobeyed.

If they ignored the LORD’s laws, all these curses would reach them.

## Deuteronomy 28:16

The curse would reach them in town and in the country.

## Deuteronomy 28:17

It would reach their baskets and their bowls for making bread.

## Deuteronomy 28:18

It would reach their children and crops, their calves and lambs.

## Deuteronomy 28:19

It would reach them when they came in and when they went out.

## Illness and dry ground

## Deuteronomy 28:20

The LORD would send curses, confusion and rebukes into all their work.

Because they had turned away and done evil, they would soon perish.

## Deuteronomy 28:21

The LORD would send a deadly sickness that stayed with them.

It would destroy them in the land they were entering.

## Deuteronomy 28:22

The LORD would send illness that wasted their bodies, fever and swelling.

Heat and drought would come.

Disease and mildew would ruin crops.

These troubles would last until the people died.

## Deuteronomy 28:23

The sky would seem as hard as bronze.

The ground would seem as hard as iron.

**Reading help**

The metal sky and ground are pictures of drought. Rain stops and the soil becomes hard.

## Deuteronomy 28:24

Dust would fall on their land in place of rain.

It would keep falling until they perished.

**Pause and answer**

What would fall instead of rain?

## Enemies defeat Israel

## Deuteronomy 28:25

The LORD would let their enemies defeat them.

Israel would advance in one direction and flee in seven.

Kingdoms everywhere would look on Israel with horror.

## Deuteronomy 28:26

Birds and wild animals would feed on their dead bodies.

No one would chase the animals away.

## Deuteronomy 28:27

The LORD would send the painful boils Israel knew in Egypt.

They would have lumps, scabs and itching that no one could cure.

## Deuteronomy 28:28

The LORD would bring blindness and confusion.

The people would lose their ability to think clearly.

## Deuteronomy 28:29

Even at noon they would feel their way as if in darkness.

Their work would come to nothing.

Others would rob and mistreat them every day.

No one would rescue them.

## Families lose homes and children

## Deuteronomy 28:30

A man would be engaged to a woman, and another man would rape her.

People would build houses they could never live in.

They would plant vineyards but never enjoy the grapes.

## Deuteronomy 28:31

They would see someone kill their ox and get none of the meat.

Someone would take their donkey and never return it.

Their enemies would take their sheep.

No one would help.

## Deuteronomy 28:32

Another nation would take their sons and daughters.

Day after day they would strain their eyes looking for their children.

They could do nothing to bring them back.

## Deuteronomy 28:33

Strangers would eat the crops Israel had worked to grow.

Others would mistreat them and crush them all their lives.

## Deuteronomy 28:34

The terrible things they saw would overwhelm their minds.

## Deuteronomy 28:35

The LORD would send painful sores that no one could heal.

The sores would cover their knees and thighs and spread from head to foot.

## Exile and ruined harvests

## Deuteronomy 28:36

The LORD would take Israel and its chosen king to an unfamiliar nation.

Even their ancestors had never known it.

There Israel would worship gods made of wood and stone.

## Deuteronomy 28:37

The LORD would drive them among other nations.

People there would look on them with horror and make fun of them.

## Deuteronomy 28:38

They would plant plenty of seed and gather little.

Locusts would eat the crops.

## Deuteronomy 28:39

They would plant vines and care for them.

Worms would eat the grapes.

There would be no grapes to gather and no wine to drink.

**Pause and answer**

Why would there be no grapes to gather?

## Deuteronomy 28:40

Olive trees would grow throughout their land.

The olives would fall before the people could get oil to rub on their skin.

## Deuteronomy 28:41

They would have sons and daughters.

Others would take their children away as prisoners.

## Deuteronomy 28:42

Great swarms of locusts would eat their trees and crops.

## Others gain power

## Deuteronomy 28:43

Foreigners living among them would gain more and more power.

Israel would have less and less.

## Deuteronomy 28:44

Foreigners would lend to Israel, and Israel would have nothing to lend them.

The foreigners would be the head and Israel the tail.

Now others would lead.

## Deuteronomy 28:45

Moses said these curses would follow Israel and catch up with them.

They would destroy Israel because the people had disobeyed the LORD’s commands.

## Deuteronomy 28:46

These curses would remain a terrible sign for Israel and the generations to come.

## Deuteronomy 28:47

When Israel had plenty, they had failed to serve the LORD with joy.

## Deuteronomy 28:48

So they would serve enemies the LORD sent.

They would lack food, water, clothes and everything else.

The LORD would put an iron yoke on them until they perished.

**Reading help**

A yoke is a frame laid across the neck. Here the iron yoke shows how harshly enemies would rule Israel.

## An army from far away

## Deuteronomy 28:49

The LORD would bring a nation from far away.

It would come down on Israel like an eagle.

Israel would not understand its language.

## Deuteronomy 28:50

Those people would act without mercy.

They would show no respect for the old and no pity for the young.

## Deuteronomy 28:51

The invaders would eat the young animals and the crops.

They would leave no grain, new wine, oil, calves or lambs.

Israel would have nothing left to live on.

## Deuteronomy 28:52

The invaders would surround every city in the land the LORD gave Israel.

The people trusted their high, strong walls.

Those walls would fall.

## Hunger inside the cities

## Deuteronomy 28:53

Trapped inside and starving, parents would eat the flesh of their own children.

These were the sons and daughters the LORD had given them.

## Deuteronomy 28:54

Even a gentle man would refuse to share with his brother, his dear wife or his surviving children.

## Deuteronomy 28:55

He would eat the flesh of his children and keep it to himself.

In the city surrounded by enemies, he would have nothing else.

## Deuteronomy 28:56

Moses described a gentle woman who hardly ever set foot on the ground.

Even she would refuse to share with her dear husband, son or daughter.

## Deuteronomy 28:57

She would secretly eat her afterbirth and the children she bore.

With enemies surrounding the city, she would have no other food.

**Reading help**

Afterbirth is the tissue that comes out after a baby is born.

## Illnesses that stay

## Deuteronomy 28:58

Moses urged Israel to obey every law written in the book.

They must deeply honor the glorious name of the LORD their God.

## Deuteronomy 28:59

If they disobeyed, the LORD would send terrible disasters on them and their descendants.

Severe plagues and painful illnesses would last a long time.

## Deuteronomy 28:60

He would bring back the diseases they had feared in Egypt.

Those diseases would stay with them.

## Deuteronomy 28:61

Even illnesses and plagues missing from the Book of the Law would come.

The LORD would send them until Israel perished.

## Deuteronomy 28:62

Israel had once been as numerous as the stars.

Only a few would remain because they had disobeyed the LORD.

## Far from home

## Deuteronomy 28:63

The LORD had taken pleasure in helping Israel grow and do well.

Moses warned that the LORD would likewise take pleasure in bringing about their destruction.

They would lose their place in the land they were entering.

## Deuteronomy 28:64

The LORD would scatter Israel among nations across the whole earth.

There they would serve gods of wood and stone that neither they nor their ancestors had known.

## Deuteronomy 28:65

In those nations they would find no place to rest.

The LORD would let fear shake them, their sight fail and their hope fade.

## Deuteronomy 28:66

They would fear for their lives day and night.

They would never know whether they would survive.

## Deuteronomy 28:67

Each morning they would wish for evening.

Each evening they would wish for morning.

What they saw would fill them with dread.

**Pause and answer**

What would the people wish for in the evening?

## Deuteronomy 28:68

The LORD would send them back to Egypt on ships.

Moses had said they would never see that route again.

There men and women would offer themselves to their enemies as slaves.

No one would buy them.
