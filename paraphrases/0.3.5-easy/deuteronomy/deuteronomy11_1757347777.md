# Deuteronomy 11 — Rain for the land and a choice for Israel

**About this chapter**

Moses asks Israel to remember what they have seen. He tells them how rain will help their crops grow across the Jordan.

## Remember what you saw

## Deuteronomy 11:1

Moses told Israel to love the LORD their God.

They must always do what he asked.

They must obey all his laws and commands.

## Deuteronomy 11:2

Moses reminded the people that they had seen how the LORD trained them.

Their children had not seen this for themselves.

The people had seen the LORD use his great power.

## Deuteronomy 11:3

They had seen the amazing signs the LORD gave in Egypt.

They remembered what he did to Pharaoh and his whole land.

Pharaoh was the king of Egypt.

## Deuteronomy 11:4

Egypt’s army chased Israel with horses and chariots.

The LORD made the water of the Red Sea cover them.

He destroyed that army completely.

**Reading help**

A chariot was a cart pulled by horses.

## Deuteronomy 11:5

The people remembered what the LORD had done for them in the wilderness.

He had cared for them all the way here.

## What happened to Dathan and Abiram

## Deuteronomy 11:6

All Israel saw what happened to Dathan and Abiram.

Their father was Eliab, from the tribe of Reuben.

The ground split open beneath them.

They fell in with their families, tents and every living thing they owned.

## Deuteronomy 11:7

The people Moses spoke to had seen these great acts themselves.

**Pause and answer**

Who had seen these things with their own eyes?

## The land across the Jordan

## Deuteronomy 11:8

Moses told them to obey every command he gave that day.

Then they would have strength to cross the Jordan and take the land.

## Deuteronomy 11:9

The LORD had promised the land to their ancestors and their families after them.

He wanted Israel to live there a long time.

Moses called it a land flowing with milk and honey.

**Reading help**

“Flowing with milk and honey” describes land with plenty of food.

## Deuteronomy 11:10

In Egypt the people had planted seeds.

They went on foot to water their crops, as people water a vegetable garden.

## Deuteronomy 11:11

Across the Jordan they would find mountains and valleys.

There the ground soaked up rain from the sky.

## Deuteronomy 11:12

The LORD cared for that land.

He watched over it from the first day of the year to the last.

## Rain for the harvest

## Deuteronomy 11:13

Moses asked the people to obey the commands he gave them that day.

They must love the LORD and serve him with their whole heart and soul.

## Deuteronomy 11:14

If they obeyed, the LORD promised rain at the right times.

He would send autumn rain and spring rain.

They could gather grain, new wine and oil.

![A stalk of ripe grain](../pictures/grain.svg)

Rain helps grain grow until it is ready to gather.

**Pause and answer**

In which two seasons did the LORD promise rain?

## Deuteronomy 11:15

The LORD would give their animals grass in the fields.

The people would have enough to eat.

## Deuteronomy 11:16

Moses warned them to take care.

They might feel drawn to bow down and worship other gods.

## Deuteronomy 11:17

Then the LORD would be angry and stop the rain.

Their land would grow no crops.

They would soon die in the good land he was giving them.

## Teach the words at home

## Deuteronomy 11:18

Moses told them to keep these words in their hearts and minds.

They must tie the words on their hands and foreheads as reminders.

**Reading help**

Keeping words in your heart and mind means remembering them and letting them guide what you do.

## Deuteronomy 11:19

They must teach the words to their children.

They could talk about them at home and along the road.

They could talk about them at bedtime and on getting up.

## Deuteronomy 11:20

They must write the words on their doorposts and gates.

**Pause and answer**

Where could the children see the words at home?

## Deuteronomy 11:21

Then they and their children could live long in the land the LORD promised their ancestors.

This promise reached as far into the future as the sky would stand above the earth.

## The land Israel would receive

## Deuteronomy 11:22

Moses told them to follow all these commands carefully.

They must love the LORD, live as he taught them and stay faithful to him.

## Deuteronomy 11:23

Then the LORD would drive out the nations ahead of them.

Israel would receive the land of nations larger and stronger than Israel.

## Deuteronomy 11:24

Every place they set foot would belong to them.

Their land would reach from the wilderness to Lebanon.

It would stretch from the Euphrates River to the Western Sea.

## Deuteronomy 11:25

No one would be able to stand against Israel.

Wherever they went, the LORD would make the people there fear them.

The LORD had promised this.

## A blessing and a curse

## Deuteronomy 11:26

Moses set a blessing and a curse before Israel that day.

**Reading help**

Here a blessing is the good the LORD promises.

A curse tells of harm that will come if Israel turns away.

## Deuteronomy 11:27

They would receive the blessing if they obeyed the LORD’s commands that Moses gave them.

## Deuteronomy 11:28

They would face the curse if they disobeyed and left the way Moses taught them.

They would do this by following gods they had never known.

## Two mountains across the Jordan

## Deuteronomy 11:29

When the LORD brought them into the land, they must announce the blessing on Mount Gerizim.

They must announce the curse on Mount Ebal.

**Pause and answer**

On which mountain would Israel announce the blessing?

## Deuteronomy 11:30

Those mountains lay across the Jordan, west of the road toward the sunset.

Canaanites lived there in the Arabah, opposite Gilgal, near the Oak of Moreh.

## Deuteronomy 11:31

Israel was about to cross the Jordan.

The LORD was giving them land to take and settle in.

## Deuteronomy 11:32

Once they settled there, they must carefully follow every law Moses gave them that day.
