# Deuteronomy 8 — Remember who gave you food

**About this chapter**

Moses reminds Israel what hunger felt like. He asks them to remember the LORD when they have plenty to eat.

## What Israel learned in the wilderness

## Deuteronomy 8:1

Moses told Israel to follow every command he gave that day.

Then they could live and grow in number.

They could enter the land the LORD had promised their ancestors and make it their home.

## Deuteronomy 8:2

Moses asked them to remember their 40 years in the wilderness.

The LORD their God had led them all the way.

He humbled them and tested them.

Would they choose to keep his commands?

**Reading help**

To humble the people meant to help them see how much they needed the LORD.

## Deuteronomy 8:3

The LORD humbled them by letting them feel hungry.

Then he gave them manna to eat.

Neither they nor their ancestors had known this food before.

The LORD was teaching them that bread alone cannot keep people alive.

Their lives depend on every word the LORD speaks.

**Pause and answer**

What food did the LORD give Israel in the wilderness?

## The LORD teaches his people

## Deuteronomy 8:4

For 40 years their clothes did not wear out.

Their feet did not swell.

## Deuteronomy 8:5

Moses wanted them to understand why the LORD corrected them.

A father corrects his son to teach him.

The LORD their God corrected them like that.

## Deuteronomy 8:6

So they must obey the LORD’s commands.

They must live as he taught them and honor him deeply.

## A land with plenty to eat

## Deuteronomy 8:7

The LORD was bringing them into a good land.

Water bubbled up from springs.

Streams flowed through the valleys and hills.

## Deuteronomy 8:8

Wheat and barley grew there.

Grapevines, fig trees and pomegranates grew there too.

The land also had olive oil and honey.

![Grapes growing on a vine](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

Point to one food Israel would find in the land.

## Deuteronomy 8:9

There they would have plenty to eat.

They would have everything they needed.

They could get iron from the rocks.

They could dig copper from the hills.

## Deuteronomy 8:10

When they had eaten their fill, they must praise the LORD.

He was the one who gave them this good land.

**Pause and answer**

Whom should Israel praise after eating?

## When Israel has enough

## Deuteronomy 8:11

Moses warned them about forgetting the LORD their God.

They must keep the commands and laws Moses gave them that day.

## Deuteronomy 8:12

Moses asked them to think about the days ahead.

They would have enough to eat.

They would build fine houses and live in them.

## Deuteronomy 8:13

Their herds and flocks would grow.

They would have more silver and gold.

They would have more of everything they owned.

## Deuteronomy 8:14

Then they might become proud and forget the LORD.

They had been slaves in Egypt.

The LORD their God had brought them out.

## Who gave Israel water and food?

## Deuteronomy 8:15

The LORD led them through a huge, frightening wilderness.

Snakes with deadly bites and scorpions lived there.

The land was dry, and the people were thirsty.

The LORD gave them water from hard rock called flint.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the water come from?

## Deuteronomy 8:16

The LORD fed them manna in the wilderness.

Their ancestors had never eaten it.

He humbled them and tested them.

In the end he wanted them to do well.

## Where does the strength to gain wealth come from?

## Deuteronomy 8:17

Moses warned them about something they might think.

They might say, “I made myself rich by my own strength.”

## Deuteronomy 8:18

Moses told them to remember who gave them that strength.

The LORD their God gave them the ability to gain wealth.

In this way he kept the covenant he had sworn to their ancestors.

That promise still held.

**Reading help**

A covenant is a solemn agreement.

**Pause and answer**

Who gave Israel the strength to gain wealth?

## Deuteronomy 8:19

Moses warned Israel that day about forgetting the LORD.

If they bowed down and worshiped other gods, they would surely die.

## Deuteronomy 8:20

The LORD had destroyed nations as Israel advanced.

Israel too would perish if they disobeyed the LORD their God.
