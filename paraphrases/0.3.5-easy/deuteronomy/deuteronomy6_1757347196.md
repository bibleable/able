# Deuteronomy 6 — Love the LORD and teach your children

**About this chapter**

Moses tells Israel how to remember the LORD at home. He gives families an answer for a child who asks why they keep his laws.

## Moses teaches Israel

## Deuteronomy 6:1

Moses spoke to Israel.

The LORD had told Moses to teach them these commands and laws.

They must follow them in the land they would soon receive.

## Deuteronomy 6:2

They must honor the LORD deeply by keeping all his commands throughout their lives.

Their children and grandchildren must do the same.

Then they could live long lives.

## Deuteronomy 6:3

Moses asked Israel to listen and obey carefully.

Then they could do well and grow into a large people.

The God of their ancestors had promised them a land flowing with milk and honey.

**Reading help**

“Flowing with milk and honey” describes land with plenty of food.

## Love the LORD

## Deuteronomy 6:4

Moses said to the people, “Listen, Israel! The LORD our God is one LORD.”

## Deuteronomy 6:5

Love the LORD your God with all your heart.

Love him with all your soul and all your strength.

**Reading help**

Heart, soul and strength mean all that you are.

## Deuteronomy 6:6

Keep the words I give you today in your hearts.

**Reading help**

Keeping words in your heart means remembering them and letting them guide what you do.

## Talk about the words every day

## Deuteronomy 6:7

Teach these words to your children again and again.

Talk about them when you sit at home.

Talk about them when you walk along the road.

Talk about them when you lie down and get up.

**Pause and answer**

When could a family talk about these words outside the home?

## Deuteronomy 6:8

Tie these words on your hands as reminders.

Tie them on your foreheads too.

## Deuteronomy 6:9

Write these words on the doorposts of your homes.

Write them on your gates too.

## Remember who brought you out

## Deuteronomy 6:10

The LORD promised Abraham, Isaac and Jacob that he would give you land.

He will bring you into that land.

You will find great, beautiful cities that others built.

## Deuteronomy 6:11

Others filled the houses with good things and dug the wells.

Others planted the vineyards and the olive trees.

You will eat until you have enough.

![Grapes growing on a vine](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

A vineyard is land where people grow grapevines.

## Deuteronomy 6:12

When you have enough, remember the LORD.

You were slaves in Egypt.

The LORD brought you out.

**Pause and answer**

Whom must Israel remember when they have enough to eat?

## Serve the LORD alone

## Deuteronomy 6:13

Honor the LORD your God deeply.

Serve him alone.

When you make a solemn promise, use his name.

## Deuteronomy 6:14

The peoples around you worship other gods.

Never follow those gods.

## Deuteronomy 6:15

The LORD your God lives among you.

He is a jealous God who wants your worship for himself alone.

If you follow other gods, he will be angry.

He will destroy you completely.

## Do what the LORD says is good

## Deuteronomy 6:16

Do not test the LORD your God as you did at Massah.

**Reading help**

To test God here means to demand that he prove he is with you.

## Deuteronomy 6:17

Take care to obey all the LORD’s commands.

Keep the teachings and laws he gave you.

## Deuteronomy 6:18

Do what the LORD sees as right and good.

Then things can go well for you.

You can enter the good land the LORD promised your ancestors and make it your home.

## Deuteronomy 6:19

The LORD will drive out all your enemies ahead of you, just as he promised.

## When your child asks why

## Deuteronomy 6:20

One day your son may ask what these laws mean.

He may ask why the LORD gave them to you.

## Deuteronomy 6:21

Tell your son what happened to us.

We were slaves of Pharaoh, the king of Egypt.

The LORD used his great power to bring us out.

**Pause and answer**

Who brought Israel out of Egypt?

## Deuteronomy 6:22

We saw the LORD do great and terrible things in Egypt.

These signs brought ruin on Egypt, Pharaoh and his whole household.

## Deuteronomy 6:23

The LORD brought us out of Egypt to lead us here.

He gave us the land he had promised our ancestors.

## Deuteronomy 6:24

The LORD told us to obey all these laws and honor him deeply.

He wanted us to do well and stay alive.

He has kept us alive to this day.

## Deuteronomy 6:25

When we carefully keep every command the LORD gave us, we do what is right in his sight.

**Pause and answer**

How could the people do what was right in the LORD’s sight?
