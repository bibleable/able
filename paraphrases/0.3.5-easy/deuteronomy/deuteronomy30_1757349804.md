# Deuteronomy 30 — Choose life

**About this chapter**

Moses speaks about a time when Israel will be far from home. He tells the people how to turn back to the LORD.

## Far from home

## Deuteronomy 30:1

Moses asked Israel to look ahead.

The blessings and curses he had described would come.

Even in the nations where the LORD had sent them away, the people would remember his words.

## Deuteronomy 30:2

They and their children could turn back to the LORD.

They could obey everything Moses taught them that day.

They must do this with all their heart and soul.

## Deuteronomy 30:3

Then the LORD would show them compassion and free them from captivity.

He would gather them from every nation where he had scattered them.

**Reading help**

Compassion means caring about people who suffer and wanting to help them.

## The LORD will gather his people

## Deuteronomy 30:4

Even if they lived at the farthest edge of the earth, the LORD would find them there.

He would bring them home.

**Pause and answer**

Who would bring the scattered people home?

## Deuteronomy 30:5

The LORD would bring them back to their ancestors’ land.

They would have that land as their own.

They would grow more numerous and do even better than their ancestors.

## Deuteronomy 30:6

Moses said the LORD would circumcise their hearts and their descendants’ hearts.

They would love him with all their heart and soul.

In this way they would live.

**Reading help**

Circumcision was a sign on the body of belonging to God.

Here Moses means God would change the people inwardly so they could love him.

## Deuteronomy 30:7

The LORD would bring these curses on their enemies.

Those enemies had hated Israel and treated them cruelly.

## Israel will listen again

## Deuteronomy 30:8

Israel would listen to the LORD again.

They would obey all the commands Moses gave them that day.

## Deuteronomy 30:9

The LORD would bless their work.

They would have children, their animals would have young, and their crops would grow well.

The LORD would delight in their good fortune again, as he had for their ancestors.

## Deuteronomy 30:10

Moses told them when this would happen.

They must follow the commands written in the Book of the Law.

They must turn back to the LORD with all their heart and soul.

![An open scroll with lines of writing](../pictures/scroll.svg)

The scroll reminds us of the written commands.

**Pause and answer**

Where could the people find the written commands?

## The command is near

## Deuteronomy 30:11

Moses told Israel they could obey this command.

It was within their reach.

## Deuteronomy 30:12

No one had to climb to heaven to get it.

They did not need someone to bring it down and tell them what to do.

## Deuteronomy 30:13

No one had to cross the sea to get it.

They did not need someone to bring it back and tell them what to do.

## Deuteronomy 30:14

The word was already near them, in their mouths and hearts.

They could say it, remember it and do what it said.

**Pause and answer**

What could the people do with the words they already knew?

## The choice Moses gives Israel

## Deuteronomy 30:15

That day Moses gave Israel a choice.

They could choose life and good things, or death and disaster.

## Deuteronomy 30:16

Moses told them to love the LORD their God.

They must live as he taught them and keep his laws.

Then they would live and grow in number.

The LORD would bless them in their new land.

## Deuteronomy 30:17

But they might turn away and stop listening.

They might bow down to other gods and worship them.

## Deuteronomy 30:18

Moses warned that if they did this, they would perish.

Their time in the land across the Jordan would be short.

## Choose life for yourselves and your children

## Deuteronomy 30:19

Moses called heaven and earth to witness the choice he gave Israel.

He set before them life and death, blessing and curse.

He urged them to choose life so they and their descendants could live.

**Reading help**

A witness hears or sees what happens.

Moses spoke as if heaven and earth could hear him offer Israel this choice.

## Deuteronomy 30:20

Choosing life meant loving the LORD, obeying him and staying close to him.

The LORD was their life.

He would give them long lives in the land he had promised Abraham, Isaac and Jacob.

**Pause and answer**

What did choosing life mean for Israel?
