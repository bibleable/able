# Deuteronomy 13 — Moses warns Israel about following other gods

**About this chapter**

Moses describes three ways people might urge Israel to worship other gods. He explains the law Israel was to follow in each case.

## A sign that comes true

## Deuteronomy 13:1

Moses asked Israel to imagine a prophet among them.

Or someone might tell them about a dream.

This person might say that something amazing would happen as a sign.

**Reading help**

A prophet was someone who claimed to speak a message from God.

## Deuteronomy 13:2

The sign might happen just as the person said.

Then the person might ask Israel to worship other gods.

These would be gods Israel had never known.

## Deuteronomy 13:3

Moses told Israel to refuse that message.

The LORD their God was testing their love.

Did they love him with their whole heart and soul?

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Moses ask Israel to love?

## Israel must stay faithful

## Deuteronomy 13:4

Israel must follow the LORD and honor him deeply.

They must listen to him and keep his commands.

They must serve him and stay close to him.

**Reading help**

Staying close to the LORD means continuing to trust and obey him.

## Deuteronomy 13:5

The law Moses gave Israel called for that prophet or dreamer to die.

The LORD had freed Israel from slavery in Egypt.

This person had urged Israel to turn against him.

Moses said Israel must remove this evil from among them.

## Someone close makes a secret request

## Deuteronomy 13:6

Moses spoke next about a secret invitation.

It might come from a brother, son or daughter.

It might come from a beloved wife or closest friend.

That person might whisper, “Let’s worship other gods.”

Neither Israel nor their ancestors had known these gods.

**Pause and answer**

Who might make the request in secret?

## Deuteronomy 13:7

They might be gods of people living nearby.

Or they might be gods of people far away, anywhere on earth.

## Deuteronomy 13:8

Moses told Israel to refuse, even when someone close spoke.

They must neither agree nor act on those words.

The law allowed no pity or protection for that person.

## The penalty under Israel’s law

## Deuteronomy 13:9

Under this law, that person faced death.

The person who heard the invitation had to act first.

The rest of the community then took part.

## Deuteronomy 13:10

The penalty Moses named was death by stoning.

The person had tried to turn someone away from the LORD.

The LORD was the one who freed Israel from slavery in Egypt.

## Deuteronomy 13:11

All Israel would hear what had happened and be afraid.

Moses said this would keep others from doing the same wrong.

## A report about a town

## Deuteronomy 13:12

Moses then spoke about a report Israel might hear.

It might concern a town the LORD had given them to live in.

![An open scroll with lines of writing](../pictures/scroll.svg)

The scroll is a reminder of the law Moses taught.

## Deuteronomy 13:13

People might say that some men had led their own town astray.

The men had urged the town to serve gods Israel had never known.

## Deuteronomy 13:14

Israel must ask questions and check the report carefully.

They must find out for certain whether the people had done this wrong.

**Pause and answer**

What did Israel have to find out before judging the town?

## What the law said about the town

## Deuteronomy 13:15

If the report proved true, the law called for the town’s destruction.

The sentence included the deaths of all its people and livestock.

## Deuteronomy 13:16

The law set apart the whole town as a burnt offering to the LORD.

That included all its goods, gathered in the town square.

The town would lie in ruins forever.

No one was to rebuild it.

## Deuteronomy 13:17

No one could keep anything set apart for destruction.

Then the LORD would turn from his great anger.

He would show Israel mercy and care for them.

He would make them grow in number, as he had promised their ancestors.

**Reading help**

To show mercy means to treat someone with kindness when they could face punishment.

**Pause and answer**

What could anyone keep from the town?

## Deuteronomy 13:18

Moses told Israel to obey the LORD their God.

They must keep every command Moses gave them that day.

That was how they would do what the LORD saw as right.
