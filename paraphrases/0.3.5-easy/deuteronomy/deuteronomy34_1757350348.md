# Deuteronomy 34 — Moses sees the land and Joshua leads Israel

**About this chapter**

Moses sees the land the LORD promised to Israel. After Moses dies, the people mourn him and follow Joshua.

## The view from Mount Nebo

## Deuteronomy 34:1

Moses left the plains of Moab and climbed Mount Nebo.

He reached the top of Pisgah, facing Jericho.

The LORD showed him the land from Gilead to Dan.

![A mountain](../pictures/mountain.svg)

Point to the top, where Moses stood to look across the land.

## Deuteronomy 34:2

Moses saw all of Naphtali and the lands of Ephraim and Manasseh.

He saw Judah as far as the Western Sea.

## Deuteronomy 34:3

He saw the Negev and the land from the Valley of Jericho to Zoar.

People called Jericho the City of Palms.

## Deuteronomy 34:4

The LORD had promised Abraham, Isaac and Jacob to give this land to their descendants.

Now he let Moses see it with his own eyes.

But Moses would never cross into it.

**Reading help**

Descendants are children, grandchildren and later family members.

**Pause and answer**

Who had promised this land to Israel’s ancestors?

## Israel mourns for Moses

## Deuteronomy 34:5

Moses, the LORD’s servant, died there in Moab.

It happened as the LORD had said.

## Deuteronomy 34:6

The LORD buried Moses in a valley facing Beth-peor in Moab.

No one knows where his grave is.

## Deuteronomy 34:7

Moses was 120 years old when he died.

He could still see clearly and was still strong.

## Deuteronomy 34:8

Israel wept for Moses on the plains of Moab.

For 30 days the people mourned.

Then their time of mourning ended.

**Pause and answer**

How long did Israel mourn for Moses?

## Joshua takes the lead

## Deuteronomy 34:9

Moses had laid his hands on Joshua, son of Nun.

Because of this, the spirit of wisdom filled Joshua.

Israel listened to Joshua and did what the LORD had commanded Moses.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did Israel listen to after Moses died?

## Israel remembers Moses

## Deuteronomy 34:10

Israel had never again seen a prophet like Moses.

The LORD knew him face to face.

**Reading help**

“Face to face” means the LORD knew Moses closely and spoke to him directly.

## Deuteronomy 34:11

The LORD had sent Moses to do signs and wonders in Egypt.

Pharaoh, his officials and the whole land saw them.

No other prophet in Israel had done such things.

## Deuteronomy 34:12

All Israel had seen the mighty things Moses did.

His deeds filled the people with awe.

No other prophet in Israel had done what Moses did.
