# Deuteronomy 10 — What the LORD asks of Israel

**About this chapter**

Moses remembers carrying two new tablets down the mountain. He tells Israel how to show love for the LORD and for people from other lands.

## Two new stone tablets

## Deuteronomy 10:1

The LORD told Moses to cut two flat pieces of stone.

These tablets must be like the first two.

Moses must come up the mountain to meet the LORD.

He must also make a wooden chest called an ark.

## Deuteronomy 10:2

The LORD would write on the new tablets.

He would use the same words as before.

Moses had broken the first tablets.

He must put the new ones in the ark.

## Deuteronomy 10:3

Moses made the ark from acacia wood.

He cut two stone tablets like the first ones.

Then he carried them up the mountain.

## Deuteronomy 10:4

The LORD wrote the Ten Commandments on the tablets again.

Israel had gathered at the mountain and heard those same words.

The LORD had spoken them from the fire.

Now the LORD gave Moses the tablets.

## Deuteronomy 10:5

Moses carried the tablets down the mountain.

He put them in the ark as the LORD had commanded.

They were still there when Moses spoke to Israel.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Moses put the new tablets?

## Eleazar becomes priest

## Deuteronomy 10:6

Israel traveled from Beeroth Bene-jaakan to Moserah.

Aaron died there, and the people buried him.

Aaron’s son Eleazar became priest in his place.

## Deuteronomy 10:7

The people went from Moserah to Gudgodah.

Then they went to Jotbathah, a land with streams.

## The work of the Levites

## Deuteronomy 10:8

The LORD chose the tribe of Levi for special work.

They carried the ark of the LORD’s covenant.

They stood before the LORD to serve him.

They spoke blessings in his name.

They still did this work when Moses spoke.

**Reading help**

A covenant is a solemn agreement.

The ark held the tablets with the words of the LORD’s covenant.

**Pause and answer**

Which tribe carried the ark?

## Deuteronomy 10:9

This is why Levi received no share of land alongside the other tribes.

The LORD himself was their share, just as he promised.

## The LORD listens to Moses

## Deuteronomy 10:10

Moses stayed on the mountain for 40 days and 40 nights, as before.

Again the LORD listened to Moses.

He agreed to spare Israel.

## Deuteronomy 10:11

The LORD told Moses to lead the people onward.

They would enter the land and make it their home.

The LORD had sworn to give it to their ancestors.

**Pause and answer**

Who would lead the people onward?

## Love the LORD and follow his commands

## Deuteronomy 10:12

Moses asked Israel to consider what the LORD wanted from them.

They must honor the LORD deeply and live as he taught them.

They must love him and serve him with all their heart and soul.

## Deuteronomy 10:13

They must keep the LORD’s commands and laws.

Moses gave them these commands for their own good.

## Deuteronomy 10:14

Even the highest heavens belong to the LORD.

The earth and everything in it belong to him too.

## Deuteronomy 10:15

The LORD loved Israel’s ancestors.

Of all the peoples, he chose their descendants.

The Israelites Moses spoke to were those descendants.

## Treat people fairly

## Deuteronomy 10:16

Moses told them to circumcise their hearts.

He told them to stop being stubborn.

**Reading help**

Circumcision was a sign on the body of belonging to God.

Moses used it as a picture.

He wanted the people to change inwardly and obey God willingly.

## Deuteronomy 10:17

The LORD is God over all gods and Lord over all lords.

He is great and strong and fills people with awe.

He judges everyone fairly.

No one can pay him to change his judgment.

## Deuteronomy 10:18

He makes sure children without fathers and widows receive fair treatment.

He loves people from other lands.

He gives them food and clothes.

**Reading help**

A widow is a woman whose husband has died.

![A cloak](../pictures/cloak.svg)

A cloak is clothing someone can wear for warmth.

## Deuteronomy 10:19

Israel must also love people from other lands.

The Israelites themselves had lived as foreigners in Egypt.

**Pause and answer**

Where had the Israelites lived as foreigners?

## Remember what the LORD has done

## Deuteronomy 10:20

Israel must honor the LORD deeply and serve him.

They must stay faithful to him.

When making solemn promises, they must use his name.

## Deuteronomy 10:21

The LORD is their God, the one they should praise.

They had seen the great and wonderful things he did for them.

## Deuteronomy 10:22

Only 70 of their ancestors went to Egypt.

Now the LORD had made Israel as numerous as the stars.

Moses meant that Israel had grown into a very large people.

**Pause and answer**

How many of their ancestors went to Egypt?
