# Deuteronomy 31 — Joshua will lead Israel

**About this chapter**

Moses prepares Joshua to lead Israel. The people will hear the law read aloud and learn a song they can pass on.

## Moses speaks about crossing the Jordan

## Deuteronomy 31:1

Moses finished giving this message to all Israel.

## Deuteronomy 31:2

Then Moses told them he was 120 years old.

He could no longer lead them as before.

The LORD had told him he would never cross the Jordan.

## Deuteronomy 31:3

The LORD himself would cross ahead of Israel.

He would destroy the nations there, and Israel would take their land.

Joshua would lead the people across, as the LORD had said.

## Deuteronomy 31:4

The LORD would do what he had done to Sihon and Og.

He had destroyed those Amorite kings and their kingdoms.

## Deuteronomy 31:5

The LORD would give Israel victory over those nations.

Israel must follow the commands Moses had given about them.

## Deuteronomy 31:6

Moses told the people to be strong and brave.

They need not fear those nations.

The LORD their God would go with them.

He would never leave them.

## Joshua hears the promise

## Deuteronomy 31:7

Moses called Joshua forward so all Israel could hear.

He told Joshua to be strong and brave.

Joshua would go with Israel into the land the LORD had promised their ancestors.

He would give the people their shares of the land.

**Pause and answer**

Who would lead the people across the Jordan?

## Deuteronomy 31:8

The LORD himself would go ahead of Joshua.

He would stay with Joshua and never leave him.

Joshua could take courage.

## Read the law to everyone

## Deuteronomy 31:9

Moses wrote down the law and gave it to the priests and all Israel’s elders.

These priests came from Levi’s tribe.

They carried the ark of the LORD’s covenant.

**Reading help**

A covenant is a solemn agreement.

The ark was the special chest that held the stone tablets of the covenant.

## Deuteronomy 31:10

Moses gave them instructions for the end of every seven years.

That was the year when lenders canceled debts.

They must follow these instructions during the Feast of Shelters.

## Deuteronomy 31:11

All Israel would gather before the LORD in the place he chose.

The priests and elders must read the law aloud so everyone could hear.

![An open scroll with lines of writing](../pictures/scroll.svg)

One person could read the written words aloud for the whole gathering.

## Deuteronomy 31:12

They must gather men, women, children and foreigners living in their towns.

Everyone could listen and learn to honor the LORD deeply.

Everyone could learn to obey his law carefully.

**Pause and answer**

Who could come and hear the law?

## Deuteronomy 31:13

Even children who did not yet know the law would hear it.

They would learn to honor the LORD as long as Israel lived in the land across the Jordan.

## Moses and Joshua go to the tent

## Deuteronomy 31:14

The LORD told Moses that he would soon die.

Moses must bring Joshua to the Tent of Meeting.

There the LORD would give Joshua his work.

Moses and Joshua went to the tent together.

## Deuteronomy 31:15

The LORD appeared in a tall column of cloud.

The cloud stood over the entrance to the tent.

## The LORD warns Moses

## Deuteronomy 31:16

The LORD told Moses what would happen after his death.

Israel would worship the foreign gods of its new land.

The people would leave the LORD and break their covenant with him.

## Deuteronomy 31:17

The LORD said he would be angry and turn away.

Destruction and many troubles would come upon Israel.

The people would ask whether they suffered because their God was no longer with them.

## Deuteronomy 31:18

The LORD said he would hide his face from them.

He would turn away because they had done wrong and worshiped other gods.

**Reading help**

Hiding his face is a picture of God withdrawing his help.

## A song Israel will remember

## Deuteronomy 31:19

The LORD wanted a song written down and taught to Israel.

The people must learn to say its words.

The song would serve as the LORD’s witness against them.

## Deuteronomy 31:20

The LORD would bring them into the land he had promised their ancestors.

It was a rich land flowing with milk and honey.

They would have plenty to eat and do well.

Then they would worship other gods, reject the LORD and break their covenant.

## Deuteronomy 31:21

When trouble came, their descendants would still remember the words.

The song would show that the people had heard the LORD’s warning.

Even before giving them the land, he knew what they wanted to do.

**Pause and answer**

How would the song remind later generations of the warning?

## Deuteronomy 31:22

That very day Moses wrote down the song.

He taught it to the people of Israel.

## The LORD speaks to Joshua

## Deuteronomy 31:23

The LORD gave Joshua, son of Nun, his work.

He told Joshua to be strong and brave.

Joshua would bring Israel into the land the LORD had promised them.

The LORD promised to be with Joshua.

## Keep the book beside the ark

## Deuteronomy 31:24

Moses finished writing the whole law in a book.

## Deuteronomy 31:25

Then Moses spoke to the Levites who carried the ark of the LORD’s covenant.

## Deuteronomy 31:26

They must put the Book of the Law beside the ark.

It would stay there as a witness against Israel.

The written law would show what Israel had agreed to obey.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Moses tell the Levites to put the book?

## Deuteronomy 31:27

Moses knew how stubborn the people were.

They already disobeyed the LORD while Moses was alive.

Moses expected them to rebel even more after his death.

## Israel gathers to hear the song

## Deuteronomy 31:28

Moses asked for all the tribal elders and officers to gather.

He wanted them to hear his words.

He called heaven and earth to witness what he said against them.

**Reading help**

A witness hears or sees what happens.

Moses spoke as if heaven and earth could hear his warning.

## Deuteronomy 31:29

Moses knew the people would turn from his teaching after his death.

They would do what the LORD saw as evil.

Their actions would anger the LORD and bring disaster upon them.

## Deuteronomy 31:30

Then Moses spoke the words of the song aloud.

All Israel heard it from beginning to end.
