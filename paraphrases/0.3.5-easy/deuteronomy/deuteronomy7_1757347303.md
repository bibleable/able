# Deuteronomy 7 — Moses reminds Israel why the LORD chose them

**About this chapter**

Moses speaks to Israel before the people enter the land. He reminds them how the LORD freed them from slavery.

## Seven nations in the land

## Deuteronomy 7:1

Moses spoke about the land Israel was entering.

The LORD would drive out seven nations larger and stronger than Israel.

Moses named the Hittites, Girgashites, Amorites and Canaanites.

He also named the Perizzites, Hivites and Jebusites.

## Deuteronomy 7:2

Moses said the LORD would give Israel victory over those nations.

He told Israel to destroy them completely.

Israel must make no peace agreement with them and show them no mercy.

## Deuteronomy 7:3

Moses told Israel that their families must not marry into those nations.

Their daughters must not marry the sons of those nations.

Their sons must not marry the daughters of those nations.

## Deuteronomy 7:4

Moses said those families would turn Israel’s sons away from the LORD.

Israel’s sons would serve other gods.

Then the LORD would be angry and quickly destroy Israel.

## Deuteronomy 7:5

Moses told Israel to tear down the altars of those nations.

They must break the stone pillars used in worship.

They must cut down the poles used to worship Asherah.

They must burn the idols.

**Reading help**

An idol is an object people worship as a god.

## Israel belongs to the LORD

## Deuteronomy 7:6

Israel was holy to the LORD their God.

Out of all the peoples on earth, he had chosen Israel as his own.

Israel was precious to him.

## Deuteronomy 7:7

Israel was the smallest of all those peoples.

The size of their nation was no reason for the LORD to choose them.

## Deuteronomy 7:8

The LORD loved Israel.

He kept the promise he had sworn to their ancestors.

With his great power he freed them from slavery under Pharaoh, king of Egypt.

**Pause and answer**

Who freed Israel from slavery in Egypt?

## The LORD keeps his promise

## Deuteronomy 7:9

Moses told Israel that the LORD is God.

He is faithful and keeps his covenant.

His faithful love reaches a thousand generations of people who love and obey him.

**Reading help**

A covenant is a solemn agreement. A generation means people born around the same time.

## Deuteronomy 7:10

The LORD would deal directly with those who hated him.

He would destroy them without delay.

## Deuteronomy 7:11

So Moses told Israel to obey the commands and laws he gave them that day.

## Blessings in the land

## Deuteronomy 7:12

If Israel listened and obeyed carefully, the LORD would keep his covenant.

He would show them the faithful love he had promised their ancestors.

## Deuteronomy 7:13

The LORD would love and bless them in the land he had promised.

Their families would grow.

He would bless their children, grain, new wine and oil.

Their cattle would have calves and their sheep would have lambs.

![A stalk of ripe grain](../pictures/grain.svg)

Grain was one of the crops Israel would grow.

**Pause and answer**

What young animals would the sheep have?

## Deuteronomy 7:14

The LORD would bless Israel more than any other people.

Their men and women would be able to have children.

Their livestock would also be able to have young.

## Deuteronomy 7:15

The LORD would take sickness away from Israel.

He would spare them the terrible diseases they knew in Egypt.

Those diseases would strike the people who hated Israel.

## Moses reminds Israel about Egypt

## Deuteronomy 7:16

Moses told Israel to destroy the peoples the LORD handed over to them without pity.

He warned Israel against worshiping their gods.

Such worship would catch Israel like a trap.

**Reading help**

A trap holds what it catches. Moses meant that worshiping other gods would lead Israel away from the LORD.

## Deuteronomy 7:17

Moses knew the people might wonder how they could drive out stronger nations.

## Deuteronomy 7:18

He told them they need not be afraid.

They could remember what the LORD did to Pharaoh and all Egypt.

**Pause and answer**

What did Moses ask Israel to remember?

## Deuteronomy 7:19

They had seen terrible trials, signs and wonders in Egypt.

The LORD had used his great power to bring them out.

He would do the same to the nations they feared.

## Little by little

## Deuteronomy 7:20

Moses said the LORD would send hornets against those nations.

Even the survivors in hiding would die.

**Reading help**

A hornet is a large insect that stings.

## Deuteronomy 7:21

Israel need not live in terror.

The LORD their God was among them.

His greatness and power filled people with awe.

## Deuteronomy 7:22

The LORD would drive those nations out little by little.

If they all left at once, wild animals would grow too numerous.

**Pause and answer**

Why would the nations leave little by little?

## Deuteronomy 7:23

The LORD would give Israel victory over those nations.

He would throw them into confusion until they faced complete destruction.

## Deuteronomy 7:24

The LORD would hand their kings over to Israel.

Moses said Israel would wipe out even the memory of those kings.

No one would stand against Israel.

Israel would destroy them.

## Leave the silver and gold

## Deuteronomy 7:25

Moses told Israel to burn the images of those gods.

Some images had silver or gold on them.

Israel must neither long for those metals nor take them.

The metals could draw Israel into worship the LORD hated.

**Pause and answer**

What were the people to leave on the images?

## Deuteronomy 7:26

Moses warned Israel to keep such things out of their homes.

The LORD had set those things apart for destruction.

Anyone who brought one home would face destruction too.

Israel must treat those objects as things to reject completely.
