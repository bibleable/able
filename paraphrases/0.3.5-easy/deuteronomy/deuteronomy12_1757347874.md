# Deuteronomy 12 — The place the LORD will choose

**About this chapter**

Moses tells Israel where families may eat their meals. Some food is set apart to share before the LORD.

## Worship in the new land

## Deuteronomy 12:1

Moses taught Israel laws to follow carefully throughout their lives.

They would live in the land the LORD had given them.

He was the God of their ancestors.

## Deuteronomy 12:2

The nations Israel would drive out had places for worshiping their gods.

These places stood on mountains, on hills and under leafy trees.

Moses told Israel to destroy all those places.

## Deuteronomy 12:3

Israel must tear down their altars and break their sacred stone pillars.

They must burn the poles used to worship Asherah.

They must cut down the idols.

They must remove the names of those gods from every place.

## Deuteronomy 12:4

Israel must never worship the LORD that way.

## Bring gifts to the chosen place

## Deuteronomy 12:5

The LORD would choose a place in the land of one of Israel’s tribes.

He would put his name there.

The people must seek out that place and go there to worship him.

**Reading help**

Putting his name there meant the LORD chose this place for people to meet with him.

## Deuteronomy 12:6

They must bring their burnt offerings and other sacrifices there.

They must bring their tithes and the special gifts they lifted up to the LORD.

They must bring gifts they had promised and gifts they freely chose to give.

They must also bring the firstborn animals from their herds and flocks.

**Reading help**

A tithe is one part out of ten equal parts.

Firstborn means the first young animal born to its mother.

## Deuteronomy 12:7

There whole households would eat together before the LORD.

They would celebrate all their work because the LORD had blessed them.

**Pause and answer**

Who ate together before the LORD?

## After Israel crosses the Jordan

## Deuteronomy 12:8

Until then, each person had done what seemed right to them.

Moses said this practice must end.

## Deuteronomy 12:9

Israel had yet to reach its place of rest.

The LORD was giving them land of their own.

## Deuteronomy 12:10

They would cross the Jordan and settle in that land.

The LORD would give them rest from their enemies.

Then they could live in safety.

## Deuteronomy 12:11

The LORD would choose the place for his name.

There they must bring their burnt offerings and sacrifices.

They must bring their tithes and special gifts.

They must bring the best gifts they had promised the LORD.

## Include the Levites

## Deuteronomy 12:12

Whole families must celebrate before the LORD together.

That included sons, daughters, and male and female servants.

They must welcome the Levites living in their towns.

The Levites had no share of land like the other tribes.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the Levites need to share the celebration?

## Deuteronomy 12:13

The people must never choose just any place for burnt offerings.

## Deuteronomy 12:14

They must bring burnt offerings only to the place the LORD chose within one tribe’s land.

There they must do everything Moses commanded.

## What families can eat at home

## Deuteronomy 12:15

In their own towns, people could kill animals for food whenever they wanted meat.

They could eat from what the LORD had given them.

People could eat this meat as they ate gazelle or deer.

They could eat whether they were clean or unclean for worship.

**Reading help**

Clean and unclean describe whether a person could take part in worship at that time.

## Deuteronomy 12:16

They must never eat the blood.

They must pour it on the ground like water.

## What families share at the chosen place

## Deuteronomy 12:17

At home they must never eat the tithe of grain, new wine or oil.

This rule also covered the firstborn of their herds and flocks.

It covered promised gifts, freely chosen gifts and special gifts.

![A stalk of ripe grain](../pictures/grain.svg)

The people set aside one tenth of their grain.

## Deuteronomy 12:18

They must eat these gifts before the LORD at his chosen place.

Their sons, daughters and servants must share the meal.

The Levites living in their towns must share it too.

Together they must celebrate all their work before the LORD.

**Pause and answer**

Where did families eat the gifts set apart for the LORD?

## Deuteronomy 12:19

They must keep caring for the Levites as long as they lived in their land.

## When the chosen place is far away

## Deuteronomy 12:20

The LORD had promised to give Israel more land.

When that happened, they could eat meat whenever they wanted.

## Deuteronomy 12:21

Some might live far from the place the LORD chose for his name.

They could kill animals from the herds and flocks he gave them, as Moses had instructed.

They could eat the meat in their towns whenever they wanted.

## Deuteronomy 12:22

They could eat it just as they ate gazelle or deer.

This included people who were clean or unclean for worship.

## Deuteronomy 12:23

They must never eat the blood.

The animal’s life was in its blood.

They must never eat the life with the meat.

## Deuteronomy 12:24

They must pour the blood on the ground like water.

They must never eat it.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Israel pour the blood from an animal killed for food?

## Deuteronomy 12:25

The LORD saw this as the right thing to do.

If they obeyed, things could go well for them and their children.

## What goes on the altar

## Deuteronomy 12:26

They must take gifts set apart for the LORD to his chosen place.

They must also take the offerings they had promised him.

## Deuteronomy 12:27

The meat and blood of burnt offerings belonged on the LORD’s altar.

For other sacrifices, they must pour the blood beside the altar.

They could eat the meat from those other sacrifices.

**Reading help**

An altar was a place where people brought sacrifices to God.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the blood from other sacrifices go?

## Deuteronomy 12:28

Moses told Israel to obey all these commands carefully.

Then they would do what the LORD saw as good and right.

Things could go well for them and their children always.

## Moses warns Israel about copying other nations

## Deuteronomy 12:29

The LORD would remove the nations ahead of Israel.

Israel would drive them out and settle in their land.

## Deuteronomy 12:30

Moses warned Israel about copying the worship of those nations.

Their ways could catch Israel like a trap.

Israel must never ask how those nations worshiped so they could do the same.

## Deuteronomy 12:31

Those nations did things the LORD hated when they worshiped.

They even burned their sons and daughters as gifts to their gods.

Israel must never worship the LORD that way.

## Deuteronomy 12:32

Moses told Israel to do everything he commanded.

They must never add a command or leave one out.
