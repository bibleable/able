# Deuteronomy 32 — The song of Moses

**About this chapter**

Moses sings about the LORD’s care for Israel and Israel’s unfaithfulness. Afterward, the LORD tells Moses where he will see the promised land.

## Teaching like rain

## Deuteronomy 32:1

Moses began his song by calling heaven and earth to listen.

## Deuteronomy 32:2

May my teaching fall like rain.

May my words settle like dew on new grass.

May they help people grow as showers help young plants.

## Deuteronomy 32:3

I will tell of the LORD.

Praise our God for his greatness!

## The LORD is the Rock

## Deuteronomy 32:4

The LORD is our Rock.

Everything he does is perfect and fair.

He is faithful and always does what is right.

**Reading help**

A large rock stands firm. Calling God the Rock means his people can depend on him.

## Deuteronomy 32:5

Yet his people did wrong and turned against him.

They acted unlike his children.

Their own crooked ways brought them shame.

## Deuteronomy 32:6

Moses asked Israel why they treated the LORD so foolishly.

He was their Father.

He made them and established them as a people.

## Remember how Israel began

## Deuteronomy 32:7

Remember long ago.

Ask your father to tell you what happened.

Ask your elders, and they will teach you.

**Pause and answer**

Who could tell Israel about the past?

## Deuteronomy 32:8

God Most High gave each nation its land.

He divided the peoples and marked their borders.

He set those borders according to the number of the sons of God.

## Deuteronomy 32:9

The LORD chose Jacob’s people as his own share.

## An eagle caring for its young

## Deuteronomy 32:10

He found Israel in a howling, empty desert.

He surrounded them and taught them.

He guarded them as carefully as someone guards their own eye.

## Deuteronomy 32:11

An eagle stirs its nest and hovers over its young.

It spreads its wings to catch and carry them.

That is how the LORD cared for Israel.

**Pause and answer**

How does the eagle care for its young?

## Deuteronomy 32:12

The LORD alone led Israel.

No foreign god helped him.

## Deuteronomy 32:13

He brought Israel to the high places of the land.

The fields gave them food.

Even rocky places gave them honey and oil.

## Deuteronomy 32:14

They ate curds from cattle and drank milk from their flocks.

They enjoyed rich lamb, rams from Bashan, goats and fine wheat.

They drank wine from the best grapes.

## Israel turns away

## Deuteronomy 32:15

Moses called Israel Jeshurun in his song.

He pictured a well-fed animal kicking after eating its fill.

Israel had plenty but left the God who made them.

They despised the Rock who saved them.

## Deuteronomy 32:16

They worshiped foreign gods and made the LORD jealous.

They did things he hated and made him angry.

## Deuteronomy 32:17

They offered sacrifices to demons instead of God.

They served strange new gods their ancestors had never honored.

## Deuteronomy 32:18

Israel ignored the Rock who gave them life.

They forgot the God who gave them birth.

## The LORD answers Israel

## Deuteronomy 32:19

The LORD saw what his sons and daughters did.

They made him angry, and he rejected them.

## Deuteronomy 32:20

The LORD said he would hide his face from them and see what became of them.

His children had turned away and broken faith with him.

## Deuteronomy 32:21

Their worthless idols had stirred his jealousy and anger.

Now he would make Israel jealous of people they hardly counted as a nation.

A nation without understanding would make Israel angry.

## Deuteronomy 32:22

The LORD pictured his anger as a fire reaching down to Sheol, the place of the dead.

It burned the earth and its crops.

It scorched even the bases of the mountains.

## Trouble inside and outside

## Deuteronomy 32:23

He would send one disaster after another.

He pictured them as arrows that would strike Israel.

## Deuteronomy 32:24

Hunger would wear the people down.

Terrible diseases would spread among them.

Wild animals would bite them, and deadly snakes would strike from the dust.

## Deuteronomy 32:25

Outside their homes war would take their children.

Inside, terror would reach young men and women, babies and old people.

## Deuteronomy 32:26

The LORD said he had thought of destroying Israel completely.

No one would even remember them.

## Deuteronomy 32:27

But the LORD did not want their enemies to mock him.

The enemies might claim that their own strength had won.

They might deny that the LORD had done these things.

**Pause and answer**

Who might claim the victory as their own?

## If only Israel understood

## Deuteronomy 32:28

Israel had no wise counsel.

The people lacked understanding.

## Deuteronomy 32:29

If only they were wise, they would understand where their choices led.

## Deuteronomy 32:30

How could one enemy chase a thousand Israelites?

How could two make ten thousand run away?

This could happen only because their Rock, the LORD, had handed them over.

## Deuteronomy 32:31

Even Israel’s enemies admitted their own rock was unlike Israel’s Rock.

## Deuteronomy 32:32

The song pictures a vine from Sodom and the fields of Gomorrah.

Its bunches of grapes are bitter and poisonous.

## Deuteronomy 32:33

Its wine is like deadly snake venom.

These pictures show how harmful evil is.

## The LORD remembers

## Deuteronomy 32:34

The LORD said he kept these things sealed in his storehouse.

He would remember them.

## Deuteronomy 32:35

The LORD himself would repay the wrong.

Their feet would slip when the time came.

Disaster would soon overtake them.

## Deuteronomy 32:36

The LORD would act for his people and show his servants mercy.

He would see their strength was gone.

No one would be left, slave or free.

## Who can give shelter?

## Deuteronomy 32:37

The LORD would ask where their other gods were.

Israel had trusted those gods for shelter.

## Deuteronomy 32:38

Israel had given those gods the fat of sacrifices and offerings of wine.

Could those gods come and help them now?

Could they give them shelter?

## Deuteronomy 32:39

The LORD said he alone was God.

He brings death and gives life.

He wounds and he heals.

No one can snatch anyone from his hand.

## The LORD promises to judge

## Deuteronomy 32:40

The LORD raised his hand toward heaven to make a solemn promise.

He swore by his own life, which never ends.

## Deuteronomy 32:41

He pictured himself holding a shining sword ready for judgment.

He would repay the enemies who hated him.

## Deuteronomy 32:42

The song pictures arrows drinking blood and a sword eating flesh.

The LORD’s judgment would reach the fallen, the captives and the enemy leaders.

## Deuteronomy 32:43

Moses called on heaven to rejoice and on all God’s angels to worship him.

He called on the nations to rejoice with God’s people.

God would punish the enemies who killed his children and repay those who hated him.

He would cleanse his land and his people.

## Teach the song to the children

## Deuteronomy 32:44

Moses came with Joshua, son of Nun.

All the people heard Moses speak every word of the song.

## Deuteronomy 32:45

Moses finished reciting the song to all Israel.

## Deuteronomy 32:46

He told them to take his words to heart.

They must teach their children to obey the whole law carefully.

**Pause and answer**

What must the people teach their children?

## Deuteronomy 32:47

Their lives depended on these words.

By following them, Israel would live long in the land across the Jordan.

## Moses will see Canaan

## Deuteronomy 32:48

That same day the LORD spoke to Moses.

## Deuteronomy 32:49

He told Moses to climb Mount Nebo in the Abarim mountains.

It stood in Moab, across from Jericho.

From there Moses could see Canaan, the land God was giving Israel.

![A mountain](../pictures/mountain.svg)

Point to the top, where Moses would look out over the land.

**Pause and answer**

Which mountain did the LORD tell Moses to climb?

## Deuteronomy 32:50

Moses would die on the mountain and join his people who had died.

His brother Aaron had died on Mount Hor and joined his people too.

## Deuteronomy 32:51

At the waters of Meribah-kadesh, Moses and Aaron had been unfaithful to the LORD.

There in the Wilderness of Zin, they failed to honor him as holy before Israel.

## Deuteronomy 32:52

Moses would see Israel’s land from a distance.

He would never enter it.
