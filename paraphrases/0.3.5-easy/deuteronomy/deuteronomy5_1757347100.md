# Deuteronomy 5 — Moses reminds Israel of the Ten Commandments

**About this chapter**

Moses reminds Israel of the commands the LORD spoke at Horeb. The people remember hearing his voice from the fire.

## Moses calls the people together

## Deuteronomy 5:1

Moses called all Israel together.

He asked the people to listen to the laws he taught that day.

They must learn them and follow them carefully.

## Deuteronomy 5:2

Moses said the LORD their God had made a covenant with them at Horeb.

**Reading help**

A covenant is a solemn agreement.

## Deuteronomy 5:3

Moses said this covenant was with all of them alive there that day.

It was not a covenant made with their ancestors.

## Deuteronomy 5:4

The LORD had spoken face to face with Israel from the fire on the mountain.

This means the people heard him speak to them directly.

## Deuteronomy 5:5

Moses stood between the LORD and the people to tell them the LORD’s words.

The people feared the fire and would not climb the mountain.

Moses reminded them what the LORD had said.

**Pause and answer**

Why did the people stay off the mountain?

## Worship the LORD alone

**The LORD’s words to Israel**

## Deuteronomy 5:6

I am the LORD your God.

I brought you out of Egypt, where you were slaves.

## Deuteronomy 5:7

You must have no other gods before me.

## Deuteronomy 5:8

Never make an idol for yourself.

Never shape one like anything in the sky, on land or in the water.

**Reading help**

An idol is something people make and worship as a god.

## Deuteronomy 5:9

Do not bow down to idols or worship them.

I am the LORD your God.

I am a jealous God who calls you to worship me alone.

When people hate me, I punish their sin.

That punishment reaches their children, even the third and fourth generations.

## Deuteronomy 5:10

I show faithful love to those who love me and keep my commands.

My love reaches a thousand generations.

## Deuteronomy 5:11

Treat the name of the LORD your God with respect.

The LORD will punish anyone who misuses his name.

## Give everyone a day of rest

## Deuteronomy 5:12

Keep the Sabbath day holy, as the LORD your God commanded.

Set that day apart for him.

## Deuteronomy 5:13

Do your work on six days.

## Deuteronomy 5:14

The seventh day is a Sabbath for the LORD your God.

You and your children must rest from work.

Your male and female servants must rest just as you do.

Give your ox, your donkey and all your animals rest.

People from other lands living in your towns must rest too.

![Days one through six are workdays. Day seven is for rest.](../pictures/sabbath-week.svg)

Point to the day set apart for rest.

**Pause and answer**

Who could rest along with the families they served?

## Deuteronomy 5:15

Remember that you were slaves in Egypt.

The LORD your God used his great power to bring you out.

That is why he told you to keep the Sabbath.

## How to treat other people

## Deuteronomy 5:16

Honor your father and mother, as the LORD your God commanded.

Then you may live long and do well in the land he gives you.

## Deuteronomy 5:17

You must not murder.

## Deuteronomy 5:18

You must not commit adultery.

**Reading help**

Adultery means having sex with someone else’s husband or wife, or with someone besides your own husband or wife.

## Deuteronomy 5:19

You must not steal.

## Deuteronomy 5:20

Never give false evidence against your neighbor.

Tell the truth about what happened.

## Deuteronomy 5:21

Do not covet your neighbor’s wife.

Do not covet your neighbor’s house, field, servants, ox or donkey.

This applies to everything that belongs to your neighbor.

**Reading help**

To covet something is to want it for yourself even though it belongs to someone else.

## The people hear the LORD

## Deuteronomy 5:22

Moses said the LORD had spoken these commands aloud to everyone.

His voice came from fire, cloud and deep darkness on the mountain.

He added nothing more.

He wrote the commands on two stone tablets and gave them to Moses.

**Pause and answer**

What did the LORD write the commands on?

## Deuteronomy 5:23

Fire blazed on the mountain.

The people heard the voice coming from the darkness.

All the tribal leaders and elders came to Moses.

## Deuteronomy 5:24

The leaders said the LORD had shown them his glory and greatness.

They had heard his voice from the fire.

Now they knew people could hear God speak and still live.

## The leaders ask Moses to listen

## Deuteronomy 5:25

But the leaders feared the great fire would burn them up.

They feared they would die if the LORD kept speaking to them.

## Deuteronomy 5:26

The leaders asked who else had ever heard the living God speak from fire and survived.

## Deuteronomy 5:27

They asked Moses to go near and listen to the LORD.

Moses could tell them everything the LORD said.

They promised to listen and obey.

**Pause and answer**

What did the leaders promise to do?

## The LORD answers Moses

## Deuteronomy 5:28

The LORD heard what the leaders told Moses.

He told Moses the people had spoken well.

## Deuteronomy 5:29

The LORD wanted them always to honor him deeply and keep every command.

Then things would go well for them and their children forever.

## Deuteronomy 5:30

The LORD told Moses to send the people back to their tents.

## Deuteronomy 5:31

Moses himself must stay with the LORD.

The LORD would give Moses all the commands and laws.

Moses must teach Israel to follow them in their new land.

**Pause and answer**

Who stayed to hear the LORD’s instructions?

## Deuteronomy 5:32

Moses told Israel to do exactly what the LORD had commanded.

He pictured it as staying on a path without turning right or left.

## Deuteronomy 5:33

Following that path meant doing everything the LORD told them.

Then they could live and do well.

They could live long in the land they would receive.
