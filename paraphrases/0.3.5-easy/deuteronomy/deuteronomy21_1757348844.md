# Deuteronomy 21 — An unknown killer and laws for families

**About this chapter**

Moses explains what a town must do when a killer is unknown. He also gives laws about marriage, family property and burial.

## When no one knew who killed a person

## Deuteronomy 21:1

Moses spoke about a death in Israel’s new land.

Someone might find a murdered person in a field.

No one knew who the killer was.

## Deuteronomy 21:2

The elders and judges must go out to the field.

They must measure the distance from the body to each nearby town.

**Reading help**

Elders were leaders in a town.

## Deuteronomy 21:3

The elders of the nearest town must take a heifer.

This young cow must never have worn a yoke or done farm work.

**Pause and answer**

Which town’s elders had to bring the heifer?

## Deuteronomy 21:4

The elders must bring the heifer to a valley with running water.

No one must have plowed or planted the ground there.

The ceremony included breaking the heifer’s neck beside the stream.

## The elders speak before the LORD

## Deuteronomy 21:5

The priests from Levi’s tribe must come forward.

The LORD chose them to serve him and give blessings in his name.

They also decided cases about disputes and attacks.

## Deuteronomy 21:6

All the elders of the nearest town must wash their hands over the dead heifer beside the stream.

## Deuteronomy 21:7

The elders must say that they did not kill this person.

They did not see the killing either.

**Pause and answer**

What did the elders say they had not seen?

## Deuteronomy 21:8

They must ask the LORD to accept this act of atonement for Israel, the people he had freed.

They must ask him to forgive Israel for the innocent person’s death.

In this way the LORD would take away the guilt of the killing.

**Reading help**

Atonement means dealing with guilt so people can have peace with God.

## Deuteronomy 21:9

By doing what the LORD said was right, Israel would be free of that guilt.

## A woman taken captive in war

## Deuteronomy 21:10

Moses described a time when the LORD gave Israel victory in war.

The Israelites took prisoners.

## Deuteronomy 21:11

An Israelite man might find a captive woman beautiful.

He might want her to be his wife.

## Deuteronomy 21:12

The man must bring the woman into his home.

She must shave her head and trim her nails.

## Deuteronomy 21:13

She must put away the clothes she wore as a captive.

She must have a full month to mourn her parents in his home.

Only then could he have sex with her and become her husband.

She would be his wife.

**Reading help**

To mourn means to grieve for someone you have lost.

**Pause and answer**

How long did the captive woman have to mourn?

## Deuteronomy 21:14

If he no longer wanted her, he must let her go wherever she chose.

He must never sell her or keep her as a slave.

He had already dishonored her.

## The rights of the oldest son

## Deuteronomy 21:15

Moses described a father who had sons by two wives.

He loved one wife but did not love the other.

The wife he did not love had his firstborn son.

## Deuteronomy 21:16

When the father divided his property, he must respect the firstborn son’s rights.

He could not give those rights to the son of the wife he loved.

## Deuteronomy 21:17

The father must recognize his oldest son as his firstborn.

That son had the right to two shares of the property.

He was the first son his father had.

## A charge brought by both parents

## Deuteronomy 21:18

Moses described a son who kept rebelling against both parents.

Even when they corrected him, he refused to listen.

## Deuteronomy 21:19

His parents must take him to the gate of their town.

There they must bring him before the elders.

## Deuteronomy 21:20

The parents must explain their charge to the elders.

Their son was stubborn and refused to obey them.

They accused him of eating too much and getting drunk.

## Deuteronomy 21:21

Under this law, the men of the town carried out a death sentence by stoning.

Moses said this would remove the evil from the community.

All Israel would hear about it and be afraid.

## Burial on the same day

## Deuteronomy 21:22

Moses spoke about someone who had committed a crime punishable by death.

After the execution, the people might hang the body on a tree.

## Deuteronomy 21:23

The people had to bury the body that same day.

It could not stay on the tree overnight.

Moses said someone hanging on a tree was under God’s curse.

Israel must keep the land the LORD gave them holy.
