# 1 Corinthians 14 — Speak so others can understand

**About this chapter**

Paul writes to the church in Corinth.

He asks the believers to think about their listeners.

## Gifts that help other people

## 1 Corinthians 14:1

Make love your aim.

Be eager for the gifts the Spirit gives.

Especially seek the gift of prophecy.

**Help with these words**

Prophecy means speaking a message from God.

## 1 Corinthians 14:2

Someone speaking in a tongue speaks to God.

The other people do not understand the words.

Through the Spirit, the speaker says things that remain hidden from them.

**Help with these words**

Speaking in a tongue means speaking words the Spirit gives.

Listeners may need someone to explain them.

## 1 Corinthians 14:3

Someone who prophesies speaks to people.

Those words help people grow strong.

They give people courage and comfort.

## 1 Corinthians 14:4

Speaking in a tongue helps the speaker grow strong.

Speaking a message from God helps the whole church grow strong.

## 1 Corinthians 14:5

I wish you could all speak in tongues.

Even more, I want you to prophesy.

Prophecy does more for the church than speaking in tongues alone.

But someone can explain what the words mean.

Then the whole church can grow strong.

**Pause and answer**

Who receives help when someone prophesies?

## A tune people can recognize

## 1 Corinthians 14:6

Brothers and sisters, suppose I visit and speak in tongues.

How will that help you?

You need to hear something God has shown me.

Or I could share knowledge, a prophecy, or a lesson.

## 1 Corinthians 14:7

Think of a flute or a harp.

You need to hear the different notes to recognize the tune.

## 1 Corinthians 14:8

Suppose a trumpet gives an unclear call.

Who will know to get ready for battle?

## 1 Corinthians 14:9

The same is true when you speak.

Use words people can understand.

Otherwise, it is like speaking into empty air.

## 1 Corinthians 14:10

People around the world speak many different languages.

Every language has meaning.

## 1 Corinthians 14:11

Suppose I cannot understand the language someone speaks.

We will each sound like a foreigner to the other.

## 1 Corinthians 14:12

You are eager for gifts from the Spirit.

Put that effort into gifts that help the whole church grow.

**Pause and answer**

What helps you recognize a tune?

## Pray and sing with understanding

## 1 Corinthians 14:13

If you speak in a tongue, pray for another gift too.

Ask God to help you explain the words.

## 1 Corinthians 14:14

When I pray in a tongue, my spirit prays.

But my mind does not grasp the words.

## 1 Corinthians 14:15

So what will I do?

I will pray with my spirit and with my mind.

I will sing with my spirit and with my mind.

## 1 Corinthians 14:16

Suppose you thank God with your spirit.

Someone there cannot understand your words.

How can that person say “Amen” to your prayer?

**Help with these words**

Here “Amen” means you agree with the prayer.

## 1 Corinthians 14:17

You may have thanked God well.

Still, the other person has received no help.

## 1 Corinthians 14:18

I thank God that I speak in tongues more than any of you.

## 1 Corinthians 14:19

But when the church meets, I want to teach others.

I would choose five words people understand over ten thousand words in a tongue.

**Pause and answer**

Why would Paul choose five words people understand?

## Think about the visitor

## 1 Corinthians 14:20

Brothers and sisters, think like adults.

Be like babies when it comes to doing evil.

But think things through like grown people.

**Help with these words**

Paul uses babies as a picture of innocence.

He wants believers to have no practice doing evil.

## 1 Corinthians 14:21

In the Law, the Lord says he will speak to his people.

He will use strangers who speak other languages.

Even then his people will refuse to listen.

## 1 Corinthians 14:22

Speaking in tongues serves as a sign for unbelievers.

Prophecy serves as a sign for believers.

## 1 Corinthians 14:23

Suppose the whole church meets and everyone speaks in tongues.

Visitors come in who do not believe or understand.

They will think you have lost your minds.

## 1 Corinthians 14:24

Now suppose those visitors hear everyone speaking messages from God.

What they hear shows them their sin.

They must answer for what they have done.

## 1 Corinthians 14:25

Even their secret thoughts come to light.

Then they bow low and worship God.

They say, “God really is here among you!”

**Pause and answer**

What do the visitors say when their secret thoughts come to light?

## Take turns when the church meets

## 1 Corinthians 14:26

Brothers and sisters, each of you brings something to share.

One has a song of praise or a lesson.

Another has something God has shown them.

Another speaks in a tongue or explains the words.

Everything you share should help the church grow.

## 1 Corinthians 14:27

Two people may speak in tongues, or at most three.

Take turns speaking.

Someone must explain what the words mean.

| First speaker | Next speaker |
| --- | --- |
| Speaks while others listen. | Waits for a turn. |

## 1 Corinthians 14:28

If no one can explain, keep quiet in the meeting.

Speak privately to yourself and to God.

## 1 Corinthians 14:29

Two or three prophets should speak.

The others should think carefully about what they hear.

## 1 Corinthians 14:30

God may show something to someone who is sitting down.

Then the first speaker should stop.

## 1 Corinthians 14:31

You can all give God’s messages in turn.

That way everyone can learn and gain courage.

## 1 Corinthians 14:32

A prophet can control when to speak.

## 1 Corinthians 14:33

God brings peace instead of confusion.

This is true in all the churches of God’s people.

**Pause and answer**

What should a speaker do if no one can explain the tongue?

## Paul gives the church instructions

## 1 Corinthians 14:34

Women should keep quiet when the churches meet.

They must not speak at the meeting.

They must accept authority, as the law says.

## 1 Corinthians 14:35

If they want to ask about something, they should ask their own husbands at home.

It brings dishonor for a woman to speak in the church meeting.

## 1 Corinthians 14:36

Did God’s word begin with you in Corinth?

Has it reached only you?

## 1 Corinthians 14:37

Do you think you are a prophet or a spiritual person?

Then recognize whose command I am writing to you.

It comes from the Lord.

## 1 Corinthians 14:38

Anyone who ignores this will also be ignored.

## 1 Corinthians 14:39

So, brothers and sisters, be eager to prophesy.

Let people speak in tongues too.

## 1 Corinthians 14:40

Do everything in a fitting and orderly way.

**Pause and answer**

Whose command does Paul say he is passing on?
