# 1 Corinthians 6 — Settle disputes fairly and honour God with your body

**About this chapter**

Paul asks why believers in Corinth are taking each other to court.

He also explains why what they do with their bodies matters to God.

## Believers take each other to court

## 1 Corinthians 6:1

Suppose you have a complaint against another believer.

Why take it to judges who do not follow God?

Why not bring it to God’s people?

## 1 Corinthians 6:2

God’s people will judge the world.

Surely you can settle small disputes among yourselves.

## 1 Corinthians 6:3

Do you know that we will judge angels?

Then surely we can settle matters in this life.

## 1 Corinthians 6:4

When you have an everyday dispute, whom do you ask to judge it?

Do you choose people who have no place in the church?

## 1 Corinthians 6:5

You should feel ashamed about this.

Surely someone among you is wise enough to settle a disagreement between believers.

## 1 Corinthians 6:6

Yet one believer takes another believer to court.

People who do not believe in Christ hear the case.

**Pause and answer**

Who does Paul think could help settle an everyday dispute between believers?

## Stop cheating each other

## 1 Corinthians 6:7

When you take each other to court like this, you have already lost.

Why not accept a wrong done to you?

Why not accept the loss when someone cheats you?

## 1 Corinthians 6:8

Instead, some of you cheat and do wrong.

You even do this to your own brothers and sisters in Christ!

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul say some believers are doing to each other?

## Paul reminds the believers how God changed them

## 1 Corinthians 6:9

Do not fool yourselves.

People who do evil will not inherit God’s kingdom.

Think of people who do sexual wrong or worship idols.

Think of people who are sexually unfaithful in marriage or men who have sex with men.

## 1 Corinthians 6:10

The same warning applies to people who steal or are greedy.

It applies to people who get drunk, abuse others with words or cheat them.

They will not inherit God’s kingdom.

**Help with these words**

To inherit God’s kingdom means to receive a place among the people God rules.

## 1 Corinthians 6:11

Some of you once lived in these ways.

But God washed you clean and set you apart for himself.

He made you right with him.

He did this in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ and by his Spirit.

**Help with these words**

Washing is a word picture here. Paul is speaking about God taking away their sin.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul say God has done for the believers?

## Our bodies matter to the Lord

## 1 Corinthians 6:12

“I am free to do anything,” someone says.

But does everything help me?

“I am free to do anything.”

Yet I will not let anything become my master.

## 1 Corinthians 6:13

“Food is for the stomach, and the stomach is for food.”

God will bring both to an end.

God did not give us bodies for sexual wrongdoing.

Our bodies are for the Lord, and the Lord is for our bodies.

## 1 Corinthians 6:14

God used his power to raise the Lord Jesus from the dead.

God will raise us too.

**Pause and answer**

What will God do for us just as he did for Jesus?

## Honour God with your body

## 1 Corinthians 6:15

Your bodies are parts of Christ’s body.

Should I take what belongs to Christ and join it to a prostitute?

Never!

**Help with these words**

A prostitute is someone who has sex in return for payment.

## 1 Corinthians 6:16

A man who has sex with a prostitute joins his body to hers.

The Scriptures say, “The two will become one body.”

![An open scroll like the Scriptures Paul quotes about two people becoming one body.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## 1 Corinthians 6:17

A person who joins themselves to the Lord is one with him in spirit.

## 1 Corinthians 6:18

Run from sexual wrongdoing.

Other wrongs a person does are outside their body.

A person who does sexual wrong sins against their own body.

## 1 Corinthians 6:19

Your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit.

God gave you his Spirit, and his Spirit lives in you.

You do not belong only to yourself.

**Help with these words**

A temple is a place set apart for God. Paul uses this picture to explain why your body matters to God.

## 1 Corinthians 6:20

God paid a price for you.

So use your body to honour God.

**Pause and answer**

Why does Paul call a believer’s body a temple?
