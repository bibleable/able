# 1 Corinthians 7 — Paul answers questions about marriage

**About this chapter**

The believers in Corinth wrote to Paul about marriage.

Paul answers people who are married, unmarried or widowed.

## Paul answers the letter from Corinth

## 1 Corinthians 7:1

You wrote to me about several things.

Here is the first: it is good to live without sex.

![An open scroll, like the letter the believers sent Paul.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## 1 Corinthians 7:2

Yet sexual wrongdoing is common.

Each man should have his own wife.

Each woman should have her own husband.

## 1 Corinthians 7:3

A husband should care for his wife’s sexual needs.

A wife should care for her husband’s sexual needs too.

## 1 Corinthians 7:4

A wife shares authority over her body with her husband.

In the same way, a husband shares authority over his body with his wife.

## 1 Corinthians 7:5

Do not withhold sex from each other.

You may both agree to stop for a short time to pray.

Then come together again.

Otherwise Satan may tempt you when you find it hard to control your desires.

## 1 Corinthians 7:6

I am giving you permission here.

I am not giving you a command.

## 1 Corinthians 7:7

I wish everyone could live unmarried as I do.

But God gives different gifts to different people.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul say the husband and wife should agree on together?

## Advice for unmarried people and a command for married people

## 1 Corinthians 7:8

To unmarried people and to widows I say this.

It is good to stay unmarried, as I am.

## 1 Corinthians 7:9

But if you cannot control your sexual desires, you may marry.

It is better to marry than to live consumed by desire.

## 1 Corinthians 7:10

Now I have a command for married people.

This command comes from the Lord.

A wife must not separate from her husband.

## 1 Corinthians 7:11

If she does separate, she must stay unmarried or make peace and return to her husband.

A husband must not divorce his wife.

**Pause and answer**

Whose command does Paul pass on to married believers?

## When one partner does not believe

## 1 Corinthians 7:12

For the others I give my own instruction here.

I have no command from the Lord to pass on about this.

Suppose a believer’s wife does not believe in Christ.

If she wants to stay with him, he must not divorce her.

## 1 Corinthians 7:13

Suppose a believer’s husband does not believe in Christ.

If he wants to stay with her, she must not divorce him.

## 1 Corinthians 7:14

God sets the unbelieving husband apart through his believing wife.

He also sets the unbelieving wife apart through her believing husband.

Otherwise your children would be unclean.

As it is, they are holy.

**Help with these words**

Here “holy” means set apart for God. Paul is talking about the family’s relationship to God.

## 1 Corinthians 7:15

If the partner who does not believe leaves, let them go.

In that case the believer is not bound to keep the partner there.

God has called you to live in peace.

## 1 Corinthians 7:16

If you are a wife, how do you know whether you will help save your husband?

If you are a husband, how do you know whether you will help save your wife?

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul say if the partner who does not believe chooses to leave?

## Follow God in the life you have

## 1 Corinthians 7:17

Each of you should live the life the Lord has given you.

Follow God where he called you.

I give this instruction in all the churches.

## 1 Corinthians 7:18

Was a man circumcised when God called him?

He should not try to undo it.

Was he uncircumcised when God called him?

He does not need to receive circumcision.

**Help with these words**

Circumcision is a sign on a boy’s or man’s body that marks God’s agreement with the Jewish people.

## 1 Corinthians 7:19

Having or lacking that sign does not count here.

What counts is doing what God commands.

## 1 Corinthians 7:20

Each of you should stay in the situation you were in when God called you.

## 1 Corinthians 7:21

Were you a slave when God called you?

Do not let that trouble you.

If you have the chance to become free, take it.

## 1 Corinthians 7:22

If you were a slave, the Lord called you and made you free in him.

If you were free, you now belong to Christ as his servant.

## 1 Corinthians 7:23

God paid a price for you.

Do not become slaves of other people.

## 1 Corinthians 7:24

Brothers and sisters, stay with God in the situation you were in when he called you.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul say a slave can do if freedom becomes possible?

## Paul gives advice for a difficult time

## 1 Corinthians 7:25

You also asked about people who have never had sex.

I have no command from the Lord about them.

But the Lord has shown me mercy and made me trustworthy.

So I will give my judgment.

## 1 Corinthians 7:26

We are living through a difficult time.

That is why I think it is good to stay as you are.

## 1 Corinthians 7:27

If you are committed to a wife, do not try to leave.

If you are free of that commitment, do not look for a wife.

## 1 Corinthians 7:28

But if you marry, you have not sinned.

A young woman who marries has not sinned either.

Married people will have troubles in this life.

I want to spare you those troubles.

## 1 Corinthians 7:29

Brothers and sisters, time is short.

From now on, even married men should live as though they were unmarried.

## 1 Corinthians 7:30

Those who cry should live as though they were not crying.

Those who rejoice should live as though they were not rejoicing.

Those who buy things should live as though they owned nothing.

## 1 Corinthians 7:31

Use the things of this world without depending on them.

The world as we know it is passing away.

**Help with these words**

Paul has just told married people to care for each other. Here he reminds everyone that marriage, possessions and present feelings will not last for ever.

**Pause and answer**

Why does Paul tell people not to depend on what they own?

## Paul wants believers to serve the Lord freely

## 1 Corinthians 7:32

I want you to be free from worry.

An unmarried man can give his attention to the Lord’s work.

He thinks about how to please the Lord.

## 1 Corinthians 7:33

A married man also has everyday matters to look after.

He thinks about how to please his wife.

## 1 Corinthians 7:34

So his attention is divided.

An unmarried woman can attend to the Lord’s work.

She wants to be holy in body and spirit.

A married woman also attends to everyday matters.

She thinks about how to please her husband.

## 1 Corinthians 7:35

I say this for your good.

I do not want to take your choice away.

I want you to live well and serve the Lord with your full attention.

**Pause and answer**

Why does Paul give this advice?

## Decisions about marriage

## 1 Corinthians 7:36

Suppose a man thinks he is treating his fiancée unfairly.

She is past her early youth, and they ought to marry.

Let him do as he wishes.

They may marry without sinning.

**Help with these words**

A fiancée is a woman someone has promised to marry.

## 1 Corinthians 7:37

Another man may be sure he should remain unmarried.

No one is forcing his decision.

He can control his desires and has decided against marrying his fiancée.

He does well.

## 1 Corinthians 7:38

So the man who marries does well.

In my judgment, the man who stays unmarried does even better.

## 1 Corinthians 7:39

A wife is bound to her husband while he lives.

If her husband dies, she is free to marry whom she wishes.

The man she marries must belong to the Lord.

## 1 Corinthians 7:40

In my judgment she will be happier if she stays unmarried.

And I believe I too have God’s Spirit.

**Pause and answer**

What choice does Paul say a widow is free to make?
