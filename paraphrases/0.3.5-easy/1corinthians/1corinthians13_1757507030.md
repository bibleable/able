# 1 Corinthians 13 — Love is the greatest

**About this chapter**

Paul describes how to recognize love in everyday life.

He explains why love lasts.

## Even great gifts need love

## 1 Corinthians 13:1

Suppose I could speak the languages of people and angels.

Without love, I would sound like a noisy gong or cymbal.

**Help with these words**

Gongs and cymbals are metal instruments.

They make a loud sound when someone strikes them.

Paul compares words without love to that sound.

## 1 Corinthians 13:2

Suppose I could speak messages from God.

Suppose I understood every mystery and knew everything.

Suppose I trusted God enough to move mountains.

Without love, I would be nothing.

![A high mountain](../pictures/mountain.svg)

**Help with these words**

Moving a mountain pictures doing something that seems impossible.

## 1 Corinthians 13:3

I could give poor people everything I own.

I could even give up my body and boast about it.

Without love, I would gain nothing.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul need even if he gives everything away?

## What love looks like

## 1 Corinthians 13:4

Love is patient.

Love is kind.

Someone who loves does not envy others.

Someone who loves does not brag or act proud.

## 1 Corinthians 13:5

Love does not treat people rudely.

Love does not insist on its own way.

Love is slow to get angry.

Love keeps no list of wrongs people have done.

## 1 Corinthians 13:6

Love takes no joy in evil.

Love rejoices in the truth.

## 1 Corinthians 13:7

Love bears all things.

Love keeps trusting.

Love keeps hoping.

Love holds on through everything.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul say about love when someone does wrong?

## Love lasts

## 1 Corinthians 13:8

Love never comes to an end.

One day people will stop speaking messages from God.

Speaking in tongues will stop.

The knowledge we have now will pass away.

## 1 Corinthians 13:9

We know only part of the truth now.

We can tell only part when we speak God’s message.

## 1 Corinthians 13:10

One day what is complete will come.

Then what is only partly known will pass away.

## 1 Corinthians 13:11

When I was a child, I spoke like a child.

I thought things through like a child.

When I grew up, I left those ways behind.

## 1 Corinthians 13:12

Now we see like someone looking into a dim mirror.

Then we will see face to face.

Now I know only in part.

Then I will know fully, as God already knows me fully.

**Help with these words**

A dim mirror gives a picture that is hard to see.

Paul uses it to explain how little we understand now.

## 1 Corinthians 13:13

Faith, hope, and love remain.

The greatest of these is love.

**Pause and answer**

Which of these three does Paul call the greatest?
