# 1 Corinthians 1 — Paul asks the believers in Corinth to stay together

**About this chapter**

People in Corinth are quarrelling about which leader they follow.

Paul reminds them who died on the cross for them.

## A letter to the believers in Corinth

## 1 Corinthians 1:1

This letter comes from Paul and our brother Sosthenes.

God chose me, Paul, to be an apostle of Christ Jesus.

**Help with these words**

An apostle is someone Jesus sends to speak for him.

## 1 Corinthians 1:2

We write to God’s church in Corinth.

Christ Jesus has set you apart for God.

God calls you to live as his holy people.

You belong with everyone everywhere who calls on Jesus Christ.

He is their Lord and ours.

## 1 Corinthians 1:3

May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you kindness and peace.

**Pause and answer**

Who else shares the same Lord as the believers in Corinth?

## Paul thanks God

## 1 Corinthians 1:4

I always thank my God for you.

Through Christ Jesus, God has shown you great kindness.

## 1 Corinthians 1:5

Because you belong to Christ, God has given you so much.

He has helped you both to speak and to understand.

## 1 Corinthians 1:6

What has happened among you shows that our message about Christ is true.

## 1 Corinthians 1:7

So you have every gift you need from God’s Spirit.

You wait eagerly for the day our Lord Jesus Christ appears.

## 1 Corinthians 1:8

He will keep you strong to the end.

Then no one can find fault with you on the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.

## 1 Corinthians 1:9

You can trust God to keep his word.

He called you to share life with his Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul say God can do for the believers as they wait?

## Believers are taking sides

## 1 Corinthians 1:10

Brothers and sisters, I appeal to you in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.

Come to agreement with each other.

Do not split into groups that oppose each other.

Stand together in what you think and believe.

## 1 Corinthians 1:11

Some people from Chloe’s household have told me something troubling.

My brothers and sisters, you are quarrelling.

## 1 Corinthians 1:12

One of you says, “I follow Paul.”

Another says, “I follow Apollos.”

Another says, “I follow Cephas.”

Still another says, “I follow Christ.”

**Help with these words**

Cephas is another name for Peter.

## 1 Corinthians 1:13

Can anyone divide Christ into pieces?

Did I, Paul, die on a cross for you?

Did anyone baptise you in my name?

**Pause and answer**

What were people in Corinth saying as they took sides?

## Paul explains his work

## 1 Corinthians 1:14

I thank God that I baptised only Crispus and Gaius among you.

## 1 Corinthians 1:15

So no one can say you received baptism in my name.

## 1 Corinthians 1:16

I also baptised the people in Stephanas’s household.

Beyond that, I cannot remember baptising anyone else.

**Help with these words**

Baptism uses water to show that someone belongs to Jesus.

## 1 Corinthians 1:17

Christ sent me to tell the good news rather than to baptise.

I do not depend on clever words.

I do not want my words to take away the power of Christ’s cross.

**Help with these words**

When Paul says “Christ’s cross,” he means what Jesus did for people by dying on the cross.

**Pause and answer**

What work did Christ send Paul to do?

## The message about the cross

## 1 Corinthians 1:18

People on the way to destruction think the message about the cross is foolish.

To us whom God is saving, this message shows God’s power.

## 1 Corinthians 1:19

The Scriptures tell us what God says.

“I will bring the wisdom of wise people to nothing.

I will overturn the clever ideas of those who understand so much.”

![An open scroll, like the writings Paul quotes.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## 1 Corinthians 1:20

What can the wise person say now?

What about the teacher of the law?

What about the person who debates the ideas of our time?

God has shown how foolish the world’s wisdom can be.

## 1 Corinthians 1:21

God in his wisdom let people see the limits of their own wisdom.

It could not bring them to know him.

God chose to save those who believe the message people call foolish.

## 1 Corinthians 1:22

Jewish people ask to see signs of God’s power.

Greeks search for wisdom.

## 1 Corinthians 1:23

We tell people that Christ died on a cross.

This message trips up Jewish listeners.

Gentile listeners think it is foolish.

**Help with these words**

Gentiles are people who are not Jewish. When Paul says the message trips people up, he means they find it hard to accept.

## 1 Corinthians 1:24

But God has called people from both groups, Jews and Greeks.

For them, Christ is God’s power and God’s wisdom.

## 1 Corinthians 1:25

What people call foolish in God is wiser than human wisdom.

What they call weak in God is stronger than human strength.

**Pause and answer**

What does the message about the cross show those whom God is saving?

## Remember whom God chose

## 1 Corinthians 1:26

Brothers and sisters, remember when God called you.

Few of you were people the world called wise.

Few had great power.

Few came from important families.

## 1 Corinthians 1:27

God chose what the world calls foolish to shame the wise.

God chose what the world calls weak to shame the strong.

## 1 Corinthians 1:28

God chose people and things the world looked down on.

He chose what others counted as nothing.

He did this to bring to nothing what the world counted as important.

## 1 Corinthians 1:29

So no one can boast before God.

## 1 Corinthians 1:30

God is the reason you belong to Christ Jesus.

Christ is God’s wisdom for us.

Christ makes us right with God.

He sets us apart for God and sets us free from sin.

## 1 Corinthians 1:31

So the Scriptures say, “If you boast, boast about the Lord.”

**Pause and answer**

Whom does Paul tell the believers to boast about?
