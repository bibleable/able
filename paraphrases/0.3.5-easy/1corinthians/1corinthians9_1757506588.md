# 1 Corinthians 9 — Paul shares the good news free of charge

**About this chapter**

Paul writes to the believers in Corinth.

He tells them why he chose to teach them about Jesus without asking for pay.

## Paul answers questions about his work

## 1 Corinthians 9:1

I am free.

I am an apostle.

I have seen Jesus our Lord.

You came to believe in the Lord through my work.

**Help with these words**

An apostle is someone Jesus sent to speak for him.

## 1 Corinthians 9:2

Other people may doubt that I am an apostle.

But you know I am one.

Your life with the Lord shows that he sent me.

## 1 Corinthians 9:3

Here is my answer to the people who question my work.

## 1 Corinthians 9:4

We have the right to receive food and drink for our work.

## 1 Corinthians 9:5

We have the right to travel with a wife who believes in Jesus.

The other apostles do this.

So do the brothers of the Lord and Cephas, also called Peter.

## 1 Corinthians 9:6

Are Barnabas and I the only ones who must earn our living by doing other work?

**Pause and answer**

How do the believers in Corinth know that the Lord sent Paul?

## Workers share in what they grow

## 1 Corinthians 9:7

A soldier does not have to pay to serve in the army.

A person who plants grapevines gets to eat some grapes.

A person who cares for a flock gets to drink some of its milk.

![Grapes growing on a vine](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

## 1 Corinthians 9:8

These examples come from everyday life.

God’s law says the same thing.

## 1 Corinthians 9:9

The law God gave Moses says, “Let an ox eat while it walks on the grain.”

Do you think God had only oxen in mind?

**Help with these words**

An ox is a strong farm animal.

Farmers used oxen to walk on cut stalks to loosen the grain.

A cover over an ox’s mouth would stop it from eating.

## 1 Corinthians 9:10

God had us in mind too.

One worker prepares the ground for planting.

Another worker separates the grain from the stalks.

Both workers can expect a share of the crop.

## 1 Corinthians 9:11

We planted a seed among you when we taught you about God.

Is it too much to ask you to share the things we need to live?

## 1 Corinthians 9:12

Other teachers have a right to your help.

We have even more reason to ask for it.

But we chose to go without that help.

We will put up with hardship so people can hear the good news about Christ.

**Pause and answer**

What can someone who plants grapevines expect to eat?

## Why Paul preaches without pay

## 1 Corinthians 9:13

People who work in the temple eat some of the food people bring there.

Those who serve at the altar receive a share of the gifts people lay on it.

## 1 Corinthians 9:14

In the same way, the Lord said that people who preach the good news should earn their living from that work.

## 1 Corinthians 9:15

I have chosen to go without this help.

I am not writing to ask you to start paying me.

I would rather die than lose the one thing I can take pride in.

## 1 Corinthians 9:16

Telling the good news gives me no reason to boast.

It is work I must do.

How terrible it would be for me if I kept the good news to myself!

## 1 Corinthians 9:17

If I chose this work for myself, I could expect a reward.

Even if I had no choice, God has still trusted me to do this work.

## 1 Corinthians 9:18

So what reward do I get?

I get to give people the good news free of charge.

I can do that because I choose to give up my right to pay.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul call his reward?

## Paul meets people where they are

## 1 Corinthians 9:19

I am free, and no one owns me.

Yet I choose to serve everyone like a slave.

I want to help as many people as I can come to trust Christ.

## 1 Corinthians 9:20

When I am with Jewish people, I follow their customs to help them trust Christ.

When I am with people who live by the law of Moses, I live as they do.

I do this to win them over even though that law does not rule me.

## 1 Corinthians 9:21

When I am with people who live without the law of Moses, I live as they do to help them trust Christ.

I still obey God, because I live by the law of Christ.

## 1 Corinthians 9:22

When I am with people who are weak, I become weak like them to win them over.

I change how I live among different people.

I use every way I can so that some may be saved.

## 1 Corinthians 9:23

I do all this for the good news.

I want to share in the good it brings.

**Pause and answer**

Why does Paul change how he lives among different people?

## Run to win the prize

## 1 Corinthians 9:24

Think of a race.

Every runner runs, but only one wins the prize.

Run like someone who means to win.

## 1 Corinthians 9:25

Athletes train hard and control what they do.

They do it to win a crown of leaves that will dry up.

We do it for a crown that will last forever.

## 1 Corinthians 9:26

So when I run, I keep my goal in sight.

When I box, I aim my punches instead of hitting empty air.

## 1 Corinthians 9:27

I train my body and make it obey me.

After telling others the good news, I want to be sure I can still receive the prize myself.

**Pause and answer**

How long will the crown Paul runs for last?
