# 1 Corinthians 3 — Paul plants, Apollos waters, and God gives growth

**About this chapter**

People in Corinth have been taking sides over their teachers.

Paul describes how two teachers can work together for God.

## Paul explains why he began with simple teaching

## 1 Corinthians 3:1

Brothers and sisters, I could not speak to you as people following God’s Spirit.

You were acting like people who follow the world’s ways.

You were like babies in your life with Christ.

## 1 Corinthians 3:2

I gave you milk because you could not yet take solid food.

Even now you are not ready.

**Help with these words**

Paul is talking about what he taught. Milk stands for the first things people learn about Christ. Solid food stands for teaching they can take in as their faith grows.

## 1 Corinthians 3:3

You still act in the world’s way.

You are jealous of each other.

You quarrel with each other.

Isn’t that how people act when they follow only their own ways?

## 1 Corinthians 3:4

One of you says, “I follow Paul.”

Another says, “I follow Apollos.”

When you take sides like this, you are following human ways.

**Pause and answer**

What were the believers doing that made Paul say they still needed milk?

## Two workers care for one field

## 1 Corinthians 3:5

What is Apollos’s job?

What is mine?

We are servants who helped you come to faith.

The Lord gave each of us work to do.

## 1 Corinthians 3:6

I planted the seed.

Apollos watered it.

God made it grow.

![A stalk of grain that has grown from a seed.](../pictures/grain.svg)

**Help with these words**

Paul first told the people in Corinth about Christ. Then Apollos helped them learn more. Paul compares their work to caring for a crop.

## 1 Corinthians 3:7

The one who plants cannot make the seed grow.

Neither can the one who waters it.

God is the one who gives growth.

## 1 Corinthians 3:8

The two workers share one purpose.

God will reward each one for their own work.

## 1 Corinthians 3:9

Apollos and I work together for God.

You are God’s field.

You are also God’s building.

**Pause and answer**

Who made the seed grow in Paul’s picture?

## Builders must take care how they build

## 1 Corinthians 3:10

God gave me the gift of working like a skilled builder.

I laid the foundation.

Now someone else is building on it.

Each builder must take care how they build.

## 1 Corinthians 3:11

The foundation is already there.

That foundation is Jesus Christ.

No one can lay a different one.

**Help with these words**

A foundation is the base that holds a building up. Paul means that everything the church does depends on Jesus.

## 1 Corinthians 3:12

A builder might use gold, silver or precious stones.

Another might use wood, hay or straw.

## 1 Corinthians 3:13

The day God judges will show what each person has built.

Fire will test the quality of each person’s work.

## 1 Corinthians 3:14

If the work lasts, the builder will receive a reward.

## 1 Corinthians 3:15

If the work burns up, the builder will suffer loss.

God will save the builder, like someone escaping through flames.

**Help with these words**

Paul pictures a fire testing a building. He is asking those who teach the church to think about whether their work will last.

**Pause and answer**

Who is the foundation of the church?

## Together you are God’s temple

## 1 Corinthians 3:16

Do you know that together you are God’s temple?

God’s Spirit lives among you.

## 1 Corinthians 3:17

If anyone destroys God’s temple, God will destroy that person.

God’s temple is holy.

Together you are that temple.

**Help with these words**

A temple is a place set apart for worship. Here Paul calls the people who belong to God his temple.

**Pause and answer**

Where does Paul say God’s Spirit lives?

## You belong to Christ

## 1 Corinthians 3:18

Do not fool yourselves.

Perhaps you think you are wise by the world’s standards.

Be willing to look foolish so you can become truly wise.

## 1 Corinthians 3:19

God sees how foolish the world’s wisdom is.

The Scriptures say, “God catches clever people in their own tricks.”

## 1 Corinthians 3:20

The Scriptures also say, “The Lord knows that the plans of the wise come to nothing.”

## 1 Corinthians 3:21

So stop boasting about human leaders.

Everything is yours.

## 1 Corinthians 3:22

Paul, Apollos and Cephas all belong to you.

So do the world, life and death.

The present and the future are yours too.

**Help with these words**

Cephas is another name for Peter. Paul reminds the believers that all these leaders serve them.

## 1 Corinthians 3:23

You belong to Christ.

And Christ belongs to God.

**Pause and answer**

To whom do all the believers belong?
