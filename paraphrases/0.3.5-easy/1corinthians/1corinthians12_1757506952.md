# 1 Corinthians 12 — One Spirit gives many gifts

**About this chapter**

Paul asks the believers to think about a body.

An eye and an ear do different jobs.

Each believer also has a part to play.

## How to recognize the Spirit’s work

## 1 Corinthians 12:1

Brothers and sisters, I want you to understand spiritual gifts.

**Help with these words**

A spiritual gift is an ability God’s Spirit gives to help others.

## 1 Corinthians 12:2

Remember when you worshiped idols.

People led you away to worship things that could not speak.

## 1 Corinthians 12:3

No one speaking by God’s Spirit says, “May Jesus be cursed.”

Only the Holy Spirit enables someone to say, “Jesus is Lord.”

**Pause and answer**

Who helps people say that Jesus is Lord?

## One Spirit gives different gifts

## 1 Corinthians 12:4

The gifts are different.

The same Spirit gives them.

## 1 Corinthians 12:5

People serve in different ways.

They all serve the same Lord.

## 1 Corinthians 12:6

People do different kinds of work.

The same God does all this work through them all.

## 1 Corinthians 12:7

The Spirit shows his work through each person.

He does this to help everyone.

## 1 Corinthians 12:8

The Spirit gives one person wise words to share.

The same Spirit helps another share knowledge.

## 1 Corinthians 12:9

The same Spirit gives someone faith.

That one Spirit gives someone else gifts for healing people.

## 1 Corinthians 12:10

The Spirit helps one person do miracles.

He helps another speak a message from God.

He helps another tell which spirit a message comes from.

He helps another speak in different tongues.

He helps someone else explain what those words mean.

**Help with these words**

Speaking in tongues means speaking words the Spirit gives.

A listener may need someone to explain the words.

## 1 Corinthians 12:11

One Spirit does all these things.

The Spirit chooses which gift to give each person.

**Pause and answer**

Who should benefit from the gifts the Spirit gives?

## One body has many parts

## 1 Corinthians 12:12

Think about your body.

It has many parts.

Together the parts make one body.

It is like that with Christ.

## 1 Corinthians 12:13

Some of us are Jews and some are Greeks.

Some are slaves and some are free.

Through baptism, one Spirit joined us into one body.

We all received the same Spirit to drink.

**Help with these words**

Paul uses drinking as a picture of receiving God’s Spirit.

The body in this picture means all who belong to Christ.

## 1 Corinthians 12:14

A body needs many different parts.

## 1 Corinthians 12:15

Suppose a foot said, “I am not a hand.

So I do not belong to the body.”

The foot would still belong.

## 1 Corinthians 12:16

Suppose an ear said, “I am not an eye.

So I do not belong to the body.”

The ear would still belong.

## 1 Corinthians 12:17

If the whole body were an eye, how could it hear?

If the whole body were an ear, how could it smell?

![An eye sees. An ear hears.](../pictures/eye-and-ear.svg)

## 1 Corinthians 12:18

God put each part of the body where he wanted it.

## 1 Corinthians 12:19

If every part were the same, where would the body be?

## 1 Corinthians 12:20

There are many parts.

Together they form one body.

**Pause and answer**

In Paul’s example, why does the body need an ear?

## Every part matters

## 1 Corinthians 12:21

The eye cannot tell the hand, “I do not need you.”

The head cannot tell the feet, “I do not need you.”

## 1 Corinthians 12:22

Some parts seem weaker.

Yet the body needs those parts.

## 1 Corinthians 12:23

We give extra care to parts we think deserve less honor.

We take special care to cover our private parts.

## 1 Corinthians 12:24

Other parts need no special covering.

God put the body together.

He gave greater honor to the parts that lacked honor.

## 1 Corinthians 12:25

God wanted the body to stay together.

He wanted each part to care for the others.

## 1 Corinthians 12:26

When one part hurts, every part hurts with it.

When one part receives honor, every part shares its joy.

## 1 Corinthians 12:27

Together you are Christ’s body.

Each of you belongs to that body.

**Pause and answer**

What happens to the whole body when one part hurts?

## Different ways to serve

## 1 Corinthians 12:28

God gave people work to do in the church.

First come apostles, then prophets, then teachers.

Others do miracles or have gifts for healing people.

Some help people or organize the work.

Others speak in different tongues.

**Help with these words**

An apostle is someone Jesus sends to speak for him.

A prophet speaks a message from God.

## 1 Corinthians 12:29

Is everyone an apostle?

Is everyone a prophet or a teacher?

Does everyone do miracles?

## 1 Corinthians 12:30

Does everyone have gifts for healing people?

Does everyone speak in tongues?

Can everyone explain what those words mean?

## 1 Corinthians 12:31

Be eager for the greater gifts.

Now I will show you the best way of all.

**Pause and answer**

Name one way a person can serve the church.
