# 1 Corinthians 10 — Remember Israel and care for others

**About this chapter**

Paul reminds the believers in Corinth about Israel’s journey.

Then Paul helps them decide what to do at meals.

## God cared for Israel in the desert

## 1 Corinthians 10:1

Brothers and sisters, remember our ancestors who left Egypt.

God’s cloud guided them all.

They all crossed the sea.

## 1 Corinthians 10:2

In the cloud and sea, they all received a kind of baptism.

It joined them together as people who followed Moses.

## 1 Corinthians 10:3

They all ate the same food God gave them.

## 1 Corinthians 10:4

They all drank the same water God gave them.

They drank from a spiritual rock that went with them.

That rock was Christ.

**Help with these words**

Paul calls Christ a rock.

Just as the people received water from a rock, they received care from Christ.

## 1 Corinthians 10:5

Yet most of them displeased God.

They died there in the desert.

**Pause and answer**

Who gave the people food and water?

## Learn from what happened to Israel

## 1 Corinthians 10:6

Their story warns us.

They wanted evil things.

We must guard against wanting those things too.

## 1 Corinthians 10:7

Some of them worshiped idols.

Keep away from idol worship.

Scripture tells us what the people did.

They sat down to eat and drink.

Then they got up for a wild celebration.

**Help with these words**

An idol is an object people worship as a god.

## 1 Corinthians 10:8

Some of them sinned in the way they had sex.

We must keep away from sexual sin.

In one day, 23,000 of them died.

## 1 Corinthians 10:9

Some of them put Christ to the test.

Snakes killed those people.

We must never put Christ to the test.

## 1 Corinthians 10:10

Some of them complained against God.

The angel who brought death killed them.

Take care with your own words.

## 1 Corinthians 10:11

Their story is an example for us.

People wrote it down to warn us.

We live in the time those earlier ages pointed toward.

**Pause and answer**

Why did people write down Israel’s story?

## God helps you when you want to do wrong

## 1 Corinthians 10:12

You may think you stand firm.

Be careful, or you too may fall into sin.

## 1 Corinthians 10:13

Other people face the same temptations you face.

You can trust God.

God sets a limit on what you must bear.

When temptation comes, God gives you a way through.

With God’s help, you can resist it.

**Help with these words**

A temptation is a pull to do something wrong.

**Pause and answer**

Who helps you resist temptation?

## A shared cup and a shared loaf

## 1 Corinthians 10:14

So, dear friends, keep far away from idol worship.

## 1 Corinthians 10:15

You can think this through.

Decide for yourselves whether my words are true.

## 1 Corinthians 10:16

We thank God for the cup we share.

When we drink, we share in Christ’s blood.

When we break the bread, we share in Christ’s body.

![A drinking cup with a handle](../pictures/cup.svg)

## 1 Corinthians 10:17

There are many of us.

Yet we all eat from one loaf.

So we are one body together.

**Help with these words**

Here, “one body” means the believers belong together in Christ.

## 1 Corinthians 10:18

Think about the people of Israel.

They bring gifts to God at the altar.

Those who eat from these gifts share in that worship.

**Pause and answer**

How many loaves does Paul describe the believers sharing?

## Give your worship to the Lord

## 1 Corinthians 10:19

Does this mean an idol has power?

Does giving food to an idol make the food special?

## 1 Corinthians 10:20

No.

People who worship idols offer their gifts to demons.

They do not offer those gifts to God.

I want you to keep away from worship involving demons.

**Help with these words**

Demons are evil spirits.

## 1 Corinthians 10:21

You cannot share both the Lord’s cup and the demons’ cup.

You cannot share meals at both tables.

## 1 Corinthians 10:22

Do we want to make the Lord jealous?

Are we stronger than the Lord?

**Pause and answer**

Whose table does Paul tell believers to keep away from?

## When someone offers you a meal

## 1 Corinthians 10:23

You say, “I am free to do anything.”

But some things do no good.

Some things do nothing to help people grow.

## 1 Corinthians 10:24

Think about what will help the other person too.

## 1 Corinthians 10:25

You may eat any meat sold at the market.

Your conscience need not trouble you with questions about it.

**Help with these words**

Your conscience is your sense of right and wrong.

## 1 Corinthians 10:26

Scripture tells us why.

The earth and everything in it belong to the Lord.

## 1 Corinthians 10:27

Someone who does not believe in Christ may invite you to eat.

If you want to go, eat what they serve.

You can eat with a clear conscience.

## 1 Corinthians 10:28

But someone may tell you, “People offered this food to an idol.”

Then leave that food alone for that person’s sake.

## 1 Corinthians 10:29

I mean the other person’s conscience.

Your own conscience is clear.

Why should someone else’s conscience decide what I am free to do?

## 1 Corinthians 10:30

If I thank God for my food, why blame me for eating it?

**Pause and answer**

What should you do if someone says the food was offered to an idol?

## Honor God in what you do

## 1 Corinthians 10:31

When you eat or drink, honor God.

Honor God in everything you do.

## 1 Corinthians 10:32

Take care that your actions do not lead others into sin.

Think of Jewish people, Greek people, and God’s church.

## 1 Corinthians 10:33

I too try to please everyone in what I do.

I look for what will help many people.

Their good matters more to me than my own gain.

I want God to save them.

**Pause and answer**

Whose good does Paul look for when he makes a choice?
