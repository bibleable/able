# 1 Corinthians 4 — Paul asks the believers to follow his example

**About this chapter**

Paul explains why he leaves judgment to the Lord.

He tells his friends in Corinth why he has sent Timothy.

## The Lord judges his servants

## 1 Corinthians 4:1

Think of us as servants of Christ.

God has trusted us with the truths he once kept hidden.

## 1 Corinthians 4:2

When someone trusts you with a task, you must be faithful.

You must do the work they gave you.

## 1 Corinthians 4:3

Your judgment of me matters little to me.

The same is true of a human court’s judgment.

I do not even pass judgment on myself.

## 1 Corinthians 4:4

My conscience is clear.

Yet that alone does not prove me innocent.

The Lord is the one who judges me.

## 1 Corinthians 4:5

So wait for the Lord to come before you pass judgment.

He will uncover what people have hidden in the dark.

He will show why people did what they did.

Then each person will receive their praise from God.

**Pause and answer**

Who knows why a person did something that others could see only from the outside?

## Remember what you have received

## 1 Corinthians 4:6

Brothers and sisters, I have used Apollos and myself as examples for you.

Learn from us to stay within what the Scriptures say.

Then you will stop boasting that one leader is better than another.

![An open scroll, like the Scriptures Paul asks the believers to follow.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## 1 Corinthians 4:7

Who made you better than anyone else?

What do you have that you did not receive?

If it was a gift, why boast as though you earned it?

## 1 Corinthians 4:8

You act as if you already have everything you want!

You are already rich!

You have even become kings without us!

I wish you really were kings.

Then we could rule with you.

**Help with these words**

Paul calls them kings to challenge their pride. He wants them to notice how highly they think of themselves.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul ask people to remember before they boast?

## What the apostles live through

## 1 Corinthians 4:9

It seems God has put us apostles last in a parade.

We are like prisoners who must face death while others watch.

The whole world watches us, both angels and people.

## 1 Corinthians 4:10

People think we are fools for Christ.

You think you are wise in Christ!

We are weak, and you are strong!

People honour you and look down on us.

## 1 Corinthians 4:11

Even now we go hungry and thirsty.

We have little to wear.

People beat us.

We have no home.

## 1 Corinthians 4:12

We work hard with our own hands.

When people insult us, we ask God to bless them.

When people hurt us for our faith, we keep going.

## 1 Corinthians 4:13

When people tell lies about us, we answer gently.

Even now people treat us like rubbish they want to throw away.

**Pause and answer**

How do the apostles answer people who tell lies about them?

## Paul sends Timothy to remind them how he lives

## 1 Corinthians 4:14

I write because I love you like my own children.

I want to warn you without putting you to shame.

## 1 Corinthians 4:15

Even if ten thousand people guided you in following Christ, you would have few fathers.

I became your father in Christ Jesus when I brought you the good news.

**Help with these words**

Paul calls himself their father because he helped them begin their life of faith.

## 1 Corinthians 4:16

So I urge you to follow my example.

## 1 Corinthians 4:17

That is why I have sent Timothy to you.

I love him like a son.

He faithfully follows the Lord.

Timothy will remind you how I live as a follower of Jesus Christ.

That is what I teach in every church.

**Pause and answer**

What will Timothy remind the believers about?

## Paul hopes to visit

## 1 Corinthians 4:18

Some of you have become proud.

You act as though I will never visit you.

## 1 Corinthians 4:19

If the Lord wants me to, I will come soon.

Then I will hear what these proud people say.

I will also find out what power they really have.

## 1 Corinthians 4:20

God’s kingdom means God rules with power.

Words alone cannot show that power.

## 1 Corinthians 4:21

How would you like me to come?

Should I bring a stick for discipline?

Or should I come with love and gentleness?

**Help with these words**

The stick pictures a stern correction. Paul asks the believers to consider what kind of visit their behaviour calls for.

**Pause and answer**

How does Paul describe the gentler visit he could make?
