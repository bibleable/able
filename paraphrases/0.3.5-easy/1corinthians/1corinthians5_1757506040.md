# 1 Corinthians 5 — Paul asks the church to face wrongdoing

**About this chapter**

Paul writes about a man in the church at Corinth.

He asks the believers to take seriously how one person’s actions affect others.

## Paul hears a troubling report

## 1 Corinthians 5:1

People have told me about sexual wrongdoing among you.

A man is having sex with his father’s wife.

Even people who worship other gods do not accept this.

## 1 Corinthians 5:2

Yet you are proud!

This should have made you grieve.

You should have removed this man from your church community.

## 1 Corinthians 5:3

I am far away, but I am with you in spirit.

I have already judged the man who did this, just as I would if I were there.

**Help with these words**

Paul cannot attend the meeting. He tells the church that he shares in its decision even from far away.

## 1 Corinthians 5:4

Gather together in the name of our Lord Jesus.

I will be with you in spirit.

The Lord Jesus will be with you in power.

## 1 Corinthians 5:5

When you meet, hand this man over to Satan for the destruction of his flesh.

My hope is that the Lord will save his spirit on the day the Lord comes.

**Help with these words**

The action Paul asks the church to take is to remove the man from their community. His hope for the man reaches beyond that decision.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul hope will happen to the man on the Lord’s day?

## A little yeast affects all the dough

## 1 Corinthians 5:6

You have no reason to boast.

Think about what happens when you make bread.

Even a little yeast works through all the dough.

![A small lump of dough grows larger as the yeast works through it.](../pictures/yeast-and-dough.svg)

## 1 Corinthians 5:7

Clear out the old yeast.

Be like fresh dough made without yeast.

That is what you really are.

Christ, our Passover lamb, gave his life as a sacrifice.

**Help with these words**

At Passover, Jewish families remembered how God led their people out of Egypt. They ate bread made without yeast. Paul calls Christ their Passover lamb because Christ died for them.

## 1 Corinthians 5:8

So let us keep the feast.

Leave behind the old yeast of wishing people harm and doing evil.

Let us be like bread made without yeast.

Let us be sincere and truthful.

**Help with these words**

Paul compares wrongdoing to yeast because a little can affect the whole church.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul want to take the place of wishing people harm?

## Paul explains whom he meant

## 1 Corinthians 5:9

In my earlier letter, I told you to keep away from people who do sexual wrong.

## 1 Corinthians 5:10

I did not mean everyone outside the church who acts that way.

Think of people who always want more, cheat others or worship idols.

To avoid them all, you would have to leave this world!

## 1 Corinthians 5:11

I mean someone who calls himself a fellow believer yet lives this way.

He may do sexual wrong or always want more for himself.

He may worship idols, abuse people with words, get drunk or cheat others.

Do not keep company with such a person.

Do not even share a meal with him.

**Help with these words**

An idol is an object people worship as a god. To cheat someone means to trick them out of what belongs to them.

## 1 Corinthians 5:12

Is it my place to judge people outside the church?

You are responsible for judging what happens inside the church.

## 1 Corinthians 5:13

God will judge those outside.

The Scriptures say, “Remove the person who does evil from among you.”

**Pause and answer**

Whom does Paul say God will judge?
