# 1 Corinthians 15 — God raised Christ from death

**About this chapter**

Some believers in Corinth say the dead never rise.

Paul reminds them who saw Jesus alive.

Then he explains what this means for them.

## Remember the good news

## 1 Corinthians 15:1

Brothers and sisters, remember the good news I told you.

You welcomed that news.

You still stand firm in it.

## 1 Corinthians 15:2

God saves you through this good news.

Hold firmly to the message I gave you.

Otherwise your trust was for nothing.

## 1 Corinthians 15:3

I passed on the most important message I had received.

Christ died for our sins, just as Scripture said.

## 1 Corinthians 15:4

People buried Christ.

On the third day, God raised Christ from death.

Scripture said this would happen.

**Help with these words**

Scripture means the sacred writings.

## 1 Corinthians 15:5

Christ appeared to Peter, also called Cephas.

Then he appeared to the group called the Twelve.

## 1 Corinthians 15:6

Next, more than five hundred believers saw Christ at once.

Most are still alive as I write.

Some have died.

## 1 Corinthians 15:7

Then Christ appeared to James.

After that, all the apostles saw Christ.

**Pause and answer**

How many people saw Christ together on one occasion?

## Christ appeared to Paul

## 1 Corinthians 15:8

Last of all, Christ appeared to me too.

I was like a child born at the wrong time.

**Help with these words**

Paul compares himself to a baby whose birth came at an unexpected time.

The other apostles saw the risen Christ before Paul did.

## 1 Corinthians 15:9

I am the least of the apostles.

I attacked God’s church.

So I do not deserve the name “apostle.”

## 1 Corinthians 15:10

Yet God’s grace made me who I am.

God’s kindness to me bore fruit.

I worked harder than the other apostles.

It was God’s grace at work in me.

**Help with these words**

Grace means kindness God gives freely.

Here, bearing fruit means producing a good result.

## 1 Corinthians 15:11

The other apostles and I preach the same message.

That is the message you believed.

**Pause and answer**

Who gave Paul the grace to do his work?

## What if the dead never rise?

## 1 Corinthians 15:12

We tell you that God raised Christ from death.

So why do some of you say the dead never rise?

**Help with these words**

Resurrection means rising from death to life.

## 1 Corinthians 15:13

If the dead never rise, then Christ never rose either.

## 1 Corinthians 15:14

If Christ never rose, our message is empty.

Your trust in Christ is empty too.

## 1 Corinthians 15:15

That would make us false witnesses about God.

We told you that God raised Christ.

If the dead never rise, our words about God are false.

## 1 Corinthians 15:16

Think it through again.

If the dead never rise, then Christ never rose either.

## 1 Corinthians 15:17

If Christ never rose, trusting him does you no good.

You are still in your sins.

## 1 Corinthians 15:18

Then those who trusted Christ and died are lost too.

## 1 Corinthians 15:19

Suppose we could hope in Christ only during this life.

Then we deserve pity more than anyone else.

**Pause and answer**

What would it mean for Paul’s message if Christ never rose?

## Christ rose first

## 1 Corinthians 15:20

But God really did raise Christ from death.

Christ is like the first part of a harvest.

Others who have died will follow him.

## 1 Corinthians 15:21

Death came through one man.

Through one man the dead also rise.

## 1 Corinthians 15:22

Because we belong to Adam’s family, we all die.

So also, in Christ, all will receive life.

## 1 Corinthians 15:23

Each comes in turn.

Christ rose first.

When Christ comes back, those who belong to him will rise.

## 1 Corinthians 15:24

Then the end will come.

Christ will bring every other rule and power to an end.

Then he will hand the kingdom to God the Father.

## 1 Corinthians 15:25

Christ must rule until every enemy lies under his feet.

**Help with these words**

Enemies under a king’s feet pictures a king defeating them.

## 1 Corinthians 15:26

Death is the last enemy Christ will destroy.

**Pause and answer**

Which enemy will Christ destroy last?

## God rules over everything

## 1 Corinthians 15:27

Scripture says God put everything under Christ’s feet.

God himself is the one who did this.

So “everything” here does not include God himself.

## 1 Corinthians 15:28

God will put all things under his Son’s rule.

Then the Son himself will accept God’s rule.

In this way God will be all in all.

**Pause and answer**

Who gave Christ authority over everything?

## Why face danger?

## 1 Corinthians 15:29

Think of the people who receive baptism for the dead.

Why do that if the dead never rise?

## 1 Corinthians 15:30

And why do we face danger every hour?

## 1 Corinthians 15:31

Brothers and sisters, I face death every day.

That is as sure as the pride I take in you because of Christ Jesus our Lord.

## 1 Corinthians 15:32

I fought wild beasts in Ephesus.

What would I gain if I did that for this life alone?

If the dead never rise, we might as well say, “Let’s eat and drink.

Tomorrow we die.”

## 1 Corinthians 15:33

Do not let anyone fool you.

Bad company can spoil good habits.

## 1 Corinthians 15:34

Come back to your senses.

Stop sinning.

Some of you do not know God.

I say this to make you feel ashamed.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul say bad company can do?

## What can a seed show us?

## 1 Corinthians 15:35

Someone may ask, “How will the dead rise?”

“What kind of body will they have?”

## 1 Corinthians 15:36

How foolish to miss this!

A seed you plant must die before new life grows.

## 1 Corinthians 15:37

Think of planting wheat or another crop.

You plant a bare seed.

The plant that will grow looks different.

## 1 Corinthians 15:38

God gives each seed the form he chose for it.

Each kind of seed grows into its own kind of plant.

![A stalk of wheat grown from a seed](../pictures/grain.svg)

## 1 Corinthians 15:39

Living creatures have different kinds of flesh too.

Think of people, animals, birds, and fish.

## 1 Corinthians 15:40

There are bodies in the sky and bodies on earth.

Each has its own kind of beauty.

## 1 Corinthians 15:41

The sun shines in its own way.

The moon and stars shine in theirs.

Even the stars differ in brightness.

**Pause and answer**

What goes into the soil when someone plants wheat?

## The body God raises

## 1 Corinthians 15:42

It is like that when God raises the dead.

The body we bury can decay.

God raises a body that will never decay.

## 1 Corinthians 15:43

We bury the body in dishonor and weakness.

God raises it in glory and strength.

## 1 Corinthians 15:44

We bury a natural body.

God raises a spiritual body.

Both kinds of body are real.

## 1 Corinthians 15:45

Scripture says the first man, Adam, became a living person.

The last Adam became a spirit who gives life.

**Help with these words**

Paul calls Jesus the last Adam.

Adam received life from God.

Jesus gives life to others.

## 1 Corinthians 15:46

The natural body comes first.

The spiritual body comes after it.

## 1 Corinthians 15:47

God made the first man from the dust.

The second man came from heaven.

## 1 Corinthians 15:48

People of the earth are like the man from earth.

People of heaven are like the man from heaven.

## 1 Corinthians 15:49

We have been like the man made from dust.

We will also be like the man from heaven.

**Pause and answer**

What will be different about the body God raises?

## God will change us

## 1 Corinthians 15:50

Brothers and sisters, our bodies of flesh and blood cannot inherit God’s kingdom as they are.

What can decay cannot inherit what lasts forever.

## 1 Corinthians 15:51

Listen to this truth that was hidden.

Some of us will still be alive.

But God will change us all.

## 1 Corinthians 15:52

It will happen in the blink of an eye.

The last trumpet will sound.

God will raise the dead with bodies that never decay.

And God will change us.

## 1 Corinthians 15:53

Our bodies can decay.

They must become bodies that never decay.

Our bodies can die.

They must become bodies that never die.

## 1 Corinthians 15:54

Then our bodies will never decay or die.

These words of Scripture will come true.

Victory has swallowed up death.

## 1 Corinthians 15:55

Death, where is your victory?

Death, where is your sting?

**Help with these words**

Paul speaks to death as if it could sting.

He asks these questions because death will lose its power.

## 1 Corinthians 15:56

Sin gives death its sting.

The law gives sin its power.

## 1 Corinthians 15:57

Thank God!

Through our Lord Jesus Christ, God gives us the victory.

## 1 Corinthians 15:58

So, dear brothers and sisters, stand firm.

Keep giving yourselves fully to the Lord’s work.

You know your work for the Lord matters.

**Pause and answer**

What sound does Paul say people will hear when God changes them?
