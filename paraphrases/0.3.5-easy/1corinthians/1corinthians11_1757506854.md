# 1 Corinthians 11 — When believers meet together

**About this chapter**

Paul writes to the believers in Corinth about worship.

Some people go hungry when the church meets to eat.

Paul reminds everyone what Jesus did with bread and a cup.

## Follow the example of Christ

## 1 Corinthians 11:1

Follow my example, just as I follow Christ’s example.

## 1 Corinthians 11:2

I praise you for remembering me in everything.

You keep doing what I taught you to do.

## 1 Corinthians 11:3

Here is something I want you to understand.

Christ is the head of every man.

The man is the head of the woman.

God is the head of Christ.

**Help with these words**

Paul uses “head” as a picture here.

He uses it to describe how people relate to one another and to God.

**Pause and answer**

Whose example does Paul follow?

## Head coverings during worship

## 1 Corinthians 11:4

A man might pray or prophesy with his head covered.

When he does this, he dishonors his head.

**Help with these words**

To pray means to speak to God.

To prophesy means to speak a message from God.

## 1 Corinthians 11:5

A woman might pray or prophesy with her head uncovered.

When she does this, she dishonors her head.

It is as if someone had shaved her head.

## 1 Corinthians 11:6

If she leaves her head uncovered, she might as well cut off her hair.

If cutting or shaving her hair brings her shame, she should cover her head.

## 1 Corinthians 11:7

A man should leave his head uncovered.

He bears God’s image and shows God’s glory.

The woman shows the man’s glory.

## 1 Corinthians 11:8

The first man did not come from the first woman.

The first woman came from the first man.

## 1 Corinthians 11:9

God did not make the man for the woman.

God made the woman for the man.

## 1 Corinthians 11:10

For this reason, a woman should wear a sign of authority on her head.

This matters because of the angels.

**Pause and answer**

What does Paul tell a man to do with his head covering when he prays?

## Men and women need each other

## 1 Corinthians 11:11

Yet women who belong to the Lord need men.

Men who belong to the Lord also need women.

## 1 Corinthians 11:12

The first woman came from a man.

Now a woman gives birth to a man.

And everything comes from God.

## 1 Corinthians 11:13

Think this through for yourselves.

Is it right for a woman to pray to God with her head uncovered?

## 1 Corinthians 11:14

Think about what nature itself teaches you.

Doesn’t long hair bring shame to a man?

## 1 Corinthians 11:15

But a woman’s long hair brings her honor.

Her long hair gives her a covering.

## 1 Corinthians 11:16

Someone may want to argue about this.

We have no other practice.

The other churches of God have no other practice either.

**Pause and answer**

Where does everything come from?

## Some people go hungry at the meal

## 1 Corinthians 11:17

Now I must speak about your meetings.

I cannot praise you for these.

Your meetings do more harm than good.

## 1 Corinthians 11:18

I hear you split into groups when the church meets.

I believe at least part of what I hear.

## 1 Corinthians 11:19

There have to be differences among you.

These show which of you have God’s approval.

## 1 Corinthians 11:20

You meet for a meal.

But the meal you eat is no longer the Lord’s Supper.

**Help with these words**

The Lord’s Supper is a meal believers share to remember Jesus.

## 1 Corinthians 11:21

Each person starts eating without sharing any food.

One person goes hungry.

Another drinks so much that they get drunk.

## 1 Corinthians 11:22

You have homes where you can eat and drink.

Do you look down on God’s church?

Why shame the people who have nothing?

Should I praise you for this?

I cannot praise you.

**Pause and answer**

What happens to someone who has no food when others refuse to share?

## Remember Jesus with bread and a cup

## 1 Corinthians 11:23

I passed on what the Lord taught me.

On the night someone betrayed him, the Lord Jesus took bread.

## 1 Corinthians 11:24

Jesus thanked God and broke the bread.

Jesus said, “This is my body, which is for you.

Do this to remember me.”

## 1 Corinthians 11:25

After supper, Jesus took the cup in the same way.

Jesus said, “This cup is the new covenant in my blood.

Each time you drink it, remember me.”

![A drinking cup with a handle](../pictures/cup.svg)

**Help with these words**

A covenant is a lasting agreement.

Jesus speaks of his blood because he will give his life.

## 1 Corinthians 11:26

Each time you eat this bread and drink from this cup, you announce that the Lord died.

Keep doing this until the Lord comes again.

**Pause and answer**

Who did Jesus ask people to remember?

## Think about how you take part

## 1 Corinthians 11:27

Someone may eat this bread or drink from the Lord’s cup in a way that dishonors him.

That person sins against the Lord’s body and blood.

## 1 Corinthians 11:28

Each of you must first look at your own actions.

Then eat the bread and drink from the cup.

## 1 Corinthians 11:29

Someone may eat and drink without recognizing the body.

That person brings God’s judgment on themselves by eating and drinking.

## 1 Corinthians 11:30

That is why many among you are weak and sick.

Some of you have even died.

## 1 Corinthians 11:31

If we judged our own actions rightly, we would escape judgment.

## 1 Corinthians 11:32

When the Lord judges us, he corrects us.

He does this to spare us when he condemns the world.

**Help with these words**

To condemn someone means to declare them guilty and sentence them to punishment.

**Pause and answer**

What should you do before eating the bread?

## Wait for each other

## 1 Corinthians 11:33

So, brothers and sisters, wait for each other when you meet to eat.

## 1 Corinthians 11:34

Anyone who is hungry should eat at home.

Then your meetings will not bring God’s judgment on you.

When I come, I will teach you about the other matters.

**Pause and answer**

When the church meets to eat, who should wait?
