# 1 Corinthians 16 — A gift for Jerusalem and greetings from Paul

**About this chapter**

Paul tells the believers how to save money for a gift.

He ends his letter with greetings from friends.

## Save a gift for Jerusalem

## 1 Corinthians 16:1

You asked about collecting money for God’s people.

Follow the instructions I gave the churches in Galatia.

## 1 Corinthians 16:2

On the first day of every week, set something aside.

Each person can save a share of what they earned.

Then you will have the gift ready when I come.

![Two small coins set beside each other](../pictures/two-coins.svg)

## 1 Corinthians 16:3

When I arrive, you will choose people you trust.

I will send them with letters to Jerusalem.

They will carry your gift there.

## 1 Corinthians 16:4

If it seems best for me to go too, they can travel with me.

**Pause and answer**

When should each person set money aside?

## Paul hopes to stay awhile

## 1 Corinthians 16:5

I plan to travel through Macedonia.

After that, I will come to you.

## 1 Corinthians 16:6

Perhaps I can stay with you for a while.

I might even spend the winter with you.

Then you can help me travel to my next stop.

## 1 Corinthians 16:7

I want more than a quick visit.

I hope to stay awhile, if the Lord allows it.

## 1 Corinthians 16:8

For now I will stay in Ephesus until Pentecost.

## 1 Corinthians 16:9

A great door has opened for me here.

I can do much good work, even though many people oppose me.

**Help with these words**

An open door is a picture of a chance to do the Lord’s work.

Pentecost was a Jewish feast.

## Welcome Timothy

## 1 Corinthians 16:10

If Timothy comes, help him feel safe among you.

Timothy does the Lord’s work just as I do.

## 1 Corinthians 16:11

Treat Timothy with respect.

Send him on his way in peace.

I am waiting for him to return with the other believers.

## 1 Corinthians 16:12

I urged our brother Apollos to visit you with the others.

Apollos did not want to come now.

He will come when he has the chance.

**Pause and answer**

How does Paul want the church to treat Timothy?

## Do everything with love

## 1 Corinthians 16:13

Stay alert.

Stand firm in your faith.

Be brave.

Be strong.

## 1 Corinthians 16:14

Do everything with love.

## 1 Corinthians 16:15

You know Stephanas and the people in his house.

They were the first in Achaia to believe in Christ.

They have given themselves to serving God’s people.

Brothers and sisters, I ask this of you.

## 1 Corinthians 16:16

Follow the lead of people like them.

Do the same for everyone who works hard alongside them.

## 1 Corinthians 16:17

I am glad Stephanas, Fortunatus, and Achaicus came.

They gave me the help you could not give from far away.

## 1 Corinthians 16:18

They cheered my spirit and yours too.

Show people like these how much you value them.

**Pause and answer**

How did Stephanas and his household use their time?

## Greetings to the church

## 1 Corinthians 16:19

The churches in the province of Asia greet you.

Aquila and Prisca send warm greetings in the Lord.

The church in their house greets you too.

## 1 Corinthians 16:20

All the believers here send greetings.

Greet one another with a holy kiss.

**Help with these words**

A holy kiss was a way believers greeted one another as family.

## 1 Corinthians 16:21

I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand.

## 1 Corinthians 16:22

If anyone has no love for the Lord, let that person be under a curse.

Come, Lord!

## 1 Corinthians 16:23

May the Lord Jesus show you his grace.

## 1 Corinthians 16:24

I send my love to all of you in Christ Jesus.

Amen.
