# 1 Corinthians 2 — God’s Spirit helps us understand

**About this chapter**

Paul remembers trembling as he spoke in Corinth.

He explains why believers can trust God’s power.

## Paul remembers his visit to Corinth

## 1 Corinthians 2:1

Brothers and sisters, I came to tell you about God.

I did not try to impress you with fine speeches or clever ideas.

## 1 Corinthians 2:2

While I was with you, I chose to focus on one thing: Jesus Christ and his death on the cross.

## 1 Corinthians 2:3

When I came to you, I felt weak.

I shook with fear.

## 1 Corinthians 2:4

My message did not depend on clever arguments.

As I spoke, the Holy Spirit showed his power.

## 1 Corinthians 2:5

Then your faith could rest on God’s power.

It would not depend on how wise a speaker seemed.

**Pause and answer**

What did Paul want the believers to depend on?

## God’s wisdom was hidden

## 1 Corinthians 2:6

We do speak words of wisdom among people who have grown in faith.

This wisdom does not come from the world around us.

It does not come from today’s rulers, whose power is coming to an end.

## 1 Corinthians 2:7

We speak about God’s wisdom, which he kept hidden.

Before time began, God planned that through this wisdom we would share in his glory.

## 1 Corinthians 2:8

None of the rulers understood God’s wisdom.

If they had, they would never have put the Lord of glory to death on a cross.

**Help with these words**

“The Lord of glory” means Jesus.

## 1 Corinthians 2:9

The Scriptures say, “No one has seen what God has prepared for those who love him.

No one has heard it.

No one has even imagined it.”

## The Spirit knows God’s thoughts

## 1 Corinthians 2:10

God has now shown us these things through his Spirit.

The Spirit searches everything, even the deepest things of God.

## 1 Corinthians 2:11

Who knows your thoughts as your own spirit does?

In the same way, only God’s Spirit knows God’s thoughts.

## 1 Corinthians 2:12

We received the Spirit who comes from God.

We did not receive the spirit of this world.

God’s Spirit helps us understand the gifts God freely gives us.

## 1 Corinthians 2:13

We speak about those gifts in words the Spirit teaches us.

Human wisdom did not teach us those words.

We explain what the Spirit shows us using words from the Spirit.

**Pause and answer**

Who helps us understand what God has freely given us?

## The Spirit helps us see what matters

## 1 Corinthians 2:14

A person without God’s Spirit does not accept what the Spirit gives.

It seems foolish to them.

They cannot understand it on their own.

It takes God’s Spirit to recognise what these things mean.

## 1 Corinthians 2:15

A person who has the Spirit can weigh up all things.

Yet no one else can sit in judgment over that person.

## 1 Corinthians 2:16

The Scriptures ask, “Who knows the Lord’s thoughts well enough to teach him?”

Yet we have the mind of Christ.

**Help with these words**

Having the mind of Christ means his Spirit helps us understand things as Christ does.
