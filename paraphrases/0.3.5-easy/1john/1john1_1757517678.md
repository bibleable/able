# 1 John 1 — Walk in the light

**About this chapter**

John tells what he and others experienced with Jesus.

He asks us to tell God the truth about our sins.

## We saw and heard Jesus

## 1 John 1:1

Jesus was there from the beginning.

We heard him speak.

We saw him with our own eyes.

We looked closely at him.

We touched him with our own hands.

He is the Word of life.

![An eye and an ear.](../pictures/eye-and-ear.svg)

Point to the ear. John and his friends heard Jesus speak.

## 1 John 1:2

This life appeared to us.

We saw him and tell you what we saw.

He is the life that lasts forever.

He was with the Father and appeared to us.

## 1 John 1:3

We tell you what we saw and heard.

We want you to share in our life together.

We share this life with God the Father and his Son, Jesus Christ.

## 1 John 1:4

We write this so our joy may be complete.

**Pause and answer**

Who shares life with the Father and his Son?

## Living in God’s light

## 1 John 1:5

Here is the message we heard from Jesus and bring to you.

God is light.

There is no darkness in him at all.

Light helps us picture God’s goodness.

There is no evil in God.

## 1 John 1:6

Suppose we say we share life with God.

Yet we keep walking in darkness.

Walking in darkness means living in ways that are wrong.

Then we are lying.

Our lives go against the truth.

## 1 John 1:7

God is in the light.

When we walk in the light, we live by his truth.

Then we share life with one another.

Jesus, God’s Son, gave his blood for us.

His blood washes away all our sin.

**Pause and answer**

What does walking in the light mean?

## Tell God what you have done wrong

## 1 John 1:8

If we say we have no sin, we fool ourselves.

The truth has no place in us.

## 1 John 1:9

Confess your sins to God.

That means telling God what you have done wrong.

God is faithful and does what is right.

He forgives our sins and washes away all our wrongdoing.

## 1 John 1:10

Suppose we say we have never sinned.

Then we are calling God a liar.

His word has no place in us.

**Pause and answer**

What does God do when we confess our sins?
