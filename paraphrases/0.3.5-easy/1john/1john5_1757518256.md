# 1 John 5 — God gives life through his Son

**About this chapter**

John explains why his readers can be sure of eternal life.

He also tells them how to pray for each other.

## Trust Jesus and love God’s children

## 1 John 5:1

Everyone who believes Jesus is the Christ is God’s child.

Christ means the king God promised to send.

Whoever loves the Father loves his children too.

## 1 John 5:2

How can we tell we love God’s children?

We love God and obey his commands.

## 1 John 5:3

Loving God means keeping his commands.

His commands are not too heavy for us to carry.

## 1 John 5:4

Everyone born of God overcomes the world that opposes him.

We win this victory through faith.

Faith means trusting God.

## 1 John 5:5

Who overcomes the world?

The person who believes Jesus is God’s Son.

Who overcomes the world?

## Three witnesses agree

## 1 John 5:6

Jesus Christ came by water and blood.

John asks us to remember both the water and the blood.

The Spirit tells us this is true.

The Spirit is truth.

## 1 John 5:7

Three witnesses speak about Jesus.

A witness tells what is true about someone or something.

## 1 John 5:8

The three witnesses are the Spirit, the water and the blood.

All three agree.

**Pause and answer**

What do the three witnesses agree about?

## What God says about his Son

## 1 John 5:9

We accept what people tell us as witnesses.

God’s word as a witness carries even more weight.

God himself has spoken about his Son.

## 1 John 5:10

Whoever trusts God’s Son carries that message within them.

Whoever refuses to believe God calls him a liar.

They reject what God has said about his Son.

## 1 John 5:11

Here is what God tells us.

God has given us life that lasts forever.

This life is in his Son.

## 1 John 5:12

Whoever has the Son has this life.

Whoever does not have God’s Son does not have this life.

## 1 John 5:13

I write to you who trust in God’s Son.

I want you to know you have eternal life.

## Pray for one another

## 1 John 5:14

We can approach God with confidence.

When we ask for what he wants, he hears us.

## 1 John 5:15

We know he hears us when we ask in this way.

So we know we have what we asked him for.

## 1 John 5:16

Suppose you see another believer sin.

If that sin does not lead to death, pray for them.

God will give life to those who sin in that way.

There is also sin that leads to death.

I am not asking you to pray about that sin.

## 1 John 5:17

All wrongdoing is sin.

Some sin does not lead to death.

**Pause and answer**

What does John ask you to do for a believer whose sin does not lead to death?

## Know whom you belong to

## 1 John 5:18

We know God’s children do not keep on sinning.

God’s Son protects them.

The evil one cannot lay hold of them.

## 1 John 5:19

We know we belong to God.

The whole world lies under the evil one’s power.

## 1 John 5:20

We know God’s Son has come.

He helps us understand so we can know the true God.

We belong to the true God through his Son, Jesus Christ.

He is the true God and eternal life.

## 1 John 5:21

Dear children, keep away from idols.

An idol is something people worship in God’s place.
