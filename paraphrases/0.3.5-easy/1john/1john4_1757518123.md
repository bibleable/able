# 1 John 4 — God loved us first

**About this chapter**

John asks his friends to test what teachers say.

He explains how God showed his love.

## Where does the message come from?

## 1 John 4:1

Dear friends, many people claim to speak for God.

Many false prophets have gone out into the world.

Test the spirit behind each message before you believe it.

Find out whether it comes from God.

## 1 John 4:2

Here is how to recognize God’s Spirit.

A spirit from God openly declares that Jesus Christ came in the flesh.

This means Jesus came as a real human being.

## 1 John 4:3

A spirit that refuses to acknowledge Jesus does not come from God.

It is the spirit of the antichrist, the one who opposes Christ.

You heard that this spirit was coming.

It is already here in the world.

**Pause and answer**

What does God’s Spirit say about Jesus?

## Who listens to the truth?

## 1 John 4:4

Dear children, you belong to God.

You have overcome those false prophets.

The one who lives in you is greater than the one in the world.

## 1 John 4:5

Those teachers belong to the world that opposes God.

They speak as the world thinks.

That is why the world listens to them.

## 1 John 4:6

We belong to God.

Whoever knows God listens to us.

Whoever does not belong to God refuses to listen.

This helps us recognize the Spirit who tells the truth.

It also helps us recognize a spirit that tells lies.

![An eye and an ear.](../pictures/eye-and-ear.svg)

Point to the ear. Think about whose message you listen to.

**Pause and answer**

Why could the readers overcome the false prophets?

## How God showed his love

## 1 John 4:7

Dear friends, let us love each other.

Love comes from God.

Everyone who loves is God’s child and knows him.

## 1 John 4:8

Anyone who does not love does not know God.

God is love.

## 1 John 4:9

God showed his love among us in this way.

He sent his only Son into the world.

Through his Son we can live.

## 1 John 4:10

Love began with God loving us.

He sent his Son to offer himself for our sins.

That sacrifice makes things right between us and God.

## 1 John 4:11

Dear friends, God loved us that much.

So we should love each other.

## 1 John 4:12

No one has ever seen God.

Yet when we love each other, God stays in us.

His love reaches its goal in us.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did God send into the world?

## God gives us his Spirit

## 1 John 4:13

God has given us his Spirit.

That is how we know we remain in him and he remains in us.

## 1 John 4:14

We saw what happened and now tell you about it.

The Father sent his Son to save the world.

## 1 John 4:15

Anyone may openly say that Jesus is God’s Son.

God stays in the person who acknowledges Jesus in this way.

That person stays close to God.

## 1 John 4:16

We have come to know and trust God’s love for us.

God is love.

Whoever continues in love stays close to God.

God stays in that person.

**Pause and answer**

What did the Father send his Son to do?

## Love drives out fear

## 1 John 4:17

This is how love reaches its goal among us.

We can face the day God judges us with confidence.

Even in this world we are like Jesus.

## 1 John 4:18

Love has no room for fear.

When love reaches its goal, it drives fear away.

Here John means fear of punishment.

Someone who still fears punishment has yet to grow fully in love.

## 1 John 4:19

We love because God loved us first.

## 1 John 4:20

Someone might say, “I love God,” yet hate another believer.

That person lies.

They can see their brother or sister.

If they cannot love that person, how can they love God, whom they cannot see?

## 1 John 4:21

God has given us this command.

Whoever loves God must also love their brother or sister.

**Pause and answer**

Who loved first?
