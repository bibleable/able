# 1 John 3 — Show love by helping

**About this chapter**

John explains what it means to be God’s children.

He asks us to notice when someone needs help.

## The Father calls us his children

## 1 John 3:1

Look how much the Father loves us!

He calls us his children.

We really are his children.

The world did not recognize him.

That is why it does not recognize us.

## 1 John 3:2

Dear friends, we are God’s children now.

We do not yet know everything we will become.

When Christ appears, we will see him as he is.

Then we know we will be like him.

## 1 John 3:3

Everyone who puts this hope in Christ seeks to live a pure life.

Christ himself is pure.

**Pause and answer**

What does the Father call us?

## Why Christ came

## 1 John 3:4

Anyone who keeps on sinning keeps breaking God’s law.

That is what sin means here.

## 1 John 3:5

You know why Christ came.

He came to take away sins.

There is no sin in him.

## 1 John 3:6

Anyone who stays close to Christ cannot keep on sinning.

Whoever keeps on sinning has neither seen nor known him.

## 1 John 3:7

Dear children, let no one trick you.

Someone who practices doing right is righteous.

Christ himself is righteous.

Righteous means doing what is right before God.

## 1 John 3:8

Anyone who makes a practice of sin belongs to the devil.

The devil has sinned from the beginning.

God’s Son came to destroy the devil’s work.

## 1 John 3:9

A child of God refuses to keep practicing sin.

John says God’s seed stays in that person.

Think of a seed as the start of new life.

Because God has given them birth, they cannot keep living in sin.

## 1 John 3:10

Here is how to recognize God’s children and the devil’s children.

Anyone who refuses to do right does not belong to God.

The same goes for someone who refuses to love another believer.

**Pause and answer**

What did God’s Son come to destroy?

## Remember Cain and his brother

## 1 John 3:11

You heard this message from the beginning.

Love one another.

## 1 John 3:12

Remember Cain.

He belonged to the evil one and murdered his brother.

Why did he kill him?

Cain did evil, and his brother did what was right.

## 1 John 3:13

So, brothers and sisters, the world may hate you.

Do not let that surprise you.

## 1 John 3:14

We love our brothers and sisters.

That tells us we have passed from death to life.

Someone who does not love remains in death.

## 1 John 3:15

Anyone who hates another believer is a murderer.

You know that eternal life does not live in a murderer.

**Pause and answer**

What tells us we have passed from death to life?

## What love looks like

## 1 John 3:16

Jesus gave his life for us.

That shows us what love is.

We too should give our lives for our brothers and sisters.

## 1 John 3:17

Suppose you have enough to share.

You see another believer who needs help.

If you shut your heart to them, how can God’s love live in you?

![A woven basket.](../pictures/basket.svg)

Picture bringing a basket of food to someone who needs it.

## 1 John 3:18

Dear children, let us show real love.

Let our actions make our words true.

**Pause and answer**

How could you help someone who needs food?

## Come to God with confidence

## 1 John 3:19

This is how we know we belong to the truth.

This can steady our hearts when we come before God.

## 1 John 3:20

Sometimes our own hearts tell us we are guilty.

Even then, God is greater than our hearts.

He knows everything.

## 1 John 3:21

Dear friends, when our hearts do not accuse us, we can approach God with confidence.

## 1 John 3:22

We receive from God what we ask.

We keep his commands and do what pleases him.

## 1 John 3:23

God commands us to trust his Son, Jesus Christ.

He also commands us to love one another.

## 1 John 3:24

Those who obey God’s commands stay close to him.

God stays in them.

How do we know God stays in us?

We know by the Spirit he has given us.

**Pause and answer**

Who helps us know that God stays in us?
