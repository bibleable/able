# 1 John 2 — Love each other and stay close to Christ

**About this chapter**

John writes to people he loves like family.

He helps them recognize the truth about Jesus.

## Jesus speaks for us

## 1 John 2:1

My dear children, I write so you will avoid sin.

If anyone sins, Jesus Christ speaks to the Father for us.

Jesus is the one who always does right.

## 1 John 2:2

Jesus offered himself as the sacrifice for our sins.

He did this for the sins of the whole world too.

## 1 John 2:3

How can we be sure we know him?

We can tell by whether we obey his commands.

## 1 John 2:4

Someone may say, “I know him,” yet ignore his commands.

That person lies.

The truth has no place in them.

## 1 John 2:5

When someone obeys his word, God’s love reaches its goal in that person.

Here is how we can tell we belong to him.

## 1 John 2:6

Anyone who says they stay close to Jesus must live as Jesus lived.

**Pause and answer**

Who speaks to the Father for us?

## Love another believer

## 1 John 2:7

Dear friends, you have known this command from the beginning.

It is the same message you heard then.

![A scroll for a written message.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

Picture a reader opening the scroll and reading the command aloud.

## 1 John 2:8

Yet I can also call it a new command.

You see its truth in Jesus and in your lives.

The darkness is passing.

The true light already shines.

## 1 John 2:9

Suppose someone says, “I live in the light,” but hates another believer.

That person still lives in darkness.

## 1 John 2:10

Anyone who loves another believer stays in the light.

There is nothing in them to make them stumble.

Stumbling here means falling into wrongdoing.

## 1 John 2:11

Anyone who hates another believer lives and walks in darkness.

Darkness keeps that person from seeing where they are going.

Hatred keeps them from seeing how to live.

**Pause and answer**

What keeps someone in darkness?

## Why John writes to this family

## 1 John 2:12

Dear children, I write because God has forgiven your sins through Jesus’ name.

## 1 John 2:13

Fathers, I write because you know the one who was there from the beginning.

Young men, I write because you have overcome the evil one.

The evil one is the devil.

Children, I have written because you know the Father.

## 1 John 2:14

Fathers, you know the one who was there from the beginning.

Young men, I have written because you are strong.

God’s word stays in you.

You have overcome the evil one.

**Pause and answer**

What stays in the young men John writes to?

## What lasts forever?

## 1 John 2:15

Do not set your love on the world or what it offers.

Here the world means the way of life that opposes God.

When someone loves that way, love for the Father has no place in them.

## 1 John 2:16

Think of the things people crave for their bodies.

Think of the things they see and long to own.

Think of how they boast about what they have.

Those desires come from the world.

They do not come from the Father.

## 1 John 2:17

The world and its desires are passing away.

Whoever does what God wants will live forever.

**Pause and answer**

Who will live forever?

## People who deny the Son

## 1 John 2:18

Children, we have reached the last hour.

This means the last days are here.

You heard that the antichrist is coming.

An antichrist is someone who stands against Christ.

Many antichrists have already come.

That is how we know it is the last hour.

## 1 John 2:19

These people left us.

If they had belonged to us, they would have stayed.

By leaving, they showed that none of them belonged to us.

## 1 John 2:20

You have received an anointing from the Holy One.

John uses this word for the gift that teaches you.

All of you know the truth.

## 1 John 2:21

I write because you already know the truth.

You know that no lie comes from the truth.

## 1 John 2:22

Someone who denies that Jesus is the Christ tells a lie.

Christ means the king God promised to send.

Whoever denies the Father and the Son is an antichrist.

## 1 John 2:23

Whoever rejects the Son does not share life with the Father.

Whoever openly acknowledges the Son shares life with the Father too.

**Pause and answer**

Who shares life with the Father?

## Hold on to what you heard

## 1 John 2:24

Hold on to the message you heard from the beginning.

If it stays in you, you will stay close to the Son and the Father.

## 1 John 2:25

He himself promised us life that lasts forever.

## 1 John 2:26

I have written about people who want to trick you.

## 1 John 2:27

The anointing you received from him stays in you.

You do not need someone else to teach you.

His gift teaches you about all things.

What it teaches is true.

Stay close to him, just as you were taught.

## 1 John 2:28

Now, dear children, stay close to Christ.

Then when Christ returns, we can face him with confidence.

We will have no need to hide in shame.

## 1 John 2:29

You know he does what is right.

So you know that everyone who practices doing right is his child.

Being born of God means receiving new life from him.

**Pause and answer**

What has God promised us?
