# Zephaniah 2 — Seek the LORD while there is time

**About this chapter**

Zephaniah calls the people together. He tells them what will happen to the nations around Judah.

## Come together and seek the LORD

## Zephaniah 2:1

People of Judah, you have acted shamefully.

Come together. Gather now.

## Zephaniah 2:2

Come before the LORD carries out his decision.

Time passes quickly, like dry husks blowing away.

Come before the day when the LORD shows his fierce anger.

## Zephaniah 2:3

All who humbly obey the LORD, seek him.

Do what is right. Be humble before him.

Perhaps the LORD will keep you safe on that day.

## The cities along the sea

## Zephaniah 2:4

People will leave Gaza. Ashkelon will lie in ruins.

At noon enemies will drive people out of Ashdod.

They will tear the city of Ekron from its place.

## Zephaniah 2:5

Trouble is coming to the Cherethites along the coast.

The LORD speaks against this part of Canaan.

It is the land of the Philistines.

He says, “I will destroy you. No one will remain.”

## Zephaniah 2:6

Grass will grow along the coast.

Shepherds will have wells for water.

They will have pens where they can keep their sheep.

## Zephaniah 2:7

The people left in Judah will have this coast.

They will find grass for their flocks there.

At night they will rest among Ashkelon’s houses.

The LORD their God will care for them.

He will bring back those who were taken away.

## Moab and Ammon insulted the LORD’s people

## Zephaniah 2:8

The LORD said, “I heard Moab insult my people.

I heard the people of Ammon mock them.

They threatened to take my people’s land.”

## Zephaniah 2:9

Israel’s God, the LORD who leads heaven’s armies, made a promise.

“As surely as I live, Moab will become like Sodom.

Ammon will become like Gomorrah.”

Those cities had been destroyed.

Only weeds and pits of salt would remain forever.

Those left in Judah would take their enemies’ goods and land.

## Zephaniah 2:10

This would happen because Moab and Ammon were proud.

They had made fun of the LORD’s people.

## Zephaniah 2:11

The LORD will fill those nations with fear.

He will leave the gods of the earth without food offerings.

People on every shore will bow to the LORD.

Each nation will worship him in its own land.

## Cush and Assyria

## Zephaniah 2:12

The LORD said to the people of Cush, “My sword will strike you too.”

They would die in war.

## Zephaniah 2:13

The LORD will act against Assyria in the north.

He will destroy it.

Its city of Nineveh will stand empty and dry as a desert.

## Zephaniah 2:14

Herds and wild animals will rest inside Nineveh.

Two kinds of owls will perch on the stone pillars.

Their calls will echo from the windows.

Doorways will stand empty. Cedar beams will lie uncovered.

## Zephaniah 2:15

Nineveh once felt safe and free of trouble.

Its people said, “No other city is like ours!”

Now animals will rest in the ruined city.

People passing by will hiss and shake their fists at it.
