# Zephaniah 3 — The LORD promises to bring his people home

**About this chapter**

Jerusalem’s leaders hurt the people. The LORD promises a day when his people can live safely again.

## The LORD sees what happens in Jerusalem

## Zephaniah 3:1

Trouble is coming to Jerusalem.

Its people have rebelled against God.

Their wrongdoing has made the city unclean.

The powerful mistreat others.

## Zephaniah 3:2

The people will not listen to anyone.

They refuse to change when someone corrects them.

They do not trust the LORD.

They do not come near to their God.

## Zephaniah 3:3

The leaders are like lions that roar at their prey.

The judges are like hungry wolves at night.

They take everything. Nothing remains by morning.

## Zephaniah 3:4

The prophets speak carelessly. People cannot trust them.

The priests dishonor God’s holy place.

They twist God’s law.

## Zephaniah 3:5

The LORD lives among them. He always does right.

Each morning he shows people what is just.

He never fails. Yet those doing wrong feel no shame.

## The LORD calls the city to listen

## Zephaniah 3:6

The LORD said, “I destroyed nations and their towers.

I emptied their streets. Nobody walks there.

I ruined their cities. No one lives there now.”

## Zephaniah 3:7

The LORD thought Jerusalem’s people would respect him and accept correction.

Then they could keep their homes despite their wrongdoing.

Instead they got up early to do more wrong.

## Zephaniah 3:8

The LORD said, “Wait for me.

I will stand up and speak against the nations.

I have decided to gather their kingdoms together.

I will show them my fierce anger.”

His anger at their unfaithfulness will burn across the earth like fire.

## People from many lands will worship the LORD

## Zephaniah 3:9

The LORD said he would make the peoples’ speech pure.

Then they could all call on him.

They would serve him together, side by side.

## Zephaniah 3:10

“Some of my people live far away, beyond the rivers of Cush.

They will come and bring me a gift.”

## Zephaniah 3:11

The LORD spoke to Jerusalem.

“On that day you will no longer bear shame for disobeying me.

I will remove those who boast about themselves.

People on my holy mountain will no longer boast.”

## Zephaniah 3:12

“I will leave gentle and humble people among you.

They will trust me to keep them safe.”

## Zephaniah 3:13

Those left in Israel will stop doing wrong.

They will stop lying and tricking others.

Like sheep in a safe pasture, they will eat and rest.

No one will frighten them.

## Jerusalem can sing for joy

## Zephaniah 3:14

People of Zion, sing for joy!

Zion is another name for Jerusalem.

People of Israel, shout with joy!

People of Jerusalem, be glad with all your heart!

## Zephaniah 3:15

The LORD has ended your punishment.

He has driven your enemy away.

The LORD, Israel’s king, is here with you.

You will no longer live in fear of harm.

## Zephaniah 3:16

On that day people will tell Jerusalem,

“People of Zion, do not be afraid.

Do not lose courage.”

## Zephaniah 3:17

“The LORD your God is with you.

He is strong enough to save you.

You bring him joy. His love will calm you.

He will sing for joy over you.”

## The LORD will gather his people

## Zephaniah 3:18

The LORD said, “Some of you grieve because you miss the special feasts.

I will gather you. You will no longer bear disgrace.”

## Zephaniah 3:19

“I will act against everyone who hurts you.

I will save those who have trouble walking.

I will gather those who have been scattered.

People everywhere will honor those who once suffered shame.”

## Zephaniah 3:20

“At that time I will gather you and bring you home.

People in every land will know you and praise you.

You will see me bring back those taken away.”

The LORD made this promise.
