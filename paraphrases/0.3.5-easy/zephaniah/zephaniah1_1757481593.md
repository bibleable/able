# Zephaniah 1 — The LORD warns Judah

**About this chapter**

Zephaniah warns the people of Judah. The day when the LORD will judge them is close.

## The LORD speaks to Zephaniah

## Zephaniah 1:1

Josiah son of Amon was king of Judah.

The LORD gave Zephaniah a message.

Zephaniah’s father was Cushi. Cushi’s father was Gedaliah.

Gedaliah’s father was Amariah. Amariah’s father was Hezekiah.

## Zephaniah 1:2

The LORD said, “I will sweep everything off the earth.”

He meant that he would remove everything.

## Zephaniah 1:3

“I will sweep away people and animals.

I will sweep away birds and fish.

I will remove the idols and the people who worship them.”

Idols are things people worship as gods.

The LORD said he would remove people from the earth.

## The LORD speaks against false worship

## Zephaniah 1:4

The LORD said he would act against Judah and Jerusalem.

He would end all worship of the god Baal there.

He would wipe out even the names of the priests who served idols.

## Zephaniah 1:5

Some people bowed to the stars on their roofs.

Others promised loyalty to the LORD.

Yet they made promises in the name of Milcom too.

Milcom was another god they worshiped.

## Zephaniah 1:6

Others had stopped following the LORD.

They did not look for him.

They did not ask him what to do.

## The LORD will search Jerusalem

## Zephaniah 1:7

Be quiet before the Lord GOD.

The Day of the LORD is near.

This is the day when the LORD will judge people.

Zephaniah describes it as a sacrifice the LORD has prepared.

The LORD has set apart his guests for it.

## Zephaniah 1:8

The LORD said, “On that day I will punish the leaders.

I will punish the king’s sons.

I will punish everyone who wears foreign clothes.”

## Zephaniah 1:9

The LORD would punish those who leaped over the doorway.

They hurt people and cheated them.

That was how they filled their master’s house with goods.

## Zephaniah 1:10

People would cry out at Jerusalem’s Fish Gate.

People would weep loudly in the city’s Second District.

A loud crash would come from the hills.

## Zephaniah 1:11

Zephaniah told people in the area called the Hollow to weep.

The traders there would fall silent.

Those who weighed silver to buy and sell would perish.

## Zephaniah 1:12

The LORD said, “I will search Jerusalem with lamps.”

A lamp helps someone see in dark places.

The LORD would find and punish people who felt safe ignoring him.

They thought, “The LORD will do nothing, good or bad.”

## Zephaniah 1:13

Enemies would take their riches and wreck their houses.

People would build houses but never live in them.

They would plant grapes but never drink the wine.

## The day is coming quickly

## Zephaniah 1:14

The great Day of the LORD is close.

It is coming quickly.

Even strong warriors will cry out in pain.

## Zephaniah 1:15

On that day the LORD will show his anger.

People will suffer. Buildings will fall into ruins.

Dark clouds will cover the sky.

## Zephaniah 1:16

Horns will sound. Soldiers will shout.

They will attack cities with strong walls and high towers.

## Zephaniah 1:17

The LORD said the people had sinned against him.

In their distress they would feel their way like people who cannot see.

Their blood would spill on the ground like dust.

Their bodies would lie there like waste.

## Zephaniah 1:18

Their silver and gold could never save them.

The LORD’s anger at their unfaithfulness would burn like fire.

It would spread over the whole earth.

The LORD would suddenly end the lives of all its people.
