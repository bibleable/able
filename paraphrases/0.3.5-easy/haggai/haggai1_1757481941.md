# Haggai 1 — The people begin to rebuild the temple

**About this chapter**

The temple in Judah lay broken. The LORD sent Haggai to speak to the people.

## Haggai brings a message

## Haggai 1:1

Darius was in his second year as king.

It was day one of the sixth month.

The LORD spoke through Haggai, his prophet.

A prophet brings people a message from God.

Haggai spoke to Zerubbabel, the son of Shealtiel.

Zerubbabel governed Judah.

Haggai also spoke to Joshua, the son of Jehozadak.

Joshua was the high priest. He led the priests.

## Haggai 1:2

The LORD leads the armies of heaven.

He said, “The people say it is too soon to rebuild my temple.”

The temple was the house where people worshiped the LORD.

## Haggai 1:3

The LORD gave Haggai a question for the people.

## Haggai 1:4

The LORD asked, “You live in houses with wood panels.

My house still lies broken.

Is this the time to enjoy your own houses?”

## The people have too little

## Haggai 1:5

The LORD said, “Think carefully about what you do.”

## Haggai 1:6

“You plant many seeds. Yet you gather little grain.

You eat and drink. Yet you still want more.

You wear clothes. Yet you still feel cold.

You work for money. It soon runs out.

It is like putting money in a bag with holes.”

![A stalk of grain like the grain the people grew.](../pictures/grain.svg)

## Haggai 1:7

Again the LORD said, “Think carefully about what you do.”

## Haggai 1:8

“Go up into the hills. Bring down wood.

Use the wood to rebuild my house.

Then I will take joy in my house.

You will show me honor there.”

## Haggai 1:9

The LORD said, “You hoped for a large harvest.

You brought home very little. I blew it away.

Why? Each of you hurried to work on your own house.

My house still lay broken.”

## Haggai 1:10

“That is why no dew fell from the sky.”

Dew is the water that gathers on plants overnight.

The land gave the people little food.

## Haggai 1:11

The LORD said, “I called for a long dry time.”

The fields and mountains dried up.

People had little grain, new wine, or olive oil.

Everything they grew suffered.

The dry weather hurt people and animals too.

It spoiled the work people did.

## The people listen and begin work

## Haggai 1:12

Zerubbabel, Joshua, and all the people who remained in Judah listened.

They obeyed the LORD their God.

They obeyed Haggai because the LORD had sent him.

The people showed deep respect for the LORD.

## Haggai 1:13

Haggai brought the people this message from the LORD.

“I am with you,” the LORD said.

## Haggai 1:14

The LORD moved Zerubbabel and Joshua to act.

He moved the people to act too.

Together they began work on the temple of their God.

## Haggai 1:15

They began on day 24 of the sixth month.

Darius was still in his second year as king.
