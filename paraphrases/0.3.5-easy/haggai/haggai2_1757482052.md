# Haggai 2 — The LORD encourages the temple builders

**About this chapter**

Some people remembered the old temple. The LORD encouraged the people rebuilding it. Later he gave Haggai a promise for Zerubbabel.

## The LORD tells the builders to be strong

## Haggai 2:1

On day 21 of the seventh month, the LORD spoke again.

He gave Haggai the prophet a message.

## Haggai 2:2

The LORD told Haggai to speak to Zerubbabel, son of Shealtiel.

Zerubbabel governed Judah.

Haggai must also speak to Joshua, son of Jehozadak.

Joshua was the high priest. He led the priests.

The message was for all the people who remained too.

## Haggai 2:3

The LORD asked, “Who remembers how beautiful this temple once was?

How does it look to you now?

Does it seem like almost nothing beside the old temple?”

## Haggai 2:4

The LORD said, “Be strong, Zerubbabel.

Be strong, Joshua. Be strong, all you people.

Keep working. I am with you.”

These were the words of the LORD who leads heaven’s armies.

## Haggai 2:5

The LORD reminded them of his promise.

“I made this promise when you came out of Egypt.

My Spirit is still with you. Do not be afraid.”

## The LORD promises peace

## Haggai 2:6

The LORD said, “Soon I will shake everything again.

I will shake the sky and the earth.

I will shake the sea and the dry land.”

## Haggai 2:7

“I will shake all the nations.

People will come with their treasures.

I will fill this temple with my glory.”

His glory shows how great he is.

## Haggai 2:8

The LORD said, “The silver belongs to me.

The gold belongs to me too.”

## Haggai 2:9

“This temple will show my greatness even more than before.

Here I will give you peace,” the LORD said.

## Haggai asks the priests two questions

## Haggai 2:10

Later the LORD spoke to Haggai again.

It was day 24 of the ninth month.

Darius was in his second year as king.

## Haggai 2:11

The LORD said, “Ask the priests what my law says.”

## Haggai 2:12

Haggai asked, “Suppose someone wraps holy meat in his clothes.”

Holy meat was meat set apart for God.

“Suppose the cloth touches bread, stew, wine, oil, or other food.

Does the food become holy?”

The priests answered, “No.”

## Haggai 2:13

Haggai asked about someone who had touched a dead body.

God’s law called that person unclean for worship.

Haggai asked, “If that person touches the food, does it become unclean?”

The priests answered, “Yes.”

## Haggai 2:14

Then Haggai gave the LORD’s answer.

“That is how it is with these people before me.

What they do is unclean for worship.

So are the gifts they bring me.”

## The LORD asks the people to remember their harvests

## Haggai 2:15

The LORD said, “From today on, think carefully.

Remember the time before you began laying stones for my temple.”

## Haggai 2:16

“You expected 20 measures of grain.

You found only 10.

You went to the place where you pressed grapes.

You expected 50 measures of wine.

You found only 20.”

![A stalk of grain like the grain the people gathered.](../pictures/grain.svg)

## Haggai 2:17

The LORD said, “I sent diseases that damaged your plants.

I sent hail.” Hail is ice that falls from the sky.

All their hard work suffered.

The LORD said, “Yet you did not turn back to me.”

## Haggai 2:18

The LORD said, “Remember this day.

It is day 24 of the ninth month.

Today you laid the foundation of my temple.”

A foundation is the base that holds a building up.

## Haggai 2:19

The LORD asked, “Is there still seed in the barn?

Your grapevines have not yet grown grapes.

Your fig, pomegranate, and olive trees have no fruit yet.

But from today on, I will bless you.”

## The LORD gives Zerubbabel a promise

## Haggai 2:20

That same day the LORD spoke to Haggai a second time.

It was day 24 of the month.

## Haggai 2:21

The LORD gave Haggai a message for Zerubbabel, Judah’s governor.

“I am about to shake the sky and the earth.”

## Haggai 2:22

“I will bring down kings.

I will break the power of their kingdoms.

I will overturn their war chariots with their riders.

Horses and riders will fall.

The men will turn their swords against one another.”

## Haggai 2:23

The LORD said, “On that day I will take you, Zerubbabel, son of Shealtiel.

You are my servant. I have chosen you.

You will be like my signet ring.”

A king pressed his signet ring into soft clay.

Its mark showed that the king gave the order.

The ring was a sign of authority.
