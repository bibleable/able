# 2 Kings 3 — Water fills the valley

**About this chapter**

Three kings set out to fight Moab. When their water runs out, they ask Elisha for help.

## Jehoram becomes king of Israel

## 2 Kings 3:1

Jehoram, Ahab’s son, became king of Israel.

Jehoshaphat had ruled Judah for 18 years when Jehoram became king.

Jehoram ruled in Samaria for 12 years.

## 2 Kings 3:2

The LORD saw Jehoram do evil.

Still, Jehoram did one thing differently from his parents.

He removed the pillar his father made for worshiping Baal.

## 2 Kings 3:3

Long before, Jeroboam son of Nebat had led Israel into sin.

Jehoram kept doing those same wrong things.

## Moab stops obeying Israel

## 2 Kings 3:4

Mesha, king of Moab, raised sheep.

He used to pay the king of Israel with 100,000 lambs.

He also gave him the wool from 100,000 rams.

**Reading help**

A lamb is a young sheep. A ram is a male sheep.

## 2 Kings 3:5

After King Ahab died, Mesha refused to obey Israel’s king.

## 2 Kings 3:6

King Jehoram left Samaria and called Israel’s men together for war.

## 2 Kings 3:7

Jehoram sent word to Jehoshaphat, king of Judah.

“The king of Moab has turned against me.

Will you come and fight Moab with me?”

Jehoshaphat answered, “I will go with you.

My people and my horses are yours too.”

## Three kings run out of water

## 2 Kings 3:8

Jehoshaphat asked, “Which way shall we go?”

Jehoram answered, “Through the desert of Edom.”

## 2 Kings 3:9

The kings of Israel, Judah, and Edom set out together.

For seven days they followed a winding road.

Then they ran out of water for their army and animals.

**Pause and answer**

What did the three kings run out of?

## 2 Kings 3:10

Jehoram cried, “What a disaster!

The LORD has brought us three kings together.

Now Moab will capture us!”

## The kings go to Elisha

## 2 Kings 3:11

Jehoshaphat asked, “Is there a prophet of the LORD here?

We can ask him what the LORD says.”

One of Jehoram’s servants answered, “Elisha son of Shaphat is here.

He used to pour water on Elijah’s hands.”

**Reading help**

Elisha helped Elijah wash his hands. This was one way Elisha served Elijah.

## 2 Kings 3:12

Jehoshaphat said, “Elisha speaks the LORD’s word.”

So the three kings went down to see Elisha.

## 2 Kings 3:13

Elisha asked Jehoram, “Why come to me?

Go to the prophets your father and mother followed!”

Jehoram answered, “No! The LORD brought us three kings here.

Now Moab will capture us!”

## 2 Kings 3:14

Elisha answered, “I serve the living LORD who commands heaven’s armies.

As surely as he lives, I tell you this.

I respect Jehoshaphat, king of Judah.

Because he is here, I will listen to you.

Otherwise I would not even look at you.

## Elisha gives the LORD’s answer

## 2 Kings 3:15

Now bring me someone who plays the harp.”

As the musician played, the LORD’s power came upon Elisha.

![A harp with strings that a musician can pluck to make music.](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

## 2 Kings 3:16

Elisha said, “The LORD says, ‘Dig ditches throughout this valley.’

## 2 Kings 3:17

The LORD says, ‘You will see neither wind nor rain.

Yet water will fill this valley.

You, your cattle, and your other animals will drink.’

## 2 Kings 3:18

This is easy for the LORD.

He will also give you victory over Moab.

## 2 Kings 3:19

You will attack Moab’s walled cities and other important cities.

Its good trees will fall and its springs will stop flowing.

Stones will cover its good fields.”

## Water comes from Edom

## 2 Kings 3:20

The next morning came the time for the morning offering to God.

Suddenly water flowed from the direction of Edom.

Water filled the land.

**Pause and answer**

Which direction did the water come from?

## 2 Kings 3:21

The people of Moab heard the kings had come to fight.

Everyone old enough to fight gathered at Moab’s border.

## 2 Kings 3:22

Early that morning, sunlight shone on the water.

From Moab’s side, the water looked as red as blood.

## 2 Kings 3:23

The people of Moab cried, “That is blood!

The kings must have fought and killed each other.

Come, let us take what they left!”

**Pause and answer**

What did the people of Moab think they saw?

## The Israelites fight in Moab

## 2 Kings 3:24

The Moabites came to Israel’s camp.

Israel’s soldiers got up and attacked them.

The Moabites ran away.

Israel’s soldiers entered Moab and killed many there.

## 2 Kings 3:25

Israel’s soldiers destroyed the cities.

Stones covered the good fields.

The soldiers blocked the springs and cut down the good trees.

Only the stone walls of Kir-haraseth still stood.

Men with slings surrounded that city and attacked it too.

## 2 Kings 3:26

Mesha saw that he was losing the battle.

He and 700 men carrying swords tried to reach Edom’s king.

They could not break through.

## 2 Kings 3:27

Then Mesha took his oldest son, the next in line to be king.

On the city wall, Mesha sacrificed his son as a burnt offering.

Fierce anger rose against Israel.

So the Israelites left Moab and went home.
