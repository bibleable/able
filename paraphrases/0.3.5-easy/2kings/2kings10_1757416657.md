# 2 Kings 10 — Jehu rules Israel

**About this chapter**

Jehu ends the rule of Ahab’s family. He tears down Baal’s temple. But the people of Israel still worship the golden calves.

## Jehu writes to the leaders in Samaria

## 2 Kings 10:1

Ahab had 70 sons in Samaria.

Jehu sent letters there to the officials from Jezreel.

He also wrote to the elders and to the men raising Ahab’s sons.

## 2 Kings 10:2

Jehu wrote, “Your master’s sons are with you.

You have horses, chariots, weapons, and a city with strong walls.

## 2 Kings 10:3

Choose the son you think would be the best king.

Put him on his father’s throne.

Then fight for your master’s family.”

![A rolled scroll like those used for written messages.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## 2 Kings 10:4

The leaders were afraid. They said, “Two kings could not stand against Jehu.

How can we?”

## 2 Kings 10:5

The men in charge of the palace and city sent Jehu an answer.

The elders and the men raising the sons joined them.

“We are your servants. We will obey you.

We will choose no king. Do what you think best.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the leaders decide about choosing a king?

## The leaders receive a second letter

## 2 Kings 10:6

Jehu wrote a second letter to the city leaders who were raising the 70 sons.

He demanded proof that the leaders were on his side.

He wanted the sons’ heads in Jezreel by the next day.

## 2 Kings 10:7

When the letter came, the leaders killed all 70 sons.

They sent their heads in baskets to Jehu at Jezreel.

## 2 Kings 10:8

A messenger told Jehu the heads had arrived.

Jehu had them left in two piles by the city gate until morning.

## Jehu speaks at the city gate

## 2 Kings 10:9

In the morning, Jehu stood before the people.

He said, “You are innocent. I plotted against my master and killed him.

But who killed all these men?”

## 2 Kings 10:10

Jehu continued, “Every word the LORD spoke against Ahab’s family will come true.

The LORD has done what he promised through his servant Elijah.”

## 2 Kings 10:11

Jehu killed the rest of Ahab’s family in Jezreel.

He also killed Ahab’s leading men, close friends, and priests.

None of them survived.

## Jehu meets relatives of Ahaziah

## 2 Kings 10:12

Jehu set out for Samaria.

On the way, he reached a place called Beth-eked of the Shepherds.

## 2 Kings 10:13

There Jehu met relatives of Ahaziah, king of Judah.

“Who are you?” Jehu asked.

They answered, “We are Ahaziah’s relatives.

We have come to greet the sons of the king and the queen mother.”

**Reading help**

The queen mother is the king’s mother.

## 2 Kings 10:14

Jehu had his men take them prisoner.

His men killed all 42 at the well of Beth-eked.

## Jehonadab rides with Jehu

## 2 Kings 10:15

Farther along, Jehu met Jehonadab, son of Rechab.

Jehu greeted him. “Are you as loyal to me as I am to you?”

“I am,” said Jehonadab. Jehu asked for his hand.

Jehonadab held it out, and Jehu helped him into his chariot.

## 2 Kings 10:16

Jehu said, “Come and see how much I care about serving the LORD!”

Jehonadab rode with Jehu.

**Pause and answer**

Who rode with Jehu in his chariot?

## 2 Kings 10:17

In Samaria, Jehu killed everyone left there from Ahab’s family.

This was what the LORD had told Elijah.

## Jehu calls a gathering for Baal

## 2 Kings 10:18

Jehu gathered the people.

He said, “Ahab served Baal a little. I will serve Baal much more.”

## 2 Kings 10:19

Jehu said he would offer a great sacrifice to Baal.

He called for every prophet, priest, and servant of Baal.

He threatened death to anyone who stayed away.

Jehu was lying. He meant to kill Baal’s servants.

## 2 Kings 10:20

At Jehu’s command, the people announced a special gathering to worship Baal.

## 2 Kings 10:21

Word went out across Israel. All Baal’s servants came.

They filled Baal’s temple from end to end.

## Inside Baal’s temple

## 2 Kings 10:22

Jehu spoke to the man who looked after the clothes.

At Jehu’s request, the man brought clothes for Baal’s servants.

## 2 Kings 10:23

Jehu and Jehonadab entered the temple.

Jehu wanted only Baal’s servants there.

He asked whether anyone there served the LORD.

## 2 Kings 10:24

The offerings began, including offerings burned on the altar.

Eighty men stood outside. Jehu threatened them with death if anyone escaped.

## 2 Kings 10:25

When the burnt offering ended, Jehu gave the order to kill.

The guards and officers killed the worshipers.

They carried the bodies outside and entered the temple’s inner room.

## The people tear down the temple

## 2 Kings 10:26

They brought out the pillar used to worship Baal and burned it.

## 2 Kings 10:27

They broke down Baal’s pillar and tore down the temple.

People used the site as a toilet.

They still used it that way when this account was written.

## 2 Kings 10:28

That was how Jehu put an end to Baal worship in Israel.

## 2 Kings 10:29

Yet Jehu kept the sin that Jeroboam, son of Nebat, had led Israel into.

People still worshiped the golden calves at Bethel and Dan.

**Reading help**

The golden calves were statues shaped like young cattle. People bowed to them in worship.

## The LORD speaks to Jehu

## 2 Kings 10:30

The LORD told Jehu, “You did what I wanted done to Ahab’s family.

In this you did what was right in my eyes.

Four generations of your family will rule Israel after you.”

## 2 Kings 10:31

Yet Jehu did not follow the LORD’s teaching with his whole heart.

The LORD was Israel’s God.

Jehu kept the sins Jeroboam had led Israel into.

**Pause and answer**

What did people still worship at Bethel and Dan?

## Israel loses land

## 2 Kings 10:32

In those days the LORD began to take land away from Israel.

Hazael, king of Aram, defeated Israel’s people across their land.

## 2 Kings 10:33

Israel lost land east of the Jordan River.

This included Gilead, where Gad, Reuben, and Manasseh had land.

It stretched from Aroer by the Arnon Valley through Gilead to Bashan.

## 2 Kings 10:34

The Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel tells more about Jehu.

It records what Jehu did and how much power he had.

## 2 Kings 10:35

Jehu died, and his people buried him in Samaria.

His son Jehoahaz became king in his place.

## 2 Kings 10:36

Jehu had ruled Israel from Samaria for 28 years.
