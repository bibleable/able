# 2 Kings 2 — Elisha picks up Elijah’s cloak

**About this chapter**

Elisha stays with his teacher Elijah. After the LORD takes Elijah to heaven, Elisha returns to the Jordan River alone.

## Elisha stays with Elijah

## 2 Kings 2:1

Elijah and Elisha set out from Gilgal.

Soon the LORD would take Elijah up to heaven in a whirlwind.

**Reading help**

A whirlwind is a strong wind that spins around.

## 2 Kings 2:2

Elijah told Elisha, “Please stay here.

The LORD has sent me to Bethel.”

Elisha answered, “As surely as the LORD lives and you live, I will stay with you.”

So they went to Bethel together.

## 2 Kings 2:3

A group of prophets from Bethel came out to meet Elisha.

They asked, “Do you know the LORD will take your master away today?”

“Yes, I know,” Elisha answered.

“Please do not speak about it.”

## The two men go on to the Jordan

## 2 Kings 2:4

Elijah told Elisha, “Please stay here.

The LORD has sent me to Jericho.”

Elisha answered, “As surely as the LORD lives and you live, I will stay with you.”

So they went to Jericho together.

## 2 Kings 2:5

A group of prophets in Jericho came to Elisha.

They asked, “Do you know the LORD will take your master away today?”

“Yes, I know,” Elisha answered.

“Please do not speak about it.”

## 2 Kings 2:6

Elijah told Elisha, “Please stay here.

The LORD has sent me to the Jordan River.”

Elisha answered, “As surely as the LORD lives and you live, I will stay with you.”

So the two men went on together.

## Elijah and Elisha cross the river

## 2 Kings 2:7

Elijah and Elisha stood beside the Jordan.

Fifty men from the group of prophets watched from a distance.

## 2 Kings 2:8

Elijah rolled up his cloak and struck the water.

The water moved apart on both sides.

Elijah and Elisha walked across on dry ground.

## 2 Kings 2:9

On the other side, Elijah asked Elisha a question.

“What can I do for you before the LORD takes me away?”

Elisha answered, “Please let me receive a double share of your spirit.”

**Reading help**

A double share was the share an eldest son received. Elisha asks to carry on Elijah’s work as a prophet.

## 2 Kings 2:10

Elijah said, “You have asked for something hard.

If you see the LORD take me away, you will receive it.

If you do not see me go, you will not receive it.”

## The LORD takes Elijah to heaven

## 2 Kings 2:11

The two men walked on, talking together.

Suddenly horses of fire and a chariot of fire came between them.

Elijah went up to heaven in a whirlwind.

## 2 Kings 2:12

Elisha saw him go and cried out, “My father! My father! The chariots and horsemen of Israel!”

Then Elisha could no longer see Elijah.

Elisha tore his own clothes in two.

**Reading help**

Elisha calls his teacher “my father.” Tearing his clothes shows his sorrow.

## Elisha crosses the Jordan again

## 2 Kings 2:13

Elijah’s cloak had fallen to the ground.

Elisha picked it up.

He went back to the edge of the Jordan.

## 2 Kings 2:14

Elisha struck the water with Elijah’s cloak.

He asked, “Where now is the LORD, the God of Elijah?”

The water moved apart on both sides.

Elisha walked across.

## 2 Kings 2:15

The prophets from Jericho saw what happened.

They said, “The spirit of Elijah now rests on Elisha.”

They went to meet Elisha and bowed low before him.

**Reading help**

The prophets saw that God was helping Elisha, just as God had helped Elijah.

## Fifty men look for Elijah

## 2 Kings 2:16

The prophets said, “We have 50 brave men here.

Please let them look for your master.

Perhaps the LORD’s Spirit carried him to a mountain or a valley.”

Elisha answered, “Do not send them.”

## 2 Kings 2:17

The prophets kept asking until Elisha felt embarrassed.

At last he said, “Send them.”

The 50 men looked for Elijah for three days.

They could not find him.

## 2 Kings 2:18

The men came back to Elisha in Jericho.

He asked, “Didn’t I tell you not to go?”

**Pause and answer**

How long did the men look for Elijah?

## The LORD makes the water good

## 2 Kings 2:19

The men of Jericho told Elisha, “Sir, look at our city.

It is in a good place.

But the water is bad, and the land grows no crops.”

## 2 Kings 2:20

Elisha said, “Bring me a new bowl with some salt in it.”

They brought him the bowl.

## 2 Kings 2:21

Elisha went to the spring where the water came from.

He threw the salt into the spring.

He said, “The LORD says, ‘I have made this water good again.

It will no longer cause death or keep the land from growing crops.’”

## 2 Kings 2:22

The water stayed good, just as Elisha had said.

It was still good when this account was written.

**Pause and answer**

Who said, “I have made this water good again”?

## Boys shout at Elisha near Bethel

## 2 Kings 2:23

Elisha left Jericho and walked up the road to Bethel.

Some boys came out of the city and made fun of him.

They shouted, “Go up, bald head! Go up, bald head!”

## 2 Kings 2:24

Elisha turned around and looked at them.

In the LORD’s name, he called down a curse on them.

Two female bears came out of the woods.

They badly hurt 42 of the boys.

**Reading help**

Here a curse means words asking God to punish someone.

## 2 Kings 2:25

Elisha went on to Mount Carmel.

Then he returned to Samaria.
