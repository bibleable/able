# 2 Kings 9 — Jehu becomes king of Israel

**About this chapter**

A young prophet brings Jehu a message from the LORD. Jehu rides to Jezreel, where the kings of Israel and Judah meet him on Naboth’s land.

## Elisha sends a young prophet to Jehu

## 2 Kings 9:1

Elisha called a young man from the group of prophets.

“Tuck your cloak into your belt,” Elisha said.

“Take this jar of oil to Ramoth-gilead.

## 2 Kings 9:2

Look for Jehu, son of Jehoshaphat and grandson of Nimshi.

Take Jehu away from the others into a private room.

## 2 Kings 9:3

Pour the oil on his head. Give him the LORD’s message.

‘I anoint you king of Israel.’

Then open the door and run. Do not wait.”

## 2 Kings 9:4

The young prophet went to Ramoth-gilead.

## 2 Kings 9:5

The army commanders were sitting together.

The young prophet said, “Commander, I have a message for you.”

Jehu asked, “Which one of us?”

The prophet answered, “You, commander.”

## The prophet pours oil on Jehu

## 2 Kings 9:6

Jehu stood up and went into the house.

The prophet poured oil on Jehu’s head.

He said, “The LORD, the God of Israel, says, ‘I anoint you king over my people Israel.

**Reading help**

To anoint someone means to pour oil on them. Here the oil shows that God has chosen Jehu as king.

## 2 Kings 9:7

You will bring down the family of your master Ahab.

Jezebel killed my prophets and many others who served me.

I will punish Ahab’s family for those deaths.

## 2 Kings 9:8

Ahab’s whole family will come to an end.

Every male in his family in Israel will die.

This includes slaves and free men alike.

## 2 Kings 9:9

Ahab’s family will end like the families of two other kings.

They were Jeroboam son of Nebat and Baasha son of Ahijah.

## 2 Kings 9:10

Dogs will eat Jezebel’s body on the land at Jezreel.

No one will bury her.’”

Then the young prophet opened the door and ran.

## The officers call Jehu king

## 2 Kings 9:11

Jehu went back to the other officers.

They asked, “Is all well? Why did that madman come to you?”

Jehu said, “You know people like him and the things they say.”

## 2 Kings 9:12

They answered, “That is a lie! Tell us!”

Jehu said, “Among other things, he gave me the LORD’s message.

‘I anoint you king of Israel.’”

## 2 Kings 9:13

Each officer quickly spread his cloak under Jehu on the steps.

They blew a ram’s horn and shouted, “Jehu is king!”

## Jehu rides to Jezreel

## 2 Kings 9:14

Jehu, Nimshi’s grandson, plotted against Joram, king of Israel.

Joram and Israel’s army had been defending Ramoth-gilead against Hazael, king of Aram.

## 2 Kings 9:15

Aram’s soldiers had wounded Joram in that battle.

Joram had returned to Jezreel to get well.

Jehu told the commanders, “If you want me as king, keep this news from reaching Jezreel.”

## 2 Kings 9:16

Jehu rode to Jezreel in his chariot.

Joram was resting there. Ahaziah, king of Judah, was visiting Joram.

## Two riders meet Jehu

## 2 Kings 9:17

A guard stood on a tower in Jezreel.

“I see soldiers coming!” he called.

Joram said, “Send a rider to ask if they come in peace.”

## 2 Kings 9:18

The rider met Jehu and asked the king’s question.

“Have you come in peace?”

Jehu answered, “What do you know about peace? Ride behind me.”

The guard reported, “The rider reached them. He is staying with them.”

## 2 Kings 9:19

Joram sent another rider. He also asked Jehu, “Have you come in peace?”

Jehu answered, “What do you know about peace? Ride behind me.”

## 2 Kings 9:20

The guard reported, “The second rider is staying with them too.

I think Jehu is driving the chariot. I know his wild driving!”

## Joram meets Jehu on Naboth’s land

## 2 Kings 9:21

Joram called for his chariot.

Joram of Israel and Ahaziah of Judah each rode out in a chariot.

They met Jehu on land that had belonged to Naboth of Jezreel.

**Reading help**

Naboth had owned a vineyard here. Ahab took the land after Jezebel had Naboth killed. [Read Naboth’s story in 1 Kings 21.](../1kings/1kings21_1757415158.md)

## 2 Kings 9:22

Joram asked, “Have you come in peace, Jehu?”

Jehu answered, “How can there be peace?

Your mother Jezebel still worships idols and practices witchcraft.”

## 2 Kings 9:23

Joram turned to flee. He called to Ahaziah, “Jehu has betrayed us!”

## 2 Kings 9:24

Jehu shot Joram with an arrow.

It pierced his heart. Joram fell dead in his chariot.

## Jehu remembers what the LORD said

## 2 Kings 9:25

Jehu told his officer Bidkar, “Put Joram’s body in Naboth’s field.

Remember when we rode behind Joram’s father Ahab?

We heard the LORD’s message against Ahab.

## 2 Kings 9:26

The LORD said, ‘Yesterday I saw the blood of Naboth and his sons.

I will repay you here on this land.’

So put Joram’s body there, as the LORD said.”

## Ahaziah dies at Megiddo

## 2 Kings 9:27

Ahaziah, king of Judah, fled toward Beth-haggan.

Jehu followed and ordered his men to attack Ahaziah too.

They wounded him in his chariot on the hill of Gur near Ibleam.

Ahaziah escaped to Megiddo, where he died.

## 2 Kings 9:28

His servants took his body to Jerusalem in a chariot.

They buried him with his ancestors in the City of David.

## 2 Kings 9:29

Ahaziah had become king of Judah in the eleventh year that Joram, Ahab’s son, ruled Israel.

## Jezebel calls down from a window

## 2 Kings 9:30

Jezebel heard that Jehu had reached Jezreel.

She put paint around her eyes and decorated her head.

Then she looked down from a window.

## 2 Kings 9:31

As Jehu came through the gate, Jezebel called out, “Have you come in peace, Zimri, you murderer of your master?”

**Reading help**

Zimri was an earlier king who killed his master. Jezebel uses his name to accuse Jehu of doing the same thing.

## 2 Kings 9:32

Jehu looked up at the window.

“Who is on my side?” he called. “Who?”

Two or three royal servants looked down at him.

## 2 Kings 9:33

Jehu shouted for them to throw Jezebel down. They did.

Her blood splashed on the wall and on the horses.

The horses trampled her.

## Jehu remembers Elijah’s words

## 2 Kings 9:34

Jehu went inside to eat and drink.

He said, “Go and bury that cursed woman.

She was a king’s daughter.”

## 2 Kings 9:35

The men went out to bury Jezebel.

Only her skull, her feet, and the palms of her hands remained.

## 2 Kings 9:36

The men told Jehu what they found.

Jehu said, “The LORD spoke about this through Elijah from Tishbe.

The LORD said, ‘Dogs will eat Jezebel’s body at Jezreel.

## 2 Kings 9:37

What remains of her will lie like dung on that field.

No one will be able to say, “This is Jezebel.”’”
