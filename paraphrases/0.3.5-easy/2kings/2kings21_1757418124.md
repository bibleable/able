# 2 Kings 21 — The LORD warns Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

Manasseh brings idols into the LORD’s temple. The LORD sends prophets to warn Judah. Later, Manasseh’s son Amon follows his father’s example.

## Manasseh rebuilds places of worship

## 2 Kings 21:1

Manasseh was 12 when he became king of Judah.

He ruled in Jerusalem for 55 years.

His mother was Hephzibah.

## 2 Kings 21:2

Manasseh did things the LORD saw as evil.

He copied the terrible practices of the nations the LORD had driven out before Israel.

## 2 Kings 21:3

His father Hezekiah had torn down the hilltop places of worship.

Manasseh built them again. He built altars for Baal.

Like King Ahab of Israel, he made a pole for worshiping Asherah.

Manasseh also worshiped the sun, moon, and stars.

## Manasseh puts other altars in the temple

## 2 Kings 21:4

Manasseh even built altars in the LORD’s temple.

The LORD had said, “In Jerusalem I will put my name.”

## 2 Kings 21:5

The temple had two courtyards.

In both, Manasseh built altars for worshiping the sun, moon, and stars.

**Reading help**

A courtyard is an open space beside a building, with walls around it.

## 2 Kings 21:6

Manasseh burned his own son as a sacrifice.

He used magic and looked for signs of the future.

He asked people who claimed to speak with spirits for advice.

The evil he did made the LORD very angry.

## The people turn away from the LORD’s promise

## 2 Kings 21:7

Manasseh put his carved Asherah pole inside the temple.

The LORD had told David and his son Solomon, “Of all the places in Israel, I chose Jerusalem.

This temple and this city will bear my name forever.

## 2 Kings 21:8

I gave your ancestors this land.

I will never again make the Israelites leave it if they obey all my commands.

My servant Moses gave them my whole law.”

## 2 Kings 21:9

The people refused to listen. Manasseh led them into sin.

They did even worse things than the nations the LORD had destroyed before them.

## The prophets bring a warning

## 2 Kings 21:10

The LORD sent a message through his servants the prophets.

## 2 Kings 21:11

They said, “King Manasseh has done terrible things.

He has done worse than the Amorites who lived here before him.

He has led Judah to sin by worshiping his idols.

## 2 Kings 21:12

So the LORD, the God of Israel, says, ‘Disaster is coming to Jerusalem and Judah.

The news will make the ears of everyone who hears it ring.

## 2 Kings 21:13

I will use the same measuring line I used for Samaria.

I will use the same weighted line I used for Ahab’s family.

I will wipe Jerusalem like a bowl that someone wipes clean and turns upside down.

![An empty bowl standing upright, then turned upside down.](../pictures/jerusalem-empty-bowl.svg)

**Reading help**

Builders use lines to measure and check their work. The LORD means he will judge Jerusalem by the same standard he used for Samaria and Ahab’s family. The empty bowl shows how little will be left in Jerusalem.

## 2 Kings 21:14

I will give up those who remain of my people.

I will hand them over to their enemies.

Their enemies will take them and everything they own.

## 2 Kings 21:15

Their ancestors came out of Egypt long ago.

From that day until now, my people have kept doing evil.

They have made me angry.’”

**Pause and answer**

What does the LORD say the enemies will take?

## Manasseh’s rule ends

## 2 Kings 21:16

Manasseh also killed many innocent people.

Their blood filled Jerusalem from one end to the other.

Manasseh led the people of Judah to do evil in the LORD’s sight.

## 2 Kings 21:17

The Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah tells more about Manasseh.

It records what he did, including his sin.

## 2 Kings 21:18

Manasseh died. His people buried him in his palace garden, the garden of Uzza.

His son Amon became king.

## Amon follows his father’s example

## 2 Kings 21:19

Amon was 22 when he became king.

He ruled in Jerusalem for two years.

His mother was Meshullemeth from Jotbah. Her father was Haruz.

## 2 Kings 21:20

Like his father Manasseh, Amon did what the LORD saw as evil.

## 2 Kings 21:21

Amon followed his father’s example in everything.

He served and worshiped the same idols as his father.

## 2 Kings 21:22

Amon turned away from the LORD, the God of his ancestors.

He refused to live as the LORD taught.

## Josiah becomes king

## 2 Kings 21:23

Amon’s servants plotted against him.

They killed him in his palace.

## 2 Kings 21:24

The people of Judah killed those who had plotted against Amon.

Then they made Amon’s son Josiah king.

## 2 Kings 21:25

The Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah records the other things Amon did.

## 2 Kings 21:26

The people buried Amon in his tomb in the garden of Uzza.

His son Josiah ruled Judah after him.
