# 2 Kings 19 — Hezekiah spreads a letter before the LORD

**About this chapter**

Assyria’s king threatens Jerusalem. Hezekiah brings the letter to the LORD and asks for help. Isaiah brings back the LORD’s answer.

## Hezekiah asks Isaiah to pray

## 2 Kings 19:1

King Hezekiah heard what Assyria’s spokesman had said.

He tore his clothes and put on rough sackcloth.

Then he went into the LORD’s temple.

## 2 Kings 19:2

He sent Eliakim, who managed the palace, and Shebna, his secretary, to Isaiah the prophet.

The leading priests went too. They all wore sackcloth.

Isaiah was the son of Amoz.

## 2 Kings 19:3

They took this message from Hezekiah.

“Today we face trouble, blame, and shame.

We are like a mother whose baby is ready to be born.

She has no strength left to give birth.

**Reading help**

Hezekiah compares his people to an exhausted mother. They have reached a moment of danger and need help.

## 2 Kings 19:4

Assyria’s king sent his spokesman to insult the living God.

Perhaps the LORD your God will hear and rebuke him.

Please pray for those of us who are still alive.”

## Isaiah sends an answer

## 2 Kings 19:5

Hezekiah’s servants went to Isaiah.

## 2 Kings 19:6

Isaiah said, “Tell Hezekiah what the LORD says.

‘You heard Assyria’s servants insult me. Do not be afraid of their words.

## 2 Kings 19:7

I will put a spirit in their king.

When he hears a rumor, he will go home.

There I will cause him to die by the sword.’”

**Pause and answer**

Who did Hezekiah ask to pray for his people?

## Sennacherib sends another threat

## 2 Kings 19:8

Assyria’s spokesman heard that his king had left Lachish.

He went back and found the king fighting at Libnah.

## 2 Kings 19:9

Sennacherib, king of Assyria, heard a warning.

Tirhakah, king of Cush, was coming to fight him.

Sennacherib sent another message to Hezekiah.

## 2 Kings 19:10

He told his messengers, “Say this to Hezekiah, king of Judah.

‘You trust your God. Do not let him fool you by saying Jerusalem will escape me.

## 2 Kings 19:11

You know what Assyria’s kings did to other countries.

They destroyed them. Do you think you will escape?

## 2 Kings 19:12

My ancestors destroyed Gozan, Haran, and Rezeph.

They also destroyed the people of Eden in Telassar.

Did their gods save them?

## 2 Kings 19:13

Where are the kings of Hamath and Arpad?

Where are the kings of Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah?’”

## Hezekiah brings the letter to the temple

## 2 Kings 19:14

Hezekiah took the letter from the messengers and read it.

He went to the LORD’s temple.

There he spread the letter out before the LORD.

## 2 Kings 19:15

Hezekiah prayed, “LORD, God of Israel, you sit as king between the cherubim.

You alone are God over every kingdom on earth.

You made heaven and earth.

**Reading help**

Cherubim are heavenly beings. Figures of two cherubim stood over the sacred chest in the temple.

## 2 Kings 19:16

LORD, please listen. Please look and see.

Hear Sennacherib’s words. He sent them to insult you, the living God.

## 2 Kings 19:17

LORD, it is true. Assyria’s kings destroyed those nations and their lands.

## 2 Kings 19:18

They threw the nations’ gods into the fire.

People had made them from wood and stone.

That is why the fire could destroy them.

## 2 Kings 19:19

LORD our God, please save us from Sennacherib.

Then every kingdom on earth will know that you alone are God.”

**Pause and answer**

What did Hezekiah do with the letter at the temple?

## The LORD answers Hezekiah

## 2 Kings 19:20

Isaiah sent Hezekiah this answer from the LORD, Israel’s God.

“I heard your prayer about Sennacherib, king of Assyria.”

## 2 Kings 19:21

Isaiah gave the LORD’s message against Sennacherib.

“The young daughter of Zion laughs at you.

Jerusalem shakes her head as you leave.

**Reading help**

Zion is a name for Jerusalem. The LORD speaks of the city as a young woman. She represents the people who live there.

## 2 Kings 19:22

Who have you insulted? Who heard you shout and boast?

You raised your proud eyes against the Holy One of Israel!

## The LORD repeats Sennacherib’s boasts

## 2 Kings 19:23

Your servants insulted the Lord for you. You boasted, ‘My many chariots climbed the highest mountains in Lebanon.

I cut down its tallest cedars and finest cypress trees.

I reached its farthest places and thickest forests.

## 2 Kings 19:24

I dug wells and drank water in foreign lands.

Under my feet, all Egypt’s streams dried up.’

## The LORD tells Sennacherib he must go home

## 2 Kings 19:25

Sennacherib, have you never heard what I planned long ago?

Now I have made it happen.

You broke walled cities into heaps of stone.

## 2 Kings 19:26

The people there had no power left.

They were afraid and ashamed.

They were like young plants in a field, like grass on a roof that dries up before it grows.

## 2 Kings 19:27

I know when you sit down. I know when you come and go.

I know how you rage against me.

## 2 Kings 19:28

I have heard your anger and your proud words.

I will put my hook in your nose and my bit in your mouth.

I will turn you back the way you came.”

**Reading help**

A bit is a piece that fits in an animal’s mouth so someone can guide it. The picture means that the LORD will make Sennacherib turn back.

## A sign for the people of Judah

## 2 Kings 19:29

Isaiah gave Hezekiah a sign.

“This year you will eat what grows by itself.

Next year you will eat what grows that way again.

In the third year you will plant seed and gather crops.

You will plant vineyards and eat their grapes.

## 2 Kings 19:30

The people left in Judah will be like a plant.

Its roots grow down, and its fruit grows above.

![A vine bearing bunches of grapes.](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

**Reading help**

A plant with strong roots can grow fruit. In the same way, the people who survive will settle in Judah and grow strong again.

## 2 Kings 19:31

People will survive and go out from Jerusalem, from Mount Zion.

The LORD who commands heaven’s armies is determined to do this.

## The LORD promises to protect Jerusalem

## 2 Kings 19:32

The LORD says this about Assyria’s king.

‘He will never enter Jerusalem or shoot an arrow into it.

He will never come against it with a shield or build a ramp against its wall.

## 2 Kings 19:33

He will go home the way he came.

He will never enter this city. I, the LORD, have spoken.

## 2 Kings 19:34

I will protect this city and save it.

I will do this for my own sake and for my servant David.’”

## Sennacherib returns to Nineveh

## 2 Kings 19:35

That night the LORD’s angel went into the Assyrian camp.

The angel struck down 185,000 men.

In the morning, those who got up saw the bodies.

## 2 Kings 19:36

Sennacherib left the camp and went home.

He stayed in Nineveh.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Sennacherib go after he left the camp?

## 2 Kings 19:37

One day Sennacherib was worshiping in the temple of his god Nisroch.

His sons Adrammelech and Sharezer killed him there.

They escaped to the land of Ararat.

Another son, Esar-haddon, became king.
