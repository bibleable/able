# 2 Kings 4 — Oil for a widow and life for a child

**About this chapter**

A widow needs help to keep her sons with her. In Shunem, another mother comes to Elisha when her child dies. Later, Elisha helps people who need food.

## A widow asks Elisha for help

## 2 Kings 4:1

A widow cried out to Elisha for help.

“My husband served with the prophets. You know he honored the LORD.

He owed a man money. Now that man wants to take my two sons as slaves!”

## 2 Kings 4:2

Elisha asked, “How can I help you?

What do you have at home?”

She answered, “All I have is one jar of oil.”

## Empty jars fill with oil

## 2 Kings 4:3

Elisha said, “Borrow empty jars from all your neighbors.

Bring home as many as you can.

## 2 Kings 4:4

Go inside with your sons and shut the door.

Pour oil into each jar. Set the full jars aside.”

## 2 Kings 4:5

The widow went home. She and her sons shut the door.

Her sons kept bringing jars. She kept pouring oil.

## 2 Kings 4:6

When the jars were full, she said, “Bring another jar.”

Her son answered, “There are no more jars.”

Then the oil stopped flowing.

## 2 Kings 4:7

The widow told Elisha what had happened.

Elisha said, “Sell the oil. Pay what you owe.

Use the rest to live on with your sons.”

## A woman makes room for Elisha

## 2 Kings 4:8

One day Elisha came to a town called Shunem.

A respected woman there urged him to stay for a meal.

After that, Elisha stopped to eat whenever he passed by.

## 2 Kings 4:9

The woman told her husband, “This man often visits us.

I know he is a holy man who serves God.

## 2 Kings 4:10

Let us make him a small room upstairs.

We can put a bed, table, chair, and lamp there.

Then he can stay there when he visits.”

## 2 Kings 4:11

One day Elisha came back.

He went upstairs to his room to rest.

## Elisha promises the woman a son

## 2 Kings 4:12

Elisha told his servant Gehazi, “Call the woman from Shunem.”

Gehazi called her, and she came.

## 2 Kings 4:13

Elisha asked Gehazi to give her this message.

“You have taken such care of us. How can we help you?

Shall we speak to the king or the army commander for you?”

She answered, “I have a home among my own people.”

## 2 Kings 4:14

Elisha asked Gehazi, “Then what can we do for her?”

Gehazi said, “She has no son, and her husband is old.”

## 2 Kings 4:15

Elisha said, “Call her.”

Gehazi called the woman. She stood in the doorway.

## 2 Kings 4:16

Elisha said, “This time next year, you will hold a son in your arms.”

She answered, “No, sir! You are a man of God.

Please do not lie to me.”

## 2 Kings 4:17

The woman became pregnant. At that same time the next year, she had a son.

It happened just as Elisha had said.

**Pause and answer**

What happened a year after Elisha spoke to the woman?

## The boy dies

## 2 Kings 4:18

The boy grew. One day he went to his father in the fields.

His father was with the workers gathering the crops.

## 2 Kings 4:19

The boy cried out to his father, “My head! My head!”

His father told a servant, “Carry him to his mother.”

## 2 Kings 4:20

The servant carried him home. The boy sat on his mother’s lap until noon.

Then he died.

## 2 Kings 4:21

His mother carried him upstairs and laid him on Elisha’s bed.

She shut the door and went out.

## His mother goes to Mount Carmel

## 2 Kings 4:22

She called to her husband, “Please send a servant and a donkey.

I want to go quickly to the man of God and come back.”

## 2 Kings 4:23

Her husband asked, “Why see him today?

Today is neither the New Moon festival nor the Sabbath.”

She answered, “Everything is all right.”

**Reading help**

The New Moon festival marked the start of a month. The Sabbath was the weekly day of rest.

## 2 Kings 4:24

She put a saddle on the donkey.

She told her servant, “Go ahead quickly.

Slow down only if I ask you to.”

## 2 Kings 4:25

She traveled to Mount Carmel, where Elisha was staying.

Elisha saw her far away. He told Gehazi, “Look! It’s the woman from Shunem.

## 2 Kings 4:26

Run to meet her. Ask how she is.

Ask about her husband and her child too.”

She told Gehazi, “Everything is all right.”

## The mother holds on to Elisha

## 2 Kings 4:27

At the mountain, she held tightly to Elisha’s feet.

Gehazi tried to push her away.

Elisha said, “Leave her alone. Something has hurt her deeply.

The LORD has kept it from me. He has not told me what happened.”

## 2 Kings 4:28

She asked Elisha, “Sir, did I ask you for a son?

Didn’t I ask you to tell me the truth?”

## 2 Kings 4:29

Elisha told Gehazi, “Tuck up your robe. Take my walking stick and go.

Do not stop to greet anyone. If anyone greets you, keep going.

Lay my stick on the boy’s face.”

## 2 Kings 4:30

The mother told Elisha, “As surely as the LORD lives and you live, I will stay with you.”

So Elisha got up and followed her.

## 2 Kings 4:31

Gehazi went ahead. He laid the stick on the boy’s face.

The boy made no sound. He did not move.

Gehazi went back to meet Elisha.

He said, “The boy has not woken up.”

## The boy opens his eyes

## 2 Kings 4:32

Elisha reached the house. The boy lay dead on Elisha’s bed.

## 2 Kings 4:33

Elisha went into the room and shut the door.

Alone with the boy, Elisha prayed to the LORD.

## 2 Kings 4:34

Elisha climbed onto the bed and lay over the boy.

He placed his mouth against the boy’s mouth, his eyes against the boy’s eyes, and his hands against the boy’s hands.

The boy’s body grew warm.

## 2 Kings 4:35

Elisha got up and walked back and forth across the room.

Then he lay over the boy again.

The boy sneezed seven times and opened his eyes.

## 2 Kings 4:36

Elisha told Gehazi, “Call the woman from Shunem.”

Gehazi called her. When she came, Elisha said, “Pick up your son.”

## 2 Kings 4:37

She fell at Elisha’s feet and bowed to the ground.

Then she picked up her son and left.

## Elisha shares stew in Gilgal

## 2 Kings 4:38

Elisha returned to Gilgal. There was very little food in the land.

A group of prophets sat at Elisha’s feet.

He told his servant, “Put the big pot on the fire.

Make stew for the prophets.”

## 2 Kings 4:39

One man went to the fields to gather herbs.

He found gourds growing on a wild vine.

He carried home as many as his robe could hold.

No one knew what they were. He cut them up into the stew.

## 2 Kings 4:40

The men received their stew. When they tasted it, they cried out to Elisha.

“Man of God, there is death in the pot!”

They could not eat it.

**Reading help**

The men meant that eating the stew could kill them.

## 2 Kings 4:41

Elisha asked for flour and threw it into the pot.

He said, “Serve the stew to the people.”

Now there was nothing harmful in the pot.

## Twenty loaves feed a hundred people

## 2 Kings 4:42

A man from Baal-shalishah brought Elisha a sack with 20 loaves of bread.

He had made the bread from the first ripe barley grain.

Elisha said, “Give the people this bread to eat.”

## 2 Kings 4:43

His servant asked, “How can I feed 100 men with 20 loaves?”

Elisha said again, “Give them the bread.

The LORD says, ‘They will eat and have some left over.’”

## 2 Kings 4:44

The servant gave them the bread. They ate and had some left over.

It happened just as the LORD had said.

What happened to the bread after everyone ate?
