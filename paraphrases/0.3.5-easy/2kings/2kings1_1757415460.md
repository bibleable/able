# 2 Kings 1 — Elijah brings a message to Ahaziah

**About this chapter**

King Ahaziah wants to know if he will get well. Elijah brings him the LORD’s answer.

## Ahaziah sends messengers to Ekron

## 2 Kings 1:1

After King Ahab died, the people of Moab rebelled.

They refused to obey Israel any longer.

## 2 Kings 1:2

King Ahaziah was in an upstairs room in Samaria.

He fell through a screen made of crossed strips.

The fall hurt him.

Ahaziah sent messengers to ask Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron, a question.

“Will I get well?”

## 2 Kings 1:3

The angel of the LORD spoke to Elijah from Tishbe.

“Go and meet the king’s messengers.

Ask them, ‘Why are you going to Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron?

Is there no God in Israel?’

## 2 Kings 1:4

Give the king this message from the LORD.

‘You will never get up from your bed.

You will die.’”

So Elijah went.

**Reading help**

The LORD’s question reminds Ahaziah that he can ask the God of Israel for an answer.

## The messengers come back

## 2 Kings 1:5

The messengers came back to Ahaziah.

The king asked, “Why have you come back?”

## 2 Kings 1:6

They answered, “A man met us.

He told us to bring you this message from the LORD.

‘Is there no God in Israel?

Why did you send men to ask Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron?

Because you did this, you will never get up from your bed.

You will die.’”

## 2 Kings 1:7

Ahaziah asked, “What did the man who met you look like?”

## 2 Kings 1:8

They answered, “He was a hairy man.

He wore a leather belt around his waist.”

The king said, “That was Elijah from Tishbe!”

## Two captains order Elijah to come down

## 2 Kings 1:9

Ahaziah sent a captain and 50 men to Elijah.

Elijah was sitting on top of a hill.

The captain went up to him.

“Man of God, the king orders you to come down.”

**Reading help**

The captain calls Elijah a “man of God.” Elijah is a prophet. He speaks God’s message to people.

## 2 Kings 1:10

Elijah answered, “If I am a man of God, may fire come down from heaven.

May it burn up you and your 50 men.”

Fire came down from heaven.

The captain and all 50 men died in the fire.

## 2 Kings 1:11

Ahaziah sent a second captain with 50 men.

The captain said, “Man of God, the king orders you to come down at once!”

## 2 Kings 1:12

Elijah answered again, “If I am a man of God, may fire come down from heaven.

May it burn up you and your 50 men.”

God’s fire came down from heaven.

The second captain and his 50 men died.

## The third captain asks to live

## 2 Kings 1:13

Ahaziah sent a third captain with 50 men.

This captain went up and knelt before Elijah.

He begged, “Man of God, our lives are precious.

Please spare my life and the lives of these 50 men who serve you.

## 2 Kings 1:14

Fire came down from heaven.

It burned up the first two captains and their men.

Please let me live.”

## 2 Kings 1:15

The angel of the LORD told Elijah, “Go down with this captain.

You do not need to fear him.”

Elijah got up and went with the captain to the king.

## Elijah speaks to Ahaziah

## 2 Kings 1:16

Elijah told Ahaziah, “The LORD asks, ‘Is there no God in Israel whose word you can seek?

Why did you send messengers to Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron?

Because you did this, you will never get up from your bed.

You will die.’”

## 2 Kings 1:17

Ahaziah died, just as the LORD had told Elijah.

Ahaziah had no son.

So Jehoram became king of Israel.

Another man named Jehoram was king of Judah.

He was Jehoshaphat’s son, then in his second year as king.

## 2 Kings 1:18

The Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel tells more about Ahaziah.

It records the other things he did as king.
