# 2 Kings 15 — Kings change in Israel and Judah

**About this chapter**

Azariah rules Judah for many years. In Israel, several kings take power during his lifetime. Later, his son Jotham rules Judah.

## Azariah rules Judah

## 2 Kings 15:1

Azariah, son of Amaziah, became king of Judah.

Jeroboam was then in his twenty-seventh year as Israel’s king.

**Reading help**

Azariah is also called Uzziah. Both names belong to the same king of Judah.

## 2 Kings 15:2

Azariah was 16 when he became king.

He ruled in Jerusalem for 52 years.

His mother was Jecoliah from Jerusalem.

## 2 Kings 15:3

Like his father Amaziah, Azariah did what the LORD said was right.

## 2 Kings 15:4

The places of worship on the hills remained.

People still offered sacrifices and burned incense there.

## Jotham helps govern Judah

## 2 Kings 15:5

The LORD gave Azariah a skin disease called leprosy.

Azariah had it for the rest of his life.

He lived in a separate house.

His son Jotham managed the palace and governed the people.

## 2 Kings 15:6

The Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah records the other things Azariah did.

## 2 Kings 15:7

Azariah died. His people buried him near his ancestors in the City of David.

His son Jotham became king of Judah.

**Pause and answer**

## Zechariah rules Israel for six months

## 2 Kings 15:8

Zechariah, son of Jeroboam, became king of Israel.

Azariah was in his thirty-eighth year as Judah’s king.

Zechariah ruled in Samaria for six months.

## 2 Kings 15:9

The LORD saw Zechariah do evil, as his ancestors had done.

He kept doing the wrong things an earlier king, Jeroboam son of Nebat, had led Israel to do.

## 2 Kings 15:10

Shallum, son of Jabesh, made a plan against Zechariah.

He killed Zechariah in front of the people.

Then Shallum became king of Israel.

## 2 Kings 15:11

The Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel records what else Zechariah did.

## 2 Kings 15:12

The LORD had promised Jehu that four generations of his family would rule Israel after him.

That promise came true.

**Reading help**

Zechariah was the fourth king in Jehu’s family after Jehu. [Read the promise in 2 Kings 10.](2kings10_1757416657.md)

## Shallum rules Israel for one month

## 2 Kings 15:13

Shallum became king in the thirty-ninth year that Uzziah ruled Judah.

Shallum ruled in Samaria for one full month.

## 2 Kings 15:14

Menahem, son of Gadi, came from Tirzah to Samaria.

He killed Shallum and became king in his place.

## 2 Kings 15:15

The Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel tells more about Shallum.

It records his plot to take power.

## 2 Kings 15:16

Menahem went from Tirzah to attack Tiphsah and the people nearby.

Tiphsah had refused to open its gates to him.

He tore open the pregnant women there.

## Menahem pays the king of Assyria

## 2 Kings 15:17

Menahem became king of Israel in Azariah’s thirty-ninth year as king of Judah.

Menahem ruled in Samaria for ten years.

## 2 Kings 15:18

The LORD saw Menahem do evil.

Throughout his rule, he kept doing the wrong things Jeroboam son of Nebat had led Israel to do.

## 2 Kings 15:19

Pul, king of Assyria, invaded Israel.

Menahem gave Pul 1,000 talents of silver.

He wanted Pul’s help to stay in power.

## 2 Kings 15:20

Menahem made each wealthy man in Israel pay 50 shekels of silver.

This paid for his gift to Assyria’s king.

Then Pul left Israel.

**Reading help**

People weighed silver to make payments. A shekel was a small weight. A talent was a much larger weight.

**Pause and answer**

Why did Menahem give silver to Pul?

## 2 Kings 15:21

The Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel records the other things Menahem did.

## 2 Kings 15:22

Menahem died, and his son Pekahiah became king.

## Pekahiah rules Israel for two years

## 2 Kings 15:23

Pekahiah became king of Israel in Azariah’s fiftieth year as king of Judah.

Pekahiah ruled in Samaria for two years.

## 2 Kings 15:24

The LORD saw Pekahiah do evil.

He kept doing the wrong things Jeroboam son of Nebat had led Israel to do.

## 2 Kings 15:25

One of Pekahiah’s officers was Pekah, son of Remaliah.

Pekah plotted against Pekahiah with Argob, Arieh, and 50 men from Gilead.

Pekah killed Pekahiah in the stronghold of the palace in Samaria.

Then Pekah became king.

## 2 Kings 15:26

The Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel records the other things Pekahiah did.

## Israel loses land under Pekah

## 2 Kings 15:27

Pekah became king of Israel in Azariah’s fifty-second year as king of Judah.

Pekah ruled in Samaria for 20 years.

## 2 Kings 15:28

The LORD saw Pekah do evil.

He kept doing the wrong things Jeroboam son of Nebat had led Israel to do.

## 2 Kings 15:29

While Pekah ruled, Tiglath-pileser, king of Assyria, captured cities in Israel.

He took Ijon, Abel-beth-maacah, Janoah, Kedesh, and Hazor.

He also took Gilead and Galilee, including all of Naphtali’s land.

He forced the people to leave their homes for Assyria.

## 2 Kings 15:30

Hoshea, son of Elah, made a plan against Pekah.

In the twentieth year of Jotham son of Uzziah, Hoshea killed Pekah.

Hoshea became king of Israel.

## 2 Kings 15:31

The Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel records the other things Pekah did.

## Jotham rules Judah

## 2 Kings 15:32

Jotham, son of Uzziah, became king of Judah.

Pekah was then in his second year as Israel’s king.

## 2 Kings 15:33

Jotham was 25 when he became king.

He ruled in Jerusalem for 16 years.

His mother was Jerusha, daughter of Zadok.

## 2 Kings 15:34

Like his father Uzziah, Jotham did what the LORD said was right.

## 2 Kings 15:35

The places of worship on the hills remained.

People still offered sacrifices and burned incense there.

Jotham rebuilt the Upper Gate of the LORD’s temple.

## 2 Kings 15:36

The Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah records the other things Jotham did.

## 2 Kings 15:37

In those days the LORD began sending two kings against Judah.

They were Rezin of Aram and Pekah of Israel.

## 2 Kings 15:38

Jotham died. His people buried him with his ancestors in the City of David.

His son Ahaz became king of Judah.
