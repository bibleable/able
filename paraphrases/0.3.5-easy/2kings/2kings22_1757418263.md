# 2 Kings 22 — Josiah hears the Book of the Law

**About this chapter**

During repairs to the temple, Hilkiah finds the Book of the Law. When Josiah hears it, he sends five men to ask the LORD what its words mean for Judah.

## Josiah follows the LORD

## 2 Kings 22:1

Josiah was eight when he became king of Judah.

He ruled in Jerusalem for 31 years.

His mother was Jedidah, daughter of Adaiah, from Bozkath.

## 2 Kings 22:2

Josiah did what pleased the LORD.

He followed the example of his ancestor David.

He stayed on that path all his life.

## Josiah sends money for repairs

## 2 Kings 22:3

In his eighteenth year as king, Josiah sent his secretary Shaphan to the LORD’s temple.

Shaphan was Azaliah’s son and Meshullam’s grandson.

Josiah told him what to do.

## 2 Kings 22:4

“Go to Hilkiah, the chief priest.

The people have brought money to the LORD’s temple.

The doorkeepers have collected it. Ask Hilkiah to count it.

## 2 Kings 22:5

Give the money to the men in charge of repairs.

They will pay the workers who fix the temple.

## 2 Kings 22:6

The carpenters, builders, and stoneworkers need wood and cut stone.

Use the money to buy these materials.

## 2 Kings 22:7

You do not need to ask for a report on every payment.

The men who handle the money are honest.”

**Pause and answer**

Why did Josiah trust the men with the money?

## Hilkiah finds the book

## 2 Kings 22:8

Hilkiah told Shaphan, “I found the Book of the Law in the LORD’s temple!”

Hilkiah gave Shaphan the book, and Shaphan read it.

![A scroll unrolled to show its writing.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

**Reading help**

The Book of the Law contained God’s instructions for his people. A scroll is a long sheet that rolls up.

## 2 Kings 22:9

Shaphan went back to King Josiah.

He said, “Your servants have taken the money from the temple.

They have given it to the workers and the men in charge.”

## 2 Kings 22:10

Then Shaphan said, “Hilkiah the priest gave me a book.”

Shaphan read it aloud to the king.

## Josiah asks for the LORD’s answer

## 2 Kings 22:11

When Josiah heard the words of the Book of the Law, he tore his clothes.

**Reading help**

Josiah tore his clothes to show his sorrow.

## 2 Kings 22:12

Josiah gave an order to five men.

They were Hilkiah the priest, Shaphan the secretary, and Shaphan’s son Ahikam.

The other two were Achbor, son of Micaiah, and Asaiah, the king’s servant.

## 2 Kings 22:13

Josiah said, “Ask the LORD about the words in this book.

Ask for me and for everyone in Judah.

The LORD is very angry with us.

Our ancestors did not do what the book tells us to do.”

## Huldah gives a warning

## 2 Kings 22:14

The five men went to speak to Huldah, a prophet.

She lived in the Second District of Jerusalem.

Her husband Shallum looked after the clothing.

He was Tikvah’s son and Harhas’s grandson.

**Pause and answer**

Who did the five men go to for the LORD’s answer?

## 2 Kings 22:15

Huldah said, “The LORD, the God of Israel, has a message.

Take these words to the man who sent you.

## 2 Kings 22:16

‘The king of Judah has heard the words of this book.

I will bring the disaster it describes on this place and its people.

## 2 Kings 22:17

They have left me and burned incense to other gods.

They have made me angry by what their hands have made.

My anger against this place burns like a fire no one can put out.’

## Huldah gives a message for Josiah

## 2 Kings 22:18

The king of Judah sent you to ask the LORD.

Give the king this answer from the LORD, the God of Israel.

‘You heard my words.

## 2 Kings 22:19

I warned that this place would become a ruin and its people a curse.

You took my warning to heart and humbled yourself before me.

You tore your clothes and wept before me.

I have heard you, says the LORD.

**Reading help**

People would speak of the ruined city when they wished disaster on someone. That is what it means to become a curse.

## 2 Kings 22:20

When you die, your people will bury you in peace with your ancestors.

You will not see the disaster I will bring on this place.’”

The five men took Huldah’s answer back to Josiah.
