# 2 Kings 13 — Elisha gives the king a sign

**About this chapter**

Jehoahaz asks the LORD to help Israel. Later his son Jehoash visits Elisha. Elisha gives the king a sign about what will happen to Israel’s cities.

## Jehoahaz asks the LORD for help

## 2 Kings 13:1

Joash, son of Ahaziah, was in his twenty-third year as king of Judah.

That year Jehoahaz, son of Jehu, became Israel’s king.

Jehoahaz ruled in Samaria for 17 years.

## 2 Kings 13:2

The LORD saw Jehoahaz do evil.

Long before, Jeroboam son of Nebat had led Israel into sin.

Jehoahaz kept doing those same things.

## 2 Kings 13:3

The LORD grew angry with Israel.

Again and again he let Hazael, king of Aram, overpower Israel.

Hazael’s son Ben-hadad also had power over Israel.

## 2 Kings 13:4

Jehoahaz asked the LORD for help.

The LORD saw how cruelly Aram’s king treated Israel.

So the LORD listened to Jehoahaz.

## 2 Kings 13:5

The LORD sent someone to rescue Israel.

The people escaped Aram’s control.

They could live in their homes again, as before.

Who listened when Jehoahaz asked for help?

## Israel keeps following Jeroboam’s example

## 2 Kings 13:6

Yet Israel kept doing the wrong things Jeroboam’s family had taught them.

The pole used to worship Asherah still stood in Samaria.

## 2 Kings 13:7

Jehoahaz had just 50 horsemen, 10 chariots, and 10,000 soldiers on foot.

Aram’s king had crushed the rest of Israel’s army.

They were like dust where people beat out grain.

**Reading help**

Beating the grain leaves dust on the ground. The picture shows how little remained of the army.

## 2 Kings 13:8

The Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel tells more about Jehoahaz.

It records what he did and how powerful he was.

## 2 Kings 13:9

Jehoahaz died, and his people buried him in Samaria.

His son Jehoash became king of Israel.

## Jehoash rules Israel

## 2 Kings 13:10

Joash was in his thirty-seventh year as king of Judah when Jehoash became king of Israel.

Jehoash ruled in Samaria for 16 years.

**Reading help**

Joash and Jehoash are different kings here. Joash rules Judah. Jehoash rules Israel.

## 2 Kings 13:11

The LORD saw Jehoash do evil.

Jehoash kept doing the wrong things Jeroboam son of Nebat had led Israel to do.

## 2 Kings 13:12

The Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel tells more about Jehoash.

It records his deeds and his strength.

It tells about his war with Amaziah, king of Judah.

## 2 Kings 13:13

Jehoash died, and Jeroboam became king after him.

The people buried Jehoash with Israel’s kings in Samaria.

## Jehoash visits Elisha

## 2 Kings 13:14

Earlier, Elisha had become so sick that he was dying.

Jehoash, king of Israel, came to see him.

The king wept over Elisha.

He cried, “My father! My father! The chariots and horsemen of Israel!”

**Reading help**

Jehoash calls Elisha “my father” to honor him. His cry recalls what Elisha said when he lost his own teacher. [Read about Elijah and Elisha.](2kings2_1757415573.md)

## 2 Kings 13:15

Elisha asked Jehoash to get a bow and some arrows.

Jehoash brought them.

## 2 Kings 13:16

Elisha asked the king to hold the bow.

Jehoash held it, and Elisha laid his hands on the king’s hands.

## 2 Kings 13:17

At Elisha’s word, Jehoash opened the east window and shot an arrow.

Elisha said, “This arrow shows the LORD will give you victory over Aram.

At Aphek you will fight Aram’s army until you defeat it.”

## The king strikes the ground three times

## 2 Kings 13:18

Elisha asked Jehoash to take the arrows and strike the ground.

Jehoash struck the ground three times and stopped.

## 2 Kings 13:19

Elisha was angry with Jehoash.

“You should have struck the ground five or six times,” he said.

“Then you would have defeated Aram for good.

Now you will defeat Aram only three times.”

How many times did Jehoash strike the ground?

## A man comes back to life

## 2 Kings 13:20

Elisha died, and the people buried him.

Each spring, groups of men from Moab came to raid the land.

## 2 Kings 13:21

One day some Israelites were burying a man.

Suddenly they saw men coming to raid the land.

They threw the dead man into Elisha’s tomb.

His body touched Elisha’s bones. The man came back to life and stood up.

![An example of a tomb with an opening in the rock.](../pictures/tomb.svg)

**Reading help**

A tomb is a place where people lay someone who has died.

## Jehoash wins back Israel’s cities

## 2 Kings 13:22

Hazael, king of Aram, had treated Israel cruelly all the years Jehoahaz ruled.

## 2 Kings 13:23

The LORD showed Israel kindness and mercy.

He cared for them because of his covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

Up to the time of this account, the LORD had refused to destroy them or send them away from him.

**Reading help**

A covenant is a serious promise. The LORD remembered his promise to Israel’s ancestors.

## 2 Kings 13:24

Hazael died, and his son Ben-hadad became king of Aram.

## 2 Kings 13:25

Jehoash of Israel defeated Ben-hadad of Aram three times.

Jehoash won back Israel’s cities.

These were the cities Hazael had taken from Jehoash’s father, Jehoahaz.
