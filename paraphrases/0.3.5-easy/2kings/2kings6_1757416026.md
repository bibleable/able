# 2 Kings 6 — Elisha asks the LORD to open eyes

**About this chapter**

Elisha helps a man recover a borrowed axe head. Later, soldiers come to capture Elisha. The chapter ends when the people in Samaria have almost no food left.

## The prophets need a bigger home

## 2 Kings 6:1

A group of prophets spoke to Elisha.

“The place where we meet with you is too small.

## 2 Kings 6:2

Please let us go to the Jordan River.

Each of us can get a log there.

We can build a place to live.”

Elisha said, “Go.”

## 2 Kings 6:3

One man asked, “Please come with us.”

Elisha said, “I will come.”

## 2 Kings 6:4

Elisha went with them to the Jordan.

The men began cutting down trees.

## A borrowed axe head falls into the river

## 2 Kings 6:5

One man was cutting down a tree.

The iron head of his axe fell into the river.

He cried, “Oh, master! I borrowed that axe!”

## 2 Kings 6:6

Elisha asked, “Where did it fall?”

The man showed him the place.

Elisha cut a stick and threw it there.

The iron axe head floated up.

![The iron head of the axe floats at the surface of the water.](../pictures/floating-axe-head.svg)

## 2 Kings 6:7

Elisha said, “Lift it out.”

The man reached out and took it.

**Pause and answer**

Why was the man upset when the axe head fell?

## Elisha warns Israel’s king

## 2 Kings 6:8

The king of Aram was at war with Israel.

He talked with his officers about where to camp.

He told them the place he had chosen.

## 2 Kings 6:9

Elisha sent a warning to the king of Israel.

“Keep away from that place. Aram’s soldiers are going there.”

## 2 Kings 6:10

Israel’s king sent word to the place Elisha named.

Elisha warned the king many times.

So the king knew where he had to be careful.

## 2 Kings 6:11

The king of Aram grew angry.

He called his officers together.

“Which one of us is helping Israel’s king?” he asked.

## 2 Kings 6:12

One officer said, “None of us, my king.

Elisha is a prophet in Israel.

He tells Israel’s king even what you say in your bedroom.”

## An army comes to Dothan

## 2 Kings 6:13

The king said, “Find out where Elisha is.

I want to send men to capture him.”

Word came back that Elisha was in Dothan.

## 2 Kings 6:14

The king of Aram sent a large army there.

The soldiers had horses and chariots.

That night they surrounded Dothan.

## 2 Kings 6:15

Early the next morning, Elisha’s servant went outside.

He saw soldiers, horses, and chariots all around the city.

“Oh, master!” he asked. “What can we do?”

## Elisha prays for his servant

## 2 Kings 6:16

Elisha said, “Do not be afraid.

There are more with us than with them.”

## 2 Kings 6:17

Elisha prayed, “LORD, please open his eyes so he can see.”

The LORD let the young man see.

The hills around Elisha were full of horses and chariots of fire.

**Reading help**

The servant could already see the soldiers from Aram. Now the LORD showed him something he could not see before.

## 2 Kings 6:18

The soldiers of Aram came down toward Elisha.

Elisha prayed, “LORD, please make these men blind.”

The LORD did what Elisha asked.

## The soldiers reach Samaria

## 2 Kings 6:19

Elisha told the soldiers, “This is the wrong road and the wrong city.

Follow me. I will take you to the man you want.”

Elisha led them to Samaria.

## 2 Kings 6:20

Inside Samaria, Elisha prayed, “LORD, open their eyes so they can see.”

The LORD gave the soldiers their sight again.

They looked around and saw they were in Samaria.

## 2 Kings 6:21

Israel’s king saw the soldiers.

He asked Elisha, “My father, shall I kill them?

Shall I kill them?”

## 2 Kings 6:22

Elisha said, “Let them live.

Would you kill prisoners you captured with your sword or bow?

Give these men food and water.

Then let them go home to their king.”

## 2 Kings 6:23

Israel’s king gave them a feast.

When the soldiers finished eating and drinking, he sent them home.

They returned to their king.

The groups from Aram stopped coming to raid Israel.

**Pause and answer**

What did Israel’s king give the soldiers before they went home?

## People in Samaria run out of food

## 2 Kings 6:24

Some time later, King Ben-hadad gathered Aram’s whole army.

The army marched to Samaria and surrounded the city.

## 2 Kings 6:25

The army stayed so long that the people were starving.

Even a donkey’s head cost 80 shekels of silver.

A small cupful of dove droppings cost five shekels of silver.

**Reading help**

People weighed silver in units called shekels. These prices show how little food was left.

## 2 Kings 6:26

Israel’s king was walking along the city wall.

A woman called out, “Please help me, my king!”

## 2 Kings 6:27

He answered, “If the LORD does not help you, how can I?

Where can I find grain or wine for you?”

## A mother tells the king what happened

## 2 Kings 6:28

Then the king asked, “What has happened?”

She said, “Another woman told me, ‘Give us your son to eat today.

Tomorrow we will eat my son.’

## 2 Kings 6:29

We cooked my son and ate him.

The next day I asked her to give us her son.

But she had hidden him.”

## 2 Kings 6:30

The king heard her words and tore his clothes.

As he walked along the wall, the people saw what he wore underneath.

Rough sackcloth lay against his skin.

**Reading help**

People wore rough sackcloth to show deep sorrow.

## The king blames Elisha

## 2 Kings 6:31

The king said, “Elisha son of Shaphat must lose his head today.

May God punish me severely if Elisha lives!”

## 2 Kings 6:32

Elisha was sitting at home with the leaders of the people.

The king sent a messenger ahead of him.

Before the man arrived, Elisha told the leaders, “That murderer has sent someone to cut off my head.

When the messenger comes, shut the door. Keep him outside.

Can you hear his master coming behind him?”

## 2 Kings 6:33

The messenger arrived while Elisha was still speaking.

The king said, “This disaster comes from the LORD.

Why should I wait for the LORD any longer?”

**Pause and answer**

Who was sitting with Elisha when the messenger came?
