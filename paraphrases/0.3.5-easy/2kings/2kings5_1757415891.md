# 2 Kings 5 — Naaman washes in the Jordan

**About this chapter**

A girl from Israel tells Naaman’s wife where to find help. Naaman travels to Israel. After he returns to thank Elisha, Gehazi wants a gift for himself.

## A girl tells Naaman’s wife about a prophet

## 2 Kings 5:1

Naaman led the army of Aram. He was a brave soldier.

The LORD had given Aram victory through Naaman.

The king of Aram thought highly of him.

Naaman had a skin disease called leprosy.

## 2 Kings 5:2

Men from Aram had taken a young girl from Israel.

She now served Naaman’s wife.

## 2 Kings 5:3

The girl told Naaman’s wife, “I wish my master would go to Samaria!

The prophet there could heal him of his leprosy.”

## 2 Kings 5:4

Naaman went to the king of Aram.

He told the king what the girl from Israel had said.

## Naaman takes a letter to Israel

## 2 Kings 5:5

The king said, “Go. Take my letter to the king of Israel.”

Naaman set out with ten talents of silver and 6,000 shekels of gold.

He also took ten sets of clothes.

**Reading help**

People weighed silver and gold. A talent was a large unit of weight. A shekel was a much smaller unit.

## 2 Kings 5:6

Naaman gave the letter to the king of Israel.

It said, “I am sending my servant Naaman to you.

Please heal him of his leprosy.”

## 2 Kings 5:7

The king of Israel read the letter and tore his clothes.

“Am I God?” he asked. “Can I take life or give it?

How can he expect me to heal this man?

The king of Aram must want a quarrel with me!”

## 2 Kings 5:8

Elisha heard that the king of Israel had torn his clothes.

Elisha sent him this message. “Why tear your clothes?

Send Naaman to me. Then he will know Israel has a prophet.”

**Pause and answer**

Who asked the king to send Naaman to him?

## Elisha sends Naaman a message

## 2 Kings 5:9

Naaman came with his horses and chariots.

He stopped at the door of Elisha’s house.

## 2 Kings 5:10

Elisha sent a messenger to Naaman.

“Wash yourself seven times in the Jordan River.

Your skin will heal, and you will be clean.”

## 2 Kings 5:11

Naaman walked away angry. He said, “I expected Elisha to come out.

I thought he would call on the LORD his God.

I thought he would wave his hand over my sore skin and heal me.

## 2 Kings 5:12

Damascus has two rivers, the Abanah and the Pharpar.

Aren’t they better than all the rivers of Israel?

Couldn’t I wash there and be clean?”

Naaman turned away in anger.

## Naaman listens to his servants

## 2 Kings 5:13

Naaman’s servants came to him.

“My father,” they said, “suppose the prophet asked you to do something great.

Wouldn’t you do it? He only asks you to wash and be clean.”

**Reading help**

The servants call Naaman “my father” to show respect.

## 2 Kings 5:14

So Naaman went down to the Jordan.

He dipped himself in the river seven times, as Elisha had said.

His skin became like a little child’s skin. He was clean.

## Naaman offers Elisha a gift

## 2 Kings 5:15

Naaman and everyone with him went back to Elisha.

Naaman said, “Now I know Israel’s God is the only God in all the earth.

Please take a gift from me.”

## 2 Kings 5:16

Elisha answered, “As surely as the LORD I serve lives, I will take nothing.”

Naaman urged him to take a gift. Elisha still refused.

## 2 Kings 5:17

Naaman said, “Then please give me some soil from this land.

Let me take as much as two mules can carry.

From now on, I will offer sacrifices only to the LORD.

I will give my burnt offerings to him alone.

## 2 Kings 5:18

There is one thing I ask the LORD to forgive.

My king leans on my arm when he worships in Rimmon’s temple.

When the king bows, I bow there too.

May the LORD forgive me for this.”

**Reading help**

Rimmon was a god people worshiped in Aram.

## Gehazi runs after Naaman

## 2 Kings 5:19

Elisha said, “Go in peace.”

Naaman left and traveled a short way.

## 2 Kings 5:20

Gehazi, Elisha’s servant, said to himself, “My master let Naaman from Aram go with all his gifts.

As surely as the LORD lives, I will run after Naaman.

I will get something from him.”

## 2 Kings 5:21

Gehazi ran after Naaman. Naaman saw him coming.

Naaman got down from his chariot to meet him.

He asked, “Is everything all right?”

## 2 Kings 5:22

Gehazi answered, “Everything is all right.”

Then Gehazi told a lie. “Elisha sent me.

Two young prophets have just come from the hills of Ephraim.

Please give them a talent of silver and two sets of clothes.”

## 2 Kings 5:23

Naaman urged him, “Please take two talents of silver.”

Naaman tied up the silver in two bags.

Two servants carried the bags and clothes ahead of Gehazi.

## Elisha questions Gehazi

## 2 Kings 5:24

When they reached the hill, Gehazi took the gifts.

He put them in the house.

He sent the two servants away.

## 2 Kings 5:25

Gehazi went in and stood before Elisha.

Elisha asked, “Where have you been, Gehazi?”

Gehazi answered, “I have not been anywhere.”

## 2 Kings 5:26

Elisha said, “My spirit went with you when Naaman stepped down from his chariot.

Is this a time to take money and clothes?

Is it a time to gain olive trees, vineyards, sheep, and cattle?

Is it a time to gain men and women as servants?

## 2 Kings 5:27

Because of this, you will have Naaman’s leprosy.

It will stay with your family through every generation.”

Gehazi left Elisha. He had leprosy, and his skin looked white as snow.
