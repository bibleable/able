# 2 Kings 11 — Joash becomes king of Judah

**About this chapter**

Athaliah rules Judah while her grandson Joash lives in hiding. His aunt Jehosheba saves him. Later, Jehoiada the priest brings him out to meet the people.

## An aunt saves her nephew

## 2 Kings 11:1

Athaliah learned that her son Ahaziah was dead.

He had been king of Judah.

Athaliah set out to kill everyone who could become king.

## 2 Kings 11:2

Jehosheba was King Joram’s daughter and Ahaziah’s sister.

While Athaliah’s men were killing the princes, Jehosheba rescued her nephew Joash.

Joash was Ahaziah’s son.

Jehosheba hid him and the woman who cared for him in a bedroom.

Athaliah did not find Joash. He lived.

## 2 Kings 11:3

Joash stayed hidden with his nurse in the LORD’s temple.

For six years, Athaliah ruled Judah.

## Jehoiada shows the guards the king’s son

## 2 Kings 11:4

In the seventh year, Jehoiada the priest called the commanders, the Carites, and the guards to the temple.

They made a serious promise to him.

Then Jehoiada showed them Joash, the king’s son.

## 2 Kings 11:5

Jehoiada gave the men their duties for the Sabbath.

Some of the guards coming to work would watch the palace.

**Reading help**

The Sabbath was the weekly day of rest. The guards still had work to do that day.

## 2 Kings 11:6

Others would watch the Sur Gate and the gate behind the guards.

The guards would take turns watching the temple.

## 2 Kings 11:7

Two groups usually finished work on the Sabbath.

This time they would stay to guard the temple for the king.

## 2 Kings 11:8

Jehoiada wanted armed guards with Joash wherever he went.

Anyone who tried to break through them would die.

## The people welcome Joash as king

## 2 Kings 11:9

The commanders did what Jehoiada had told them.

They brought their men to the priest.

Even the guards whose work had ended came with them.

## 2 Kings 11:10

Jehoiada gave the commanders spears and shields from the temple.

They had belonged to King David.

## 2 Kings 11:11

The guards took their places around Joash near the altar.

They stood across the temple area, from south to north.

## 2 Kings 11:12

Jehoiada brought Joash out and put the crown on him.

He gave Joash the written law and declared him king.

They poured oil on Joash. The people clapped and shouted, “Long live the king!”

![A scroll. People wrote on a long sheet and rolled it up.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

**Reading help**

The written law is called the Testimony here. It reminded the king of God’s instructions. Pouring oil on Joash showed the people that he was king.

## Athaliah hears the crowd

## 2 Kings 11:13

Athaliah heard the guards and the people.

She went to the LORD’s temple to see what was happening.

## 2 Kings 11:14

Joash stood by the pillar where kings usually stood.

Officers and trumpet players stood beside him.

People cheered and blew trumpets.

Athaliah tore her clothes and screamed, “Treason! Treason!”

**Reading help**

Athaliah shouted “Treason!” She meant that the people had turned against her as ruler.

## 2 Kings 11:15

Jehoiada told the commanders to take Athaliah outside.

He ordered them to kill anyone who followed her.

He would not let them kill her inside the LORD’s temple.

## 2 Kings 11:16

The guards seized Athaliah at the palace entrance used by horses.

They killed her there.

## The king and the people make a promise

## 2 Kings 11:17

Jehoiada led Joash and the people in making a covenant with the LORD.

They promised to be the LORD’s people.

Joash and the people also made a covenant with each other.

**Reading help**

A covenant is a serious promise about how people will live or act.

**Pause and answer**

Whose people did Joash and the people of Judah promise to be?

## 2 Kings 11:18

The people tore down Baal’s temple.

They smashed the altars and the statues used in worship.

They killed Mattan, Baal’s priest, in front of the altars.

Jehoiada stationed guards at the LORD’s temple.

## Joash sits on the throne

## 2 Kings 11:19

Jehoiada led Joash from the temple to the palace.

The commanders, Carites, guards, and people went with them.

They entered through the Gate of the Guards.

Joash sat down on the royal throne.

## 2 Kings 11:20

The people of Judah celebrated.

The city was quiet again after Athaliah died at the palace.

## 2 Kings 11:21

Joash was seven years old when he became king.
