# 1 Chronicles 22 — David helps Solomon get ready to build the temple

**About this chapter**

David wants to build a temple, a house where people will worship the LORD. The LORD has chosen David’s son Solomon to build it. David gathers what Solomon will need.

## David gathers stone, metal, and wood

## 1 Chronicles 22:1

David said, “Here is the place for the house of the LORD God.

Here Israel will have an altar for burnt offerings.”

An altar is a place where people bring gifts to God.

They burn these offerings on the altar.

## 1 Chronicles 22:2

David called together foreigners who lived in Israel.

He chose some to cut and shape stones for God’s house.

## 1 Chronicles 22:3

David gathered iron for the gate nails and other metal parts.

He gathered more bronze than anyone could weigh.

Bronze is a kind of metal.

## 1 Chronicles 22:4

People from Sidon and Tyre brought David cedar logs.

They brought more logs than anyone could count.

## 1 Chronicles 22:5

David said, “My son Solomon is young.

He has little experience.

The LORD’s house must be so beautiful that people everywhere hear about it.

I must get things ready.”

So David gathered many supplies before he died.

## David tells Solomon what the LORD promised

## 1 Chronicles 22:6

David called his son Solomon to him.

He told Solomon to build a house for the LORD, Israel’s God.

## 1 Chronicles 22:7

David said, “My son, I wanted very much to build that house.

I wanted it to honor the name of the LORD my God.

## 1 Chronicles 22:8

But the LORD told me, ‘You fought great wars.

I saw how many people you killed.

Because you shed so much blood, you must leave this work to someone else.

## 1 Chronicles 22:9

You will have a son named Solomon.

He will be a man of peace.

I will give him rest from enemies on every side.

Israel will live in peace while he is king.

## 1 Chronicles 22:10

Solomon will build the house that honors my name.

He will be my son, and I will be his Father.

I will make his kingdom over Israel last forever.’

## David encourages his son

## 1 Chronicles 22:11

My son, may the LORD be with you.

May you succeed in building the LORD’s house, as he promised.

## 1 Chronicles 22:12

When the LORD makes you Israel’s leader, may he give you understanding.

May he help you make wise choices and keep his law.

## 1 Chronicles 22:13

The LORD gave Moses commands for Israel.

Follow them carefully, and you will succeed.

Be strong and brave. Take heart when you feel afraid.

## 1 Chronicles 22:14

I worked hard to gather treasure for the LORD’s house.

I gathered 100,000 talents of gold and 1,000,000 talents of silver.

A talent was a measure of weight.

There is more bronze and iron than anyone can weigh.

I also gathered wood and stone. You can add more.

## 1 Chronicles 22:15

Many skilled workers are ready to help you.

Some cut stones. Others build walls or work with wood.

There are workers for every kind of job.

## 1 Chronicles 22:16

More workers than you can count shape gold, silver, bronze, and iron.

Now start the work. May the LORD be with you.”

## David asks Israel’s leaders to help

## 1 Chronicles 22:17

David ordered all Israel’s leaders to help Solomon.

## 1 Chronicles 22:18

David told them, “The LORD your God is with you.

He has given you peace on every side.

He gave me power over the people in this land.

Now the land is under the rule of the LORD and his people.

## 1 Chronicles 22:19

Give your whole heart to seeking the LORD your God.

Begin building his holy house.

Then bring in the ark of the LORD’s covenant.

The ark is the sacred chest that reminds Israel of God’s promise.

Bring the other holy things there too.

This temple will honor the LORD’s name.”
