# 1 Chronicles 10 — The death of King Saul

**About this chapter**

King Saul and three of his sons die in battle. The people of Jabesh bring them home for burial. The chapter tells why the LORD gave the kingdom to David.

## Israel loses the battle

## 1 Chronicles 10:1

The Philistines fought against Israel.

Israel’s men ran from them.

Many died on Mount Gilboa.

## 1 Chronicles 10:2

The Philistines chased King Saul and his sons.

They killed three of Saul’s sons.

Their names were Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua.

## 1 Chronicles 10:3

The fighting around Saul grew fierce.

Men with bows caught up with Saul and wounded him.

## 1 Chronicles 10:4

Saul spoke to the man who carried his armor.

He said, “Kill me with your sword.

I am afraid the Philistines will torture me.”

The man was terrified and refused.

Then Saul took his own life.

## 1 Chronicles 10:5

The man who carried Saul’s armor saw that Saul was dead.

He too took his own life.

## 1 Chronicles 10:6

Saul, his three sons, and all his household died together.

## The Philistines take the cities

## 1 Chronicles 10:7

The Israelites in the valley saw their army run away.

They saw that Saul and his sons had died.

The people left their cities and fled.

The Philistines came and took over those cities.

## 1 Chronicles 10:8

The next day the Philistines came to take things from the dead.

They found Saul and his sons on Mount Gilboa.

## 1 Chronicles 10:9

They stripped Saul’s body and cut off his head.

They took his armor.

They sent messengers throughout their land to announce the news.

The messengers told the people and brought the news to the temples of their idols.

## 1 Chronicles 10:10

The Philistines put Saul’s armor in the temple of their gods.

They hung his head in the temple of Dagon, a god they worshiped.

## The people of Jabesh bury Saul and his sons

## 1 Chronicles 10:11

The people of Jabesh-gilead heard what the Philistines had done to Saul.

## 1 Chronicles 10:12

The brave men of Jabesh went to get the bodies.

They brought Saul and his sons back to Jabesh.

They buried their bones under the oak tree there.

The people fasted for seven days.

They went without food to show their grief.

## David becomes king

## 1 Chronicles 10:13

Saul died because he had been unfaithful to the LORD.

He disobeyed the LORD’s words.

He even asked a medium to guide him.

A medium is someone people ask to speak to the dead.

## 1 Chronicles 10:14

Saul did not ask the LORD to guide him.

So the LORD brought Saul’s life to an end.

He gave the kingdom to David, the son of Jesse.
