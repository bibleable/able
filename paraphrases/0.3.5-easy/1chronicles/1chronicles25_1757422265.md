# 1 Chronicles 25 — Musicians take turns leading worship

**About this chapter**

Three families make music for the LORD.

They form groups so each family knows when to serve.

## Three families of musicians

## 1 Chronicles 25:1

David and his army commanders chose musicians for a special task.

They chose sons of Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun.

These men gave messages from God with music.

They played lyres, harps, and cymbals.

Lyres and harps have strings.

Cymbals are metal plates that musicians strike together.

## 1 Chronicles 25:2

Asaph led his sons Zaccur, Joseph, Nethaniah, and Asarelah.

Asaph gave messages from God under the king’s direction.

## 1 Chronicles 25:3

Jeduthun led six of his sons.

Their names were Gedaliah, Zeri, Jeshaiah, Shimei, Hashabiah, and Mattithiah.

Jeduthun played the harp and gave messages from God.

He thanked and praised the LORD.

![A harp with strings, like the instrument Jeduthun played as he thanked the LORD.](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

## 1 Chronicles 25:4

Heman’s sons included Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shebuel, and Jerimoth.

There were also Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, and Romamti-ezer.

The other four were Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, and Mahazioth.

## 1 Chronicles 25:5

Heman was the king’s seer.

A seer was someone God gave messages to.

God had promised to honor Heman.

God gave him fourteen sons and three daughters.

## The musicians draw lots

## 1 Chronicles 25:6

The fathers led their families in making music in the LORD’s house.

They played cymbals, harps, and lyres as they served God.

The king directed Asaph, Jeduthun, and Heman.

## 1 Chronicles 25:7

Their relatives joined them.

All had learned the songs of the LORD and sang them well.

There were 288 trained musicians altogether.

## 1 Chronicles 25:8

The musicians drew lots to find out when to serve.

Drawing lots meant making a choice using marked objects.

Young and old took part.

Teachers and students drew lots alike.
