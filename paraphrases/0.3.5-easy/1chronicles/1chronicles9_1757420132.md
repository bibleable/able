# 1 Chronicles 9 — Families return and serve in Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

Families returned to their homes after exile. In Jerusalem, some guarded the gates of God’s house. Others cared for the bread, the music, and the things used in worship. The chapter ends with Saul’s family record.

## Families come home

## 1 Chronicles 9:1

The Book of the Kings of Israel listed all Israel by family.

The people of Judah were unfaithful to God.

So they had to leave their land and live in Babylon.

## 1 Chronicles 9:2

The first people to settle again on their own land included ordinary Israelites, priests, Levites, and temple servants.

They went back to their towns.

## 1 Chronicles 9:3

Some families from Judah, Benjamin, Ephraim, and Manasseh lived in Jerusalem.

## 1 Chronicles 9:4

Uthai belonged to the family of Perez, Judah’s son.

Uthai’s father was Ammihud.

Ammihud’s father was Omri.

Omri’s father was Imri.

Imri’s father was Bani.

## 1 Chronicles 9:5

Asaiah, the firstborn son in his family, came from the Shilonites.

His sons lived there too.

## 1 Chronicles 9:6

Jeuel and 690 relatives came from Zerah’s family.

## Families from Benjamin

## 1 Chronicles 9:7

Sallu came from Benjamin’s family.

Sallu’s father was Meshullam.

Meshullam’s father was Hodaviah.

Hodaviah’s father was Hassenuah.

## 1 Chronicles 9:8

Ibneiah was Jeroham’s son.

Elah’s father was Uzzi, and Uzzi’s father was Michri.

Another man named Meshullam was Shephatiah’s son.

Shephatiah’s father was Reuel.

Reuel’s father was Ibnijah.

## 1 Chronicles 9:9

Their family record listed 956 of their relatives.

These men led their families.

## Priests who served in Jerusalem

## 1 Chronicles 9:10

The priests included Jedaiah, Jehoiarib, and Jachin.

## 1 Chronicles 9:11

Azariah was another priest.

His father was Hilkiah.

The family line went back through Meshullam, Zadok, and Meraioth to Ahitub.

This family held the post of chief official at God’s temple.

## 1 Chronicles 9:12

Adaiah’s father was Jeroham.

Jeroham’s father was Pashhur, and Pashhur’s father was Malchijah.

Another priest was Maasai, son of Adiel.

Adiel’s father was Jahzerah.

Jahzerah’s father was Meshullam.

Meshullam’s father was Meshillemith, and Meshillemith’s father was Immer.

## 1 Chronicles 9:13

The record names 1,760 of their relatives.

They led families and had the skills to serve in God’s house.

## Levites in Jerusalem

## 1 Chronicles 9:14

Shemaiah was one of the Levites.

His father was Hasshub.

Hasshub’s father was Azrikam.

Azrikam’s father was Hashabiah, from Merari’s family.

## 1 Chronicles 9:15

Other Levites were Bakbakkar, Heresh, Galal, and Mattaniah.

Mattaniah’s father was Mica.

Mica’s father was Zichri, and Zichri’s father was Asaph.

## 1 Chronicles 9:16

Obadiah’s father was Shemaiah.

Shemaiah’s father was Galal, and Galal’s father was Jeduthun.

Berechiah’s father was Asa, son of Elkanah.

This family lived in the villages around Netophah.

## The people who guarded the gates

## 1 Chronicles 9:17

Shallum, Akkub, Talmon, Ahiman, and their relatives guarded the gates.

Shallum was their leader.

## 1 Chronicles 9:18

Shallum had guarded the King’s Gate on the east side.

These gatekeepers came from the camp of the Levites.

## 1 Chronicles 9:19

Shallum’s father was Kore.

Kore’s father was Ebiasaph, son of Korah.

Shallum and his relatives from Korah’s family guarded the entrances to the sacred tent.

Their ancestors had guarded the entrance to the LORD’s dwelling place too.

## 1 Chronicles 9:20

Long before, Phinehas, son of Eleazar, had led the gatekeepers.

The LORD was with him.

## 1 Chronicles 9:21

Zechariah, son of Meshelemiah, guarded the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.

## 1 Chronicles 9:22

In all, 212 men had the job of guarding the entrances.

Their villages kept records of their families.

David and Samuel the seer had given them this responsibility.

A seer was someone who received messages from God.

## 1 Chronicles 9:23

So these men and their families after them guarded the gates of the LORD’s house.

People called this house the Tent.

## Day and night at the gates

## 1 Chronicles 9:24

The gatekeepers stood on all four sides.

They guarded the east, west, north, and south.

## 1 Chronicles 9:25

Their relatives came from their villages when their turn came.

Each group helped for seven days.

## 1 Chronicles 9:26

The four chief gatekeepers were Levites.

They cared for the rooms in God’s house.

They also guarded the places where people kept valuable gifts.

## 1 Chronicles 9:27

They stayed around God’s house at night to guard it.

Each morning they opened it.

## Supplies, bread, and music

## 1 Chronicles 9:28

Some Levites cared for the objects people used in worship.

They counted the objects each time someone took them out.

They counted them again when someone brought them back.

## 1 Chronicles 9:29

Others cared for the furniture and other objects in the holy place.

They also cared for the fine flour, wine, oil, frankincense, and spices.

Frankincense gives off a sweet smell when it burns.

## 1 Chronicles 9:30

Some sons of the priests mixed the spices.

## 1 Chronicles 9:31

Mattithiah had the job of baking bread.

He was a Levite, the first son of Shallum from Korah’s family.

## 1 Chronicles 9:32

Some relatives from Kohath’s family prepared special bread every Sabbath.

The Sabbath was the weekly day of rest.

They set the bread in rows before God.

## 1 Chronicles 9:33

The musicians who led Levite families stayed in rooms at the temple.

They were on duty day and night.

So they were free from other jobs.

## 1 Chronicles 9:34

These Levites lived in Jerusalem.

The family records named them as leaders of their families.

## The family at Gibeon

## 1 Chronicles 9:35

Jeiel, the father of Gibeon, lived in Gibeon.

His wife’s name was Maacah.

## 1 Chronicles 9:36

Jeiel’s first son was Abdon.

Then came Zur, Kish, Baal, Ner, and Nadab.

## 1 Chronicles 9:37

Gedor, Ahio, Zechariah, and Mikloth were also Jeiel’s sons.

## 1 Chronicles 9:38

Mikloth’s son was Shimeam.

They too lived near their relatives in Jerusalem.

## Saul and his family

## 1 Chronicles 9:39

Ner was Kish’s father.

Kish was Saul’s father.

Saul’s sons were Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadab, and Esh-baal.

![Kish, his son Saul, and Saul’s son Jonathan.](../pictures/kish-saul-jonathan.svg)

Follow the family line from Kish to his grandson Jonathan.

## 1 Chronicles 9:40

Jonathan’s son was Merib-baal.

Merib-baal was Micah’s father.

## 1 Chronicles 9:41

Micah’s sons were Pithon, Melech, Tahrea, and Ahaz.

## 1 Chronicles 9:42

Ahaz was Jarah’s father.

Jarah’s sons were Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri.

Zimri was Moza’s father.

## 1 Chronicles 9:43

Moza was Binea’s father.

Binea was Rephaiah’s father.

Rephaiah was Elasah’s father.

Elasah was Azel’s father.

## 1 Chronicles 9:44

Azel had six sons.

Their names were Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan.
