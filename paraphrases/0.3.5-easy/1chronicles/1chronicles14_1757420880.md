# 1 Chronicles 14 — David asks God what to do

**About this chapter**

David rules Israel from Jerusalem. When the Philistines attack twice, David asks God what to do each time.

## A palace for David

## 1 Chronicles 14:1

Hiram, the king of Tyre, sent messengers to David.

He also sent cedar logs and workers skilled with stone and wood.

They came to build a palace for David.

## 1 Chronicles 14:2

David understood that the LORD had made him secure as king of Israel.

The LORD had made the kingdom great for the good of His people.

## David’s children in Jerusalem

## 1 Chronicles 14:3

David married more wives in Jerusalem.

He had more sons and daughters there.

## 1 Chronicles 14:4

Here are the names of David’s children born in Jerusalem.

First come Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon.

## 1 Chronicles 14:5

Next come Ibhar, Elishua, and Elpelet.

## 1 Chronicles 14:6

Then come Nogah, Nepheg, and Japhia.

## 1 Chronicles 14:7

The last names on the list are Elishama, Beeliada, and Eliphelet.

## David asks God for help

## 1 Chronicles 14:8

The Philistines heard that Israel had anointed David king.

Anointing a king meant pouring oil on him to mark him as king.

David now ruled all Israel.

The Philistines came looking for him.

David heard the news and went out to face them.

## 1 Chronicles 14:9

The Philistines had attacked the Valley of Rephaim.

## 1 Chronicles 14:10

David asked God, “Should I go and fight the Philistines?

Will You help me defeat them?”

The LORD answered, “Go. I will give you victory over them.”

## 1 Chronicles 14:11

David and his men defeated the Philistines at Baal-perazim.

David said, “Water breaks through with great force in a flood.

That is how God helped me break through my enemies.”

That is why they called the place Baal-perazim.

![Water rises above the riverbanks and spreads across the land.](../pictures/river-overflows.svg)

## 1 Chronicles 14:12

The Philistines left their idols there when they fled.

David ordered his men to burn those objects of worship.

## David asks God again

## 1 Chronicles 14:13

The Philistines came back and attacked the valley again.

## 1 Chronicles 14:14

David asked God what to do this time.

God told David to go around the Philistines.

He was to face them near the balsam trees.

## 1 Chronicles 14:15

God said, “Listen for the sound of marching in the treetops.

When you hear it, go into battle.

That sound means I have gone ahead of you against the Philistine camp.”

## 1 Chronicles 14:16

David did what God told him.

David and his men defeated the Philistine army from Gibeon to Gezer.

## 1 Chronicles 14:17

People in every land heard about David.

The LORD made all the nations fear him.
