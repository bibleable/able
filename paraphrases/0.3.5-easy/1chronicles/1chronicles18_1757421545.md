# 1 Chronicles 18 — David’s victories and the people who served with him

**About this chapter**

David wins battles and sets aside gifts for the LORD. The chapter also names the people who help him govern Israel.

## David defeats neighboring armies

## 1 Chronicles 18:1

Some time later, David defeated the Philistines and brought them under his rule.

He took the city of Gath and its villages from them.

## 1 Chronicles 18:2

David also defeated the people of Moab.

They came under his rule and had to make payments to him.

Such payments to a ruler were called tribute.

## 1 Chronicles 18:3

Hadadezer, king of Zobah, marched out to take control along the Euphrates River.

David defeated him as far as Hamath.

## 1 Chronicles 18:4

David captured 1,000 chariots and 7,000 men who drove them.

He also captured 20,000 soldiers who fought on foot.

He kept 100 horses for the chariots.

He injured the other horses so they could no longer pull chariots.

## 1 Chronicles 18:5

Aramean soldiers from Damascus came to help King Hadadezer.

David’s army killed 22,000 of them.

## 1 Chronicles 18:6

David stationed soldiers in the region of Damascus.

The Arameans came under David’s rule and paid him tribute.

Wherever David went, the LORD gave him victory.

## Gold and bronze come to Jerusalem

## 1 Chronicles 18:7

David took the gold shields from Hadadezer’s officers.

He brought the shields to Jerusalem.

## 1 Chronicles 18:8

David took a great deal of bronze from Tibhath and Cun.

Both cities belonged to Hadadezer.

Later Solomon used that metal to make the bronze Sea, a huge basin for water.

He also used it to make pillars and other bronze objects.

## 1 Chronicles 18:9

Tou, the king of Hamath, heard the news.

David had defeated King Hadadezer’s whole army.

## 1 Chronicles 18:10

Hadadezer had been at war with Tou.

So Tou sent his son Hadoram to greet David.

Hadoram congratulated David and blessed him for defeating Hadadezer.

He brought objects made of gold, silver, and bronze.

## 1 Chronicles 18:11

David set these gifts apart for the LORD.

He did the same with the silver and gold he had taken from other peoples.

These included Edom, Moab, the Ammonites, the Philistines, and the Amalekites.

## Victory in Edom

## 1 Chronicles 18:12

Abishai was the son of Zeruiah.

He and his men killed 18,000 Edomites in the Valley of Salt.

## 1 Chronicles 18:13

Soldiers were stationed in Edom, and all the Edomites came under David’s rule.

Wherever David went, the LORD gave him victory.

## The people who help David govern

## 1 Chronicles 18:14

David ruled over all Israel.

He did what was right and judged fairly for all his people.

## 1 Chronicles 18:15

Joab, the son of Zeruiah, led the army.

Jehoshaphat, the son of Ahilud, kept the official records.

## 1 Chronicles 18:16

Zadok, the son of Ahitub, was a priest.

Ahimelech, the son of Abiathar, was also a priest.

Shavsha was the scribe, an official who wrote documents.

## 1 Chronicles 18:17

Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada, led the Cherethites and Pelethites.

These were groups of men who served the king.

David’s sons served at his side as leading officials.
