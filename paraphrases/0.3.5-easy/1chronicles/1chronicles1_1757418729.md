# 1 Chronicles 1 — The families from Adam to Abraham and beyond

**About this chapter**

This chapter follows families through many generations. It begins with Adam and leads to Abraham. It also names the people who ruled Edom.

## From Adam to Noah

## 1 Chronicles 1:1

The family record begins with Adam.

Adam was Seth’s father.

Seth was Enosh’s father.

## 1 Chronicles 1:2

Next in the family came Kenan, Mahalalel, and Jared.

## 1 Chronicles 1:3

Then came Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech, and Noah.

## 1 Chronicles 1:4

Noah had three sons named Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

## The family of Japheth

## 1 Chronicles 1:5

Japheth’s sons were Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras.

## 1 Chronicles 1:6

Gomer’s sons were Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah.

## 1 Chronicles 1:7

Elishah and Tarshish came from Javan.

The Kittites and the Rodanites also came from Javan.

## The family of Ham

## 1 Chronicles 1:8

Ham’s sons were Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan.

## 1 Chronicles 1:9

Cush’s sons were Seba, Havilah, Sabta, Raamah, and Sabteca.

Raamah’s sons were Sheba and Dedan.

## 1 Chronicles 1:10

Cush was Nimrod’s father.

Nimrod became a powerful man on earth.

## 1 Chronicles 1:11

Several peoples came from Mizraim’s family.

They included the Ludites, Anamites, Lehabites, and Naphtuhites.

## 1 Chronicles 1:12

The Pathrusites, Casluhites, and Caphtorites also came from Mizraim.

The Philistines came from the Casluhites.

## 1 Chronicles 1:13

Sidon was Canaan’s first son.

The Hittites also came from Canaan.

## 1 Chronicles 1:14

The Jebusites, Amorites, and Girgashites came from Canaan too.

## 1 Chronicles 1:15

So did the Hivites, Arkites, and Sinites.

## 1 Chronicles 1:16

The Arvadites, Zemarites, and Hamathites also came from Canaan.

## The family of Shem

## 1 Chronicles 1:17

Shem’s sons were Elam, Asshur, Arphaxad, Lud, and Aram.

Aram’s sons were Uz, Hul, Gether, and Meshech.

## 1 Chronicles 1:18

Arphaxad was Shelah’s father.

Shelah was Eber’s father.

## 1 Chronicles 1:19

Eber had two sons, Peleg and Joktan.

Peleg got his name because the earth was divided during his lifetime.

## 1 Chronicles 1:20

Joktan’s sons included Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, and Jerah.

## 1 Chronicles 1:21

Hadoram, Uzal, and Diklah were also Joktan’s sons.

## 1 Chronicles 1:22

So were Obal, Abimael, and Sheba.

## 1 Chronicles 1:23

Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab were also Joktan’s sons.

## From Shem to Abraham

## 1 Chronicles 1:24

Here is the family line from Shem to Abram.

Shem was Arphaxad’s father.

Arphaxad was Shelah’s father.

## 1 Chronicles 1:25

Next came Eber, then Peleg, then Reu.

## 1 Chronicles 1:26

Then came Serug, Nahor, and Terah.

## 1 Chronicles 1:27

Terah was Abram’s father.

Abram’s other name was Abraham.

## 1 Chronicles 1:28

Isaac and Ishmael were sons of Abraham.

## The families of Ishmael and Keturah

## 1 Chronicles 1:29

Here are names from their families.

Nebaioth was Ishmael’s first son.

Next came Kedar, Adbeel, and Mibsam.

## 1 Chronicles 1:30

Ishmael’s sons also included Mishma, Dumah, Massa, Hadad, and Tema.

## 1 Chronicles 1:31

Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah were also Ishmael’s sons.

## 1 Chronicles 1:32

Keturah was Abraham’s concubine.

This meant she had a lower place in his household than a full wife.

Their sons were Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah.

Jokshan’s sons were Sheba and Dedan.

## 1 Chronicles 1:33

Midian’s sons were Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida, and Eldaah.

All these people belonged to Keturah’s family.

## Isaac and the family of Esau

## 1 Chronicles 1:34

Abraham was Isaac’s father.

Isaac’s sons were Esau and Israel.

Israel was another name for Jacob.

## 1 Chronicles 1:35

Esau’s sons were Eliphaz, Reuel, Jeush, Jalam, and Korah.

## 1 Chronicles 1:36

Eliphaz’s sons included Teman, Omar, Zepho, Gatam, and Kenaz.

Eliphaz and Timna also had a son named Amalek.

## 1 Chronicles 1:37

Reuel’s sons were Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah.

## The family of Seir

## 1 Chronicles 1:38

Seir’s sons were Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan.

## 1 Chronicles 1:39

Lotan’s sons were Hori and Homam.

Timna was Lotan’s sister.

## 1 Chronicles 1:40

Shobal’s sons were Alvan, Manahath, Ebal, Shepho, and Onam.

Zibeon’s sons were Aiah and Anah.

## 1 Chronicles 1:41

Anah’s son was Dishon.

Dishon’s sons were Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran.

## 1 Chronicles 1:42

Ezer’s sons were Bilhan, Zaavan, and Akan.

Dishan’s sons were Uz and Aran.

## The kings of Edom

## 1 Chronicles 1:43

Edom had kings before Israel had a king.

One king of Edom was Bela, the son of Beor.

His city was Dinhabah.

## 1 Chronicles 1:44

When Bela died, Jobab became king.

Jobab was the son of Zerah from Bozrah.

## 1 Chronicles 1:45

When Jobab died, Husham became king.

Husham came from the land of the Temanites.

## 1 Chronicles 1:46

When Husham died, Hadad became king.

Hadad was Bedad’s son.

Hadad defeated the Midianites in the land of Moab.

His city was Avith.

## 1 Chronicles 1:47

When Hadad died, Samlah from Masrekah became king.

## 1 Chronicles 1:48

When Samlah died, Shaul became king.

Shaul came from Rehoboth on the Euphrates River.

## 1 Chronicles 1:49

When Shaul died, Baal-hanan became king.

Baal-hanan was Achbor’s son.

## 1 Chronicles 1:50

When Baal-hanan died, Hadad became king.

His city was Pau.

His wife was Mehetabel.

Mehetabel’s mother was Matred.

Matred was the daughter of Me-zahab.

## The chiefs of Edom

## 1 Chronicles 1:51

Then Hadad died.

Edom also had chiefs, or local leaders.

They included Timna, Alvah, and Jetheth.

## 1 Chronicles 1:52

Oholibamah, Elah, and Pinon were also chiefs.

## 1 Chronicles 1:53

So were Kenaz, Teman, and Mibzar.

## 1 Chronicles 1:54

Magdiel and Iram were chiefs too.

These were the chiefs of Edom.
