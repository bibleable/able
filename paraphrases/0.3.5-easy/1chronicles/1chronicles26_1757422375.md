# 1 Chronicles 26 — Families guard the gates and care for the gifts

**About this chapter**

The Levites had different jobs. Some guarded the gates of the LORD’s house. Others cared for valuable gifts. Others served as officers and judges across Israel.

## The gatekeepers and their families

## 1 Chronicles 26:1

The gatekeepers worked in groups.

One group came from Korah’s family.

Meshelemiah belonged to this group.

His father was Kore, from Asaph’s family.

## 1 Chronicles 26:2

Meshelemiah’s first son was Zechariah.

Jediael came second, Zebadiah third, and Jathniel fourth.

## 1 Chronicles 26:3

Elam came fifth, Jehohanan sixth, and Eliehoenai seventh.

## 1 Chronicles 26:4

Obed-edom’s first son was Shemaiah.

Jehozabad came second and Joah third.

Sachar came fourth and Nethanel fifth.

## 1 Chronicles 26:5

Ammiel came sixth, Issachar seventh, and Peullethai eighth.

God had blessed Obed-edom.

## 1 Chronicles 26:6

Shemaiah’s sons led their families.

They were strong men who knew how to do the work.

## 1 Chronicles 26:7

Shemaiah’s sons included Othni, Rephael, Obed, and Elzabad.

Their family also included Elihu and Semachiah, both capable men.

## 1 Chronicles 26:8

All these men came from Obed-edom’s family.

Counting their sons and brothers, there were 62 men.

They had the strength and skill for the work.

## 1 Chronicles 26:9

Meshelemiah’s sons and brothers were also skilled.

There were 18 of them.

## Hosah’s family

## 1 Chronicles 26:10

Hosah belonged to Merari’s family.

Hosah put his son Shimri first among his sons.

An older son had been born before Shimri.

## 1 Chronicles 26:11

Hilkiah came second, Tebaliah third, and Zechariah fourth.

Hosah’s sons and brothers numbered 13 in all.

## 1 Chronicles 26:12

Each group of gatekeepers had leaders.

Like their relatives, the gatekeepers had work to do at the LORD’s house.

## Each family receives a place to guard

## 1 Chronicles 26:13

The families drew lots for each gate.

Drawing lots meant using marked pieces to make a choice.

Young and old took part alike.

## 1 Chronicles 26:14

Shelemiah drew the east gate.

His son Zechariah gave wise advice.

The lot gave Zechariah the north gate.

## 1 Chronicles 26:15

Obed-edom drew the south gate.

His sons drew the storehouses, where people kept supplies.

## 1 Chronicles 26:16

Shuppim and Hosah drew the west gate and the Shallecheth Gate.

The Shallecheth Gate stood by the road leading uphill.

Guards took turns keeping watch.

## 1 Chronicles 26:17

Each day six Levites guarded the east side.

Four guarded the north and four guarded the south.

Two pairs guarded the storehouse.

## 1 Chronicles 26:18

On the west side, four guarded the road.

Two guarded the courtyard, an open space.

## 1 Chronicles 26:19

These groups of gatekeepers came from two families.

Their ancestors were Korah and Merari.

## Levites care for the treasuries

## 1 Chronicles 26:20

Other Levites cared for the treasuries of God’s house.

Treasuries were places to keep valuable things safe.

Some held gifts that people set apart for God.

## 1 Chronicles 26:21

Ladan came from Gershon’s family.

Jehieli was one of the family leaders who came from Ladan.

## 1 Chronicles 26:22

Jehieli’s sons were Zetham and his brother Joel.

These brothers cared for the treasuries of the LORD’s house.

## 1 Chronicles 26:23

Other Levites came from the families of Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.

## 1 Chronicles 26:24

Shebuel was the officer in charge of the treasuries.

His family came from Gershom, the son of Moses.

## 1 Chronicles 26:25

Shebuel also had relatives from Eliezer’s family.

Eliezer was Rehabiah’s father.

Rehabiah was Jeshaiah’s father.

Jeshaiah was Joram’s father.

Joram was Zichri’s father.

Zichri was Shelomith’s father.

## Gifts for the house of the LORD

## 1 Chronicles 26:26

Shelomith and his brothers guarded the gifts set apart for God.

King David and the army leaders had given these gifts.

Some givers were family heads who led thousands or hundreds of men.

## 1 Chronicles 26:27

They had given some of the goods they took in battle.

These gifts would pay for repairs to the LORD’s house.

## 1 Chronicles 26:28

Samuel the seer, a man who received messages from God, had given gifts too.

So had Saul son of Kish, Abner son of Ner, and Joab son of Zeruiah.

Shelomith and his brothers cared for these and all the other gifts.

## Officers and judges serve Israel

## 1 Chronicles 26:29

Chenaniah and his sons came from Izhar’s family.

They worked away from God’s house.

They served Israel as officers and judges.

## 1 Chronicles 26:30

Hashabiah and his relatives came from Hebron’s family.

These 1,700 skilled men served west of the Jordan River.

They managed work there for the LORD and for the king.

## 1 Chronicles 26:31

The family records named Jerijah as leader of Hebron’s family.

When David had ruled for 40 years, people searched the records.

They found strong, skilled men of Hebron’s family at Jazer in Gilead.

## 1 Chronicles 26:32

Jerijah had 2,700 relatives who were skilled family leaders.

King David put them in charge of work for God and the king.

They served the tribes of Reuben and Gad and half the tribe of Manasseh.
