# 1 Chronicles 20 — Joab captures Rabbah

**About this chapter**

Joab leads King David’s army against the Ammonites. Later, David’s men fight the Philistines.

## Joab takes the city

## 1 Chronicles 20:1

Spring was the time when kings went to war.

David stayed in Jerusalem.

Joab led the army into the land of the Ammonites.

His soldiers laid waste to the land.

Then they surrounded the city of Rabbah.

Joab attacked Rabbah and tore it down.

## 1 Chronicles 20:2

David took the crown from the Ammonite king’s head.

The crown held precious stones.

Its gold weighed one talent, a heavy measure of weight.

They put the crown on David’s head.

David also took many valuable things from the city.

![A royal crown beside an armband.](../pictures/sauls-crown-and-armband.svg)

## 1 Chronicles 20:3

David brought the people out of Rabbah.

He made them work with saws, iron picks, and axes.

He did the same in all the Ammonite cities.

Then David and all his soldiers went back to Jerusalem.

## Battles with the Philistines

## 1 Chronicles 20:4

Later, Israel fought the Philistines at Gezer.

Sibbecai, a Hushathite, killed Sippai.

Sippai came from a people called the Rephaim.

Israel brought the Philistines under its control.

## 1 Chronicles 20:5

Israel fought the Philistines again.

Elhanan, the son of Jair, killed Lahmi.

Lahmi was the brother of Goliath from Gath.

The pole of Lahmi’s spear was like a weaver’s beam.

That is a large piece of wood used in making cloth.

## A battle at Gath

## 1 Chronicles 20:6

Another battle took place at Gath.

A very tall man there belonged to Rapha’s family.

He had six fingers on each hand.

He had six toes on each foot.

That made twenty-four fingers and toes in all.

## 1 Chronicles 20:7

This man shouted insults at Israel.

Jonathan killed him.

Jonathan’s father was Shimei, David’s brother.

## 1 Chronicles 20:8

These men from Gath came from Rapha’s family.

They died in battle against David and his men.
