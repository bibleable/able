# 1 Chronicles 21 — David counts the people and asks God for mercy

**About this chapter**

David orders Joab to count the people of Israel. David later admits to God that he did wrong. When many people die, David asks God to spare the people.

## Joab warns David

## 1 Chronicles 21:1

Satan acted against Israel.

He urged David to take a census.

A census is a count of the people.

## 1 Chronicles 21:2

David told Joab and the army commanders, “Go through Israel from Beersheba to Dan.

Count the people and bring me the total.

I want to know how many there are.”

## 1 Chronicles 21:3

Joab answered, “May the LORD give you a hundred times as many troops! My king, all these people already serve you.

Why do you want this count? Why bring guilt on Israel?”

## 1 Chronicles 21:4

David insisted.

So Joab traveled throughout Israel.

Then he came back to Jerusalem.

## 1 Chronicles 21:5

Joab reported the number of men who could fight with swords.

All Israel had 1,100,000 such men.

That number included 470,000 men from Judah.

## 1 Chronicles 21:6

Joab hated the king’s order.

So he left the tribes of Levi and Benjamin out of the count.

## David admits his sin

## 1 Chronicles 21:7

God saw that David’s order was evil.

So God punished Israel.

## 1 Chronicles 21:8

David said to God, “I have sinned greatly by doing this.

I am your servant.

Please take away my guilt.

What I did was very foolish.”

## 1 Chronicles 21:9

The LORD spoke to Gad.

Gad was David’s seer, a man who received messages from God.

## 1 Chronicles 21:10

The LORD told Gad, “Go to David.

Tell him I offer him three choices.

I will do whichever one he chooses.”

## Gad tells David the three choices

## 1 Chronicles 21:11

Gad went to David and said, “The LORD says you must choose.

## 1 Chronicles 21:12

You can have three years of famine, with too little food to eat.

Or for three months your enemies can defeat you and catch you with their swords.

Or you can face the LORD’s sword for three days.

That means a deadly sickness will spread through the land.

The LORD’s angel will bring destruction throughout Israel.

What answer shall I take to the LORD?”

## 1 Chronicles 21:13

David told Gad, “I am in deep distress.

Let the LORD decide what happens to me.

He shows great mercy.

Please keep me out of human hands.”

## 1 Chronicles 21:14

So the LORD sent a deadly sickness through Israel.

Seventy thousand men of Israel died.

## The LORD stops the angel

## 1 Chronicles 21:15

God sent an angel to destroy Jerusalem.

As the angel began, the LORD saw what was happening.

He decided to stop the disaster.

He told the angel, “Enough! Stop now!” The angel stood by the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.

A threshing floor was a place for separating grain from stalks.

## 1 Chronicles 21:16

David looked up.

The LORD’s angel stood between heaven and earth.

The angel held a sword out over Jerusalem.

David and the elders wore rough cloth to show their grief.

They fell with their faces to the ground.

## 1 Chronicles 21:17

David said to God, “I gave the order to count the people.

I am the one who sinned and did wrong.

These people are like sheep in my care.

What have they done? LORD my God, let the punishment fall on me and my father’s family.

Please stop this sickness among your people.”

## David goes to Ornan

## 1 Chronicles 21:18

The LORD’s angel gave Gad a message for David.

David must go to Ornan’s threshing floor.

There he must build an altar to the LORD.

An altar is a place to offer gifts to God.

## 1 Chronicles 21:19

Gad gave David the LORD’s message.

So David went up to the threshing floor.

## 1 Chronicles 21:20

Ornan was separating the wheat from the stalks.

He turned and saw the angel.

His four sons hid.

![A stalk of grain with seeds at the top.](../pictures/grain.svg)

Find the seeds at the top of the stalk. On the threshing floor, Ornan separated the seeds from the stalks.

## 1 Chronicles 21:21

Then David came.

When Ornan saw David, he left the threshing floor.

He bowed with his face to the ground before the king.

## David pays for the threshing floor

## 1 Chronicles 21:22

David said to Ornan, “Sell me this threshing floor for its full price.

I want to build an altar to the LORD here.

Then the sickness among the people can stop.”

## 1 Chronicles 21:23

Ornan said, “Take it, my king! Do whatever you think best.

Take my oxen for burnt offerings.

Use my wooden threshing tools for firewood.

Take my wheat for a grain offering.

I give it all.”

## 1 Chronicles 21:24

King David answered, “I must pay you the full price.

These things belong to you.

I cannot give them to the LORD as my own gift.

My burnt offerings must cost me something.”

## 1 Chronicles 21:25

So David paid Ornan 600 shekels of gold for the place.

A shekel was a measure of weight.

## The LORD answers David

## 1 Chronicles 21:26

David built an altar to the LORD there.

He gave burnt offerings and offerings to celebrate peace with God.

David called to the LORD.

The LORD answered by sending fire from heaven onto the altar.

## 1 Chronicles 21:27

Then the LORD spoke to the angel.

The angel put his sword back into its cover.

## 1 Chronicles 21:28

David saw that the LORD had answered him at Ornan’s threshing floor.

So David offered sacrifices there.

## 1 Chronicles 21:29

Moses had made the LORD’s sacred tent in the wilderness.

That tent and the altar for burnt offerings were now at the place of worship in Gibeon.

## 1 Chronicles 21:30

David was afraid of the sword of the LORD’s angel.

So he could not go to Gibeon to ask God what to do.
