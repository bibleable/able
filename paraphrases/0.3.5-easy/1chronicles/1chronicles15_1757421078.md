# 1 Chronicles 15 — The Levites carry the ark to Jerusalem

**About this chapter**

The ark was a sacred chest. It reminded Israel of the covenant, the agreement God made with his people. David prepared a tent for the ark. The people made music as the Levites carried it to Jerusalem.

## David prepares a tent

## 1 Chronicles 15:1

David built houses for himself in the City of David.

He also prepared a place for the ark of God.

He put up a tent there for it.

## 1 Chronicles 15:2

David said, “Only the Levites may carry the ark of God.

The LORD chose them to carry his ark.

He chose them to serve him forever.”

## 1 Chronicles 15:3

David called all Israel together in Jerusalem.

They would bring the ark of the LORD to the place David had prepared.

## Six Levite families gather

## 1 Chronicles 15:4

David gathered Aaron’s family and the other Levites.

The Levites belonged to the tribe of Levi.

## 1 Chronicles 15:5

Uriel led the family of Kohath.

He came with 120 of his relatives.

## 1 Chronicles 15:6

Asaiah led the family of Merari.

He came with 220 of his relatives.

## 1 Chronicles 15:7

Joel led the family of Gershom.

He came with 130 of his relatives.

## 1 Chronicles 15:8

Shemaiah led the family of Elizaphan.

He came with 200 of his relatives.

## 1 Chronicles 15:9

Eliel led the family of Hebron.

He came with 80 of his relatives.

## 1 Chronicles 15:10

Amminadab led the family of Uzziel.

He came with 112 of his relatives.

## The Levites get ready to carry the ark

## 1 Chronicles 15:11

David called two priests, Zadok and Abiathar.

He also called the Levites Uriel, Asaiah, Joel, Shemaiah, Eliel, and Amminadab.

## 1 Chronicles 15:12

David said, “You lead the families of Levi.

You and your relatives must make yourselves ready for holy work.

Then bring the ark of the LORD, Israel’s God, to the place I prepared.

## 1 Chronicles 15:13

You Levites were absent the first time.

That is why the LORD our God acted in anger against us.

We failed to ask him how to carry the ark.”

## 1 Chronicles 15:14

So the priests and Levites made themselves ready for holy work.

They would bring up the ark of the LORD, Israel’s God.

## 1 Chronicles 15:15

The Levites rested the poles on their shoulders.

With those poles they carried the ark of God.

This was what the LORD had told Moses to command.

## David asks for joyful music

## 1 Chronicles 15:16

David told the Levite leaders to choose singers from their families.

The singers would sing out with joy.

Musicians would play harps, lyres, and cymbals.

Harps and lyres have strings.

## 1 Chronicles 15:17

The Levites chose Heman, the son of Joel.

They also chose his relative Asaph, the son of Berechiah.

They chose Ethan, the son of Kushaiah, from Merari’s family.

## 1 Chronicles 15:18

Other Levites served under them.

They included Zechariah, Jaaziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, and Eliab.

Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, and Mikneiah also served.

Obed-edom and Jeiel, who guarded the gates, joined them.

## 1 Chronicles 15:19

Heman, Asaph, and Ethan played the bronze cymbals.

Cymbals are metal plates that musicians strike together.

## 1 Chronicles 15:20

Zechariah, Aziel, Shemiramoth, and Jehiel played the harps.

Unni, Eliab, Maaseiah, and Benaiah played with them.

They followed a musical direction called Alamoth.

## 1 Chronicles 15:21

Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Obed-edom, Jeiel, and Azaziah played the lyres.

They led the music.

They followed a musical direction called Sheminith.

## 1 Chronicles 15:22

Chenaniah was a Levite leader.

He directed the music because he knew it so well.

## Priests and guards walk with the ark

## 1 Chronicles 15:23

Berechiah and Elkanah guarded the ark.

## 1 Chronicles 15:24

Seven priests blew trumpets in front of the ark of God.

They were Shebaniah, Joshaphat, Nethanel, Amasai, Zechariah, Benaiah, and Eliezer.

Obed-edom and Jehiah also guarded the ark.

## The people bring the ark to Jerusalem

## 1 Chronicles 15:25

David went with Israel’s elders and the commanders of groups of a thousand.

They went joyfully to Obed-edom’s house.

They had come for the ark of the LORD’s covenant.

## 1 Chronicles 15:26

God helped the Levites carry the ark of his covenant.

So the people offered God seven bulls and seven rams.

A ram is a male sheep.

## 1 Chronicles 15:27

David wore a robe made of fine linen cloth.

The Levites carrying the ark wore linen robes too.

So did the singers and their music leader, Chenaniah.

David also wore a linen garment called an ephod.

## 1 Chronicles 15:28

All Israel brought up the ark of the LORD’s covenant.

People shouted and blew rams’ horns and trumpets.

Others struck cymbals and played harps and lyres.

## 1 Chronicles 15:29

The ark of the LORD’s covenant entered the City of David.

Michal, Saul’s daughter, looked down from a window.

She saw King David dancing and celebrating.

In her heart she looked down on David.

She thought he deserved no respect.
