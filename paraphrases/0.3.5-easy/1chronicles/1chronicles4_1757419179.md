# 1 Chronicles 4 — The families of Judah and Simeon

**About this chapter**

The family records tell us more than people’s names. They tell how Jabez prayed, what work some families did, and where the people of Simeon settled.

## Families from Judah

## 1 Chronicles 4:1

Perez, Hezron, Carmi, Hur, and Shobal came from Judah’s family.

## 1 Chronicles 4:2

Shobal’s son Reaiah was the father of Jahath.

Jahath was the father of Ahumai and Lahad.

The Zorathite families came from them.

## 1 Chronicles 4:3

Etam’s sons were Jezreel, Ishma, and Idbash.

Their sister’s name was Hazzelelponi.

## 1 Chronicles 4:4

Penuel was Gedor’s father.

Ezer was Hushah’s father.

They came from the family of Hur.

Hur was Ephrathah’s first child and the father of Bethlehem.

## The family of Ashhur

## 1 Chronicles 4:5

Ashhur, the father of Tekoa, had two wives.

Their names were Helah and Naarah.

## 1 Chronicles 4:6

Naarah had four sons with Ashhur.

They were Ahuzzam, Hepher, Temeni, and Haahashtari.

## 1 Chronicles 4:7

Helah’s sons included Zereth, Zohar, and Ethnan.

## 1 Chronicles 4:8

Koz was also her son.

Koz was the father of Anub and Zobebah.

The families of Aharhel, Harum’s son, also came from Koz.

## Jabez asks God for help

## 1 Chronicles 4:9

Jabez received more honor than his brothers.

His mother had named him Jabez because she felt pain when she gave birth to him.

## 1 Chronicles 4:10

Jabez prayed to the God of Israel.

“Please bless me and give me more land.

Stay with me and help me.

Keep me safe from harm so I can be free from pain.” God gave Jabez what he asked for.

## Families and skilled workers

## 1 Chronicles 4:11

Chelub was Shuhah’s brother.

Chelub was Mehir’s father.

Mehir was Eshton’s father.

## 1 Chronicles 4:12

Eshton’s sons were Beth-rapha, Paseah, and Tehinnah.

Tehinnah was Ir-nahash’s father.

These men belonged to Recah.

## 1 Chronicles 4:13

Kenaz had two sons, Othniel and Seraiah.

Othniel’s sons were Hathath and Meonothai.

## 1 Chronicles 4:14

Meonothai was Ophrah’s father.

Seraiah was Joab’s father.

The people of Ge-harashim came from Joab’s family.

The place got its name because skilled workers lived there.

## 1 Chronicles 4:15

Caleb was the son of Jephunneh.

Caleb’s sons were Iru, Elah, and Naam.

Elah’s son was Kenaz.

## 1 Chronicles 4:16

Jehallelel’s sons were Ziph, Ziphah, Tiria, and Asarel.

## The family of Mered and other families

## 1 Chronicles 4:17

Ezrah’s sons were Jether, Mered, Epher, and Jalon.

Mered and his wife Bithiah had Miriam, Shammai, and Ishbah.

Ishbah was Eshtemoa’s father.

## 1 Chronicles 4:18

Bithiah was the daughter of Pharaoh, king of Egypt.

Mered also married a woman from Judah.

Her sons were Jered, Heber, and Jekuthiel.

Jered was Gedor’s father.

Heber was Soco’s father.

Jekuthiel was Zanoah’s father.

## 1 Chronicles 4:19

Hodiah married Naham’s sister.

Her sons were the fathers of Keilah the Garmite and Eshtemoa the Maacathite.

## 1 Chronicles 4:20

Shimon’s sons were Amnon, Rinnah, Ben-hanan, and Tilon.

Zoheth and Ben-zoheth came from Ishi’s family.

## People who make cloth and pots

## 1 Chronicles 4:21

Shelah was Judah’s son.

Shelah’s family included Er, the father of Lecah, and Laadah, the father of Mareshah.

It also included the families who made linen cloth at Beth-ashbea.

## 1 Chronicles 4:22

Old records also name Jokim and the men of Cozeba.

They name Joash and Saraph, who ruled in Moab and Jashubi-lehem.

## 1 Chronicles 4:23

These people were potters.

They shaped clay into things like jars.

They lived at Netaim and Gederah and worked for the king.

## Families from Simeon

## 1 Chronicles 4:24

Nemuel, Jamin, Jarib, Zerah, and Shaul came from Simeon’s family.

## 1 Chronicles 4:25

Shaul’s sons were Shallum, Mibsam, and Mishma.

## 1 Chronicles 4:26

Mishma’s sons were Hammuel, Zaccur, and Shimei.

## 1 Chronicles 4:27

Shimei had 16 sons and 6 daughters.

His brothers had few children.

So their whole family stayed smaller than Judah’s family.

## The towns where they lived

## 1 Chronicles 4:28

The people of Simeon lived in Beersheba, Moladah, and Hazar-shual.

## 1 Chronicles 4:29

They also lived in Bilhah, Ezem, and Tolad.

## 1 Chronicles 4:30

Other towns were Bethuel, Hormah, and Ziklag.

## 1 Chronicles 4:31

They also lived in Beth-marcaboth, Hazar-susim, Beth-biri, and Shaaraim.

These towns were theirs until David ruled as king.

## 1 Chronicles 4:32

They had five more towns.

These were Etam, Ain, Rimmon, Tochen, and Ashan.

## 1 Chronicles 4:33

They lived in the villages around these towns as far as Baal.

They kept records of their families.

## The family leaders

## 1 Chronicles 4:34

The records name Meshobab, Jamlech, and Joshah.

Joshah was Amaziah’s son.

## 1 Chronicles 4:35

They also name Joel and Jehu.

Jehu’s father was Joshibiah.

Joshibiah’s father was Seraiah.

Seraiah’s father was Asiel.

## 1 Chronicles 4:36

Other names were Elioenai, Jaakobah, Jeshohaiah, Asaiah, Adiel, Jesimiel, and Benaiah.

## 1 Chronicles 4:37

The records also name Ziza.

Ziza’s father was Shiphi.

Shiphi’s father was Allon.

Allon’s father was Jedaiah.

Jedaiah’s father was Shimri.

Shimri’s father was Shemaiah.

## 1 Chronicles 4:38

All these men led their families.

Their families grew very large.

## A search for pasture

## 1 Chronicles 4:39

They needed grass for their flocks to eat.

So they went to the entrance of Gedor, on the east side of the valley.

## 1 Chronicles 4:40

They found plenty of good grass there.

The land was wide, peaceful, and quiet.

People from Ham’s family had lived there before.

## 1 Chronicles 4:41

The leaders named above came when Hezekiah was king of Judah.

They attacked the people of Ham in their homes.

They also attacked the Meunites who lived there.

They destroyed those communities and settled in their place.

There was grass there for their flocks.

## 1 Chronicles 4:42

Another 500 people from Simeon went to Mount Seir.

Four sons of Ishi led them.

They were Pelatiah, Neariah, Rephaiah, and Uzziel.

## 1 Chronicles 4:43

They killed the remaining Amalekites who had escaped.

The people of Simeon still lived there when this record was written.
