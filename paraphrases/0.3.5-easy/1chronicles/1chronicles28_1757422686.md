# 1 Chronicles 28 — David gives Solomon the temple plans

**About this chapter**

David calls Israel’s leaders to Jerusalem. He tells them why his son Solomon will build the temple. The temple will be a house where people worship the LORD.

## David speaks to Israel’s leaders

## 1 Chronicles 28:1

David called all Israel’s leaders to Jerusalem.

The leaders of the tribes and those in the king’s service came.

Army officers who led thousands or hundreds came too.

Some officials cared for the royal family’s land, goods, and cattle.

They came with the palace officials and all the brave warriors.

## 1 Chronicles 28:2

King David stood up and said, “My brothers and my people, listen.

I wanted to build a house for the LORD’s sacred chest.

The chest is called the ark of the covenant.

It reminds us of God’s promise to his people.

The chest would have a resting place there.

The house would be like a footstool for our God, our King.

I had already begun to get things ready.

## 1 Chronicles 28:3

But God told me, ‘You have fought wars and killed people.

Someone else must build the house that honors my name.’

## 1 Chronicles 28:4

Yet the LORD, Israel’s God, chose my family to rule Israel forever.

He chose Judah as the tribe to lead the others.

From Judah he chose my father’s family.

Of all my father’s sons, he was pleased to choose me as king.

## 1 Chronicles 28:5

The LORD has given me many sons.

He chose my son Solomon to sit on the throne.

Israel is the LORD’s kingdom.

## 1 Chronicles 28:6

God told me, ‘Solomon will build my house and its courtyards.

Courtyards are open spaces around a building.

I have chosen Solomon as my son. I will be his Father.

## 1 Chronicles 28:7

Solomon obeys my laws and commands today.

If he keeps obeying them faithfully, I will make his kingdom last forever.’

## 1 Chronicles 28:8

All Israel is gathered here, and our God hears us.

Learn what the LORD your God commands. Obey all his commands.

Then you can keep this good land.

You can pass it down to your family forever.

## David speaks to Solomon

## 1 Chronicles 28:9

Solomon, my son, get to know the God I serve.

Serve him willingly and with your whole heart.

The LORD knows what each person thinks.

He knows why you want to do something.

If you seek him, you will find him.

If you leave him, he will turn you away forever.

## 1 Chronicles 28:10

Remember, the LORD chose you to build his holy house.

Be strong and do the work.”

## Solomon receives the plans

## 1 Chronicles 28:11

David gave Solomon the building plans.

They showed the entrance porch, buildings, and storage rooms.

They showed the upper rooms and the rooms farther inside.

One room was for the mercy seat, the cover of the sacred chest.

![A plan showing the main hall and the inner room behind it.](../pictures/solomons-temple-rooms.svg)

## 1 Chronicles 28:12

David’s plans also showed the courtyards and the rooms around them.

Some rooms would hold the temple treasures.

Others would hold things people had set apart for God.

## 1 Chronicles 28:13

The plans described the work of the priests and Levites.

The Levites were members of Levi’s tribe who served at the temple.

David listed the groups and all their jobs.

He also listed the things they would use.

## 1 Chronicles 28:14

For each gold object, David listed the weight of gold needed.

He did the same for every silver object.

## 1 Chronicles 28:15

Each lampstand had lamps to go with it.

The plans gave the weight for each gold stand and its lamps.

They gave the weight for each silver set too.

Each set had its own use.

## 1 Chronicles 28:16

Some tables held bread that people set before God.

David listed how much gold each bread table needed.

He listed the silver needed for the silver tables too.

## 1 Chronicles 28:17

The forks, bowls for sprinkling, and pitchers needed pure gold.

David listed how much gold to use for them.

He gave a weight for each gold dish and each silver bowl.

## 1 Chronicles 28:18

David listed the weight of pure gold for the incense altar.

Priests burned incense there to make sweet-smelling smoke.

The plans showed a chariot formed by gold figures called cherubim.

Their wings would stretch out over the LORD’s sacred chest.

## Solomon will have help

## 1 Chronicles 28:19

David said, “The LORD guided me as I wrote these plans.

He helped me understand every detail.”

## 1 Chronicles 28:20

David told Solomon, “Be strong and brave. Do the work.

Take heart. The LORD, my God, is with you.

He will stay with you and help you until you finish.

Then the people can serve him in his house.

## 1 Chronicles 28:21

The groups of priests and Levites are ready to serve God there.

Workers with every kind of skill are willing to help you.

The officials and all the people will follow your orders.”
