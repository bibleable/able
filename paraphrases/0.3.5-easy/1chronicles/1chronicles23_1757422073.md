# 1 Chronicles 23 — David gives the Levites their work

**About this chapter**

The Levites were people from the family of Levi. David gave them different jobs. Some cared for the house of the LORD. Others made music to praise him.

## David gives out the jobs

## 1 Chronicles 23:1

David was an old man.

He made his son Solomon king over Israel.

## 1 Chronicles 23:2

David gathered all the leaders of Israel.

He also called the priests and the other Levites together.

## 1 Chronicles 23:3

They counted the Levite men aged 30 and older.

There were 38,000 men.

## 1 Chronicles 23:4

David said, “I want 24,000 men to lead the work at the house of the LORD.

Another 6,000 will serve as officers and judges.

## 1 Chronicles 23:5

Another 4,000 will guard the gates.

The other 4,000 will praise the LORD.

They will play the instruments I made for praising him.”

![A harp with strings to pluck](../pictures/davids-harp.svg)

A harp is one instrument people played to praise the LORD.

## 1 Chronicles 23:6

David put the Levites into three groups.

Each group came from one of Levi’s sons.

Their names were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.

## The families of Gershon

## 1 Chronicles 23:7

Two families came from Gershon.

They were the families of Ladan and Shimei.

## 1 Chronicles 23:8

Ladan had three sons.

Jehiel came first, then Zetham and Joel.

## 1 Chronicles 23:9

Shimei had three sons named Shelomoth, Haziel, and Haran.

These men led families in Ladan’s group.

## 1 Chronicles 23:10

The record also lists four sons of Shimei.

Their names were Jahath, Zina, Jeush, and Beriah.

## 1 Chronicles 23:11

Jahath came first.

Zizah came second.

Jeush and Beriah had few sons.

So their families counted as one family.

They shared one job.

## The families of Kohath

## 1 Chronicles 23:12

Kohath had four sons.

Their names were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.

## 1 Chronicles 23:13

Amram’s sons were Aaron and Moses.

Aaron and his family after him had special work forever.

They set apart the most holy things for God.

They burned incense, which gives off sweet-smelling smoke.

They served the LORD and spoke blessings in his name.

## 1 Chronicles 23:14

Moses was a man of God.

His sons belonged to the tribe of Levi.

## 1 Chronicles 23:15

Moses had two sons, Gershom and Eliezer.

## 1 Chronicles 23:16

Shebuel came first in Gershom’s family.

## 1 Chronicles 23:17

Eliezer had just one son, Rehabiah.

But Rehabiah had many sons.

## 1 Chronicles 23:18

Shelomith came first among Izhar’s sons.

## 1 Chronicles 23:19

Jeriah was Hebron’s first son.

Amariah came second, Jahaziel third, and Jekameam fourth.

## 1 Chronicles 23:20

Micah was Uzziel’s first son.

Isshiah came second.

## The families of Merari

## 1 Chronicles 23:21

Merari had two sons, Mahli and Mushi.

Mahli had two sons, Eleazar and Kish.

## 1 Chronicles 23:22

Eleazar had daughters.

He died without having any sons.

His daughters married their cousins, the sons of Kish.

## 1 Chronicles 23:23

Mushi had three sons.

Their names were Mahli, Eder, and Jeremoth.

## The Levites help the priests

## 1 Chronicles 23:24

These were the families that came from Levi.

The record listed the family leaders by name.

Levites aged 20 and older worked at the house of the LORD.

## 1 Chronicles 23:25

David had said, “The LORD is the God of Israel.

He has given his people rest.

He has come to live in Jerusalem forever.

## 1 Chronicles 23:26

So the Levites can stop carrying the holy tent.

They can also stop carrying the things used inside it.”

## 1 Chronicles 23:27

In his final orders, David said to count the Levites aged 20 and older.

## 1 Chronicles 23:28

Their job was to help the priests from Aaron’s family.

They cared for the courtyards and rooms at God’s house.

They made the holy things clean for worship.

They also helped with the other work there.

## 1 Chronicles 23:29

The Levites set bread in rows before God.

They prepared fine flour for grain gifts.

They made thin bread without yeast.

They mixed and baked.

They checked all the amounts and sizes.

## 1 Chronicles 23:30

Every morning the Levites stood to thank and praise the LORD.

They did this every evening too.

## 1 Chronicles 23:31

The priests burned whole offerings for the LORD on special days.

These included the weekly Sabbath, the start of each month, and the feasts.

Each time, the required number of Levites came to serve.

## 1 Chronicles 23:32

The Levites cared for the Tent of Meeting and the Holy Place.

They worked under their relatives, the priests from Aaron’s family.

Together they served at the house of the LORD.
