# 1 Chronicles 17 — The LORD makes a promise to David’s family

**About this chapter**

David wants to build a house for the LORD. The LORD sends Nathan to David with a promise about a different kind of house: the family that will rule after David.

## David speaks to Nathan

## 1 Chronicles 17:1

David had settled into his palace.

He told Nathan the prophet, “I live in a house built of cedar wood.

But the LORD’s ark is in a tent.” The ark was a sacred chest that reminded Israel of God’s covenant, his promise to his people.

![A tent beside a house. David lived in a house, while the ark stayed in a tent.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

## 1 Chronicles 17:2

Nathan said, “Do what you have in mind.

God is with you.”

## 1 Chronicles 17:3

But that night God spoke to Nathan.

## 1 Chronicles 17:4

“Go to my servant David.

Tell him what I, the LORD, say.

Someone else will build the house where I will live.

## 1 Chronicles 17:5

Since I brought Israel out of Egypt, I have never lived in a house.

My dwelling has been a tent that moved from place to place.

## 1 Chronicles 17:6

I traveled with the people of Israel.

I chose leaders to care for them as shepherds care for sheep.

Did I ever ask those leaders to build me a cedar house?

## The LORD promises a home and a king

## 1 Chronicles 17:7

Tell my servant David what the LORD of Hosts says.” This title means the LORD who leads the armies of heaven.

“David, you used to follow sheep in the pasture.

I took you from there to lead my people Israel.

## 1 Chronicles 17:8

Wherever you went, I was with you.

I removed the enemies who stood in your way.

I will make your name as well known as the greatest names in the land.

## 1 Chronicles 17:9

I will give my people Israel a place of their own.

I will plant them there like a tree that can take root.

They will have a home where no one disturbs them.

Evil people used to make them suffer.

## 1 Chronicles 17:10

That suffering continued when I appointed judges to lead Israel.

I will bring all your enemies under control.

And I, the LORD, will build a house for you.” Here “house” means David’s royal family.

## 1 Chronicles 17:11

“When you die and join your ancestors, one of your own sons will follow you.

I will make his kingdom secure.

## 1 Chronicles 17:12

Your son will build a house for me.

I will make his royal throne last forever.

## 1 Chronicles 17:13

I will be his Father, and he will be my son.

I took my faithful love away from the king who ruled before you.

My faithful love will stay with your son.

## 1 Chronicles 17:14

I will put him over my house and my kingdom forever.

His throne will always stand.”

## 1 Chronicles 17:15

Nathan told David everything God had shown him.

**Pause and answer**

Who did the LORD say would build a house for him?

## David sits before the LORD

## 1 Chronicles 17:16

King David went in and sat before the LORD.

He prayed, “LORD God, who am I?

What is so special about my family that you have brought us this far?

## 1 Chronicles 17:17

God, you have done even more.

You have told me what lies ahead for my family.

LORD God, you have treated me as someone worthy of great honor.

## 1 Chronicles 17:18

What more can I say about the honor you have given me?

You know me, your servant.

## 1 Chronicles 17:19

LORD, you chose to do this great thing for me.

It was what you wanted.

You let me hear all these great promises.

## 1 Chronicles 17:20

LORD, there is no one like you.

Everything we have heard tells us that you alone are God.

## 1 Chronicles 17:21

What other nation is like Israel?

You went to Egypt to free us and make us your own people.

You drove out nations before us.

The wonderful things you did made your name known.

## 1 Chronicles 17:22

You made Israel your own people forever.

You, LORD, became our God.

## David asks God to keep his promise

## 1 Chronicles 17:23

Now, LORD, let your word to me and my family stand forever.

Do what you promised.

## 1 Chronicles 17:24

Then people will honor your name forever.

They will say, ‘The LORD of Hosts, Israel’s God, rules over Israel.’ May my family stand firm before you.

## 1 Chronicles 17:25

My God, you told me that you would build a house for me.

Your promise about my family gives me courage to pray to you.

## 1 Chronicles 17:26

LORD, you are God!

You have promised this good thing to me, your servant.

## 1 Chronicles 17:27

You have chosen to bless my family so that it will continue before you forever.

LORD, you have given your blessing.

That blessing will last forever.”
