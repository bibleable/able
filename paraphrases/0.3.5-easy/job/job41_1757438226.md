# Job 41 — The LORD describes Leviathan

**About this chapter**

The LORD asks Job about a powerful creature called Leviathan. Picture his scales, his teeth, and the trail he leaves in the sea.

## Can Job catch Leviathan?

## Job 41:1

Job, can you pull Leviathan in with a fishhook?

Can you tie his tongue down with a rope?

## Job 41:2

Can you put a cord through his nose?

Can you put a hook through his jaw?

## Job 41:3

Will he beg you to spare him?

Will he speak softly to you?

## Job 41:4

Will he make a lasting agreement with you?

Will he agree to be your slave for life?

## Job 41:5

Can you play with him like a pet bird?

Can you put him on a leash for your young women?

## Job 41:6

Will traders exchange goods for him?

Will the sellers cut him up and share him?

## Job 41:7

Can you fill his thick skin with hunting spears?

Can you fill his head with fishing spears?

## Who can stand against the LORD?

## Job 41:8

Lay a hand on him just once.

You will remember that struggle.

You will never try again!

## Job 41:9

Whoever hopes to defeat him hopes in vain.

Just seeing him is enough to make you weak.

## Job 41:10

No one is fierce enough to stir Leviathan up.

Who, then, can stand against me?

## Job 41:11

Who ever gave me something I must pay back?

Everything under the sky belongs to me.

**Pause and answer**

Who says everything under the sky belongs to him?

## Leviathan’s teeth and scales

## Job 41:12

I must tell you about his powerful limbs.

Look how strong and graceful his body is.

## Job 41:13

Who can peel off his outer coat?

Who dares come near with straps to guide him?

## Job 41:14

Who dares pull his jaws apart?

Fearsome teeth line his mouth.

## Job 41:15

He proudly wears rows of scales.

Each row fits tightly against the next.

## Job 41:16

Each scale lies close against its neighbor.

Even air cannot slip between them.

## Job 41:17

One scale grips the next.

No one can pull them apart.

## His breath and his strength

## Job 41:18

Light flashes when he snorts.

His eyes shine like the first light of morning.

## Job 41:19

Burning sticks seem to stream from his mouth.

Bright sparks shoot out.

## Job 41:20

Smoke rolls out of his nose.

It looks like a boiling pot over burning reeds.

## Job 41:21

His breath makes coals catch fire.

Flames pour out of his mouth.

## Job 41:22

His neck is full of strength.

Wherever he goes, terror goes ahead of him.

## Job 41:23

The folds of his flesh fit tightly together.

They hold firm and never shift.

## Job 41:24

His chest is hard as rock.

It is as hard as the bottom stone of a grain mill.

## Even strong people back away

## Job 41:25

When Leviathan rises, even strong people shake with fear.

When he thrashes about, they back away.

## Job 41:26

A sword can reach him without hurting him.

Spears, darts, and arrows do him no harm.

## Job 41:27

To him, iron is as weak as straw.

Bronze is like rotten wood.

## Job 41:28

No arrow makes him run away.

Stones from a sling seem like dry husks to him.

## Job 41:29

A heavy club seems like straw to him.

He laughs when he hears a spear coming.

## Leviathan moves through the sea

## Job 41:30

His belly is rough like broken pieces of pottery.

It scrapes the mud like a farmer’s threshing sled.

![A brown jar made of clay.](../pictures/clay-jar.svg)

**Picture the rough belly**

Imagine the sharp edges if this clay jar broke. The LORD compares Leviathan’s belly to those rough pieces. A threshing sled is a heavy board with sharp parts underneath. Farmers dragged it across stalks to loosen the grain.

## Job 41:31

He stirs the deep water like a boiling pot.

The sea swirls like ointment in a jar.

## Job 41:32

A shining trail follows him through the sea.

The white foam looks like white hair on the water.

## Job 41:33

No creature on earth is his equal.

Nothing makes him afraid.

## Job 41:34

He looks down on every proud creature.

He is king over them all.

**Pause and answer**

Tell in your own words what the water looks like after Leviathan passes.
