# Job 14 — Job asks if a person can live again

**About this chapter**

Job speaks to God about how short life is. He sees new shoots on a tree stump and wonders whether a person who dies can live again.

## A life as short as a flower

## Job 14:1

Every person born to a woman has a short life.

Those few days are full of trouble.

## Job 14:2

A person is like a flower that opens and fades.

Our lives pass like shadows that soon disappear.

## Job 14:3

God, do you watch someone whose life is so short?

Will you bring that person before you for judgment?

## Job 14:4

Who can bring something clean out of something unclean?

No one can!

## Job asks God to give people rest

## Job 14:5

You have decided how many days a person will live.

You know the number of months.

No one can live beyond the limit you set.

## Job 14:6

Please look away and give that person a rest.

Let him enjoy his day like a hired worker.

## A tree can grow again

## Job 14:7

There is still hope for a tree someone cuts down.

It can sprout again.

Its new shoots can keep growing.

## Job 14:8

Its roots may grow old in the earth.

Its stump may die in the ground.

## Job 14:9

Yet at the first hint of water, buds appear.

New twigs grow as they would on a young tree.

## Job asks what happens when a person dies

## Job 14:10

But a person dies and lies still.

When someone takes a last breath, where is that person?

## Job 14:11

Water vanishes from a sea.

A riverbed dries up.

## Job 14:12

In the same way, a person lies down in death and does not get up.

As long as the heavens last, no one wakes that person.

**Reading help**

Job speaks of death as sleep. He wonders what could wake someone from that sleep.

## Job longs for God to remember him

## Job 14:13

God, I wish you would hide me in Sheol, the place of the dead.

Keep me hidden until your anger passes.

Set a time to remember me again!

## Job 14:14

When someone dies, will that person live again?

Through all my days of hard service, I will wait.

I will wait for you to give me new life.

## Job 14:15

You would call me, and I would answer.

You made me with your hands.

You would long to see me again.

## Job 14:16

Then you would count my steps.

Yet you would no longer keep count of my sins.

## Job 14:17

It would be as if you sealed my wrongs in a bag.

You would cover over the wrong I had done.

**Pause and answer**

What does Job hope God will stop counting?

## Water wears away stone

## Job 14:18

But even a mountain breaks apart and crumbles.

A rock comes loose from its place.

## Job 14:19

Water wears away stones.

Rushing water carries away the soil.

That is how you wear away a person’s hope.

## Job 14:20

You overpower a person for good, and he dies.

You change the look on his face and send him away.

## Job 14:21

If his sons receive honor, he never hears of it.

If they lose respect, he never knows.

## Job 14:22

He feels only his own pain.

He grieves only for himself.

**Pause and answer**
