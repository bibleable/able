# Job 18 — Bildad speaks of a lamp going out

**About this chapter**

Bildad speaks to Job again. He describes what he believes happens to a person who does evil.

## Bildad challenges Job

## Job 18:1

Bildad the Shuhite answered Job.

## Job 18:2

When will you finish these speeches?

Think carefully, and then we can talk.

## Job 18:3

Why do you think we are like cattle?

Why do you think we understand so little?

## Job 18:4

Job, you tear at yourself in anger.

Should everyone leave the earth because of you?

Should the rocks move from their places for you?

**Pause and answer**

What does Bildad want Job to do before they talk?

## A lamp goes out

## Job 18:5

A wicked man’s lamp goes out.

His fire gives no more light.

## Job 18:6

His tent grows dark.

Even the lamp beside him goes out.

![A small clay lamp gives light in the dark.](../pictures/davids-lamp.svg)

**Reading help**

Imagine the flame going out in a dark tent. Bildad uses that picture to describe a life coming to an end.

## Job 18:7

Once he walked with long, strong steps.

Now his steps grow short.

His own plans trip him up.

**Pause and answer**

What trips the man up in Bildad’s picture?

## A man walks into a trap

## Job 18:8

His own feet take him into a net.

He walks straight into it.

## Job 18:9

A trap catches his heel.

It holds him fast.

## Job 18:10

A loop of rope lies hidden on the ground.

A trap waits on the path he takes.

## Job 18:11

At every step he sees something that frightens him.

Fear closes in from every side.

**Pause and answer**

How does the man feel at every step?

## The shelter of a tent is gone

## Job 18:12

His strength runs out.

Disaster stands ready beside him.

## Job 18:13

Patches of his skin waste away.

What I call death’s firstborn eats away at his arms and legs.

## Job 18:14

He loses the safety of his tent.

He must go to the king of terrors.

**Reading help**

Bildad speaks of death as a king people fear. He describes the sickness that brings death as its child.

## Job 18:15

Fire takes over his tent.

Burning sulfur falls on his home like rain.

**Pause and answer**

Where had the man felt safe?

## A family name disappears

## Job 18:16

He is like a tree with dried-up roots below.

Its branches above shrivel and die.

## Job 18:17

People on earth forget him.

No one in the land remembers his name.

## Job 18:18

He must leave the light and go into darkness.

He has no place left among the living.

## Job 18:19

He leaves no children or grandchildren among his people.

No one from his family survives where he lived.

## Job 18:20

People in the west are shocked at what happened to him.

People in the east tremble with fear.

## Job 18:21

That, Job, is what becomes of a wicked person’s home.

That is what awaits a person who does not know God.

**Pause and answer**

Who remembers the man’s name?
