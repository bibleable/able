# Job 40 — Job answers, and the LORD describes Behemoth

**About this chapter**

Job answers the LORD. Then the LORD asks Job about power and fairness. He tells Job to look at a strong animal called Behemoth.

## The LORD asks Job for an answer

## Job 40:1

The LORD spoke to Job again.

## Job 40:2

Job, you find fault with Almighty God.

Will you keep arguing with me?

If you challenge God, give me an answer.

## Job puts his hand over his mouth

## Job 40:3

Then Job answered the LORD.

## Job 40:4

I am so small.

What answer could I give you?

I put my hand over my mouth.

## Job 40:5

I spoke once, but I have no answer.

I spoke twice, but I have nothing to add.

**Pause and answer**

What does Job do with his hand when he has nothing more to say?

## The LORD speaks again

## Job 40:6

Then the LORD answered Job from the whirlwind.

## Job 40:7

Job, stand firm like a man.

I will ask the questions.

You will give me the answers.

## Job 40:8

Would you set aside my fair judgment?

Would you call me wrong to prove yourself right?

## Job 40:9

Is your arm as strong as mine?

Can your voice roar like thunder as mine does?

## Can Job bring the proud low?

## Job 40:10

Then dress yourself like a great king.

Wear honor and glory like royal clothes.

## Job 40:11

Let your fierce anger loose.

Look at each proud person and bring them low.

## Job 40:12

Look at each proud person and humble them.

Crush those who do evil right where they stand.

## Job 40:13

Bury them all together in the dust.

Shut them in the grave.

## Job 40:14

Then I will agree that your own right hand can save you.

**What does the right hand mean here?**

The LORD is speaking about Job’s own strength. He asks whether Job has enough power to save himself.

## The LORD points to Behemoth

## Job 40:15

Look at Behemoth.

I made him just as I made you.

He eats grass like an ox.

## Job 40:16

Look how strong his hips are.

See the powerful muscles in his belly.

## Job 40:17

His tail sways like a cedar tree.

Strong bands hold the muscles in his thighs together.

## Job 40:18

His bones are like metal tubes made of bronze.

His legs are like bars of iron.

**Bones like metal**

Bronze and iron are strong metals. The LORD compares the animal’s bones to metal to show their strength.

## Job 40:19

Behemoth stands first among the creatures God made.

Only his Maker can draw a sword against him.

## Behemoth rests by the water

## Job 40:20

The hills grow food for Behemoth.

Wild animals play nearby.

## Job 40:21

He lies under the lotus plants.

Tall reeds hide him in the wet ground.

## Job 40:22

Lotus plants shade him and keep him out of sight.

Willow trees grow around him by the stream.

![A tall reed bending on its stem.](../pictures/bending-reed.svg)

**Look at the reed**

Reeds grow together in wet ground. Imagine many stems hiding the resting animal.

## Job 40:23

Even a raging river cannot frighten Behemoth.

He stays calm when the Jordan River reaches his mouth.

## Job 40:24

Can anyone catch him while he watches?

Can anyone trap him by piercing his nose?

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, how does Behemoth act when the river rises?
