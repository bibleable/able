# Job 27 — Job promises to speak the truth

**About this chapter**

Job tells his friends why he refuses to admit to their accusations. Then he describes what he believes happens to a cruel person.

## Job makes a promise

## Job 27:1

Job continued speaking to his friends.

## Job 27:2

As surely as God lives, I make this promise.

God has denied me justice.

The Almighty has filled me with bitterness.

## Job 27:3

My promise stands as long as I breathe.

God himself gives me that breath.

## Job 27:4

I will speak no evil.

I will tell no lies.

## Job 27:5

My friends, I will never say your accusations are right.

I will hold on to my honesty until I die.

## Job 27:6

I still say I have done what is right.

I will hold to that claim.

As long as I live, my conscience will be clear.

**Reading help**

Your conscience is your sense of whether you have done right or wrong.

**Pause and answer**

What does Job promise to keep doing as long as he lives?

## Job asks what hope his enemy has

## Job 27:7

May my enemy share the fate of a wicked person.

May whoever stands against me share the fate of the unjust.

## Job 27:8

What hope does someone who rejects God have when God ends his life?

## Job 27:9

When trouble comes, will God hear that person cry for help?

## Job 27:10

Will that person find joy in the Almighty?

Will he keep calling out to God?

**Pause and answer**

Whom does Job say a person can call on in trouble?

## Job tells his friends to consider what they have seen

## Job 27:11

I will teach you about God’s power.

I will tell you openly what the Almighty does.

## Job 27:12

You have all seen these things for yourselves.

Why keep saying things that mean so little?

## Job 27:13

Here is what a wicked person receives from God.

This is the share the Almighty gives to cruel people.

## Job 27:14

Even if the man has many sons, the sword awaits them.

His children will go hungry.

## Job 27:15

Those who survive will die of disease and be buried.

Their widows will not weep for them.

**Pause and answer**

What does Job say the cruel man’s children will lack?

## Someone else will wear the clothes

## Job 27:16

He may heap up silver like piles of dust.

He may pile up clothes like heaps of clay.

## Job 27:17

But people who do right will wear those clothes.

Innocent people will share his silver.

## Job 27:18

The house he builds is like a moth’s cocoon.

It is like the little hut a guard puts up.

![A simple shelter with posts and a roof of branches.](../pictures/feast-shelter.svg)

Job compares the house to shelters that do not last long.

## Job 27:19

He lies down rich for the last time.

When he opens his eyes, everything is gone.

**Pause and answer**

Who will wear the clothes the man has stored?

## A storm in the night

## Job 27:20

Fear rushes over him like a flood.

A storm sweeps him away in the night.

## Job 27:21

The east wind carries him off.

It sweeps him away from his home.

## Job 27:22

The wind strikes him without mercy.

He runs as fast as he can to escape it.

## Job 27:23

It is as if the wind claps its hands at him.

It hisses at him and drives him from his home.

**Pause and answer**

What carries the man away from his home?
