# Job 13 — Job asks God to hear his case

**About this chapter**

Job asks his friends to listen. Then he speaks to God as someone bringing a case before a judge.

## Job wants God to hear him

## Job 13:1

I have seen all this myself.

I have heard it and understood it.

## Job 13:2

My friends, I know what you know.

I understand as much as you do.

## Job 13:3

I want to speak to the Almighty.

I want God to hear my case.

## Job 13:4

You tell lies about me.

You are like doctors who do nothing to help.

## Job 13:5

If only you would keep quiet!

That would be the wise thing to do.

**Pause and answer**

Whom does Job want to hear his case?

## Job challenges his friends

## Job 13:6

Now listen to my reasons.

Please hear what I have to say.

## Job 13:7

Will you say things that are wrong to speak for God?

Will you tell lies for him?

## Job 13:8

Will you take God’s side without judging fairly?

Will you try to argue his case?

## Job 13:9

What would happen if God examined you?

Could you fool him as you might fool another person?

## Job 13:10

If you secretly took sides, God would certainly correct you.

## Job 13:11

Wouldn’t God’s greatness make you tremble?

Wouldn’t you be overcome with fear?

## Job 13:12

Your wise sayings crumble like ashes.

Your arguments are as weak as walls of clay.

**Pause and answer**

What does Job say his friends’ arguments are like?

## Job is ready to speak

## Job 13:13

Be quiet and let me speak.

Whatever happens to me afterward, let it come.

## Job 13:14

Why would I put my life in danger like this?

## Job 13:15

Even if God kills me, I will still hope in him.

I will tell him face to face why I acted as I did.

## Job 13:16

Coming before God is my hope of rescue.

Someone who rejects God cannot stand before him.

## Job 13:17

Listen closely to what I say.

Take my words to heart.

## Job 13:18

I have my case ready.

I know I will be shown to be right.

## Job 13:19

Can anyone bring a charge against me?

If anyone can, I will fall silent and die.

**Pause and answer**

What does Job still have, even if God kills him?

## Job speaks to God

## Job 13:20

God, please do two things for me.

Then I will not have to hide from you.

## Job 13:21

Take your heavy hand off me.

Stop frightening me so much.

## Job 13:22

Then call me, and I will answer.

Or let me speak first, and you can answer me.

## Job 13:23

How many wrong things have I done?

Show me where I have sinned.

## Job 13:24

Why do you turn your face away from me?

Why do you treat me as your enemy?

**Pause and answer**

What does Job want God to show him?

## A leaf in the wind

## Job 13:25

Would you frighten a leaf blowing in the wind?

Would you chase the dry husks that blow away from grain?

![A loose leaf blows along in the wind.](../pictures/job-windblown-leaf.svg)

**Reading help**

Job feels as helpless as a dry leaf. He wonders why God would pursue someone so weak.

## Job 13:26

You write down harsh charges against me.

You make me answer for wrongs I did when I was young.

## Job 13:27

You lock my feet in wooden blocks called stocks.

You watch every path I take.

You limit every step I can take.

## Job 13:28

A person wastes away like something rotting.

It is like a piece of clothing that moths have eaten.

**Pause and answer**

What do the wooden blocks keep Job from doing?
