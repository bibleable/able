# Job 15 — Eliphaz challenges Job again

**About this chapter**

Eliphaz thinks Job has spoken wrongly about God. He tells Job what he believes happens to wicked people.

## Eliphaz accuses Job

## Job 15:1

Eliphaz the Temanite answered Job again.

## Job 15:2

Job, would a wise man give such empty advice?

It is as if you had filled yourself with hot wind from the east.

## Job 15:3

Would a wise man argue with words that help nobody?

What good are speeches like that?

## Job 15:4

Your words weaken people’s respect for God.

You make it harder for people to think quietly before him.

## Job 15:5

Your wrongdoing teaches you what to say.

You choose your words like someone trying to trick people.

## Job 15:6

Your own words declare you guilty.

I only have to listen.

You speak against yourself.

**Pause and answer**

Who says that Job’s words declare him guilty?

## Eliphaz questions Job’s wisdom

## Job 15:7

Were you the first person ever born?

Were you here before the hills?

## Job 15:8

Did you hear God speak in his council?

Are you the only one with wisdom?

## Job 15:9

What do you know that we have missed?

What can you understand that we cannot?

## Job 15:10

People with gray hair are on our side.

Some are much older than your father.

**Pause and answer**

Who does Eliphaz say agrees with him?

## Eliphaz questions Job’s words about God

## Job 15:11

Is the comfort God offers too little for you?

Are gentle words too little for you?

## Job 15:12

Why let your feelings sweep you away?

Why do your eyes flash with anger?

## Job 15:13

Why do you turn against God?

Why do you say such things?

## Job 15:14

How can any human being be pure?

How can anyone born to a woman be entirely right before God?

## Job 15:15

God does not put his trust even in his holy ones.

Even the heavens are not pure in his eyes.

## Job 15:16

How much less can he trust people who do wrong?

They do harm as readily as they drink water.

**Pause and answer**

What does Eliphaz compare doing wrong to?

## What Eliphaz says about wicked people

## Job 15:17

Listen to me, Job.

I will tell you what I have seen.

## Job 15:18

Wise people have spoken about these things.

Their ancestors knew them too.

## Job 15:19

Those ancestors had the land to themselves.

No foreigner passed among them then.

## Job 15:20

A wicked man twists in pain all his days.

A cruel man has only a few years to live.

## Job 15:21

He hears sounds that frighten him.

Even when all goes well, someone comes to destroy him.

## Job 15:22

He sees no way back from the darkness.

He expects to die by the sword.

## Job 15:23

He wanders about like a meal waiting for vultures.

He knows his dark day is near.

## Job 15:24

Fear and pain come over him.

They overpower him like a king ready to attack.

**Pause and answer**

What does Eliphaz say the wicked man hears?

## A man who stands against God

## Job 15:25

He raised his hand against God.

He boasted that he could stand against the Almighty.

## Job 15:26

He rushed straight at God with a thick shield covered in studs.

## Job 15:27

His face is fat and his belly is round.

## Job 15:28

Yet he will live in ruined cities.

He will move into empty houses that will fall to pieces.

## Job 15:29

His riches will not last.

The things he owns will no longer spread across the land.

## A vine that loses its fruit

## Job 15:30

He will never escape the darkness.

He is like a plant whose shoots shrivel in a fire.

God will blow him away with his breath.

## Job 15:31

He fools himself if he trusts in empty things.

All he will get in return is emptiness.

## Job 15:32

He will receive that payment before his time is up.

He is like a branch that never grows green.

## Job 15:33

He is like a vine that loses its grapes before they ripen.

He is like an olive tree whose flowers fall off.

**Reading help**

Eliphaz asks Job to picture a vine losing its grapes too soon. He thinks a wicked person’s plans will fail that way.

**Pause and answer**

What happens to the grapes before they ripen?

## Job 15:34

Nothing will grow from the work of those who reject God.

Fire will burn the tents of people who take bribes.

## Job 15:35

They make plans that bring trouble.

Those plans give birth to evil.

Deep inside, they are full of lies.
