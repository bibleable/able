# Job 24 — Job asks when God will judge

**About this chapter**

Job asks why people who trust God have to wait for justice. He describes families who lose their land, their animals, and even their children.

## People steal land and animals

## Job 24:1

Why does the Almighty set no time for judgment?

Why do those who know God never see the day he judges?

## Job 24:2

Some people move the stones that mark the edges of fields.

They steal flocks and let them graze.

![A boundary stone marks where one field ends and the next begins.](../pictures/field-boundary.svg)

**Reading help**

Moving a boundary stone lets someone claim part of a neighbor’s field.

## Job 24:3

They drive away a donkey that belongs to children without a father.

They take a widow’s ox until she repays a debt.

## Job 24:4

They push people in need off the road.

Poor people throughout the land have to hide.

**Pause and answer**

Whose ox do people take to collect a debt?

## Poor families search for food

## Job 24:5

Like wild donkeys, the poor search the desert for food.

They must feed their children with what they find there.

## Job 24:6

They gather animal feed in the fields.

They pick the grapes left behind in vineyards that belong to wicked people.

## Job 24:7

They spend the night without clothes.

They have nothing to keep them warm.

## Job 24:8

Rain in the mountains soaks them.

With no shelter, they huddle against the rocks.

**Pause and answer**

Where do poor families shelter from the rain?

## Workers carry grain but go hungry

## Job 24:9

People take a fatherless baby from its mother’s breast.

They seize a poor mother’s nursing child to collect a debt.

## Job 24:10

The poor walk about without clothes.

They carry bundles of grain while they themselves go hungry.

## Job 24:11

Inside the walls they crush olives for oil.

They press grapes with their feet to make wine.

Still they go thirsty.

## Job 24:12

In the city people groan.

Wounded people cry for help.

Yet God charges no one with doing wrong.

**Pause and answer**

What do the hungry workers carry?

## People who hide in the dark

## Job 24:13

Some people turn against the light.

They do not know its ways or follow its paths.

## Job 24:14

When daylight fades, a murderer goes out.

He kills people who are poor and need help.

At night he acts like a thief.

## Job 24:15

A man who wants to commit adultery waits for dusk.

He means to sleep with someone else’s wife.

He covers his face and thinks, “No one will see me.”

## Job 24:16

At night thieves break into homes.

By day they shut themselves inside and avoid the light.

## Job 24:17

Deep darkness is like morning to them.

The things that frighten others at night are familiar friends to them.

**Pause and answer**

Why does the man in verse 15 wait for dusk?

## Job considers what happens to wrongdoers

## Job 24:18

These people are like foam floating on water.

Their share of the land lies under a curse.

No one goes to their vineyards.

## Job 24:19

Heat and drought dry up water from melting snow.

In the same way, Sheol takes away those who sin.

Sheol is the place of the dead.

## Job 24:20

Even their mothers forget them.

Worms feed on them, and no one remembers them.

Wrongdoing breaks like a tree snapping in two.

## Job 24:21

They take advantage of women who have no children.

They show no kindness to widows.

## God sees even the powerful

## Job 24:22

Yet God can drag even powerful people away.

They may rise to power, but their lives are never certain.

## Job 24:23

God lets them feel safe.

All the time, he sees what they do.

## Job 24:24

They rise high for a moment, then they are gone.

Like everyone else, they fall and are gathered up.

They are like heads of grain cut at harvest.

**Pause and answer**

Who sees what powerful people do even when they feel safe?

## Job 24:25

If this is untrue, who can show that I have lied?

Who can prove my words are worthless?
