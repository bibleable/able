# Job 25 — Bildad asks who can be pure before God

**About this chapter**

Bildad answers Job. He looks at the heavens and says how small people seem to him beside God.

## God rules in heaven

## Job 25:1

Bildad the Shuhite answered Job.

## Job 25:2

God rules over all.

His greatness fills us with awe.

He keeps peace in the heights of heaven.

## Job 25:3

Who can count all the troops God commands?

Is there anyone his light does not reach?

**Pause and answer**

Where does Bildad say God keeps peace?

## Bildad points to the stars

## Job 25:4

How can anyone stand before God and be found innocent?

How can anyone born to a woman be pure?

## Job 25:5

Even the moon seems dim before God.

Even the stars seem less than pure to him.

![Stars shine in the night sky. Bildad asks how they look to God.](../pictures/job-night-without-dawn.svg)

## Job 25:6

Then how can a human being be pure?

To me, a person seems as small as a maggot or a worm beside God.

**Reading help**

A maggot is a young fly that looks like a tiny worm. Bildad uses these small creatures to describe how lowly he thinks people are.
