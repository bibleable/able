# Job 32 — Elihu speaks after the others fall silent

**About this chapter**

Job’s three friends have finished speaking. A younger man named Elihu has been listening. Now Elihu wants the others to hear him.

## Why Elihu is angry

## Job 32:1

Job believed he had done right.

So his three friends stopped answering him.

## Job 32:2

Elihu grew angry with Job.

Elihu was the son of Barachel the Buzite.

He belonged to the family of Ram.

Job had defended himself.

Elihu thought Job should have defended God.

## Job 32:3

Elihu was angry with the three friends too.

They had said Job was guilty.

Yet none could show where Job was wrong.

## Job 32:4

Elihu had waited to speak to Job.

The other men were older than Elihu.

## Job 32:5

Now the three friends had nothing more to say.

When Elihu saw this, his anger grew.

## Why Elihu waited

## Job 32:6

Then Elihu, son of Barachel the Buzite, spoke.

“I am young, and you are old.

So I was afraid to tell you what I know.

## Job 32:7

I thought older people should speak first.

I thought long years of life should teach wisdom.

## Job 32:8

But people have a spirit within them.

The breath of Almighty God helps people understand.

## Job 32:9

Young people can be wise too.

They too can understand what is fair.

## Job 32:10

So please listen to me.

I will tell you what I know.

**A word to know**

Almighty means that God has all power.

## Elihu listened to each friend

## Job 32:11

I waited while you spoke.

I listened to the reasons you gave.

Sometimes you had to search for words.

## Job 32:12

I listened closely to every one of you.

Yet none of you showed where Job was wrong.

None of you could answer what Job said.

## Job 32:13

You must not say, ‘We have found wisdom.

Only God can show Job where he is wrong.’

## Job 32:14

Job has not spoken against me.

I will give him a different answer from yours.

## Why Elihu wants to speak

## Job 32:15

Job’s friends are at a loss.

They cannot find another word to say.

## Job 32:16

Must I still wait?

They stand there in silence.

They have stopped answering Job.

## Job 32:17

Now I too will answer.

I will tell you what I know.

## Job 32:18

I have so much to say.

Deep inside, I feel I must speak.

## Job 32:19

Inside I feel like wine in a closed bag.

I feel like a new wineskin about to burst.

![A leather bag bulges below its tightly tied neck.](../pictures/job-wineskin.svg)

**What does the picture mean?**

A wineskin is a bag made from animal skin. People kept wine in it. As new wine forms, gas builds up inside the closed bag. Elihu feels pressure like that as he holds his words inside.

## Job 32:20

Speaking will bring me relief.

I must open my mouth and answer.

## Elihu promises to speak fairly

## Job 32:21

I will treat each person fairly.

I will never praise someone just to please them.

## Job 32:22

I do not know how to give false praise.

If I did, God who made me would quickly take me away.”
