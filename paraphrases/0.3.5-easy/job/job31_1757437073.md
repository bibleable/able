# Job 31 — Job asks God to hear his case

**About this chapter**

Job asks God to hear his side. He talks about the people in his house, the strangers at his door, and the people who worked his fields.

## Job speaks about his promises

## Job 31:1

I made a promise about how I would use my eyes. I would never stare at a young woman because I wanted sex with her.

## Job 31:2

What could I expect from God above if I broke that promise?

## Job 31:3

Surely trouble comes to those who do wrong.

## Job 31:4

God sees where I go. He counts every step I take.

## Job 31:5

Have I told lies? Have I rushed to cheat someone?

## Job 31:6

Let God weigh me on fair scales. Then he will see that I am honest.

## Job 31:7

Suppose I left the right path. Suppose I grabbed something because I saw it and wanted it. Suppose my hands did wrong.

## Job 31:8

Then let someone else eat the food I planted. Let my crops be pulled up by the roots.

## Job speaks about his neighbor’s wife

## Job 31:9

Suppose I wanted my neighbor’s wife. Suppose I waited in secret at his door.

## Job 31:10

Then let my wife grind grain for someone else. Let other men sleep with her.

## Job 31:11

Taking my neighbor’s wife would be a terrible crime. A judge should hear that case.

## Job 31:12

Such a wrong is like a fire. It burns all the way down to the place of destruction. It could destroy my whole harvest.

## Job remembers the people who needed his help

## Job 31:13

Suppose a servant came to me with a complaint. What if I refused to listen to that man or woman?

## Job 31:14

What would I do when God stood up to judge me? What answer could I give him?

## Job 31:15

God made me inside my mother. The same God made each of my servants inside their mothers.

## Job 31:16

Have I refused poor people what they needed? Have I left a widow looking for help until she lost hope?

## Job 31:17

Have I kept my food to myself when a child without a father needed some?

## Job 31:18

Since I was young, I have cared for such children like a father. All my life I have helped widows find their way.

## Job 31:19

Have I seen someone dying for lack of clothes? Have I seen a poor person shivering without a coat?

## Job 31:20

Didn’t that person thank me from the heart when the wool from my sheep kept them warm?

## Job 31:21

Have I raised my hand against a child without a father because I knew the men at the city gate would take my side?

## Job 31:22

If I did, let that arm fall from my shoulder. Let it be torn from its joint.

## Job 31:23

I was afraid of the harm God could bring on me. How could I stand against a God so great?

**A note about the city gate**

People brought disputes to the city gate for a decision. Job says he could have had people there on his side.

**Pause and answer**

Why does Job say he should listen to a servant’s complaint?

## Job asks what he has trusted

## Job 31:24

Have I trusted my gold? Have I ever said to a lump of pure gold, “You will keep me safe”?

## Job 31:25

Have I taken pride in my riches because I earned so much?

## Job 31:26

I have seen the sun shining bright. I have watched the beautiful moon cross the sky.

## Job 31:27

But did I secretly want to worship them? Did I kiss my hand and blow them a kiss?

## Job 31:28

That too would be a crime for a judge to hear. I would have turned away from God above.

## Job speaks about enemies and strangers

## Job 31:29

Was I ever glad to see an enemy ruined? Did I cheer when trouble came to him?

## Job 31:30

I never used my mouth to do that wrong. I never called down a curse to ask that he might die.

## Job 31:31

Ask the people in my house. They say, “Who ever left our table hungry?”

## Job 31:32

No stranger had to sleep in the street. I opened my door to travelers.

## Job 31:33

Did I hide my wrongs as Adam did? Did I try to keep my guilt a secret?

## Job 31:34

Was I so afraid of the crowd and of what the families around me would say that I kept quiet and stayed indoors?

## Job signs his name

## Job 31:35

If only someone would hear my case! Here, I sign my name to it. Let the Almighty answer me. Let the one who accuses me write down the charges.

## Job 31:36

I would carry that paper on my shoulder for everyone to see. I would wear it on my head like a crown.

## Job 31:37

I would tell God about every step I took. I would come before him as boldly as a prince.

## Job speaks about his fields

## Job 31:38

Imagine my land crying out against me. Imagine the rows I plowed filling with tears.

## Job 31:39

Have I eaten what grew there without paying for it? Have I crushed the spirits of the people who farmed it?

## Job 31:40

If I have, let thorn bushes grow where I planted wheat. Let foul-smelling weeds grow where I planted barley.

Job finished speaking.
