# Job 37 — Elihu asks Job to stand still and listen

**About this chapter**

Elihu hears thunder and thinks about God’s voice. He asks Job to stop and think about the sky.

## Elihu listens to the thunder

## Job 37:1

My heart pounds at the sound.

It feels as if my heart will jump.

## Job 37:2

Listen closely to the thunder.

Hear the deep rumble of God’s voice.

## Job 37:3

God sends lightning across the whole sky.

It flashes to the ends of the earth.

## Job 37:4

Then comes a roar.

God’s great voice thunders.

As God speaks, the lightning keeps flashing.

## Job 37:5

How wonderful is the sound of God’s voice!

God does great things we cannot understand.

## Snow and rain stop the day’s work

## Job 37:6

God tells the snow to fall on the ground.

God tells the gentle rain to pour down hard.

## Job 37:7

God brings everyone’s hands to a stop.

Then everyone can see what God has done.

**What happens to people’s hands?**

Think of workers putting down their tools in a storm. Elihu says the weather makes people stop their work.

## Job 37:8

The wild animals go into their shelters.

They settle down in their dens.

## Job 37:9

The storm comes out from its room.

Strong winds from the north bring the cold.

## Job 37:10

God breathes, and ice forms.

Wide stretches of water freeze.

## God directs the clouds

## Job 37:11

God fills the clouds with water.

God sends lightning flashing through them.

## Job 37:12

The clouds turn and swirl wherever God directs them.

Across the earth, they do everything God commands.

## Job 37:13

God may send clouds to punish people.

God may send them to water the land.

God does this with love that lasts.

## Elihu asks Job about the sky

## Job 37:14

Job, listen to this.

Stand still.

Think about the wonderful things God does.

## Job 37:15

Do you know how God sends the clouds?

How does God make lightning flash?

## Job 37:16

How do the clouds stay up there?

These wonders are the work of God, who knows everything.

## Job 37:17

The south wind blows.

The land grows quiet, and your clothes feel hot.

## Job 37:18

Can you spread out the sky as God does?

It looks as strong as a bronze mirror.

**What is a bronze mirror?**

Bronze is a strong metal. People polished it until they could see their faces in it. The bright sky reminds Elihu of a great sheet of polished metal.

## What can people say to God?

## Job 37:19

Tell us what to say to God.

We are in the dark.

How can we put our case into words?

## Job 37:20

Should anyone tell God I want to speak?

Would anyone ask to be swallowed up?

**What does Elihu mean?**

Being in the dark means Elihu cannot understand enough to argue his case. He speaks of being swallowed up to show the danger he feels.

## Elihu speaks of God’s greatness

## Job 37:21

The wind sweeps the sky clear.

Then the sun shines too brightly to look at.

## Job 37:22

God comes from the north in golden light.

The sight of God’s greatness fills us with awe.

## Job 37:23

Almighty God is beyond our reach.

How great is God’s power!

God does what is right and fair.

God never uses that power to crush people unjustly.

## Job 37:24

That is why people stand in fear of God.

God gives wise people no special treatment.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what does Elihu ask Job to think about when he stands still?
