# Job 42 — Job prays for his friends

**About this chapter**

Job answers the LORD. Then the LORD speaks to Job’s friends. Job prays for them, and his family comes to eat with him.

## Job answers the LORD

## Job 42:1

Then Job answered the LORD.

## Job 42:2

I know you can do anything.

No one can stop what you plan to do.

## Job 42:3

You asked, ‘Who speaks about my plans without understanding them?’

I did that.

I spoke about things too wonderful for me to understand.

## Job 42:4

You told me, ‘Listen while I speak.

I will ask questions, and you will answer me.’

## Job 42:5

Before, I heard about you with my ears.

Now I have seen you with my own eyes.

## Job 42:6

So I am ashamed of myself.

I sit in dust and ashes and repent.

**A word to know**

To repent means to turn from what you have done wrong. Sitting in dust and ashes showed how deeply sorry Job felt.

## The LORD speaks to Eliphaz

## Job 42:7

After speaking to Job, the LORD spoke to Eliphaz the Temanite.

“I am angry with you and your two friends.

My servant Job spoke the truth about me.

You did not.

## Job 42:8

Take seven bulls and seven rams to my servant Job.

Offer them to me as a burnt offering for yourselves.

Job will pray for you.

I will accept his prayer.

I will spare you the punishment your foolish words deserve.

My servant Job spoke rightly about me.

You did not.”

**What is a burnt offering?**

A ram is a male sheep. In a burnt offering, people gave an animal to God and burned it on an altar.

## Job 42:9

Eliphaz the Temanite went with Bildad the Shuhite and Zophar the Naamathite.

The three friends did what the LORD told them.

The LORD accepted Job’s prayer.

**Pause and answer**

Who prayed for the three friends?

## Job’s family and friends visit

## Job 42:10

After Job prayed for his friends, the LORD made him wealthy again.

The LORD gave Job twice what he had owned before.

## Job 42:11

Job’s brothers, sisters, and old friends came to his house.

They shared a meal with him.

They comforted Job after all the trouble the LORD had brought him.

Each guest gave Job a piece of silver and a gold ring.

## Job’s later years

## Job 42:12

The LORD blessed Job even more in his later years.

Job had 14,000 sheep and 6,000 camels.

He had 1,000 pairs of oxen and 1,000 female donkeys.

![A sheep pen with an open gate.](../pictures/sheep-pen.svg)

**Look at the sheep pen**

The pen is a place to keep sheep together. Sheep were just one kind of animal in Job’s large herds.

## Job 42:13

Job also had seven sons and three daughters.

## Job 42:14

Job named his first daughter Jemimah.

He named his second daughter Keziah.

He named his third daughter Keren-happuch.

## Job 42:15

Job’s daughters were the most beautiful women in the land.

Job gave his daughters a share of the family property along with their brothers.

## Job 42:16

After this Job lived another 140 years.

He saw four generations of his family grow up.

## Job 42:17

Job died an old man after a long, full life.
