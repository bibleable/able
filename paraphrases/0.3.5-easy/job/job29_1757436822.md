# Job 29 — Job remembers the days when people listened

**About this chapter**

Job remembers his children around him and his place at the city gate. He tells how he helped people who had no one to speak for them.

## Job misses God’s friendship

## Job 29:1

Job continued speaking.

## Job 29:2

How I miss those months long ago!

In those days God watched over me.

## Job 29:3

His lamp shone above my head.

By his light I could walk through the dark.

![A small oil lamp gives light in the dark.](../pictures/davids-lamp.svg)

Job remembers how God guided him, as a lamp lights a path.

## Job 29:4

I was in the best years of my life.

God was a friend to me in my home.

## Job 29:5

The Almighty was still with me.

My children gathered around me.

## Job 29:6

I had so much cream, I could have bathed my feet in it.

Even the rocks seemed to pour out streams of oil for me.

**Pause and answer**

Who gathered around Job at home?

## A seat by the city gate

## Job 29:7

I used to go to the city gate.

I took my seat in the open square.

## Job 29:8

Young men saw me and stepped aside.

Old men stood up.

## Job 29:9

Princes stopped speaking.

They covered their mouths with their hands.

## Job 29:10

The nobles fell silent too.

It was as if their tongues stuck to the roofs of their mouths.

**Pause and answer**

What did the old men do when Job arrived?

## Job remembers the people he helped

## Job 29:11

People who heard me called me blessed.

People who saw what I did spoke well of me.

## Job 29:12

I rescued poor people who called for help.

I helped children who had no father and no one to help them.

## Job 29:13

A dying man spoke a blessing over me.

I gave a widow reason to sing for joy.

## Job 29:14

Doing right was like getting dressed each day.

Justice was the robe I wore and the turban on my head.

## Job 29:15

I was eyes for people who could not see.

I was feet for people who could not walk.

## Job 29:16

I cared for people in need as a father would.

I spoke up for strangers when they needed a fair hearing.

## Job 29:17

I broke the fangs of those who did wrong.

I pulled the people they hurt free from their teeth.

**Reading help**

Job pictures a wild animal holding its prey. He means he rescued people from those who hurt them.

**Pause and answer**

Who spoke up for strangers when they needed a fair hearing?

## The future Job once expected

## Job 29:18

I thought I would live a long life and die in my own home.

It felt as safe as a nest.

My days would be as many as grains of sand.

## Job 29:19

I pictured myself as a tree with roots reaching water.

Dew would settle on my branches each night.

## Job 29:20

I thought people would always honor me.

The bow in my hand would be strong again.

**Pause and answer**

Where did Job expect to spend his last days?

## People waited for Job’s advice

## Job 29:21

People listened, eager to hear what I would say.

They waited quietly for my advice.

## Job 29:22

After I spoke, they had nothing to add.

My words settled on them like dew.

## Job 29:23

They waited for my words as people wait for rain.

They drank them in like showers in spring.

## Job 29:24

When I smiled at them, they could hardly believe it.

Seeing my face light up meant a great deal to them.

## Job 29:25

I chose the way we would go.

I sat among them as their leader, like a king among his troops.

I comforted those who grieved.

**Pause and answer**

What did people wait quietly to hear?
