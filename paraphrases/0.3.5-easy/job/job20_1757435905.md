# Job 20 — Zophar says stolen wealth will not last

**About this chapter**

Zophar speaks to Job again. He describes a man who grows rich by hurting others. Zophar tells Job what he believes will happen to that man.

## Zophar feels insulted

## Job 20:1

Then Zophar the Naamathite answered Job.

## Job 20:2

My thoughts trouble me.

I am so upset that I must answer you.

## Job 20:3

I heard your sharp words and felt insulted.

Let me tell you how I understand things.

**Pause and answer**

Why does Zophar feel he must answer Job?

## A short time of success

## Job 20:4

Job, surely you know how it has always been.

Think back to when people first lived on earth.

## Job 20:5

A wicked person’s victory lasts only a little while.

Someone who rejects God enjoys only a moment of joy.

## Job 20:6

Imagine a man so proud he seems to reach the sky.

His head seems to touch the clouds.

## Job 20:7

Even so, he will disappear forever like his own dung.

People who once saw him will ask, “Where is he?”

## Job 20:8

He will vanish like a dream.

Try to find him, and he will be gone like a vision in the night.

## Job 20:9

Those who knew him will never see him again.

Even the place where he lived will see him no more.

**Pause and answer**

What does Zophar compare the disappearing man to?

## Wealth that must be given back

## Job 20:10

His sons will ask poor people for their goodwill.

The man himself must give back his wealth.

## Job 20:11

His body may be strong and young.

Yet all that strength will lie with him in the dust.

## Job 20:12

Doing evil tastes sweet to him.

He hides it under his tongue like a sweet bite of food.

## Job 20:13

He wants to keep tasting it.

He cannot bear to let it go.

## Job 20:14

But in his stomach the food turns sour.

What tasted sweet becomes like the poison of a cobra.

## Job 20:15

He gulps down wealth, then vomits it up.

God makes him give it all back.

**Pause and answer**

What does Zophar say God makes the man give back?

## Job 20:16

It is as if he drinks poison from cobras.

A poisonous snake bites him, and he dies.

## The man has hurt poor people

## Job 20:17

He will never enjoy rivers flowing with honey and cream.

**Reading help**

The rivers picture having more than enough good food. Zophar says the man will miss out on that plenty.

## Job 20:18

He must give back what he worked for before he can enjoy it.

He cannot enjoy the money he made buying and selling.

## Job 20:19

He treated poor people cruelly and left them without help.

He took houses that other people had built.

![A tent and a house. Zophar says the man took homes that others had built.](../pictures/tent-and-house.svg)

## Job 20:20

However much he gets, he always wants more.

He cannot get away with his treasure.

## Job 20:21

He takes until there is nothing left.

His wealth will not last.

## Job 20:22

Even when he has plenty, he will suffer.

Misery will strike him with all its force.

**Pause and answer**

Whose houses did the man take?

## Zophar describes the man’s end

## Job 20:23

As he fills his stomach, God will turn against him in anger.

God’s anger will pour down like rain as he eats.

## Job 20:24

He runs from an iron weapon.

But an arrow with a bronze tip strikes him.

## Job 20:25

He pulls the arrow from his back.

Its shining tip comes out of his liver.

Terror fills him.

## Job 20:26

Darkness waits for his treasures.

A fire no one needs to fan will burn him up.

It will burn whatever is left in his tent.

## Job 20:27

The heavens will show what he has done wrong.

The earth itself will stand against him.

## Job 20:28

On the day God shows his anger, the man will lose everything in his house.

His belongings will be swept away like water.

## Job 20:29

This is the share God sets aside for a wicked person.

This is what God has decided to give him.

**Pause and answer**

Who is telling Job what he thinks will happen to a wicked person?
