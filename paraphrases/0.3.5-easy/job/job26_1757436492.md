# Job 26 — Job speaks of a whisper and thunder

**About this chapter**

Job answers his friend Bildad. Job describes the sky, the sea, and how little anyone knows of all that God can do.

## Job questions Bildad’s advice

## Job 26:1

Then Job answered Bildad.

## Job 26:2

What help you have given people without power!

How you have rescued those whose arms are weak!

## Job 26:3

What advice you have given those who lack wisdom!

What deep understanding you have shared!

**Reading help**

Job is speaking sharply. His words sound like praise, but he feels Bildad’s advice has given him no help.

## Job 26:4

Who did you think you were speaking to?

Whose spirit spoke through you?

**Pause and answer**

How does Job feel about the help Bildad has given?

## God sees even the place of the dead

## Job 26:5

Even the dead beneath the waters tremble.

Those who live in the waters tremble too.

## Job 26:6

God sees everything in Sheol, the place of the dead.

Even Abaddon, the place of destruction, lies open before him.

## Job 26:7

God stretches the northern sky over empty space.

He hangs the earth on nothing.

## God rules the clouds and the sea

## Job 26:8

God gathers water inside his clouds.

The clouds hold its weight without bursting.

## Job 26:9

God spreads a cloud over the full moon.

It hides the moon’s face.

![A cloud crosses the full moon and hides part of it from view.](../pictures/job-cloud-over-moon.svg)

## Job 26:10

God draws a line across the waters.

This is the horizon, where light meets darkness.

**Pause and answer**

What does God spread over the moon?

## Job 26:11

When God speaks sharply, even the foundations of heaven shake.

It is as if his words have startled them.

## How much of God’s power do we hear?

## Job 26:12

God’s power stirred up the sea.

With his understanding he shattered Rahab, the great sea monster.

## Job 26:13

God cleared the skies with his breath.

His hand pierced the serpent as it fled.

## Job 26:14

These are only glimpses of what God does.

What we hear of him is barely a whisper.

Who could understand the thunder of his power?

**Pause and answer**

What sound does Job compare the little we hear of God to?
