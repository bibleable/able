# Job 5 — Eliphaz tells Job to ask God for help

**About this chapter**

Eliphaz is still speaking to his friend Job. He describes what he believes God will do for Job.

## Eliphaz describes a foolish man

## Job 5:1

Job, call out if you wish.

Who will answer you?

Which of the holy ones in heaven will you ask for help?

## Job 5:2

Bitter anger can kill a foolish person.

Wanting what others have can destroy someone who lacks sense.

## Job 5:3

I saw a foolish man settle down like a tree taking root.

He seemed secure.

Then suddenly a curse fell on his home.

## Job 5:4

His sons are far from safe.

They lose their case in court.

No one speaks up for them.

## Job 5:5

Hungry people eat his crops.

They take even the grain among the thorns.

Others long for his wealth as thirsty people long for water.

**Pause and answer**

Who speaks up for the man’s sons in court?

## Sparks rise and people face trouble

## Job 5:6

Distress does not rise out of the dust.

Trouble does not grow from the soil like a plant.

## Job 5:7

Yet people are born to face trouble.

That is as sure as sparks rising from a fire.

## Eliphaz tells Job where he would turn

## Job 5:8

Job, if I were you, I would ask God for help.

I would tell him my case.

## Job 5:9

God does great things beyond our understanding.

No one can count all his wonders.

## Job 5:10

God sends rain on the earth.

He waters the fields.

**Pause and answer**

Whom does Eliphaz tell Job to ask for help?

## Job 5:11

God raises up people others look down on.

He brings people who grieve to a place of safety.

## God helps people in need

## Job 5:12

God stops the plans of people who trick others.

Their plans fail.

## Job 5:13

God catches clever people in their own tricks.

He sweeps their plans away.

## Job 5:14

Even in daylight, it is dark for them.

At noon they feel for their way as if it were night.

## Job 5:15

God saves people in need from words that cut like a sword.

He rescues them from people who use power against them.

## Job 5:16

So poor people have hope.

Those who speak unjustly fall silent.

**Pause and answer**

What gives poor people hope, according to Eliphaz?

## Eliphaz speaks about correction

## Job 5:17

God blesses the person he corrects.

Job, when the Almighty corrects you, take it seriously.

The Almighty is a name for God, who has all power.

## Job 5:18

God wounds, and he also wraps the wound.

He strikes, and his hands also heal.

## Job 5:19

Six times when disaster comes, God will rescue you.

Even the seventh time, you will come through unharmed.

## Job 5:20

When food runs out, God will save you from death.

In battle, he will save you from the sword.

## Job 5:21

God will shelter you from cruel words that hurt like a whip.

When disaster comes, you will not be afraid.

## Job 5:22

You will laugh in the face of ruin and hunger.

You will have no reason to fear wild animals.

**Pause and answer**

What does Eliphaz say God does for a wound?

## What Eliphaz expects for Job

## Job 5:23

Even the stones in your field will seem to have made peace with you.

Wild animals will live in peace with you too.

## Job 5:24

You will know your tent is safe.

When you check your home, everything will be in place.

## Job 5:25

You will have many children and grandchildren.

Your family will be as numerous as blades of grass.

## Job 5:26

You will live a full life and keep your strength until death.

You will be like ripe grain gathered at harvest time.

## Job 5:27

We have looked into this, Job.

We know it is true.

Listen and see for yourself.
