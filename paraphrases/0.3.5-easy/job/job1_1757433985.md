# Job 1 — Four messengers bring Job terrible news

**About this chapter**

Job cares for his family and honors God. Then four messengers come, one after another. Each brings news of a loss.

## Job and his family

## Job 1:1

Job lived in the land of Uz.

He was honest and did what was right.

Job respected God deeply and turned away from evil.

## Job 1:2

Job had seven sons and three daughters.

## Job 1:3

Job owned 7,000 sheep and 3,000 camels.

He had 500 pairs of oxen and 500 female donkeys.

Each pair of oxen worked together.

Job also had many servants.

No one in the East was greater than Job.

![Two oxen joined by a wooden bar. The bar is called a yoke. It keeps the oxen together as they pull.](../pictures/job-yoke.svg)

## Job 1:4

Job’s sons took turns giving feasts in their homes.

They invited their three sisters to eat and drink with them.

## Job 1:5

After each round of feasts, Job sent for his children.

He wanted to make them clean before God.

Early in the morning he burned an offering for each child.

Job thought, “Perhaps my children sinned.

Perhaps they cursed God in their thoughts.”

Job did this regularly.

**Pause and answer**

Whom did the brothers invite to their feasts?

## The LORD speaks with Satan

## Job 1:6

One day the sons of God came before the LORD.

These were beings from heaven.

Satan came with them.

## Job 1:7

The LORD asked Satan, “Where have you come from?”

Satan answered, “I have been walking back and forth across the earth.”

## Job 1:8

The LORD asked, “Have you noticed my servant Job?

No one on earth is like him.

He is honest and does what is right.

He respects God deeply and turns away from evil.”

## Satan questions why Job serves God

## Job 1:9

Satan answered the LORD, “Does Job respect you for nothing?

## Job 1:10

“You protect Job, his family, and everything he owns.

It is as if you put a hedge around them.

You have blessed Job’s work.

He owns more and more throughout the land.

## Job 1:11

“But strike everything he owns.

Then Job will curse you to your face.”

## Job 1:12

The LORD told Satan, “Very well.

You have power over everything Job owns.

But you must not hurt Job himself.”

Then Satan left the LORD’s presence.

**Pause and answer**

What did the LORD tell Satan he must not do?

## News about the animals and servants

## Job 1:13

One day Job’s sons and daughters gathered at their oldest brother’s house.

They were eating and drinking wine together.

## Job 1:14

A messenger came to Job.

He said, “The oxen were plowing the ground.

The donkeys were eating grass nearby.

## Job 1:15

“Suddenly the Sabeans attacked and took the animals away.

They killed the servants with swords.

I alone escaped to tell you!”

## Job 1:16

The first messenger was still speaking when another arrived.

The second said, “Fire from God fell from the sky.

It burned up the sheep and the servants.

I alone escaped to tell you!”

## Job 1:17

The second messenger was still speaking when another arrived.

The third said, “Three groups of Chaldeans took the camels.

They killed the servants with swords.

I alone escaped to tell you!”

**Pause and answer**

What animals did the third messenger say the Chaldeans took?

## News about Job’s children

## Job 1:18

The third messenger was still speaking when another arrived.

The fourth said, “Your sons and daughters were at their oldest brother’s house.

They were eating and drinking wine.

## Job 1:19

“Suddenly a strong wind came from the desert.

It struck all four corners of the house.

The house fell on your children.

They are dead.

I alone escaped to tell you!”

**Pause and answer**

Where were Job’s children when the wind came?

## Job grieves and worships

## Job 1:20

Job stood up and tore his robe.

He shaved his head to show his grief.

Then Job bowed to the ground and worshiped God.

## Job 1:21

Job said, “I was naked when my mother gave birth to me.

When I die, I will take nothing with me.

The LORD gave, and the LORD has taken away.

I praise the name of the LORD.”

## Job 1:22

Through all this, Job did not sin.

He did not accuse God of doing wrong.

**Pause and answer**

What did Job do when he bowed to the ground?
