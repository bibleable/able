# Job 38 — The LORD answers Job from the whirlwind

**About this chapter**

The LORD speaks directly to Job. His questions take Job from the bottom of the sea to the stars. Then he asks Job to think about hungry animals.

## The LORD speaks to Job

## Job 38:1

Then the LORD answered Job from a whirlwind.

The LORD said these words.

**A word to know**

A whirlwind is a powerful wind that spins.

## Job 38:2

Who is making my plans hard to understand?

You speak about things you do not know.

## Job 38:3

Job, stand firm like a man.

I will ask the questions.

You will give me the answers.

## Who built the earth?

## Job 38:4

Where were you when I laid the earth’s foundations?

Tell me, if you understand.

## Job 38:5

Who decided how big the earth should be?

Surely you know!

Who stretched a measuring line across it?

## Job 38:6

What holds up its foundations?

Who put its cornerstone in place?

## Job 38:7

The morning stars sang together then.

All the sons of God shouted for joy.

![A stone at the corner of a building.](../pictures/cornerstone.svg)

**Look at the corner stone**

A builder lays a cornerstone where two walls meet. The LORD describes making the earth as if he were building a house. The sons of God are heavenly beings.

## Who set a limit for the sea?

## Job 38:8

The sea burst out like a baby being born.

Who shut doors around the sea?

## Job 38:9

I dressed the sea in clouds.

I wrapped it in thick darkness like a blanket.

## Job 38:10

I set a limit for the sea.

I put doors there and barred them shut.

## Job 38:11

I told the sea, ‘You may come this far.

Here your proud waves must stop.’

**Pause and answer**

Who tells the waves where to stop?

## Who calls the morning?

## Job 38:12

Job, have you ever told the morning to come?

Have you shown the dawn where to shine?

## Job 38:13

Have you made daylight reach the earth’s far edges and shake the wicked loose?

## Job 38:14

Daylight shows the earth’s shape.

It looks like clay when a stamp presses into it.

The hills stand out like folds in cloth.

## Job 38:15

Those who do evil lose their light.

The arm they raise to strike breaks.

## Where do light and darkness live?

## Job 38:16

Have you reached the springs deep under the sea?

Have you walked through its deepest valleys?

## Job 38:17

Have you seen the gates of death?

Have you seen the gates leading into death’s shadow?

## Job 38:18

Have you seen how far the earth reaches?

Tell me, if you know all this.

## Job 38:19

Which road leads to the home of light?

Where does darkness live?

## Job 38:20

Could you lead darkness back to its border?

Do you know the paths to its home?

## Job 38:21

Surely you know!

You were already alive then!

You have lived so many years!

**What does the LORD mean?**

Job was born long after the earth began. The LORD’s words remind Job how little of this he has seen.

## Who sends the rain?

## Job 38:22

Have you entered my rooms full of snow?

Have you seen where I keep the hail?

## Job 38:23

I keep them for times of trouble.

I keep them for the day of battle.

**A word to know**

Hail is ice that falls from the sky in lumps.

## Job 38:24

Which way does lightning spread?

Which way does the east wind blow across the earth?

## Job 38:25

Who cuts a path for rushing water?

Who opens a way for the lightning?

## Job 38:26

Who sends rain to land where nothing grows?

Who waters a desert where no one lives?

## Job 38:27

Who gives that thirsty land enough water?

Who makes soft new grass grow there?

## Where do dew and ice come from?

## Job 38:28

Does rain have a father?

Who brings drops of dew into being?

## Job 38:29

Who gives birth to the ice?

Who brings frost down from the sky?

## Job 38:30

Water grows as hard as stone.

Ice covers even the deep water.

## Who guides the stars?

## Job 38:31

Can you tie together the stars called the Pleiades?

Can you loosen the belt of stars called Orion’s belt?

## Job 38:32

Can you bring out each group of stars in its season?

Can you guide the stars called the Bear and her cubs?

**Pictures in the stars**

People gave names to patterns they saw in the stars. One pattern reminded them of a belt. Another reminded them of bears.

## Job 38:33

Do you know the rules the skies follow?

Can you make those rules govern the earth?

## Who gives the clouds their orders?

## Job 38:34

Can you call out to the clouds and make them cover you with a flood?

## Job 38:35

Can you send flashes of lightning on their way?

Do they say to you, ‘Here we are’?

## Job 38:36

Who makes people wise deep inside?

Who gives them minds that understand?

## Job 38:37

Who is wise enough to count the clouds?

Who can tip the sky’s water jars

## Job 38:38

when dust hardens into a lump and clumps of earth stick together?

**What are the sky’s water jars?**

Think of someone tipping a jar to pour water. The LORD uses this picture to describe rain falling from clouds.

## Who feeds the lions and the ravens?

## Job 38:39

Can you hunt food for a mother lion?

Can you find enough to feed the young lions

## Job 38:40

when they crouch in their dens or hide in bushes, waiting for an animal?

## Job 38:41

Who feeds the raven when its young have no food?

The young birds wander about and cry to God.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what do the young ravens do when they need food?
