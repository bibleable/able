# Job 33 — Elihu tells Job how God speaks

**About this chapter**

Elihu speaks to Job. First he repeats what he heard Job say. Then Elihu explains how he believes God warns people and saves lives.

## Elihu asks Job to listen

## Job 33:1

Job, listen to me now.

Please hear all I have to say.

## Job 33:2

I am ready to speak.

The words are on my tongue.

## Job 33:3

I want to speak honestly.

I will tell you what I know.

## Job 33:4

The Spirit of God made me.

Almighty God gives me breath and life.

## Job 33:5

If I am wrong, show me how.

Get your reasons ready and face me.

## Job 33:6

You and I stand before the same God.

God shaped me from clay too.

![A brown jar with a round belly and a narrow neck.](../pictures/clay-jar.svg)

**Look at the jar**

A potter shapes soft clay to make a jar. Elihu uses clay to remind Job that God made them both.

## Job 33:7

You have no reason to fear me.

I will not use my strength against you.

## Elihu repeats what he heard Job say

## Job 33:8

Job, I heard what you said.

I heard you say these words.

## Job 33:9

‘I am clean.

I have done nothing wrong.

There is no evil in me.

## Job 33:10

Yet God looks for reasons to blame me.

God treats me as an enemy.

## Job 33:11

God locks my feet between heavy pieces of wood.

God watches every step I take.’

**Pause and answer**

In the words Elihu remembers, who watches Job’s steps?

## Elihu gives his answer

## Job 33:12

Job, I think you are wrong about this.

Here is my answer.

God is greater than any of us.

## Job 33:13

Why complain that God never answers our questions?

## Job 33:14

God speaks in more than one way.

Yet people miss what God says.

## Job 33:15

Think of someone sleeping deeply in bed.

God can show that person something in a dream.

## Job 33:16

God makes the sleeper hear a warning.

The warning can fill the sleeper with fear.

## Job 33:17

God wants people to turn from doing wrong.

God wants to keep them from becoming proud.

## Job 33:18

God wants to save them from the Pit.

God wants to keep a sword from taking their lives.

**What is the Pit?**

The Pit is a name for the place of the dead. Here, saving someone from the Pit means keeping that person alive.

## Elihu describes a sick man

## Job 33:19

Pain can also teach a person.

Think of a man lying in bed.

His bones ache without a break.

## Job 33:20

He cannot stand the sight of bread.

Even his favorite food makes him feel sick.

## Job 33:21

His body grows thin.

Bones that once were hidden now stick out.

## Job 33:22

He comes close to the Pit.

The messengers of death are near.

## A messenger speaks for the sick man

## Job 33:23

Suppose a messenger comes to help the sick man.

Only one in a thousand might speak for him.

The messenger shows him the right thing to do.

## Job 33:24

The messenger cares about him and says,

‘Spare him from going down to the Pit.

I have found the price to set him free.’

## Job 33:25

Then the man’s skin becomes fresh like a child’s.

He feels young again.

## Job 33:26

He prays, and God welcomes him.

He sees God’s face and shouts with joy.

God puts things right between them again.

## The man sings about what God did

## Job 33:27

Then the man sings to others,

‘I sinned.

I twisted what was right.

Yet I escaped the punishment I deserved.

## Job 33:28

God saved me from going down to the Pit.

I will live to see the light.’

## Job 33:29

God does these things for a person again.

God may do them twice or even three times.

## Job 33:30

God brings that person back from the Pit.

That person lives to see the light again.

**Pause and answer**

Tell in your own words what seeing the light means for the sick man.

## Elihu invites Job to answer

## Job 33:31

Job, pay attention and listen to me.

Be quiet now so I can speak.

## Job 33:32

If you have an answer, speak up.

I would like to show that you are right.

## Job 33:33

If you have no answer, listen quietly.

I will teach you how to be wise.
