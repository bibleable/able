# Job 17 — Job asks where his hope has gone

**About this chapter**

Job feels close to death. His friends speak as if better days are near. Job asks what will become of his hope.

## Job asks God to stand by him

## Job 17:1

My spirit is broken.

My days are going out like a flame.

The grave is waiting for me.

## Job 17:2

People stand around me and make fun of me.

I have to watch them act against me.

## Job 17:3

God, you ask for a pledge.

Please give me that pledge yourself.

Who else will promise to answer for me?

**Reading help**

A pledge is a firm promise. Job asks God himself to stand behind him.

## Job 17:4

You have kept these people from understanding.

So you will not give them honor.

**Pause and answer**

Whom does Job ask to give him a pledge?

## Job 17:5

If a man speaks against his friends for money, his children will lose their sight.

## People mock Job

## Job 17:6

God has made my name a word people use to mock me.

People spit in my face.

## Job 17:7

Grief has made my eyes grow dim.

My body is like a shadow of what it was.

## Job 17:8

People who do right are shocked at what they see.

The innocent speak out against those who reject God.

## Job 17:9

Still, those who do right keep going.

Those with clean hands grow stronger.

Clean hands here mean that a person has done no wrong.

**Pause and answer**

What does Job mean by clean hands?

## Job answers his friends

## Job 17:10

Come back, all of you.

Try again to answer me.

I still cannot find one wise person among you.

## Job 17:11

My days have slipped away.

My plans have come to an end.

So have the hopes I held deep inside.

## Job 17:12

My friends speak as if night were day.

They say light is near when all I see is darkness.

![Stars in a dark sky above the horizon. Job says he still sees darkness when his friends speak of light.](../pictures/job-night-without-dawn.svg)

**Pause and answer**

What do Job’s friends say is near?

## Job asks what will happen to his hope

## Job 17:13

Suppose I look to Sheol as my home.

Sheol is the place of the dead.

Suppose I make my bed there in the dark.

## Job 17:14

Suppose I call decay my father.

Suppose I call the worm my mother or my sister.

**Reading help**

Job imagines the grave as his home. Decay and worms would be as close to him there as family.

## Job 17:15

Where would my hope be then?

Who could see any hope for me?

## Job 17:16

Will my hope go down to the gates of Sheol?

Will my hope and I go down into the dust together?

**Pause and answer**

What does Job picture going down into the dust with him?
