# Job 22 — Eliphaz accuses Job of hurting others

**About this chapter**

Eliphaz claims Job has ignored people who needed help. He tells Job what he thinks will happen if Job returns to God.

## Eliphaz questions Job

## Job 22:1

Eliphaz the Temanite answered Job.

## Job 22:2

Job, what can anyone do for God?

Can even a wise person give him anything he needs?

## Job 22:3

Does it please the Almighty that you do right?

What does God gain if you live without fault?

## Job 22:4

Does God correct you because you respect him?

Is that why he brings you before him for judgment?

## Job 22:5

Haven’t you done great harm?

Is there any end to the wrong you do?

**Pause and answer**

Who is accusing Job?

## What Eliphaz says Job did

## Job 22:6

You made your own people hand over things to guarantee their debts.

You had no need to do that.

You even took clothes from people who had nothing else to wear.

## Job 22:7

You gave no water to tired people.

You kept food from people who were starving.

![A drinking cup. Eliphaz says Job refused water to tired people.](../pictures/cup.svg)

## Job 22:8

Yet a powerful man owned the land.

A man people honored lived there.

## Job 22:9

You sent widows away with nothing.

Children without fathers lost what little strength they had.

## Job 22:10

That is why traps surround you.

Danger comes suddenly and fills you with fear.

## Job 22:11

Darkness keeps you from seeing.

Floodwater covers you.

**Pause and answer**

What does Eliphaz say Job kept from hungry people?

## What Eliphaz thinks Job believes

## Job 22:12

Isn’t God high in the heavens?

Look how high even the farthest stars are!

## Job 22:13

Yet you say, “What does God know?

How can he judge us through thick darkness?

## Job 22:14

“Thick clouds keep God from seeing us as he walks across the sky.”

**Reading help**

Eliphaz is telling Job what he thinks Job has said about God.

## Eliphaz recalls people who turned from God

## Job 22:15

Will you keep following the old path that wicked people took?

## Job 22:16

They died before their time.

A flood swept away the very ground they built on.

## Job 22:17

They told God, “Leave us alone!

What can the Almighty do to us?”

## Job 22:18

Yet God filled their houses with good things.

I want nothing to do with their advice.

**Pause and answer**

Who filled those houses with good things?

## Job 22:19

People who do right see what happens and rejoice.

Innocent people make fun of the wicked.

## Job 22:20

They say, “Our enemies are gone.

Fire has burned up all their extra wealth.”

## Eliphaz tells Job to make peace with God

## Job 22:21

Job, make peace with God.

Then good things will come to you.

## Job 22:22

Listen to what God teaches you.

Keep his words in your heart.

Remember them.

## Job 22:23

If you return to the Almighty, he will restore you.

Put a stop to wrongdoing in your home.

## Job 22:24

Lay your gold in the dirt.

Leave even the gold from Ophir among the stones in the valley.

## Job 22:25

Then the Almighty himself will be your treasure.

You will value him like gold and the finest silver.

**Pause and answer**

What does Eliphaz say Job could treasure more than gold?

## What Eliphaz expects God to do

## Job 22:26

You will find joy in the Almighty.

You will lift your face to God.

## Job 22:27

You will pray to God, and he will hear you.

You will keep the promises you made to him.

## Job 22:28

You will carry out what you decide to do.

Light will shine on the path ahead of you.

## Job 22:29

When others fall low, you will say, “Lift them up!” God will rescue those who have fallen low.

## Job 22:30

God will rescue even someone who is guilty.

He will do this because your hands are clean.

**Reading help**

Clean hands picture a person who does what is right.

**Pause and answer**

What does Eliphaz say God will do when Job prays?
