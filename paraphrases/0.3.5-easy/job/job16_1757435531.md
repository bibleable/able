# Job 16 — Job says his friends give him no comfort

**About this chapter**

Job says his friends’ words have only added to his pain. He describes how he feels and asks for someone to speak for him.

## Job answers his friends

## Job 16:1

Then Job answered his friends.

## Job 16:2

I have heard words like these many times.

You have all done a poor job of comforting me.

## Job 16:3

Will your long speeches ever end?

What makes you keep speaking against me?

## Job 16:4

Suppose you were suffering as I am.

I too could pile up words against you.

I could shake my head at you.

## Job 16:5

But I would speak to give you courage.

My words would help ease your pain.

**Pause and answer**

How does Job say he would speak to a suffering friend?

## Job tells God how worn out he feels

## Job 16:6

When I speak, my own pain stays with me.

If I keep quiet, how will it go away?

## Job 16:7

God has worn me out.

God, you have brought ruin on my whole family.

## Job 16:8

You have tied me up.

It is as if that counts as evidence against me.

Even my weak body seems to accuse me.

## Job 16:9

In his anger God tears at me like an enemy.

He grinds his teeth at me.

His fierce stare seems to go right through me.

**Pause and answer**

What seems to accuse Job besides other people’s words?

## Job feels surrounded

## Job 16:10

People open their mouths to shout at me.

They slap my cheeks to shame me.

They gather together against me.

## Job 16:11

God has handed me over to people who do wrong.

He has left me in the grip of cruel people.

## Job 16:12

I was at peace until God shattered me.

He took me by the neck and crushed me.

I feel like a target he has set up.

## Job 16:13

I picture archers all around me.

God pierces my kidneys without mercy.

The bitter fluid inside me spills onto the ground.

## Job 16:14

He wounds me again and again.

He rushes at me like a strong warrior.

**Pause and answer**

What does Job compare himself to when he describes the archers?

## Job wears clothes of mourning

## Job 16:15

I sewed sackcloth over my skin.

I buried my horn in the dust.

![A piece of sackcloth. Thick threads cross over and under one another.](../pictures/job-sackcloth.svg)

**Reading help**

Sackcloth was rough cloth people wore to show grief. An animal’s horn was a picture of strength. Job pictures his strength lying in the dust.

## Job 16:16

My face is red from crying.

Dark shadows ring my eyes.

## Job 16:17

Yet I have hurt no one with my hands.

My prayer to God is sincere.

**Pause and answer**

Why is Job’s face red?

## Job calls for a witness

## Job 16:18

Earth, do not cover my blood!

Do not let my cry for help fall silent.

## Job 16:19

Even now I have a witness in heaven.

Someone there speaks on my behalf.

## Job 16:20

My own friends make fun of me.

My eyes fill with tears as I turn to God.

## Job 16:21

If only someone could plead my case with God the way one person pleads with a neighbor!

## Job 16:22

In a few years I will take the road no one comes back from.

**Reading help**

Job pictures death as a road. He means that he expects to die.

**Pause and answer**

Where does Job say his witness is?
