# Job 11 — Zophar tells Job to turn to God

**About this chapter**

Zophar answers Job for the first time. He believes Job has done wrong. He tells Job what he thinks will happen if Job turns to God.

## Zophar challenges Job’s words

## Job 11:1

Then Zophar the Naamathite, one of Job’s friends, answered him.

## Job 11:2

Job, should all your words go unanswered?

Does talking so much prove you are right?

## Job 11:3

Must the rest of us keep quiet while you talk?

Should no one correct you when you mock us?

## Job 11:4

You say to God, “What I teach is right.

You can see that I am innocent.”

**Pause and answer**

Who says Job has spoken too much?

## What Zophar wishes God would say

## Job 11:5

If only God would speak against you himself!

## Job 11:6

I wish he would show you the secrets of wisdom.

True wisdom has two sides.

Job, God is punishing you less than your sins deserve.

**Reading help**

Zophar thinks there is more to know than Job can see. The claim about Job’s guilt comes from Zophar.

**Pause and answer**

Who claims Job deserves punishment?

## How far does God’s wisdom reach?

## Job 11:7

Can you understand everything about God?

Can you find the limits of the Almighty?

## Job 11:8

These things are higher than the heavens.

How can you reach them?

They go deeper than Sheol, the place of the dead.

How much can you know?

## Job 11:9

They stretch farther than the earth.

They are wider than the sea.

## Job 11:10

Suppose God comes to put you in prison.

Suppose he calls a court together.

Who could stop him?

## Job 11:11

God knows when people lie.

When he sees someone do wrong, does he overlook it?

## Job 11:12

Can a foolish person become wise?

That is like expecting a wild donkey to give birth to a human baby!

**Pause and answer**

What does Zophar say God knows about people who lie?

## Zophar urges Job to pray

## Job 11:13

Job, turn your whole heart toward God.

Lift your hands to him in prayer.

## Job 11:14

Stop doing what is wrong.

Make sure no one in your home treats others unfairly.

## Job 11:15

Then you can hold your head high without shame.

You will stand firm without fear.

**Pause and answer**

What does Zophar tell Job to change in his home?

## What Zophar expects to happen

## Job 11:16

You will forget your suffering.

You will remember it like water that has flowed past.

![A stream with water in one season and a dry bed later. Zophar says Job will remember his trouble like water that has passed.](../pictures/job-seasonal-stream.svg)

## Job 11:17

Your life will be brighter than the sun at noon.

Even your dark times will be like morning light.

## Job 11:18

You will feel safe because you have hope.

You will look around and lie down in safety.

## Job 11:19

You will rest without fear.

Many people will come asking for your help and goodwill.

**Pause and answer**

What does Zophar compare the memory of suffering to?

## Job 11:20

But the eyes of wicked people will fail them.

They will find no way to escape.

Their only hope will be to breathe their last.
