# Job 35 — Elihu asks Job to look at the sky

**About this chapter**

Elihu speaks to Job and his friends. Elihu talks about the good and the harm people do. He also tells them why he thinks some cries for help go unanswered.

## Elihu repeats Job’s question

## Job 35:1

Elihu kept speaking.

## Job 35:2

Job, you say, ‘I am more just than God.’

Do you think that is a fair thing to say?

## Job 35:3

You ask, ‘What good does it do me to keep from sinning?

What do I gain?’

## Job 35:4

I will answer you, Job.

Your friends will hear my answer too.

## Elihu points to the clouds

## Job 35:5

Look up at the sky.

See how high the clouds are above you.

![A cloud, then a small wisp, then clear sky.](../pictures/job-fading-cloud.svg)

**Look at the first picture**

Find the cloud. Think of looking up from the ground. Elihu asks Job to look that high above himself.

## Job 35:6

When you sin, how does that hurt God?

Suppose you keep doing wrong.

What can you do to God?

## Job 35:7

When you do right, what do you give God?

What does God receive from your hand?

## Job 35:8

The wrong you do hurts other people like you.

The good you do helps other people like you.

**Pause and answer**

Who does Elihu say you help when you do good?

## People cry for help

## Job 35:9

People cry out when powerful people treat them cruelly.

They beg for someone to free them.

## Job 35:10

Yet no one asks, ‘Where is God who made me?

Where is God who gives us songs at night?

## Job 35:11

God teaches us more than he teaches the animals.

God makes us wiser than the birds.’

## Job 35:12

People cry out there, but God gives no answer.

Evil people are full of pride.

## Job 35:13

God does not listen to empty cries for help.

Almighty God pays no attention to those cries.

**What are empty cries?**

Elihu means people can ask God for help without really wanting to turn to God.

## Elihu speaks about Job’s case

## Job 35:14

Job, why would God listen when you say this?

You say you cannot see God.

You say God has your case before him.

Now you must wait for God.

## Job 35:15

You also say God is angry but has not punished anyone.

You say God hardly notices when people act foolishly.

## Job 35:16

So Job speaks, but his words do no good.

He says much about things he does not understand.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what does Elihu say Job is waiting for?
