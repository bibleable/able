# Job 34 — Elihu says God judges fairly

**About this chapter**

Elihu is still speaking. He asks his listeners to consider whether God is fair. Elihu also gives his own view of Job’s words.

## Elihu asks the listeners to test his words

## Job 34:1

Elihu went on speaking.

## Job 34:2

You wise men, listen to me.

You have learned a great deal.

Hear what I say.

## Job 34:3

You taste food with your mouth.

In the same way, your ears test words.

**What does Elihu mean?**

When you taste food, you decide what you think of it. Elihu asks his listeners to think carefully about the words they hear.

## Job 34:4

Let us decide what is right.

Together we can learn what is good.

## Elihu tells the listeners what he heard Job say

## Job 34:5

Job has said, ‘I have done what is right.

Yet God has denied me a fair hearing.

## Job 34:6

Why would I lie about my case?

My wound will never heal.

Yet I have done nothing wrong.’

## Job 34:7

Who else is like Job?

He drinks in mocking words like water.

**A picture in words**

People drink water easily. Elihu says Job takes in mocking words just as easily.

## Job 34:8

He spends time with people who do evil.

He walks alongside them.

## Job 34:9

For Job has said, ‘What good does it do anyone to take joy in God?’

## Elihu says God judges fairly

## Job 34:10

You men who understand, listen to me.

God would never do wrong.

Almighty God would never treat anyone unfairly.

## Job 34:11

God sees how each person lives.

God pays each one back for what they do.

## Job 34:12

Yes, this is true.

God does no evil.

Almighty God never twists a fair decision.

## Job 34:13

Who put God in charge of the earth?

Who chose God to rule the whole world?

## Job 34:14

Suppose God decided to take back his Spirit.

Suppose God took back the breath he gives.

## Job 34:15

Then all living things would die together.

Every human being would turn to dust again.

## God made rulers and poor people alike

## Job 34:16

If you understand, listen to this.

Hear what I have to say.

## Job 34:17

Could anyone who hates fairness rule the world?

God is fair and strong.

Will you say God is guilty?

## Job 34:18

God can tell a king, ‘You are worthless.’

God can tell leaders, ‘You do evil.’

## Job 34:19

God gives princes no special treatment.

Rich people count no more than poor people.

God made them all.

**Pause and answer**

Why does Elihu say rich and poor people count the same to God?

## Job 34:20

People can die in a moment, even at midnight.

Their bodies shake, and they die.

Even powerful people fall without anyone touching them.

## God sees what rulers do

## Job 34:21

God watches how each person lives.

God sees every step they take.

## Job 34:22

People who do evil may try to hide.

Even the deepest darkness cannot hide them from God.

## Job 34:23

God already knows enough to judge a person.

God has no need to call that person into court.

## Job 34:24

God brings down powerful people without asking questions.

God puts others in their place.

## Job 34:25

God knows what those rulers have done.

In the night God brings them down and crushes them.

## Job 34:26

God punishes them for doing evil.

Everyone can see it happen.

## Job 34:27

Those rulers turned away from God.

They paid no attention to how God wanted them to live.

## Job 34:28

Poor people cried out because those rulers hurt them.

God heard the people cry for help.

![An eye beside an ear.](../pictures/eye-and-ear.svg)

**Look at the picture**

The eye can remind you that God sees what rulers do. The ear can remind you that God hears people cry for help.

## Elihu speaks about God’s silence

## Job 34:29

If God stays silent, who can call him guilty?

If God hides his face, who can see him?

Still, God watches over each person and each nation.

## Job 34:30

God keeps rulers who reject him from taking power.

God keeps them from trapping the people.

## Job 34:31

Suppose someone said to God, ‘I have suffered my punishment.

I will stop doing wrong.

## Job 34:32

Show me the wrong I cannot see.

If I have done wrong, I will stop.’

## Job 34:33

Job, you have rejected God.

Should God repay you on terms you set?

You must choose for yourself.

Tell me what you know.

## Elihu accuses Job

## Job 34:34

Wise people who listen to me will say this.

## Job 34:35

‘Job speaks about things he does not know.

His words show how little he understands.’

## Job 34:36

I wish someone would test Job’s answers thoroughly.

He answers the way an evil man would.

## Job 34:37

Job has sinned.

Now he also openly turns against God.

Here among us he claps his hands in scorn.

He says more and more against God.

**A word to know**

Scorn means treating someone as if they deserve no respect. Elihu thinks Job’s clapping shows scorn.

**Pause and answer**

In your own words, what does Elihu accuse Job of doing?
