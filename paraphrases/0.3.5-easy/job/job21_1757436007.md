# Job 21 — Job asks why wicked people prosper

**About this chapter**

Job asks his friends to consider people who do wrong yet grow old in comfort. He tells them why their answers bring him no comfort.

## Job asks his friends to listen

## Job 21:1

Job answered his friends.

## Job 21:2

Listen carefully to what I say.

You can comfort me by listening.

## Job 21:3

Be patient while I speak.

When I finish, you can make fun of me again.

## Job 21:4

Am I complaining to another human being?

Why should I not lose patience?

## Job 21:5

Look at me.

Let what you see shock you into silence.

Put your hand over your mouth.

## Job 21:6

When I think about it, fear grips me.

My whole body shakes.

**Pause and answer**

How does Job say his friends can comfort him?

## Some wicked people live in safety

## Job 21:7

Why do wicked people live so long?

Why do they grow old and gain power?

## Job 21:8

They see their children settle down around them.

They live to see their families grow.

## Job 21:9

They feel safe at home.

God does not strike them with his rod to punish them.

## Job 21:10

Their bulls breed successfully.

Their cows carry their calves until they give birth.

## Job 21:11

Their little children pour outside like a flock of sheep.

The children skip and dance.

## Job 21:12

They sing to the sound of tambourines and lyres.

They celebrate as the flute plays.

A lyre is a musical instrument with strings.

## Job 21:13

They have plenty throughout their lives.

Then they go in peace to Sheol, the place of the dead.

**Pause and answer**

What do the children do when they hear music?

## What these people say to God

## Job 21:14

Yet these people tell God, “Leave us alone.

We have no wish to learn your ways.

## Job 21:15

“Who is the Almighty that we should serve him?

What would we gain by praying to him?”

## Job 21:16

Still, these people do not control their own good fortune.

I keep far away from their advice.

## Job questions what his friends have said

## Job 21:17

How often does the lamp of a wicked person go out?

How often does disaster come?

How often does God destroy such people in his anger?

![An oil lamp with its flame still burning.](../pictures/davids-lamp.svg)

**Reading help**

Bildad spoke of a lamp going out when a wicked person dies. Job asks how often that really happens.

## Job 21:18

Does the wind really carry them off like straw?

Does a storm sweep them away like dry husks of grain?

## Job 21:19

People say, “God saves a man’s punishment for his children.”

Let the man himself feel the punishment for what he did.

## Job 21:20

Let him see his own ruin.

Let him drink the cup of the Almighty’s anger himself.

**Reading help**

Drinking the cup means facing the punishment oneself.

## Job 21:21

Once his months of life run out, what does he care about the family he leaves behind?

**Pause and answer**

Who does Job think should feel the punishment for wrongdoing?

## Two men with different lives

## Job 21:22

Who can teach God anything?

God judges even those in the highest places.

## Job 21:23

One man dies with his strength still in him.

He has lived in safety and comfort.

## Job 21:24

He has had plenty to eat.

Even the marrow inside his bones is healthy.

## Job 21:25

Another man dies full of sorrow.

He has never known what it means to have plenty.

## Job 21:26

Yet both men lie in the dust.

Worms cover them both.

**Pause and answer**

What happens to both men after they die?

## Job asks what travelers have seen

## Job 21:27

My friends, I know what you are thinking.

I know how you plan to use your words against me.

## Job 21:28

You ask, “Where is the great man’s house now?

Where are the tents where wicked people lived?”

## Job 21:29

Have you asked people who travel the roads?

Will you listen to what they have seen?

## Job 21:30

They tell of a wicked man who escapes disaster.

He comes through the day of anger unharmed.

## Job 21:31

Who tells such a man to his face what he has done wrong?

Who makes him pay for what he did?

## Job 21:32

People carry him to his grave.

Someone keeps watch over his tomb.

## Job 21:33

He rests beneath the soil of the valley as if it were sweet to him.

Everyone follows him to the grave.

Countless others have gone before him.

## Job 21:34

How can words like yours bring me comfort?

Your answers are full of things that are false.

**Pause and answer**

Whom does Job ask his friends to listen to besides him?
