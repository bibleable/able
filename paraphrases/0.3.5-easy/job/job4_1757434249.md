# Job 4 — Eliphaz answers Job

**About this chapter**

Job’s friend Eliphaz speaks for the first time. He tells Job what he believes about suffering. Then he describes a voice he heard at night.

## Eliphaz remembers how Job helped others

## Job 4:1

Eliphaz the Temanite, one of Job’s friends, answered him.

## Job 4:2

Job, will it wear you out if I speak?

Still, how can I keep quiet?

## Job 4:3

You have taught many people.

When their hands grew weak, you gave them strength.

## Job 4:4

When people stumbled, your words helped them stand.

When their knees gave way, you helped them stand firm.

**Reading help**

Eliphaz pictures people whose knees give way. He remembers how Job encouraged people who felt weak.

## Job 4:5

Now trouble has come to you, and you are tired.

It has struck you, and you are frightened.

## Job 4:6

You have always shown deep respect for God.

Does that give you courage now?

You have lived honestly.

Does that give you hope?

**Pause and answer**

How had Job helped people who were losing strength?

## Eliphaz compares doing wrong to planting seeds

## Job 4:7

Please think about this, Job.

When has an innocent person ever perished?

Where have people who do right been wiped out?

## Job 4:8

Here is what I have seen.

Those who do wrong are like farmers planting seeds.

They plant trouble, and trouble is the crop they gather.

![A stalk of grain. A farmer plants seed and later gathers a crop. Eliphaz uses that picture for trouble.](../pictures/grain.svg)

## Job 4:9

God breathes on them, and they die.

His anger sweeps them away like a blast of wind.

## Job 4:10

A lion roars.

A fierce lion growls.

Yet even young lions lose their teeth when those teeth break.

## Job 4:11

The old lion dies when it finds nothing to eat.

The young cubs of the mother lion scatter.

**Pause and answer**

In Eliphaz’s picture, what crop comes from planting trouble?

## Eliphaz hears a whisper at night

## Job 4:12

A message came to me in secret.

I heard only a whisper.

## Job 4:13

At night, when people fall into deep sleep, I saw things that troubled me.

## Job 4:14

I was so afraid that my whole body shook.

## Job 4:15

A spirit slipped past my face.

The hair on my body stood on end.

## Job 4:16

Then it stood still.

I saw a shape but could not tell what it looked like.

I heard a voice whisper.

**Pause and answer**

What did Eliphaz hear when the shape stood still?

## What the voice asked

## Job 4:17

“Can a human being do what is right better than God?

Can anyone be more free from wrong than the God who made them?

## Job 4:18

“God does not put his trust in his servants.

He finds fault even with his angels.

## Job 4:19

“How much less can he trust people?

They live in houses of clay that stand on dust.

They can be crushed as easily as a moth.

**Reading help**

The voice pictures a human body as a house made of clay. It says a person’s life can break as easily as clay.

## Job 4:20

“Between morning and evening their lives can break apart.

They die and are gone forever.

No one even notices.

## Job 4:21

“Isn’t their life like a tent when someone pulls up its ropes?

They die before they learn wisdom.”

**Reading help**

Ropes hold a tent in place. When they come loose, the tent can fall. The voice uses that picture for the end of a life.

**Pause and answer**

What happens to a tent when someone pulls up its ropes?
