# Job 19 — Job asks for pity and speaks of his Redeemer

**About this chapter**

Job tells his friends how much their words hurt. He asks for kindness. He also tells them why he still hopes to see God.

## Job answers his friends

## Job 19:1

Job answered his friends.

## Job 19:2

How long will you keep hurting me?

Your words crush me.

## Job 19:3

Ten times now you have spoken against me.

You treat me badly and feel no shame.

## Job 19:4

Even if I have done wrong, that wrong is mine to answer for.

## Job 19:5

You think you are better than I am.

You use my shame against me.

## Job 19:6

Then understand this.

God has wronged me.

I feel caught in a net he has drawn around me.

**Pause and answer**

What does Job say his friends use against him?

## Job feels trapped

## Job 19:7

I cry out, “Someone is hurting me!”

No one answers.

I call for help, but no one gives me justice.

## Job 19:8

God has blocked my way.

I cannot get past.

Darkness covers every path ahead of me.

## Job 19:9

He has taken away my honor.

It is as if he took a crown from my head.

## Job 19:10

He tears me down on every side until nothing is left.

He pulls up my hope like a tree, roots and all.

## Job 19:11

God burns with anger against me.

He treats me as one of his enemies.

## Job 19:12

His troops come toward me together.

They pile up earth to reach me.

They camp all around my tent.

**Pause and answer**

What does Job compare his lost hope to?

## People Job loves turn away

## Job 19:13

God has taken my brothers far from me.

The people I used to know have left me.

## Job 19:14

My relatives have let me down.

My friends have forgotten me.

## Job 19:15

Even my guests and the women who serve me treat me as a stranger.

To them I am a foreigner.

## Job 19:16

I call my servant and beg him to come.

He gives me no answer.

## Job 19:17

My wife cannot stand the smell of my breath.

Even my own family cannot stand to be near me.

## Job 19:18

Even little boys look down on me.

When I come near, they make fun of me.

## Job 19:19

All my closest friends look down on me.

The people I love have turned against me.

**Pause and answer**

How do Job’s closest friends treat him?

## Job asks his friends for pity

## Job 19:20

My skin and flesh cling to my bones.

I have barely escaped with my life.

## Job 19:21

My friends, have pity on me!

Please have pity.

God’s hand has struck me.

## Job 19:22

Why do you keep hurting me as God does?

It is as if you can never get enough of eating my flesh.

**Reading help**

Job is describing how much his friends’ attacks hurt him.

## Job wants his words to last

## Job 19:23

I wish someone would write down my words.

I want someone to put them in a book.

![An open scroll holds lines of writing. Job wants a lasting record of his words.](../pictures/scroll.svg)

## Job 19:24

They could use a pointed iron tool to write on lead, a soft metal.

Or they could carve my words in stone to last forever.

**Pause and answer**

Why does Job want his words carved in stone?

## Job 19:25

I know my Redeemer lives.

In the end he will stand on the earth.

**Reading help**

A redeemer is someone who stands up for another person to help or rescue them. Job calls this person his Redeemer.

## Job 19:26

Even after my skin wastes away, I will see God in my own body.

## Job 19:27

I myself will see him with my own eyes.

He will be no stranger to me.

How I long for that!

**Pause and answer**

Whom does Job long to see with his own eyes?

## Job warns his friends

## Job 19:28

You may say, “Job himself is the cause of his trouble.

Let us keep after him.”

## Job 19:29

Then you too should fear the sword.

Anger can bring punishment by the sword.

Remember that you too will face judgment.
