# Job 30 — Job cries out for help

**About this chapter**

Job remembers when people listened to him. Now people laugh at him. He tells God how much it hurts to call for help and hear no answer.

## Job remembers the fathers of the men who mock him

## Job 30:1

Now men younger than I am laugh at me.

I would not have let their fathers work beside my sheepdogs.

## Job 30:2

What help could those men have given me?

Their strength was gone.

## Job 30:3

Hunger and poverty had left them thin.

At night they scraped for food on dry, empty land.

## Job 30:4

Among the bushes they picked a plant called mallow.

They ate the roots of broom trees.

## Job 30:5

People drove them away.

People shouted at them as if they were thieves.

## Job 30:6

They lived on the slopes of dry river valleys.

They sheltered among rocks and in holes in the ground.

**Pause and answer**

Where did the hungry men find shelter?

## Job 30:7

They cried out among the bushes.

They huddled together under stinging plants called nettles.

## Job 30:8

I thought of them as people without sense or standing.

Others drove them off the land.

## Job describes the crowd

## Job 30:9

Now these young men sing songs to mock me.

They use my name as an insult.

## Job 30:10

They find me disgusting and keep away from me.

Yet they do not hesitate to spit in my face.

## Job 30:11

God has taken the string off my bow.

He has taken away my strength and made me suffer.

Now these men hold nothing back when they face me.

## Job 30:12

A crowd rises against me on my right.

They set traps for my feet.

I picture them building ramps as if they were attacking a city.

## Job 30:13

They tear up my path.

They gain from my ruin, and no one stops them.

## Job 30:14

They come like an army through a gap in a wall.

Through the broken wall they keep pouring in.

## Job 30:15

Terror comes at me from every side.

My honor blows away in the wind.

My good life has vanished like a cloud.

**Pause and answer**

What does Job compare his vanished good life to?

## Pain keeps Job awake

## Job 30:16

I feel my life draining away.

Suffering holds me in its grip day after day.

## Job 30:17

At night the pain cuts deep into my bones.

The aching never stops.

## Job 30:18

God grabs my clothes with great force.

He takes hold of me by the collar.

## Job 30:19

He throws me into the mud.

I feel like dust and ashes.

**Pause and answer**

When does Job say the pain cuts into his bones?

## Job speaks to God

## Job 30:20

God, I cry to you for help.

You give no answer.

I stand up, and you just look at me.

## Job 30:21

You have turned against me without mercy.

You use your strength against me.

## Job 30:22

You lift me into the wind and sweep me along.

You toss me about in the storm.

## Job 30:23

I know you will bring me to death.

That is where every living person must go.

**Pause and answer**

What happens when Job calls to God for help?

## Job remembers weeping for others

## Job 30:24

Surely no one would strike someone already ruined while that person cries for help!

## Job 30:25

Did I not weep for people in trouble?

Did I not grieve for people in need?

## Job 30:26

I hoped for good, but trouble came.

I looked for light, but darkness fell.

## Job 30:27

Everything inside me churns.

I cannot rest.

Day after day, more suffering meets me.

## Music that sounds like weeping

## Job 30:28

My skin has darkened, though the sun did not burn it.

When the people gather, I stand and cry for help.

## Job 30:29

I feel like a brother to the jackals.

The ostriches seem like my companions.

**Reading help**

Job names two animals that live in wild places. He feels far from the people who once welcomed him.

## Job 30:30

My skin turns black and peels.

Fever burns deep in my bones.

## Job 30:31

My harp plays songs of grief.

My flute sounds like someone weeping.
