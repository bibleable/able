# Job 7 — Job asks God for a moment of rest

**About this chapter**

Job lies awake in pain. He tells God how short his life feels and asks why God watches him so closely.

## Job describes his days and nights

## Job 7:1

Must we work so hard here on earth?

Our days are like the days of a hired worker.

## Job 7:2

A slave longs for shade.

A hired worker waits for pay.

## Job 7:3

For months now, nothing I do seems to matter.

Night after night, I suffer.

## Job 7:4

When I lie down, I ask, “When can I get up?”

The night goes on and on.

I toss and turn until morning.

## Job 7:5

Worms and hard dirt cover my body.

My skin cracks, and my sores ooze.

**Pause and answer**

What does Job do while he waits for morning?

## Life passes quickly

## Job 7:6

My days fly faster than a weaver’s shuttle.

A weaver moves the shuttle quickly through threads to make cloth.

My days run out, and I have no hope.

## Job 7:7

God, remember how short my life is.

It lasts only a breath.

I will never see happy days again.

## Job 7:8

Those who see me now will see me no more.

You will look for me, but I will be gone.

## Job 7:9

A cloud fades away and disappears.

So it is with someone who goes down to Sheol.

Sheol is the place of the dead.

That person does not come back.

![A cloud grows smaller until only clear sky remains. Job uses a fading cloud to picture a life ending.](../pictures/job-fading-cloud.svg)

## Job 7:10

People who die never return to their homes.

Even the place where they lived seems to forget them.

**Pause and answer**

What happens to the cloud that Job describes?

## Job speaks openly to God

## Job 7:11

So I will speak freely.

I will tell you how deeply I hurt.

I will complain because my grief is bitter.

## Job 7:12

Am I the sea?

Am I a monster in its depths?

Why do you keep me under guard?

## Job 7:13

I tell myself, “My bed will give me comfort.

If I lie down, perhaps my pain will ease.”

## Job 7:14

But then, God, you frighten me with dreams.

I see things in the night that terrify me.

## Job 7:15

I would rather choke and die than keep living in this body.

## Job 7:16

I cannot bear my life!

I have no wish to live forever.

Leave me alone.

My days pass as quickly as a breath.

**Pause and answer**

What frightens Job when he lies down?

## Job asks why God keeps watching

## Job 7:17

Why do people matter so much to you?

Why do you pay such close attention to us?

## Job 7:18

You watch us every morning.

Why do you test us every moment?

## Job 7:19

Will you ever look away from me?

Will you give me even enough time to swallow?

## Job 7:20

God, you watch everyone.

If I have sinned, what have I done to you?

Why have you made me your target?

Have I become a burden to you?

## Job 7:21

Why do you not forgive the wrong I have done?

Why do you not take my sin away?

Soon I will lie in the dust of the grave.

You will look for me, but I will be gone.
