# Job 10 — Job asks God why he suffers

**About this chapter**

Job speaks to God about his pain. He remembers the care God took in making him. He asks why that same God now seems to be against him.

## Job asks God to explain

## Job 10:1

I hate my life.

I will say what troubles me.

I will speak about the bitterness I feel inside.

## Job 10:2

This is what I will say to God.

Please do not declare me guilty.

Tell me why you bring a case against me.

## Job 10:3

Does it please you to treat me so harshly?

You made me with your own hands.

Why reject me and favor the plans of wicked people?

**Pause and answer**

What does Job ask God to explain?

## Job asks how God sees him

## Job 10:4

Do you have human eyes?

Do you see things the way people do?

## Job 10:5

Do your days pass like the days of someone who must die?

Are your years like ours?

## Job 10:6

Is that why you look for wrong in me?

Why do you search for some sin I have committed?

## Job 10:7

You know I am not guilty.

No one can rescue me from your power.

**Pause and answer**

Who does Job say could rescue him from God’s power?

## Job remembers how God made him

## Job 10:8

Your hands formed every part of me.

Will you turn against me now and destroy me?

## Job 10:9

Please remember how you shaped me like clay.

Will you turn me back into dust?

![A rounded clay jar with a narrow neck. A potter shapes soft clay by hand.](../pictures/clay-jar.svg)

**Reading help**

Job pictures God as a potter who shaped him with care. When Job speaks of becoming dust again, he means dying.

## Job 10:10

Did you not pour me out like milk?

Did you not give me shape, as milk thickens into cheese?

**Reading help**

Job is remembering how his body took shape before birth. He compares this change to milk becoming thick.

## Job 10:11

You covered me with skin and flesh.

You joined me together with bones and strong bands called sinews.

## Job 10:12

You gave me life and showed me faithful love.

Your care has kept me alive.

**Pause and answer**

What does Job say kept him alive?

## Job feels accused

## Job 10:13

Yet you kept your thoughts hidden from me.

I know what you had in mind.

## Job 10:14

If I sinned, you would notice.

You would hold me responsible for that wrong.

## Job 10:15

If I am guilty, how terrible for me!

Even if I am innocent, I cannot lift my head.

I feel so much shame.

I know how much I suffer.

## Job 10:16

If I held my head high, you would hunt me like a lion.

Again you would show your power against me.

## Job 10:17

You bring new witnesses to speak against me.

Your anger with me grows.

One trouble after another sweeps over me like waves.

**Pause and answer**

What does Job compare his troubles to?

## Job asks for a little comfort

## Job 10:18

Why did you let me be born?

I wish I had died before anyone saw me.

## Job 10:19

I wish it were as if I had never lived.

I wish someone had carried me straight from birth to the grave.

## Job 10:20

I have so few days left.

Leave me alone so I can have a little comfort.

## Job 10:21

Soon I will go where everything is dark and gloomy.

I will never come back.

## Job 10:22

That land is utterly dark.

Shadows cover it and nothing is in order.

Even the light there seems dark.

**Pause and answer**

What does Job ask for in the days he has left?
