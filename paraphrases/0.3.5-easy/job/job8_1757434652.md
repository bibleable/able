# Job 8 — Bildad tells Job to seek God

**About this chapter**

Bildad speaks to his friend Job. He tells Job why he thinks people suffer and what he thinks Job should do.

## Bildad challenges Job

## Job 8:1

Then Bildad the Shuhite, one of Job’s friends, answered Job.

## Job 8:2

Bildad said, “Job, how long will you talk like this?

Your words sound like a strong wind blowing.”

## Job 8:3

Bildad asked, “Does God ever judge unfairly?

Does the Almighty ever twist what is right?”

## Job 8:4

Bildad told Job, “Your children sinned against God.

God let them suffer the result of turning against him.”

**Pause and answer**

Who says Job’s children sinned?

## Bildad urges Job to ask for mercy

## Job 8:5

Bildad urged Job to turn to God with all his heart.

He told Job to ask the Almighty for mercy.

## Job 8:6

He told Job, “If you are pure and honest, God will act for you even now.

He will give you back the home that was rightly yours.

## Job 8:7

“You had little at the start.

In the days ahead, you will have plenty.”

**Pause and answer**

What does Bildad tell Job to ask God for?

## What earlier generations learned

## Job 8:8

Bildad went on, “Ask what past generations knew.

Think about what their parents learned.

## Job 8:9

“We have lived such a short time.

It is as if we were born yesterday.

We know so little.

Our days on earth pass like a shadow.

## Job 8:10

“Those who came before us can teach you.

Their words tell you what they learned.”

**Pause and answer**

Whose words does Bildad tell Job to consider?

## Reeds need water

## Job 8:11

Bildad asked, “Can papyrus grow without a marsh?

Can reeds grow tall without water?”

Papyrus is a tall plant that grows in wet ground.

![A tall reed with a bending stalk. Bildad asks what happens to a reed without water.](../pictures/bending-reed.svg)

## Job 8:12

Even before anyone cuts the young plants, they dry up.

They dry up faster than grass.

## Job 8:13

Bildad said, “That is what happens to people who forget God.

Their hope dies like those plants.

## A web gives way and a plant is pulled up

## Job 8:14

“What such a person trusts is weak.

It is like a spider’s web.

## Job 8:15

“He leans on the web, and it gives way.

He holds on tight, but it cannot hold him up.

**Pause and answer**

What happens when someone leans on the web?

## Job 8:16

“He is like a plant with plenty of water and sunshine.

Its new shoots spread across the garden.

## Job 8:17

“Its roots wrap around a pile of rocks.

The plant finds a place among the stones.

## Job 8:18

“Then someone pulls the plant out by its roots.

It is as if the ground says, ‘I never saw you.’

## Job 8:19

“That is all the joy the plant had there.

Other plants grow from the soil in its place.”

**Pause and answer**

What grows where the old plant stood?

## What Bildad expects for Job

## Job 8:20

Bildad told Job, “God will not turn away an innocent person.

He will not give strength to people who do evil.

## Job 8:21

“He will make you laugh again.

You will shout for joy.

## Job 8:22

“Shame will cover your enemies like clothing.

The homes of those who do evil will be gone.”
