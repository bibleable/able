# Job 2 — Three friends sit with Job

**About this chapter**

Job becomes ill after losing his children and possessions. Three friends hear what has happened and come to be with him.

## The LORD speaks about Job

## Job 2:1

Another day the sons of God came before the LORD.

These beings from heaven came to stand before him.

Satan came to stand before the LORD too.

## Job 2:2

The LORD asked Satan, “Where have you come from?”

Satan answered, “I have been walking back and forth across the earth.”

## Job 2:3

The LORD asked, “Have you noticed my servant Job?

No one on earth is like him.

He is honest and does what is right.

He respects God deeply and turns away from evil.

You urged me to ruin him without cause.

Even now, Job holds on to what is right.”

**Pause and answer**

What did the LORD say Job still held on to?

## Satan questions Job’s loyalty

## Job 2:4

Satan answered, “Skin for skin!

A man will give everything he owns to stay alive.”

## Job 2:5

Satan said, “But hurt Job’s own flesh and bones.

Then he will curse you to your face.”

## Job 2:6

The LORD told Satan, “Very well.

Job is in your power.

But you must let him live.”

**Pause and answer**

What limit did the LORD give Satan?

## Job suffers painful sores

## Job 2:7

Satan left the LORD and struck Job with painful boils.

These swollen sores covered Job from head to foot.

## Job 2:8

Job sat among the ashes.

He picked up a piece of broken pottery.

He scraped his skin with it.

## Job 2:9

Job’s wife asked, “Are you still holding on to what is right?

Curse God and die!”

## Job 2:10

Job answered, “You are speaking like a foolish woman.

We accept good things from God.

Should we refuse to accept trouble?”

Through all this, Job said nothing sinful.

**Pause and answer**

What did Job say people should accept as well as good things?

## Job’s friends come to comfort him

## Job 2:11

Three friends heard about Job’s troubles.

They were Eliphaz the Temanite, Bildad the Shuhite, and Zophar the Naamathite.

Each man left his home.

They met and went together to comfort Job in his grief.

## Job 2:12

From a distance, they could hardly recognize Job.

They cried aloud.

Each friend tore his robe and threw dust over his head.

This showed how deeply they grieved.

## Job 2:13

They sat on the ground with Job for seven days and seven nights.

They saw how much Job was suffering.

So none of them spoke a word to him.

How did the three friends spend those seven days with Job?
