# Titus 2 — Show others how to do good

**About this chapter**

Paul tells Titus how to teach people of different ages.

He asks Titus to show by his own actions what he teaches.

## Help older and younger people learn

## Titus 2:1

Titus, teach what agrees with the truth you have learned.

## Titus 2:2

Teach older men to think clearly and act with dignity.

They should control themselves, trust God, love others, and keep going when life is hard.

## Titus 2:3

Older women should also show respect for God in how they live.

They must avoid spreading lies or becoming dependent on wine.

They should teach what is good.

## Titus 2:4

Then they can teach younger women to love their husbands and children.

## Titus 2:5

Teach them to control themselves, live pure lives, and care for their homes.

They should be kind and yield to their own husbands.

Then people will have no grounds to speak against God’s message.

**Pause and answer**

Who can help younger women learn to care for their families?

## Give people an example to follow

## Titus 2:6

Urge younger men to control themselves too.

## Titus 2:7

Titus, show others how to do good by doing it yourself.

Teach honestly and with dignity.

| When Titus teaches | When Titus acts |
| --- | --- |
| Speak honestly. | Do the good you teach. |

## Titus 2:8

Use helpful words that no one can fairly criticize.

Then anyone who opposes us will feel ashamed.

They will have nothing bad to say about us.

**Pause and answer**

How can Titus show people what doing good looks like?

## Be trustworthy in your work

## Titus 2:9

Tell slaves to obey their masters in everything.

They should try to please them and avoid arguing.

## Titus 2:10

They must never steal from them.

They should show in every way that people can trust them.

Their actions can help others see the beauty of the teaching about God our Savior.

**Pause and answer**

How can someone show that others can trust them?

## God’s grace teaches us how to live

## Titus 2:11

God has shown his grace, bringing salvation to everyone.

**Help with these words**

Grace is kindness God gives freely.

Salvation means God saving people.

## Titus 2:12

His grace teaches us to turn from ways that dishonor God and from selfish desires.

It teaches us to live wisely and do right.

We can honor God in the way we live today.

## Titus 2:13

We wait with joyful hope for our great God and Savior, Jesus Christ, to appear in glory.

## Titus 2:14

Jesus gave himself to set us free from every kind of wrongdoing.

He wanted to make us pure, a people who belong to him.

He wants us to be eager to do good.

## Titus 2:15

Teach these things, Titus.

Use your full authority to encourage people and correct them.

Let no one look down on you.

**Pause and answer**

What does Jesus want his people to be eager to do?
