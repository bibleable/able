# Titus 3 — Be ready to help

**About this chapter**

Paul reminds Titus how God showed people mercy.

He ends with a request for two travelers who need help.

## Show care for everyone

## Titus 3:1

Remind believers to respect and obey those who govern them.

Tell them to be ready to do good whenever they can.

## Titus 3:2

They should speak evil of no one.

They should seek peace and be gentle.

They should treat everyone with care.

## Titus 3:3

We too were once foolish and disobedient.

People led us astray.

All kinds of desires and pleasures ruled us like slave owners.

We lived with envy and ill will.

Others hated us, and we hated one another.

**Pause and answer**

Whom should believers treat with care?

## God gives new life

## Titus 3:4

Then God our Savior showed how kind he is.

He showed his love for people.

## Titus 3:5

God saved us because he had mercy on us.

Our good deeds did not earn this.

He washed us clean and gave us new birth.

The Holy Spirit made us new.

**Help with these words**

New birth is a picture of starting a new life with God.

## Titus 3:6

God gave us his Spirit generously through Jesus Christ our Savior.

## Titus 3:7

By his kindness God put us right with him.

Now we are heirs who look forward to life forever.

**Help with these words**

Heirs are people who will receive what has been set aside for them.

## Titus 3:8

You can trust these words.

Titus, keep teaching these things clearly.

People who believe God should give their time and care to doing good.

This is worthwhile and helps people.

**Pause and answer**

Why did God save us?

## Keep away from useless quarrels

## Titus 3:9

Avoid foolish disputes and arguments about lists of ancestors.

Stay out of quarrels about the law.

These arguments help no one.

## Titus 3:10

If someone divides the church, warn that person once.

Warn them a second time.

If they keep doing it, have nothing more to do with them.

## Titus 3:11

You know such a person has turned aside and keeps sinning.

Their own actions show that they are guilty.

**Pause and answer**

What should Titus do before he stops associating with someone who divides the church?

## Help people who are traveling

## Titus 3:12

I will send either Artemas or Tychicus to you.

When that happens, do your best to join me in Nicopolis.

I have decided to spend the winter there.

## Titus 3:13

Help Zenas the lawyer and Apollos prepare for their journey.

Make sure they have everything they need.

## Titus 3:14

Our people must learn to keep doing good.

When someone has an urgent need, they should help.

Then their lives will bear fruit.

![Grapes growing on a vine](../pictures/vine-and-grapes.svg)

**Help with these words**

Fruit here means the good that comes from what people do.

## Titus 3:15

Everyone here sends greetings.

Greet the believers who love us.

May God show kindness to all of you.

**Pause and answer**

Which two travelers does Paul ask Titus to help?
